# Editorial Decisions

- Translate `Zarb-e-Kaleem` as a poetic English work, not as summary or commentary.
- Urdu is authoritative for semantic meaning; transliteration is authoritative for traceability, stanza boundaries, line order, and repetitions.
- Every nonblank source line maps to one English line. Blank lines separating stanzas are preserved.
- Preserve `ﷺ` only for Muhammad ﷺ. Omit honorifics for all other people and do not use Latin honorific abbreviations.
- Use `Loulak` rather than `La-Law-Laka`.
- Render abbreviated Shahadah expressions as “there is no god but Allah” when the English line requires the complete meaning.
- Keep difficult imagery vivid and retain open questions rather than explaining them.
- Exact source title, slug, and URL are retained in metadata; the English title is supplied separately for readers.
