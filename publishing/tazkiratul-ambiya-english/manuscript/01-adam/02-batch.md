## Shaitan Lost His Chance for Forgiveness

When Shaitan initially refused to prostrate to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, he turned away and stood. The angels performed one prostration first, and then seeing him standing, they performed a second prostration as gratitude.

During the time of Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, Shaitan said to him: "When you speak to Allah ta'ala, do also intercede for me." When Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> presented this before the Lord, Allah ta'ala said: "Go to Iblees and tell him to go and prostrate at the grave of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and I will forgive his sins." Shaitan replied: "I did not prostrate to the living Adam, so how can I prostrate to the dead Adam?" Thus he refused.

According to some narrations, after a hundred thousand years, Iblees will be brought out of Hell and Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> will be brought out of Paradise, and Shaitan will be told to prostrate to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. But he will refuse, and he will be thrown back into the fire of Hell.

**Benefit:** The first to call a prophet "dead" after his departure from this world is Shaitan. Even today, his followers and associates call the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> "dead." And whatever the father does, the children do the same — this is not surprising, whether they are biological children or spiritual followers.

## Why the Names Iblees and Shaitan?

Before Shaitan was rejected, his name in Syriac was Azazeel, and in Arabic it was Haris. When he refused the command of Allah ta'ala, he was named Iblees, which means "far removed from good" and "despairing of Allah's mercy." He is also called Shaitan. If its root is "shatana," it means "one who turns away from truth." If derived from "shaitaan," it means "one who is destroyed and burnt."

## The Creation of Hazrat Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup>

After the angels had prostrated to Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Iblees was rejected due to his refusal and arrogance, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — who was created from clay — had no companion of his kind in Paradise because the angels were of a different species. Therefore, Allah ta'ala caused sleep to overcome him. Then from his left rib, Hazrat Hawwa was created, and flesh was placed in its place.

When Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> awoke, he found Hazrat Hawwa sitting near his head. He asked: "Who are you?" She said: "I am a woman." He asked: "Why were you created?" She said: "So that you may find comfort in me."

The angels tested the knowledge of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> by asking: "Who is this?" He said: "This is a woman." They asked: "Why is she called a woman (aurat)?" He said: "Because she was created from a man (mard)." They asked: "What is her name?" He said: "Hawwa." They asked: "Why was she named Hawwa?" He said: "A living thing is called 'hayy,' and she was created from a living being, so she was named Hawwa."

According to one narration, Hazrat Hawwa was created in Paradise after the prostration of the angels. According to another narration, the body of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was prepared on earth and the soul was also entered into him on earth, and Hazrat Hawwa was also created on earth. Then both were taken to Paradise.

## The Marriage of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and the Mahr

When Hazrat Hawwa was created, Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> inclined toward her and intended to extend his hand in affection. The angels said: "O Adam, wait! First pay the mahr." He asked: "What is the mahr?" The angels said: "The mahr is that you recite durood (salutations) upon the Noble Prophet ﷺ."

According to one narration, he was instructed to recite durood three times, and according to another, seventy times. The point of agreement is that the mahr of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was that he should recite durood upon the Noble Prophet ﷺ. Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> recited durood, and the marriage was solemnized with the angels as witnesses.

From this, it is also known that indeed the Noble Prophet ﷺ is the means (waseela) for every existing thing — to the extent that he is the means even for his father, Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

According to another narration, after the marriage, the angels seated Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> on a golden throne and took them to Paradise in the same way that kings are carried with honor. It was as if, upon the return of the wedding procession, the angels were carrying the couple in a golden palanquin.

## The Difference Between the Law of Nature and the Law of Custom

Allah ta'ala's noble custom is that ordinarily He has established means for things. Similarly, in the creation of humans, the law of custom has been placed under means — that children are born from mother and father. But this is not the law of nature (divine power).

Allah ta'ala has established an example of the law of nature: "I can also do it this way. I have no need of means." Without a man and a woman, by His own power, He created a body from clay, breathed the soul into it, and created Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. And without a woman, by splitting a man's rib, contrary to common custom, He created Hazrat Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup>. This made it clear that He is capable of creating offspring from men without women. And creating a son from a woman without her husband also made it clear that this is not far from His power — that is, the birth of Hazrat Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> from Hazrat Maryam happened according to a common method, but no man was involved. It was only the effect of the breath of Jibreel the Trustworthy, because Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> has no father.

## The Wisdom in Prohibiting the Tree to Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup>

If Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had not been in Paradise but had been on earth from the beginning, the command "And do not go near that tree" would not have been necessary, nor would the forgetfulness have occurred. But he was in Paradise, and his living on earth and becoming Allah's vicegerent on earth was the Lord's intention. Even before his creation, Allah ta'ala had said:

> "Indeed, I am going to place a vicegerent on the earth."

From this, it is understood that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did not forget his Beloved and the Beloved's intention — that is, Allah ta'ala and His intention that he would be His vicegerent on earth was not forgotten by Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Rather, it happened according to Allah ta'ala's will. However, forgetfulness occurred in a matter other than that — in being prohibited from going near a particular tree, forgetfulness occurred, which became the cause of his coming to earth.

At this point, the objection should not arise that Allah ta'ala was capable of bringing Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to earth without this forgetfulness — indeed, His power is absolute. But He Himself has connected the manifestation of His power to wise means. Adam's forgetfulness is included among these means. Along with Allah ta'ala being All-Powerful, He is also All-Wise, and it is not befitting for the Wise to act contrary to wisdom. Observance of wisdom does not negate power.

That apparent slip of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> should not be considered a sin in reality, and it should be pondered that Allah ta'ala placed Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> in Paradise and forbade him from going near a particular tree, and gave Shaitan the power to become the cause of Adam's slip in violating this prohibition. After the slip occurred, Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> becoming Allah's vicegerent on earth — which was the divine intention — was wisely fulfilled. With minimal reflection, it can be understood that Allah ta'ala created all these wise means to bring about His intention and purpose.

## Forgetfulness Occurred from Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

> "And do not go near this tree, lest you become among the wrongdoers."

Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> both went near that tree and, despite Allah ta'ala's prohibition, ate from it. In this situation, the apparent meaning of the noble verse would be that Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> both became wrongdoers. But it must be remembered that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was Allah's first vicegerent on earth and His prophet. Allah's prophet and His vicegerent can never be a wrongdoer. If anyone calls Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> a wrongdoer, he himself would be considered a wrongdoer and a disbeliever.

### The Meaning of Zulm (Wrongdoing)

Zulm means to place something in other than its proper place. In the Noble Quran, the word zulm is also used for shirk (associating partners with Allah). Violating rights and disobeying the command of the rightful ruler are also called zulm. In fact, every sin and disobedience is zulm.

Allah ta'ala commands His servants only to do that which is within their ability and choice. The Quran says:

> "Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity."

It is evident that doing or not doing something forgetfully is not within a person's control. In such a situation, the prohibition "Do not go near this tree" cannot mean "Do not go near this tree even forgetfully, otherwise you will become among the wrongdoers."

Now the question is: did Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> intentionally go near that tree, or forgetfully without intention? Let us hear from the Noble Quran itself:

> "And indeed, We had taken a covenant from Adam before, but he forgot, and We did not find in him any intention."

It is established that no kind of zulm occurred from Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. He did not commit shirk, nor did he violate anyone's rights, nor did any sin or disobedience issue from him. Just as a fasting person eating and drinking forgetfully is not a sin, similarly, Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> eating from the tree forgetfully was not a sin. Indeed, he is pure from sins and, due to being a prophet, is sinless (masoom).

The apparent slip that Shaitan became the cause of — in reality, it was not a sin, but rather divine wisdoms are attached to it. In this way, Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> saying "O our Lord, we have wronged ourselves" is not evidence of his being a wrongdoer, but rather it is based on his perfect servitude and extreme attention and humility in the court of the Generous Lord.

## What Is the Meaning of Shaitan's Deception?

> "So Shaitan deceived them through that tree and removed them from where they were."

The command for Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> was not to go near that tree. Shaitan wanted them to disobey this divine command. So through whispers, he said to both of them: "Shall I show you a tree, by eating which you will remain in Paradise forever and you will be granted such a kingdom in which no kind of weakness will ever arise?"

Shaitan repeatedly created whispers in their hearts and, swearing oaths through the language of whispers, said: "I am your well-wisher. Your Lord has only forbidden you from this tree lest you become angels or lest you remain in Paradise forever." Ultimately, through deception, he made them inclined to eat from the tree.

Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> ate from the tree. As soon as they ate, their heavenly garments fell away, and they covered their bodies with the leaves of the trees of Paradise. They were then sent down from Paradise to the earth.

Up to this point, Shaitan's desire was fulfilled, but he failed in his actual purpose. His real desire was that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> should remember Allah's prohibition and intentionally eat from the tree, thus becoming disobedient and rebellious, and be expelled from Paradise. Therefore, through whispers, he reminded them of Allah's prohibition. But divine protection (ismat) saved them from disobedience. Before eating from the tree, the divine prohibition was forgotten by them, as Allah ta'ala said:

> "Adam forgot, and We did not find in him any intention."

Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was saved from intentionally violating the divine command, and Shaitan failed in his real purpose. This is the reason why Allah ta'ala used the word "azalla" (to cause to slip) instead of "adalla" (to cause to go astray) — that is, He said that Shaitan deceived them, not that he led them astray.

### Why Did Shaitan Cast Whispers?

After the angels prostrated to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, he and his wife were commanded to reside in Paradise, and it was said: "Eat freely whatever you wish here, but do not go near that tree." Through the prohibited tree, Shaitan deceived both of them and removed them from the environment of prosperity, carefreeness, and luxury — that is, from Paradise.

Another reason was that when Shaitan refused to prostrate to Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and showed arrogance, Allah ta'ala said: "You are rejected. Get out of here." The fire of spite and envy blazed in Shaitan's heart, and he said: "Just as I have been expelled humiliated and disgraced, I will also expel Adam, Hawwa, and their descendants from Paradise in the same way, and I will lead them astray just as I have been led astray — that is, I will take my full revenge from them."

### How Was Shaitan Able to Deceive?

When Shaitan refused to prostrate, he was commanded to leave Paradise. At that very moment, he sought respite from Allah ta'ala. Although Allah ta'ala had said: "Your authority will not work upon My sincere servants," Shaitan took advantage of the respite and cast whispers into Adam's heart, causing him to forget.

## How Does Shaitan Cast Whispers into People?

Allah ta'ala says:

> "Indeed, he and his tribe see you from where you cannot see them."

That is, Shaitan and the lesser devils under his influence can see people wherever they may be and can cast whispers into them, even though people do not see them.

"Indeed, Shaitan flows through man like blood flows through the veins."

### From Where Did Shaitan Speak?

The conversation that Shaitan had with Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup> was through powerful whispers. From the earth itself, through the language of whispers, he said what he wanted to say. Since he was expelled from Paradise, he was neither permitted to ascend to the heavens nor could he climb there.

Neither the Noble Quran nor any hadith mentions that Shaitan reached Paradise near Adam and Hawwa <sup>عليها السلام</sup>. In the Noble Quran, only these words appear: "Shaitan cast whispers into both of them." And in Surah Ta-Ha, verse 120: "Shaitan cast whispers into him (Adam)." For Shaitan to cast whispers, it is not necessary for him to physically go near someone, nor is it necessary for the one being whispered to see him.

**Warning:** The statements that prove Shaitan entered Paradise through a snake, or that Shaitan sat at the gate of Paradise and cast whispers, are from the condemned Israelite traditions. Ibn Kathir said: "Here, the commentators have narrated many Israelite reports." And Imam Razi said: "It is necessary that such narrations be completely disregarded."

**Benefit:** Allah ta'ala has granted Shaitan such power of influence that he can cast whispers into the hearts of people anywhere. And Hazrat Izraail, the Angel of Death, has such power that he can take souls in every corner of the earth in a single moment. ### An Objection and Its Response

**Objection:** Allah ta'ala says: "And Adam disobeyed his Lord and went astray." Here, many translators have translated "asa" as "disobeyed the command," meaning he committed a fault, and "ghawa" as "deviated from the path, went astray, fell into error, strayed from the right path." So how can it be said that only forgetfulness occurred from Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and he did not commit any crime or sin?

**Response:** These translators have not translated correctly here. Look at the translation of A'la Hazrat Maulana Ahmad Raza Khan Bareilvi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup>. He has translated this verse as:

> "And Adam forgot, so his intention was not to oppose the command."

This makes it clear that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did not intentionally disobey. He forgot, and his intention was not to go against the command. The word "asa" in Arabic can mean both "disobeyed" and "forgot." The correct meaning here is "forgot," as supported by other verses of the Quran and authentic hadith. The noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> are protected from sin, and their slips are only matters of forgetfulness or of leaving the better option, which Allah ta'ala in His grace forgives and even turns into a means of elevation.
