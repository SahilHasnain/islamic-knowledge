# Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

## Hazrat Adam Safiullah <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

The first prophet of Allah ta'ala, the leader of mankind, and the foremost in the chain of prophethood — Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Allah ta'ala honored him with the station of vicegerency and blessed him with the knowledge of all things. His name is mentioned in the Noble Quran at several places, and many events of his life have been narrated for our guidance and reflection.

After creating the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, Allah ta'ala desired to create a vicegerent on earth. Thus He made known His intention to the angels, demonstrating His infinite wisdom and power.

### Allah Ta'ala's Consultation with the Angels

Allah ta'ala says in the Noble Quran:

> "And mention when your Lord said to the angels: Indeed, I am going to place a vicegerent on the earth."

When Allah ta'ala willed to create Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, He informed the angels of His decision. This was not because Allah ta'ala was in need of their counsel, but for several wise purposes:

### The Wisdom Behind the Consultation

**1)** To honor the angels, Allah ta'ala informed them of this great creation beforehand, so that they might know the status of this new creation.

**2)** To teach the angels that the knowledge of the unseen belongs solely to Allah ta'ala, and they are not aware of the divine mysteries.

**3)** To prepare the angels for what was to come, so that when they would be commanded to prostrate before Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, they would understand something of the wisdom behind it.

**4)** To demonstrate His power of creating from opposites — from clay, which is dark and dense, He would create a being of light and knowledge.

**5)** To show that consultation is a praiseworthy practice, even for one who has no need of it, so that His servants may learn to consult one another in their affairs.

**Important Note:** This consultation does not imply any hesitation or uncertainty in Allah ta'ala's knowledge and power. Rather, it is a manifestation of His wisdom and a teaching for His creation.

## The Purpose of Creating a Vicegerent

The purpose of creating a vicegerent (khaleefa) on earth is that he should implement Allah's commands on earth, establish justice, and guide people toward the truth. The vicegerent of Allah is one who:

1. Knows Allah ta'ala and makes Him known to others
2. Establishes Allah's laws on earth
3. Commands good and forbids evil
4. Leads people toward spiritual perfection
5. Serves as a bridge between Allah and His creation

The station of vicegerency is a great honor, and the one chosen for it must possess the highest qualities of knowledge, wisdom, and character.

### The Current Condition of Muslims

The decline of the Muslims in the present age is due to their distance from the true spirit of the vicegerency. When Muslims possessed firm faith, loved Allah and His Messenger ﷺ above all else, and possessed the spirit of jihad and the desire for martyrdom, they achieved greatness. But when these qualities weakened, so did their position in the world.

If Muslims today develop complete faith, prefer the love of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ over everything else, and cultivate the spirit of striving in the way of Allah, there is no reason why they cannot regain their lost glory — the same glory that the Companions possessed in their time, when the mere awe of Muslims would cause the disbelievers to tremble.

## The Angels' Question of Astonishment

When Allah ta'ala consulted the angels about creating a vicegerent, the angels asked in astonishment:

> "Will You place therein one who will spread corruption and shed blood? While we glorify You with praise and sanctify You."

The angels did not object to Allah ta'ala nor did they oppose His decision. Rather, Allah ta'ala had already given them the knowledge that the vicegerent He would create would be composed of the four opposing elements — fire, earth, water, and air — which are contrary to one another. This knowledge had been given to the angels either by Allah ta'ala informing them directly or by unveiling the Preserved Tablet to them.

They understood that from opposing and conflicting elements, nothing but corruption would arise. A vicegerent is appointed to establish goodness on earth, to guide people to righteousness, to perfect souls, and to implement Allah's commands. How could this be achieved by one whose very nature was based on conflict?

Their question was either to uncover hidden wisdom or expressed their astonishment at how those who would spread corruption could populate the earth and bring about reform.

**Note:** This was an ijtihadi error on the part of the angels. They thought that perhaps all humans would be like this, whereas the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup>, due to their sinlessness, are pure and righteous. And those who are under Allah's protection are free from spreading corruption.

Considering their own glorification, sanctification, and sinlessness, the angels considered themselves more deserving of the vicegerency. To expose this limitation in their knowledge, Allah ta'ala said:

> "Indeed, I know what you do not know."

Mere glorification and sanctification are not the criteria for vicegerency, nor does being composed of opposing elements prevent one from attaining this station. Rather, the criterion for vicegerency is that the vicegerent should himself practice what he commands others to do. Therefore, all humans will not be corrupt and given to unjust bloodshed. Among them will be some who are sinless and deserving of the station of divine vicegerency.

## The Knowledge of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

> "And Allah taught Adam the names of all things, then presented them to the angels and said: 'Inform Me of the names of these, if you are truthful.'"

Hazrat Ibn Abbas, Ikrama, Qatada, Mujahid, and Ibn Zubair <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهم</sup> have stated that Allah ta'ala granted Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> the knowledge of the names of all things, to the extent that even the names of large and small vessels were taught to him.

Some narrators attribute to Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> the statement that Allah ta'ala granted Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> the knowledge of everything that has happened and everything that will happen.

Both interpretations point to the same meaning — that Allah ta'ala granted him knowledge of all things and their names, whether they existed before or would come into existence later.

Imam Razi <sup>رحمه الله</sup> stated that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was granted knowledge of the qualities, benefits, and specific properties of all things.

According to one statement, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was taught all languages. According to another, he was made aware of the names of all the angels. And according to yet another, he was informed of the names of all the stars.

Allama Aaloosi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup>, after narrating various statements, quoted Hakeem Tirmidhi's view that in this noble verse, "names" refer to the divine names. He then stated that in his view, the correct position — and one accepted by all the friends of Allah — is that the requirements of the station of vicegerency demand that he be granted knowledge of the names of all things. Whether those things are celestial or terrestrial, substantial or accidental, the names of all of them are called the names of Allah ta'ala, because all things point to Allah ta'ala's essence, and the manifestations of Allah ta'ala's essence are revealed through all things, although Allah ta'ala is not confined within them.

## Why Were the Names Taught to Adam and Not the Angels?

Knowledge of meanings through words is acquired from a teacher. The one who teaches is called a teacher (mu'allim) and the one who learns is called a student (mut'allim). It is not necessary that a student gains knowledge simply because the teacher teaches — the student must possess the capacity to understand. If the student has the ability to comprehend, then the teacher's instruction will have an effect on him. We observe this daily — the same teacher teaches the same class, all students are taught equally, yet some become capable and others do not.

When Allah ta'ala intended to grant Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> the station of vicegerency, He first granted him the capacity to understand all things, their conditions, and their names. But the angels were not granted this capacity to understand the details of every thing, because they were not intended to be appointed to the station of vicegerency.

### How Was Knowledge Given to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>?

Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was given knowledge of all things — meaning Allah ta'ala showed him each species from among His creation and told him its name. For example, a horse was shown and it was said, "This is called a horse." A camel was shown and it was said, "This is called a camel." In this manner, every thing was shown and its name was told.

Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> possessed the distinction that he was told the names of all things in every language. He was taught all the languages that would ever come into existence — their knowledge was granted to him in advance.

**Benefit:** When Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was given knowledge of all things and taught their names in every language, what then must be the station of the Chief of the Prophets ﷺ in terms of knowledge? A'la Hazrat Maulana Ahmad Raza Bareilvi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> has written:

> "The Merciful taught His Beloved the Quran. Muhammad was created as the soul of humanity. He was taught the knowledge of 'what has been and what will be.'"

A'la Hazrat called the Noble Prophet ﷺ the soul of humanity. Allama Aaloosi wrote in his commentary:

"All of existence is one body, and the Noble Prophet is its soul. The body cannot stand without the soul. From this it is understood that the Noble Prophet ﷺ is the soul of the universe."

And from A'la Hazrat's translation, it becomes clear that Ilmul Bayan means that knowledge was granted to the Beloved ﷺ. Regarding this, Allama Qurtubi's commentary *Al-Jaami' li Ahkaamil Bayan* may be consulted. In Ilmul Bayan, the pronoun in the accusative refers to "man," and by it is meant the Noble Prophet ﷺ.

The meaning of "Bayan" is either the knowledge of halal and haram and guidance from misguidance, or as has been explained, "Bayan" means the knowledge of "what has been and what will be," because the Noble Prophet ﷺ has mentioned the first and the last and the events of the Day of Judgment. Thus he possesses the knowledge of "what has been and what will be."

## The Virtues of Knowledge — Intellectual and Narrated

A brief discussion on the virtues of knowledge, based on *Tafseer-e-Kabeer* and *Azeezi*:

Faqeeh Abullais Samarqandi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> stated that attending the company of a scholar has seven benefits, whether one acquires knowledge from him or not:

1. One is counted among the seekers of knowledge, and their reward is known.
2. As long as one remains in that gathering, he refrains from sins.
3. When one leaves his home with the intention of seeking knowledge, he receives a reward for every step.
4. Divine mercy descends upon the circle of knowledge, and the attendee shares in it.
5. One hears the remembrance of knowledge, which is an act of worship.
6. When one hears a difficult issue that he does not understand and his heart becomes constrained, he is counted among those with broken hearts (which are deserving of mercy) in the sight of Allah ta'ala.
7. Respect for knowledge and hatred of ignorance develop in his heart.

Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> stated that religious knowledge is superior to wealth in seven ways:

1. Knowledge is the inheritance of the prophets, while wealth is the inheritance of Pharaoh (Firaun), Haman, Shaddad, and Nimrod.
2. Wealth decreases when spent, but knowledge increases.
3. Wealth must be guarded by a person, but knowledge guards the person.
4. After death, wealth remains in the world, while religious knowledge accompanies one in the grave.
5. Wealth is acquired by both believer and disbeliever alike, but the benefit of knowledge (success in the grave and the Hereafter) is attained only by the believer.
6. Many people have no need of the wealthy, but no one is indifferent to the scholar.
7. Knowledge provides strength to cross the Bridge of Siraat, while wealth causes weakness.

**Seven prophets received great benefits through knowledge:**

1. Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was given superiority over the angels through knowledge, and the angels were commanded to prostrate before him.
2. Hazrat Khizr <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — though there is disagreement among scholars as to whether he was a prophet or a saint — met Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> through knowledge, and the difference between the apparent and hidden aspects of certain things became clear.
3. Hazrat Yusuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, through knowledge of dream interpretation, was released from prison, reached the royal court, and was appointed as treasurer and administrator of all state affairs.
4. Hazrat Suleman <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was blessed with a queen like Bilqis as a wife through knowledge, and she attained faith through his knowledge.
5. Hazrat Dawood <sup>عليه السلام</sup] was granted kingship along with prophethood through knowledge.
6. Hazrat Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> refuted the accusation against his mother, Hazrat Maryam, through knowledge.
7. The Noble Prophet ﷺ was granted knowledge greater than all of creation and was elevated to the station of divine vicegerency, the Grand Intercession, and the highest ranks.

**Seven things mentioned in the Noble Quran as not being equal:**

1. The scholar and the ignorant are not equal.
2. The impure and the pure are not equal.
3. The inhabitant of Hell and the inhabitant of Paradise are not equal.
4. The blind and the seeing — meaning one with knowledge and faith and one without them — are not equal.
5. Darkness and light — meaning the illumination of knowledge and faith, and the darkness resulting from their absence — are not equal.
6. Cold and heat are not equal.
7. The living and the dead are not equal.

Hazrat Ali Murtaza <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> stated that the world stands upon four types of people:

1. A scholar who practices his knowledge — one whose actions conform to the commandments of the world after acquiring religious knowledge.
2. Ignorant people who love the scholars — indeed, through the company of scholars, they will develop a desire for good deeds and will certainly acquire some knowledge of worldly matters.
3. Wealthy people who are generous — a wealthy person who spends in the way of Allah ta'ala holds a high rank and is a means of maintaining the system of the world.
4. Poor people who have no wealth but are patient with their meager means and hard work — the world also stands through the patient poor.

Allah ta'ala says:

> "Indeed, among His servants, only the scholars fear Allah."

In this verse, if there is a 'pesh' (the Urdu vowel producing 'oo' sound) on the word "Allah" and a 'zabar' on the word "ulama," the meaning would be: "Allah ta'ala grants honor and dignity to the scholars among His servants."

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said:

"When a teacher of religious knowledge dies, the birds of the sky, the animals of the earth, and the fish in the seas weep for him."

Hazrat Aamir Jehani <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> narrated a hadith:

"On the Day of Judgment, the ink of the seeker of religious knowledge and the blood of the martyr will be brought. Neither will be found superior to the other."

Hazrat Mus'ab bin Zubair <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> said to his son:

"O my son, acquire knowledge. If you have wealth, knowledge will be your adornment. And if you have no wealth, knowledge itself will be your wealth."

**Note:** Imam Razi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> has discussed the virtue of knowledge at great length at this point. Only a brief portion has been mentioned here.

## The Creation of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

Allah ta'ala says:

> "Indeed, We created your origin, Adam, from clay."

He further says:

> "Remember when your Lord said to the angels: Indeed, I am going to create a mortal from clay."

Here, "mortal" (bashar) means a human being whose outer covering will be skin — he will not have wool like sheep, hair like goats, fur like camels, feathers like birds, or a peel like fruits.

> "Indeed, We created man from clinging clay."

Here too, "man" refers to their origin, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

> "Indeed, We created man — that is, Adam — from black, dry, changed clay."

"Salsaal" refers to clay that has become dry and makes a sound when tapped. Black clay is called "hama." Clay whose odor has changed is called "masnoon."

> "Man — that is, Adam — was created from clay like a dry, ringing pottery."

These verses mention the various stages of Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> creation. First, dry clay was brought. Then it was kneaded into wet clay. Then it became sticky clay. Then it was left until it dried and began to make a sound and its odor changed. Then, after being left longer, it became like pottery.

### From Where Was the Clay Brought?

Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was sent to earth to bring clay for the creation of Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> pure body. When he came and intended to take clay from the earth, the earth pleaded with great humility, weeping and wailing: "If the persons created from my clay commit bloodshed or go to Hell due to sin, I will be distressed."

Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, seeing the earth's humility, returned and reported the whole matter to Allah ta'ala. Similarly, Hazrat Israfeel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came and returned. Hazrat Mikaail <sup>عليه السلام</sup> also came and returned. After all of them, Hazrat Izraail <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came. The earth also spoke to him with the same humility, but he said: "Should I accept your word or act upon Allah ta'ala's command? I have been commanded by Allah ta'ala, so I must take the clay." He paid no attention to the earth's humility and, in accordance with the command of Allah ta'ala, took clay from the earth and presented it before his Lord. For this reason, Allah ta'ala entrusted the task of taking souls to him — so that it would not be that if it had been assigned to Jibreel, Mikaail, or Israfeel, they might show compassion upon seeing the soul's loved ones weeping when they come to take a soul, and leave without taking it.

### What Kind of Clay Was Taken?

A marfoo' hadith is narrated from Hazrat Abu Moosa Ash'ari <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>:

"Indeed, Allah ta'ala commanded to bring a handful of clay from all of the earth. In that clay, particles of every kind were included — red, white, black, and intermediate colors. Similarly, some clay was taken from soft earth and some from hard earth, pure and impure clay were both included. Whatever colors of clay were placed in his body, the same colors are found in his descendants. Likewise, some are soft-hearted and some hard-hearted, some are good and some are evil."

Some scholars have stated that sixty kinds of colors were included in Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> clay, and all of them are found in his descendants.

### Why Are Springs Flowing on Earth?

When Allah ta'ala intended to create Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, He told the earth: "I am going to create from you a creation who will be obedient to Me. I will admit them into Paradise, and those who disobey Me, I will throw into the fire of Hell." Hearing this, the earth asked: "O Allah, will the creation created from me go into the fire of Hell?" The Lord said: "Yes." Then the earth wept so much that springs gushed forth from its weeping, which will continue to flow until the Day of Judgment.

### Why Does Man Have Less Joy and More Grief?

When Hazrat Izraail <sup>عليه السلام</sup> brought the clay, Allah ta'ala commanded him to place it near the mountains of Safa and Marwa — that is, where the Ka'aba is today. Then the angels were commanded to make it into clay with various waters. Then rain fell upon it for forty days — thirty-nine days of sorrow and grief, and one day of joy. Therefore, man experiences more grief and sorrow and less joy. Then it was dried with various winds until it became clay that made a sound, and Allah ta'ala Himself, with His perfect power, fashioned his body.

### The Angels Were Astonished at Adam's Form

When the body of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was prepared and placed, the angels would pass by and see this form. They were astonished at this creation. Nothing like it had ever been seen before. They admired this form and recognized its excellence.

### The Entry of the Soul into Adam's Body

When Allah ta'ala commanded the soul to enter Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> body, it hesitated. The soul was commanded: "Enter reluctantly and leave reluctantly." When the soul reached near the body, it found the body dark and narrow and hesitated to enter.

According to some narrations, at that point, the body was illuminated with the Light of Mustafa ﷺ — that is, that light was placed as a trust on Adam's forehead. Then the soul began to enter slowly. When it reached the head, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> sneezed. When it reached the tongue, he said "Alhamdulillah," and Allah ta'ala responded with "Yarhamkullah." Allah ta'ala said: "O Abu Muhammad (this and Abul Bashar are his titles), I have created you only for My praise."

When the soul reached the waist, he tried to get up but fell down, because the soul had not yet reached the lower parts. Allah ta'ala said:

> "Man was created hasty."

Then when the soul spread throughout the body, he was commanded to greet the angels. He said "Assalamualaikum," and the angels replied "Wa alaikum assalam." Allah ta'ala said: "This will be the manner of greeting for you and your descendants."

He asked: "What will my descendants be?" Then all his descendants were presented before him.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Indeed, Allah ta'ala created Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, then passed His hand of power over his back and brought forth his descendants. Then He said: 'I have created these for Paradise, and they will do the deeds of the people of Paradise.' Then Allah ta'ala passed His hand of power over his back again and brought forth the rest of his descendants, and the Lord said: 'These I have created for Hell, and they will do the deeds of the people of Hell.'"

## The Command for the Angels to Prostrate Before Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

Even before the creation of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, Allah ta'ala had commanded the angels: "You are to prostrate before My vicegerent." After the creation of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, all things were presented before the angels and their names were asked. When the angels expressed their inability, then Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was asked, and he told the names of all things. Then the command was given. Allah ta'ala says:

> "And remember when We said to the angels: Prostrate to Adam. So they all prostrated, except Iblees — he refused and was arrogant, and he became among the disbelievers."

The angels were commanded to perform a prostration of respect (sajda e tazeemi), just as the brothers of Hazrat Yusuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> prostrated before him out of respect. In the shariah of our Noble Prophet ﷺ, prostration of respect has been declared prohibited. Prostration for the purpose of worship has never been permissible for anyone other than Allah ta'ala in any shariah.

Allama Aaloosi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> states:

"Nevertheless, the first to prostrate was Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, then Mikaail, then Israfeel, then Izraail <sup>عليهم السلام</sup>, then all the angels. For this reason, Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was granted the highest rank — the great task of bringing revelation (the Quran) to the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> was entrusted to him."

Some scholars have said that the first to prostrate was Hazrat Israfeel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and therefore the entire Quran was inscribed on his forehead.

### Adam Was Made the Manifestation of the True Vicegerent

Although apparently the first vicegerent is Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, in reality the first vicegerent is our Noble Prophet ﷺ. The Noble Prophet ﷺ himself said:

> "I was a Prophet when Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was between soul and body."

Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, by the command of Allah ta'ala, took clay from the place of the Noble Prophet's ﷺ blessed grave. It was kneaded with the water of Tasneem, immersed in the rivers of Paradise, and taken through the heavens and the earth — for this reason, the angels had recognized the Noble Prophet ﷺ even before Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Then that clay was mixed with the body of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and Adam's forehead was illuminated with the Light of Muhammad ﷺ.

It was this Light of Muhammad that was the actual reason for the angels' prostration.

Imam Razi <sup>رحمه الله</sup> said in *Tafseer-e-Kabeer*:

"Indeed, the angels were commanded to prostrate to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> because the light of Muhammad ﷺ was placed on his forehead."

Allama Aaloosi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> states:

"That is, in reality, the Holy Prophet ﷺ is the greatest vicegerent of Allah ta'ala among His creation, and the foremost Imam in the heavens and the high earths is the Holy Prophet ﷺ. If the Holy Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had not been, neither Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> would have been created, nor anything besides him."

From the statements of Allama Razi and Allama Aaloosi, it becomes clear that the greatest vicegerent is the Holy Prophet ﷺ. The vicegerency of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is a manifestation of his vicegerency.

## How Many Angels Are There?

Only Allah ta'ala knows the number of angels. Although the Noble Prophet ﷺ was given knowledge of all the heavens and the earth and thus knows their number, he did not mention it. However, Allama Razi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> mentioned that the number of angels is extremely vast, because the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "There is a commotion in the heaven," and this is indeed true — there is no space of even one step where an angel is not in prostration or bowing.

All humans, jinn, animals, birds, and aquatic creatures are only one-tenth of the angels inhabiting the earth. Then, when added to these, they are one-tenth of the angels of the first heaven. This chain continues through the seven heavens, and then compared to the angels of the Throne and the bearers of the Throne, all of these are like a single drop compared to the ocean.

### What Is the Origin of Iblees?

Some scholars hold that Iblees is separate from the angels, because the angels were created from light while he was created from fire. Allah ta'ala says:

> "Iblees was from the jinn, so he disobeyed the command of his Lord."

The question arises: how was the command to prostrate given to him, when apparently the command was only for the angels? The answer given by these scholars is that due to his excessive worship, he was included among the angels and the same commands applied to him — that is, the command was applied to him predominantly, just as when a command is given to leaders, their subordinates are also included in the command.

However, some verifying scholars — including Allama Baghwi, Wahidi, Qazi Baizawi, and Allama Aaloosi — hold that Iblees was from the angels.

## Why Are Angels Called Jinn?

Allama Razi is of the opinion that it was from the angels. Allama Aaloosi states:

"Although the Lord called Iblees 'jinn,' and in the narration of Hazrat Aisha <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> he is also called jinn, calling him jinn does not affect his being an angel. There is no contradiction because sometimes 'jinn' is used for a separate creation as opposed to angels, and sometimes a type of angel is also called jinn."

Allama Aaloosi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> explains why angels are called jinn:

"Because they are hidden from the sight of people. Hidden things are called jinn, therefore angels are also called jinn."

There is also no harm in Iblees being created from fire while angels were created from light, nor can this prove any defect in his being an angel. Fire and light have the same substance — they are of the same genus, differing only in accidents. That which is mixed with smoke is fire, and that which is pure and transparent is light. Just as clay, sand, stone, antimony, etc., have the same substance and genus but differ in accidents.

### Iblees Was Rejected Due to Arrogance

The reason for Iblees's refusal of Allah ta'ala's command was his arrogance. When the Lord asked him: "Why did you not prostrate, when My command was given?" He replied:

> "I am better than him. You created me from fire and created him from clay."

That is, one who is superior in status does not prostrate before an inferior. Iblees failed to recognize the true station of Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. He could not understand that the station of Allah's prophet is higher than that of the angels. The Lord said:

> "Then get out of Paradise! You are rejected. And indeed, upon you is My curse until the Day of Judgment."

One who worshipped for thousands of years, who was brought near to the Lord — in a single moment, due to disrespect toward the station of a prophet, became rejected, was expelled from Paradise, and was made deserving of curse until the Day of Judgment.

### Shaitan's Request Was Granted

> "He said: 'Give me respite until the Day they are resurrected.' He said: 'You are among those given respite.'"

From this, it is understood that there were two requests. In the first, he asked for respite until the Day of Judgment. This was not accepted; instead, he was given respite until the first trumpet blast. In the second, he asked for respite until the Day of Resurrection — meaning until all are raised.

> "He said: 'Because You have led me astray, I will surely sit in wait for them on Your straight path. Then I will come to them from before them and from behind them, and from their right and from their left. And You will not find most of them grateful.'"

Although Shaitan had firmly resolved to plunge people into doubts and evil, and he was hopeful of success, he still said: "You will not find most of them grateful." At another place, Shaitan expressed his inability to deceive the righteous:

> "He said: 'O my Lord! By the means by which You have led me astray, I will certainly make things appear attractive to them on earth, and I will certainly lead all of them astray, except Your chosen servants among them.'"

Shaitan said: "I will present evil deeds as attractive and embellished before people. In this way, they will deviate from the straight path through my deception. But O Allah, Your righteous, sincere, and chosen servants — my deceit will have no effect on them."

Allah ta'ala had already told Shaitan:

> "Indeed, over My servants, you shall have no authority."

Thus, Shaitan surrounds people from all directions and casts whispers into their hearts, inclining them toward falsehood. He prevents some from obedience and leads them astray.

## Five Categories of People Regarding the Effect of Satanic Whispers

Man is a combination of body and soul. The soul is a subtle creation from the higher realm, containing the realities, perfections, and all benefits of the higher world. The body was created from clay, so it contains material effects, characteristics, and the qualities of earthly creatures.

The capacity to become Allah's vicegerent was given to every human being through the combination of body and soul. But as a result of Shaitan's effort to deprive man of this blessing, five groups of people emerged:

**1) First Group:** Those who completely came under Shaitan's control and rebelled against divine vicegerency. They wasted the capacity for vicegerency entirely. They had no connection with Allah ta'ala's oneness and His recognition. They turned away from goodness in both worlds and the paths of eternal salvation. They had no strength left to achieve any spiritual excellence. Even the knowledge of material benefits and their acquisition was denied to them. These are people devoid of intellect and wisdom — ignorant disbelievers and polytheists.

**2) Second Group:** Those in whom the physical capacity remained, but who went astray due to Shaitan's misguidance and destroyed their spiritual capacity. Therefore, they were deprived of fulfilling spiritual requirements. Let alone the recognition of Allah ta'ala, they even denied the existence of Allah ta'ala. They considered only body and matter as their purpose and turned the rest of their capacity toward material things. They became lost in intellectual complexities. Some of them achieved great success in modern discoveries and material inventions. They invented countless useful things — airplanes that fly in the sky, means of communication between earth and heaven through light and satellites, astonishing instruments, and now they have reached the final stage of progress where they have created missiles that can strike thousands of miles for the destruction of humanity. They have made atom bombs, napalm bombs, aircraft faster than sound with which the earth can be turned into a scene of destruction in seconds, and with atom bombs, the entire globe can be blown up and destroyed in the blink of an eye.

That capacity for divine vicegerency, which was meant for the physical, spiritual, worldly, and otherworldly benefits of the human race, was dedicated to instruments of destruction. Now the situation has reached such a point that those who invented these weapons themselves feel threatened by them. They constantly fear that the instruments they themselves invented might at any moment strike them, and they too might become a morsel of death along with the globe.

**3) Third Group:** Those in whom the capacity for divine vicegerency existed, but Shaitan's deception had such an effect on them that they fell into negligence and laziness, and they did not fully utilize their capacity. These are the common Muslims who have acquired some physical and spiritual benefits to a small degree, but due to not fully utilizing their abilities, they could not attain complete control over spirituality or materiality. Although they did not achieve the station of vicegerency, they also did not rebel against divine vicegerency — that is, they did not wash their hands of faith.

However, it should be noted that within this group there are two types: those on whom Shaitan's effect is less, and those on whom Shaitan's effect is very great. Although they do not leave faith, they become immersed in many sins.

**4) Fourth Group:** This is the group of Allah's special servants in whom the physical, spiritual, intellectual, and practical capacities granted by Allah ta'ala were fully present, and Shaitan's misguidance could not harm their capacity in any way. Allah ta'ala had already addressed Shaitan and said:

> "Indeed, over My chosen servants, you shall have no authority."

This sacred group comprises the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> and those perfect ones who follow them. They utilized the capacity granted by Allah ta'ala to its fullest and attained the station of divine vicegerency. They gained control over spirituality and materiality according to wisdom and expediency, and fulfilled the requirements of divine vicegerency to perfection.

**5) Fifth Group:** Those who, in their view, considered only the soul and its requirements and ignored physicality and materiality. These are people who remained engaged in ascetic exercises and spiritual struggles according to their own understanding. Among them are those who have faith in Allah ta'ala's oneness — they remain protected from Shaitan's misguidance. And some do not have faith in Allah ta'ala's oneness — Shaitan completely removes them from the path of salvation. Some benefited from their ascetic exercises and struggles and attained spirituality, though they were deprived of worldly and material benefits. Others, despite their ascetic exercises and struggles, attained neither spirituality nor worldly and material benefits.
