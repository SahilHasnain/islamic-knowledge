## The Noble Prophets Are Pure from Sins

Allah ta'ala says:

> "A slip occurred from Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> in the command of his Lord — he did not find the path to what he intended."

This translation makes it clear that this slip occurred forgetfully — there was no sin or deviation in it. At this point, Imam Razi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> states in *Tafseer-e-Kabeer*:

"Although the apparent meaning of the Noble Quran indicates that disobedience and deviation occurred from Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, no one has the right to say that Hazrat Adam disobeyed the divine command and went astray. The purpose is that Allah ta'ala used these words — He has the right to use whatever words He wishes for His servant, and He alone is aware of their true meaning."

At this point, Allama Aaloosi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> writes in *Ruhul Ma'ani*:

"Qazi Abu Bakr bin Arabi has clearly stated that words like disobedience, going astray, being misled — when we cannot attribute such words to our parents and forefathers, who are like us in humanity and inferior to the noble prophets — how can such words be attributed to the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup>, and especially to Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, who are the chosen, honored, and superior in every respect?"

In *Ma'alimul Tanzeel* it is stated:

"It is certain that applying words like 'disobedient,' 'gone astray,' or 'misguided' to Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is not permissible."

### Proofs That Prophets Are Free from Sins

The noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> are pure from both minor and major sins. By the grace of Allah, it is impossible for sins to occur from the noble prophets <sup>عليهم السلام</sup>. Imam Razi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> has established several proofs for this:

**1)** If sins were to occur from the noble prophets, their rank would be lower than that of the sinful, disobedient people among their own nations — and this is not permissible. The reason is that the noble prophets possess very high ranks and are granted great honor and nobility. If those who possess high rank commit sins, they are considered far worse. Allah ta'ala said:

> "O wives of the Prophet! Whoever among you commits a clear indecency, the punishment will be doubled for her."

This refers to negligence in obeying the husband and treating him with bad conduct, because Allah ta'ala keeps the wives of the prophets pure from fornication. In any case, the more special and virtuous a person is, if a fault occurs from him, that fault is considered more severe than the faults of others.

Similarly, if a married person (muhsan) commits fornication, he is stoned, while an unmarried person receives one hundred lashes. This is because the status of the married person is higher. Since there is consensus that the rank of a prophet cannot be lower than any member of his nation, how could his rank be lower than that of sinners?

**2)** A sinner is a transgressor (fasiq). If a prophet were to commit a sin, he would become a transgressor. The testimony of a transgressor is not accepted, as Allah ta'ala said:

> "If a transgressor brings you news, verify it."

**3)** If major sins were permissible from the noble prophets, it would become necessary to rebuke them and strictly forbid them. Thus causing harm to the noble prophets would not be prohibited — whereas causing harm to the noble prophets is prohibited. Allah ta'ala said:

> "Indeed, those who harm Allah and His Messenger — upon them is the curse of Allah in this world and the Hereafter."

**4)** Every prophet's nation is obligated to obey their prophet, just as we have been commanded. Allah ta'ala said through the blessed tongue of the Noble Prophet ﷺ: "Obey me." If sins were permissible from the prophet, then the nation would be obligated to obey even his sins. Thus sins would be both prohibited and obligatory at the same time — how can this be possible?

**5)** Our intellect clearly understands that the station of a prophet is very high. The prophet is the trustee of Allah's revelation and the vicegerent of Allah on earth. How can it be that he hears Allah's command "Do not do this" and yet gives precedence to his own desires and does it? Paying no attention to Allah's prohibition and the fear of His punishment is extremely evil and impossible.

**6)** Those who commit sins are deserving of punishment. Allah ta'ala said:

> "And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger — indeed, for them is the fire of Hell, wherein they will abide forever."

Similarly, those who commit sins are deserving of curse. Allah ta'ala said:

> "Beware! The curse of Allah is upon the wrongdoers."

If the noble prophets were to commit sins, they would be deserving of punishment and curse — whereas there is consensus of the Ummah that the noble prophets cannot be deserving of punishment or curse.

**7)** The noble prophets command people to obey Allah ta'ala. If they themselves do not practice it, the following verse would apply to them:

> "Do you command people to righteousness and forget yourselves, while you recite the Book? Do you not understand?"

When even an ordinary adviser can be rebuked in this manner, how can it be possible for the noble prophets — who possess the highest ranks — to advise others and not practice it themselves?

Hazrat Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> said to his people:

> "And I do not intend to do what I forbid you from. I only intend to set things right as far as I am able."

**8)** Allah ta'ala mentions the noble prophets:

> "Indeed, they used to hasten to do good deeds."

Khairat means to do every good deed and avoid every evil deed. This makes it clear that the noble prophets only do good deeds and avoid evil deeds — therefore, sins cannot occur from them.

**9)** Allah ta'ala says regarding the noble prophets:

> "Indeed, they are, in Our sight, among the chosen and excellent."

When this was mentioned in absolute terms without separating any particular quality or habit, it indicates that all their deeds are good. If any evil deed were found, the phrase would be: "So-and-so is among the chosen and excellent, except for such-and-such deed, in which he is not good." Since no such qualification was mentioned regarding the noble prophets, it is clear that all their deeds are good and no sin occurs from them.

**10)** Allah ta'ala, while quoting Shaitan's statement, said:

> "By Your honor, I will surely lead all of them astray, except Your sincere servants among them."

Shaitan admitted his inability, saying: "O Allah, Your sincere servants are beyond my reach."

Allah ta'ala called Ibrahim, Ishaq, and Yaqoob <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> His sincere servants. Allah ta'ala said:

> "Indeed, We distinguished them with a pure quality — the remembrance of the Home."

Regarding Hazrat Yusuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the Lord said:

> "Indeed, he was among Our sincere servants."

When it is established that some noble prophets are sincere, and Shaitan is unable to mislead sincere servants according to his own admission, then the same ruling applies to all noble prophets — for no one claims that some prophets are sinful and others are not.

**11)** "And indeed, Iblees made his assumption about them come true, and they followed him, except a group of believers."

This noble verse makes it clear that a group of believers did not obey Shaitan. Those who did not obey Shaitan are also not sinners. Now, is this group the noble prophets or other people? If they are the noble prophets, then certainly the ruling for all prophets is the same. And if they are not prophets, then it is still clear that the noble prophets cannot be sinners — because if the prophets were sinners and others were not, then the non-prophet would surpass the prophet in rank. This cannot happen, as the entire Ummah agrees that no one can attain the rank of a prophet.

**12)** Allah ta'ala has mentioned two categories of His creation. Regarding one, He said:

> "They are the party of Shaitan. Beware! Indeed, the party of Shaitan is the loser."

Regarding the other, He said:

> "They are the party of Allah. Beware! Indeed, the party of Allah is the successful."

There is no doubt that the party of Shaitan consists of those who do deeds that Shaitan likes, and Shaitan's liked deeds are sins. Every person who disobeys Allah ta'ala and commits sins will be in the party of Shaitan. If, Allah forbid, the noble prophets also committed sins, they would be included in the party of Shaitan and be among the losers.

Can any Muslim say that the righteous and pious people of the Ummah are the party of Allah and will be successful, while the noble prophets are included in the party of Shaitan and are among the losers? This can never happen. For any Muslim to even think this is to destroy his worldly and religious life.

**13)** The noble prophets are superior to the angels. Therefore, it is necessary that no sin can occur from them. Regarding the angels, Allah ta'ala said:

> "They do not precede Him in speech, and they act according to His command."

And further:

> "And they do not disobey Allah in what He commands them, and they do what they are commanded."

When the angels act according to Allah's command and do not violate it, how can it be that the noble prophets — who are superior to them — do not act according to Allah's command and violate it? Sinners cannot even be equal to the righteous, let alone superior. Allah ta'ala said:

> "Shall We make those who believe and do righteous deeds like those who spread corruption on earth? Or shall We make the righteous like the wicked?"

**14)** The Noble Prophet ﷺ purchased a she-camel from a Bedouin and paid him the price. The Bedouin then demanded the price again. The Prophet ﷺ said: "I have already paid you." The Bedouin asked for witnesses. The Prophet ﷺ thought: "Who will bear witness? No one was present at that time." Hazrat Khuzaima <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> said: "O Messenger of Allah! I bear witness that you have paid the Bedouin the price of the she-camel." The Prophet ﷺ asked: "How could you bear witness when you were not present?" He replied: "O Messenger of Allah! You inform us of the news of the heavens, and we believe you. So how can we not believe you regarding the payment for this she-camel? How can this be?" The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Henceforth, wherever Khuzaima alone bears witness, his testimony will be considered equal to that of two persons."

If, Allah forbid, the noble prophets committed sins, Hazrat Khuzaima would never have borne witness. Rather, he would have thought that the Noble Prophet ﷺ could also lie like us.

**15)** Allah ta'ala said to Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup>:

> "Indeed, I am going to make you a leader for the people."

An Imam is one whom people follow and obey. If sins occurred from the prophets, then following and obeying those sins would also become necessary. It is impossible that the prophet both forbids sins and commits them, and then commands people to follow his sins.

**16)** Allah ta'ala said:

> "The covenant of prophethood and leadership is for the righteous and those deserving of Allah's proximity — not for the wrongdoers."

Sinners can never become prophets. It is clearly understood that the noble prophets do not commit sins, because sinners are not granted the station of prophethood.

## The Descent of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hazrat Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> to Earth

> "And thus He removed them from where they were residing."

That is, in Paradise, Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> were permitted to reside and eat all kinds of fruits and blessings of Paradise — except they were forbidden from one tree. When Shaitan, appearing as a well-wisher, swearing oaths and giving advice, succeeded in casting whispers into them, they had to leave Paradise and its blessings. Allah ta'ala commanded:

> "And We said: All of you descend — some of you are enemies to others."

And at another place:

> "The Lord said: Both of you descend from Paradise together."

The purpose of both verses is that Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, along with their descendants who were to come into existence until the Day of Judgment, were commanded to descend to earth. And it was said: "Your descendants will be enemies to one another."

It should be noted that Shaitan had been rejected and sent to earth before their descent. His descent is not mentioned here. Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was sent down in Sarandeep (Sri Lanka), Hazrat Hawwa was sent down in Jeddah, and Shaitan had already been sent down to Ila (a place in Iraq).

When Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came to earth, his heavenly garments had been removed, and he came covering his body with the leaves of the trees of Paradise.

Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> states that the land of India is excellent, green, and fragrant with various scents because when Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came to that land, he had the leaves of the trees of Paradise on his body. Those leaves, carried by the wind, landed on various trees, making them fragrant forever.

### What Did Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Bring from Paradise?

Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> brought various kinds of seeds, three types of fruits, the Black Stone (Hajr-e-Aswad — the black stone set in the Ka'aba), and the staff (Asa) which later came into the hands of Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — its length was ten yards. He also brought some gold and silver and some agricultural tools with him.

Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was so engrossed in weeping and wailing that he became heedless of those seeds. Shaitan took the opportunity and touched them. Whichever seed he touched became poisonous, and whichever remained protected from his touch retained its benefit.

Three types of heavenly fruits came with Sayyiduna Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>:
1. Those that are eaten whole
2. Those whose outer part is eaten and the pit is thrown away — like dates, mangoes, etc.
3. Those whose outer peel is thrown away and the inner part is eaten

According to a sound narration, iron tools also came with him — a pair of tongs for holding iron, a hammer, and an anvil — along with the Black Stone.

A sound narration states that when the Black Stone came from Paradise, its light could be seen for miles. The boundaries of the Haram (sacred precinct) were established up to where its rays reached.

When Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came to the world, he felt great loneliness and anxiety. Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, by divine command, came to earth and called out the Adhan (call to prayer) in a loud voice. When Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> heard the name of the Holy Prophet ﷺ in the Adhan, his loneliness disappeared. All these events are established through authentic hadith, which Shah Abdul Azeez Muhaddis Dehlavi has compiled in *Tafseer Azeezi* at this point.

### The Means of Livelihood of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

In the same *Tafseer Azeezi*, it is stated that the first person to do the work of weaving cloth was Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Later, he remained engaged in agriculture. The means of livelihood of Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was carpentry, Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was tailoring, Hazrat Hood and Hazrat Saaleh <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> were trade, Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did farming, Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> tended goats for some time, Hazrat Dawood <sup>عليه السلام</sup> made chainmail, Hazrat Suleman <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — despite being such a great king — made fans and baskets from leaves to earn his living, and Hazrat Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did not adopt any profession but always traveled, saying: "The One who gave me food in the morning will also give me food in the evening."

**Benefit:** In *Tafseer Azeezi* at the same point, it is mentioned that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> never drank water from wells but always drank rainwater. Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was the first to make silver coins (rupees) and gold coins (ashrafis).

### Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Repentance

> "Then Adam received certain words from his Lord, and Allah turned to him in mercy. Indeed, He is the Oft-Returning, the Most Merciful."

The meaning of "talaqqa" is to go forward and meet — that is, to welcome. So the meaning is that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> welcomed the words of Allah with love and honor, like honored guests and beloved friends. What were those words? Allama Abu Hayyan said:

"Allah ta'ala did not specify those words but merely informed us of vague words through 'Adam received certain words from his Lord.' Therefore, there are several transmitted statements from the scholars regarding their identification."

1) Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> and other scholars said those words were:
> "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."

2) It is transmitted from Abdullah bin Masood <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> that the words were:
> "Glorified are You, O Allah, and with Your praise. Blessed is Your name and exalted is Your majesty. There is no god but You."

3) It is transmitted from Wahab and Muhammad bin Ka'ab that the words were:
> "Glorified are You, O Allah, and with Your praise. I have committed evil and wronged myself, so forgive me — indeed, You are the Best of forgivers."
This saying is also attributed to Abdullah bin Abbas.

4) Another saying is that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> saw "Muhammad" written on the base of the Throne, and he made that blessed name his means of intercession. This last saying has been narrated by Hazrat Abdul Azeez Muhaddis Dehlavi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> from Tibrani, Baihaqi, and Hakim, from Hazrat Farooq-e-Azam <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, in *Tafseer Azeezi*:

> "O Allah, I seek forgiveness through the means of Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ."

This is also transmitted from Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> through Ibnul Manzar in the same *Tafseer Azeezi*.

Allama Sayyed Mehmood Aaloosi Hanafi Baghdadi said:

"One saying is that Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> saw 'Muhammad' written on the base of the Throne, and he made the Holy Prophet his means of intercession — that is, those words are 'Muhammad Rasulullah.'" Allama Aaloosi states: "When in the Quran, Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is called Kalimatullah (the Word of Allah), then the greatest soul, the Beloved Prophet ﷺ, being called Kalimatullah is certainly established. Neither Isa nor Moosa — in fact, no one in the realm of existence — but all are the manifestations of the lights of the Holy Prophet and the fragrant flowers of his garden of beauty."

If Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had not presented the blessed name of the Noble Prophet ﷺ as a means, and similarly, if Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had not taken the means of his blessed name, neither would Adam's repentance have been accepted nor would Nooh have been saved from drowning.

### When Was Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Repentance Accepted?

When Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> repented through these words, Allah ta'ala turned His merciful attention toward him and accepted the repentance immediately.

According to some scholars, Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> receiving the words from Allah ta'ala, repenting through them, and their acceptance occurred after the descent from Paradise. The repentance was accepted after hundreds of years — 200, 300, or more — during which he passed his time in sighs, weeping, wailing, and remorse. Shah Abdul Azeez <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> has stated this in *Tafseer Azeezi*.

But the truth is that even before leaving Paradise, Allah ta'ala had granted Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> those words, and he repented at that very moment, which was accepted. At that very moment, Allah ta'ala forgave his mistake. However, it is possible that despite forgiveness, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> remained engaged in weeping and wailing for years due to remorse over his slip — which is required by fear and reverence and is proof of perfect servitude.

**Benefit:** Allah ta'ala did not say "Fataaba alaihima" (He accepted both their repentances) — meaning He did not specify both separately — because women are subordinate to men. When the man is mentioned, the woman's mention is automatically included.

**Note:** Allama Ahmad Sayeed Kazmi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> has preferred the saying of *Ruhul Ma'ani* that Adam's repentance was accepted before coming to earth, and the weeping on earth was for humility. However, Mufti Ahmad Yaar Khan <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> stated in *Tafseer Naimi* (page 289) that this saying is weak. If repentance was accepted before coming to earth, then why the separation from the wife and the hardships? That is, the Lord does not put someone in hardship without reason after forgiving them.

### On Which Day Was Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Repentance Accepted?

Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> repentance was accepted on Friday. His creation and his descent from Paradise also occurred on Friday, and it was the day of Ashura — the 10th of Muharram.

It should be noted that important events occurred on Ashura, which was a Friday: Adam's repentance, Nooh's ark coming to rest on land, Yunus coming out of the fish's belly, Ayyub's cure, Moosa's deliverance from Pharaoh (Firaun) and Pharaoh's (Firaun's) drowning, Yaqoob meeting Yusuf, and Hazrat Imam Husain's martyrdom in Karbala — all occurred on the 10th of Muharram. The scholars spend the night of the 11th in ease and comfort. Ahl-e-Sunnat arrange gatherings for the isaal-e-sawab (dedication of reward) of Hazrat Ghaus-e-Paak on the night of the 11th, which in reality is also an expression of joy for the blessings attained by all those great personalities.

### The Reunion of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hazrat Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>

When they came to earth, Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> descended on a mountain in the Sarandeep region of India (Sri Lanka), and Hazrat Hawwa descended in Jeddah. After the acceptance of repentance, both met at a place called Arafat. They recognized each other — therefore, that plain is called Arafat, meaning "the place of recognition."

When Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> came from Paradise, the Arabic language was taken from him — that is, he forgot it. For many days, he spoke the Syriac language. After the repentance was accepted, Arabic was granted again. Then Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> called out to all the animals of the world: "O animals! Allah ta'ala has sent His vicegerent to you. Obey and submit to him." The aquatic animals raised their heads and showed obedience, and the land animals gathered around Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> began to pass his hand over them. Those he touched became domestic and tame — like horses, camels, goats, dogs, cats, etc. And those he did not touch remained wild — like deer, etc.

### Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Prayer for His Children

After this event, Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> submitted: "Master, my children are very weak and Iblees's deception is very severe. If You do not help them, how will they escape from Iblees?" The divine command came: "O Adam, there are commandments for you and your children, and there will be commandments. We will place an angel with every human being." Then Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was pleased and expressed gratitude.

### Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Offspring

Hazrat Hawwa became pregnant from Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> twenty or forty times. In each pregnancy, two children were born — one male and one female. The rule for the children of one pregnancy with the children of another pregnancy was like that of children from different parents — that is, the boy of the first pregnancy would marry the girl of the second, and the boy of the second would marry the girl of the first.

**Note:** Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had two children from each pregnancy, except Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Hazrat Hawwa presented Hazrat Shees alone — there was no twin sister with him. This was only for the honor and respect of the Noble Prophet ﷺ that the Master of the Kingdom caused her to conceive only one child, because the light of the Noble Prophet ﷺ was transferred from Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> made a will to him that this blessed light must be transferred through pure women. Then Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> made the same will to his son. This chain continued until Hazrat Abdul Muttalib, with each person instructing his son to transfer this light through pure wombs.
