## Hazrat Uzair <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

> "Or like the one who passed by a township while it had collapsed on its roofs. He said: 'How will Allah bring this to life after its death?' So Allah caused him to die for a hundred years, then raised him. He said: 'How long have you remained?' He replied: 'I have remained a day or part of a day.' He said: 'Rather, you have remained a hundred years. Look at your food and drink — they have not changed. And look at your donkey. And We will make you a sign for the people. And look at the bones, how We raise them and then clothe them with flesh.' So when it became clear to him, he said: 'I know that Allah is over all things competent.'"

### The Destruction of Baitul Muqaddas

Banu Israel lived in Baitul Muqaddas. When they transgressed all limits in sin, disobedience, and immorality, and did not follow their prophet, Bakht-e-Nasar launched a fierce attack and took control. This happened approximately 600 years before Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

He had 600,000 standard-bearers, and under each banner was countless troops. He destroyed Baitul Muqaddas, burned all copies of the Torah, killed about one-third of Banu Israel, kept one-third in humiliation in Syria, and took one-third as captives. Among those captives were Hazrat Uzair and Hazrat Daniyaal, who were children at that time.

### 100 Years of Death

After a long time, some captives were freed. Uzair <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was among them. He passed by Baitul Muqaddas, now completely ruined. He wandered around but saw no one. However, the gardens were full of various fruit-laden trees with no one to eat from them. He picked some figs and grapes, ate them, extracted their juice and drank it, and kept some figs, grapes, and juice as provisions.

When he saw the ruined settlement, he said in amazement and sorrow: "How will my Lord revive this? What a great power that would be!" — this was not doubt in Allah's power but an expression of wonder.

Allah ta'ala wished to acquaint him with His perfect power. Hazrat Uzair tied his donkey, placed his food and drink nearby, and fell asleep. While sleeping, death was made to overtake him. His donkey also died. This happened around midday.

### The Liberation of Banu Israel

Just as a mosquito entered Namrood's nose, similarly a mosquito entered Bakht-e-Nasar's nose and he died. Around 70 years later, a king of Persia arrived with his armies at Baitul Muqaddas and rebuilt it better than before. Banu Israel, who had scattered due to Bakht-e-Nasar's oppression, resettled in Baitul Muqaddas. Over thirty years, they recovered considerably, and their population increased.

### Uzair is Revived

After 100 years, Allah ta'ala revived Uzair <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. During this entire period, Allah kept him hidden from people's sight — no wild animal, bird, or insect could see him.

Death had come to him in the morning before midday, and he was revived in the evening. This is why when the Lord asked him how long he had stayed, he replied: "A day or even less" — he thought he had only lain down that morning. Then he reconsidered: "Not even a full day has passed — only part of a day." Allah ta'ala told him: "A hundred years have passed."

### The Resurrection of the Donkey

SubhanAllah — what immense power the Master of the Kingdom possesses! Uzair's body remained completely preserved for 100 years. His food and drink remained unchanged. And before his eyes, the Lord revived the donkey, displaying His power. The bones of the donkey were commanded to come together, flesh was clothed upon them, and the donkey was called to life — all in an instant.

### Return to the City

Uzair <sup>عليه السلام</sup> returned to the city and found it even more prosperous than before. New generations of Banu Israel had been born. When he had died, he was around 40 years old, and he was still 40. When he had left the city, his son was 18; now his son was 118, and his grandsons had grown old.

When he arrived at his home, he met an old bondwoman of his who was now 120 years old. Her legs had become weak with age, she could barely walk, and her sight was gone. Due to centuries of change, the buildings looked different and he could not be certain which was his own house.

He asked the old woman: "Is this Uzair's house?" She began to cry and said: "After so long, who is mentioning Uzair? He has been gone for a hundred years." He said: "I am Uzair. Allah kept me dead for a hundred years and has now revived me."

She said: "If you are truthful, pray to Allah to restore my sight so I can see and recognize you — Allah used to accept Uzair's prayers. If your prayer is accepted, I will know you are truly Uzair."

He prayed, and her sight was restored. Her legs also healed and she could walk again. Now she recognized him. She took him to his son and family, saying: "Uzair has come!" Everyone was amazed — how could Uzair return after a hundred years? The old woman said: "Don't you see me? Through his prayer, Allah gave me sight and made me able to walk."

His son said: "My father had black hair between his shoulders in the shape of a crescent moon." When his shoulders were checked, the hair was exactly as described.

### Uzair Restores the Torah

Bakht-e-Nasar had burned all copies of the Torah. No one remembered it — only the prophets used to memorize scriptures. The people said: "If you are Uzair, recite the Torah to us."

He dictated the entire Torah to them word for word, not letting a single letter differ. Then a man said: "I heard from my grandfather that due to Bakht-e-Nasar's oppression, my grandfather had buried a copy of the Torah." Hazrat Uzair <sup>عليه السلام</sup> pointed out the exact spot where it was buried. When that copy was excavated and compared with what Uzair had written, not a single word differed.

Seeing this miracle, people began to say that Uzair was the son of Allah.

Allah ta'ala said: **"The Jews say Uzair is the son of Allah."**

## Hazrat Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

### Sent to Two Nations

Hazrat Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was sent as a messenger to two nations: **Madyan** and **As'haabe Ika** (People of the Thicket).

Since he was from the tribe of Madyan, the Quran says: "And to Madyan, their brother Shoaib." But for As'haabe Ika, it does not say "their brother" — only "when Shoaib said to them." The punishments for the two nations were also different, as will be mentioned later. However, since they lived close to each other and had mutual relations, their practices were similar, and Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> preached to both similarly.

### Lineage

Madyan was originally the name of a son of Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. His descendants were called Madyan, and the tribe named Madyan built a city also called Madyan. Hazrat Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was from this tribe.

His lineage is stated as: **Shoaib bin Nuwaib bin Madyan bin Ibrahim alaihissalam**.

### Three Commands

> "And to Madyan, their brother Shoaib. He said: 'O my people, worship Allah — you have no god besides Him. Indeed, clear proof has come to you from your Lord. So give full measure and weight, and do not diminish people's things, and do not cause corruption in the land after its reformation. That is better for you, if you are believers.'"

**First**: Worship Allah alone and forbid worship of others.

**Second**: Proof of prophethood — "Clear proof has come to you" — *bayyinah* here refers to miracles. Every claimant to prophethood must present a miracle. The exact nature of Shoaib's miracle is not clearly mentioned in the Quran or Hadith. However, the author of *Kashshaaf* narrates that he gave Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> the staff with which he defeated the magicians. Also, he told Moosa that his goats would bear black-and-white offspring, and it happened.

**Third**: Prohibition of evil — especially giving short measure and weight. Shoaib's people were merchants who cheated in weighing. After that, he commanded generally: "Do not diminish people's things" — this includes all forms of taking wealth unjustly: theft, bribery, robbery, deceit.

And "Do not cause corruption in the land after its reformation" — spreading corruption destroys religion and the world. Allah had established reform through His prophets; do not disrupt this order.

### Lawful Provision is Best

> "The provision of Allah that remains is better for you, if you are believers."

If you stop cheating and give people their full rights, what remains with you will contain blessing. The reward of obedience to the Lord is far greater than worldly wealth in status and rank.

This can only be understood by one who has faith and certainty — that death will come, that there is another world beyond this one where accounting will take place, and that reward and punishment exist.

The prophet said: "My work is only to advise you. I do not have the power to stop you from evil. If you do not abandon your bad deeds and your blessings are taken away, I cannot save you."

### The People's Mocking Reply

> "They said: 'O Shoaib, does your prayer command you that we should abandon what our fathers worshipped or that we should not do what we wish with our wealth? Indeed, you are the forbearing, the right-minded!'"

They said sarcastically: "You think you are very prayerful, honest, and religious. Your prayers tell you to turn us away from our fathers' religion and stop us from gathering wealth. Indeed, you consider yourself very wise and righteous — but we consider you a fool. How can we accept your words?"

### The Prophet's Response

> "He said: 'O my people, tell me — if I am upon clear proof from my Lord, and He has provided me with good provision from Himself, and I do not wish to oppose you in what I forbid you from... I only desire reform as far as I am able. And my success is only from Allah. In Him I put my trust, and to Him I turn.'"

Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> said: "Allah has given me the knowledge of guidance and prophethood, and abundant lawful provision. He was very wealthy. Can it be that with such great blessings, I should betray His revelation and commands?"

Note: The term **اِنَّكَ لَنْتَ الْحَلِيمُ الرَّشِيدُ** also means they acknowledged his intelligence and resolve: "You are resourceful and wise — so why do you stop us from our forefathers' religion?" The prophet replied: "If you acknowledge my wisdom, then understand that I want to lead you to a better path, and I myself follow it. It cannot be that I forbid you from evil deeds while committing them myself."

### Opposition Invites Punishment

> "And do not let your hostility towards me lead you to the same punishment that struck the people of Nooh, or the people of Hood, or the people of Saaleh. And the people of Looth are not far from you."

Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> warned that the destroyed towns of Looth were near their region and their era was also near. He urged them to turn to Allah and seek forgiveness — Allah is Most Generous, Most Merciful, and never deprives those who turn to Him of His mercy.

### Threats from the People

> "They said: 'O Shoaib, we do not understand much of what you say, and indeed we see you as weak among us. Were it not for your clan, we would have stoned you. And you are not respected among us.'"

Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> spoke to them in their own language, yet they claimed not to understand — due to their intense hatred, they paid no attention. What they heard, they dismissed as worthless.

They said: "You are from our tribe, and your family is respected among us. But you yourself are nothing to us. We only spare you because of your family — otherwise, we would have stoned you to death."

### The Prophet's Amazement at Their Foolishness

> "He said: 'O my people, is my family more honored by you than Allah, and you have taken Him as something behind your backs? Indeed, my Lord encompasses what you do.'"

Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> expressed amazement: "You respect my family, sparing me for their sake, yet you throw Allah's commands behind your backs and do not obey Him. If you wanted to protect me, you should have protected me as Allah's prophet — that would bring you His pleasure. Know that my Lord is aware of all your deeds — you can never escape His encompassing power."

### The Arrogant Leaders' Ultimatum

> "The arrogant chiefs of his people said: 'O Shoaib, we will surely expel you and those who believe with you from our city, or you must return to our religion.'"

The arrogant chiefs and leaders threatened him: "Return to our religion — you and your followers. If you do not accept our religion, we will expel you from our city."

### The Prophet Does Not Fear Threats

> "He said: 'Even if we are averse? We would be inventing lies against Allah if we returned to your religion after Allah has saved us from it.'"

Shoaib replied in decisive words: "Should we enter a religion we detest? That can never happen. Allah has done us a great favor by protecting us from your false religion. If we returned to it at your bidding, that would be forging lies against Allah."

### The Final Verdict

> "O my people, do as you wish. I too am doing (what I must). Soon you will know upon whom comes a punishment that disgraces him, and who is a liar. And wait — I too am waiting with you."

Shoaib said: "Do whatever you can. If you can harm me, try. But know that I am not without support — I have the One who has granted me great powers. Do your work, I will do mine."

They asked: "What will happen then?" He replied: "Soon you will know who receives the humiliating punishment and who is the liar. The moment of decision is coming — wait, and I too shall wait."

### The Punishment of Madyan

> "And when Our command came, We saved Shoaib and those who believed with him by mercy from Us. And the blast seized the wrongdoers, and they lay dead in their homes as if they had never lived there. So away with Madyan, just as Samood were destroyed."

Allah sent an angel whose voice killed them all. It was Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — when he gave a mighty, thundering shout, every soul departed. They were found fallen on their faces in their homes, as if they had never lived there.

Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضی اللہ تعالیٰ عنہما</sup> said: "Allah did not give two nations the same type of punishment except Shoaib's nation and Saaleh's nation. Both were destroyed by a mighty blast — but for Saaleh's people the sound came from below, and for Shoaib's people it came from above."

### Shoaib and As'haabe Ika

> "The People of the Thicket denied the messengers. Shoaib said to them: 'Will you not fear Allah? Indeed, I am a trustworthy messenger to you. So fear Allah and obey me. I do not ask you for any reward — my reward is only with the Lord of the worlds. Give full measure and do not be among those who give less. Weigh with an accurate balance. Do not diminish people's things, and do not spread corruption in the land. And fear Him who created you and the earlier generations.'"

**Who were As'haabe Ika?** Some scholars considered Madyan and As'haabe Ika to be two names for the same nation. But the correct view is that they were two separate nations living in different regions. However, since their areas were very close and both were from Ibrahim's lineage, one prophet — Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — was appointed for both.

Both nations lived near major trade routes and were merchants. The same moral corruptions common among merchants were found in both. Both had abandoned the belief in *tawheed* and were entangled in the curse of *shirk*. Therefore, Shoaib's counsel was the same for both.

"Ika" in Arabic refers to a place dense with thick trees. This nation lived in such a wooded area, hence called As'haabul Ika. Some recited it as "Laika," considering it the name of a specific settlement.

Like Madyan, As'haabe Ika's entire prosperity depended on dishonesty and deceit. They could have been reformed by listening to Shoaib, but they refused to recognize their error. Instead, they accused him: "Someone has cast a spell on you. No sensible person would give advice that destroys his people's economy. First, get treatment for your mind, then come advise us."

### The Challenge and the Punishment

> "They said: 'You are but one of those bewitched. You are nothing but a human like us. Indeed, we think you are among the liars. So drop upon us a piece of the sky, if you are of the truthful.'"

They said: "We will not believe you. If you can, drop a piece of the sky on us." They thought punishment would never come, proving Shoaib a liar.

SubhanAllah — what immense patience! They called him a liar, bewitched, deranged, and even challenged him. Yet he did not curse them but entrusted the matter to Allah: "My Lord knows best what you do."

### The Day of the Canopy

> "So they denied him, and the punishment of the Day of the Canopy seized them. Indeed, it was a punishment of a tremendous day."

When they repeatedly rejected Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, Allah sent punishment upon them. For seven days, Allah withheld the wind and subjected them to scorching sand. Their breath was choked — neither shade nor water could help them. In distress, they fled toward the jungle, and a cloud came and provided shade, giving them coolness and pleasant breeze. As they gathered under that cloud, suddenly fire rained down upon them, burning them all to ashes. They were completely destroyed — their name and trace wiped out, as if no settlement had ever existed there.

> "Indeed, in this is a sign, but most of them were not believers. And indeed, your Lord — He is the Almighty, the Most Merciful."
