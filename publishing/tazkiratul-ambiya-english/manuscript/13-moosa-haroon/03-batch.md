## Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — Continued

### The Term Completed and the Return to Egypt

> "When Moosa completed the term and set out with his family, he perceived a fire from the direction of Toor. He said to his family: 'Stay here — I have seen a fire. Perhaps I can bring you news from there or a burning ember so that you may warm yourselves.' When he came to it, he was called from the right side of the valley, in the blessed spot, from the tree: 'O Moosa, indeed I am Allah, the Lord of all worlds.'"

Shoaib <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had mentioned eight years and ten years of service. Which term did Moosa complete? Imam Bukhari and others narrate from Ibn Abbas <sup>رضی اللہ تعالیٰ عنہما</sup> that he was asked which term Moosa completed. He replied: "Moosa completed the longer term — ten years." The eight years were obligatory, and the additional two were voluntary — a means of greater purity and reward. The Messenger of Allah acts upon what he says.

When he completed ten years, he sought Shoaib's permission to go to Egypt to visit his mother and brother. He thought enough time had passed since the killing of the Copt and the matter might have cooled down. Shoaib granted permission. He took his wife, one mount, some goats, essential provisions, and — fearing the kings of Syria — he avoided the main road and took a desert route. He lost his way, his goats scattered, and there was no water nearby.

### At Mount Toor

He arrived at the western side of the valley of Tawa near Toor on a Friday night that was extremely cold. His son was born there. He saw a fire on the left side of the road toward Toor and told his wife: "Stay here — I will bring an ember from there or light a fire and bring it so you can warm yourselves."

Note: The Quran uses words for ember, flame, and blazing flame — meaning "I will bring an ember, or a flame, or kindle something there so I may learn the way if there is someone there."

When Moosa approached, he saw a fire blazing slowly. When he came closer, the fire intensified. A great blazing fire appeared — but the amazing sight was that the fire was coming from a tree, and the more intense the fire became, the greener the leaves of the tree grew. He stood lost in thought: how could fire be fierce while the leaves remained green? After long contemplation, his mind reached no conclusion.

Note: There are four types of fire:

1. Fire that consumes but does not drink — this is the fire of this world.
2. Fire that both consumes and drinks — the fire of the stomach (digestion).
3. Fire that neither consumes nor drinks — the fire Moosa saw.
4. Fire that drinks but does not consume — the fire mentioned by Allah in the verse: "فَاشْرَبُوا عَلَيْهِ" (then drink upon it).

### The Call from the Tree

When Moosa intended to kindle the fire, it moved away from him. He stood lost in thought. Then he was called by Allah ta'ala from the tree on the right side of the valley in the blessed spot. This was the beginning of his prophethood.

The staff that had been passed down from prophet to prophet was now with Moosa. It was about to become the first miracle of his prophethood — the staff that would turn into a serpent and swallow the ropes and staffs of the magicians.
