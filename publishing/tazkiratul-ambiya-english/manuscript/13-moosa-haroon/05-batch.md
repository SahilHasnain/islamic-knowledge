## Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — Continued

### The Chiefs Warn Firaun

The chiefs of Firaun's people counseled him: "Will you allow Moosa and his followers to spread corruption in the land and abandon you and your gods?" They said: "If you leave Moosa free, he will turn the people away from you and your gods."

### The Drought and Punishments (Plagues)

Allah sent upon Firaun and his people a severe drought (qehat saali) — years of famine with scarce harvest. Each time a punishment struck, they would call Moosa and promise: "Pray to your Lord — if He removes this punishment from us, we will surely believe." Moosa would pray, the punishment would lift, but they would break their promise.

After one month of ease, another punishment would come — various types of afflictions (floods, locusts, lice, frogs, blood) — each lasting a week. This continued as a cycle of punishment, repentance, and betrayal.

### The Command to Leave Egypt

Allah commanded Moosa to take Bani Israel out of Egypt by night. The route was identified by the blessed chest of Yusuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, which was retrieved from the Nile — when it was brought out, the dark night became illuminated, showing them the way.

### Striking the Sea

At dawn, Firaun gathered his forces and pursued. When Moosa and Bani Israel reached the sea, they were trapped — the sea before them, the enemy behind. The people despaired. Allah commanded Moosa to strike the sea with his staff. He did, and the sea parted. Twelve paths opened, one for each tribe, with walls of water towering like great mountains on either side. Lamps lit the seabed paths.

### Firaun Drowns

Firaun and his army entered the sea after them. When Firaun saw the water closing in, he cried out: "I believe that there is no god but He in whom the children of Israel believe, and I am of those who submit."

But it was too late. Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> stuffed mud into Firaun's mouth so his declaration would not bring mercy. Allah said: "Now? While you were disobedient before and were of the mischief-makers? Today We will preserve your body so you may be a sign for those after you."

### Bani Israel's Ingratitude

After crossing the sea and witnessing the destruction of Firaun, Bani Israel passed by a people who were devoted to their idols. They said: "O Moosa, make for us a god just as they have gods." Moosa replied: "Indeed you are an ignorant people. What they are engaged in is destruction."

### Moosa Goes to Mount Toor for the Torah

Allah commanded Moosa to fast for thirty days, then added ten more — completing forty days of seclusion (chilla) on Mount Toor. During this time, the Torah was revealed to him.

### The Desire to See Allah

Moosa <sup>عليه السلام} asked: "My Lord, show me Yourself that I may look upon You." Allah said: "You will not see Me, but look at the mountain; if it remains in its place, then you will see Me." When His Lord manifested to the mountain, it crumbled to dust, and Moosa fell unconscious. When he recovered, he said: "Glory be to You, I turn to You in repentance, and I am the first of the believers."

### The Golden Calf

While Moosa was on Toor, Saamri (Samiri) collected the jewelry of Bani Israel (borrowed from the Egyptians during the exodus) and molded a calf from gold — a body that lowed like a cow. He said: "This is your god and the god of Moosa — he has forgotten."

Allah had already revealed to Moosa on Toor: "We have tested your people after you, and Saamri has led them astray." Moosa returned in anger and grief.

### Moosa Rebukes Haroon

Moosa seized his brother Haroon by the head and beard, saying: "Why did you not follow me? Why did you disobey my command?" Haroon replied: "O son of my mother, the people overpowered me and almost killed me. Do not let the enemies rejoice over my misfortune and do not count me among the wrongdoers." Moosa then prayed: "My Lord, forgive me and my brother and admit us into Your mercy — You are the Most Merciful of the merciful."

### Saamri's Punishment

Moosa asked Saamri: "What led you to do this?" He replied: "I saw what they did not see — I took a handful from the footprint of the messenger (Jibreel's horse) and cast it into the fire." Moosa said: "Go away — your punishment in this life is that you will say 'Do not touch me.' And you have an appointment which you will not miss."

### The Calf Repents and the Covenant Taken

Bani Israel were commanded to repent — the guilty were to kill the innocent among themselves, and the innocent to kill the guilty, until Allah accepted their repentance.

Moosa brought them the Torah on tablets. Some found the commandments heavy and refused. Allah lifted the mountain over them as a canopy, and they were forced to accept the covenant.

### Amalek and the Command for Jihad

Allah commanded Bani Israel to enter the Holy Land and fight the Amalekites (Amaalqa). But they refused, saying: "O Moosa, a mighty people dwell there — we will never enter it until they leave. If they leave, we will enter." They said: "Go, you and your Lord, and fight — we will sit here."

Moosa prayed: "My Lord, I control only myself and my brother — separate us from this defiant people." Allah decreed the land forbidden to them for forty years — they wandered in the desert (Maidaan Teeh).

### Blessings in the Desert

Despite their rebellion, Allah provided:
- **Manna**: A sweet, honey-like substance that fell from the sky
- **Salwa**: Quails that flocked to their camp (described as roasted meat falling from the sky, or live birds they caught)
- **Water from stone**: When Moosa struck a rock with his staff, twelve springs gushed forth — one for each tribe

Yet they complained: "We cannot bear one kind of food. Pray to your Lord to bring us what the earth grows — cucumbers, garlic, lentils, onions." Moosa said: "Would you exchange what is better for what is lesser? Go down to any city and you will find what you ask."

### The Cow of Bani Israel

(Connected to Qaaroon's story — then the cow incident)

A wealthy man was murdered by his nephew for inheritance. The body was left at a crossroads. Bani Israel brought the case to Moosa. Allah commanded them to slaughter a cow and strike the corpse with its tongue (or part of it) — the dead man would be brought back to life and identify the killer.

They asked: "Do you mock us?" Moosa said: "I seek refuge with Allah from being among the ignorant."

"Pray to your Lord to specify what cow."

Allah said: "An old cow, neither young nor old — between the two conditions."

"Pray again — what color?"

"Pure yellow, pleasing to the beholders."

"Pray again — she is indistinguishable to us."

"Neither trained to plow nor water the field — sound, no blemish."

They found it with a poor orphan boy and his widowed mother. The cow was priceless — they paid a huge sum (nearly the cow's weight in gold). When they finally slaughtered it and struck the corpse, the murdered man sat up and named his killer, then died again.

The wisdom: The wealthy man had left hidden wealth for his orphaned children. By Allah's design, the cow could only be obtained from the widow and orphans, transferring the treasure to them.
