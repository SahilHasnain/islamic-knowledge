## Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — Continued

### The Miracles

At Toor, Allah ta'ala commanded Moosa: "Throw down your staff." When he did, it became a massive serpent, rushing with great speed. Then he was commanded to place his hand under his armpit — when he brought it out, it shone with a brilliant, luminous white light. These two miracles — the staff and the shining hand — were granted to Moosa <sup>عليه السلام> as proof of his prophethood.

### Moosa's Prayer for His Tongue

When commanded to go to Firaun, Moosa <sup>عليه السلام> prayed: "My Lord, expand my chest and make my task easy, and remove the knot from my tongue so they may understand my speech. And appoint for me a helper from my family — Haroon, my brother."

The reason: when Moosa was a child in Firaun's palace, a test was devised. Firaun placed a burning coal and a ruby before the young Moosa. Moosa reached for the ruby, but Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام> guided his hand to the coal. When the coal burned his tongue, he put it in his mouth, resulting in a speech impediment. He now prayed for healing so that his message would be clearly understood.

Allah replied: "You have been granted your request, O Moosa."

### The Test of Fire and Ruby

This incident occurred when Moosa was a toddler in Firaun's palace. The courtiers warned Firaun that the child might be the one destined to overthrow him. To test the child, Firaun placed a tray with a burning coal and a precious ruby (or gold). If Moosa chose the ruby, it would show intelligence and confirm the threat. But Jibreel guided his hand to the coal. Moosa put the coal in his mouth, burning his tongue and causing the speech impediment. This is why Firaun's threat was considered neutralized.

### Command to Preach to Firaun

Allah said: "Go to Firaun — he has transgressed all bounds. Speak to him gently; perhaps he may take heed or fear."

Moosa and Haroon <sup>عليهما السلام> went to Firaun and delivered Allah's message: "We are messengers of the Lord of the worlds. Send the children of Israel with us, and do not torment them."

### Firaun's Confrontation

> **"Did we not raise you among us as a child, and you stayed among us for many years of your life? And you did that deed which you did, and you are of the ungrateful."**

Firaun reminded Moosa that he was raised in the palace. Moosa replied: "I did that deed when I was unaware. Then I fled from you when I feared you. Then my Lord granted me judgment and made me one of the messengers. And as for this favor you remind me of — is it that you enslaved the children of Israel?"

### The Debate

Firaun asked: "Who is your Lord, O Moosa?"

Moosa replied: "Our Lord is He who gave everything its form, then guided it."

Firaun asked about past generations. Moosa said: "Knowledge of that is with my Lord, in a Book. My Lord does not err nor forget."

### Firaun Threatens

Firaun said: "If you take any god besides me, I will surely put you among the imprisoned."

Moosa replied: "Even if I bring you clear proof?" Firaun said: "Bring it, if you are truthful."

Moosa threw his staff — it became a clear serpent. He drew out his hand — it was white to the beholders.

### The Contest of the Magicians

Firaun said: "This is sorcery. Assemble the magicians." The appointed day was the festival day. The magicians of Egypt were gathered — the greatest experts in sorcery.

They asked: "O Moosa, will you throw, or shall we be the first to throw?"

Moosa said: "Throw what you will throw."

They threw their ropes and staffs, and by their sorcery, they appeared as moving serpents. Moosa felt fear in his heart. Allah revealed: "Do not fear — indeed, you shall prevail."

Moosa threw his staff. It swallowed all their false sorcery. The magicians fell down in prostration. They said: "We believe in the Lord of Haroon and Moosa."

### The Magicians Convert

Firaun was enraged: "You believed in him before I gave you permission! He is your chief who taught you sorcery. I will cut off your hands and feet from opposite sides and crucify you on palm trunks."

They replied: "We will not prefer you over the clear proofs that have come to us. By Him who created us, decide what you will decide — you can only decide in this worldly life. Indeed, we have believed in our Lord, that He may forgive us our sins and the sorcery you compelled us to perform."

### A Believer from Firaun's Family

A believing man from Firaun's family who concealed his faith said: "Will you kill a man for saying 'My Lord is Allah' when he has brought you clear proofs? If he is lying, his lie is upon him. But if he is truthful, some of what he threatens will strike you."

He continued: "O my people, I fear for you the likes of the day that befell the earlier nations — the people of Nooh, Aad, Samood, and those after them. I fear for you the Day of Calling — the Day you turn back in flight, with no protector from Allah."

But Firaun said: "I only show you what I see, and I only guide you to the right path."
