### Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is the direct son of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — there is no intermediary between them. Hazrat Adam's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> children were born as twins, but Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was born alone. He was born alone due to the miracle of the Noble Prophet ﷺ — no other child was born with him.

Ibn Jauzi narrates in his book *Wafa* from Ka'ab bin Ahbaar <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>: "When Allah ta'ala intended the creation of Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ, He commanded Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. He took a fistful of white soil from the place of the Noble Prophet's ﷺ blessed grave. It was kneaded with the water of the Tasneem stream, then taken around the heavens. The angels recognized Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ before Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Then the Noor of Muhammad appeared on the forehead of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was told, 'This is your son, the Chief of the Messengers.' When Hazrat Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> became pregnant with Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, that Noor was transferred from Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to Hazrat Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>. Hazrat Hawwa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> used to give birth to two children at a time, but She was made to give birth to him alone. In reality, Allah ta'ala kept only one child in Hazrat Hawwa's <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> womb — in this was the honor of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. Then that Noor continued to be transferred from pure wombs to pure wombs, until your respected mother Hazrat Aamina gave birth to you from Hazrat Abdullah."
