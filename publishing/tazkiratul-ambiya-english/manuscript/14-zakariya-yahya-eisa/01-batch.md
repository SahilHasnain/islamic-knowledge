## Hazrat Zakariya, Hazrat Yahya, Hazrat Eisa <sup>عليهم السلام</sup>

### Introduction — The Family of Imraan

The mother of Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was Maryam. Her mother's name was Hanna and father's name was Imraan. Hanna had a sister named Eisha. Both were daughters of Faaqooz. Imraan married Hanna, and Zakariya <sup>عليه السلام</sup> married Eisha. Therefore, Maryam and Yahya <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> were maternal cousins (khaalazaad).

Some narrate that Zakariya's wife was the sister of Maryam, but the majority view is the first.

### Hanna's Vow

Hanna was barren and had reached old age. One day she saw a bird feeding its chicks, and her heart yearned for a child. She prayed to Allah.

When she became pregnant, she vowed: "O my Lord, I dedicate to You what is in my womb — free for Your service — so accept from me. Indeed You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."

She intended a son, as only boys were dedicated to the service of Baitul Muqaddas. When she gave birth to a girl, she said with regret: "My Lord, I have delivered a female — and the male is not like the female."

Allah replied: "Allah knows best what she delivered. I have named her Maryam, and I seek refuge for her and her offspring from the accursed Satan."

### Maryam's Upbringing

Allah accepted Maryam graciously and caused her to grow beautifully. The scholars of Bani Israel drew lots (qurra) for who would be her guardian. Three times the lot fell to Zakariya <sup>عليه السلام> — once by drawing quills from under a cloth, once by casting quills into the Jordan river (his floated against the current), and once by casting again.

Zakariya <sup>عليه السلام> took her into his care. He kept her in a chamber in the mosque, accessible only by stairs, and would lock the door behind him when he left.

### Miraculous Provision

Whenever Zakariya visited Maryam in her chamber, he found her with summer fruits in winter and winter fruits in summer. He asked: "O Maryam, where does this come from?" She replied: "It is from Allah. Indeed Allah provides for whom He wills without account."

This is counted as a karaamat (miracle of a saint) for Maryam — she was not a prophet.

### Zakariya's Prayer for a Child

Seeing Allah's power in providing Maryam with out-of-season fruits, Zakariya's heart turned to asking for a child despite his old age and his wife's barrenness.

He prayed secretly, at night, calling in a low voice: "O my Lord, my bones have grown weak, my head is white with age, and I have never been disappointed in my prayer to You. Grant me a pure offspring — one who will inherit from me and from the family of Yaqoob, and make him pleasing to You."

He feared that his relatives after him would neglect the religion.

### Glad Tidings of Yahya

While he stood praying in the chamber, the angels called: "Allah gives you glad tidings of Yahya, who will confirm a word from Allah — a leader, chaste, and a prophet among the righteous."

The "word from Allah" refers to Eisa <sup>عليه السلام> — Yahya would be the first to affirm him. Yahya was six months older than Eisa. Their mothers met during pregnancy, and Zakariya's wife said: "I feel my child prostrating to your child" — this was a dream or kashf indicating Yahya would follow Eisa.

### Yahya's Name

Allah Himself named the child "Yahya" — a name never used before. It derives from the root meaning "life," signifying:
- Allah gave life to his mother's barren womb through him
- He gave life to his heart through faith and obedience
- He was granted the life of martyrdom (the martyrs are alive with their Lord)

### Yahya's Virtues

Yahya <sup>عليه السلام> was granted:
- **Sayyid** — a leader, noble, forbearing, one to whom people turn in hardship
- **Hasoor** — chaste, with no desire for women, devoted entirely to worship. This was virtuous in his shariah; in our shariah, marriage is sunnah
- **Prophet** — from the righteous
- Wisdom and judgment from childhood

### Yahya's Martyrdom

Yahya <sup>عليه السلام> was martyred by a tyrant ruler. A woman (some narratives mention Herodias) plotted against him because he forbade her marriage to the king. The king ordered his execution. His head was presented on a platter.

### Zakariya's Martyrdom

When Zakariya <sup>عليه السلام> learned of Yahya's murder, he fled. The enemy pursued him. A tree split open by Allah's command and concealed him. But Iblees showed the enemy the hem of his garment sticking out. They sawed the tree in half, killing Zakariya <sup>عليه السلام> along with it.

### Maryam's Virtue

Maryam <sup>رضي الله عنها</sup> was chosen above all women of the worlds. She was purified and exalted. She was among the greatest women of Paradise — one of the four perfect women: Aasiya binte Muzahim (Firaun's wife), Maryam binte Imraan, Khadija binte Khuwaylid, and Fatima binte Muhammad ﷺ.

### Jibreel's Visit

Allah sent Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام> to Maryam in the form of a well-proportioned man. When she saw him, she said: "I seek refuge in the Most Merciful from you — if you fear Allah."

He said: "I am only a messenger from your Lord — to grant you a pure boy."

She said: "How can I have a boy when no man has touched me, nor have I been unchaste?"

He said: "Thus your Lord has said: It is easy for Me. We will make him a sign for the people and a mercy from Us. It is a matter decreed."

### The Conception

Jibreel breathed into the opening of her garment. The spirit entered her womb, and she conceived. This was unlike human conception — it was the direct command of Allah: "Be."

### The Birth

When the pains of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a date-palm, she cried: "Oh, I wish I had died before this and was forgotten, completely forgotten."

But Jibreel (or Eisa himself) called from beneath her: "Do not grieve. Your Lord has placed a stream beneath you. Shake the trunk of the date-palm toward you — it will drop fresh ripe dates upon you. So eat, drink, and be comforted."

### Maryam's Return

She carried the infant to her people. They said: "O Maryam, you have committed a terrible thing. O sister of Haroon, your father was not an evil man, nor was your mother unchaste."

She pointed to the child. They said: "How can we speak to one who is a child in the cradle?"

### Eisa Speaks in the Cradle

Eisa <sup>عليه السلام> said:

> **"Indeed I am the servant of Allah. He has given me the Book and made me a prophet. He has made me blessed wherever I am, and has enjoined upon me prayer and zakah as long as I live — and dutifulness to my mother. He has not made me arrogant or wretched. Peace be upon me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am raised alive."**

### Titles and Attributes of Eisa

The Quran gives Eisa <sup>عليه السلام> several titles:

- **Kalimah** (Word of Allah) — because he was created by the direct word "Be" (كُن), without a father
- **Maseeh** — because he would touch (masah) the sick and heal them, or because he was anointed with blessings
- **Wajeeh** (Eminent) — honored in this world and the next, among those nearest to Allah
- **Mutakallim** — he spoke in the cradle (infancy) and in adulthood
- **Saaleheen** (Righteous) — among the righteous
- **Roohullah** (Spirit from Allah) — a spirit created by Allah, honored

He was supported by Roohul Quds (Jibreel). He was given the Book (Injeel) and wisdom.
