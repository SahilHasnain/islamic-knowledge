### Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

Hazrat Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> father's name is Lamak bin Matoo bin Shalakh bin Akhnookh (which is the name of Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>).

He <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was commanded to announce his prophethood after the age of 40, and he remained among his people for 950 years, preaching to them. Allah ta'ala said: "So he remained among them a thousand years less fifty."

He lived 250 years after the flood. His total lifespan is 1240 years — although there are other statements, this one is generally considered correct.

### What Did Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Preach to His People?

Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> preached the oneness of Allah and abstinence from sins to his people for 950 years, and along with that, he continued to warn them of Allah ta'ala's punishment.

He said: "O my people! I am a clear warner to you, that you worship Allah and fear Him, and obey me. He will forgive you some of your sins and grant you respite for an appointed term. Indeed, when Allah's promise comes, it cannot be delayed — if only you knew."

He also said to his people: "If you worship anyone other than Allah, then I fear for you the punishment of a great Day."

While preaching, he further instructed: "If you worship anyone other than Allah, I warn you of a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter."

In this noble verse, along with the punishment of the Hereafter, his people were clearly warned of destruction and ruin in this world as well, so that perhaps it might have some effect on the people.

And he said: "And I do not ask you for any reward for it; my reward is only upon the Lord of all the worlds."

That is: I am not demanding any wealth or property as payment for calling you to Allah ta'ala's oneness, worshipping only Him, fearing only Him, and warning you of destruction in faith and worldly life if you worship anyone besides Him. If you adopt the path I am showing you, you will succeed; otherwise, you will be disgraced, destroyed and ruined, suffering loss in faith and world. I have no personal interest in conveying Allah ta'ala's commands to you. I have no greed for any position, wealth, or property. I am preaching to you only by Allah's command, for Allah's pleasure. Only my Allah ta'ala is to grant me reward and recompense — with His boundless mercy, I have no need of you.

### What Effect Did Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Preaching Have on His People?

He <sup>عليه السلام</sup> preached day and night for 950 years, but instead of coming closer, the people moved further away. To avoid hearing his speech, they would plug their ears with their fingers, and (<i>may Allah forbid</i>) would cover their faces in hatred of him. Allah ta'ala said: "He said, 'O my Lord, I called my people night and day, but my calling only increased their flight. And whenever I called them that You may forgive them, they put their fingers in their ears and covered themselves with their garments and persisted in obstinacy and showed great arrogance.'"

That is, Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> submitted to Allah ta'ala: "O Allah! I did not fall short or show laziness in conveying Your commands, but my people, instead of accepting and coming closer, moved further away."

As he continued preaching, instead of decreasing, the people's distance only increased. Such was the state of Allah's Prophet's compassion for his people that he wanted to guide them to the path by which they could attain salvation — that Allah may forgive them, be pleased with them, and they may draw near to Allah. But such was the misfortune of the people that they hated him so much that they were not prepared to listen to him and could not bear to see him. They would put their fingers in their ears so as not to hear his speech and the proofs he presented, and they would cover their faces so that (<i>may Allah forbid</i>) they would not even see the face of Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

Those upon whom Allah bestows His grace listen to the words of Allah's friends and come to faith, abstain from sins, and adopt righteousness and piety. Those who are in the grip of Satan give names like "indifference," "foolishness," "obsolescence" to those who give guidance and become rebels against the faith.

### Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Tried Every Method of Preaching

He <sup>عليه السلام</sup> preached at all times — that is, day and night. Then he explained to each person gently and in soft tones: "Worship Allah, fear your Lord." But the people refused to listen, plugging their ears with their fingers. They showed aversion to seeing him by covering their faces with cloth. They stubbornly persisted in their disbelief. Due to arrogance, they rejected the commands of Allah ta'ala. Then he addressed them in open public gatherings and taught them the path of truth, but it had no effect on them. Then he preached both openly and privately, but this method too could not bring the people to the right path. Allah ta'ala presented this theme as follows: "Nooh said, 'Then I called them publicly, then I spoke to them openly and also spoke to them in private and secret.'"

### How Many People Accepted Faith?

Despite preaching for such a long period, only a small group of believers is seen: three of his sons — Saam, Haam, and Yaafis — and their three wives, and one wife of Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and 70 men and women. These were the faithful people who were also on the ark. That is, including Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, a total of 78 people were on the ark, with equal numbers of men and women.

### The Reason for Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> People Not Accepting Faith

1) "Then the chiefs of his people who disbelieved said, 'We see you only as a man like ourselves.'" That is, one reason for the people not accepting faith was that they considered Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to be a human like themselves. They thought that someone like themselves could never be a prophet. They were unaware that a prophet has two states — one human and the other spiritual. They said, "A prophet should be an angel."

> "The chiefs of his people who disbelieved said, 'This is nothing but a man like you who desires to become superior to you. If Allah had willed, He would have sent down angels. We have not heard of this among our forefathers.'"

That is, they said: "Why should we believe in Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>? He is like us. He claims prophethood to become greater than us. We have never heard from any of our forefathers that a human can be a prophet. If the Lord wanted to send a prophet, He would have sent an angel as a prophet."

2) The second reason for the people not accepting faith was: "We, the elite, and lowly people cannot be upon the same religion." The people said: "And we do not see anyone following you except the lowest among us, with a superficial view."

That is, the chiefs of the people said: "Only the poor and lowly people have believed in you, and they too have accepted faith superficially, without thought and understanding. If they had thought about it, they would not have accepted faith — or perhaps they did not have the capacity to think. How can we become like them by believing along with such lowly people?" Thus, due to arrogance, they did not accept faith.

> "They said, 'Shall we believe in you while the lowliest follow you?'"

3) The third reason for their not accepting faith was that they thought: "You and those who believe with you do not hold any superiority over us." That is, they failed to understand the stature of the Prophet. They could not comprehend the Prophet's greatness, and it did not occur to them that in the sight of Allah ta'ala, abundance of wealth and property is not a reason for superiority. Rather, faith and piety are the reason for superiority — this is the cause of proximity to Allah ta'ala. Allah ta'ala mentioned this reason for their not accepting faith: "And we do not see any superiority in you over us."

### The Response of Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

> "Do you wonder that there has come to you a reminder from your Lord through a man from among you, that he may warn you (of Allah's wrath) and that you may become righteous and that you may receive mercy?"

That is, he removed their doubt. They could not understand that a human being could attain the rank of prophethood and messengerhood and receive divine effusion directly from the Essence of the Lord and convey it to the people. They thought that only an angel could do this. So he said: "Your wonder and confusion are baseless. There is no impossibility if Allah ta'ala wishes to honor His complete and chosen servant with the blessing of prophethood."

Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> answered their second reason for refusing faith in a few words:

> "And I am not one to drive away those who have believed. Indeed, they are to meet their Lord. But I see you are a people who are ignorant (of reality)."

That is, they said to Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>: "Wretched people are always gathered around you. We do not wish to go to a place where such dirty, filthy, and lowly people have assembled. Order them to leave your presence, then we will come to you." You will recall that the disbelievers made a similar demand to the Noble Prophet <sup>عليه الصلاة والسلام</sup> as well. Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> gave a clear answer: "It is impossible for me to order these lovers of truth to leave my presence for your sake. You may be great in your own eyes, but in my sight, the status and rank that these moths of the heart burn around the lamp of light can never be that of the vultures that pounce on the filthy corpse of the world. Here, the standard of status and rank is sincerity and piety, not wealth and riches." He also said to them: "You may be very proud of your intellect and wisdom, but in my view, you are ignorant and unaware people who have not yet realized that the secret of human honor does not lie in abundance of wealth, but in purity of heart, nobility of character, and maturity of morals."

### The Initial Affliction on Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> People

Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> repeatedly preached to his people, but obtained nothing from them except denial. When they refused to accept Allah ta'ala's commands, Allah ta'ala initially seized them to shake them up — their rains stopped falling, their women became barren, and the birth of children ceased.

### The Compassion of Allah's Prophet

The people were repeatedly refusing, but Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, with patience and forbearance, was trying to save them from the fire of Hell and worldly difficulties, showing them the methods by which they could save themselves from calamities and afflictions. Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> mentions before Allah ta'ala the method he taught the people to emerge from their difficulties.

> "So I said, 'Ask forgiveness from your Lord; indeed, He is Oft-Forgiving. He will send upon you abundant rain from the sky, and aid you with wealth and sons, and make for you gardens and make for you rivers. What is the matter with you that you do not hope for honor from Allah?'"

That is, he told his people that the solution to their difficulties lies only in believing in Allah ta'ala, remembering Him, fearing Him, and seeking forgiveness from Him. But the people remained persistent in their obstinacy. Their arrogance had crossed all limits, and they were saying to each other:

> "And they said, 'Never forsake your gods and never forsake Wadd, nor Suwa', nor Yaghuth, nor Ya'uq, nor Nasr.'" (These are the names of their idols.)

**Benefit:** It is narrated from Hazrat Rabi'a bin Sabih that a man came to Hazrat Hasan and complained of drought. He said, "Seek forgiveness from Allah." Another man mentioned his poverty to him. He also told him, "Seek forgiveness from your Lord." Another man said, "Pray to your Lord to grant me a son." He also told him, "Seek forgiveness from your Allah." Another man mentioned that his gardens had dried up, fruits had decreased, and the land's yield had reduced, and requested dua. He also told him, "Seek forgiveness from Allah ta'ala."

Those present said, "The complaints of the people were different, but you told them all the same practice." He replied: "Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> commanded his people to seek forgiveness for the solution to all difficulties — that is, this practice is proven from the Holy Quran."

### What Titles Did the People Give Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>?

Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was the prophet of Allah ta'ala who had devoted himself day and night to saving his people from worldly and otherworldly punishment and bringing them to the right path. But the people had given him titles such as misguided, liar, madman, etc.

> "The chiefs said, 'We see you in clear error.'"
