### How Great Was the Flood?

Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, after commanding his people to board the ark and take Allah's name, made it clear that the ark was not in reality the means of salvation from such a great flood — rather, it was only by Allah ta'ala's grace that it would sail, and only by His grace would it anchor. His purpose was: do not rely on the ark, but rely only on Allah ta'ala's Essence — this ark is merely a means.

When the flood had begun from the *tannoor*, the heavens were commanded to send down rain and the earth was commanded to cause springs to gush forth. The waters of the heavens and the earth together presented a terrifying scene. Allah ta'ala said:

> "So We opened the gates of the heaven with water pouring forth, and caused the earth to gush forth with springs, so the waters met for a matter already destined."

The waters of the earth and sky together caused such severe flooding that when the waves rose, they appeared like huge, towering mountains. Allah ta'ala said:

> "And it (the ark) was carrying them through waves like mountains."

In the commentary of this verse, Allama Razi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> states that waves rise high when the wind is also strong and severe. This indicates that along with heavy rains and the earth releasing water, severe storms were also blowing, causing waves that reached the peaks of mountains.

### The Ark Sailed and Reached Its Destination

Hazrat Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ark set sail on the 10th of Rajab and anchored on Mount Judi on the 10th of Muharram. The ark remained in the continuous flood for 6 months. Upon gaining salvation from the flood on the 10th of Muharram, Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and his people observed a fast.

> "Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> himself observed a fast and commanded all the people with him, as well as the wild animals and other animals — all observed a fast, offering thanks to Allah."

SubhanAllah! The animals recognize the greatness of the Prophet; if foolish people do not recognize it, they should mourn their misfortune.

**Benefit:** In Musnad Ahmad and others, it is narrated from Hazrat Abu Huraira <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> that the Noble Prophet ﷺ once passed by some Jews who were fasting on the day of Ashura. He asked, "Why are you fasting?" He was told, "On this day, Allah ta'ala granted salvation to Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and the Children of Israel, and drowned Firaun. And on this day, Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ark anchored on Mount Judi. Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> observed a fast to give thanks to Allah." So the Noble Prophet ﷺ said, "I have more right than Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to fast on this day." He ﷺ himself observed a fast and commanded the companions to do likewise. It should be noted that Hazrat Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was also born on the day of Ashura.

### One of Nooh's Sons Was Drowned

Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had four sons. Three of them were believers who boarded the ark with him — their names were Saam, Haam, and Yaafis. One son was a hypocrite named Kan'aan, who did not board the ark and was drowned. He was a hypocrite — outwardly he appeared to be a believer, but in reality he was a disbeliever and sided with the disbelievers. Allah ta'ala mentioned the incident of his drowning:

> "And Nooh called to his son, and he was standing apart: 'O my son, board with us and do not be with the disbelievers.' He said, 'I will take refuge on a mountain — it will protect me from the water.' (Nooh said,) 'Today there is no protector from Allah's command except for whom He shows mercy.' And a wave came between them, so he was among those who drowned."

This son of Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was riding a horse, strutting with pride, saying, "I will climb a mountain and save myself." Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was telling him, "Today, no one can escape Allah's punishment except those whom He has mercy upon." This exchange was taking place between them when the waves raised by the flood came between them, and Kan'aan was drowned.

### Nooh's Plea for His Son

> "Nooh called upon his Lord: 'O my Lord, my son is indeed from my household, and surely Your promise is true, and You are the greatest of all judges.' Allah ta'ala said, 'O Nooh, he is not from your household. Indeed, his conduct is unrighteous. So do not ask Me for that of which you have no knowledge. I admonish you not to be among the ignorant.' He said, 'O my Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking You for that of which I have no knowledge. And if You do not forgive me and have mercy upon me, I will be among the losers.'"

Sheikh Abu Mansoor Matureedi <sup>رحمه الله تعالى</sup> said that Nooh's son Kan'aan was a hypocrite — he outwardly showed himself to be a believer before him. If he had revealed his disbelief, he would not have pleaded with Allah ta'ala for his salvation.

Allah ta'ala had promised Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> that his household would be granted salvation. Therefore, he submitted concerning his son, and that too due to his hypocrisy. Allah ta'ala said: "His deeds are not good" — that is, he was a disbeliever. The first bond is that of faith. If this is attained, then the bond of kinship also benefits; otherwise, there is no benefit.

**Note:** Hazrat Muhammad Ali Baaqar and Hazrat Hasan Basri <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> said that Kan'aan was the son of Nooh's wife from another husband — he was not his own son. It is narrated from Hazrat Ali Murtaza that he would recite: وَنَادَىٰ نُوحٌ ابْنَهَا — "Nooh called out to her (his wife's) son to board the ark."

Hazrat Qataada asked Hazrat Hasan <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, and he said, "By Allah, he was not his son." He asked, "How is that, when Allah ta'ala, while relating Nooh's words, said, 'Indeed, my son is from my household'?" Hazrat Hasan Basri replied, "This supports my statement. Nooh said, 'my son is from my household' — he did not say, 'my son who is my own.'"

### One of Nooh's Wives Was Also Drowned

One of Hazrat Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> wives, whose name was Waaliha, was a disbeliever. She used to tell people, "(may Allah forbid) Nooh is mad — do not listen to him." She too was drowned. According to the statement in *Tafseer Jalalain*, his son Kan'aan and his wife are both mentioned — that is, both were disbelievers and were drowned.

**Note:** Many narrators narrated from Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup>: "No prophet's wife was ever a fornicator." Ashras elevated the chain of this hadith to the Noble Prophet ﷺ as *marfoo'*. In *Tafseer Kashshaaf*, it is stated that the wives of the noble prophets were not fornicators; however, there is no harm in disbelief occurring from them, because fornication has been considered evil in every age, and people's nature has remained averse to it. Therefore, if such an act were found in a Prophet's wife, the Prophet would have a defect and flaw. Allah ta'ala created the noble prophets pure from defects and flaws. But disbelievers considered disbelief to be their rightful religion, so disbelief was considered a perfection rather than a flaw in their view. Hence, disbelief could be found in the wives of the noble prophets.

### The End of the Flood

> "And it was said, 'O earth, swallow your water, and O sky, withhold (your rain).' And the water subsided, and the matter was accomplished, and the ark rested on Mount Judi. And it was said, 'Away with the wrongdoing people.'"

That is, when the drowning of the disbelievers was completed, the flood was ended. The rain was stopped. The earth was commanded to absorb the water upon it. Only those people or animals that were on the ark survived. All other humans, cattle, birds, and wild animals were drowned. The drowning was a punishment for the mature, sane disbelievers, and for those others who were drowned, it was not a punishment.

It appears in a noble hadith: "When Allah ta'ala sends down punishment upon a people, all — good and bad — become afflicted in this punishment. Then when they are raised, each will be raised according to his own deeds."

That is, the punishment in the world for the righteous will not be for disgrace — therefore, they will be raised on the Day of Judgment according to their good deeds.

### Why Did the Ark Rest on Mount Judi?

All the mountains were boasting of their height and strutting with pride, but Mount Judi continued to express its humility before Allah. Therefore, Allah ta'ala granted it the honor that Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ark came and rested upon it.

> "Whoever showed humility, Allah ta'ala granted him elevation."

It should be noted that Mount Judi is in the region of Mawsil or Syria. It should also be noted that the water did not enter the noble Haram.
