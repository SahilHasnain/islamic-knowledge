### Threats and Harsh Treatment from the People

Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> said in response: "There is no error in me — I am a messenger of my Lord ta'ala. I convey to you the messages of your Lord and I wish you well. And I possess knowledge from Allah that you do not possess."

> "Rather, we consider you a liar."

That is, "You and all those who have believed with you are liars, because you all make the same claim," or, "You are false in your claim of prophethood, and they are false in confirming you."

> "He is nothing but a madman, so wait for him for a while."

That is, "Wait — perhaps he may recover from his madness and abandon his claim of prophethood, or death may come to him, and we will be rid of him." Some serious-minded people also said, "Wait — if he is a true prophet, Allah will help him, and if he is false, Allah will disgrace him, and we will be free of him." But those who thought this way could not attain faith. When Allah ta'ala's help came for Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, they too were drowned.

> "Before them, the people of Nooh denied. They called Our servant a liar and said, 'He is mad,' and he was rebuked."

While consoling the Noble Prophet ﷺ, it was said: "Before these Quraish, the people of Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> also denied. They called him a liar, called him mad, and threatened him — saying if he did not abandon his claim of prophethood, they would abuse him and stone him."

As for the threats and harsh treatment of the people toward Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>:

> "They said, 'O Nooh, if you do not desist, you will surely be stoned.'"

In *Tafseer Jalalain*, two meanings of *yarjumu* are stated: one is that you will be abused, and the other is that you will be stoned.

Ibn Asaakar narrated from Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> that Hazrat Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> people beat him severely, wounding him badly. They wrapped him in woolen cloth and threw him in his house, thinking that he <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had died. But he emerged from that state and again began to call them to the truth.

One day, an old man walking with a staff was carrying his young son. Seeing Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, he said to his son, "O my son, be careful not to fall into this old man's trap — do not let him deceive you." Hearing his father's words, the son said, "O my father, put me down and give me your staff." The father put down his son and handed him the staff. That young boy approached Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and struck him with the staff. It hit his head, and he was wounded — blood began to flow. Seeing this incident, Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> pleaded before his Lord: "O Allah, You see how Your servants are treating me. O Allah, if You wish to keep Your servants alive, then guide them, or else grant me patience until You make a decision — for You are the Best of Judges." Allah ta'ala said: "And it was revealed to Nooh that none of your people will believe except those who have already believed. So do not grieve over what they do."

### The Supplication of Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

> "Nooh said, 'O my Lord, help me, for they have denied me.'"

In this prayer, there is also an indication that he submitted a request to his Lord to destroy and annihilate his people.

> "He said, 'O my Lord, my people have denied me, so judge between me and them decisively, and save me and the Muslims who are with me.'"

> "So he called upon his Lord: 'I am overcome, so take vengeance for me.'"

> "And Nooh said, 'O my Lord, do not leave upon the earth any dweller from among the disbelievers. For if You leave them, they will mislead Your servants, and they will beget none but wicked, disbelieving children.'"

### Why Did He Pray for the Destruction of His People?

Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did not pray for the destruction of his people because they had threatened to abuse him or stone him. The only reason was: "O Allah, You Yourself have informed that no one else will come to faith besides those who have already believed. Since there is no hope of faith from them, and from their children only wickedness and ingratitude can be expected, what is the benefit of keeping them alive? Destroy and annihilate them."

> "O Allah, I have become helpless and overcome. Help me for the sake of Your religion and Your believing servants."

### Allah Commanded the Building of the Ark

After Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> supplication, Allah ta'ala commanded that his people would be drowned. He said: "Prepare an ark for the protection of yourself and the believers." Allah ta'ala said: "And build the ark under Our observation and Our inspiration, and do not address Me concerning those who have wronged — indeed, they are to be drowned."

That is, do not make any supplication for the punishment of the disbelievers, because the decision of their drowning has been finalized. Their time of drowning has also been fixed, so do not ask for haste either, because the punishment will not come before its appointed time. One of his wives and one son were also disbelievers — they too would drown. Do not even pray for their salvation, because there will be no amendment in the decision to drown all the disbelievers.

### The Ark's Design Was Prepared Through Allah ta'ala's Revelation and Jibreel's Assistance

Ibn Asaakar narrated from Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> that Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> did not know how to build an ark. So Allah ta'ala revealed to him: make the front part like a rooster's head, the middle part like the belly of a bird, and the rear part like a rooster's tail. Make doors around it, strengthen it with nails, and coat it with tar on every side except the bottom.

Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and some other angels also assisted in building the ark.

### What Was the Ark Like?

The length of the ark was 300 cubits (450 feet), the width 50 cubits (75 feet), and the height 30 cubits (45 feet). It was made from teak wood, and its construction took two years. The ark had three levels: in the first level were wild animals, predators, and insects; in the middle part were domestic animals, cattle, and the like; and in the uppermost level were Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and the believers with him, along with their food and drink.

### The Number of People on the Ark

Only those people who believed in him were on the ark, as mentioned earlier. His three sons and each of their wives, and Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> himself, and one of his wives — these were 8 household members. Plus 70 other people who had accepted faith — thus the total number on the ark was 78. Allama Aalusi considered a narration that there were 79 people besides him — that is, 72 plus 7 from his tribe and he himself — making 80 people total. He deemed this narration more correct.

It should be noted that one of Nooh's <sup>عليه السلام</sup> wives and a son Canaan were disbelievers and were drowned. Their mention will come later, insha Allah.

### The People's Mockery at the Ark

Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was building the ark by Allah ta'ala's command. The people would ask him, "What will you do with this?" He would reply, "It will be useful in the flood." Hearing this, the people would mock: "There is no river of water in this area. What need is there for such a large ship to be useful in water? (<i>may Allah forbid</i>) This is sheer foolishness!"

Sometimes they said, "First you claimed prophethood, now you have become a carpenter." They mocked in this way. Allah ta'ala mentioned their behavior: "And Nooh constructed the ark, and whenever the chiefs of his people passed by him, they mocked him. He said, 'If you mock us, then we will mock you as you mock.'"

That is, today if you object to our building the ark and make fun of us, then when you are destroyed, we too will rejoice at the destruction of Allah's enemies.

### The Flood Began from the Tannoor

Allah ta'ala had informed Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>: "When water starts coming out of the *tannoor* (oven), understand that the flood is coming. At that time, board the ark — take one pair of every animal and the believers with you." This *tannoor* was in Kufa. The correct view is that it was an ordinary oven in which his wife baked bread — from it the flood began. Allah ta'ala said: "Until when Our command came and the oven overflowed."

That is, just as a pot boils, the oven began to boil. Then he <sup>عليه السلام</sup> knew that the flood was about to come.

### The Command to Board the Ark and Recite the Prayer

When water began to emerge from the *tannoor*, Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> commanded all the believers: "Board the ark now, and begin with the name of Allah when boarding."

> "And he said, 'Board it in the name of Allah — its course and its anchorage. Indeed, my Lord is Oft-Forgiving, Most Merciful.'"
