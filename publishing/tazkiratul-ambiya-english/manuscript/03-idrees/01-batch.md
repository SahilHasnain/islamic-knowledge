### Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

There is a gap of 1000 years between Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. He is the great-grandfather of Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

**The Lineage of Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>**
Akhnookh bin Yurid bin Mehalabil bin Anoosh bin Qaitaan bin Shees bin Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

**The Lineage of Hazrat Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>**
Nooh bin Lamak bin Matoo Shalakh bin Akhnookh (Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>).

**The Inventions of Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>**
The first to observe the stars and perform calculations is proven to be from him. But it should be noted that his observation of the stars was according to Allah's will. In his calculations, there was no estimation — rather, whatever was inspired into his heart, he would state. That is, the calculation of the stars was given to him as a miracle. In today's age, calculating the stars and predicting future events is forbidden, and believing in them with certainty is disbelief.

He was the first to introduce writing with the pen. He was the first to wear clothes — before him, people used animal hides. He was the first to introduce weighing things and measuring clothes etc. He was also the first to invent weapons. He is the first messenger after Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and 30 scrolls were revealed to him.

### Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Being Raised to the Heavens

Allah ta'ala has commanded:

> "And mention Idrees in the Book. Indeed, he was a truthful one, a prophet. And We raised him to a high station."

One meaning of being raised to a high station in this noble verse is: He honored him with the station of prophethood and granted him His special proximity, bestowing upon him elevation and high rank.

The second meaning of elevation is that he was taken to a high place. This meaning is more appropriate, because Allah ta'ala mentioned ورفعناه ماكن علها — where a place is mentioned, it refers to the height of a place, not the height of ranks.

The detail of the high place is that he was taken up to the heaven, and this is the correct path. In the hadith of Bukhari and Muslim, the Noble Prophet ﷺ saw Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> on the fourth heaven on the night of Meraaj.

It is narrated from Hazrat Ka'ab Ahbaar <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> and others that Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> said to the Angel of Death, Hazrat Izraail <sup>عليه السلام</sup>: "I wish to taste what death is like. Seize my soul and show me." He complied with his command, seized his soul, and then immediately returned it, and he came back to life.

Then he said: "Show me Hell so that my fear of Allah may increase." In compliance with his command, he was taken to the door of Hell. He said to the angel named Maalik, who is the warden of Hell: "Open it, I wish to pass through it." So it was done accordingly, and he passed over it. Then he said to the Angel of Death: "Show me Paradise." He took him to Paradise as per his command. He commanded that the doors of Paradise be opened, and they were opened for him, and he entered Paradise. After waiting for some time, the Angel of Death said: "Now you should return to your place on earth." He said: "I will not go anywhere from here, because Allah ta'ala has said: 'Every soul shall taste death.' I have tasted death. Then Allah ta'ala has made this condition for entering Paradise that every person must pass over Hell. I have also passed over Hell. Now I have entered Paradise. Those who enter Paradise cannot be removed from there, because Allah ta'ala's own noble command is: 'The people of Paradise will not be removed from Paradise.' According to Allah ta'ala's own commands, I must remain here — I cannot be removed from here."

After this statement of Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, Allah ta'ala said to the Angel of Death: "O Izraail, My servant Idrees has done everything according to My will. Let him remain here." He <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is still alive in the heavens.

**Note:** Among the proofs of Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the blessed words of the Holy Quran are mentioned, even though it had not been revealed at that time. But Allah's word is eternal — it was written on the Preserved Tablet even at that time. Since prophets possess knowledge of the unseen through Allah ta'ala's grant, Hazrat Idrees <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had sight of the Preserved Tablet. Therefore, his reasoning from the Holy Quran is correct.
