### Hazrat Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — Lineage and People

Hazrat Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was sent to the people of Samood (Samood). He was also from the same tribe. "Samood" was the name of a man whose descendants were called the people of Samood. Their lineage connects back to Nooh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>: Samood bin Aabir bin Iram bin Saam bin Nooh.

The lineage of Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is: Saaleh bin Ubaid bin Maasikh bin Abd bin Jawaar bin Samood.

The gap between Hazrat Hood <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and Hazrat Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was 100 years — Saaleh came 100 years after Hood. His age was 280 years.

> "And to Samood We sent their fellow-tribesman Saaleh. He said, 'O my people, worship Allah. You have no deity other than Him. He produced you from the earth and settled you in it, so ask forgiveness of Him and then repent to Him. Indeed, my Lord is near and responsive.'"

> "And to Samood We sent their fellow-tribesman Saaleh. He said, 'O my people, worship Allah. You have no deity other than Him.' They then became two factions quarreling."

> "And when their fellow-tribesman Saaleh said, 'Will you not fear Allah? Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger. So fear Allah and obey me. And I do not ask you for any reward for it; my reward is only from the Lord of the worlds.'"

### The Way of the Prophets in Calling Their People

The noble practice of the prophets was: when they claimed prophethood, they would first command the people to abandon idol worship and worship Allah alone — there is no deity but Him. Then they would declare their prophethood so the people would ask for miracles and the prophets could show them. When the people denied and did not desist, they would warn them of Allah's punishment. Still, if they persisted in disbelief, Allah's punishment would come upon them.

Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> told his people: "If you desire Allah's mercy, seek forgiveness for your sins and turn to Him. Allah's mercy is near to the believers."

### You Will Not Remain in This World Forever

> "Will you be left secure in what is here — amid gardens and springs, and crops and palm trees with delicate spathes, and you carve houses from the mountains with great skill? So fear Allah and obey me. And do not obey the command of the extravagant, who cause corruption in the land and do not reform."

Hazrat Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> told his people: "Your belief that you will remain here comfortably amid blessings is false. The Lord who gave you gardens, springs, crops, and delicate palm trees can also take them away. Do not boast of worldly blessings — you must eventually leave this world. You carve houses from mountains with great skill, which makes you think you will live forever. But this thinking is entirely wrong. You are following those who transgress, spread corruption, and do no good. Do not obey them, or you will be destroyed. Success lies only in turning to Allah and obeying me."

### Two Factions Emerged from His Preaching

> "And We sent to Samood their fellow-tribesman Saaleh — 'Worship Allah' — and at once they became two factions quarreling. He said, 'O my people, why do you hasten to evil before good? Why do you not ask forgiveness of Allah, that you may receive mercy?'"

Some people believed in Saaleh's message, while others persisted in disbelief. Thus two factions emerged, constantly arguing with each other.

### The People's Response to Saaleh

> "Indeed, the dwellers of Hijr denied the messengers."

"Hijr" is a valley between Madinah and Syria where the people of Samood lived. They denied their prophet Saaleh. Since denying one prophet is like denying all prophets — all prophets call to the oneness of Allah and His worship, and each prophet calls to belief in all other prophets — Allah said "they denied the messengers" even though they were outwardly denying only one prophet.

Sometimes the people said: "O Saaleh, before this you were among us as one of promise. Do you forbid us to worship what our fathers worshipped? Indeed, we are in disquieting doubt about that to which you invite us."

They said: "We considered you very intelligent, with great expectations that you would support our religion and strengthen our ways."

He was also very kind to the poor and needy, visiting the sick. They said: "Seeing these qualities, we thought you would be among our friends and support us. How have you started this enmity and hatred toward us? We are astonished that you forbid us from worshipping our fathers' deities. Now we have begun to doubt you — we think you want to lead us into some great deception."

### Saaleh's Response to His People

> "He said, 'O my people, have you considered: if I am upon clear evidence from my Lord and He has granted me mercy from Himself, who would protect me from Allah if I disobey Him? So you would not increase me except in loss.'"

The meaning: "Allah has given me clear proofs and graced me with His mercy. That is why I am showing kindness to you by guiding you to the path of your success. The false path on which you seek my help involves disobedience to Allah, and disobedience to Allah is nothing but loss."

> "They said, 'We consider you and those with you as a bad omen.'"

When Saaleh was sent and the people denied him, rain stopped, famine struck, and people began dying of hunger. They attributed this to Saaleh's coming and considered his arrival an ill omen.

> "He said, 'Your omen is with Allah. Rather, you are a people being tried.'"

Hazrat Ibn Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> said: Saaleh meant that the misfortune that came to them was from Allah due to their disbelief. They were afflicted with a trial — considering their false religion correct and persisting in idol worship was itself a trial for them.

### They Called Him a Sorcerer

> "They said, 'You have been bewitched.'"

Meaning: "Sorcery has been repeatedly cast upon you, so your mind is not sound. You speak foolishly with us, asking us to abandon the religion of our forefathers."

### They Called Him Arrogant

> "They said, 'Shall we follow a single man from among us? Then we would surely be in error and madness. Has the message been sent down upon him from among us? Rather, he is an insolent liar.'"

They said: "Should we, in such numbers, follow one man — also a human like us? This is the act of madmen. We are intelligent people. Was no one else worthy of prophethood? This man is lying in his claim and behaving insolently."

Allah ta'ala refuted them: "They will know tomorrow who is the insolent liar." When they are afflicted with punishment in this world and the Hereafter, they will know who was lying and arrogantly persisting in falsehood.

### The People Demand a Miracle

> "They said, 'You are but a man like us. So bring a sign, if you are truthful.' He said, 'This is a she-camel. For her is a turn of drinking, and for you is a turn of drinking, on a specified day. And do not touch her with harm, lest the punishment of a great day seize you.'"

### A Brief Account of the People of Samood

After the people of Aad, Allah ta'ala settled the people of Samood in the land. He granted them long lives and the skill to carve houses from mountains with great craftsmanship. He gave them prosperity and wealth. But they began disobeying Allah and spreading corruption in the land, so Allah sent Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> to them — a nobleman from their own tribe.

He warned them. They demanded a sign. He asked, "What sign do you seek?" They said: "Come with us outside the city for our festival. We will pray to our deities, and you pray to your Lord. Whose prayer is accepted, we will follow him."

Saaleh went out with them. They prayed to their idols, but their prayers were not accepted. A chief named "Janda bin Amar" pointed to a solitary rock called "Kaafiya" and said: "Bring forth from this rock a pregnant she-camel. If you do this, we will confirm your truthfulness."

Saaleh asked: "If I do this, will you surely believe?" They said yes. He performed supererogatory prayers and supplicated to his Lord. Allah honored his prayer. The people watched as the rocky mountain began to experience the same pangs as an animal in labor — groaning and trembling — until the rock split open before their eyes and a pregnant she-camel emerged, her body covered in wool, her belly large.

Seeing this miracle, Janda bin Amar and a few others accepted faith. But Zawaab bin Amar, Habaab, and Rabbaab bin Sam'ar prevented the rest from believing. These were the custodians of the idol temple, and the third was a soothsayer.

Right before their eyes, the she-camel gave birth to a calf the size of eighty normal camels.

### The Arrangement of Water

As Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had declared by Allah's command: one day the she-camel would drink, and the next day was for them, their livestock, and all other animals and birds.

The she-camel and her calf would graze in the wild. On her designated day, she and her calf would drink all the water — she would put her mouth to the well and not lift it until the water was gone. She would give so much milk that the people would drink and fill all their vessels.

In summer, when the she-camel grazed in the upper parts of the valleys, the people's livestock would flee to the lower parts. In winter, when she grazed in the lower parts, their livestock would flee to the upper parts. This caused great difficulty, and they decided to remove the she-camel from their way — to kill it so they could be rid of it.

### They Hamstrung the She-Camel

> "Then they denied him and hamstrung the she-camel."

Saaleh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> had already told them not to touch the she-camel with harm or they would be afflicted with punishment. But they did not desist. When they hamstrung her, he told them: "Now the punishment is near."

> "Then they hamstrung her, and he said, 'Enjoy yourselves in your homes for three days. That is a promise not to be belied.'"

### Three Days Before the Punishment

When Saaleh warned them that they had only three days left to remain in their homes and enjoy themselves before the punishment overtook them, the people asked how those three days would pass. He said:

- **First day:** your faces will turn yellow.
- **Second day:** they will turn red.
- **Third day:** they will turn black.
- **Fourth day:** the punishment will come upon you.

Even when their faces turned black and they became certain of the punishment, they were not granted the ability to repent and believe — because repentance and faith at the point of certain destruction are not accepted.

### Who Hamstrung the She-Camel?

> "When the most wretched of them arose."

The most wretched one was Qaddar bin Saalif — his complexion was ruddy tinged with yellow, his eyes were blue, and his stature was short.

Although one man physically cut the camel's hamstrings, he did so with everyone's consultation and consent. That is why the Quran uses the plural form — "they hamstrung her." They all deserved the punishment. If only one person had acted and the others had objected or disapproved, the punishment would not have come upon them.

### The Plot to Kill Saaleh

> "And there were in the city nine family heads who spread corruption in the land and did not reform. They said, 'Swear to one another by Allah that we will attack Saaleh and his household by night and then say to his heir, "We were not present at the destruction of his family, and indeed we are truthful."' And they planned a plan, and We planned a plan while they perceived not."

Allama Qurtubi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> writes: They made this conspiracy after hamstringing the she-camel. When Saaleh told them they had three days before destruction, instead heeding the final warning and repenting, they plotted to kill him. They thought: "Punishment will come — but before it arrives, let us finish off Saaleh and his followers."

On the night they planned to attack Saaleh's house, Allah sent angels to protect His messenger. As the conspirators rushed with drawn swords to attack, the angels pelted them with stones. They could see the stones but not who was throwing them. They were all destroyed that very night — the last night of the three-day respite.

### The Punishment on the Disbelievers of Samood

> "So they hamstrung the she-camel and rebelled against the command of their Lord and said, 'O Saaleh, bring us what you promise us, if you are of the messengers.' So the earthquake seized them, and they became within their homes fallen prone."

The punishment upon Samood was twofold: an earthquake from the earth and a terrible blast from the sky — the shrieking cry of Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> .

Three words are used in the Quran for their punishment: *zalzala* (earthquake), *sayha* (cry/blast), and *taaghiya* (transgression). A'la Hazrat Maulana Ahmad Raza Khan Barelvi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> translated *taaghiya* as "transgression" — meaning they were destroyed due to their transgressive crimes.

### Saaleh and the Believers Are Saved

> "Then when Our command came, We saved Saaleh and those who believed with him, by mercy from Us, and from the disgrace of that day. Indeed, your Lord is the Powerful, the Exalted in Might."

This is Allah's great power: in the same land, the same region, the disbelievers were destroyed by earthquake and horrific blast, while His prophet and the believers were not harmed in the least. The disbelievers were humiliated and disgraced, while the believers were saved from this horrific destruction and its disgrace.

Muslims should take heed from this incident, fear Allah's punishment, and maintain complete faith in the Lord's great power.
