# Fatawa Razaviya Standard English Glossary

| Term | Preferred English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| فتویٰ | fatwa | Retain the established Islamic term |
| قول | saying / position | Use “position” in legal discussion when clearer |
| تقلید | taqlid | Explain on first substantial use when necessary |
| مجتہد | mujtahid | Established scholarly term |
| مشائخ | mashayikh | Established scholarly term |
| صاحبین | Sahibayn | The two principal students of Imam Abu Hanifah |
| سند | chain of transmission | Use “sanad” when discussing the formal chain |
