# Fatawa Razaviya Standard English Editorial Decisions

## Project Boundary

This is a standard faithful English translation, separate from the Easy Roman Urdu adaptation.
It must preserve the complete source meaning, structure, citations, and devotional tone.

## Source Authority

The source of truth is `db/fatawa_e_razvia.db`. The extracted source batches in this project are
traceability artifacts generated from that database.

## Pilot

The pilot is volume `Jild-1-Part-1`, lesson `472`, the five-record sanad section. It is used to
approve terminology, treatment of Arabic quotations, footnotes, and English register before
larger risalah sections are translated.

## Arabic Script

Arabic is preserved for Quranic verses, complete hadith quotations, duas, Arabic honorifics,
and wording whose exact form is significant. Ordinary names, titles, and scholarly terminology
may be transliterated in the English prose.

This approach was approved after review of the lesson 472 pilot. Future batches should follow
the same standard-faithful English register and preservation rules.

The separate risalah `تدبیر فلاح ونجات واصلاح` has been moved to
`publishing/tadbir-e-falah-w-najat-w-islah/`, where its English and Easy Roman Urdu editions
are maintained independently.
