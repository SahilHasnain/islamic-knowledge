# Fatawa Razaviya Standard Faithful English Style Guide

## Translation Method

- Translate the complete Urdu source faithfully into standard English.
- Do not summarize, omit, modernize away, or add independent commentary.
- Preserve every ruling, explanation, example, quotation, footnote, citation, and section boundary.
- Retain the author's scholarly and devotional tone.

## Arabic and Honorifics

- Preserve Quranic verses, complete hadith quotations, duas, and Arabic honorifics in Arabic.
- Give an English meaning alongside Arabic quotations when needed for comprehension.
- Preserve `ﷺ` and honorifics such as `رضی الله تعالی عنہ`.
- Transliterate ordinary names, titles, scholarly terminology, and book titles where this produces readable English.

## Adab

- Use reverent wording for the Prophet ﷺ, Ambiya, Sahaba, Ahl-e-Bait, and scholars.
- Never use “died” or “death” directly for the Prophet ﷺ. Use “passed away,” “departed,” or “was called back.”
- Do not use diminishing expressions such as “no more than” for the Prophet ﷺ.
- Render `قُلْ` and similar vocatives reverently, such as “Say (O Beloved)” where applicable.

## Review

Check every batch for complete source coverage, ordering, meaning, Arabic preservation, citations,
honorifics, adab, and accidental omissions.
