## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:121

`Source: tafseerId=50252; ayatId=130; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** اَلَّذِیْنَ اٰتَیْنٰهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ یَتْلُوْنَهٗ حَقَّ تِلَاوَتِهٖؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِهٖؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّكْفُرْ بِهٖ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْخٰسِرُوْنَ۠   (121)

**Roman Urdu:**

{یَتْلُوْنَهٗ: Woh is ki tilaawat karte hain.} Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ maane farmaate hain: Yeh aayat un logon ke muta'alliq naazil hui jo Hazrat Ja'far bin Abi Talib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke saath baargaah-e-Risaalat mein haazir hue thay, in ki ta'daad chaalees thi, battis Ahle Habshah aur aath Shaami raahib thay, in mein Bahiraa raahib bhi thay jinhon ne bachpan mein safar-e-Shaam mein Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko pehchaana tha. (خازن، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۲۱، ۱ / ۸۴-۸۵)

Ayat ka matlab yeh hai ke haqeeqat mein Tauraat Shareef par imaan laane waale wohi hain jo is ki tilaawat ka haq ada karte hain aur baghair tehreef o tabdeel kiye parhte hain aur is ke ma'ni ko samajhte aur maante hain aur is mein Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ki na't o sifat dekh kar aap par imaan laate hain aur jo Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke munkir hote hain woh Tauraat par imaan nahin rakhte.

## Quran-e-Majeed ke Huqooq

Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Kitaabullah ke bahut se huqooq bhi hain. Quran ka haq yeh hai ke is ki ta'zeem ki jaye, is se muhabbat ki jaye, is ki tilaawat ki jaye, isay samjha jaye, is par imaan rakha jaye, is par amal kiya jaye aur isay doosron tak pohnchaya jaye. Targheeb ke liye yahaan hum tilaawat-e-Quran ke chand zaahiri aur baatini aadaab zikr karte hain taake Musalman Quran-e-Azeem ki is tarah tilaawat karein jaisa tilaawat karne ka haq hai.

## Tilaawat-e-Quran ke Zaahiri Aadaab

Quran-e-Majeed ki tilaawat karne waale ko darj-e-zail 6 zaahiri cheezon ka khayaal rakhna chahiye.

(1)… Ba-wuzu ho kar, Qiblah-ru ho kar, mo'addab ho kar aur aajiz o inkisaari ke saath baithe.

(2)… Aahistah parhe aur is ke ma'ani mein ghaur o fikr kare, tilaawat-e-Quran karne mein jald-baazi se kaam na le.

(3)… Dauran-e-tilaawat rona bhi chahiye aur agar rona na aaye to rone jaisi shakal bana le.

(4)… Har aayat ki tilaawat ka haq baja laaye.

(5)… Agar qira'at se riyaakaari ka andesha ho ya kisi ki namaaz mein khalal parta ho to aahistah aahistah tilaawat kare.

(6)… Jahaan tak mumkin ho Quran-e-Paak ko khush-alhaani ke saath parhe.

## Tilaawat-e-Quran ke Baatini Aadaab

Quran-e-Kareem ki tilaawat karne waale ko darj-e-zail 6 baatini cheezon ka bhi khayaal rakhna chahiye.

(1)… Quran-e-Majeed ki azmat dil mein bithaye.

(2)… Quran-e-Majeed parhne se pehle Allah Ta'ala ki azmat dil mein bithaye aur khayaal kare ke yeh kis azeem zaat ka kalaam hai aur main kis bhaari kaam ke liye baitha hoon.

(3)… Quran-e-Kareem ki tilaawat karte waqt dil ko haazir rakhe, idhar udhar khayaal na kare, bure khayaalaat se dil ko aaloodah na kare aur jo be-khayaali mein parh chuka usay az-sar-e-nau tawajjuh se parhe.

(4)… Har hukm ke ma'ni mein ghaur o fikr kare, agar samajh mein na aaye to usay baar baar parhe aur agar kisi aayat ke parhne se lazzat mehsoos ho to usay phir parhe ke yeh dobaara parhna zyada tilaawat karne se behtar hai.

(5)… Jis tarah aayaat ka mazmoon tabdeel hota rahe isi tarah mazmoon ke mutabiq dil ki kaifiyat bhi badalti rahe aur Quran ke rang mein rangti jaye.

(6)… Quran-e-Majeed ki tilaawat is tarah kare ke goya yeh Quran Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah se sun raha hai aur khayaal kare ke abhi is zaat ki jaanib se sun raha hoon. (کیمیاء سعادت،کتاب ارکانِ مسلمانی، اصل ہشتم قرآن خواندن، آدابِ تلاوت، ۲۴۱-۲۴۷، ملخصاً)
