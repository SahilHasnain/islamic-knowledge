## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:122

`Source: tafseerId=50253; ayatId=131; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** یٰبَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَتِیَ الَّتِیْۤ اَنْعَمْتُ عَلَیْكُمْ وَ اَنِّیْ فَضَّلْتُكُمْ عَلَى الْعٰلَمِیْنَ(122)

**Roman Urdu:**

{یٰبَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ: Ae Bani Israeel.} Yahaan se aik baar phir Bani Israeel ko Allah Ta'ala ki ne'matein yaad dilaayi ja rahi hain taake un par qaayam ki gayi hujjat mazeed mazboot ho jaye. Is aayat se do masail maloom hue:

(1)… Nabi ki aulaad hona baa'is-e-izzat hai aur Allah Ta'ala ki rahmat hai.

(2)… Allah Ta'ala ki ne'maton ka charcha karna, zikr karna shukr ki aik qism hai. Lihaaza Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ki wilaadat-e-mubaarakah ka tazkirah karna ya is ki mehfil karna isi qism mein daakhil hai.

{اَنِّیْ فَضَّلْتُكُمْ: Be-shak main ne tumhein fazeelat di.} Bani Israeel apne zamaane mein tamaam logon se afzal thay kyunke yeh nabiyon ki aulaad thay aur in mein saaliheen bahut thay, ab Huzoor Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ka inkaar kar ke aur sar-kashi kar ke zaleel ho gaye. Is se maloom hua ke izzat Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ke qadam se wabastah hai, jo in ka ho gaya izzat paa gaya aur jo in se phir gaya zaleel ho gaya. A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmaate hain:

Bakhuda Khuda ka yahi hai dar, nahin aur koi mafar maqar

Jo wahaan se ho yehin aa ke ho jo yahaan nahin woh wahaan nahin
