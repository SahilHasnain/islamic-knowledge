# Faqeer ki Fiqh-e-Roshan ki Sanad

## Source Record 17231 | Hadith No. 97

Fiqh-e-roshan mein meri sanad buzurg Hanafi ulama, mashhoor muftiyon,
musannifeen, Imamon aur mashayikh se musalsal hai. Allah Ta'ala ka shukr hai
ke is sanad ke kai tareeqe hain. Un mein se ek nihayat ba-azmat tareeqa yeh hai:

Main fiqh ki riwayat Haram ke buzurg aur Makkah Mukarramah ke Hanafi mufti,
Maulana Abdul Rahman Siraj se karta hoon. Woh apne walid, buzurg mufti Maulana
Abdullah Siraj Makki se riwayat karte hain. Woh Makkah ke mufti Sayyid Jamal bin
Abdullah bin Umar se riwayat karte hain. Un se Shaykh-e-Jaleel Muhammad Abid
Ansari Madani, un se Shaykh Yusuf bin Muhammad bin Alauddin Mizjaji, un se
Shaykh Abdul Qadir bin Khalil, aur un se Shaykh Ismail bin Abdullah riwayat
karte hain. Shaykh Ismail Ali Zadah Bukhari ke naam se mashhoor hain.

Shaykh Ismail se Allah Ta'ala ke aarif Shaykh Abdul Ghani bin Ismail bin Abdul
Ghani Nabulsi riwayat karte hain. Woh *Al-Hadiqat-un-Nadiyyah*, *Al-Matalib-ul-
*Sharh-ud-Durar wal-Ghurar* ke musannif, se riwayat karte hain. Un ke do buzurg
shaykh Ahmad Shubri aur Hasan Sharnablali hain.

Is sanad mein Imam-e-Azam tak sattais waseele hain. Imam-e-Azam se Huzoor
Pur-Noor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ tak chaar waseele hain.

## Source Record 17232 | Hadith No. 98

*Durar wal-Ghurar* ke hashiyah-nigar *Noor-ul-Idah* aur is ki dono sharhon
*Maraqi-ul-Falah* aur *Imdad-ul-Fattah* ke musannif hain. Un ki pehli riwayat
Shaykh Umar bin Najim, *An-Nahr-ul-Faiq* ke musannif, Shams Hanuti, *Fatawa* ke
musannif, aur Shaykh Ali Maqdisi, *Nazm-ul-Kanz* ke sharah-nigar, se hai.

Doosri riwayat Shaykh Abdullah Nahreeri, Shaykh Muhammad bin Abdul Rahman
Maysari, Shaykh Muhammad bin Ahmad Hamawi aur Shaykh Ahmad Muhibbi se hai.
Yeh sab log Shaykh Ahmad bin Yunus Shalbi, jo *Fatawa* ke musannif hain, se
riwayat karte hain.

Woh Siraajuddin Abdul Barr bin Shihnah, *Al-Wahbaniyyah* ke sharah-nigar, se
riwayat karte hain. Woh Kamal bin Humam, *Fath-ul-Qadeer* ke musannif, se
riwayat karte hain. Woh Siraaj, Qari-e-Hidayah, se, woh Alauddin Sayrafi se,
aur woh Sayyid Jalaluddin Khabbazi, *Sharh-ul-Hidayah* ke sharah-nigar, se
riwayat karte hain.

### Footnote 1

Meri riwayaton mein yeh naam fa ke saath hai aur yahi zyada mashhoor hai. Ise
meem ke saath Sirami bhi kaha jata hai, jaisa ke *Fath-ul-Qadeer*, Tahtawi aur
*Radd-ul-Muhtar* mein aaya hai. Fa ke saath Siraf, Fars mein samandar ke kinare
Kerman ke qareeb ek basti ka naam hai. Mashhoor nahwi Abu Saeed isi jagah ke
rehne wale the. Meem ke saath Siram Room ka ek shehar hai. Nizam Yahya bin
Yusuf bin Fahd nahwi, jo Allamah Taftazani ke shagird the, isi shehar ke rehne
wale the.

### Footnote 2

Meri is riwayat mein bhi isi tarah hai. Meri doosri riwayat mein Siraaj Hanuti,
Ibrahim Karki, Shaykh Muhibbuddin Aqsarai, Qari-e-Hidayah aur Sayrafi ke zariye
yeh lafz aata hai: “Main Sayyid se riwayat karta hoon.”

**Baqi sanad agle safhe par hai.**

## Source Record 17233 | Hadith No. 99

Jalaluddin bin Shamsuddin Kurlani se riwayat hai. Woh Abdul Aziz bin Muhammad
bin Ahmad Bukhari se riwayat karte hain.

Yeh Sayyid Jalaluddin *Al-Kifayah Sharh-ul-Hidayah* ke musannif hain. Woh
Hidayah ki pehli sharh *An-Nihayah* ke musannif Husamuddin Sughnaqi ke shagird
the. Khabbazi, *Al-Mughni fil-Usul* ke musannif Umar bin Muhammad bin Amr, ne
bhi Hidayah ki sharh likhi. Yeh dono *Al-Kashf wat-Tahqiq* ke musannif Allamah
Abdul Aziz Bukhari ke shagird the. Wallahu Ta'ala a'lam. 12 minhu dama
fayzuhu (T).

### Footnote

Meri riwayat mein isi tarah hai. Sayyid Tahtawi aur Sayyid Shami ki sanad mein
bhi Fakhr-ul-Islam ke baad Shams-ul-A'immah Sarakhsi aur phir Shams-ul-A'immah
Halwani ka zikr hai. Main kehta hoon ke musalsal sanad mein yeh ek izafi naam
hai, kyunki Imam Fakhr-ul-Islam ne fiqh ka ilm Shams-ul-A'immah Halwani se
bina kisi waseele ke hasil kiya tha. Allamah Zahabi ne *Siyar A'lam-in-Nubala*
mein un ke tazkire mein likha hai:

**Baqi bayan agle safhe par hai.**

## Source Record 17234 | Hadith No. 100

Shams-ul-A'immah Sarakhsi, Fakhr-ul-Islam Bazdawi aur un ke bhai Sadr-ul-Islam
ne Imam Halwani se ilm hasil kiya. Allamah Zahabi ne bataya ke Imam Halwani ka
wisaal 456 Hijri mein Bukhara mein hua. Fakhr-ul-Islam ka wisaal Rajab 482
Hijri mein Kash mein hua. Un ki paidaish taqreeban 400 Hijri mein hui thi. Is
liye Imam Halwani ke wisaal ke waqt un ki umar lagbhag chhappan saal thi. 12
minhu dama fayzuhu.

### Footnote

Meri is riwayat mein bhi isi tarah hai aur Tahtawi aur Shami ki sanad mein bhi.
Mashhoor yeh hai ke un ki kuniyat Abu Muhammad aur naam Abdullah bin Muhammad
tha. Meri doosri riwayat mein bhi isi tarah hai. Us riwayat mein Izzuddin Ahmad
bin Muzaffar aur upar zikr kiye gaye Abdul Aziz Bukhari, dono Hafizuddin Bukhari
se riwayat karte hain. Woh Shams-ul-A'immah Kardari se, woh Badr-ul-A'immah
Umar Warsaki se, woh Imam Ruknuddin Abdul Rahman Kahani se, woh Fakhr-ul-Quzaat
Arsabandi se, aur woh Imad-ul-Islam Abdul Rahim Zawzani se riwayat karte hain.

**Baqi sanad agle safhe par hai.**

## Source Record 17235 | Hadith No. 101

Qazi Imam Abu Zayd Dabbusi se riwayat hai. Woh Ustad Abu Jafar Asturushni se,
woh Abul Hasan Ali Nasafi se, aur woh Imam Fazli se riwayat karte hain. Imam
Fazli ne kaha ke humein Imam Abu Muhammad Abdullah bin Muhammad bin Yaqoob
Sabzumuni Harisi ne bayan kiya, aur sanad aage chalti hai.

Shayad un ki do kuniyatein hon: (1) Abu Muhammad, (2) Abu Abdullah. Wallahu
Ta'ala a'lam. 12 minhu dama fayzuhu (T).

Abu Hafs Bukhari apne walid Ahmad bin Hafs se riwayat karte hain. Ahmad bin
Hafs mashhoor Imam Abu Hafs Kabir hain. Woh Imam Hujjat Abu Abdullah Muhammad
bin Hasan Shaybani se, woh Imam-e-Azam Abu Hanifa se, woh Hammad se, woh Ibrahim
se, woh Alqamah aur Aswad se, woh Abdullah bin Masood رضی الله تعالی عنہ se,
aur woh Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ se riwayat karte hain.
