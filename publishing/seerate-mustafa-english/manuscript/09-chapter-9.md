## Chapter 9: The Fourth Year of Hijra — 4 AH

The fourth year of Hijra also passed with various skirmishes against the disbelievers. After the decisive victory of Badr, the Muslims' awe had been established over all the Arab tribes, and for some time all the tribes sat quietly. But when news of the Muslims' heavy losses at Uhud spread, once again all the tribes suddenly rose up to destroy Islam and the Muslims. The Muslims were compelled to engage in battles for their defence. Among the famous expeditions of 4 AH are the following.

### Sariyya-e-Abu Salama

On 1st Muharram, 4 AH, someone suddenly brought news to Madina that Tulaiha bin Khuwailid and Salama bin Khuwailid — both brothers — had gathered an army of disbelievers and were marching to attack Madina. The Beloved ﷺ sent Hazrat Abu Salama with 150 mujahideen to confront that army, which included respected Muhajireen and Ansar like Hazrat Abu Sabra and Hazrat Abu Ubaida. But when the disbelievers learned that the Muslim army was approaching, they fled, leaving behind many camels and goats, which the Muslim mujahideen took as spoils of war. No battle occurred.

### Sariyya-e-Abdullah bin Anees

In Muharram, 4 AH, news came that Khalid bin Sufyan Hazali was gathering an army to attack Madina. The Beloved ﷺ sent Hazrat Abdullah bin Anees to confront him. Hazrat Abdullah found an opportunity, killed Khalid bin Sufyan Hazali, cut off his head, brought it to Madina, and placed it at the feet of the Chief of Both Worlds ﷺ.

The Beloved ﷺ was pleased with Hazrat Abdullah bin Anees's bravery and valour. He gifted him his staff and said: "You will walk proudly with this staff in your hand in Paradise." Hazrat Abdullah submitted: "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! Will this blessed staff remain with me as a token on the Day of Judgement?" at the time of his death, he made a will that this staff be placed in his shroud. (`Zurqani`)

### The Incident of Rajee

There is a place called Rajee between Asfaan and Makka. The soil of this place was stained with the sacred blood of seven noble Companions, which is why this event is known as Sariyya-e-Rajee. This painful incident also occurred in 4 AH.

The story is this: some men from the tribes of Azal and Qaarah came to the court of prophethood and submitted: "Our tribesmen have accepted Islam. Please send some Companions to teach them the beliefs and practices of Islam." At their request, the Beloved ﷺ sent 10 selected Companions under the leadership of Hazrat Aasim bin Sabit.

When this sacred caravan reached the place of Rajee, the treacherous disbelievers broke their promise. The disbelievers of the Banu Lahyan tribe gathered 200 men and attacked these 10 Muslims. The Muslims climbed a high hill for protection. The disbelievers began shooting arrows, and the Muslims rained stones from the hilltop.

Realizing they could not finish off the Muslims with weapons, the disbelievers resorted to deception. They called out: "O Muslims! We grant you amnesty and take you under our protection. Come down from the hill."

Hazrat Aasim bin Sabit said: "I cannot bring myself to accept a disbeliever's protection." Saying this, he prayed to Allah: "O Allah! Inform Your Messenger of our condition." Then, filled with the spirit of jihad, he descended from the hill and, fighting hand-to-hand with the disbelievers, was martyred along with six of his companions.

Since Hazrat Aasim had killed many great Quraish chiefs on the day of Badr, when the disbelievers of Makka learned of his martyrdom, they sent some men to Rajee to bring back a body part for identification, to confirm that Aasim had indeed been killed. But when the disbelievers reached the site searching for his body, they witnessed this miracle of the martyr: countless bees had formed a protective ring around his body, making it impossible to approach. The disbelievers of Makka returned unsuccessful.

**The Fate of Khubaib, Zaid, and Abdullah**

The remaining three — Hazrat Khubaib, Hazrat Zaid bin Dasina, and Hazrat Abdullah bin Tariq — trusted in the disbelievers' amnesty and descended. But the disbelievers broke their word and began tying them with bowstrings. Seeing this, Hazrat Abdullah bin Tariq said: "This is your first act of treachery. For me, it is better to be martyred like my companions." So he fought the disbelievers and was martyred.

But Hazrat Khubaib and Hazrat Zaid bin Dasina had already been tied up, so they were helpless. The disbelievers took them to Makka and sold them. Hazrat Khubaib had killed Haris bin Aamir in the Battle of Uhud, so his sons bought Khubaib to avenge their father's blood. Hazrat Zaid bin Dasina was bought by Safwan, the son of Umayya, with the intention of killing him.

**The Martyrdom of Hazrat Khubaib**

The disbelievers kept Hazrat Khubaib imprisoned for a few days, then took him outside the boundaries of the Haram and crucified him. Hazrat Khubaib asked the executioners for permission to pray two rakats of salah. They allowed him. He performed a very brief two-rakat prayer and said: "O assembly of disbelievers! My heart desired to pray longer, for this was my last prayer in life. But I feared you might think I was afraid of death."

The disbelievers crucified him. At that moment, he recited these verses:

*"Since I am being killed as a Muslim, I do not care on which side I am slain. All this is for Allah. If He wills, He will send blessings upon these severed pieces of my body."*

Haris bin Aamir's son Abu Sarua killed him. But by the glory of Allah, this same Abu Sarua and his two brothers, Uqba and Hujair, later honoured Islam and were blessed with the companionship of the Prophet.

**The Grave of Hazrat Khubaib**

Allah Ta'ala informed the Beloved ﷺ of Hazrat Khubaib's martyrdom through revelation. The Beloved said to the Companions: "Whoever brings down Khubaib's body from the cross will have Paradise." Hearing this glad tiding, Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam and Hazrat Miqdad bin Al Aswad travelled by night and hid by day until they reached the place of Taneem, where Khubaib's cross stood. Forty disbelievers were sleeping as guards around the cross. These two gentlemen lowered the body from the cross, placed it on a horse, and departed.

Even after forty days, the body was fresh and moist, and fresh blood was still dripping from the wounds. In the morning, 75 swift horsemen of the Quraish pursued them and caught up. When these gentlemen saw that the Quraish horsemen would capture them, they lifted Hazrat Khubaib's blessed body from the horse and placed it on the ground. By the glory of Allah, the ground immediately split open and swallowed the blessed body, then became level again with no trace of the crack remaining. This is why Hazrat Khubaib is known as "Baliul Ard" (the one swallowed by the earth).

After this, these gentlemen said to the disbelievers: "We are two lions returning to our jungle. If you can, stop us — otherwise go your way." The disbelievers did not see the body with them, so they returned to Makka. When both noble Companions narrated the whole account at the court of prophethood, Hazrat Jibreel was also present. He said: "O Messenger of Allah! We, the assembly of angels, are also proud of this feat of your two companions." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Martyrdom of Hazrat Zaid

A large number of Quraish disbelievers gathered to watch the execution of Hazrat Zaid bin Dasina, including Abu Sufyan. When he was placed on the cross and the executioner took the sword in hand, Abu Sufyan said: "O Zaid! Tell me truthfully — if Muhammad were being killed in your place at this moment, would you be pleased?" Hearing Abu Sufyan's taunt, Hazrat Zaid trembled with emotion and said in a voice filled with passion: "By Allah's oath! I would consider it most dear to sacrifice my own life, but I could never bear even a thorn pricking the blessed foot of my beloved Prophet ﷺ."

*"I am proud of my fate — it is for the sake of Muhammad's name. Let my head be severed while your sole rejects it. All this I can bear, but this I cannot — that a thorn should prick the sole of his foot."*

Hearing this, Abu Sufyan said: "I have seen many great lovers, but I have never seen the like of Muhammad's lovers." Nastaas, Safwan's slave, struck his neck with the sword.

### The Incident of Beere Muawwana

In Safar, 4 AH, the famous incident of Beere Muawwana occurred. Abu Baraa Aamir bin Malik — who was known as "Malaibul Asinnah" (one who plays with spears) due to his bravery — came to the court of prophethood. The Beloved ﷺ invited him to Islam. He neither accepted Islam nor expressed any aversion to it, but instead requested: "Send some of your selected Companions to our region. I hope they will accept the invitation to Islam." The Beloved said: "I fear the disbelievers of Najd." Abu Bara said: "I guarantee the safety of your Companions' lives and property."

After this, the Beloved ﷺ sent 70 selected righteous Companions, who were known as "Qurra" (the reciters). When these gentlemen reached Beere Muawwana, they halted. The caravan leader, Hazrat Hiraam bin Malhaan, took the Beloved's letter and went alone to Aamir bin Tufail — the chief of the tribe and Abu Bara's nephew. He did not even read the letter but signalled to a man, who killed Hazrat Hiraam with a spear from behind.

Aamir bin Tufail then gathered the tribes of Raal, Zaqwan, Asiyya, and Banu Lahyan and prepared an army, marching to attack the Companions. The Companions waited a long time for Hiraam's return at Beere Muawwana. When it grew too late, they advanced. On the way, they encountered Aamir bin Tufail's army, and battle began.

The disbelievers martyred all the Companions except Hazrat Amr bin Umayya Jamri. Among these martyrs was Aamir bin Fuhaira, about whom Aamir bin Tufail said that after being killed, his body rose high into the sky and then came back to earth. After this, his body could not be found, for the angels had buried him.

Aamir bin Tufail released Hazrat Amr bin Umayya Jamri, saying: "My mother had vowed to free a slave, so I free you." He cut a lock of his hair and let him go.

When Hazrat Amr bin Umayya Zamri reached the place of Qarqarah, he stopped in the shade of a tree. Two men from the Banu Kilaab tribe were also resting there. When both fell asleep, Hazrat Amr bin Umayya killed those two disbelievers, thinking happily that he had avenged the Companions' blood. But the Beloved ﷺ had already granted amnesty to those two men, which Hazrat Amr bin Umayya did not know.

When he reached Madina and narrated the entire account at the court of prophethood, the news of the martyrdom of the Companions of Beere Muawwana caused such immense grief to the Master of Prophethood that never in his entire blessed life had he experienced such sorrow. For an entire month, the Beloved ﷺ cursed the tribes of Raal, Zaqwan, Asiyya, and Banu Lahyan in the Fajr prayer. And for the two men killed by Hazrat Amr bin Umayya Zamri, the Beloved ﷺ announced the payment of blood money. (Bukhari, 1/136)

### Ghazwa-e-Banu Nazeer

Hazrat Amr bin Umayya Zamri had killed two men of the Banu Kilaab tribe, and the Beloved ﷺ had announced the payment of their blood money. To discuss this matter, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ went to the tribe of Banu Nazeer, the Jews, because there was a treaty between him and these Jews.

But the Jews are indeed a people with an extremely evil mentality. Despite having made a treaty, the hearts of these wicked people were filled with the fire of enmity and obstinacy toward the Prophet of Islam ﷺ. Although the Beloved ﷺ treated them well due to their being People of the Book, these people were always busy uprooting Islam and opposing the founder of Islam. Enmity and obstinacy toward the Muslims, collaboration and alliance with the disbelievers and hypocrites — this was always the conduct of these traitors.

So on this occasion, when the Messenger of Allah went to these Jews, they outwardly displayed good manners, but inwardly they devised a terrifying conspiracy and an extremely dangerous plan. The Beloved ﷺ was accompanied by Hazrat Abu Bakr, Hazrat Umar, and Hazrat Ali. The Jews seated all these gentlemen with great ceremony under a wall and consulted among themselves: "Drop a huge, heavy stone from the roof onto them so that all may be crushed to death." Accordingly, Amr bin Jahash climbed onto the roof for this purpose.

But the True Nourisher of the universe informed His Beloved ﷺ of this dirty conspiracy of the Jews through revelation. So the Beloved immediately rose quietly and, with his companions, returned to Madina. He informed the Companions of the Jews' conspiracy and, after consulting the Ansar and Muhajireen, sent a messenger to those Jews:

"Since you have broken the treaty with this treacherous and murderous conspiracy, you are given ten days to leave Madina. After that, whoever of you is found here will be killed."

Upon hearing this decree of the King of Madina, the Jews of Banu Nazeer prepared to be exiled. But the chief of the hypocrites, Abdullah bin Ubayy, became their supporter. He sent word: "Do not leave Madina at all. We are ready to help you with two thousand men. Besides, two powerful tribes — Banu Quraiza and Banu Ghatfaan — will also help you."

When the Jews of Banu Nazeer received this great support, they became bold as lions and sent word to the Beloved ﷺ: "We cannot leave Madina. Do whatever you think fit." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

After this reply, the Beloved ﷺ entrusted the leadership of Masjid Nabawi to Hazrat Ibn Umm Maktum and himself marched toward Banu Nazeer and laid siege to their fort. The siege lasted 15 days. All supplies from outside were cut off, and the Jews were completely besieged and helpless.

But at this moment, neither did the hypocrite Abdullah bin Ubayy come to help the Jews, nor did Banu Quraiza and Banu Ghatfaan provide any assistance. Concerning these traitors, Allah Ta'ala revealed:

*"Their example is like that of Satan when he says to man, 'Disbelieve.' Then when he disbelieves, he says, 'I am free of you. Indeed, I fear Allah, the Lord of the worlds.'"* (Al-Hashr: 16)

That is, just as Satan incites a person to disbelieve, but when the person falls into disbelief, Satan quietly slips away — similarly, the hypocrites emboldened the Jews of Banu Nazeer and made them fight the Beloved of Allah. But when the Jews faced battle, the hypocrites hid in their homes.

During the siege, the Beloved ﷺ also had some date-palm trees around the fort cut down, because the Jews might hide behind the trees and launch surprise attacks, causing difficulty for the Muslim army. Regarding the cutting of these trees, the Muslims split into two groups. Some thought the trees should not be cut because after victory they would become spoils of war. Others said cutting them would destroy the Jews' hiding places and enrage them, which was the objective.

On this occasion, this verse of Surah Al-Hashr was revealed:

*"Whatever palm trees you cut down or left standing on their roots — it was by Allah's permission, so that He might disgrace the defiantly disobedient."* (Al-Hashr: 5)

That is, both actions were correct — cutting some trees and leaving others — because both were by Allah's command and permission.

In any case, the Jews of Banu Nazeer, weary of the siege, finally agreed to leave Madina on the condition that they could take as much of their possessions as could be loaded on their camels. The Beloved ﷺ accepted their condition. All the Jews of Banu Nazeer loaded their belongings onto 600 camels and left Madina in a procession, singing and playing music. Some went to Khaibar, and most went to Syria, settling in Azra'aat and Uraiha.

After they left, the Muslims searched their homes and found 50 iron helmets, 50 coats of armour, and 300 swords, which came into the Beloved's possession.

Allah Ta'ala mentioned this expulsion of the Banu Nazeer Jews in Surah Al-Hashr of the Holy Quran:

*"It is He who expelled the disbelievers among the People of the Book from their homes at the first gathering. You did not think they would leave, and they thought their fortresses would protect them from Allah. But Allah came upon them from where they had not expected, and He cast terror into their hearts. They destroyed their homes with their own hands and the hands of the believers. So take warning, O people of insight."* (Al-Hashr: 2)

### Badr Sughra (The Minor Badr)

When returning from Uhud, Abu Sufyan had said: "Next year, we will meet again at Badr." Accordingly, in the month of Zulqada, 4 AH, the Beloved ﷺ entrusted the administration of Madina to Hazrat Abdullah bin Rawaha and set out for Badr with the army. He waited for the disbelievers for eight days.

On the other side, Abu Sufyan also set out with his army. After travelling one stage, he told his army: "This year is not suitable for war. There is such a severe drought that there is neither food and water for men nor grass and fodder for animals." Saying this, Abu Sufyan returned to Makka.

The Muslims had some trade goods with them. When no battle occurred, they traded and earned good profit, then returned to Madina. (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### Miscellaneous Events of 4 AH

(1) After Ghazwa-e-Banu Nazeer, when the Ansar said, "O Messenger of Allah! All the spoils from Banu Nazeer are yours. Give them to our Muhajir brothers. We do not desire anything from them," the Beloved ﷺ was pleased and prayed: "O Allah! Have mercy on the Ansar, the children of the Ansar, and the children of the children of the Ansar."

(2) In this year, the Beloved's grandson, Hazrat Abdullah bin Usman Ghani, was pecked in the eye by a chicken. From the shock, he remained restless for two nights and passed away.

(3) In this year, the Beloved's pure wife, Hazrat Zainab bint Khuzaima, passed away.

(4) In this year, the Beloved ﷺ married Hazrat Ummul Momineen Umm Salama.

(5) In this year, Hazrat Ali's respected mother, Hazrat Fatima bint Asad, passed away. The Beloved ﷺ gave his sacred shirt for her shroud. He himself descended into her grave and placed her body with his blessed hands, saying: "Besides Fatima bint Asad, no one has ever been saved from the pressure of the grave." Hazrat Umar bin Abdul Aziz narrates that only five blessed individuals were honoured by the Beloved himself descending into their graves: (1) Hazrat Khadija, (2) a son of Hazrat Khadija, (3) Abdullah Muzni known as Zul Bijadain, (4) Hazrat Aisha's mother Umm Rooman, (5) Hazrat Fatima bint Asad, the mother of Hazrat Ali.

(6) In this year, on 4th Shaban, 4 AH, Hazrat Imam Husain was born.

(7) In this year, a Jew committed adultery with a Jewess. When this case was presented to the court of prophethood, the Beloved ﷺ ruled stoning according to both the Torah and the Quran.

(8) In this year, Taama bin Ubairaq, who was a Muslim, committed theft. The Beloved ﷺ, according to the Quranic command, gave the order to cut off his hand. At this, he fled to Makka. There too he committed theft, and the people of Makka killed him — or a wall fell on him and he died, or he was thrown into a river. Some say he apostatized.

(9) According to some historians, the prohibition of wine was also revealed in this year. According to others, it was in 6 AH, and some say 8 AH.
