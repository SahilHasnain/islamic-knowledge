## Chapter 5: The Radiance of Prophethood in Madina

The old name of Madina Munawwara was "Yasrab". When the Beloved ﷺ settled in this city, its name became Madinatun Nabi. Then this name was shortened and became famous as Madina. Historically, this is a very ancient city.

When the Beloved ﷺ proclaimed prophethood, two Arab tribes were settled in this city — Aus and Khazraj — and some Jews were also there. Aus and Khazraj were idol worshippers like the disbelievers of Quraish, and the Jews were the People of the Book. Aus and Khazraj initially lived together with great unity and harmony, but then, according to the nature of the Arabs, fights began between these two tribes. The last battle, which is famous in Arab history as the Battle of Ba'aas, was so horrific and bloody that almost all the renowned warriors of Aus and Khazraj were killed fighting, and both tribes became extremely weak.

The Jews, although very few in number, were educated. Therefore, Aus and Khazraj were always overawed by the Jews' intellectual superiority and remained under their influence.

After accepting Islam, by the blessing of the sacred teaching and training of the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, all the old differences of Aus and Khazraj ended, and these two tribes began to live together like sugar and milk. And since these people provided unparalleled support and assistance to Islam and the Muslims with their body, mind and wealth, the Beloved ﷺ honoured these fortunate people with the exalted title of "Ansar". The Noble Quran also praised the good fortune of these self-sacrificing supporters of Islam in various places for their assistance to the Messenger and help to the Muslims. By the law of Shariah, love for the Ansar and having good faith towards them is binding for faith and obligatory for the entire Muslim Ummah.

### How Did Islam Spread in Madina?

The Ansar were idol worshippers, but through their association with the Jews, they knew that the Prophet of the Last Age was to appear. The Jews of Madina often threatened both tribes of Aus and Khazraj, saying: "When the Prophet of the Last Age appears, we will join his army and wipe you idol worshippers off the face of the earth." Therefore, both the Jews and the Ansar were awaiting the arrival of the Prophet of the Last Age.

In the 11th year of prophethood, the Beloved ﷺ went to the field of Mina as usual to invite the tribes coming for Hajj to Islam. He began to recite verses of the Noble Quran and present Islam before the people. The Beloved ﷺ was near Aqba (the valley), where today Masjidul Aqba stands, when 6 people from the Khazraj tribe came to him.

The Beloved ﷺ asked them their names and lineage, then recited some verses of the Quran and invited them to Islam. These people were extremely impressed. Looking at each other on their way back, they said, "The Prophet of the Last Age whose glad tidings the Jews have been giving — undoubtedly, this Prophet is him. We must ensure that the Jews do not accept Islam before us." Saying this, all of them became Muslims together.

On returning to Madina, they also invited their family members and relatives to Islam. The names of those 6 fortunate people are:

(1) Hazrat Uqba bin Aamir bin Naabi (2) Hazrat Abu Umama As'ad bin Jararah (3) Hazrat Auf bin Haaris (4) Hazrat Raafe bin Malik (5) Hazrat Qutba bin Aamir bin Hadeeda (6) Hazrat Jabir bin Abdullah bin Rayyab (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, 2/15`)

### The First Pledge of Aqba

In the following year, the 12th year of prophethood, during the season of Hajj, 12 people from Madina came secretly to this valley of Mina. They honoured Islam and pledged allegiance to the Beloved ﷺ. In the history of Islam, this pledge is called the First Pledge of Aqba.

Along with this, these people also requested that a teacher be sent with them to teach the commandments of Islam. So the Beloved ﷺ sent Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair to Madina Munawwara with them. He stayed at the house of Hazrat As'ad bin Jararah in Madina and began to go from house to house among the Ansar, preaching Islam. Every day, one or two new people entered the embrace of Islam. Gradually, Islam spread from Madina to Quba, house by house.

The chief of the Aus tribe, Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz, was a very brave and influential person. When Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair presented Islam before him, he initially showed dislike and aversion to Islam. But when Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair recited the Noble Quran to him, his heart immediately softened and he was so affected that he became blessed with the fortune of faith. With his acceptance of Islam, the entire Aus tribe entered the fold of Islam.

In the same year, on the famous 27th night of Rajab, the Beloved ﷺ was granted the physical Ascension (Meraj). During this journey of Meraj, the five daily prayers were made obligatory. Its detailed account will come in the chapter on miracles, if Allah wills.

### The Second Pledge of Aqba

One year after this, in the 13th year of prophethood, during the season of Hajj, approximately 72 people from Madina secretly came to this valley of Mina, hiding from their idol-worshipping companions. They pledged allegiance on the blessed hand of the Beloved ﷺ and made this covenant: "We will sacrifice our lives for the protection of the Beloved ﷺ and Islam."

On this occasion, the Beloved's uncle Hazrat Abbas was also present, who had not yet become a Muslim. He said to the people of Madina: "Listen! Muhammad is respected and honoured in every way in his family, Bani Hashim. We have always shielded him against his enemies. Now you wish to take him to your homeland. So listen — if you can support him till death, then it is best. Otherwise, withdraw right now."

Hearing this, Hazrat Baraa bin Aazib angrily said, "We have been raised in the laps of swords." Hazrat Bara bin Aazib could only say this much when Hazrat Abul Haisam interrupted and said, "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! We have old ties with the Jews. It is clear that after we become Muslims, these ties will break. We fear that when Allah Ta'ala grants you dominance, you might leave us and go back to your homeland of Makka."

Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ smiled and replied: "Be assured. Your blood is my blood. Believe that my life and death are with you. I am yours and you are mine. Your enemy is my enemy and your friend is my friend." (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib, 1/317`)

When the Ansar were taking this pledge, Hazrat Saad bin Jararah or Hazrat Abbas bin Nazla said, "O my brothers! Do you also know what you are pledging upon? Understand well — this is a declaration of war against all Arabs and non-Arabs." The Ansar said in a very passionate tone, "Yes! Yes! We are pledging upon this."

After the pledge was completed, the Beloved ﷺ appointed 12 people as Naqeebs (leaders) from this group. Among them, 9 were from the Khazraj tribe and 3 from the Aus tribe. Their blessed names are:

(1) Hazrat Abu Umama As'ad bin Jararah (2) Hazrat Saad bin Rabee (3) Hazrat Abdullah bin Rawaha (4) Hazrat Raafe bin Malik (5) Hazrat Baraa bin Maroor (6) Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr (7) Hazrat Saad bin Obada (8) Hazrat Munzir bin Umar (9) Hazrat Obada bin Saabit — these 9 are from Khazraj.

(10) Hazrat Usaid bin Huzair (11) Hazrat Saad bin Khaisma (12) Hazrat Abul Haisam bin Taihaan — these 3 are from Aus. (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib, 1/317`)

After this, all these honoured people went to their respective homes. In the morning, when the Quraish came to know of this, they became enraged like a blazing fire. They scolded the people of Madina and asked, "Have you pledged allegiance to fight against us with Muhammad?" Some non-Muslim companions of the Ansar expressed their ignorance. Hearing this, the Quraish left. But when, after investigation and inquiry, details of some Ansar's pledge were found out, the Quraish became beside themselves with rage and pursued the pledge-makers for arrest. However, the Quraish could not catch anyone except Hazrat Saad bin Obada.

The Quraish brought Hazrat Saad bin Obada to Makka and imprisoned him. But when Zubair bin Mutim and Haarib bin Harb bin Umayya found out, both of them told the Quraish: "For God's sake, release Saad bin Obada immediately, otherwise your trade with Syria will be endangered." Hearing this, the Quraish released Hazrat Saad bin Obada from prison, and he safely reached Madina. (`Seerat Ibne Hisham, 4/449`)

### The Migration to Madina

When Islam and the Muslims found a safe haven in Madina Munawwara, the Beloved ﷺ gave general permission to the esteemed Companions to leave Makka and migrate to Madina. The first to migrate was Hazrat Abu Salamah. After him, one after another, other people also began to set out for Madina. When the disbelievers of Quraish came to know, they started stopping and hindering them. But people continued the process of migration in secret. Gradually, many esteemed Companions left for Madina Munawwara. Only those people remained in Makka who were either imprisoned by the disbelievers or were helpless due to their poverty.

Since the Beloved had not yet received the command of migration from Allah, he remained in Makka. He also kept Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and Hazrat Ali Murtaza with him. So these two moths of the lamp of prophethood also stayed in Makka with him.

### The Conference of the Disbelievers

When the disbelievers of Makka saw that the Beloved ﷺ and the Muslims now had supporters outside Makka in Madina, and that the Ansar had given protection to the Muslims going to Madina, the disbelievers of Makka began to feel the danger that Muhammad ﷺ might also go to Madina and, from there, lead an army of his supporters to attack Makka.

To close the door on this danger, the disbelievers of Makka convened a major conference in their Darun Nadwa (House of Council). This was such a powerful gathering of the disbelievers of Makka that there was no wise and influential person in Makka who did not participate. Notably, Abu Sufyan, Abu Jahl, Utba, Zubair bin Mutim, Nazr bin Haaris, Abul Bakhtari, Jama bin Aswad, Hakeem bin Hizaam, Umayya bin Khalaf and many other Quraish chiefs were present in this assembly.

The accursed Satan also came, wrapped in a blanket, in the form of an old elder. The Quraish chiefs asked his name and lineage. He said, "I am the elder of Najd. I have come to this conference so that I may also present my opinion in your matter." Hearing this, the Quraish chiefs included Iblees in their conference as well, and the proceedings of the conference began.

When the matter of the Beloved ﷺ was presented, Abul Bakhtari gave the opinion: "Imprison him in a room, tie his hands and feet, and give him food and water through a hole." The Elder of Najd (Satan) said, "This opinion is not good. By Allah's oath! If you imprison him in a house, his self-sacrificing companions will surely come to know and will free him at the risk of their lives."

Abul Aswad Rabia bin Amr Aamiri suggested: "Exile him from Makka so that he goes and lives in some other city. This way we will be rid of his Quran recitation and his preaching of Islam." Hearing this, the Elder of Najd became angry and said, "Curse be upon your opinion! Do you not know how much sweetness, effect and charm there is in Muhammad's speech? By Allah's oath! If you exile him, he will recite and spread the Quran throughout Arabia, make all the Arab tribes his followers, and then bring a great army and attack you with such force that you will be helpless. Then, apart from becoming his slaves, nothing will work for you. So do not even think of exiling him."

Then Abu Jahl said, "Sir! There is an opinion in my mind that has not occurred to anyone else." Hearing this, everyone's ears perked up and they asked eagerly, "Tell us, what is it?" Abu Jahl said, "My opinion is that one famous warrior from each tribe should draw his sword and all together attack at once and kill Muhammad ﷺ. By this method, the blood money will fall upon all the tribes. Obviously, the Bani Hashim do not have the strength to fight all the tribes to avenge this blood. Therefore, they will agree to accept blood money, and we will easily pay the amount together."

Hearing Abu Jahl's bloody proposal, the Elder of Najd jumped with joy and said, "Indeed, this plan is absolutely correct. No other proposal is acceptable." So all members of the conference passed this proposal unanimously. The council was adjourned, and each person went to his home with this dreadful resolve.

Allah, the Holy, mentions this incident in the following verse of the Noble Quran:

*"O Beloved! Remember when the disbelievers were plotting against you — to imprison you, or to martyr you, or to exile you. They were plotting, and Allah was executing His concealed plan, and Allah is the best of planners."* (Al-Anfal: 30)

What was Allah Ta'ala's concealed plan? See its manifestation on the next page — how He protected His Beloved and how the Ever-Living Sustainer overturned the entire scheme of the disbelievers. (`Ibne Hisham`)

### The Incident of the Beloved's Migration

When the disbelievers had agreed on the murder of the Beloved ﷺ and the conference had ended, and they had left for their homes, Hazrat Jibreel Ameen descended with the command of the Lord of the Worlds: "O Beloved! Do not sleep on your bed tonight. Migrate to Madina."

So at the time of midday, the Beloved ﷺ went to the house of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and said to him, "Send everyone in the house away. I need to consult you." Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique submitted, "O Messenger of Allah! May my parents be sacrificed for you. There is no one here except your wife (Hazrat Aisha)." (At that time, the Beloved's marriage with Hazrat Aisha had already taken place.)

The Beloved ﷺ said, "O Abu Bakr! Allah Ta'ala has granted me permission to migrate." Hazrat Abu Bakr submitted, "May my parents be sacrificed for you! Please grant me the honour of accompanying you." The Beloved ﷺ accepted his request.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique had been preparing two she-camels for four months, feeding them babool leaves, so that they would serve as rides for the migration. He submitted, "O Messenger of Allah! Please accept one of these she-camels." The Beloved ﷺ said, "Accepted, but I will pay its price." Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, reluctantly, was compelled to accept this command of the Messenger.

Hazrat Aisha Siddiqa was very young at that time, but her elder sister Hazrat Asma prepared the travel provisions. She placed food in the bag and, tearing her waistband into two pieces, tied the bag with one and tied the mouth of the water-skin with the other. This is the honour of which she is remembered by the exalted title of "Zaatunnataqain" (The One with Two Waistbands).

After this, the Beloved ﷺ hired a disbeliever named Abdullah bin Uraiqat, who was an expert of routes, as a guide for payment. Entrusting both she-camels to him, he said, "Come with both she-camels to the Cave of Saur after three nights." After making all these arrangements, the Beloved ﷺ came to his house. (`Bukhari, 1/553`)

### The Siege of the House of Prophethood

According to their plan, the disbelievers of Makka surrounded the house of prophethood and waited for the Beloved ﷺ to fall asleep so that they could make a murderous attack on him. At that time, only Ali Murtaza was with the Beloved ﷺ in the house.

Although the disbelievers of Makka were the worst enemies of the Mercy to the Worlds, despite this, the disbelievers had so much trust in the Beloved's honesty and trustworthiness that they would keep their valuable goods and belongings with him as trusts. At that time too, many trusts were in the house of prophethood. The Beloved said to Hazrat Ali, "Sleep on my bed covered with my green sheet. After I leave, return all the trusts of the Quraish to their owners and then come to Madina."

This was a very frightening and extremely dangerous moment. Hazrat Ali knew that the disbelievers of Makka had resolved to kill the Beloved ﷺ. But Hazrat Ali had complete faith that since the Master of the Prophets had commanded him to return all the trusts of the Quraish and come to Madina, he would remain alive and reach Madina. The bed of the Messenger of Allah — which was a bed of thorns today — became a bed of flowers for Hazrat Ali. He slept on the bed peacefully until morning, enjoying a sweet sleep. Proud of this feat, the Lion of Allah said in his poetry:

*"I put my life at risk and protected that noble being who is the best and most exalted among all who walk the earth and perform tawaf of the Kaaba, the Hateem."*

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was concerned that the disbelievers of Makka might play a secret trick on him, but the Merciful Lord saved him from the secret plotting of the disbelievers.

The Master of the Prophets ﷺ made the Lion of Allah sleep on the bed of prophethood, took a handful of dust in his hand, recited the opening verses of Surah Ya Sin, and came out of the house of prophethood. He threw dust on the heads of the besieging disbelievers and walked right through their gathering. No one saw him, no one knew anything. A person who was not present in that gathering later informed them: "Muhammad has left from here, and as he passed, he threw dust on your heads." Those unfortunate people passed their hands over their heads — indeed, dust and dirt was on their heads. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, 2/57`)

The Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ came out of his house and stood near the place of Hazoora. He looked at the Kaaba with great longing and said: "O city of Makka! You are dearer to me than the entire world. If my people had not expelled me from you, I would not have settled anywhere else."

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique had already agreed on the appointed place. He too arrived there. Thinking that the disbelievers of Makka might recognize their path from their footprints and follow them, and also seeing that the Beloved's delicate feet had become injured, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique lifted the Beloved ﷺ onto his shoulders and, treading over thorny bushes and sharp stones, reached the Cave of Saur that very night.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique entered the cave first, cleaned it thoroughly, and tore pieces from his own clothing to plug all the holes in the cave. Then the Master of the Prophets ﷺ entered the cave. He placed his blessed head in Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique's lap and fell asleep. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique had plugged one hole with his heel. A snake from inside that hole bit the companion of the cave's foot repeatedly. But the self-sacrificing Siddique did not move his foot, thinking that the Mercy to the Worlds' peaceful sleep should not be disturbed. But due to the severity of the pain, a few drops of the companion of the cave's tears fell on the cheek of the Chief of Creation. The Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ awoke. Seeing his companion of the cave crying, he became distressed. He asked, "Abu Bakr! What happened?" He submitted, "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! A snake has bitten me." Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ applied his blessed saliva to the wound, and the entire pain vanished instantly. The Master of the Prophets ﷺ remained in that cave for three nights.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique's young son Hazrat Abdullah would sleep at the mouth of the cave every night. In the early morning, he would go to Makka and find out what schemes the Quraish were devising. Whatever news he gathered, he would come in the evening and submit before the Beloved ﷺ. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique's slave Hazrat Aamir bin Fuhaira would come near the cave late at night with goats from the pasture. The milk of those goats would be drunk by the King of Both Worlds ﷺ and his companion of the cave.

The Beloved ﷺ had entered the Cave of Saur. On the other side, when the disbelievers who had besieged the house of prophethood entered the house in the morning, they found Hazrat Ali on the bed of prophethood. The oppressors questioned him briefly and then released him. Then, in search of the Beloved ﷺ, they combed every corner of Makka and its surroundings. Eventually, searching and searching, they reached the Cave of Saur. But the spider had woven a web at the mouth of the cave, and a pigeon had laid eggs at the edge.

Seeing this scene, the disbelievers of Quraish said among themselves: "If any person were present in this cave, neither would the spider have spun a web nor would the pigeon have laid eggs here." Hearing the footsteps of the disbelievers, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique became somewhat worried and submitted, "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! Our enemies have come so close that if they look at their feet, they will see us." The Beloved ﷺ said: "Do not fear! Allah is with us." After this, Allah Ta'ala sent such tranquillity and calm upon the heart of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique that he became completely fearless.

These are the self-sacrifices of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique about which the famous poet of the court of prophethood, Hazrat Hassan bin Saabit Ansari, said so beautifully:

*"And two — the other (Abu Bakr) — when he climbed the mountain and reached the lofty cave in this state that the enemy was circling around them..."*

*"And he (Abu Bakr) was the beloved of the Messenger of Allah. All of creation knows that the Beloved ﷺ has placed no one equal to him."* (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib`)

In any case, on the fourth day, on Monday, the 12th of Rabiul Awwal, the Beloved ﷺ came out of the Cave of Saur. Abdullah bin Uraiqat, whom the Beloved ﷺ had hired as a guide, was present at the Cave of Saur with two she-camels as agreed. The Beloved ﷺ mounted his she-camel. On one she-camel sat Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and Hazrat Aamir bin Fuhaira. Abdullah bin Uraiqat began to walk ahead on foot. They left the common route and started travelling on unfamiliar paths along the seashore.

### The Reward of 100 Camels

Meanwhile, the people of Makka had announced that whoever captured Muhammad ﷺ would receive a reward of 100 camels. Lured by this valuable reward, many greedy people began searching for the Beloved ﷺ. Some even pursued him to far-off destinations.

### Umme Maabad's Goat

On the second day, at the place of Qudaid, the Beloved ﷺ passed by the house of Umme Maabad Aatika bint Khalid Khuzaiya. Umme Maabad was an elderly woman who would sit in the courtyard of her tent and give food and water to travellers. The Beloved ﷺ intended to buy some food from her, but she had nothing available.

The Beloved ﷺ noticed a very thin goat in one corner of her tent and asked, "Does this give milk?" Umme Maabad said, "No." The Beloved ﷺ said, "If you permit me, I will milk it." Umme Maabad gave permission. The Beloved recited Bismillah and touched its udder. The udder filled with milk, and so much milk came out that everyone drank to their fill. All of Umme Maabad's vessels were filled with milk. Seeing this miracle, Umme Maabad and her husband both became blessed with Islam.

It is narrated that this goat of Umme Maabad remained alive until 18 Hijri and kept giving milk continuously. During the caliphate of Hazrat Umar, when there was a severe famine and the milk in the udders of all animals dried up, even then this goat gave milk morning and evening. (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib`)

### Suraqa's Horse

When the Beloved ﷺ moved on from Umme Maabad's house, a famous horseman of Makka, Suraqa bin Malik bin Jasham, appeared pursuing him on a swift horse. He came close and intended to attack, but his horse stumbled and he fell. But the reward of 100 camels was no ordinary thing. Greed for the reward spurred him on again. He moved forward with the intention of attacking. By the prayer of the Beloved, his horse's legs sank into the rocky ground up to the knees.

Seeing this miracle, Suraqa began to tremble with fear and terror and cried out, "Aman! Aman!" The heart of the Master of the Prophets was an ocean of mercy and kindness. Seeing Suraqa's helplessness and weeping, his river of mercy swelled. He prayed, and the earth released his horse. After this, Suraqa submitted, "Write a document of amnesty for me." By the command of the Beloved ﷺ, Hazrat Aamir bin Fuhaira wrote a document of amnesty for Suraqa. Suraqa placed that document in his quiver and returned.

On the way, whoever inquired about the Beloved ﷺ, Suraqa would turn them back saying, "I searched very far but Hazrat is not in that direction." While returning, Suraqa also presented some travel provisions as a gift to the Beloved ﷺ, but he did not accept them. (`Bukhari, Bab-ul-Hijrat`)

Suraqa did not become a Muslim at that time, but the impression of the Beloved's prophetic greatness and the truth of Islam settled in his heart. Later, when the Beloved ﷺ was camped at Ji'ranah after the conquest of Makka and the battles of Taif and Hunain, Suraqa presented himself at the court of prophethood with that same document of amnesty and accepted Islam with a large group of his tribe.

It should be noted that this is the same Suraqa bin Malik about whom the Beloved ﷺ gave news of the unseen, saying: "O Suraqa! What will be your state when the bracelets of Kisra, the King of Persia, will be put on your hands?" Years after this statement, during Hazrat Umar's caliphate, Iran was conquered and the bracelets of Kisra were brought to the court of the caliphate. To verify and confirm the command of the King of Both Worlds, the Commander of the Faithful, Hazrat Umar, put those bracelets on Suraqa bin Malik and said: "O Suraqa! Say: All praise is for Allah Ta'ala who took these bracelets from the King of Persia and put them on Suraqa, a Bedouin." Hazrat Suraqa passed away in 24 Hijri, when Hazrat Usman Ghani was on the throne of the caliphate.

### Buraida Aslami's Banner

When the Beloved ﷺ neared Madina, Buraida Aslami came with 70 horsemen of the Bani Saham tribe to capture him, lured by the reward of 100 camels. But when they came before the Beloved ﷺ and asked, "Who are you?" the Beloved ﷺ said, "I am Muhammad bin Abdullah and the Messenger of Allah." The majesty and splendour of prophethood affected their hearts to such an extent that they immediately recited the Kalima of Shahadah and entered the fold of Islam.

With complete devotion, they submitted this request: "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! It is my wish that the Beloved's entry into Madina should be accompanied by a banner." Saying this, he took off his turban, tied it to his spear, and became the standard-bearer of the Master of the Prophets, walking ahead until Madina. Then he asked, "O Messenger of Allah! Where will you stay in Madina?" The King of Both Worlds ﷺ said: "My she-camel is commanded by Allah. Wherever it sits down, that will be my place of stay."

### Hazrat Zubair's Valuable Clothes

In this journey, by a beautiful coincidence, they met Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam, the son of the Beloved's aunt Hazrat Safiyya. He was returning from Syria with trade goods. He presented some fine clothes as a gift to the Master of the Prophets ﷺ and Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, which the King of Both Worlds and Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique accepted.

### The Emperor of Prophethood Enters Madina

The news of the arrival of the Master of the Prophets ﷺ had already reached Madina. The mention of his coming was on the tongues of even women and children. The people of Madina were extremely eager and restless to see him. Every day, from morning, they would go outside the city, standing in anticipation, ready to welcome him. When the sun became hot, they would return to their homes with regret and sorrow.

One day, after the people of Madina had returned as usual after waiting for him, a Jew saw from his fort that the riding animal of the King of Both Worlds ﷺ had neared Madina. He called out in a loud voice: "O people of Madina! Behold, the caravan of mercy for whom you used to wait every day has arrived!" Hearing this, all the Ansar adorned themselves with weapons and, overwhelmed with joy and ecstasy, rushed out of their homes to welcome the King of Both Worlds. The cries of Allahu Akbar echoed through the entire city.

At a distance of three miles from Madina Munawwara — where today the Masjid Quba stands — the Beloved ﷺ arrived on the 12th of Rabiul Awwal. He stayed at the house of Hazrat Kulsum bin Hadam in the tribe of Amr bin Auf. The family members shouted Allahu Akbar with passionate fervour at this honour and pride that the King of Both Worlds had become their guest.

From all sides, the Ansar came, overwhelmed with joy, and presented their offerings of greetings and devotion at the court of prophethood. Many esteemed Companions who had migrated to Madina Munawwara before the Beloved were also staying in that house. Hazrat Ali, according to the command of the Prophet, had returned the trusts of the Quraish and left Makka on the third day. He too arrived in Madina and stayed in this same house.

Hazrat Umme Kulsum bin Hadam and her family members remained occupied day and night in serving these sacred guests. Allahu Akbar! What a luminous gathering there must have been in the family of Amr bin Auf — the Chief of the Prophets, the Chief of the Saints, and the noble Companions. The moon, the sun, and the stars, looking at this gathering in wonder, would have said in their silent language: "It is hard to decide whether the assembly of the heavens is more radiant today or the house of Hazrat Kulsum bin Hadam."

Every child of the Amr bin Auf family, smiling with joy, would have sung in their silent language:

*"I sacrifice myself at his feet, by whose gracious arrival ruined cities are turned into gardens."*

`اللهم صل وسلم وبارك على سيدنا محمد وعلى آله وصحبه وبارك وسلم`
