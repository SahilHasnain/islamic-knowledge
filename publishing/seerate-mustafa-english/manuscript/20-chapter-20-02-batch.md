Qaazi Iyaaz included it in Shifa, Hafiz Ibn Sayyidunnas in 'Bashar illabib,' and Hafiz Alauddin Mughaltaayi in 'Azzahrul Baasim.' Hafiz Ahmad bin Saaleh said: 'This should suffice you, and scholars should not lag behind regarding this hadith, for it is among the greatest miracles of prophethood.' The hadith memorizers (Huffaz) have considered it objectionable that Ibn Jauzi included this hadith in his book 'Kitaabul Mauzoo'aat.'"

### The Sun Halted

Among the heavenly miracles of the Holy Prophet, similar to the miracle of the sun's return, is the miracle of the moving sun halting — a great miracle that occurred during the day after the Night of Ascension (Me'raj). Younus bin Bukair narrates from Ibn Ishaq that when the disbelievers of Quraish asked the Holy Prophet about the condition of their caravan coming from Syria to Makka, he said: "Yes, I saw your caravan on the road to Baitul Muqaddas, and it will arrive in Makka on Wednesday." The Quraish came out of the city on Wednesday and awaited the arrival of their caravan until sunset, but the caravan did not appear. At that time, the Holy Prophet supplicated to Allah, and Allah halted the sun, extending the day by an hour until the caravan arrived. (Zurqani, Vol. 5, p. 116; Shifa, Vol. 1, p. 185)

Note that the miracle of the sun's halting (Habsushshams) is not exclusive to the Holy Prophet. Among the previous prophets, this miracle also appeared for Sayyidina Yush'a bin Noon (Alaihissalam). The account is that on a Friday, he was fighting the Jabbarin (tyrants) at Baitul Muqaddas when the sun began to set and there was a danger that if the sun set, Saturday would begin, and according to Mosaic law, Jihad was not permitted on Saturday. At that time, Allah stopped the sun from moving for an hour until Yush'a bin Noon achieved victory over the Jabbarin and completed the Jihad. (Tafseer Jalalain, Surah Maida, p. 98)

### The Ascension (Me'raj Shareef)

Among the heavenly miracles of the Holy Prophet, the event of Me'raj is of great importance, completely transcending our material world and far beyond the bounds of human intellect's estimation and conjecture.

The Me'raj is also called "Asra." "Asra" means to travel by night or to take by night. Since Allah Almighty described the event of Me'raj in the Holy Quran using the word "Asra," the event became known as "Asra." And because in the hadith describing the Me'raj, the Holy Prophet used the word "Urija Bi" (I was taken up), this is why the event is called "Me'raj."

In hadith and books of Seerah, this event has been narrated by a very large number of noble Companions. Allama Zurqani counted 45 Companions by name who narrated the hadith of Me'raj. We have given a somewhat detailed account of this in our book "Noorani Taqreerein."

**When Did the Me'raj Occur?**

There is great disagreement about the date, day, and month of the Me'raj. However, there is unanimous agreement that Me'raj occurred after the revelation began and before the Hijrah, and that it took place in Makka. Ibn Qutaiba Deenwari (d. 267 AH), Ibn Abdul Barr (d. 463 AH), Imam Rafeyi, and Imam Nawawi wrote that the Me'raj occurred in the month of Rajab. Muhaddis Abdul Ghani Maqdasi specified the 27th of Rajab. Allama Zurqani wrote that people act upon this, and some historians hold that this is the strongest narration. (Zurqani, Vol. 1, pp. 355-357)

**How Many Times and How Did the Me'raj Occur?**

The correct view of the majority of scholars of the Ummah is that the Me'raj occurred only once, with body and soul together, in a wakeful state. This is the view of the majority of Companions, Successors, jurists, muhadditheen, and the noble Sufis. Allama Mulla Ahmad Jeewan (Rahmatullahi Ta'ala Alaih), the teacher of Aurangzeb Alamgir, wrote: "The most correct saying is that Me'raj occurred with body and soul together in a wakeful state. This is the way of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah. Therefore, whoever says the Me'raj was only spiritual or only in a dream is a heretic, misguided, a misleader, and a transgressor." (Tafseerate Ahmadiya)

**Seeing Allah (Deedare Ilaahi):**

Did the Holy Prophet see Allah during the Me'raj? The righteous predecessors (Salaf) differed on this issue. Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) and some Companions said that the Prophet did not see Allah during the Me'raj. They interpreted the relevant verses to mean that he saw Sayyidina Jibreel in his original form with 600 wings. Some predecessors, like Sayyidina Saeed bin Jubair, remained silent on whether he saw or did not see. However, a large group of noble Companions and Successors said that the Holy Prophet saw Allah with his physical eyes.

Abdullah bin Al Harith narrates that Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas and Sayyidina Ka'ab (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) gathered in a meeting. Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas said: "Whatever anyone may say, we Banu Hashim say that undoubtedly Muhammad saw his Lord twice during the Me'raj." Hearing this, Sayyidina Ka'ab shouted so loudly that the mountains echoed, saying: "Indeed, Sayyidina Moosa spoke to Allah, and Muhammad saw Allah."

Similarly, Sayyidina Abu Zar Ghifari (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) said in his commentary on Surah Najm, verse 11: "The Prophet saw his Lord." Similarly, Sayyidina Mu'az bin Jabal (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) narrated from the Holy Prophet: "I saw my Lord."

Muhaddis Abdurrazzaq reports that Imam Hasan Basri swore that Muhammad indeed saw his Lord. Some historians narrate that Sayyidina Abdullah bin Mas'ood held the same view. Ibn Ishaq reports that Marwan, the governor of Madina, asked Sayyidina Abu Huraira: "Did Muhammad see his Lord?" He replied: "Yes."

Naqqash mentioned about Imam Ahmad bin Hambal (Rahimahullah) that he said: "I follow the view of Abdullah bin Abbas that the Holy Prophet saw Allah — he saw, he saw, he saw..." — he continued saying "saw" until his breath ran out. (Shifa, Vol. 1, pp. 119-120)

In Sahih Bukhari, at the end of the Me'raj narration from Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) related by Shareek bin Abdullah, it states: "The Holy Prophet reached Sidratul Muntaha, and the Honorable Almighty (Allah) drew near and came close until there remained a distance of two bows or even less." (Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 220)

In any case, the way of Ahl al-Sunnah is that the Holy Prophet saw the sacred person of Allah with his physical eyes on the night of Me'raj. Apart from the issue of seeing, there is a narration that deserves special attention: Allah invited His Beloved to the Exalted Throne with utmost splendor and grandeur, honored him in the chamber of secrets with the discourse of intimacy... But despite these boundless favors, not showing His vision to His Beloved and preventing it — this is a matter that is difficult to accept from the perspective of love and affection. For a glorious host to deprive his glorious guest of his meeting and not show him his sight — this is very incomprehensible to those who have the taste of love and affection. Therefore, we, the group of lovers, will continue to say until our last breath, like Imam Ahmad bin Hambal:

> "What secret can remain hidden from you
> When even Allah is not concealed from you? Upon you be thousands of salutations."

**Brief Account of the Me'raj:**

On the night of Me'raj, the roof of the Prophet's house opened, and suddenly Sayyidina Jibreel descended with several angels. They took him to the Sacred Mosque (Kaaba), split open his blessed chest, and washed his heart with Zamzam water. Then they filled a tray with faith and wisdom and poured it into his chest, closing the incision.

Then the Prophet mounted the Buraq and proceeded to Baitul Muqaddas (Jerusalem). The Buraq's speed was such that its step would land where its sight ended. At Baitul Muqaddas, the Prophet tied the Buraq to the same ring where the prophets used to tie their mounts. Then he led all the prophets and messengers present there in a two-rak'at prayer. (Tafseer Roohul Bayan, Vol. 5, p. 112)

When leaving, Sayyidina Jibreel presented him with two cups — one of wine and one of milk. The Prophet chose the cup of milk. Jibreel said: "You have chosen the natural disposition (Fitrah). If you had chosen the wine, your Ummah would have gone astray."

Then Jibreel took him up to the heavens:
- **First Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Aadam (Alaihissalam)
- **Second Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Yahya and Sayyidina Eisa (cousins)
- **Third Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Yousuf (Alaihissalam)
- **Fourth Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Idris (Alaihissalam)
- **Fifth Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Haroon (Alaihissalam)
- **Sixth Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Moosa (Alaihissalam)
- **Seventh Heaven:** Met Sayyidina Ibrahim (Alaihissalam), leaning against Baitul Mamoor, which is entered daily by 70,000 angels. Every prophet welcomed him saying: "Welcome, O righteous Prophet!"

Then he was shown Paradise. Then he reached Sidratul Muntaha. When the light of divine splendor fell upon this tree, its form changed instantly and such diverse lights appeared in it that words cannot describe. Here Jibreel stopped, saying: "I cannot go beyond this."

Then Allah Almighty called him to the Throne — indeed beyond the Throne, wherever He willed — granted him audience, and in the chamber of secrets, those intimacies were exchanged whose delicacy and subtlety words cannot bear. In the Holy Quran, Allah has expressed this reality through the hint of "فَأَوْحَىٰ إِلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ مَا أَوْحَىٰ" (Then He revealed to His bondsman what He revealed).

At the divine court, besides countless gifts, three special favors were granted:

1. The last verses of Surah Baqarah
2. The glad tidings that every person of his Ummah who did not commit shirk would be forgiven
3. **50 daily prayers** were ordained

When the Prophet returned with these divine gifts, Sayyidina Moosa (Alaihissalam) advised him: "Your Ummah cannot bear the burden of 50 prayers. Go back and ask for a reduction." Following Moosa's advice, the Prophet repeatedly went back and forth between the divine court and Moosa, until only 5 daily prayers remained. Allah said to His Beloved: "My word cannot change. O Beloved! These 5 prayers will be 50 for your Ummah. The prayers will be 5, but I will give your Ummah the reward of 50 prayers."

Then the Prophet toured the realms of dominion well, observed and witnessed the divine signs, descended from the heavens to the earth, entered Baitul Muqaddas, and mounted Buraq. On the way, he saw all the stages to Makka and also the Quraish caravan. After completing all these stages, he reached Masjid al-Haram. Since much of the night remained, he slept and woke up at dawn.

When he recounted the night's events to the Quraish, their leaders were extremely astonished. Some hypocrites called him a liar, while others posed various questions. Since most Quraish leaders had repeatedly seen Baitul Muqaddas and knew the Prophet had never been there, they began testing him with questions about its doors, walls, and mihrabs. At that moment, Allah immediately presented the complete architectural plan of Baitul Muqaddas before the Prophet's prophetic vision. As the disbelievers of Quraish kept asking questions, the Prophet kept looking at the building and giving correct answers. (Bukhari, Madarijun Nubuwwah, Muslim Shareef, Tafseer Roohul Ma'ani)

**The Conveyances of the Me'raj Journey:**

Imam Alaayi wrote in his commentary that the Prophet traveled on 5 types of conveyances during the Me'raj: from Makka to Baitul Muqaddas on Buraq; from Baitul Muqaddas to the first heaven on stairs of light; from the first to the seventh heaven on the wings of angels; from the seventh heaven to Sidratul Muntaha on Jibreel's wing; from Sidratul Muntaha to the station of Qaba Qausain on Rafraf. (Tafseer Roohul Ma'ani, Vol. 5, p. 10)

**The Stations of the Me'raj Journey:**

From Baitul Muqaddas to Qaba Qausain, the Prophet halted at 10 stations, conversing at each and observing many divine signs: (1) First Heaven (2) Second Heaven (3) Third Heaven (4) Fourth Heaven (5) Fifth Heaven (6) Sixth Heaven (7) Seventh Heaven (8) Sidratul Muntaha (9) Maqam Mustawa — where he heard the sounds of the movements of the universe of divine power (10) The Exalted Throne (Arsh Aazam).

### The Miracle of the Clouds

Sayyidina Anas bin Maalik (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "There was a severe drought in Arabia. When the Holy Prophet ascended the pulpit to deliver the sermon, a Bedouin stood up and complained: 'O Messenger of Allah! Animals are dying and children are perishing from hunger due to lack of rain. Please pray.' At that time, there was not a trace of cloud in the sky. But as soon as the Messenger of Allah raised his blessed hand, clouds gathered from all directions like mountains. Before he had even descended from the pulpit, raindrops were falling on his blessed beard. Rain continued to pour for 8 consecutive days.

"The following Friday, when the Prophet ascended the pulpit, the same Bedouin (or another) stood up and cried loudly: 'O Messenger of Allah! Houses have collapsed and livestock have drowned. Please pray that the rain stops.' Hearing this, the Prophet again raised his blessed hand and prayed: 'O Allah! Let it rain around us, not upon us.' Then he gestured toward the clouds with his blessed hand, and the clouds cleared away from Madina, and the rain stopped in Madina and its surroundings." (Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 127)

**An Important Observation:**

These few heavenly miracles that have been mentioned demonstrate that the Holy Prophet, through the power granted by Allah, also exercises authority in heavenly wonders, and his God-given sovereignty is not limited to the earth but also prevails over the heavenly creation. In Tirmizi, there is a hadith: "Every prophet has two ministers from the inhabitants of the heavens and two from the inhabitants of the earth. My two heavenly ministers are Jibreel and Mika'il, and my two earthly ministers are Abu Bakr and Umar." (Mishkat, Vol. 2, p. 560)

Obviously, a king's ministers remain within the boundaries of his kingdom. If the Holy Prophet's God-given sovereignty did not extend to the heavens, how could Jibreel and Mika'il reside in the heavens as his two ministers? Therefore, it is established that the kingship of the King of Madina, by divine grant, extends over all creation of the heavens and the earth.

### The Holy Quran

Among the miracles of the prophethood of the Greatest Messenger, the Holy Quran is also a very exalted miracle and a decisive sign of his truthfulness. Indeed, if it is called the "Greatest of Miracles," this would be the disclosure of a truth that cannot be hidden, because the other miracles of the Holy Prophet manifested in their own time and were witnessed by the people of his era, but the Holy Quran is his great miracle that will remain until the Day of Judgment.

Who does not know that Allah challenged the eloquent Arabs to produce something like the Quran, saying:

> قُل لَّئِنِ اجْتَمَعَتِ الْإِنسُ وَالْجِنُّ عَلَىٰ أَن يَأْتُوا بِمِثْلِ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنِ لَا يَأْتُونَ بِمِثْلِهِ وَلَوْ كَانَ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍ ظَهِيرًا

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): If all mankind and jinn gathered together to produce the like of this Quran, they could not produce its like, even if they helped one another." (Al-Isra: 88)

But no one was willing to accept this divine challenge. Then the Quran challenged:

> قُلْ فَأْتُوا بِعَشْرِ سُوَرٍ مِّثْلِهِ مُفْتَرَيَاتٍ

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): Then bring ten surahs like it, fabricated." (Hud: 13)

But despite extreme effort, even this could not be done. Then the Quran challenged:

> فَأْتُوا بِسُورَةٍ مِّن مِّثْلِهِ

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): Then bring one surah like it." (Al-Baqarah: 23)

Allahu Akbar! Witness the lofty and miraculous eloquence and rhetoric of the Great Quran — all the eloquent orators and rhetoricians of Arabia, whose eloquent poetry and oratorical brilliance had made their mark on the world, despite all their efforts, could not produce speech like even one surah of the Quran. The Quran even went so far as to say:

> فَلْيَأْتُوا بِحَدِيثٍ مِّثْلِهِ

**Translation:** "Then let them produce a discourse like it." (At-Tur: 34)

In short, four times the Holy Quran challenged, defied, and provoked the eloquent Arabs to produce something like it. But world history bears witness that despite 1400 long years having passed, no one has been able to accept this divine challenge or produce even one surah like the Quran. This is a proof brighter than the sun that the Holy Quran is an eternal miracle of the Seal of the Prophets that no one has been able to match, nor can anyone until the Day of Judgment.

### Knowledge of the Unseen (Ilme Ghaib)

Among the miracles of the Holy Prophet is his "Knowledge of the Unseen." The entire Ummah agrees that essential knowledge of the unseen belongs to none but Allah, but Allah grants knowledge of the unseen to His chosen ones — His prophets and messengers. This knowledge is called "Ataayi" (granted). The Holy Quran says:

> عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ فَلَا يُظْهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيْبِهِ أَحَدًا (26) إِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولٍ

**Translation:** "(Allah is) the Knower of the unseen, and He does not reveal His unseen to anyone, except to a messenger whom He has chosen." (Al-Jinn: 26-27)

Elsewhere the Quran says:

> وَمَا كَانَ اللَّهُ لِيُطْلِعَكُمْ عَلَى الْغَيْبِ وَلَٰكِنَّ اللَّهَ يَجْتَبِي مِن رُّسُلِهِ مَن يَشَاءُ

**Translation:** "It is not Allah's way to inform you of the unseen, but Allah chooses from His messengers whom He wills." (Aale Imran: 179)

Consequently, Allah granted countless unseen matters to His beloved Prophet. He gave thousands of reports of the unseen to his Ummah. Some are mentioned in the Holy Quran, while thousands more are recorded in hadith books and the volumes of Seerah and history.

### Prophecies of the Unseen

**The Romans Will Conquer:**

In 614 CE, a great war began between the kings of Rome and Persia. 26,000 Jews joined the Persian army and massacred 60,000 Christians. By 616 CE, the Persian king had triumphed, and the Roman army was completely defeated. The Roman king was a People of the Book (Christian), while the Persian king was a fire-worshipping Magian. Therefore, the Muslims were grieved by the Roman defeat, while the disbelievers rejoiced. The disbelievers taunted the Muslims: "You and the Christians are People of the Book, while we and the Persians are without a Book. Just as our brethren have triumphed over yours, so shall we one day triumph over you."

At that time, the Roman situation was so deplorable that they had lost every inch of their eastern possessions. The treasury was empty, the army was scattered, revolts were rising across the land, and the Roman emperor was utterly incompetent. No one could imagine that the Roman king could overcome the Persian king. But at such a time, the truthful Prophet announced through the Quran to the disbelievers of Makka:

> غُلِبَتِ الرُّومُ (2) فِي أَدْنَى الْأَرْضِ وَهُم مِّن بَعْدِ غَلَبِهِمْ سَيَغْلِبُونَ (3) فِي بِضْعِ سِنِينَ

**Translation:** "The Romans have been defeated in the nearest land, and they, after their defeat, will be victorious within a few years." (Ar-Rum: 2-4)

**Destruction of Quraish After Hijrah:**

The Holy Prophet had migrated with utmost destitution. The noble Companions had scattered — some to Abyssinia, some to Madina. Could anyone even imagine that this destitute and impoverished caravan of Muslims would one day emerge from Madina so powerful that it would crush the unconquerable military might of the Quraish? But the Beloved of the Knower of the Unseen, the Knower of the Unseen, had been announcing this news of the unseen a year before the Hijrah:

> وَإِن كَادُوا لَيَسْتَفِزُّونَكَ مِنَ الْأَرْضِ لِيُخْرِجُوكَ مِنْهَا وَإِذًا لَّا يَلْبَثُونَ خِلَافَكَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا

**Translation:** "They were about to drive you from the land to expel you from it, and then they would not remain after you except for a little while." (Al-Isra: 76)

This prophecy was fulfilled verbatim. Just one year later, at the Battle of Badr, the decisive victory of the Muslims eliminated the leaders of the Quraish, cutting the root of their military power.

**Muslims Will Become Kings One Day:**

After the Hijrah, the Quraish were beside themselves with vengeful fury. After their great defeat, the spirit of revenge drove them mad. They incited all Arab tribes to attack the Muslims. For 8 continuous years, bloody battles raged. The Muslims faced hardship, starvation, killing, and all kinds of morale-shattering calamities. They did not have a moment's peace. They spent nights awake in fear and terror, guarding the house of the Mercy for the Worlds.

Yet in the midst of this distress and disarray, the Sultan of Both Worlds announced through the Quran that the Muslims would be crowned with "Khilafat of the Earth" — kingship of religion and the world.

> وَعَدَ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَيَسْتَخْلِفَنَّهُمْ فِي الْأَرْضِ كَمَا اسْتَخْلَفَ الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِهِمْ

**Translation:** "Allah has promised those among you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will surely make them successors on the earth, as He made successors those before them." (An-Nur: 55)

Could anyone imagine that the oppressed and helpless group of Muslims, whom the Quraish had crushed with various torments, who had left everything and taken shelter in Madina, who had not even found peaceful sleep there — that such a day would come when they would attain such sovereignty that under Allah's heaven and on Allah's earth they would fear none but Allah? But the world witnessed this prophecy being fulfilled. The Muslims became kings and ruled the world so successfully that all the civilized governments of the world were scattered before them.

**The Prophecy of the Conquest of Makka:**

The Holy Prophet migrated from Makka in the darkness of night, with his companion in the cave, staying at Thawr. His enemies searched every corner of Makka for him. Could anyone imagine that the truthful Prophet who left his beloved homeland weeping would one day enter Makka as a conqueror, with victorious grandeur and majesty, waving the flag of his decisive victory? But the Prophet of the Unseen announced through the Quran:

> إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ (1) وَرَأَيْتَ النَّاسَ يَدْخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ اللَّهِ أَفْوَاجًا

**Translation:** "O Beloved, when the help of Allah and the victory (of Makka) comes, and you see people entering the religion of Allah in multitudes." (An-Nasr: 1-2)

This prophecy was fulfilled verbatim. In 8 AH, Makka was conquered, and the Prophet entered Makka with divine armies, with grandeur and majesty. He performed two rak'ats in the Kaaba, and the people of Arabia began entering Islam in multitudes.

**The Prophecy of Badr Victory:**

When there were only 313 Muslims, utterly helpless, weak, and destitute, could anyone imagine that against them an army of 1000 formidable fighters, fully armed with all military equipment, would be defeated, with 70 killed and 70 captured? But years before the Battle of Badr, verses were revealed in Makka, and the truthful Prophet announced the glad tidings of this decisive Islamic victory many years earlier:

> سَيُهْزَمُ الْجَمْعُ وَيُوَلُّونَ الدُّبُرَ

**Translation:** "The multitude will be defeated, and they will turn their backs." (Al-Qamar: 45)

> وَلَوْ قَاتَلَكُمُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لَوَلَّوُا الْأَدْبَارَ ثُمَّ لَا يَجِدُونَ وَلِيًّا وَلَا نَصِيرًا

**Translation:** "If those who disbelieve fight you, they will certainly turn their backs; then they will find neither protector nor helper." (Al-Fath: 22)
