### The Entry into Makka

The Chief of the Worlds entered Makka as a conqueror, mounted on his camel Qaswa, wearing a black turban. As the Prophet entered, he lowered his head in humility to Allah — so low that his blessed beard nearly touched the saddle. In that state of gratitude, he recited Surah Al-Fateh.

At the same time, he was surrounded by 10,000 mujahideen, and his sacred standard fluttered beside him. He had instructed that no one was to fight unless attacked.

The first area he entered was the neighbourhood of Bani Saad bin Laith. Some naïve youths of the Quraish were waiting in ambush, but his companions quickly subdued them. The Beloved's first words upon setting foot in the land of Makka were a decree of mercy:

**"Whoever lays down his arms is safe. Whoever closes his door is safe. Whoever enters the house of Abu Sufyan is safe. Whoever enters the Kaaba is safe."**

This was the proclamation of amnesty. The bitterest enemies, who had persecuted the Muslims for years, were granted safety. One of the Prophet's commanders, Hazrat Saad bin Obada, had been reciting verses of war as he entered. When the Prophet heard this, he immediately replaced him with Hazrat Ali, instructing Ali to proclaim the message of peace and amnesty instead.

### The Chief of the Worlds Enters Makka

When the Beloved entered Makka as a conqueror, he was mounted on his she-camel Qaswa, wearing a black turban. He was in such a state of humility before Allah that his blessed chin almost touched the saddle. He recited Surah Al-Fateh in gratitude.

He went straight to the Kaaba. The sacred House was filled with idols — 360 of them. With a stick in his hand, he struck each idol, reciting:

*"Truth has come and falsehood has vanished. Indeed, falsehood is ever bound to vanish."* (Al-Isra: 81)

As he pointed at each idol, it fell on its face. The Kaaba was cleansed of all traces of polytheism. Hazrat Usman bin Talha, the key-bearer, was called, and the Kaaba was opened.

Inside the Kaaba, the walls were covered with pictures. He ordered them all erased. After performing two rakats of prayer inside, he stood at the door and addressed the gathered crowd.

### The General Amnesty

All the oppressors and criminals stood before him — those who had laid thorns in his path, who had pelted him with stones, who had attacked him repeatedly, who had bloodied his blessed face and broken his blessed tooth at Uhud, who had tried to strangle him with a sheet, who had caused his daughter Zainab to miscarry by spearing her camel, who had tortured Hazrat Bilal, Hazrat Suhaib, Hazrat Ammar, Hazrat Khabbab, and other Companions on burning sands, who had killed his beloved uncle Hamza and mutilated his body, who had chewed his liver.

Now these criminals stood trembling, expecting execution. But the King of the Prophets looked at them with eyes of mercy and asked:

**"What do you think I will do with you today?"**

They replied: "You are a generous brother, the son of a generous brother." All eyes were fixed on the Prophet's face. Then, unexpectedly, the Conqueror of Makka declared:

**"There is no reproach upon you today. Go! You are all free!"** (`Zurqani`, Vol. 2, Page 328)

This completely unexpected declaration of amnesty brought tears of remorse to the criminals' eyes. The cry of "La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur Rasulullah" echoed from every corner of the Kaaba.

The disbelievers had usurped the properties, homes, and shops of the Muhajireen. Now was the time to restore their rights. But the Prophet ordered the Muhajireen to gift all their properties to the people of Makka willingly.

Allahu Akbar! History, tell us: Is there such a beautiful page in any conqueror's life? Has any conqueror ever treated his enemies with such kindness?

### The Second Sermon

The next day, the Beloved delivered another sermon, teaching the rulings and etiquette of the sacred sanctuary: that shedding blood, hunting animals, cutting trees (except Izkhir grass) are forbidden in the Haram. Allah granted His Prophet permission to fight in Makka for only one hour, then until the Day of Judgement, it is forbidden for anyone.

### The Ansar's Fear of Separation

When the Ansar saw the Beloved's generous treatment of the Quraish and his extended stay in Makka, they feared he might settle in his beloved hometown and leave them. When the Prophet learned of this, he said: "Ma'az-Allah! O Ansar! My life and my death are with you."

Hearing this, tears of joy flowed from the Ansar's eyes, and they said: "O Messenger of Allah! Whatever we thought or said was only because of our love for you. The thought of separation from you was unbearable for us." (`Zurqani`, Vol. 2, Page 333; `Seerate Ibne Hisham`, Vol. 2, Page 416)

### The Adhan on the Kaaba's Roof

When prayer time came, the Beloved ordered Hazrat Bilal to climb the Kaaba's roof and give the Adhan. When the faith-inspiring cry of "Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar" rose, the walls of the Haram and the doors of the Kaaba shone with the signs of faith. But some new Muslims — still half-hearted — felt a sting of tribal pride. Hazrat Itab bin Usaid said: "Allah saved my father's honour — he died before hearing this voice." Another Quraish chief muttered: "Life is now worthless."

But later, through the blessed company of the Prophet, the sun of faith shone in Itab's heart, and he became a sincere Muslim. The Beloved later appointed him governor of Makka. (`Seerate Ibne Hisham`, Vol. 2, Pages 413, 440)

### The Pledge of Islam

After this, the Beloved sat on an elevated place at the foot of Mount Safa, and people came eagerly to pledge allegiance to Islam on his blessed hand. After the men, it was the women's turn. For each woman who accepted the terms, he would say: "I have accepted your pledge." Hazrat Aisha says: "By Allah's oath! The Prophet's hand never touched any woman's hand during the pledge. He only accepted verbally." (Bukhari)

#### Hind binte Utba

Among these women, Hind binte Utba — the wife of Abu Sufyan and mother of Amir Muawiya — came veiled. This was the same Hind who, at Uhud, had ripped open Hazrat Hamza's stomach, chewed his liver, and strung his ears, nose, and eyes into a necklace.

The conversation between her and the Beloved:

The Beloved: "Do not associate anyone with Allah."
Hind: "You did not take this pledge from the men, but we accept it."

The Beloved: "Do not steal."
Hind: "I sometimes take from my husband (Abu Sufyan)'s wealth. I do not know if this is allowed."

The Beloved: "Do not kill your children."
Hind: "We raised our children, and when they grew up, you killed them at Badr. Now you and they know best."

Regardless, Hazrat Abu Sufyan and his wife Hind both became Muslims (may Allah be pleased with them). Initially, there may have been some weakness in their faith, but later they became sincere Muslims and died in the state of faith.

Hazrat Aisha relates that Hind binte Utba came to the court of prophethood and said: "O Messenger of Allah! There was no family on earth whose humiliation I desired more than yours. But now, there is no family on earth whose honour I desire more than yours." (Bukhari)

Similarly, when Abu Sufyan was sitting in the Masjid-ul-Haram and the Beloved passed by, he thought in his heart: "What power does he have that he overcomes us?" The Beloved perceived his hidden thought, came near, placed his hand on his chest, and said: "We overcome by Allah's power." Hearing this, Abu Sufyan said loudly: "I bear witness that you are indeed Allah's Messenger." (Zurqani)

### The End of Idol Worship

As recorded earlier, the Beloved had destroyed all idols and pictures in and around the Kaaba. But around Makka, there were other major idol centres — Laat, Manaat, Sawa'a, and Uzza — each worshipped by different tribes. The Beloved sent detachments of Companions to destroy all these idols, eliminating every trace of idol worship from Makka and its surroundings. (`Zurqani`, Vol. 2, Page 349)

Thus, the successor of Hazrat Ibrahim Khalilullah completed the mission of his great forefather. Indeed, the greatest purpose of the Conquest of Makka was to end shirk and idol worship and establish the supremacy of the Oneness of Allah. This great objective was fully achieved.

### Unforgivable Criminals

After the conquest, the Beloved declared a general amnesty. But a few criminals were sentenced to death unless they accepted Islam. Some of these were:

(1) **Abdul Uzza bin Khatal**: He had become Muslim, was sent to collect Zakat animals, quarrelled with a companion, killed him, apostatised, and fled to Makka. On the day of conquest, he was killed by Hazrat Saeed bin Harees and Abu Barza Aslami.

(2) **Huvairas bin Naqeed**: A poet who composed satires against the Prophet. Hazrat Ali killed him.

(3) **Muqees bin Sababa**: Killed by Nameela bin Abdullah.

(4) **Haris bin Talatala**: Also killed by Hazrat Ali.

(5) **Kuraiba**: Ibn Khatal's slave girl who sang satires against the Beloved. She was also executed.

#### Those Who Fled Makka

Four individuals fled Makka but later returned and were forgiven:

(1) **Ikrima bin Abu Jahl**: Fled to Yemen. His wife Umme Hakeem (who had accepted Islam) followed him, informed him of the Prophet's forgiveness, and brought him back. When the Beloved saw Ikrima approaching, he was so overjoyed that his cloak fell off in his haste to welcome him. Ikrima happily accepted Islam. (Muatta Imam Malik)

(2) **Safwan bin Umayya**: Fled to Jeddah. Hazrat Umair bin Wahab interceded, and the Beloved gave him his own turban as a sign of protection. Safwan did not accept Islam until after the Battle of Hunain, but eventually became Muslim. (Tabrani)

(3) **Ka'ab bin Zuhair**: Came to Madina in 9 AH, accepted Islam, and recited his famous poem "Banat Su'ad" in praise of the Beloved. The Beloved was so pleased that he gave him his blessed cloak. When Amir Muawiya offered 10,000 dirhams for this cloak, Ka'ab refused. After his death, Muawiya acquired it from his heirs for 20,000 dirhams, and it remained a sacred relic among Muslim rulers. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

(4) **Wahshi**: The same man who killed Hazrat Hamza at Uhud. He fled to Taif but later came to Madina with a delegation from Taif and accepted Islam. The Beloved listened to his account of his uncle's murder with deep sorrow but forgave him, saying: "Wahshi! Do not come before me." Wahshi was deeply grieved by this. However, during the caliphate of Abu Bakr, when Musailmatul Kazzab claimed prophethood, Wahshi joined the Muslim army and killed Musailmatul Kazzab with the same spear. He used to say: "In the days of ignorance, I killed the best of men (Hamza), and in Islam, I killed the worst of men (Musailmatul Kazzab)."

When Wahshi confessed his crimes and asked if Allah would forgive someone like him, the following verse was revealed:

*"Say (O Beloved): O My servants who have transgressed against themselves! Do not despair of Allah's mercy. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Truly, He is the Oft-Forgiving, the Most Merciful."* (Az-Zumar: 53)

### The Administration of Makka

The Beloved appointed Hazrat Itab bin Usaid as the governor of Makka, and Hazrat Ma'az bin Jabal was assigned to teach the new Muslims the laws and rulings of Islam. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

There is disagreement about how many days the Prophet stayed in Makka after the conquest. Abu Dawood says 17 days, Tirmizi says 18, and Imam Bukhari, from Hazrat Ibn Abbas, records 19 days. The reconciliation is that different narrations included or excluded the days of arrival and departure.

Similarly, there is disagreement about the exact date of the conquest — 13th, 16th, 17th, 18th, or 20th Ramadan. Muhammad bin Ishaq's narration of 20th Ramadan, 8 AH, is widely accepted. (Zurqani)

### The Battle of Hunain

Hunain is a place between Makka and Taif. In Islamic history, this battle is also called Ghazwa-e-Hawazin because it was fought against the Bani Hawazin tribe.

After the Conquest of Makka, most of the Arabian tribes entered the fold of Islam. Those who had already believed in the truth of Islam but had held back for fear of the Quraish now embraced it freely. The conquest of the Kaaba convinced every Arab that Islam was the truth.

However, two tribes — **Hawazin** and **Saqeef** — settled at Hunain reacted differently. Filled with tribal pride, they decided to attack the Muslims while they were gathered in Makka. The Beloved sent Hazrat Abdullah bin Abi Hadrad to investigate. He reported that these tribes had gathered all their forces under the command of Malik bin Auf, with the aged Duraid bin Al Summa (over 100 years old) accompanying as an advisor. They had brought their women, children, and even animals to the battlefield so that no soldier would think of fleeing.

In Shawwal, 8 AH, the Beloved assembled an army of 12,000 — 10,000 Muhajireen and Ansar who had come from Madina, and 2,000 new Muslims who had converted after the Conquest of Makka. As the army marched towards Hunain with such splendour, some Companions involuntarily remarked: "Today, who can overcome us?"

#### The Battle

But Allah did not approve of this pride in numbers. The Hawazin and Saqeef archers launched a devastating volley of arrows, and suddenly the 2,000 new Muslims and some Quraish converts turned and fled. Their flight caused even the Ansar and Muhajireen to waver.

The Chief of the Worlds looked around and saw that only a handful of devoted companions remained. Twelve thousand had fled, but the feet of the Prophet of Allah did not falter. Alone, he stood firm as a mountain on his white mule, advancing forward, reciting:

**"I am the Prophet — this is no lie. I am the son of Abdul Muttalib."**

He called out to the right: "O Ansar!" and they immediately responded: "We are present, O Messenger of Allah!" He called to the left: "O Muhajireen!" and they too responded. He then ordered Hazrat Abbas (who had a very loud voice) to call the Ansar and Muhajireen. Abbas shouted: "Ya Ma'asharal Ansar! Ya Lal Muhajireen!"

Instantly, the entire army turned back. Those whose horses could not turn due to the crush threw away their armour and ran on foot. They fell upon the disbelievers with such devotion that in a moment the battle turned. The disbelievers fled — some were killed, the rest captured. The tribe of Saqeef fought desperately, losing 70 of their bravest men. When their standard-bearer Usman bin Abdullah was killed, they too fled. A manifest victory was granted, and immense spoils were captured. (Bukhari)

This is the very event described in the Quran:

*"Indeed, Allah has given you victory in many fields, and on the day of Hunain, when your great numbers pleased you — but they availed you nothing, and the earth, despite its vastness, became straitened for you. Then you turned back in retreat. Then Allah sent down His tranquillity upon His Messenger and upon the believers, and sent down forces you could not see, and punished those who disbelieved. And that is the recompense of the disbelievers."* (At-Tawbah: 25-26)

### The Battle of Autaas

After their defeat at Hunain, some of the disbelievers gathered at Autaas, while others took shelter in the fort of Taif. To complete the victory, it was necessary to attack both.

The Beloved sent a force under Hazrat Abu Aamir Ash'ari towards Autaas. Duraid bin Al Summa came out with several thousand men. Duraid's son shot an arrow into Hazrat Abu Aamir's knee. Hazrat Abu Moosa Ash'ari (his nephew) rushed to him. When he asked who had shot him, his uncle gestured towards the man. Abu Moosa chased and killed him after a duel. He then extracted the poisoned arrow from his uncle's knee — but because it was poisoned, water began to flow instead of blood. Hazrat Abu Aamir made Abu Moosa the commander and said: "Convey my greetings to the Messenger of Allah and ask him to pray for me." He then passed away.

When Abu Moosa returned to the Prophet, he found him resting on a bed with marks of palm fibre on his blessed back and side. The Beloved performed ablution, raised his hands so high that Abu Moosa could see the whiteness of his armpits, and prayed: "O Allah! Raise Abu Aamir above many of Your servants on the Day of Judgement." Abu Moosa then asked for a prayer for himself, and the Beloved prayed: "O Allah! Forgive the sins of Abdullah bin Qais (Abu Moosa) and admit him to an honourable place on the Day of Judgement." (Bukhari)

Hazrat Abu Moosa killed Duraid's son. Duraid himself, seated in a howdaj due to extreme old age, was killed by Hazrat Rabiya bin Rafee'a with his own sword. After this, the disbelievers laid down their arms and all were captured.

Among the captives was Hazrat "Sheema" — the Beloved's foster-sister (daughter of Hazrat Halima Sa'diya). When captured, she said: "I am your Prophet's sister." When brought to the court of prophethood, the Beloved recognised her. His eyes filled with tears of love. He spread his blessed cloak on the ground, seated her on it, gave her some camels and goats, and said: "You are free. If you wish, stay with me, or if you prefer to go home, I will arrange your safe return." She chose to return to her tribe and was escorted with great honour. (Tabrani)

### The Siege of Taif

As mentioned, some of the defeated forces from Hunain had taken refuge in the fort of Taif. Taif was a well-fortified city with strong walls and a powerful citadel. Its chief was Urwah bin Mas'ood Saqafi (son-in-law of Abu Sufyan). The Saqeef tribe living there was considered equal to the Quraish in honour and nobility.

The disbelievers had stocked a year's provisions in the fort. The Muslim army besieged Taif, but the defenders rained down such heavy arrows that the army had to pull back. For 18 days, the siege continued, but Taif could not be conquered.

The Beloved consulted military experts. Hazrat Naufal bin Muawiya submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! The fox has gone into its den. If we keep trying, it may be caught, but if we leave it, there is no real danger." So the Beloved ordered the siege lifted. (Zurqani)

In this siege, 12 Companions were martyred (7 Quraish, 4 Ansar, 1 from Bani Lais). Among the wounded was Hazrat Abdullah bin Abu Bakr (son of Abu Bakr), who was struck by an arrow. He recovered but the wound reopened during Abu Bakr's caliphate, leading to his death.

During the siege, the Beloved had two tents pitched for Hazrat Umme Salama and Hazrat Zainab. He would pray between these two tents throughout the siege. After Saqeef accepted Islam, the people built a mosque at this spot. (Zurqani)

#### The Destruction of Idols near Taif

Before setting out for Taif, the Beloved sent Hazrat Tufail bin Amr Dausi with an army to destroy the temple of "Zul Kafain" — the idol of Umar bin Hamama Dausi made of wood. Hazrat Tufail burned the idol, reciting:

"O Zul Kafain! I am not your servant. My birth is greater than your birth. I have set fire to your heart."

He also brought a mangonel (catapult) from there — the first mangonel used in Islam — to break the fort of Taif. But the defenders threw heated iron rods, preventing its success.

Hazrat Ali was also sent to destroy the idol temples of Saqeef around Taif, which he did completely. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

When departing Taif, the Companions asked the Beloved to curse the Saqeef tribe. Instead, he prayed: "O Allah! Guide the Saqeef and bring them to me." His prayer was accepted — the entire tribe later accepted Islam.

### The Distribution of Spoils

After lifting the siege, the Beloved came to Zi'irrana, where the spoils had been gathered: 24,000 camels, over 40,000 goats, quantities of silver, and 6,000 captives. (Seerate Ibne Hisham)

The Beloved waited for the tribes' relatives to come for the captives, but when no one came, he ordered the distribution. He granted large gifts to the new Muslim chiefs of Makka and its surroundings — 300 camels to some, 200 to others, 100 to others.

Some young Ansari men remarked: "The Messenger of Allah is giving generously to the Quraish, but has no thought for us, although our swords are still dripping with blood." Others said among themselves: "When there is fierce fighting, we Ansar are called. But when the spoils are distributed, others receive them."

When the Beloved heard this, he gathered all the Ansar in a tent and addressed them: "O Ansar! Is it not true that you were misguided, and Allah guided you through me? You were poor, and Allah enriched you through me?"

To every statement, the Ansar replied: "Indeed, Allah and His Messenger have the greatest favour upon us."

The Beloved then said: "O Ansar! Do you not find it acceptable that others go home with wealth and camels, while you take the Prophet of Allah home with you? By Allah's oath! What you take home is far greater than all the wealth they carry."

Hearing this, the Ansar cried out: "O Messenger of Allah! We are satisfied! We only want the Messenger of Allah!" Many wept uncontrollably, their beards wet with tears. The Beloved explained that the Makkans were new Muslims whose hearts needed to be softened, while the Ansar's faith was firm. He then said: "If not for the Hijra, I would have been one of the Ansar. If all people go one way and the Ansar go another, I would go with the Ansar." (Bukhari)

#### The Release of Captives

After the distribution, Zuhair Abu Sard, chief of Bani Saad, came with some nobles and requested the release of the captives, giving an emotional speech: "O Muhammad! You drank the milk of our tribe's women. Among these captives are your foster-aunts and maternal aunts. If any Arab king had drunk our women's milk, we would have far greater expectations from him. We expect even more from you."

Deeply moved, the Beloved said: "I waited long for you, but you delayed. Whatever captives have come to my share and my family's share, I have freed them all. As for the general release, present your request at the time of congregation."

At Zuhr prayer, they presented their request before the congregation. The Beloved said: "I have authority only over my own share. But I recommend that all Muslims release their captives." All the Ansar, Muhajireen, and other mujahideen responded: "Our shares too are at your disposal. Release them all." Thus, 6,000 captives were freed in one stroke.

According to another Bukhari narration, the Beloved waited ten days for Hawazin's delegation. When they did not come, he distributed the spoils. When the delegation finally came and accepted Islam, they requested the return of their wealth and captives. The Beloved said: "I love the truth. I cannot return both. Choose either your wealth or your captives." They chose the captives.

The Beloved then addressed the army: "These are your brothers who have come in a state of repentance. I recommend that you release your captives willingly. Whoever does so, I guarantee compensation from the next spoils Allah grants me." Everyone agreed.

#### The Prophet's Knowledge of the Unseen

The Beloved asked about Malik bin Auf (the enemy commander). When told he was in Taif, he said: "Tell him that if he comes to me as a Muslim, I will return all his wealth and give him 100 camels besides." Malik bin Auf came to Madina, accepted Islam, and was given all this. He was so impressed by the Prophet's noble character that he composed a beautiful poem in his praise:

"I have neither seen nor heard of anyone among all mankind like Muhammad. He is the most faithful in fulfilling promises and the most generous in giving abundant wealth. And if you wish to ask him about tomorrow's news, he will tell you."

### Umrah of Zi'irrana

After this, the Beloved assumed Ihram from Zi'irrana, performed Umrah in Makka, and returned to Madina, entering it in Zulqada, 8 AH.

### Miscellaneous Events of 8 AH

#### (1) Birth and Death of Hazrat Ibrahim

This year, the Prophet's son Hazrat Ibrahim was born to Hazrat Maria Qibtiya. The Beloved loved him immensely. He passed away at about one and a half years of age.

On the same day, a solar eclipse occurred. The Arabs believed that eclipses happened at the death of great people. To dispel this superstition, the Beloved delivered a sermon saying: "The sun and moon do not eclipse due to anyone's death or life. Allah causes them to eclipse to instil fear in His servants." He then led the congregation in Salat-ul-Kusoof (eclipse prayer). (Bukhari)

#### (2) Death of Hazrat Zainab

The Beloved's daughter Zainab (wife of Abul Aas bin Rabee'a) passed away this year, leaving a son named Ali and a daughter named Umama. Hazrat Fatima later willed that Ali marry Umama after her death.

#### (3) Price Control

When grain prices rose sharply in Madina, the Companions requested the Prophet to fix prices. He refused, saying: "Allah is the One who fixes prices, who constricts and expands provision. It is He who provides sustenance." (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (4) The Pulpit (Mimbar)

According to some historians, the wooden pulpit was placed in the Prophet's Mosque this year. Previously, the Beloved used to lean against a pillar while delivering sermons. The pulpit was built and donated by an Ansari woman. When Amir Muawiya tried to move it to Syria as a blessed relic, darkness fell over the city, and stars appeared during the day. He apologised and instead added steps at the bottom so that no one after the Rashidun Caliphs would stand on the original steps.

#### (5) Delegation of Abdil Qais

This year, the delegation of the Abdil Qais tribe came to serve the Beloved. He welcomed them and prayed: "O Allah! Forgive Abdil Qais." When they arrived, they dismounted and ran to kiss the Prophet's blessed feet, and he did not forbid them. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)
