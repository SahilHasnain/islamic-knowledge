## Chapter 20: Miracles of Prophethood (Continued)

### More Miracles of Food Multiplication

**The Miraculous Ghee Container (Continued):**

Umme Malik would send a container of ghee (clarified butter) as a gift to the Prophet. Such extraordinary baraka appeared in it that whenever her sons asked for curry and none was available in the house, she would take ghee from that container. For a prolonged period, she continued this practice. One day, she squeezed the container completely dry. When she came to the Prophet, he asked: "Did you squeeze the container?" She replied: "Yes." He said: "If you had not squeezed it and left it as it was, ghee would have continued to come from it forever." (Muslim, Mishkat)

**The Blessed Bowl of Samura:**

Sayyidina Samura bin Jundub narrates: "We were with the Messenger of Allah in a gathering when he asked for food. Each person brought whatever little they had — some brought dates, some brought flour, some brought clarified butter. Soon there was a bowl of food large enough for a goat to sit on. But 1,400 people ate from that bowl until they were full, and also filled their travel bags. After eating, the Prophet asked for water. A companion brought a small vessel of water. The Prophet poured it into the bowl and placed his blessed hand in it — 1,400 people performed ablution from that water." (Muslim)

**The Blessed Liver:**

In a journey, 130 Companions were with the Prophet. He asked if anyone had food. A man brought some flour, which was kneaded. Then a large, robust goat was brought. The Prophet bought it, had it slaughtered and its liver roasted. All 130 men ate from that liver until they were full, with much remaining. The Prophet asked for the remaining meat to be distributed among those present. (Muslim)

**The Prophet Feeds Ashaab al-Suffa:**

Abu Huraira was once extremely hungry. He saw the Prophet and followed him. The Prophet understood his condition from his blessed insight. At home, a bowl of milk was present. The Prophet commanded Abu Huraira to call all the Ashaab al-Suffa (the poor Companions who lived in the mosque, about 80 people). Abu Huraira thought: "One small bowl of milk — and the Prophet wants to call 80 people?" But the Prophet kept commanding, and Abu Huraira kept calling them one by one. All of them came, drank until full, and left. Then the Prophet said: "Now you and I remain." He gave the bowl to Abu Huraira, who drank until full. The bowl remained as full as before. (Bukhari)

Allama Aala Hazrat (Rahmatullahi Ta'ala Alaih) referred to this miracle in his poetry:

> "How was that bowl of milk, O Abu Huraira,
> From which seventy Companions' mouths were filled?"

### Miracles of Supplication (Dua)

**Famine Upon the Quraish:**

When the disbelievers of Quraish crossed all bounds of persecution against the Prophet and his Companions, the Prophet prayed for famine upon them. Allah sent such a severe famine upon Makka that people were reduced to boiling bones and eating dry hides. Finally, Abu Sufyan came to the Prophet and begged for mercy, saying: "O Muhammad! You claim to be a mercy for the worlds — your people are dying of hunger." The Prophet prayed, and the famine lifted. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**The Turning of the Cloak:**

During a severe drought, a Bedouin stood up during a Friday sermon and said: "O Messenger of Allah! Our wealth is destroyed and our children are starving. Pray for us!" The Prophet raised his hands and prayed until rain poured for days. The next Friday, the same or another Bedouin said: "O Messenger of Allah! Our houses have collapsed and our livestock have drowned. Pray for us to stop the rain!" The Prophet said: "O Allah! Let it rain around us, not upon us." He gestured toward the clouds, and they parted, leaving Madina in sunshine. (Bukhari)

**Madina's Climate Improved:**

When the Muhajireen first arrived in Madina, they suffered from its climate and widespread epidemics. They would nostalgically recite poetry about Makka. Seeing this, the Prophet prayed: "O Allah! Make Madina as beloved to us as Makka, or even more. And make its climate healthy and wholesome for us." The climate of Madina improved, and it became one of the healthiest places. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**The Naval Jihad Prophecy:**

Once the Prophet was sleeping in the house of Umme Hiraam. He woke up smiling. When asked why, he said: "A group of my Ummah was presented before me, riding ships in the sea for Jihad, sitting on thrones like kings." Umme Hiraam said: "O Messenger of Allah! Pray that I am among them." He prayed. During the caliphate of Muawiya, when naval warfare began, Umme Hiraam joined the first Muslim navy. When they landed on dry land, she fell from her mount and attained martyrdom. (Bukhari)

**The Dead Child Revived:**

A man said: "I will not believe until you revive my dead daughter." The Prophet said: "Show me her grave." The man showed the grave. The Prophet called the girl by name — she came out of the grave, saying: "O Messenger of Allah! I am at your service!" The Prophet asked: "Do you wish to return to the world?" She said: "No, O Messenger of Allah! I have found Allah more merciful than my parents, and the Hereafter better than the world." (Zurqani)

**The Cooked Goat Revived:**

Jabir narrates: "I slaughtered a goat, cooked it, and prepared some bread without salt. I went to the Prophet and invited him. He took a few Companions and came. When the food was presented, the Prophet gathered the bones, passed his hand over them, and prayed. The goat was revived, stood up, and walked away." (Abu Nuaim, Ibn al-Munzir)

### Miracles in the World of Jinn

**A Jinn Encourages Islam:**

Sawad bin Qarib narrates: "A Jinn was under my control and would inform me of future events. I would share these predictions with people and collect fees. One day, the Jinn came and said: 'Wake up and come to your senses! Go to the chief of Banu Hashim, who has appeared as a Prophet from the lineage of Lu'ayy bin Ghalib. He invites people to the truth. Accept his invitation, for after him, no one else can intercede for you or benefit you.'" Sawad came to the Prophet and embraced Islam. (Behaqi)

**The Jinn's Message of Salam:**

Ibn Sa'd narrates from Jaad Qais Muradi: "We were four men traveling from our homeland for Hajj. In a forest of Yemen, we heard someone reciting poetry. We listened carefully. The poem said: 'O travelers! When you reach Zamzam and Hateem, convey our salam to Muhammad, whom Allah has sent as His Messenger. Tell him we are obedient to his religion, for Masih bin Maryam willed this to us.'" The travelers conveyed the message, and the Prophet confirmed the Jinn's words. (Tabqaat Ibn Sa'd)

**The Jinn Seeking Knowledge:**

Once a Companions caught a snake in the mosque. The Prophet said: "That was not a snake — it was a Jinn sent by a community of Jinn who had forgotten some verses. He was sent to ask me for those verses. I told him the verses, and he memorized them and left." (Mishkat)

### Miracles in the Elements

**Springs from the Blessed Fingers:**

The hadith collections show that water flowed from the Prophet's blessed fingers on approximately 13 occasions. One famous example: In 6 AH, the Prophet set out from Madina for Umrah. At Hudaibiya, they camped at a dry well. Due to the large number of people, the well's water was exhausted. The Prophet took an arrow from his quiver, placed it in the well, or dipped his hand into what little water remained — and water gushed forth like a spring. Everyone drank and performed ablution until satisfied. (Bukhari)

Allama Aala Hazrat wrote:

> "His fingers are overflowing with grace, the thirsty crowd forward sways,
> Rivers from the five fingers spring forth — what a wondrous sight!"

**The Earth Rejected the Dead Body of a Hypocrite:**

A Christian embraced Islam and began living in the Prophet's company. He had learned Surah Baqarah and Aale Imran and was a skilled calligrapher, so he was entrusted with writing revelation. But this ill-fated man apostatized and joined the disbelievers. He claimed: "The Prophet knows only what I wrote for him." Divine punishment caught him — he died, and the earth rejected his body. The disbelievers would bury him, and the earth would throw his body back out. This continued three times, fulfilling the Quranic warning. (Bukhari)

**The Miraculous Wind at Khandaq:**

When the Confederates (Ahzab) laid siege to Madina, the Muslims faced extreme hardship and terror. At that critical moment, the Prophet's miracle appeared: a fierce windstorm from the east with such force that it filled the disbelievers' eyes with dust and gravel, extinguished their cooking fires, overturned their pots, uprooted their tent pegs, tore their tents apart, and caused their horses to collide with each other. This wind was such a severe punishment upon them that their resolve collapsed and they retreated in defeat. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**The Fire of Hijaz Confirms the Prophecy:**

The Prophet had foretold: "A great fire will appear from the land of Hijaz." This appeared in 654 AH. Imam Qurtubi wrote: "That fire appeared near Madina, flowing like a wave, burning everything in its path as far as a village in Yemen, reducing it to ash. But toward Madina, cool, pleasant breezes like morning winds came from that fire. When the fire reached a stone whose half was inside the Haram of Madina and half outside, only the part outside the Haram was burned — the part inside the Haram remained untouched, and the fire was extinguished instantly." (Tarikhul Khulafa)

**The Tablecloth That Fire Would Not Burn:**

Anas's tablecloth — because the Prophet had wiped his blessed hands and face with it — would be thrown into a blazing oven, but the fire would not burn it; instead, it would emerge clean and pure. (Masnawi)

### An Important Note

These thousands of miracles of the King of Both Worlds, the Emperor of the Two Abodes, are but a few among countless wonders. The books of hadith and Seerah are replete with such accounts. We have presented these few miracles in simple language without any embellishment, so that seekers of truth may understand with absolute certainty that the Holy Prophet is the truthful Messenger of Allah. His miracles encompass all realms of existence.

### The Enemies' Futile Efforts

There have always been enemies of Islam who, due to their narrow-mindedness and spiritual blindness, have attempted to reduce the Prophet's station to that of an ordinary human being. They repeatedly declare: "In the Seerah of the Prophet of Allah, one should not mention those perfections that would elevate the Prophet of Islam above ordinary people" (May Allah forgive them). 

However, the consensus of Ahl al-Haqq is that Allah has gathered in the blessed person of the Holy Prophet all the miracles granted to all the prophets before him, and beyond that, has distinguished His final Prophet with countless miracles that are his exclusive distinctions (Khasais).

### The Special Characteristics (Khasais) of the Prophet

The scholars have enumerated many special distinctions of the Holy Prophet that were not granted to any other prophet:

1. The station of Praise (Maqam-e-Mahmood) — the greatest intercession
2. The Banner of Praise (Liwa al-Hamd)
3. The Fountain of Kauthar (Hawd al-Kauthar)
4. Leadership of all mankind on the Day of Judgment
5. The first to rise from the grave on the Day of Resurrection
6. The first to enter Paradise
7. His splitting of the chest (Shaqq al-Sadr)
8. The honor of Me'raj (Ascension) and the conveyance of Buraq
9. His Book is protected from alteration and corruption, and Allah has taken the responsibility of its preservation until the Day of Judgment
10. Ayatul Kursi was granted to him
11. The keys of the treasures of the earth were granted to him
12. He was blessed with the miracle of Jawami al-Kalam (comprehensive speech)
13. He was distinguished with the honor of universal prophethood (Risalat-e-Aamma)
14. The miracle of the splitting of the moon appeared in his confirmation
15. The spoils of war were made lawful for him
16. The entire earth was made a mosque and a means of purification (tayammum)
17. He is the most honored of all creation in the court of Allah
18. Munkar and Nakir will question the deceased regarding him in the grave
19. Marriage to his pure wives was prohibited after him
20. Every person offering prayer is obligated to send salam upon him in tashahud
21. If the Prophet calls someone while they are in prayer, they must respond immediately, and their prayer is not invalidated by doing so
22. Allah made him the arbiter of His Shariah — he could declare things permissible or impermissible as Allah willed
23. His pulpit and his grave — Allah has united the Prophet and his pulpit such that they will not separate

These are some of the special distinctions that Allah Almighty granted exclusively to His Beloved Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him.
