### The Incident of Masjid-e-Zirar

The hypocrites built a mosque opposite Masjid-e-Quba with the intention of causing harm, spreading disbelief, and dividing the Muslims. It became a hub of their conspiracies. A Christianized Ansari named Abu Aamir (whom the Prophet called Abu Aamir Fasiq) told them: "Keep preparing secretly for war. I will bring armies from Caesar to uproot Islam from this land."

When the Beloved was about to set out for Tabuk, a group of deceitful hypocrites came and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! We have built a mosque for the sick and disabled. Please pray in it once so it may be accepted." The Beloved replied: "I am leaving for Jihad now. I have no time." Despite their insistence, the Beloved did not set foot in it.

When he returned from Tabuk, many verses of Surah At-Tawbah were revealed exposing the hypocrites' conspiracies. Specifically about this mosque, the verses declared:

*"And those who took a mosque for causing harm, disbelief, and division among the believers, and as a post for those who warred against Allah and His Messenger before — they will surely swear: We intended nothing but good. And Allah testifies that they are liars. Never stand in it. A mosque founded on piety from the first day is more worthy that you stand in it."* (At-Tawbah: 107-108)

After these verses were revealed, the Beloved ordered Hazrat Malik bin Dakhsham and Hazrat Ma'an bin Adi: "Destroy this mosque and burn it down."

### Hajj with Hazrat Abu Bakr

After returning from Tabuk, in Zulqada, 9 AH, the Beloved sent a caravan of 300 Muslims from Madina to Makka for Hajj. He appointed:
- Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique as **Ameer-ul-Hajj** (commander of the pilgrimage)
- Hazrat Ali Murtaza as **Naqeeb-e-Islam**
- Hazrat Saad bin Abi Waqqas, Hazrat Jabir bin Abdullah, and Hazrat Abu Huraira as teachers
- 20 sacrificial camels were also sent

Hazrat Abu Bakr delivered sermons in the Haram, at Arafat, and at Mina. Then Hazrat Ali stood and recited 40 verses of Surah Bara'at (At-Tawbah), proclaiming:

- No polytheist may enter the Kaaba from now on.
- No one may perform Tawaf naked.
- After four months, all security for disbelievers and polytheists is revoked.

The Companions proclaimed this announcement loudly until they went hoarse. After this proclamation, disbelievers and polytheists began embracing Islam in droves. (Tabrani, Zurqani)

### Miscellaneous Events of 9 AH

(1) **Zakat**: This year, peace prevailed throughout the land, and the ruling of Zakat was revealed. Officials were appointed for its collection. (Zurqani)

(2) **Jizya**: For non-Muslim communities under Islamic rule, the ruling of Jizya was revealed in Surah At-Tawbah:29 — *"Until they pay Jizya with willing submission and feel themselves subdued."*

(3) **Prohibition of Interest**: The prohibition of Riba was revealed, and the Beloved strongly proclaimed it in his sermon during Hajjatul Wida the following year.

(4) **Death of the Negus**: The King of Abyssinia, Hazrat As'hama, who had sheltered the Muslim migrants, passed away. The Beloved offered his funeral prayer in absentia in Madina.

(5) **Death of Abdullah bin Ubayy**: The chief hypocrite died this year. His son, Hazrat Abdullah (a sincere Muslim), requested the Beloved to pray for him. The Beloved, showing kindness, gave his own shirt as a shroud and prayed the funeral prayer despite Hazrat Umar's objections. But after this, the following verse was revealed:

*"And never pray over any of them who dies, nor stand at his grave. Indeed, they disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger and died in defiance."* (At-Tawbah: 84)

After this verse, the Beloved never again prayed for any hypocrite.

### Wufoodul Arab — The Year of Delegations

The Beloved continuously sent preachers, officials, and mujahideen to all parts of Arabia for the propagation of Islam. Some tribes accepted Islam directly from these preachers, while others desired to come personally to the court of prophethood and declare their faith. They would come as representatives of their tribes, hear the message from the Prophet's own blessed tongue, accept Islam, and then return to convert their entire tribe.

These delegations came at various times, but after the Conquest of Makka, a great transformation occurred in Arabia. The truth of Islam became manifest. People who could not bear even to hear the name of Islam were now drawn to it like moths to the Prophet's light. Since most delegations arrived in 9 AH, this year is called **Sanatil Wufood** (the Year of Delegations).

#### The Beloved's Reception of Delegations

The Beloved took special care in welcoming delegations. He would dress in his finest attire, order his Companions to do the same, house the guests in the best accommodations, and receive them with a cheerful face. He would listen attentively to their needs and gift them according to their rank.

#### (1) The Delegation of Saqeef

When the Beloved returned from Taif after the Battle of Hunain, the great chief of Saqeef, **Urwah bin Mas'ood Saqafi**, came to the court and accepted Islam with full willingness. He was a noble and loyal man (mentioned earlier in the account of the Treaty of Hudaibiya).

After accepting Islam, he requested: "O Messenger of Allah! Permit me to go to my people and preach Islam." Given permission, he climbed onto the roof of his house in Taif and announced his conversion, inviting his tribe to Islam. Enraged, the people of Saqeef shot him with arrows until he was martyred.

But then they reconsidered: all Arab tribes had accepted Islam. How long could they fight? Fearing Muslim retaliation, they sent their chief **Abde Yaaleel bin Amr** with other nobles to Madina. They said: "We accept Islam on condition that our idol Laat is not destroyed for three years."

The Beloved flatly refused: "Islam cannot tolerate idol worship even for a moment. The idol will be destroyed. However, you need not break it yourselves — I will send Abu Sufyan and Mughira bin Sh'aba to do it."

They accepted Islam. The Beloved appointed **Hazrat Usman bin Al Aas** as their chief and sent Abu Sufyan and Mughira to Taif, who broke the idol Laat into pieces.

#### (2) The Delegation of Kanda (Kinda)

These 70-80 horsemen from Yemen arrived in Madina in great style — well-groomed, wearing silk-bordered gowns, fully armed. When they presented themselves, the Beloved asked: "Have you accepted Islam?" They replied: "Yes." He then asked: "Then why are you wearing silk?" They immediately tore the silk borders from their gowns. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (3) The Delegation of Bani Ash'ar

These nobles of Yemen entered Madina reciting verses of love in chorus:

"Tomorrow we will meet our Beloved — Muhammad and his Companions."

Hazrat Abu Huraira relates that he heard the Beloved say: "The people of Yemen have come. They are the softest of hearts. Faith is Yemeni, and wisdom is Yemeni. In the people who tend goats there is calm and dignity; in the camel-herders there is pride and arrogance."

By the blessing of this prophecy, the people of Yemen remained rich in knowledge, purity of heart, and divine wisdom.

#### (4) The Delegation of Bani Asad

Some individuals from this tribe came and accepted Islam gladly. Then, showing favour, they said: "O Messenger of Allah! We came from a very distant land in severe drought, hungry, and we accepted Islam without your army attacking us."

Allah revealed verses in response:

*"They regard it as a favour to you that they have accepted Islam. Say (O Beloved): Do not count your Islam as a favour to me. Rather, Allah has conferred favour upon you, that He has guided you to faith, if you are truthful."* (Al-Hujuraat: 17)

#### (5) The Delegation of Bani Fazaara

Led by Uyaina bin Hasan, 20 men came, declared Islam, and reported: "O Messenger of Allah! Our land is stricken by severe drought and famine. Please pray for rain." On Friday, the Beloved prayed on the pulpit. Immediately it rained — continuously for a week. The next Friday, an Arab said: "O Messenger of Allah! The cattle are dying, the children are wailing, and all roads are cut off. Pray that the rain falls on mountains and not on settlements." The Beloved prayed, the clouds dispersed, and after 8 days, the sun shone over Madina. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (6) The Delegation of Bani Murrah

Thirteen men came with their chief Haris bin Auf. All accepted Islam and complained of drought. The Beloved prayed: "O Allah! Quench them with rain." Then he ordered Hazrat Bilal to give each man 10 uqiya of silver and 400 dirhams, with 12 uqiya for the chief. When they returned home, they found it had rained in their towns at the same time the Beloved had prayed.

#### (7) The Delegation of Bani Al Buka'a

Hazrat Muawiya bin Saur bin Ubaad, aged 100, came with his son Basheer and others. They accepted Islam and requested the Beloved to pass his blessed hand over the boy's head. The Beloved did so, gave some goats, and prayed for blessings for them. Thereafter, whenever drought struck, their homes remained protected from famine.

#### (8) The Delegation of Bani Kinana

Led by Waasila bin Asqa'a, they accepted Islam with great devotion. When Waasila's father disowned him, he returned to Madina and joined the Companions of Suffa. After the Beloved's passing, he moved to Basra and later to Damascus, where he died in 85 AH.

#### (9) The Delegation of Bani Hilaal

Ziyaad bin Abdullah accepted Islam and entered the home of his aunt, Hazrat Maimuna (the Prophet's wife). When the Beloved entered and learned Ziyaad was his wife's nephew, he passed his blessed hand over his head and face. From that day, a light and blessing was always seen on Ziyaad's face. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (10) The Delegation of Zam'aam bin Sa'laba

Zam'aam, a handsome man with long hair, came from Bani Saad bin Bakr. He tied his camel in the mosque and asked: "Which of you is Muhammad?" The Companions pointed to the Beloved, who was reclining.

Zam'aam: "O son of Abdul Muttalib! I will ask you some questions with harshness. Do not be angry."

The Beloved: "Ask whatever you wish."

Zam'aam: "By the oath of that Allah who is your Lord and the Lord of all mankind, did Allah send you as a messenger to us?"

The Beloved: "Yes."

Zam'aam: "By Allah's oath, did Allah make prayer, fasting, Hajj, and Zakat obligatory?"

The Beloved: "Yes."

Zam'aam: "I believe in all you have said. I am Zam'aam bin Sa'laba. My tribe sent me to learn your religion and convey it to them."

He accepted Islam and returned home. He gathered his tribe and began denouncing their idols Laat, Uzza, Manaat, and Hubl. His tribe was shocked and warned: "Be quiet, or our gods will afflict you with leprosy and madness!"

Zam'aam replied: "O foolish people! What harm can these stone idols do? Allah has sent a Messenger and a Book to save you from this ignorance. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah and Muhammad is His Messenger."

He preached so passionately that by the end of the night, the entire tribe — men and women — had accepted Islam, destroyed their idols, built a mosque, and became steadfast in prayer. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (11) The Delegation of Balli

Hazrat Abu Ruwaifa'a introduced his tribesmen to the Beloved, who welcomed them warmly. An old man named Abuzzaif asked: "O Messenger of Allah! I love hospitality. Will I be rewarded for it?"

The Beloved: "After becoming Muslim, whoever you host — rich or poor — you will be rewarded."

Abuzzaif: "For how many days is the guest entitled to hospitality?"

The Beloved: "Three days. After that, whatever you feed him is charity." (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (12) The Delegation of Tujeeb

Thirteen men came, bringing the Zakat of their tribe's wealth and livestock. The Beloved welcomed them warmly but said: "Take this Zakat back to your land and distribute it among your own poor."

They replied: "O Messenger of Allah! We have already given so much to our poor that this surplus remains." So the Beloved accepted it, hosted them generously, and honoured them with gifts on departure.

He asked: "Is anyone from your tribe left behind who has not seen me?" They mentioned a young man guarding their homes. The Beloved said: "Send him to me."

When the young man arrived, he said: "O Messenger of Allah! I did not come for wealth. My only wish is that Allah forgive me, have mercy on me, and grant me contentment and independence of heart."

The Beloved was delighted and prayed: "O Allah! Forgive him, have mercy on him, and grant him contentment." He then appointed him chief and imam of his tribe. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (13) The Delegation of Muzaina

Hazrat No'man bin Muqarrin relates: 400 of our tribe came to serve the Beloved. When we were leaving, he told Hazrat Umar: "Give them gifts."

Hazrat Umar submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! I have very few dates at home. They may not be pleased with such a small gift."

The Beloved insisted. When Hazrat Umar took the 400 men home, he was astonished to find a huge mound of dates. He said: "Take as many as you wish." The last person to take was No'man himself, and the mound looked untouched.

(No'man was the standard-bearer of Muzaina on the day of the Conquest of Makka. Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas'ood used to say: "Some houses are of faith, some of hypocrisy. The house of Muqarrin is a house of faith.")

#### (14) The Delegation of Daus

Their leader, **Hazrat Tufail bin Amr Dausi**, had already accepted Islam before the Hijra. His story is remarkable. He was a wise poet who came to Makka on some business. The Quraish warned him: "Do not meet Muhammad! His speech has magic — whoever hears it loses his religion and family."

Falling for their deception, Tufail stuffed cotton in his ears. But one day in the Haram, he heard the Beloved reciting the Quran in Fajr prayer. The eloquence and beauty of the Quran entered his heart. When the Beloved returned home, Tufail followed, sat respectfully, narrated the whole story, and said: "By Allah's oath! I have never heard speech more eloquent. Tell me, what is Islam?"

The Beloved presented Islam to him, and Tufail immediately accepted it.

He then asked: "O Messenger of Allah! Grant me a sign or miracle so my people will believe me." The Beloved prayed: "O Allah! Grant him a special light." A light appeared between his eyes like a lamp. Tufail asked that it be transferred to his head, and his head then shone like a lantern.

He returned to his tribe. Though his parents and wife accepted Islam, the tribe resisted. He returned to the Beloved, who told him: "Go back, be gentle, and keep inviting them." He continued until 70-80 households accepted Islam. He brought them all to Khaibar to present to the Beloved, who joyfully gave them a share in the spoils of Khaibar. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (15) The Delegation of Bani Abas

They submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! Our preachers told us that Islam is not accepted without Hijra. Should we sell everything and migrate to Madina?"

The Beloved said: "Hijra is not obligatory for you. What is important is that wherever you live, you fear Allah and live a life of piety and abstinence." (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

#### (16) The Delegation of Daaram (Lakhm)

Ten men came, led by Hani bin Habeeb, bringing gifts of horses, a silk gown, and a waterskin of wine. The Beloved accepted the horses and gown but rejected the wine, saying: "Allah has forbidden it." When Hani asked permission to sell it, the Beloved said: "The One who forbade drinking it also forbade buying and selling it. Pour it out on the ground."

The Beloved gave the silk gown to his uncle Hazrat Abbas, who asked: "What shall I do with it since silk is forbidden for men?" The Beloved said: "Remove the gold and make jewellery for your women, and sell the silk for its value." Abbas sold the gown for 8,000 dirhams.

#### (17) The Delegation of Ghaamad

Ten men came in 10 AH. They had left a boy to guard their belongings. When the boy fell asleep, a thief stole a bag. While the delegation was in the Prophet's court, he suddenly said: "A thief stole your bag, but your young man has recovered it."

They returned to find the boy saying: "A thief stole the bag. I chased him, and he dropped it into a hole. I dug it out and brought it back." They exclaimed: "Surely, he is the true Prophet!" All accepted Islam.

#### (18) The Delegation of Najran

Sixty Christian horsemen from Najran came — 24 nobles and 30 others who held the religious and political leadership of their community. Their leaders were Abdul Maseeh (Aaqib), Aiham (Sayyid), and Abu Haarisa bin Alqama.

They debated extensively with the Beloved, especially about Eisa. They refused to accept that Eisa was born without a father. The verse of Mubaahala was revealed:

*"Indeed, the example of Eisa to Allah is like that of Adam. He created him from dust; then He said to him: Be — and he was."* (Aale Imran: 59)

And the call for mutual imprecation:

*"Then whoever argues with you (O Beloved) about him after this knowledge has come to you, say: Come, let us call our sons and your sons, our women and your women, ourselves and yourselves, then supplicate earnestly and invoke the curse of Allah upon the liars."* (Aale Imran: 61)

The Beloved brought Hasan, Husain, Ali, and Fatima for the Mubaahala. When the Christians saw this, they refused the contest and agreed to pay Jizya instead.
