## Chapter 17: The Prophet's Noble Features and Attributes (Shamayil Wa Khasayil)

### His Blessed Appearance

Just as Allah Almighty distinguished the Prophet ﷺ with perfection of character above all the earlier and later peoples, He also created him unmatched and unparalleled in beauty of form. What can we understand of the unique glory of the Holy Prophet ﷺ? The noble Companions (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhum), who witnessed the manifestations of prophetic beauty day and night, in travel and at home, depicted the excellence and perfection of the Beloved of Allah's ﷺ unparalleled beauty in such a manner that one can only say what a poet of the Prophet beautifully expressed:

*"Allah has not created the likeness of Muhammad,*
*And I know that He never will."*

(Hayatul Haiwan Damiri, Vol. 1, p. 42)

The Companion of the Prophet and his court poet, Sayyidina Hassan bin Thabit (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), described the unique glory of the prophetic beauty in his Hamziya ode:

*"My eyes have never seen anyone more beautiful than you,*
*Nor has any woman given birth to anyone more perfect than you."*

*"You are created free from every flaw and defect,*
*As if you were created exactly as you yourself wished to be."*

Allama Busairi (Rahimahullah) said in his Qasida-e-Burda:

*"His beauties have no partner in their excellence,*
*The beauty within him is indivisible."*

A'la Hazrat Maulana Ahmad Raza Khan Bareilwi (may his secret be sanctified) also painted this theme in exquisite verse:

*"Your character, Allah called 'Great';*
*Your form, Allah created 'Beautiful'.*
*None has been, nor will be, like you,*
*I swear by the Creator of beauty and grace!"*

The entire Ummah believes that in the proportion of limbs and beauty of form, the Prophet of the End Times ﷺ is unmatched and unparalleled. The scholars of hadith and Seerah have described, with authentic narrations, the proportion and beauty of each of his blessed limbs. In this brief book, to create a vision of beauty, we present some verses from the versified "Hulya-e-Mubaraka" of Maulana Muhammad Kamil Sahib, Charagh Rabbani Nomani Walidpuri (may Allah have mercy on him), so that this book may be honored by his blessings.

### The Blessed Hulya (Description)

*How can I write of the being of the Truth?*
*How can I write of the description of the Light of Allah?*
*But the beauty of the Mercy to the Worlds*
*Will shine forth in the darkness of the grave.*
*Therefore the thought has come to me*
*To briefly describe this matchless beauty,*
*So that my friends may recognize him,*
*And his remembrance may become easy.*

*He was of medium stature, with a pure body,*
*His complexion was white tinged with red.*
*His limbs were like pieces of the moon,*
*Beautiful, as if cast in a round mold.*
*His forehead was bright and wide,*
*The moon has spots — his was spotless.*
*His two eyebrows were like two crescent moons meeting,*
*Both were joined together.*
*Was it the joining of two full moons of Eid?*
*Or was it the nearness of the two bows (Qausain)?*
*His eyes were large, beautiful, and naturally kohl-rimmed,*
*Seeing them, even the houris would sacrifice themselves.*
*Both ears were beautiful and noble,*
*With beauty came high perceptiveness.*
*His face was like a clear mirror,*
*Everyone saw their own reflection in it.*
*Up to the chest, the hair of the Beloved of Allah,*
*Was beautiful, thick, and black in color.*
*The clothing of his pure body was mostly white —*
*An izaar, a jubba, or a shirt.*
*His turban was often green,*
*But sometimes black or white and plain.*
*I describe his distinguished mark:*
*There is no one like him in both worlds.*

### His Blessed Complexion

Sayyidina Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) relates that the Prophet's ﷺ complexion was fair and white. It seemed as though his blessed body was fashioned from silver. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)

Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet's ﷺ body was extremely soft and delicate. I have never seen silk or brocade softer or more delicate than his body, nor have I ever smelled any fragrance better than the fragrance of his blessed body." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 502)

Sayyidina Ka'ab bin Malik (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) said: "When the Prophet ﷺ was happy, his blessed face would shine so brightly that it seemed like a piece of the moon, and we would recognize his joy and happiness from this state." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 503)

Drops of sweat on his blessed face would glisten like pearls and had a fragrance better than musk and ambergris. Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) relates that his mother, Sayyida Umme Sulaim (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha), would spread a leather bedspread for the Prophet ﷺ to take his afternoon nap. She would collect the sweat from his blessed body in a bottle and mix it into her perfume. Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) instructed that after his death, the same fragrance containing the Prophet's sweat be applied to his body and shroud. (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 929)

### His Blessed Body Cast No Shadow

The Prophet's ﷺ blessed body cast no shadow. Hakim Tirmizi (d. 255 AH) narrated in his book "Nawadirul Usool" from Zaqwan Tabi'i (may Allah have mercy on him) that the Prophet's shadow did not fall in sunlight or moonlight. Imam Ibn Saba' (may Allah have mercy on him) states that this was among the Prophet's special characteristics — his shadow did not fall on the ground because he was light (nur). When he walked in sunlight or moonlight, his shadow was not visible. Some say the hadith in which the Prophet prayed "O Allah! Make all my limbs light" and concluded with "O Allah! Make me entirely light" supports this. When he was entirely light, where could his shadow fall?

Abdullah bin Mubarak and Ibnul Jauzi (may Allah have mercy on them) also narrated from Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) that the Prophet ﷺ had no shadow. (Zurqani, Vol. 5, p. 249)

### Protected from Flies, Mosquitoes, and Lice

Imam Fakhruddin Razi (may Allah have mercy on him) and Allama Hijazi (may Allah have mercy on him) relate that not only his body but even his clothes were never touched by flies, his clothes never had lice, and no bedbug or mosquito ever bit him. Aburrabi'a Sulaiman bin Saba' (may Allah have mercy on him) explained in his book "Shifaus Sudoor Fi A'lamu Nubuwwatir Rasool" that one reason for this was that the Prophet ﷺ was light (nur). Flies and lice are attracted by filth and foul odor, and since the Prophet was pure from all types of impurities and his body was fragrant, he was protected from these things. Imam Sabati (may Allah have mercy on him) also wrote about this in detail in "A'zamul Mawaarid". (Zurqani, Vol. 5, p. 249)

### The Seal of Prophethood

Between the Prophet's ﷺ shoulder blades was the Seal of Prophethood, about the size of a pigeon's egg. It was a raised piece of flesh inclined toward redness. Sayyidina Jabir bin Samurah (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "I saw the Seal of Prophethood between the Prophet's shoulders, which was like a pigeon's egg — a red, raised lump of flesh." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 3; Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 205)

In one narration, the Seal of Prophethood was the size of a pigeon's egg and bore the inscription: "There is no god but Allah; He has no partner" and "Wherever you may be, help will come to you." Another narration says the Seal of Prophethood was a shining light. The narrators likened its external shape and size to a pigeon's egg. (Hashiya Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 205)

### His Blessed Height

Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet ﷺ was neither very tall nor very short, but of medium height. His blessed body was extremely beautiful. When he walked, he would walk with a slight forward lean." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 1)

Similarly, Sayyidina Ali (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet ﷺ was neither tall nor short, but of medium stature. When walking, it seemed as though he was descending from a height. I have never seen anyone like him, neither before nor after." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 1)

The noble Companions agree that he was of medium height, but it was a miraculous quality that amidst thousands of people, his blessed head would appear the tallest.

### His Blessed Head

Sayyidina Ali (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), describing the Prophet's features, said: "Zakhmurras" — meaning his blessed head was large (a sign of grandeur and dignity). (Shamayil Tirmizi)

### His Blessed Hair

The Prophet's ﷺ hair was neither curly nor completely straight, but in between. His blessed hair first reached to his earlobes, then beautiful locks hung to his shoulders. However, during the Farewell Pilgrimage, he had his hair shaved. A'la Hazrat Maulana Shah Ahmad Raza Khan Bareilwi described the three states of his blessed hair in two exquisite verses:

*"Until the ears, they heard the cry; now to the shoulders they extend,*
*Becoming a support for the wandering homeless ones."*
*"At the final Hajj, they were distributed among the Ummah in wanderings,*
*The locks set forth to intercede for the ill-fated ones."*

The Prophet ﷺ would often apply oil to his hair and sometimes comb it. In his later years, he would part his hair in the middle. His blessed hair remained black until the end of his life; no more than twenty white hairs appeared on his head and blessed beard. (Shamayil Tirmizi, pp. 4-5)

When the Prophet ﷺ had his blessed hair shaved during the Farewell Pilgrimage, it was distributed among the noble Companions (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhum) as blessed relics. The Companions preserved these blessed hairs with immense devotion, cherishing them more than their own lives.

Sayyida Umme Salmah (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) kept these blessed hairs in a bottle. When someone was afflicted by the evil eye or fell ill, she would dip the bottle in water and give it to them, and healing would be attained. (Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 875)

### His Blessed Face

The Prophet's ﷺ blessed face was the mirror of Divine beauty and the manifestation of the lights of Divine effulgence. It was extremely handsome, full-fleshed, and somewhat rounded. Sayyidina Jabir bin Samurah (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "Once I saw the Prophet ﷺ on a moonlit night. I looked at the moon and then at his blessed face, and I saw that his face was more beautiful than the moon."

Someone asked Sayyidina Bara bin Aazib (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu): "Was the Prophet's face (in brilliance) like a sword?" He replied: "No, rather the Prophet's face was like the moon."

Sayyidina Ali (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), describing the Prophet's features, said: "Whoever saw him suddenly would be awed by his majesty, but whoever came to know him would love him."

Sayyidina Bara bin Aazib (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was more handsome than all people and had the best character." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 502)

Sayyidina Abdullah bin Salam (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) said of his blessed face: "When I carefully observed the Prophet's ﷺ blessed face, I recognized that his face was not the face of a liar." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 168)

An Arab poet beautifully described the beauty of the Prophet's blessed face:

*"He is a Prophet in beauty as well —*
*Everything about him is a miracle of beauty,*
*But especially his face is a great sign (a magnificent miracle).*
*Bilal would call the Adhan at Subh-e-Sadiq,*
*Seeing the radiance of his bright forehead."*

### His Blessed Eyebrows

The Prophet's ﷺ eyebrows were long, fine, and thick with hair. Both eyebrows were so joined that from a distance they appeared connected. Between the two eyebrows was a vein that would swell when he was angry.

### His Blessed Eyes

The Prophet's ﷺ eyes were large and naturally kohl-rimmed. His eyelashes were thick and long. The black of his pupils was intensely black and the white of his eyes was intensely white, with fine reddish lines in them. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2; Dalayilun Nubuwwah, p. 54)

A miracle of the Prophet's ﷺ eyes was that he could see simultaneously in front and behind, left and right, above and below, day and night, in darkness and light, all equally. (Zurqani ala al-Mawahib, Vol. 5, p. 246; Khasayisul Kubra, Vol. 1, p. 61)

Narrations in Bukhari and Muslim state: "O people! Perform your bowing and prostration properly, for by Allah! I can see you even from behind me." The author of Mirqat commented that this is among the miracles granted to the Prophet ﷺ.

Furthermore, the Prophet's sight was not limited to physical objects; he would also see things beyond perception and sensation that are not ordinarily visible. Bukhari records: "By Allah! Your bowing and humility are not hidden from my sight." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 59)

Subhan Allah! What can one say of the miracle of the beloved Mustafa's ﷺ luminous eyes? He could see the bowing — indeed, even the humility — of the worshippers from behind his back. "Khushoo" (humility) is a state of the heart involving fear and submission — something not visible to the eye. Yet behold the miracle of the prophetic vision: he saw with his eyes even that which is not ordinarily visible!

### His Blessed Nose

The Prophet's ﷺ nose was beautiful, long, and high, with a light shining upon it. One who did not look carefully would think his nose was very high, but in reality his nose was not very high; the impression of height was due to the light that radiated from his blessed nose. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2, etc.)

### His Blessed Forehead

Sayyidina Hind Abi Haala (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), describing the Prophet's face, says: "His blessed forehead was wide and spacious." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)

There was a natural luminous radiance on his forehead. The poet of the court of prophethood, Sayyidina Hassan bin Thabit (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), seeing this beautiful radiant scene, said:

*"When his blessed forehead appears in the dark night,*
*It shines like a bright lamp in the darkness of the night."*

### His Blessed Ears

Like his eyes, his ears also possessed miraculous qualities. The Prophet ﷺ himself said: "I see what you do not see and I hear what you do not hear." (Khasayisul Kubra, Vol. 1, p. 67)

This hadith proves that the Prophet's power of hearing and sight was unique and miraculous. He would hear sounds from far and near equally well. His ally from the tribe of Khuzaa, as you have read in the account of the Conquest of Makka, called out to him for help from a distance of three days' journey, and the Prophet heard his cry. Allama Zurqani commented on this hadith: "If the Prophet heard a caller's cry from three days' distance, it is not far-fetched, for he would hear the rustling of the heavens while seated on the earth, indeed, he would even hear the sound of the moon falling in prostration beneath the Throne." (Khasayisul Kubra, Vol. 1, p. 53; Hashiya al-Daultuk Makkiya, p. 180)

### His Blessed Mouth

Sayyidina Hind bin Abi Haala (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet's cheeks were soft, delicate, and smooth. His mouth was wide, and his teeth were well-spaced and bright. When he spoke, light emanated from between his front teeth. When he smiled in darkness, the radiance of his blessed teeth would light up the surroundings." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2; Khasayisul Kubra, Vol. 1, p. 74)

He never yawned — this is a characteristic of all prophets, as yawning comes from Satan, and the prophets are protected and infallible from Satan's influence. (Zurqani, Vol. 5, p. 248)

### His Blessed Tongue

The Prophet's ﷺ tongue was the interpreter of Divine revelation, the fountainhead of verses, and the treasury of miracles. Its eloquence and rhetoric reached the heights of inimitability; the greatest masters of eloquence and oratory were astounded upon hearing his speech.

*"Before you, the great masters of Arab eloquence are subdued —*
*Some say there is no tongue in the mouth, rather, no life in the body!"*

The miracle of his blessed tongue was that whatever he uttered would instantly become a miracle manifest in the world of existence.

### His Blessed Saliva

The Prophet's ﷺ saliva was a cure for the wounded and the ill, and an antidote for poison. As you will read in the account of miracles, when a snake bit Sayyidina Abu Bakr Siddique (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) in the Cave of Thaur...

### His Blessed Voice

It is among the characteristics of the noble prophets that they are handsome and melodious of voice. But the Chief of the Messengers ﷺ was more handsome and had a sweeter, more melodious, and more pleasant voice than all other prophets. Along with his beautiful voice, he spoke with such clarity that those near and far could all hear his sacred words clearly from their places during sermons.

### His Blessed Neck

Sayyidina Hind bin Abi Haala (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) described: "The Prophet's ﷺ neck was extremely well-proportioned, like a flask-shaped (beautiful), and of medium thickness. It was incomparably beautiful, clear and transparent like silver." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)

### His Blessed Hands

The Prophet's ﷺ palms were full-fleshed, his wrists were long, his arms were extended and well-fleshed. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)

Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "I never found any silk or brocade softer than the Prophet's ﷺ palm, nor any fragrance better than his fragrance." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 502)

Whoever shook hands with the Prophet ﷺ would find their hands fragrant for the entire day. Whichever child's head he passed his blessed hand over would be distinguished from all other children by its fragrance. Sayyidina Jabir bin Samurah (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) relates: "I offered Zuhr prayer with the Prophet ﷺ, and then he headed toward his home, and I accompanied him. Seeing him, small children ran toward him, and he passed his blessed hand over each child's cheek. When I came before him, he also placed his blessed hand on my cheek. I felt the coolness of his blessed hand on my cheek and such fragrance emanated as if he had taken his hand from a perfumer's box." (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 256)

What miracles and extraordinary acts manifested from this blessed hand will be read in the section on miracles.

### His Blessed Stomach and Chest

The Prophet's ﷺ stomach and chest were both even and level. His chest was not higher than his stomach, nor his stomach higher than his chest. His chest was broad, and from the upper part of his chest to his navel ran a thin line of hair. His blessed chest and entire stomach were free of hair. However, there was some hair on his shoulders and forearms. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)

His stomach was a world of patience and contentment, and his chest was the vessel of the lights of Divine knowledge, the treasury of Divine revelation.

### His Blessed Feet

The Prophet's ﷺ feet were broad and full-fleshed, his heels had little flesh, and the arch of his foot was high, not touching the ground. His shins were somewhat slender, clear, and transparent. Such was the softness and delicacy of his feet that water would not stay on them at all. (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2; Madarijun Nubuwwah)

He would place his blessed foot on the ground with great dignity and humility. Sayyidina Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "I have never seen anyone walk faster than the Prophet ﷺ — it was as if the earth was folded up for him. We would try to keep pace with him and would struggle, but he would walk with great dignity and ease, yet still remain ahead of us all." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 2)
