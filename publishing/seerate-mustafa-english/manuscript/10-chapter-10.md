## Chapter 10: The Fifth Year of Hijra — 5 AH

After the Battle of Uhud, news of the Muslims' heavy losses spread. Due to the joint conspiracies of the Quraish disbelievers and the Jews, the morale of all the disbelieving tribes rose so high that everyone was possessed with the urge to attack Madina. Therefore, the fifth year of Hijra also witnessed many battles between disbelief and Islam. Here we mention some famous expeditions.

### Ghazwa-e-Zaaturriqa

First, the tribes of Anmaar and Saalba intended to attack Madina. The Beloved ﷺ took 400 noble Companions and set out from Madina on 10th Muharram, 5 AH, reaching a place called Zaaturriqa. But upon hearing of his arrival, these disbelievers fled and hid in the mountains, so no battle occurred. Some polytheist women were found, whom the Companions captured.

At that time, the Muslims were in a state of extreme poverty and hardship. Hazrat Abu Moosa Ash'ari narrates: "There were so few riding animals that six people shared one camel, taking turns riding it. Walking on the rocky ground had wounded our feet, and our toenails had fallen off. So we wrapped cloth rags around our feet. This is why this Ghazwa is called Ghazwa-e-Zaaturriqa (the Ghazwa of Patches)."

Some historians say that the stones there were white and black in colour, and the ground appeared as if white and black patches were sewn together. Others say there was a tree called Zaaturriqa at that place. All these reasons may be true. The famous scholar of Seerah, Ibn Saad, says that in this Ghazwa, the Beloved ﷺ first offered Salatul Khauf (the prayer of fear).

### Ghazwa-e-Doomtul Jandal

In Rabiul Awwal, 5 AH, news came that a very large army was gathering at a fort called Doomtul Jandal, located between Madina and Damascus, to attack Madina. The Beloved ﷺ set out with 1,000 Companions to confront them. When the polytheists learned of this, they fled, leaving behind their cattle and shepherds. The Companions took all those animals as spoils of war. The Beloved stayed there for three days, dispatching Companions to various locations. No battle occurred in this Ghazwa either. The Beloved remained outside Madina for a total of some days.

### Ghazwa-e-Muraisi (Ghazwa-e-Bani Al Mustaliq)

This Ghazwa is also known as Ghazwa-e-Bani Al Mustaliq. Muraisi is a place eight stages from Madina. A branch of the Khuza'a tribe, Banu Al Mustaliq, lived here, and their chief was Haris bin Zarar. He too had gathered an army to attack Madina. When news reached Madina, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ set out with the army on 2nd Shaban, 5 AH, leaving Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa as his deputy in Madina. In this Ghazwa, Hazrat Aisha and Hazrat Umm Salama also accompanied him.

When Haris bin Zarar learned of the Beloved's arrival, such terror seized him that he and his army scattered and fled. However, the residents of Muraisi itself faced the Muslim army and showered arrows on the Muslims. But when the Muslims attacked together, ten disbelievers were killed, one Muslim was honoured with martyrdom, and the rest — more than 700 — were captured. Two thousand camels and five thousand goats came into the Companions' hands as spoils of war.

Ghazwa-e-Muraisi is not particularly significant as a battle, but some very important events occurred during it, making it a significant and glorious chapter in prophetic history. Some of these famous events are as follows.

#### The Mischief of the Hypocrites

Many hypocrites also participated in this battle, lured by the spoils of war. One day, a Muhajir and an Ansari argued over water. The Muhajir cried out loudly, "O Muhajireen! Help!" and the Ansari cried, "O Ansar! Help!" Hearing these cries, the Ansar and Muhajireen came running, and the matter escalated to the point of near-fighting.

Abdullah bin Ubayy, the chief of the hypocrites, saw a golden opportunity for mischief. To inflame the situation, he said to the Ansar: "Look! This is what you have done — you fattened your dog so that it would eat you. You Ansar yourselves have encouraged these Muhajireen. Now stop all financial support and help for them. These are lowly people, and we Ansar are honourable. When we return to Madina, the honourable will surely drive out the lowly from Madina."

When the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ heard the noise of this commotion, he said to the Ansar and Muhajireen: "Are you raising the slogans of the Jahiliyyah?" As soon as the Prophet's blessed face appeared, the Ansar and Muhajireen cooled down like ice. The Mercy to the Worlds' ﷺ few words flowed such a river of love that the Ansar and Muhajireen blended like milk and sugar.

When Hazrat Umar heard of Abdullah bin Ubayy's ugly words, he became so enraged that he came with a drawn sword and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! Permit me to cut off this hypocrite's head." The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ said with extreme gentleness: "O Umar! Beware, do not do this. Otherwise, the disbelievers will spread the news that Muhammad has started killing his own companions." Hearing this, Hazrat Umar fell completely silent.

But the news spread throughout the army. Strangely, as great an enemy of Islam and its founder as Abdullah bin Ubayy was, his son was an even greater true lover of Islam and a devoted Companion of the Beloved ﷺ. His name was also Abdullah. When he learned of his father's mischief, he came to the court of prophethood burning with rage and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! If you desire my father's execution, then I wish to cut off my father's head with my own sword and place it at your feet." The Beloved said: "No, never. I will never treat your father badly."

Another narration says that at the valley of Aqeeq near Madina, the son stopped his father Abdullah bin Ubayy and said: "You have insulted the Muhajireen and the Messenger of Allah. By Allah's oath! I will not let you enter Madina until the Messenger of Allah ﷺ permits and until you say with your own tongue that the Prophet ﷺ is the most honourable of all the children of Adam, and you are the most lowly of all the people of the world."

Everyone watched this scene with extreme amazement. When the Beloved ﷺ arrived and saw the son blocking the father's way and Abdullah bin Ubayy shouting loudly, "I am the most lowly, and the Chief of the Worlds is the most honourable," the Beloved ﷺ ordered: "Let him go so that he may enter Madina."

#### The Marriage to Hazrat Juwairiya

Among the disbelievers captured in Ghazwa-e-Muraisi was the daughter of the tribal chief Haris bin Zarar, Hazrat Juwairiya. When all the prisoners were distributed as slaves and maids among the Muslim mujahideen, Hazrat Juwairiya fell to the share of Hazrat Sabit bin Qais. He told her that if she paid a certain amount, he would free her. Having no money, Hazrat Juwairiya came to the court of the Beloved ﷺ and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! I am the daughter of the chief of my tribe, Haris bin Zarar, and I have become a Muslim. Hazrat Sabit bin Qais has promised to free me in exchange for a certain amount. Please help me pay this amount so I may be freed."

The Beloved ﷺ said: "If I offer you something better than this, will you accept?" She asked what it was. The Beloved said: "I wish to pay the entire amount on your behalf, free you, and then marry you, so that your family's honour and dignity may remain intact." Hazrat Juwairiya happily agreed. The Beloved ﷺ paid the entire amount from his own pocket and married Hazrat Juwairiya.

When news spread through the army that the Beloved ﷺ had married Hazrat Juwairiya, all the mujahideen immediately freed all the maids and slaves from that tribe. Every soldier of the Islamic army said: "The tribe into which the Messenger of Allah has married — no person from that tribe can remain a slave."

Hazrat Aisha said: "I have never seen a marriage more blessed than that of Hazrat Juwairiya. It became the means of freeing an entire tribe, Banu Al Mustaliq, from slavery." (Abu Dawood)

Hazrat Juwairiya's original name was Barrah. The Beloved ﷺ changed it to Juwairiya.

#### The Incident of Ifk (The Slander)

When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was returning from this Ghazwa, he camped at a stage one night. Hazrat Aisha travelled in a covered litter (howdaj). Some specific men were assigned to load and unload this howdaj onto the camel. Hazrat Aisha had gone out of the camp shortly before departure to relieve herself. When she returned, she found that her necklace had broken and fallen somewhere. She went back to search for it. This time, she was delayed somewhat, and the army moved on.

The men loading the howdaj assumed that the Mother of the Believers was inside, so they loaded it onto the camel and the entire caravan departed. When Hazrat Aisha returned to the campsite, there was no one there. Terrified of being alone and finding it dangerous to walk alone in the dark night, she lay down, thinking, "When they do not find me at the next camp, they will surely come back searching for me." She fell asleep.

A Companion named Hazrat Safwan bin Muattal always followed behind the army to pick up any fallen belongings. When he reached this campsite, he saw Hazrat Aisha. Since the verse of hijab had not yet been revealed, he had seen the Mother of the Believers before and recognized her. Thinking she was dead, he recited "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'oon." Hearing this, she woke up. Hazrat Safwan bin Muattal Sulmi immediately seated her on his camel and, holding the camel's reins, walked on foot until he reached the Beloved ﷺ at the next camp.

The chief of the hypocrites, Abdullah bin Ubayy, turned this incident into an opportunity to slander Hazrat Aisha. He widely publicized this accusation, creating such an uproar in Madina that the filthy slander spread everywhere. Some Muslims — like Hazrat Hassan bin Sabit, Hazrat Mistah bin Asasa, and Hazrat Hamna bint Jahash — also contributed to spreading this slander.

The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ was deeply grieved and pained by this inflammatory accusation. Sincere Muslims were also extremely distressed. Hazrat Aisha fell severely ill as soon as she reached Madina. Being a veiled lady, she took to her bed and had no knowledge whatsoever of this fabricated slander.

Although the Beloved had complete knowledge and certainty of Hazrat Aisha's purity and chastity, since it concerned his own wife, he did not consider it appropriate to announce his wife's innocence from his own tongue. He began to wait for divine revelation. Meanwhile, he consulted his sincere Companions on this matter to ascertain their views. (Bukhari)

When the Beloved discussed this slander with Hazrat Umar, he submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! This hypocrite is certainly lying. Allah Ta'ala does not allow even a fly — which sits on filth — to sit on your pure body. How then could the Holy and Pure Lord ever allow a woman guilty of such evil to remain in your marriage?"

Hazrat Usman Ghani said: "O Messenger of Allah! Allah Ta'ala did not allow even your shadow to fall on the ground lest someone's foot step on it. Would the All-Pure Lord ever allow anyone to commit such indecency with your honoured wife?"

Hazrat Ali suggested: "O Messenger of Allah! Once, filth got on your sandals, and Allah sent Hazrat Jibreel to inform you to remove your blessed sandals. If Hazrat Aisha were guilty, Allah Ta'ala would surely have revealed to you to separate her."

When Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari heard of this slander, he asked his wife: "O wife! Tell me truthfully — if I were in Safwan's place, could you ever imagine that I could do such a thing with the Prophet's pure wife?" His wife replied: "If I were the Prophet's wife instead of Aisha, by Allah's oath! I could never commit such treachery. So how could Hazrat Aisha, who is thousands of times better than me, and Hazrat Safwan bin Muattal, who is far better than you, ever do such a thing?"

A narration in Bukhari says that when the Beloved ﷺ consulted Hazrat Ali and Hazrat Usama on this matter, Hazrat Usama immediately said: "O Messenger of Allah! She is your wife, and we know nothing but good of her." Hazrat Ali replied: "O Messenger of Allah! Allah Ta'ala has not placed any restriction upon you. There are many women besides her. You may ask her maid, Hazrat Bareera. She will tell you the truth."

When the Beloved asked Hazrat Bareera, she submitted: "By the oath of that Being Who has sent you as the true Messenger! I have never seen any fault in Hazrat Aisha. However, she is young — she sometimes leaves the kneaded dough and falls asleep, and the goat comes and eats it."

Then the Beloved ﷺ asked his respected wife, Hazrat Zainab bint Jahash, who was equal to Hazrat Aisha in beauty. She swore and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! I protect my ears and my eyes. By Allah's oath! I know only good of Hazrat Aisha."

After this, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ stood on the pulpit one day and said to the Muslims: "Who will excuse me from or help me against the person who has caused me grief by slandering my wife? By Allah's oath! I know only good of my wife in every way. And those people (the hypocrites) have mentioned a man — Safwan bin Muattal — of whom I know only good."

From this speech of the Beloved on the pulpit, it became clear that he had complete knowledge and certainty of the innocence, purity, and chastity of both Hazrat Aisha and Hazrat Safwan. Even before revelation came, the Beloved knew with certainty that the hypocrites were liars and the Mother of the Believers was chaste. Otherwise, he would never have sworn on the pulpit and publicly declared the goodness of both before a congregation. The reason he did not make a public announcement earlier was that he did not consider it appropriate to declare his wife's purity from his own tongue. When the hypocrites exceeded all limits in their uproar, the Beloved ﷺ expressed his sacred opinion from the pulpit. But even now, he awaited divine revelation for a public declaration.

It has been written earlier that upon returning from the journey, the Mother of the Believers, Hazrat Aisha, had fallen ill and taken to her bed, so she was completely unaware of this storm of slander. When she recovered somewhat and went out to the desert one night with a Companion for relieving herself, she heard this heart-wrenching, soul-tearing news from her. She was dealt a severe blow and became weak from intense grief and sorrow. Her illness worsened, and she wept bitterly day and night.

Finally, unable to bear this deadly shock, she obtained permission from the Beloved ﷺ and went to her mother's house. There she mentioned this cursed news to her mother. Her mother comforted her considerably, but she kept weeping continuously.

In this state, the Beloved ﷺ suddenly arrived and said: "O Aisha! Such news has been spread about you. If you are chaste and this news is false, then soon the Lord of the Worlds will announce your innocence through revelation. Otherwise, repent and seek forgiveness, for when a person repents and seeks forgiveness from Allah, He forgives his sins."

Hearing the Beloved's words, Hazrat Aisha's tears stopped completely. She asked her father, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, to answer the Prophet. He said: "I do not know what to say to the Prophet." Then she asked her mother to answer. Her mother said the same. Then Hazrat Aisha herself gave this reply to the Beloved:

"What people have said is an unfounded rumour that has settled in people's hearts, and some have come to believe it. If I say I am chaste, people will not believe me. If I confess to this evil, everyone will accept it, although Allah knows I am innocent and pure of this accusation. My example at this moment is like that of the father of Hazrat Yusuf (Hazrat Yaqoob). So I say what he said: *'So patience is most fitting. And Allah is the one sought for help against that which you describe.'*"

Saying this, she turned away and said: "Allah knows I am innocent of this accusation, and I am certain that Allah will surely manifest my innocence."

No sooner had Hazrat Aisha given this reply than the Beloved ﷺ had not even risen from his place, and everyone was still seated, when suddenly revelation began to descend upon the Beloved ﷺ. The restlessness of revelation began. Although it was intensely cold, drops of sweat like pearls began to drip from the Beloved's body.

When the revelation concluded, the Beloved ﷺ smiled and said: "O Aisha! Thank Allah and praise Him, for He has announced your innocence and purity." The Beloved ﷺ recited ten verses from Surah An-Noor. After these verses were revealed, the faces of the hypocrites were blackened, and the sun of the Mother of the Believers' chastity shone with such full brilliance that it illuminated the hearts of Muslims until the Day of Judgement.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique was extremely angry with Hazrat Mistah bin Asasa. Mistah was his cousin. His father had died in childhood, and Hazrat Abu Bakr had raised him. Due to his poverty, Hazrat Abu Bakr always supported him financially. Despite this, Mistah bin Asasa had contributed to fabricating and spreading this slander. In anger, Hazrat Abu Bakr swore he would never help Mistah again. On this occasion, Allah Ta'ala revealed this verse:

*"Let not those among you who are blessed with grace and abundance swear not to give to their relatives, the needy, and those who emigrate in the way of Allah. Let them pardon and forgive. Do you not love that Allah should forgive you? And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful."* (An-Noor: 22)

Hearing this verse, Hazrat Abu Bakr broke his oath and resumed supporting Mistah bin Asasa as before.

Then the Beloved ﷺ delivered a sermon in Masjid Nabawi and recited the verses of Surah An-Noor to the congregation. Four people who spread the slander — Hazrat Hassan bin Sabit, Hazrat Mistah bin Asasa, Hazrat Hamna bint Jahash, and the chief of the hypocrites Abdullah bin Ubayy — were each given 80 lashes as the punishment for slander (hadd-e-qazf).

The commentator of Bukhari, Allama Kirmani, says: "Hazrat Aisha's innocence and purity are definitive and established by the Quran. If anyone doubts this even slightly, he is a disbeliever." All other jurists of the Ummah hold the same position.

#### The Revelation of the Verse of Tayammum

Ibn Abdul Bar, Ibn Saad, Ibn Habban, and other muhadditheen and scholars of seerah say that the verse of Tayammum was revealed during this very Ghazwa-e-Muraisi. However, in Rauzatul Ahbaab, it is written that the verse of Tayammum was revealed in a different Ghazwa.

The context of revelation mentioned in Bukhari is this: Hazrat Aisha says, "We were with the Beloved ﷺ on a journey. When we reached Baida or Zaatul Jaish, my necklace broke and fell somewhere. The Beloved ﷺ and some people stopped to search for it, and there was no water there. Some people came to Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and complained, 'Do you not see what Aisha has done? She has made the Prophet and the Companions stay here, and there is no water!' Hearing this, Hazrat Abu Bakr came to me and said whatever Allah willed (harsh and soft words). He even poked me in the side with his hand in anger. At that time, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was resting with his blessed head on my thigh, so I could not move despite being hit.

"In the morning, when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ woke up, there was still no water. Suddenly, the verse of Tayammum was revealed. So the Beloved ﷺ and all the Companions performed Tayammum and offered the Fajr prayer. On this occasion, Hazrat Usaid bin Huzair happily said: 'O family of Abu Bakr! This is not your first blessing.' Then we made the camel stand up, and we found the necklace under it."

This hadith does not name any specific Ghazwa. However, the commentator of Bukhari, Allama Ibn Hajar, says this incident occurred during Ghazwa-e-Bani Al Mustaliq, also known as Ghazwa-e-Muraisi, during which the incident of Ifk took place. In this Ghazwa, the Beloved ﷺ remained outside Madina for 28 days.

### The Battle of Khandaq (Ahzaab)

Among all the battles of 5 AH, this battle is the most famous and decisive. A trench was dug, so it is called the Battle of Khandaq (Trench). And since all the Arab disbelievers united to fight this battle against Islam, it is also called the Battle of Ahzaab (the Confederates). The Holy Quran mentions this battle by this very name.

#### The Cause of the Battle of Khandaq

In the previous pages, we have written that when the Jews of Banu Nazeer were expelled from Madina, some of their chiefs settled in Khaibar. The Jews of Khaibar honoured and respected them so much that they accepted Salam bin Mishkam, Ibn Abdul Huqaiq, Huyay bin Akhtab, and Kinana bin Arrabi as their leaders.

Since these people were filled with rage against the Muslims and the fire of revenge was burning in their hearts, they devised a scheme for a powerful attack on Madina. For this purpose, these three went to Makka and met the Quraish disbelievers, saying: "If you support us, we can wipe the Muslims off the face of existence." The Quraish disbelievers, already eager for this, immediately agreed with the Jews.

After securing the cooperation of the Quraish, these three Jews then approached the tribe of Banu Ghatfaan. By tempting them with half of Khaibar's income, they persuaded them to fight against the Muslims. Then Banu Ghatfaan persuaded their ally, Banu Asad, to join the war. Meanwhile, the Jews made their ally, the tribe of Banu As'ad, their partner. The Quraish, based on kinship, brought the tribe of Banu Sulaim into the alliance. In short, all the disbelieving tribes of Arabia joined together to prepare a formidable army of ten thousand, with Abu Sufyan as the commander-in-chief of this entire army.

#### The Muslim Preparation

When news of this unification and terrifying attack by all the disbelieving Arab tribes reached Madina, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ gathered his Companions and consulted how to face this attack. Hazrat Salman Farsi gave the opinion: "To go out of the city and face such a large army in open battle, as in Uhud, would be against wisdom. It is more appropriate to stay within the city and defend against this attack. We should dig a trench around the city on the side from which the disbelievers' attack is expected, so that the entire enemy force cannot attack at once."

Since three sides of Madina had narrow alleys of houses and clusters of date-palm trees, attack from those three sides was impossible. Only one side of Madina was open. So it was decided that five deep trenches would be dug on this side.

Accordingly, on 8th Zulqada, 5 AH, the Beloved ﷺ set out with 3,000 noble Companions and began digging the trench. The Beloved himself marked out the trench with his blessed hand and divided the work, assigning 10 yards to every 10 men. The trench was completed in about 20 days.

Hazrat Anas narrates: "The Beloved came to the trench and saw the Ansar and Muhajireen digging with great enthusiasm in the winter morning despite hunger. He was deeply moved and began reciting this Rajaz:

*'O Allah! There is no life but the life of the Hereafter. So forgive the Ansar and the Muhajireen.'*

In response, the Ansar and Muhajireen joined their voices and began reciting:

*"We are those who have pledged allegiance to Muhammad for jihad, as long as we live, forever."*

Hazrat Baraa bin Aazib says: "The Beloved himself also dug the trench and threw out the earth, until dust settled on his blessed stomach. While throwing the earth, he would recite these verses of Rajaz to encourage the Companions:

*'By Allah! If it were not for Allah's grace, we would not have found guidance, nor would we have given charity nor prayed. So O Allah! Send down tranquillity upon us and keep us steadfast in battle. Indeed, these (disbelievers) have oppressed us. Whenever they intended fitna, we refused.'*

The Beloved would repeatedly emphasize the word 'Abayna' (we refused) in a loud voice."

#### The Miracle of the Rock

Hazrat Jabir narrates: "While digging the trench, suddenly a rock appeared that no one could break. When we reported this to the court of prophethood, the Beloved rose. He had not eaten for three days, and a stone was tied to his blessed stomach due to hunger. He struck the rock with his pickaxe, and it crumbled like a mound of sand."

Another narration says that he struck the rock three times with the pickaxe. With each strike, a light emerged from it, and in that light, he saw the cities of Syria, Iran, and Yemen. He gave glad tidings to the Companions of the conquest of these three lands. The narration in Nasa'i says that he announced the conquests of the palaces of Caesar, the palaces of Kisra, and the palaces of Abyssinia.

#### The Feast of Hazrat Jabir

Hazrat Jabir says: "Seeing the blessed stomach with stones tied due to hunger, my heart was moved. I obtained permission from the Beloved and went home. I asked my wife if there was any food. She said there was only one sa'a (measure) of barley. I told her to grind it quickly and knead the dough. I also hurriedly slaughtered a baby goat we had raised at home and made meat pieces.

"My wife said, 'Hurry and prepare the meat and bread. I will call the Beloved.' As I was leaving, my wife said, 'Make sure to bring only the Beloved and a few Companions. The food is little — do not disgrace me.'

"I went to the trench and secretly submitted: 'O Messenger of Allah! I have prepared bread from one sa'a of barley and the meat of a baby goat. Please come with just a few people.' Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ announced: 'O trench-diggers! Jabir has invited for food. So all of you go to his house and eat.' Then he said to me: 'Do not cook the bread until I come.'

"The Beloved arrived, put his blessed saliva into the kneaded dough, and prayed for blessings. He also put his blessed saliva into the pot of meat. Then he ordered the bread to be cooked and said, 'Do not take the pot off the stove.' As the bread was baked, Jabir's wife kept taking out meat from the pot. One thousand people ate to their full, yet the dough remained as much as before, and the pot continued boiling on the stove."

#### The Blessed Dates

Similarly, a young girl came with some dates in her hand. The Beloved ﷺ asked what it was. She replied, "Some dates that my mother sent for my father's breakfast." The Beloved took the dates in his blessed hand, spread them on a cloth, and called all the people of the trench, saying: "Eat to your fill." All the people of the trench ate their fill of those dates.

Both of these incidents are among the miracles of the Beloved ﷺ.

#### The Battle Formation

After the trench was completed, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ gathered the women and children in a secure fort in Madina. Appointing Hazrat Ibn Umm Maktum as his deputy in Madina, he set out with 3,000 Ansar and Muhajireen and camped at the foot of Mount Sala. Sala was behind him and the trench before him. The flag of the Muhajireen was given to Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa, and the standard-bearer of the Ansar was made Hazrat Saad bin Obada.

#### The Enemy Attack

The Quraish and their allies attacked the Muslims with an army of ten thousand. From three sides, the disbelievers' army descended upon Madina with such force that a storm of dust and darkness rose in the city's atmosphere. Listen to the description of this terrifying attack and the battle array of the enemy army from the Quran:

*"When they came upon you from above you and from below you, and when eyes shifted and hearts reached the throats, and you assumed about Allah various assumptions. There the believers were tried and shaken with a severe shaking."* (Al-Ahzab: 10)

The hypocrites who stood shoulder to shoulder with the Muslims, upon seeing the disbelievers' army, turned cowardly and slipped away. At that moment, the veil of their hypocrisy was torn. They began asking permission to go home, as Allah says in the Quran:

*"And a group among them asked permission of the Prophet, saying, 'Indeed our houses are exposed,' while they were not exposed. They only wanted to flee."* (Al-Ahzab: 13)

#### The Believers' Steadfastness

But when the true lovers of Islam — the Muhajireen and Ansar — saw the stormy onslaught of the army of disbelievers, they stood firm with their chests bared. The mountains of Sala and Uhud raised their heads and watched the determination of these mujahideen with amazement. See the picture of these self-sacrificing Companions' faith-based bravery inscribed on the pages of the Quran:

*"And when the believers saw the confederates, they said, 'This is what Allah and His Messenger had promised us, and Allah and His Messenger spoke the truth.' And it increased them only in faith and submission."* (Al-Ahzab: 22)

#### The Treachery of Banu Quraiza

The Jews of Banu Quraiza had been neutral until now. But the Jews of Banu Nazeer tried to bring them into the confederate army. Huyay bin Akhtab, on Abu Sufyan's advice, went to Kaab bin Asad, the chief of Banu Quraiza. At first, Kaab would not open his door, saying, "We are allies of Muhammad. We have always found him faithful to his treaties, so we consider it against good conduct to break our treaty with him."

But the Jews of Banu Nazeer insisted so strongly and tempted him in so many ways that finally Kaab bin Asad agreed to break the treaty. When Banu Quraiza broke the treaty and joined the disbelievers, the Quraish and Abu Sufyan were overjoyed.

When news of this reached the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ, he sent Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz and Hazrat Saad bin Obada to investigate the situation at Banu Quraiza. They went and confirmed that Banu Quraiza had indeed broken the treaty. When these two respected Companions reminded Banu Quraiza of their treaty, those evil Jews shamelessly said: "We do not know who Muhammad is or what a treaty is. We never had any treaty."

Hearing this, both gentlemen returned and informed the Beloved ﷺ of the situation. The Beloved exclaimed "Allahu Akbar" in a loud voice and said: "O Muslims! Do not be afraid or grieve over this. It contains glad tidings for you."

When the army of disbelievers advanced, they stopped at the sight of the trench and laid siege to the city of Madina. The siege continued for about a month with such severity that the Beloved and his Companions went hungry for many days.

#### The Ansar's Firm Resolve

Seeing the Muslims' distress from the siege, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ considered whether the Muhajireen and Ansar might lose heart. He intended to make a treaty with Uyaina bin Hasan, the chief of Ghatfaan, on the condition that he would leave the Quraish and take one-third of Madina's produce. But when the Beloved ﷺ shared this thought with Hazrat Saad bin Obada, both submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! If this is a revelation from Allah, we have no right to object. But if it is your opinion, then — O Messenger of Allah! — when we were in a state of disbelief, these tribes could not take a single date from us without a fight. Now that Allah has honoured us with Islam and the grace of your servitude, how can we give our wealth to these disbelievers? We will give them not piles of dates but the gift of spears and swords, until Allah decides between us and them."

Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ was pleased and completely reassured.

#### Amr bin Abd Wudd is Killed

Hand-to-hand fighting was not possible due to the trench, and the disbelievers were perplexed about how to cross it. But every day, arrows and stones were exchanged from both sides. Finally, one day, Amr bin Abd Wudd, Ikrima bin Abu Jahl, Habeera bin Abi Wahb, Zaraar bin Al Khattab, and some other disbelieving warriors said to Banu Kinana: "Come, today show us a real fight. Let us see who the real horsemen are." They came to the trench and, finding a narrow spot, leaped their horses across.

Amr bin Abd Wudd was at the forefront. Although he was a frail old man of 90, he was considered equal to a thousand horsemen in bravery. He was wounded in the Battle of Badr and had fled. He had sworn he would not put oil in his hair until he took revenge on the Muslims. He advanced and began loudly challenging: "Who will come to face me?" He said this three times. Each time, Hazrat Ali, the Lion of Allah, rose and said, "I will." The Beloved ﷺ stopped him: "Ali, this is Amr bin Abd Wudd." Hazrat Ali replied: "Yes, I know he is Amr bin Abd Wudd. But I will fight him."

Hearing this, the Chief of Prophethood handed his special sword, Zulfiqar, to Haidar-e-Karrar with his blessed hand. He tied the turban on his head with his blessed hands and prayed: "O Allah! Help Ali!"

Hazrat Asadullahil Ghalib Ali bin Abi Talib stood before him with the dignity of a true warrior, and this dialogue began:

**Hazrat Ali:** "O Amr bin Abd Wudd! Become a Muslim!"

**Amr:** "That is impossible! Never, never!"

**Hazrat Ali:** "Then turn back from the fight!"

**Amr:** "I do not accept this either!"

**Hazrat Ali:** "Then fight me!"

**Amr** (laughing): "I never imagined that there was anyone in the world who would challenge me."

**Hazrat Ali:** "But I want to fight you."

**Amr:** "What is your name?"

**Hazrat Ali:** "Ali bin Abi Talib."

**Amr:** "O nephew! You are very young. I do not wish to shed your blood."

**Hazrat Ali:** "But I very much wish to shed your blood."

Hearing these hot words, Amr's blood boiled. The Lion of Allah was on foot, and Amr was on horseback. On being shamed, he dismounted, cut his horse's legs with his sword, and advanced with a naked sword. He struck a powerful blow at the Lion of Allah. Hazrat Ali blocked it with his shield. The blow was so hard that, cutting through the shield and the turban, it struck his forehead. Though the wound was not deep, it remained as a lifelong mark on his forehead.

The Lion of Allah, Hazrat Ali, trembled and roared: "O Amr! Beware! Now it is my turn." Saying this, Hazrat Ali struck such a well-aimed blow with Zulfiqar that the sword cut through the enemy's shoulder and reached his waist. He writhed and fell to the ground, dying instantly. The battlefield cried out through its tongue of circumstance:

*"The king of men, the lion of Yazdan, the strength of the Almighty — Zulfiqar, the sword of Ali, the victory of Ali!"*

Hazrat Ali killed him and turned to leave. Hazrat Umar said: "O Ali! Why did you not take Amr bin Abd Wudd's armour? There is no better armour in all of Arabia." Hazrat Ali replied: "O Umar! The blow of Zulfiqar made him writhe and fall so that his private parts were exposed. Due to modesty, I turned my face away."

#### Naufal Killed

After this, Naufal, burning with rage, came to the battlefield and called out: "Who will come to face me?" Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam swooped on him like lightning and struck such a blow that he was cut in two. The sword cut through the saddle and reached the horse's back. People said: "O Zubair! Your sword is unmatched." He replied: "What is a sword? One needs strength in the wrist and skill in the strike."

Habeera and Zaraar also advanced with great arrogance, but when they saw the blow of Zulfiqar, they trembled and fled. The remaining horsemen who had crossed the trench also ran away. Ikrima, the son of Abu Jahl, was so desperate that he threw his spear and fled, only finding composure after crossing the trench.

Some historians say that Naufal was killed by Hazrat Ali, while others say that Naufal, intending to attack the Beloved, tried to leap his horse across the trench but fell in and broke his neck. The Quraish offered ten thousand dirhams for his body to bury him with honour. The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ refused the money and said: "We have no need of this body. The polytheists may take it and bury it. We have no objection."

#### The Battle Intensifies

That day was extremely fierce. Fighting continued all day, with arrows and stones exchanged from both sides continuously. No mujahid could leave his position. Khalid bin Waleed crossed the trench with his unit at one point and launched a sudden attack on the blessed tent of the Beloved. But Hazrat Usaid bin Huzair spotted him and rushed with 200 mujahideen. He engaged Khalid bin Waleed's unit in hand-to-hand combat and fought fiercely, preventing the disbelievers from reaching the Prophet's tent.

In this fierce battle, the Beloved's Asr prayer was missed. Bukhari narrates that Hazrat Umar came to the court of prophethood after sunset, cursing the disbelievers, and submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! I could not pray Asr." The Beloved said: "I have not yet prayed Asr either." Then, in the valley of Bathaan, after sunset, he prayed the missed Asr and then Maghrib. He prayed against the disbelievers:

*"O Allah! Fill their homes and graves with fire. They prevented us from the middle prayer (Asr) until the sun set."*

The Beloved also prayed on the day of Khandaq:

*"O Allah! Revealer of the Book! Swift to account! Defeat these armies of disbelievers. O Allah! Defeat them and shake them!"*

#### Zubair Honoured with "Disciple"

During the siege, when it was difficult for anyone to leave the city, the Beloved said three times: "Who will bring news of the army of disbelievers?" All three times, Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam — the son of the Beloved's aunt Hazrat Safiyya — said: "I will, O Messenger of Allah." Pleased with Zubair's self-sacrifice, the Chief of Both Worlds said:

*"Every prophet has a disciple, and my disciple is Zubair."*

Thus, Hazrat Zubair was honoured with the title of Hawari (disciple) in the court of prophethood, a title not given to any other Companion.

#### The Martyrdom of Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz

The Muslims' losses in this battle were very few — only six Muslims were honoured with martyrdom. However, the Ansar lost their greatest pillar — Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz, the supreme chief of the Aus tribe, was wounded by an arrow in this battle and could not recover.

The story of his martyrdom is this: he was wearing a short coat of armour and going to fight with a spear, full of enthusiasm, when a disbeliever named Ibnul Arqa took aim and shot an arrow that severed a vein called Akhal. After the battle, the Beloved ﷺ set up a tent for him in Masjid Nabawi and began his treatment. He himself cauterized the wound twice with his blessed hand.

During this time, he once went to Banu Quraiza and delivered his verdict about the Jews, which will be mentioned under Ghazwa-e-Quraiza. After that, he returned to his tent, and his wound had begun to heal. But in his desire for martyrdom, he prayed to the Lord of the Worlds:

"O Allah! You know that I desire nothing more than to fight the Quraish who denied Your Messenger and drove him from his homeland. O Allah! I think You have ended the war between us and the Quraish. But if there is still any war left with the Quraish, keep me alive so that I may fight them in Your path. And if there is no war left, then tear this wound open and take my life from this wound."

As soon as his prayer ended, the wound suddenly burst open, and blood flowed into the tent of the Ghifar tribe inside Masjid Nabawi. They were startled and said, "O people of the tent! What blood is this that has reached our tent?" When people looked, blood was flowing from Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz's wound, and he died from this wound.

The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ said: "The Throne of Allah shook at the death of Saad bin Muaaz. Seventy thousand angels attended his funeral. When his grave was dug, the fragrance of musk spread from it."

At the time of death, the Chief of the Worlds was seated beside his head. He opened his eyes, took one last look at the blessed face of prophethood, and recited the kalima in a loud voice, saying: "O Messenger of Allah! You have fulfilled the right of prophethood." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

#### The Bravery of Hazrat Safiyya

During the Battle of Khandaq, a situation arose when the Jews saw that the entire Muslim army was engaged in battle at the trench. They suddenly attacked the fort where the Muslim women and children were sheltered. A Jew reached the gate. The Beloved's aunt, Hazrat Safiyya, saw him. She told Hazrat Hassan bin Sabit: "Kill this Jew, otherwise he will go and tell the enemy about our situation here." Hazrat Hassan did not have the courage to attack the Jew at that moment. Seeing this, Hazrat Safiyya herself pulled out a tent peg and struck the Jew's head with such force that his skull split. She then cut off his head and threw it out of the fort. Seeing this, the attacking Jews became convinced that there was an army inside the fort as well. Fearing this, they did not dare to attack from this side again.

#### How the Disbelievers Fled

Hazrat Nuaim bin Masood Ashjai was a highly honoured chief of the Ghatfaan tribe. Both the Quraish and the Jews had complete trust in him. He had become a Muslim, but the disbelievers did not know of his Islam. He requested at the court of prophethood: "O Messenger of Allah! If you permit me, I will speak to both the Jews and the Quraish in such a way that division will arise between them." The Beloved permitted him. So he spoke to the Jews and the Quraish separately in such a way that division did indeed arise between them.

Abu Sufyan was already distressed by the severe winter, the long siege, and the depletion of the army's rations. When he learned that the Jews had abandoned them, his morale collapsed completely. Then suddenly, the wrath of the All-Powerful and the anger of the All-Compelling struck the disbelievers' army. A violent wind came from the east, uprooting and sweeping away tents. Such terror and panic seized the disbelievers that they had no choice but to flee. This is the wind that the Holy Lord mentioned in the Quran:

*"O you who have believed! Remember the favour of Allah upon you when armies came to you, and We sent upon them a wind and armies you did not see. And Allah is ever Seeing of what you do."* (Al-Ahzab: 9)

Abu Sufyan announced to his army: "Rations have run out, the weather is extremely bad, and the Jews have abandoned us. Our siege is useless now." Saying this, he ordered the retreat and fled. The army of Ghatfaan also departed. Banu Quraiza also left the siege and returned to their forts. With their departure, the atmosphere of Madina was cleared of the dust of the disbelievers.

### Ghazwa-e-Bani Quraiza

The Beloved ﷺ had returned home from the Battle of Khandaq and removed his armour to bathe. He had not yet sat down comfortably when Hazrat Jibreel suddenly arrived and said: "O Messenger of Allah! You have put down your armour, but the assembly of angels has not yet put down its armour. Allah's command is that you should proceed toward Banu Quraiza, because they broke the treaty and openly joined the disbelievers in the Battle of Khandaq to attack Madina."

So the Beloved ﷺ announced: "Let no one remove his armour. Proceed toward Banu Quraiza." The Beloved himself also armed himself, mounted his horse named Laheef, and proceeded with the army. He camped near a well of Banu Quraiza.

Banu Quraiza were also fully prepared for battle. When Hazrat Ali approached their forts, those cruel, treaty-breaking Jews insulted the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ. The Beloved laid siege to their forts, which continued for about a month. The Jews, weary, proposed: "Let Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz give whatever verdict he wishes concerning us; we accept it."

Hazrat Saad bin Muaaz had been severely wounded by an arrow in the Battle of Khandaq. Despite this condition, he mounted a donkey and went to Banu Quraiza. He gave this verdict:

"The fighting men shall be killed, the women and children taken captive, and the wealth and possessions of the Jews shall be taken as spoils of war and distributed among the mujahideen."

Hearing this verdict, the Beloved ﷺ said: "Without doubt, you have pronounced the same verdict concerning these Jews that is Allah's verdict."

In accordance with this verdict, the fighting forces of Banu Quraiza were executed, the women and children taken captive, and their wealth and goods taken as spoils by the Muslim mujahideen. Thus, the Muslims were permanently safe from the evil and mischief of this wicked, treaty-breaking tribe.

When the Jewish chief Huyay bin Akhtab was brought to the place of execution, before being killed, he said: "O Muhammad! By Allah's oath! I do not regret at all that I opposed you. But the truth is that whoever forsakes Allah, Allah also forsakes him. There is no difficulty in complying with Allah's command. The killing of Banu Quraiza was a divine decree. It was written in the Torah. It was a punishment that Allah had decreed for the Children of Israel."

This is the same ill-fated Huyay bin Akhtab who, when exiled from Madina to Khaibar, had pledged that he would not help anyone against the Prophet and had made Allah his guarantor. But at the Battle of Khandaq, he broke that treaty. You have read in the previous pages how that tyrant toured all the Arab tribes, inciting them to attack Madina, then incited Banu Quraiza to break their treaty, and himself joined the disbelievers in the Battle of Khandaq.

### Miscellaneous Events of 5 AH

(1) In this year, the Beloved ﷺ married Hazrat Zainab bint Jahash.

(2) In this year, hijab (veiling) was made obligatory upon Muslim women.

(3) In this year, the punishment of Qazf (false accusation of adultery), the rules of Li'an (mutual imprecation), and Zihar (a form of divorce) were revealed.

(4) In this year, the verse of Tayammum was revealed.

(5) In this year, the command for Salatul Khauf (the prayer of fear) was revealed.
