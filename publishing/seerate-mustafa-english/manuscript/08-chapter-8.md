## Chapter 8: The Third Year of Hijra — 3 AH

### The Battle of Uhud

The most significant event of this year was the Battle of Uhud. "Uhud" is the name of a mountain approximately three miles from Madina Munawwara. Since this great battle between truth and falsehood took place at the foot of this mountain, this fight became known as Ghazwa-e-Uhud. The Lord of the Worlds has mentioned these events in various verses of the Holy Quran.

### The Cause of the Battle of Uhud

You have already read that in the Battle of Badr, seventy disbelievers were killed and seventy were captured. Among those killed were mostly the chiefs — indeed, the crowned heads — of the Quraish. As a result, every house in Makka had become a house of mourning. Every child of the Quraish was burning with the fire of revenge, restless to fight the Muslims. The Arabs — and especially the Quraish — considered it a unique point of honour that avenging the blood of each and every slain was such a great obligation that fulfilling it became the very purpose of their lives.

For this reason, when they came to their senses, they decided: "Our nation's chiefs have been killed. Taking revenge for this is our national duty. Therefore, we desire that whatever profit the Quraish's joint trade has made this year should all be collected in the nation's war fund. With this money, we should purchase the best weapons and quickly strengthen our military power. Then, with a great army, we should march on Madina and annihilate the founder of Islam and the Muslims."

Abu Sufyan gladly accepted this request of the Quraish. However, the Quraish had learned from the Battle of Badr that fighting the Muslims was no easy task. Confronting storms and tempests, clashing with ocean waves, colliding with mountains is very easy, but fighting the lovers of Muhammad Rasulullah ﷺ is an extremely difficult undertaking. Therefore, they considered it absolutely necessary to greatly increase their military strength. So they began preparing weapons and equipment for war.

Hind (Abu Sufyan's wife) had her father Utba killed by Hamza in the Battle of Badr, and Zubair bin Mutim's uncle was also killed by him. On this account, Hind incited Wahshi — who was a slave of Zubair bin Mutim — to kill Hazrat Hamza. She promised that if he killed Hazrat Hamza, he would be freed as a reward for this service.

### The March on Madina

The army of the disbelievers set out from Makka with immense fervour and thorough preparation. Abu Sufyan was made the commander of this formidable army.

Hazrat Abbas, the Beloved's uncle, who had secretly become a Muslim and still lived in Makka, wrote a letter informing the Beloved ﷺ of the Quraish's military mobilization. When this alarming news reached the Beloved, on the 5th of Shawwal, 3 AH, he sent both sons of Hazrat Adi bin Fuzala — Hazrat Anas and Hazrat Moonas — as spies to bring news of the Quraish army. These two brought back the disturbing news.

### The Muslim Consultation

The Beloved consulted the Companions about where and how this battle should be fought. Most of the Muhajireen and the elders of the Ansar gave the opinion that the women and children should be secured inside the forts, and the enemy should be confronted from within the city. Abdullah bin Ubayy, the chief of the hypocrites, was also present in that gathering and gave the same opinion.

However, some Companions who had not had the honour of participating in the Battle of Badr — some young men — were strongly opposed to this opinion. They submitted with an almost unanimous voice: "O Messenger of Allah! The enemy has never been able to intimidate us like this before. If we fight within the city, the disbelievers will consider us cowards. Therefore, it is better that we go outside the city and face the enemy on the open battlefield."

The Beloved ﷺ accepted this opinion. He went out of the city with the Companions, numbering one thousand. However, when they reached a certain point on the way, the Beloved gave the order to turn back due to some opposition to the command.

### The Hypocrites' Betrayal

The Beloved said: "We will not seek help from polytheists against polytheists." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`) So the Jewish contingent turned back.

Then Abdullah bin Ubayy, who had come with the Beloved ﷺ with 300 men, also turned back, saying: "Muhammad ﷺ did not accept my advice. He will follow his own way and get everyone killed fighting." He rubbed one hand against the other in anger and left, and 300 hypocrites went with him. Thus, the Muslim numbers dropped from 1,000 to 700.

Hazrat Umar submitted: "These people are very faithless. Today the number of Muslims is small, but Allah is sufficient. You may return to Madina." But the Beloved ﷺ did not heed Hazrat Umar's words and advanced until he reached a place where some young men who had not been able to participate in the Battle of Badr were hovering around the Beloved, eager to seek permission for their self-sacrifice.

### The Young Volunteers

The Beloved ﷺ began turning them back due to their young age. One young man immediately stood on his tiptoes to appear taller, and this trick worked — he was included in the army.

Similarly, Hazrat Samurah, a young boy, when he was being turned back, submitted: "I am stronger than Raafe bin Khadeej." Hazrat Raafe said: "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! I am stronger than him." The Beloved ﷺ had both of them sit down and tested their wrestling strength. Both began to wrestle with full force, and Hazrat Samurah pinned Hazrat Raafe bin Khadeej. Since Hazrat Raafe was 15 years old and Hazrat Samurah was 13, the Beloved ﷺ declared Hazrat Raafe the stronger of the two and included both in the army. Thus both were added to the 700. (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Battle Formation

When the Beloved ﷺ reached the battlefield, he first arranged the troops in ranks. He appointed Hazrat Zubair bin Awam as the commander of the right flank and Hazrat Miqdad bin Amr as the commander of the left flank. Then the Beloved ﷺ prayed for the commencement of battle.

He appointed a unit of 50 archers to guard a mountain pass and made Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair their commander, giving this order: "Listen! Whether we are defeated or victorious, do not leave your position until I myself come and give you the order. Even if you see the disbelievers approaching from behind, do not abandon your post."

When the Beloved ﷺ had organized the army in this manner, he stood before the ranks and prayed with his back toward the enemy and his blessed face toward Madina, supplicating for the jihad.

After this, the army of the disbelievers appeared from the front. Hazrat Jibreel Alaihissalam gave the Beloved the glad tidings: "O Muhammad! Allah Ta'ala has promised to grant you victory by helping your army through angels." The Beloved was filled with joy at this news and, as a sign of happiness, even removed his armour and placed it on the ground. (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Battle Begins

The first person to step forward from the ranks of the polytheists for battle was Abu Aamir Ausi. Because of his worship and asceticism, the people of Madina used to call him "Rahib" (the monk), but the Messenger of Allah ﷺ had named him "Fasiq" (the transgressor). In the time of Jahiliyyah, this man was the chief of his tribe, Aus. When Islam came, he accepted Islam but then apostatized and went to Makka, joining the disbelievers. Seeing the Beloved's dispositions, he encouraged the disbelievers, saying: "I will lure them into such a situation that they will neither be able to kill nor be killed." He came forward separately from the army of the disbelievers, stood before the ranks, and addressed the Beloved: "The disgraceful and humiliating actions of your people are enough to separate me from you." The Beloved gave him no reply, and it was clearly announced from the Beloved's direction that the battle would be initiated by the disbelievers. "We will not begin the fight."

The standard-bearer of the polytheists, Talha bin Abu Talha, came forward and issued a challenge. As soon as he stepped into the arena, the lion of Allah, Hazrat Ali, struck him with his sword. Talha fell down, and his private parts became exposed. The shameless man began begging Hazrat Ali to forgive him. Hazrat Ali said: "Seeing that shameless man uncovered, I felt such shame that I turned my face away." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

After Talha, his brother Usman bin Abu Talha advanced, reciting Rajaz poetry and making an attack.

### The Prophet's Sword

The Beloved ﷺ drew his sword from its sheath and, holding it in his blessed hand, said: "Who will take this sword and give it its due?" Upon hearing this, many Companions became eager. Hazrat Abu Dujana submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! What is its due?" The Beloved said: "That you kill the disbelievers with it until it becomes bent."

Hazrat Abu Dujana submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! I take this sword with its due." Then he asked: "What is the sword's due?" — "To kill the disbelievers with it and to pursue them." Then Hazrat Abu Dujana tied the red cloth band around his head — a band that was tied in the turban and worn as a badge of bravery in battle. As soon as he tied the red cloth band, a stir ran through the army. Voices arose from every side: "Abu Dujana has tied the band of death!"

### The Martyrdom of Hazrat Hamza

Wahshi, the slave of Zubair bin Mutim, was lying in wait for an opportunity. He saw Hazrat Hamza striking down one disbeliever after another with his sword. He reached a place from where he could strike. He threw his spear from a distance, which struck the Prophet's uncle in the navel and pierced through to his back. Even in this condition, Hazrat Hamza advanced toward him with his sword, but unable to bear the wound, he fell and was honoured with martyrdom. (Bukhari, Baab Qatle Hamza)

### The Martyrdom of Hazrat Hanzala

Another Companion, Hazrat Hanzala, was also fighting valiantly when suddenly Shaddad bin Al Aswad attacked him from behind, blocked his blow, and martyred him. Concerning Hazrat Hanzala, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ said: "The angels are giving Hanzala a bath." When his wife was asked about his condition, she said that he had left for battle while in a state of janabah (ritual impurity due to marital intimacy).

### The Archers Disobey

When the Muslims gained the upper hand and the disbelievers began to flee, the disbelievers' women who were playing drums and singing began to flee as well. The Muslims started collecting the spoils of war. The commander of the archers, Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair, restrained his unit from disobedience, but other archers who had been stationed at the pass said: "The battle is over. Now let us also go and collect the spoils." One of their officers, Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair, tried to stop them by reminding them of the Prophet's command, but those Muslim archers did not listen. They abandoned their position and became busy looting the spoils of war.

Khalid bin Waleed, an officer of the disbelievers' army, was watching this scene from the mountain height. When he saw that the pass was empty of guards, he took a unit of disbelievers and attacked them.

### The Prophet is Wounded

Arrows were raining from all directions. One arrow struck the Beloved's blessed tooth, causing his blessed tooth to be martyred. This sudden attack of the disbelievers snatched even the sword from the Muslims' tongues and announced an extremely horrific scene. The Companions became anxious.

### The Martyrdom of Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair

Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair, who bore some resemblance to the Beloved ﷺ, stood holding the Prophet to his chest, reciting in a loud voice:

*"Wa ma Muhammadun illa rasool — qad khalat min qablihir rusul"* (And Muhammad is but a messenger; messengers have passed before him.)

Then Ibn Qamia struck him with an arrow and martyred him. Seeing him fall to the ground, the disbelievers raised a cry: "Muhammad has been killed!" This cry caused utter havoc. Hearing this, the Muslims became completely distraught and confused, and began to flee from the battlefield.

### The Flight and the Steadfast

Even great brave men lost their nerve. The Muslims split into three groups. Some fled as far as the outskirts of Madina. Some were so stunned that they became dead-hearted, staying where they were either saving themselves or continuing to fight. Some — about twelve in number — remained steadfast with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. In this chaos and stampede, many completely lost hope, and even those who wanted to fight with valour became trapped in the pincer movement of the enemy's two-pronged attack, rendered helpless.

Where was the Chief of both Worlds, and in what condition was he? No one knew.

Hazrat Ali, the Lion of Allah, kept swinging his sword, scattering the enemy's ranks, but he kept turning in every direction looking for the Messenger of Allah. Not seeing the Prophet's blessed face, he was in a state of extreme anxiety and restlessness.

Hazrat Anas bin Nazr, the uncle of Hazrat Anas, fought his way forward and advanced ahead of the battlefield. There he saw some Muslims who had thrown down their weapons in despair. He asked: "What are you sitting here doing?" They replied: "What should we fight for now? The one for whom we were fighting has been martyred." Hazrat Anas bin Nazr said: "If indeed the Messenger of Allah ﷺ has been martyred, then what is the use of living after him?"

### The Rallying Cry

A voice cried out with all its might: "O Muslims! Come here! The Messenger of Allah ﷺ is here!"

Hearing this voice, life poured back into all the self-sacrificing Companions. From every direction, Muslims came running. The disbelievers also redirected their attack from all sides and concentrated all their force on killing the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ. The army of the disbelievers surrounded him completely from every direction.

### The Lame Man's Paradise

Hazrat Amr bin Jamooh Ansari was lame. When setting out from home, he had prayed: "O Allah! Do not decree that I return from the battlefield to my family." He had four sons who were also engaged in jihad. People tried to stop him from fighting due to his lameness, so he went pleading to the Beloved ﷺ: "O Messenger of Allah! Permit me to fight. I desire that I too may enter Paradise limping." His restlessness and tears moved the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, and he granted permission. Overjoyed, he plunged into the enemy ranks with one of his sons.

Hazrat Abu Talha narrates: "I saw Hazrat Amr bin Jamooh walking through the battlefield saying, 'By Allah's oath! I am eager for Paradise.' His son supported him as they fought with incredible bravery, until both were honoured with martyrdom and reached the gardens of Paradise."

After the battle, his wife Hind (the wife of Amr bin Jamooh) arrived and loaded the bodies of her husband, brother, and son onto a camel to take them to Madina for burial. Despite thousands of attempts, the camel would not take a single step toward Madina — it kept running back toward the battlefield. When Hind reported this to the Beloved ﷺ, he asked: "Did Amr bin Jamooh say anything when leaving home?" She replied: "Yes, he prayed, 'O Allah! Do not decree that I return from the battlefield to my family.'" The Beloved said: "That is why the camel will not go toward Madina." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Prophet Severely Wounded

In this state of chaos and confusion, while the scattered Muslims had not yet gathered around the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, Abdullah bin Qamia — a famously brave warrior of the Quraish — suddenly spotted the Beloved. Like lightning, he tore through the ranks and launched a deadly attack on the Chief of Both Worlds. The tyrant struck the Prophet's blessed face with his sword with full force, causing two links of the helmet chain to pierce into his blessed cheek. Another disbeliever struck the Prophet's holy face with such a stone that two of his blessed teeth were martyred and his lower blessed lip was wounded.

In this condition, the accursed Ubayy bin Khalaf advanced on his horse, intending to martyr the Beloved. The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ took a small spear from his devoted Companion Hazrat Haris bin Samma and struck Ubayy bin Khalaf's neck. The wound was superficial, but Ubayy trembled and fled. Back in his army, he began complaining of unbearable pain. His companions said: "This is just a scratch. Why are you so distressed?" He replied: "You do not know. Once, Muhammad ﷺ said to me, 'I will kill you.' Even if he had only spat on me, I would consider my death certain."

The story is this: Ubayy bin Khalaf had raised a horse in Makka named "Aud." He would graze it daily, saying, "I will ride this horse to kill Muhammad." When the Beloved ﷺ heard of this, he said: "Insha'Allah, I will kill Ubayy bin Khalaf." So Ubayy came to Uhud on that same horse. The spear wound caused him such agony that he writhed and wailed throughout the return journey until he died at the place of Saraf on the way back from Uhud.

As for the accursed Ibn Qamia, who had struck the Prophet's blessed cheek with his sword, the All-Powerful, All-Compelling Lord sent a mountain goat against him, which gored him repeatedly until he became like a sieve and was thrown down from the mountain height, his body shattered into pieces.

### The Companions' Self-Sacrifice

When the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ was wounded, the disbelievers began striking him with arrows and swords from all directions. Their overwhelming onslaught surrounded him from every side. Seeing this, the devoted Companions' blood boiled with the passion of self-sacrifice. Placing their lives in their hands, they leaped into the fire of this battle to save him, forming a circle around him.

Hazrat Abu Dujana bent over, becoming a shield for the Beloved, bearing on his back all the swords that rained down from every direction. He did not let a single sword or spear strike reach the Beloved.

Hazrat Talha's sacrifice was such that he would block the disbelievers' sword strikes with his own hand, until one of his hands was severed and paralysed, and his body bore 35 or 39 wounds. In short, the devoted Companions did not care for their own lives in protecting the Beloved, fighting with such bravery and valour that history cannot provide a parallel.

Hazrat Abu Talha was a famous marksman. At this moment, he rained so many arrows that several bows broke. He kept the Beloved ﷺ seated behind his back so that no enemy arrow or sword could reach him. Whenever the Beloved lifted his head to see the enemy army, Hazrat Talha would plead: "O Messenger of Allah! May my parents be sacrificed for you! Do not lift your head, lest an enemy arrow strike you. Keep yourself behind my back — my chest is a shield for you." (Bukhari, Ghazwa-e-Badr)

Hazrat Qatada bin Noman Ansari kept his face toward the enemy to protect the Prophet's blessed face. Suddenly, an enemy arrow struck his eye, and his eyeball came out onto his cheek. The Beloved ﷺ picked up his eye with his blessed hand, placed it back in its socket, and prayed: "O Allah! Save Qatada's eye, for he saved the face of Your Messenger." It is famous that that eye became brighter and more beautiful than the other. (`Zurqani`)

Hazrat Saad bin Abi Waqqas was also supremely skilled in archery. He too was rapidly shooting arrows in defence of the Beloved, and the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ himself would pick up arrows with his blessed hand and hand them to him, saying: "O Saad! Keep shooting! May my parents be sacrificed for you." (Bukhari, Ghazwa-e-Uhud)

### The Prophet's Prayer for His People

The cruel disbelievers were showering arrows on the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ with extreme brutality, yet even then, his blessed tongue bore this prayer:

*"O Allah! Forgive my people, for they do not know."*

### The Prophet Falls into a Pit

The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ was weakening from the injury to his blessed tooth and the wounds on his blessed face. In this condition, he fell into one of the pits that Abu Aamir Fasiq had dug and concealed so that the Muslims would fall into them unknowingly.

Hazrat Ali took the Beloved's blessed hand, and Hazrat Talha bin Obaidullah lifted him up. Hazrat Abu Ubaida bin Al Jarrah gripped with his teeth the ring of the helmet chain link that had pierced the blessed face, and pulled it with such force that one of his teeth broke and fell to the ground. When he pulled the second link with his teeth, another tooth broke.

Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri's father, Hazrat Malik bin Sinan, sucked and drank the blood flowing from the blessed face with passionate devotion, not letting a single drop fall to the ground. The Beloved ﷺ asked: "O Malik bin Sinan! Have you drunk my blood?" He submitted: "Yes, O Messenger of Allah." The Beloved said: "The one who has drunk my blood — how dare Hellfire touch him?" (`Zurqani`)

### The Retreat to the Mountain

In this condition, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ climbed with his devoted Companions to the height of the mountain, where it was difficult for the disbelievers to reach. Abu Sufyan saw this and tried to climb the mountain with his army, but Hazrat Umar and other devoted Companions rained such stones on the disbelievers that Abu Sufyan could not withstand it and descended.

The Chief of the Worlds ﷺ was in a mountain crevice with a few Companions, blood flowing from his blessed face. Hazrat Ali was bringing water in his shield, and Hazrat Fatima Zahra was washing the blood with her hands, but the blood would not stop. Finally, a piece of a date-palm mat was burnt, and its ash was placed on the wound, which immediately stopped the bleeding.

### Abu Sufyan's Challenge and the Response

As Abu Sufyan began to withdraw from the battlefield, he climbed a hill and called out loudly: "Is Muhammad here?" The Beloved instructed: "Do not answer him." Then he called: "Is Abu Bakr here?" Again, "Do not answer." Then he called: "Is Umar here?" Receiving no reply, Abu Sufyan said arrogantly: "They have all been killed. If they were alive, they would surely have answered me."

Hearing this, Hazrat Umar could not contain himself and shouted: "O enemy of Allah! You lie! We are all alive!"

Then Abu Sufyan, in the pride of his victory, raised the cry: "Hubal! Be exalted! O Hubal! Be exalted!" (Hubal was the Quraish's chief idol.)

The Beloved ﷺ told the Companions: "You too respond to him." They asked: "What shall we say?" He said: "Say: *Allahumma A'la wa Ajall* — Allah is most High and Great."

Abu Sufyan then said: "We have Uzza (the idol), and you have no Uzza." The Beloved ﷺ instructed: "Respond: *Allahu Mawlana wa la Mawla lakum* — Allah is our Protector, and you have no protector."

Abu Sufyan then declared with great pride: "Today is the answer and revenge for Badr. In war, sometimes there is victory, sometimes defeat. O Muslims! Our army has cut off the ears and noses of your slain and disfigured their faces. But I neither ordered this, nor do I feel any regret or sorrow over it." Saying this, Abu Sufyan left the field.

### Hind the Liver-Eater

The women of the Quraish, in their frenzy to avenge the Battle of Badr, went among the bodies of the honoured martyrs, cutting off their ears, noses, and other body parts, disfiguring their faces. Abu Sufyan's wife Hind displayed such brutality that she made a necklace of these body parts and wore it around her neck.

Hind was searching for the sacred body of Hazrat Hamza, because Hazrat Hamza had killed her father Utba on the day of Badr. When this heartless woman found Hamza's body, she ripped open his stomach with a dagger, took out his liver, and tried to chew it — but could not swallow it and spat it out. The title "Hind the Liver-Eater" in history is based on this incident. (Hind and her husband Abu Sufyan accepted Islam on the day of the Conquest of Makka in Ramadan, 8 AH.)

### The Will of Saad bin Arrabi

Hazrat Zaid bin Sabit narrates: "I was sent by the Beloved to search for the body of Hazrat Saad bin Arrabi. I found him in his last moments. He said to me: 'Convey my salaam to the Messenger of Allah, and give this message to my people after greeting them: As long as even one of you remains alive, if the Messenger of Allah is harmed by the disbelievers, no excuse of yours will be accepted in Allah's court.' Having said this, his soul departed."

### The Heroism of Muslim Women

In the Battle of Uhud, women also participated with great spirit of jihad, just like men. Hazrat Anas narrates about Hazrat Aisha and Hazrat Umm Sulaim: "Both had their skirts rolled up, carrying water skins and bringing water for the mujahideen, especially giving water to the wounded."

Similarly, Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri's mother, Hazrat Umm Saleet, also kept bringing water skins and giving water to the mujahideen. (Bukhari, 2/582)

### The Self-Sacrifice of Hazrat Umm Ammara

Hazrat Umm Ammara, whose name was Naseeba, came to Uhud with her husband Hazrat Zaid bin Aasim and two sons, Hazrat Ammara and Hazrat Abdullah. At first, she gave water to the mujahideen. But when she saw the terrifying scene of the disbelievers' onslaught against the Beloved ﷺ, she threw down the water skin, took a dagger, and stood facing the enemy with her chest bared.

She blocked every arrow and sword strike aimed at the Prophet, receiving 13 wounds on her head and neck. When the accursed Ibn Qamia struck at the Beloved ﷺ with his sword, Umm Ammara stepped forward and took the blow on her own body. The wound on her shoulder was so deep that it formed a hollow. She then struck Ibn Qamia forcefully on the shoulder, but he was wearing a double coat of armour and survived.

Her son Hazrat Abdullah says: "A disbeliever wounded me, and my wound would not stop bleeding. My mother Umm Ammara immediately tore her cloth and bandaged the wound, saying, 'Get up, my son! Stand and resume jihad!' By chance, that same disbeliever came before the Beloved ﷺ, who said, 'O Umm Ammara! This is the one who wounded your son.' Hearing this, Hazrat Umm Ammara lunged at the disbeliever and struck his leg with such force that he fell and could not get up, crawling away on his buttocks. Seeing this, the Beloved ﷺ smiled and said: 'O Umm Ammara! Thank Allah, who granted you such strength and courage to fight in His path.'"

Hazrat Umm Ammara submitted: "O Messenger of Allah! Pray that we may be honoured to serve you in Paradise as well." At that moment, the Beloved prayed for her, her husband, and her sons: "O Allah! Make them all my companions in Paradise."

Hazrat Umm Ammara continued to say throughout her life: "After the Beloved's prayer, no matter how great a calamity befalls me in this world, I have no concern for it." (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Courage of Hazrat Safiyya

The Beloved's aunt, Hazrat Safiyya, came to see the body of her brother Hazrat Hamza. The Beloved ordered her son Hazrat Zubair: "Do not let my aunt see her brother's body." Hazrat Safiyya said: "I already know everything about my brother. I do not consider this too great a sacrifice in Allah's path." With the Beloved's permission, she went to the body and saw that her brother's ears, nose, and eyes were all cut and mutilated, his stomach was torn open, and his liver lay chewed. Seeing this, this lion-hearted woman said nothing but *"Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'oon"* and, praying for his forgiveness, returned.

### The Patience of an Ansari Woman

An Ansari woman's husband, father, and brother were all martyred in this battle. People brought her the news of each martyrdom one by one, but each time she only asked: "Tell me — how is the Messenger of Allah ﷺ?" When they told her that he was alive and safe, involuntarily her tongue uttered the meaning of this couplet:

*"I am content — the Protector of the helpless is alive and safe. I care not if the whole world is gone."*

Allahu Akbar! What can one say about this lion-hearted woman's patience and selflessness? Three mountains of grief fell upon her heart — the killing of her husband, father, and brother — yet her tongue of circumstance declared:

*"I too, and my father, my husband, and my brother, are all sacrificed. O martyr of the faith! As long as you are safe, what are we?"*

### The Honoured Martyrs

In this battle, seventy noble Companions tasted the cup of martyrdom — among them 4 Muhajireen and 66 Ansar. Thirty disbelievers were also killed, in utter humiliation. (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

But such was the poverty of the Muslims that they did not even have cloth for shrouds for these martyrs. Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair's condition was such that at the time of martyrdom, he had only one large sheet on his body — when his head was covered, his feet were uncovered, and when his feet were covered, his head was uncovered. Finally, his head was covered and *Izkhir* grass was placed over his feet. The martyrs, drenched in blood, were buried two in one grave. The one who knew more of the Quran was placed in front.

### Visiting the Graves of the Martyrs

The Beloved ﷺ would visit the graves of the martyrs of Uhud. After him, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and Hazrat Umar Farooq also followed this practice. Once, the Beloved ﷺ went to the graves of the martyrs of Uhud and said: "O Allah! Your Messenger bears witness that this group gave their lives seeking Your pleasure." Then he added: "Any Muslim who visits these martyrs' graves until the Day of Judgement and greets them — these martyrs will respond to that greeting."

Hazrat Fatima Khuzaiya narrates: "One day I was passing through the field of Uhud. When I reached near Hazrat Hamza's grave, I said, *'Assalamu alaika ya Amma Rasulillah'* (Peace be upon you, O uncle of the Messenger of Allah). I heard a voice in my ears: *'Wa alaikas salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.'*" (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Life of the Martyrs

Forty-six years later, when some graves of the martyrs of Uhud were opened, their shrouds were intact and their bodies were fresh and moist. All the people of Madina and others saw that the martyrs had their hands placed over their wounds — and when the hands were lifted, fresh blood began to flow. (`Madarijun Nubuwwah`)

### The Killing of Kaab bin Ashraf

Among the Jews, Kaab bin Ashraf was extremely wealthy. He paid salaries from his own treasury to the Jewish scholars and religious leaders. Besides wealth, he was highly accomplished in poetry, which gave him special influence not only among the Jews but over all the Arab tribes. He harboured intense enmity toward the Beloved ﷺ. The Muslim victory at Badr and the killing of the Quraish chiefs caused him immense grief and shock.

He went to Makka to offer condolences to the Quraish and wrote such a heart-wrenching elegy for those killed at Badr that whenever it was recited in gatherings, mourning would break out. He would recite this elegy to the Quraish and weep bitterly, making others weep too. He met Abu Sufyan in Makka and incited him to take revenge on the Muslims for Badr. He even brought Abu Sufyan to the Haram, and together with the disbelievers of Makka, held the Kaaba's curtain and swore to take revenge on the Muslims.

When he returned to Madina, he began writing satirical verses against the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ and indulging in all kinds of insolence and disrespect toward the Prophet. Not stopping there, he even planned to have the Beloved secretly assassinated.

These actions of the Jew Kaab bin Ashraf were a complete violation of the treaty made between the Jews and the Ansar — that the Jews would remain neutral in the conflict between the Muslims and the Quraish. The Muslims tolerated this for a long time. But when the life of the founder of Islam was endangered, Hazrat Muhammad bin Muslima took Hazrat Abu Naaila, Hazrat Abbad bin Bishar, Hazrat Haris bin Aus, and Hazrat Abu Abas with him. They went to Kaab bin Ashraf's house at night and killed him at the gate of his fort on Rabiul Awwal, 3 AH. In the morning, they presented his head at the court of prophethood, placing it at the feet of the Chief of Both Worlds ﷺ.

In this operation, Hazrat Haris bin Aus had been wounded by a sword. Muhammad bin Muslima and others carried him on their shoulders to the court of prophethood. The Beloved applied his blessed saliva to the wound, and he was completely healed instantly.

### Ghazwa-e-Ghatfaan

In Rabiul Awwal, 3 AH, the Beloved ﷺ received news that a famous warrior of Najd, Dasur bin Al Haris Muharibi, had prepared an army to attack Madina. Upon hearing this, the Beloved set out with 400 Companions to confront him. When Dasur learned that the Messenger of Allah had arrived in his territory, he fled and climbed the mountains with his army. However, one of his men named Habban was captured and immediately accepted Islam.

It so happened that a heavy rain fell that day. The Beloved ﷺ lay under a tree drying his clothes. The disbelievers on the mountain height saw that the Prophet was alone and far from his Companions. Dasur immediately descended from the mountain like lightning, naked sword in hand, and standing over the Prophet's blessed head with his sword raised, said: "Now tell me — who will save you from me?"

The Beloved replied: "My Allah will save me." In an instant, Jibreel Alaihissalam descended to earth and struck Dasur's chest with such a punch that his sword fell from his hand and he stood stunned. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ immediately picked up the sword and said: "Now speak — who will save you from my sword?" Dasur said in a trembling, choked voice: "No one." The Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ took pity on his helplessness and forgave him.

Dasur was deeply affected by this prophetic character. He recited the kalima and became a Muslim. He then returned to his people and began preaching Islam. No battle took place in this Ghazwa, and the Beloved remained outside Madina for 11 or 15 days before returning. (`Zurqani`)

Some historians have placed the sword-drawing incident at the occasion of Ghazwa-e-Zaaturriqa, but the truth is that two such incidents occurred in prophetic history. At Ghazwa-e-Ghatfaan, the one who raised the sword over the Prophet's head was Dasur bin Haris Muharibi, who became Muslim and became the cause of his people's Islam. At Ghazwa-e-Zaaturriqa, the person who raised the sword over the Prophet was named Ghauraas. He did not accept Islam but remained a disbeliever until his death. However, he did make a pledge that he would never fight the Beloved. (`Zurqani`)

### Miscellaneous Events of 3 AH

The following events also occurred in the third year of Hijra:

(1) On the 15th of Ramadan, 3 AH, Hazrat Imam Hasan was born.

(2) In this year, the Chief of the Worlds ﷺ married Hazrat Hafsa. Hazrat Hafsa was the daughter of Hazrat Umar Farooq, who had become a widow around the time of the Battle of Badr. Her detailed circumstances will be written later in the section on the Mothers of the Believers.

(3) In this year, Hazrat Usman Ghani married the Beloved's daughter, Hazrat Umm Kulsum.

(4) The commandments and laws of inheritance were also revealed in this year. Until now, the *zawil arhaam* (distant relatives) had no share. Detailed instructions regarding their rights were revealed.

(5) Until now, marriage with polytheist women was permissible for Muslims, but in 3 AH, the prohibition was revealed, and marriage with polytheist women was declared permanently forbidden for Muslims.
