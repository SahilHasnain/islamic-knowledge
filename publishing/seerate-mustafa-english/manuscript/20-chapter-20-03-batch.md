## Chapter 20: Miracles of Prophethood (Continued)

### Prophecy: Jews Will Be Defeated

The Jewish tribes of Madina and its surroundings — Banu Quraiza, Banu Nadir, and Banu Qainuqa — were extremely wealthy, exceedingly warlike, and formidable fighters. They took great pride in their military might. When news of the Muslims' decisive victory at Badr reached these Jews, they taunted the Muslims: "The Quraish tribes are unfamiliar with the art of war and lack combat skill, which is why they lost. If the Muslims ever face us, who are warlike and courageous, they will remember the taste of their mother's milk!"

The situation indeed appeared such that no one could imagine the Muslims overcoming these Jewish warlords. But the truthful Prophet had already declared to the Jews years earlier:

> أَمْ حَسِبْتُمْ أَن تَدْخُلُوا الْجَنَّةَ وَلَمَّا يَأْتِكُم مَّثَلُ الَّذِينَ خَلَوْا مِن قَبْلِكُم ۖ مَّسَّتْهُمُ الْبَأْسَاءُ وَالضَّرَّاءُ وَزُلْزِلُوا حَتَّىٰ يَقُولَ الرَّسُولُ وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مَعَهُ مَتَىٰ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ ۗ أَلَا إِنَّ نَصْرَ اللَّهِ قَرِيبٌ

**Translation:** "Or do you think you will enter Paradise while there has not yet come to you the likeness of those who passed away before you? They were touched by poverty and hardship and were shaken until the messenger and those who believed with him said, 'When is the help of Allah?' Unquestionably, the help of Allah is near." (Al-Baqara: 214)

And indeed, the prophecy was fulfilled. All three Jewish tribes of Madina were driven out or destroyed. Banu Qainuqa were expelled in 2 AH, Banu Nadir in 4 AH, and Banu Quraiza faced their fate in 5 AH. The Quraish and the idolaters, seeing the fate of these mighty Jewish tribes, realized the truth of the Prophet's message.

### Quranic Prophecies Beyond the Prophetic Era

The Quran's prophecies of the unseen were not limited to battles during the Prophet's time. Even after his departure, during the era of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the Quran had already foretold events that would unfold in the Arabian peninsula — the great bloody battles that would take place after the Prophet's departure. The Quranic verses regarding the fate of the apostates and those who would rebel against the rightly guided Khilafat were fulfilled exactly as revealed.

### Muslims Will Conquer Persia and Rome

When the Holy Prophet migrated to Madina, the Muslims were so destitute and helpless that no one could imagine that these penniless, starving Muslims would one day overthrow the thrones of the mighty empires of Caesar and Chosroes. But the Knower of the Unseen, the truthful Prophet, announced with complete certainty to his Ummah: "O Muslims! You will soon conquer Constantinople, and the keys of the treasures of Caesar and Chosroes will be in your hands. The banner of your rule will fly over Egypt. You will fight the Turks, whose eyes are small and faces are broad..." (Ahmad, Bukhari, Muslim)

Among these prophecies, the conquest of Constantinople was foretold centuries before it happened. The Prophet said:

> "You will certainly conquer Constantinople. What an excellent leader its leader will be, and what an excellent army that army will be!" (Ahmad, Hakim)

This prophecy was fulfilled in 857 AH (1453 CE) when Sultan Mehmed Fatih conquered Constantinople. The Prophet had also said: "The Day of Judgment will not come until the Muslims fight the Turks, a people with small eyes, ruddy faces, and flat noses."

### Prophecy: Yemen, Syria, and Iraq Will Be Conquered

The Holy Prophet gave news of the conquest of Yemen, Syria, and Iraq many years before they happened. He said: "Yemen will be conquered, and people will come from Madina to Yemen, driving their mounts and bringing their families and followers — even though staying in Madina would be better for them, if only they knew. Then Syria will be conquered, and people will drive their mounts from Madina. Then Iraq will be conquered..."

He also said regarding Egypt: "When you conquer Egypt, treat its people well, for they have a covenant of protection and are your relatives by marriage." (Muslim)

### Prophecy: Baitul Muqaddas Will Be Conquered

Years before the conquest of Baitul Muqaddas (Jerusalem), the Holy Prophet told his Ummah: "Count six things before the Day of Judgment: (1) My death, (2) The conquest of Baitul Muqaddas, (3)..." (Bukhari) This was fulfilled in 16 AH during the caliphate of Umar Farooq.

### Prophecy: Treasure of Chosroes Will Be Distributed

Sayyidina Adi bin Hatim narrates: "I was with the Prophet when a man complained of poverty. Then another complained of highway robbery. The Prophet said: 'O Adi! Have you seen the city of Hira?' I said no. He said: 'If you live long, you will see a woman travel alone from Hira to Makka, fearing none but Allah.' I thought to myself: 'Where will the bandits of Tai who set the city on fire go?' Then the Prophet said: 'If you live long, you will see the treasures of Chosroes being opened by the Muslims.' And he said: 'O Adi! If you live long, you will see a man coming with a handful of gold and silver, looking for someone to accept it in charity, finding no one — because everyone will be rich.'" (Bukhari)

Sayyidina Adi bin Hatim lived to see all these prophecies fulfilled.

### Prophecy: Khaibar Conquered by Ali

One day before the conquest of Khaibar, the Holy Prophet announced: "Tomorrow I will give the banner to a man who loves Allah and His Messenger, and Allah will grant victory through him." When morning came, Ali was not present — he had sore eyes. The Prophet sent for him, applied his blessed saliva to Ali's eyes, and prayed, whereupon Ali was instantly cured. The Prophet handed him the banner, and Khaibar was conquered that very day.

This prophecy is a clear example of the Prophet's knowledge of the unseen — knowing a full day in advance what would happen.

### Prophecy: The Bloody Fitna of Banu Umayya

Islamic history bears witness that in 70 AH, the young rulers of Banu Umayya sparked such bloody fitnas that every Muslim should seek Allah's protection from them. The truthful Prophet had foretold these events years in advance. (Bukhari)

### Prophecy: Muslims Will Fight the Turks

Abu Huraira reports that the Prophet said: "The Day of Judgment will not come until you fight a people whose shoes are made of hair, and until you fight the Turks, a people with small eyes, ruddy faces, and flat noses." (Bukhari, Muslim)

### Glad Tidings of Jihad in India

The Prophet said: "There are two groups of my Ummah whom Allah has freed from Hellfire: one group is those who wage Jihad in India, and the other is those who will be with Eisa bin Maryam."

Abu Huraira would say: "The Prophet promised us Jihad in India. If I live to see that time, I will sacrifice my life and wealth in that path. And if I am martyred in that Jihad, I will be the best of martyrs." (Tabarani)

### Prophecy: Where People Will Die

Before the Battle of Badr, the Prophet took the Companions to the battlefield and drew lines with his staff, saying: "This is where so-and-so will be killed. This is Abu Jahl's place of death. Here the Quraish chief will fall." The Companions saw each and every enemy fall exactly where the Prophet had indicated.

### Prophecy: The Prophet's Own Departure

The Prophet informed his Companions of his impending departure before the Farewell Pilgrimage. When he sent Mu'az bin Jabal as governor of Yemen, he told him privately of his coming departure. After the Prophet's departure, Mu'az said: "I knew the Prophet would not remain long in this world."

Similarly, the Prophet told Fatima: "O Fatima! You will be the first among my family to join me." She replied: "Then I laughed, thinking how soon I would be reunited with the Prophet." Scholars know that both these prophecies were fulfilled — he departed from that same illness, and Fatima followed him six months later.

### Prophecy: Umar and Usman Will Be Martyred

Anas narrates that once the Prophet climbed Mount Uhud with Abu Bakr, Umar, and Usman. The mountain shook, and the Prophet said: "Be still, O Uhud! For upon you are a Prophet, a Siddique, and two martyrs (Umar and Usman)." This prophecy was fulfilled exactly as foretold.

### Prophecy: The Cursed Man Who Will Kill Ali

The Prophet said: "The most wretched of people are two: the red one of Samood who killed the she-camel of Saleh, and the one who will strike you here, O Ali" — pointing to his neck. This prophecy was fulfilled on 17th Ramadan, 40 AH, when Abdurrahman bin Muljim al-Khariji struck Ali with a sword, leading to his martyrdom two days later.

### Prophecy: The Great Fire of Hijaz

Abu Huraira reports that the Prophet said: "The Day of Judgment will not come until a fire emerges from the land of Hijaz that illuminates the necks of camels in Basra." (Muslim)

This prophecy manifested in 654 AH. Imam Nawawi wrote in his commentary: "This fire appeared in our time, in 654 AH, inside Madina. It was so huge that it spread from the eastern side of Madina to the hills of Hurra. News of this fire reached Syria and other lands. The people of Madina gathered near the Prophet's grave seeking refuge. The fire continued burning for over a month until it gradually extinguished, leaving no trace." (Tarikhul Khulafa)

### Leaders of Fitna

Hudhaifa bin Yaman says: "By Allah, I do not know if my companions have forgotten or are pretending to be ignorant. By Allah, until the end of the world, all the leaders of fitna whose followers number 300 or more — their names, their fathers' names, and their tribes — have been told to us by the Messenger of Allah." (Abu Dawood)

### The Comprehensive Sermon

Sayyidina Abu Zar Ghifari says: "When the Prophet was on his sickbed, we were around him. He said: 'O Abu Zar! Bring me a scribe.' Then the Prophet dictated his will to the scribe. He dictated until he covered many matters. Then he said: 'This is the end of what was dictated.' But I continued to remember more that had not been written." And Abu Dharr said: "The Prophet delivered a sermon one day while the sun was still up. He descended to pray Asr, then ascended the pulpit and continued the sermon until sunset. In that single day's sermon, he informed us of all events that would occur until the Day of Judgment." (Mishkat)

### An Important Note

The prophecies mentioned above are but a few among thousands of unseen events that the Holy Prophet shared with his Ummah. Thousands more are recorded in the Sihah Sitta and other collections of hadith. These are countless testimonies proving that the Holy Prophet possessed knowledge of the unseen granted by Allah. Among the scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah, there is consensus that the Prophet's knowledge of the unseen was vast and comprehensive.

### Earthly Miracles

Having discussed heavenly miracles and prophecies of the unseen, it is now appropriate to mention some examples of the countless miracles that appeared on earth. These are presented so that readers may understand, with the clarity of sunlight, that there is no corner of Allah's creation where the sovereignty and spiritual authority of the Mercy for the Worlds does not prevail.

### Rocks Crumble and Give Way

In the account of the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq), it is narrated that when the Companions encountered an enormous rock that could not be broken, they informed the Prophet. He took a pickaxe and struck the rock — a flash of lightning emanated from the blow, illuminating the entire trench. He struck again, and another flash appeared. After the third strike, the rock crumbled into pieces. The Prophet interpreted each flash as the conquests of Syria, Persia, and Yemen that would be granted to his Ummah. (Madarijun Nubuwwah)

### The Miraculous Dispersion of Dust

On the night of the Hijrah, when the Prophet threw a handful of dust at the disbelievers surrounding the house of prophethood, that handful of dust fell on all the disbelievers' faces, blinding them temporarily so that the Prophet could pass safely through their midst.

### Observation

These five authentic accounts testify that the sovereignty of the Prophet's miracles and spiritual authority extends over the world of inanimate objects (Aalame Jamadaat). Every particle of the inanimate world knows, recognizes, and accepts that he is the true Messenger of Allah, and considers obedience to him as obligatory.

### The Tree Came Walking

Abdullah bin Umar narrates: "We were with the Prophet on a journey. A Bedouin came to him, and the Prophet invited him to Islam. The Bedouin asked: 'Is there any witness to your prophethood?' The Prophet replied: 'Yes, that tree at the edge of the field will bear witness to my prophethood.' He called the tree, and it began plowing through the earth, tearing the ground, and came before the Prophet. It testified three times with a loud voice that he was the Messenger of Allah. Then the Prophet gestured, and the tree returned to its place, walking back through the earth."

Muhaddis Bazzar, Imam Behaqi, and Imam Baghawi also recorded: "The Prophet took hold of a branch and commanded it to walk. The tree walked alongside him like a camel led by its halter. Then the Prophet took hold of another tree's branch, and it too walked. The two trees met together, and the Prophet relieved himself behind them. Then he commanded them, and they walked back, plowing the earth, and stood at their original places." (Zurqani)

Imam Buseri referred to this miracle in his Qasida-e-Burda:

> "When called, the tree came prostrating,
> Walking on its trunk without feet, to his presence."

### The Glowing Branch

Imam Ahmad narrates from Abu Saeed Khudri that once Qatada bin Noman prayed Isha behind the Prophet. The night was extremely dark and cloudy. When returning, the Prophet gave him a branch from a tree and said: "Go to your home without fear. This branch will be so illuminated in your hand that ten men in front of you and ten behind you can walk in its light. And when you reach home, you will see a black thing — strike it and drive it out of the house." It happened exactly as foretold: the branch lit up, Qatada walked home in its light, and drove out the black thing (a snake or evil spirit).

### The Swords of the Companions

On the day of Badr, the Prophet held a sword and said: "Who will take this sword with its due?" Many Companions sought it, but the Prophet did not give it to them. Then Ukkasha bin Mihsan stood and took it. The Prophet said: "You have taken it — may you fulfill its due and fight with it until your neck is cut." Ukkasha indeed fought valiantly and was martyred in the Battle of Yamama during Abu Bakr's caliphate.

Similarly, the Prophet gave his sword "Zulfiqar" to Ali on the day of Uhud. Another sword, "Qale'a," remained in the Prophet's possession and later came to the caliphs. During Abbasid rule, Caliph Mutasim Billah purchased it from a governor for 200 dinars. Ukkasha's sword was named "Aun." Both swords remained as memorials of the Prophet's miracles. (Zurqani)

### The Weeping Pillar (Haneen al-Jiz)

In Masjid Nabawi, there was originally no pulpit (minbar). The Prophet used to lean against a date palm trunk while delivering sermons. When an Ansari woman built a small pulpit of three steps and placed it in the mosque, the Prophet began using it. Suddenly, the trunk began to cry like a child. Some narrations say it moaned like a she-camel.

The Prophet descended from the pulpit, embraced the trunk, and said: "O trunk! If you wish, I can return you to your original place in the garden so you may become a green tree again, bearing fruit forever. Or, if you prefer, I will pray to Allah to make you a tree of Paradise so that Allah's saints may eat your fruit." The trunk replied in a loud voice: "O Messenger of Allah! I desire to become a tree of Paradise so that Allah's saints may eat my fruit and I may attain eternal life." The Prophet said: "I grant your wish."

He then commanded that the trunk be buried under the pulpit or attached to the ceiling — differing narrations reconciled by scholars as two separate events. After the Prophet's time, when the mosque was rebuilt, the trunk was retrieved and dear Companions kept it as a blessed relic.

This hadith of the weeping pillar is considered Mutawatir (mass-transmitted) by scholars. (Shifa)

### The Wild Camel Submits

When the Prophet entered Madina, a wild camel had gone mad and was attacking people. The Companions warned him: "O Messenger of Allah! This camel bites like a dog!" The Prophet said: "I have no fear of it." He walked toward the camel. When the camel saw him, it came forward, bowed its head, and prostrated to him. The Prophet passed his merciful hand over its head and neck — the camel became completely gentle and obedient. He handed it to its owner and said: "Every creature of Allah knows and accepts that I am Allah's Messenger, but the disbelievers among jinn and mankind refuse to acknowledge my prophethood." (Bazzar, Tabarani)

### The Dry Goat Gave Milk

Abdullah bin Mas'ood narrates: "I was a young boy, herding the goats of Uqba bin Abi Mueet in Makka. The Prophet and Abu Bakr passed by me. The Prophet asked: 'O boy! If your goats have milk, give us some to drink.' I said: 'I am not the owner — I am only a shepherd entrusted with them. How can I give milk without the owner's permission?' The Prophet asked: 'Do you have a goat that has never mated with a male?' I said yes, and brought a goat that had never been mounted. The Prophet placed his hand on its udder and said Bismillah. The udder filled with milk. He milked it, and both he and Abu Bakr drank. Then he commanded the udder: 'Return to your original state,' and it did."

Abdullah bin Mas'ood said: "I then asked: 'O Messenger of Allah! Teach me this speech from the Quran.' The Prophet said: 'You are an intelligent boy.' I learned 70 surahs from the Prophet's blessed mouth. And this miracle played a great role in my accepting Islam." (Tabqaat Ibn Sa'd)

### The Preaching Wolf

Abu Huraira narrates: "A wolf seized a goat, but the shepherd attacked the wolf and rescued the goat. The wolf fled, sat on a hill, and said: 'O shepherd! Allah provided me my sustenance, but you snatched it from me!' The shepherd was astonished: 'By Allah! I have never seen anything more amazing — a wolf speaking Arabic!' The wolf said: 'By the One who created me, there is a greater wonder: in Madina, a man is teaching people the truth. You should go to him.'"

The shepherd came to the Prophet, narrated the incident, and embraced Islam. The Prophet said: "Go back — you will find all your goats safe." When the shepherd returned, he was amazed to see the wolf guarding the goats, and none had gone missing. He slaughtered a goat for the wolf, who ate it and left. (Zurqani)

### The Lizard That Testified

Abdullah bin Umar narrates that a Bedouin from Banu Sulaim passed by the Prophet's gathering, holding a lizard (goh) he had caught. When the Bedouin asked who that person was, people told him it was Muhammad. The Bedouin approached and asked: "O Muhammad! Do you claim to be a prophet?" The Prophet said: "Yes." The Bedouin said: "Then let this lizard testify for you." The Prophet placed the lizard on the ground, and it spoke in clear Arabic: "I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, and that you, O Prophet, are the Seal of the Prophets."

The Bedouin was so moved that he immediately accepted Islam and said: "O Messenger of Allah! When I came to you, there was no one I disliked more than you, but now you are dearer to me than my children and even my own life." The Prophet said: "Praise be to Allah who has guided you to a religion that will always prevail and never be defeated." (Tabarani, Behaqi, Hakim)

### Observation

Hundreds of such miracles are recorded. These few accounts demonstrate beyond doubt that all animals on earth know, recognize, and accept the Holy Prophet as the final messenger, and every creature is obedient to his command.

### Miracles in the Human Realm

### Multiplication of Food

The entire world knows that the early period of Islam was spent in extreme poverty and hunger. Many days passed without the Muslims having anything to eat. In such dire circumstances, the miracle of the Prophet multiplying food came to the rescue of these destitute Muslims.

Sayyidina Eisa (Alaihissalam) fed several hundred people with seven loaves and seven fish — undoubtedly a great miracle mentioned in both Injil and Quran. But the hand of the Mercy for the Worlds produced such miraculous abundance hundreds of times.

**Abu Talha's Feast:**

Anas narrates: "When the verse 'O you who believe! Feed your brothers before the day when there is no trading or friendship' was revealed, the Prophet asked: 'O Abu Talha! Did your mother send food?' Abu Talha's wife, Umme Sulaim, had prepared a small amount of food with a few dates and sent it. The Prophet took his Companions to Abu Talha's house. Abu Talha received them warmly. The Prophet asked Umme Sulaim: 'Bring what you have.' She brought those few loaves. The Prophet commanded that they be broken into crumbs. He blessed the food, and the entire company ate until full, with food remaining." (Bukhari)

**The Date Debt Miraculously Paid:**

Jabir narrates: "My father was martyred at Uhud, leaving me with debts. I came to the Prophet and explained my situation. The Prophet went to my date store, walked around one heap of dates, prayed, and then sat on it. Such baraka (blessing) appeared that all debts were paid, and as many dates remained as were given to the creditors." (Bukhari)

**Abu Huraira's Bag of Dates:**

Abu Huraira says: "I brought some dates to the Prophet and asked him to pray for baraka. The Prophet gathered them, prayed, and said: 'Keep them in your bag. Whenever you need, take from them, but never empty the bag completely.' I continued eating from that bag and feeding others for 30 years. After the Prophet's departure, during Usman's caliphate, when I was governor, I still had those dates. When I left my post, the bag was still full." (Ahmad)

**The Butter Container:**

Umme Malik would send a container of butter to the Prophet as a gift. Such extraordinary baraka appeared in it that whenever her sons asked for curry and there was none, she would take butter from that container. For a long time, she kept taking butter from it. One day, she squeezed the container completely dry. When she came to the Prophet, he asked: "Did you squeeze the container?" She said yes. He said: "If you had not squeezed it and left it as it was, butter would have continued to come from it forever." (Muslim)

**The Blessed Bowl:**

Samura narrates: "We were with the Prophet in a gathering when he asked for food. Each person brought whatever little they had, and soon there was a bowl of food large enough for a goat to sit in. But 1,400 people ate from it until full, and also filled their bags. After eating, the Prophet asked for water. A companion brought a small vessel of water. The Prophet poured it into the bowl and placed his blessed hand in it — 1,400 people performed ablution from that water." (Muslim)

**The Blessed Liver:**

In a journey with 130 Companions, the Prophet asked if anyone had food. A man brought some flour, which was kneaded. Then a large, robust goat was brought. The Prophet bought it, had it slaughtered and its liver roasted. All 130 men ate from that liver until full, with much remaining. (Muslim)

**Ashaab al-Suffa Fed:**

Abu Huraira once brought some food to the Prophet, but the Prophet commanded him to call all the Ashaab al-Suffa (the poor Companions who lived in the mosque). Abu Huraira thought: "There are so many and so little food." But the Prophet kept commanding, and Abu Huraira kept calling them. All 80 people ate until full, and the bowl remained full. Then the Prophet ate what remained. (Bukhari)

### Healing Miracles

**Eye of Hazrat Ali:**

We have already mentioned in the account of Khaibar that Ali had severe eye pain — so severe that Salamah bin Akwa had to lead him by the hand. The Prophet applied his blessed saliva to Ali's eyes and prayed, whereupon they were instantly cured, as if he had never suffered from any ailment.

**Qatada's Eye Restored:**

At Uhud, an arrow struck Qatada bin Noman's eye, causing it to come out onto his cheek. He ran to the Prophet, who placed the eye back into its socket with his blessed hand and passed it over. The eye was completely restored and was even better than before. The Prophet also prayed for him to be among the people of Paradise, and he was blessed with both favors. His descendants would pride themselves: "Our grandfather's eye was healed by the Prophet's blessed hand."

**Broken Leg Healed:**

In Bukhari, a lengthy hadith mentions that when Abdullah bin Ateeq went to assassinate the Jewish leader Abu Rafi, he fell from a ladder, breaking his leg. His companions brought him to the Prophet. After hearing the full account, the Prophet passed his blessed hand over the broken leg — it was instantly cured, as if it had never been injured.

**Sword Wound Healed:**

At Khaibar, Salamah bin Akwa received a deep sword wound on his leg. He immediately came to the Prophet, who blew on the wound three times — it healed instantly. (Bukhari)

**The Mute Child Began to Speak:**

During the Farewell Pilgrimage, a woman from the tribe of Khas'am brought her child to the Prophet and said: "O Messenger of Allah! This is my only son, and he cannot speak." The Prophet asked for water, washed his hands, and rinsed his mouth. He said: "Give this water to the child to drink and sprinkle on him." The following year, the woman came and told people that her son had been cured and was speaking. (Ibn Majah)

**The Epileptic Boy Cured:**

A woman brought her son who suffered from epileptic fits. The Prophet passed his merciful hand over the boy's chest and prayed. The boy had a violent bout of vomiting, and a black puppy-like creature emerged. The boy was completely cured. (Mishkat)

**Burns Healed:**

Muhammad bin Hatib was a Companion who as a child fell into a fire from his mother's lap and was badly burned. His mother brought him to the Prophet, who applied his blessed saliva to the burns and prayed. The mother said: "I had not even lifted the child before the wound was completely healed." (Musnad Ahmad)

**Memory Restored:**

In multiple narrations in Bukhari, Abu Huraira complained of weak memory. The Prophet said: "Spread out your cloak." He spread it. The Prophet passed his hand over it and said: "Now gather it." He did so. From that day, Abu Huraira never forgot any hadith he heard. (Bukhari)
