## Chapter 19: The Holy Household (Mutalliqeen-e-Risaalat)

### The Pure Wives (Azwaaje Mutahharat)

Due to the blessed marriage of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, his pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) hold a very high rank. Many clear verses of the Quran were revealed concerning their glory, mentioning their greatness and exalted status. Allah, the Holy and Pure, says in the Holy Quran:

> يَا نِسَاءَ النَّبِيِّ لَسْتُنَّ كَأَحَدٍ مِّنَ النِّسَاءِ إِنِ اتَّقَيْتُنَّ

**Translation:** "O wives of the Prophet! You are not like any other women, if you fear Allah." (Al-Ahzab: 32)

In another verse He says:

> النَّبِيُّ أَوْلَىٰ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ مِنْ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَأَزْوَاجُهُ أُمَّهَاتُهُمْ

**Translation:** "The Prophet is closer to the believers than their own selves, and his wives are their mothers." (Al-Ahzab: 6)

It is a unanimously agreed-upon matter of the Ummah that the wives of the Holy Prophet ﷺ are like real mothers in two respects.

First: marriage to them is permanently forbidden for anyone. Second: honoring and respecting them is obligatory upon every member of the Ummah, just as it is for one's real mother — rather, even more so. However, in matters of gaze and seclusion, the ruling regarding the pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) is not the same as that of a real mother. Because Allah Almighty says in the Holy Quran:

> وَإِذَا سَأَلْتُمُوهُنَّ مَتَاعًا فَاسْأَلُوهُنَّ مِن وَرَاءِ حِجَابٍ

**Translation:** "And when you ask them (the Prophet's wives) for something, ask them from behind a curtain." (Al-Ahzab: 53)

A Muslim can see his real mother and can sit and talk with her in private, but for every Muslim, hijab (purdah) is obligatory with the holy wives of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, and sitting or meeting with them in seclusion is forbidden.

Similarly, the parents of a real mother are the grandmother and grandfather of the children, and the real mother's brothers and sisters are the maternal uncles and aunts. But the parents of the pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) are not the grandmother and grandfather of the Ummah, nor are their brothers and sisters the maternal uncles and aunts of the Ummah.

This ruling applies to all the pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) whom the Holy Prophet ﷺ married, whether they passed away before him or after him. Each and every one of them is a mother of the Ummah, deserving of greater honor and respect for every member of the Ummah than their own real mother. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 216)

### The Number of Pure Wives and the Order of Their Marriages

Historians differ somewhat on the total number, but there is no disagreement about eleven Mothers of the Believers (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn). Among them, Sayyida Khadija and Sayyida Zainab bint Khuzaima (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) had passed away during the Prophet's own lifetime, while 9 wives were present at the time of the Prophet's ﷺ blessed passing.

Among these 11 mothers of the Ummah, 6 were from the noble houses of the Quraish. Their blessed names are:

1. **Khadija bint Khuwailad**
2. **Ayesha bint Abu Bakr Siddique**
3. **Hafsa bint Umar Farooq**
4. **Umm Habiba bint Abu Sufyan**
5. **Umm Salma bint Abu Umayya**
6. **Sauda bint Zam'aa** (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn)

And 4 pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) were not from the Quraish but belonged to other Arab tribes:

1. **Zainab bint Jahsh**
2. **Maymuna bint Harith**
3. **Zainab bint Khuzaima** — "Ummul Masakeen"
4. **Juwayriyya bint Harith**

And one wife, **Safiyya bint Huyaiy**, was not of Arab descent but was a noble woman of the tribe of Banu Israel.

There is also no disagreement among historians that the Holy Prophet ﷺ first married Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha), and as long as she lived, he did not contract marriage with any other woman. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, pp. 218-219)

### Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha)

She is the first companion of life of the Holy Prophet ﷺ. Her father's name is Khuwailad bin Asad, and her mother's name is bint Zaida. She was a highly respected and extremely wealthy lady of the Quraish tribe. We have already written in the third chapter of this book that the people of Makka, on account of her purity and chastity, remembered her by the title "Taahira" (the Pure).

Observing the character, habits, beauty of form, and perfection of character of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, she herself expressed the desire to marry him, and then the marriage was duly solemnized, the detailed account of which has already passed.

Allama Ibn Atheer and Imam Zahbi state that the entire Ummah agrees that she was the first to believe in the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. In the early days of Islam, when waves of opposition against the Prophet ﷺ were rising from all sides, in such difficult times, she alone was the one who, as the Prophet's comforting companion, became the cause of solace for his heart. With what courage and steadfastness she faced dangers and hardships in those fearful and perilous times, and in what manner she presented her sacrifice with body, heart, and wealth at the court of prophethood — in this distinction she holds a special superiority over all the pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn). Consequently, Waliyyuddin Iraqi states that the correct saying and the chosen school of thought is that among the Mothers of the Believers, Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) is the most virtuous.

Several hadith have also been narrated concerning her virtues. Sayyidina Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) narrates that Sayyidina Jibreel (Alaihissalam) came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and said: "O Muhammad (ﷺ)! This is Khadija who is coming to you with a vessel containing food. When she comes to you, give her the greetings of her Lord and my greetings, and give her the glad tidings that in Paradise she has a house made of pearls in which there will be neither noise nor any hardship." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 539)

Imam Ahmad, Abu Dawood, and Nasa'i narrate from Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) that the most excellent women of Paradise are Sayyida Khadija, Sayyida Fatima, Sayyida Maryam, and Sayyida Aasiya (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn). (Zurqani, Vol. 3, pp. 223-224)

Similarly, it is narrated that once when Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) heard the Holy Prophet ﷺ praising Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) excessively, she felt jealous and said: "Now Allah has given you a better wife than her." Hearing this, the Prophet ﷺ said: "No, by Allah's oath! I have not found a better wife than Khadija. When everyone disbelieved in me, she believed in me. When everyone rejected me, she affirmed me. When no one was willing to give me anything, she gave me all her wealth, and from her womb Allah gave me children." (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 224)

Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) says: "Among the pure wives, I felt the most jealousy toward Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha), even though I had never seen her. The reason for my jealousy was that the Holy Prophet ﷺ would mention her virtues very frequently, and it often happened that when the Prophet ﷺ slaughtered a goat, he would send some meat to the friends of Khadija. This used to annoy me, and sometimes I would say: 'Khadija was nothing but a wife in this world.'"

Hearing this remark from me, the Prophet would say: "Yes, yes, indeed she was. It was from her womb that Allah gave me children." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 539)

Imam Tabarani narrates from Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) a hadith that the Holy Prophet ﷺ fed Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) grapes of Paradise in this world. Imam Suhaili has also narrated this hadith. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 226)

Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) remained honored with the service of the Holy Prophet ﷺ for 25 years. Three years before the Hijrah, at the age of 65, she passed away in the month of Ramadan in the honored city of Makka. The Holy Prophet ﷺ himself descended into her grave in the famous cemetery of Makka, Hajoon (Jannatul Ma'ala), and laid her to rest with his own blessed hands. Since the command for the funeral prayer (Janaza) had not yet been revealed at that time, the Prophet ﷺ did not lead her funeral prayer. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 227)

### Sayyida Sauda (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha)

Her father's name is "Zam'aa" and her mother's name is Shamoos bint Qais bin Amr. She was first married to her paternal cousin Sakran bin Amr. Both husband and wife accepted Islam in the early days of Islam, and both also migrated to Abyssinia in the second migration. When they returned from Abyssinia to Makka, her husband Sakran bin Amr (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) passed away, and she became a widow. She had a son named "Abdurrahman."

Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) states that Sayyida Sauda (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) saw a dream in which the Holy Prophet ﷺ was walking toward her on foot and placed his blessed foot on her neck. When Sayyida Sauda narrated this dream to her husband, he said: "If your dream is true, then I will die very soon, and the Holy Prophet ﷺ will marry you." The next night, Sayyida Sauda saw another dream: a moon fell and landed on her chest. In the morning, she mentioned this dream to her husband as well. Her husband, Sayyidina Sakran (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu), was startled and said: "If this dream of yours is true, then I will die very shortly, and after me you will marry the Holy Prophet ﷺ." And so it happened — Sayyidina Sakran fell ill that very day and passed away after a few days. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 227)

After the passing of Sayyida Khadija (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha), the Holy Prophet ﷺ remained extremely grieved and sad all the time. Seeing this, Sayyida Khaula bint Hakeem (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) submitted a proposal to the Holy Prophet ﷺ: "O Messenger of Allah! You should marry Sayyida Sauda (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) so that your household may flourish and the companionship of a loyal and serving wife may alleviate your grief." The Prophet ﷺ accepted her sincere advice. Consequently, Sayyida Khaula (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) spoke with Sayyida Sauda's father and arranged the match.

The marriage took place, and she entered the fold of the Mothers of the Believers. Throughout her life, she remained honored with the distinction of being the Prophet's wife and remained loyal and devoted to him with intense love and devotion. She was very generous and open-handed. Once, Sayyidina Umar (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) sent a bag filled with dirhams to her. She asked: "What is this?" The bearer said: "These are dirhams." She said: "Are dirhams sent in a date-bag?" Saying this, she immediately distributed all those dirhams among the poor and needy of Madina.

She narrated 5 hadith, which are recorded in famous hadith books. One of these hadith is also found in Sahih Bukhari. Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas and Sayyidina Yahya bin Abdurrahman (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) are among her most prominent students.

There are different and contradictory opinions regarding the year of her death. Imam Zahbi and Imam Bukhari consider the narration correct that she passed away in the last period of Sayyidina Umar's caliphate, in the year 23 AH in Madina. However, Waqidi gives preference to the view that her death occurred in 54 AH. The author of Akmaal has also recorded her year of death as Shawwal 54 AH. But Allama Ibn Hajar Asqalani, in his book Taqribut Tahzeeb, has written that her death occurred in Shawwal 55 AH. And Allah knows best. (Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 229)

### Sayyida Ayesha Siddiqa (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha)

She is the light of the eyes and virtuous daughter of the Commander of the Faithful, Sayyidina Abu Bakr Siddique (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu). Her honored mother's name is "Umme Rooman." She was 6 years old when the Holy Prophet ﷺ, in the 10th year of the proclamation of prophethood, in the month of Shawwal, 3 years before the Hijrah, married her. In Shawwal of 2 AH, she entered the prophetic household in Madina and remained honored with the Prophet's companionship for 9 years. Among the pure wives, she alone was a virgin and was the most beloved wife in the court of prophethood.

The Holy Prophet ﷺ said about her: "Revelation has never come to me while I was under the blanket of any wife except Ayesha. When she sleeps with me on the bed of prophethood, even in that state, divine revelation descends upon me." (Bukhari, Vol. 1, p. 532)

Bukhari and Muslim narrate that the Holy Prophet ﷺ said to Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha): "For three nights, I kept seeing in my dream that an angel was bringing you wrapped in a silk cloth and placing you before me, saying, 'She is your wife.' When I removed the cloth and looked at your face, it was you. I said to myself, 'If this dream is from Allah, then He will surely fulfill it.'" (Mishkat, Vol. 2, p. 573)

In the sciences of jurisprudence and hadith, her rank among the pure wives (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhunn) is very high. She narrated 2210 hadith from the Holy Prophet ﷺ. Among the hadith narrated by her, 174 are found in both Bukhari and Muslim, 54 are found only in Bukhari, and 68 are those that only Imam Muslim recorded in his book Sahih Muslim. Besides these, the remaining hadith are recorded in other books of hadith.

Ibn Sa'ad has narrated from Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) that she herself used to say: "I have been granted 10 distinctions over all the pure wives that no other pure wife has received."

1. The Holy Prophet ﷺ did not marry any other virgin except me.
2. Among the pure wives, there is no one whose both parents were Muhajir (emigrants) except me.
3. Allah revealed from heaven in the Quran the declaration of my innocence and purity.
4. Before marriage, Sayyidina Jibreel (Alaihissalam) brought my picture in a silk cloth and showed it to the Holy Prophet ﷺ, and he saw me in his dreams for three nights.
5. The Holy Prophet ﷺ and I used to perform Ghusl (ritual bath) from the same vessel — this honor was not granted to any other pure wife except me.
6. The Holy Prophet ﷺ used to pray Tahajjud while I lay before him. No other Mother of the Believers was honored with such gracious love from the Holy Prophet ﷺ.
7. I used to sleep with the Holy Prophet ﷺ under the same blanket, and divine revelation would descend upon him. This is an honor from Allah that no other pure wife of the Holy Prophet ﷺ received besides me.
8. At the time of the Prophet's blessed passing, I was sitting holding him in my lap, and his blessed head was between my chest and throat, and in that state the Holy Prophet ﷺ passed away.
9. The Holy Prophet ﷺ passed away on the day of my turn.
10. The blessed grave of the Holy Prophet ﷺ was made in my house specifically.

(Zurqani, Vol. 3, p. 323)

Her rank in worship was also very eminent. Her nephew, Imam Qasim bin Muhammad bin Abu Bakr Siddique (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) states that Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) was regular in offering Tahajjud prayer daily without fail and often observed fasting as well.

In matters of generosity, charity, and good works, she was particularly distinguished among all the Mothers of the Believers. Umme Surrah (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) says: "I was with Sayyida Ayesha when one lakh dirhams arrived. She immediately distributed all those dirhams among the people and did not keep a single dirham at home. That day she was fasting. I said: 'You could have kept one dirham to buy meat for breaking your fast.' She replied: 'If you had mentioned this earlier, I would have ordered meat for one dirham.'"

Sayyidina Urwah bin Zubair (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma), who was her nephew, says: "Apart from jurisprudence and hadith, I have not found anyone who knew Arabic poetry better than Sayyida Ayesha. During conversation, she would recite some couplet at every occasion, which was always very apt."

She was also highly skilled in the science of medicine and the treatment of the sick. Sayyidina Urwah bin Zubair (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) says: "One day I said in amazement: 'O mother! I am amazed at your knowledge of hadith and jurisprudence, but I am even more amazed at your knowledge of medicine. Where did you learn it?' Hearing this, Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) patted my shoulder and said: 'O Urwah! Whenever the Holy Prophet ﷺ fell ill near the end of his life, delegations of Arabs would come from all directions and recommend various treatments. I learned medicine from there.'"

She passed away on Tuesday, the 17th of Ramadan, 57 or 58 AH, at the age of 67, in Madina. Sayyidina Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) led her funeral prayer. After the Isha prayer, she was laid to rest in Jannatul Baqi at night.
