## Chapter 6: The First Year of Hijra — 1 AH

### Masjid Quba

The first task in Quba was the construction of a mosque. For this purpose, the Beloved ﷺ selected a piece of land belonging to Hazrat Kulsum bin Hadam where the dates of the Amr bin Auf family used to be dried. At this very place, the Beloved ﷺ laid the foundation of a mosque with his own blessed hands. This is the same mosque that is famous today as Masjid Quba, about which this verse of the Quran was revealed:

*"Indeed, the mosque whose foundation was laid from the first day upon piety is more worthy that you stand in it. In it are men who love to purify themselves, and Allah loves those who purify themselves."* (At-Taubah: 108)

In the construction of this blessed mosque, the Beloved ﷺ himself participated alongside the esteemed Companions, carrying large stones with his own blessed hands to the extent that his blessed back would bend under their weight. If any of his self-sacrificing Companions submitted, "O Messenger of Allah! May our parents be sacrificed for you. Leave it, we will carry it," the Beloved ﷺ would leave it to please him, but would pick up another stone of the same weight and bring it himself to place in the structure. To create enthusiasm and fervour for the construction work, the Beloved ﷺ would recite these verses of Hazrat Abdullah bin Rawaha in unison with the Companions:

*"Successful is the one who builds a mosque, and recites the Quran sitting and standing, and does not spend the night sleeping."* (`Wafaul Wafa, 1/180`)

### Masjidul Jumuah

After completing the construction of Masjid Quba in a stay of 14 or 24 days, the Beloved ﷺ set out from Quba towards the city of Madina on a Friday. On the way, he offered the first Friday prayer at the mosque of the Bani Saalim tribe. This is the same mosque that is still famous today as Masjidul Jumuah.

When the people of the city came to know, people from all directions, driven by passionate eagerness, rushed to welcome him with longing. The relatives of the Beloved's grandfather Abdul Muttalib from the maternal side — Banu Najjar — armed themselves and came in groups, walking with ecstatic steps all the way to the city of Quba with their swords drawn.

The Beloved expressed gratitude for the love of all the tribes along the way and continued on, giving blessings and prayers to everyone. As he neared the city, the fervour and excitement of the people of Madina reached such a pitch that the veiled ladies climbed onto the roofs of their houses and began reciting these welcoming verses:

*"The full moon has risen upon us from the valleys of Wida. Gratitude to Allah is incumbent upon us as long as any supplicant continues to supplicate to Allah."*

*"O the exalted one who has been sent among us! You have brought a religion worthy of obedience. With your arrival, Madina has been honoured. Welcome, O best of callers!"*

*"We have donned Yemeni garments, although before this we used to wear patched clothes. May Allah continue to send blessings upon you as long as those who strive for Allah continue to strive."*

The little girls of Madina, swaying in joy and playing the oud and duff, sang this song:

*"We are the girls of Banu Najjar. How wonderful that Hazrat Muhammad has become our neighbour."*

The Master of the Prophets ﷺ was moved by the joy and deep love of these girls and asked, "O girls! Do you love me?" The girls said in one voice, "Yes! Yes!" Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ smiled with happiness and said, "I too love you."

Small boys and young children ran in groups through the streets of Madina, crying out in joy at the arrival of the Beloved ﷺ. The Companion of the Messenger, Hazrat Bara bin Aazib, says: "The happiness, delight, lights and radiances that appeared after the arrival of the Chief of the Worlds in Madina had never appeared before nor after."

### The House of Abu Ayyub Ansari

All the Ansar tribes along the route, with immense joy, would grab the reins of the she-camel and submit, "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! Honour our homes with your presence." But the Beloved would tell all these devotees, "Let go of my she-camel's reins. Wherever Allah wills, my she-camel will sit down."

So at the place where today the blessed Masjid Nabawi stands, near the house of Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari, the Beloved's she-camel sat down. With the Beloved's permission, Hazrat Ayyub Ansari took his belongings to his home, and the Beloved stayed at his house. Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari offered the upper floor, but the Beloved ﷺ, considering the convenience of visitors, chose the lower floor.

Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari would send food to the Beloved at both times, and he and his wife would eat the leftover food as a blessing. Wherever the impression of the Beloved's fingers was on the food, Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari would pick that morsel for blessings. In every word and action, he demonstrated boundless respect, reverence, devotion and self-sacrifice.

Once, a water pitcher broke on the upper floor. Concerned that water might seep down to the lower floor and cause any discomfort to the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, Hazrat Abu Ayyub dried all the water with his own blanket. This was the only blanket in the house, which became wet. The husband and wife endured the cold all night but could not bear even the slightest discomfort to the Beloved ﷺ.

Hazrat Abu Ayyub Ansari had the honour of hosting the Master of the Prophets ﷺ for seven months. When the blessed Masjid Nabawi and the rooms around it were ready, the Beloved ﷺ moved into those rooms with his pure wives. (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib`)

### The Conversion of Hazrat Abdullah bin Salam

Hazrat Abdullah bin Salam was the greatest scholar of the Jews in Madina. He himself narrates: "When the Beloved ﷺ migrated from Makka and came to Madina, and people came from all directions in crowds to visit him, I too presented myself in his sacred service. As soon as my eyes fell on the prophetic countenance, my heart decided at first glance that this face could not be the face of a liar. Then the Beloved said in his sermon:

'O people! Spread the greeting of Salam, feed food, maintain family ties, and pray at night when people are asleep.'

Hazrat Abdullah bin Salam says: 'I saw the Beloved once and heard these four sentences, and I was so affected that the world of my heart changed entirely, and I became blessed with Islam.' The acceptance of Islam by Hazrat Abdullah bin Salam was such a major event that it caused turmoil among the Jews of Madina." (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

### The Beloved's Family Arrives in Madina

While the Master of the Prophets was still staying at Hazrat Abu Ayyub's house, he sent his slave Zaid bin Harisa and Hazrat Abu Raafe to Makka with 500 dirhams and two camels, so that they could bring the Beloved's family to Madina. So these two went and brought the Beloved's two daughters, Hazrat Fatima and Hazrat Umme Kulsum, his honourable wife Hazrat Sauda, Hazrat Osama bin Zaid, and Hazrat Umme Ayman to Madina.

The Beloved's daughter Hazrat Zainab could not come because her husband Hazrat Abul Aas bin Arrabi stopped her in Makka. The Beloved's daughter Hazrat Ruqayya was in Habsha with her husband Hazrat Usman Ghani. Along with these people, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique's son Hazrat Abdullah also brought his family from Makka to Madina. Among them was Hazrat Bibi Aisha. All these people stayed at the house of Hazrat Harisa bin Nomaan upon arriving in Madina.

### The Construction of Masjid Nabawi

There was no place in Madina where Muslims could offer congregational prayer, so the construction of a mosque was essential. Near the Beloved's place of stay was a garden of Banu Najjar. The Beloved ﷺ wanted to purchase that garden for constructing a mosque. The owners said, "O Messenger of Allah! We will take its price (reward) from Allah Himself," and offered the land for the mosque for free. But since this land actually belonged to two orphans, the Beloved sent for them. Those orphan children also wished to donate the land for the mosque, but the Beloved ﷺ did not approve of this. Therefore, he paid its price from the wealth of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

On this land, there were some trees, some ruins, and some graves of polytheists. The Beloved ordered the trees to be cut down and the graves of the polytheists to be dug up and removed. Then the land was levelled, and the Beloved himself laid the foundation of the mosque with his own blessed hands. The walls were made of unbaked bricks, and the roof was made of date-palm leaves on date-palm trunks. When it rained, the roof would leak.

In the construction of this mosque, the Beloved ﷺ himself, along with the Companions, carried mud bricks and recited this couplet in rajaz metre to inspire the Companions:

*"O Allah! Goodness is only the goodness of the Hereafter. So O Allah! Forgive the Ansar and the Muhajireen."*

The name of this mosque is Masjid Nabawi. This mosque was free from all worldly ornamentation and was a true and correct depiction of the simplicity of Islam. The first building of this mosque was 60 cubits long and 54 cubits wide. Its Qibla was initially towards Baitul Muqaddas, but when the Qibla was changed towards the Kaaba, a new door was made on the northern side. After this, in different periods, Masjid Nabawi was renovated and expanded.

On one side of the mosque was a platform with a roof made of date-palm leaves. This platform was called "Suffa". The Companions who did not have homes would sleep and sit on this platform. These people are known as "Ashaab-e-Suffa". (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

### The Rooms of the Pure Wives

Adjacent to Masjid Nabawi, the Beloved ﷺ also built rooms for his pure wives. As new pure wives came, additional rooms were built. The walls were of unbaked bricks, 10 cubits long and 6-7 cubits wide. The roof was of date-palm leaves, so low that a standing man could touch it. The doors had no wooden panels; curtains of blankets or matting hung instead. (`Tabqaat Ibne Saad`)

Allahu Akbar! Such was the house of the Emperor of Both Worlds, whose threshold was a source of blessing and pride even for Jibreel to kiss. Allah Allah! The King of the Two Universes, whom the Creator of the Universe made His guest and seated on the Throne of the Exalted Firmament, on whose head He placed the crown of His love, gave him the keys to the treasures of the earth, made him the master of all kinds of authority in the universe — every command of whose tongue was the key to "Be!", whose one glance of grace gave the reins of the fate of the nations of the world into the hands of those whose hands used to hold the reins of camels. Allahu Akbar! Such was the condition of the harem of that Emperor of Prophethood who is the Sultan of Both Worlds and the King of the Two Universes. O sun! Speak, O moon! Tell — you have circled the earth countless times, but have your eyes ever seen such a scene of simplicity anywhere?

### Houses for the Muhajireen

The Muhajireen had left everything in Makka and come to Madina. The Beloved ﷺ arranged for their accommodation in the vicinity of Masjid Nabawi as well. The Ansar made great sacrifices. With great generosity, they gave their homes and lands to their Muhajir brothers and provided all kinds of assistance in the construction of houses, which brought great ease to the Muhajireen.

The first Ansari who offered his house as a gift to the Beloved ﷺ was the fortunate Hazrat Harisa bin Nomaan. The rooms of the pure wives were built on the land of Hazrat Harisa bin Nomaan.

### Hazrat Aisha's Departure to the Beloved's Home

Hazrat Aisha's marriage to the Beloved ﷺ had already taken place in Makka before the migration. However, her departure to the Beloved's home took place in Madina in the first year of Hijra. The Beloved ﷺ hosted a walima feast with a cup of milk. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

### The Beginning of the Adhan

The construction of Masjid Nabawi was complete, but there was no means to gather people for prayer times to organize congregational prayer. In this regard, the Beloved ﷺ consulted with the esteemed Companions. Some suggested lighting a fire at prayer times, while others proposed using a bell (naqoos). But the Master of the Prophets did not approve of these methods of non-Muslims.

Hazrat Umar proposed that a person should be sent at every prayer time to announce the prayer throughout the Muslim settlement. The Beloved ﷺ approved this proposal and commanded Hazrat Bilal to call the people at prayer times. So he would announce "Assalato Jaamia" (Prayer is gathering) at the times of the five prayers.

During this period, a Companion, Hazrat Abdullah bin Zaid Ansari, saw in a dream someone reciting the words of the Shariah-adhan. After this, the Beloved ﷺ and Hazrat Umar and other Companions also saw similar dreams. The Beloved ﷺ accepted this as being from Allah and commanded Hazrat Abdullah bin Zaid, "Teach the words of the adhan to Bilal, for he has a louder voice than you." From that very day, the method of the Shariah-adhan began, which continues to this day and will continue until the Day of Judgement. (`Zurqani`)

### The Brotherhood of Ansar and Muhajireen

The Muhajireen had come to Madina in a state of utter destitution, leaving their families behind completely empty-handed. In a foreign land, they felt the pain of loneliness, strangeness and separation from their families. There is no doubt that the Ansar left no stone unturned in hosting and comforting these Muhajireen. But the Muhajireen did not wish to live for long as dependents of others, for they had always been accustomed to earning with their own hands.

Therefore, it was necessary to make some arrangement to remove the difficulties of the Muhajireen and provide them with a permanent means of livelihood. So the Beloved ﷺ decided to establish a bond of brotherhood between the Ansar and the Muhajireen, making them brothers to each other. This would remove the feeling of loneliness and helplessness from the hearts of the Muhajireen, and by becoming each other's helpers, the problem of the Muhajireen's livelihood would also be solved.

So, after the construction of Masjid Nabawi, one day the Beloved ﷺ gathered the Ansar and Muhajireen at the house of Hazrat Anas bin Malik. At that time, the number of Muhajireen was 45 or 50. The Beloved ﷺ addressed the Ansar and said, "These Muhajireen are your brothers." Then, calling two people at a time from the Ansar and Muhajireen, he said, "You are brothers to each other." As soon as the Beloved gave this command, this bond of brotherhood became exactly like a real brotherly relationship.

The Ansar took the Muhajireen to their homes, brought out every single item, and said, "You are our brother, so half of all these belongings is yours and half is mine." It went so far that Hazrat Saad bin Rabee Ansari, who had been made the brother of Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf, had two wives. Hazrat Saad bin Rabee Ansari said to Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf, "Whichever of my wives you like, I will divorce her, and you may marry her."

Allahu Akbar! There is no doubt that this self-sacrifice of the Ansar was a unique masterpiece whose like can hardly be found in the history of the nations of the world. But what approach the Muhajireen adopted was also a historically exemplary achievement.

Hearing Hazrat Saad bin Rabee's sincere offer, Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf thanked him and said, "May Allah Ta'ala bless you with all this wealth and family. Just show me the way to the market." They showed him the way to Madina's famous market, "Qainuqaa". Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf went to the market, bought some ghee and cheese, and kept trading until evening. He continued going to the market daily in this manner, and within a short time became quite wealthy. He accumulated so much capital that he married and set up his home.

When he presented himself at the court of prophethood, the Beloved ﷺ asked, "How much dower did you give your wife?" He submitted, "Gold equal to five dirhams." The Beloved said, "May Allah Ta'ala grant you blessings. Host a walima feast, even if it is with one goat." (`Bukhari, Bab-ul-Walima`)

Gradually, Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf's business saw such blessings and progress that he himself said, "If I touch dust, it turns to gold." It is narrated that his trade caravan would arrive loaded on 700 camels, and on the day his merchandise reached Madina, there would be a great fanfare.

Following Hazrat Abdurrahman bin Auf's example, other Muhajireen also opened shops. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique traded in cloth, Hazrat Usman began trading in dates, Hazrat Umar also became occupied with trade. Other Muhajireen also started small and large businesses. In short, although the Ansar's homes were a permanent guesthouse for the Muhajireen, the Muhajireen did not remain a burden on the Ansar for long. Through their hard work and immense effort, they soon stood on their own feet.

The famous historian of Islam, Allama Ibne Abdul Barr, says that this pact of brotherhood took place between the Ansar and the Muhajireen. Apart from this, a special pact of brotherhood was also made among the Muhajireen themselves, in which the Beloved ﷺ made one Muhajir the brother of another Muhajir. When brotherhood was established between Hazrat Abu Bakr and Hazrat Umar, Hazrat Talha and Hazrat Zubair, Hazrat Usman and Hazrat Abdurrahman, Hazrat Ali submitted at the court of prophethood: "O Messenger of Allah! You have made every Companion the brother of another. So who is my brother?" The Beloved ﷺ said, "You are my brother in this world and the Hereafter."

### The Treaty with the Jews

In Madina, besides the Ansar, many Jews also lived. There were three tribes of these Jews: Banu Qainuqaa, Banu Nazeer, and Quraiza. They lived around Madina in extremely strong fortresses and fortified settlements. Before the migration, there had always been conflict between the Jews and the Ansar, and that conflict still existed. The two tribes of the Ansar, Aus and Khazraj, had become very weak because the great chiefs and famous warriors of both tribes had killed each other in the famous battle of "Junge Ba'aas". The Jews were always engaged in such schemes and mischief that the two tribes of the Ansar would keep fighting each other and never unite.

For these reasons, the Master of the Prophets felt the need for a treaty regarding the future relationship between the Jews and the Muslims, so that both parties could live in peace and no clash or conflict would occur. So he called the Ansar and the Jews and had a treaty document written, which was signed by both parties.

The summary of the clauses of this treaty is as follows:

(1) The existing practice regarding blood money and ransom will continue as before.
(2) The Jews will have religious freedom. No interference will be made in their religious rites.
(3) Jews and Muslims will maintain friendly conduct.
(4) If a Jew or a Muslim faces war with someone, one party will help the other.
(5) If Madina is attacked, both parties will together confront the attacker.
(6) Neither party will give shelter to the Quraish or their supporters.
(7) If one party makes peace with an enemy, the other party will also be included in that peace settlement. However, religious war will be exempt from this. (`Seerat Ibne Hisham`)

### Prayer for Madina

Since the climate of Madina was not good, various kinds of epidemics and diseases would frequently spread. As a result, many Muhajireen began to fall ill. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique and Hazrat Bilal became afflicted with severe shivering fever. In the intensity of the fever, these people remembered their homeland Makka, cursed the disbelievers of Makka, and recited verses in longing for the mountains and grass of Makka.

On this occasion, the Beloved ﷺ prayed: "O Allah! Instil in our hearts such love for Madina as is the love for Makka, or even more. Bless the climate of Madina with health. Grant blessings in the measures and weights of Madina. And transfer the fever of Madina towards Juhfa." (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

### Hazrat Salman Farsi Becomes a Muslim

Among the incidents of 1 AH, the conversion of Hazrat Salman Farsi is also very important. He was from Persia. His ancestors, and indeed the entire population of his country, were Zoroastrians (fire-worshippers). Disillusioned with his ancestral religion, he set out from his homeland in search of the true religion. But bandits captured him, made him their slave, and then sold him. He was sold several times and remained in the slavery of various people. In this way, he reached Madina.

For some time, he lived as a Christian and also kept contact with the Jews. In this manner, he had acquired considerable knowledge of the Torah and the Gospel. He presented himself at the court of prophethood. On the first day, he brought a tray of fresh dates and said, "This is charity." The Beloved ﷺ said, "Take this away from before me and give it to the poor and needy, for I do not eat charity." The next day, he came with a platter of dates and said, "This is a gift." He placed it before the Beloved. The Beloved ﷺ signalled the Companions to begin eating and himself ate as well.

Meanwhile, Hazrat Salman Farsi looked at the area between the Beloved's shoulders and saw the seal of prophethood. Since he had already read the signs of the Prophet of the Last Age in the Torah and the Gospel, he immediately accepted Islam.

### Increase in the Rakat of Prayers

Until now, the obligatory prayers had only two rakat each. But in the very first year of migration, when the Beloved ﷺ came to Madina, Zuhr, Asr, and Isha were made four rakat each. However, in the state of travel, the same two rakat remained, which is called Qasr (shortening) of prayers in the state of travel. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat`)

### The Demise of Three Self-Sacrificing Companions

In this year, three extremely distinguished and self-sacrificing Companions passed away. They were, in truth, true devotees of Islam and very great supporters and helpers.

**First:** Hazrat Kulsum bin Hadam. He was the fortunate Ansari of Madina in whose house the Master of the Prophets first stayed when he migrated to Quba. Great Muhajir Companions also stayed in his house. He hosted the King of Both Worlds as his guest and served him so well that his name will shine like a star on the pages of the history of prophethood until the Day of Judgement.

**Second:** Hazrat Baraa bin Maroor Ansari. He was the person who was the first to pledge allegiance on the blessed hand of the Beloved in the Second Pledge of Aqba, and he was among the Naqeebs of the Khazraj tribe.

**Third:** Hazrat As'ad bin Jararah Ansari. He participated in both the First and the Second Pledge of Aqba. He was the first person to spread Islam in Madina and deliver the message of Islam to every house.

When these three respected Companions passed away, the hypocrites and the Jews celebrated and began to taunt the Beloved, saying, "If he were a prophet, why would Allah Ta'ala inflict these blows upon him?" At that very time, by Allah's will, two very great chiefs of the disbelievers also died in a state of disgrace. One was Aas bin Waail Sahmi, who was the father of Hazrat Amr bin Al Aas, the conqueror of Egypt. The other was Waleed bin Mugheera, the father of Hazrat Khalid Saifullah.

It is narrated that at the time of his death throes, Waleed bin Mugheera became very restless. He began to writhe, weep, and cry out in distress. Abu Jahl asked, "O uncle! What is the reason for your restlessness and weeping?" Waleed bin Mugheera said, "Nephew, I am weeping so restlessly because I fear that after me, Muhammad's religion will spread in Makka." Hearing this, Abu Sufyan comforted him and said, "Uncle! Do not grieve at all. I am alive. I will not let the religion of Islam spread in Makka."

Abu Sufyan remained firm on this pledge. Until the conquest of Makka, he kept fighting against Islam. But on the day of the conquest of Makka, Abu Sufyan accepted Islam, and then became such a sincere Muslim that he spent his entire life striving in jihad for the support and protection of Islam. In these very battles, his eyes were injured by arrows of the disbelievers and their sight was lost. This is the same Hazrat Abu Sufyan whose son is Hazrat Ameer Muawiya.

### The Birth of Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair

In this same year, 1 AH, Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair was born. He was the first child born among the Muhajireen after the migration. His mother, Hazrat Bibi Asma, the daughter of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, brought him to the court of prophethood right after his birth. The Beloved ﷺ placed him in his lap, chewed a date, and put it into his mouth. Thus, the first food to enter his stomach was the blessed saliva of the Beloved.

The Muslims were extremely happy at the birth of Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubair, because the Jews of Madina used to say, "We have cast such a spell on the Muhajireen that no child will be born to them." (`Zurqani, 1/460`)
