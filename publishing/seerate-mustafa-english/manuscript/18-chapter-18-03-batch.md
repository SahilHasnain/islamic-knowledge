## Chapter 18: The Prophetic Character (Continued)

### Prophetic Medicine (Tibb-e-Nabawi)

The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "O servants of Allah! Use remedies, for Allah has created a cure for every disease except one." The Companions asked: "O Messenger of Allah! Which disease has no cure?" He replied: "Old age." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 25)

Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) narrated that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Among the methods of treatment you use, the best four are: Sa'oot (administering medicine through the nose), Ladood (administering medicine from one side of the mouth), Hijamah (cupping — extracting blood by applying cups), and Mashi (taking a purgative/laxative)." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 26)

The Prophet ﷺ himself used some remedies and informed his Ummah of the properties and benefits of others. We mention a few of these here as a blessing, so that the pages of this brief book may not remain deprived of this important chapter of Prophetic Medicine.

**Ismid (Black Isfahani Kohl):** The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "Keep using Ismid, for it sharpens the eyesight and promotes eyelash growth." (Ibn Majah, p. 257) Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) says: "The Holy Prophet had a kohl container containing Ismid kohl, and he would apply three strokes to each eye every night before sleeping." (Shamayil Tirmizi, p. 5)

**Henna (Mehndi):** When the Prophet got a boil or was pricked by a thorn, he would apply henna to it. (Ibn Majah, p. 258)

**Black Seed (Kalonji / Shooniz / Mungrila):** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Keep using this, for it cures every disease except death." (Ibn Majah, p. 254; Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 848)

**Talbeena (A porridge made of flour, water, honey, and oil):** When someone in the Prophet's household suffered from severe fever, he would order this food to be prepared, saying: "This food strengthens the heart of the grief-stricken and removes illness from the heart just as you remove dirt from your faces." (Ibn Majah, p. 254; Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 849)

**Honey (Asal):** A man came to the Prophet and complained that his brother had diarrhea. The Prophet said: "Give him honey." The man returned and said: "The diarrhea hasn't stopped." The Prophet again said: "Give him honey." The man came a third time, saying: "I have already tried that." The Prophet said: "Allah is true and your brother's stomach is false. Give him honey." The man gave him honey, and he was cured. (Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 848)

The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever licks honey three mornings every month will not be afflicted by any great calamity." (Ibn Majah, p. 255) He also said: "Hold fast to two cures: honey and the Quran." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Vinegar (Sirka):** The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best condiment is vinegar. O Allah! Bless vinegar, for it was the condiment of the prophets. A house that has vinegar will never be in need." (Ibn Majah, p. 246)

**Olive Oil (Rogane Zaitoon):** The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "Use olive oil as a condiment and apply it to your bodies, for it comes from a blessed tree." In another hadith: "Eat olive oil and apply it to your bodies, for it is a blessed thing." (Ibn Majah, p. 246)

**Fattening Remedy (Musammin):** Sayyida Ayesha (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) says: "When my mother intended to send me to the Prophet's house, she tried to fatten me up, but nothing worked. However, when I started eating cucumber with fresh dates, I became well-built." (Ibn Majah, p. 246) Sayyidina Abdullah bin Jafar (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet ﷺ used to eat cucumber with fresh dates." (Ibn Majah, p. 246)

**Night Meal (Asha):** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not skip the night meal. If nothing else is available, eat a handful of dates, for skipping the night meal causes premature aging." (Ibn Majah, p. 248)

**Prevention (Himyah):** The Prophet visited Sayyida Ummul Munzir (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) with Sayyidina Ali (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu). She presented a bunch of fresh and ripe dates, and the Prophet began eating. When Sayyidina Ali also reached for them, the Prophet said: "O Ali! You have just recovered from illness; do not eat these." Then Sayyida Ummul Munzir cooked barley and beetroot, and the Prophet said to Ali: "Eat this, for it is very beneficial for you." (Ibn Majah, p. 254)

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not force your sick to eat or drink; Allah feeds them and gives them drink." (Ibn Majah, p. 254)

**Ginger (Zanjabeel / Sonth):** The king of Rome sent the Prophet a container full of ginger as a gift. The Prophet gave a piece to each of his Companions to eat. Abu Nuaim narrated this in his book "Tibb-e-Nabawi."

**Ajwa Dates:** Among the dates of Madina is a variety called "Ajwa." The Prophet said: "Ajwa is from Paradise, and it is a cure for madness or poison." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Truffle (Kamah / Kakar Mata / Snake's Umbrella):** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Truffle is like the Manna that was sent down to the Children of Israel (likewise free and beneficial), and its water is a cure for the eyes." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Sana (A senna-based medicine):** The Prophet asked Sayyida Asma bint Umais (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha): "What do you use as a laxative?" She said: "Shabram (a purgative)." The Prophet said: "That is a very hot medicine." He ordered her to use Sana instead, saying: "If there was anything that could cure death, it would be Sana." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Sannut:** There is disagreement among commentators about its meaning, but physicians have preferred a specific interpretation: honey that has been kept in a ghee container and has absorbed some effects of the ghee. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Keep using Sana and Sannut, for in them is a cure for all diseases except death." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Poison (Sam):** Sayyidina Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ forbade the use of impure medicine, that is, poison." (Ibn Majah, p. 255)

**Indian Oud (Qist Sheer):** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Use this Indian Oud, for it contains seven cures. For throat inflammation, it should be used as snuff; for pleurisy, it should be taken as a decoction." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Irkunnisa (Sciatica Remedy):** Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) heard the Prophet ﷺ say: "For sciatica, take the tail of a pasture-fed goat, cut it into three pieces, and eat one piece on an empty stomach for three days. This contains a cure for sciatica." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Forbidden Remedies:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has sent down both the disease and the cure, and He has made a cure for every disease. Therefore, treat yourselves, but do not treat yourselves with forbidden things."

**Wine/Intoxicants:** Sayyidina Suwaid bin Tariq (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) asked the Prophet about wine. He forbade it. When asked again, he forbade it. The third time, Suwaid said: "O Prophet of Allah! It is a medicine." The Prophet said: "No, it is a disease." (Abu Dawood, Vol. 2, p. 185)

**Treatment of Wounds:** Sayyidina Sah'l bin Sa'ad Sa'idi (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "During the Battle of Uhud, the Prophet's blessed tooth was broken and his iron helmet was crushed on his head. Sayyida Fatima (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) was washing the blood from his face while Sayyidina Ali (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) was pouring water from a shield. When the bleeding increased, Sayyida Fatima took a piece of a date-palm mat, burned it to ashes, and applied the ashes to the wounds, stopping the bleeding." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Plague (Ta'oon):** The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "It is a punishment that Allah sent upon the Children of Israel. When you hear that plague has broken out in a land, do not enter it. And if plague breaks out in a land where you are, do not flee from it." (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 338)

**Unqualified Physician:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever practices medicine without knowledge of medicine is liable (for any harm caused to the patient)." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Fever:** Someone cursed fever in the Prophet's presence. He said: "Do not curse fever, for it removes sins just as fire removes impurities from iron." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Treatment of Fever:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Fever is from the heat of Hell. Therefore, cool it with water (by drinking and bathing)." (Ibn Majah, p. 256)

**Note:** This treatment is for a specific type of fever common in Arabia, which physicians call bilious fever or heatstroke fever. It is not the treatment for all types of fever. Therefore, this treatment may not be effective for all fevers. One should first have the fever properly diagnosed by a competent physician before undertaking treatment. (And Allah knows best.)

### Prophetic Supplications

Supplications hold a very high status in the court of Allah Almighty. Just as He has placed great effects in medicines, so too has He placed great effects in supplications. Allah Almighty has repeatedly commanded His servants to supplicate, saying: "Call upon Me; I will respond to you."

The Holy Prophet ﷺ also emphasized the importance and benefits of supplications, encouraging his Ummah to make them, saying: "Nothing is more honored in the court of Allah than supplication." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 173) He went so far as to say: "Supplication is the essence of worship," and "Whoever does not supplicate to Allah, Allah becomes angry with him." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 176)

Therefore, alongside Prophetic Medicine, we record here some supplications that were part of the Prophet's practice, whose virtues and benefits he taught his Ummah and instructed them to recite, so that this book may be honored by this sacred chapter of the Prophetic way and Muslims may be enriched by the countless benefits of these supplications in this world and the Hereafter.

**Protection from All Calamities:** The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites this supplication three times in the morning and evening will not be harmed by anything in the world." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 173)

> بِسْمِ اللهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

*"In the name of Allah, with Whose name nothing in the earth or the heavens can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."*

**Supplication for Sleeping:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites this three times before sleeping, Allah will forgive all his sins, even if they are like the leaves of trees or the grains of sand." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 174)

> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ الْعَظِيمَ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيَّ الْقَيُّومَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

*"I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Mighty, besides Whom there is no deity, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining, and I turn to Him in repentance."*

The Holy Prophet used to recite this upon sleeping:

> اللهم بِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

*"O Allah! In Your name I die and live."*

And upon waking:

> الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

*"All praise is for Allah Who gave us life after death, and to Him is the resurrection."*

(Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 177)

**Supplication for Waking at Night:** The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever wakes at night and recites this supplication, then whatever he prays for will be accepted, and if he performs wudu and prays, his prayer will be accepted." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 177)

> لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ

*"There is no deity but Allah alone, He has no partner. To Him belongs the kingdom and all praise, and He has power over all things. Glory be to Allah, all praise is for Allah, there is no deity but Allah, Allah is the Greatest, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah."*

**Supplication for Leaving Home:** The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites this when leaving home will have his difficulties removed, be protected from the evil of enemies, and Satan will distance himself from him." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 180)

> بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ

*"In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah."*

**Supplication for Entering the Market:** The Prophet said: "Whoever recites these words upon entering the market, Allah will record a million good deeds, erase a million sins, and raise a million ranks." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 180)

> لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ وَهُوَ حَيٌّ لَا يَمُوتُ بِيَدِهِ الْخَيْرُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

*"There is no deity but Allah alone, He has no partner. To Him belongs the kingdom and all praise. He gives life and causes death, and He is Ever-Living and does not die. In His hand is all good, and He has power over all things."*

**Supplication for Travel:** Sayyidina Abdullah bin Sarjis (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "When the Prophet set out for travel, he would recite this supplication." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 181)

> اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الْأَهْلِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَبِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ

*"O Allah! You are the Companion in travel and the Successor over family. O Allah! I seek refuge in You from the hardships of travel, from a distressing sight, and from an evil return concerning wealth and family."*

**Supplication for Returning from Travel:** When the Prophet returned to Madina from a journey, he would recite this supplication. (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 182)

> آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ

*"We are returning, repenting, worshipping, and praising our Lord."*

**Supplication for Halting at a Place:** The Prophet of Mercy said: "Whoever halts at a place during travel and recites this, nothing will harm him there." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 181)

> أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

*"I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created."*

**Supplication for Distress:** Sayyidina Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhuma) says: "When the Prophet was afflicted by distress or anxiety, he would recite this supplication." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 181)

> لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ الْعَظِيمُ الْحَلِيمُ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ رَبُّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبُّ الْأَرْضِ وَرَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ

*"There is no deity but Allah, the Mighty, the Forbearing. There is no deity but Allah, the Lord of the Mighty Throne. There is no deity but Allah, the Lord of the heavens and the Lord of the earth and the Lord of the Mighty Throne."*

**Supplication for Seeing Someone Afflicted:** The Prophet said: "Whoever sees a person afflicted by calamity (ill or in difficulty) and recites this, he will be protected from that calamity for his entire life." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 181)

> الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي عَافَانِي مِمَّا ابْتَلَاكَ بِهِ وَفَضَّلَنِي عَلَى كَثِيرٍ مِمَّنْ خَلَقَ تَفْضِيلًا

*"All praise is for Allah who saved me from that which He has afflicted you with, and preferred me greatly above many of those He has created."*

**Supplication for Bidding Farewell:** When the Prophet bid farewell to someone, he would say these words:

> أَسْتَوْدِعُ اللهَ دِينَكَ وَأَمَانَتَكَ وَخَوَاتِيمَ عَمَلِكَ

*"I entrust your religion, your trust, and the last of your deeds to Allah."*

(Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 182)

**Supplication After Eating:** Sayyidina Abu Umama (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "When the dining cloth was lifted from before the Holy Prophet, he would recite this." (Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 183)

> الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ حَمْدًا كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ، غَيْرَ مَكْفِيٍّ وَلَا مُوَدَّعٍ وَلَا مُسْتَغْنًى عَنْهُ رَبَّنَا

*"All praise is for Allah — abundant, pure, and blessed praise — not insufficient, nor departed from, nor dispensed with, O our Lord."*

**Supplication for Wind:** When a windstorm arose, the Prophet would recite:

> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ خَيْرِهَا وَخَيْرِ مَا فِيهَا وَخَيْرِ مَا أُرْسِلَتْ بِهِ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا وَشَرِّ مَا أُرْسِلَتْ بِهِ

*"O Allah! I ask You for its good and the good of what is in it and the good of what it has been sent with, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of what is in it and the evil of what it has been sent with."*

(Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 183)

**Supplication for Thunder:** When thunder and lightning occurred, the Prophet would recite:

> اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَقْتُلْنَا بِغَضَبِكَ وَلَا تُهْلِكْنَا بِعَذَابِكَ وَعَافِنَا قَبْلَ ذَلِكَ

*"O Allah! Do not kill us with Your wrath, nor destroy us with Your punishment, and grant us safety before that."*

(Tirmizi, Vol. 2, p. 183)

**Supplication When Fearing a People:** The Prophet said: "If one fears a people or an army for his life or wealth, he should recite this." (Abu Dawood, Vol. 1, p. 222)

> اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَجْعَلُكَ فِي نُحُورِهِمْ وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شُرُورِهِمْ

*"O Allah! We place You before them (to confront them) and seek refuge in You from their evils."*

**Supplication for Debt Repayment:** Sayyidina Abu Saeed Khudri (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "One day the Prophet entered the mosque and saw Sayyidina Abu Umama Ansari (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) sitting there at a time when prayer was not due. He asked: 'O Abu Umama! Why are you sitting in the mosque at this time?' Abu Umama replied: 'O Messenger of Allah! I am overwhelmed by worries and debts.' The Prophet said: 'Shall I teach you words by which Allah will remove your worries and repay your debts? Recite this morning and evening.'" (Abu Dawood, Vol. 1, p. 224)

> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحُزْنِ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ غَلَبَةِ الدَّيْنِ وَقَهْرِ الرِّجَالِ

*"O Allah! I seek refuge in You from anxiety and grief, from weakness and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from the burden of debt and the oppression of men."*

Sayyidina Abu Umama (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "When I recited this supplication, my worries disappeared and Allah repaid my debts."

**Send Abundant Blessings on Friday:** The Prophet said: "The best of your days is Friday. So send abundant blessings upon me on this day, for your blessings are presented to me." The Companions asked: "O Messenger of Allah! After your blessed body has decayed and turned into bones, how will our blessings be presented to you?" The Prophet replied: "Allah has forbidden the earth to consume the bodies of the prophets." (Abu Dawood, Vol. 1, p. 221)

**Important Note:** This hadith establishes that the sacred bodies of all prophets remain preserved in their graves, and Allah has forbidden the earth from causing any change or decay to their sacred bodies. If this is the state of all prophets, then how could the earth consume the blessed body of the Chief of Prophets, the Chief of Messengers, the Imam of Prophets, and the Seal of Prophets? Therefore, it is the belief of all scholars and saints of the Ummah that the Holy Prophet ﷺ is alive in his blessed grave and, by Allah's command, continues to exercise great powers, resolving the difficulties and answering the calls of his Ummah through his prophetic powers and miraculous strength.

Remember well: whoever holds a belief contrary to this is certainly insolent toward the sacred court, of evil creed, misguided, and outside the fold of Ahl al-Sunnah.

**Supplication Upon Hearing a Rooster:** The Prophet said: "When you hear a rooster crow, ask Allah for His grace, for it has seen an angel." (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 351)

> أَسْأَلُ اللهَ مِنْ فَضْلِهِ

*"I ask Allah for His grace."*

**Supplication Upon Hearing a Donkey:** When you hear a donkey bray, seek refuge in Allah from Satan. (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 351)

> أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ

*"I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan."*

**A Treasure of Paradise:** Sayyidina Abdullah bin Qais (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) says: "The Prophet asked me: 'Shall I guide you to a word that is a treasure of Paradise?' I said: 'O Messenger of Allah! Yes.' He said: 'There is no power nor strength except with Allah (لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ).'" (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 342)

**A Ticket to Paradise:** The Prophet said: "Whoever recites this supplication consistently, Paradise becomes obligatory for him." The supplication is:

> رَضِيتُ بِاللهِ رَبًّا وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللهُ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ رَسُولًا

*"I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muhammad ﷺ as my Messenger."*

(Abu Dawood, Vol. 1, p. 221)

**Sayyidul Istighfar (The Chief of Supplications for Forgiveness):** The Prophet said: "If a Muslim recites this supplication with a firm heart during the day and dies before evening, he will be among the people of Paradise. And if he recites it at night and dies before morning, he will be among the people of Paradise. This is Sayyidul Istighfar:

> اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

*"O Allah! You are my Lord; there is no deity but You. You created me and I am Your servant, and I am upon Your covenant and promise as much as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your favor upon me and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You."*

(Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 933)

**Supplication for Intercourse:** The Prophet said: "If a Muslim recites this before intimate relations with his wife, Satan will never harm any child born from that union." The supplication is:

> بِسْمِ اللهِ اللَّهُمَّ جَنِّبْنَا الشَّيْطَانَ وَجَنِّبِ الشَّيْطَانَ مَا رَزَقْتَنَا

*"In the name of Allah. O Allah! Keep Satan away from us and keep Satan away from what You have bestowed upon us (the child)."*

(Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 945)

**Supplication for Healing:** Abdul Azeez bin Suhaib and Thabit Bunani (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) came to Sayyidina Anas (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu). Thabit said: "O Abu Hamza! I am ill." Sayyidina Anas said: "Shall I perform the healing incantation upon you that the Prophet used to recite for the sick?" He then recited:

> اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ مُذْهِبَ الْبَاسِ اشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي لَا شَافِيَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا

*"O Allah! Lord of mankind, Remover of hardship, heal — You are the Healer, there is no healer but You — with a healing that leaves no illness behind."*

(Bukhari, Vol. 2, p. 855)

**Supplication for Calamity and Receiving Better in Return:** Sayyida Umme Salma (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha), Mother of the Believers, says: "I heard the Prophet say: 'If a Muslim is afflicted by a calamity and recites this, Allah will give him something better than what he lost.'"

> إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ اللَّهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي فِي مُصِيبَتِي وَاخْلُفْ لِي خَيْرًا مِنْهَا

*"Surely, to Allah we belong and to Him we shall return. O Allah! Reward me for my affliction and give me something better than it in return."*

Sayyida Umme Salma (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anha) says: "When my husband Abu Salma (Radi Allahu Ta'ala Anhu) passed away, I thought: 'Who could be better than Abu Salma?' But then I recited this supplication, and Allah gave me a husband better than Abu Salma — the Messenger of Allah ﷺ married me." (Muslim, Vol. 2, p. 300)
