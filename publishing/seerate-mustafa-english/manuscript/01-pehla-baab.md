The genealogy of Huzoor ﷺ up to "Adnan" is established through authentic chains of narration with the agreement of historians. Beyond that, there is considerable difference of opinion regarding the names, and Huzoor ﷺ himself would mention the lineage only up to "Adnan." (`Karmani, with reference to Hashiya Bukhari, Volume 1, Page 543`)

However, all historians are agreed that "Adnan" is from the progeny of Hazrat Ismail <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and Hazrat Ismail <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is the noble son of Hazrat Ibrahim Khaleelullah <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

### Family Nobility

The family and lineage of Huzoor-e-Akram ﷺ is the noblest and most exalted of all the families of the entire world in terms of honor and nobility. This is such a reality that even the worst enemies of Huzoor ﷺ, the disbelievers of Makkah, could never deny it. When Abu Sufyan was still in a state of disbelief, in the grand court of the Roman emperor Heraclius, he acknowledged this truth — that the Noble Prophet ﷺ was of a noble family. (`Sahih Bukhari, Volume 1, Page 4`)

At that time, Abu Sufyan was among the worst enemies of Huzoor ﷺ and would have seized upon any opportunity to find some flaw in the holy person of Huzoor ﷺ in order to lower his standing in the eyes of the Roman emperor. Yet despite this, he could not deny the nobility of the Prophet's ﷺ lineage.

There is a narration in Sahih Muslim that Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> chose "Kinana" from among the progeny of Hazrat Ismail <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and from "Kinana" He chose "Quraish," and from "Quraish" He chose "Bani Hashim," and from "Bani Hashim" He chose me. (`Mishkat, Faza'il-e-Sayyid al-Mursaleen`)

In any case, there is an established reality here:

> *There is none like him in the loftiness of lineage —*
> *No one of honor and lineage, no one of blessings and grace equals him.*

That is, the family of Huzoor-e-Anwar ﷺ is of such exalted rank that no person of honor and lineage, no one of blessings and greatness, can match Huzoor ﷺ.

### Quraish

In the Prophetic lineage of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ, all the noble personalities are distinguished and renowned for their particular virtues and qualities. However, there are certain personalities who shone like stars through their excellence and perfection. Among these gifted personalities is "Fihr bin Malik." His title is "Quraish," and his descendants are called Quraishi or "Quraish."

Fihr bin Malik was called Quraish because "Quraish" is the name of a sea creature of tremendous power that devours other sea animals and always prevails over them, never being defeated. Since Fihr bin Malik, through his courage and God-given strength, dominated all the Arab tribes, all the people of Arabia began to call him by the title "Quraish." Concerning this, the verse of Shamrakh bin Amr Himyari is very famous:

> *Quraish is a creature that dwells in the sea,*
> *And after it, the tribe of Quraish was named Quraish.*

(`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 76`)

The lineage chains of both parents of Huzoor ﷺ meet at "Fihr bin Malik," therefore Huzoor-e-Akram ﷺ is Quraishi from both his father's and mother's side.

### Hashim

The great-grandfather of Huzoor ﷺ, Hashim, was a man of great dignity and authority. His real name was "Amr." He was extremely brave, immensely generous, and of the highest rank in hospitality. One year, a severe famine struck Arabia and people became needy for even a single grain. Hashim went to Syria, purchased dry bread, and arrived in Makkah during the days of Hajj. He crumbled the bread, made *sareed* with camel meat broth, and fed all the pilgrims to their fill. From that day onward, people began to call him Hashim (the one who crumbles bread). (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 2, Page 8`)

Since he was the eldest and most capable among all the sons of Abde Manaf, after Abde Manaf he became the custodian and spiritual successor of the Kaaba. He was very handsome, beautiful, and dignified. When he reached maturity, he married the daughter of a chief of the Khazraj tribe in Madinah, whose name was Salma. His son Abdul Muttalib was born in Madinah. Since Hashim passed away at the age of twenty-five at a place called "Ghaza" on his way to Syria, Abdul Muttalib grew up in Madinah in the house of his maternal grandfather. When he reached seven or eight years of age, he came to Makkah and began living with his family.

### Abdul Muttalib

The real name of Huzoor's ﷺ grandfather Abdul Muttalib is Shaiba. He was of a very noble soul, a devout worshipper and ascetic. He would take a prayer mat to the Cave of Hira and remain occupied in the worship of Allah continuously for many days. During the month of Ramadan, he would often observe i'tikaf in the Cave of Hira and remain secluded in contemplation of Allah.

The Light of Prophethood of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to shine upon his forehead, and the fragrance of musk would emanate from his body. The people of Arabia, especially the Quraish, had great devotion for him. Whenever a calamity befell the people of Makkah or a drought occurred, they would take Abdul Muttalib with them to the mountain and, making him a means, would supplicate in the court of Allah — and their supplication would be accepted.

He would strictly forbid people from burying their daughters alive and would cut off the hand of a thief. He would even feed birds at his dining table, earning him the title "Muttaimuttair" (the one who feeds birds). He considered wine and adultery forbidden and was a monotheist (*muwahhid*) in his belief. The well of Zamzam, which had completely dried up, was re-excavated and restored by him, and he quenched the people with the water of Zamzam. He too became the custodian and spiritual successor of the Kaaba. The incident of the People of the Elephant occurred during his time. He passed away at the age of 120.

### The Incident of the People of the Elephant

Only fifty-five days before the birth of Huzoor ﷺ, the king of Yemen, "Abraha," leading an army of elephants, launched an attack on Makkah to destroy the Kaaba.

The reason was that Abraha had built a very grand and magnificent cathedral in the capital of Yemen, San'a, and began striving so that the people of Arabia would come to Yemen to perform pilgrimage to this cathedral instead of the Kaaba. When the people of Makkah learned of this, a man from the tribe of Kinana, burning with rage and anger, went to Yemen and defiled the cathedral by excreting in it and smearing it with filth. When Abraha heard of this, he flew into an uncontrollable rage and attacked Makkah with an army of elephants to destroy the Kaaba. The advance detachment of his army seized all the camels and other livestock of the people of Makkah. Among these were two hundred or four hundred camels belonging to Abdul Muttalib. (`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 85`)

Abdul Muttalib was greatly distressed by this incident. He went to the army to speak about the matter. When Abraha learned that the chief of the Quraish had come to meet him, he called him to his tent. When he saw Abdul Muttalib — a tall, awe-inspiring, and extremely handsome man with the glory and majesty of the Light of Prophethood shining upon his forehead — Abraha became overawed at the very sight. Involuntarily, he descended from his royal throne and stood up to honor and respect him. He seated Abdul Muttalib beside him and asked: "Tell me, O chief of the Quraish! What is the purpose of your honored arrival here?"

Abdul Muttalib replied: "Our camels, goats, and other livestock that your army soldiers have driven away — kindly return all those animals to us."

Hearing this, Abraha said: "O chief of the Quraish! I thought you were a man of great courage and splendor, but by asking me about your camels, you have diminished your honor in my eyes. What reality do camels and goats have? I have come to destroy and demolish your Kaaba, yet you have said nothing about that!"

Abdul Muttalib said: "I am concerned with my camels. The Kaaba is not my house; it is the House of Allah. He will protect His own House. I have no worry whatsoever about the Kaaba."

Hearing this, Abraha said in his Pharaonic tone: "O chief of Makkah! Listen! I will raze the Kaaba to the ground, smashing brick upon brick, and erase its name and sign from the face of the earth. Because the people of Makkah have greatly disrespected my cathedral, I consider it necessary to annihilate the Kaaba in revenge."

Abdul Muttalib said: "Then you may do as you please, and Allah will do as He pleases. Who am I to plead with you?"

After this conversation, Abraha ordered all the animals to be returned. Abdul Muttalib brought all the camels and goats back to his home and said to the people of Makkah: "Take your belongings and livestock and leave Makkah. Ascend the mountain peaks and hide in the caves, seeking shelter."

Having said this to the people of Makkah, he himself took a few men from his family and went to the Kaaba. Grasping the ring of the door, with extreme restlessness and weeping, he began to supplicate in the Divine court:

> *رَحالَكَ فَأمِن عَى رَحلِه َمِ نعَى المَرءِ إنّى الّلُهَم*
> *آلَكَ اليَومَ وعابِدِيهِ الصَّليبِ آلى يَلِعَى وانصُر*

"O Allah! Every man protects his own house, so You too protect Your House. And against the cross-worshippers and the worshippers of the cross (the Christians), grant victory to those who follow Your command."

Abdul Muttalib made this supplication and then ascended a mountain peak with his family to witness the manifestation of Allah's power.

The next morning, when Abraha advanced with his mighty army and rows of elephants to destroy the Kaaba and reached a place called "Maghmas," his own elephant — whose name was Mahmud — suddenly sat down and refused to move. No matter how much they beat it and repeatedly goaded it, the elephant would not get up.

In this very state, the Divine wrath manifested itself in the form of *Ababil* (flocks of birds). Small birds, flock after flock, each carrying three small pebbles in their beaks and claws, began to approach the Sacred Sanctuary from the direction of the sea. These army-like clouds of *Ababil* pelted Abraha's army with such force that in an instant the limbs of Abraha's army and his elephants were torn apart. The pelting of the *Ababil* was such a strike of the wrath and anger of the All-Powerful, the Almighty, that whenever a pebble struck the head of an elephant rider, it would pierce through his body and pass through the elephant's body as well. Not a single man of Abraha's army remained alive. All of them, along with Abraha and his elephants, were destroyed and annihilated in such a manner that the pieces of their bodies were scattered on the ground.

Regarding this incident, Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> says in Surah al-Fil of the Noble Qur'an:

> **أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَابِ الْفِيلِ**
> **أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِي تَضْلِيلٍ**
> **وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ**
> **تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ**
> **فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍ**

"Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the People of the Elephant? Did He not cause their plan to go astray? And He sent against them flocks of birds, striking them with stones of baked clay, and made them like consumed stubble."

When Abraha and his army met this end, Abdul Muttalib descended from the mountain and gave thanks to Allah. This miracle of his became renowned far and wide, and all the people of Arabia began to regard him as a God-guided saint worthy of respect.

### Hazrat Abdullah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>

Hazrat Abdullah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> is the revered father of our Huzoor, the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ. He was the most beloved and cherished among all the sons of Abdul Muttalib. Since the Muhammadan Light was manifest upon his forehead in its full splendor and glory, he was the embodiment of beauty and excellence, the mirror of outward grace and inward perfection, and unparalleled in purity and piety. All the beautiful women of the Quraish tribe were captivated by his beauty and wished to marry him. However, Abdul Muttalib was searching for such a woman for him who, along with beauty, would also excel in nobility of lineage and in purity and piety.

By a remarkable coincidence, one day Hazrat Abdullah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> went to the jungle for hunting. The Jews of Syria had recognized from certain signs that this was the revered father of the Prophet of the End Times. Consequently, those Jews repeatedly attempted to kill Hazrat Abdullah. On this occasion as well, a very large armed group of those Jews went into the jungle with the intention of treacherously assassinating Hazrat Abdullah in solitude. But once again, Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> saved him through His grace and mercy. From the unseen realm, certain horsemen suddenly appeared who bore no resemblance at all to the people of this world. Those horsemen routed the Jews and safely escorted Hazrat Abdullah to his home.

Wahab bin Manaaf was also in that jungle and witnessed all this with his own eyes. Consequently, he developed immense love and devotion for Hazrat Abdullah. Upon returning home, he resolved to marry his beloved daughter, Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, to Hazrat Abdullah. He conveyed this heartfelt desire to Abdul Muttalib through some of his friends. By Allah's design, all the qualities that Abdul Muttalib was seeking in a bride for his beloved son Hazrat Abdullah were present in Amina bint Wahab. Abdul Muttalib gladly accepted this proposal.

Thus, at the age of twenty-four, Hazrat Abdullah's marriage to Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> was solemnized. The Muhammadan Light transferred from Hazrat Abdullah and became manifest in the purified womb of Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>. When two months of the blessed pregnancy had passed, Abdul Muttalib sent Hazrat Abdullah to Madinah to fetch dates, or dispatched him to Syria for trade. On his way back, he fell ill in Madinah at the home of his maternal uncles, Banu Adi bin Najjar. After remaining ill for a month, he passed away at the age of twenty-five and was buried in the house of Dar-e-Nabigha. (`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 101`)

When the caravan returned to Makkah and informed Abdul Muttalib of Hazrat Abdullah's illness, he sent his eldest son, Haris, to Madinah to check on him. But before Haris could reach Madinah, Hazrat Abdullah had already departed for the eternal abode. When Haris returned to Makkah and brought the news of his death, the entire household became a house of mourning, and lamentation spread through every home of Banu Hashim.

Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> herself composed such a heart-wrenching elegy for her departed husband that even today the heart is filled with sorrow upon hearing it.

It is narrated that upon the death of Hazrat Abdullah, the angels grieved and said with great regret: "O Allah! Your Prophet has become an orphan." Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> said: "What is it to you? I am his Supporter and Protector." (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 2, Page 14`)

The inheritance left by Hazrat Abdullah consisted of a slave girl named Umm Ayman (whose name was Barqah), some camels, and a few goats. This entire inheritance came to Huzoor, the Master of the Universe ﷺ. Umm Ayman looked after Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ in his childhood — she fed him, clothed him, and provided for all his needs. Therefore, throughout his life, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ continued to show kindness to Umm Ayman. He married her to his beloved adopted son, Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, and from her womb Hazrat Usama <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was born.

### The Faith of the Parents of Huzoor ﷺ

Concerning the respected parents of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ, there is a difference of opinion among the scholars as to whether both of them are believers or not. Some scholars do not regard both of them as believers, while other scholars have adopted a position of restraint in this matter, saying that one should refrain from calling them believers or disbelievers and should entrust this knowledge to Allah.

However, the verifying scholars of the Ahl as-Sunnah — such as Imam Jalaluddin Suyuti, Allama Ibn Hajar Haitami, Imam Qurtubi, Hafiz ash-Sham Ibn Naseeruddin, Hafiz Shamsuddin Dimashqi, Qazi Abu Bakr Ibn al-Arabi Maliki, Shaykh Abdul Haq Muhaddith Dehlawi, and the author of *Al-Ikleel*, Maulana Abdul Haq Muhajir Madani — their belief and statement is that both the parents of Huzoor ﷺ are undoubtedly and unquestionably believers.

Concerning this, Hazrat Shaykh Abdul Haq Muhaddith Dehlawi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has stated:

"Not regarding the parents of Huzoor ﷺ as believers was the way of the earlier scholars (*mutaqaddimeen*). However, the later scholars (*mutakhireen*), through research, have established this matter that the parents of Huzoor ﷺ — indeed, all the forefathers of Huzoor ﷺ from Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — were all believers. The later scholars have three methods of proving the faith of these honored figures:

**First:** That the parents and all the forefathers of Huzoor ﷺ were upon the religion of Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, hence they were believers.

**Second:** That all these honored figures passed away before the announcement of the Prophet's ﷺ prophethood, in an era called the "Period of Interruption" (*Fatrat*), and the call of faith of Huzoor ﷺ did not reach these people at all. Therefore, they can by no means be called disbelievers; rather, they will be called believers.

**Third:** That Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> resurrected these honored figures from their graves, they recited the kalimah and affirmed Huzoor ﷺ. Although the hadith regarding the resurrection of the parents of Huzoor ﷺ is weak in itself, its supporting chains are so numerous that this hadith has reached the rank of *sahih* and *hasan*.

And this is that knowledge which remained hidden from the earlier scholars, but which Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> disclosed to the later scholars. And Allah chooses whom He wills for His grace and mercy."

Shaykh Jalaluddin Suyuti <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has authored several treatises on this subject, proving this matter with evidence and refuting the doubts of the opponents. (`Ashtaul Lam'aat, Volume 1, Page 718`)

Similarly, the foremost of the exegetes, Hazrat Shaykh Ismayeel Haqqi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has stated:

Imam Qurtubi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> wrote in his book *Tazkira* that Hazrat Aisha <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> said: "When Huzoor ﷺ took us along during the Farewell Pilgrimage and we passed through the valley of 'Hujoon,' he was overcome with grief and began to weep. Seeing Huzoor ﷺ weep, I too began to weep. Then Huzoor ﷺ dismounted from his she-camel and after a while returned to me, smiling happily. I asked, 'O Messenger of Allah ﷺ! May my parents be sacrificed for you! What is the matter? You dismounted overcome with grief and returned smiling happily?'"

Huzoor ﷺ said: "I had gone to visit the grave of my mother, Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>. I asked Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> to resurrect her, and Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> resurrected her and she accepted faith." (`Tafseer Roohul Bayan, Volume 1, Page 218`)

And in *Al-Ashbah wan-Nazair* it is stated that it is permissible to curse every person who died in a state of disbelief, except the parents of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, because proof exists that Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> resurrected both of them and they accepted faith. (`Al-Ashbah wan-Nazair, Page 248`)

Moreover, their being resurrected and accepting faith is neither rationally impossible nor legally impossible, because it is proven from the Qur'an that the murdered person of the Children of Israel was brought back to life and named his killer. Similarly, several dead were revived by the blessed hand of Hazrat Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. (`Roohul Bayan`)

When these matters are established, then what can prevent the parents of Huzoor ﷺ from being resurrected to faith? As for the hadith in which it appears: "I sought permission to seek forgiveness for my mother, but I was not granted permission" — this hadith pertains to a time long before the parents of Huzoor ﷺ were resurrected to faith. Because the resurrection of the parents of Huzoor ﷺ to faith occurred at the time of the Farewell Pilgrimage (which is an event only a few months before the passing of Huzoor ﷺ). The ranks and stations of Huzoor ﷺ were ever-increasing, so it is possible that earlier, the Supreme Lord had not bestowed this honor upon Huzoor ﷺ that his parents become Muslims, but later He honored Huzoor ﷺ with this grace and favor as well — that He made his parents people of faith. (`Tafseer Roohul Bayan`)

And Qazi Imam Abu Bakr Ibn al-Arabi Maliki <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> was asked about a person who says that the forefathers of Huzoor ﷺ are in Hell. He replied: "That person is accursed, because Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> has said in the Noble Qur'an:

> **إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يُؤْذُونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ لَعَنَهُمُ اللَّهُ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا مُّهِينًا**

'Indeed, those who cause hurt to Allah and His Messenger — Allah has cursed them in this world and the Hereafter, and has prepared for them a humiliating punishment.' (`Al-Ahzab: 57`)

Hafiz Shamsuddin Dimashqi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has expressed this matter in his poetic verses as follows:

> *فَصَلِّ مَزِيدًا — للنَّبِيِّ الْهُدَى*
> *حَبَاهُ رَؤُوفًا — بِهِ أَكْنِي وَفَضْلِي يَلِي*

"Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> bestowed upon the Prophet ﷺ a virtue beyond all virtues — indeed, Allah is most Merciful to him."

> *وَأَبَاهُ وَكَذَا أُمَّهُ فَأَحْيَا — لَطِيفًا فَضَالِ بِهِ الْمَيَّانِ*

"Because the Lord of Glory, through His gentle grace, resurrected the father and mother of Huzoor ﷺ so that they might believe in him."

(There is another verse whose translation is:) "So accept this matter, because the Generous Lord is capable of it, even if the hadith is weak." (`Tafseer Roohul Bayan, Volume 1, Page 217`)

The author of *Al-Ikleel*, Hazrat Allama Shaykh Abdul Haq Muhajir Madani <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup>, has written: "Allah resurrected the parents of Huzoor ﷺ to the extent that both of them believed and then passed away." This hadith is authentic, and those muhaddithin who have declared it authentic include Imam Qurtubi and the Hafiz of Hadith of Syria, Ibn Naseeruddin. To object to this is out of place and unjustified, because the nature of miracles and special distinctions is that they occur contrary to established norms and habits.

Consequently, the faith of the parents of Huzoor ﷺ after death is beneficial for them, although for others such faith would not be beneficial. The reason is that the parents of Huzoor ﷺ, due to their relationship with the Messenger, possess a perfection that others do not.

Regarding the hadith of Huzoor ﷺ: "Would that I knew what was done with my parents" — Imam Suyuti has stated in *Durr al-Manthur* that this hadith is *mursal* and weak in chain of transmission.

In any case, after reading the above excerpts taken from reliable books, the exigency of intense devotion and faithful love for Huzoor ﷺ demands that respect and reverence be maintained for the parents of Huzoor ﷺ and for all his forefathers, indeed for all his relatives — with the exception of those relatives whose being disbelievers and inhabitants of Hell is definitively proven through the Qur'an and hadith, such as Abu Lahab and his wife. For all other relatives, it is necessary to observe respect and reverence. Those who are connected to Huzoor ﷺ through kinship — any disrespect or insolence towards them will certainly cause hurt to Huzoor ﷺ. You have read the command of the Qur'an: those who cause hurt to Allah and His Messenger are cursed in this world and the Hereafter.

On this subject, A'la Hazrat, Maulana Shah Ahmad Raza Khan Barelvi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup>, also has a research-based treatise named *Shumool al-Islam li Aabaa'il Kiraam*, in which he has written in extremely detailed and well-evidenced manner that the forefathers of Huzoor ﷺ are monotheists (*muwahhid*) and Muslims.

### The Manifestation of the Blessings of Prophethood

Just as before the rising of the sun, the fading of the stars, the whiteness of the true dawn, and the redness of the twilight begin to herald the coming of the sun — in the same way, when the time for the rising of the Sun of Messengership drew near, many such wondrous events and extraordinary phenomena began to appear throughout the world as signs, shaking the entire universe and giving the glad tidings that the Sun of Messengership was about to rise in its full splendor and glory.

Accordingly, the destruction of the People of the Elephant, the sudden lush greening of the Arabian land through the rain of mercy, the end of years of drought and the onset of prosperity throughout the land, the idols falling on their faces, the fire of the Magians in Persia that had been burning for a thousand years being extinguished in an instant, the palace of Kisra shaking and its fourteen balconies collapsing, the lake of "Sawah" between "Hamdan" and "Qum" — six miles long and six miles wide — suddenly drying up completely, the dry river of the Samawah valley between Syria and Kufa suddenly flowing again, and a light emanating from the body of the mother of Huzoor ﷺ that illuminated the palaces of Basra — all these incidents are links in the same chain of miraculous signs that occurred before the arrival of Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, heralding to the universe:

> *Blessed is that King who is about to emerge from behind the curtain —*
> *The time of beggary at his doorstep is about to come.*

The extraordinary and mind-boggling events that occur before the announcement of prophethood are called in Shariah terminology *Irhaas* (foundational signs), and after the announcement of prophethood, these same are called *Mu'jizat* (miracles). Therefore, all the above-mentioned events are *Irhaas* that appeared before Huzoor ﷺ announced his prophethood, which we have described under the heading "Blessings of Prophethood." The number of such events, which are called *Irhaas*, is very large. Some of them have been mentioned, and please read the other events as well.

(1) Muhaddith Abu Nuaim has narrated in his book *Dala'il an-Nubuwwah* through the chain of Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> that when the Light of Prophethood of Huzoor ﷺ transferred from the sacred loins of Hazrat Abdullah into the purified womb of Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, all the animals of the earth, especially those of the Quraish, were granted speech by Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> and they announced in eloquent language: "Today, the sacred Messenger of Allah has manifested Himself in the mother's womb, upon whose head lies the crown of leadership of the entire world, and who is the lamp that will illuminate the whole universe." The animals of the East gave glad tidings to the animals of the West. Similarly, the animals of the seas and rivers conveyed this good news to one another: "The time of the blessed birth of Hazrat Abul Qasim ﷺ has drawn near." (`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 108`)

(2) Khatib Baghdadi has narrated with his chain of transmission that the revered mother of Huzoor ﷺ, Hazrat Bibi Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, said: "When Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ was born, I saw a very large cloud approaching, in which there was light and the sounds of horses neighing and birds flying, and some human voices could also be heard. Then suddenly, Huzoor ﷺ disappeared from before me, and I heard a caller announcing: 'Take Muhammad ﷺ around the East and the West, and also make him journey across the seas, so that the entire universe may know his name, his description, and his attributes. Present him before all living creation — jinn and mankind, angels and beasts and birds — and grant him the form of Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the knowledge of Hazrat Shees <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the courage of Hazrat Nuh <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the friendship of Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the eloquence of Hazrat Ismail <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the contentment of Hazrat Ishaq <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the eloquence of Hazrat Salih <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the wisdom of Hazrat Lut <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the glad tidings of Hazrat Yaqub <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the severity of Hazrat Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the patience of Hazrat Ayyub <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the obedience of Hazrat Yunus <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the jihad of Hazrat Yusha <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the voice of Hazrat Dawud <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the love of Hazrat Danyal <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, the chastity of Hazrat Yahya <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and the asceticism of Hazrat Isa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — adorn him with the perfections of all the Prophets and with beautiful morals.'"

After this, the cloud dispersed. Then she said: "I saw that Huzoor ﷺ was wrapped in a green silk cloth, and water was dripping from that cloth. And a herald was announcing: 'Wonderful! Wonderful! What an excellent gift! Muhammad ﷺ has been granted dominion over the entire world, and nothing remains in the universe that is not under his control and authority.'"

Now I saw the blessed face — it was shining like the full moon of the fourteenth night, and from his body came the fragrance of pure musk. Then three persons appeared — one had a silver ewer in his hand, the second had a tray of green emerald, and the third had a shining ring. After washing the ring seven times, he affixed the Seal of Prophethood between the two blessed shoulders of Huzoor ﷺ. Then Huzoor ﷺ was wrapped in the silk cloth and lifted up, and after a moment, he was handed back to me.

(3) The revered mother of Huzoor ﷺ herself says: "During the pregnancy, a very strange condition prevailed upon me. I would feel neither hunger nor thirst, nor the heat of the sun, and my body was constantly fragrant with the scent of musk. And I was always hearing that a voice was saying to me: 'You are carrying the Lord of the Universe, the Master of all Creation ﷺ.' When he was born, a light emerged from me that illuminated the palaces of Basra." (`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 109`)

(4) Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> narrates that during the pregnancy, Hazrat Amina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> would see that flocks of birds would descend and, forming a canopy over her, would shelter her from the heat of the sun. Even women came from the unseen realm to serve her, bringing dishes in covered trays. And during this entire period of pregnancy, she was always in a state of purity (*taharat*). (`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 109`)

### The Blessed Birth and the Days of Infancy

The generally accepted and most authentic opinion among the scholars is that Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ was born on the eighth, ninth, tenth, or twelfth of Rabi' al-Awwal, on a Monday. (`Sharh al-Mawahib`)

Some scholars say that on the night of the blessed birth, all the idols in the Kaaba fell on their faces. The fire of the Magians in Persia, which had been burning continuously for a thousand years, was extinguished that very night. The palace of Kisra shook and fourteen of its balconies collapsed. The lake of Sawah dried up completely. And the sacred mother of Huzoor ﷺ saw in a dream that a voice came saying: "You have given birth to the best of creation. Name him Muhammad ﷺ."

The wisdom behind naming him Muhammad — in Arabic morphology, the word "Muhammad" is on the pattern of "مُفَعَّل" which denotes repetition and intensity. Thus, "Muhammad" means: one who is praised again and again. Because of the countless excellences, perfections, moral qualities, and praiseworthy attributes present in the personality of Huzoor ﷺ, he will be praised immensely in the entire universe — before him, after him, all are showering and will continue to shower praises upon him. No one has praised his Lord as much as he did, nor will anyone ever be able to praise his Lord as he did. It is because of his praise that Allah has been praised in the universe. On the Day of Judgment as well, he will praise his Lord and the entire creation will praise him. Therefore, the name Muhammad is the most excellent fit for his rank.

The blessed birth took place in the house of Abdul Muttalib, located in the noble valley of Makkah Mukarrama. Hazrat Aamina <sup>رضي الله عنها</sup> said: "When the time of birth drew near, I saw from the unseen realm a white silk cloth spread from the sky to the earth, and a voice came saying, 'Conceal him from the eyes of people.' Then the Kaaba, which was an idol-house, suddenly split at its door and a light from my side entered the Kaaba, and I heard a voice saying: 'The one who is the master of the Kaaba is being born.'" In this moment when the blessed birth of Huzoor ﷺ occurred, Hazrat Aamina <sup>رضي الله عنها</sup> was alone, and no one from her family was present with her.

When the blessed birth took place, his noble grandfather, Abdul Muttalib, was performing the circumambulation of the Kaaba. He was given the glad tidings that a son had been born to his son Hazrat Abdullah. Overjoyed, Abdul Muttalib hurried from the Haram to his home. Trembling with the ecstasy of love, he embraced his grandson. He then took him to the Kaaba, supplicated for blessings and goodness, and named him Muhammad.

Upon hearing the news, Abu Lahab's slave-girl, Suwaiba, went running joyfully and informed Abu Lahab of the birth of his nephew. In his joy, Abu Lahab freed Suwaiba by a gesture of his index finger. The fruit of this act was that after his death, his family saw him in a dream and asked about his state. He raised his finger and said: "After parting from you, I have received nothing to eat or drink except that water is given to me through this finger because of having freed Suwaiba." (`Bukhari, Volume 3, Hadith: 5101`)

On this occasion, Hazrat Shaykh Abdul Haq Muhaddith Dehlawi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has written a very thought-provoking and insightful observation that is extremely delightful for the people of love. He writes:

"In this is a proof for those who celebrate the *Milad* — that they express joy on the blessed night of the birth of Huzoor ﷺ and spend their wealth. The meaning is that when Abu Lahab, who was a disbeliever and whose condemnation was revealed in the Qur'an, was given reward for rejoicing at the birth of Huzoor ﷺ and for spending milk for his slave-girl, then what will be the condition of that Muslim who, overflowing with love for Huzoor ﷺ, expresses joy and spends his wealth?" (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 2, Page 19`)

### The Birthplace (Mawludunnabi)

The house in which the birth of Huzoor ﷺ took place is known in Islamic history as "Mawludunnabi" (the birthplace of the Prophet). This is a highly blessed place. The Islamic rulers built a very magnificent structure over this blessed memorial, where the people of the Two Holy Sanctuaries and Muslims coming from all over the world would hold gatherings of *Milad Shareef* day and night, reciting *salawat* and *salam*.

Hazrat Shah Waliullah Muhaddith Dehlawi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> wrote in his book *Fuyooz al-Haramain*:

"I once attended that gathering of *Milad* which was held at the Mawludunnabi in Makkah Mukarrama on the 12th of Rabi' al-Awwal. While the account of the birth was being recited, I suddenly saw some lights rising from that gathering. I contemplated those lights and came to know that they were the lights of Divine Mercy and of His angels, which attend such gatherings." (`Fuyooz al-Haramain`)

When the Najdi government gained control of the Hijaz, along with the domes of the noble cemeteries of Jannat al-Ma'la and Jannat al-Baqi, the Najdi government also demolished and razed this sacred memorial. For years, this blessed place lay in ruins. However, when I visited this center of blessing and goodness in June 1959, I saw a small building there which was locked. Some Arabs told me that now this building contains a small library and a small office. Now at this place, neither can *Milad Shareef* be held, nor is there permission to recite *salawat* and *salam*. I stood with my companions at some distance from the building and quietly recited *salawat* and *salam*, and such emotion overcame me that I wept for some time.

### The Period of Nursing

Huzoor ﷺ first drank the milk of Abu Lahab's slave-girl, Hazrat Suwaiba. Then he was nourished by the milk of his revered mother, Hazrat Aamina. Then Hazrat Halima Sa'diyya took him with her to her tribe and continued to nurse him. The period of nursing was spent with her.

It was the custom of the nobility of Arab that they would send their children to the surrounding villages to be nursed. In the clean, pure air of the countryside, the children would gain good health and physical strength, and they would also learn the pure and eloquent Arabic language, because the language of the city, due to mixing with outsiders, does not remain pure and eloquent.

Hazrat Halima <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> relates: "I set out for Makkah along with the women of Bani Sa'd in search of children to nurse. That year, a severe famine had struck Arabia. I had an infant in my lap, but due to extreme poverty, there was so little milk in my breasts that it could not suffice him. Throughout the night, the child would writhe in hunger and cry bitterly, and we would sit up all night trying to soothe and comfort him. We also had a she-camel, but she had no milk either. The mule I was riding on the journey to Makkah Mukarrama was so weak that it could not keep pace with the caravan, and my companions had also grown weary of it. With great difficulty, this journey was completed.

When this caravan reached Makkah Mukarrama, whichever woman saw Rasulullah ﷺ and heard that he was an orphan, no woman was willing to take him, because due to his being an orphan, there was no hope of receiving much reward or recompense.

Meanwhile, the star of Hazrat Halima Sa'diyya's destiny was higher than the Pleiades and brighter than the moon. The lack of milk in her breasts became for her the cause of an increase in mercy, because seeing her lack of milk, no one consented to give her their child.

Hazrat Halima Sa'diyya said to her husband, Haris bin Abdul Uzza: 'It does not seem right that I should return empty-handed. It is better that I take this orphan.' Her husband agreed, and Hazrat Halima brought that orphan pearl — not only the houses of Hazrat Halima and Hazrat Aamina, but the East and West of the entire universe were to be illuminated by him.

It was the great grace of the Holy Lord that Hazrat Halima's sleeping destiny awakened and the Master of the Universe came into her lap. When she brought him to her tent and sat down to nurse him, the blessings of prophethood began to manifest like the mercy-laden raincloud. Behold the glory of Allah! So much milk descended in Hazrat Halima's blessed breast that both the Mercy to the Worlds and her nursing brother drank milk to their fill and both fell asleep comfortably. Meanwhile, when they looked at the she-camel, her udders were filled with milk. Hazrat Halima's husband milked her, and both husband and wife drank milk to their hearts' content and both slept peacefully and comfortably all night.

Hazrat Halima's husband, seeing these blessings of the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, was astounded and said: 'Halima! You have brought a very blessed child.' Hazrat Halima said: 'Indeed, I too have the same hope that he is an extremely blessed child and has come to us as the mercy of Allah, and I expect that now our house will be filled with goodness and blessings.' (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 2, Page 19` and `Mawahibul Laduniyyah ma'a Sharh Zurqani, Volume 1, Page 79`)

Hazrat Halima says: "After this, we set out from Makkah Mukarrama with the Master of the Universe ﷺ in my lap toward our village. Now that same mule began to walk so fast that no one else's mount could catch up to its dust. The women of the caravan, astonished, said to me: 'O Halima! Is this the same mule you came riding on, or have you bought another fast-paced mule?' In short, we reached our home. A severe drought had struck there; the udders of all the animals had dried up. But as soon as I set foot in my house, my goats' udders filled with milk. Now, every day when my goats returned from the pasture, their udders would be full of milk, although in the entire settlement no one else could get a single drop of milk from their animals. The people of my tribe said to their shepherds: 'You too graze your animals in the same place where Halima's animals graze.' So everyone began to graze their livestock in the same pasture where my goats grazed. But there was no involvement of the pasture or the jungle in this; it was solely the grace of the blessings of prophethood of the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ, which no one from my tribe could understand except me and my husband."

In short, in this manner, they continued to witness the blessings of Huzoor ﷺ at every moment and every step, until two full years passed and I weaned him. His health and growth were so much better than other children that at two years of age, Huzoor ﷺ appeared to be quite well-grown. Now, according to custom, we brought the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ to his mother, and she honored us generously with gifts and rewards.

Although according to custom we no longer had the right to keep the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ with us, due to the blessings of prophethood, we could not bear his separation for even a moment. By a strange coincidence, that year an epidemic disease had spread in Makkah Mukarrama, so we used the epidemic as a pretext and convinced Hazrat Bibi Aamina to let us take him back. Thus, we brought the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ back to our home, and our house once again became a mine of blessings and mercies. He lived with us in great happiness and joy.

When Huzoor ﷺ grew a little older, he would go outside and watch other boys playing, but he himself always remained aloof from all kinds of games and sports. One day he said to me: 'Mother! I do not see my other brothers and sisters. Where do these people go every morning?' I said: 'They go to graze the goats.' Hearing this, he said: 'Kind mother! Please send me as well with my brothers and sisters.' So, compelled by Huzoor ﷺ's insistence, Hazrat Halima allowed him to go to the pasture with her children. Every day he would go to where Hazrat Halima grazed the goats and tend to their care — which is the practice of all the Prophets and Messengers. Even in childhood, through his action, Huzoor ﷺ manifested one of his characteristics of prophethood.

### The Splitting of the Chest (Shaqq-e-Sadr)

One day, Huzoor ﷺ was in the pasture when suddenly Hazrat Halima's son, Jamrah, came running, gasping and trembling, to his home and said to his mother, Hazrat Halima: 'Mother! A terrible thing has happened! Three men dressed in very white clothes made Muhammad ﷺ lie down, split open his stomach, and I left him in that state and ran away!' Hearing this, Hazrat Halima and her husband both became distraught and ran, panicked, into the jungle. There they saw Huzoor ﷺ sitting, but his face was pale and sad from fear and fright. Hazrat Halima's husband, with great affection, kissed him and asked in a very compassionate tone: 'Son! What happened?'

Huzoor ﷺ said: 'Three men whose clothes were very white and clean came to me, made me lie down, split open my stomach, took something out from it and threw it away, put something into my stomach, and sewed up the incision. But I felt not even a tiny bit of pain.' (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 2, Page 21`)

Hearing this incident, both Hazrat Halima and her husband became extremely worried. The husband said: 'Halima! I fear that some evil influence may have affected him. Therefore, very quickly take him back to his family.' After this, Hazrat Halima brought Huzoor ﷺ to Makkah Mukarrama, because from this incident she had developed the fear that perhaps now she would not be able to protect him adequately. When Hazrat Halima reached Makkah Mukarrama and entrusted him to his revered mother, she asked: 'Halima! You took my child to your home with such great desire and love — what is the reason for bringing him back so soon?'

When Hazrat Halima related the incident of the splitting of the stomach and expressed the suspicion of an evil influence, Hazrat Bibi Aamina said: 'Never! By Allah, no jinn or devil can ever have any influence over the apple of my eye! My son has a great station.' Then she recounted the amazing events of the pregnancy and the time of birth, satisfying Hazrat Halima. Hazrat Halima entrusted Huzoor ﷺ to his revered mother and returned to her village. Huzoor ﷺ now began to be raised in the nurturing lap of his revered mother.

### How Many Times Did Shaqq-e-Sadr Occur?

Hazrat Maulana Shah Abdul Aziz Muhaddith Dehlawi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has stated in the commentary of Surah Alam Nashrah that the blessed chest of Huzoor ﷺ was split open four times and filled with the treasure of light and wisdom:

**First:** When Huzoor ﷺ was at the home of Hazrat Halima — as already mentioned. The wisdom of this was that Huzoor ﷺ might remain protected from those whispers and thoughts to which children are prone, inclining them toward play and mischief.

**Second:** At the age of ten years, so that Huzoor ﷺ might become secure from the dangerous temptations of youthful passions.

**Third:** The *Shaqq-e-Sadr* in the Cave of Hira, when the light of tranquility (*sakinah*) was filled in Huzoor ﷺ's heart, so that he might bear the great and heavy burden of Divine Revelation.

**Fourth:** On the Night of Ascension (*Miraj*), the blessed chest of Huzoor ﷺ was split open and filled with the treasures of light and wisdom, so that such vastness and capacity might be created in his blessed heart that he could bear the manifestations of the Divine Vision and the awe and majesty of the Divine Word.

### Umm Ayman

When Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ arrived in Makkah Mukarrama from Hazrat Halima's home and began to live with his respected mother, Hazrat Umm Ayman — who was the slave-girl of his revered father — became engaged day and night, heart and soul, in serving and caring for Huzoor ﷺ. Umm Ayman's name was Barakah. She had come to Huzoor ﷺ as inheritance from his father. She would feed him, clothe him, and wash his clothes. Huzoor ﷺ married her to his freed slave, Hazrat Zaid bin Harisa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, from whom Hazrat Usama bin Zaid <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> was born. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, Volume 1, Page 97`)

### Childhood Traits

Hazrat Halima relates: "The cradle of Huzoor ﷺ would move by the swinging of angels. In childhood, he would raise his finger and point toward the moon, and the moon would move according to the gesture of Huzoor ﷺ's finger. When his tongue opened, the very first words that came from his blessed lips were:

> *All praise belongs to Allah, the Lord of all the worlds.*

Unlike the habit of children, he never relieved himself in his clothes. Rather, he would always excrete at a fixed time. If ever Huzoor ﷺ's private parts became exposed, he would weep and cry out, and until the private parts were covered, he would find no peace or rest. And if I delayed in covering his private parts, someone from the unseen would cover them. When he became able to walk on his feet, he would go outside and watch the children playing, but he himself would not join in the games. The boys would call him to play, but he would say: 'I have not been created for play.'"

### The Passing of Hazrat Aamina <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>

When the blessed age of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ reached six years, his revered mother took him to Madinah Munawwarah to visit her relatives in Bani Adi bin Najjar (her maternal uncles), or to visit the grave of her husband. Hazrat Umm Ayman, the slave-girl of Huzoor ﷺ's revered father, also accompanied them on this journey. On the return journey from there, at a village called "Abwa," Hazrat Bibi Aamina passed away and was buried there. The shade of the revered father had been removed even before birth. Now the nurturing lap of the revered mother had also come to an end. But this orphan pearl — who is to be nurtured in the lap of mercy that makes him reach his goal — is independent of all these apparent means of upbringing.

After the passing of Hazrat Bibi Aamina, Hazrat Umm Ayman brought Huzoor ﷺ to Makkah Mukarrama and entrusted him to his grandfather, Abdul Muttalib. His grandfather raised him in his nurturing lap with extreme affection and love, and Hazrat Umm Ayman continued to serve him. When his blessed age reached eight years, his grandfather Abdul Muttalib also passed away.

### In the Care of Abu Talib

After the death of Abdul Muttalib, Huzoor ﷺ's uncle, Abu Talib, took him into his nurturing lap. Huzoor ﷺ's beautiful qualities and the lovely, heart-captivating traits of his childhood so captivated Abu Talib that he kept Huzoor ﷺ with him at all times, inside and outside the house. He would feed him with his own food, give him to drink, spread his bedding near himself, and never let him be out of his sight for even a moment.

Abu Talib relates: "I never saw that Huzoor ﷺ ever told a lie at any time, or ever deceived anyone, or ever caused anyone any harm, or ever went to worthless boys to play with them, or ever said anything contrary to good manners. He was always extremely good-natured, of noble habits, high character, and of the highest degree of piety and abstinence."

### The Prayer for Rain by Huzoor ﷺ

Once, a terrifying drought struck the land of Arabia. The people of Makkah intended to plead before the idols for help. But a handsome, dignified old man said to the people of Makkah: "O people of Makkah! Among us is Abu Talib, who is from the progeny of the founder of the Kaaba, Hazrat Ibrahim Khalilullah <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and is also the custodian and caretaker of the Kaaba. We should go to him and request him to pray."

So the chieftains of Arabia presented themselves before Abu Talib and pleaded: "O Abu Talib! The fire of drought has scorched all of Arabia. Animals are thirsting for grass and water, and humans, unable to obtain food and water, are writhing and breathing their last. The caravans have stopped coming and going, and devastation and ruin prevail everywhere. Please pray for rain."

Hearing the plea of the Arabs, Abu Talib's heart was moved. He brought Huzoor ﷺ with him, seated him against the Kaaba, and began to pray. During the prayer, Huzoor ﷺ raised his blessed finger toward the sky. Instantly, clouds appeared from all sides, and immediately such a heavy rain of mercy fell that the land of Arabia was drenched. Water was seen everywhere in the jungles and plains. The dry, barren lands became lush and verdant. The drought was repelled, the famine ended, and all of Arabia became prosperous and happy.

Abu Talib referred to this incident in one of his long odes composed in praise of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ:

> *And the one of fair complexion through whose radiant face rain is sought from the clouds —*
> *He is the shelter of the orphans and the guardian of the widows.*

(`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 190`)

### The Title of "Ummi"

The title of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ is "Ummi." This word has two meanings. Either it is a derivative of "Umm al-Qura," which is the title of Makkah Mukarrama — hence "Ummi" means the inhabitant of Makkah Mukarrama. Or "Ummi" means that Huzoor ﷺ never learned reading or writing from any human being in this world. This is a very great miracle of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ: no one in the world taught or instructed him, yet the Holy Lord granted him such immense knowledge that his blessed breast became the treasure-house of the knowledge and wisdom of the ancients and the later ones, and a Book was revealed to him whose glory is that "it is an exposition of all things." Hazrat Maulana Jami <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> has said beautifully:

> *My Beloved ﷺ neither went to school nor learned to write —*
> *Yet with a gesture of His eye and brow, He taught hundreds of teachers a lesson.*

It is obvious: when one's Teacher and Instructor is the Creator of the Universe, what need would he have to acquire knowledge from any other teacher? A'la Hazrat, Fazil Barelvi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup>, said:

> *Why should such an Ummi be obligated to any teacher?*
> *Is "Read! Your Lord is the Most Generous" not sufficient?*

What is the real secret behind Huzoor ﷺ's title of "Ummi"? Only the Knower of the Unseen can tell. But outwardly, a few wisdoms and benefits can be understood:

**First:** That the one who is to teach knowledge and wisdom to the entire world is Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ himself, and his Teacher should be no one but the Lord of the Universe, so that no one can ever say, "The Prophet was my student whom I taught."

**Second:** That no one could ever think that "So-and-so was the teacher of Huzoor ﷺ, so perhaps he might be more knowledgeable than Huzoor ﷺ."

**Third:** That no one could ever entertain the suspicion that since Huzoor ﷺ was an educated man, he himself composed the verses of the Qur'an and presented them, and that the Qur'an is his own authored speech.

**Fourth:** When Huzoor ﷺ teaches the Book and Wisdom to the entire world, no one can say that he has seen earlier books and is presenting such priceless and revolutionary teachings to the world.

**Fifth:** If Huzoor ﷺ had a teacher, he would have had to honor him, whereas the Creator of the Universe had created Huzoor ﷺ so that the entire world should honor him. Therefore, Allah <sup>جل شأنه</sup> did not allow it that His Beloved should kneel in learning before anyone, or that anyone should be his teacher.

### The Journey to Syria and Bahira

When the age of Huzoor ﷺ reached twelve years, Abu Talib undertook a journey to Syria for trade. Since Abu Talib had an exceedingly intense love for Huzoor ﷺ, he took him along on this journey as well. Huzoor ﷺ made three trading journeys before the announcement of prophethood — twice to Syria and once to Yemen. This is the first journey to Syria.

During this journey, Huzoor ﷺ stayed with the monk Bahira (a Christian ascetic) in Busra. Seeing Huzoor ﷺ, he immediately recognized him from the signs of the Final Prophet mentioned in the Torah and the Gospel. With great devotion and respect, he invited Huzoor ﷺ's caravan and said to Abu Talib: "This child is the Leader of the entire world and the Messenger of the Lord of the Worlds, whom Allah has sent as Mercy to the Worlds. I have seen that the trees and stones prostrate to him, and the Seal of Prophethood is between his two shoulders. Therefore, it is best for you not to take him further ahead. Sell your merchandise here and return to Makkah quickly, because the Jews in Syria are his great enemies; as soon as he reaches there, they will kill him."

Upon Bahira's warning, Abu Talib began to feel the danger. So he sold his merchandise right there and quickly returned with Huzoor ﷺ to Makkah Mukarrama. At the time of departure, Bahira also gave Huzoor ﷺ some provisions for the journey with great devotion. (`Tirmizi, Volume 5, Page 306, Hadith No. 3640`)

## Third Chapter: Deeds Before the Announcement of Prophethood

### The Battle of Fujjar

Before Islam, a long series of wars continued among the Arabs. Among these battles, one famous battle is known as "Harb al-Fujjar" (the Battle of the Transgressors). The Arabs considered it sinful to fight in the months of Dhul Qa'dah, Dhul Hijjah, Muharram, and Rajab, to the extent that people would generally keep their swords sheathed and remove the spearheads from their lances during these months. However, despite this, sometimes such emergency situations arose that they were compelled to fight even in these months, so the Arabs called these battles "Huroob al-Fujjar" (Wars of Transgression).

The last Harb al-Fujjar, which occurred between the tribes of Quraish and Qais, took place when Huzoor ﷺ's blessed age was twenty years. Since the Quraish were on the right side in this battle, Huzoor ﷺ, along with Abu Talib and his other uncles, also participated in this battle. However, he did not raise a weapon against anyone; he only did this much: he kept picking up arrows and handing them to his uncles. In this battle, first Qais and then Quraish emerged victorious, and finally the battle ended with a peace settlement.

### Hilf al-Fudool (The Alliance of Excellence)

Due to the daily battles, hundreds of Arab households had been ruined. Lawlessness and daily plunder had destroyed the peace and tranquility of the land. No one considered their life or property safe. There was no peace in the day, no rest at night. Tired of this dreadful situation, some peace-loving people launched a reform movement after the end of Harb al-Fujjar. Thus, the great chieftains of Bani Hashim, Bani Zahra, Bani Asad, and other Quraish tribes gathered at the house of Abdullah bin Jad'an. Huzoor ﷺ's uncle, Zubair bin Abdul Muttalib, proposed that a treaty should be made to improve the current situation.

Accordingly, the chieftains of the Quraish families made a treaty based on the principle of coexistence and mutual respect, and swore an oath that they would:

1. Remove lawlessness from the land.
2. Protect travelers.
3. Continue to help the poor.
4. Support the oppressed.
5. Not allow any oppressor or usurper to remain in Makkah.

Huzoor ﷺ also participated in this treaty, and he held this treaty so dear that even after the announcement of prophethood, he would say: "I was so happy with this treaty that if someone had given me red camels in exchange for it, I would not have been so happy. And today in Islam as well, if any oppressed person calls out 'O people of Hilf al-Fudool!' asking for my help, I am ready to help him."

This historic treaty is called "Hilf al-Fudool" because long before this treaty of the Quraish, exactly such a treaty had been made in Makkah among the chieftains of the tribe of Jarham. And since the people of the tribe of Jarham who were the movers of that treaty were all named Fadl — meaning Fadl bin Haris, Fadl bin Wada'a, and Fadl bin Fudala — this treaty was named Hilf al-Fudool, i.e., the alliance of those few men whose names were Fadl.

(`Seerat Ibn Hisham, Volume 1, Page 134`)

### The Second Journey to Syria

When Huzoor ﷺ's blessed age reached approximately twenty-five years, his trustworthiness and truthfulness had become renowned far and wide. Hazrat Khadija <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> was a very wealthy woman of Makkah. Her husband had passed away. She needed a trustworthy person with whom she could send her trade goods to Syria. So her discerning eye selected Huzoor ﷺ for this task. She sent word: "You take my merchandise to Syria. I will give you double what I give to others, based on your trustworthiness and honesty."

Huzoor ﷺ accepted her request and departed for Syria with the trade goods. On this journey, Hazrat Khadija also sent her trusted slave, Maisarah, with Huzoor ﷺ to serve him. When Huzoor ﷺ reached the city of Busra in Syria, he stayed near the monastery of a monk named Nastura. Nastura had known Maisarah for a long time. Seeing Huzoor ﷺ's face, Nastura came to Maisarah and asked: "O Maisarah! Who is this person who has alighted under this tree?"

Maisarah replied: "He is from Makkah, the light of the eyes of the Banu Hashim family. His name is Muhammad and his title is 'Amin' (the Trustworthy)."

Nastura said: "No one other than a Prophet has ever alighted under this tree until today. Therefore, I am fully convinced that this is the Final Prophet, because all the signs of the Final Prophet that I have read in the Torah and the Gospel — I see all of them in him. Would that I were alive when he announces his prophethood! Then I would fully assist him and spend my entire life serving him with complete devotion. O Maisarah! I advise and exhort you: Beware! Do not separate from him for even a moment, and serve him with utmost sincerity and devotion, because Allah <sup>تعالىٰ</sup> has honored him with being the Seal of the Prophets."

Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ quickly sold the merchandise in the marketplace of Busra and returned to Makkah Mukarrama. On the return journey, when Huzoor's caravan was entering the city of Makkah, Hazrat Bibi Khadija was sitting on a balcony watching the arrival of the caravan. When her gaze fell upon Huzoor ﷺ, she saw that two angels were shading him from the sun with their wings. This luminous scene had a special effect on Hazrat Bibi Khadija's heart, and she kept watching this beautiful spectacle with intense devotion and overwhelming love.

After several days, she spoke of this to her slave Maisarah, and Maisarah told her: "I witnessed this same scene throughout the entire journey. Besides this, I observed many strange and wonderful things." Then Maisarah also related the conversation of the monk Nastura and his devotion and love. Hearing this, Hazrat Bibi Khadija developed an immense spiritual attachment and boundless devotion and love for Huzoor ﷺ, to the extent that her heart inclined toward marriage with Huzoor ﷺ.

### The Marriage

Hazrat Bibi Khadija <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> was an extremely noble and chaste lady, along with being wealthy. The people of Makkah used to call her "Tahira" (the Pure) because of her purity and piety. Her age was forty years. She had first married Abu Hala bin Zararah Tamimi, from whom two sons, Hind bin Abu Hala and Hala bin Abu Hala, were born. After Abu Hala's passing, Hazrat Khadija married Ateeq bin Aabid Makhzumi, from whom also two children were born — a son, Abdullah bin Ateeq, and a daughter, Hind bint Ateeq. Hazrat Khadija's second husband, Ateeq, had also passed away. The great chieftains of the Quraish were desirous of marrying her, but she rejected all proposals. However, after seeing the Prophetic morals and habits of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ and hearing of his amazing circumstances, her heart inclined toward him to such an extent that she spontaneously developed a desire for marriage with him in her heart. Whereas she had rejected the proposals of the great wealthy men and chieftains of Makkah and had decided that at the age of forty she would not marry a third time — yet now she herself sent for Huzoor ﷺ's paternal aunt, Hazrat Safiyya, who was the wife of her brother Awwam bin Khuwailid, to obtain more information about Huzoor's personal circumstances. Then, through Nafisa bint Umayya, she herself sent the proposal of marriage to Huzoor ﷺ.

The famous Imam of Seerah, Muhammad bin Ishaq, has written that the reason Hazrat Khadija herself stated for liking this match was, in her own words:

> "I have chosen you because of your good character and your truthfulness."

(`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 200`)

Huzoor ﷺ presented this proposal before his uncle, Abu Talib, and the other elders of the family. Who could refuse marriage to a woman as pure, noble, intelligent, and wealthy as Hazrat Khadija? All the family members accepted this proposal with great happiness, and the date for the marriage was set.

Huzoor ﷺ took Hazrat Hamza <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, Abu Talib, and his other uncles, along with other members of the family and the nobility of Bani Hashim and the chieftains of Mudar, as his wedding procession to the house of Hazrat Bibi Khadija, and the marriage was solemnized.

At the time of this marriage, Abu Talib delivered an extremely eloquent and impressive sermon. From this sermon, one can well gauge how Huzoor's family elders viewed him before the announcement of prophethood, and what effect his morals and habits had on these people. The translation of Abu Talib's sermon is as follows:

> "All praises are for Allah, who has made us from the progeny of Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and the descendants of Hazrat Ismail <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, and created us in the lineage of Ma'ad and Mudar, and made us the guardians of His House (the Kaaba) and the caretakers of His Sanctuary, and granted us the house of knowledge and wisdom and a Sanctuary of peace, and made us rulers over the people.
>
> This son of my brother, Muhammad bin Abdullah, is such a young man that when compared with anyone from the Quraish, he surpasses him in every aspect. Yes, he has little wealth, but wealth is a fleeting shadow and a changing thing.
>
> To proceed: My nephew Muhammad is one whose relationship and nearness and love you all know well. He is marrying Khadija bint Khuwailid, and he sets twenty camels from my wealth as the dowry. His future is extremely brilliant, magnificent, and of exalted rank."

(`Zurqani alal Mawahib, Volume 1, Page 201`)

When Abu Talib finished his soul-stirring sermon, Hazrat Bibi Khadija's cousin, Waraqa bin Naufal, also stood and delivered a splendid sermon, the substance of which was:

> "Praise be to Allah, who has made us as He has made us..."

This marriage was happily concluded. The union of Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ and Hazrat Khadija <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> was a marriage of the most exalted blessings and fulfilled the will of Allah that His Beloved should have a companion whose wealth and devotion would support the mission of Islam in its earliest and most vulnerable days.
