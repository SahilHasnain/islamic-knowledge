## Chapter 21: The Rights of the Prophet Upon the Ummah

O our Master, upon You be boundless Durood!
O our Treasure, upon You be millions of Salutations!

### The Rights of the Holy Prophet Upon His Ummah

The Holy Prophet endured countless hardships for the guidance and reformation of his Ummah, and for their success and well-being. You have read something of the difficulties he faced in this path in this book. Now, regarding the boundless love he had for his Ummah, his concern for their salvation and forgiveness, and the quality of his mercy and compassion toward every single member of his Ummah — the Quran itself bears witness:

> لَقَدْ جَاءَكُمْ رَسُولٌ مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيْكُم بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ رَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ

**Translation:** "There has come to you a Messenger from among yourselves. Grievous to him is your suffering; he is deeply concerned for you, and to the believers he is full of compassion and mercy." (At-Tawba: 128)

Allama Qazi Iyaaz Maliki has explained the sacred rights of the Prophet in great detail in his book Shifa Shareef. Here, with utmost brevity, we present a summary covering eight rights:

1. **Iman Birrasool** — Faith in the Messenger
2. **Itteba-e-Sunnat** — Following the Sunnah
3. **Ita'at-e-Rasool** — Obedience to the Messenger
4. **Muhabbat-e-Rasool** — Love of the Messenger
5. **Tazeem-e-Rasool** — Reverence for the Messenger
6. **Madh-e-Rasool** — Praise of the Messenger
7. **Durood Shareef** — Sending blessings upon the Messenger
8. **Ziyarat-e-Qabr-e-Anwar** — Visiting the blessed grave

### (1) Iman Birrasool — Faith in the Messenger

It is absolutely obligatory upon every member of the Ummah to have faith in the prophethood and messengership of the Holy Prophet, and to accept with sincere conviction everything he brought from Allah Almighty. Every believer knows that without faith in the Messenger, no one can be a Muslim. Allah Almighty says:

> فَآمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): O mankind, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all. So believe in Allah and His Messenger." (Al-A'raf: 158)

### (2) Itteba-e-Sunnat — Following the Sunnah

Following the blessed Seerah and sacred Sunnah of the Holy Prophet is obligatory and necessary upon every Muslim. The Lord of Honor commands:

> قُلْ إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ اللَّهَ فَاتَّبِعُونِي يُحْبِبْكُمُ اللَّهُ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): If you love Allah, then follow me; Allah will love you and forgive you your sins." (Aale Imran: 31)

This is why the shining stars of the heaven of the Ummah, the guiding moons of guidance — the beloved Companions — considered following every noble Sunnah as the purpose of their lives. So much so that when Abdullah bin Umar returned from a journey, he would dismount and perform two rak'ats at the place where the Prophet had performed two rak'ats. Similarly, the Companions would catch the dust from the Prophet's shoes in their eyes as a blessing.

When Abu Huraira was invited to eat a roasted whole goat, he refused, saying: "The Prophet departed from this world without ever filling his stomach with barley bread. How can I bring myself to eat such luxurious food?"

### (3) Ita'at-e-Rasool — Obedience to the Messenger

The Holy Quran commands:

> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَطِيعُوا اللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُوا الرَّسُولَ

**Translation:** "O you who believe! Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you." (An-Nisa: 59)

> مَّن يُطِعِ الرَّسُولَ فَقَدْ أَطَاعَ اللَّهَ

**Translation:** "Whoever obeys the Messenger has indeed obeyed Allah." (An-Nisa: 80)

> وَمَن يُطِعِ اللَّهَ وَالرَّسُولَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ مَعَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِم مِّنَ النَّبِيِّينَ وَالصِّدِّيقِينَ وَالشُّهَدَاءِ وَالصَّالِحِينَ

**Translation:** "And whoever obeys Allah and the Messenger — those are with the ones upon whom Allah has bestowed favor, from the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous." (An-Nisa: 69)

These sacred verses declare that without obedience to the Messenger, Islam cannot even be conceived, and that those who obey the Messenger will attain such lofty ranks as to dwell with the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous.

Abdullah bin Mas'ood threw away his gold ring when he learned that the Prophet had thrown away his. He also rejected three types of food that the Prophet had rejected, saying: "I fear that if I accept them, I may be punished."

### (4) Muhabbat-e-Rasool — Love of the Messenger

Every Muslim is obligated to love the Holy Prophet more than anything in the world. The Holy Quran says:

> قُلْ إِن كَانَ آبَاؤُكُمْ وَأَبْنَاؤُكُمْ وَإِخْوَانُكُمْ وَأَزْوَاجُكُمْ وَعَشِيرَتُكُمْ وَأَمْوَالٌ اقْتَرَفْتُمُوهَا وَتِجَارَةٌ تَخْشَوْنَ كَسَادَهَا وَمَسَاكِنُ تَرْضَوْنَهَا أَحَبَّ إِلَيْكُم مِّنَ اللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَجِهَادٍ فِي سَبِيلِهِ فَتَرَبَّصُوا حَتَّىٰ يَأْتِيَ اللَّهُ بِأَمْرِهِ

**Translation:** "Say (O Beloved): If your fathers, your sons, your brothers, your wives, your clan, the wealth you have earned, the commerce you fear may decline, and the homes you love are dearer to you than Allah and His Messenger and striving in His cause, then wait until Allah brings about His command." (At-Tawba: 24)

The Prophet himself said: "None of you can have perfect faith until I am dearer to him than his father, his child, and all mankind." (Bukhari, Muslim)

**The Love of the Companions:**

How passionately the Companions loved the Holy Prophet! Consider these accounts:

**The Old Woman of Bani Deenar:**

At Uhud, when Satan spread the news that the Prophet had been martyred, Madina was shaken to its core. A woman from Bani Deenar, overcome with emotion, rushed from her home toward the battlefield. On the way, she received news of the martyrdom of her father, her brother, and her husband — one after another. Each time she said: "What news of the Prophet?" When they told her: "The Prophet is fine," she said: "Show me to him." When she saw the Prophet's blessed face, she said: "All my calamities are nothing now that you are safe."

**Urwa bin Mas'ood's Testimony:**

Urwa bin Mas'ood (before accepting Islam) observed the Companions' love for the Prophet. He said: "By Allah, I have seen kings — the courts of Caesar, Chosroes, and the Negus — but I never saw any king treated with the reverence that Muhammad is treated by his Companions. When he gives an order, they rush to fulfill it. When he performs ablution, they scramble for the water he used. When he speaks, they lower their voices in his presence. They never stare at him; they look down with respect."

**The Bedouin's Transformation:**

A Bedouin came to the Prophet and embraced Islam. He said: "O Messenger of Allah! When I came to you, there was no one on earth I disliked more than you. But now, you are dearer to me than my family, my wealth, and my own life."

**Bilal's Deathbed:**

When Bilal's time of death came, his wife cried: "O sorrow!" Hearing this, Bilal said from his deathbed: "What joy! Tomorrow I shall meet my beloved ones — Muhammad and his Companions!"

**Love for What the Prophet Loved:**

Anas says: "A tailor invited the Prophet to a meal, and I went with him. Barley bread and soup with dried meat and pumpkin pieces were served. I saw the Prophet picking the pumpkin pieces from around the bowl. From that day on, I have always loved pumpkin."

Imam Abu Yusuf (the student of Imam Abu Hanifa) was once told that someone said: "I do not like pumpkin." Hearing this, Imam Abu Yusuf drew his sword and said: "Renew your faith (shahada); otherwise I will kill you!" This was because the Prophet loved pumpkin, and insulting what the Prophet loved is close to insulting the Prophet himself.

**Signs of Love for the Prophet:**

Scholars have listed certain signs that indicate the lamp of love for the Prophet is burning in a person's heart:

1. Following his sayings and actions, acting upon his Sunnah, obeying his commands and prohibitions — in short, fully adhering to the purified Shariah
2. Abundantly remembering him, reciting much Durood Shareef, attending gatherings where he is mentioned — such as Milad Shareef and religious assemblies
3. Loving and showing respect to all those connected to the Prophet — the Companions, his pure wives, his noble Ahl al-Bayt, the city of Madina, the blessed grave, Masjid Nabawi, and his relics

### (5) Tazeem-e-Rasool — Reverence for the Messenger

Allah Almighty commands:

> إِنَّا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ شَاهِدًا وَمُبَشِّرًا وَنَذِيرًا لِّتُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَتُعَزِّرُوهُ وَتُوَقِّرُوهُ

**Translation:** "Indeed, We have sent you (O Beloved) as a Present Eyewitness (Haazir and Naazir), a bearer of good news, and a warner, so that you may believe in Allah and His Messenger, and support him and revere him." (Al-Fath: 8-9)

**One Who Insults the Prophet is a Disbeliever:**

Allama Qazi Iyaaz Maliki said: "There is consensus of the entire Ummah that anyone who curses the Prophet or insults his person, his family, his religion, or any aspect of his blessed being is a disbeliever. Anyone who doubts his disbelief and punishment is also a disbeliever. The worldly punishment for such a person is execution." (Shifa)

Similarly, those who insult the Companions, the Ahl al-Bayt, or the pure wives of the Prophet are cursed.

**The Companions' Reverence:**

Urwa bin Mas'ood observed: "When the Prophet spoke, the Companions would sit with bowed heads, as still as if birds were perched on them. When he fell silent, they would speak. They never argued in his presence. Whoever spoke in his presence would speak only while he listened attentively."

**Amr bin al-Aas's Three Stages:**

Amr bin al-Aas said on his deathbed: "In my first stage, there was no one I hated more than the Prophet. When I embraced Islam, there was no one I loved more than him. And my reverence for him was such that I could not bring myself to look him in the eye out of awe."

**Baraa's Respect:**

Baraa bin Azib said: "I wanted to ask the Prophet something, but due to my immense respect and awe of him, I could not ask for years."

### Reverence for the Prophet's Relics

The Companions treated everything connected to the Prophet as sacred and blessed:

1. **The Prophet's Hair:** Companions would catch the Prophet's hair as it fell during shaving, considering it blessed. Saabit Bunani was entrusted by Anas with a blessed hair of the Prophet, to be placed under his tongue after his death.

2. **The Prophet's Pulpit:** Abdullah bin Umar would pass his hand over the spot where the Prophet sat on the minbar and then wipe his face with it.

3. **Abu Mahzoora's Hair:** Abu Mahzoora, the muazzin of Masjid al-Haram, would never cut his forelock because the Prophet had passed his blessed hand over it.

4. **The Prophet's Cloak:** A Companion was given a piece of the Prophet's cloak. His grandson said: "That blessed piece remained with us for 30 years. We would wash it and give the water to the sick for healing."

5. **The Water Bag:** Kabsha Ansariya cut off the mouth of her water bag after the Prophet drank from it, keeping it as a blessed relic.

6. **Imam Malik's Reverence for Madina:** Imam Malik never rode any animal in Madina throughout his life, out of respect for the city of the Prophet.

7. **Ahmad bin Fazlviyya's Reverence for the Bow:** On hearing that the Prophet had held a bow, this great warrior never touched a bow without ablution.

8. **Imam Malik's Fatwa on Madina's Soil:** When someone said Madina's soil was bad, Imam Malik ordered 30 lashes and imprisonment for the insolent person.

### (6) Madh-e-Rasool — Praise of the Prophet

Praising the Prophet is the blessed way of the prophets and messengers. Allah Almighty has sent down the Holy Quran as a beautiful bouquet of various kinds of flowers of praise for His Beloved. Throughout the Quran, the signs of the Prophet's praises and qualities shine as brightly as stars scattering their light across the sky. The previous heavenly books also declared that every prophet and messenger was a herald of the praise of Allah's Beloved, trumpeting the excellence of Mustafa throughout their lives.

In the era of the Companions, thousands of noble Companions praised the Prophet in every street and market. Imam Ahmad's book of hadith contains the praises of the Prophet by Hassan bin Thabit, Ka'ab bin Zuhair, Abdullah bin Rawaha, and others.

### (7) Durood Shareef — Sending Blessings Upon the Prophet

It is obligatory upon every Muslim to send Durood Shareef upon the Prophet. The Creator of the universe commands:

> إِنَّ اللَّهَ وَمَلَائِكَتَهُ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ ۚ يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا صَلُّوا عَلَيْهِ وَسَلِّمُوا تَسْلِيمًا

**Translation:** "Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet. O you who believe! Send blessings upon him and greet him with the proper greeting of peace." (Al-Ahzab: 56)

The Prophet said: "Whoever sends Durood upon me once, Allah sends ten mercies upon him."

Allahu Akbar! What can we say of the Beloved's station! A lowly and contemptible servant sends blessings upon the Prophet of Allah, and the King of All Worlds sends ten mercies upon that servant!

### (8) Ziyarat-e-Qabr-e-Anwar — Visiting the Blessed Grave

For every Muslim, visiting the Prophet's grave is an act of immense blessing and honor. The Quran says:

> وَلَوْ أَنَّهُمْ إِذ ظَّلَمُوا أَنفُسَهُمْ جَاءُوكَ فَاسْتَغْفَرُوا اللَّهَ وَاسْتَغْفَرَ لَهُمُ الرَّسُولُ لَوَجَدُوا اللَّهَ تَوَّابًا رَّحِيمًا

**Translation:** "And if, when they wronged themselves, they had come to you and asked forgiveness of Allah, and the Messenger had asked forgiveness for them, they would have found Allah ever Forgiving, Most Merciful." (An-Nisa: 64)

In this verse, the Most Merciful has laid down three conditions for the forgiveness of sinners: (1) Presentation at the court of the Messenger, (2) Seeking forgiveness, and (3) The Messenger's prayer for forgiveness. This command is not limited to the Prophet's worldly life — visiting his blessed grave is also considered presenting oneself at his court. Scholars have clearly stated that the grace of the Prophet's court did not cease with his departure.

**Virtues of Visiting the Prophet's Grave:**

1. "Whoever visits my grave, my intercession becomes obligatory for him." (Darqutni, Behaqi)
2. "Whoever performs Hajj of the House of Allah and does not visit me has wronged me." (Ibn Adi)
3. "Whoever visits me after my death, it is as if he visited me during my life. Whoever dies in either of the two Harams (Makkah or Madina) will be raised among the secure on the Day of Judgment." (Darqutni)

From the blessed era of the Companions until today, Muslims from all over the world continue to visit the illuminated grave, seeking his intercession and calling upon him for help (tawassul and istighasa). This blessed practice will continue until the Day of Judgment.

**The Bedouin's Tawassul After the Prophet's departure:**

It is narrated from Ali that three days after the Prophet's departure, a Bedouin Muslim came and threw himself at the blessed grave, sprinkling its soil on himself, and said: "O Messenger of Allah! You said, and we heard from you, the word of Allah. And my soul loves what you loved. Stand before your Lord and intercede for me." The Prophet appeared to him in a dream and said: "Go, you are forgiven."

**Objections Against Visiting the Grave and Their Refutation:**

Some people, following Ibn Taymiyya, consider visiting the graves of prophets and saints as forbidden and impermissible. They even say it is haram to travel for the purpose of visiting the Prophet's grave.

The famous hadith "La tashuddur rihaal" (Do not tie saddles except for three mosques) is cited by them as proof. However, all commentators of hadith have explained that this hadith means: "Do not tie saddles with the intention of prayer except for three mosques." The word "Masjid" clearly excludes the destination. When other narrations of the same hadith explicitly mention "Illi Masjid" (except for the mosque), the intended meaning is clear — this hadith refers specifically to mosques for the purpose of prayer. Visiting the Prophet's grave is a separate act of worship with its own virtue and has never been prohibited by any scholar of Ahl al-Sunnah.

**Tawassul (Seeking Intermediation):**

Tawassul means to present the Prophet as a means in one's supplication to Allah. This is established from the Quran, Sunnah, and the practice of the righteous predecessors.

**(1) Tawassul Before the Prophet's Birth:**

When Adam (Alaihissalam) was sent down to earth, he prayed: "O my Lord! I ask You by the right of Muhammad to forgive me." Allah said: "O Adam! How did you know Muhammad when I have not yet created him?" Adam replied: "O my Lord! When You breathed Your spirit into me and I raised my head, I saw written on the pillars of the Throne: 'There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.' I knew that You would only join with Your Name the name of the most beloved of creation to You." Allah forgave him.

**(2) Tawassul During the Prophet's Life:**

The famous hadith of the blind man: A blind man came to the Prophet and asked for prayer to restore his sight. The Prophet taught him to pray: "O Allah! I ask You and turn to You through Your Prophet, the Prophet of Mercy. O Muhammad! I turn through you to my Lord for my need, that He may fulfill it." The man was cured. (Tirmizi, Nasa'i)

**Fatima bint Asad's Funeral:**

When the Prophet's aunt Fatima bint Asad (mother of Ali) passed away, the Prophet wrapped her in his own cloak as a shroud, dug her grave with his blessed hands, and lay in the grave praying for her. By Allah, that grave will receive the rain of mercy until the Day of Judgment!

**(3) Tawassul After the Prophet's departure:**

After the Prophet's departure, the Companions continued to make him their means in their supplications and called upon him for help.

**Istighasa for Rain:**

During Umar's caliphate, when drought struck, Bilal bin Harith came to the Prophet's grave and said: "O Messenger of Allah! Ask Allah for rain for your Ummah, for they are perishing!" The Prophet appeared to him in a dream and said: "Go to Umar and tell him rain will come." This is well-documented in historical sources.

**Abdullah bin Qart at the Grave:**

After the Battle of Yarmook, Abdullah bin Qart returned to Madina and realized he had forgotten to send salam at the Prophet's grave. He returned and found Aisha, Abbas, Ali, Hasan, and Husayn there. When he asked them to pray for victory, Ali and Abbas raised their hands and prayed: "O Allah! By the right of the Prophet by whose intercession Adam's prayer was accepted, make this path easy for Abdullah bin Qart."

**The Hungry Scholars in the Mosque:**

Three great scholars — Tabarani, Abu Bakr Makri, and Abu Shaikh — were starving in Masjid Nabawi (in a year of famine). When Isha time came, Tabarani went to the Prophet's grave and said: "O Messenger of Allah! We are hungry!" He returned, and soon after, a man arrived with two servants carrying baskets of food. They ate and the food remained. (This story was later confirmed by the man as a dream instruction from the Prophet himself.)

**The Blessed Gifts of the Prophet:**

The famous hadith scholar Muhammad bin Munkadir (d. 205 AH) narrates: A man deposited 80 dinars with my father as a trust, saying: "If you need them before my return, use them." In a year of drought, my father spent the money. When the man returned from Jihad and demanded his money, my father promised to return it the next day. That night, my father spent the night in Masjid Nabawi. The Prophet appeared to him in a dream, gave him 80 dinars, and said: "Take this and give it to him." When he woke up, he found 80 dinars under his head.

**Imam Abu Hanifa's Istighasa:**

If we were to write all such examples, the book would become too long. As an example, we present three verses from Imam Abu Hanifa's poem in which he presented his plea at the court of the Messenger:

> "O Master of masters! I have come to you with pure intent.
> I hope for your pleasure and seek refuge in your sanctuary."

**Meaning:** O Sayyidus Sadaat! I have come to you with a pure intention. I am hopeful of your pleasure and have sought refuge in your shelter. My creation is such that it makes me turn away from You, but my hope is that You will turn toward me. I have no one besides You in this world.
