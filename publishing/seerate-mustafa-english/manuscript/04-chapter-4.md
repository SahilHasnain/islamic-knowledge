## Chapter 4: From the Announcement of Prophethood to the Allegiance of Aqaba

### The Beginning of Revelation

When the blessed life of the Master of the Prophets ﷺ reached its fortieth year, a sudden change appeared in his sacred being. He developed a love for solitude. He began to enjoy sitting alone and worshipping Allah. He would often be found deep in thought. Most of his time was spent observing the scenes of nature and contemplating the systems of the universe. Day and night, he remained immersed in the contemplation of the Creator's essence and attributes, and occupied with thinking about the correction of his people's corrupted condition and the means to remedy it.

In those days, a new thing also happened — the Beloved ﷺ began to see good dreams. Every dream he saw was so true that its interpretation would become clear like the morning light.

### The Cave of Hira

About three miles from Makkah Mukarrama, on a mountain called Jabal Hira, there is a cave known as Ghaar-e-Hira. The Beloved ﷺ would often take several days' worth of food and water and remain engaged in the worship of Allah in the peaceful atmosphere of this cave. When the food and water ran out, he would sometimes go home to get more, and sometimes Hazrat Bibi Khadija would send provisions to the cave. Even today, this luminous cave exists in its original condition and is a place of visitation for the people.

### The First Revelation

One day, the Beloved ﷺ was occupied in worship inside the Cave of Hira when, all of a sudden, an angel appeared in the cave. This was Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, who has always carried Allah's message to His messengers. The angel immediately said: "Read!"

The Beloved ﷺ replied: "I am not one who reads."

The angel held the Beloved ﷺ and, with great warmth, embraced him firmly. Then releasing him, he said: "Read!"

The Beloved ﷺ again said: "I am not one who reads."

The angel embraced him a second time and, releasing him, said: "Read!"

The Beloved ﷺ gave the same answer: "I am not one who reads."

A third time, the angel pressed him to his chest with great force and then said:

> *Read in the name of your Lord who created.* (Surah Al-Alaq, 96:1)

This was the very first revelation sent down to the Beloved ﷺ. After memorising these verses, the Master of the Prophets ﷺ returned home. But this incident had come upon him so unexpectedly that a tremor had fallen upon his blessed heart. He said to his household: "Cover me with a cloak! Cover me with a cloak!"

When his fear subsided and he felt some calm, he related to Hazrat Khadijah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> what had happened in the cave. He said: "I fear for my life."

Hearing this, Hazrat Khadijah said: "No, never! There is no danger to your life. By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you. You treat your relatives with the best conduct, you carry the burdens of others, you host travellers, and you help people in their troubles and difficulties for the sake of truth and justice."

After this, Hazrat Khadijah took the Beloved ﷺ to her cousin, Waraqa bin Naufal. Waraqa was among those who believed in the Oneness of God. He had become disgusted with the polytheism and idol-worship of the people of Makkah and had adopted the Christian faith. He used to translate the Gospel from Hebrew into Arabic. He had become very old and blind.

Hazrat Khadijah said to him: "Brother, listen to what your nephew has to say."

Waraqa bin Naufal asked: "Tell me, what have you seen?"

The Beloved ﷺ related the entire incident of the Cave of Hira. Hearing this, Waraqa bin Naufal said: "This is the same angel that Allah sent to Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>."

Then Waraqa said: "Would that I were a strong young man at the time of your announcement of prophethood! Would that I remained alive until the time when your people drive you out of Makkah!"

The Beloved ﷺ asked in surprise: "Will the people of Makkah drive me out?"

Waraqa replied: "Yes. Whoever has brought what you have brought has always been met with hostility."

### The Interval in Revelation

After this, the revelation stopped for some days. The Beloved ﷺ became restless and anxious, waiting for revelation. One day, as he was walking outside his home, someone called out: "O Muhammad!" He looked up at the sky and saw the same angel who had come to him in the cave, sitting on a throne between the heavens and the earth. Seeing this, a feeling of fear came over his blessed heart. He returned home, lay down, and said to his household: "Cover me with a blanket! Cover me with a blanket!"

While he lay covered with the blanket, the opening verses of Surah Al-Muddassir were revealed. The command of the Lord came down:

> *O you who are enveloped in a cloak! Arise and warn. And glorify your Lord. And purify your garments. And shun idols.* (Surah Al-Muddassir, 74:1-5)

After the revelation of these verses, the Master of the Prophets ﷺ was appointed to the station of calling to Islam. In accordance with the command of Allah, he girded his loins for the call to truth and the propagation of Islam.

### Three Stages of Calling to Islam

**First Stage:**

For three years, the Master of the Prophets ﷺ carried out the duty of preaching Islam in extreme secrecy and confidentiality. During this period, the first to accept faith among women was Hazrat Bibi Khadijah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>. Among free men, the first was Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>. Among boys, the first was Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>. And among slaves, the first was Hazrat Zaid bin Haarisa <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>.

Then, through the invitation and preaching of Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, Hazrat Usman, Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam, Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf, Hazrat Saad bin Abi Waqqas, and Hazrat Talha bin Ubaidullah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهم</sup> soon entered the fold of Islam. After a few days, Hazrat Abu Ubaida bin Al Jarrah, Hazrat Abu Salma Abdullah bin Abdul Asad, Hazrat Arqam bin Abu Arqam, Hazrat Usman bin Mazoon, and his two brothers, Hazrat Qudama and Hazrat Abdullah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهم</sup>, also accepted Islam.

After some time, Hazrat Abuzar Ghifari, Hazrat Suhaib Roomi, Hazrat Ubaida bin Al Haaris bin Abdul Muttalib, Sayeed bin Zaid bin Amr bin Nufail, and his wife Fatima bint Al Khattab (the sister of Hazrat Umar) also accepted Islam. The Beloved's aunt, Hazrat Ummul Fazl (the wife of Hazrat Abbas bin Abdul Muttalib), and Hazrat Asma bint Abu Bakr Siddique also became Muslims. Besides them, many other men and women were honoured with accepting Islam.

It is worth noting that those who accepted Islam first — the ones honoured with the title of *Sabiqeen-e-Awwaleen* (the first and foremost) — were precisely those people who were naturally of good disposition and were already searching for the true religion. They were disgusted and fed up with the polytheism and idol-worship of the Quraish of Makkah and the polytheistic customs of the Jahiliyyah. So when they saw the manifestation of the true religion in the company of the truthful Prophet ﷺ, these fortunate people began to sacrifice themselves like moths around the lamp of prophethood and were honoured with Islam.

**Second Stage:**

After three years of this secret call to Islam, a community of Muslims had been prepared. Then Allah revealed the verse of Surah Ash-Shu'ara to His Beloved, commanding: "O Beloved! Warn your close relatives."

So one day, the Beloved ﷺ climbed the hill of Safa and called out: "O assembly of Quraish!"

When all the Quraish had gathered, the Beloved ﷺ said: "O my people! If I tell you that an army is hiding behind this mountain, ready to attack you, would you believe me?"

Everyone said with one voice: "Yes, yes! We would certainly believe you, because we have always found you to be truthful and trustworthy."

The Beloved ﷺ said: "Then I say to you that I am warning you of the punishment of Allah. If you do not bring faith, the punishment of Allah will descend upon you."

Hearing this, all the Quraish — including his uncle Abu Lahab — became extremely angry and left. They began to speak ill of the Beloved ﷺ.

**Third Stage:**

Now the time came when, in the fourth year of the announcement of prophethood, the verse of Surah Al-Hijr was revealed: "So proclaim what you are commanded." The Lord of Honour commanded: "O Beloved! Proclaim openly what you have been commanded."

After this, the Beloved ﷺ began to preach Islam openly and started speaking against polytheism and idol-worship publicly. All the Quraish — indeed all the people of Makkah, indeed the whole of Arabia — girded themselves in opposition to him. A long series of harassment and persecution of the Beloved ﷺ and the Muslims began.

### Oppression and Persecution of the Mercy to the Worlds

The disbelievers of Makkah could not kill the Beloved ﷺ for fear of revenge from the Banu Hashim and the outbreak of war. But they began to pour a mountain of oppression and persecution upon him through all kinds of troubles and harassment. First of all, they launched a strong propaganda campaign in every street and marketplace, calling the Beloved ﷺ a poet, a sorcerer, a soothsayer, and a madman. They set gangs of mischievous boys behind him who would follow him on the roads, mock him, and throw stones at him. These ignorant people would place thorns and dirt on the paths where the Beloved ﷺ walked. When the Beloved ﷺ would pass by, they would boast with pride. Whenever the Beloved ﷺ would sit in any gathering, they would surround him and ask absurd questions and engage in pointless arguments, just to harass him. They would even bribe poets and orators to compose satirical verses against the Beloved ﷺ.

It is narrated that once the Beloved ﷺ was praying in the Haram of the Kaaba when a heartless disbeliever, Uqba bin Abi Muit, put a loop of his cloak around the Beloved's neck and pulled it so hard that he began to choke. Seeing this, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> rushed over, pushed Uqba away, and said: "Do you kill a man who says that his Lord is Allah?" In this scuffle, Hazrat Abu Bakr struck the disbelievers and was also struck by them. (`Zurqani, 1/252`)

Seeing the miracles and spiritual influences of the Beloved ﷺ, the disbelievers called him the greatest sorcerer. When the Beloved ﷺ recited the Noble Qur'an, these disbelievers would curse the Qur'an, the one who brought it (Jibreel), the one who sent it (Allah), and the Beloved ﷺ himself. They would station guards in the streets so that the sound of the Qur'an would not reach anyone's ears. They would clap their hands and whistle, making so much noise that the voice of the Qur'an could not be heard.

Wherever the Beloved ﷺ would recite the Qur'an in a public gathering or a fair of the disbelievers and call them to faith, his uncle Abu Lahab would follow behind him, shouting: "O people! This nephew of mine is a liar! He has gone mad! Do not listen to a word he says!"

Once, the Beloved ﷺ went to the market of Zul Majaaz to preach Islam and called people to the word of truth. Abu Jahl began to throw dust at him and said: "O people! Do not fall for his deception. He wants you to abandon the worship of Lat and Uzza."

Similarly, once the Beloved ﷺ was praying in the Haram of the Kaaba. During his prayer, Abu Jahl said: "Is there anyone who will take the womb of a camel slaughtered by the family of so-and-so and place it on his shoulders while he is in prostration?" Hearing this, the disbeliever Uqba bin Abi Muit got up, brought that womb, and placed it on the blessed shoulders of the Beloved ﷺ while he was in prostration. The Beloved ﷺ remained in prostration for a long time, with the womb on his shoulder and neck, while the disbelievers laughed and rolled on top of one another in mirth.

Finally, Hazrat Fatima <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, who was still a young girl, came and, rebuking the disbelievers, removed the womb from the blessed shoulders of the Beloved ﷺ. The Beloved's blessed heart was deeply pained by this mischief of the Quraish. After finishing his prayer, he raised his hands and prayed three times: "O Allah! Seize the Quraish in Your grip!"

Then he named Abu Jahl, Utba bin Rabia, Shaiba bin Rabia, Waleed bin Utba, Umayya bin Khalaf, and Ammara bin Waleed in his supplication: "O Allah! Seize these people in Your grip!"

Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> says: "By Allah! I saw all these disbelievers on the day of Badr — their corpses were lying on the ground. Then all these disbelievers' bodies were dragged with great disgrace and thrown into a pit of Badr, and the Beloved ﷺ said: 'May the curse of Allah be upon the people of this pit!'" (`Bukhari, 1/74`)

### Some Mischievous Disbelievers

Among the disbelievers of Makkah who were very active in enmity and harassment of the Beloved ﷺ, the names of some mischievous ones are:

(1) Abu Lahab (2) Abu Jahl (3) Aswad bin Abde Yaghoos (4) Haaris bin Qais bin Adi (5) Waleed bin Mughira (6) Umayya bin Khalaf (7) Ubay bin Khalaf (8) Abu Qais bin Faqiha (9) Aas bin Waail (10) Nazr bin Haaris (11) Munabbe bin Al Hajjaj (12) Zuhair bin Abi Umayya (13) Saaib bin Saifi (14) Adi bin Hamra (15) Aswad bin Abdul Asad (16) Aas bin Sayeed bin Al Aas (17) Aas bin Hashim (18) Uqba bin Abi Muit (19) Hakam bin Al Aas

All of these were neighbours of the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ. Most of them were wealthy and powerful, and remained engaged day and night in harassing the Master of the Universe.

### Oppression of the Muslims

Along with the Beloved ﷺ, the disbelievers of Makkah also poured mountains of oppression and persecution upon the poor Muslims. The land of Makkah cried out. It would have been easier for the disbelievers to kill these Muslims outright. But that would not have quenched their vengeful passion. They thought it was a matter of honour to torture the Muslims so much that they would abandon Islam and return to polytheism and idol-worship. So instead of killing them, they subjected the Muslims to all kinds of punishments and harassment.

But by Allah! Those intoxicated with the wine of Tawheed presented such a spectacle of steadfastness and constancy that the peaks of mountains raised their heads in astonishment. The hard-hearted, merciless, and beast-like disbelievers left no cruelty or oppression untried against these helpless and defenceless Muslims. Yet not a single Muslim's foot wavered by even a hair's breadth. Not a single Muslim child turned away from Islam and became a disbeliever or apostate.

The disbelievers of Makkah inflicted dreadful atrocities upon these helpless Muslims and subjected them to such soul-shattering torments that even mountains would have trembled. In the scorching heat of the Arabian desert, when the sand burned like an oven, they would wound the backs of these Muslims with lashes, then make them lie on their backs on the burning sand. They would place such heavy stones on their chests that they could not turn over. They would heat iron rods and brand the bodies of the Muslims. They would drown them in water until they choked. They would roll the Muslims in mats and smoke them through the nose until breathing became impossible and they would lose consciousness from agony.

Hazrat Khabbab bin Al Arat <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> accepted Islam at a time when the Beloved ﷺ was staying in the house of Hazrat Arqam bin Abu Arqam and only a few people had become Muslims. The Quraish tortured him severely. They made him lie on burning coals, and a man stood on his chest until the coals were extinguished by the fat and moisture of his back. Years later, when he narrated this before Hazrat Umar and showed his back, there were white marks and spots on it. Seeing this heartbreaking scene, Hazrat Umar's heart filled with grief and he wept. (`Tabqaat Ibn Saad, Volume 3`)

Hazrat Bilal <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was a slave of the disbeliever Umayya bin Khalaf. They would tie a rope around his neck and drag him through the streets and markets. They would beat him with sticks. At midday, in the scorching heat, they would make him lie on the burning sand and place such a heavy stone on his chest that his tongue would protrude. Umayya would say: "Abandon Islam, or you will die choking like this!" But even in this state, Hazrat Bilal's brow did not furrow. He would loudly cry out "Ahad, Ahad!" and proclaim with a strong voice: "Allah is One! Allah is One!" (`Seerat Ibn Hisham`)

Hazrat Ammar bin Yasir <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was made to lie on hot sand, and the Quraish beat him so severely that he would lose consciousness. His mother, Hazrat Bibi Sumayya <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup>, was killed by Abu Jahl with a spear thrust below her navel because she accepted Islam. She was the first martyr of Islam. Hazrat Ammar's father, Hazrat Yasir <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, was also beaten to death by the disbelievers.

Hazrat Suhaib Roomi <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was tortured by the disbelievers of Makkah in countless ways. They would beat him mercilessly until he remained unconscious for hours. When he decided to migrate, the disbelievers of Makkah said: "You can leave all your wealth and possessions here and go to Madina." He gladly kicked away the wealth of the world and left for Madina, taking only his faith with him. (`Zurqani alal Mawahib`)

Hazrat Abu Faqiha <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was a slave of the disbeliever Safwan bin Umayya and had become a Muslim at the same time as Hazrat Bilal. When Safwan learned of his Islam, he put a rope noose around his neck, dragged him, made him lie on the burning ground, and placed a heavy stone on his chest. As the disbelievers were dragging him, they happened to pass by a lizard hole. Safwan taunted him: "Look! Is this your God?" Hazrat Abu Faqiha replied: "O son of a disbeliever! Be quiet! My Lord and your Lord is Allah." Hearing this, Safwan became enraged and choked him so hard that he lost consciousness, and people thought he had died.

Similarly, Hazrat Aamir bin Fuhaira <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> was beaten so severely that every limb of his body ached.

Hazrat Bibi Lubaina was a slave-girl. During the time when Hazrat Umar was still a disbeliever, he would beat this poor slave-girl so much that he would tire himself out. But Hazrat Lubaina would not even say "uff." With great courage and steadfastness, she would say: "O Umar! If you do not believe in the true Messenger of Allah, then Allah will surely take revenge upon you."

Hazrat Zaneera was a slave-girl of Hazrat Umar's household. When she became a Muslim, the disbelievers beat her so severely that she lost her eyesight. But the Lord of Honour, through the prayer of the Master of the Prophets ﷺ, restored her sight. The polytheists said: "This is the effect of Muhammad's magic."

Similarly, Hazrat Bibi Nahdiya and Hazrat Bibi Umme Ubais were also slave-girls. After embracing Islam, the disbelievers of Makkah inflicted countless torments upon them. But these saintly women endured these great calamities with patience and gratitude, and their feet did not waver from Islam.

The Beloved's companion in the cave, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, sacrificed his wealth for Islam in countless ways. A glimpse of this is that he saved the lives of most of these helpless and defenceless Muslims. He purchased Hazrat Bilal, Hazrat Aamir bin Fuhaira, Abu Faqiha, Lubaina, Zaneera, Nahdiya, and Umme Unais — all these slaves — for large sums, freed them, and rescued these oppressed people from the tortures of the disbelievers.

When Hazrat Abuzar Ghifari <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> entered the fold of Islam, he stayed in the Haram of the Kaaba for several days as a traveller. He would loudly proclaim his Islam every day, and every day the Quraish would beat him until he was covered in blood. During those days, he had nothing to eat or drink except Zamzam water. (`Bukhari, 1/544`)

It should be noted that the cruelty of the disbelievers of Makkah was not limited only to the poor and slaves. Even the wealthy and noble chieftains were not spared by these oppressors for the crime of accepting Islam.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, who was one of the respected and distinguished nobles of Makkah, was beaten so badly by the Quraish in the Haram of the Kaaba that his head was covered in blood. Similarly, Hazrat Usman Ghani, who was extremely wealthy and powerful — when he became a Muslim, it was not strangers but his own uncle who tied him with ropes and beat him severely. Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam was a man of great prestige and influence. When he accepted Islam, his uncle wrapped him in a mat and smoked him through the nose until he began to choke.

Hazrat Umar's cousin and brother-in-law, Hazrat Sayeed bin Zaid, was a chieftain of great status and honour. When Hazrat Umar learned of his Islam, he tied him with a rope and beat him. At the same time, Hazrat Umar struck his own sister, Hazrat Bibi Fatima bint Al Khattab, such a hard slap that her earrings fell off and blood flowed from her face.

### The Delegation of the Quraish to the Beloved ﷺ

One day, the chieftains of the Quraish were sitting in the Haram of the Kaaba, thinking: "Despite so much trouble and hardship, why does Muhammad not stop his preaching? What is his real goal? Perhaps he desires honour and leadership or wealth and power."

So they all sent Utba bin Rabia to the Beloved ﷺ, saying: "Find out his heart's desire somehow."

Utba bin Rabia came to the Beloved ﷺ and said: "O Muhammad! What is your aim with this call to Islam? Do you want leadership of Makkah? Or do you desire honour and wealth? Whatever wish is in your heart, say it openly. I guarantee that if you abandon this call to Islam, all of Makkah will be under your command. Every desire and wish of yours will be fulfilled."

Upon hearing Utba's captivating speech, the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ recited some verses of the Noble Qur'an in reply. Hearing them, Utba was so deeply affected that every hair of his body trembled with the fear of Allah's Majesty. He placed his hand over the Beloved's mouth and said: "I beg you by the bond of kinship — stop! My heart is bursting from the greatness of this speech."

Utba returned from the court of prophethood, but a new revolution had taken place in the world of his heart. Utba was a great orator, eloquent and fluent. He told the chieftains of the Quraish: "The speech that Muhammad presents is neither magic, nor soothsaying, nor poetry. It is something else entirely. My advice is — leave him alone. If he succeeds and prevails over all of Arabia, then our honour as Quraish will increase. Otherwise, Arabia itself will destroy him."

But the stubborn disbelievers of the Quraish did not accept Utba's sincere and wise counsel. Instead, they increased their opposition and harassment.

### The Delegation of the Quraish to Abu Talib

There were also some peace-loving people among the disbelievers of the Quraish. They wanted the matter to be settled through dialogue and negotiation. So some respected leaders of the Quraish came to Abu Talib and complained about the preaching of the Beloved ﷺ and his speeches against idol-worship. Abu Talib sent them away with great gentleness and persuasion.

But the Beloved ﷺ, obeying the command of Allah, continued to openly condemn polytheism and idol-worship and preach the call to Tawheed. So the anger of the Quraish flared up again. All the chieftains of the Quraish — Utba, Shaiba, Abu Sufyan, Aas bin Hisham, Abu Jahl, Waleed bin Mughira, Aas bin Waail, and others — came together to Abu Talib and said: "Your nephew insults our gods. Either step aside and hand him over to us, or come out openly on the battlefield with him, so that one of the two sides prevails."

Abu Talib saw the temper of the Quraish and understood that a very dangerous and critical moment had arrived. He could not face all the Quraish alone. So he said to the Beloved ﷺ in a very sincere and affectionate tone: "My dear nephew! Have mercy on your old uncle's white beard. Do not place such a burden on me in my old age that I cannot bear it. Until now, every child of the Quraish respected me. But today, the tone and attitude of the Quraish chieftains have become so hostile that they will not hesitate to draw swords against you and me. My advice is — stop your call for a few days."

Until now, the only apparent supporter and helper of the Beloved ﷺ was Abu Talib. Now the Beloved ﷺ saw that his uncle's feet were also wavering. Hearing his uncle's words, the Beloved ﷺ said in a choked voice filled with emotion:

"Uncle dear! By Allah, if the Quraish place the sun in my right hand and the moon in my left, I will not abandon this duty. Either Allah will complete this work, or I will lay down my life for the religion of Islam."

Hearing this passionate speech, Abu Talib's heart melted. He was so deeply moved that every drop of blood in his Hashimi veins boiled with love for his nephew. With great fervour, he said: "Go, my nephew! Go, I am with you. As long as I live, no one can harm a hair of your head." (`Seerat Ibn Hisham, 1/266`)

### The Migration to Habsha — Year 5 of Prophethood

When the disbelievers of Makkah had made life unbearable for the Muslims through oppression and persecution, the Beloved ﷺ ordered the Muslims to go to Habsha and seek refuge there.

**Najjashi:** The king of Habsha was named Ashama, and his title was Najjashi. He was a follower of the Christian faith, but he was very just and compassionate. He was a great scholar of the Torah, the Gospel, and other revealed books.

In the fifth year of the announcement of prophethood, in the month of Rajab, 11 men and 4 women migrated towards Habsha. The blessed names of these noble migrants are:

(1, 2) Hazrat Usman Ghani with his wife, Hazrat Ruqayya <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup> (the daughter of the Beloved ﷺ)
(3, 4) Hazrat Abu Huzaifa with his wife, Hazrat Sahla bint Suhail <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup>
(5, 6) Hazrat Abu Salamah with his wife, Hazrat Umme Salamah <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup>
(7, 8) Hazrat Aamir bin Rabia with his wife, Hazrat Laila bint Abi Hashma <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهما</sup>
(9) Hazrat Zubair bin Al Awwam
(10) Hazrat Mus'ab bin Umair
(11) Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf
(12) Hazrat Usman bin Mazoon
(13) Hazrat Abu Sabra bin Abu Raham (or Hatib bin Amr)
(14) Hazrat Suhail bin Baiza
(15) Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood
(`Zurqani alal Mawahib`)

When the disbelievers of Makkah learned of their migration, they pursued them. But the migrants had already boarded the ship and departed. So the disbelievers returned empty-handed. This caravan of migrants reached the land of Habsha and began to worship Allah in peace and security.

After a few days, a rumour suddenly spread that the disbelievers of Makkah had accepted Islam. Hearing this, some people returned from Habsha to Makkah. But upon arrival, they found the rumour was false. Some of them went back to Habsha, while others stayed in Makkah in hiding. But the Quraish found them and subjected them to even greater oppression than before. So the Beloved ﷺ again ordered the Muslims to go to Habsha. Consequently, those who had returned from Habsha, along with other oppressed Muslims — a total of 83 men and 18 women — migrated to Habsha. (`Zurqani alal Mawahib`)

### The Delegation of the Disbelievers to the Court of Najjashi

All the migrants began to live in Habsha in complete peace and security. But how could the disbelievers of Makkah allow the children of Tawheed to live anywhere in comfort? These oppressors sent Amr bin Al Aas and Ammara bin Waleed as their delegates to the court of the king of Habsha, along with some gifts.

These two presented the gifts to Najjashi and, prostrating before the king, began to complain: "O King! Some criminals have fled from Makkah and taken refuge in your land. Please hand these criminals over to us."

Hearing this, King Najjashi summoned the Muslims to the court. The brother of Hazrat Ali, Hazrat Ja'far <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup>, stepped forward as the representative of the Muslims to speak. According to court etiquette, he did not prostrate to the king but only greeted him with salam and stood. The courtiers objected, but Hazrat Ja'far replied: "Our Messenger ﷺ has forbidden us from prostrating to anyone other than Allah. Therefore, I cannot prostrate to the king." (`Zurqani alal Mawahib`)

Then Hazrat Ja'far bin Abi Talib began his speech in the royal court:

"O King! We were an ignorant people. We practised polytheism and idol-worship. We were engaged in plunder, theft, robbery, oppression, all kinds of immoralities, and evil deeds. Then Allah sent a Messenger to us from among ourselves. We already knew his lineage, truthfulness, and trustworthiness. That Messenger forbade us from polytheism and idol-worship and commanded us to worship only the One God. He prohibited us from all kinds of oppression, evil, and immorality. We believed in him, abandoned polytheism and idol-worship, and repented from all evil deeds. This is our only crime. Because of this, our people have become our mortal enemies. They persecuted us so much that we bade farewell to our homeland and have come to live in peace under the shadow of your kingdom. Now these people want to force us to return to that same misguidance."

King Najjashi was deeply moved by Hazrat Ja'far's speech. Seeing this, the delegate of the Quraish, Amr bin Al Aas, shot his final arrow: "O King! These Muslims hold a belief about your prophet, Hazrat Eisa, that is contrary to your own faith."

Hearing this, King Najjashi asked Hazrat Ja'far about this matter. Hazrat Ja'far recited Surah Maryam. The Word of the Lord had such a deep effect on Najjashi's heart that he was overcome with emotion and tears flowed from his eyes.

Hazrat Ja'far said: "Our Messenger has told us that Hazrat Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is the servant of Allah and His Messenger. He was born of the virgin Maryam <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنها</sup> by the power of Allah, as a sign of His might — without a father."

King Najjashi listened to Hazrat Ja'far's speech with great attention and said: "Indeed, the Gospel and the Qur'an are two lights from the same sun of guidance. Indeed, Hazrat Eisa is the servant of Allah and His Messenger. And I bear witness that Muhammad ﷺ is the same Messenger of Allah whose glad tidings were given by Hazrat Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> in the Gospel. If I were not bound by the rules of the kingdom, I would go to Makkah myself, straighten the sandals of the Master of the Prophets ﷺ, and wash his feet."

Hearing the king's speech, his courtiers — who were staunch Christians — became angry and agitated. But King Najjashi, in a fit of faith, silenced them all. He returned the gifts of the Quraish and expelled Amr bin Al Aas and Ammara bin Waleed from the court. He told the Muslims: "Live in my kingdom wherever you wish, in peace and comfort. No one can harm you." (`Zurqani, 1/288`)

It should be noted that King Najjashi had become a Muslim. When he passed away, the Beloved ﷺ performed his funeral prayer in Madina Munawwara, even though King Najjashi had died in Habsha and was buried there. The Beloved ﷺ offered his funeral prayer in absentia and prayed for his forgiveness.

### Hazrat Abu Bakr and Ibn Dughanna

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique also set out for Habsha. But when he reached the place of Barqul Ghammad, he met Malik bin Dughanna, the chieftain of the Qaara tribe, on the way. He asked, "Why, O Abu Bakr! Where are you going?" Mentioning the oppressions of the people of Makka, he said, "I have left my homeland of Makka and will roam in the wide earth of Allah and worship Allah." Ibn Dughanna said, "O Abu Bakr! A man like you can neither leave the city nor be made to leave. You carry the burdens of others. You host the guests of the Haram. You earn and help the poor and needy. You assist and aid everyone in good works. Come back to Makka with me. I am taking you under my protection."

Ibn Dughanna brought him back to Makka by force and announced to all the disbelievers of Makka: "I have taken Abu Bakr under my protection. Beware! No one should trouble him." The disbelievers of Makka said, "We accept this on the condition that Abu Bakr recites the Quran hidden inside his house so that the sound of the Quran does not reach the ears of our women and children." Ibn Dughanna accepted the condition of the disbelievers. Hazrat Abu Bakr continued to recite the Quran inside his house for a few days. But Hazrat Abu Bakr's Islamic zeal and fervour could not tolerate that the false deities Laat and Uzza be worshipped openly while the worship of Allah Ta'ala, the True Deity, be done hidden inside a house.

So he built a mosque in his courtyard outside his house and began to offer prayers in that mosque openly and recite the Quran in a loud voice. The women and children of the disbelievers of Makka began to gather in crowds and listen to the Quran. Seeing this scene, the disbelievers of Makka summoned Ibn Dughanna and complained, "Abu Bakr recites the Quran outside the house. Our women and children gather around him to listen. This causes us great distress. So tell him, either he should recite the Quran inside the house or you should absolve yourself of the responsibility of your protection."

So Ibn Dughanna said to Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique, "O Abu Bakr! Either recite the Quran hidden inside the house or I will withdraw from my protection. After that, if the disbelievers of Makka trouble you, I will not be responsible." Saying this, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique said, "O Ibn Dughanna! You may absolve yourself of the responsibility of protection. The protection of Allah Ta'ala is enough for me, and I am pleased and content with His will." (`Bukhari, 1/307`)

### Hazrat Hamza Accepts Islam

In the sixth year of the proclamation of prophethood, two such personalities entered the fold of Islam — Hazrat Hamza and Hazrat Umar — with whom the banner of Islam's glory and honour and its dignity and prosperity rose very high. Among the uncles of the Beloved, Hazrat Hamza had a very deep love for him and was only two or three years older. Since he too had drunk the milk of Hazrat Suwaiba, he was also the Beloved's foster brother.

Hazrat Hamza was very strong and brave and very fond of hunting. Every morning he would leave home with his bow and arrows and return from hunting in the evening. He would go to the Haram, perform tawaf of Khana Kaaba, and sit for a while in the gathering of Quraish chiefs. One day, as usual, he returned from hunting. The slave-girl of Ibn Zud'aan and his own sister Hazrat Safiyya told him what disrespect and insolence Abu Jahl had shown towards his nephew Muhammad today. Hearing this, Hazrat Hamza's blood boiled with rage. He immediately reached Masjid-e-Haraam with his bow and arrows and struck Abu Jahl on the head with such force that his head split open. He said, "You abuse my nephew? Do you not know that I too follow his religion?" Seeing this, some people of the Bani Makhzoom tribe stepped forward to help Abu Jahl. But Abu Jahl, thinking that a war might break out with Bani Hashim, said, "O Bani Makhzoom! Leave Hamza alone. Indeed, today I gave his nephew very foul kinds of abuses." (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, 2/44`)

After becoming a Muslim, Hazrat Hamza began to recite these verses loudly:

*"I praise Allah Ta'ala when He guided my heart to Islam and the true religion."*

*"When the commandments of Islam are recited before us, the tears of the wise flow."*

*"And the chosen Ahmad of Allah is our leader. So O disbelievers! Do not overcome him with your false nonsense."*

*"By Allah's oath! We will not hand him over to the nation of disbelievers, although we have not yet settled scores with these disbelievers by swords."* (`Zurqani`)

### Hazrat Umar's Conversion

On the third day after Hazrat Hamza accepted Islam, Hazrat Umar also became blessed with the wealth of Islam. There are many narrations regarding his honouring of Islam.

One narration is that one day, filled with rage, he set out with a drawn sword intending to finish the Prophet of Islam with that very sword. By chance, he met Hazrat Nuaim bin Abdullah Qureshi on the way. He had already become a Muslim, but Hazrat Umar did not know of his Islam. Hazrat Nuaim bin Abdullah asked, "Why, O Umar! Where are you going with a drawn sword in the heat of this afternoon?" He said, "I have set out from home today to settle the affair of the founder of Islam." He said, "First tend to your own household. Your sister Fatima bint Al Khattab and your brother-in-law Sayeed bin Zaid have also become Muslims."

Hearing this, he arrived at his sister's house and knocked on the door. Inside the house, some Muslims were secretly reciting the Quran. Hearing Hazrat Umar's voice, everyone became frightened. They left the pages of the Quran and hid here and there. His sister opened the door. Hazrat Umar shouted, "O enemy of your own life! Have you too become a Muslim?" Then he lunged at his brother-in-law Hazrat Sayeed bin Zaid, grabbed his beard, threw him to the ground, sat on his chest and began to beat him. His sister rushed to save her husband. Hazrat Umar slapped her so hard that her earrings broke and fell, and her face became covered in blood.

His sister said clearly, "O Umar! Listen. Do whatever you can, but now Islam cannot leave the heart." Hazrat Umar saw his sister's bloodied face and heard her sentence full of resolve and steadfastness. Tenderness overcame him and he immediately softened. He stood silently for a while, then said, "Alright, show me what you were reciting." His sister placed the pages of the Quran before him. He picked them up and looked. His eyes fell on this verse: "The purity of Allah proclaims whatever is in the heavens and the earth, and He is the Mighty, the Wise."

Every word of this verse struck like an arrow of truth and pierced deep into his heart. Every hair of his body began to tremble. When he reached the verse "Believe in Allah and His Messenger", he became completely overwhelmed and involuntarily cried out: "I bear witness that there is no god but Allah and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."

This was the time when the Master of the Prophets was staying at the house of Hazrat Arqam bin Abu Arqam. Hazrat Umar left his sister's house and went straight to the house of Hazrat Arqam. He found the door closed. He knocked. The people inside peeked through the crack in the door. They saw Hazrat Umar standing with a drawn sword. Everyone became worried. No one had the courage to open the door. But Hazrat Hamza said in a loud voice, "Open the door and let him in. If he has come with good intention, he will be welcomed. Otherwise, he will be beheaded with his own sword."

Hazrat Umar stepped inside. The Beloved ﷺ himself came forward, took Hazrat Umar's arm and said, "O son of Khattab! Become a Muslim. How long will you keep fighting me?" Hazrat Umar recited the Kalima in a loud voice. The Beloved ﷺ raised the takbeer in joy, and all those present shouted Allahu Akbar with such force that the mountains of Makka echoed. Then Hazrat Umar said, "O Messenger of Allah! What is the meaning of worshipping in hiding? Rise, let us go to the Kaaba and worship Allah openly. By Allah's oath! In whichever gatherings I sat opposing Islam while in the state of disbelief, I will now announce my Islam in all those gatherings."

Then the Beloved ﷺ set out with the group of Companions in two rows. Hazrat Hamza walked ahead of one row and Hazrat Umar ahead of the other. They entered Masjid-e-Haraam in this manner and offered prayer. Hazrat Umar announced his Islam before the polytheists in the Haram of Kaaba. Hearing this, the disbelievers rushed from all sides and began to beat Hazrat Umar. Hazrat Umar also began to fight them. A great commotion broke out.

Meanwhile, Hazrat Umar's maternal uncle Abu Jahl arrived. He asked, "What is this commotion?" People told him that Hazrat Umar had become a Muslim, so the people were angry and attacking him. Hearing this, Abu Jahl stood by the Hateem of Kaaba and signaled with his sleeve, announcing: "I have given my nephew Umar protection." Hearing Abu Jahl's announcement, everyone stepped back.

Hazrat Umar says: "After accepting Islam, I kept striking the disbelievers and receiving their blows until Allah Ta'ala granted dominance to Islam." (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib`)

Another reason for Hazrat Umar becoming a Muslim is also mentioned. Hazrat Umar himself used to say: "In the state of disbelief, I was present near the idols of the Quraish. Meanwhile, a man brought a calf and slaughtered it in the name of the idols. Then someone shouted very loudly:

*'The matter is becoming clear. The matter of pride is becoming clear. The matter of the eloquent one is becoming clear. There is no god but Allah.'*

"Hearing this voice, everyone fled from there. But I resolved that I would never leave this place without investigating who was calling out. After this, the same voice came again: 'O open enemy! A thing of success is that an eloquent man is saying La Ilaha Illallah.' Although there was no one else near the idols except me. Immediately after this, the Beloved ﷺ announced his prophethood. Hazrat Umar was deeply affected by this incident. Therefore, this incident also has some role among the causes of his accepting Islam." (`Bukhari, 1/546`)

When the disbelievers of Makka troubled Hazrat Umar excessively, Aas bin Waail Sahmi also gave him protection. He was Hazrat Umar's ally in the time of ignorance. So Hazrat Umar remained safe from the beatings of the disbelievers.

### Shibe Abi Talib — 7th Year of Prophethood

In the seventh year of the proclamation of prophethood, when the disbelievers of Makka saw that the number of Muslims was increasing day by day and brave men of Quraish like Hazrat Hamza and Hazrat Umar had also entered the fold of Islam, they became beside themselves with rage. All the chieftains of Quraish and other disbelievers of Makka devised a scheme that the Beloved ﷺ and his family should be completely boycotted. These people should be confined to a narrow, dark place and their food and water should be cut off so that they would be completely destroyed and ruined.

In accordance with this dreadful proposal, all the tribes of Quraish made a pact among themselves:

(1) No one shall marry into the family of Bani Hashim.
(2) No one shall buy or sell any kind of goods from these people.
(3) No one shall have any association, greeting, conversation or meeting with these people.
(4) No one shall allow any food or drink to reach these people.

Mansoor bin Ikrama wrote this agreement, and all the chieftains of Quraish signed it. This document was hung inside the Kaaba.

Abu Talib was compelled to take the Master of the Prophets ﷺ and all other family members to the mountain valley known as Shibe Abu Talib and seek refuge there. Except for Abu Lahab, the disbelievers of the Bani Hashim family also supported the Beloved ﷺ in this matter out of tribal loyalty and protection. All of them became confined in this narrow, dark valley and began living the life of prisoners.

These three years passed with such severity and hardship that Bani Hashim would cook and eat leaves of trees and dry hides. Their children would cry day and night from the intensity of hunger and thirst. The hard-hearted and cruel disbelievers had stationed guards everywhere so that no food or water could reach inside the valley. (`Zurqani Alal Mawahib`)

For three continuous years, the Beloved ﷺ and the family of Bani Hashim endured these mind-numbing tribulations. Eventually, some compassionate people among the Quraish themselves felt pity for the sufferings of Bani Hashim. They started a movement to break this cruel agreement. So Hisham bin Amr Aamiri, Zuhair bin Abi Umayya, Mutim bin Adi, Abul Bakhtari, Jama bin Al Aswad and others went together to the Haram of Kaaba.

Zuhair, who was the grandson of Abdul Muttalib, addressed the Quraish disbelievers with an impassioned speech. He said, "O people! What kind of justice is this? We are living comfortably while the children of Bani Hashim are writhing and crying from hunger and thirst. By Allah's oath! I will never sit in peace until this barbaric agreement is torn and trampled underfoot."

Hearing this speech, Abu Jahl reacted angrily and said, "Beware! You dare not touch this agreement." Jama challenged Abu Jahl and rebuked him with such force that Abu Jahl was silenced. Similarly, Mutim bin Adi and Hisham bin Amr also spoke firmly and scolded Abu Jahl. Abul Bakhtari said clearly, "O Abu Jahl! We were never pleased with this cruel agreement, nor are we bound by it now."

In the same gathering, Abu Talib was also sitting. He said, "O people! My nephew says that termites have eaten this agreement, and only the place where Allah's name was written has been left untouched by them. My opinion is that you take out this document and look at it. If termites have indeed eaten it, then tear it up and throw it away. And if my nephew's statement proves false, then I will hand Muhammad over to you."

Hearing this, Mutim bin Adi went inside the Kaaba, brought down the document, and everyone saw that, indeed, except for the name of Allah Ta'ala, the entire document had been eaten by termites. Mutim bin Adi tore up the document and threw it away before everyone. Then some brave men of Quraish — although all were still in a state of disbelief at that time — took up weapons, went to the valley, brought out every single person of the Bani Hashim family, and restored their homes. This incident took place in the 10th year of prophethood.

Mansoor bin Ikrama, who had written this agreement, was struck by Divine punishment — his hand became paralyzed and withered. (`Madarijun Nubuwwat, 2/42`)

### The Year of Sorrow — 10th Year of Prophethood

The Master of the Prophets ﷺ came out of Shibe Abi Talib and arrived at his home. Only a few days of respite from the oppression of the Quraish disbelievers had passed when Abu Talib fell ill. Eight months after coming out of the valley, he passed away.

The demise of Abu Talib was a very heart-rending and soul-piercing incident. Since childhood, Abu Talib had raised the Beloved ﷺ with such love and affection, and at every turn of life supported and aided him with such sacrifice, standing shield against his enemies and facing calamities — how could the Beloved ﷺ forget this?

**Abu Talib's End**

When Abu Talib became afflicted with the illness of death, the Beloved ﷺ went to him and said, "O uncle! Recite the Kalima. This is the Kalima through which I will insist before the court of Allah for your forgiveness." At that time, Abu Jahl and Abdullah bin Abi Umayya were present near Abu Talib. Both said to Abu Talib, "O Abu Talib! Will you turn away from the religion of Abdul Muttalib?" Both kept speaking to Abu Talib until Abu Talib did not recite the Kalima. Rather, his final words were: "I am on the religion of Abdul Muttalib." Saying this, his soul departed.

The Beloved, the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ said, "I will keep praying for your forgiveness until Allah Ta'ala forbids me." After this, this verse was revealed: "It is not permissible for the Prophet and the believers to seek forgiveness for the polytheists, even if they are relatives, after it has become clear to them that the polytheists are the people of Hell." (`Bukhari, 1/448`)

### The Demise of Hazrat Bibi Khadija

The wound of Abu Talib's death was still fresh on the blessed heart of the Master of the Prophets ﷺ when, three or five days after Abu Talib's death, Hazrat Bibi Khadija also passed away from this world. After Abu Talib, the person who sacrificed her body, mind and wealth the most in supporting and aiding the Mercy to the Worlds was the esteemed personality of Hazrat Bibi Khadija.

At a time when there was no sincere advisor and sympathizer in the world for the Beloved ﷺ , it was Hazrat Bibi Khadija who, with complete devotion, would console and comfort him at every moment of distress. Therefore, with the deaths of both Abu Talib and Hazrat Bibi Khadija, his helper and his comforter both left this world. This caused such a great shock to his delicate heart that he named this year "Aamul Huzn" (The Year of Sorrow).

Hazrat Khadija passed away in Ramadan of the 10th year of prophethood. At the time of death, she was 65 years old. She was buried in the place of Hujoon (the cemetery of Jannatul Maala). The Beloved, the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ himself descended into her grave and laid her blessed body in the earth with his own sacred hands.

### The Journey to Taif and Beyond

Seeing the stubbornness and obstinacy of the people of Makka, when the Beloved, the Mercy to the Worlds ﷺ lost hope of their accepting faith, he turned towards the settlements near Makka for the propagation of Islam. In this connection, the Beloved ﷺ also travelled to Taif. In this journey, the Beloved ﷺ's slave Hazrat Zaid bin Haarisa was also with him.

Wealthy nobles and chieftains lived in Taif. Among these chiefs, the family of Amr was considered the leader of all the tribes. These were three brothers: Abde Yaalil, Masood, and Habeeb. The Beloved ﷺ went to each of them and presented the invitation of Islam. None of the three accepted Islam. Instead, they gave extremely obscene and insolent replies. Not only this, these ill-fated men incited the hooligans of Taif to mistreat the Beloved ﷺ .

So this wicked gang of ruffians and vagabonds fell upon the Beloved ﷺ from all sides. These embodiments of mischief showered stones on him until his blessed feet were wounded and covered in blood, and his socks and sandals filled with blood. When the Beloved ﷺ would sit down, exhausted from his wounds, these oppressors would cruelly grab his arm and make him stand. And when he began to walk, they would again rain stones on him, along with taunts, abuses, clapping and ridicule.

Hazrat Zaid bin Haarisa would run and take the stones aimed at the Beloved ﷺ on his own body, shielding him, until he too became drenched in blood and, overcome by his injuries, became helpless. Eventually, the Beloved ﷺ took shelter in a vineyard. This vineyard belonged to Utna bin Rabiya, a well-known disbeliever of Makka.

Seeing the condition of the Beloved ﷺ , Utna bin Rabiya and his brother Shaiba bin Rabiya felt pity. Despite being disbelievers, tribal honour stirred within them. So these two disbelievers allowed the Beloved ﷺ to stay in their vineyard and sent a bunch of grapes to him through their Christian slave, Addaas.

The Beloved recited Bismillah and touched the bunch. Addaas said in surprise, "The people of this region do not utter this Kalima." The Beloved ﷺ asked him, "Where is your homeland?" Addaas said, "I am a resident of the city of Nainwa." The Beloved ﷺ said, "That is the city of Hazrat Yunus bin Matta. He too was a prophet of Allah, like me." Hearing this, Addaas began to kiss the Beloved's hands and feet and immediately accepted Islam by reciting the Kalima.

In this same journey, when the Beloved ﷺ was at the place of Nakhla and was reciting the Quran in the Tahajjud prayer at night, a group of the Jinn of Naseebain presented themselves before him. Hearing the Quran, all these Jinn became Muslims. Then these Jinn returned and told their people. Thereafter, groups of Jinn came to Makka Mukarrama and accepted Islam in droves. Allah Ta'ala has mentioned this incident in the opening verses of Surah Al-Jinn of the Noble Quran. (`Zurqani`)

The Beloved ﷺ stayed at the place of Nakhla for a few days. Then he came to the place of Hira and sent a message to Mutim bin Adi, a prominent chief of the Quraish: "Can you give me your protection?" It was the custom of Arabia that when someone sought protection or support, they could not refuse, even if he was a great enemy. So Mutim bin Adi gave the Beloved ﷺ his protection. He ordered his sons: "Go to the Haram armed." Mutim bin Adi himself mounted his horse and brought the Beloved ﷺ to Makka. He took him inside the Haram of Kaaba and announced in the public gathering: "I have given Muhammad ﷺ protection."

After this, the Beloved ﷺ calmly kissed the Black Stone, performed tawaf of the Kaaba, offered prayer in the Haram, and Mutim bin Adi and his sons escorted him to his house under the shadow of their swords.

A long time after this journey, Ummul Momineen Hazrat Aisha asked the Master of the Prophets ﷺ : "O Messenger of Allah! Has any day been more severe for you than the day of the Battle of Uhud?" The Beloved ﷺ said, "Yes, O Aisha! That day was even more severe for me than the day of Uhud — when I invited Abde Yaalil, the chief of Taif, to Islam. He rejected the invitation of Islam with contempt, and the people of Taif stoned me. I walked on with my head bowed in this grief and pain until I reached the place of Qarnu Thaalib, where my senses returned to me.

"When I arrived there and raised my head, what did I see? A cloud was shading me. From that cloud, Hazrat Jibreel called me and said: 'Allah Ta'ala has heard what your people said and their reply. Now the angel of the mountains is present before you.' He greeted me and said, 'O Muhammad ﷺ ! Allah Ta'ala has sent me to you so that you may command me as you wish and I shall execute it. If you wish that I should bring together the two mountains of Akhshabain (Abu Qubais and Qaiqaan) upon these disbelievers, I will do so.'

"Hearing this, the Beloved ﷺ replied: 'No. Rather, I hope that Allah Ta'ala will create from their progeny such people who will worship only Allah Ta'ala and will not associate partners with Him.'" (`Bukhari, 1/458`)

### Preaching to the Tribes

The practice of the Beloved Prophet ﷺ was that during the time of Hajj, when distant Arab tribes gathered in Makka, the Beloved ﷺ would visit all the tribes and invite people to Islam. Similarly, various fairs were held in Arabia where distant Arab tribes gathered. The Beloved ﷺ would also go to these fairs for the propagation of Islam. So at the great fairs of Akkaz, Muzna, and Zul Majaz, the Beloved ﷺ presented the invitation of Islam before the Arab tribes.

The Beloved ﷺ presented Islam before all the famous tribes of the Arabs: Banu Aamir, Muharib, Fazara, Ghassan, Murrah, Sulaim, Abs, Banu Nasr, Kanda, Qalb, Uzra, Hazarima and others. But his uncle Abu Lahab would accompany him everywhere. When the Beloved preached to any tribe, Abu Lahab would shout, "He has turned away from religion. He is lying."

When the Beloved ﷺ went to the tribe of Banu Zahl bin Shaiban, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique was also with him. The chief of this tribe, Mafrooq, turned his attention towards him and asked, "O brother of Quraish! What religion do you present before the people?" The Beloved ﷺ said, "Allah is One and I am His Messenger." Then he recited a few verses of Surah Al-Anaam.

These people were extremely impressed by his speech and the Quranic verses. But they said, "How can we suddenly abandon the religion of our family which we have been following for ages? Besides, we are subjects under the influence of the King of Persia, Kisra, and we have made a pact that we will not be under anyone else's influence except the King." The Beloved ﷺ praised their frankness and said, "Very well. Allah is the Supporter, Helper, and Assistant of His religion."
