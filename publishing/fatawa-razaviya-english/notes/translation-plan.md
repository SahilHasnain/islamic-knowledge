# Fatawa Razaviya Standard English Translation Plan

## Objective

Produce a complete, faithful English translation of Fatawa Razaviya from the Urdu source,
preserving its legal reasoning, scholarly references, devotional language, and structure.

## Workflow

1. Extract complete source units from `db/fatawa_e_razvia.db` with traceable row metadata.
2. Translate every Urdu passage without summarizing or omitting content.
3. Preserve essential Arabic quotations and honorifics, with English meanings where appropriate.
4. Apply reverent English consistent with Kanzul Iman English where Quranic wording is involved.
5. Review terminology and record decisions in the glossary and editorial notes.
6. Validate coverage, ordering, citations, Arabic, honorifics, and adab.
7. Keep each manuscript file below 1,000 lines and split only at complete logical boundaries.

## Pilot

Begin with volume `59`, lesson `472`, records `17231–17235`, the five-record sanad section.
Do not scale to longer sections until this pilot is reviewed.
