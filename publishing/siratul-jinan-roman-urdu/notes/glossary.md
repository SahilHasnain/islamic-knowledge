# Sirat-ul-Jinan Roman Urdu Glossary

This glossary records approved Urdu → Roman spellings for the transliteration project. Add terms as romanization decisions are made.

Terms that remain in Arabic script (Quranic Arabic, duas, hadith quotations, Islamic phrases, and Arabic honorifics) are never romanized and are therefore not listed here.

## Romanization Scheme Notes

- Use apostrophes for Urdu letters not present in English (e.g., `ع` as `'`, `ء` as `'`) following the approved convention.
- Long vowels and nasalization follow established Roman Urdu spellings; do not invent new spellings for common words.
- Words with established Roman Urdu forms (e.g., `Allah`, `nabi`, `sunnat`) keep those forms.
