## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:58

`Source: tafseerId=50196; ayatId=67; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ قُلْنَا ادْخُلُوْا هٰذِهِ الْقَرْیَةَ فَكُلُوْا مِنْهَا حَیْثُ شِئْتُمْ رَغَدًا وَّ ادْخُلُوا الْبَابَ سُجَّدًا وَّ قُوْلُوْا حِطَّةٌ نَّغْفِرْ لَكُمْ خَطٰیٰكُمْؕ-وَ سَنَزِیْدُ الْمُحْسِنِیْنَ(58)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ اِذْ قُلْنَا ادْخُلُوْا هٰذِهِ الْقَرْیَةَ: Aur jab hum ne kaha is shehar mein daakhil ho jao.} Is shehar se ''بیتُ المقدس'' muraad hai ya Arihaa jo Bait-ul-Muqaddas ke qareeb hai jis mein Amaaliqah aabaad thay aur woh use khaali kar gaye thay, wahan ghalle mywe bikasrat thay. Is basti ke darwaaze mein daakhil hone ka farmaaya gaya aur yeh darwaaza un ke liye Ka'bah ki tarah tha aur us mein daakhil hona aur us ki taraf sajda karna gunaahon ki maafi ka sabab tha. Bani Israeel ko hukm yeh tha ke darwaaze mein sajda karte hue daakhil hon aur zabaan se ''حِطَّةٌ'' kehte jaayen (yeh kalma istighfaar tha.) Unhonne donon ahkaam ki mukhaalifat ki aur sajda karte hue daakhil hone ki bajaaye sareenon ke bal ghisatte hue daakhil hue aur taubah o istighfaar ka kalma parhne ki bajaaye mazaaq ke taur par ''حَبَّۃٌ فِی شَعْرۃٍ'' kehne lage jis ka ma'ani tha: baal mein daana. Is mazaaq aur nafarmani ki saza mein un par taa'oon musallat kiya gaya jis se hazaaron Israeeli halaak ho gaye.
