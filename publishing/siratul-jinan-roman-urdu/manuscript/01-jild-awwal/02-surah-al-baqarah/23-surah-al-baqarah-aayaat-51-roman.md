## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:51

`Source: tafseerId=50191; ayatId=60; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ وٰعَدْنَا مُوْسٰۤى اَرْبَعِیْنَ لَیْلَةً ثُمَّ اتَّخَذْتُمُ الْعِجْلَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِهٖ وَ اَنْتُمْ ظٰلِمُوْنَ(51)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ اِذْ وٰعَدْنَا مُوْسٰۤى اَرْبَعِیْنَ لَیْلَةً: Aur jab hum ne Moosa se chaalees raaton ka wa'da farmaaya.} Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayat mein Bani Israeel par ki gayi jo ne'mat bayaan hui, is ka khulaasa yeh hai ke Firaun aur Firauniyon ki halaakat ke baad jab Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Bani Israeel ko le kar Misr ki taraf lote to un ki darkhwaast par Allah Ta'ala ne Tauraat ata farmane ka wa'da farmaaya aur is ke liye tees din aur phir das din ka izaafa kar ke chaalees din ki muddat muqarrar hui, jaisa ke Surah A'raaf aayat 142 mein hai. In chaalees dinon mein Zul-Qa'dah ka poora maheena aur das din Zul-Hijjah ke shaamil thay. Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام apni qaum par Hazrat Haaroon عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apna naaib bana kar Tauraat haasil karne ko Tur par tashreef le gaye, chaalees din raat wahan thehre aur is arsah mein kisi se baat cheet na ki. Allah Ta'ala ne takhtiyon par tahriri soorat mein Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Tauraat ata farmaayi.

Doosri taraf Saamri ne jawahraat se mazeen sone ka ek bachhda bana kar qaum se kaha ke yeh tumhara ma'bool hai. Woh log ek maheenay tak Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka intezaar karne ke baad Saamri ke bahkaane se bachhde ki pooja karne lage, in pooja karne waalon mein tamaam Bani Israeel shaamil thay, sirf Hazrat Haaroon عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Aap ke baarah hazaar saathi is shirk se door o nafoor rahe. Jab Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام waapas tashreef laaye to qaum ki haalat dekh kar unhein tanbeeh ki aur unhein un ke gunaah ka kaffaara bataya, chunanchah jab unhonne Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke bataaye hue tareeqe ke mutabiq taubah ki to Allah Ta'ala ne unhein ma'af kar diya. In murtad hone waalon ki taubah ka bayaan aayat number 53 ke baad aa raha hai.
