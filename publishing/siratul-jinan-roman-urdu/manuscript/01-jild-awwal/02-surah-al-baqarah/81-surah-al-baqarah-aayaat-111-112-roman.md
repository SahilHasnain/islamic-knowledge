## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:111

`Source: tafseerId=50242; ayatId=120; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالُوْا لَنْ یَّدْخُلَ الْجَنَّةَ اِلَّا مَنْ كَانَ هُوْدًا اَوْ نَصٰرٰىؕ-تِلْكَ اَمَانِیُّهُمْؕ-قُلْ هَاتُوْا بُرْهَانَكُمْ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِیْنَ(111)

**Roman Urdu:**

{لَنْ یَّدْخُلَ الْجَنَّةَ: Hargiz jannat mein daakhil nahin honge.} Yahoodi Musalmanon se kehte thay ke jannat mein sirf Yahoodi jayenge aur Isai kehte thay ke sirf wohi jannat mein daakhil honge. Yeh guftagu Musalmanon ko behkaane ke liye thi, un ki tardeed mein yeh aayat-e-kareemah naazil hui, jis mein farmaaya gaya ke un ki yeh baat un ki apni raay se hai, Tauraat o Injeel mein aisa kuchh nahin farmaaya gaya, agar woh sache hain to apni is baat par koi daleel laayen.

{اَمَانِیُّهُمْ: Un ki man gharat tamaannain.} Baghair kisi bunyaad ke jannat mein daakhile ke zabaani dawey karna jahaalat o himaaqat hai. Aik umeed hai jise Arabi mein ''رَجا'' kehte hain aur aik khaam khayaali hai jise Arabi mein ''اُمْنِیَّہْ'' kehte hain. Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne is mauzoo par bara pyara kalaam irshad farmaaya hai. Is ka khaulasah mulahiza farmain. Chanaanchay aap رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmaate hain: Rajaa yani umeed dil ki is raahat ka naam hai jo mehboob cheez ke intezaar se haasil hoti hai lekin yeh mehboob jis ki tawaqqo ki ja rahi hai is ka koi sabab hona chahiye lihaaza agar is ka intezaar aksar asbaab ke saath hai to usay ''رَجا'' yani umeed kehte hain aur agar asbaab bilkul na hon ya iztiraab ke saath hon to usay umeed nahin balke dhoka aur bewuqoofi kaha jaye ga aur agar asbaab ke hone ya na hone ka ilm na ho to is intezaar ko tamanna kehte hain. Ahl-e-dil hazraat jaante hain ke duniya aakhirat ki kheti hai aur dil zameen ki tarah hai, imaan is mein beej ki heysiyyat rakhta hai aur ibaadat zameen ko ulta palat karne, saaf karne aur nahrein khodne aur in zameenon mein paani jaari karne ki tarah hain aur dil jo duniya mein gharaq aur dooba hua hai us banjar zameen ki tarah hai jis mein beej koi phal nahin laata aur qiyaamat ka din fasal kaatne ka din hai aur har shakhs wohi kaate ga jo us ne boya ho ga aur kheti ka barhna imaan ke beej ke baghair naamumkin hai aur jab dil mein khabaasat aur bure akhlaaq hon to imaan bahut kam nafa deta hai jaise banjar zameen mein beej se fasal paida nahin hoti to munasib yahi hai ke aadmi ke maghfirat ki umeed rakhne ko kheti waale par qiyaas kiya jaye yani jo shakhs achhi zameen haasil karta hai aur is mein umdah beej daalta hai jo na to kharaab hota hai aur na hi badboodaar aur phir is ki tamaam zarooriyaat ko poora karta hai. Zarooriyaat se muraad waqt par paani dena, zameen ko kaanton aur ghaas phoons neze in tamaam kharabiyon se paak karna jo beej ko barhne se rokti hain ya kharaab kar deti hain. To jo yeh sab kaam kare phir Allah Ta'ala ke fazl ka muntazir ho kar baith jaye ke woh zameen ko bijli ki garaj aur deegar aafaat se bachaye ga yahaan tak ke kheti apni takmeel ko pahunch jaye to is intezaar ko raja yani umeed kehte hain aur agar sakht zameen mein beej daale jo shor-zadah ho aur bulandi par ho jis tak paani nahin pahunch sakta aur beej ki parwa bhi na kare aur phir is ke katne ka intezaar kare to is intezaar ko bewuqoofi aur dhoka kehte hain umeed nahin kehte aur agar achhi zameen mein beej daala lekin is mein paani nahin hai ab woh baarish ke intezaar mein hai aur yeh aisa waqt hai jis mein aam taur par baarish nahin barasti to is intezaar ko tamanna kehte hain ''رَجا'' nahin kehte to goya rajaa ka lafz kisi aisi pasandeedah cheez ke intezaar par sadiq aata hai jis ke liye woh tamaam asbaab tayaar kar diye gaye hon jo bande ke ikhtiyaar mein hain, sirf wohi asbaab baaqi reh gaye jo bande ke ikhtiyaar mein nahin hain aur woh Allah Ta'ala ka fazl hai jis ki wajah se tamaam nuqsaan-deh aur faasad karne waale asbaab kheti se door ho jaate hain pas jab banda imaan ka beej daalta hai aur is ko ibaadat ka paani pilaata hai, dil ko bad-akhlaqi ke kaanton se paak karta hai aur Allah Ta'ala ke fazl ka marte dam tak muntazir rehta hai, maghfirat tak pahunchaane waale achhe khatime ka intezaar karta hai to yeh intezaar haqeeqi rajaa (umeed) hai yeh zaati taur par qaabil-e-tareef hai aur agar imaan ke beej ko ibaadat ka paani na diya jaye ya dil ko bure akhlaaq se mulawwas chhod diya jaye aur dunyawi lazzat mein munhamik ho jaye aur phir maghfirat ka intezaar kare to is ka intezaar aik bewuqoof aur dhoke mein mubtila shakhs ka intezaar hai. Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaaya ''اَلْاَحْمَقُ مَنِ اتَّبَعَ نَفْسَہٗ ہَوَاھَا وَتَمَنّٰی عَلَی اللہِ الْاَمَانِیَّ'' bewuqoof woh hai jo apne nafs ko khwaahishaat ke peeche laata hai aur (is ke bawajood) Allah Ta'ala se tamaannain karta hai (ke woh bara kareem aur Ghafoor o Raheem hai, is se aur is ke a'maal se be-parwah hai is liye Allah Ta'ala usay koi saza nahin dega balke jannat mein daakhil kar dega aur woh is ki man-chahi cheezein usay dega). (فیض القدیر، حرف الہمزۃ، ۲ / ۴۴۵، تحت الحدیث: ۲۰۲۵، احیاء علوم الدین، کتاب الخوف والرجاء، بیان حقیقۃ الرجاء، ۴ / ۱۷۵)

## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:112

`Source: tafseerId=50243; ayatId=121; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** بَلٰىۗ-مَنْ اَسْلَمَ وَجْهَهٗ لِلّٰهِ وَ هُوَ مُحْسِنٌ فَلَهٗۤ اَجْرُهٗ عِنْدَ رَبِّهٖ ۪-وَ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَیْهِمْ وَ لَا هُمْ یَحْزَنُوْنَ۠    (112)

**Roman Urdu:**

{بَلٰى : Kiyon nahin.} Jannat mein daakhile ka haqeeqi miyaar imaan-e-sahih aur amal-e-saalih hai aur kisi bhi zamane aur kisi bhi nasl o qaum ka aadmi agar sahih imaan o amal rakhta hai to woh jannat mein jaye ga. Albatteh yeh yaad rahe ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ke elaan-e-nubuwwat ke baad aap ki nubuwwat na maanne waale ka imaan qatan sahih nahin ho sakta aur koi bhi amal imaan ke baghair saalih nahin ho sakta, goya jo Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan rakhe aur amal-e-saalih kare woh jannat ka mustahiqq hai.

Chonke Yahoodiyon aur Isaiyon ne kaha tha ke un ke siwa koi jannat mein daakhil nahin hoga to is ke jawab mein farmaaya gaya ke un ke ilawa koi jannat mein kiyon daakhil nahin hoga jabke Allah Ta'ala ka qanoon yeh hai ke jo bhi imaan sahih aur amal saalih le kar aaye ga woh jannat mein daakhil hoga.
