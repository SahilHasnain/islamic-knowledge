## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:73

`Source: tafseerId=50207; ayatId=82; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** فَقُلْنَا اضْرِبُوْهُ بِبَعْضِهَاؕ-كَذٰلِكَ یُحْیِ اللّٰهُ الْمَوْتٰىۙ-وَ یُرِیْكُمْ اٰیٰتِهٖ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَعْقِلُوْنَ(73)

**Roman Urdu:**

{فَقُلْنَا: To hum ne farmaaya.} Bani Israeel ne gaaye zibah kar ke us ke kisi uzoo se murdah ko maara woh be-hukm-e-ilaahi zindah ho gaya, us ke halaq se khoon ka fawaara jaari tha, us ne apne chachazaad bhaai ke baare mein bataaya ke us ne mujhe qatl kiya, ab us ko bhi iqraar karna parha aur Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne is par qisaas ka hukm farmaaya. Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein Allah Ta'ala ne murde ko zindah karne ke is waqea se Qiyaamah ke din uthaaye jaane par daleel qaaim ki ke tum samajh lo ke jaise Allah Ta'ala ne is murde ko zindah kiya isi tarah woh Qiyaamah ke din bhi murdon ko zindah farmaayega kyunke woh murde zindah karne par qaadir hai aur roz-e-jazaa murdon ko zindah karna aur hisaab lena haq hai.
