## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:85

`Source: tafseerId=50217; ayatId=94; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** ثُمَّ اَنْتُمْ هٰۤؤُلَآءِ تَقْتُلُوْنَ اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ تُخْرِجُوْنَ فَرِیْقًا مِّنْكُمْ مِّنْ دِیَارِهِمْ٘-تَظٰهَرُوْنَ عَلَیْهِمْ بِالْاِثْمِ وَ الْعُدْوَانِؕ-وَ اِنْ یَّاْتُوْكُمْ اُسٰرٰى تُفٰدُوْهُمْ وَ هُوَ مُحَرَّمٌ عَلَیْكُمْ اِخْرَاجُهُمْؕ-اَفَتُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِبَعْضِ الْكِتٰبِ وَ تَكْفُرُوْنَ بِبَعْضٍۚ-فَمَا جَزَآءُ مَنْ یَّفْعَلُ ذٰلِكَ مِنْكُمْ اِلَّا خِزْیٌ فِی الْحَیٰوةِ الدُّنْیَاۚ-وَ یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِ یُرَدُّوْنَ اِلٰۤى اَشَدِّ الْعَذَابِؕ-وَ مَا اللّٰهُ بِغَافِلٍ عَمَّا تَعْمَلُوْنَ(85)

**Roman Urdu:**

{ثُمَّ اَنْتُمْ هٰۤؤُلَآءِ: Phir yeh jo tum ho.} Is aayat mein Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ke muqaddas zamaane mein maujood Yahoodiyon ke ajeeb o gharib tarz-e-amal ka bayaan hai, is ka pasmanzar yeh hai ke Tauraat mein Bani Israeel ko teen hukm diye gaye thay: (۱) ek doosre ko qatal na karna. (۲) ek doosre ko jila-watan na karna. (۳) agar woh apni qaum ke kisi mard ya aurat ko is haal mein paayein ke use ghulaam ya laundi bana liya gaya hai to use khareed kar azaad kar dein. Nasal dar nasal yeh ahd chalta raha yahan tak ke Madinah Munawwarah ke gird-o-nawaah mein aabaad Yahoodiyon ke do qabaa'il Bani Qurayzah aur Bani Nadheer mein muntaqil hua, us waqt Madinah Shareef mein mushrikon ke do qabeelay Aus aur Khazraj rehte thay, Bani Qurayzah Aus qabeelay ke haleef thay aur Bani Nadheer Khazraj qabeelay ke haleef thay aur har ek qabeelah ne apne haleef ke saath qasam utha rakhi thi ke agar hum mein se kisi par koi hamla-aawar ho to doosra us ki madad karega. Jab Aus aur Khazraj ke darmiyaan jang hoti to Bani Qurayzah Aus ki aur Bani Nadheer Khazraj ki madad ke liye aate thay aur apne haleef ke saath ho kar aapas mein ek doosre par talwaar chalaate aur Bani Qurayzah Bani Nadheer ko aur woh Bani Qurayzah ko qatal karte, un ke ghar weeraan kar dete aur unhein un ki rahaaish-gahon se nikaal dete thay, lekin jab un ki qaum ke logon ko un ke haleef qaid kar lete to woh un ko maal de kar chhuda lete thay, masalan agar Bani Nadheer ka koi shakhs Aus qabeelay ke haath mein giraftaar hota to Bani Qurayzah Aus qabeelay ko maali mu'aawizah de kar us ko chhuda lete halaanke agar wohi shakhs ladaai ke waqt un ke haath aa jaata to use qatal karne mein hargiz daryagh na karte thay. Logon ne un se kaha ke tum bhi ajeeb log ho ke ek doosre ko qatal bhi karte ho aur fidyah de kar chhuda bhi lete ho. Yahoodiyon ne kaha: humein Tauraat mein yeh hukm diya gaya hai ke hum apne qaidiyon ko fidyah de kar chhuda lein, is par un se kaha gaya ke phir tum ek doosre ko qatal kyun karte ho halaanke Tauraat mein tumhein is cheez se bhi to mana kiya gaya hai. Yahoodiyon ne kaha: jang ke dauran ek doosre ko qatal karne se agarche hamaara Allah Ta'ala ke saath kiya hua ahd toot jaata hai lekin humein is baat se sharm aati hai ke hamaare haleef zaleel hon kyunke agar hum ne ek doosre ko qatal aur jila-watan na kiya to is mein hamaare haleefon ki zillat hai aur kyunke fidyah de kar chhuda lene mein un ki zillat nahin hai is liye hum is ahd ko poora kar dete hain. Yahoodiyon ki is harkat par unhein is aayat mein malaamat ki ja rahi hai ke jab tum ne apnon ki khoon-rezi na karne, un ko bastiyon se na nikaalne aur un ke aseer-on ko chhudaane ka ahd kiya tha to yeh kya do-rangi hai ke tum ek doosre ko qatal aur jila-watan karne mein to hargiz daryagh nahin karte aur jab koi giraftaar ho jaaye to use fidyah de kar chhuda lete ho aur jin baaton ka tum ne ahd kiya tha un mein se kuchh baaton ko maanna aur kuchh ko na maanna kya maani rakhta hai! Jab tum ek doosre ko qatal aur jila-watan karne se baaz na rahe to tum ne ahd-shikni ki aur haraam ke murtakib hue.

{اَفَتُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِبَعْضِ الْكِتٰبِ: Kya kitaab ke kuchh hisse par imaan rakhte ho.} Bani Israeel ka amali imaan naaqis tha ke kuchh hisse par amal karte thay aur kuchh par nahin aur is par farmaaya gaya ke kya tum kitaab ke kuchh hisse par imaan rakhte ho aur kuchh se inkaar karte ho. Is aayat se maloom hua ke shari'at ke tamaam ahkaam par imaan rakhna zaroori hai aur tamaam zaroori ahkaam par amal karna bhi zaroori hai. Koi shakhs kisi waqt bhi shari'at ki paabandi se azaad nahin ho sakta aur khud ko tareeqat ka naam le kar ya kisi bhi tareeqay se shari'at se azaad kehne waale kaafir hain. Is ki tafseel jaanne ke liye Fataawa Razwiyyah ki 21ween jild mein maujood A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki kitaab ''مَقَالُ الْعُرَفَاءُ بِاِعْزَازِ شَرْعٍ وَعلماء (علماء اور شریعت کی عظمت پر اہل ِمعرفت کا کلام)'' ka mutaala'ah farmaayein. Yaad rahe ke azmat-e-tauheed ko maanna lekin azmat-e-risaalat se inkaar karna bhi isi zumeer mein aata hai ke kya tum kitaab ke kuchh hisse ko maante ho aur kuchh hisse ka inkaar karte ho.

{خِزْیٌ فِی الْحَیٰوةِ الدُّنْیَا: Dunya ki zindagi mein ruswaai.} Bani Israeel ki dunya mein to yeh ruswaai hui ke Bani Qurayzah 3 hijri mein maaray gaye aur Bani Nadheer is se pehle hi jila-watan kar diye gaye. Is se maloom hua ke kisi ki tarafdaari mein deen ki mukhaalifat karna aakhirawi azaab ke ilaawah dunya mein bhi zillat o ruswaai ka baa'is hota hai. Dunya ke bare bare mu'aamilaat ko is aayat ke tehet dekha ja sakta hai. Neez yeh bhi maloom hua ke kabhi gunaahon ki shaamat se dunyawi aafaat bhi aa jaati hain.

{وَ مَا اللّٰهُ بِغَافِلٍ: Aur Allah ghaafil nahin.} Is tarah ki aayaat jin mein yeh mafhoom ho ke Allah Ta'ala tumhaare a'maal se ghaafil nahin un mein nafarmaanon ke liye shadeed wa'eed hoti hai ke Allah Ta'ala tumhaare af'aal se be-khabar nahin hai, tumhaari nafarmaniyon par shadeed azaab dega. Yonhi aisi aayaat mein mo'minin o saaliheen ke liye khush-khabri hoti hai ke unhein a'maal-e-hasanah ki behtareen jaza milegi.
