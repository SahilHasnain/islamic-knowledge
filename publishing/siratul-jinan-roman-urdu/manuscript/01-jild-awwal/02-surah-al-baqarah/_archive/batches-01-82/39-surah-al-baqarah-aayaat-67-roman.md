## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:67

`Source: tafseerId=50204; ayatId=76; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى لِقَوْمِهٖۤ اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَاْمُرُكُمْ اَنْ تَذْبَحُوْا بَقَرَةًؕ-قَالُوْۤا اَتَتَّخِذُنَا هُزُوًاؕ-قَالَ اَعُوْذُ بِاللّٰهِ اَنْ اَكُوْنَ مِنَ الْجٰهِلِیْنَ(67)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى: Aur jab Moosa ne farmaaya.} In aayaat mein Yahoodiyon ko jo waqea yaad dilaaya ja raha hai us ka khulaasah yeh hai ke Bani Israeel mein aik maaldaar shakhs Aameel ko us ke azeez ne khufiyah taur par qatl kar ke doosre mohalla mein daal diya taake us ki miraas bhi le aur khoon baha bhi aur phir daawa kar diya ke mujhe khoon baha dilwaaya jaaye. Qaatil ka pata nahin chalta tha. Wahan ke logon ne Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se darkhwaast ki ke Aap dua farmaayein ke Allah Ta'ala haqeeqat haal zaahir farmaaye, is par hukm hua ke aik gaaye zibah kar ke us ka koi hissa maqtool ke maarein, woh zindah ho kar qaatil ke baare mein bata dega. Logon ne hairaani se kaha ke kya Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام hum se mazaaq kar rahe hain kyunke maqtool ka haal maloom hone aur gaaye ke zibah karne mein koi munasibat maloom nahin hoti. Is par Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne jawab diya, ''Main is baat se Allah Ta'ala ki panaah maangta hoon ke main mazaaq kar ke jaahilon mein se hojaa'oon.'' Jab Bani Israeel ne samajh liya ke gaaye ka zibah karna mazaaq nahin balke baqaedah hukm hai to unhon ne Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se us ke ausaaf daryaaft kiye aur baar baar sawaal kar ke woh log qaidein badhaate gaye aur bil-aaqir yeh hukm hua ke aisi gaaye zibah karo jo na boordhi ho aur na bohat kam umar balke darmiyaani umar ki ho, badan par koi daagh na ho, aik hi rang ki ho, rang aankhon ko bhaane waala ho, is gaaye ne kabhi kheiti baari ki ho na kabhi kheiti ko paani diya ho. Aakhri sawaal mein unhon ne kaha ke ab hum ان شآء اللہ raah paalenge. Behr-haal jab sab kuchh tai ho gaya to un ki tasalli ho gayi phir unhon ne gaaye ki talaash shuru kar di.

Un ke atraaf mein aisi sirf aik gaaye thi, us ka haal yeh tha ke Bani Israeel mein aik saaleh shakhs thay, un ka aik chhota bachcha tha aur un ke paas aik gaaye ke bachche ke ilaawah aur kuchh na raha tha, unhon ne us ki gardan par mohar laga kar Allah Ta'ala ke naam par chhor diya aur baargaah-e-haq mein arz ki: Yaa Rabb! عَزَّوَجَلَّ main is bachhiya ko is farzand ke liye tere paas wadee'at rakhta hoon taake jab yeh farzand bada ho to yeh us ke kaam aaye. Is nek shakhs ka to inteqaal ho gaya lekin woh bachhiya jangal mein Allah Ta'ala ki hifaazat mein parwarish paati rahi, yeh ladka jab bada hua to saaleh o muttaqi bana aur maan ka farmaanbardar tha. Aik din us ki waalidah ne kaha: Ae noor-e-nazar! Tere baap ne tere liye fulaan jangal mein Khuda ke naam aik bachhiya chhorhi thi woh ab jawaan ho gayi hogi, Allah Ta'ala se dua karo ke woh tujhe ata farmaaye aur tu use jangal se le aa. Ladka jangal mein gaya aur us ne gaaye ko jangal mein dekha aur waalidah ki bataayi hui alaamatein us mein paayeen aur us ko Allah Ta'ala ki qasam de kar bulaaya to woh haazir ho gayi. Woh jawaan us gaaye ko waalidah ki khidmat mein laaya. Waalidah ne baazaar mein le ja kar teen deenaar par farokht karne ka hukm diya aur yeh shart ki ke soda hone par phir us ki ijaazat haasil ki jaaye, us zamaane mein gaaye ki qeemat un atraaf mein teen deenaar hi thi. Jawaan jab us gaaye ko baazaar mein laaya to aik farishtah kharidaar ki soorat mein aaya aur us ne gaaye ki qeemat chhe deenaar laga di magar yeh shart rakhi ke jawaan apni waalidah se ijaazat nahin lega. Jawaan ne yeh manzoor na kiya aur waalidah se tamaam qissah kaha, us ki waalidah ne chhe deenaar qeemat manzoor karne ki to ijaazat di magar bechne mein phir dobara apni marzi daryaaft karne ki shart laga di. Jawaan phir baazaar mein aaya, is martabah farishteh ne baarah deenaar qeemat lagaai aur kaha ke waalidah ki ijaazat par mauqoof na rakho. Jo unhon ne na maana aur waalidah ko ittila di, woh saahib-e-firaasat aurat samajh gayi ke yeh kharidaar nahin koi farishtah hai jo aazmaish ke liye aata hai. Bete se kaha ke ab ki martabah is kharidaar se yeh kehna ke Aap humein is gaaye ke farokht karne ka hukm dete hain ya nahin? Ladke ne yahi kaha to farishteh ne jawab diya ke abhi is ko roke raho, jab Bani Israeel kharidne aayein to is ki qeemat yeh muqarrar karna ke is ki khaal mein sona bhar diya jaaye. Jawaan gaaye ko ghar laaya aur jab Bani Israeel justaju karte hue us ke makaan par pahunche to yahi qeemat tai ki aur Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki zamaanat par woh gaaye Bani Israeel ke sipurd ki. (خازن، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۶۷، ۱ / ۶۰-۶۱)

Gaaye zibah karne waale waqea se maloom hone waale masa'il:

Is waqea se kai cheezein maloom huiin:

(1)… Nabi ke farmaan par baghair hichkichaahat amal karna chahiye aur amal karne ki bajaye aqli dhakausle banaana be-adabon ka kaam hai.

(2)… Paighambar jhooth, dil lagi aur kisi ka mazaaq uraana waghairah aibon se paak hain albattah khush-tabee'i aik mahmood sifat hai yeh un mein paayi ja sakti hai.

(3)… Shar'i ahkaamaat se muta'allig be-jaa behas mashaqqat ka sabab hai. Hadees-e-shareef mein hai ke agar Bani Israeel behas na nikaalte to jo gaaye zibah kar dete wahi kaafi ho jaati. (در منثور، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۷۰، ۱ / ۱۸۹)

(4)… اِنْ شَآءَ اللہ kehne ki bohat barkat hai. Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: agar Bani Israeel وَ اِنَّاۤ اِنْ شَآءَ اللّٰهُ لَمُهْتَدُوْنَ na kehte to kabhi woh gaaye na paate. (کشف الخفاء، حرف الشین المعجمۃ، ۲ / ۷، رقم: ۱۵۲۸)

(5)… Jo apne ayaal ko Allah Ta'ala ke sipurd kare Allah Ta'ala us ki umdah parwarish farmaata hai.

(6)… Jo apna maal Allah Ta'ala ke bharosah par us ki amaanat mein de Allah Ta'ala us mein barkat deta hai.

(7)… Waalidain ki farmaanbardaari Allah Ta'ala ko pasand hai.

(8)… Maan baap ki khidmat o ita'at karne waalon ko donon jahaanon mein milta hai.
