## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:23

`Source: tafseerId=50166; ayatId=32; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ فِیْ رَیْبٍ مِّمَّا نَزَّلْنَا عَلٰى عَبْدِنَا فَاْتُوْا بِسُوْرَةٍ مِّنْ مِّثْلِهٖ۪-وَ ادْعُوْا شُهَدَآءَكُمْ مِّنْ دُوْنِ اللّٰهِ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِیْنَ(23)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ فِیْ رَیْبٍ: aur agar tumhein kuchh shak ho.} Is se pehli aayaat mein Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat o wahdaniyat ka bayan hua aur yahan se Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat aur Quran-e-Karim ke Allah Ta'ala ki be-misl kitaab hone ki woh qaahir daleel bayan farmayi ja rahi hai jo taalib-e-saadiq ko itminaan bakhshe aur munkiron ko aajiz kar de. Allah Ta'ala ki azmat ki sab se badi daleel Muhammad-e-Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ hain aur Muhammad-e-Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki azmat ki sab se badi daleel Quran hai, lihaaza is rukoo mein tarteeb se in sab ko bayan kiya gaya hai.

{عَلٰى عَبْدِنَا: apne khaas bande par.} Is aayat mein khaas bande se Huzoor-e-poor-noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ murad hain. (مدارک، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۲۳، ص۳۵) Yahan is andaaz-e-ta'bir mein Nabi-e-Rahmat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki shaan-e-mahboobiyat ki taraf bhi ishaara hai. A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ kya khoob farmate hain:

Lekin Raza ne khatm-e-sukhan is par kar diya

Khaaliq ka bandah khalq ka aaqa kahoon tujhe

{فَاْتُوْا بِسُوْرَةٍ مِّنْ مِّثْلِهٖ: to tum is jaisi ek surah bana lao.} Aayat ke is hisse aur is ke baad wali aayat mein Quran ke be-misl hone par do-tok alfaaz mein ek khuli daleel di ja rahi hai ke apni fasaahat o balaaghat par naaz karne walon ko challenge hai ke agar tum Quran ko Allah Ta'ala ki kitaab nahin balke kisi insaan ki tasneef samajhte ho to chunke tum bhi insaan ho lihaaza is jaisi ek surah bana kar le aao jo fasaahat o balaaghat, husn-e-tarteeb, ghaib ki khabrein dene aur digar umoor mein Quran-e-Pak ki misl ho aur agar aisi koi surah balke aayat tak na bana sako to samajh lo ke Quran Allah Ta'ala ki kitaab hai aur Allah Ta'ala ki kitaab ka inkaar karne walon ka anjaam dozakh hai jo bataur-e-khaas kaafiron ke liye tayyar ki gayi hai.

Note: Yeh challenge qiyaamat tak tamaam insaanon ke liye hai, aaj bhi Quran ko Muhammad-e-Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ki tasneef kehne wale kuffar to bohat hain magar Quran ki misl ek aayat banane wala aaj tak koi saamne nahin aaya aur jis ne is ka daawa kiya, us ka pol khud hi chand dinon mein khul gaya.

I'jaaz-e-Quran ki wajohaat:

Quran-e-Majeed woh be-misl kitaab hai ke log apne tamaam tar kamaalaat ke bawajood Quran-e-Pak jaisa kalaam banane se aajiz hain aur jin o ins mil kar bhi us ki aayaat jaisi ek aayat bhi nahin bana sakte, us ki bunyadi wajah yeh hai ke Quran-e-Majeed Allah Ta'ala ka kalaam hai aur makhlooq mein kisi ke paas itni taaqat nahin ke woh Allah Ta'ala ke kalaam ki misl kalaam bana sake aur yehi wajah hai ke sadiyan guzarne ke bawajood aaj tak koi bhi Quran-e-Majeed ke diye hue challenge ka jawaab nahin de saka aur na hi qiyaamat tak koi de sakega. Quran-e-Pak ke be-misl hone ki bohat si wajohaat hain jinhein ulama o mufassireen ne apni kitabon mein bohat sharah o basat ke saath bayan farmaya hai, hum yahan par in mein se sirf teen wajohaat bayan karte hain. Tafseel ke liye badi tafaaseer ki taraf rujoo farmayein.

(1)… Fasaahat o balaaghat:

Arab ke log fasaahat o balaaghat ke maydaan ke shahsawaar the aur un ki safon mein bohat se aise log maujood the jo ke balaaghat ke fun mein a'la-tareen mansab rakhne wale, umdah alfaaz bolne wale, chhote aur bade jumlon ko badi fasaahat se tayyar karne wale the aur thode kalaam mein behtareen tasarruf kar lete the, apni muraad ko bade umdah andaaz mein bayan karte, kalaam mein fasaahat o balaaghat ke tamaam funoon ki ri'aayat karte aur aise maahir the ke fasaahat o balaaghat ke jis darwaze se chahte daakhil ho jate the, al-gharz duniya mein har taraf un ki fasaahat o balaaghat ka danka bajta tha aur log fasaahat o balaaghat mein un ka muqaabla karne ki taab na rakhte the.

In ahl-e-Arab ko fasaahat o balaaghat ke maydaan mein agar kisi ne aajiz kiya hai to woh kalaam-e-Quran-e-Majeed hai, is muqaddas kitaab ki fasaahat o balaaghat ne ahl-e-Arab ki aqloon ko hairan kar diya aur apni misl laane se aajiz kar diya.

(2)… Tilaawat-e-Quran ki taaseer:

Quran-e-Majeed ke be-misl hone ki ek daleel yeh bhi hai ke ise parhne aur sunne wala kabhi seer nahin hota aur na hi is se uktaata hai balke woh us ki jitni zyada tilaawat karta hai utni hi zyada sheereeni aur lazzat paata hai aur baar baar us ki tilaawat karne se us ki muhabbat dil mein raasikh hoti jaati hai aur is ke alawa koi aur kalaam agarche woh kitni hi khoobi wala aur kitna hi faseeh o baligh kyun na ho use baar baar parhne se dil ukta jata hai aur jab use dobara parha jaye to tabi'at bezaar ho jati hai. Quran-e-Majeed ki is shaan ke baare mein Hazrat Haarith رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marwi hai, Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtaza کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم farmate hain, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya ''Quran woh hai jis ki barkat se khwahishaat bigadti nahin aur jis se doosri zubaanein mushtabah nahin hoti, ulama is se seer nahin hote, yeh baar baar dahraaye jaane se puraana nahin hota aur is ke ajaa'ibaat khatam nahin hote.'' (ترمذی، کتاب فضائل القرآن، باب ما جاء فی فضل القرآن، ۴ / ۴۱۴-۴۱۵، الحدیث: ۲۹۱۵)

(3)… Ghaib ki khabrein:

Quran-e-Pak mein mustaqbil ke mutalliq jo khabrein di gayin woh tamaam ki tamaam poori huin masalan zamana-e-nabwi mein roomiyon ke iraniyon par ghalib aane ki khabar di gayi aur woh 100% poori hui.

## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:24

`Source: tafseerId=50167; ayatId=33; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** فَاِنْ لَّمْ تَفْعَلُوْا وَ لَنْ تَفْعَلُوْا فَاتَّقُوا النَّارَ الَّتِیْ وَ قُوْدُهَا النَّاسُ وَ الْحِجَارَةُ ۚۖ-اُعِدَّتْ لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ(24)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ قُوْدُهَا النَّاسُ وَ الْحِجَارَةُ: is ka indhan aadmi aur patthar hain.} Is aayat mein aadmi se kaafir aur patthar se woh but murad hain jinhein kuffar poojte hain aur un ki muhabbat mein Quran-e-Pak aur Rasool-e-Karim صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka inkaar karte hain. Pattharon ka jahannam mein jaana un pattharon ki saza nahin balke un ke pujaariyon ki saza ke liye hoga yani pujaariyon ko in pattharon ke saath saza di jayegi.

{اُعِدَّتْ لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ: woh kaafiron ke liye tayyar ki gayi hai.} Is se maloom hua ke dozakh paida ho chuki hai kyunke yahan maazi ke alfaaz hain, neez ''kafron ke liye'' farmane mein yeh bhi ishaara hai ke momineen Allah Ta'ala ke fazl o karam se jahannam mein hamesha daakhile se mahfooz rahenge kyunke jahannam bataur-e-khaas kaafiron ke liye paida ki gayi hai.

## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:25

`Source: tafseerId=50168; ayatId=34; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ بَشِّرِ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ اَنَّ لَهُمْ جَنّٰتٍ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْاَنْهٰرُؕ-كُلَّمَا رُزِقُوْا مِنْهَا مِنْ ثَمَرَةٍ رِّزْقًاۙ-قَالُوْا هٰذَا الَّذِیْ رُزِقْنَا مِنْ قَبْلُۙ-وَ اُتُوْا بِهٖ مُتَشَابِهًاؕ-وَ لَهُمْ فِیْهَاۤ اَزْوَاجٌ مُّطَهَّرَةٌۗۙ-وَّ هُمْ فِیْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ(25)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ بَشِّرِ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ: aur un logon ko khushkhabri do jo imaan laaye aur unhonne achhe amal kiye.} Allah Ta'ala ki sunnat yeh hai ke Quran mein tarheeb yani darane ke saath targhib bhi zikr farmata hai, isi liye kuffar aur un ke aamaal o azaab ke zikr ke baad momineen aur un ke aamaal o sawaab ka zikr farmaya aur unhein jannat ki bushra di. Sualihaat yani nekiyan woh amal hain jo sharan achhe hon, in mein faraaiz o nawafil sab daakhil hain. Yahan bhi imaan aur amal ko juda juda bayan kiya jis se maloom hua ke amal imaan ka juzw nahin hain. (تفسیر مدارک، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۲۵، ص۳۸)

{وَ اُتُوْا بِهٖ مُتَشَابِهًا: aur unhein milta julta phal diya gaya.} Jannat ke phal rangat mein aapas mein milte julte honge magar zaaqe mein juda juda honge, is liye ek dafa milne ke baad jab dobaara phal milenge to jannati kahenge ke yeh phal to humein pehle bhi mil chuka hai magar jab woh khaayenge to is se nayi lazzat paayenge aur un ka lutf bohat zyada ho jayega. Is ka yeh bhi ma'ani bayan kiya gaya hai ke unhein dunyawi phalon se milte julte phal diye jayenge taake woh un phalon se maanoos rahein lekin jannati phal zaaqe mein dunyawi phalon se bohat a'la honge.

{اَزْوَاجٌ مُّطَهَّرَةٌ: paakeeza biwiyan.} Jannati biwiyan khwah hoorain hon ya aur sab ki sab tamaam naapaakiyon aur gandgiyon se bara hon, na jism par mail hoga na koi aur gandgi. Is ke saath hi woh bad-mizaji aur bad-khulqi se bhi paak hongi. (تفسیر مدارک، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۲۵، ص۳۹)

{وَ هُمْ فِیْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ: woh un baghon mein hamesha rahenge.} Jannati na kabhi fana honge aur na jannat se nikaale jayenge. Lihaaza jannat aur ahl-e-jannat ke liye fana nahin.
