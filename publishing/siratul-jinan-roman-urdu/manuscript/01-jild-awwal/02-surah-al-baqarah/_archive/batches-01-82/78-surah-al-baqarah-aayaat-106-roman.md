## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:106

`Source: tafseerId=50237; ayatId=115; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** مَا نَنْسَخْ مِنْ اٰیَةٍ اَوْ نُنْسِهَا نَاْتِ بِخَیْرٍ مِّنْهَاۤ اَوْ مِثْلِهَاؕ-اَلَمْ تَعْلَمْ اَنَّ اللّٰهَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ(106)

**Roman Urdu:**

{مَا نَنْسَخْ: Hum jo mansookh farmaayen.} Naskh ka ma'ni hai: saabiqah hukm ko kisi ba'd waali daleel-e-shar'i se utha dena. (خازن، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۰۶، ۱ / ۷۷) Aur yeh haqeeqat mein saabiqah hukm ki muddat ki intiha ka bayaan hota hai. Is aayat ka Shaan-e-Nuzool yeh hai ke Quran-e-Kareem ne guzishtah sharee'aton aur kitaabon ko mansookh farmaaya to kuffaar ko badi wahshat hui aur unhon ne is par ai'traazaat kiye, is par yeh aayat-e-mubarakah naazil hui aur bataaya gaya ke mansookh bhi Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se hai aur naasikh bhi, dono 'ain hikmat hain aur naasikh kabhi mansookh se ziyada aasaan aur nafa' bakhsh hota hai lihaaza qudrat-e-ilaahi par yaqeen rakhne waale ko is mein taraddud ki koi gunjaish nahin. Kainaat mein mushaahidah kiya jaata hai ke Allah Ta'ala din se raat ko, garma se sarma ko, jawaani se bachpan ko, beemaari se tandrusti ko, bahaar se khizaan ko mansookh farmaata hai. Yeh tamaam naskh o tabdeel us ki qudrat ke dalaail hain to aik aayat aur aik hukm ke mansookh hone mein kya ta'ajjub? Naskh haqeeqat mein saabiqah hukm ki muddat ka bayaan hota hai ke woh hukm is muddat ke liye tha aur ab woh muddat poori ho gayi. Sirf yeh tha ke humein woh muddat maloom na thi aur naasikh ke aane se maloom ho gayi. Kuffaar ka ai'traaz to jahaalat o na-samajhi ki wajah se tha lekin Ahle Kitaab ko to kisi bhi soorat yeh ai'traaz nahin karna chahiye tha kyonke Hazrat Aadam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki sharee'at ke bahut se ahkaam to woh bhi mansookh maante hain jaise behen bhaai ka aapas mein nikaah, yunhi Yahudiyon se pehle Haftah ke din dunyawi kaam haraam na thay, un par haraam hue, neeze Tauraat mein hai ke Hazrat Nooh عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki ummat ke liye tamaam jaanwar halaal thay jabke Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par bahut se haraam kar diye gaye. In tamaam cheezon ke hote hue naskh ka inkaar kis tarah mumkin hai.

Naskh ke chand ahkaam:

(1)…Jis tarah koi aayat doosri aayat se mansookh hoti hai isi tarah hadees-e-mutawaatir se bhi aayat mansookh hoti hai.

(2)…Kabhi sirf tilaawat mansookh hoti hai aur kabhi sirf hukm mansookh hota hai aur kabhi tilaawat o hukm dono mansookh hote hain. Baihaqi Shareef mein hai ke aik Ansaari sahabi raat ko tahajjud ke liye uthe aur Surah-e-Fatihah ke ba'd jo soorat hamesha parha karte thay us ko parhna chaaha lekin woh bilkul yaad na aayi aur siwaaye Bismillah ke kuchh na parh sake. Subah ko doosre as'haab se is ka zikr kiya to un hazraat ne farmaaya: hamaara bhi yehi haal hai, woh soorat humein bhi yaad thi aur ab hamaare haafizah mein bhi na rahi. Sab ne baargaah-e-risaalat mein waqia arz kiya to Huzoor Pur Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: aaj raat woh soorat utha li gayi. Is ke hukm o tilaawat dono mansookh hue jin kaghazon par woh likhi gayi thi un par naqsh tak baaqi na rahe. (دلائل النبوہ للبیہقی، باب ما جاء فی تألیف القرآن۔۔۔ الخ، ۷ / ۱۵۷، ملخصاً)

{نَاْتِ بِخَیْرٍ: Hum behtar le aayen ge.} Farmaaya ke hum kisi aayat ko mansookh farma dein ya bhula dein to us ki jagah ziyada aasaan aur ziyada sawaab waala ya kam az kam pehle waale hukm ke baraabar hukm le aayen ge. Pehle se ziyada sahuulat waale ki misaal hai jaise pehle das guna tak ke lashkar se jihaad ka hukm tha phir sirf do guna tak ke lashkar se jihaad mein date rahne ka hukm naazil hua. Ziyada sawaab ki misaal hai jaise pehle aik qaul ke mutaabiq rozay ki taaqat rakhne waale ko bhi fidyah dene ki ijaazat thi lekin ba'd mein us par rozay ka hukm hi muta'ayyan kar diya jo fidyah se ziyada sawaab waala hukm hai. Sahuulat mein baraabar ke hukm ki misaal hai jaise Bait-ul-Muqaddas se pher kar Khana-e-Ka'bah ko qiblah bana diya gaya halaanke dono ki taraf munh kar ke namaz parhne mein baraabar darje ki sahuulat hai.
