## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:19

`Source: tafseerId=50163; ayatId=28; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** اَوْ كَصَیِّبٍ مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ فِیْهِ ظُلُمٰتٌ وَّ رَعْدٌ وَّ بَرْقٌۚ-یَجْعَلُوْنَ اَصَابِعَهُمْ فِیْۤ اٰذَانِهِمْ مِّنَ الصَّوَاعِقِ حَذَرَ الْمَوْتِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ مُحِیْطٌۢ بِالْكٰفِرِیْنَ(19)

**Roman Urdu:**

{اَوْ كَصَیِّبٍ مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ: ya jaise aasmaan se baarish.} Hidayat ke badle gumraahi khareedne walon ki yeh doosri misaal bayan ki gayi hai aur yeh un munafiqeen ka haal hai jo dil se Islam qubool karne aur na karne mein mutaraddid rehte the un ke baare mein farmaya ke jis tarah andheri raat aur baadal o baarish ki taareekiyon mein musaafir mutahayyir hota hai, jab bijli chamakti hai toh kuchh chal leta hai jab andhera hota hai toh khara reh jata hai isi tarah Islam ke ghalbah aur mojzaat ki roshni aur aaraam ke waqt munafiq Islam ki taraf raaghib hote hain aur jab koi mushiqat pesh aati hai toh kufr ki taareeki mein khare reh jate hain aur Islam se hatne lagte hain aur yehi maqaam apne aur begaane, mukhlis aur munafiq ki pehchaan ka hota hai. Munafiqon ki isi tarah ki haalat Surah Noor aayat number 48 aur 49 mein bhi bayan ki gayi hai.

{عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ: Allah Ta'ala har shay par qaadir hai.} Shay usi ko kehte hain jise Allah Ta'ala chahe aur jo mashiyat yani chaahne ke tehat aa sake. Har mumkin cheez shay mein daakhil hai aur har shay Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat mein hai aur jo mumkin nahin balke waajib ya muhaal hai us se Allah Ta'ala ke iraadah aur qudrat ka talluq hi nahin hota jaise Allah Ta'ala ki zaat o sifaat waajib hain is liye qudrat ke tehat daakhil nahin masalan yeh nahin ho sakta hai ke Allah Ta'ala chahe toh apna ilm khatam kar ke be-ilm ho jaye ya mu'az-zallah jhoot bole. Yaad rahe ke in cheezon ka Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat ke tehat na aana us ki qudrat mein naqs o kami ki wajah se nahin balke yeh in cheezon ka naqs hai ke in mein yeh salahiyat nahin ke Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat se mutalliq ho sakein.

## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:21

`Source: tafseerId=50164; ayatId=30; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ اعْبُدُوْا رَبَّكُمُ الَّذِیْ خَلَقَكُمْ وَ الَّذِیْنَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُوْنَۙ    (21)

**Roman Urdu:**

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ: ae logo!} Surah Baqarah ke shuru mein bataya gaya ke yeh kitaab muttaqeen ki hidayat ke liye nazil hui, phir muttaqeen ke ausaaf zikr farmaye, is ke baad is se munharif hone wale firqon ka aur un ke ahwaal ka zikr farmaya taake sa'adat-mand insaan hidayat o taqwa ki taraf raaghib ho aur nafarmani o baghawat se bache, ab taqwa haasil karne ka tareeqah bataya ja raha hai aur woh tareeqah ibaadat aur ita'at-e-ilaahi hai. ''یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ'' ke zariye tamaam insaanon se khitaab hai aur is baat ka ishaara hai ke insaani sharafat isi mein hai ke aadmi taqwa haasil kare aur Allah Ta'ala ka bandah bane.

Ibaadat ki tareef:

Ibaadat us intehaai ta'zeem ka naam hai jo bandah apni uboodiyat yani bandah hone aur mabood ki uloohiyat yani mabood hone ke itiqaad aur itraaf ke saath baja laye. Yahan ibaadat tauheed aur is ke alawa apni tamaam qismon ko shaamil hai. Kaafiron ko ibaadat ka hukm is maani mein hai ke woh sab se bunyadi ibaadat yani imaan layein aur is ke baad digar aamaal baja layein.

{لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُوْنَ: taake tumhein parhezgaari mile.} Is se maloom hua ke ibaadat ka faidah aabid hi ko milta hai jabke Allah Ta'ala is se paak hai ke us ko ibaadat ya aur kisi cheez se nafa haasil ho.

## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:22

`Source: tafseerId=50165; ayatId=31; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْاَرْضَ فِرَاشًا وَّ السَّمَآءَ بِنَآءً۪-وَّ اَنْزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَآءِ مَآءً فَاَخْرَجَ بِهٖ مِنَ الثَّمَرٰتِ رِزْقًا لَّكُمْۚ-فَلَا تَجْعَلُوْا لِلّٰهِ اَنْدَادًا وَّ اَنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُوْنَ(22)

**Roman Urdu:**

{اَلَّذِیْ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْاَرْضَ فِرَاشًا وَّ السَّمَآءَ بِنَآءً: jis ne tumhare liye zameen ko bichhona aur aasmaan ko chhat banaya.} Is aayat aur is se oopar wali aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne apni in nemton ko bayan farmaya hai:

(1)… Makhlooq ko adam se wujood mein lana.

(2)… Aasmaan o zameen ko paida karna.

(3)… Aasmaan o zameen se makhlooq ke rizq ka muhayya karna.

(4)… Aasmaan se baarish utarna aur zameen se nabaataat ugana.

Jab aadmi ki zindagi ka ek ek lamha aur ek ek pal Allah Ta'ala ki nemton mein dooba hua hai toh us Maalik-e-Haqeeqi ko chhor kar kisi aur ka ibaadat-guzar banna kis qadar nashukri hai? Yunhi aise Kareem Khuda ki yaad se ghaflat bhi kitni badi nashukri hai.
