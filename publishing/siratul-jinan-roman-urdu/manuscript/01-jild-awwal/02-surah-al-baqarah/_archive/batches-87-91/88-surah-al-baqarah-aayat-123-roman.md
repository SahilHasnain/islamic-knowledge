## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:123

`Source: tafseerId=50254; ayatId=132; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اتَّقُوْا یَوْمًا لَّا تَجْزِیْ نَفْسٌ عَنْ نَّفْسٍ شَیْــٴًـا وَّ لَا یُقْبَلُ مِنْهَا عَدْلٌ وَّ لَا تَنْفَعُهَا شَفَاعَةٌ وَّ لَا هُمْ یُنْصَرُوْنَ(123)

**Roman Urdu:**

{لَا تَنْفَعُهَا شَفَاعَةٌ: Na kaafir ko sifaarish nafa degi.} Yahaan bunyaadi taur par un Yahoodiyon ka rad hai jo kehte thay hamare baap dada bare buzurg thay, woh hamaari shafaa'at kar ke humein chhura lenge. Unhein farmaaya ja raha hai ke shafaa'at kaafir ke liye nahin hai. Goya yahaan kaafir ka bayaan hai ke kaafir ki taraf se koi badla na banega aur na is se koi mu'aawazah le kar isay chhora jaye aur na koi is ki shafaa'at karega aur bil-farz agar koi kare to kaafir ke haq mein shafaa'at qubool nahin ki jaayegi aur na kaafiron ki madad ki jaayegi. Kaafiron ke muqaable mein Musalmanon ki shafaa'at bhi hogi jaise Quran mein beesiun jagah hai aur Musalmanon ki madad bhi hogi jaise Bukhari o Muslim ki hadeeson mein hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ qiyaamat mein jagah jagah apne ummatiyon ki madad farmaayenge.
