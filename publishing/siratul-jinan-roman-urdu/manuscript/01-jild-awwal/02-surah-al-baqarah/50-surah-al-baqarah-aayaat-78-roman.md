## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:78

`Source: tafseerId=50211; ayatId=87; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ مِنْهُمْ اُمِّیُّوْنَ لَا یَعْلَمُوْنَ الْكِتٰبَ اِلَّاۤ اَمَانِیَّ وَ اِنْ هُمْ اِلَّا یَظُنُّوْنَٜ      (78)

**Roman Urdu:**

{اُمِّیُّوْنَ: Un parh.} Yahan se Yahoodiyon ke doosre giroh ka tazkirah hai, farmaaya gaya ke ab Yahoodiyon ke doosre giroh ki suniye ke un mein kuchh an parh hain jo kitaab yani Tauraat ko khud to nahin jaante balke apne mufaad parast sardaaron ke bayaanat par hi ultay seedhay khayaal o gumaan mein pare hue hain ke un ki to bakhshish ho hi jaayegi. Yeh unhi man gharat khayaalaat mein khush hain.
