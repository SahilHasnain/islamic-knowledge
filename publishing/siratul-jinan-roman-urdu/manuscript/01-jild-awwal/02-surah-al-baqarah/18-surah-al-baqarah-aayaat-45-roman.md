## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:45

`Source: tafseerId=50186; ayatId=54; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ اسْتَعِیْنُوْا بِالصَّبْرِ وَ الصَّلٰوةِؕ-وَ اِنَّهَا لَكَبِیْرَةٌ اِلَّا عَلَى الْخٰشِعِیْنَۙ    (45)

**Roman Urdu:**

{وَ اسْتَعِیْنُوْا بِالصَّبْرِ وَ الصَّلٰوةِ: aur sabr aur namaaz se madad haasil karo.} Is aayat ki ek tafseer yeh hai ke is se pehli aayaat mein Bani Israeel ko Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan laane, un ki shariat par amal karne, sardaari tark karne aur mansab o maal ki mohabbat dil se nikaal dene ka hukm diya gaya aur is aayat mein un se farmaya ja raha hai ke Ae Bani Israeel! Apne nafs ko lazaton se rokne ke liye sabr se madad chaho aur agar sabr ke saath saath namaaz se bhi madad haasil karo to sardaari aur mansab o maal ki mohabbat dil se nikaalna tumhare liye aasaan ho jaayega, beshak namaaz zaroor bhaari hai albattah un logon par bhaari nahin jo dil se meri taraf jhukte hain. Doosri tafseer yeh hai ke Ae Musalmano! Tum razaa-e-ilaahi ke husool aur apni haajaton ki takmeel mein sabr aur namaaz se madad chaho. (خازن، البقرۃ، تحت الآیۃ: ۴۵، ۱ / ۵۰)

سبحان اللہ! Kya paakeeza taleem hai. Sabr ki wajah se qalbi quwwat mein izaafa hai aur namaaz ki barkat se Allah Ta'ala se ta'alluq mazboot hota hai aur yeh donon cheezein pareshaaniyon ko bardasht karne aur unhein door karne mein sab se badi mu'aavin hain.

{اِنَّهَا لَكَبِیْرَةٌ اِلَّا عَلَى الْخٰشِعِیْنَ: beshak namaaz zaroor bhaari hai magar un par jo dil se meri taraf jhukte hain.} Aayat ke is hisse aur is ke baad wali aayat ka khulaasa yeh hai ke gunaahon aur khwahishaat se bhare hue dilon par namaaz bahut bojhal hoti hai aur ishq o mohabbat-e-ilaahi se labreiz aur khauf-e-Khuda se jhuke hue dilon par namaaz bojh nahin balke namaaz un ke liye lazzat o suroor aur roohaani o qalbi mi'raaj ka sabab banti hai aur yeh woh log hain jo apne Rabb عَزَّوَجَلَّ se mulaaqaat par yaqeen rakhte hain aur us Maula Kareem ke didaar ki tarp rakhte hain.

Is mein bashaarat hai ke aakhirat mein mo'minin ko didaar-e-ilaahi ki ne'mat naseeb hogi, neez Allah Ta'ala se mulaaqaat aur us ki taraf rujoo' mein targheeb o tarheeb donon hain aur in donon ko pesh-e-nazar rakhna namaaz mein khushoo' paida hone ka zariyah hai, goya namaaz mein khushoo' o khuzoo' qaayem rakhne ka hukm bhi diya aur is ke husool ka tareeqa bhi bata diya.
