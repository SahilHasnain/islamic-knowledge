## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:39

`Source: tafseerId=54508; ayatId=48; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** وَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا وَ كَذَّبُوْا بِاٰیٰتِنَاۤ اُولٰٓىٕكَ اَصْحٰبُ النَّارِۚ-هُمْ فِیْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ۠    (39)

**Roman Urdu:**

Is aayat ki tafseer guzishtah aayat ki tafseer ke zimn mein bayaan ho chuki hai.
