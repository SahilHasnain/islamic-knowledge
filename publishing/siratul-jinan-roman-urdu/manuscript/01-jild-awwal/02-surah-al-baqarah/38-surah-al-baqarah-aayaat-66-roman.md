## Al-Baqarah — Ayat 2:66

`Source: tafseerId=50203; ayatId=75; para=1; volume=1`

**Arabic:** فَجَعَلْنٰهَا نَكَالًا لِّمَا بَیْنَ یَدَیْهَا وَ مَا خَلْفَهَا وَ مَوْعِظَةً لِّلْمُتَّقِیْنَ(66)

**Roman Urdu:**

{نَكَالًا: Ibrat.} Is se maloom hua ke Quran-e-Pak mein azaab ke waqea'aat hamaari ibrat o naseehat ke liye bayaan kiye gaye hain lehaza Quran-e-Pak ke huqooq mein se hai ke is tarah ke waqea'aat o aayaat parh kar apni islaah ki taraf bhi tawajjuh ki jaaye.
