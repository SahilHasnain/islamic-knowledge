# Musannif Ke Baare Mein

**Naam:** Iyaaz bin Moosa bin Iyaaz Maliki <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup>

**Kunniyat:** Abul Fazl

**Paidaish:** 476 Hijri, jo 1083 Iswi ke barabar hai

**Paidaish ki jagah:** Sabtah

**Khandani pasmanzar:** Aap <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ke buzurg Andalus mein rehte the. Aapke dada wahan se Faas chale gaye aur phir Faas se Sabta tashreef le gaye.

## Taleem Aur Tarbiyat

Aap <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ka bachpan aur jawani ka ibtidai hissa Sabta mein guzra. Wahan ke bade Ulama aur Mashaikh se aapne ilm hasil kiya. Bees saal ki umr mein Hafiz-ul-Hadees Abu Ali Ghasani Sadafi ne aapko hadees riwayat karne ki ijazat de di thi. Hazrate Abu Ali Ghasani ke wisal ke baad aap Andalus tashreef le gaye.

Risala Nigaar, Lucknow ke Ulama number mein likha hai ke aapki ibtidai taleem watan mein hui. Phir aap Qurtuba tashreef le gaye aur wahan saikdon teachers (ustaadon) se mukhtalif uloom aur funoon hasil kiye.

Abul Qasim bin Bashkawal <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> apni kitab Kitabus Sila mein likhte hain ke Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ilm hasil karne ke liye Andalus gaye aur Qurtuba mein Ulama ki ek badi jamaat se ilm seekha. Aapne hadees ka bohot bada zakheera jama kiya. Hadees Shareef ki taraf aapki khaas tawajjo thi aur aap ise jama karne ka bohot ehtimam karte the.

Aap <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> bohot zahanat, samajh aur gehri soch rakhte the. Fiqh ke masail mein aap Imam Malik <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ke pairo the aur Maliki mazhab ke bade Ulama mein aapka shumar hota hai.

## Qaazi Ka Ohda

Aapne kaafi arsa Sabta mein Qaazi ka kaam kiya. Apni achhi seerat ki wajah se aapne logon ke dil jeet liye. Phir aap Gharnata chale gaye, jahan aapko Qaazi ka ohda diya gaya. Lekin aap wahan zyada der nahin rahe aur wapas Qurtuba aa gaye. 531 Hijri, yani 1132 Iswi mein, aapko Qurtuba mein Qaazi banaya gaya.

Muhammad bin Hammad Sabti <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> kehte hain ke aap 21 saal ki umr mein munazra karne lage the aur 35 saal ki umr mein Qaazi ke ohde par faiz hue.

## Aapke Ustaad Aur Shagird

Aapne bohot bade Ulama se ilm hasil kiya. Aapke ustaadon ke naam darjano tak pahunchte hain. Isi tarah aapse ilm hasil karne wale shagirdon ki bhi badi tadaad thi aur un mein bhi bohot bade Ulama shamil the.

## Khaas Khubiyan

Aap hadees, Uloom-e-Hadees, lughat, nahw, Arabi kalaam aur Arab ke tareekh aur nasab ke ilm mein apne waqt ke Imam the. Aap shayari bhi karte the aur bohot si kitabein likhne wale buzurg the.

Hazrat Muhammad bin Hammad Sabti kehte hain ke Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ke zamane mein Sabta mein unse zyada kitabein likhne wala koi nahin tha. Aapne apne shehar mein aisi bulandi aur fazeelat hasil ki jo kisi aur ko hasil na hui. Lekin ilm aur fazeelat ne aapke andar Allah ka khauf aur aajizi aur badha di.

## Shifa Shareef

Aap <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne taqreeban 22 kitabein likhin. Ye sab uloom ka bohot qeemti khazana hain. Tamam Ulama in kitabon ki qadr karte aaye hain aur ye silsila aaj tak jaari hai.

Imam Allama Muhiyuddin Sharaf Nawawi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne apni Sharah Muslim mein kai jagah aapki kitab ka hawala diya hai. Imam Badruddin ne Umdatul Qaari mein aur Hafiz-ul-Hadees Ibne Hajar Asqalani ne Fathul Baari mein aapki kitabon se hadees ke faide aur nuqte bayan kiye hain. Sharah likhne wale jab kehte hain, "Qaazi ne kaha," to aksar unki murad Qaazi Iyaaz hi hote hain.

Aapki tamam kitabon mein sabse zyada maqbooliyat Shifa Shareef ko mili. Balki aapki doosri kitabon aur aapke naam ke baqi rehne ka ek bada sabab bhi yehi kitab hai.

## Bargah-e-Risalat Mein Ash-Shifa Ki Maqbooliyat

Tazkiratul Huffaz mein Imam Zahbi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> likhte hain ke ek baar Qaazi Iyaaz ke bhatije ne khwab mein dekha ke Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> Rasoolullah ﷺ ke saath sone ke takht par baithe hain. Ye manzar dekh kar un par haibat taari ho gayi.

Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne farmaya: "Aye bhatije! Kitabush Shifa ko mazbooti se pakde raho aur ise apne liye raasta dikhane wali kitab banao." Goya aapne ishara kiya ke jo maqam tum aaj dekh rahe ho, wo Ash-Shifa likhne ki wajah se mila hai.

## Ash-Shifa Ka Maqam

Bargah-e-Risalat mein maqbooliyat milne ke baad har zamane mein Ulama aur Sulaha ke nazdeek is kitab ka khaas maqam ban gaya. Unhone nazm aur nasr mein iski tareef ki.

Is kitab ki taqreeban 26 sharahen aur talkheesen likhi ja chuki hain. In mein Sharhe Mulla Ali Qaari aur Naseemur Riyaaz zyada mashhoor hain. Naseemur Riyaaz Hazrate Allama Ahmad Shahabuddin Khifaji <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ki kitab hai.

## Ash-Shifa Padhne Ki Fazilat

Hazrate Allama Ahmad Shahabuddin Khifaji <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> farmate hain ke Shifa Shareef ka naam uske maqsad ke bilkul mutabiq hai. Salaf-e-Saliheen farmate hain ke is kitab ko padhna bimariyon se shifa aur mushkilaat ke liye behtareen aur mujarrab amal hai. Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki barkat se is kitab ko padhne wale ko doobne, jalne aur taaoon ki bimari se nijat milti hai.

Ghaliban is kitab ke baare mein ye bhi farmaya gaya hai ke jis ghar mein ye kitab ho, wahan jaadu asar nahin karta.

## Wisaal

Aap <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne 9 Jumadal Aakhirah 544 Hijri, jo 1145 Iswi ke barabar hai, Jumuah ki raat wisaal farmaya. Aap Marakash mein madfoon hue. Aapki umr mubarak taqreeban 69 saal thi.

## Sadrul Afazil Ki Aap Par Inayaat

Aapke mohtaram ustaad Huzoor Sadrul Afazil, Maulana Sayyid Nayimuddin Muradabadi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup>, aap par bohot shafqat farmate the aur aapko deeni kaamon mein apne saath rakhte the.

1940 mein jab Sadrul Afazil ne apni tafseer Khazainul Irfan dobara chapwane ka irada kiya to tarjuma aur tafseer ke likhe hue papers (drafts) ko theek karne ke kaam mein aapko apna shareek banaya.

Is baat se bhi andaza lagaiye ke jab Sadrul Afazil ke wisaal ka waqt aaya to unka sar aapki god mein tha. Unhone apne kamre se sab logon ko bahar jaane ko kaha, hatta ke apne beton ko bhi. Kuchh der baad unka wisaal ho gaya.

Aapne Shifa Shareef ke ilawa bhi kai kitabon ke tarjume kiye. Taqseem-e-Mulk ke baad aap 1950 Iswi mein Pakistan tashreef le gaye. 1971 mein aapka wisaal hua.

Allah Ta'ala aapke darajaat buland farmaye.
