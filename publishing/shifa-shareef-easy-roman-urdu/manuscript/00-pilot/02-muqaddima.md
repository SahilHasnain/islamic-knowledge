# Muqaddima

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> hi ke liye sab tareefein hain. Wo apne buland naam mein sab se juda hai aur us naam ke saath khaas hai. Uske siwa koi aakhri maqsad nahin aur uske siwa koi talab ke laaiq nahin. Wo haqeeqat mein zaahir hai, sirf waham aur khayal nahin. Wo apni paak zaat ke aitibar se posheeda hai, na ke isliye ke wo maujood nahin. Usne apni rahmat aur ilm se poori kainaat ko gher rakha hai aur apne mahboob bandon ko apne bohot se karam aur nematon se nawaza hai.

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> ne unhi logon mein se unki taraf ek behtareen Rasool bheja. Aap ﷺ Arab aur Ajam sab ke liye be-misaal hain. Aap asal, nasab aur sharafat mein sab se paakiza hain. Aap aqal, samajh, danai aur burdbaari mein sab se aage hain. Ilm aur baseerat mein sab se mazboot hain. Himmat aur pakke irade mein sab se qawi hain. Rahm aur karam mein sab se zyada shafeeq aur meharban hain.

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> ne Aap ﷺ ke rooh aur jism ko har bashari gandagi se paak rakha aur har kharabi (aib) aur kami se door rakha. Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko aisi hikmat aur samajh di jiski wajah se andhi aankhein, ghafil dil aur behre kaan bhi hidayat pa gaye.

Jiske naseeb mein Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> ne nekbakhti likhi hai, wahi Aap ﷺ par imaan laata hai aur Aap ﷺ ki izzat aur madad karta hai. Aur jis par Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> ne badbakhti likh di hai, wahi Aap ﷺ ko jhutlata hai aur Aap ﷺ ke mojizaat se munh modta hai. Kyunki jo shakhs duniya mein andha raha, wo aakhirat mein bhi andha rahega.

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> Aap ﷺ par hamesha badhti aur phailti hui rahmatein aur salaam bheje. Aap ﷺ ke Aal aur Ashab par bhi poora salaam ho.

Amma baad! Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> mere aur tumhare dilon ko yaqeen ke noor se roshan kare aur hum par aisi meharbani farmaye jaisi apne chuninda mahboob bandon par farmata hai. Allah ne unhein apni paak bargah mein khaas maqam diya aur apni muhabbat mein aisa magan kiya ke wo makhlooq ki taraf se be-niyaz ho gaye.

Allah ne unhein apni saltanat ke ajeeb raaz aur apni qudrat ki nishaniyan dekhne ke liye khaas kiya. Unke saaf dilon ko khushi di aur apni azmat aur shaan se unki aqal ko hairat mein daal diya. Un mahboob bandon ne sirf Allah ki zaat ko apna sab se bada maqsad banaya. Duniya aur deen mein Allah ke jalwe dekhne ke siwa unka kisi aur se koi talluq na raha. Wo Allah ke husn aur jalal ko dekhne mein magan rahe, uski qudrat ki nishaniyon par ghor karte rahe, usi se talluq rakha aur usi par bharosa karna apne liye izzat samjha.

Wo Allah ke is irshad ke deewane the: "Allah kaho, phir unhein chhod do ke wo apni behoodgi mein khelte rahen" (Al-An'aam: 91).

Tumne mujhse baar baar kaha ke main aisi kitab tayyar karun jisme Huzoor ﷺ ke huqooq aur Aap ﷺ ki izzat aur takreem ka wajib hona bayan ho. Is mein un logon ka bhi zikr ho jo is martabe ki ahmiyat se ghaflat ki wajah se ya Aap ﷺ ke buland maqam ke huqooq ada karne mein bohot kamzor rahe. Tumne ye bhi kaha ke main apne buzurgon aur Imamon ke aqwaal, unke waqiat aur unki misaalein bayan karun.

Ye kaam bohot mushkil hai aur ek khatarnak buland pahad chadhne jaisa hai, isliye mera dil isse dara hua hai. Lekin main apne aur tumhare liye Allah se bakhshish aur sawaab ki umeed rakhta hoon. Kyunki ye Huzoor ﷺ ki tareef aur sana ka maqam hai. Is mein Huzoor ﷺ ki wo khaas sifatein bayan hongi jo Aap ﷺ se pehle kisi aur makhlooq mein jama nahin hui thin.

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> humare toote hue dilon ko theek kare, humare bade gunah bakhsh de aur humare kaamon ko humari nijat ka zariya bana de.

## Kitab Ki Taqseem

Is kitab mein chaar qismein (topics) hain.

Pehli qism mein chaar baab (sections) hain. In mein pehle baab mein das faslein (chapters), doosre baab mein chhe faslein, teesre baab mein pandrah faslein aur chauthe baab mein untees faslein hain.

Doosri qism mein chaar baab hain. Pehle baab mein chaar faslein, doosre baab mein chhe faslein, teesre baab mein chhe faslein aur chauthe baab mein nau faslein hain.

Teesri qism mein do baab hain. Pehle baab mein solah faslein aur doosre baab mein aath faslein hain.

Phir chauthi qism hai jisme teen baab hain. Pehle baab mein nau faslein, doosre baab mein nau faslein aur teesre baab mein nau faslein hain. Teesre baab ke saath kitab mukammal hoti hai.
