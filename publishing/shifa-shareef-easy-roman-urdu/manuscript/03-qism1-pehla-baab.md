# Pehli Qism

## Aayaton Mein Huzoor ﷺ Ki Shaan Aur Azmat

Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> us shakhs ko seedhe raaste par chalne ki taufeeq de jise Allah ne thoda bhi ilm aur samajh di hai. Aise shakhs se ye baat chhupi nahin reh sakti ke Allah ne humare Nabi ﷺ ko bohot buland maqam diya hai. Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko aisi fazilaton, khoobiyon aur khaas sifaton se nawaza hai jin ka poora andaaza lagana mumkin nahin. Aap ﷺ ka martaba itna buland hai ke logon ke qalam aur zubaan iski poori tareef bayan karne se thak jaate hain.

In fazilaton mein se kuchh ka zikr Allah ne Quran mein khud farmaya hai. Beshak Allah ne Aap ﷺ par be-had karam farmaya aur Aap ﷺ ko har kharabi se paak rakha.

Hazarat Anas <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> se riwayat hai ke Meraj ki raat Huzoor ﷺ ki khidmat mein Buraaq pesh kiya gaya. Us par zeen bandhi hui thi aur lagaam lagi thi. Buraaq ne Huzoor ﷺ ko sawar karne mein thoda hichkichaya. Hazarat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne farmaya: "Kya tum Huzoor ﷺ ke saamne shokhi karte ho? Khabardar! Tum par Huzoor ﷺ se badh kar koi izzat wala sawar nahin hua."

Riwayat bayan karne wale kehte hain ke ye sun kar Buraaq sharminda ho gaya, us par paseena aa gaya aur usne apni gardan jhuka di (Sunan Tirmizi).

# Pehla Baab: Quran Mein Huzoor ﷺ Ki Sana

Jaan lo! Quran-e-Kareem mein bohot si aayatein hain jo Huzoor ﷺ ki shaan bayan karti hain. Yahan hum un aayaton ka zikr karenge jin ka matlab aasani se samajh mein aata hai. Humne is baab ko das faslon mein bayan kiya hai.

## Pehli Fasl: Huzoor ﷺ Ki Shaan Mein Nazil Hone Wali Aayatein

Is fasl mein un aayaton ka zikr hai jo Huzoor ﷺ ki tareef, sana aur khoobiyon ke baare mein nazil hui hain. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Tumhare paas tum mein se ek Rasool tashreef laaye" (Taubah: 128).

Hazrat Faqeeh Abulais Nasr Samarqandi <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> farmate hain ke is ka ek matlab ye bhi hai: "Tum mein sab se zyada nafees aur behtareen zaat." Allah Ta'ala ne ek aur jagah farmaya:

"Un mein unhi mein se ek Rasool bheja jise wo achhi tarah pehchante hain" (Aal-e-Imran: 164 ki tafseer).

Log Huzoor ﷺ ke martabe, maqam, sachchai aur amanat ko achhi tarah jaante the. Arab ka koi aisa qabila nahin tha jisme Huzoor ﷺ ki koi rishtedari ya qarabat na ho (Durr-e-Mansoor).

Hazrat Ibne Abbas ke nazdeek Allah ke is irshad "magar qarabat ki muhabbat" (Shoora: 23) ka matlab ye hai ke poora Arab Huzoor ﷺ ko achhi tarah jaanta aur pehchanta tha (Bukhari wa Tabrani).

Surah Taubah ki aakhri aayaton mein bataya gaya hai ke jab momino ko duniya ya aakhirat mein koi takleef pahunchti hai to Huzoor ﷺ ko bhi us se ranj hota hai. Allah Ta'ala ne is sifat ki tareef farmayi. Baaz Ulama ne farmaya ke Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko apne khaas naamon mein se do naam, 'Raoof' aur 'Raheem', ata farmaye hain.

Isi tarah doosri aayaton mein bhi Huzoor ﷺ ka zikr hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Beshak Allah ne Musalmanon par bada ehsan kiya ke un mein unhi mein se ek Rasool bheja" (Aal-e-Imran: 164).

Aur farmaya:

"Wahi hai jisne anpad logon mein unhi mein se ek Rasool bheja" (Jumuah: 2).

Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> se riwayat hai ke unhone Huzoor ﷺ se Quran ki is aayat "unhi mein se" ke baare mein poocha. Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: Is se murad tumhare nasab aur sasural mein mujhe Rasool bana kar bhejna hai. Mere baap-dada, Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se lekar aaj tak, sab nikah se paida hue hain; un mein zina nahin hua.

Ibne Kalbi kehte hain ke unhone Huzoor ﷺ ki paanch sau maon (mothers) ke halaat likhe hain, lekin un mein na zina paya aur na zamana-e-jahiliyyat ki buri rasmein.

Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ki ita'at ko apni ita'at kaha hai. Allah farmata hai:

"Jisne Rasool ka hukm maana, usne yaqeenan Allah ka hukm maana" (Nisa: 80).

Aur farmaya:

"Aur humne tumhein na bheja magar saare jahaan ke liye rahmat bana kar" (Ambiya: 107).

Abu Bakr Muhammad bin Tahir kehte hain ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko Rahmat se sajaya hai. Aap ﷺ poore ke poore rahmat hain aur Aap ﷺ ki har sifat aur har aadat makhlooq ke liye rahmat hai. Jisne Aap ﷺ ki rahmat ka hissa paaya, wahi deen aur duniya mein nijat paane wala hai.

Huzoor ﷺ ki zahiri zindagi bhi rahmat hai aur Aap ﷺ ka duniya se tashreef le jaana bhi rahmat hai. Huzoor ﷺ khud farmate hain: "Meri ye zindagi bhi tumhare liye behtar hai aur yahan se tashreef le jaana bhi tumhare liye behtar hai."

Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Jab Allah kisi ummat par rahmat farmane ka irada karta hai to pehle us ummat ke Nabi ko duniya se bula leta hai. Phir Allah us ummat ke haal aur mustaqbil par karam farmata hai" (Sahih Muslim).

Faqeeh Abulais Nasr Samarqandi kehte hain ke yahan Rahmat ka matlab aam jinn aur insan par rahmat karna hai. Munafiqon ke liye is rahmat ka matlab ye hai ke wo qatl se mehfooz rahe, aur kafiron ke liye iska matlab ye hai ke un par azaab der se aaye.

Ek din Huzoor ﷺ ne Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se farmaya: "Kya tumhein bhi meri rahmat ka hissa mila hai?" Unhone arz ki: "Haan, main apne anjaam se darta tha. Phir Allah ne meri tareef mein ye aayat nazil farmayi: Jo quwwat wala hai, Arsh ke Malik ke paas izzat wala hai, wahan uska hukm maana jaata hai aur wo amanatdar hai (Takweer: 20-21). Jab ye aayat Aap par nazil hui to ab main be-khauf hoon."

Hazrat Jafar bin Muhammad Sadiq ne Allah ke is farman "Aye Mahboob! tum par daahini taraf walon ki taraf se salaam hai" (Waqiah: 91) ke baare mein farmaya ke Aap ﷺ ki wajah se Ashab-e-Yameen ko salamati mili.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Allah aasmanon aur zameen ka Noor hai. Uske Noor ki misaal aisi hai jaise ek taaq mein chiragh ho. Wo chiragh ek sheeshe mein ho aur wo sheesha aisa ho jaise chamakta hua sitara. Wo chiragh barkat wale zaitoon ke darakht se roshan hota hai" (Noor: 35).

Kaab Ahbaar aur Ibne Jabeer kehte hain ke is aayat mein Noor se murad Huzoor ﷺ hain. Is aayat mein Noor ki jo misaal di gayi hai, uske baare mein Sehal bin Abdullah kehte hain ke taaq mein Noor se murad ye hai ke Aap ﷺ apne paak baap-dada ki naslon mein the. Misbah yani chiragh se murad Aap ﷺ ka dil-e-mubarak hai aur sheeshe se murad Aap ﷺ ka seena-e-anwar hai. Wo roshan sitare ki tarah hai, kyunki us mein imaan aur hikmat hai. Mubarak darakht se murad Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke Noor ki wajah se roshan hona hai. Iske aur bhi matlab bayan kiye gaye hain.

Allah Ta'ala ne doosri jagahon par bhi Huzoor ﷺ ko Noor kaha hai. Allah farmata hai:

"Beshak Allah ki taraf se tumhare paas ek Noor aaya aur roshan kitab" (Maaidah: 15).

Ek aur jagah farmaya:

"Aye ghaib ki khabrein dene wale Nabi! Beshak humne tumhein bheja haazir-o-naazir, khushkhabri dene wala, dar sunane wala, Allah ke hukm se Allah ki taraf bulane wala aur roshan chiragh bana kar" (Ahzaab: 45-46).

Isi tarah Allah ne farmaya:

"Kya humne tumhara seena khol nahin diya?" (Alam Nashrah: 1).

Allah ne Aap ﷺ ka seena mubarak khol kar wasee kar diya. Yahan seena se murad dil-e-mubarak hai. Hazrat Ibne Abbas farmate hain ke Aap ﷺ ka seena Noor-e-Islam ke liye khol diya gaya (Tafseer Durr-e-Mansoor).

Sehal kehte hain ke Aap ﷺ ka seena Noor-e-Risalat ke liye khola gaya. Hazrat Hasan farmate hain ke Aap ﷺ ka seena ilm aur hikmat se bhar diya gaya. Baaz mufassireen ne iska ye matlab bhi bayan kiya hai: Kya humne Aap ﷺ ke dil ko paak nahin kar diya, yahan tak ke wo ab bure waswason ko qabool nahin karta?

Aur Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur tum par se tumhara wo bojh utaar diya jisne tumhari peeth tod di thi" (Alam Nashrah: 2-3).

Baaz log is bojh se zamana-e-jahiliyyat ka bojh murad lete hain. Baaz kehte hain ke is se wo bojh murad hai jo izhaar-e-risalat ke waqt Aap ﷺ ki kamar par wahi-e-risalat ke bojh ki wajah se aa gaya tha, yahan tak ke Aap ﷺ ne risalat ka paigham logon tak pahuncha diya.

Baaz kehte hain ke is se murad ye hai ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko masoom banaya. Agar Allah Aap ﷺ ko masoom na banata to laghzishon ka bojh kamar ko bhaari kar deta.

Aur Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur humne tumhare liye tumhara zikr buland kar diya" (Alam Nashrah: 4).

Yahya bin Adam kehte hain ke Aap ﷺ ka zikr buland karne se murad nubuwwat ka elan hai. Baaz kehte hain ke Allah farmata hai: Aye Mahboob! Jab mera banda mujhe yaad karega to mere saath tumhein bhi yaad karega, jaise Kalima-e-Tayyiba mein hota hai. Baaz log is se azaan aur iqamat murad lete hain.

Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> farmate hain ke Allah ka ye farman Huzoor ﷺ ke liye Allah ki bargah mein izzat, azmat, sharafat, buland maqam aur Aap ﷺ ki buzurgi par badi daleel hai. Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke dil-e-mubarak ko imaan aur hidayat ke liye khol diya, ilm aur hikmat ko mehfooz rakhne ke liye wasee kar diya aur jahiliyyat ka bojh Aap ﷺ se door kar diya.

Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke zikr ko itna buland kiya ke apne naam ke saath Aap ﷺ ka naam mila diya. Hazrat Qatada farmate hain ke Allah ne duniya aur aakhirat mein Aap ﷺ ka zikr itna buland kiya ke koi khateeb, kalima-e-shahadat padhne wala ya namaz padhne wala aisa nahin jo Allah ke naam ke saath Huzoor ﷺ ka naam na leta ho (Dalail-un-Nubuwwat).

Hazrat Abu Sayeed Khudri se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ farmate hain: Ek din Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aaye aur arz ki: "Kya Aap jaante hain ke Aapka zikr kis tarah buland kiya gaya?" Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Allah hi behtar jaanta hai." Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne arz ki: "Jab Allah ko yaad kiya jaata hai to uske saath Aapko bhi yaad kiya jaata hai" (Ibne Hibban).

Ibne Ata kehte hain ke imaan ki takmeel Huzoor ﷺ ke zikr ke saath hoti hai. Wo kehte hain ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke zikr ko apna zikr qarar diya hai. Isliye jisne Huzoor ﷺ ka zikr kiya, usne Allah ka zikr kiya.

Hazrat Jafar bin Muhammad Sadiq farmate hain: Jo shakhs Aap ﷺ ki risalat ka iqrar karega, usne Allah ki ruboobiyat ka iqrar kiya.

Baaz Ulama ne is se maqam-e-shafaat bhi murad liya hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Allah aur Rasool ke farmanbardar raho" (Aal-e-Imran: 132).

Ek aur jagah farmaya:

"Imaan lao Allah aur Allah ke Rasool par" (Nisa: 136).

Yahan Allah ke naam ke saath Huzoor ﷺ ka naam milaaya gaya hai. Isi tarah kisi aur ka naam Allah ke naam ke saath milaana jaaiz nahin.

Ek aur jagah farmaya:

"Aye Mahboob! tum farma do: Aye logon! Agar tum Allah se muhabbat rakhte ho to mere farmanbardar ho jao, Allah tumhein apna mahboob bana lega" (Aal-e-Imran: 31).

Ek riwayat mein hai ke jab ye aayat nazil hui to kafir kehne lage: معاذ الله, Huzoor ﷺ chahte hain ke hum unhein Khuda bana lein, jaise Nasara ne Hazrat Eisa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko Khuda bana liya. Is par Allah Ta'ala ne unhein ruswa karne ke liye ye aayat nazil farmayi:

"Tum farma do: Allah aur Rasool ka hukm maano" (Aal-e-Imran: 32).

Is aayat mein Allah ne apni farmanbardari ko Huzoor ﷺ ki farmanbardari ke saath mila diya hai. Hazrat Mujahid is aayat "Sun lo! Allah ki yaad hi mein dilon ko chain milta hai" (Raad: 28) ki tafseer mein kehte hain ke yahan Allah ke zikr se murad Huzoor ﷺ aur Sahaba-e-Kiram hain.

## Dusri Fasl: Allah Ta'ala Ne Huzoor ﷺ Ko Shahid Banaya Aur Aap ﷺ Ki Sana Farmayi

Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko khitab karte hue farmaya:

"Aye ghaib ki khabrein dene wale Nabi! Beshak humne tumhein haazir-o-naazir, khushkhabri dene wala, dar sunane wala, Allah ke hukm se Allah ki taraf bulane wala aur roshan chiragh bana kar bheja" (Ahzaab: 45-46).

Allah Ta'ala ne is aayat mein Huzoor ﷺ ki tareef mein kai buland martabe aur achhi sifatein bayan farmayi hain. Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko apni ummat par Shahid yani haazir-o-naazir banaya, kyunki Aap ﷺ ne Allah ki taraf se risalat ka paigham ummat tak pahunchaya. Ye Huzoor ﷺ ki khaas sifat hai.

Aap ﷺ farmanbardaron ko khushkhabri dene wale hain, nafarmanon ko dar sunane wale hain, Tauheed aur ibadat ki taraf bulane wale hain aur haq ke liye roshan chiragh hain.

Huzoor ﷺ ki in sifaton ka zikr Taurait mein bhi milta hai. Baaz riwayaton mein aaya hai ke Huzoor ﷺ bazaaron mein cheekhne-chillane wale nahin honge aur be-hayai ko apna tareeqa nahin banayenge. Allah Aap ﷺ ko har khoobi se sajaega. Sukoon Aap ﷺ ka libaas hoga, neki Aap ﷺ ka tareeqa hoga, Aap ﷺ ke seene mein taqwa hoga, hikmat Aap ﷺ ki aqal hogi, sachchai aur wafadari Aap ﷺ ki aadat hogi, maaf karna aur logon ki bhalai chahna Aap ﷺ ka akhlaq hoga, insaf Aap ﷺ ki seerat hogi, haq Aap ﷺ ki shariat hogi, Islam Aap ﷺ ka deen hoga aur Ahmad Aap ﷺ ka mubarak naam hoga.

Aap ﷺ ke zariye gumrah logon ko hidayat milegi, Aap ﷺ ki wajah se jahalat door hogi aur ilm aam hoga. Ye sifatein Aap ﷺ ki pichhli kitabon mein bhi maujood hain.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Wo log is Rasool ki pairwi karenge jo be-padha Nabi hain aur ghaib ki khabrein dete hain" (Aaraaf: 157).

Aur farmaya:

"Allah ki kitni badi meharbani hai ke Aye Mahboob! tum unke liye narm-dil hue" (Aal-e-Imran: 159).

Hazrat Faqeeh Abulais Nasr Samarqandi kehte hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne logon ko apna ehsan yaad dilaya ke humne Rasool ko mominon ke liye Raheem aur Raoof banaya hai. Agar Allah Aap ﷺ ko sakht bolne wala banata to log Aap ﷺ ke paas se chale jaate. Lekin Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko maaf karne wala, sakhi, narm-dil, khush-mizaj, nek aur bohot meharban banaya.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur isi tarah humne tumhein sab ummaton se afzal banaya taake tum logon par gawah ho aur ye Rasool tumhare nigehban aur gawah hon" (Baqarah: 143).

Abul Hasan Qaalibi kehte hain ke Allah ne is aayat mein humare Nabi ﷺ ki fazilat aur Aap ﷺ ki ummat ki fazilat zahir farmayi hai. Ek aur aayat mein Allah farmata hai:

"Allah ne pichhli kitabon mein tumhara naam Musalman rakha hai" (Hajj: 78).

Is mein ye ishara bhi hai ke Rasool ﷺ tum par gawah hain aur Aye ummat-e-Muhammad! tum doosre logon par gawah ho. Isi tarah Allah farmata hai:

"Us waqt kya haal hoga jab hum har ummat mein se ek gawah laayenge aur Aye Mahboob! tumhein un sab par gawah aur nigehban bana kar laayenge" (Nisa: 41).

Allah Ta'ala ne is ummat ko ye fazilat di hai ke wo pehle Ambiya ki tableegh ke sach hone ki gawahi degi aur humare Nabi ﷺ unki gawahi denge.

## Teesri Fasl: Allah Ka Huzoor ﷺ Ko Bade Ehsan Ke Saath Yaad Karna

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Allah tumhein maaf kare, tumne unhein kyun ijazat de di?" (Taubah: 43).

Abu Muhammad Makki kehte hain ke is aayat ki shuruat "Allah tumhein maaf kare" se hui hai. Is mein Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko samjhate hue bhi izzat di hai.

Aun bin Abdullah kehte hain ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko pehle aapki laghzish ki khabar nahin di, balki pehle ye bataya ke "Allah tumhein maaf kare" aur uske baad laghzish ka zikr kiya. Subhan Allah!

Baaz Ulama kehte hain ke agar aayat ki shuruat is jumle se na hoti to haibate kalaam se Huzoor ﷺ ka qalb-e-mubarak phat jaata. Lekin Allah ne apni rahmat se pehle hi Huzoor ﷺ ko maafi ki khabar de di, isliye Aap ﷺ ko dil ka sukoon hasil ho gaya. Iske baad farmaya ke Aap ﷺ ne unhein peeche rehne ki ijazat kyun di, taake ujr pesh karne mein saaf pata chal jaaye ke kaun sachha hai aur kaun jhoota.

Allah ke is andaaz-e-khitab se ishara milta hai ke Allah ki bargah mein Huzoor ﷺ ka bohot buland maqam hai. Jin logon ko samajh aur baseerat mili hai, unse ye baat chhupi nahin.

Kuchh log samajhte hain ke is aayat mein معاذ الله Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko daanta hai, halanki Huzoor ﷺ is se paak hain. Aap ﷺ ko ijazat dene ka ikhtiyar diya gaya tha. Jab Huzoor ﷺ ne unhein ijazat de di to Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko bataya ke agar Aap ﷺ unhein ijazat na dete to ye log apne nifaq ki wajah se ghar mein hi baithe rehte. Allah ne ye bhi bata diya ke unhein ijazat dene mein koi harj nahin tha.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Agar hum tumhein mazboot na rakhte to qareeb tha ke tum unki taraf thoda sa jhuk jaate" (Isra: 74).

Baaz Ulama kehte hain ke pehle Ambiya <sup>عليهم السلام</sup> se laghzish ke baad Allah ne itaab farmaya, lekin humare Nabi ﷺ ko laghzish hone se pehle hi samjhaya gaya, taake laghzish ke hone ka raasta hi band ho jaaye aur muhabbat ke adab ki hifazat bhi rahe. Huzoor ﷺ par Allah ka ye bohot bada lutf aur karam hai.

Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Humein maloom hai ke jo baat ye log keh rahe hain us se tumhein ranj hota hai. Ye log tumhein nahin, balki us baat ko jhutlate hain jo tum laaye ho" (Anaam: 33).

Iski tafseer mein Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> farmate hain ke Abu Jahl ne kaha tha: Hum tumhein nahin jhutlate, balki jo tum laaye ho use jhutlate hain. Is par Allah Ta'ala ne ye aayat nazil farmayi.

Ek riwayat mein hai ke jab Aap ﷺ ki qaum ne Aap ﷺ ko jhutlaya to Aap ﷺ ko ranj hua. Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aaye aur arz ki: "Aapko kis baat ka gham hai?" Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Meri qaum ne mujhe jhutla diya." Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne arz ki: "Aapki qaum ne Aap ﷺ ko nahin jhutlaya. Wo jaante hain ke Aap ﷺ sachhe hain."

Allah Ta'ala ne is aayat mein Huzoor ﷺ ko tasalli di ke wo Aap ﷺ ko nahin, balki Aap ﷺ ko jhutlane wale Allah ki aayaton ka inkar karte hain. Allah farmata hai:

"Balki zalim Allah ki aayaton ka inkar karte hain" (Naml: 14).

Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko is ilzam se paak qarar diya aur aayaton ko jhutlane aur Huzoor ﷺ se dushmani ka bojh kafiron par daal diya. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur unhone inkar kiya, halanki unke dil un par yaqeen rakhte the, zulm aur takabbur ki wajah se" (Naml: 14).

Phir Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko izzat di aur Aap ﷺ ka dil is tarah mazboot kiya ke pehle Rasoolon ka haal bayan farmaya aur un par ghalaba aur madad ka wada kiya. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur tumse pehle bhi Rasool jhutlaye gaye" (Anaam: 34).

Huzoor ﷺ ki khaas sifaton mein se ye bhi hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne doosre tamam Ambiya ko unke naam se pukara, lekin Huzoor ﷺ ko naam lekar mukhatib nahin farmaya.

## Chauthi Fasl: Allah Ka Huzoor ﷺ Ke Martabe Ki Qasam Khana

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aye Mahboob! Tumhari jaan ki qasam, beshak wo apni mastiyon mein bhatak rahe hain" (Hijr: 72).

Tafseer karne wale sab is baat par muttahid hain ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ki mubarak umr ki qasam khayi hai. Hazrat Ibne Abbas farmate hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ se zyada kisi ko apni bargah mein izzat wala paida nahin kiya. Aur maine nahin suna ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke ilawa kisi aur ki zindagi ki qasam khayi ho.

Abul Jauza kehte hain ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ke ilawa kisi ki zindagi ki qasam nahin khayi, kyunki Allah ki bargah mein Huzoor ﷺ se zyada izzat wala koi nahin.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Hikmat wale Quran ki qasam" (Yaseen: 1-2).

'Yaseen' ke matlab ke baare mein mufassireen ke kai aqwaal hain. Abu Muhammad Makki riwayat karte hain ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Allah ki bargah mein mere das naam hain. Un mein Taaha aur Yaseen bhi hain."

Abu Abdurrahman Salami ne Hazrat Jafar Sadiq se riwayat kiya ke Yaseen se murad Huzoor ﷺ hain. Hazrat Ibne Abbas is se "Aye Insan" murad lete hain. Ye bhi kaha gaya hai ke Yaseen Allah ke naamon mein se ek naam hai.

Phir Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Hikmat wale Quran ki qasam! Beshak tum Rasoolon mein se ho" (Yaseen: 2-3).

Agar Yaseen ko Huzoor ﷺ ke naamon mein se ek naam maana jaaye aur ye Aap ﷺ ki qasam ho, to is mein Aap ﷺ ke guzre hue zamane mein bhi izzat ka bayan hai. Aur agar Yaseen ko pukarne ke maani mein liya jaaye, to is surat mein doosri qasam Aap ﷺ ki risalat ko sabit karne ke liye hogi.

Khulasa ye hai ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ke naam aur Quran ki qasam kha kar farmaya: "Beshak Aap Rasoolon mein se hain" aur bandon tak Allah ka paigham pahunchate hain. Naqqash <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> kehte hain ke Quran mein Allah ne kisi Nabi ki risalat ki qasam nahin khayi, siwaye Huzoor ﷺ ke. Is mein Huzoor ﷺ ki badi izzat aur bulandi hai.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Mujhe is shehar ki qasam, Aye Mahboob! tum is shehar mein tashreef farma ho" (Balad: 1-2).

Baaz ne kaha hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ke Makkah se bahar tashreef le jaane ke baad Allah ne is shehar ki qasam nahin khayi. Yahan shehar se sab ke nazdeek Makkah Mukarramah murad hai.

Aur Allah farmata hai:

"Aur tumhare baap Ibrahim ki qasam aur uski aulaad ki ke tum ho" (Balad: 3).

Jo shakhs baap se Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> murad leta hai, uske liye ye ek aam baat hai. Baaz log Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aur unki aulaad murad lete hain. Lekin ye aayat Huzoor ﷺ ki taraf ishara kar rahi hai, kyunki is Surah mein do jagah Huzoor ﷺ ki qasam ka zikr hai.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Alif Laam Meem. Ye buland martabe wali kitab hai, is mein koi shak nahin" (Baqarah: 1-2).

Hazrat Ibne Abbas farmate hain ke Alif Laam Meem qasam ke huroof hain. Yani Allah Ta'ala ne in huroof ki qasam khayi hai. Iske aur bhi aqwaal hain.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Qaaf, izzat wale Quran ki qasam" (Qaaf: 1).

Ibne Ata <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> iski tafseer mein kehte hain ke Allah ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ke dil ki quwwat ki qasam khayi, kyunki Huzoor ﷺ Allah ke khitab aur Allah ki nishaniyon ko dekhne ki taqat rakhte hain.

Phir Allah farmata hai:

"Us pyare chamakte taare Muhammad ﷺ ki qasam" (Najm: 1).

Is aayat ki tafseer mein Hazrat Jafar bin Muhammad kehte hain ke 'Wan-Najm' se murad Huzoor ﷺ hain aur sitare se Huzoor ﷺ ka qalb-e-mubarak murad hai. 'Chamakne' ka matlab ye hai ke Aap ﷺ ka dil Allah ke noor se roshan ho gaya aur ghairullah se alag ho gaya.

Ibne Ata ne aayat "Subah ki qasam aur das raaton ki qasam" (Fajr: 1-2) ki tafseer mein kaha ke yahan subah se murad Huzoor ﷺ hain, kyunki Aap ﷺ hi ki wajah se imaan ka ujala phaila.

## Paanchwi Fasl: Allah Ka Us Maqam Aur Martabe Ki Qasam Khana Jo Bargah-e-Ilahi Mein Huzoor ﷺ Ko Mila Hai

Allah Ta'ala ne Surah Duha mein Huzoor ﷺ ki shaan bayan farmayi. Is Surah ke nazil hone ke waqt ke baare mein Ulama ke alag-alag aqwaal hain. Baaz ne kaha ke Huzoor ﷺ ne kisi wajah se raat ko ibadat karna kuchh der ke liye chhod diya tha. Is par ek aurat ne buri baatein kehni shuru kar di thin. Baaz kehte hain ke wahi der se aane par mushrikeen ne tarah-tarah ki baatein shuru kar di thin. Is par ye Surah nazil hui.

Faqeeh Abulais Nasr Samarqandi kehte hain ke is Surah mein Huzoor ﷺ ke khaas maqam aur Aap ﷺ ki azmat bayan hui hai.

Pehli baat ye hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne qasam ke saath Aap ﷺ ka haal bayan farmaya:

"Qasam hai din ke roshan chehre aur raat ke baalon ki jab wo dhalak kar aa jaaye" (Duha: 1-2).

Ye Huzoor ﷺ ki bohot badi buzurgi hai.

Doosri baat ye hai ke Allah ki bargah mein Aap ﷺ ka bohot buland maqam hai. Allah farmata hai:

"Tumhare Rab ne na tumhein chhoda aur na tumse naraz hua" (Duha: 3).

Yani Allah ne na Aap ﷺ ko chhoda aur na Aap ﷺ ko na-pasand kiya. Baaz ne kaha ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko pasand karne ke baad kabhi nahin chhoda.

Teesri baat ye hai ke Allah farmata hai:

"Aur beshak tumhare liye baad ka waqt pehle se behtar hai" (Duha: 4).

Ibne Ishaq ne kaha ke Aap ﷺ ka aakhirat ka maqam Allah ke nazdeek us maqam se bohot bada hai jo Aap ﷺ ko duniya mein izzat aur karam ke saath diya gaya.

Sehal <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> kehte hain ke aakhirat mein Aap ﷺ ke liye jo shafaat aur Maqam-e-Mahmood rakha gaya hai, wo duniya mein di hui nematon se behtar hai.

Chauthi baat ye hai ke Allah farmata hai:

"Aur qareeb hai ke tumhara Rab tumhein itna dega ke tum raazi ho jaoge" (Duha: 5).

Ye aayatein duniya aur aakhirat ki bohot si buzurgiyon, nekbakhtiyon aur inaamon ko apne andar samete hue hain. Ibne Ishaq ne kaha ke Allah Aap ﷺ ko duniya mein aasani aur khushhaali aur aakhirat mein sawaab de kar raazi karega.

Baaz ne kaha ke Allah Aap ﷺ ko Hauz-e-Kausar aur shafaat ata farmayega. Ye bhi bayan kiya gaya hai ke Huzoor ﷺ us waqt tak raazi nahin honge jab tak Aap ﷺ ka ek bhi ummati Dozakh mein rahega.

Paanchwi baat ye hai ke is Surah ke aakhir tak Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ par ki hui nematon aur inaamon ka zikr farmaya hai.

Chhatti baat ye hai ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko apni nematon ka zikr karne aur un par shukr ada karne ka hukm diya. Allah ne farmaya:

"Aur apne Rab ki nemat ka khoob charcha karo" (Duha: 11).

Nemat ka shukr ye hai ke uska zikr kiya jaaye. Ye hukm Huzoor ﷺ ke liye khaas hai, lekin Aap ﷺ ki ummat ke liye bhi aam hai.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Us pyare chamakte taare Muhammad ﷺ ki qasam, jab wo Meraj se utare, yahan tak ke unhone apne Rab ki badi-badi nishaniyan dekhin" (Najm: 1-18).

Mufassireen ne iske kai matlab bayan kiye hain. Hazrat Jafar bin Muhammad se riwayat hai ke is se Huzoor ﷺ murad hain.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Un cheezon ki qasam jo pehle peeche hat jaati hain, phir seedhi chalti hain aur phir ruk jaati hain, yahan tak ke Quran shaitan ka kaha hua nahin hai" (Takweer: 15-25).

Is mein qasam hai ke ye Quran izzat wale Rasool ka padh kar sunaya hua hai, jo Allah ke nazdeek bohot izzat wale hain.

Ali bin Eisa <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne kaha ke yahan Huzoor ﷺ murad hain aur ye tamam sifatein Aap ﷺ ki hain. Doosre Ulama ne kaha ke yahan Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> murad hain, is surat mein ye sifatein unki hongi.

Ek riwayat mein hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne apne Rab ko dekha ya Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko unki asli surat mein dekha.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur ye Nabi ghaib ki baat batane mein kanjoos nahin" (Takweer: 24).

Iska ek matlab ye bhi hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ilm aur hikmat ki baatein batane mein kanjoosi nahin karte. Is sifat par sab ka ittifaq hai ke ye Huzoor ﷺ ki sifat hai.

## Chhati Fasl: Allah Ka Huzoor ﷺ Par Shafqat Aur Karam Farmana

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aye Mahboob! Humne tum par Quran isliye nahin utara ke tum mushkil mein pad jao" (Taaha: 1-2).

'Taaha' ke baare mein baaz ne kaha ke ye Huzoor ﷺ ke naamon mein se ek naam hai. Baaz ne kaha ke ye Allah ka naam hai aur baaz ne kaha ke is se murad aadmi ya insan hai.

Ye aayatein us waqt nazil hui jab Huzoor ﷺ raat ko ibadat aur jaagne mein bohot mehnat karte the. Hazrat Rabee bin Anas se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ namaz padhte waqt ek paon par khade hote aur doosra paon utha lete. Is par Allah Ta'ala ne Taaha nazil farmaya:

"Aye Mahboob! Apne paon zameen par rakhiye. Humne ye Quran isliye nahin utara ke Aap mushkil mein pad jaayen."

Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ par shafqat aur karam farmate hue kaha:

"To kahin tum unke peeche apni jaan par khel jaoge agar wo is baat par imaan na laayein, is gham mein?" (Kahaf: 6).

Yani Aye Mahboob! Kya Aap unke imaan na laane ke gham mein apni jaan ko halakat mein daal denge?

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Kahin tum apni jaan par khel jaoge unke gham mein ke wo imaan nahin laaye" (Shu'ara: 3).

Phir farmaya:

"Agar hum chahein to aasman se un par aisi nishani utaar dein ke unki gardanen uske saamne jhuk jaayen" (Shuara: 4).

Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya:

"Jo hukm tumhein diya gaya hai use khul kar bata do aur mushrikon se munh pher lo. Hum un mazaaq udaane walon ke muqable mein tumhare liye kaafi hain jo Allah ke saath kisi aur ko mabood banate hain. Ab wo jald jaan lenge. Humein maloom hai ke unki baaton se tumhara dil tang hota hai. Isliye apne Rab ki tareef ke saath uski paaki bayan karo, sajda karne walon mein shamil raho aur marte dam tak apne Rab ki ibadat karte raho" (Hijr: 94-99).

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Aye Mahboob! Tumse pehle Rasoolon ka bhi mazaaq udaaya gaya tha" (Anaam: 10).

Makki <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> kehte hain ke Allah ne is zikr ke zariye Huzoor ﷺ ko tasalli di aur mushrikon ki sakhtiyon par Aap ﷺ ko sabr ki taqat di. Allah ne bataya ke jo badnaseeb shakhs Aap ﷺ par zyadati karega, us par bhi wahi azaab aayega jo pehle Rasoolon ko jhutlane walon par aaya.

Isi tasalli mein Allah ne farmaya:

"Agar ye log tumhein jhutlayen to tumse pehle bhi kitne hi Rasool jhutlaye ja chuke hain."

Aur farmaya:

"Isi tarah jab in logon ke paas unse pehle Rasool aaye to unhone bhi kaha: Ye jadugar hai ya deewana" (Zaariyaat: 52).

Allah Ta'ala ne guzri hui ummaton ka zikr kar ke Huzoor ﷺ ko izzat di aur bataya ke Aap ﷺ se pehle bhi Ambiya ke saath aisa hi hua. Phir Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko tasalli di aur farmaya: "Aap inse munh pher lijiye."

## Saatwi Fasl: Quran Mein Ambiya Par Huzoor ﷺ Ke Fazail

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur yaad karo jab humne Paighambaron se unka ahad liya ke jo kitab aur hikmat hum tumhein dein, phir tumhare paas wo Rasool aaye jo tumhari kitabon ki tasdeeq kare to tum zaroor us par imaan lana aur zaroor uski madad karna. Allah ne farmaya: Kya tumne iqraar kiya aur is par mera zimma liya? Sabne arz ki: Humne iqraar kiya. Allah ne farmaya: To ek doosre par gawah ho jao aur main tumhare saath gawahon mein hoon" (Aal-e-Imran: 81).

Abul Hasan Qaalibi kehte hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko aisi fazilat di jo kisi aur ko nahin mili aur is aayat mein us fazilat ko zahir farmaya.

Mufassireen farmate hain ke Allah ne wahi ke zariye Ambiya se ahad liya. Koi Nabi aisa nahin aaya jisne Huzoor ﷺ ki tareef aur khoobiyan bayan na ki hon. Unse ahad liya gaya ke agar tum Huzoor ﷺ ka zamana pao to zaroor Aap ﷺ par imaan lana.

Baaz Ulama kehte hain ke har Nabi ko hukm diya gaya tha ke wo ye ahad apni qaum ko bataye aur unse bhi ahad le ke wo apne baad aane walon ko ye baat batate rahen. Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> se bhi yahi riwayat hai ke Hazrat Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se lekar har Nabi ko bhejne se pehle unse ye ahad liya gaya aur unki qaum se bhi ahad lene ka hukm diya gaya. Phir aakhir mein Huzoor ﷺ ko mab'oos farmaya gaya.

Hazrat Qatada kehte hain ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Paidaish ke lihaz se main sab Ambiya se pehle hoon aur mab'oos kiye jaane ke lihaz se sab se aakhir mein hoon."

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Ye Rasool hain jin mein humne ek ko doosre par fazilat di" (Baqarah: 253).

Tafseer mein hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko Arab aur Ajam sab ki taraf bheja. Aap ﷺ ke liye jang mein haasil hone wala maal (maal-e-ghaneemat) halal kiya gaya, jo pehle kisi Nabi ke liye halal nahin tha, aur Aap ﷺ ke haathon mojizaat zahir hue.

Ambiya mein kisi ko jo fazilat aur buzurgi di gayi, Huzoor ﷺ ko bhi wo sab aur is se zyada ata hui.

Is fazilat mein ye bhi shamil hai ke doosre Ambiya ko unke naam se pukara gaya, lekin Huzoor ﷺ ko Quran mein nubuwwat aur risalat ke martabe se pukara gaya.

Faqeeh Abulais Nasr Samarqandi is aayat "Aur beshak Ibrahim usi ke giroh mein se hain" (Saffaat: 83) ki tafseer mein riwayat karte hain ke Hazrat Ibrahim <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Huzoor ﷺ ke giroh aur Aap ﷺ ke deen par the.

## Aathwi Fasl: Allah Ka Huzoor ﷺ Par Durood Bhejna Aur Aap ﷺ Ki Wajah Se Azaab Door Karna

Allah Ta'ala batata hai ke hum Huzoor ﷺ par durood bhejte hain, Aap ﷺ ki madad karte hain aur Aap ﷺ ki wajah se azaab door karte hain. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Allah aisa nahin ke unhein azaab de jab tak Aye Mahboob! tum un mein maujood ho" (Anfaal: 33).

Yani jab tak Huzoor ﷺ Makkah mein maujood the, Allah ne ahle Makkah ko azaab nahin diya. Jab Huzoor ﷺ Makkah se hijrat kar ke tashreef le gaye aur Makkah mein Musalman kam reh gaye, to ye aayat nazil hui:

"Aur Allah unhein azaab dene wala nahin jab tak wo bakhshish maang rahe hain" (Anfaal: 33).

Ye Allah ke is farman ki tarah hai:

"Agar wo alag ho jaate to hum unhein zaroor azaab dete" (Fateh: 25).

Allah farmata hai: "Aur agar kuchh Musalman mard na hote" (Fateh: 25). Jab Musalman bhi hijrat kar ke nikal gaye to ye aayat nazil hui:

"Unhein kya hai ke Allah unhein azaab na de?" (Anfaal: 34).

Is se Huzoor ﷺ ka bohot buland maqam maloom hota hai. Jab Aap ﷺ Makkah mein maujood the aur jab Aap ﷺ Makkah se tashreef le gaye, dono surat mein Musalmanon ki wajah se ahle Makkah par azaab nahin aaya. Jab sab Musalman Makkah se hijrat kar gaye to Allah ne un par Musalmanon ko ghalib kar diya. Musalmanon ne unhein shikast di aur unke shehron, zameenon aur maal ke waris bane. Is aayat ki aur bhi kai tafseerein hain.

Abu Burda <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ke walid se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: "Allah ne meri ummat ke liye meri wajah se do aman rakhe hain. Ek ye ke jab tak main un mein hoon, Allah unhein azaab nahin dega. Doosra ye ke jab tak bakhshish maangne wale Musalman maujood hain, Allah unhein azaab nahin dega. Jab main wisaal farmaunga to tum logon ke liye istighfar baqi rahega" (Tirmizi).

Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur humne tumhein nahin bheja magar saare jahaan ke liye rahmat bana kar" (Ambiya: 107).

Huzoor ﷺ farmate hain: "Main Sahaba ke liye aman hoon." Ek riwayat mein hai ke Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: "Main bid'at se aman hoon." Baaz Ulama ne is ka matlab ikhtilaf aur fitnon se aman liya hai.

Baaz kehte hain ke jab tak Huzoor ﷺ hum mein zahiri zindagi ke saath maujood rahe, Aap ﷺ ka hona bada aman tha. Ab jab tak Aap ﷺ ki sunnat zinda rahegi, aman bhi baqi rahega. Jab sunnat mar jaayegi to bala aur fitne ka intezar karna (Sahih Muslim).

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Beshak Allah aur uske farishte us Nabi par durood bhejte hain jo ghaib ki khabrein dete hain. Aye imaan walo! Tum bhi un par durood aur khoob salaam bhejo" (Ahzaab: 56).

Allah Ta'ala ne khud apne Nabi ﷺ par durood bheja, phir farishton ke zariye durood bheja aur Musalmanon ko bhi Huzoor ﷺ par durood-o-salaam bhejne ka hukm diya. Is se Huzoor ﷺ ki badi fazilat zahir hoti hai.

Abu Bakr bin Khorak <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne riwayat kiya ke baaz Ulama ne Huzoor ﷺ ke is irshad "Namaz mein meri aankhon ki thandak rakhi gayi hai" ka ye matlab bataya hai ke Allah aur uske farishte Huzoor ﷺ par durood bhejte hain aur Musalmanon ko bhi Qayamat tak durood bhejne ka hukm diya gaya hai.

Jab farishte ya Musalman Huzoor ﷺ par salaat bhejte hain to us ka matlab durood aur dua hota hai. Jab Allah Ta'ala Huzoor ﷺ par salaat bhejta hai to us ka matlab rahmat hota hai. Ek riwayat mein salaat ka matlab barkat bhi bataya gaya hai.

Huzoor ﷺ ne khud durood bhejne ki taleem di aur salaat aur barkat ka farq bhi samjhaya. Anqareeb hum Huzoor ﷺ par durood bhejne ke ahkaam bayan karenge.

Baaz ilm wale 'Kaaf Haa Yaa Ain Saad' ki tafseer mein kehte hain ke 'Kaaf' se murad Huzoor ﷺ ke liye Allah ki taraf se kifayat hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: "Kya Allah apne bande ke liye kaafi nahin?" (Zumar: 36).

'Haa' se murad Huzoor ﷺ ke liye Allah ki hidayat hai. Allah farmata hai: "Aur tumhein seedhi raah dikha de" (Fateh: 2).

'Yaa' se murad Huzoor ﷺ ki madad aur taqat hai. Allah farmata hai: "Aur apni khaas madad se Aap ﷺ ko mazboot kiya" (Anfaal: 62).

'Ain' se murad Huzoor ﷺ ki hifazat hai. Allah farmata hai: "Aur Allah tumhari logon se hifazat karega" (Maaidah: 67).

'Saad' se murad Huzoor ﷺ par durood bhejna hai. Allah farmata hai: "Beshak Allah aur uske farishte us Nabi par durood bhejte hain jo ghaib ki khabrein dete hain" (Ahzaab: 56).

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Agar tum unke khilaf madad karo to Allah unka madadgar hai, aur Jibreel aur nek imaan wale bhi" (Tahreem: 4).

'Nek imaan wale' ke baare mein baaz ne kaha ke is se Ambiya ya farishte murad hain. Baaz ne kaha ke Hazrat Abu Bakr aur Hazrat Umar ya Hazrat Ali <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهم</sup> murad hain. Ye bhi kaha gaya hai ke is se aam momin murad hain.

## Nawi'n Fasl: Surah Fateh Mein Huzoor ﷺ Ki Buzurgi

Surah Fateh mein Huzoor ﷺ ki bohot si karamatein aur buzurgiyan bayan ki gayi hain. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Beshak humne tumhare liye roshan fatah farma di" se lekar "unke haathon par Allah ka haath hai" tak (Fateh: 1-10).

Ye aayatein Huzoor ﷺ ki tareef, Aap ﷺ ke martabe aur Allah ki bargah mein Aap ﷺ ke buland maqam ko zahir karti hain. Aap ﷺ ka maqam itna buland hai ke uski poori tareef qalam aur zubaan se mumkin nahin.

Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko khabar di ke wo Aap ﷺ ko dushmanon par ghalib karega, Aap ﷺ ke deen ko buland karega aur Aap ﷺ ke agle-pichhle ummatiyon ko Aap ﷺ ki wajah se bakhsh dega. Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke dushmanon ko zaleel kiya, Aap ﷺ ke liye bade-bade shehron ko fatah kiya, Aap ﷺ ka zikr buland kiya, Aap ﷺ ko seedhi raah dikhayi aur Aap ﷺ ki ummat ki maghfirat farmayi.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Beshak humne tumhein haazir-o-naazir, khushkhabri dene wala aur dar sunane wala bana kar bheja" (Fateh: 8).

Is aayat mein Huzoor ﷺ ki khoobiyan bayan ki gayi hain. Aap ﷺ apni ummat ke liye risalat ka paigham pahunchane wale gawah hain. Aap ﷺ farmanbardaron ko jannat ki khushkhabri dete hain, nafarmanon ko azaab se darte hain aur logon ko Tauheed aur ibadat ki taraf bulate hain.

Ibne Ata kehte hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne is Surah mein Huzoor ﷺ ke liye kai nematein jama kar di hain. Fatah-e-Mubeen, maghfirat, muhabbat ka izhar, nemat ko poora karna, khaas fazilat aur hidayat, ye sab Huzoor ﷺ ki buzurgi ki nishaniyan hain.

Maghfirat ka matlab ye hai ke Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ko har kharabi aur kami se paak rakha. Nemat poori karne ka matlab ye hai ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko kamil martabe diye. Hidayat ka matlab ye hai ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko apni nishaniyan dekhne ka mauqa diya.

Hazrat Jafar bin Muhammad farmate hain ke Huzoor ﷺ par nemat poori karne ka matlab ye hai ke Allah ne Aap ﷺ ko apna Habeeb banaya, Aap ﷺ ki zindagi ki qasam khayi, Aap ﷺ ki wajah se pehli shariaton ki jagah Aap ﷺ ki shariat jaari ki (mansookh kiya), Aap ﷺ ko buland maqam tak pahunchaya aur Arab-o-Ajam ki taraf bheja.

Allah ne Aap ﷺ ke liye jang mein haasil hone wala maal (maal-e-ghaneemat) halal kiya, Aap ﷺ ko Shafee yani sifarish karne wala aur Mushaffe yani jiski sifarish qubool ki jaaye banaya, Aap ﷺ ko Adam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki aulaad ka sardaar banaya aur Aap ﷺ ke zikr ko apne zikr ke saath mila diya.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Jo tumse bai'at karte hain, wo Allah hi se bai'at karte hain. Unke haathon par Allah ka haath hai" (Fateh: 10).

Yani Bai'at-e-Rizwan ke waqt Sahaba asal mein Allah Ta'ala se ahad kar rahe the. 'Allah ka haath' se murad Allah ki qudrat, sawaab, ehsan ya ahad bhi bataya gaya hai. Ye sab milte-julte maani hain. Is aayat mein Bai'at lene wale, yani humare Nabi ﷺ ki bhi izzat bayan hui hai. Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Tumne unhein qatl nahin kiya, balki Allah ne unhein qatl kiya. Aur Aye Mahboob! Wo mitti jo tumne phenki, tumne nahin phenki, balki Allah ne phenki" (Anfaal: 17).

## Duswi Fasl: Kitab-e-Majeed Mein Huzoor ﷺ Ka Zikr-e-Mubarak

Quran-e-Kareem mein Huzoor ﷺ ke Meraj ke waqie ka bhi zikr hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Allah tumhari logon se hifazat karega" (Maaidah: 67).

Aur farmata hai:

"Aye Mahboob! Yaad karo jab kafir tumhare khilaf chaal chal rahe the" (Anfaal: 30).

Aur farmata hai:

"Agar tum Mahboob ki madad na karo to Allah ne unki madad farmayi" (Taubah: 40).

Kafiron ne Huzoor ﷺ ko takleef dene aur Aap ﷺ ko qatl karne ka irada kiya tha. Wo chupke-chupke mashware karte the. Allah ne Huzoor ﷺ ki madad farmayi aur unki saari chaalein nakaam kar dein.

Hijrat ke waqt jab Huzoor ﷺ apne paak ghar se bahar tashreef laane lage to Allah ne kafiron ki aankhon par parda daal diya, isliye wo Aap ﷺ ko dekh na sake. Ghaare Saur mein bhi kafiron ki talash nakaam hui. Is waqie mein Huzoor ﷺ par Allah ki taraf se tasalli nazil hui aur Suraqah bin Malik ka waqia bhi pesh aaya.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aye Mahboob! Beshak humne tumhein bohot si khubiyan ata farmayi hain. To tum apne Rab ke liye namaz padho aur qurbani karo. Beshak tumhara dushman hi har bhalai se mahroom hai" (Kausar: 1-3).

'Kausar' se murad ek Hauz ya Jannat ki nehar hai. Baaz ne is se bohot zyada bhalai, shafaat, bohot se mojizaat, nubuwwat ki nemat ya Allah ki pehchan murad li hai.

Phir Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ke dushmanon ko jawab diya aur farmaya: "Tumhara dushman aur tumse dushmani rakhne wala hi be-nasl aur be-khair hai."

Aur farmata hai:

"Aur beshak humne tumhein saat aayatein di hain jo baar-baar padhi jaati hain aur azmat wala Quran diya hai" (Hijr: 87).

Iski tafseer mein hai ke 'saat aayaton' se murad Quran ki pehli saat suratein hain jo Quran-e-Azeem mein Umm-ul-Quran hain. Baaz ne is se Surah Fatiha murad li hai, baaz ne Quran ke saat ahkaam aur baaz ne Huzoor ﷺ ki saat sifatein murad li hain.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Aye Mahboob! Humne tumhari taraf ye yaadgaar kitab utari" (Nahl: 44).

Aur farmata hai:

"Aur Aye Mahboob! Humne tumhein aisi risalat ke saath bheja jo tamam logon ko gherne wali hai, khushkhabri dene wali aur dar sunane wali hai" (Saba: 28).

Aur farmata hai:

"Tum farma do: Aye logon! Main tum sab ki taraf Allah ka Rasool hoon" (Aaraaf: 158).

Qaazi Iyaaz <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> farmate hain ke ye Huzoor ﷺ ki khaas sifatein hain. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Humne har Rasool ko uski qaum ki zubaan mein bheja taake wo unhein saaf-saaf samjha de" (Ibrahim: 4).

Pehle Ambiya ko unki apni qaum ke liye bheja gaya, lekin Huzoor ﷺ ko poori makhlooq ki taraf bheja gaya. Huzoor ﷺ khud farmate hain: "Mujhe gore aur kale, yani Arab aur Ajam, sab ki taraf bheja gaya hai."

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Ye Nabi Musalmanon ke liye unki jaan se bhi zyada malik hain" (Ahzaab: 6).

Aur farmata hai:

"Aur unki biwiyan Musalmanon ki maaein hain" (Ahzaab: 6).

Iska matlab ye hai ke Huzoor ﷺ jo hukm dein, Musalmanon ko us par amal karna hai aur apni raaye chhod deni hai. Huzoor ﷺ humare Aaqa hain aur hum Aap ﷺ ke ghulam hain. Huzoor ﷺ ki tamam azwaj ki izzat hum par apni maon jaisi wajib hai.

Huzoor ﷺ ke baad unki azwaj se nikah haraam hai. Ye Huzoor ﷺ ki khaas izzat hai. Ye nikah isliye bhi haraam hai ke wo Jannat mein bhi Huzoor ﷺ ki biwiyan hongi.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

"Aur Allah ne tum par kitab aur hikmat utari aur tumhein wo sab sikhaya jo tum nahin jaante the. Allah ka tum par bohot bada fazl hai" (Nisa: 113).

'Allah ke bade fazl' se murad nubuwwat ke saath milne wala bohot bada fazl hai. Baaz ne kaha ke is se wo fazilat murad hai jo azal se Huzoor ﷺ ke liye muqarrar thi.

Waasti <sup>رحمة الله تعالى عليه</sup> ne ishara kiya hai ke is aayat mein ye bhi ishara hai ke Huzoor ﷺ mein Allah ke deedar ko bardasht karne ki taqat hai, jo Hazrat Moosa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> mein nahin thi.
