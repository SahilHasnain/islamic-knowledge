# First View: Conclusion and Second View

In 1318 AH, these people sent me a similar questionnaire in Delhi. In response, I wrote a book entitled:

> **"انباء المصطفى بحال سر و اخفى"**

That book brought a calamity upon the Wahhabis. They were denying what Allah Almighty had stated in the Quran, even though their claims negated their own faith and stood as clear testimony to their loss. Because of these blasphemous statements, they became unbelievers and apostates.

Then the Wahhabis committed an enormous act of disbelief concerning the Leader of the Prophets ﷺ. They said that the Prophet ﷺ had not known the circumstances of his own end, nor the end of his Ummah. This, too, is a denial of verses that are perfectly clear:

1. “Indeed, your Hereafter is better for you than your worldly life.” (Ad-Duha: 4)
2. “Indeed, soon your Lord will give you so much that you will be pleased.” (Ad-Duha: 5)
3. “On that Day, Allah will not disgrace the Prophet or the believers who are with him. Their light will run before them and behind them.” (At-Tahrim: 8)
4. “Soon your Lord will grant you the Praiseworthy Station.” (Bani Isra'il: 79)
5. “Allah only intends to remove impurity from you, O people of the Prophet’s household, and to purify you thoroughly.” (Al-Ahzab: 33)
6. “So that Allah may admit the believing men and believing women into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will remain forever; He will remove their sins from them. That is a tremendous attainment with Allah.” (Al-Fath: 5)
7. “Indeed, We have granted you a clear victory, so that He may forgive your earlier and later sins through you, complete His favour upon you, and guide you to a straight path. Allah is your Helper and the Bestower of honour.” (Al-Fath: 1)
8. “Blessed is He Who, if He wills, can give you something better than that: Gardens beneath which rivers flow, and He can make lofty palaces for you.” (Al-Furqan: 10)

Countless Hadith, transmitted successively with a single meaning, have come concerning the interpretation and explanation of these verses. They form an ocean without shore, and it would be difficult to enumerate them. But after the Word of Allah and the Hadith of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, what speech is there upon which one should place faith?

## The Second View

### The Difference Between Intrinsic and Bestowed Knowledge

After studying the preceding pages, even a person with a short-sighted view will have his eyes illuminated by the light of these verses: the combined knowledge of all creation cannot even raise the suspicion that it equals the knowledge of our Lord, the Lord of all the worlds.

There cannot be even a particle of doubt in a Muslim’s heart that the knowledge of Allah Almighty and the knowledge of His creation could be compared or considered equal.

These people do not even understand the following distinctions: Allah’s knowledge is intrinsic, while the knowledge of creation is bestowed; Allah’s knowledge is necessary by virtue of His Being, while the knowledge of creation is originated, since all creation is originated. An attribute cannot precede the one it describes. Allah’s knowledge is not created, whereas the knowledge of creation is created. Allah’s knowledge is not under anyone’s power or command. Divine knowledge is always necessary and enduring, while created knowledge is originated and temporary. Divine knowledge cannot change in any way, whereas the knowledge of creation is subject to change.

Despite these differences and distinctions, could anyone imagine equality? Only those wretched people upon whom Allah’s curse has fallen would become entangled in such confusion. They have become deaf to the truth, and their eyes have been deprived of light.

With complete conviction and faith, we can say that even to imagine that the knowledge of the Beloved ﷺ encompasses Allah’s knowledge is a false notion. Equality with Divine knowledge is impossible. Given these clear and vast differences, how could there be equality between Divine knowledge, which is intrinsic, and the knowledge of Mustafa ﷺ, which is bestowed?

Between the knowledge of Allah Almighty and that of the accepted Messenger ﷺ, there is no shared quality except the letters “I-L-M” (the letters forming the word *ilm*, “knowledge”).

We have established through decisive proofs that the notion that Divine knowledge is limited is false, both rationally and according to the Sacred Law.

When the Wahhabis read the books of the Imams of religion and their followers, study the Prophet’s ﷺ knowledge of the unseen in the light of the Quran and Hadith, and come to the conclusion that the Beloved ﷺ knows all events from the first day to the last—past and future—they begin issuing rulings of disbelief and shirk against those scholars. They accuse them of equating Divine knowledge with the knowledge of the Prophet ﷺ.

Those who make such judgements are extremely misguided and mistaken. They themselves fall into pits of disbelief and shirk. They have made limited, finite knowledge equal to Allah’s unlimited, infinite knowledge. They bear witness—Allah forbid—that the knowledge of Allah and the knowledge of creation are equal.

If, according to their own understanding, knowledge were truly infinite and immeasurable, how could they consider the knowledge of the Beloved ﷺ—which is finite and bestowed—to be equal to it? They would not fall into equating the knowledge of the Creator with that of creation.

When they display their intellect steeped in ignorance, they mock Allah’s infinite knowledge and describe it as deficient. May Allah Almighty drown such people and protect us from their tribulations.
