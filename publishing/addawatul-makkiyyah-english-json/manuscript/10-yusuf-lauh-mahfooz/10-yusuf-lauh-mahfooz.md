# The Knowledge of Sayyiduna Yusuf and the Preserved Tablet

## Sayyiduna Yusuf’s News to the Egyptians

Allah’s Prophet Sayyiduna Yusuf عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام told the Egyptians:

> “For seven years you will continue farming as usual. After those seven years, a time will come when abundant rain will enable people to grow crops. Then, for seven years, rain will cease and famine will prevail. After fifteen years, rain will fall so abundantly that grapevines will burst forth from the earth, and people will quench their thirst with grape juice.”

I continue to relate the details of these incidents, although such events are beyond number and will continue to occur until the Day of Judgement. In particular, Allah Almighty has granted His servants such knowledge of the five matters of the unseen that it needs no proof. No person of reason or wisdom disputes it. All these unseen matters are written in the Preserved Tablet, and angels and Awliya are also informed of what is in the Preserved Tablet. The station of the Prophets—and especially that of the Leader of the Prophets ﷺ—is exalted and supreme. Only one deprived of reason and faith would deny these perfections.

## The Clear Tablet

Allah Almighty has described the Tablet with the word “Mubeen” (clear) (Ya-Sin: 2). A thing is *mubeen* when it is clear and manifest, with no ambiguity. If the Preserved Tablet were hidden from Allah’s creation, in what sense would it be a “Clear Tablet”?

Imam Baydawi says that all the unseen matters of the heavens and earth are preserved in the Preserved Tablet; there is no unseen matter that is absent from the Clear Book. Imam Baghawi writes that every unseen matter is present in the Preserved Tablet. Imam Nasafi calls it the “Clear Tablet” in *Madarik-ut-Tanzil* and says that the angels see the Preserved Tablet: it is radiant, manifest, and clear.

Hazrat Mulla Ali Qari رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ writes in *Mirqat Sharif*:

> “All knowledge of what has happened and what will happen is inscribed in the Preserved Tablet. In other words, everything in the universe is written in it. When the verses of the Holy Quran descended, all these inscriptions from the Preserved Tablet were conveyed to the Beloved ﷺ. The Tablet contains finite knowledge and cannot encompass the infinite. Only those things that will occur from the first day until the establishment of the Day of Judgement are written in the Tablet. Therefore, in my view, there is no proof that the matters of the unseen concerning what has happened and what will happen are excluded from it. If the timing of events is indeed known from the Preserved Tablet, then surely the Noble Prophet ﷺ is aware of it.
>
> “If it is accepted that Allah Almighty kept some of this knowledge separate from the Preserved Tablet, then no inference can be drawn from the Quranic verses, and both possibilities would remain open. It is certain that the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ is not limited to the Preserved Tablet. The Preserved Tablet is but one wave from the oceans of his ﷺ knowledge, one stream from his boundless sea.
>
> “This is why I do not claim certainty regarding this knowledge, nor am I certain of its negation; I say only what Imam Taftazani wrote in his commentary on *Sharh al-Aqa'id*: Allah Almighty has informed some chosen Prophets of knowledge of the unseen. This belief is certain; the belief that knowledge of the unseen is bestowed is also certain.”

Imam Qastalani said that Allah Almighty informed His Prophets and Awliya of some of His unseen matters, and made His Noble Beloved ﷺ aware of the five matters of the unseen. We shall shortly present the illuminating views of Allama Bayjuri, Allama Shanwani, and Hazrat Abdul Aziz Dabbagh رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ on this subject. I shall then explain the views of such eminent figures as Allama Madabighi and the learned knower of Allah, Allama Fazil Ishmawi, and establish through their decisive proofs that Allah Almighty bestowed knowledge of the unseen upon His Noble Beloved ﷺ. I shall also explain that the angels were granted knowledge of the blowing of the Trumpet before the Day of Judgement, and present the proofs of Imam Fakhruddin Razi on this introductory point.

## The Prophet ﷺ as the Fountainhead of Knowledge

We have already stated that all created beings received knowledge through the means of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, and that the Noble Prophet’s ﷺ blessed being is the fountainhead of every kind of knowledge. It is also established that the one who gives knowledge must himself be knowledgeable and more learned.

Therefore, the Noble Prophet ﷺ knowing what will happen before the Day of Judgement and being fully informed of knowledge from pre-eternity to eternity does not contradict the verses of Allah. Nor does it contradict those verses that the Noble Prophet ﷺ is more learned than those to whom he distributed knowledge. Once we have reached the conclusion that all his ﷺ knowledge comes from the signs of Allah, this thought naturally springs to mind: the Prophet ﷺ was granted knowledge of these five matters, but was commanded to conceal it.

There is no doubt that both these statements and views are found among the scholars, and the eminent Imams did not reject them. Imam Jalaluddin Suyuti رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ devoted an entire chapter to the knowledge of Mustafa ﷺ in his world-renowned *Khasa'is al-Kubra*. There he commented on the views of the scholars: Was the Prophet ﷺ also granted knowledge of the five matters, knowledge of the Hour, and knowledge of the soul? Was he ﷺ commanded to conceal it?

## Whether the Prophet ﷺ Was Commanded to Conceal It

Allama Muhammad ibn Sayyid Allama Abdul Rasul Barzanji Madani رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ mentioned both views. He said that the knowledge of the Hour is momentous, and Allah Almighty reserved knowledge of it for Himself and did not disclose it to any of creation. He taught it only to the Noble Prophet ﷺ, while forbidding him ﷺ to inform others, so that the fear and awe of the Hour would remain. He stated:

> **وعلمها النبي**

“And He taught its knowledge to the Prophet.”

On further study, the Allama states that it was certainly taught to the Noble Prophet ﷺ. The Allama preferred this view.

The Wahhabis wrote a treatise on this subject and published it under the name of the aforementioned Hazrat, thereby deceiving people through discussion of the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. But when the views were brought forward, the Wahhabis were left without an answer.

It is their habit that when they cannot manage without presenting evidence, they publish fabricated books and appear as warriors in falsehood. For a time, they present their statements as equal to those of the just, truthful, and reliable scholars; but the time comes when their lies are exposed. They sometimes falsely attribute their corrupt ideas to Mulla Ali Qari, sometimes to Ibn Kathir, sometimes to Ismail Haqqi رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ, and sometimes babble by citing a treatise of Allama Suyuti. These are fabrications against Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal and lies against Imam Jalaluddin Suyuti. In this way, they also fabricate statements against Allama Mulla Ali Qari.

## The Wisdom of Advance Knowledge of the Preserved Tablet

The summary of the statements of Imam Jalaluddin Suyuti has been presented. They also openly lie against Allama Mulla Ali Qari.

The wisdom in teaching the knowledge of the Preserved Tablet in advance is that faith and affirmation may increase, and the angels may know beforehand who deserves help and who deserves condemnation, and each person’s station may be known in advance.

Shah Abdul Aziz Dehlawi رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ writes in *Tafsir-e-Azeezi* that being informed of the knowledge of the Preserved Tablet means that knowledge of events that will become manifest in reality is granted beforehand—whether by viewing the inscriptions on the Tablet or through Allah’s grace, by which the person is informed. Many accounts of the Awliya of Allah establish that they studied the designs of the Tablet.

### My Gaze Is on the Preserved Tablet

Imam Shatnufi and other Imams have related with an authentic chain that the son of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, the Ghaus of both humans and jinn, Sayyiduna Ghaus-ul-Azam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, used to say:

> **عيني في اللوح المحفوظ**

“My eye is always fixed upon the Preserved Tablet.”

Allah Almighty distributes wise matters on Laylat al-Qadr by His command. This establishes that, apart from knowledge of the Hour, four kinds of knowledge are distributed among the angels before the Day of Judgement, because the angels have tasks to carry out.

## Israfil and the Trumpet

Sayyiduna Israfil عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام is informed of his duty to blow the Trumpet before the Day of Judgement so that he can blow it as soon as the command arrives. Israfil عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام folded one wing when the Noble Prophet ﷺ came into this world; he will spread the other when the command to blow the Trumpet is given.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said, “How can I be at ease when the one who will blow the Trumpet has placed it in his mouth, is listening attentively for the Divine command, and has bowed his forehead?” This Hadith is recorded in Tirmidhi from Sayyiduna Abu Sa'id رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ.

That angel stands upon both knees, while Israfil عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام keeps his gaze fixed upon the wing that is spread. When he lowers that wing, the Trumpet will be blown. The interval between permission to blow and his lowering the wing is but a single movement. Since a movement takes place in time, it is necessary that he know of it before it occurs, whether one moment beforehand or thousands of years beforehand.

If such knowledge is necessary for a near angel, then how could it be impossible for the Beloved? It is not impossible that the Prophet ﷺ be informed two thousand years before the Day of Judgement, while being commanded not to tell others.[^4]

It is not impossible that some angels, some people, and especially some noble Prophets عَلَيْهِمُ السَّلَام be granted knowledge of it. ## The Mu'tazilah’s Interpretation

The Mu'tazilah also use these verses as proof to deny miracles of the Awliya. In *Sharh al-Maqasid*, the Allama answers the Mu'tazilah that “the unseen” here is not general but absolute or specific—that is, the time of the Day of Judgement—and there is a contextual indicator in the noble verse, for it mentions the Hour. It is not impossible that some angels, some people, and especially some noble Prophets عَلَيْهِمُ السَّلَام be granted knowledge of it.

It is established that Messengers are given knowledge of the unseen, though opinions may differ concerning the Awliya. Imam Qastalani states in *Irshad al-Bari*, his commentary on Sahih Bukhari, that no one besides Allah Almighty knows when the Hour will come; however, Allah opens the doors of the unseen to whichever chosen Messenger He is pleased with. A Wali follows a Messenger and receives knowledge through him.

Shah Abdul Aziz Dehlawi’s revered father, Shah Waliullah قُدِّسَ سِرُّهُ, recorded his own experience in *Tafhimat-e-Ilahiyyah*: in a particular spiritual event, he was shown the time when the Day of Judgement would be established and the heavens would split.

## Further Testimony

When he returned to the world of wakefulness, the event felt like a dream. If such conditions are disclosed to Awliya, then—Glory be to Allah!—where does the Noble Prophet ﷺ stand, and where does his knowledge stand?

Hazrat Imam Nawawi رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ writes in his commentary on *Futuhat Ilahiyyah*, and again in the marginal notes to his other commentary, *Fath-ul-Mubin*, that the Noble Prophet ﷺ has knowledge of the Day of Judgement. The true position is that one group of scholars said Allah Almighty called our Prophet ﷺ into His presence only after disclosing hidden matters to him. Yes, the Noble Prophet ﷺ was also given the command and choice whether to disclose them or not.

Usmawi, in *Salat*, and Sayyidi Ahmad Kabir Badri, in his commentary, judged this statement sound. These are all rays of the Divine declaration: “We have sent down the Quran to you, explaining everything clearly.” The truth shone forth through the light of the Quran as clouds part from before the sun.

We therefore need not discuss the details of the fifth unseen matter, which are also disclosed to the noble Awliya.
