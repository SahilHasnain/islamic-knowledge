# My Request

## The Intercession of the Prophet ﷺ

Sayyiduna Sawad ibn Qarib رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ first denied everything besides Allah. Second, he affirmed that the Noble Prophet ﷺ certainly possesses knowledge of the unseen. Third, he believed that the Prophet ﷺ intercedes. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said:

> “I have been granted the gift of intercession.”

## Intercession in This World

This Hadith is recorded in Sahih Muslim.

The Wahhabis have a strange logic. They say that people should not appeal to the Noble Prophet ﷺ for intercession in this world, claiming that he ﷺ cannot intercede during his worldly life. Yet Allah Almighty says to His Beloved ﷺ:

> “Seek forgiveness for the believing men and women who are closely connected to you.” (Muhammad: 19)

At another place, He says:

> “When they come to you after having wronged their own souls and ask forgiveness from Allah, and the Messenger seeks forgiveness for them, they will surely find Allah Most Accepting of repentance, Most Merciful.” (An-Nisa: 64)

The Wahhabis have pushed these verses aside and, when translating them, act with extreme miserliness.

### Fourth

Sayyiduna Sawad ibn Qarib رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ also believed that the intercession of the Noble Prophet ﷺ is the closest. Yet the leader of the Wahhabis, Maulwi Ismail Dehlvi, writes in *Taqwiyat-ul-Iman* that when Allah wishes to devise a means to forgive a repentant person, He will make anyone He wishes an intercessor; no one has any special distinction. The restriction to those who are remorseful and repentant is there because, according to the aforementioned Dehlvi, intercession is only for the remorseful person, not for a sinner who was unable to repent.

### Fifth

The Noble Prophet ﷺ is closest to Allah Almighty in intercession.

## The Intercession of the Prophet ﷺ

All other intercessors will intercede through him ﷺ. This is why Sayyiduna Sawad ibn Qarib appealed to the court of the Noble Prophet ﷺ.

### Sixth

Intercession is an exclusive distinction of the Sovereign of the Universe ﷺ. The other intercessors will intercede in the court of the Noble Prophet ﷺ; in the Court of Allah, there will be no intercessor without him ﷺ. He ﷺ said, “I am the master of the intercession of all the Prophets.” He was not boasting; he was speaking of Allah’s kindness and gift.

### Seventh

It is also established from Sayyiduna Sawad’s words that whoever holds fast to the Noble Prophet’s ﷺ mantle, he ﷺ comes to that person’s aid. How contrary to the words of this renowned Companion is the view of the Wahhabi leader, Maulwi Ismail Dehlvi, who writes that the Prophet عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام will not even be able to help his own daughter—so what mention is there of anyone else?

All the thoughts mentioned above belong to a Companion who was a lover of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and honoured in his sight. Everything stated in this Hadith is reflected in the Prophet’s ﷺ pure life.

Those unaware of the station of Mustafa ﷺ cite this verse concerning the Prophet’s ﷺ—Allah forbid—lack of knowledge:

## The Messengers’ Knowledge and Divine Grant

> “On the Day when Allah gathers the Messengers, He will say, ‘What response did you receive?’ They will say, ‘We have no knowledge.’” (Al-Ma'idah: 109)

What proof does this noble verse provide that the noble Prophets lacked knowledge? They spoke the absolute truth and denied their own intrinsic knowledge. Indeed, when a shadow stands before the original, it has no independent standing. When the angels were presented before Allah Almighty, they said:

> “Glory be to You! We have no knowledge except what You have taught us.” (Al-Baqarah: 32)

This is a statement about bestowed knowledge. The noble Prophets عَلَيْهِمُ السَّلَام show even greater reverence before the Divine Court than the angels; therefore, they denied their own knowledge before Allah.

The summary is that everything exists through Allah Almighty, and nothing exists without His gift. We have reached the conclusion held by the noble Imams: whoever claims knowledge independently, without the gift of Allah, is being refuted.

Some of our scholars have quoted from the commentary on *Jami' al-Saghir* that the statement of the Noble Prophet ﷺ—that no one besides Allah Almighty knows these five matters—means that no one knows them independently. But if Allah Almighty informs and teaches someone, that is His favour. People who possess knowledge disclosed by Allah are present in this very world. The Prophets are, of course, the noble Prophets; we have also found such knowledge among most of the followers of the Noble Prophet ﷺ—and it is all a gift from Allah Almighty, by the honour of the Leader of the Prophets ﷺ.
