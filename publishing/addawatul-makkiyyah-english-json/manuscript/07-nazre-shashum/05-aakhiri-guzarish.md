# A Final Request

**Source:** `content/books/addawatul-makkiyyah.json`, JSON pages 76–77 (printed pages 72–73), from the heading “Aakhiri Guzarish” through the paragraph before “Huzoor ke Ummatiyo par Ghuyoob ke Inamaat.”

## Printed Page 72

These five matters of the unseen that we have discussed are among the manifest knowledge of the unseen. Their number is known to Allah Almighty—or to whomever He has taught this knowledge. The Noble Prophet ﷺ was never miserly in sharing these unseen matters.

In *Jami' al-Nihayah*, this narration is stated clearly: Allah Almighty did not call His Prophet ﷺ back from this world until He had informed him of everything.

In light of these Hadith, allow me to say that we have presented the noble verses that explain this meaning, and we have cited authentic Hadith that clarify the matter. In all these references, it is nowhere said that the five matters are known intrinsically. Rather, these five things were disclosed openly through Allah Almighty’s gift. This knowledge is not exclusive to Allah; it is granted to created beings through Allah’s bestowal. Acquiring knowledge from Allah Almighty requires a means.

Hazrat Abdul Aziz Dabbagh رَحْمَةُ اللّٰهِ تَعَالَى عَلَيْهِ explains in his book *Ibriz* that not a single one of these five matters was concealed from the Noble Prophet ﷺ.
