# The Wisdom Behind the Exclusivity of Five Matters

## Different Counts in Hadith

In the preceding pages, we discussed the five matters of the unseen. Now we shall express our thoughts in the light of the background to these matters and the wisdom that led to their being singled out. The Noble Quran does not state a number of five, nor does it use the word “five.” Rather, in view of the manner of expression, it mentions matters whose number is at least five. Keep this saying of the Noble Prophet ﷺ in mind. He ﷺ said:

> “Allah Almighty granted me five things that He had not granted to anyone before me.”

Although the Noble Prophet ﷺ was favoured with countless gifts—gifts beyond number—the same Hadith is also expressed in these words:

> “I was given excellence over the earlier Prophets through six things.”

Do these two Hadith contradict one another? Certainly not. The two Hadith give different numbers when counting the excellences. We find many authentic Hadith, accepted as reliable by the Imams, that give different counts and appear to negate one another numerically. I have collected many such weak Hadith in my book:

> **"البحث الفاحص عن طرق أحاديث الخصائص"**

Their numbers vary, and the count in one Hadith does not match that in another; yet the special qualities described in these Hadith number more than thirty—so what comparison is there with five or six? Likewise, *Jami' al-Saghir* and its abridgements and compilations mention counts of three, four, and five. These numbers do not establish a binding limit.

You may say that, apart from these Hadith, there must be a Quranic purpose or wisdom in mentioning the five matters under discussion. I wish to shed light on this wisdom and subtle point.

## Other Matters of the Unseen

In my view, there is both wisdom and a subtle point behind this. The point is lofty, and the wisdom is sublime. Most importantly, because of their limited understanding, the Wahhabis discuss only those matters of the unseen that are mentioned in these verses. In reality, many matters of the unseen besides these five are mentioned. These five matters are not even one-thousandth of the thousands and millions of unseen matters.

Allah Almighty is the Knower of the Unseen, the All-Aware of all hidden matters. Every one of His Attributes is unseen. The record of deeds is unseen; the Barzakh (intermediate realm after death) is unseen; Paradise is unseen; Hell is unseen; the Reckoning is unseen; the Plain of Judgement is unseen; people being gathered there is unseen; and being raised from the graves is unseen. These are beyond our counting. The point is not that these other unseen matters are more important than the five under discussion. Rather, Allah Almighty mentioned only these five matters in the noble verse under discussion instead of mentioning all those other unseen matters.

In reality, the age in which the Noble Prophet ﷺ was sent was full of soothsayers, who beguiled people in astonishing and ridiculous ways. In particular, they told people: “We know the unseen. We possess knowledge of *ramal* (divination using sand), knowledge of the stars, experience in *qiyafah* (judging by physical features or tracks), and knowledge of *iyaafah* (interpreting omens). We can calculate matters from *tehare* and *haanse*.[^1]”

They kept leading people through all kinds of calculations in order to satisfy their own desires and passions. They did not discuss the Divine Being, Divine Attributes, the Hereafter, or the angels, nor did people come to them to inquire about such matters. They would tell people only when it would rain, whether trade would bring loss or profit, how a profit or loss would occur, and whether a traveller would return safely home.

## The Soothsayers’ Claims and Knowledge of Judgement

Allah Almighty made clear to those soothsayers and the people caught in their grip that they were liars, making false claims and deceiving people. Knowledge of such things belongs to the Majestic Lord, the Creator and Master of them.

Alongside these worldly events and occurrences, Allah Almighty also mentioned knowledge of the Day of Judgement: besides these matters, Allah will inform us of the conditions that will arise when the Day of Judgement is established. You debate the death of one or two people, while Allah Almighty knows the Day of Reckoning and the day of the end of the entire universe.

Astrologers are merely people who follow conjecture. Regarding ordinary occurrences, they continually offer only speculative conclusions. They have no certain rule by which to determine the ruin of one household or the death of one person; they themselves have no confidence in their speculation.

## Astrology and Conjecture

The positions of stars, *jog* (astrological conjunctions), their mutual relations, and their indications are all presented uncertainly. Sometimes a prediction proves correct, but most often it proves wrong. They prepare charts for a child’s birth and charts of a person’s lifespan, yet are deceived about many moments and events throughout the year and keep giving incorrect results.

One star has some effect on a house, but astrologers do not understand the mutual conflict of forces and qualities. They cannot judge what brings good or harm; they merely make guesses. They decide according to whichever side seems to weigh more, yet remain unaware of the forces that bring about revolutions in the world. Only the Holy Quran, through its decisive judgements, satisfies the intellects of the world.

The conjunction of Zuhal and Mushtari, Kaatino, Barho, Aatishi Hamal, and Asdaqoos[^2] in a person’s birth chart—such as the conjunction that occurred at the time of the Flood of Nuh—is beyond the capacity of these soothsayers and astrologers to understand.

Conjunctions that occur according to calculation may be understood approximately, as they were in earlier times. But how many years will pass before such a conjunction or alignment occurs? At what degree of which zodiac sign will it take place? At what minute and in which direction? How many days will it last? For how long will one star keep another hidden, and at what time will it become visible? These are the questions of astrologers. Yet only that Powerful Knower, the Judge of judges, knows this calculation; its measure is set by Him alone.

By mentioning the Day of Judgement, Allah Almighty has demolished all such conjectures and declared: “If you are truthful in your calculations, then estimate the Day of Judgement instead of estimating the death of one person”—which would have been easier.

## The Wisdom in Mentioning These Five Matters

This is the wisdom and subtle point behind these five matters of the unseen—and Allah Almighty knows best what the true unseen is. Soothsayers’ guesswork has no reality. This point can enter the mind only through Allah’s grace and the aid of the Noble Prophet ﷺ.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said that there are five things which no one knows except Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty also said, “O Messenger, tell them that no one in the heavens and earth knows the unseen except Allah.” The Noble Prophet ﷺ mentioned only five matters, whereas Allah Almighty spoke in general terms. We accept this, because the general wording and the particular count do not contradict one another: no one besides Allah knows these five matters, and Allah alone is the true Knower of the unseen.

Among all Arabic sayings, the Noble Prophet ﷺ selected this statement:

> “Whatever comes to us besides Allah is unreal.”

## Levels of Understanding the Declaration of Divine Unity

For ordinary people, “There is no deity except Allah” means that there is no deity besides Allah. For the elect, it means that there is no object of devotion besides Allah Almighty. For the select among the elect, it means that none is beheld except Allah. For those who have attained the highest station, it means that none exists except Allah.

All these meanings are correct and true. Faith rests upon the first meaning, righteousness upon the second, the spiritual path upon the third, and arrival unto Allah upon the fourth. May Allah Almighty grant us a share in all these meanings.
