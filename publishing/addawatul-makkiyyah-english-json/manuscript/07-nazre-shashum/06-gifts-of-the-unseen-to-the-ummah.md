# Gifts of the Unseen to the Prophet’s ﷺ Ummah

**Source:** `content/books/addawatul-makkiyyah.json`, JSON pages 77–84 (printed pages 73–80), from “Huzoor ke Ummatiyo par Ghuyoob ke Inamaat” through the paragraph before “Bahjatul Asraar aur Iske Musannif.”

## Printed Page 73 — The Prophet’s ﷺ Knowledge and the Spiritual Poles

The Noble Prophet ﷺ is the master of the knowledge of *maa kaana wa maa yakoon* (what has happened and what will happen). Seven of the *qutb* (spiritual poles) of the world among his followers also know these five matters of the unseen, although these seven poles are subordinate to the seven *Ghaus* (supreme spiritual helpers). What comparison can there be between a Ghaus and these men who have been informed of these five kinds of knowledge—and the Leader of the Prophets ﷺ, who is informed of the knowledge of those before and after?

By the seven *qutb*, I mean those *abdal* (a rank of Awliya) who are above the seventy *abdal* and below the two Imams.

## Printed Page 74 — The Knowledge of the Awliya

In *Ibriz*, Abdul Aziz Dabbagh writes further: how could knowledge of the five unseen matters remain concealed from the Noble Prophet ﷺ when none of the spiritually empowered members of his Ummah can exercise such spiritual authority until he has been granted knowledge of these five matters?

We alert those who deny this: let them read the books of these people, hear their statements, and stop rejecting the Awliya of Allah. To reject them is to destroy religion, and Allah Almighty will requite those who deny them.

I regret the low level of learning of the person who counts the five matters of the unseen among the knowledge exclusive to Allah Almighty. I cannot affirm his claim that this knowledge of the five matters is exclusive to Allah. If his statement were accepted, we would have to conclude that Allah Almighty taught all other knowledge of the unseen to His noble Prophets—especially His Beloved ﷺ—and made only these five matters exclusive to Himself, informing neither His Messenger nor any other Prophet of them.

That is false. Otherwise, one would have to accept that all the other knowledge of the unseen—apart from these five matters—concerning the Being of the Lord of lords and all His Attributes, which He alone knows, was within the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. Then even Allah Almighty’s infinite knowledge would become part of the Prophet’s ﷺ knowledge. Yet, as we have explained above, the scholars of Ahl-e-Sunnat cannot even conceive that any created being could encompass Allah Almighty’s infinite knowledge.

This is the ignorance of the Wahhabis. They have resolved to diminish the station of the Noble Prophet ﷺ; instead of speaking openly, they divide his ﷺ knowledge of the unseen into quantities and limits, then use deception and fraud to deny it.

## Printed Pages 75–76 — What Was in the Womb of Ummul Fazl?

Al-Khatib and Nu'aym narrate in *Dala'il-un-Nubuwwah* from Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا that Sayyidatuna Ummul Fazl رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا told him:

“Once, I passed in front of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, and he ﷺ said, ‘You are pregnant; there is a boy in your womb. When he is born, bring him to me.’ I replied, ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, how could I be pregnant? I feel no sign of pregnancy, especially since Quraysh have made all men and women swear that no man will approach a woman.’ The Noble Prophet ﷺ said, ‘The oath is one matter, but what I have told you is true.’”

Sayyidatuna Ummul Fazl رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا said that some time later she gave birth to a boy. She lifted him and took him to the service of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. He ﷺ recited the Adhan in the child’s right ear and the Iqamah in his left ear, placed his blessed saliva in the child’s mouth, named him Abdullah, and said, “Take away the father of the Caliphs.”

She told her husband, Sayyiduna Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, what had happened. He too came to the service of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and said that Ummul Fazl had told him this. The Prophet ﷺ replied, “What I said is true. This child is the father of Caliphs. Among them will be a Caliph named Saffah and a Caliph named Mahdi.”

The Noble Prophet ﷺ knew what was in the womb of Ummul Fazl—and more: he knew about the child’s life and the names of the Caliphs who would descend from him many generations later. He knew the children who would come, generation after generation. He announced, “Take away the father of the Caliphs,” and then said that there would be a Saffah and a Mahdi.

## Printed Page 76 — Sayyiduna Siddiq Gave Glad Tidings of a Daughter Before Her Birth

Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا says that her revered father, Sayyiduna Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, had gifted her twenty *wasq* (a traditional measure) of dates from his estate in Ghabah and instructed her to have them harvested from the trees.

When the time of Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq’s رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ passing drew near, he called Ummul Momineen Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا to him and said:

“Daughter, there is nothing in the world dearer to me than you, and I worry greatly that you may face hardship after me. I had given you the dates, so have them harvested now. If they come into your possession while I am still alive, they are yours. Otherwise, you will receive only the share that is due to you among the heirs. You have two brothers and two sisters, so divide the inheritance according to Allah Almighty’s commands.”

Sayyidatuna Aishah replied, “My revered father, even if my share were twice as much, I would still divide it among my brothers and sisters. But tell me: why do you say I have two sisters, when I have only one sister, Asma?” He replied, “The daughter of Kharijah is pregnant; she will give birth to a girl. I know this.”

## Printed Page 77 — The Account in *Tabaqat Ibn Sa'd*

Ibn Sa'd gives this narration in *Tabaqat* as follows: Sayyiduna Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ said, “I have received inspiration concerning what is in the womb of the daughter of Kharijah: it will be a girl. I enjoin you to treat her well.” A daughter named Umm Kulthum was born to Sayyiduna Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ.

Hadith state that an angel is appointed over the womb. The angel forms the child, determines whether it will be male or female, shapes its beauty or ugliness, sets its lifespan and provision, and then records whether it will be wretched or of good disposition.

## Printed Page 78 — The Banner of Khaybar

In the two Sahih collections, a Hadith of Khaybar is narrated from Sayyiduna Sahl ibn Sa'd رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said:

> “By Allah, tomorrow I shall give the banner to a man through whose hands Khaybar will be conquered. He loves Allah and His Messenger, and Allah and His Messenger love him.”

The next day, the Prophet ﷺ gave the banner to Sayyiduna Ali رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. The Noble Prophet ﷺ had sworn by Allah when making this statement; he ﷺ knew with certainty what would happen the next day.[^1]

### News of the Place of His Passing

The Noble Prophet ﷺ knew that his passing would take place in Madinah. He gathered the Ansar (Helpers of Madinah) and said, “My life is where your lives are, and my passing will be where yours is.” This Hadith is narrated in Muslim from Sayyiduna Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ.

### The Prophet ﷺ Told Mu'adh ibn Jabal of His Departure

When the Noble Prophet ﷺ appointed Sayyiduna Mu'adh ibn Jabal رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ governor of Yemen and sent him there, he ﷺ asked, “Mu'adh, after this year, you will not meet me in this world; but remember, you will visit my mosque and come to my grave.”

[^1]: The source adds that the Noble Prophet ﷺ foretold many unseen events that would occur centuries later: wars, tribulations, the descent of Sayyiduna Masih عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام, the appearance of Imam Mahdi and the Dajjal, the incursion of Ya'juj and Ma'juj, Dabbat al-Ard, and other events.

## Printed Page 79 — Events of Badr and Sayyiduna Ali’s Passing

Imam Ahmad recorded this blessed Hadith in his *Musnad*. Another Hadith is narrated in Sahih Muslim from Sayyiduna Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ: during the Battle of Badr, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ announced to the noble Companions that a certain polytheist would be killed at a particular place. He ﷺ pointed to each person’s place of death on the ground. Sayyiduna Anas said, “When the battle was decided in the evening, I saw with my own eyes that the body of each unbeliever lay at the very place the Noble Prophet ﷺ had marked and named.” Sayyiduna Faruq-e-Azam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ affirmed the Prophet’s ﷺ statement when he saw those bodies.

On the night of his martyrdom, Sayyiduna Ali رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ repeatedly went outside, looked toward the sky, and said, “By Allah, I have not been misinformed, nor am I speaking falsely. This is the very night that was promised to me.”

## Printed Page 80 — The Foretelling of Sayyiduna Uthman’s Passing

A narration from another Companion of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, Sayyiduna Aqra رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, is graded *hasan*. He was a freed servant of Sayyiduna Abu Ayyub Ansari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. The narration says:

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Salam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ went to the people of Makkah rather than to the Egyptian rebels and said, “Do not kill Sayyiduna Uthman رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. He will leave this world in forty days.” They refused. A few days later, he returned and told them, “Do not kill him. In fifteen days he will pass on to the True One.”

We have written before that the words of the noble Companions and exalted Awliya are a boundless ocean with no shore, and drawing from it does not diminish its waters. Yet we consider it necessary to mention a few incidents in addition to the Hadith, so that the hearts of those who reject Hadith may burst and they may abandon their views.
