## Printed Page 9

The Leader of the Prophets ﷺ beholds the Being and Attributes of Allah Almighty in such a manner that no distortion or shortcoming enters his blessed sight. Today people doubt and argue about what his sight had already witnessed. Allah Almighty revealed His Quran to him, and the Quran explains everything clearly. The Noble Prophet ﷺ knew what has happened and what is yet to happen. His sight encompassed all knowledge of the past and the future. He knew kinds of knowledge that have neither limit nor number, and no one besides him attained access to that knowledge.

If the knowledge of Sayyiduna Adam, all worldly knowledge, and the knowledge of the Preserved Tablet were gathered and set beside the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, they would appear like a drop of water beside an ocean. In the same way, all the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ is like a splash—or a handful of water—beside the ocean of Allah Almighty’s infinite knowledge. The Noble Prophet ﷺ seeks help from his Lord, while the whole universe seeks help through him ﷺ. All the knowledge possessed by the learned people of the world is a gift from the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and has reached them through him. All this knowledge came from his blessed court and was received from his door.

## Printed Page 10

During the days when I was staying in Makkah Mukarramah, some people from India presented me with a questionnaire concerning the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. It was Monday, the afternoon of 25 Dhu al-Hijjah 1323 AH. As soon as I saw it, I suspected that it had been put together by those Wahhabis who had begun a campaign of insulting speech against Allah and His Messenger in India and had written books on this subject. Makkah Mukarramah is a peaceful and secure city, and it is home to countless religious scholars. If a Sunni encounters a religious question here, he can ask them. The scholars of Makkah Mukarramah are boundless oceans of religious knowledge; people do not leave them to seek out narrow streams.

The noble scholars of Makkah Mukarramah are our leaders. They give comprehensive answers to the objections raised by the Wahhabis about the knowledge of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and other matters. On one or two occasions, they explained such questions so clearly that all the faithful became reassured: the rust was removed from their hearts, their minds were illuminated, and their defects were corrected. These explanations brought the Wahhabis into a state of despair. By the grace and generosity of my Lord, I, this weak servant—Ahmad Raza Khan Barelvi—also continue to follow the radiant Sunnah of my forefathers and to bring the Wahhabiyyah to ruin. I have authored more than two hundred books and have repeatedly invited the leading Wahhabis to debate—not merely once or twice, but many times—yet they have been unable even to answer and have remained bewildered.

## Printed Page 11

Those who sit in India and insult the Noble Prophet ﷺ, persist in abuse and slander, and attribute lies and falsehood to Allah Almighty flee from the field of debate. They run away and disappear, then cannot face it again for the rest of their lives. Most of them have already departed from this world; those who remain will soon leave it in the same disgrace. Their end will be marked by bewilderment and confusion.

They learned that I was staying in Makkah Mukarramah for only a few days. I had no reference books with me; I was occupied with visiting the House of Allah and was preparing to travel to the city of my Master and Lord, Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. At such a time, they contrived a questionnaire and put it before me. They hoped that, without books, and while preparing for Madinah al-Munawwarah, I would be unable to answer. Then they could rejoice and say, “Ahmad Raza Khan could not answer,” and take revenge for their humiliation. At first I remained silent, although I had silenced their leaders many times before. But what did they know? I was under the protection of the firm religion. The one who supports and aids the religion is himself granted victory and protection. Allah Almighty has power: when He says to a thing, “Be,” it comes to be. By His grace, Allah Almighty also gave me strength. Then it occurred to me to prepare two kinds of answers to this questionnaire: one for the people of truth and for circulation in treatises, so that readers might find the path of guidance, and another for those stubborn attackers. So I took up my pen and prepared for each of them an answer suited to what they deserved.

### The First Discussion

#### The Foundation of Religion

Remember that religion rests upon whatever brings salvation in the Hereafter. Belief in the entire Holy Quran is essential. In the world there are many misguided people who believe in some verses of Allah Almighty but disregard others. The Qadariyyah sect is well known among them. They considered themselves the creators of their own actions—that is, their deeds. They believed in this noble verse:

“We did not wrong them; rather, they themselves used to wrong their own souls.” (An-Nahl: 118)

But they rejected this noble verse:

“Allah has created you and what you do.” (As-Saffat: 96)

There is also the Jabariyyah sect. They regarded a human being as wholly compelled, like a stone. They believed in this noble verse:

“You cannot will unless Allah, the Lord of all the worlds, wills.”

## Printed Page 13

The Jabariyyah reject this verse:

“We repaid them for their rebellion; and We certainly speak the truth.” (Al-An'am: 146)

The Kharijites call even a person who commits a major sin an unbeliever. They believe in this noble verse:

“Indeed, the wicked will surely be in Hell; they will enter it on the Day of Judgement.” (Al-Infitar: 14–15)

Then they reject this noble verse:

“Indeed, Allah does not forgive associating partners with Him, but He forgives whatever is lesser than that for whomever He wills.” (An-Nisa: 116)

The belief of the Murji'ah sect is that no sin can harm a Muslim, whatever sin he may commit. They believe in this noble verse:

“Do not despair of Allah’s mercy. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins; He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful.” (Az-Zumar: 53)

But they reject this noble verse:

“Whoever does evil will be requited for it.”

There are thousands of examples of this kind that can be presented concerning different religions and sects. Books of Islamic theology contain many such proofs.
