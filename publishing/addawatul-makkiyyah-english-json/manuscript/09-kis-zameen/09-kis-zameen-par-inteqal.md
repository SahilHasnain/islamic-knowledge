# Where Will the Passing Take Place?

This Hadith is narrated by Ibn Sakan, Ibn Mundah, and Ibn Asakir رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ. They relate:

> “I fell ill, and the Noble Prophet ﷺ came to visit me. When I saw him ﷺ, I said, ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, I fear that I may pass away during this illness.’ He ﷺ replied, ‘No, that will not happen. You will live. You will migrate toward Syria, and your passing will take place on a hill in Palestine.’”

He passed away during the Caliphate of the Commander of the Faithful, Sayyiduna Umar Faruq-e-Azam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, and was buried in Ramla.
