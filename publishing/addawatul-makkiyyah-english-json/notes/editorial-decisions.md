# Addawatul Makkiyyah English Editorial Decisions

## Independent Source Boundary

This edition is translated only from `content/books/addawatul-makkiyyah.json`. The existing
English project is unrelated and is not a source, reference, or style authority for this edition.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the author's argument and polemical force without intensifying, weakening, or
  editorially correcting the source.
- Render Quranic translations from the wording present in the JSON; retain source references.
- Keep Arabic quotations and honorifics exactly as present in the JSON.
- Urdu and Roman Urdu poetry is translated into English in this edition. Preserve its line
  structure, speaker, addressee, devotional meaning, and poetic repetition/rhyme where feasible.
- Keep print-page/JSON-page mappings in internal project notes. Do not expose JSON identifiers or
  source-mapping statements in the publishable manuscript. Keep only headings that belong to the
  author's argument or provide natural editorial organization for readers; actual page numbering
  belongs to the final layout.
- Keep JSON/source tracking and translator-facing uncertainty annotations in internal project
  notes, not in the reader-facing manuscript. Where wording is unresolved, preserve a natural
  faithful rendering in the manuscript and record the uncertainty here for later editorial review.
- The source phrase “Basharte lashe” at JSON page 24 appears textually unclear. Retain it as
  transliterated wording with an explicit note that its intended technical sense is unclear;
  do not silently normalize or supply an interpretation.
- The divination terms `tehare` and `haanse` on JSON page 68 are unclear in the source record.
  Preserve them transliterated and flag the uncertainty rather than assigning meanings.
- JSON page 20 contains `----` in the technical sentence “Aisa ilm saabit karne ke liye kisi ek
  fard ka ---- hona zaroori hai.” The English draft supplies “existence” as a likely reconstruction;
  this remains an editorial inference pending a clearer source edition.
- JSON page 42 cites the teaching quotation as An-Nisa:13; its wording matches An-Nisa:113 and
  another occurrence in the same source gives 113. The English manuscript uses An-Nisa:113, with
  the source mismatch retained here for traceability.
- The Ghaus-ul-Azam account's stated total of 72 unseen matters does not reconcile with “62 plus
  five more.” Preserve the author's stated figures in the manuscript; do not silently alter them.
