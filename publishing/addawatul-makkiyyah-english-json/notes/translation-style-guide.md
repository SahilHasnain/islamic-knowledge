# Addawatul Makkiyyah English Translation Style Guide

## Source and Scope

- Translate directly from the complete records in `content/books/addawatul-makkiyyah.json`.
- This is an independent translation. Do not consult, quote, compare against, or reuse the
  existing `publishing/addawatul-makkiyyah-english/` translation or its files.
- Preserve the author's complete argument, sequence, certainty, examples, quotations, citations,
  names, and devotional tone. Do not summarize, omit, or add commentary.
- Use source page numbers and JSON `pageNumber` values for traceability; distinguish printed
  page numbers from PDF/extraction page numbers when both are available.
- Preserve exact Arabic Quranic quotations, hadith, duas, honorifics, and `ﷺ` from the JSON.
- Where the source gives a published Quran translation in Roman Urdu, translate its wording
  faithfully into English and identify the cited surah/verse. Do not substitute another version.
- Urdu poetry must be transliterated into Roman Urdu, not translated. Preserve line structure,
  speaker, addressee, devotional meaning, radif, and qafiyah; resolve pronouns from the whole poem.
- Preserve quotations as quotations and retain bibliographic details as given.

## Adab and Terminology

- Follow repository-wide Prophet ﷺ adab requirements.
- Preserve the symbol `ﷺ` wherever present in the source.
- Maintain a glossary and record editorial decisions as translation proceeds.
- Explain technical or specialized terms at first use when needed for clarity, without weakening
  or changing the author's claims.

## Batch and Review

- Translate complete sequential source-page ranges directly from full JSON records, not previews.
- Compare every completed batch page by page against the source JSON before proceeding.
- Preserve page/section boundaries and do not split paragraphs, quotations, or citations.
- Keep each manuscript file at or below 1,000 lines; split only at complete page or section
  boundaries.
- Check completeness, ordering, names, quotations, citations, Arabic preservation, adab, and
  accidental omissions after each batch.
