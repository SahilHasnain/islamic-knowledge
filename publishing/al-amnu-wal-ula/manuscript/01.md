## Istefta

*(The question that reached Aala Hazrat)*

Istefta from Dehli, sent by Maulvi Muhammad Karamatullah Khan Saheb (who is a khalifa of Hazrat Haaji Imdadullah Muhajir Makki), 21 Jumadal Ukhra 1311 Hijri.

What do the Ulama of the Deen say about this matter...

Zaid says that reading Duroode Taj and Dalailul Khairat constitutes shirk and an evil innovation, because the following words appear in Duroode Taj:

ْ َدَافِعِ الْبََلَ ءِ والْوَ باَءِ والْقَحْ طِ وَاْلْ َ رَضِ وَاْلْ َل"

He says that in the mention of Rasoole Akram <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, these words are problematic. And he calls it an evil innovation because this Durood (was compiled) hundreds of years after the era of the Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.

The reply to this (i.e., to Zaid's claims) is that this Durood is full of blessings and goodness. Zaid is ignorant of Arabic — he does not understand that Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is the cause of the warding off of calamities, even though the true warding off belongs to Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup>. In Mukhtasarul Muaani:

ِاَنْبَتَ الرَ بِیِعِ الْبَقَل

it is stated that according to the believers this is metaphorical, and according to the disbelievers it is literal. Besides this (it is in the Qur'an):

Translation: "And it is not befitting Allah's Majesty to punish them while you are among them." (`al-Anfal: 83`)

And (at another place):

Translation: "And We have not sent you but as a mercy to all the worlds." (`al-Anbiya: 107`)

Our claim is supported by elders. And was it not that in the year Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup> was born, the famine and plague were averted? Besides this, the statement of Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is recorded in the Noble Qur'an in this manner:

Translation: "That I may give you a pure son." (`Maryam: 19`)

Here, according to Zaid's logic, even Hazrat Jibreel <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — ma'az Allah — has become a mushrik, because he refers to himself as "Wahhab." So whatever answer comes from Zaid's side will apply equally to us. And since this Durood is customary and widespread among the Ulama and Mashayikh of greatness, they all become mushrik in Zaid's view. Furthermore, Zaid himself cannot escape from this deliberate shirk, because he also calls poison a killer, medicines as warding-off and curative (meaning that medicines bring healing), and Hazrat Shah Waliullah Muhaddise Dehelvi in his Qasida refers to Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup> as the one who wards off (evil). There are many more evidences, but there is no room for them in this brief text.

As for the claim that something becomes an evil innovation merely because it was compiled hundreds of years later — this too is evidence of Zaid's foolishness. Zaid himself reads the Khutbah Jumu'ah of Maulvi Ismail Saheb from the top of the mimbar. Does he have any hadith for this, or were these from the era of Rasoolullah <sup>عليه السلام</sup>? The reading of these khutbahs (which were written hundreds of years later) became Sunnah for Zaid, yet the reading of the Durood compiled from the treasures of truth became an evil innovation? Yes, the forms of Durood that are transmitted from Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup> himself — even we consider their reading to be superior and better.

However, the Ulama of deep understanding and the Fuqaha of perfection, in their state of love and devotion, have composed Durood Shareef with magnificent words. Among them is Janab Ghausus Saqalain Mahboobe Subhani, and Hazrat Shaykh Abdul Haq Muhaddise Dehelvi has recorded it in Jazbul Quloob. Hazrat Shaykh himself has authored a separate treatise on this subject, and all the Durood composed by the great Mashayikh are included in it. And in "Sharh Sufr as-Sa'adah" there are thirty-six forms transmitted from Rasoole Khuda <sup>عليه السلام</sup> himself, and more from the Sahaba-e-Kiraam and Tabayeen <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنهم</sup>.

Zaid the ignorant has declared all of these revered scholars to be mushrik — ma'az Allah. Now the Ulama of Islam are asked whether Zaid's statement is correct and in accordance with the creed of the righteous predecessors, or whether Amr's statement is? May the details and particulars be explained clearly. May Allah grant you the best reward.

## Al Jawab

## Muqaddima

O Muslims! Your Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup> has removed from you the affliction of madness and the fitna of the Fitna-angle.

It is not surprising that Zaid the unrestrained utters such words — the foundation of the Wahabi creed is, to the extent possible, the erasure of the remembrance of Huzoor Sayyidul Insi wal Jaan <sup>عليه السلام</sup> and the diminishing of the love of the Beloved of Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup> in the hearts of Muslims.

Translation: "And the wrongdoer will soon know at what turning-point he will be overturned." (`ash-Shu'ara: 227`)

But the surprise is at those Sunni Muslims who lend ear to such impure statements. There have always been, and will always be, many heedless people in the world. Why should a Muslim of correct belief pay any attention to them? The remedy for such people is silence in the presence (of the scholars), forgetfulness in the gossip, and at all times and in all states, a greater enthusiasm for the pure remembrance of their incomparable Beloved <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — so that the opponents themselves burn and perish in their own fire.

## The Belief of the Ulama and Aimma of the Deen Regarding the Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

There is no room to present the statements of the Aimma and Ulama in refutation of this group, because those whom you, with your belief, call Aimma and Ulama — in their view, you too are mushrik and innovators — ma'az Allah. It was they who compiled and spread the Durood-e-Mahmood from numerous books and sources.

Your beloved Prophet Muhammad Mustafa Daafe-e-Bala <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — Allah <sup>عز وجل</sup>'s greatest Khalifa, the helper and means of every blessing, the source of every goodness and mercy, the intercessor and sufficer, the distributor of bounties, the remover of grief, and the warder-off of hardships — it was they who wrote all of this, whose resounding descriptions have made the heavens echo with those published works. The faqeer has gathered numerous excellent instructions and concise texts in the valuable book "Saltanatul Mustafa," upon seeing which, praise be to Allah, faith is renewed and the food of certainty is placed upon the face of conviction.

---

## The Leader of the Wahabis Had Been Declaring All Scholars as Kafir for 600 Years

*(Here, the reference is to Muhammad bin Abdul Wahhab Najdi, who created fitna in the Deen)*

So in their view, they truly inherited this shirk and innovation from him. Ultimately, the founder of their sect, the Najdi Shaykh, shamelessly declared that all the Ulama who had passed over the past 600 years were kafir (as Hazrat Allama Sayyidi Ahmad bin Zain Dahlan Makki <sup>قدس سرّه</sup> has mentioned in Ad-Durrus Sunniyah).

Where is the opportunity to show the Ahadees! After all, all the books of Ahadees — Sahih, Sunan, Masanid, Mua'ajim, and others — were compiled after the era of Huzoor <sup>عليه السلام</sup>. So according to their logic, all the compilers — ma'az Allah — are innovators and people of innovation. As for the "Verse" — the Lord of Honor, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala, has invited (the believers) in an absolute, unrestricted manner, with specific wording, expression, time, and count, to send Durood and Salam upon His Habeeb <sup>عليه السلام</sup>:

Translation: "O you who believe! Send abundant blessings upon him and greet him with peace." (`al-Ahzab: 56`)

So Dalailul Khairat, Duroode Taj, and similar works — all fall within the scope of this general command. They too appear acceptable to them, because in these books and forms there are the exalted attributes and praises of Huzoor Daafeul Bala <sup>عليه السلام</sup>.
