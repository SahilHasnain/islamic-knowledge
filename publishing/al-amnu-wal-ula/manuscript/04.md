## The Awliya Exercise Authority and Resolve Difficulties Even After Passing from This World

Maulana Shah Abdul Azeez Saheb writes in "Tafseere Azeezi" about the state of the great Awliya after their passing:

"The Awliya of Allah exercise authority in this world even after their passing. Their state of absorption and the exaltation of their ranks do not diminish their attention in this direction. They manifest the perfections of their inner states, and the needy people seek the resolution of their difficulties and the fulfillment of their needs from them alone, and they achieve their objectives." (*Tafseere Azeezi, Paara 30, pg. 113*)

(*Pay attention — the authority of the Awliya in this world after their passing, and how different this is from the resolution of difficulties and the warding off of calamities.*)
