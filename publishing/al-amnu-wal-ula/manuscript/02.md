## According to the Wahabi, There Should Be Less Praise of Huzoor

Their Imamut Taifa (Ismail Dehelvi) has ruled: "Even in a praise that is like a human's praise, exercise restraint." (*Taqwiyatul Imaan*) Besides this, in the Wazifa-e-Durood, one must take the blessed name hundreds of times. And their own Imam has written that taking the name is shirk. Now, should one follow the explicit statement of one's own Imam, or the command of your Lord? Yes, if you show the works of Imamut Taifa himself and his ancestors and elders, perhaps some understanding may follow — because if one says something against Imamut Taifa, one's faith is ruined; and if one remains silent before his elders, how can that settle the matter? In such a place, the rhyme of ill-omen is tight.

## According to the Wahabis, Shah Abdul Azeez and Shah Waliullah Dehelvi Were Innovators!

For example, first ask: O people! Simply on the charge that the scholars of the Deen, the compilers of books — may Allah have mercy on them — were not from the era of the Blessed Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, will their books be called innovation and they themselves declared as people of innovation — ma'az Allah? Or does this verdict apply equally to Imamut Taifa and his maternal uncle, the father of Shari'ah (i.e., the father in the knowledge of Shari'ah) and the grandfather of Tareeqat (the grandfather in the path), Janab Maulana Shah Abdul Azeez, and his grandfather in lineage and grandfather in Shari'ah, and the great-grandfather in Tareeqat, Shah Waliullah Dehelvi, and further his grandfather in lineage and teacher, and the grandfather of grandfathers in allegiance, Shah Abdur Raheem, and other elders of the Dehli household?

Were these reverend scholars from the blessed era (the era of the Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup>)? Were their books compiled at that very time? Did they write Durood in their books' introductions in dozens of different forms, all of which — exactly as they are — are established from Huzoor Daafeul Bala <sup>عليه السلام</sup>? If they are, then point them out. And if not, then is it not brazenness and tyranny that their books be called innovation and they be called innovators? Has this legislative command also come down in the Batinism of Ismaili:

"For your ancestors it is permissible, but not for others."

Their own Imam has clearly written that even upon some non-prophets (among whom he has included his own spiritual master and great-grandfather), the Batin revelation comes — which contains legislative commands. They are at once the feet of the prophets and the investigators themselves. They are the students of the prophets, and we are the teachers of the prophets. They are, like the prophets, infallible.

(*See Sirat-e-Mustaqeem, pg. 38*)

Misguidance and evil religion have blackened their faces — is prophethood the name of some tree? Allah's Majesty — these are the very people who openly make their teachers and spiritual masters into prophets! And the Imam and Aimma of the Shari'ah...

And the Ulama of Ahlus-Sunnah are blamed for this crime — that they increased in the forms of Durood — ma'az Allah, innovators are defamed!

**Secondly:** Is this threatening verdict only for sending Durood upon Huzoor Daafeul Bala <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, or does it also apply to the inventions of the household of Imamut Taifa? Shah Saheb's "Qaulul Jameel" — of which the guarantor is the guarantor — in this very "Qaulul Jameel" has written clearly regarding the etiquette of his spiritual masters and the practices of devotion:

"Our companionship and practice are indeed connected to the Prophet <sup>عليه السلام</sup> — even though these etiquettes and practices are not the Sunnah." (*Qaulul Jameel*)

Shah Abdul Azeez Saheb states in the Hashiya:

"In the same way, the leaders of the path have instituted gatherings and assemblies through specific remembrances."

(*Maulvi Khurram Ali, who wrote "Naseehatul Muslimeen," has quoted this statement of Shah Saheb in the translation of Qaulul Jameel, "Shifaul Aleel":*)

"Meaning, such matters should not be considered contrary to the Shari'ah or as falling within evil innovation, as some of limited understanding suppose."

## Consider the Ruling of "Tasawwure Shaykh"!

And listen — in this same Qaulul Jameel, the practice of Tasawwure Shaykh (visualization of the spiritual master) is described among the practices of the Naqshbandi Mashayikh:

"When the Shaykh is absent, one should visualize his form before oneself with love and reverence, and the benefit that his love used to give — now this form will give." (pg. 97)

In Shifaul Aleel, it is quoted from Maulana Shah Abdul Azeez Saheb:

"The truth is that this path is closer than all other paths." (pg. 95)

## The Command to Observe Wazaif (Prescribed Remembrances)

In Maktoobat of Mirza Mazhar Sahib-e-Jaane-Jaan (whom Shah Waliullah Saheb writes in his Maktoobat as "Nafs-e-Zakiyyah, Qayyim Tariqa Ahmadiyah, Da'iye Sunnate Nabawiyyah"):

"'Dua-e-Hizbul Bahr' — the morning and evening Wazifa, and the blessed Khatm of the Khawajgaan — should be read daily for the solution of difficulties." (*Maktoobat, pg. 74*)

Pay attention to the words "morning and evening" and "daily" — it is the same insistence and continuity that the people of the school consider objectionable. These are the very callers whose own Sunnah has declared innovation and given the ruling of innovation. Furthermore, regarding this Mujaddidi Khatm, the same Maktoobat state:

"After this, make the morning gathering obligatory." (pg. 41)

And in the same:

"After this, the morning gathering should be observed." (pg. 92)

## Imamut Taifa Himself Being an Innovator

Forget everything else — Imamut Taifa himself writes in "Sirat-e-Mustaqeem":

"The practices appropriate for every time and the devotions in accordance with every era are different. This is the reason that the investigators of every path among the elders have tried to change the practices and actions — for various reasons, depending on what benefit they saw or what the circumstances demanded. Therefore, a chapter of this book has been designated for such new practices that were initiated according to the requirements of their respective times." (pg. 8)

Allah be praised! Justice — why were these people not called innovators? And what about Tasawwure Shaykh, which Janab Shah Marhoom (the late Shah) describes as closer than all other paths? Is this faith built upon the idol of Taqwiyatul Imaan, or are the Aimma of the Batin Shari'ah of Ismaili exempted?
