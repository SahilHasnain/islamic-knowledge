# Armaghan-e-Hijaz English Translation Style Guide

## Scope

- This is a complete poetic English translation, not a summary, commentary, or adaptation.
- Translate from the Urdu semantic source, using the supplied transliteration for traceability and pronunciation checks.
- Preserve the exact JSON order, source slugs, titles, URLs, stanza sequence, and entry boundaries.
- Preserve every actual poetic line, stanza, speaker change, dialogue turn, question, repetition, image, and philosophical movement.
- Preserve structural labels and speaker labels where present. Labels are metadata or stage directions, not ordinary poetic lines.
- Do not split a kalam or stanza between manuscript files.

## Language and Adab

- Use literary, rhythmic English while retaining the source's meaning, force, irony, imagery, and philosophical motion.
- Do not add explanation inside poem text. Necessary translation decisions belong in the editorial notes.
- Preserve `ﷺ` only when the source refers to Muhammad ﷺ. Omit non-Prophet and Latin honorifics.
- Use `Loulak`, never `La-Law-Laka`.
- Complete an abbreviated Shahadah as “there is no god but Allah”.
- Do not use Urdu or Devanagari in poem text, HTML, or translator commentary.
- Do not romanize Arabic quotations that are explicitly retained; where the English translation renders them, preserve their religious meaning and register.

## Structure and Traceability

- Each entry records the source index, kalam number, exact English-facing source title, slug, URL, and source line counts.
- Do not trust parenthetical or bracketed English titles embedded in `title_roman`; they are reference material only. Derive each English title from the Urdu title and the poem's complete meaning, then record the source title separately.
- Source line counts count nonblank source lines, including labels, while stanza blank lines are structural separators.
- English heading and label lines are counted separately from poetic lines in the batch register.
- Preserve quotations, parenthetical directions, and section markers in their source positions.
