# Editorial Decisions

- Parenthetical or bracketed English wording embedded in `title_roman` is not authoritative. English titles are derived from the Urdu title and the complete poem, while the source title is recorded separately.

- The English title field uses each source record's exact `title_roman` value, including source punctuation, spacing, capitalization, and parenthetical English glosses.
- Source slugs, URLs, and JSON order are copied exactly.
- Speaker names are rendered as English labels where the source gives a label; labels and stage directions are not counted as ordinary poetic lines.
- The first Kalam 001 label is rendered as “Iblees (to his counsellors)”; its source Urdu includes the parenthetical direction while the source transliteration does not, creating the documented one-line count difference.
- Kalam 004 retains the source's speaker labels and the parenthetical direction “(to its corpse)” as a separate structural line; the source Urdu/transliteration counts differ because the transliteration combines English glosses and label formatting.
- The Persian quotation in Kalam 002 is translated in English rather than left as Urdu or transliteration.
- `Loulak` is the approved spelling wherever that term appears in later work.
- Abbreviated Shahadah references are completed as “there is no god but Allah”.
- Only Muhammad receives `ﷺ`; non-Prophet and Latin honorifics are omitted.
