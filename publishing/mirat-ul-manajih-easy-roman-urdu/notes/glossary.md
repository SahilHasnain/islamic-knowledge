# Glossary

- `Easy Roman Urdu`: Saaf aur aasaan Roman Urdu; ma'ni aur daleel kam nahi ki jayegi.
- `Niyyat`: Dil ka irada; is book ke context mein Allah aur Rasool ko raazi karne ka ikhlaas bhi.
- `Hijrat`: Allah ki raza ke liye watan chhorna; gunah chhorna bhi lughwi hijrat hai.
- `Shoba`: Shaakh; hadith mein imani khaslat.
- `Ihsan`: Ikhlaas ke saath ibadat karna aur Allah ki nigrani ka yaqeen rakhna.
- `Raza bil-qaza`: Allah ke faisle par raazi rehna.
- `Ummat-e-da'wat`: Tamam insaan jin tak Nabi ﷺ ki da'wat pahunchti hai.
- `Amaal-e-ghair maqsooda`: Woh kaam jo asal maqsad ke taur par ibadat nahi, magar saaf niyyat se sawab ka sabab bante hain.
- `Emigration`: Manuscript wording uses `hijrat`, not an English replacement, because it is an established Islamic term.
