## Hadith 182

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=182`; `source_id=414`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ  قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : " نَزَلَ الْقُرْآنُ عَلَى خَمْسَةِ أَوْجُهٍ: حَلَالٍ وَحَرَامٍ وَمُحْكَمٍ وَمُتَشَابِهٍ وَأَمْثَالٍ. فَأَحِلُّوا الْحَلَالَ وَحَرِّمُوا الْحَرَامَ وَاعْمَلُوا بِالْمُحْكَمِ وَآمِنُوا بِالْمُتَشَابِهِ وَاعْتَبِرُوا بِالْأَمْثَالِ".هَذَا لَفْظُ الْمَصَابِيْحِ. وَرَواہُ الْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي شُعَبِ الْاِيْمَانِ وَلَفْظُهٗ: «فَاعْمَلُوا بِالْحَلَالِ وَاجْتَنِبُوا الْحَرَامَ وَاتَّبِعُوا الْمُحْكَمَ»

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Hurairah se. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke Quran paanch qisam par utra: halaal, haraam, muhkam aur mutashabih **1** aur misaalen **2**. Is liye halaal ko halaal jaano, haraam ko haraam maano, muhkam par amal karo, mutashabih par iman lao **3** aur misaalon se ibrat pakdo **4**. Yeh Masabih ke alfaaz hain. Baihaqi ne Shu'ab-ul-Iman mein riwayat kiya, jis ke alfaaz yeh hain: Halaal par amal karo, haraam se bacho aur muhkam ki pairwi karo.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** In ka zikr ijmali taur par farmaya gaya hai. Jaise "اُحِلَّ لَکُمُ الطَّیِّبٰتُ" ya "وَیُحَرِّمُ عَلَیۡہِمُ الْخَبٰٓئِثَ". In dono aayaton mein ijmali taur par tamam halaal aur haraam ka zikr aa gaya hai.

**2.** Muhkam ke istilahi ma'ni na-mansookh aayaten hain, lekin yahan khuli aur wazeh aayaten murad hain, kyunki is ke muqable mein mutashabih farmaya gaya hai. **متشابہ** woh aayaten hain jin ke ma'ni ya murad samajh mein na aa sake. **امثال** se pichhli ummaton ke qisse ya misaalen murad hain.

**3.** Yani mutashabih ki jo bhi murad hai woh haq hai, agarche hamein us par ittila nahin.

**4.** Pichhli qaumon par jin wajuh se azaab aaye, tum unhen chhor do. Is se qiyas-e-shar'i ka saboot hua.

## Hadith 183

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=183`; `source_id=415`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ الله صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : " الْأَمْرُ ثَلَاثَةٌ: أَمْرٌ بَيِّنٌ رُشْدُهُ فَاتَّبِعْهُ وَأَمْرٌ بَيِّنٌ غَيُّهُ فَاجْتَنِبْهُ وَأَمْرٌ اخْتُلِفَ فِيهِ فَكِلْهُ إِلَى اللهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ)رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ibn Abbas se. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke cheezen teen tarah ki hain: Ek woh jis ka hidayat hona zahir hai, us ki pairwi karo. Ek woh jis ka gumrahi hona zahir hai, us se bacho. Ek woh jis mein ikhtilaf hai, use Allah عزوجل ke hawale karo **1**. (Ahmad)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani ahkaam-e-shar'iyyah teen tarah ke hain. Baaz yaqini taur par achche hain, jaise roza aur namaz. Baaz yaqini taur par bure hain, jaise Ahle Kitaab ke melon aur thelon mein jaana aur un se mel-jol rakhna. Aur baaz aise hain jo ek pehlu se achche aur ek pehlu se bure maloom hote hain. Misal ke taur par woh cheezen jin ke halaal ya haraam hone ki daleelen maujood hain, jaise gadhe ka jhootha paani jise Shariat mein mashkook kaha gaya hai, ya Qayamat ke din ka ta'ayyun aur kafiron ke bachchon ka hukm waghera. Chahiye yeh ke halaal par be-dhadak amal kare, haraam se zaroor bache aur mushtabihat se ehtiyaat kare. Is hadith ka matlab yeh nahin ke ek halaal cheez ko koi shakhs apni raaye se haraam keh de to woh cheez mushtabah ban jaayegi. Tamam Musalman Milad aur Urs waghera ko halaal jaanein aur ek aadmi use haraam jaane to yeh cheezen mushtabah nahin hongi, balki baghair daleel haraam kehne wale ka qawl radd hoga.

## Hadith 184

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=184`; `source_id=416`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

عَنْ مُعَاذِ بْنِ جَبَلٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ ذِئْبُ الْإِنْسَانِ كَذِئْبِ الْغَنَمِ يَأْخُذُ الشَّاذَّةَ وَالْقَاصِيَةَ وَالنَّاحِيَةَ وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَالشِّعَابَ وَعَلَيْكُمْ بِالْجَمَاعَةِ وَالْعَامَّةِ» . رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Mu'az bin Jabal se. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke Shaitan aadmi ka bheeriya hai, jaise bakriyon ka bheeriya alag, door aur kinare wali bakri ko pakadta hai **1**. Tum ghaatiyon se bacho **2** aur Jamaat-e-Muslimeen aur awaam ko lazim pakdo **3**. (Ahmad)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** **شاذّہ** woh bakri hai jo apni jins wali bakriyon se nafrat kare aur galle se door rahe. **قاصیہ** woh hai jo nafrat to na kare lekin charne ke liye rewar se alag ho jaaye. **ناحیہ** woh hai jo rewar se alag na ho lekin kinare kinare chale. Tashbeeh ka khulasa yeh hai ke duniya ek jungle hai jismein hum bakriyon ki tarah hain. Shaitan bheeriya hai jo har waqt hamari taak mein hai. Jo Jamaat-e-Muslimeen se alag raha woh Shaitan ka shikar ho gaya.

**2.** **شِعَاب**، **شُعْبَةٌ** ki jama hai. Do pahadon ke darmiyan tang raaste ko shoba kehte hain. Wahan keeron, makoron, dakuon, choron balki jinnat ka bhi khatra rehta hai. Yahan Musalmanon ke woh firqe murad hain jo Ahl-e-Sunnat wal-Jamaat ke khilaaf hain.

**3.** Yani woh aqeede ikhtiyar kar lo jo **عامۃ المسلمین** ke hon, kyunki isi Jamaat mein Allah ke Wali bhi hain. Jhooti jamaaton aur firqon se alag raho. Is ki tafseer pichhli hadith mein guzar chuki hai ke bade giroh ki pairwi karo, aur woh hadith bhi ke jise Musalman achcha samjhein woh Allah ke nazdeek bhi achcha hai. **الحمدﷲ!** Hamesha se Ahl-e-Sunnat ki aksariyat rahi hai aur hai. Aam Musalman muqallid hain, buzurgon ke mu'taqid hain aur Milad Shareef aur Fatiha ko achcha jaante hain. In ke ilawa saari jamaaten mil kar bhi Ahl-e-Sunnat ki aadhi nahin, is liye Ahl-e-Sunnat hi barhaq hain. Jo in se hata woh Shaitan ka shikar hoga. Is ki tafseer pehle bhi guzar chuki hai.

## Hadith 185

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «مَنْ فَارَقَ الْجَمَاعَةَ شِبْرًا فَقَدْ خَلَعَ رَبْقَةَ الْإِسْلَامِ مِنْ عُنُقِهٖ» . رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَأَبُو دَاوُدَ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Zar se. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne: Jo Jamaat se ek baalisht bhi bichhda, us ne Islam ki rassi apni gardan se utaar di **1**. (Ahmad aur Abu Dawood)

Bakri wahi mehfooz rehti hai jo mekh se bandhi rahe. Maalik ki qaid se azaad ho jaana bakri ki halaakat hai. Musalmanon ki Jamaat Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki rassi hai, jis mein har Sunni bandha hua hai. Yeh na samjho ke sirf farz ka inkar khatarnaak hai; kabhi mustahabbat ka inkar bhi halaakat ka sabab ban jaata hai. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Salam ne sirf oont ke gosht se bachna chaha tha ke Rab ne farmaya: "یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا ادْخُلُوْا فِی السِّلْمِ كَآفَّةً۪ وَ لَا تَتَّبِعُوْا خُطُوٰتِ الشَّیْطٰنِ".

## Hadith 186

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=186`; `source_id=425`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْْْ مَالِكِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ مُرْسَلًا قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : « " تَرَكْتُ فِيكُمْ أَمْرَيْنِ لَنْ تَضِلُّوا مَا تَمَسَّكْتُمْ بِهِمَا: كِتَابَ الله وَسُنَّةَ رَسُولِهٖ». رَوَاهُ فِي الْمُوَطَّأ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Malik bin Anas se mursal **1**. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke main ne tum mein do cheezen chhodi hain: jab tak tum un dono ko mazbooti se thaame rahoge, gumrah nahin hoge: Allah ki Kitaab aur us ke Rasool ﷺ ki Sunnat **2**. Yeh riwayat Muwatta mein hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Muhaddiseen ke nazdeek mursal woh hadith hai jis mein Sahabi ka zikr na ho aur Taabi'i yeh keh dein ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya. Lekin fuqaha ke nazdeek woh hadith bhi mursal hai jis mein Taabi'i aur Sahabi dono chhoot gaye hon aur Taba-Taabi'i yeh kah dein ke Huzoor ﷺ ne yeh farmaya. Yahan fiqhi mursal murad hai, kyunki Imam Malik Taabi'i nahin, Taba-Taabi'i hain; woh farmate hain ke Huzoor ﷺ ne irshad farmaya.

**2.** Allah ki Kitaab se Quran-e-Kareem ki ghair-mansookh aayaten murad hain. Sunnat se woh ahadith murad hain jo Ummat ke liye qaabil-e-amal hain. Mansookh aayaten aur ahadith, aur isi tarah Huzoor ﷺ ke khaasais par amal mumkin nahin. Is hadith se maloom hua ke deen ke asli usool Quran aur Sunnat hain. Huzoor ﷺ ke zamane mein ijma mumkin nahin tha, aur mujtahideen ka qiyas Kitaab-o-Sunnat se mila hua hai: agar aayat par qiyas ho to woh Quran se mila hua hai aur agar Sunnat par ho to Sunnat se mila hua hai. Is liye yahan in dono ka alag zikr nahin kiya gaya. Imam ki taqleed Kitaab-o-Sunnat samajhne ke liye hai, unhen chhorne ke liye nahin. Is liye yeh hadith ghair-muqallidon ki daleel nahin ban sakti. Jab woh hazraat hadith samajhne ke liye ilm-e-sarf-o-nahw, lughat aur adab se madad lete hain to agar hum bhi is ke liye fiqh se madad lein to kya harj hai? Is ki poori bahs hamari kitaab "Jaa-ul-Haq", hissa Awwal mein dekho.

## Hadith 187

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=187`; `source_id=426`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ غُضَيْفِ بْنِ الْحَارِثِ اَلثَّمَالِيِّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  :(مَا أَحْدَثَ قَوْمٌ بِدْعَةً إِلَّا رُفِعَ مِثْلُهَا مِنَ السُّنَّةِ فَتَمَسُّكٌ بِسُنَّةٍ خَيْرٌ مِنْ إِحْدَاثِ بِدْعَةٍ)رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat Hazrat Ghuzaif bin Haris Samali **1** se hai. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke koi qaum koi bid'at ijad nahin karti magar utni hi Sunnat utha li jaati hai **2**. Is liye Sunnat ko thaamna bid'at ijad karne se behtar hai **3**. (Ahmad)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ke Sahabi hone mein ikhtilaf hai. Ibn Hibban ne "Kitaab-us-Siqaat" mein likha hai ke Ghuzaif farmate hain: Huzoor ﷺ ke zamane mein paida hua aur bachpan mein Aap ﷺ se musafaha aur bai'at ki. Agar yeh riwayat sahih hai to aap Sahabi hain. Samala qabeela Bani Azd ki ek shaakh hai, jis se aap ka ta'alluq tha; isi liye Samali kahe jaate hain.

**2.** Yeh hadith un tamam ahadith ki tafseer hai jin mein bid'at ki buraiyan aayi hain. Yani buri bid'at wahi amal hai jo Sunnat ke khilaaf ijad kiya jaaye aur jis par amal karne se Sunnat chhoot jaaye. Misal ke taur par Arabic mein khutba aur azaan dena Sunnat hai. Ab Urdu mein azaan dena is Sunnat ko mita dega, kyunki Urdu mein azaan dene wala Arabic mein azaan nahin de saka. Isi tarah sar dhak kar bait-ul-khala jaana Sunnat hai; jo shakhs nange sar bait-ul-khala jaaye woh is Sunnat par amal na kar saka. Har buri bid'at ka yahi haal hai: chhoti bid'at chhoti Sunnat ko mita degi aur badi bid'at badi Sunnat ko. "مثلها" se yahi murad hai. Bid'at-e-hasana Sunnat ko nahin mitati, balki kabhi Sunnat ko raaij karti hai. Dekho, ilm-e-deen sikhana Sunnat hai. Ab is ke liye kitaben chhapna, madrasa banana, wahan taleem ka nisaab aur course banana, agarche bid'aten hain, magar Sunnat ki madadgar hain, mukhalif nahin. Buzurgon ki yaadgaren qaayam karna Sunnat hai. Ab is ke liye Milad Shareef ki mehfilen aur Urs ki majlisen qaayam karna us ki madadgar hain, mukhalif nahin. Isi jagah Mirqaat ne farmaya hai ke bid'at-e-hasana Sunnat se mili hui hai.

**3.** Yahan khair shar ke muqable mein hai. Yani buri bid'aten ijad karna bura hai aur un ke muqable mein Sunnat par amal karna achcha hai, kyunki Sunnat mein roshni hai aur buri bid'at mein tareeki. Is ka matlab yeh nahin ke buri bid'aten bhi theek hain aur Sunnaten achchi.

## Hadith 188

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=188`; `source_id=427`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ حَسَّانَ قَالَ: «مَا ابْتَدَعَ قَوْمٌ بِدْعَةً فِي دِينِهِمْ إِلَّا نَزَعَ اللّٰهُ  مِنْ سُنَّتِهِمْ مِثْلَهَا ثُمَّ لَا يُعِيْدُهَا إِلَيْهِمْ إِلٰی يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ.» رَوَاهُ الدَّارمِيُّ "

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Hassan se **1**. Farmaya: koi qaum apne deen mein bid'at ijad nahin karti magar Allah Ta'ala un ki Sunnat mein se utni hi Sunnat utha leta hai **2**, phir use Qayamat tak un mein wapas nahin karta **3**. (Darimi)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam shareef Hassan bin Sabit aur kuniyat Abul-Waleed thi. Aap Ansari aur Khazraji the, Arab ke shu'ara ke taj aur Huzoor ﷺ ke mehboob shair the. Aap Mustafa ﷺ ki madh aur naat padhte the. Huzoor ﷺ aap ke liye apni Masjid mein mimbar bichhwate the. Aap us par khade ho kar ishare karte hue Huzoor ﷺ ke naatiya qaseede padhte the. Aap ki umr ek sau bees saal hui, jin mein se saath saal kufr mein aur phir saath saal Islam mein guzare. Khilafat-e-Haidari mein 40 Hijri se kuch pehle aap ka inteqal hua, رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ. InshaAllah Qayamat tak saare naat-go aur naat-khwan Hazrat Hassan ke jhande tale honge: "یَوْمَ نَدْعُوۡا کُلَّ اُنَاسٍۭ بِاِمٰمِہِمْ".

**2.** Is ki sharh abhi guzar chuki hai. Deen ki qaid se maloom hua ke bid'at-e-saiyyah hamesha deen hi mein hogi; dunyawi ijad ko bid'at-e-saiyyah nahin kaha jaayega. Bid'at ki jitni buraiyan bayan hui hain woh sab us bid'at ke baare mein hain jo deen mein ho aur Sunnat ko mita dene wali ho. Aur agar deen se murad aqeede hain, jaisa ke zahir hai, to hadith bilkul saaf hai.

**3.** Yani jis qaum mein buri bid'aton ki aadat pad gayi, phir use Sunnat ki taraf lautne ki taufeeq nahin milti. Sunnat darakht hai aur yeh bid'aten us ka phawda (shovel) hain. Jab darakht jad se ukhaad diya jaaye to phir nahin lagta.

## Hadith 189

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=189`; `source_id=428`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ إِبْرَاهِيْمَ ابْنِ مَيْسَرَةِ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «مَنْ وَقَّرَ صَاحِبَ بِدْعَةٍ فَقَدْ أَعَانَ عَلٰی هَدْمِ الْإِسْلَامِ» . رَوَاهُ الْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي شُعَبِ الْاِيْمَانِ مُرْسَلًا

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ibrahim bin Maysara se **1**. Farmaya Rasool Allah ﷺ ne ke jis ne bid'ati ki ta'zeem ki, yaqeenan us ne Islam dhaane mein madad di **2**. Ise Baihaqi ne "Shu'ab-ul-Iman" mein mursal riwayat kiya hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap Taabi'i hain, Taif Shareef ke rehne wale aur muttaqi parhezgar hain. Is liye yeh hadith mursal hai, kyunki is mein Sahabi ka zikr nahin.

Yani be-deen logon ki ta'zeem Islam ko veeran karna hai, kyunki hamari ta'zeem se awam ke dil mein un ki aqeedat paida hogi aur woh un ka shikar ho jaayenge. عَن جَابِرٍ: أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ  عَنْهُ أَتَى رَسُولَ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ   بِنُسْخَةٍ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ فَقَالَ يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ هٰذِهٖ نُسْخَةٌ مِنَ التَّوْرَاةِ فَسَكَتَ فَجَعَلَ يَقْرَأُ وَوَجْهُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ    يَتَغَيَّرُ فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ ثَكِلَتْكَ الثَّوَاكِلُ مَا تَرٰى مَا بِوَجْهِ رَسُولِ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ   فَنَظَرَ عُمَرُ إِلٰی وَجْهِ رَسُولِ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ   فَقَالَ أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ مِنْ غَضَبِ اللهِ وَغَضَبِ رَسُوْلِهٖ رَضِيْنَا  بِاللّٰهِ  رَبًّا وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِيْنًا وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيًّا فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : " وَالَّذِيْ نَفْسُ مُحَمَّدٍ بِيَدِهٖ لَوْ بَدَا لَكُمْ مُوسٰی فَاتَّبَعْتُمُوْهُ وَتَرَكْتُمُوْنِيْ لَضَلَلْتُمْ عَنْ سَوَاءِ السَّبِيلِ وَلَوْ كَانَ حَيًّا وَأَدْرَكَ نُبُوَّتِيْ لَاتَّبَعَنِيْ)رَوَاهُ الدَّارمِيُّ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Jabir se ke Hazrat Umar bin Khattab رضی اللہ عنہ Rasool Allah ﷺ ki khidmat mein Taurat ka ek nuskhah laaye. Arz kiya: Ya Rasool Allah ﷺ! Yeh Taurat ka nuskhah hai. Huzoor ﷺ khamosh rahe **1**. Hazrat Umar use padhne lage aur Rasool Allah ﷺ ka chehra-e-anwar badalne laga. Abu Bakr ne farmaya: Tumhein rone waliyan royen! Kya tum Rasool Allah ﷺ ke chehra-e-anwar ka haal nahin dekhte **2**? Hazrat Umar ne Huzoor ﷺ ke chehra-e-anwar ko dekha aur arz ki: Main Allah Ta'ala ke ghazab aur Rasool Allah ﷺ ke ghazab se Allah ki panaah maangta hoon. Hum Allah Ta'ala ke Rab hone, Islam ke Deen hone aur Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ ke Nabi hone par raazi hain **3**. Tab Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: Us Zaat ki qasam jis ke qabze mein Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ ki jaan hai! Agar Moosa علیہ السلام ab tumhare saamne zahir ho jaate aur tum un ki pairwi karte aur mujhe chhor dete to seedhe raaste se bhatak jaate **4**. Agar Moosa علیہ السلام zinda hote aur meri Nabuwat paate to meri pairwi hi karte **5**. (Darimi)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yeh khamoshi narazgi ki thi ke Hazrat Umar Yahudiyon ke paas kyun jaate hain aur Taurat mein kya dhoondhte hain. Lekin Hazrat Umar ne samjha ke yeh khamoshi ijazat ki hai, is liye padhna shuru kar diya. Is wajah se Farooq-e-Azam ke is amal par koi aitiraz nahin; ijtihadi khata maaf hai.

**2.** Baat yeh thi ke Hazrat Umar ke paas kagaz tha aur aap use padhne mein mashghool the. Hazrat Siddiq-e-Akbar Huzoor ﷺ ke chehra-e-anwar ko dekh rahe the. Siddiq-e-Akbar ka yeh kalam maut ki dua ke liye nahin tha, balki Arab ke muhaware ke mutabiq ghazab ka izhar tha. Aap ki narazgi is liye thi ke Hazrat Farooq ka yeh amal Huzoor ﷺ ki takleef ka sabab ban raha tha; yeh nafs ke liye nahin, Huzoor ﷺ ke liye thi. Is se yeh sabit nahin hota ke Sahaba aapas mein bughz rakhte the.

**3.** Hazrat Farooq-e-Azam hamesha Huzoor ﷺ ko raazi karne ke liye yahi alfaaz arz karte the. In alfaaz mein apni wafadari ka izhar hai: Hamari yeh khata **معاذ اللّٰه** sar-kashi ki wajah se nahin; hum to purane aastana-bos aur be-zar bande hain.

**4.** Yani tum gumrah ho jaoge. Is se chand masail maloom hue. Pehla yeh ke ab Qayamat tak hidayat Huzoor ﷺ ki pairwi mein munhasir hai. Agar asli Taurat aur Injeel mil jaaye, balki khud Taurat aur Injeel wale Nabi tashreef le aayein, tab bhi hidayat Huzoor ﷺ hi ke paas milegi. Pichhli aasmani kitaaben pehle hidayat thin, ab nahin. Chand, taare aur chiragh raat ko roshni dete hain, din mein nahin. Jawan aadmi ghutti aur maa ke doodh se zinda nahin reh sakta. Doosra yeh ke Quran-o-Sunnat ke siwa doosri kitaabon se hidayat haasil karna aur unhen padhna mana hai. Teesra yeh ke koi shakhs apne iman par bharosa na kare, har kitaab na padhe aur har ek ka wa'iz na sune. Jab Hazrat Umar jaise Sahabi ko Taurat jaisi kitaab padhne se rok diya gaya to hum kis ginti mein hain? Iman ki daulat choraha par na rakho, warna chori ho jaayegi.

**5.** Yani zahiri taur par zinda hote, warna haqeeqat mein to woh zinda hain. (Mirqaat) Kyunki un ka Deen mansookh ho chuka hai. Isi liye Meraj ki raat tamam Ambiya ne hamare Huzoor ﷺ ke Deen ki namaz Huzoor ﷺ ki iqtida mein ada ki. Moosa علیہ السلام Khizr علیہ السلام ke paas pahunch kar Taurat ke ahkaam jaari na farma sake. Agarche Taurat baaqi thi, magar Hazrat Khizr par us ka nifaaz nahin tha.

## Hadith 195

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=195`; `source_id=434`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «كَلَامِيْ لَا يَنْسَخُ كَلَامَ اللهِ وَكَلَامُ اللهِ يَنْسَخُ كَلَامِيْ وَكَلَامُ اللهِ يَنْسَخُ بَعْضَهٗ بَعْضًا»

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai unhi se. Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Mera kalam Allah Ta'ala ke kalam ko mansookh nahin karta **1**, aur Allah Ta'ala ka kalam mere kalam ko mansookh karta hai. Allah Ta'ala ka kalam baaz ko baaz se mansookh karta hai **2**.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani hadith se Quran ki aayat tilawat ke taur par mansookh nahin ho sakti, lekin hukm ke taur par bahut si aayaten ahadith se mansookh hain. Chunanche hadith "لا وصیۃ للوارث" se waris ke liye jaayez wasiyat sabit karne wali aayaten mansookh hain. Isi tarah Huzoor ﷺ ka farman ke Ambiya ki miraas taqseem nahin hoti, Huzoor ﷺ ke haq mein miraas ki aayaton ka nasikh hai. Sajda-e-ta'zeemi ka jaayez hona Quran se sabit hai, magar hadith se mansookh hai. Ya yahan "کَلَامِیْ" se Huzoor ﷺ ke ijtihadat murad hain, yani mera ijtihadi kalam Quran ke hukm ko mansookh nahin karega. Is liye hadith wazeh hai.

**2.** Yaad rahe ke naskh ki chaar sooraten hain: Quran ka Quran se naskh, jaise kafiron par narmi wali aayaten jihad ki aayaton se mansookh hain; hadith ka hadith se naskh, jaise ziyarat-e-quboor pehle hadith ki ro se mana thi, phir hadith hi ne use jaayez kiya. Huzoor ﷺ farmate hain: "اَ لَافَزُوْرُوْھَا"۔ Quran ka hadith se naskh, jaise sajda-e-tahiyyat; aur hadith ka Quran se naskh, jaise Bait-ul-Muqaddas ka qibla hona hadith se tha, phir Quran se mansookh hua jab Rab ne farmaya: "وُجُوۡهکُمْ شَطْرَہٗ"۔ Is ki poori tahqiq hamari "Tafseer Naeemi" para سوم mein dekho.

## Hadith 197

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=197`; `source_id=436`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Imaan ka bayan`; chapter `Quran-o-Sunnat mazbooti se pakarne ka baab`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِيْ ثَعْلَبَةَ الْخُشَنِيِّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «إِنَّ اللهَ فَرَضَ فَرَائِضَ فَلَا تُضَيِّعُوْهَا وَحَرَّمَ حُرُمَاتٍ فَلَا تَنْتَهِكُوْهَا وَحَدَّ حُدُوْدًا فَلَا تَعْتَدُوْهَا وَسَكَتَ عَنْ أَشْيَاءَ مِنْ غَيْرِ نِسْيَانٍ فَلَا تَبْحَثُوْا عَنْهَا» . رَوَى الْأَحَادِيثَ الثَّلَاثَةَ الدَّارَقُطْنِيُّ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Sa'laba Khushani se **1**. Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Allah Ta'ala ne kuch farz laazim farmaye hain, unhen zaya na karo **2**. Kuch haraam cheezon ko haraam farmaya hai, un ki hurmat na todo **3**. Kuch hudood muqarrar farmayi hain, un se aage na badho **4**. Kuch cheezon ke baare mein bhool ki wajah se nahin, balki khamoshi ikhtiyar farmayi hai; un ke baare mein bahs na karo **5**. In teenon ahadith ko Daarqutni ne riwayat kiya hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam Jarsum bin Naashir hai. Aap qabeela Bani Qaza'ah ke khandan Khashan se ta'alluq rakhte the. Aap buland martaba Sahabi the aur Bai'at-e-Rizwan mein haazir hue. Aap ki wajah se aap ki qaum Islam laayi. Aap Shaam mein muqeem rahe. 75 Hijri mein aap ka inteqal hua. Aap se chalis ahadith riwayat ki gayi hain.

**2.** Yani farz aamaal Quran se sabit hon ya hadith se, un par zaroor pabandi karo aur ikhlaas ke saath ada karo. Yaad rahe ke farz woh hai jis ka saboot bhi yaqini ho aur jis ka talab kiya jaana bhi yaqini ho. Is ka taarik faasiq hota hai aur is ka munkir kaafir hota hai.

**3.** Is tarah ke haraam cheez ke qareeb bhi na jao; karna to door ki baat hai.

**4.** Yani halaal aur haraam ki hudood na todo. Namazen paanch farz hain; unhen chaar ya chhe na maano. Maal ki zakaat ka chaliswan hissa farz hai; is se kam ya zyada ko farz na samjho. Chaar auraton tak nikah jaayez hai; paanchwin ko halaal aur chauthi ko haraam na samjho, waghera.

**5.** Yani baaz cheezon ki halaal ya haraam hone ki wazahat Quran ya hadith mein saaf taur par nahin aayi. Un ki bahs mein na pado; woh mubah hain, un par amal karte raho. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: "عَفی اللّٰهُ عَنْہَا"۔ Huzoor ﷺ farmate hain: Jis cheez ke baare mein khamoshi hai woh maaf hai, jaisa ke "کتاب الاطعمہ" mein aayega. (Mirqaat waghera)

## Hadith 198

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=198`; `source_id=443`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «بَلِّغُوْا عَنِّيْ وَلَوْ آيَةً وَحَدِّثُوْا عَنْ بَنِيْ إِسْرَائِيْلَ وَلَا حَرَجَ وَمَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ» . رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr se. Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Meri taraf se logon tak pahuncha do, agarche ek hi aayat ho **1**. Bani Israeel se waqiaat suno aur logon ko bayan karo, is mein koi harj nahin **2**. Jo jaan-boojh kar mujh par jhoot baandhe woh apna thikana Jahannam mein bana le **3**. (Bukhari)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aayat ke lughwi ma'ni alamat aur nishan ke hain. Is lihaz se Huzoor ﷺ ke mu'jizaat, ahadith, ahkaam aur Quran ki aayaten sab aayaten hain. Istilahi taur par Quran ke us jumle ko aayat kehte hain jis ka alag naam na ho; jis mazmoon ka naam ho use Surah kehte hain. Yahan aayat se lughwi ma'ni murad hain. Yani jise koi masla, hadith ya Quran Shareef ki aayat yaad ho, woh use doosre tak pahuncha de. Tableegh sirf ulama par farz nahin; har Musalman apne ilm ke mutabiq muballigh hai. Mumkin hai ke yahan aayat ke istilahi ma'ni murad hon aur aayat ke alfaaz, ma'ni, matlab aur masail sab murad hon. Yani jise ek aayat yaad ho aur us se muta'alliq kuch masail maloom hon, woh logon tak pahuncha de. Tableegh bhi bahut aham ibadat hai.

**2.** Yani Bani Israeel se un ke qisse, khabren aur misaalen suno aur logon ko bayan karo, jab tak woh Islam ke khilaaf na hon. Yaad rahe ke Bani Israeel se khabren lene ki ijazat hai, lekin Taurat aur Injeel ke ahkaam lene ki manaahi hai, kyunki un kitabon ke ahkaam mansookh ho chuke hain, khabren mansookh nahin hui. Is liye yeh hadith Hazrat Umar Farooq ki us riwayat ke khilaaf nahin jismein Huzoor ﷺ ne unhen Taurat padhne se mana farmaya tha; wahan ahkaam liye ja rahe the. Is liye dono ahadith muhkams hain, koi mansookh nahin.

**3.** Yani jhooti ahadith ghadhne wala dozakh mein jaayega. Is se maloom hua ke hadith ghadna gunah-e-kabeera, balki kabhi kufr bhi hai, kyunki is mein jhoot bhi hai aur Deen mein fitna phailana bhi. Baaz jaahil sufiyon ne namaz-e-tahajjud aur Quran ki suraton ki fazeelat ke baare mein kuch ahadith ghadhin; unhen is se ibrat pakadni chahiye. Yaad rahe ke hadith-e-mawzu' yani ghadi hui hadith alag hai aur hadith-e-zaeef alag. Hadith-e-zaeef fazaail-e-a'maal mein qaabil-e-aitibar hai, lekin hadith-e-mawzu' kahin qaabil-e-aitibar nahin. Isi liye muhaddiseen ne khidmat-e-hadith mein apni zindagiyan laga dein. **الحمدﷲ!** In koshishon se mawzu' ahadith alag kar di gayin. Yahan "jaan-boojh kar" ki qaid hai; agar koi be-khabri mein mawzu' hadith bayan kar de to gunahgaar nahin.

Note: Yeh hadith mutawaatir hai. 62 Sahaba se manqool hai, jin mein Ashra-e-Mubashshara bhi hain. Is hadith ke siwa kisi hadith mein Ashra-e-Mubashshara jama nahin hue. (Mirqaat)

## Hadith 200

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ مُعَاوِيَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : «مَنْ يُرِدِ اللّٰهُ  بِهِ خَيْرًا يُفَقِّهْهُ فِي الدِّينِ وَإِنَّمَا أَنَا قَاسِمٌ وَاللهُ يُعْطِيْ»(مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ)

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Muawiyah se **1**. Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Allah Ta'ala jis ke saath bhalaai ka irada farmata hai use Deen ki samajh ata karta hai **2**. Main to taqseem karne wala hoon aur Allah Ta'ala deta hai **3**. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

Aap ki walidah Hind bint Utbah bin Rabee'ah bin Abd Shams bin Abd Manaf thin. Aap Sulah Hudaibiyah ke saal Islam laaye, lekin Fatah-e-Makkah ke din apna Islam zahir kiya. Aap Huzoor ﷺ ke saale aur wahi likhne wale the. Khilafat-e-Farooqi mein Shaam ke haakim bane aur chalis saal wahin haakim rahe. Imam Hasan bin Ali رضی اللہ عنہما ne aap ke haq mein khilafat se dastbardari farmakar sulah kar li. Aap ka inteqal 4 Rajab 60 Hijri ko falij ki bimari se hua. Aap ki umr 78 saal hui. Aap ke paas Huzoor ﷺ ka tehband, chadar-e-mubarak, qamees-e-mubarak aur kuch mubarak baal aur naakhun the. Aap ne wasiyat ki thi ke mujhe isi mubarak libaas mein kafan dena aur mere munh aur naak mein mubarak naakhun aur baal rakh dena. Aap ke poore halaat-e-mubarak hamari kitab "Ameer Muawiyah" mein dekho.

**2.** Yani Allah Ta'ala use deeni ilm, deeni samajh aur danai ata farmata hai. Yaad rahe ke fiqh-e-zaahiri Shariat hai aur fiqh-e-baatini Tariqat aur Haqeeqat hai. Yeh hadith dono ko shaamil hai. Is hadith se do masail sabit hue. Pehla yeh ke Quran-o-Hadith ke tarjume aur alfaaz yaad kar lena ilm-e-Deen nahin; balki unhen samajhna ilm-e-Deen hai. Yahi mushkil hai. Isi liye fuqaha ki taqleed ki jaati hai. Isi wajah se tamam mufassireen aur muhaddiseen Imams-e-Mujtahideen ke muqallid hue aur apni hadith-dani par naaz nahin kiya. Rab farmata hai: "مَنۡ یُّؤْتَ الْحِکْمَۃَ فَقَدْ اُوۡتِیَ خَیْرًا کَثِیْرًا"۔ Wahan hikmat se murad fiqh hi hai. Quran-o-Hadith ke tarjume to Abu Jahl bhi jaanta tha. Doosra yeh ke Quran-o-Hadith ka ilm kamal nahin; balki unhen samajhna kamal hai. Aalim-e-Deen woh hai jis ki zaban par Allah aur Rasool ka farman ho aur dil mein un ka faiz ho. Faiz ke baghair farman bekaar hai, jaise bijli ki power ke baghair fitting bekaar hai.

**3.** Is se maloom hua ke Deen aur duniya ki saari nematen, jaise ilm, iman, maal aur aulaad, Allah Ta'ala deta hai; baantte Huzoor ﷺ hain. Jise jo mila Huzoor ﷺ ke haathon mila, kyunki yahan Allah Ta'ala ki den ke liye koi qaid nahin aur Huzoor ﷺ ki taqseem ke liye bhi koi qaid nahin. Is liye yeh khayal ghalat hai ke Aap ﷺ sirf ilm baantte hain, warna lazim aayega ke Khuda bhi sirf ilm deta hai. Yaad rahe ke Huzoor ﷺ ki den ek jaisi hai, lekin lene walon ke lene mein farq hai. Bijli ki power ek jaisi aati hai, lekin mukhtalif quwwat ke bulb apni quwwat ke mutabiq power kheenchte hain. Phir bulb ka sheesha jaisa ho us ka rang waisa hota hai. Hanafi aur Shaf'ei aise hi Qadri aur Chishti hain; rang mukhtalif hain, magar power sab mein ek hi hai. Ek hi samandar se tamaam darya nikle, lekin raaston ke lihaz se un ke naam alag ho gaye. Isi tarah Qadri, Chishti waghera un seenon ke naam hain jin se yeh faiz aa raha hai.

**2.** Yani jo log kufr ke zamane mein achche akhlaaq aur behtareen sifaat ki wajah se apne qabeele ke sardaar the, jab woh Musalman hokar ilm seekh lein to Musalmanon mein bhi sardaar hi rahenge. Islam se izzat badhti hai, kam nahin hoti. Woh log Islam se pehle kichad mein lipte hue laal the; Musalman hokar ilm wale bane to dhul kar saaf ho gaye. Is se maloom hua ke naye Musalmanon ko haqeer samajhna bahut bura hai. Aur kafiron ka sardaar Musalman hokar Musalmanon ka sardaar hi rahega, use neecha nahin kiya jayega.

## Hadith 202

Lekin doosre ke jaisi nemat apne liye bhi chaahna ghibtah (rashk) hai. Hasad har haal mein haraam hai; ghibtah do jagah jaayez hai. Yahan hasad se ghibtah murad hai.

**2.** Yani woh maal-daar sakhawat wala shakhs jise Allah achche kaamon mein maal kharch karne ki taufeeq de, aur woh ba-faiz Aalim-e-Deen jis ke ilm se log faida uthayen, rashk ke laaiq hain. **سبحان اللّٰه!** Baaz ulama ke ilm aur baaz sakhiyon ke maal se log Qayamat tak faida uthate hain. Allah Ta'ala is faqeer ki is kitab se Musalmanon ko faida pahunchaye. (Aameen)

Neki ki tamanna karne wala, in sha Allah Ta'ala, Qayamat mein nek logon ke saath hoga.

## Hadith 203

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=203`; `source_id=451`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ -رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ- قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "إِذَا مَاتَ الإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلَّا مِنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ إِلَّا مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ، أو عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهٖ، أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُوْا لَهٗ" رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ

(Muslim)

Un aamaal par sawab nahin milta; isi liye murde zindon se sawab pahunchane ki tamanna karte hain, jaisa riwayat mein hai. Sawab zindagi ke aamaal par milta hai.

Sadaqah-e-jaariyah se awqaaf murad hain, jaise masjiden, madaris aur waqf kiye hue baagh jin se log lagataar faida uthate rahen. Ilm se deeni tasaneef aur nek shagird murad hain jin se deeni faiz jaari rahe. Nek aulaad se murad Aalim aur amal karne wala beta hai. Mirqaat ne farmaya ke **يَدْعُوْا** ki qaid targheeb ke liye hai. Yani bete ko chahiye ke baap ko dua-e-khair mein yaad rakhe, hatta ke namaz mein maa-baap ke liye pehle dua kare aur baad mein salaam phere. Lekin agar nek beta dua bhi na kare, tab bhi maa-baap ko sawab milta rahega. Yaad rahe, yeh hadith us hadith ke khilaaf nahin jis mein farmaya gaya hai ke jo Islam mein achcha tareeqa jaari kare use Qayamat tak sawab milta rahega, ya yeh farmaya gaya hai ke namazi ko hamesha sawab milta rehta hai, kyunki woh sab cheezen sadaqah-e-jaariyah ya nafe' ilm mein daakhil hain.

## Hadith 204

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=204`; `source_id=452`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ الله -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "مَنْ نَفَّسَ عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ الدُّنْيَا نَفَّسَ اللّٰهُ  عَنْهُ كُرْبَةً مِن كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَمَنْ يَسَّرَ عَلَى مُعْسِرٍ يَسَّرَ اللّٰهُ  عَلَيهِ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ، وَمَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا سَتَرَهُ اللّٰهُ  فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ، وَاللّٰهُ  فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيْهِ، وَمَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيْهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللّٰهُ  لَهٗ بِهٖ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللهِ يَتْلُوْنَ كِتَابَ اللهِ وَيَتَدَارَسُوْنَهٗ بَيْنَهُمْ إِلَّا نزَلَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِيْنَةُ، وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللّٰهُ  فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ وَمَنْ بَطَّأَ بِهٖ عَمَلُهُ لَمْ يُسْرِعْ بِهٖ نَسَبُهٗ» . رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai unhi se. Farmate hain ke Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Jo kisi Muslim ko duniya ki kisi takleef se nijaat de, Allah Ta'ala Qayamat ke din us ki koi musibat door karega **1**. Jo kisi tang-dast par aasani kare, Allah Ta'ala duniya aur Aakhirat mein us par aasani farmayega **2**. Jo kisi Muslim ki pardah-poshi kare, Allah Ta'ala duniya aur Aakhirat mein us ki pardah-poshi farmayega **3**. Allah Ta'ala bande ki madad mein rehta hai jab tak banda apne bhai ki madad mein rehta hai **4**. Jo ilm ki talaash mein koi raasta tay kare, Allah Ta'ala us ki barkat se us ke liye Jannat ka raasta aasaan kar dega **5**. Koi qaum Allah Ta'ala ke gharon mein se kisi ghar mein Allah ki Kitaab padhne aur aapas mein Quran seekhne-sikhane ke liye jama nahin hoti **6**, magar un par dil ka sukoon utarta hai, unhen rahmat dhaanp leti hai aur farishte unhen gher lete hain **7**. Allah Ta'ala unhen apne paas wale giroh mein yaad farmata hai **8**. Jise us ka amal peechhe kar de, us ka nasab use aage nahin badha sakta **9**. (Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani tum kisi ki fana hone wali musibat door karo, Allah Ta'ala tum se baaqi rehne wali musibat door farmayega. Tum Momin ko duniya ka aarami waqt do, Allah Ta'ala tumhen Aakhirat ka baaqi rehne wala aaraam dega, kyunki ehsaan ka badla ehsaan hi hai. Yeh hadith bahut jaame' hai. Kisi Muslim ke paon se kaanta nikaalna bhi zaya nahin jaata. Hadith ka matlab yeh nahin ke badla sirf Qayamat mein milega; Qayamat mein badla zaroor milega, agarche kabhi duniya mein bhi mil jaata hai.

**2.** Yani jo qarzdaar ko maafi ya mohlat de, ya ghareeb ki ghurbat door kare, to in sha Allah Deen aur duniya mein us ki mushkilen aasaan hongi. Mirqaat mein farmaya gaya ke is hukm mein Momin aur kafir sab shaamil hain. Kafir musibat-zada ki musibat door karne par bhi sawab mil jaata hai. Balki hadith shareef mein hai ke ek badkaar aurat ne pyaase kutte ko paani pilakar us ki jaan bachai to Allah Ta'ala ne isi kaam par use bakhsh diya.

**3.** Ya to is tarah ke nange ko kapde pehna de, ya is tarah ke us ke chhupe hue aib zahir na kare, basharte ke unhen chhupane se Deen ya qaum ka nuqsan na ho. Agar nuqsan ho to zaroor zahir kar de: kafiron ke jasus pakadwaye, posheeda saazish karne walon ke raaz khol de aur zulman qatl ki tadbeer karne ki mazloom ko khabar de. Akhlaaq aur hain, muamlaat aur siyasi masail kuch aur hain.

**4.** Yeh alfaaz bahut jaame' hain. In mein Deen aur duniya ki har qisam ki madad shaamil hai, chahe madad badan se ho, ilm se ho ya maal waghera se.

**5.** Yani jo ilm-e-Deen seekhne ya deeni fatwa haasil karne ke liye Aalim ke ghar jaaye, chahe safar karke ya chand qadam chal kar, Allah Ta'ala us ki barkat se duniya mein Jannat ke kaam aasaan karega, marte waqt iman ata farmayega, qabr aur Hashr ke hisaab mein kamyabi dega aur Pul-Siraat par aasani ata farmayega. Jannat ke raaste mein yeh sab cheezen shaamil hain. Is se maloom hua ke ilm ke liye safar karna bahut sawab hai. Moosa علیہ السلام ilm talab karne ke liye Khizr علیہ السلام ke paas safar karke gaye. Hazrat Jabir ne ek hadith ke liye ek mahine ka safar kiya aur Abdullah bin Qais ke paas pahunche. (Mirqaat)

**6.** Yahan Allah Ta'ala ke ghar se masjiden, deeni madaris aur Sufiya ki khanqahen murad hain jo Allah ke zikr ke liye waqf hoti hain. Yahood-o-Nasaara ke ibadat-khane is mein shaamil nahin, kyunki wahan Musalman ka bila-zaroorat jaana hi mana hai. Dars-e-Quran se Quran Shareef ki tilawat aur tajweed ke ahkaam seekhna murad hai. Is mein sarf, nahw, fiqh, hadith aur tafseer waghera ke dars bhi shaamil hain, jaisa ke Mirqaat waghera mein hai. Isi liye tilawat ke baad dars ka alag zikr farmaya gaya.

**7.** Sakina Allah Ta'ala ki ek makhlooq hai. Us ke utarne se dilon ko sukoon milta hai. Kabhi woh baadal ki soorat mein zahir hoti hai aur dekhi bhi jaati hai. Us ki barkat se dil se ghairullah ka khauf door hota rehta hai. Rahmat se khaalis rahmat murad hai jo zikr karne wale ko zikr ke waqt har taraf se gher leti hai. Farishton se sair karne wale farishte murad hain jo zikr ki majlisen dhoondte phirte hain; warna amal likhne aur hifazat karne wale farishte har waqt insaan ke saath rehte hain. Maqsad yeh hai ke jahan jamaat ke saath Allah ka zikr ho raha ho, wahan yeh teen rahmaten utarti hain. Is se maloom hua ke tanha zikr ke muqable mein jamaat ke saath zikr afzal hai. Jamaat ki namaz ka darja aur bhi zyada hai, kyunki agar ek shakhs ka amal qabool ho jaaye to sab ka qabool ho jaata hai.

**8.** Yani farishton ki jamaat. Is ki sharh woh hadith hai jismein Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya ke jo Rab ko tanha yaad kare, Rab bhi use tanha yaad karta hai, aur jo jamaat mein yaad kare, Rab use farishton mein yaad karta hai. Quran-e-Kareem farmata hai: "فَاذْکُرُوۡنِیۡۤ اَذْکُرْکُمْ"۔ Rab ki is yaad ka asar yeh hota hai ke makhlooq bhi is bande ko yaad karne lagti hai. Buzurgon ke mazaraat par zaaireen ka hujoom aur wahan zikr-e-Allah ki dhoom isi yaad ka natija hai.

**9.** Yani nasab ki sharafat amal ki kami poori nahin kar sakti.

Sher:

Bandah-e-ishq shudi tark-e-nasab kun Jami

Ke darin raah falan ibn-e-falan cheeze nahin

Kya tumhen khabar nahin ke Nooh علیہ السلام ki kashti mein kutte aur billiyan jagah paa gaye, lekin un ke kafir bete Kan'an ke liye jagah na thi? Maqsad yeh hai ke sharif-un-nasab log aamaal se ghafil na hon. Is ka matlab yeh nahin ke nasab ki sharafat koi cheez hi nahin. Is ki tahqiq hamare risale "اَلْکَلَامُ الْقَبُوْلِ فِیْ طَہَارَتِ نَسَبِ الْرَسُوْلِ" mein dekho. Momin ko nasab-e-Rasool zaroor faida dega. Duniya ki tamam auraten Hazrat Fatimah Zahra ke qadmon ki paakiza mitti tak nahin pahunch sakti. Rab ne Bani Israeel se farmaya: "اَنِّیۡ فَضَّلْتُکُمْ عَلَی الْعٰلَمِیۡنَ"۔ Bani Israeel ka tamam jahaan par afzal hona is liye tha ke woh Anbiya ki aulaad the. Is liye yeh hadith kisi aayat ke khilaaf nahin.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ الله -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "إِنَّ أَوَّلَ النَّاسِ يُقْضَى عَلَيْهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ رَجُلٌ اُسْتُشْهِدَ فَأُتِيَ بِهٖ فَعَرَّفَهؔٗ نِعَمَهٗ  فَعَرَفَہَا فَقَالَ: فمَا عَمِلْتَ فِيهَا؟ قَالَ: قَاتَلْتُ فِيْكَ حَتَّى اسْتُشْهِدْتُ، قَالَ: كَذَبْتَ وَلَكِنَّكَ قَاتَلْتَ لَأَنْ يُقَالَ: جَرِيْءٌ، فَقَدْ قِيْلَ، ثُمَّ أُمِرَ بِهٖ فَسُحِبَ عَلٰى وَجهِهٖ حَتّٰى أُلْقِيَ فِي النَّارِ. وَرَجُلٌ تَعَلَّمَ الْعِلْمَ وَعَلَّمَهُ وَقَرَأَ الْقُرْآنَ، فَأُتِيَ بِهٖ فَعَرَّفَهُ نِعَمَهُ فَعَرَفَهَا، قَالَ: فَمَا عَمِلْتَ فِيْهَا؟ قَالَ: تَعَلَّمْتُ الْعِلْمَ وَعَلَّمْتُهٗ وَقَرَأْتُ فِيْكَ الْقُرْآنَ، قَالَ: كَذَبْتَ وَلَكِنَّكَ تَعَلَّمْتَ الْعِلْمَ لِيُقَالَ: إِنَّكَ عَالِمٌ، وَقَرَأْتَ الْقُرْآنَ لِيُقَالَ: هُوَ قَارِئٌ، فَقَدْ قِيْلَ، ثُمَّ أُمِرَ بِهٖ فَسُحِبَ عَلٰى وَجْهِهِ حَتّٰى أُلْقِيَ فِي النَّارِ. وَرَجُلٌ وَسَّعَ اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَأَعْطَاهُ مِنْ أَصْنَافِ الْمَالِ كُلِّهٖ، فَأُتِيَ بِهٖ فَعَرَّفَهٗ نِعَمَهٗ فَعَرَفَهَا، قَالَ: فَمَا عَمِلْتَ فِيهَا؟ قَالَ: مَا تَرَكْتُ مِنْ سَبِيلٍ تُحِبُّ أَنْ يُنْفَقَ فِيهَا إِلَّا أَنْفَقْتُ فِيهَا لَكَ، قَالَ: كَذَبْتَ وَلَكِنَّكَ فَعَلْتَ لِيُقَالَ: هُوَ جَوَادٌ، فَقَدْ قِيلَ، ثُمَّ أُمِرَ بِهٖ فَسُحِبَ بِهٖ عَلٰى وَجْهِهٖ ثُمَّ أُلْقِيَ فِي النَّارِ". رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai unhi se. Farmate hain ke Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Qayamat ke din sab se pehle jis shakhs ka faisla hoga woh ek shaheed hoga **1**. Use laaya jayega, phir Rab use apni nematen yaad dilayega aur woh unhen pehchan lega. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum ne in nematon ke badle kya amal kiya **2**? Woh arz karega: Main ne Teri raza ke liye jihad kiya, yahan tak ke shaheed ho gaya. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum jhoote ho. Tum ne is liye ladaai ki thi ke tumhen bahadur kaha jaaye; tumhen bahadur keh diya gaya **3**. Phir hukm hoga aur use munh ke bal ghaseet kar aag mein daal diya jayega **4**.

Aur ek woh shakhs hoga jis ne ilm seekha, sikhaya aur Quran padha. Use laaya jayega, Allah Ta'ala use apni nematen yaad dilayega aur woh unhen pehchan lega. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum ne in nematon ke badle kya amal kiya **5**? Woh arz karega: Main ne ilm seekha, use sikhaya aur Teri raza ke liye Quran padha. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum jhoote ho. Tum ne ilm is liye seekha tha ke tumhen Aalim kaha jaaye, aur Quran is liye padha tha ke tumhen Qaari kaha jaaye; tumhen aisa keh diya gaya. Phir hukm hoga aur use munh ke bal ghaseet kar aag mein daal diya jayega **6**.

Aur ek woh aadmi hoga jise Allah Ta'ala ne wus'at di aur har qisam ka maal ata farmaya. Use laaya jayega, Allah Ta'ala use apni nematen yaad dilayega aur woh unhen pehchan lega. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum ne in nematon ke badle kya amal kiya **7**? Woh arz karega: Main ne koi aisa raasta nahin chhoda jahan Tere liye kharch karna pasand ho, magar Teri raza ke liye wahan kharch kiya. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Tum jhoote ho. Tum ne yeh sakhawat is liye ki thi ke tumhen sakhi kaha jaaye; tumhen sakhi keh diya gaya. Phir hukm hoga, use munh ke bal ghaseeta jayega aur aag mein phenk diya jayega. (Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yeh awwaliyyat izafi hai, haqiqi nahin. Yani riyaakaron mein sab se pehle shaheed riyaakar ka faisla hoga. Is liye yeh hadith us baat ke khilaaf nahin ke sab se pehle namaz ka hisaab hoga ya zulman qatl ka hisaab pehle hoga. Ibadaton mein namaz ka, muamlaat mein qatl ka aur riya mein aise shaheed ka faisla sab se pehle hoga. Shaheed se woh shakhs murad hai jo Allah ki raah mein maara gaya.

**2.** Yani Allah Ta'ala ne tumhen andarooni aur baahari karodon nematen deen, tum ne kaunsi neki ki? Is se maloom hua ke nekiyan Rab ki nematon ka shukr bhi hain.

**3.** Yani tumhari jihad aur shahadat ka badla yeh ho gaya ke logon ne tumhari wah-wah kar di, kyunki tum ne Islam ki khidmat ke liye nahin, isi niyyat se jihad kiya tha. Is se maloom hua ke agar ghazi mein ikhlaas ho to logon ki wah-wah se sawab kam nahin hota. Woh Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se dunyawi inaam hai. Sahaba-e-Kiraam aur khud Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki dono jahaan mein wah-wah ho rahi hai. Yaad rahe, sirf maal-e-ghaneemat ya mulk haasil karne ke liye jihad karne ka anjaam bhi yahi hai. Jihad sirf Allah aur Rasool ki raza ke liye hona chahiye.

**4.** Yani nihayat zillat ke saath, mare hue kutte ki tarah taang se ghaseet kar Jahannam ke kinare se neeche phenk diya jayega. Jahannam ki gehraai aasman aur zameen ke faasle se karodon guna zyada hai. Allah Ta'ala panah de.

**5.** Tumhari yeh saari mehnat khidmat-e-Deen ke liye nahin thi, balki ilm ke zariye izzat aur maal kamaane ke liye thi. Woh tumhen haasil ho gaye; ab hum se kya chaahte ho? Isi hadith ko dekhte hue baaz ulama ne apni kitabon mein apna naam bhi nahin likha. Aur jin logon ne naam likha, un ka maqsad naamwari nahin, balki logon ki dua haasil karna tha.

**6.** Is se maloom hua ke jis tarah ikhlaas wali neki Jannat paane ka zariya hai, isi tarah riya wali neki Jahannam aur zillat paane ka sabab hai.

**7.** Yahan chaar masail yaad rakhne chahiye. Pehla yeh ke yahan riyaakar shaheed, Aalim aur sakhi hi ka zikr hua, kyunki unhon ne behtareen amal kiye the. Jab yeh amal riya se barbaad ho gaye to doosre aamaal ka kya poochna! Riya wale Hajj, Zakaat aur namaz ka bhi yahi haal hai.

Doosra yeh ke baaz riyaakar woh hain jo sirf riya ke liye nekiyan karte hain; agar un ki tareef na ho to neki karte hi nahin. Baaz woh hain jo riya ke liye achche tareeqe se amal karte hain, lekin tanhaai mein mamooli amal karte hain. Baaz woh hain jo khalwat aur jalwat mein barabar amal karte hain, magar naam-namood se khush hote hain. Yahan pehli qisam ka riyaakar murad hai. Doosri dono qisam ke riyaakaron ko asal neki ka sawab milega, lekin naaqis milega.

Teesra yeh ke is hadith mein Allah Ta'ala ke qanoon aur adl ka zikr hai; fazl alag cheez hai. Rab farmata hai: "فَاُولٰٓئِکَ یُبَدِّلُ اللّٰهُ سَیِّاٰتِہِمْ حَسَنٰتٍ"۔ Is liye yeh hadith maghfirat wali aayaton aur ahadith ke khilaaf nahin.

Sher:

Adl kare to thar-thar kaanpen oonchi shaanon wale
Fazl kare to bakhshe jaanen mujh jaise munh kaale

Chautha yeh ke Momin ki yeh saari sazayen tanhaai mein hongi, elaaniya nahin. Allah Ta'ala use zillat aur ruswaai se bachayega. Zillat aur ruswaai sirf kafiron ke liye hogi, jaisa ke Quran ki aayat se sabit hai. Sufiya farmate hain ke riya ke khauf se amal chhorna nahin chahiye; amal karte raho, kabhi ikhlaas bhi naseeb ho hi jayega. Makhiyon ke dar se khana chhor dena theek nahin.

## Hadith 206

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=206`; `source_id=3769`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "إِنَّ الله لَا يَقْبِضُ الْعِلْمَ انْتِزَاعًا يَنْتَزِعُهٗ مِنَ الْعِبَادِ، وَلَكِنْ يَقْبِضُ الْعِلْمَ بِقَبْضِ الْعُلَمَاءِحَتّٰى إِذَا لَمْ يَبْقِ عَالِمًا اتَّخَذَ النَّاسُ رُؤُوسًا جُهَّالًا، فَسُئِلُوْا فَأَفْتَوْا بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ فَضَلُّوْا وَأَضَلُّوْا". مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar se. Farmate hain ke Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Allah Ta'ala ilm ko is tarah kheench kar nahin uthayega ke bandon se kheench le, balki ulama ke inteqal se ilm uthayega **1**. Yahan tak ke jab koi Aalim baaqi nahin rahega to log jaahilon ko apna peshwa bana lenge. Un se masail pooche jayenge aur woh bina ilm ke fatwe denge. Khud bhi gumrah honge aur doosron ko bhi gumrah karenge **2**. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yeh hadith ka baqi hissa hai. Is mein bataya gaya hai ke Qayamat ke qareeb ilm uth jayega aur jahalat phail jayegi. Yani ilm is tarah nahin uthega ke log padha hua bhool jayenge, balki ulama inteqal karte rahenge aur un ke baad naye ulama paida nahin honge. Jaisa ke aaj ho raha hai: ek badi jamaat Angrezi ilm ke peechhe chal rahi hai aur Deen-e-Rasool Allah yateem hokar reh gaya hai. Yahan ilm se ilm-e-Deen murad hai.

**2.** Peshwa se Qazi, Mufti, Imam aur Sheikh murad hain jin ke zimme deeni kaam hote hain. Maqsad yeh hai ke deeni ohde jaahil log sambhaal lenge, lekin apni jahalat zahir karna pasand nahin karenge. Mas'alah poochne par yeh nahin kahenge ke hamein khabar nahin, balki bina ilm ke baat bana kar ghalat mas'alah batayenge. Is ka anjaam saamne hai: be-ilm hakeem mareez ki jaan le leta hai aur jaahil Mufti aur Khatib iman barbaad kar dete hain.

## Hadith 207

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=207`; `source_id=3785`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ شَقِيقٍ قَالَ: كَانَ عَبْدُ اللهِ بْنُ مَسْعُودٍ يُذَكِّرُ النَّاسَ فِي كُلِّ خَمِيسٍ، فَقَالَ لَهُ رَجُلٌ: يَا أَبَا عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ لَوَدِدْتُ أَنَّكَ ذَكَّرْتَنَا فِي كُلِّ يَوْمٍ، قَالَ: أَمَا إِنَّهُ يَمْنَعُنِيْ مِنْ ذٰلِكَ أَنِّي أَكْرَهُ أَنْ أُمِلَّكُمْ، وَإنِّي أَتَخَوَّلُكُمْ بِالْمَوْعِظَةِ كَمَا كَانَ رَسُولُ الله -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - يَتَخَوَّلُنَا بِهَا مَخَافَةَ السَّآمَةِ عَلَيْنَا. مُتَّفَقٌ عَلَيْهِ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Shaqiq se **1**. Woh farmate hain ke Abdullah bin Mas'ud har Jumerat ko logon ko waaz farmate the **2**. Ek shakhs ne arz kiya: Ae Abu Abdur Rahman! Meri tamanna hai ke aap hamein har din waaz farmaya karein. Aap ne farmaya: Mujhe is se yeh baat rokti hai ke main tumhen uktana pasand nahin karta **3**. Main waaz ke maamle mein tumhara waise hi lihaaz rakhta hoon jaise Huzoor ﷺ hamara lihaaz rakhte the, taake hum par uktahat na ho **4**. (Bukhari, Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam Shaqiq bin Abi Salamah aur kuniyat Abu Wa'il thi. Aap qabeela Bani Asad se the aur azeem martabe ke Taabi'i the. Aap ne Huzoor ﷺ ka zamana paaya, lekin ziyaarat na kar sake. Aap ne bade Sahaba se mulaqat ki. Aap Hazrat Umar رضی اللہ عنہ aur Sayyiduna Ibn Mas'ud رضی اللہ عنہ ke khaas saathiyon mein se the. Aap Hajjaj bin Yusuf ke zamane mein inteqal kar gaye. Allah Ta'ala un sab se raazi ho.

**2.** Is se maloom hua ke nek aamaal ke liye din aur waqt muqarrar karna shirk ya haraam nahin, balki Sunnat-e-Sahaba hai. Isi liye aaj deeni madaris mein imtihaan aur ta'teel ke liye din aur mahine, aur taleem ke liye auqaat muqarrar kiye jaate hain. Is liye Milad Shareef, Fatiha aur Urs waghera ke liye din muqarrar karna jaayez hai; ise haraam kehna ghalati hai. Mirqaat mein yahan farmaya gaya hai ke Hazrat Ibn Mas'ud ne Jumerat ko waaz ke liye is liye chuna ke yeh Juma ka padosi din hai aur is ki barkat Juma tak pahunchti hai. Baaz log har Jumerat ko Milad Shareef aur murdon ki Fatiha karte hain; un ki daleel yahi hadith hai.

**3.** Yani rozana waaz se tum ukta jaoge aur tumhara shauq baaqi nahin rahega. Is se maloom hua ke itna lamba waaz bhi nahin karna chahiye ke log ghabra jaayen, taake ilm aur waaz ki qadr kam na ho.

**4.** Yani Huzoor ﷺ bhi hamein har waqt aur har din waaz nahin sunate the, taake hum na uktaayen. Sufiya-e-Kiraam farmate hain ke jo Aalim ya Sheikh logon ke saamne har waqt Allah Allah hi karta rahe woh makkaar hai. Huzoor ﷺ ki Majlis-e-Paak mein dunyawi baatein bhi hoti thin.

## Hadith 208

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=208`; `source_id=3801`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَنَسٍ قَالَ: "كَانَ النَّبِيُّ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - إِذَا تَكَلَّمَ بِكَلِمَةٍأَعَادَهَا ثَلَاثًا حَتّٰى تُفْهَمَ عَنْهُ، وَإِذَا أَتٰى عَلٰى قَوْمٍ فَسَلَّمَ عَلَيْهِمْ سَلَّمَ عَلَيْهِمْ ثَلَاثًا". رَوَاهُ الْبُخَارِيُّ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Anas se. Woh farmate hain ke Nabi ﷺ jab koi baat farmate to use teen baar dohrate, taake log use achchi tarah samajh lein **1**. Aur jab kisi qaum ke paas tashreef laate aur unhen salaam farmate to teen baar salaam farmate **2**. (Bukhari)

**2.** Ek salaam ijazat haasil karne ke liye, doosra mulaqat ke liye aur teesra rukhsat ke liye hota hai. Is liye yeh hadith us ke khilaaf nahin ke Huzoor ﷺ mulaqat ke waqt ek salaam farmate the, kyunki wahan mulaqat ka salaam murad hai. Is se maloom hua ke ghar mein daakhil hone ki ijazat ke liye shor na machaye aur darwaza bahut na peete, balki sirf yeh kahe: Assalamu alaikum, andar aa jaaun? Yeh bhi maloom hua ke aane wala aur jaane wala salaam kare, chahe bada hi kyun na ho.

## Hadith 209

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=209`; `source_id=3809`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِيْ مَسْعُودٍ الأَنْصَارِيِّ قَالَ: جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - فَقَالَ: إِنَّهُ أُبْدِعَ بِي فَاحْمِلْنِي، فَقَالَ: "مَا عِنْدِي فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ: يَا رَسُولَ الله أَنَا أَدُلُّهٗ عَلٰی مَنْ يَحْمِلُهٗ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ الله صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "مَنْ دَلَّ عَلٰى خَيْرٍ فَلَهُ مِثْلُ أَجْرِ فَاعِلِهٖ" رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Mas'ud Ansari se **1**. Woh farmate hain ke ek shakhs Huzoor ﷺ ki khidmat mein haazir hua aur arz kiya: Mera oont thak raha hai, mujhe sawaari ata farmaiye. Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: Mere paas sawaari nahin **2**. Ek shakhs ne arz kiya: Ya Rasool Allah ﷺ! Main use aise aadmi ka pata batata hoon jo use sawaari de de. Tab Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: Jo kisi bhalaai ka raasta bataye, use bhalaai karne wale ke barabar sawab milta hai **3**. (Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam Uqbah bin Amr aur kuniyat Abu Mas'ud Ansari thi. Aap Badri the, yani Ghazwa-e-Badr mein shareek hue ya is basti mein kuch din rahe. Aap Bai'at-e-Aqabah Saniyah mein bhi shareek the. Aap Kufa mein rahe aur Hazrat Ali Murtaza رضی اللہ عنہ ki khilafat mein inteqal kiya.

**2.** Is se do masail maloom hue. Pehla yeh ke zaroorat ke waqt maangna jaayez hai, khaas taur par Huzoor ﷺ se maangna har shakhs ke liye fakhr hai. Doosra yeh ke jab koi cheez maujood na ho to saail se inkaar karna bukhl nahin. Huzoor ﷺ makhlooq-e-Khuda mein sab se bade sakhi aur data hain, lekin is waqt mana farmana mas'alah samjhane ke liye hai ke qarz lekar sakhawat na karo. Jo riwayat mein hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne kabhi "nahin" nahin farmaya, us ka matlab ya to yeh hai ke maujood cheez se mana nahin farmaya, ya yeh ke yeh nahin farmaya ke tumhen nahin denge. Is liye ahadith mein koi ta'aruz nahin.

**3.** Yani neki karne wala, neki karwane wala, neki batane wala aur mashwara dene wala sab sawab ke mustahiq hain. Is liye tumhen bhi sawab milega.

## Hadith 210

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=210`; `source_id=3817`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Pehli fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ جَرِيرٍ قَالَ: كُنَّا فِي صَدْرِ النَّهَارِ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ ، فَجَاءَهٗ قَوْمٌ عُرَاةٌ مُجْتَابِيْ النِّمَارِأَوِ الْعَبَاءِ مُتَقَلِّدِي السُّيُوْفِ عَامَّتُهُمْ مِنْ مُضَرَ بَلْ كُلُّهُمْ مِنْ مُضَرَ، فَتَمَعَّرَ وَجْهُ رَسُولِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - لِمَا رَأَى بِهِمْ مِنَ الْفَاقَةِ، فَدَخَلَ ثُمَّ خَرَجَ، فَأَمَرَ بِلَالًا فَأَذَّنَ وَأَقَامَ فَصَلَّى،ثُمَّ خَطَبَ فَقَالَ: يَاأَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمُ الَّذِيْ خَلَقَكُمْ مِنْ نَفْسٍ وَاحِدَةٍ   إِلٰی آخَرِ الآيَةِ   إِنَّ اللهَ كَانَ عَلَيْكُمْ رَقِيبًا وَالآيَةُ الَّتِي فِي الْحَشْرِ  اتَّقُوْا اللهَ وَلْتَنْظُرْ نَفْسٌ مَا قَدَّمَتْ لِغَدٍ  ، تَصَدَّقَ رَجُلٌ مِنْ دِينَارِهٖ، مِنْ دِرْهَمِهٖ، مِنْ ثَوْبِهٖ، مِنْ صَاعِ بُرِّهٖ، مِنْ صَاعِ تَمْرِهٖ، حَتّٰى قَالَ: وَلَوْ بِشِقِّ تَمْرَةٍ، قَالَ: فَجَاءَ رَجُلٌ مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ بِصُرَّةٍ كَادَتْ كَفُّهُ یَعْجِزُ عَنْهَا بَلْ قَدْ عَجِزَتْ، ثُمَّ تَتَابَعَ النَّاسُ حَتّٰى رَأَيْتُ كَوْمَينِ مِنْ طَعَامٍ وَثِيَابٍ،حَتَّى رَأَيْتُ وَجْهَ رَسُولِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ   يَتَهَلَّلُ كَأَنَّهُ مُذْهَبَةٌ،فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ : "مَن سَنَّ فِي الإِسْلَامِ سُنَّةً حَسَنَةً فَلَهُ أَجْرُهَا وَأَجْرُ مَنْ عَمِلَ بِهَا مِنْ بَعْدِهٖ مِنْ غَيرِ أَنْ يَنْقُصَ مِنْ أُجُورِهِمْ شَيْءٌ، وَمَنْ سَنَّ فِي الإِسْلَامِ سُنَّةً سَيِّئةً كَانَ عَلَيْهِ وِزْرُهَا وَوِزْرُ مَنْ عَمَلَ بِهَا مِنْ بَعْدِهٖ مِنْ غَيْرِ أَنْ يَنْقُصَ مِنْ أَوْزَارِهِمْ شَيْءٌ" رَوَاهُ مُسْلِمٌ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Jarir se **1**. Woh farmate hain ke hum subah sawere Huzoor ﷺ ke paas haazir the. Aap ﷺ ki khidmat mein ek qaum aayi jo nange badan thi, kambalon mein lipti hui thi aur talwaren gale mein latkaaye hue thi **2**. Un mein aksariyat, balki sab ke sab, qabeela Mudar se the. Un ki faaqah-zadgi dekh kar Huzoor ﷺ ke chehra-e-anwar ka rang badal gaya **3**. Aap ﷺ andar tashreef le gaye, phir baahar aaye. Aap ﷺ ne Hazrat Bilal ko hukm diya; unhon ne azaan aur takbeer kahi, phir Aap ﷺ ne namaz padhi aur khutba diya **4**. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: Ae logo! Apne Rab se daro jis ne tumhen ek jaan se paida kiya, aakhir-e-aayat tak **5**. Aur woh aayat tilawat farmayi jo Surah Hashr mein hai: Allah se daro aur har shakhs dekhe ke us ne kal ke liye kya bheja **6**. Aadmi apne dinar, dirham, kapde, gandum aur jau ke saa' mein se sadaqah kare, hatta ke farmaya: Khajoor ka aadha tukda hi sahi **7**. Hazrat Jarir farmate hain ke ek Ansari shakhs ek theli laaye jis ke bojh se us ka haath thak raha tha, balki thak hi gaya tha **8**. Phir log lagataar sadaqah laane lage, yahan tak ke main ne khane aur kapde ke do dher dekhe **9**. Yahan tak ke main ne Huzoor ﷺ ka chehra-e-anwar dekha jo chamak raha tha, goya sone ki dalee ho **10**. Tab Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Jo Islam mein achcha tareeqa jaari kare, use apne amal ka aur un logon ke amal ka sawab milega jo us ke baad us par amal karen, un ke sawab mein kuch kami kiye baghair **11**. Aur jo Islam mein bura tareeqa jaari kare, us par apni bad-amali ka gunah hoga aur un logon ki bad-amali ka bhi jo us ke baad us par amal karen, un ke gunahon mein kuch kami kiye baghair **12**. (Muslim)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam Jarir bin Abdullah Bajali tha. Aap mashhoor Sahabi aur nihayat haseen aur khush-akhlaq the. Umar Farooq رضی اللہ عنہ aap ko Yusuf علیہ السلام se tashbeeh dete the. Aap Huzoor ﷺ ke wisaal wale saal Islam laaye. Baaz riwayaton mein hai ke wisaal-e-shareef se chaalis din pehle aap ek muddat Kufa ke maqam Qarqisiya mein rahe. Aap ka inteqal 51 Hijri mein hua. Allah Ta'ala aap se raazi ho.

**2.** Yani ghurbat ki wajah se un ke paas ek kambal ke siwa badan dhaampne ke liye koi kapda nahin tha. Is ke bawajood woh ghazwon aur jihad ke shauqeen the, kyunki har ek ke paas talwar thi.

**3.** Yani un ki faqiri se Huzoor ﷺ ke dil ko bahut ranj hua aur us ke asraat chehra-e-anwar par zahir hue. Aisa kyun na hota, Aap ﷺ be-nawa faqeeron ke ghamkhwar hain. Agar Aap ﷺ hum ghareebon ka gham na karen to kaun kare? Sher:

Man az be-nawaai neem rooye zard

Gham-e-be-nawayan rukhsaar-e-zard kard

Yeh aayat "عَزِیۡزٌ عَلَیۡہِ مَاعَنِتُّمْ" ki tafseer hai.

**4.** Yeh waaz logon ko sadaqah dene par ubharne ke liye tha. Us waqt Daulat-Khana-e-Aqdas mein kuch maujood nahin tha.

**5.** Aap ﷺ ne yeh aayat mauqe ke mutabiq tilawat farmayi. Yani saare ameer aur faqeer Adam علیہ السلام ki aulaad hone ki wajah se bhai bhai hain. Ameer ko chahiye ke faqeer ki madad kare. Mirqaat mein is jagah hai ke Hazrat Hawwa رضی اللہ عنہا ke bees baar mein chaalis bachche hue: bees ladke aur bees ladkiyan.

**6.** Yani Qayamat ke liye nek aamaal, khaas taur par sadaqah aur khairaat, kiya karo.

**7.** Kyunki Rab Ta'ala ki bargah mein sadaqah ki miqdaar nahin dekhi jaati, balki dene wale ka ikhlaas dekha jaata hai. Is se maloom hua ke ghareeb aadmi apni zarooriyat mein se kuch sadaqah kare to sawab ka mustahiq hai, basharte ke woh apne bachchon aur haq rakhne walon ka haq na maare aur baad mein khud bhi bheek na maange.

**8.** Yani theli mein itna anaaj tha jo Ansari se sambhala na gaya aur zyada bojh ki wajah se theli haath se gir gayi. Zahir yahi hai ke yeh jau, gandum waghera ki badi theli hogi, jaisa ke agle mazmoon se maloom hota hai ke Bargah-e-Nabawi mein us waqt anaaj aur kapde ke dher lag gaye. Baaz Sharih ne likha hai ke woh himyani thi jo dirham aur dinar se bhari hui thi, lekin yeh zahir ke khilaaf hai. Yaad rahe ke yeh Ansari sab se pehle sadaqah laaye. Phir unhen dekh kar doosre log bhi sadaqah laaye. Isi liye Nabi ﷺ ne aage bayan hone wali tareef farmayi.

**9.** Yeh dher un jawan faqeeron mein taqseem karne ke liye jama hue the. Chunki in masakeen ki poori jamaat thi, is liye itna sadaqah kiya gaya. Is se do masail maloom hue. Pehla yeh ke zaroorat ke waqt chanda karna jaayez hai. Doosra yeh ke doosron ke liye Masjid mein sawaal karna jaayez hai. Jin ahadith mein Masjid mein maangne ki manaahi hai, wahan apne liye maangna murad hai. Is liye yeh hadith un ke khilaaf nahin.

**10.** Faqeeron ki zaroorat poori hone aur Sahaba ke sadaqah dene par khushi ki wajah se maloom hua ke Huzoor ﷺ apni Ummat ki nekiyon par khush hote hain. Jo Allah aur Rasool ko raazi karna chahe, use faqeeron ki zaroorat poori karni chahiye. Yaad rahe ke jis chaandi ke tukde par sone ka mulamma kar diya jaaye, ya jis chamde ya kapde par sone ka kaam kar diya jaaye, use Arabi mein **مذہبّہ** kehte hain. Yahan pehla ma'ni murad hai.

**11.** Yani neki ka tareeqa jaari karne wala un sab logon ke barabar sawab paayega jo us par amal karenge. Is liye jin logon ne ilm-e-fiqh, fan-e-hadith, Milad Shareef, Urs-e-Buzurgan, zikr-e-khair ki majlisen, Islami madaris aur Tareeqat ke silsile jaari kiye, unhen Qayamat tak sawab milta rahega. Yahan Islam mein achchi bid'aten jaari karne ka zikr hai, chhodi hui Sunnaten zinda karne ka nahin, jaisa ke agle muqable se maloom hota hai. Is hadith se bid'at-e-hasanah ke khair hone ka bahut mazboot saboot milta hai.

**12.** Yeh hadith un tamam ahadith ki sharh hai jin mein bid'at ki buraiyan bayan hui hain. Saaf maloom hua ke bid'at-e-saiyyah buri hai aur un ahadith mein yahi murad hai. Yeh hadith bid'at ki do qisam batati hai: bid'at-e-hasanah aur bid'at-e-saiyyah. Is mein kisi qisam ki taweel nahin ho sakti. Un logon par afsos hai jo is hadith se aankhen band karke har bid'at ko bura kehte hain, halanke khud hazaron bid'aten karte hain. Bid'at ki tahqiq aur us ki taqseem pichhle baab mein guzar chuki hai.

## Hadith 211

**2.** Yani Hazrat Muawiyah رضی اللہ عنہ ki hadith "لایزال" waghera is ummat ke sawab ke baab mein, **ان شاء اللّٰه العزیز**, bayan karenge **2**.

## Hadith 212

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=212`; `source_id=3865`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

عَنْ كَثِيرِ ابْنِ قَيْسٍ قَالَ: كنْتُ جَالِسًا مَعَ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ فِي مَسْجِدِ دِمَشْقَ فَجَاءَهُ رَجُلٌ فَقَالَ: يَا أَبَا الدَّرْدَاءِ! إِنِّي جِئْتُكَ مِنْ مَدِينَةِ الرَّسُولِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  لِحَدِيثٍ بَلَغَنِي أَنَّكَ تُحَدِّثُهُ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ ، مَا جِئْتُ لِحَاجَةٍ، قَالَ: فَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  يَقُولُ "مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَطْلُبُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَلَكَ اللّٰهُ  بِهٖ طَرِيقًا مِنْ طُرُقِ الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّ الْمَلَائِكَةَ لَتَضَعُ أَجْنِحَتَهَا رِضًا لِطَالِبِ الْعِلْمِ، وَإِنَّ الْعَالِمَ يَسْتَغْفِرُ لَهٗ مَنْ فِي السَّمَوَات وَمَنْ فِي الأَرْضِ، وَالْحِيْتَانُ فِي جَوْفِ الْمَاءِ، وَإنَّ فَضْلَ الْعَالِمِ عَلَى الْعَابِدِكَفَضْلِ الْقَمَرِ لَيْلَةَ الْبَدْرِ عَلَى سَائِرِ الْكَوَاكِبِ، وَإِنَّ الْعُلَمَاءَ وَرثَةُ الأَنْبِيَاءِ، وَإِنَّ الأَنْبِيَاءَ لَمْ يُوَرِّثُوا دِينَارًا وَلَا دِرْهَمًا، وَإِنَّمَا وَرَّثُوا الْعِلْمَ، فَمَنْ أَخَذَهٗ أَخَذَ بِحَظٍّ وَافِرٍ" رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَالتِّرْمِذِيُّ وَأَبُو داوُدَ وَابْنُ مَاجَهْ وَالدَّارِمِيُّ، وَسَمَّاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ قَيْسَ بْنَ كَثِيرٍ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Kaseer bin Qais se **1**. Woh farmate hain ke main Hazrat Abu Darda رضی اللہ عنہ ke saath Damascus ki masjid mein baitha tha. Un ke paas ek shakhs aaya aur kehne laga: Ae Abu Darda! Main Madinah-e-Rasool ﷺ se aap ke paas ek hadith sunne ke liye aaya hoon. Mujhe khabar mili hai ke aap Rasool Allah ﷺ se woh riwayat bayan karte hain **2**. Main is ke siwa kisi aur kaam ke liye nahin aaya **3**. Aap ne farmaya: Main ne Rasool Allah ﷺ ko farmate hue suna: Jo shakhs ilm ki talab mein koi raasta tay kare, Allah Ta'ala us ke liye Jannat ke raaston mein se ek raasta aasaan kar dega **4**. Bila-shubah farishte talab-e-ilm ki raza ke liye apne par bichhate hain **5**. Yaqeenan aalim ke liye aasmanon aur zameen ki makhlooq aur paani mein machhliyan maghfirat ki dua karti hain **6**. Aalim ki abid par fazilat aisi hai jaise chaudhween raat ke chand ki tamam sitaron par fazilat **7**. Ulama Ambiya ke waris hain **8**. Ambiya ne kisi ko dinar ya dirham ka waris nahin banaya; unhon ne sirf ilm ka waris banaya. To jis ne ilm haasil kiya, us ne poora hissa haasil kar liya **9**. (Is hadith ko Ahmad, Tirmizi, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah aur Darimi ne riwayat kiya. Tirmizi ne in ka naam Qais bin Kaseer bataya.)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Damascus Shaam ka dar-ul-khilafah hai. Kaseer bin Qais Tabi'i the aur Hazrat Abu Darda رضی اللہ عنہ ki sohbat paane wale the.

**2.** Zahir yahi hai ke is talab-e-ilm ne hadith ka matn pehle sun liya tha. Woh is shauq mein yahan aaye ke Sahabi ke munh se sunun, taake barkat aur yaqeen mein izafah ho. Yeh bhi mumkin hai ke unhon ne hadith ka matn na suna ho, sirf itna pata chala ho ke Hazrat Abu Darda kisi mas'alah ke baare mein hadith bayan farmate hain. Chunki Madinah ke ma'ni aam taur par shehar ke hain, is liye Madinat-ur-Rasool kaha, yani main Madinah Munawwarah se aaya hoon.

Is se do mas'ale maloom hue. Ek yeh ke talab-e-ilm ke liye safar karna buzurgon, balki Nabiyon ki Sunnat hai. Hazrat Musa علیہ السلام ilm haasil karne ke liye bahut door safar karke Hazrat Khizr علیہ السلام ke paas tashreef le gaye. Doosra yeh ke Nabi ﷺ ko sirf "Rasool" keh sakte hain, jab ke alamat se maloom ho ke yahan Huzoor ﷺ murad hain. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "یٰۤاَیُّہَا الرَّسُوۡلُ" aur farmata hai: "مَنْ یُّطِعِ الرَّسُوۡلَ". Is ko na-jaiz kehna be-daleel hai.

**3.** Yani hadith sunne ke siwa kisi dini ya dunyawi maqsad ke liye safar nahin kiya. Is se un logon ko sabaq lena chahiye jo kehte hain ke teen masajid ke siwa kisi taraf safar jaayez nahin, halanke woh khud naukri aur tijarat waghera ke liye safar karte rehte hain. Is se maloom hua ke buzurgon se mulaqat aur ziyarat-e-quboor waghera ke liye safar jaayez hai, jaisa ke Shami waghera mein hai. **ان شاء اللّٰه** "باب المساجد" mein safar ki mumani'at wali hadith ke neeche is par poori tahqiq bayan ki jaayegi. Is ke liye hamari kitab "Jaa-ul-Haq" ka mutala'ah bhi karo.

**4.** Zahir yahi hai ke yeh woh hadith nahin thi jis ko sunne ke liye woh shakhs haazir hua tha, balki un ki himmat badhane aur un ke safar ki qabooliyat ki khushkhabri dene ke liye yeh hadith sunai gayi. Ma'ni yeh hai ke jo shakhs mas'alah poochne, ilm padhne, hadith sunne waghera ke liye safar karke ya baghair safar thoda raasta tay karke jaaye, use duniya mein nek aamaal ki taufeeq milegi jo Jannat milne ka sabab hain, ya Aakhirat mein Pul Sirat se guzarna aasaan hoga aur woh Jannat mein aasani se pahunch jaayega. Imam Shafi'i farmate hain ke ilm-e-deen ki talab nafl namaz se afzal hai, kyunki yeh farz hai aur woh nafl. (Mirqaat)

**5.** Zahir yahi hai ke yahan haqiqi ma'ni murad hain: jab talab-e-ilm ilm mein mashghool hota hai to us ki baat sunne ke liye farishte neeche utar aate hain aur guftagu sunte hain, jaise tilawat-e-Quran ke waqt, ya Qayamat mein talab-e-ilm ke qadmon ke neeche farishte apne par bichhayenge. Ya ma'ni yeh hai ke farishte talab-e-ilm ke liye apni aajizi ka izhar karte hain aur us ki mushqilon ko aasaan banate hain. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "وَاخْفِضْ لَہُمَاجَنَاحَ الذُّلِّ". Is baare mein Mirqaat ne ajeeb waqi'aat bayan kiye hain.

**6.** Yani ulema-e-deen ke liye chand, sooraj, sitare aur aasmani farishte, zameen ke zarre, sabziyon ke patte, baaz jinn-o-ins aur tamam daryai jaanwar, jaise machhliyan, maghfirat ki dua karte hain. Is liye ke ulema-e-deen ki wajah se deen baaqi hai aur deen ke baaqi rehne se aalam qa'im hai. Ulama ki barkaton se baarish hoti hai aur makhlooq ko rizq milta hai. Hadith Shareef mein hai: "بِہِمْ یُمْطَرُوْنَ وَبِہِمْ یُرْزَقُوْنَ". Ulama ke uth jaane se Islam uth jaayega aur Qayamat barpa ho jaayegi. Ulama duniya ka taweez hain. (Mirqaat wa Ashi'ah)

Yaad rahe ke ulama mein ulama-e-Shariat bhi shamil hain aur ulama-e-Tariqat bhi. Balki ilm ke baghair koi shakhs Wali Allah nahin banta. Allah Ta'ala jahilon ko wali nahin banata. Farmata hai: "اِنَّمَا یَخْشَی  اللّٰهَ مِنْ عِبَادِہِ الْعُلَمٰٓؤُا". (Mirqaat)

**7.** Aalim se murad woh aalim hai jo sirf zaroori aamaal par iktifa kare aur nawafil ke bajaye ilmi khidmaat anjaam de. Abid se murad woh shakhs hai jo sirf apne zaroori masail se waqif ho aur apna waqt nawafil mein guzare. Be-deen aur fasiq aalim aur bilkul jaahil abid is guftagu mein shamil nahin. Yaad rahe ke chand sooraj se noor lekar raat mein saare aalam ko roshan kar deta hai. Isi tarah aalim Nabi ﷺ se faiz lekar dini roshni phaila deta hai. Sitare khud roshan hote hain, magar chand roshni baantne wala hai. Abid apne liye aur aalim saare aalam ke liye koshish karta hai. Abid apni kambli bachata hai aur aalim toofan se logon ki kashti nikaal laata hai. Doosron ko faidah pahunchane wala amal sirf apne tak mehdood amal se afzal hai.

**8.** سبحان اللّٰه! Jab asal waris itne buland hain to un ke waris kitne shandaar honge. Mirqaat ne farmaya ke ulama-e-mujtahideen Rasoolon ke waris hain aur ghair-mujtahid ulama Nabiyon ke waris hain. Lafz "ulama" aur "anbiya" in dono ko shamil hai. Yaad rahe ke ulama-e-Islam Huzoor ﷺ ke waris hain aur chunki Huzoor ﷺ tamam Nabiyon ki sifaat ke jaame' hain, is liye ulama saare Anbiya ke waris hue.

**9.** Yaad rahe ke baaz Anbiya duniya se be-raghbat the aur unhon ne kuch jama nahin kiya, jaise Hazrat Yahya aur Hazrat Isa علیہما السلام. Aur baaz ne bahut maal rakha, jaise Hazrat Sulaiman aur Hazrat Dawud علیہما السلام. Lekin kisi Nabi ki mali miraas taqseem nahin hui. Un ka chhoda hua maal deen ke liye waqf hota hai aur Qayamat tak ulama un ke waris hain. Isi liye ulama ko wariseen-e-Anbiya kaha jaata hai.

## Hadith 213

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=213`; `source_id=4001`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ الْبَاهِلِيّ قَالَ: ذُكِرَ لِرَسُولِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - رَجُلَانِ: أَحَدُهُمَا عَابِدٌ وَالآخَرُ عَالِمٌ، فَقَالَ رَسُولُ الله صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : "فَضْلُ الْعَالِمِ عَلَى الْعَابِدِ كَفَضْلِي عَلٰی أَدْنَاكُمْ" ثُمَّ قَالَ رَسُولُ الله -صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ : "إِنَّ اللهَ وَمَلَائِكَتَهُ وَأَهْلَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ حَتَّى الْنَّمْلَةَ فِي جُحْرِهَا، وَحَتَّى الْحُوتَ لَيُصَلُّونَ عَلٰی مُعَلِّمِ النَّاسِ الْخَيْرَ". رَوَاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Zahir yahi hai ke in se khaas mard murad nahin, balki aam sawal hai: agar do aadmiyon mein se ek aalim aur ek abid ho to kis ka darja zyada hoga? Aalim aur abid ke ma'ni hum pehle bayan kar chuke hain.

**2.** Yeh tashbeeh darje ki qisam bayan karne ke liye hai, miqdaar bayan karne ke liye nahin. Yani jis qisam ki buzurgi mujhe tamam Musalmanon par haasil hai, us qisam ki dini buzurgi aalim ko abid par haasil hai, na ke sirf dunyawi buzurgi. Agarche in dono buzurgiyon mein karoron farq hain. Badshah ko ri'aya par saltanat ki, maaldar ko faqeer par maal ki, lashkar wale ko be-kas par quwwat ki aur haseen ko badsoorat par husn ki buzurgi haasil hoti hai. Magar yeh buzurgiyan dunyawi aur fana hone wali hain. Nabi ko makhlooq par dini buzurgi haasil hai jo abad-ul-aabad tak qaim hai. Isi tarah aalim ko jaahil par buzurgi hai. Aaj Sikandar ko kisi faqeer par mulki buzurgi haasil nahin, magar Imam Abu Hanifah ko tamam muqallideen par be-panah azmat ab bhi haasil hai.

Yaad rahe ke Huzoor Anwar ﷺ ko Nabiyon par ek darje ki buzurgi hai, Sahaba par doosre darje ki, Awliya aur ulama par teesre darje ki aur awaam par chauthe darje ki. "اَدْنیٰ کُمْ" mein is aakhri darje ki taraf ishara hai. Farmate hain: "وَ  احْشُرْنِیْ   فِیْ  زُمْرَۃِ الْمَسَاکِیْنِ". Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "مَثَلُ نُوۡرِہٖ کَمِشْکٰوۃٍ فِیۡہَا مِصْبَاحٌ". Is aayat mein noor-e-ilahi ki misaal chiragh ke noor se di gayi, halanke chiragh ke noor ko us noor se kya nisbat? Isi tarah yeh bhi sirf tamseel hai.

**3.** Farishton se Arsh ko uthane wale farishte aur "اھلِ سمٰوٰت" se baaqi farishte murad hain. Allah ki salaat se us ki khaas rahmat aur makhlooq ki salaat se khaas dua-e-rahmat murad hai, warna aam rahmaten aur aam duayen tamam Musalmanon ke liye hain. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "ھُوَ الَّذِیۡ یُصَلِّیۡ عَلَیۡکُمْ وَ مَلٰٓئِکَتُہٗ" aur farmata hai: "وَ یَسْتَغْفِرُوۡنَ لِلَّذِیۡنَ اٰمَنُوۡا" الخ. Is liye yeh hadith Quran ke khilaaf nahin aur na is se yeh lazim aaya ke ulama Huzoor ﷺ ke barabar ho gaye, kyunki Rab Ta'ala Huzoor ﷺ par bhi salaat bhejta hai aur ulama par bhi.

## Hadith 218

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=218`; `source_id=1866`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَنَسٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ  عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: "طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ، وَوَاضِعُ الْعِلْمِ عِنْدَ غَيْرِ أَهْلِهٖ كَمُقَلِّدِ الْخَنَازِيرِ الْجَوْهَرَ واللُّؤلُؤَ وَالذَّهَبَ". رَوَاهُ ابْنُ مَاجَهْ، وَرَوَى الْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي "شُعَبِ الإِيمَانِ" إِلَى قَوْلِهٖ: "مُسْلِمٍ". وَقَالَ: هَذَا حَدِيثٌ مَتْنُهُ مَشْهُورٌ،وَإِسْنَادُهُ ضَعِيفٌ، وَقَدْ رُوِيَ مِنْ أَوْجُهٍ كُلُّهَا ضَعِيفٌ.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Musnad Imam Abu Hanifah mein **وَمُسْلِمَةٍ** bhi hai, yani har Musalman mard aur aurat par ilm seekhna farz hai. Ilm se zaroorat ke mutabiq shar'i masail murad hain. Is liye namaz aur roze ke zaroori masail seekhna har Musalman par farz hai; haiz aur nifas ke zaroori masail seekhna har aurat par, tijarat ke masail seekhna har tajir par aur Hajj ke masail seekhna Hajj ke liye jaane wale par farz-e-ain hai. Lekin deen ka poora aalim banna farz-e-kifayah hai: agar shehar mein ek shakhs yeh farz ada kar le to sab zimmah se bari ho jaate hain. Sufiya farmate hain ke apne nafs ki aafaton aur Shaitani asraat waghera ko jaanna bhi har Musalman ke liye zaroori hai, taake woh un se bach sake.

**2.** Yahan ilm se bareek aur gehre masail murad hain jinhen awaam samajh na saken. Yani jo aalim awaam ke saamne ghair-zaroori, bareek aur mushkil masail ya aisi aayaat aur ahadith pesh kare jin ki tafseer ki zaroorat ho, woh aisa hi bewakoof hai jaise suaron ko motiyon ka haar pehnane wala. Jaahil log aisi baatein sun kar inkar kar baithte hain. Isi liye Sayyiduna Ali Murtaza رضی اللہ عنہ farmate hain ke logon se un ki samajh ke mutabiq baat karo, warna woh Allah aur Rasool ko jhutla denge aur is ka gunaah tum par hoga.

**3.** Yani yeh hadith kai zaeef sanad se riwayat hui hai, is liye quwwat rakhti hai, kyunki sanad ki kasrat zaeef hadith ko hasan bana deti hai. (Mirqaat waghera)

## Hadith 228

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=228`; `source_id=2794`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "نَضَّرَ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا سَمِعَ مَقَالَتِي فَحَفِظَهَا وَوَعَاهَا وَأَدَّاهَا، فَرُبَّ حَامِلِ فِقْهٍ غَيْرِ فَقِيهٍ، وَرُبَّ حَامِلِ فِقْهٍ إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَفْقَهُ مِنْهُ. ثَلَاثٌ لَا يَغِلُّ عَلَيهِنَّ قَلْبُ مُسْلِمٍ: إِخْلَاصُ الْعَمَلِ لِلَّهِ، وَالنَّصِيحَةُ لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَلُزُومُ جَمَاعَتِهِمْ، فَإِنَّ دَعْوتَهُمْ تُحِيطُ مَنْ وَرَاءَهُمْ". رَوَاهُ الشَّافِعِيُّ وَالْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي "الْمدْخَلِ" 

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ibn Mas'ood se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Allah Ta'ala us bande ko hara-bhara rakhe jo mera kalam sune, use yaad rakhe, use samjhe aur use doosron tak pahuncha de **1**. Bahut se fiqh uthaane wale khud faqeeh nahin hote aur bahut se log apne se bade faqeeh tak fiqh pahunchaate hain **2**. Musalman ka dil teen cheezon mein khayanat nahin karta **3**: Allah Ta'ala ke liye amal khalis karna **4**, Musalmanon ki khairkhwaahi karna aur un ki jamaat ko lazim pakadna **5**, kyunki un ki dua un ke peechhe rehne walon ko bhi gher leti hai **6**. Ise Shafi'i aur Baihaqi ne `الْمدْخَلِ` mein riwayat kiya hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yeh hadith Qayamat tak ke muhaddiseen ko shaamil hai. Yani Allah Ta'ala hadith yaad karne wale aur use doosron tak pahunchaane wale ko duniya mein phala-phoola rakhe, Aakhirat mein us ka chehra tar-o-taazah rakhe aur use is giroh mein shaamil kare: "وُجُوۡہٌ یَّوْمَئِذٍ نَّاضِرَۃٌ اِلٰی رَبِّہَا نَاظِرَۃٌ"۔ Huzoor ﷺ ki yeh dua qabool hai. Khuddam-e-Hadith `بِفَضْلِہٖ تَعَالٰی` Deen aur duniya mein shaad-o-aabaad hain, jaisa ke tajurba bata raha hai. Hadith ka zikr karna use yaad rakhna hai aur use bhool na jaana us ki dua hai. Baaz muhaddiseen Quran ki tarah hadith bhi yaad karte hain.

**2.** Is hadith mein saaf farmaya gaya hai ke Muhaddis seedha hadith par amal na kare, warna dhoka kha sakta hai. Balki use Mujtahid Faqeeh ke saamne pesh kare, us ki taqleed karke us ke bataaye hue ma'ni par amal kare. Faqeeh roohani tabib hai aur Muhaddis roohani attar (pansari). Attar apni dukaan ki dawaen Hakeem se poochh kar hi istemal karta hai. Isi liye qareeban saare muhaddiseen muqallid hain. Woh isi hadith par amal karte hain. In logon ko un logon se ibrat pakadni chahiye jo chand ahadith ke tarjume padh kar taqleed se munh pher lete hain. Quran aur Hadith ke samandar mein khud chhalaang na lagao, kisi Imam ke jahaaz mein baith kar paar karo. Fiqh se murad woh hadith hai jis se shar'i ahkaam nikaale ja saken. Sarkar ﷺ ka maqsad yeh hai ke bahut baar aisa hoga ke meri hadith yaad karne wale masail nikaal nahin sakenge aur jin logon tak hadith pahunchegi un mein bhi masail nikaalne ki qudrat nahin hogi. Is liye Muhaddis hadith ko apne paas rok na rakhe, balki Fuqaha tak pahuncha de. Yaad rahe ke hadith ka aaghaz Huzoor ﷺ ki zaat-e-aqdas se hota hai aur us ka ikhtitaam Faqeeh tak pahunch kar hota hai.

**3.** Is jumle ki do tafseeren hain. Ek yeh ke `عَلَےٰ` ba ke ma'ni mein hai, yani jis dil mein in teen amal mein se koi amal aa jaaye, us dil mein khayanat, keena aur hasad nahin rehta. Doosri yeh ke `عَلَےٰ` apne hi ma'ni mein hai, yani Musalman ki pehchaan yeh hai ke woh in teen kaamon mein kotahi nahin karta. Pehla ma'ni zyada qawi hai. Yeh teen cheezen dil ki bimariyon ki dawaen hain.

**4.** Neki ke amal na duniya haasil karne ke liye kare aur na Jannat paane ya Dozakh se bachne ke liye, balki sirf Rab ki raza ke liye kare. Jab Rab raazi ho jaaye to sab kuch haasil ho gaya.

**5.** Is tarah ke apni taaqat ke mutabiq Musalmanon ki madad kare. Jo cheez apne liye pasand na kare, un ke liye bhi pasand na kare. Aqaid aur nek aamaal mein un ke saath rahe. Tanhaai ke muqaable mein jamaat ko tarjeeh de. Isi liye Islam ne Jumu'ah, Eidain waghera mein jamaat farz ki hai.

**6.** Yani Musalmanon ki jamaat ki dua logon ko gumraahi aur Shaitaan ke fareb se mehfooz rakhti hai. Jamaat se alag rehne wala un ki dua se mehroom hota hai. Ma'loom hua ke Musalmanon ki dua hifaazat ka qila hai.

## Hadith 229

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=229`; `source_id=2803`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَرَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَالتِّرْمِذِيُّ وَأَبُو دَاوُدَ وَابْنُ مَاجَهْ وَالدَّارِمِيُّ عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ، إِلَّا أَنَّ التِّرْمِذِيَّ وَأَبا دَاوُدَ لَمْ يَذْكرَا: "ثَلَاثٌ لَا يَغِلُّ عَلَيْهِنَّ" إِلَى آخِرِهِ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Ahmad, Tirmizi, Abu Dawood, Ibn Majah aur Darimi ne Zaid bin Sabit se riwayat kiya, lekin Tirmizi aur Abu Dawood ne "ثَلَاثٌ لَا يَغِلُّ عَلَيْهِنَّ" se aakhir tak ka zikr nahin kiya.

**Poori wazahat:** Wazahat: Is hadith aur pichhli hadith mein sirf kitab aur sanad ka farq hai.

## Hadith 230

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=230`; `source_id=2821`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - يَقُولُ: "نَضَّرَ اللَّهُ امْرَأً سَمِعَ مِنَّا شَيْئًا فَبَلَّغَهُ كَمَا سَمِعَهُ، فَرُبَّ مُبَلَّغٍ أَوْعَى لَهُ مِنْ سَامِعٍ". رَوَاهُ التِّرْمِذِيُّ وَابْنُ مَاجَهْ. 

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ibn Mas'ood se. Woh farmate hain ke main ne Rasool Allah ﷺ ko farmate hue suna: Allah Ta'ala use hara-bhara rakhe jo hum se koi baat sune **1**, phir jaisi sune waisi hi pahuncha de **2**. Bahut se jin tak baat pahunchaayi gayi hai, woh sunne wale se zyada samajhdaar hote hain. Ise Tirmizi aur Ibn Majah ne riwayat kiya hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani mujh se ya mere Sahaba se mera ya un ka koi qaul ya amal sune. Isi liye `مِنَّا` jama ka lafz aur `شَيْئًا` nakirah laaya gaya hai.

**2.** Is tarah ke ma'ni na badlein aur hadith ke alfaaz mein aisa farq na paida ho jo ma'ni badal de. Yaad rahe ke Ibn Umar, Malik bin Anas, Ibn Sirin aur doosre buzurgon ke nazdeek hadith ko ma'ni ke saath riwayat karna haraam hai, kyunki aksar lafz badalne se ma'ni badal jaata hai aur raawi ko iska pata nahin hota. Imam Hasan, Sha'bi, Nakha'i aur Mujahid waghera ke nazdeek ma'ni ke saath riwayat karna jaaiz hai, jab raawi hadith ke alfaaz is tarah badle ke ma'ni na badlein. Pehle qaul mein ehtiyaat hai aur doosre mein gunjaish. Behtar yahi hai ke alfaaz bhi na badle jaayen.

Dekhiye Hazrat Wa'il bin Hujr ne namaz ki Aameen ke baare mein farmaya: "مَدَّبِھَا صَوتَہٗ"۔ Baaz raawiyon ne ise "رَفَعَ بِھَا صَوتَہٗ" ke alfaaz se riwayat kiya. Unhon ne samjha ke dono ke ma'ni ek hi hain, lekin baad walon ko dhoka hua ke shayad is ka matlab buland aawaaz se Aameen kehna hai. Haalanki is ka tarjuma yeh tha ke Aameen ko khench kar, alif ke madd ke saath kaha. Ma'ni ke saath riwayat mein yeh khatre hain. Isi liye farmaya gaya ke jaisi sune waisi hi pahunchaaye.
