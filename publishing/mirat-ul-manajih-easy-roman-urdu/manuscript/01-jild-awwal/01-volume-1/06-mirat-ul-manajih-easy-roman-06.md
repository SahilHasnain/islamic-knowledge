## Hadith 236

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=236`; `source_id=2911`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "الْمِرَاءُ فِي الْقُرْآنِ كُفْرٌ". رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَأَبُو دَاوُدَ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Quran ke ma'ni mein jhagda karna kufr hai **1**. (Ahmad wa Abu Dawood)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani Quran ki aayaat ke ma'ni mein aisa jhagda karna jis se log shak mein mubtala ho jaayen, qareeban kufr hai, kyunki yeh logon ke kufr ka zariya banta hai. Ya mutashabih aayaat ki taweel ke maamle mein jhagda karna kufran-e-ni'mat hai. Ya Quran ki aayaat aur un ki mutawatir qiraaton ke baare mein yeh jhagda karna ke yeh Allah ka kalaam hain ya nahin, kufr hai. Ya Quran ko apni raye ke mutabiq banane ke liye jhagda karna, yani har shakhs apni raye aur banaye hue mazhab ke mutabiq us ka tarjuma ya tafseer kare, yeh kufr hai. Baharhaal hadith bilkul wazeh hai. Is ka mufassireen aur mujtahideen ke ikhtilaf se koi ta'alluq nahin; woh jhagda nahin, balki tahqeeq hai.

## Hadith 237

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=237`; `source_id=2938`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ شُعَيْبٍ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ جَدِّهِ قَالَ: سَمِعَ النَّبِيُّ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - قَوْمًا يَتَدَارَؤُونَ فِي الْقُرْآنِ فَقَالَ: "إِنَّمَا هَلَكَ مَنْ كَانَ قَبْلَكُمْ بِهَذَا: ضَرَبُوا كِتَابَ اللَّهِ بَعْضَهُ بِبَعْضٍ، وَإِنَّمَا نَزَلَ كِتَابُ اللَّهِ يُصَدِّقُ بَعْضُهُ بَعْضًا،فَلَا تُكَذِّبُوا بَعْضَهُ بِبَعْضٍ، فَمَا عَلِمْتُمْ مِنْهُ فَقُولُوا، وَمَا جَهِلْتُمْ فَكِلُوهُ إِلَى عَالِمِهِ". رَوَاهُ أَحْمَدُ وَابْنُ مَاجَهْ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Amr ibn Shu'aib se. Woh apne walid se aur woh apne dada se riwayat karte hain **1**. Farmate hain ke Nabi ﷺ ne ek giroh ko Quran ke baare mein jhagda karte suna **2**. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: Tum se pehle log isi harkat ki wajah se halaak hue: unhon ne Allah ki kitab ki ek baat ko doosri baat se takraya **3**. Allah ki kitab to is liye naazil hui hai ke us ka ek hissa doosre hisse ki tasdeeq karta hai. Is liye tum us ke ek hisse ko doosre hisse se jhutlao nahin **4**. Us mein se jitna tum jaante ho, woh bayan karo, aur jo nahin jaante use us ke jaankar ke supurd kar do **5**. (Ahmad, Ibn Majah)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Pehle bayan kiya ja chuka hai ke aap ka naam Amr ibn Shu'aib ibn Muhammad ibn Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Aas hai. Abdullah ibn Amr ibn Aas Sahabi hain aur un ke bete Muhammad Taba'i hain. Agar `جَدِّہٖ` ki zamir Amr ki taraf laute to yeh hadith mursal hai, kyunki Amr ke dada Muhammad Taba'i hain. Aur agar yeh Shu'aib ki taraf laute to hadith muttasil hai, kyunki Shu'aib ke dada Amr ibn Aas Sahabi hain. Gharz yeh hadith mudallas hai. (Mirqaat)

**2.** Is tarah ke ek shakhs apni baat ko ek aayat se sabit kar raha ho aur doosra us ke khilaaf doosri aayat pesh kar raha ho, jis se sunne wale ko yeh shak paida ho ke Quran ki aayaat ek jaisi nahin, balki un mein sakht ta'aruz aur ikhtilaf hai. Jaise ek kahe ke khair aur shar Allah ki taraf se hai; Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: `قُلْ كُلٌّ مِّنْ عِنْدِ اللّٰهِ`۔ Aur doosra kahe ke nahin, khair Rab ki taraf se hai aur shar hamari taraf se; Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: `مَاۤ أَصَابَکَ مِنَ الْحَسَنَۃِ فَمِنَ اللّٰهِ وَمَاۤ أَصَابَکَ مِنَ السَّیِّئَۃِ فَمِنْ نَفْسِکَ`۔ Quran mein jhagda yahi hai, jo haraam, balki kabhi kufr hota hai.

**3.** Yani aayaat mein ta'aruz dikhana. Yeh Nasara, Yahood aur doosre log the jo Taurat aur Injeel ki aayaat mein mail na paida kar sake, is liye bahut se firqe bana baithe. Har firqa un kitabon ki kuch aayaat se daleel pakadne laga. Allah hifazat farmaye.

**4.** Allah ki kitab se murad Quran Shareef hai, ya tamam aasmani kitaben. Pehla ma'ni zyada zahir hai. Yani Quran ki aayaat aapas mein mukhalif nahin, balki ek doosre ke mutabiq hain. Agar ta'aruz maaloom ho to hamari samajh mein kami hai, ya hum tareekh se be-khabar hain aur naasikh-o-mansookh ko nahin pehchante, ya hum ne aayaat ke ma'ni durust nahin samjhe.

**5.** `سُبْحَانَ اللّٰه!` Kya umdah taleem hai ke jaahil Quran ki tafseer ko haath na lagaaye. Jab be-ilm mareez ka ilaaj nahin karta, engine ki machine ko haath nahin lagata, balki na-tajurba-kaar hajjam sar nahin mundta, to har be-khabar Quran ki tafseer ko kyun haath lagaaye?

Latifa: Kisi ne ek Aalim se kaha: Qayamat ka din kitna bada hai? Quran use ek hazaar saal ka bhi kehta hai aur pachaas hazaar saal ka bhi. Hadith ne to kamaal kar diya; woh farmati hai ke woh chaar rak'at namaz padhne ke barabar hoga. Na Quran ka aitbaar raha na hadith ka, `العیاذ باللّٰه`! Aalim ne farmaya: Quran aur hadith dono durust hain, tumhari samajh ghalat hai. Woh din ek hazaar saal ka hai, lekin kafir ko takleef ki wajah se pachaas hazaar saal ka mehsoos hoga. Momin ko rahat ki wajah se das minute ka mehsoos hoga. Jaise ek hi raat beemar ko lambi, tandurust ko chhoti, aur mehboob ke paas guzarne wale ko chand minute ki mehsoos hoti hai.

## Hadith 238

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=238`; `source_id=2956`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنِ ابْنِ مَسْعُودٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "أُنْزِلَ الْقُرْآنُ عَلَى سَبْعَةِ أَحْرُفٍ. لِكُلِّ آيَةٍ مِنْهَا ظَهْرٌ وَبَطْنٌ، وَلِكُلِّ حَدٍّ مُطَّلَعٌ". رَوَاهُ فِي "شَرْحِ السُّنَّةِ"

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ibn Mas'ood se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Quran saat tareeqon par naazil hua **1**. Un mein se har aayat ka zahir bhi hai aur baatin bhi **2**. Aur har zahir-o-baatin ki ek had hai jahan se us ki khabar milti hai **3**. (Sharh-us-Sunnah)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Tareeqon se murad ya to Arabi lugatein hain, kyunki Arab mein saat qabeelay fasahat aur balaghat mein mashhoor the: Quraish, Saqeef, Tai, Hawazin, Muzail, Yamani aur Tamim. In ki zabanon mein aapas mein kuch farq tha, jaise Delhi aur Lucknow walon ki Urdu mein thoda farq hai. Quran ka nuzool Quraish ki zaban mein hua jo doosre qabeelon par bhaari thi. Isi liye unhein ijazat di gayi ke apni apni zaban mein tilawat karein. Zamana-e-Nabawi mein aam tilawat Quraishi zaban mein hoti thi, lekin kuch log doosri qiraaton mein bhi tilawat karte the. Huzoor ﷺ ke wisaal ke baad yeh ikhtilaf fasad ka sabab banne laga. Ahad-e-Usmani mein jab Quran Pak ko kitabi shakal mein jama kiya gaya to Quraish ki lugat ko saamne rakha gaya, jis mein Quran naazil hua tha. Baqi qiraatein khatam kar di gayin taake Musalmanon mein Yahood-o-Nasara jaisa ikhtilaf paida na ho. Is mein Quran ki tabdeeli nahin thi, balki fitne ko door karna maqsood tha. Jaise `فَلَا تَقُلْ لَّهُمَاۤ أُفٍّ` mein lafz `اف` Quraishi lugat mein alif ke pesh, fa ke shadd aur zer-o-tanween ke saath hai. Doosri lugaton mein alif ke zabar ya zer, fa ke zabar ya zer, baghair tanween aur baghair shadd ke hota hai. Ma'ni ek hi hain, lafzon mein itna farq hai. Ya is se murad saat qiraatein hain, jaise `یَا مَالِكِ مَلِكِ مَلِیْكِ یَوْمِ الدِّیْنِ` waghera. Ya ma'ni yeh hai ke Quran saat mazaameen par naazil hua: amr, nahi, misaalein, qisse, waade, wa'eedein aur naseehatein. Ya yeh ke woh saat cheezen lekar naazil hua: aqaaid, ahkaam, akhlaaq, haraam, halaal, muhkam aur mutashabih. Is ki aur bhi bahut si tafseerein ki gayi hain.

**2.** Yani Quran ki har aayat ka zahiri maqsad bhi hai aur baatini maqsad bhi. Zahiri maqsad us ka lafzi tarjuma hai aur baatini maqsad us ki murad aur maqsood. Ya zahir Shariat hai aur baatin Tariqat; ya zahir ahkaam hain aur baatin asraar; ya zahir woh hai jis par sab ulama muttali hain aur baatin woh hai jis se Sufiya-e-Kiraam ba-khabar hain; ya zahir woh hai jo naql se maaloom ho aur baatin woh jo kashf se maaloom ho. Jaise `یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا قَاتِلُوا الَّذِیْنَ یَلُوْنَكُمْ مِّنَ الْكُفَّارِ` ka zahiri ma'ni yeh hai ke apne qareebi kafiron se lohe ki talwar waghera se jihad karo. Baatini ma'ni yeh hai ke qareebi kafir apne nafs-e-ammarah ke khilaaf mujahade ki talwar aur yaar ki madad se jihad karo.

**3.** Hadd-e-matla' pahad ki choti ya ooncha teela hota hai jahan se door tak cheezen dekhi ja sakti hain. Yani Quran ke zahir aur baatin ko samajhne ke alag alag maqamaat hain. Chunanche is ka zahir ulama se aur baatin mashaikh se maaloom hota hai; ya zahir qaal se aur baatin haal se; ya zahir nahw se aur baatin fana-o-mahw se; ya zahir kitabon se aur baatin kisi ki nazar se.

She'r:

Deen na dhoond kitabon mein, aye be-khabar
Ilm-o-hikmat kitabon se, Deen nazar se
Sau kitab aur sau warq jala kar
Dil ka rukh Dildaar ki taraf kar

Gharz jaise Quran ke zahir ke alfaaz Mian Ji se, tajweed Qaari se, hifz Haafiz se, ma'ni Aalim se aur ahkaam Mujtahid se seekhe jaate hain, waise hi us ke asraar Mashaikh se haasil kiye jaayen. Har shakhs ki hadd-e-matla' alag hai. Yaad rahe ke Mashaikh woh buzurg hain jo Shariat aur Tariqat dono ke jaame' hon, Nabi Kareem ﷺ ke parwane hon aur aap ﷺ ke Deen ke sache muballigh hon. Woh jaahil Sufi jo sirf virasat mein Wali bane baithe hain, faasiq-o-faajir hain; yahan woh murad nahin.

## Hadith 239

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=239`; `source_id=2974`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ابْنِ عَمْرٍو قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "الْعِلْمُ ثَلَاثَةٌ: آيَةٌ مُحْكَمَةٌ، أَوْ سُنَّةٌ قَائِمَةٌ، أَوْ فَرِيضَةٌ عَادِلَةٌ، وَمَا كَانَ سِوَى ذَلِكَ فَهُوَ فَضْلٌ". رَوَاهُ أَبُو دَاوُدَ وَابْنُ مَاجَهْ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abdullah ibn Amr se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Ilm teen hain: muhkam aayat, qayam aur mazboot Sunnat, aur us ke barabar farz **1**. In ke siwa jo kuch hai woh ziyaadati hai **2**. (Abu Dawood, Ibn Majah)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani Deeni ilm un cheezon ko jaanne ka naam hai: tafseel ke saath woh aayaat jo mansookh na hon, sahih aur ghair-mansookh ahadith, ijma-e-Ummat aur qiyas. Yeh sab Quran aur Sunnat ki tarah wajib-ul-amal hain. Yaad rahe ke yahan fariza se murad ilm-e-faraiz (mirath) nahin, kyunki woh bhi Quran aur Sunnat mein aa chuka hai. Yahan ilm-e-fiqh murad hai. Aadilah ka ma'ni adeel aur misl hai. (Mirqaat wa Ashi'ah)

**2.** Yani in teen ke ilawa baqi uloom ilm-e-Deen nahin, balki zaaid ya fazool hain. Yaad rahe ke sarf-o-nahw waghera Quran aur hadith samajhne ke liye hain, aur Usool-e-Fiqh aur Usool-e-Hadith waghera in uloom ke khadim hain. Jo shakhs inhen apna maqsood bana le, woh bada be-waqoof hai.

She'r:

Ilm-e-Deen fiqh hai, tafseer aur hadith
Jo in ke siwa dhoonde, woh khabees hai

## Hadith 240

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=240`; `source_id=2983`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَن عَوْفِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ الأَشْجَعِيِّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : "لَا يَقُصُّ إِلَّا أَمِيرٌ أَوْ مَأْمُورٌ أَو مُخْتَالٌ". رَوَاهُ أَبُو دَاوُدَ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Auf ibn Malik Ashja'i se **1**. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Qissago'i sirf hukmaran, us ka ma'moor, ya mutakabbir shakhs karta hai **2**. Ise Abu Dawood ne riwayat kiya hai.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap mashhoor Sahabi hain. Jang-e-Khaibar mein Huzoor ﷺ ke saath the. Fatah-e-Makkah ke din qabeela Ashja' ka parcham aap ke haath mein tha. Aap Shaam mein rahe aur 73 Hijri mein wahin wisaal hua.

**2.** Istilah mein siyasi lecture aur aam khitaabon ko qissa kaha jaata hai. Agar us mein shar'i ahkaam ki tableegh ho to use wa'z-o-naseehat kehte hain. Aaj kal ke aam raaij wa'z qisse hain aur wa'izeen qass hain. Qisse ya to badshah karte hain, ya un ke maatat hukkaam, ya siyasi mutakabbir leader qaum mein apna waqar badhane ke liye karte hain. Ulama ka yeh kaam nahin. Ulama ka wa'z shar'i ahkaam ka chashma aur tableegh ka markaz hona chahiye. Yeh hadith hidayat ka khazana hai.

## Hadith 242

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=242`; `source_id=3073`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "مَنْ أُفْتِيَ بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ كَانَ إِثْمُهُ عَلَى مَنْ أَفْتَاهُ، وَمَن أَشَارَ عَلَى أَخِيهِ بِأَمْرٍ يَعْلَمُ أَنَّ الرُّشْدَ فِي غَيْرِهِ فَقَدْ خَانَهُ". رَوَاهُ أَبُو دَاوُدَ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Jo shakhs be-ilmi mein fatwa de, us ka gunah fatwa dene wale par hai **1**. Aur jo apne bhai ko kisi baat ka mashwara is haal mein de ke woh jaanta ho ke durusti is ke ilawa kisi aur baat mein hai, us ne us se khayanat ki **2**. (Abu Dawood)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Is ke do ma'ni ho sakte hain. Ek yeh ke jo shakhs ulama ko chhor kar jaahil logon se mas'alah pooche aur woh use ghalat mas'alah bata dein, to poochhne wala bhi gunahgaar hoga ke woh Aalim ko chhor kar us jaahil ke paas kyun gaya. Agar woh na poochhta to jaahil ghalat jawab na deta. Is surat mein `اَفتٰی` ke ma'ni `اِستَفتٰی` hain. Doosra yeh ke jis shakhs ko ghalat fatwa diya gaya, us ka gunah fatwa dene wale par hai. Is surat mein pehla `اُفتِی` majhool hai. Khulasa yeh hai ke be-ilm shakhs ko shar'i mas'alah batana sakht jurm hai.

**2.** Yani agar koi Musalman kisi se mashwara le aur woh jaan-boojh kar use ghalat mashwara de, taake woh kisi museebat mein phans jaaye, to woh mashwara dene wala pakka khayanat karne wala hai. Khayanat sirf maal mein nahin hoti; raaz, izzat aur mashware mein bhi hoti hai.

## Hadith 243

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=243`; `source_id=3082`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ مُعَاوِيَةَ قَالَ: إِنَّ النَّبِيَّ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - نَهَى عَنِ الأُغْلُوطَاتِ. رَوَاهُ أَبُو دَاوُدَ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Muawiyah se. Farmate hain ke Nabi ﷺ ne paheliyon se mana farmaya **1**. (Abu Dawood)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani awam ke saamne fiqhi paheliyan pesh karna aur phir unhein hal na karna, ya ulama ka ek doosre ko zaleel karne aur apni bartari dikhane ke liye shar'i paheliyan poochhna najaiz hai, kyunki yeh momin ko takleef dene ka sabab hai. Talib-e-ilm ka zehan tez karne ke liye ustad ka un se fiqhi paheliyan poochhna bilkul jaiz hai. Huzoor ﷺ ne Mi'raj mein dozakhiyon ke woh azaab bhi dekh liye jo Qayamat ke baad hone wale hain.

## Hadith 246

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=246`; `source_id=3802`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

## Hadith 247

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=247`; `source_id=3818`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

## Hadith 248

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=248`; `source_id=3850`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Doosri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ الْعُذْرِيِّ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "يَحْمِلُ هَذَا الْعِلْمَ مِنْ كُلِّ خَلَفٍ عُدُولُهُ، يَنْفُونَ عَنْهُ تَحْرِيفَ الْغَالِينَ، وَانْتِحَالَ الْمُبْطِلِينَ، وَتَأْوِيلَ الْجَاهِلين". رَوَاهُ الْبَيْهَقِيُّ فِي "كِتَابِ الْمَدْخَلِ" مُرْسَلًا وَسَنَذْكُرُ حَدِيثَ جَابِرٍ: "فَإِنَّمَا شِفَاءُ العيِّ السُّؤالُ" فِي "بَاب التَّيَمُّمِ" إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ تَعَالى.

**2.** Is mein ghaibi basharat hai ke Qayamat tak mere Deen mein nek Ulama paida hote rahenge. Woh Deen ka ilm padhte, padhaate aur us ki tableegh karte rahenge. **3.** Yani Musalmanon mein baaz jaahil, Ulama ki shakl mein saamne aakar Quran aur hadith ki ghalat taweelein aur ma'nawi tahreefein karenge. Yeh maqbool jamaat in tamam cheezon ko door karegi. الْحَمْدُﷲ! Aaj tak aisa hota raha hai aur aage bhi hoga. Dekho, Deeni Ulama ki sarparasti na hukumat karti hai na qaum, phir bhi yeh jamaat paida hoti ja rahi hai aur Deen ki khidmat barabar kar rahi hai. بَارَكَ اللّٰه فِیْہِمْ!

**4.** Is se maloom hua ke Ibrahim ibn Abdul Rahman Taabi'i hain.

## Hadith 249

**3.** Ilm se murad Deen ka ilm hai, chahe woh logon ko padhata ho, Deeni kitaben likhta ho ya dono kaam karta ho.

**4.** Yani woh `صائم الدھر` aur `قائم اللیل` tha. Shayad un ke Deen mein yeh jaiz hoga. Islam mein saal mein paanch din roze rakhna haraam hai: Shawwal ki pehli tareekh aur Baqar Eid ki daswin se terahwin tak.

**5.** Jawab mein itni lambi ibadat ka zikr Aalim ki shaan logon ke zehan mein bithane ke liye hai, warna itna kehna kaafi tha ke pehla shakhs doosre se afzal hai.

**6.** Is ki tafseer pehle guzar chuki hai ke yeh misaal fazeelat ki qisam bayan karne ke liye hai. Yani jis qisam ki buzurgi mujhe tum par haasil hai, usi qisam ki buzurgi Aalim ko Aabid par haasil hai. Jaise Rab Ta'ala ne farmaya: "مَثَلُ نُوۡرِہٖ كَمِشْكٰوۃٍ" الآیہ۔ Is se yeh lazim nahin aata ke Aalim Nabi ke barabar ho jaaye. Yaad rahe ke Deeni ilm kabhi Farz-e-Ain hota hai aur kabhi Farz-e-Kifayah, jabke zyada ibadat nafl hoti hai. Is ke ilawa Aalim ka faida makhlooq ko pahunchta hai aur Aabid ka faida sirf usi ko. Is liye Aalim Aabid se afzal hai. Adam علیہ السلام Aalim the aur farishte laakhon saal ke Aabid, lekin sajdah Aabid farishton ne Aalim ko kiya.

## Hadith 252

وَعَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ أَنَّ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ قَالَ: حَدِّثِ النَّاسَ كُلَّ جُمُعَةٍ مَرَّةً، فَإِنْ أَبَيْتَ فَمَرَّتَيْنِ، فَإِنْ أَكْثَرْتَ فَثَلَاثَ مَرَّاتٍ، وَلَا تُمِلَّ النَّاسَ هَذَا الْقُرْآنَ، وَلَا أُلْفِيَنَّكَ تَأْتِي الْقَوْمَ وَهُمْ فِي حَدِيثٍ مِنْ حَدِيثِهِمْ فَتَقُصُّ عَلَيْهِمْ فَتَقْطَعُ عَلَيْهِمْ حَدِيثَهُمْ فَتُمِلَّهُمْ، وَلَكِنْ أَنْصِتْ، فَإِذَا أَمَرُوكَ فَحَدِّثْهُمْ وَهُمْ يَشْتَهُونَهُ، وَانْظُرِ السَّجْعَ مِنَ الدُّعَاءِ فَاجْتَنِبْهُ، فَإِنِّي عَهِدْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ   وَأَصْحَابَهُ لَا يَفْعَلُونَ ذَلِك. رَوَاهُ البُخَارِيُّ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Ikrimah se **1** ke Hazrat Ibn Abbas ne farmaya: Logon ko hafte mein ek baar wa'z sunao. Agar tum na maano to do baar, aur agar bahut hi zyada karo to teen baar. Logon ko is Quran se ukta na do **2**. Main tumhein hargiz aisa na paun ke tum kisi qaum ke paas jao jab woh apni kisi baat mein mashghool hon, phir tum unhein wa'z sunana shuru kar do aur un ki baat kaat do, kyunki tum unhein ukta doge. Balki khamosh raho. Jab woh khud kahen to unhein hadees sunao, jab woh us ke shauqeen hon **3**. Aur dua mein qafiyah-daar ibarat se bacho. **Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Aap ka naam Ikrimah aur kuniyat Abu Abdullah hai. Aap Barbari asal ke the aur Hazrat Ibn Abbas ke azaad kiye hue ghulam the. Aap Makkah Mukarramah ke sab se bade Faqeeh Taabi'i the. Aap ka wisaal 107 Hijri mein hua aur aap ne assi saal ki umr paayi. (Kamaal) Ikrimah ibn Abu Jahl alag hain. Jab Ikrimah mutlaq aata hai to yahan aap hi murad hote hain.

**2.** Yani rozana wa'z na sunao; hafte mein ek, do ya teen baar sunao. Phir bhi itni der tak wa'z na karo ke log bhar jaayen, balki un ka shauq baqi rahe aur woh khud tum se khatam karne ko kahen. سبحان اللّٰه! In buzurgon ki kya umdah tarbiyat thi! Un ki majalis goya aam school bhi thin jin mein seekhna aur sikhana dono bataya jaata tha. Is se baghair zaroorat chaar chaar ghante wa'z karne wale wa'izeen ibrat pakden. Yaad rahe ke yeh irshad us jagah hai jahan log uktaate hon. Lekin agar log shauqeen hon to na roz wa'z karna bura hai aur na der tak karna. Madaris mein Ta'leem-ul-Quran ke dars rozana hote hain. Huzoor ﷺ ne ek baar Fajr se Maghrib tak wa'z farmaya. Aalim ko chahiye ke logon ke shauq ka andaza rakhe.

**3.** Yeh wa'iz ke liye doosri naseehat hai jis par use amal karna chahiye: Jahan log baat ya kaam mein mashghool hon, wahan un ki baat aur kaam band na karao. Wa'z shuru na kar do, kyunki is surat mein agarche woh kuch na kahen, magar dil mein takleef mehsoos karenge. Is mein ilm aur Aalim ki tauheen bhi hai. Is se woh wa'izeen ibrat pakden jo tez loudspeaker par aadhi aadhi raat tak taqreerein karke mazdooron aur mareezon ko pareshan karte hain aur poori basti ko jagate hain. Dekha gaya hai ke phir awam hukumat ko darkhwasten dete hain, jis par dafa 144 nafiz ki jaati hai. Kitni badi zillat aur ilm ki tauheen hai! Agar yeh wa'izeen isi farman par amal karte to yeh naubat kyun aati? Hakim aur afsar khud un se ilm seekhne un ki khidmat mein haazir hote.

**4.** Yani duaon mein takalluf se qafiyah-band ibarat istemal na karo, kyunki phir khushu-o-khuzu baqi nahin rahega aur tawajjuh achhi ibadat banane par rahegi. Is Bargah-e-Aali mein aajizi aur niyaz dekhi jaati hai, zaban ki adabiyat nahin. Yaad rahe ke Huzoor ﷺ ki aksar duaein qafiyah-band hain, magar yeh takalluf se nahin banayi gayin. Balki us **اَفْصَحُ الْفُصَحَا** ki zaban-e-mubarak se be-takalluf aur be-saakhta ada hui hain. Is liye yeh hadees un ke khilaf nahin; yahan takalluf ki mumani'at hai.

## Hadith 254

Yeh cheezein us ko us ke wisaal ke baad bhi pahunchti rehti hain **6**. (Ibn Majah, Baihaqi fi Shu'ab-ul-Iman)

**Poori wazahat:**

**3.** Is tarah ke apne haath se Quran likh kar ya khareed kar chhor gaya ho. Isi hukm mein tamam Deeni kitaben bhi hain.

**4.** Koshish se, ya apne paise se, ya apne haath se banaya ho. Isi hukm mein madaris aur khanqahen bhi hain.

**5.** Tandurusti ki qaid is liye lagayi ke marz-ul-maut mein khairat karne ka aadha sawab hai, kyunki us waqt khud use maal ki zaroorat nahin rehti. Is mein tamam sadaqah-e-jariyah aa gaye, jaise kuen khudwana, nalke lagwana aur hospital banwana waghera.

**6.** Baaz cheezein Qayamat tak aur baaz is se kam muddat tak baqi rehti hain. Jitni der sadaqah baqi rahega, utni hi der us ka ajr jaari rahega.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - مَرَّ بِمَجْلِسَيْنِ فِي مَسْجدِهِ فَقَالَ: "كِلَاهُمَا عَلَى خَيْرٍ وَأَحَدُهُمَا أَفْضَلُ مِنْ صَاحِبِهِ، أَمَّا هَؤُلَاءِ فَيَدْعُونَ اللَّهَ وَيَرْغَبُونَ إِليْهِ، فَإِنْ شَاءَ أَعْطَاهُمْ وَإِنْ شَاءَ مَنَعَهُمْ. وَأَمَّا هَؤُلَاءِ فَيَتَعَلَّمُونَ الْفِقهَ أَوِ الْعِلْمَ وَيُعَلِّمُونَ الْجَاهِلَ فَهُمْ أَفْضَلُ، وَإِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ مُعَلِّمًا"، ثمَّ جَلَسَ فِيهِمْ. رَوَاهُ الدَّارمِيُّ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abdullah ibn Amr se ke Rasool Allah ﷺ apni masjid mein do majlison ke paas se guzre **1**. Phir farmaya: Dono hi bhalai par hain, lekin in mein se ek doosri se behtar hai **2**. Yeh log Allah se dua kar rahe hain aur us ki taraf raghib hain. Agar Allah chahe to unhein de aur agar chahe to na de **3**. Aur yeh log fiqh ya ilm seekh rahe hain aur jaahil ko sikha rahe hain, is liye yahi afzal hain **4**. Main to Mu'allim bana kar bheja gaya hoon. Phir Aap ﷺ unhi mein ja kar baith gaye **5**. (Darimi)

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yani Masjid-e-Nabawi Shareef mein Sahaba ki do jamaatein do konon mein baithi hui thin. Ek kone mein ek jamaat nafl, tilawat aur doosri ibadatein kar rahi thi. Doosre kone mein doosri jamaat ilmi guftagu aur jo sabaq seekha tha us ki dohraai kar rahi thi. Huzoor ﷺ ne dono ko dekha.

**2.** Yani ilm ki majlis ibadat ki majlis se afzal hai. Is ki wajah aage aa rahi hai.

**3.** Yani aabidon ki mehnat sirf un ki apni zaat ke liye hai. Us ki qabooliyat aur sawab yaqini nahin, kyunki yeh Allah Ta'ala ke karam par mauqoof hai. Allah Ta'ala ne in cheezon ka waada nahin farmaya. Is hadith mein Mu'tazilah ka khula hua radd hai, kyunki woh ibadat ka sawab wajib aur zaroori samajhte hain. Yaad rahe ke aayat-e-kareema "اُدْعُوۡنِیۡۤ اَسْتَجِبْ" ke ma'ni yeh hain: Tum mujhe pukaro, main jawab dunga; ya tum mujh se dua karo, main sawab dunga. Dua ki qabooliyat ka waada nahin hai. Is liye yeh hadith is aayat ke khilaaf nahin.

**4.** Yani yeh log apne liye kuch nahin maang rahe, balki Deen phaila rahe hain. In ki khidmat yaqini taur par qabil-e-qadr hai. Yaad rahe ke be-amal Aalim us andhe chiragh ki tarah hai jo apne chiragh se khud faida nahin uthata, lekin doosre log faida utha lete hain. Magar na-qabool ibadat bilkul bekaar hai, jis se kisi ko faida nahin pahunchta. Is liye hadith par koi aitraaz nahin. Be-amal Aalim aise hi hai jaise beemar tabeeb doosron ka ilaaj kar de.

**5.** سبحان اللّٰه! Ilm ki majlis kitni barkat wali hai. Ab bhi Sarkar ﷺ Ulama hi mein tashreef farma rehte hain; unhein ilm ki majlis mein dhoondo. Yaad rahe ke Huzoor ﷺ agarche pehle darje ke Aabid bhi hain, lekin Huzoor ﷺ ki ibadat amali taleem hai. Is liye Aap ﷺ namaz padhte hue bhi Mu'allim hain aur Aap ﷺ ki tashreef-aawari ka asal maqsad taleem hai. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ"۔

## Hadith 258

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=258`; `source_id=3962`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ قَالَ: سُئِلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : مَا حَدُّ الْعِلْمِ الَّذِي إِذَا بَلَغَهُ الرَّجُلُ كَانَ فَقِيهًا؟ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  -: "مَنْ حَفِظَ عَلَى أُمَّتِي أَرْبَعِينَ حَدِيثًا فِي أَمْرِ دِينَهَا بَعَثَهُ اللَّهُ فَقِيهًا، وَكُنْتُ لَهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَة شَافِعًا وَشَهِيدًا".

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Abu Darda رضی اللہ عنہ se. Nabi ﷺ se poocha gaya ke ilm ki woh had kya hai jahan aadmi pahunch jaaye to Aalim ho jaata hai? Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: Jo meri Ummat tak Deen ke ahkaam ki chalis ahadith pahuncha de, Allah Ta'ala use Faqeeh bana kar uthayega aur Qayamat ke din main us ka Shafi' aur Gawah hoon ga **1**.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Is hadith ke bahut se pehlu hain: Chalis ahadith yaad karke Musalmanon ko sunana, unhein chapwa kar logon mein taqseem karna, tarjuma ya sharh karke logon ko samjhana, aur rawiyon se sun kar unhein kitaabi shakal mein jama karna, yeh sab is mein shamil hain. Yani jo kisi bhi tareeqe se Deeni masail ki chalis ahadith meri Ummat tak pahuncha de, Qayamat mein us ka hashr Ulama-e-Deen ke giroh mein hoga. Main us ke liye khaas Shafa'at karunga aur us ke Imaan aur Taqwa ki khaas gawahi dunga. Warna aam Shafa'at aur gawahi to har Musalman ko naseeb hogi. Isi hadith ki wajah se taqreeban tamam Muhaddiseen ne jab ahadith ke daftar likhe to alag Chalis Ahadith, jinhein "Arba'eeniya" kaha jaata hai, bhi jama ki. Imam Nawawi aur Sheikh Abdul Haq Dehlawi ki Arba'eeniya mashhoor hain. Faqeer ne bhi apni kitab "Saltanat-e-Mustafa" mein chalis ahadith jama ki hain.

## Hadith 259

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=259`; `source_id=3978`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  : "هَلْ تَدْرُونَ مَنْ أَجْوَدُ جُودًا؟ " قَالُوا: اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ. قَالَ: "اللَّهُ تَعَالَى أَجْوَدُ جُودًا، ثُمَّ أَنَا أَجْوَدُ بَنِي آدَمَ، وَأَجْوَدُهُمْ مِنْ بَعْدِي رَجُلٌ عَلِمَ عِلْمًا فَنَشَرَهُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ أَمِيرًا وَحْدَهُ، أَو قَالَ: أُمَّةً وَاحِدَةً".

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai Hazrat Anas ibn Malik se. Farmate hain, Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: Kya tum jaante ho ke sab se bada sakhi kaun hai? Sahaba ne arz ki: Allah aur Rasool behtar jaante hain **1**. Farmaya: Allah Ta'ala sab se bada Jowad hai **2**. Phir main aulaad-e-Adam mein sab se bada Sakhi-Daata hoon **3**. Aur mere baad sab se bada Sakhi woh shakhs hai jo ilm seekhe, phir use phailaaye **4**. Woh Qayamat mein akela Ameer ban kar, ya ek Ummat ki haisiyat se, aayega **5**.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Yeh Sahaba ka adab hai ke unhon ne na "la" kaha aur na "bala" keh kar yeh kaha ke haan, hum jaante hain, taake Huzoor ﷺ par pesh-qadmi na ho. Is se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ke saath Rasool Allah ﷺ ka zikr mila kar karna aur dono hastiyon ke liye ek hi sigha lana jaiz hai. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "اَغْنٰہُمُ اللّٰهُ وَرَسُولُہٗ". Is liye keh sakte hain: Allah aur Rasool Aalim-o-Khabir hain; Allah aur Rasool ne ghani kar diya; Allah aur Rasool bhala karein, waghera.

**2.** Arab ke muhawarah mein aam taur par Sakhi use kehte hain jo khud bhi khaaye aur doosron ko bhi khilaaye. Jowad woh hai jo khud na khaaye aur doosron ko khilaaye. Isi liye Allah Ta'ala ko Sakhi nahin kaha jaata. Sakhi ke muqabil Bakhil hai jo khud khaaye aur doosron ko na khilaaye. Jowad ka muqabil **مُمْسِكْ** hai jo na khaaye aur na kisi ko khaane de. Allah Ta'ala ki tamaam dunyawi aur ukhrawi ne'matein duniya walon ke liye hain, Allah Ta'ala ke liye nahin.

**3.** Yeh farman fakhr ke taur par nahin, balki shukr ke taur par hai. Huzoor ﷺ poori makhlooq se zyada Sakhi hain. Chunki insaan Ashraf-ul-Makhlooq hai, is liye us ka zikr farmaya. Huzoor ﷺ Jood-e-Ilaahi ke mazhar hain. Rab Ta'ala ki tamaam zahiri aur batini ne'matein Huzoor ﷺ ke haathon makhlooq ko milti hain. Huzoor ﷺ khud farmate hain: Allah deta hai aur main taqseem karne wala hoon. Is hadith mein Allah Ta'ala aur Huzoor ﷺ ki sakhawatein baghair kisi qaid ke bayan hui hain. Zahir hai ke Sakhi wahi hoga jo Maalik bhi ho. Is liye Huzoor ﷺ Maalik-e-Kaainaat hain.

**4.** Yahan baad se murad rutbe ki baad hai, zamaane ki baad nahin. Is liye is mein Sahaba-e-Kiram aur Qayamat tak aane wale Ulama sab shamil hain. Yani meri sakhawat ke baad Aalim-e-Deen ka darja hai. Maal ki sakhawat ke muqable mein ilm ki sakhawat afzal hai, aur kyun na ho: Huzoor ﷺ abr-e-rahmat hain aur Ulama-e-Deen us ka talab. Yaad rahe ke Ulama ki sakhawat mein ilm ki qaid hai, jabke Huzoor ﷺ ki sakhawat be-qaid hai. Ilm phailana dars-o-tadrees ke zariye ho ya tasneef ke zariye, dono shamil hain.

**5.** Yani us din Aalim-e-Deen Imam hoga aur saare Aabid, namazi aur shaheed waghera us ke ma'tat honge, kyunki jis ne jo neki Aalim ke batane se ki, Aalim ko us ka bhi sawab milega. Ya ek Aalim ko saare Musalmanon ke barabar sawab milega aur sab ke Hajj, Jihad waghera mein us ka hissa hoga. "Ek Ummat" hone ka matlab yahi hai. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "اِنَّ اِبْرٰہِیۡمَ کَانَ اُمَّۃً"۔

## Hadith 260

**Source metadata:** `book_id=1`; `volume_id=1`; `hadith_no=260`; `source_id=3986`; source book `Mirat-ul-Manajih Sharh Mishkat-ul-Masabih`; lesson `Ilm ki kitab`; chapter `Ilm ka bayan`; sub-chapter `Teesri fasl`.

**Arabic hadith:**

وَعَنْهُ أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ  - قَالَ: "مَنْهُومَانِ لَا يَشْبَعَانِ مَنْهُومٌ فِي الْعِلْمِ لَا يَشْبَعُ مِنْهُ، وَمَنْهُومٌ فِي الدُّنْيَا لَا يَشْبَعُ مِنْهَا". رَوَى الْبَيْهَقِيُّ الأَحَادِيثَ الثَّلَاثَةَ فِي "شُعَبِ الإِيْمَانِ" وَقَالَ: قَالَ الإِمَامُ أَحْمَدُ فِي حَدِيثِ أَبِي الدَّرْدَاءِ: هَذَا مَتْنٌ مَشْهُورٌ فِيمَا بَيْنَ النَّاسِ وَلَيْسَ لَهُ إِسْنَادٌ صَحِيحٌ.

**Lafzi tarjuma:** Riwayat hai unhi se ke Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: Do hirs wale kabhi sair nahin hote: ek ilm ka hirs wala jo ilm se sair nahin hota, aur doosra duniya ka hirs wala jo duniya se sair nahin hota **1**. Yeh teenon ahadith Baihaqi ne "Shu'ab-ul-Iman" mein riwayat ki hain. Unhon ne farmaya: Imam Ahmad ne Hazrat Abu Darda ki hadith ke baare mein farmaya ke logon mein is ka matn mashhoor hai, lekin is ki sanad sahih nahin **2**.

**Poori wazahat:**

**1.** Hirs ke ma'ni hamesha mazeed ki khwahish hain. Dunyawi hirs buri hai aur Deeni hirs achhi hai. Aalim ko ilm se kabhi sair nahin hoti. Yeh Allah Ta'ala ki ne'mat hai. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "قُلۡ رَّبِّ زِدْنِیۡ عِلْمًا". Duniya-daar duniya se sair nahin hota, jaise Jalandhar ka mareez paani se sair nahin hota. Yaad rahe ke yeh dono apne liye hain. Huzoor ﷺ Ummat ke liye hain; Aap ﷺ Ummat se lekar bhi sair nahin hote aur dekar bhi sair nahin hote. Rab Ta'ala farmata hai: "حَرِیۡصٌ عَلَیۡکُمۡ". Lafz ek hai, lekin ma'ni alag hain.

**2.** Imam Nawawi ne apni Chalis Ahadith mein farmaya ke Hazrat Abu Darda ki hadith bahut si sanad se riwayat hui hai. Woh sab zaeef hain, lekin sanad ki kasrat aur Ulama ke qabool kar lene ki wajah se hadith qawi ho jaayegi, kyunki kai sanad hone se zaeef hadith Hasan ban jaati hai. Is ke ilawa faza'il-e-a'maal mein zaeef hadith maqbool hoti hai. (Mirqaat aur Ashi'at-ul-Lama'at)
