# Flagged Translation Comparison

This file compares selected Roman Urdu word choices against the English source so they can be reviewed and standardized.

## 1. `jaanti` / `rakhti hai` for Allah

**Location:** `03-chapter-three/03-choices-and-insights.md:69`

**English source:**

> There is only one answer, and that is, it could only be by the divine directive of the One who has foreknowledge of every minuscule detail in the past, and every possible outcome required in the future, who has complete authority over all of those inter-connected matters.

**Current Roman Urdu:**

> Yeh sab Zaat-e-Ilahi ke hukm se hua jo maazi ki har bareek se bareek tafseel aur mustaqbil ke har mumkin anjaam ka pehle se ilm rakhta hai, aur in tamam jude hue maamlon par mukammal ikhtiyar rakhta hai.

**Review:**

The selected wording uses `Zaat-e-Ilahi ke hukm se` and masculine agreement for the description that follows:

> Yeh sab Zaat-e-Ilahi ke hukm se hua jo maazi ki har bareek se bareek tafseel aur mustaqbil ke har mumkin anjaam ka pehle se ilm rakhta hai, aur in tamam jude hue maamlon par mukammal ikhtiyar rakhta hai.

The same masculine agreement was also applied to the related wording in `03-chapter-three/03-choices-and-insights.md:61` and `10-chapter-ten/01-knowledge-and-patience.md:16`.

## 3. `Allah ki awaaz`

**Location:** `04-chapter-four/01-musa-and-the-mission.md:22`

**English source:**

> a voice said it was that of God, the Lord of All the Universe, who was communicating with him (28:30).

**Final Roman Urdu:**

> Unhein bataya gaya ke yeh tamam jahanon ke Rab Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se aane wali awaaz hai jo un se kalaam farma raha hai.

**Review:**

The selected review option avoids an unintended physical implication while preserving the source:

> Unhein bataya gaya ke yeh tamam jahanon ke Rab Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se aane wali awaaz hai jo un se kalaam farma raha hai.

## 4. `ilahi tasalli`

**Location:** `04-chapter-four/02-testing-times.md:53`

**English source:**

> In hindsight, this is God’s divine consoling to us

**Final Roman Urdu:**

> Is mein Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se hamare liye tasalli hai.

**Review:**

The selected wording removes the repetition in `Allah ki ilahi tasalli`:

> Is mein Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se hamare liye tasalli hai.

This is clearer and more natural in Roman Urdu.

## 5. `dekh-bhaal` for Allah

### 5a. Allah cares for His servants

**Location:** `05-chapter-five/02-fire-and-ice.md:37`

**English source:**

> God takes such great care of His servants

**Final Roman Urdu:**

> Allah Ta’ala ne apne bande par itna karam farmaya

**Review:**

The selected review option replaces `dekh-bhaal` with `karam farmaya`:

> Allah Ta’ala ne apne bande par itna karam farmaya

### 5b. Allah considers every aspect

**Location:** `05-chapter-five/01-unlimited-connection.md:119`

**English source:**

> when God Almighty takes care of His servants, He takes every aspect into account

**Final Roman Urdu:**

> jab Allah Ta’ala apne bandon par karam farmata hai to har pehlu ka lihaz rakhta hai

**Review:**

The selected review option replaces `dekh-bhaal` with `karam farmata hai`:

> jab Allah Ta’ala apne bandon par karam farmata hai to har pehlu ka lihaz rakhta hai

### 5c. Aftercare of recovery

**Location:** `05-chapter-five/01-unlimited-connection.md:125`

**English source:**

> even the ‘aftercare’ of your recovery will be tended to

**Final Roman Urdu:**

> un ki sehat-yabi ke baad ki zarooriyat ka intezam bhi Allah ke zimme hota hai

**Review:**

The selected review option renders “aftercare” as the provision of post-recovery needs:

> un ki sehat-yabi ke baad ki zarooriyat ka intezam bhi Allah ke zimme hota hai

### 5d. The finest details of life

**Location:** `07-chapter-seven/02-the-real-challenge.md:43`

**English source:**

> He who takes care of the finest of details in the story of Life

**Current Roman Urdu:**

> Pas Us Allah par bharosa karo jo zindagi ki kahani ki bareek se bareek tafseel ka bhi intezam farmata hai.

The other occurrence in `05-chapter-five/01-unlimited-connection.md:34` was also standardized as `hamari dekh-rekh farmata hai`.

**Review:**

The selected review option uses direct reference to Allah and replaces `dekh-bhaal` with `intezam farmata hai`:

> Pas Us Allah par bharosa karo jo zindagi ki kahani ki bareek se bareek tafseel ka bhi intezam farmata hai.

## 7. `dono` in the Qur’ān translation

### 7a. Surah Ar-Rahmān 55:13

**Location:** `09-chapter-nine/01-gratitude-and-choice.md:14`

**English source:**

> So which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?

**Current Roman Urdu:**

> To tum apne Rab ki kaun kaun si nematon ko jhutlaoge?

**Review:**

The English translation uses “you” without showing the Arabic dual. The selected wording follows the English source and removes `dono`:

> To tum apne Rab ki kaun kaun si nematon ko jhutlaoge?

### 7b. Repeated quotation

**Location:** `09-chapter-nine/01-gratitude-and-choice.md:40`

**English source:**

> So, which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?

**Current Roman Urdu:**

> To tum apne Rab ki kaun kaun si nematon ko jhutlaoge?

**Review:**

Use the same decision as in 7a and remove `dono` here as well.

## 8. `Noble` as an English word

**Location:** `08-chapter-eight/02-the-missing-peace.md:27`

**English source:**

> “Take it, and God will help you to pay off what you owe,” replied the Noble Messenger k.

**Current Roman Urdu:**

> Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Yeh le lo, Allah tumhare qarz ki adaigi mein tumhari madad farmayega.”

**Review:**

`Noble` should not remain in English because an appropriate reverent Roman Urdu rendering is available. Prefer:

> Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Yeh le lo, Allah tumhare qarz ki adaigi mein tumhari madad farmayega.”

## Recommended Standardization

1. Use `Allah Ta’ala` or `Us Allah` when direct reference is clearer than `Us Zaat`.
2. Use masculine agreement for direct references to Allah: `jaanta hai`, `rakhta hai`, `farmata hai`.
3. Replace repetitive `Allah ki ilahi` with `Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se` or `hukm-e-ilahi`.
4. Use `Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ` instead of `Noble Rasool ﷺ`.
5. Use `tum` rather than `tum dono` in the Surah Ar-Rahmān translation, following the English source.
6. Review `dekh-bhaal` case by case; use `karam farmana` for divine grace and `intezam farmana` for provision or aftercare.
