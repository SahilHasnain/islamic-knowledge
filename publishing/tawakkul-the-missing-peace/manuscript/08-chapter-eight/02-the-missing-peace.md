Salmān apne malik ke paas lauta aur rozana ke kaam karta raha. Ek baar phir raat ki tareeki mein woh chupke se Qubā ki taraf nikal gaya. Is baar us ne us shakhs se mulaqat ki jo Nabi hone ka da‘wa karte the. Us ne arz ki: “Main ne dekha hai ke aap khairaat mein diya hua khana nahi khate. Is liye yeh tohfa qabool farmaiye, jise main aap ki ta’zeem ke liye pesh karna chahta hoon.”

Salmān ne phir ghaur se dekha. Is baar Huzoor ﷺ ne us mein se kuch khaya aur apne ashaab ko bhi khane ka hukm diya. Salmān ne dil mein kaha: “Yeh doosri nishani bhi poori ho gayi.”

Salmān ek baar phir apne malik ki jaidad mein laut gaya aur ghulam ki haisiyat se apne kaam karta raha. Phir raat ko woh Qubā ki taraf nikal gaya. Ab tak use yaqeen ho chuka tha ke us ke ustad ne jo tamam nishaniyan batayi thin woh poori ho chuki hain. Muhammad ﷺ Arab the, Hazrat Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka deen pesh kar rahe the, aur unhon ne Madīnah ke is ilaqe ki taraf hijrat farmayi thi jo do pahariyon ke darmiyan tha aur khajooron ke darakhton ki wajah se mashhoor tha. Huzoor ﷺ khairaat qabool nahi farmate the lekin tohfa qabool farmate the. Ab sirf ek nishani baqi thi: dono shanon ke darmiyan nishan-e-nubuwwat.

Lekin Salmān is nishani ki tasdeeq kaise kar sakta tha? Qubā pohanchne par us ne logon ko nihayat ghamgeen haalat mein dekha, kyunke Huzoor ﷺ ke ashaab mein se ek ka inteqal ho gaya tha. Huzoor ﷺ do kapde pehne hue the, ek upar aur ek neeche. Salmān ne socha ke woh Huzoor ﷺ ke peechhe baithne ki koshish karega taake shanon ke darmiyan ka hissa dekh sake.

Salmān baad mein batate the ke aisa maloom hota tha jaise Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ko bilkul maloom ho ke Salmān kya dhoond raha hai. Huzoor ﷺ ne bila kisi zahiri wajah ke apna upar ka kapda zara sa neeche kiya. Salmān ne woh cheez dekh li jiske liye us ne apni azadi kho di thi: nishan-e-nubuwwat us ki aankhon ke saamne tha.

Jo kuch us ne dekha us ki khushi mein Salmān ne Huzoor ﷺ ko pakad liya aur nishan-e-nubuwwat ko bosa diya. Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Ae Salmān! Peechhe mud jao.” Salmān peechhe muda aur Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ko apni poori kahani sunayi.

Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> Ghazwa-e-Badr aur Ghazwa-e-Uhud ke daur mein bhi ghulam rahe. Aakhir Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne unhein apni azadi khareedne ka mashwara diya. Yeh koi aasaan kaam nahi tha. Aakhir kaun ek ghulam ki madad karna chahta? Un ke malik ne 300 khajoor ke darakht aur 40 Ūqiyah<sup>73</sup> sona maanga.

Jaisa ke Salmān ko andesha tha, un ke malik ne aisi mushkil shart rakhi jo namumkin lagti thi. Woh itna sona kahan se laate? 300 pauday kahan se milte? Agar itne beej mil bhi jate to unhein lagate kahan? Zarkhaiz zameen pehle hi logon ke qabze mein thi aur daldali zameen darakhton ke liye munasib nahi thi, balki us mein kaam karna bahut bada khatra tha.

Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne apne ashaab se farmaya: “Apne bhai ki madad karo.” Is par ashaab ne pauday jama karne mein Salmān ki madad ki. Ek sahabi ne kaha: “Meri taraf se tees chhote darakht.” Doosre ne bees diye, kisi ne pandrah diye aur kisi ne das. Har shakhs ne apni haisiyat ke mutabiq diya, yahan tak ke Salmān ke liye 300 chhote darakht jama ho gaye.

Ab unhein lagane ke liye zarkhaiz zameen dhoondna baqi tha. Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne phir farmaya: “Ae Salmān! Jahan yeh darakht lagne hain wahan gaddhe khod do. Jab tum farigh ho jao to mere paas aana, main apne haath se unhein lagaunga.”

Salmān ne gaddhe khod diye aur Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ka intezar karne lage. Huzoor ﷺ ne apne mubarak haathon se ek ek darakht lagaya. Jab darakht bade hue to Salmān ne kaha: “Khuda ki qasam! Har darakht poori tarah ug aaya aur in mein se koi ek bhi nahi mara.”

Salmān ne khajooron ke baagh ki milkiyat<sup>74</sup> apne malik ke hawale kar di. Malik ne darakht qabool kar liye, lekin Salmān ab bhi baqi sone ki adaigi nahi kar sake. Kuch arsa guzra. Ek Ghazwa se Huzoor ﷺ ki khidmat mein ande ke barabar sone ka ek tukda laya gaya. Huzoor ﷺ ne apne ashaab se poocha: “Us Farsi shakhs ka kya hua jis ne azadi ka mu‘ahida kiya hai?”

Salmān ko Huzoor ﷺ ke paas laya gaya. Huzoor ﷺ ne sone ka woh tukda unhein de kar farmaya: “Yeh le lo aur jo tumhare zimme hai ada kar do, ae Salmān!” Salmān ne arz ki: “Ya RasoolAllah! Yeh mere tamam qarz ko kaise poora karega? Aakhir main pehle tijarat karne wala aadmi tha aur jaanta hoon ke yeh miktar kafi nahi.”

Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Yeh le lo, Allah tumhare qarz ki adaigi mein tumhari madad farmayega.” Salmān ne woh sona le liya, halanke unhein maloom tha ke yeh baat zahiri mantiq ke mutabiq nahi. Jab unhon ne use apne malik ke saamne tola to khushi se pukar uthe: “Us Zaat ki qasam jis ke qabze mein Salmān ki jaan hai, yeh to 40 Ūqiyah ke barabar hai!”

Salmān ne apna qarz ada kar diya. Takleef-deh ghulami ke kai saal guzarne ke baad aakhirkar unhein azadi mil gayi.”

Yeh Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ki kahani thi. Unhon ne haq ki talash mein zindagi ka woh sukoon paa liya jis ki unhein itni shiddat se talaash thi. Aur ab woh hazaron meel door bataye gaye waadon ke mutabiq zahir hone wale Nabi ﷺ ke ba-kamal sahabi the!<sup>75</sup>

Bachpan se hi humein ‘Snow White and the Seven Dwarves’, ‘Beauty and the Beast’ aur ‘Sleeping Beauty’ jaisi pari-kahaniyan sunayi jati hain. In kahaniyon ke zariye har bachcha aisi zindagi ka khwab dekhna shuru kar deta hai jo “ho sakti hai”, aisi zindagi jismein be-shumar imkanaat hon, kyunke in sab kahaniyon ka anjaam khushgawar hota hai.

Khushi ki talash bachpan hi se hamare andar basa di jati hai. Lekin humein koi yeh nahi batata ke hum khushi ka peecha is liye karte hain kyunke hum zindagi mein lagataar sukoon aur rahat dhoond rahe hote hain. Insaan pareshani aur be-aarami par manfi taur par react karta hai. Isi liye hum zindagi ke safar mein gum ho chuke sukoon ki lagataar talash mein rehte hain.

Yahi wajah hai ke hum us sukoon ka peecha karte rehte hain, chahe yeh kisi purani tamanna ko poora karne ke zariye ho ya daulat ki apni pasandeeda cheez haasil karne ke zariye. Is ke baraks media mein aise karodpatiyon ki saikdon khabrein aati rehti hain jo daulatmand hone ke bawajood khudkushi kar chuke, nasha karne lage ya sharab ke sabab apni zindagi barbaad kar baithe.

Aise logon ki khabrein bhi milti hain jo apne pasandeeda peshe mein itne masroof ho gaye ke un ke paas apne ghar walon ke saath baith kar khana khane ka bhi bunyadi waqt nahi raha. Unhein aisa mehsoos hota hai jaise un ke paas “saans lene” ka bhi waqt nahi.

Beshak unhon ne zindagi bhar ke apne khwab poore kar liye, lekin badhati hui pareshani aur zindagi ke nuqsan ke ilawa unhein badle mein kya mila? Is liye humein apne aap se poochna chahiye ke hamare liye asal mein kya aham hai?

Tawakkul ke mauzu par is bayan mein Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ki kahani batati hai ke agarche hum kisi khaas raaste par chal rahe hon, lekin asal mein humein nahi maloom hota ke woh raasta humein kahan le jayega. Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ne duniya ke kisi faide ke liye nahi, balke Allah ki durust ibadat ki talash mein ek raasta ikhtiyar kiya. Unhein kya maloom tha ke un ka safar jitna pur-khatar tha, utna hi woh us waqt ke ultimate GPS ke zariye naye raaste par lagaye ja rahe the. Jo cheez humein “badi ghalati” nazar aa sakti thi, woh anjaane mein Salmān <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ke sab se behtareen intikhab par khatam hui.

Allah Ta’ala Qur’ān-e-Kareem mein farmata hai:

وَالَّذِينَ اهْتَدَوْا زَادَهُمْ هُدًى وَآتَاهُمْ تَقْوَاهُمْ

“Aur jo log hidayat paa gaye, Allah ne un ki hidayat aur zyada kar di aur unhein parhezgari ata farmayi.”<sup>76</sup>

Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ki kahani is ayat ke waade ka amali izhar samjhi ja sakti hai, kyunke woh har qeemat par Allah ki talash mein the. Unhon ne apne baap ke saamne baat rakhi, halanke woh jaante the ke is ke bure asraat honge. Isi wajah se unhein ghar mein nazarband kar diya gaya. Is ke bawajood woh hidayat aur Allah ke saamne pur-sukoon tasleem-o-raza ki haalat ki talash jaari rakhte rahe.

2020 mein Covid-19 ke phailao ki wajah se duniya bhar ke logon ne lockdown ka tajurba kiya. Kuch log zehni taur par toot gaye, halanke unhein zaroori ashya khareedne ke liye ghar se bahar nikalne ki ijazat thi. Is ke baraks Salmān apne hi ghar mein qaidiyon ki tarah zanjeeron mein jakde hue the. Woh aasani se apne baap ki baat maan kar zindagi guzarte reh sakte the. Mumkin hai ke woh bhi apne baap ki tarah apni qaum ke sardaar ban jate. Lekin woh apni zindagi ke us gumshuda sukoon ki talash mein the jo duniya ki koi cheez poora nahi kar sakti.

Woh baghair dare Fars (Iran) se Shām, Shām se Iraq, phir dobara Shām, us ke baad jadeed Turkey aur aakhirkar Arab tak safar karte rahe. Is safar mein unhon ne apni azadi kho di aur ek zaleel samjhe jaane wale ghulam ke rutbe tak pohanch gaye. Aise halaat se guzarne ke baad bhi Allah par “imaan” aur “bharosa” rakhna kisi aam shakhs ke liye aasaan nahi. Yahin sachche momin aur zahiri momin ke darmiyan bunyadi farq samajh aata hai.

Ek zahiri ya kamzor momin us waqt tak apne imaan mein mazboot rehta hai jab tak qudrati asbaab us ke haq mein kaam karte rahein. Jis lamhe halaat us ke khilaf hone lagen, woh Allah ke mansube par sawal uthana shuru kar deta hai. Is ke baraks sachcha momin apne saamne rakhe gaye har haal ke saath hum-aahang rehta hai, kyunke woh jaanta hai ke Allah ka mansuba zindagi ke nizam ko samajhne ki us ki apni salahiyat se kahin behtar hai.

Agar tum Allah par bharosa rakho to woh tumhein ek zaleel samjhe jaane wale ghulam ke maqam se uthakar qaum ka rehnuma bana sakta hai. Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ke aamaal, rawaiye aur hairat-angez tawakkul Allah Ta’ala ko itne pasand aaye ke taqreeban 1400 saal baad bhi Musalman unhein yaad kar rahe hain. Yahan tak ke un ki kahani jadeed daur mein tawakkul ki ek misaal ke taur par ek kitab ke bab ka mauzu ban gayi hai!

Salmān <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ko kya maloom tha ke woh “Salmān Ghulam” se “Sayyidunā” yani hamare sardaar, Salmān Al-Fārsī <sup>رضي الله تعالى عنه</sup> ban jayenge. Un ki kahani humein sikhati hai ke hamari kahani hum khud nahi likh rahe. Is liye humein zindagi ke asal Musannif par bharosa rakhna chahiye, jis ka qalam kabhi tedha nahi hota aur jis ki siyahi kabhi khatam nahi hoti. Behtareen anjaam tarteeb dene ki us ki salahiyat be-hadd hai, hamari kahani ke tamam kirdaron ka us ka ilm la-mehdood hai, aur us ne tamam makhlooq ke wajood mein aane se pehle hi Qalam-e-Muqaddas se hamari kahani likh di thi!

Kya aisi makhlooq ke Musannif par poora yaqeen rakhna munasib nahi? Is liye aao us par bharosa rakhein aur tasleem kar lein ke humein us simt le jaya ja raha hai jahan ka anjaam Us Zaat ne muqarrar farmaya hai jo har faisle ki mustahiq hai.

Zindagi ke safar mein jis gumshuda sukoon ke liye hum be-hadd pareshan rehte hain woh kisi door ke mulk ke ufaq se pare maujood nahi. Woh hamare andar, hamare seenon ki gehraiyon mein maujood hai. Hamara yahi dil agar Allah Ta’ala ke saath hum-aahang ho jaye to is aarzi duniya ke har guzarte lamhe mein sukoon hamara mustaqil tajurba ban jayega.
