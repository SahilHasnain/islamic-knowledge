# Bab Seesra (Chapter Three)
# Jab Anjaam Achha Ho (All's Well That Ends Well)

“Hum mein se bahut se log aisi soorat-e-haal se guzre hain jab bad-kirdar logon ki saazishon, hasad, jalan aur dushmani ne hamari duniya hila di aur apne ghaleez mansubon se humein sakht nuqsaan pahunchaaya. Is liye is bab ki kahani un logon ke liye umeed ki roshni hai jo hasad ka shikar hue hain. Unhein is qisse ki taraf dobara dekhna chahiye aur Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul rakhna chahiye, is umeed ke saath ke Allah ka mansuba hi sab se behtar hai aur aakhir mein wahi ghalib aayega!”

“Mujhe akela chhod do! Tum mere saath aisa kyun kar rahe ho? Maine tumhara kya bigada hai?” chhota bachcha baar baar cheekh raha tha, jab gaon ke badmaash ladke use utha kar le ja rahe the aur un mein se kuch apni saazish ke khul jaane ke khauf se us ki awaaz dabane ki koshish kar rahe the. “Tum mujhe kahan le ja rahe ho?” bachcha baar baar poochta raha. Das ladkon mein se ek ne kaha, “Isay qatl kar do! Abhi qatl kar do aur qissa khatam karo!” Kuch ne kaha, “Haan, haan!” lekin doosre mukhalifat karne lage: “Nahi! Hum aisa nahi kar sakte!”

Phir unhon ne bachche ko baandh diya aur bahas karte rahe, yahan tak ke sab ne use gehre kuen mein daalne ka faisla kar liya. “Gehra kuan?” das ladkon mein se ek ne poocha, kyunke sirf us naam ka zikr hi us ke andar bhi utna hi khauf paida kar raha tha jitna us ke qaidi bachche ke andar. Raat ke andhere ne un badmaash ladkon ko mauqa de diya. Woh bachche ko gaon ki hudood se bahut door le gaye, yahan tak ke ek aise kuen ke kinare pohanch gaye jo poore ilaqe mein purana, khaufnaak, sab se gehra aur is liye sab se tareek mashhoor tha. “Isay andar daal do aur jo hamara hai woh hasil kar lo,” un mein se ek ne kaha. Aur yoon das ladkon ne apne chhote qaidi ki insani cheekhon se be-niyaz ho kar use tareek kuen ki gehraiyon mein daal diya...

Hum mein se bahut se log is jaisi soorat-e-haal se guzre hain, jab kuch log mil kar dushmani ki bina par hamare khilaf khade ho jaate hain aur humein apne mahaul se nikalne ki saazish karte hain. Yeh kaam ki jagah, taleemi mahaul ya badi khandani zindagi mein bhi ho sakta hai. Aise log zulm aur aise halaat paida karke hamari awaaz daba dete hain jahan hum apni rai dene se bhi darte hain, kyunke humein anjaam ka khauf hota hai. Un ka hasad, jalan aur dushmani unhein un insani rishton se andha kar deti hai jo humein ek doosre se jodte hain, aur unhein apne aamaal ke nateejon ka bhi ehsaas nahi rehta. Aakhir mein woh humein zehni tareeki ki aisi haalat mein chhod dete hain jahan hum na-umeedi aur pareshani ka shikar ho jaate hain.

Kuch log is soorat-e-haal par gusse aur sakhti se palat kar jawab dete hain, aur is ka bhi bura anjaam hota hai. Bahut se log jab hasad karne walon ke zulm se tang aa jaate hain to had paar kar ke un ka gusse bhara zubani ya jismani muqabla karte hain. Is ke nateeje mein mukhalif unhein aisa phansa dete hain ke woh apni naukri tak kho baithte hain.

Har haal mein hasad karne wale saathiyon aur badmaash logon ke mazloomon ko zehni aur jismani sadma sehna padta hai. Lekin agar aisa koi tareeqa ho ke jawab diye baghair bhi jawab diya ja sake to?

Allah Ta’ala batata hai ke Qur’ān aisi kitaab hai jo mukhtalif maqsad poore karne ke liye nazil ki gayi hai. Un mein se ek yeh hai:

إِنَّا أَنْزَلْنَاهُ قُرْآنًا عَرَبِيًّا لَعَلَّكُمْ تَعْقِلُونَ

“Beshak humne ise Arabi Qur’ān bana kar nazil kiya taake tum samajh sako.”<sup>17</sup>

Ittifaq se Sūrah Yūsuf hi woh surah hai jismein yeh aayat maujood hai. Is mein Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke qisse ke zariye humein sikhaya gaya hai ke baghair jawab diye jawab kaise diya jaye. Is ko samajhne ke liye zaroori hai ke hum Qur’ān ke paigham par tawakkul aur bharosa rakhein, Allah ki di hui samajh ko istemal karein, aur baqi maamla Allah Ta’ala par chhod dein!

Islami riwayat ke mutabiq Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke bete the. Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke ek biwi se das bete aur doosri biwi se do bete the. Yeh barah bete Bani Israil ke buniyaadi buzurg bane. Isi liye Qur’ān mein yeh qissa un logon ke liye ghaur ka samaan rakhta hai jo samajh se kaam lete hain: yeh Qur’ān ke barahwein juz mein, barahwein surah mein aur barah rukooon mein taqseem hai.<sup>18</sup>

Yeh qissa Qur’ān mein ek khaas andaz se bayan hua hai. Yeh wahid qissa hai jo ek hi surah mein mukammal aur waqiaat ki tartib ke saath bayan kiya gaya. Is surah ne khud apne qisse ko ‘Ahsan-ul-Qasas’ yani sab se behtareen qisson ka naam diya. Tawakkul ke pehlu se is qisse ki khubsurti aur bhi wazeh ho jati hai. Isi liye Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa itni tafseel aur fasahat se ek hi surah mein bayan hua ke Qur’ān mein ise doosri jagah dohrane ki zarurat nahi rahi.

Yeh baat wazeh rehni chahiye ke hamari is kitaab ka yeh bab na to tareekhi bayan hai aur na Qur’ān ki is surah ki tafseer ka daawa. Hum is qisse ko sirf tawakkul aur Allah Ta’ala par bharose ko samajhne ke pehlu se dekhenge.

Ulama ke mutabiq Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> apne walid ki doosri biwi ke bete the. Woh nihayat haseen-o-jameel bachche the aur un ke walid un se bahut muhabbat karte the. Is wajah se un ke das sautelay bhaiyon ke dil mein purana hasad paida hua, jo aakhirkar gehri nafrat mein badal gaya. Phir unhon ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko utha le jaane aur halaak karne ki saazish ki.

Allah Ta’ala ne unhein aazmaish ke liye tayyar karne ke taur par ek khwab dikhaya, jismein unhon ne gyarah sitare, sooraj aur chaand ko apne saamne sajda karte dekha. Un ke walid bhi Allah ke Nabi the aur apne beton ke hasad se achhi tarah waqif the. Is liye unhon ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko mashwara diya ke apna khwab kisi bhai ko na batana.

Unhon ne yeh bhi bataya ke Shaytaan khula dushman hai. Yahan Qur’ān humein sikhata hai ke apne khwab aur mansube har shakhs ke saamne bayan na karo, kyunke Shaytaan ki makkaari yeh hai ke woh kabhi tumhare apne qareebi logon ko bhi tumhara dushman bana sakta hai.

Bhaiyon ka barhta hua hasad unhein apne chhote sautelay bhai ko barbaad karne ki saazish tak le gaya. Kuch bhai use qatl karna chahte the, jabke kuch ne kaha ke use kahin door chhod diya jaye, taake walid ki tawajjoh dobara sirf un ki taraf ho jaye.

Phir unhon ne apne walid se ijazat lene ka faisla kiya ke Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko apne saath khule maidan mein khelne ke liye le jayen. Unhon ne walid ko raazi karne ke liye khair-khwahon ka roop dhara aur kaha:

أَرْسِلْهُ مَعَنَا غَدًا يَرْتَعْ وَيَلْعَبْ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ

“Kal use hamare saath bhej dein, woh kuch kha pi kar khele, aur hum yaqeenan us ki hifazat karenge.”<sup>19</sup>

Qur’ān hasad karne walon ki saazishi fitrat ko qari par zahir karta hai. Aise log jhoote waadon ke mahir hote hain. Jab koi tumhein neecha dikhana chahta hai to woh kisi kaam mein tumhari madad ka waada karega, taake tumhein yaqeen ho jaye ke woh tumhari bhalai chahta hai. Lekin jab waada poora karne ka waqt aata hai to woh apni tayyar ki hui baaton aur adakari ke zariye tumhein doosri hi baat ka yaqeen dilane ki koshish karta hai.

Guftagu ko aage barhate hain. Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne apne behtar andazay ke khilaf apne pyaare Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko bhaiyon ke saath jaane ki ijazat de di. Raaste mein bhaiyon ne un ki qameez utaar li taake baad mein use apne jhoote bayan ke saboot ke taur par pesh kar saken. Phir unhon ne unhein ek gehre tareek kuen mein daal diya, jaisa ke Qur’ān farmata hai:

فَلَمَّا ذَهَبُوا بِهِ وَأَجْمَعُوا أَنْ يَجْعَلُوهُ فِي غَيَابَتِ الْجُبِّ ۚ وَأَوْحَيْنَا إِلَيْهِ لَتُنَبِّئَنَّهُمْ بِأَمْرِهِمْ هَٰذَا وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ

“Phir jab woh use le gaye aur sab ne yeh tay kar liya ke use kuen ki gehraiyon mein daal den, to humne Yūsuf ki taraf wahi ki ke tum ek din zaroor unhein un ke is kaam ki khabar doge aur us waqt woh samajh bhi na rahe honge.”<sup>20</sup>

Yahan tawakkul ka gehra dars shuru hota hai. Log is qisse ko bayan karte hain, lekin kya woh is ke gehre ma’ni par ghaur bhi karte hain? Agar yeh qissa kisi tamashe ki kahani hota to log is manzar par apne jazbaati tabsire likhte, us ki tasveerain lagate aur kehte ke yeh manzar kitna asar-angez hai. Lekin afsos! Yeh waqia 1400 saal se hamare saamne Qur’ān mein maujood hai, taake hum is se jazbaati aur fikri faida hasil karein, phir bhi hum is se judne mein buri tarah nakaam rahe hain.

Yahan ek aisa bachcha hai jise us ke apne ghar walon ne marne ke liye chhod diya. Yeh wahi log the jinhein us ki hifazat karni chahiye thi, lekin hasad itna andha kar deta hai ke aqal aur samajh dono par parda pad jata hai. Socho, yeh bachcha apne bhaiyon ke saath khushi aur aitmaad se nikla hoga, phir achanak us ki qameez zabardasti utaar li gayi aur us ka badan khul gaya. Is ke baad, kuch samajhne se pehle hi woh ek gehre, tareek aur thande kuen mein bilkul tanha tha! Kya woh us kuen se nikal sakega? Kya woh dobara apne walid ko dekh sakega? Us waqt us ke kam-umar zehen mein kitne sawal aaye honge!

Ghaur karo to hum mein se bahut se log aisi soorat-e-haal se guzre hain jab hasad, jalan aur dushmani se bhare bad-kirdar logon ki saazishon ne hamari duniya hila di.

Is liye yeh qissa hasad aur dushmani ka shikar hone walon ke liye umeed ki roshni hai. Aise logon ko is qisse ki taraf dobara dekhna chahiye aur Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul rakhna chahiye, is umeed ke saath ke Allah ka mansuba hi sab se behtar hai aur aakhir mein wahi ghalib aayega!

Yeh qissa zulm karne walon ke liye bhi ek tanbeeh hai:

لَقَدْ كَانَ فِي يُوسُفَ وَإِخْوَتِهِ آيَاتٌ لِّلسَّائِلِينَ

“Beshak Yūsuf aur un ke bhaiyon ke qisse mein poochne walon ke liye nishaniyan hain.”<sup>21</sup>

Bahut se qari yeh samajhne mein ghalti karte hain ke agarche Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> is qisse ke markazi kirdar hain, lekin Allah Ta’ala jaanta hai ke hasad karne wale log bhi kabhi Qur’ān khol kar padhte hain. Jab woh is qisse ko padhte hain to is ke zulm karne wale kirdar unhein apne hi rawayye ka aks dikha sakte hain. Yoon yeh qissa un ke liye bhi ek tanbeeh ban jata hai.

Zulm karne walon ke liye yeh qissa yaad-dihani hai ke jis cheez ke peeche woh bhaag rahe hote hain, woh ibtida hi se un ke qabze mein nahi hoti, chahe unhon ne kitni hi bareeki se mansuba bandi ki ho. Aakhir mein Allah hi sab se bada mansuba banane wala hai, aur woh zalimon ko is waqt tak khuli chhutti nahi deta jab tak un ki posheeda saazish ko saamne na le aaye. Is liye agar tum kisi ke khilaf saazish karte ho to hoshiyar raho! Tumhara raaz saamne aane mein sirf waqt lagega.

Aur un mazloomon ke liye jo hasad karne wale bad-kirdar logon ke zubani, jazbaati ya jismani zulm ka lagataar shikar hain, yeh qissa batata hai ke agar tum Allah ke Nabi ke naqsh-e-qadam par chalo to tumhari zindagi ka anjaam bhi badal sakta hai. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke poore qisse par ghaur karne se maloom hota hai ke unhon ne kabhi apni soorat-e-haal ki shikayat nahi ki. Jab bhi un ke aas-paas ke logon ne un ki barbaadi ka mansuba banaya, unhon ne Allah Ta’ala par bharosa rakha. Natije mein aakhirkar unhein kamyabi mili.

Is liye agar tum khud ko kisi tareek jagah mein pao to sabr karo aur Allah par bharosa rakho, kyunke woh hamari mahdood nazar se bahut aage dekh raha hai. Aayat 15 mein Allah Ta’ala batata hai ke Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko ek tanha kuen ki tareek gehrai mein daala gaya, lekin humein yaad rakhna chahiye ke woh tanha nahi the. Allah us din bhi un ke saath tha, jaise aaj hamare saath hai! Allah ne unhein ilham farmaya ke aakhirkar yeh maamla behtareen tareeqe se seedha kar diya jayega.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne Allah Ta’ala par itna bharosa kiya ke aayat 15 ke baad baqi chhiyanwe aayaat padhne par bhi kahin yeh nahi milta ke unhon ne apne bhaiyon se badla lene ka mutalaba kiya ho ya un ke khilaf insaaf ka daawa kiya ho.
