# Misr Mein Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup>

Jab koi shakhs Allah Ta’ala par apna tawakkul sachchai se rakhta hai to apni poori zindagi, hatta ke jab us ke paas ikhtiyar ho tab bhi, faisla Allah ke hawale kar deta hai. Woh Allah ke qanoon par amal karta hai aur yaqeen rakhta hai ke aakhirkar asal insaaf zaroor ghalib aayega.

Bhaiyon ne Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko marne ke liye chhod kar apne walid ke paas wapsi ki. Unhon ne Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki qameez ek jaanwar ke jhoote khoon se range hue pesh ki, taake apne is jhoot ko sach jaisa bana saken ke unhein kisi jaanwar ne kha liya tha. Un ke walid ka jawab Qur’ān mein yun bayan hua hai:

وَجَاءُوا أَبَاهُمْ عِشَاءً يَبْكُونَ ۝ قَالُوا يَا أَبَانَا إِنَّا ذَهَبْنَا نَسْتَبِقُ وَتَرَكْنَا يُوسُفَ عِندَ مَتَاعِنَا فَأَكَلَهُ الذِّئْبُ وَمَا أَنتَ بِمُؤْمِنٍ لَّنَا وَلَوْ كُنَّا صَادِقِينَ ۝ وَجَاءُوا عَلَىٰ قَمِيصِهِ بِدَمٍ كَذِبٍ ۚ قَالَ بَلْ سَوَّلَتْ لَكُمْ أَنفُسُكُمْ أَمْرًا ۖ فَصَبْرٌ جَمِيلٌ ۖ وَاللَّهُ الْمُسْتَعَانُ عَلَىٰ مَا تَصِفُونَ

“Aur woh raat ke waqt apne walid ke paas rote hue aaye. Unhon ne kaha: ‘Abba jaan! Hum daud ke muqable ke liye gaye the aur Yūsuf ko apne samaan ke paas chhod diya tha, phir use bhediya kha gaya. Aap hamari baat ka yaqeen nahi karenge, chahe hum sach hi keh rahe hon.’ Aur woh us ki qameez par jhoota khoon laga kar laaye. Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne kaha: ‘Nahi, tumhare dil ne tumhare liye ek baat bana li hai. Ab achha sabr hi behtar hai, aur jo kuch tum bayan kar rahe ho us par Allah hi se madad maangi jati hai.’”<sup>22</sup>

Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko un ki baat par bharosa nahi hua, lekin ghaur karo ke unhon ne apne pyaare bete ke gayab ho jaane ke bawajood apne bachchon se sakht pooch-gachh nahi ki. Is ke bajaye unhon ne Allah Ta’ala par bharosa rakhne, sabr karne aur apne aap ko rokne ka faisla kiya, kyunke unhein yaqeen tha ke waqt aane par Allah sach zahir kar dega.

Isi dauran paani ki talash mein ek guzarta hua qafila kuen ke paas ruka. Jab unhon ne kuen mein apni dol daali to unhein andar ek bachcha nazar aaya. Pata chala ke woh log ghulam khareedne-bechne wale the. Unhon ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko Palestine se chhupa kar nikala aur Misr ke bazaar mein ek wazir ke haath bech diya.

Wazir unhein apne ghar le gaya aur apni biwi se kaha ke is bachche ko izzat ke saath rakhna.

Yahan pichhle waqiaat ko saamne rakh kar ek aham baat samajhna zaroori hai. Jab Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko kuen mein daala gaya tha to woh azaad the, lekin jab kuen se bahar nikle to ghulam bana diye gaye the.

Lekin isi waqt, jab woh Misr ke Aziz ke ghar mein dakhil hue, Allah Ta’ala ne apni hikmat aur qudrat dikhai ke woh har cheez ke maamle ko apne ikhtiyar mein rakhta hai:

وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَكَّنَّا لِيُوسُفَ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلِنُعَلِّمَهُ مِن تَأْوِيلِ الْأَحَادِيثِ ۚ وَاللَّهُ غَالِبٌ عَلَىٰ أَمْرِهِ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكْثَرَ النَّاسِ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ

“Aur isi tarah humne Yūsuf ko zameen mein iqtidar diya aur taake use baaton aur waqiaat ki ta’beer ka ilm sikhayen. Allah apne kaam par poora ghalib hai, lekin aksar log nahi jaante.”<sup>23</sup>

Yeh bayan tawakkul ki gehri haqeeqat samjhata hai. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa aur hamari zindagiyan Allah ke mansube ke mutabiq aage barh rahi hain. Allah ne hum mein se har ek ke liye ek mansuba bana rakha hai. Hum jitna chahen us se bhaag sakte hain, lekin aakhir mein har maamla usi ke qabze mein hai.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka Misr mein maujood hona Allah Ta’ala ke mansube ka hissa tha. Allah ne farmaya ke humne Yūsuf ko zameen mein jagah di taake use waqiaat ki ta’beer ka ilm sikhayen. Is mein pehle hi ishara de diya gaya tha ke khwabon ki ta’beer ka un ka ilm hi Misr mein un ki maujoodgi ka ek maqsad ban sakta hai.

Is se pehle Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> nahi chahte the ke Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> apna khwab bhaiyon ko batayen, kyunke unhein andesha tha ke bhai hasad karenge aur unhein nuqsaan pahuncha sakte hain. Walid ke mashware par Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne khwab unhein nahi bataya, lekin phir bhi bhaiyon ka hasad unhein nuqsaan pahunchaane ka sabab bana. Jis cheez se Huzoor Ya’qūb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> darte aur bachne ki koshish karte the, aakhir mein wahi waqia pesh aa gaya.

Bhai Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se is liye jaan chhudana chahte the ke woh apne walid ki un se gehri muhabbat par hasad karte the. Un ka khayal tha ke agar Yūsuf raaste se hat jayen to walid un se zyada muhabbat karenge. Lekin un ke aamaal ne aise waqiaat ka silsila shuru kar diya jis se un ke walid apne bete ke gham mein mubtala hue aur un bhaiyon par pehle se bhi kam bharosa karne lage. Yoon jis maamle ko woh apni buri tadbeer se khatam karna chahte the, wahi aakhir mein un ke khilaf ho gaya.

Allah Ta’ala Qur’ān ke qari ko yaad dilata hai ke us ki qudrat aur hikmat se ghafil na ho. Hum kisi mansube ko poora karne ke liye jitni bhi koshish kar lein, agar Allah Ta’ala ne us ka poora hona muqaddar na kiya ho to woh kabhi poora nahi ho sakta.

Isi dauran wazir ki biwi Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki taraf mailan mehsoos karne lagi. Ek din jab us ka shohar ghar par nahi tha, us ne darwaza band kiya aur Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko gunah ki taraf bulaya. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne us ki peshkash thukra di, Allah ki panah maangi aur use us ke shohar ki yaad dila kar kaha ke zalimana irade rakhne wale log kabhi kamyab nahi hote.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> darwaze ki taraf daude aur woh aurat un ke peeche daudi. Us ne peeche se un ki qameez pakad li aur qameez phat gayi. Achanak un ka shohar aur ek aur shakhs wahan aa gaye.

Is mushkil soorat-e-haal mein wazir ki biwi ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> par ilzaam lagaya ke unhon ne us ke saath bad-niyyati ki hai, aur un ke liye saza ka mutalaba kiya. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne wazeh kiya ke bad-niyyati us aurat ki taraf se thi.

Ghar ke ek fard ne is uljhan ka aqalmandana hal pesh kiya. Us ne kaha ke agar qameez saamne se phati ho to aurat sach keh rahi hai, kyunke is se zahir hoga ke us ne apne aap ko bachane ke liye Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se muqabla kiya. Lekin agar qameez peeche se phati ho to Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> sach keh rahe hain, kyunke is se maloom hoga ke woh us aurat se bach kar bhaag rahe the aur woh peeche se unhein kheench rahi thi.

Jab wazir ne dekha ke qameez peeche se phati hui hai to us ne apni biwi ki makkari ko pehchan liya. Yoon us ki biwi ka qasoor aur Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki be-gunahi wazeh ho gayi.

Yahan yeh baat bhi samajhna zaroori hai ke wazir ne apni biwi ke khilaf karwai kyun nahi ki. Woh muashray ke ba-asar logon mein se tha aur use khauf tha ke us ke ghar ka yeh raaz saamne aaya to us ki izzat aur asar-o-rasookh ko nuqsaan pohanchega. Siyasi taur par bhi is khabar se us ki saakh barbaad ho sakti thi.

Is liye us ne apni biwi se kaha ke apne gunah par tauba karo, aur Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se kaha ke is poore waqie ko bhool jao.

Wazir ki biwi ka ek haseen ghulam se muhabbat karna muashray ki ba-asar aurton ke kaanon tak pohanch gaya aur woh is baat ka charcha karne lagin. Wazir ki biwi ne unhein apne ghar dawat di taake Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka husn unhein dikha sake aur woh us ki muhabbat ki wajah samajh saken. Jab un aurton ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko dekha to un ke husn se itni mutaasir hui ke unhein farishta kehne lagin.

Woh aurtein bhi Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke saath najaiz talluq ki khwahish rakhne lagin. Wazir ki biwi ne kaha ke woh bhi un ki tarah un ke husn ke saamne be-bas ho gayi hai, aur yeh ke agar Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne us ki baat na maani to unhein qaid kar diya jayega.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> be-gunah the, lekin unhein qaid karne ka faisla kiya gaya. Mumkin hai ke yeh faisla is liye kiya gaya ho ke woh apne gharon aur muashray ki ba-asar aurton ke raaz ko badnaam hone se bacha saken, khaas taur par is liye ke doosre ba-asar shoharon ki biwiyan bhi is maamle mein shamil ho chuki thin.

## Ikhtiyarat! Ikhtiyarat! Ikhtiyarat!

Yahan Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko maloom hua ke zindagi ki ek aur badi aazmaish un ke saamne hai. Woh be-gunah the, phir bhi unhein qaid kiya jaane wala tha. Zahiri taur par waziron ke asar-o-rasookh ke muqable mein apna haq manwana mumkin nahi lagta tha. Is liye unhon ne dobara Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul kiya aur dua ki:

قَالَ رَبِّ السِّجْنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدْعُونَنِي إِلَيْهِ ۖ وَإِلَّا تَصْرِفْ عَنِّي كَيْدَهُنَّ أَصْبُ إِلَيْهِنَّ وَأَكُن مِّنَ الْجَاهِلِينَ

“Unhon ne arz ki: ‘Aye mere Rab! Qaidkhana mujhe us kaam se zyada pasand hai jis ki taraf yeh aurtein mujhe bula rahi hain. Aur agar Tu un ki saazish mujh se door na karega to main un ki taraf mailan kar sakta hoon aur na-samajhon mein shamil ho sakta hoon.’”<sup>24</sup>

Ab tak ke qisse par ghaur karne wala qari is baat se muttifaq hoga ke is qisse ka asal nek shakhs hi baar baar mushkil mein mubtala ho raha hai. Yahan use insani zindagi ki sab se sakht khwahishon mein se ek ke zariye aazmaya ja raha hai, aur phir bhi us ke sahi faisle ki wajah se use saza di ja rahi hai! Jo shakhs aakhri anjaam ko nahi dekh sakta, use yahi nazar aayega. Zindagi mein sakht faislon ke waqt hum mein se aksar log isi soorat-e-haal se guzarte hain.
