# Ikhtiyarat Aur Ghaur Ke Nuqaat

Achhe logon ko hamesha do raaston mein se ek raasta chunne ki aazmaish hoti hai. Ek raasta aisa hota hai jo Aakhirat mein Allah ke ghazab ka sabab banta hai, lekin is duniya mein kuch arse ke liye maali aasoodgi aur doosri sahoolatein deta hai. Doosra raasta duniya mein mushkil la sakta hai, lekin Allah Ta’ala ki raza aur Aakhirat ke ajr tak pohanchata hai. Qur’ān farmata hai:

وَهَدَيْنَاهُ النَّجْدَيْنِ

“Aur kya humne use do raaste nahi dikhaye?”<sup>25</sup>

Masla hamesha se ikhtiyar ka raha hai. Aksar hum apne mahdood ilm ki bina par faisla karte hain. Lekin jab humein samajh aata hai ke hamara ilm mahdood aur Allah ka ilm be-hadd hai, to Allah ke hukm poore karne ke liye kiye jaane wale faislon par sawal nahi uthate. Hum sirf ek chhota sa tukda dekhte hain, jabke Allah Ta’ala ke paas poori tasveer hoti hai. Qur’ān humein yaad dilata hai ke apne faislon ke door-ras nateejon ke baare mein hum kitna kam jaante hain:

وَعَسَىٰ أَن تَكْرَهُوا شَيْئًا وَهُوَ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ ۖ وَعَسَىٰ أَن تُحِبُّوا شَيْئًا وَهُوَ شَرٌّ لَّكُمْ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ

“Mumkin hai ke tum kisi cheez ko na-pasand karo aur woh tumhare liye behtar ho, aur mumkin hai ke tum kisi cheez ko pasand karo aur woh tumhare liye nuqsaan-deh ho. Allah jaanta hai aur tum nahi jaante.”<sup>26</sup>

Kisi shakhs ke saamne bahut zyada paisa kamane ka mauqa aata hai, lekin zaroori nahi ke woh jaiz tareeqe se ho. Woh duniya ke faide ko saamne rakh kar yeh kaam kar leta hai, halanke woh har pehlu se ghalat hota hai. Woh bahut paisa kama leta hai, lekin paanch ya das saal baad jab sipahi us ke darwaze par aate hain to use apne us faisle par afsos hota hai jise use kabhi nahi karna chahiye tha.

Upar wali aayat humein sikha rahi hai ke humein mustaqbil ka na poora ilm hai aur na us par ikhtiyar. Is baat ka poora ehsaas isi bab ke aakhir tak ho jayega.

Guftagu ko dobara jodte hain. Jab wazir ne pehli baar Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko ghulam khareedne walon se khareeda tha to us ne kaha tha ke is naujawan ke saath izzat ka sulook kiya jaye. Lekin ab jab us ki apni izzat daaw par lagi to woh Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke saath be-izzati ka sulook kar raha tha. Log kabhi kabhi aise hi hote hain: apne aap ko bachane ke liye be-gunah logon ko bhi qurbaan kar dete hain. Wazir aur us ki biwi ko yeh khabar na thi ke Allah Ta’ala ka mansuba amal mein aa chuka hai.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ek nihayat mushkil soorat-e-haal mein the, lekin unhon ne gunah se azaad hone ke bajaye be-gunah qaid hona pasand kiya. Momin ke liye duniya qaidkhana aur kafir ke liye jannat hai.<sup>27</sup> Is liye apne faislon par ghaur karo.

Allah Ta’ala ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki dua sun li. Ilzaam lagane walon ne un ki be-gunahi ki nishaniyan dekh li thin, lekin phir bhi unhein qaid kar diya gaya. Woh Allah Ta’ala ke muqarrar kiye hue waqt tak qaidkhane mein rahe.

Un ke saathi qaidi unhein nek shakhs samajhte the. Un mein se do logon ne ajeeb khwab dekhe aur un ki ta’beer poochne ke liye Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se rujoo kiya. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne bataya ke un mein se ek ko qatl kar diya jayega, jabke doosra azaad ho kar badshah ka muqarrab banega. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne us shakhs se, jo azaad hone wala tha, kaha ke badshah ke paas apna maqam milne par mujhe yaad rakhna. Lekin Shaytaan ne use Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se kiya hua waada bhula diya, aur woh kuch aur saal qaidkhane mein rahe.

Ek raat badshah ne ajeeb khwab dekha: saat dubli gaayen saat moti gaayon ko kha rahi thin, aur saat hari baaliyan aur saat khushk baaliyan thin. Us ne apne mashiron se khwab ki ta’beer poochi. Unhon ne kaha ke yeh uljhan bhara khwab hai aur woh is ki ta’beer batane ki salahiyat nahi rakhte.

Us waqt woh azaad hone wala purana qaidi Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki khwabon ki ta’beer ki salahiyat bhool chuka tha, lekin aakhir use yaad aa gayi. Us ne badshah se ijazat li ke woh Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se is khwab ki ta’beer pooch kar aaye.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne khwab ki ta’beer batayi ke saat saal tak faslein khoob hongi, phir saat saal qahat aayega, aur us ke baad ek saal aisa hoga jismein khoob baarish hogi.

Badshah is ta’beer se mutaasir hua aur us ne ek qasid ko Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke paas bheja taake unhein mahal mein bulaye. Lekin Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne foran qaidkhane se bahar aane ke bajaye kaha:

وَقَالَ الْمَلِكُ ائْتُونِي بِهِ ۖ فَلَمَّا جَاءَهُ الرَّسُولُ قَالَ ارْجِعْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَاسْأَلْهُ مَا بَالُ النِّسْوَةِ اللَّاتِي قَطَّعْنَ أَيْدِيَهُنَّ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي بِكَيْدِهِنَّ عَلِيمٌ

“Aur badshah ne kaha: ‘Use mere paas lao.’ Phir jab qasid Huzoor Yūsuf ke paas aaya to unhon ne kaha: ‘Apne badshah ke paas laut jao aur us se poochho ke un aurton ka kya maamla tha jinhon ne apne haath kaat liye the. Beshak mera Rab un ki saazish ko khoob jaanta hai.’”<sup>28</sup>

Tasawwur karo: kisi gunah ki taraf bulaya jana, phir gunah se inkaar ki wajah se kai saal na-haq qaid rehna, aur aakhir mein mulk ke sab se ba-asar shakhs ka tumhein bulana. Kya tum apni izzat bahaal karne ka yeh mauqa thukra dete? Aksar log aisa na karte.

Lekin Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> us azaadi mein dilchaspi nahi rakhte the jis ki hum khwahish karte hain. Un ki pehli tarjeeh yeh thi ke sach saamne aa jaye. Badshah ki jaanch se Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki be-gunahi wazeh ho gayi aur wazir ki biwi ne iqraar kar liya ke us ne hi Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko apni taraf mailan ki dawat di thi, na ke Huzoor Yūsuf ne use.

Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne apni darkhwast ki do bunyadi wajahen bayan ki:

ذَٰلِكَ لِيَعْلَمَ أَنِّي لَمْ أَخُنْهُ بِالْغَيْبِ وَأَنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَهْدِي كَيْدَ الْخَائِنِينَ

“Yūsuf ne kaha: ‘Yeh is liye kiya tha taake hukmaran jaan le ke main ne us ki ghair-maujoodgi mein us se khayanat nahi ki, aur Allah khayanat karne walon ki saazish ko kamyab nahi hone deta.’”<sup>29</sup>

Pehli wajah yeh thi ke agar wazir ko un ki nek-naami par koi shak ho to woh insaaf ke tareeqe se door ho jaye. Doosri wajah yeh thi ke jo log un ke khilaf saazish kar rahe the, unhein yaad dila diya jaye ke aakhirkar Allah Ta’ala ka mansuba hi ghalib hota hai.

Jin logon ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> par ilzaam lagaya tha, unhon ne saboot dekhne ke bawajood apne muashrati asar-o-rasookh ko istemal karke ek be-gunah shakhs ko qaid karwa diya, taake apni izzat bacha saken. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul rakh kar is soorat-e-haal ko qabool kiya. Unhein yaqeen tha ke agar woh sab se bade Qazi par bharosa rakhenge to aakhir insaaf zaroor ghalib aayega, na ke kisi kharab insani nizaam par.

Aakhir mein jis cheez ka unhein khauf tha, wahi un ki be-gunahi sabit hone ka bunyadi sabab bani aur badshah ki nazron mein un ki maqbooliyat ka zariya bhi ban gayi.

Phir se hum dekhte hain ke hum kisi cheez se kitna hi bachne ki koshish karein, agar Allah Ta’ala ne us ka waqoo hamare liye muqaddar kar diya ho to koi use rok nahi sakta, chahe hum use taalne ke liye kitni hi koshish kar lein.

## Ghaur Ke Nuqaat

Peechhe mud kar dekhein to is khoobsurat qisse ke do nihayat gehre pehlu saamne aate hain.

Pehli baat yeh ke jab bhaiyon ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko kuen mein daala to woh azaad the. Jab woh kuen se nikle to ghulam ban chuke the. Yahan woh ghulam ki haisiyat se qaidkhane mein dakhil hue aur azaad shakhs ban kar nikle. Is se yeh dars milta hai ke jis soorat-e-haal mein hum hote hain us par hamara poora ikhtiyar nahi hota. Is liye humein Allah Ta’ala ke saamne sar jhuka kar apni har soorat-e-haal ko qabool karna chahiye, is yaqeen ke saath ke hamara tawakkul us Zaat par hai jo hamare liye behtar jaanta hai.

Doosri baat yeh ke Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> na-haq nafrat ki wajah se kuen mein dakhil hue aur ghulam ban gaye. Phir woh muhabbat ki wajah se qaidkhane mein dakhil hue aur badshah ke wazir ban kar nikle.

Tawakkul ke is khoobsurat baagh mein khilte hue dars ka ek aham phool yeh hai ke ek jaisi nazar aane wali soorat-e-haal bhi mukhtalif halaat mein bilkul mukhtalif anjaam la sakti hai, kyunke nateeja Allah Ta’ala ke ikhtiyar mein hota hai.

Isi tarah tum aaj koi kaam karo aur us ka ek nateeja nikle, phir kuch saal baad bilkul wahi kaam karo aur nateeja bilkul mukhtalif ho. Is liye ke bahut se aise asbaab kaam kar rahe hote hain jin ka insaan ko ilm nahi hota. Humein har soorat aur har waqt Allah Ta’ala par bharosa aur tawakkul rakhna chahiye ke woh hamare liye behtareen anjaam ata farmaye.

Kaun soch sakta tha ke ek door mulk ka chhota bachcha do qaidi logon ke khwabon ki ta’beer batane ke liye zaroori hoga, aur isi zariye se an-ginat log bhook se bach jayenge aur ek saltanat barbaadi se mehfooz ho jayegi? Is ka sirf ek jawab hai: yeh sab Zaat-e-Ilahi ke hukm se hua jo maazi ki har bareek se bareek tafseel aur mustaqbil ke har mumkin anjaam ka pehle se ilm rakhta hai, aur in tamam jude hue maamlon par mukammal ikhtiyar rakhta hai. Yeh sifat sirf Allah Ta’ala ki hai, jo har maujood cheez ke maamle par poora ikhtiyar rakhta hai.

Guftagu ko qisse ki taraf dobara laate hain. Badshah ne Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko apna khaas mushir bana liya. Huzoor Yūsuf <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne darkhwast ki ke unhein khazane ka zimmedar banaya jaye. Yoon woh ikhtiyar ke ek aham maqam par pohanch gaye:

وَكَذَٰلِكَ مَكَّنَّا لِيُوسُفَ فِي الْأَرْضِ يَتَبَوَّأُ مِنْهَا حَيْثُ يَشَاءُ ۖ نُصِيبُ بِرَحْمَتِنَا مَن نَّشَاءُ ۖ وَلَا نُضِيعُ أَجْرَ الْمُحْسِنِينَ

“Aur isi tarah humne Yūsuf ko zameen mein iqtidar diya ke woh jahan chahein us mein maqam banayen. Hum apni rahmat jis ke liye chahte hain khaas karte hain aur hum nek logon ka ajr zaya nahi karte.”

26. Sūrah Al-Baqarah, 2:216
27. Sahīh Muslim, Hadīth 2956
