# Bab Panjum (Chapter Five)
# Unlimited Wi-Fi

“Hamare Khaaliq ke saath jo rabta hai woh insaan ke banaye hue kisi bhi aale ya ijaad se kahin zyada mazboot hai, jin par hum aasani se bharosa karte hain. Chahe hum baadalon se bhi upar bulandiyon mein hon ya samandar ki gehraiyon mein, humein yeh yaqeen rakhna chahiye ke Allah Ta’ala ka hamare saath aisa mazboot rabta hai jo kabhi khatam nahi hota. Ab sirf imaan ki ek chhalang lagani hai aur Allah se judna hai, kyunke woh tumhare khayal se bhi zyada tumhare qareeb hai!”

Jab Zuhayr gaadi le kar mohalle ka chakkar laga raha tha to use yaqeen nahi aa raha tha ke woh kya dekh raha hai. Us ki chhoti chhoti aankhon ne itna bada hujoom kabhi nahi dekha tha. Woh ginna shuru hua: ek, do, teen... sau... paanch sau!

“Ammi!” chhote ladke ne pukara. “Yahan bahut zyada log hain! Kya ho raha hai? Kya hum bhi in lambi aur ajeeb qatārōn mein khade honge?”

Us ki shafeeq maa ne muhabbat se jawab diya, “Beta, unlimited Wi-Fi par badi sale lagi hui hai. Tum achhi tarah jaante ho ke tum aur tumhara bhai Hassaan har roz Wi-Fi ko kitna istemal karte ho.”

“Aur main bhi!” chaar bachchon mein sab se chhote Hanzalah ne kaha, jabke Farhath online Qur’ān sunne mein itni masroof thi ke us ne baat par tawajjoh hi nahi di.

Hassaan aur Zuhayr ne pareshani se ek doosre ko dekha. Unhein samajh aa gaya ke rabte se un ki muhabbat ka matlab yeh tha ke ghar mein doston se baat karte hue waqt guzarna hai to unhein is be-intiha qatār mein khada hona hi padega.

Duniya bhar ke aksar gharon ka haal aisa hi hai. Jahan sale hoti hai, har shakhs daud kar apni ‘sadi ki bachat’ hasil karna chahta hai. Apni mehnat ki kamai ko mushkil maali halaat mein bachane ki koshish mein koi harj nahi, lekin masla yeh hai ke jab sab se aham sauda hamare saamne hota hai to hum us ki asal qeemat samajh nahi paate.

Badqismati se aksar insaan sirf un cheezon ko qeemti samajhta hai jin ki dunyawi qeemat ho, aur zindagi ki un cheezon ko be-qeemat samajhta hai jo asal mein na-qabil-e-qeemat hoti hain.

Tasawwur karo ke koi company ailan kare ke tumhein unlimited Wi-Fi muft milega, sirf itni shart hai ke tum sab ko batao ke tum is brand par imaan rakhte ho. Mere khayal mein aisi company poori duniya par chha jayegi aur is ke pairokar aksar mazhabon se bhi zyada honge.

Bahut se Musalman bhai aur behnein yeh samajhne se qasir hain ke hamare Rab ne 1400 saal pehle insaniyat ko aisi hi peshkash ki thi.<sup>54</sup> Lekin humne is peshkash mein koi dilchaspi nahi li, sirf is liye ke is ke dunyawi faide foran hamari aankhon ke saamne nahi aate.

Allah Ta’ala farmata hai:

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ

“Aur tumhare Rab ne farmaya: ‘Mujhe pukaro, main tumhari dua qubool karunga.’”<sup>55</sup>

Tumne aakhri baar Allah Ta’ala ko sachche dil se kab pukara tha? Tumne aakhri baar apne haath utha kar apne Khaaliq se kab baat ki thi? Tumne apne dil ke gehre tareen raaz us Zaat ke saamne kab rakhe jo tumhare andar ke har khayal se waqif hai? Agar hum upar wali aayat par ghaur karein to samajh aata hai ke Islām ka Rab be-misaal hai.

Tasawwur karo ke ek bahut badi company ka CEO sab se upar ho aur safai karne wala sab se neeche. Kya imkān hai ke CEO aur safai karne wale ka koi khaas rabta ho? CEO sab se masroof shakhs hota hai, us ka roz ka waqt sambhalne ke liye bhi ek khaas assistant hota hai. Woh apne mulazimon ki khairiyat poochne ke liye darwaze darwaze nahi ja sakta. Woh unhein achhi tankhwah deta hai aur yahi un ke liye us ka shukr ada karne ko kaafi samjha jata hai.

Islām mein jo shakhs Allah Ta’ala ki rububiyyat ko qabool karke us ki bandagi karta hai use ‘Aabid’ yani bandah aur ibadat-guzar, ya ‘Abdullah’ yani Allah ka banda kaha jata hai. Allah Ta’ala hamara ‘Rabb’ hai, jo humein parwarish deta aur hamari dekh-rekh farmata hai.

Lekin upar wali aayat ki roshni mein dono sooraton ka farq dekho. Puri kainaat ka Rabb tumhare liye itna waqt rakhta hai ke woh farmata hai: “Mujhe pukaro aur main jawab dunga.” Yeh kitni azeem baat hai! Duniya ka kaunsa sarbarah apne tamaam mulazimon ke liye baghair waqt aur shart ki khuli mulaqat pesh kar sakta hai? Allah Ta’ala ke siwa koi nahi, phir bhi hum roz is mauqe se katrate rehte hain.

Technology aur Wi-Fi se humein ajeeb si muhabbat ho gayi hai. Hum is ki ijaad par hairan hote hain, lekin yeh nahi dekhte ke yeh kitni kamzor cheezon par munhasir hai.

Is waqt tumhara device Wi-Fi ya network access par munhasir hai taake tum doosre programs aur applications istemal kar sako. Device ek signal par, signal router par, router buland fiza mein maujood satellite ke signal par, aur woh kisi aur signal par munhasir hai. Jin ijaadon ko hum itna pasand karte hain un ki kamzoriyaan aisi hi hain. Lekin Allah Ta’ala farmata hai:

وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ ۖ فَلْيَسْتَجِيبُوا لِي وَلْيُؤْمِنُوا بِي لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُونَ

“Aur jab mere bande mere baare mein tum se poochein to main yaqeenan qareeb hoon. Pukarne wale ki dua qubool karta hoon jab woh mujhe pukarta hai. To unhein chahiye ke mera hukm maanain aur mujh par imaan laayein taake raah paayen.”<sup>56</sup>

Humein sirf puri kainaat ke Maalik aur Khaaliq tak unlimited access ki peshkash nahi ki ja rahi, balki technology se jude tamam wasail ke baraks Allah Ta’ala ‘As-Samad’ hai: woh kisi ka mohtaj nahi aur har koi har cheez mein us ka mohtaj hai. Is liye jab hum use pukarte hain to koi cheez hamara connection nahi kaat sakti.

Ab humein sirf yeh karna hai ke Allah par tawakkul rakhein aur yaqeen karein ke jab hum use is imaan ke saath pukarenge ke woh sab ki sunta hai aur jawab deta hai, to woh hamare liye behtareen tareeqe se jawab dega.

Insaan hone ki wajah se hum hamesha koi saboot chahte hain ke jab koi Allah se dua karta hai to kya Allah waqai jawab deta hai? Is ka jawab is hikmat mein hai ke Allah Ta’ala ne Ambiya aur Rusul ko kyun bheja: un ke behtareen aamaal ke baghair hamare paas seekhne ke liye amali misaalein na hotin.

Ab hum Allah ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ki zindagi se ek misaal pesh karte hain. Yeh misaal hamare Khaaliq se guftagu ki ahmiyat, Allah par imaan aur tawakkul ki qeemat ko wazeh karti hai, aur dikhati hai ke Allah hamari umeed se badh kar jawab de sakta hai.

## Huzoor ﷺ Apne Rab Se Guftagu Karte Hain

Jab Islām ka paigham Makka ke hukmaran tabqe ke ikhtiyar se bahar phailne laga to unhon ne Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ko shaheed karne ka mansuba banaya. Najd ke ek bad-batin boorhe shakhs ne unhein yeh mashwara diya tha. Allah Ta’ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ko un ki saazish se khabardaar kiya aur unhein apna ghar chhod kar Madina Munawwara hijrat karne ka hukm diya.

Ek naye daur ke aaghaz par, apne walidain ke shehar, apne ghar, khandan, doston aur yaadon ki jagah se rukhsat hote waqt kya Huzoor ﷺ ghabraye? Kya unhon ne Allah ke hukm par sawal uthaya aur yeh shikayat ki: “Main ne tumhari har baat maan li aur ab mujhe apne ghar se nikala ja raha hai?” Kya unhon ne apni soorat-e-haal ki shikayat ki? Agar kisi ko shikayat ka haq hota to woh Huzoor ﷺ the, lekin unhon ne apne haath uthaye aur apne Rab se dua ki:

وَقُلْ رَّبِّ أَدْخِلْنِي مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍ وَأَخْرِجْنِي مُخْرَجَ صِدْقٍ وَاجْعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلْطَانًا نَّصِيرًا

“Aur arz karo (Aye Mehboob ﷺ): ‘Aye mere Rab! Mujhe achhi tarah dakhil farma aur achhi tarah nikal, aur apne paas se mere liye madadgar qudrat ata farma.’”<sup>57</sup>

Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul aur imaan rakha aur apni soorat-e-haal ke baare mein apne Rab se baat ki. Musalmanon ko is misaal ke zariye sikhaya ja raha hai ke pareshani ke waqt apni soorat-e-haal ki shikayat karne ke bajaye is yaqeen par bharosa rakho ke Allah Ta’ala har anjaam ko jaanta hai. Is liye us se dua karo ke woh tumhari soorat-e-haal ko behtar kar de, kyunke woh sab se zyada qudrat wala bhi hai aur sab se zyada hikmat wala bhi. Woh hamare liye cheezein us waqt muqarrar karta hai jo hamare liye behtareen hota hai.

Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne is dua mein do cheezein maangi:

1. Makka se achhi tarah nikalna.
2. Madina Munawwara mein achhi tarah dakhil hona.

Pehli dua par ghaur karo. Is soorat-e-haal mein yeh maangna hi hairat-angez hai. Yeh us waqt ka buland hausla hai jab tawakkul Allah ki qudrat par mazboot ho: mushkil par ghalib aane ke saath Allah se aasani aur behtar anjaam bhi maangna.

Quraish Huzoor ﷺ ki jaan lene ki koshish kar rahe the aur is saazish ki wajah se unhein apne ghar se hijrat karni padi. Is ke bawajood Huzoor ﷺ Allah se achhi tarah nikalne ki dua kar rahe the. Allah Ta’ala ne safar ke har mor par na-qabil-e-tasawwur zariye se un ki hifazat ki. Yahan tak ke un ke peeche mahir nishan-daan lagaye gaye the, jinhein pakadne par sau oonton ka inaam rakha gaya tha, phir bhi Huzoor ﷺ salamati ke saath apni manzil par pohanch gaye.

Us waqt Makkah walon ko yeh maloom nahi tha ke Huzoor ﷺ ko zabardasti nikalne ka un ka amal waqiaat ka aisa silsila shuru karega jis ki gehrai tak un ki samajh nahi pohanch sakti. Aath saal baad Huzoor ﷺ usi shehar mein fatah ke saath wapas dakhil hue jahan se unhein nikala gaya tha. Yeh waqia “Fatah-e-Makka” ke naam se mashhoor hua. Dua ki qudrat dekho: Huzoor ﷺ Allah ki taraf rujoo karte waqt shehar se mazloom ki haisiyat mein nikal rahe the, lekin kuch saal baad Allah ne unhein usi shehar mein hamesha ke liye ghalib rehnuma bana kar lautaya.

Madina Munawwara mein achhi tarah dakhil hone ki doosri dua ka jawab bhi azeem tha. Jab Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ Makka se nikle to un ki qiyadat aur hukmrani ko thukra diya gaya tha. Lekin jab woh Madina Munawwara mein dakhil hue to unhon ne qiyadat talash bhi nahi ki thi, phir bhi shehar ke log un ke intezar mein jama the aur unhein apna rehnuma samajh rahe the.

Huzoor ﷺ ki yeh dua har Musalman ko yaad karni chahiye: ghar badalte waqt, shehar ya mulk badalte waqt, hawai safar ke waqt, ya sirf rozmarrah kaam ke liye ghar se nikalte waqt. Kabhi hum samajhte hain ke hum mustaqbil ki behtari ke liye soch samajh kar faisla kar rahe hain, lekin humein mustaqbil ka ilm nahi hota. Is liye na-maloom cheez mein apne andazon ki bina par qadam rakhne ke bajaye apne Rab se baat karo aur dua karo ke woh rawangi ki jagah se tumhare nikalne aur manzil mein tumhare dakhil hone dono ko kamyab bana de. Allah par tawakkul hamari rozmarrah zindagi mein hamari soch se kahin zyada ahmiyat rakhta hai.

## Tareeki Se Roshni Tak

Dua, yani haath utha kar apne Khaaliq se baat karna, itni tabdeeli paida kar sakta hai ke parindon ka ek giroh haathiyon ki fauj ko barbaad kar de.<sup>58</sup> Aur jaisa ke ab hum dekhenge, yahi dua tumhein sab se gehri tareeki se bhi nikal sakti hai. Lekin is ka bunyadi juz tawakkul hai: agar tum jo maang rahe ho woh tumhare liye behtar hai to Allah Ta’ala use zaroor ata karega, aur agar woh tumhein nahi diya gaya to woh apne bandon ko ajr dene ka behtar tareeqa jaanta hai.

Hazaron saal pehle Allah ke ek Nabi ko aisi qaum ki taraf bheja gaya jo bahut se gunahon mein mubtala thi. Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne unhein baar baar Tauhīd ki dawat di, lekin unhon ne aap ki baat na maani. Aap unhein khabardaar karte rahe ke agar woh apna rawayya na badlein to sakht azaab aayega. Phir bhi woh imaan na laaye. Aakhir ek din un ki haalat se na-umeed ho kar Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> kashti mein sawar ho kar wahan se chale gaye.

Isi dauran aasman surkh aag ki tarah nazar aane laga. Log samajh gaye ke Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> sach keh rahe the, is liye unhon ne Allah se apne gunahon ki tauba ki.

Samandar mein ek toofan utha. Maujein pahaadon ki tarah buland hotin aur kashti se takra kar toot rahi thin. Kashti ke logon ne bojh kam karne ke liye logon se kaha ke apna samaan pani mein daal dein. Woh mushrik the, is liye unhon ne qura-andazi ki. Jis ka naam nikle, use samandar mein qurbaan karna tha. Yoon woh musafiron ki jaanon ke saath juwa khel rahe the.

Qura-andazi hui aur pehli baar Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka naam nikla. Log jaante the ke aap nek shakhs hain, is liye unhon ne aap ko samandar mein daalne se inkaar kar diya.

Unhon ne dobara qura-andazi ki, lekin phir wahi naam nikla. Phir bhi woh aise nek shakhs ko samandar ke hawale karne par raazi na hue. Teesri baar qura-andazi ki gayi aur hairat-angez taur par phir Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka naam hi nikla. Ab aap samajh gaye ke yeh Allah ki taraf se ishara hai, is liye aap toofani pani mein utarne ke liye tayyar ho gaye.

Aap ko yeh maloom nahi tha ke Allah Ta’ala ne ek bade samandari jaanwar ko kashti ka peecha karne ka hukm diya hai. Jab Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> pani mein dakhil hue to us azeem jaanwar ne aap ko nigal liya.

Tasawwur bhi mushkil hai ke jab Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> bedar hue aur apne aap ko ek badi machhli ke pait mein, us ke tezabi maday ke darmiyan, tareeki mein paya to un par kya guzri hogi. Lekin Allah ke sachche bandon ka hausla yahi hota hai ke woh badtareen soorat-e-haal mein bhi apne Rab ki taraf rujoo karte aur us se dua maangte hain. Qur’ān farmata hai:

وَذَا النُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ فَنَادَىٰ فِي الظُّلُمَاتِ أَن لَّا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

“Aur machhli wale Yūnus ko yaad karo jab woh gusse mein chale gaye aur samjhe ke hum un par tangi nahi karenge. Phir unhon ne tareekiyon mein pukara: ‘Tere siwa koi mabood nahi, Tu paak hai, beshak mujh se khata hui.’”<sup>59</sup>

Yahan tareekiyon ka zikr do pehlu rakhta hai. Pehla pehlu zahiri tareeki ka hai. Ghaur karo to Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko teen tareekiyon ne gher rakha tha: pehli azeem machhli ke pait ki tareeki, doosri samandar ki gehraiyon ki tareeki, aur teesri raat ki tareeki.

Doosra pehlu aap ki jazbaati aur zehni haalat se mutaliq hai. Aaj na-umeedi ka shikar log kehte hain: “Mujhe lagta hai ke main is waqt bahut tareek jagah mein hoon.” Yeh qissa un logon ke liye hai jo zindagi mein aise band raaste par pohanch jaate hain jahan unhein koi umeed ki kiran nazar nahi aati.

Aise waqt Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa yaad karo. Aap ne tareeki ki gehraiyon mein Allah par tawakkul kiya aur dua ke zariye us ki taraf rujoo kiya. Upar wali dua un logon ke liye bhi mufeed hai jo pareshani, bechaini aur gham ka shikar hain. Is ke zariye Allah Ta’ala ki taraf rujoo karo aur umeed rakho ke woh tumhari gham-zada haalat door kar dega, jaisa ke us ne Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke saath kiya.

Allah Ta’ala ne un ki dua qubool farmayi:

فَاسْتَجَبْنَا لَهُ وَنَجَّيْنَاهُ مِنَ الْغَمِّ ۚ وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُنجِي الْمُؤْمِنِينَ

“Phir humne un ki dua qubool ki aur unhein gham se nijaat di, aur hum isi tarah mominon ko nijaat dete hain.”<sup>60</sup>

Un logon ke liye aisa hi hairat-angez anjaam muntazir hota hai jo badi mushkil mein Allah ki taraf rujoo karke us par bharosa rakhte hain.

Allah ke hukm se azeem machhli ne Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko apne pait se nikal kar sahil par daal diya. Aap kamzor the aur dhoop se bachne, pani ki kami door karne aur apni taqat bahal karne ke liye mazeed hifazat ki zarurat thi.

Zahiri taur par yeh masla alag se hal hona chahiye tha, kyunke Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne sirf machhli ke pait se azaadi ki dua ki thi. Lekin jab Allah Ta’ala apne bandon par karam farmata hai to har pehlu ka lihaz rakhta hai:

فَنَبَذْنَاهُ بِالْعَرَاءِ وَهُوَ سَقِيمٌ ۝ وَأَنبَتْنَا عَلَيْهِ شَجَرَةً مِّن يَقْطِينٍ

“Phir humne unhein khule maidan mein daal diya jabke woh beemar the, aur un par kaddu ki bel ka darakht uga diya.”<sup>61</sup>

Allah Ta’ala ne un par kaddu ki bel uga di jo unhein dhoop se bachati thi aur un ke liye ghiza ka zariya bhi bani, yahan tak ke un ki taqat wapas aa gayi. Allah par tawakkul rakhne walon ka ajr yahi hai ke un ki sehat-yabi ke baad ki zarooriyat ka intezam bhi Allah ke zimme hota hai.

Huzoor Yūnus <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa humein sikhata hai ke hamara Khaaliq ke saath rabta insaan ke banaye hue aalon aur ijaadon se kahin aage hai jin par hum bharosa karte hain. Chahe hum baadalon se upar hon ya samandar ki gehraiyon mein, humein yaqeen rakhna chahiye ke Allah Ta’ala ka hamare saath aisa mazboot rabta hai jo kabhi ishara nahi khota. Ab humein sirf imaan ki ek chhalang lagani hai aur Allah se judna hai, kyunke woh hamare khayal se bhi zyada qareeb hai.

Allah Ta’ala Qur’ān mein is baat ko wazeh farmata hai:

وَنَحْنُ أَقْرَبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنْ حَبْلِ الْوَرِيدِ

“Aur hum insaan ki rag-e-jaan se bhi zyada us ke qareeb hain.”<sup>62</sup>

Kabhi hum jazbaati tareeki mein ghire hue mehsoos karein tab bhi humein Allah par bharosa aur tawakkul qaim rakhna chahiye, is imaan ke saath ke woh humein tareeki se roshni ki taraf nikal sakta hai.
