# Aag Aur Barf

## Aag Aur Barf

Tawakkul aur un hairat-angez nateejon ki ek aur misaal un logon ke liye jo mushkil waqt mein haath utha kar Allah Ta’ala se baat karte hain, Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke bachpan ke mashhoor qisse se samjhi ja sakti hai.

Babylon ke Ur ilaqe mein bachpan hi se Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> apni qaum ki haalat se mutma’in nahi the. Woh sooraj, sitaron aur chaand ki parastish karti thi. Unhein yeh bhi na-munasib lagta tha ke log apne haathon se banaye hue buton ki ibadat karte hain. Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne unhein samjhane ki koshish ki ke but na nuqsaan pahuncha sakte hain na faida, lekin unhon ne us naujawan ki baat sunne se inkaar kar diya.

Ek din jab log apni taqreeb mein shirkat ke liye bahar gaye hue the to Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne apni baat wazeh karne ka faisla kiya. Meri pehle ki ek kitaab mein is waqie ko is tarah bayan kiya gaya hai:

“Phir Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne ek ek karke buton ko todna shuru kiya. Da‘wat ke maqsad se unhon ne bade but ko chhod diya taake logon ki aam samajh ko jagaya ja sake. Jab log apni khushi ki taqreeb se wapas aaye to unhon ne dekha ke bade but ke siwa sab but bilkul toot chuke hain. Tahqiq ke baad unhein shak hua ke yeh kaam naujawan Ibrāhīm ne kiya hai, kyunke wohi is taqreeb mein maujood nahi the. Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> se pooch-gachh aur un ki aqalmandana guftagu Qur’ān mein yun bayan hai:

قَالُوا أَأَنتَ فَعَلْتَ هَٰذَا بِآلِهَتِنَا يَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ ۝ قَالَ بَلْ فَعَلَهُ كَبِيرُهُمْ هَٰذَا فَاسْأَلُوهُمْ إِن كَانُوا يَنطِقُونَ ۝ فَرَجَعُوا إِلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ فَقَالُوا إِنَّكُمْ أَنتُمُ الظَّالِمُونَ ۝ ثُمَّ نُكِسُوا عَلَىٰ رُءُوسِهِمْ لَقَدْ عَلِمْتَ مَا هَٰؤُلَاءِ يَنطِقُونَ ۝ قَالَ أَفَتَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَا لَا يَنفَعُكُمْ شَيْئًا وَلَا يَضُرُّكُمْ ۝ أُفٍّ لَّكُمْ وَلِمَا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ ۖ أَفَلَا تَعْقِلُونَ

“Unhon ne kaha: ‘Aye Ibrāhīm! Kya tumne hamare maboodon ke saath yeh kiya hai?’ Ibrāhīm ne kaha: ‘Balki yeh kaam un ke is bade ne kiya hoga; un se pooch lo agar woh bol sakte hain.’ Phir woh apne dilon ki taraf mutawajjoh hue aur kehne lage: ‘Beshak tum khud hi zalim ho.’ Phir apne sar jhuka kar kehne lage: ‘Tum jaante ho ke yeh bol nahi sakte.’ Ibrāhīm ne kaha: ‘To kya tum Allah ke siwa unhein poojte ho jo tumhein na faida de sakte hain na nuqsaan? Tum par aur un par afsos hai jinhein tum Allah ke siwa poojte ho. Kya tum samajhte nahi?’”<sup>63</sup>

Gusse mein aa kar logon ne mutalaba kiya ke Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko saza di jaye. Unhon ne apne maboodon ki tauheen ke badle mein is naujawan ke liye ek badi aag tayyar ki. Woh chahte the ke is waqie ko misaal bana diya jaye taake aage koi un ke maboodon par sawal na uthaye. Unhon ne ek azeem bhatti banayi aur aag jalayi. Sholay itne buland the ke koi shakhs qareeb ja kar Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko aag mein nahi daal sakta tha. Is liye unhon ne unhein door se aag mein phenkne ke liye gulel jaisa ek aala tayyar kiya.

Shehar ke log dekh rahe the ke Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko us aale mein rakha gaya. Is lamhe, muashray ki ghalat baaton ko durust karne ki koshish par apni mushkil soorat-e-haal se shikayat karne ya reham ki bheek maangne ke bajaye, aap ne apne Rab ki taraf rujoo kiya aur us se dua maangi. Aap ne apne Rabb par tawakkul ka izhar karte hue kaha:

حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

“Mere liye Allah kaafi hai, aur kya hi achha kaarsaaz hai!”<sup>64</sup>

Sachche momin durust maqsad ke liye poori koshish karne ke baad Allah ke faisle ko qabool karne ke liye tayyar rehte hain, chahe anjaam kuch bhi ho. Yahan Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> naujawan the, lekin unhon ne apni mushkil soorat-e-haal ki ek baar bhi shikayat nahi ki. Unhon ne apne Khaaliq par be-larzish bharosa rakha ke woh is maamle ko jis tarah chahe behtareen tareeqe se sambhale.

Jab jallad ne jama hue logon ke saamne gulel chhodi hogi to sab log khamoshi se naujawan ke cheekhne aur takleef mein mubtala hone ka intezar kar rahe honge. Unhein apne maboodon ke khilaf kiye gaye ‘jurm’ ki saza milte dekhna chahte the. Lekin is ke bajaye gehri khamoshi chha gayi hogi. Log soch rahe honge: “Yeh cheekh kyun nahi raha? Kya hua?”

Is sawal ka jawab Qur’ān ki is aayat mein hai:

قُلْنَا يَا نَارُ كُونِي بَرْدًا وَسَلَامًا عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ

“Humne farmaya: ‘Aye aag! Ibrāhīm par thandi aur salamati wali ho ja.’”<sup>65</sup>

Aag ki fitrat jalana hai, yeh baat bachche bhi jaante hain. Lekin Allah Ta’ala aasmanon, zameen aur un ke andar ki har cheez ka Khaaliq aur Maalik hai, aag bhi usi ki makhlooq hai. Is liye us ne aag ko us ki fitrat ke baraks kaam karne ka hukm diya.

Allah Ta’ala ki qudrat hikmat se khaali nahi hoti. Agar Allah sirf aag ko ‘thandi’ hone ka hukm deta to yeh tay na hota ke woh kitne kam darja-e-hararat par qaim rahe. Allah ke hukm se woh barf jaisi sard bhi ho sakti thi. Is ke bajaye Allah Ta’ala ne apne bande par itna karam farmaya ke aag ko na sirf apni fitrat ke khilaf kaam karne ka hukm diya, balki usay itna bhi kaha ke Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke liye salamati wali rahe.

Log Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki saza ka tamasha dekhne ke liye jama hue the, lekin un ka mansuba nakaam ho gaya aur bilkul ulta anjaam saamne aaya. Unhon ne Allah Ta’ala ka mojiza aur us ki be-panah qudrat dekhi. Yeh un ke jhoote maboodon ki qudrat nahi thi, balki usi Wahid Ma’bud ki qudrat ka izhar tha jis ki taraf Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> unhein baar baar bula rahe the aur woh inkaar karte rahe.

Is qisse se do aur baatein samne aati hain.

Pehli baat yeh ke aag ko zindagi ki aazmaishon aur mushkilaat ki misaal samjha ja sakta hai. Hum ya to us ki shikayat kar sakte hain ya Allah par bharosa rakh sakte hain, is yaqeen ke saath ke woh cheezon ki fitrat ko bhi badal sakta hai, chahe hamari zahiri samajh us ki gehrai ko na samajh sake. Asal ahmiyat hamare faislon ki hai. Hum gulel mein rakhe hue Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki tarah Allah par tawakkul rakh sakte hain aur dekh sakte hain ke woh na-qabil-e-tasawwur zariye se humein apne huzoor bulandi ata karta hai, ya saamne jalti hui aag par bharosa karne wale tamashaiyon ki tarah ban sakte hain. Soch samajh kar faisla karo, kyunke faislon ke nateeje hote hain.

Doosri baat yeh ke mushkil waqt mein ‘tamashai’ log jama ho jaate hain. Humein Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki tarah hona chahiye, jo is baat par tawajjoh nahi dete the ke log un ke baare mein kya kahenge. Un ki tawajjoh is baat par thi ke un ka Rabb un ke baare mein kya sochega.

Log aur un ki rai hamare liye aham ho sakti hai, lekin humein samajhna chahiye ke zindagi ka asal maqam Aakhirat hai. Agar hum muashray ke banaye hue mayar ke aage jhuk gaye to hum Dozakh ki taraf ja rahe hain, kyunke woh mayar Allah ke tareeqe par mabni nahi. Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa dikhata hai ke Allah ka raasta hi woh wahid raasta hai jahan Allah ka banda zindagi ki jalti hui aag mein bhi thandak aur aman mehsoos kar sakta hai.

Is sukoon aur imtiyaz ki taraf safar shuru karne ke liye bas itna karna hai ke Allah ke saamne haath utha kar dua karo, har anjaam mein us par bharosa rakho aur yaqeen karo ke us ka jawab hi behtareen jawab hai.

Agar hum yaqeen ke saath Allah ki taraf rujoo karein to woh us waqt jawab dega jo hamare liye behtareen hoga, chahe humein us ki kam se kam umeed ho. Yeh connection kahin se shuru hona chahiye, aur us ka maqam tawakkul aur mehnat ke darmiyan hai. Hamare paas Allah se jurne ka unlimited connection hai. Aao is ata kiye hue connection ko istemal karein aur apne Khaaliq se kal nahi, aaj, isi lamhe, abhi baat karein!

64. Sūrah Āl-Imrān, 3:173
65. Sūrah Al-Ambiyā, 21:69. Is waqie ki mazeed taeed Sahīh Al-Bukhārī, Hadīth 4564 mein bhi milti hai.
