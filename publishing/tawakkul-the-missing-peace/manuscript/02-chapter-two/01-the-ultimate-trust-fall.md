# Bab Doosra (Chapter Two)
# Tawakkul — Sab Se Bara Bharosa (The Ultimate Trust Fall)

Bahut se log aaj ke is jadeed daur mein yeh maan'ne se chook jaate hain ke kabhi kabhi jo raasta tumhare liye tayyar kiya gaya hota hai, us se bach nikalna mumkin nahi hota. Aur jitna tum apni qismat se bhaagte ho, utna hi us ki taraf badhte chale jaate ho, kyunke Allah Ta'ala ka nizaam aisa hai ke woh humein ghooma-phira kar usi nuktay par le aata hai, aur humein ehsaas bhi nahi hota. Hum qanoon ke mutabiq chalte hain, lekin qawaneen usi ki tabedari karte hain!

“Woh har naye paida hone wale ladke ko khatam kar do!” hukm tha us hukmaran ka, jab us ke waziron ne use apni nasal se taluq rakhne wale ek ladke ke baaray mein peshgoyi sunai jo us ke zawaal ka sabab banta. Yoon us daur ka bara qatl-e-aam shuru hua, aur hukmaran ka ‘mansuba’ amal mein aa gaya. Us ki man'tiqe behas se baala thi, aur us ke aadmi us ke hukm par itne be-reham ban kar kaam kar rahe the ke maasoom bachchon aur un ke walidain par bhi taras na aaya.

Hamilah aurton ko khabardaar kar diya gaya ke agar un ke haan ladka paida hua to bachcha qatl kar diya jayega. Is khabar ke sadmay ne us zamane ki maon ko itna dahshat-zada kar diya ke woh apne beton ki muskurahat se aane wali garmiyon ka tasawwur karne ke bajaye sirf sardi ki thandi hawaon ko apni reedh ki haddi mein utarta mehsoos karti thin. Yoon walidain ko is baat ka samna karna pada ke un ke maasoom bete bina kisi wajah ke qatl kiye ja sakte the.

Aisi hi ek maa ne us khauf ke liye tayyari shuru kar di, aur bachcha janam dene ke waqt, hamilgi ke mushkil mahino aur dard ke kai ghanton se guzarte hue, ek sargoshi ne udaas khabar di: “Ladka hua hai!” Aankhon mein aansu liye, aur apne bachche ke muntazir anjaam ko jaante hue, us ne Allah se dua ki aur apna muqaddama pesh kiya.

Allah Ta’ala mazloom ki pukaar sunta hai, aur yoon us ne use ek hal ilham ki shakal mein diya: “Use daryaa mein daal do, lekin is se pehle use doodh pilao.”

Tum soch sakte ho, yeh bhala kaisa hal hua? Kahani ki us aurat ki soorat-e-haal aisi thi ke ladka paida hona khud ek badi pareshani ban chuka tha. Is liye, apni bechaini mein us ne Allah se dua ki, kyunke woh apna bachcha khone wali thi, aur hal kya tha? Phir bhi bachcha khona hi tha?

Zehni andazay se dekhain to is par aitiraz karna asaan hai, kyunke hum apni nazar ko kahani ke sirf ek pehlu tak mehdood rakhte hain. Dekho, tawakkul yeh nahi ke insaan Allah ki taraf rujoo kare aur phir us ke hukm par sawal uthaye. Balki tawakkul yeh hai ke insaan Allah ki taraf is yaqeen ke saath jhuke ke Allah ka ilm hamare ilm se be-hisaab zyada hai, aur jab hamari nazar mukhtalif rukawaton se ‘band’ ho jati hai, to us ka ilm har us cheez se aage hota hai jo hamari dekhne ki salahiyat ko dhundla karti hai. Is liye wahi hamari rehnumai ke liye sab se behtar jagah par hai, aur chahe yeh baat humein kitni hi na-munasib lage, wohi ek Zaat hai jo qawaneen ki us had se bahar hai jo hamari harkat ko muqayyad karti hai, kyunke woh sab qawaneen ka malik aur hukmaran hai!

Ab Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ka qissa dekho. Unhein us ‘badi udaas’ soorat-e-haal mein daal diya gaya tha jahan woh is khauf mein apni hamilgi guzarti thin ke agar ladka hua to bachcha barbaad kar diya jayega. Fir’aun ne is saal paida hone wale tamaam nar bachchon ko qatl karne ka sakht hukm diya tha, us khwab ke khauf se jo us ne dekha tha aur jis mein ishara tha ke Palestine ki zameen se aane wala ek ladka us ki sakht hukmarani ka anjaam banega.

Fir’aun ne badi bareeki se mansuba bandi ki, lekin use yeh samajh na aaya ke ek maa apne bachche ki salamati ke liye usi Zaat se dua kar rahi thi jo waqai ibadat ke laayiq hai.

Allah Ta’ala ne use ilham farmaya:

وَأَوْحَيْنَا إِلَىٰ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ أَنْ أَرْضِعِيهِ ۖ فَإِذَا خِفْتِ عَلَيْهِ فَأَلْقِيهِ فِي الْيَمِّ وَلَا تَخَافِي وَلَا تَحْزَنِي ۖ إِنَّا رَادُّوهُ إِلَيْكِ وَجَاعِلُوهُ مِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ

“Aur humne Musa ki walida ki taraf wahi ki ke use doodh pilao; phir jab tum us ke baare mein khauf karo to use daryaa mein daal do aur daro nahi aur gham na karo. Yaqeenan hum use tumhari taraf lauta denge aur use rasoolon mein se bana denge.”<sup>8</sup>

Tasawwur karo, yeh kitne yaqeen, kitne aitmaad aur kitne tawakkul ki misaal thi jo us ne Allah Ta’ala par kiya ke is mansube par amal kare? Yeh woh lamha tha jahan hal bhi masle jitna hi bura lag raha tha. Yeh woh soorat-e-haal thi jise sirf zaahiri soch rakhne walay log ‘dono taraf se nuqsaan’ kahenge. Ah! Lekin yahi to woh baat hai jo Allah ke mansube par yaqeen na rakhne walay kehte hain. Is liye yeh bab qari ke samne tareekh ka sab se bara bharosa pesh karta hai.<sup>9</sup>

Jo shakhs apna bharosa aur tawakkul Allah Ta’ala par rakhta hai, woh har soorat-e-haal ko ‘win-win’ samajhta hai, kyunke asal mein har cheez Allah ki marzi ke mutabiq hoti hai aur hum us ke faislay ke aage sar jhuka dete hain, chahe soorat-e-haal achi ho ya buri.

Kahani par dobara nazar daalo to Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ko sirf daryaa mein bachcha daalne ka hukm nahi diya gaya tha! Nahi! Allah Ta’ala Al-Hakīm hai, yani Hikmat wala, aur is liye us ke mansube mein badi hikmat hoti hai. Us ne pehle bachche ko doodh pilane ka hukm diya, aur jab use khauf hua ke Fir’aun ke sipahi qareeb aa sakte hain ya soorat-e-haal zyada khatarnaak ho sakti hai, tab use apne pyaare bachche ko daryaa mein daalna tha.

Is doodh pilane ke hukm mein do badi hikmaton ke moti chhupe hue the. Ek par bab ke aage chal kar guftagu hogi; doosri ko samajhne ke liye khud soorat-e-haal par ghaur karna hoga, jo yaqeenan bahut sakht rahi hogi. Socho: sipahi naye paida hone walon ko dhoond rahe hain, aur naye paida hone wale bachche se yeh umeed bhi nahi ki ja sakti ke woh soorat-e-haal ko samjhe, kyunke woh abhi duniya mein aaya hi tha. Aisi soorat mein maa ke do bunyadi masail the. Pehla aur sab se zahir masla bachche ki hifazat tha, aur doosra use chhupa kar rakhna tha. Lekin naya bachcha kisi bhi waqt ro sakta hai, aur use lapet kar khamosh rehne ko kaha nahi ja sakta. Is liye jab sipahi qareeb hote, bachche ki hifazat ka behtareen zariya yeh tha ke use doodh pilaya jaye. Jab bachcha doodh pi raha hota hai to sipahiyon ki maujoodgi ke dauran woh khamosh rehta hai, aur maa ko apne bachche ki hifazat ka behtar mauqa mil jata hai. Allah Ta’ala ke hukm mein aisi hikmat hai jo us ki kitaab ki aayaat par ghaur o fikr karne walon par zahir hoti hai. Yeh us ke mansube mein maujood foran samajh aane wali hikmat ka moti hai.

Yoon hua ke use apne bachche se juda hona pada, aur Allah ke waade par ke woh use ‘lauta dega’, us ne woh kaam kiya jo bahut kam maayein kar paati hain: us ne apne bachche se zyada Allah par bharosa kiya!

Apni is qadr wahm-zada soorat-e-haal ki wajah se ke kahin Israiliyon ke ek ladke ke haath se us ki hukmarani ulat na di jaye, Fir’aun ne aise mutaaddid waqeaat ka silsila chala diya jo maasoom nar bachchon ke qatl par khatam hua. Natija yeh hua ke Musa ki walida ke paas Allah se dua karne ke siwa koi raasta na bacha, aur Fir’aun ki mansuba bandi bachche ko kis taraf le gayi?

فَالْتَقَطَهُ آلُ فِرْعَوْنَ لِيَكُونَ لَهُمْ عَدُوًّا وَحَزَنًا ۗ إِنَّ فِرْعَوْنَ وَهَامَانَ وَجُنُودَهُمَا كَانُوا خَاطِئِينَ

“Phir Fir’aun ke ghar walon ne use utha liya, taake woh un ke liye dushman aur gham ka sabab ban jaye. Beshak Fir’aun, Haman aur un ke lashkar khata-kar the.”<sup>10</sup>

Wohi bachcha jise hukmaran door rakhna chahta tha, ab us ke darwaze par tha, aur apne saare da’we, apni daulat aur yahan tak ke apni khudaai ke da’we ke bawajood, ek maasoom bachche ki maujoodgi ne us ki sab koshishon ko be-asar kar diya!

Bahut se log aaj ke is jadeed daur mein yeh maan'ne se chook jaate hain ke kabhi kabhi jo raasta tumhare liye tayyar kiya gaya hota hai, us se bach nikalna mumkin nahi hota. Aur jitna tum apni qismat se bhaagte ho, utna hi us ki taraf badhte chale jaate ho, kyunke Allah Ta’ala ka nizaam aisa hai ke woh humein ghooma-phira kar usi nuktay par le aata hai, aur humein ehsaas bhi nahi hota, kyunke hum qanoon ke mutabiq chalte hain, lekin qawaneen usi ki tabedari karte hain!

Kabhi log batate hain ke nikah tootne ke baad woh kitne mayus hue, aur baad mein un ki shaadi usi se ho gayi jise woh deron se jaante the. Kabhi ek aisa shakhs jo deen ki be-hurmatī karta tha, usi ko un usoolon mein sukoon mil gaya jin se woh pehle sakht nafrat karta tha. Ya woh bachcha jo kisi khaas kaam se nafrat karta tha, lekin ab bari hone ke baad usi kaam par mabni karobar ka malik hai? Yahan Allah Ta’ala ki qudrat aur hikmat ka ek bahut bada haath kaam kar raha hota hai.

Dobara guftagu par aate hain: Fir’aun ka is ladke ke bare mein rawayya achha nahi tha, aur us ki bechaini lagataar barh rahi thi, lekin us ki biwi ke apne koi bachche na the aur us ne is naye paida hone wale bachche ko ek achi alamat samjha:

وَقَالَتِ امْرَأَةُ فِرْعَوْنَ قُرَّتُ عَيْنٍ لِّي وَلَكَ ۖ لَا تَقْتُلُوهُ ۖ عَسَىٰ أَن يَنفَعَنَا أَوْ نَتَّخِذَهُ وَلَدًا وَهُمْ لَا يَشْعُرُونَ

“Aur Fir’aun ki biwi ne kaha: ‘Yeh bachcha mere liye bhi aankhon ki thandak hai aur tumhare liye bhi. Isay qatl na karo. Shayad yeh hamare kaam aaye, ya hum ise beta bana lein.’ Aur woh samajh na sake ke amr waqea mein kaise tayyar ho rahe the aur un ka anjaam kya hone wala tha.”<sup>11</sup>

Qur’ān wazeh karta hai ke Fir’aun ki biwi bachche se khush thi aur us ne apne shohar ko raazi kiya ke use apne ghar mein beta bana kar rakh le, jabke us waqt woh apne aamal ke natiyon se be-khabar the.

Ab tak is qisse mein Qur’ān ne humein do mukhtalif kahaniyan di hain. Ek taraf woh maa hai jo apne bachche se muhabbat karti hai, lekin Allah ke hukm par use chhodna padta hai, aur aakhir mein us ka bachcha us ki qaum ka najat dahinda ban kar barhta hai. Doosri taraf ek aur maa hai jo bachcha paida nahi kar sakti, aur bachche ki tamanna mein mubtala hai, aur woh apne shohar ko ek anjaan bachche ko ghar lane par raazi kar leti hai, lekin yeh bachcha aakhirkar us ke shohar ki hukmarani ke zawaal ka sabab ban jata hai. Yeh do kahaniyan do aham baatein zahir karti hain: pehli, ke ek shakhs ke khud-gharz aamaal kis tarah waqeaat ka silsila shuru kar sakte hain jo aakhir mein usi ko barbaad kar de; aur doosri, ke kabhi kabhi jis cheez se tum bhaag rahe hote ho, wohi tumhari taraf aa jati hai, lekin tum use pehchan nahi paate.

In dono ulat kahaniyon ka farq yeh hai ke Fir’aun ne yeh samajh kar ke sab kuch us ke qabze mein hai, sab faislay apne haath mein le liye. Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ne is ke baraks yeh maan liya ke woh kuch karne ki haalat mein nahi thin. Unhon ne apna bharosa aur tawakkul Allah Ta’ala par rakh diya! Natija bilkul un ke haq mein nikla, kyunke un ka bachcha mehfooz tha.

To Allah ki taraf rujoo karo, kyunke agar koi Zaat aisi soorat-e-haal se bhi imkān nikal sakti hai jo na-mumkin jaisi lage, to wohi Zaat hai. Jo cheezein humein na-mumkin lagti hain, un ke liye us ke paas koi mushkil nahi!

Guftagu ko aage barhate hain: maa hone ki wajah se agle din bechaini us ke andar utar rahi thi aur Qur’āni wazahat ke mutabiq use kisi na kisi tasalli ki zarurat thi:

وَأَصْبَحَ فُؤَادُ أُمِّ مُوسَىٰ فَارِغًا ۖ إِن كَادَتْ لَتُبْدِي بِهِۦ لَوْلَآ أَن رَّبَطْنَا عَلَىٰ قَلْبِهَا لِتَكُونَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ

“Aur Musa ki maa ka dil khaali ho gaya tha; agar hum us ke dil ko mazboot na karte to woh zaroor us ke baaray mein sab kuch zaahir kar deti.”<sup>12</sup>

Yeh Qur’āni paigham ki sachchai ka husn hai. Is ne Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ko kisi be-hiss momin ki tarah pesh nahi kiya! Woh maa thin! Unhon ne yeh bachcha uthaya tha! Ji haan, unhon ne Allah par bharosa kiya tha, lekin phir bhi unhein insani jazbaat ka samna karna pada, aur Allah Ta’ala ne un ka dil mazboot kar diya. Yahan Qur’ān in baaton ki taraf tawajjoh dilaata hai:

a) insani fitrat

b) dil

c) imaan

Imaan woh cheez hai jo dil mein rehti hai, aur dil ko is liye baar baar dekha aur parakha jana chahiye taake har cheez ka tawazun barqarar rahe. Qur’ān ne yeh bhi bataya ke woh aman aur itminan hasil karne ke liye jo insaan lagataar dhoondta rehta hai, dil ko har qadam par Allah Ta’ala ki yaad mein tarbiyat deni hogi:

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

“Jo log iman laaye aur jin ke dil Allah ki yaad se sukoon paate hain; sun lo, Allah ki yaad hi mein dilon ko sukoon milta hai.”<sup>13</sup>

Insaan hamesha ya to daulat ki talash mein rehta hai ya ilm ki. Talib-e-ilm taleem ke liye qarz ya maali madad chahta hai, taake aage chal kar daulat kamaye, sukoon paaye; lekin daulat ke saath pareshaniyan aur ilm ke saath zimmedariyan bhi barh jati hain, aur phir bhi talib sukoon hi dhoond raha hota hai! Qur’ān ne 1400 saal pehle is masle ka hal bata diya tha jab us ne humein bataya ke sukoon sirf un dilon mein aata hai jin ka Allah se gehra rabta ho. Tab hi hum us ke muqarrar kiye hue qawaneen ko khushi se qabool kar sakte hain!

Dobara Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ki kahani par aate hain: unhon ne apni beti ko bheja taake woh apne bachche ki jagah se mutaliq khabar la sake.<sup>14</sup> Beti ne pata lagaya ke us ka chhota bhai ab usi Fir’aun ki hifazat mein hai jo use qatl karna chahta tha. Aur is se bhi zyada fikr ki baat yeh thi ke woh bachcha doodh pilane wali aurton ka doodh qubool nahi kar raha tha aur pareshan tha.

Is se us ki palanhar maa bhi aur zyada pareshan ho gayi, kyunke us ne bachche ko apna samajh liya tha. Ek ke baad ek doodh pilane wali aurat use doodh pilane ki koshish karti rahi, lekin bachcha doodh peene se inkaar karta raha, aur palanhar maa ki bechaini tab tak barhti rahi jab tak bachche ki behn mahal mein nahi pohanchi aur us ne ek tajweez pesh ki, jaisa ke Qur’ān ki aayi hui aayat mein bayan hai:

فَقَالَتْ هَلْ أَدُلُّكُمْ عَلَىٰ أَهْلِ بَيْتٍ يَكْفُلُونَهُ لَكُمْ وَهُمْ لَهُ نَاصِحُونَ

“Phir us ne kaha: ‘Kya main tumhein aise ghar walon ka pata doon jo tumhare liye is ki parwarish kar dein aur us ke liye khair-khwah hon?’”<sup>14</sup>

Jab woh majboor soorat-e-haal mein thi to Fir’aun ki biwi ne bachche ko us shakhs se doodh pilane ki ijazat de di, aur ab, pehle ki sab doodh pilane wali aurton ke muqable mein, bachche ne is maqbool doodh pilane wali aurat ke seene ko qubool kiya aur aakhirkaar ghiza hasil ki. Is tarah bachcha apni maa ki god mein laut aaya aur Allah Ta’ala ka waada aik aisi surat mein poora hua jo pehle namumkin lagti thi!

Is azeem qisse ke aaghaz par Allah ka hukm yeh tha ke bachche ko daryaa mein daal diya jaye, lekin doodh pilane ke baad. Kahani ki yeh chhoti si tafseel asal mein bahut aham sabit hui, kyunke doodh pilane ka hukm sirf yeh yaqeeni nahi banata tha ke bachcha khamosh aur mehfooz rahe, balki yeh bhi ke woh apni maa ke doodh ka aadi ho jaye. Yoon woh Allah ki marzi ke aage sar jhuka kar kisi aur doodh pilane wali aurat ka doodh qubool na kare! Bab ke aaghaz mein is hukm ki foran samajh aane wali hikmat bayan ho chuki thi, aur upar walay hissa mein us waade ke andar maujood aakhri hikmat wazeh ki gayi hai.

Yeh un be-shumaar imkānāt mein se sirf ek hai jo us shakhs ko hasil ho sakte hain jo Allah ki qudrat aur hikmat par apna mukammal bharosa rakhne ko tayyar ho!

### 3 Badi Aham Baatein

Is Qur’āni qisse ke do bunyadi kirdaron ka anjaam ghaur karne wale qari ke liye bilkul wazeh hai. Fir’aun ka yaqeen tha ke kyunke us ke paas badi fauj hai aur jin logon par woh zulm kar raha hai woh apna difa nahi kar sakte, is liye woh apne zulmani maqsad ko asani se poora kar lega. Us ne apna bharosa apni daulat aur hukm par rakha. Doosri taraf Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ke paas koi daulat ya sahara nahi tha, kyunke woh Fir’aun ki ghulam thin, lekin unhon ne apna bharosa us Zaat par rakha jis ke haath mein insani muamalat ka mukammal ikhtiyar hai!

Is qisse ke waqeaat ek aur aham aayat ko samajhne mein madad dete hain jo Allah par tawakkul ka ajr batati hai, kyunke Musa ki walida:

1) aisi sakht majboori mein thin jahan se nikalne ka koi raasta nazar nahi aata tha,
2) pehle hi ghareebi mein thin (ghulami ki wajah se).

Aaj ke insaan kabhi kabhi aisi tang soorat-e-haal mein phans jaate hain jahan unhein apni samaji, nafsiyati ya maali mushkilaat se nikalne ka koi raasta nazar nahi aata.

In do nuqaat ko zehan mein rakhte hue, aur Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ke tawakkul ke qisse ke saath, Qur’ān farmata hai:

وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۚ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ

“Aur jo Allah se darta hai, Allah us ke liye nikalne ka raasta bana deta hai aur usay aisi jagah se rizq ata farmata hai jahan se usay guman bhi nahi hota. Aur jo Allah par tawakkul kare to Allah us ke liye kaafi hai.”<sup>15</sup>

Anjaam mein, us ka bachcha sirf Allah ke waade ke mutabiq us ke paas lautaya hi nahi gaya, balki us ki maali haisiyat bhi buland ho gayi, kyunke woh ab Fir’aun ke god liye hue bachche ki daaya thi, aur is liye goya dobarah apne hi bete ki walida ban gayi. Subhānallāh! Us ke tareeqe kitne azeem hain!

Is aayat se chand baatein nikalti hain:

Pehli baat yeh ke momin ko apni rozī (rizq) ke liye Allah Ta’ala par poora tawakkul rakhna chahiye. Duniya mein aaj sab se bara masla apne aur apne ghar walon ke liye farahmi ka hai. Is liye maali mustahkamī ki talash mein bahut se Muslim apni soorat-e-haal se pareshan ho jate hain, aur jab soorat-e-haal bahut kharab ho jaye to kabhi kabhi woh apna Iman bhi daaw par laga dete hain. Jaisa ke pehle kaha gaya, momin ke liye Allah par bharosa saans lene jaisa asaan hona chahiye, lekin jab hum apna bharosa asbaab-e-fitri par rakhte hain to nateeja na-umeedi hi hota hai, kyunke makhlooq Khaaliq jaisi nahi hoti. Khaaliq ne jo hudood apni makhlooq ke liye tay ki hain, hum unhin mein qaid rehte hain. Badqismati se insani fitrat yeh hai ke jab tak asbaab hamare haq mein chal rahe hon, hum apne aap ko theek samajhte hain, aur jaise hi cheezein ulatna shuru hoti hain, tab Allah ki taraf rujoo karte hain. Phir jab zindagi dobara sambhal jati hai, to hum Allah ko ‘bhool’ jate hain. Yeh hamari taraf se ghalat hai. Qur’ān humein yaad dilata hai:

وَاللَّهُ خَيْرُ الرَّازِقِينَ

“Aur Allah sab se behtar rizq dene wala hai.”<sup>16</sup>

Bas zarurat is baat ki hai ke hum us par be-shak bharosa karein.

Bahut se Musalman aise hain jinhein Allah ne badi daulat se nawaza hai aur woh duniya bhar ka safar kar chuke hain, lekin Hajj ki apni farz zimmedari poori karne ka koi iraada nahi karte. Doosri taraf kuch log mahine ke aakhir mein bill bhi mushkil se ada karte hain, aur un ke apne iqraar ke mutabiq, woh kehte hain: “Shaykh? Main Hajj karne ka bahut khwahishmand tha aur niyyat bhi kar li thi, lekin Allah hi jaane paisa kahan se aaya, aur kaun kaun se log madad ke liye aage aaye, aur ab main aap ko bata raha hoon ke main Hajj ke liye ja raha hoon!”

Doosri aham baat yeh hai ke Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki kahani is baat ko wazeh karti hai ke un ki samaji aur maali haisiyat ghulami se uth kar Fir’aun ke mahal tak pohanch gayi. Pichhli aayat ne aaj ke momineen ke liye ek nihayat aham baat samjhai thi:

“Aur jo Allah se darta hai, Allah us ke liye nikalne ka raasta bana deta hai aur usay aisi jagah se rizq ata farmata hai jahan se usay guman bhi nahi hota. Aur jo Allah par tawakkul kare to Allah us ke liye kaafi hai.”

Zahir mein, aur ab tak bayan ki gayi baaton ke saath, yeh aayat samajhne mein seedhi lagti hai. Lekin jab is ke Qur’ān mein maujood maqam par ghaur kiya jaye, aur phir ise aaj ke muashray ki mutadid soorat-e-haal se milaya jaye, to maloom hota hai ke is aayat ka asal ma’ni bahut se Musalmanon ne bhula diya hai.

Yeh khaas aayat Sūrah At-Talāq mein hai, aur Talāq se murad Islami nizaam-e-talaaq hai. Aaj ke ghair-deeni adalati nizaamon ne talaaq ke baad shohar aur bachche ke huqooq ke liye kharch ki adalat bana rakhi hai. Afsos ki baat yeh hai ke pehle shohar aur biwiyan ek doosre se huqooq ke liye larte hue bahut sa waqt aur paisa zaya kar deti hain. Is dauran nikah se paida hone wale bachche do chattanon ke darmiyan phans kar reh jate hain. Biwiyon aur shoharon ke darmiyan bad-goi ki muhim chal padti hai, aur bachche kabhi kabhi poore maamle mein adalat ke andar bhi aa jate hain.

Allah Ta’ala momin ko yeh dawat de raha hai ke talaaq ya kisi bhi aur maamle mein behtar yeh hai ke banda apna tawakkul us par rakhe, jo aisi pareshan-kun soorat-e-haal se nikalne ka raasta bana deta hai, na ke aise nizaam par bharosa kare jo kharab bhi ho sakta hai. Agar hamari pyari behnein khaas taur par Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul rakhain, yeh sochte hue ke us ki dee hui hidayat talaaq ke liye makhsoos surah mein aayi hai, to Musalman adalaton ke kamron mein ek doosre se dunyawi mafaad ke liye kam larte nazar aayenge. Isi tarah agar zimmedari se kaam na lene wale shohar Allah Ta’ala ki adalat ke saamne khade kiye jayein, to woh us ke faislay se kabhi na bach sakein, aur talaq-shuda maon ki pareshaniyan kam ho jayen.

Wazeh hai ke 1400 saal baad bhi Qur’ān humein yeh sikha raha hai ke zindagi ke safar ko baghair zyada pareshaniyon ke kaise guzara jaye. Allah Ta’ala humein un ikhtiyarat ki taraf rehnumai farmaye jo usay pasand hon!

Teesri baat yeh hai ke duniya ki sab se muazzaz maayon aur un ke bete ki kahani phir se yeh sabit karti hai ke asal wa’ada sirf Allah ka wa’ada hai. Aur jis tarah us ne ek maa aur bachche ko ek namumkin si soorat-e-haal se dobara milaya, woh tumhare liye bhi bahut kuch kar sakta hai.

To is sab se bade bharose ko qabool karo, kyunke chahe raasta dhundla ho, Allah kisi anjaan zariye se tumhare liye raasta khol dega, aur is sab ke liye bas us ki maujoodgi ka ehsaas rakhna aur apna pur-aitmaad tawakkul us par rakhna hai, aur woh tumhare liye bhi nikalne ka raasta bana dega!

(Sunan Ibn Mājah, Hadīth 4303)
