# Bab Chaharum (Chapter Four)
# Jab Sab Raaste Band Ho Jayen, Allah Ghalib Rehta Hai! (When All Else Fails, God Prevails!)

“Zindagi mein kabhi kabhi humein aisi mushkil soorat-e-haal ka samna hota hai jo hamare imaan ki bunyaad ko aazmati hai. Aise lamhon mein sahi raaste se hat kar na-umeed hone ke bajaye humein un khayalat se door ho jana chahiye jo humein intezar karte hue Shaytaan ki makkaari ke panjon mein phansa sakte hain.”

“Chhup jao!” rehnuma ne sargoshi mein kaha. Woh pehle hi pareshan qaidiyon ko hidayat de raha tha ke kisi ki nazar mein aaye baghair aage badhein. Yaqeenan woh qaid the, lekin is ka matlab yeh nahi tha ke woh kisi jurm ke mujrim the. Ghulami ki zanjeeron ne unhein jakad rakha tha, lekin kai dashkon ke baad azaadi nazar aane lagi thi.

Raat ho chuki thi. Andhera jaldi farar hone ke liye behtareen tha, lekin badshah ke sipahi giddhon ki tarah hoshiyar the aur hawa ki halki si aahat par bhi tawajjoh dete the. Qaidkhane se mushkil se nikalne ke baad qaidiyon ke hausle kabhi umeed aur kabhi na-umeedi ke darmiyan badalte rahe. Jab azaadi sirf chand qadam door thi aur samandar saamne nazar aa raha tha, achanak sipahiyon ki na-pasandida awaazein peeche se qareeb aati mehsoos hui.

Kuch ne poocha, “Tum humein kahan le aaye ho?” Doosre dard se apna sar pakad kar kehne lage, “Raasta band hai, hum barbaad ho gaye!” Saamne samandar aur peeche sipahi the. Dono taraf se koi raasta na hone ki wajah se unhein haar maan leni chahiye thi. Aage pani ki gehraiyon mein imkānāt ka ek samandar ruka hua tha aur peeche sipahiyon ka samandar un ki taraf badh raha tha.

Woh apne rehnuma ki taraf palte. Un ke chehron par yaqeen se khaali hairat thi. Unhein laga ke yahi anjaam hai... lekin kya waqai aisa tha?

Zahiri soch ke mutabiq is kahani ka anjaam bilkul wazeh lagta hai. Jab kam samaan rakhne wale ghulamon ka ek giroh badshahi fauj se bhag raha ho aur pakde jaane par majboor ho gaya ho to sirf ek hi anjaam nazar aata hai. Lekin is qisse ki asal tadbeer samajhne ke liye humein us ke aaghaz ki taraf dekhna hoga, jaisa ke Bab Doosra mein bayan hua.

Fir’aun naam ka ek be-reham aur makkar hukmaran tha. Us ne khwab mein Palestine se uthti hui aag dekhi thi jo aakhirkar us ki hukmarani ko tabaah karne wali thi. Is peshgoyi ke khauf se us ne Palestine se talluq rakhne wale khandanon mein paida hone wale tamaam ladkon ko qatl karne ka hukm de diya. Is qatl ke hukm ne waqiaat ka aisa silsila shuru kiya jismein ek maa ne Allah par bharosa kiya aur use apne bachche ko daryaa mein daalne ka hukm diya gaya.

Allah Ta’ala ne aakhir us bachche ko usi Fir’aun ke ghar tak pohancha diya. Fir’aun ki nek biwi ki darkhwast par bachcha us ke ghar mein god liya gaya, aur woh bada ho kar Allah ka azeem Nabi, Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup>, bana.

Kuch saal baad Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko ek ilzaam ki wajah se wahan se hijrat karni padi. Woh Madyan ke ilaqe mein pohanche, jahan kai saal Huzoor Shu’ayb <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki nigraani mein kaam kiya aur aakhir un ki beti se nikah kiya.

Kai saal baad jab Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Misr wapas ja rahe the to raaste mein aandhi ke dauran unhon ne pahaad par aag dekhi. Woh is umeed mein us ki taraf gaye ke shayad apni biwi ke liye us mein se kuch roshni le aayen. Jab woh wahan pohanche to ek awaaz ne unhein pukara. Unhein bataya gaya ke yeh tamam jahanon ke Rab Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se aane wali awaaz hai jo un se kalaam farma raha hai.

Allah Ta’ala ne us aham lamhe mein unhein mojiza ata farmaya. Un ki asa saanp ban gayi, aur jab Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne apna daahina haath nikala to woh roshan chamakne laga. Yeh ghair-maamooli nishaniyan tamashe ke liye nahi thin. Maqsad yeh tha ke Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Fir’aun ke saamne Allah ka paigham pesh karein, lekin us se jung na karein; balki mojizaat ke zariye use samjhayen. Allah Ta’ala ne unhein sakhti ke bajaye Fir’aun se guftagu karne ka hukm diya, shayad woh apni bad-kirdari se palat aaye.

Yahan dars yeh hai ke Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Allah Ta’ala ki madad ke bawajood Fir’aun ko mita dene ke bajaye use samajhane aur taleem dene ke liye bheje gaye.

Ghaur karo to is qisse mein humein dikhaya gaya hai ke Fir’aun apne daur ki sab se badi quwwat tha. Us ke paas badi fauj aur har qism ki daulat thi, lekin Allah Ta’ala muashray ke nazdeek kisi ‘be-maaya’ shakhs ko bhi maghroor logon ko shikast dene ki qudrat de sakta hai. Tumhari daulat, jaidad aur har cheez Allah ki ata hai. Agar tum unhein durust tareeqe se istemal na karo to Allah unhein tum se utni hi aasani se le sakta hai jitni aasani se us ne tumhein ata kiya tha, aur tumhari nazar mein kamtar samjhe jaane wale kisi shakhs ke zariye bhi le sakta hai.

Is liye humein Allah par bharosa aur tawakkul rakhna chahiye, kyunke har soorat-e-haal ka anjaam usi ke ikhtiyar mein hai.

Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke paas sirf mojizati asa, roshan haath aur Allah par tawakkul tha. Isi ke saath woh apne daur ki sab se badi quwwat ka samna karne nikle. Lekin un ke saamne ek aur mushkil thi: apna maqsad poora karne ke liye unhein guftagu mein fasahat aur hikmat ki zarurat thi.<sup>44</sup> Unhon ne dua ki:

قَالَ رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي ۝ وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي ۝ وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي ۝ يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي

“Musa ne arz ki: ‘Aye mere Rab! Mere liye mera seena khol de, mere kaam ko mere liye asaan kar de, meri zubaan ki girah khol de, taake log meri baat samajh saken.’”<sup>45</sup>

Socho, Allah Ta’ala tumhein kisi kaam ke liye asa aur mojizati nishaniyon se madad de. Kya yeh kaafi nahi hona chahiye? Phir bhi Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> Allah Ta’ala se dua kar rahe the. Tawakkul ka dars yeh hai ke apni qudrat aur muashray mein apne maqam ki bina par kabhi maghroor na ho.

Yeh baat bhi nihayat aham hai ke humein apne Rab se lagataar baat karni chahiye aur us se guftagu karni chahiye. Sirf Arabi duain padhne par tawajjoh na ho, balke un ke ma’ni samajhne ki bhi koshish ho. Apne Rab se guftagu ka mauzu Bab Panjum mein bayan hoga, InshaAllah.

Doosri baat yeh hai ke zindagi mein jo bhi kaam saamne aaye, use Allah ke naam se shuru karna chahiye taake us mein barkat ho. Phir yeh bunyadi yaqeen bhi hona chahiye ke koi kaam tabhi asaan hoga jab Allah Ta’ala apne fazl se use hamare liye asaan kare. Us ki madad ke baghair hamare kisi kaam ya mansube se haqiqi faida hasil nahi ho sakta.

Is ke bawajood aasani hamesha kamyabi ki daleel nahi hoti. Kabhi mushkilaat bhi Allah ke muqarrar kiye hue mansube ka hissa hoti hain.

Teesri baat yeh hai ke ilm qudrat hai. Allah Ta’ala humein sikha raha hai ke jab hum us ke paigham ki numaindagi kar rahe hon tab bhi ilm ki talash jaari rehti hai. Ilm ki talash kisi sanad ya qabiliyat par khatam nahi hoti. Kaunsi sanad insaan ko nubuwwat ke maqam tak pohancha sakti hai? Phir bhi Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne Allah se ilm maanga. Unhon ne koi bhi ilm nahi maanga, balki apne Rab ki taraf se aane wala ilm maanga.

Aaj ke daur mein ilm wala samjha jana Allah se doori ke saath jod diya gaya hai. Natija yeh hai ke hamara bharosa Allah ke mansube ki hikmat par nahi raha, balki un baaton par ho gaya hai jinhein humein ‘ilm’ ke naam par sikhaya gaya. Yoon hamara bharosa insani mahireen ki tahqiq par tik gaya hai. Lekin jis tarah insani ilm apni rai badalta rehta hai, humein bhi har tabdeeli ke saath apni rai badalni padti hai, aur isi wajah se hum andar se be-tawazun ho jaate hain.

Is ke baraks Allah ki taraf se milne wala ilm yaqeen par mabni hota hai. Use apni baat badalne ki zarurat nahi hoti, kyunke us ki bunyaad mein koi shak nahi. Isi liye Allah Ta’ala ne Qur’ān ki doosri surah ka aaghaz farmaya:

الم ۝ ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ

“Alif-Lām-Mīm. Yeh woh kitaab hai jis mein koi shak nahi, parhezgaron ke liye hidayat hai.”<sup>46</sup>

Allah ka ilm yaqeen par mabni hai, jabke insani ilm shak se bhara hua hai. Ek waqt tha jab mahireen ka khayal tha ke zameen chapti hai aur jo shakhs is ke kinare tak pohanchega woh neeche gir jayega. Us waqt unhein apni baat par yaqeen tha. Aaj unhon ne apne purane bayanat se rujoo kar liya hai aur naye maloomat ne purane yaqeen ko ghalat sabit kar diya hai.

To hum aaj un ki baat ko sach kyun maan lein? Unhein kaise yaqeen hai ke mustaqbil mein un ki baat nahi badlegi? Koi sachcha maahir yeh nahi kahega ke aaj ki maloomat kal nahi badlegi. Agar hum insani ilm par bharosa karenge to hum aise zehni musafiron ki tarah honge jo har din apna aqeeda aur mauqif badalte rehte hain. Lekin agar hum Allah Ta’ala ke ata kiye hue ilm par imaan aur bharosa rakhein to shak ki zarurat ke baghair ilm ke ek seedhe raaste par qaim rahenge.

Guftagu ko dobara Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki taraf laate hain. Unhon ne Fir’aun ko samjhaya ke woh har had paar kar chuka hai aur ab bhi tauba ka waqt baqi hai. Lekin bigde hue dil ka haal yeh hota hai ke woh doosron ke faide ke bajaye sirf apni hukmarani ke liye khatre ko dekhta hai. Fir’aun ne Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki naseehat ko apne khilaf challenge samjha, halaanke Allah Ta’ala ne use apni islaah ke liye bahut si nishaniyan dikhayi thin:

فَأَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمُ الطُّوفَانَ وَالْجَرَادَ وَالْقُمَّلَ وَالضَّفَادِعَ وَالدَّمَ آيَاتٍ مُّفَصَّلَاتٍ فَاسْتَكْبَرُوا وَكَانُوا قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ

“Phir humne un par toofan, tiddiyan, joon, mendak aur khoon bheja; yeh alag alag nishaniyan thin. Phir bhi woh takabbur karte rahe aur woh mujrim log the.”<sup>47</sup>

Fir’aun ke da’we ko jhoota sabit karne ke liye Allah Ta’ala ne us ki qaum par sakht azaab bheje. Nau nishaniyon mein se ek ke baare mein farmaya:

وَأَدْخِلْ يَدَكَ فِي جَيْبِكَ تَخْرُجْ بَيْضَاءَ مِنْ غَيْرِ سُوءٍ ۖ فِي تِسْعِ آيَاتٍ إِلَىٰ فِرْعَوْنَ وَقَوْمِهِ ۚ إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا قَوْمًا فَاسِقِينَ

“Apna haath apni baghal mein daalo, woh baghair kisi marz ke roshan ho kar niklega. Yeh Fir’aun aur us ki qaum ki taraf nau nishaniyon mein se ek hai. Beshak woh na-farman log hain.”<sup>48</sup>

Yeh nishaniyan ‘Misr ke azaabon’ ke naam se mashhoor hui. Har baar log apne mabood Fir’aun se madad maangte. Fir’aun unhein Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke paas bhejta ke woh apne Rab se azaab hataane ki dua karein. Har baar woh waada karte ke agar azaab hata diya gaya to woh Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke paigham par imaan le aayenge.
