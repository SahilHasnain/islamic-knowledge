# Aazmaish Ke Din?

Har baar jab azaab hata diya jata to woh apna waada tod kar pehle wale rawayye ki taraf laut jaate. Aaj bhi isi tarah ka manzar roz dekhne ko milta hai. Bahut se log duniya ki zaruriyat ke peeche Allah ko bhula dete hain, lekin jab mushkil aati hai to Allah ki taraf rujoo karke bahut se waade karte hain. Jab Allah Ta’ala un ki takleef door kar deta hai to woh apna waada bhool kar purani aadaton ki taraf laut jaate hain. Imaan ki kamzori ki wajah se yeh bhool paida ho sakti hai. Fir’aun ki qaum ne bhi imaan ka izhar kiya, lekin madad ke liye Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> hi ki taraf rujoo kiya. Allah Ta’ala humein imaan par mazboot qaim rakhe!<sup>49</sup>

Dobara yeh baat kehna zaroori hai ke tawakkul us waqt tak paida nahi ho sakta jab tak Tauhīd par imaan mazboot na ho. Tauhīd ke baghair tawakkul ki tarbiyat nahi ho sakti, kyunke insaan yeh tay hi nahi kar sakta ke apna tawakkul kis mabood par rakhe.

Aakhir Allah Ta’ala ne Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko hukm diya ke tamam Bani Israil ko raat ke waqt Misr se le kar nikal jayen. Unhein yeh maloom nahi tha ke Fir’aun aur us ke sipahi un ka peecha kar rahe hain:

فَأَتْبَعُوهُم مُّشْرِقِينَ ۝ فَلَمَّا تَرَاءَى الْجَمْعَانِ قَالَ أَصْحَابُ مُوسَىٰ إِنَّا لَمُدْرَكُونَ ۝ قَالَ كَلَّا ۖ إِنَّ مَعِيَ رَبِّي سَيَهْدِينِ

“Phir Fir’aun ke log subah ke waqt un ke peeche pohanche. Jab dono giroh ek doosre ko dekhne lage to Musa ke saathiyon ne kaha: ‘Ab hum pakde gaye!’ Musa ne kaha: ‘Hargiz nahi! Mera Rab mere saath hai, ab woh mujhe raasta dikha dega.’”<sup>50</sup>

Yoon Fir’aun ke log subah ke waqt un ke peeche pohanche. Jab dono giroh ek doosre ke saamne aaye to Musa ke saathiyon ne kaha: “Woh humein pakad lenge.” Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne kaha: “Hargiz nahi! Mera Rab mere saath hai, ab woh mujhe raasta dikha dega.”

Qari ko is bab ke aaghaz ki taraf dobara tawajjoh dilate hain. Qaidiyon ka ek giroh farar ke raaste par tha aur safar ke aakhir tak pohanch gaya tha. Saamne Bahr-e-Qulzum tha, is liye woh aage nahi badh sakte the. Peeche Fir’aun aur us ke sipahi itne qareeb the ke woh wapas bhi nahi ja sakte the. Is liye kuch Bani Israil na-umeed ho gaye. Unhein laga ke dono raaste band hain aur pakda jana yaqini hai.

Us waqt Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne unhein yaad dilaya ke Allah Ta’ala un ki rehnumai karega. Yahan tawakkul ke do dars saamne aate hain.

Ek taraf mominon ka ek giroh hai jo Allah ke raaste par chalne ki koshish kar chuka hai, lekin pakde jaane ke khauf ke saamne haar maan leta hai, kyunke zahiri soch ke mutabiq yahi ek raasta nazar aata hai. Doosri taraf Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> hain jo usi raaste par khade hain, lekin Allah ke waade par un ka yaqeen aur tawakkul aisa hai ke nihayat na-umeedi ke lamhe mein bhi woh pur-aitmaad hain.

Yahi sachche imaan walon ka rawayya hai. Samandar ki gehraiyan aur dushmanon ki taqat Allah Ta’ala ke liye koi muqabla nahi ban sakti, jo sab se zyada qudrat wala hai.

Us waqt jab woh Shaytaan aur samandar ke darmiyan ghir gaye, Allah Ta’ala ne apni dasween nishani zahir farmayi. Qur’ān bayan karta hai:

فَأَوْحَيْنَا إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰ أَنِ اضْرِب بِّعَصَاكَ الْبَحْرَ ۖ فَانفَلَقَ فَكَانَ كُلُّ فِرْقٍ كَالطَّوْدِ الْعَظِيمِ ۝ وَأَزْلَفْنَا ثَمَّ الْآخَرِينَ ۝ وَأَنجَيْنَا مُوسَىٰ وَمَن مَّعَهُ أَجْمَعِينَ ۝ ثُمَّ أَغْرَقْنَا الْآخَرِينَ

“Phir humne Musa ki taraf wahi ki ke apni asa samandar par maaro. Yoon samandar phat gaya aur har hissa bade pahaad ki tarah ho gaya. Humne doosre giroh ko bhi us jagah qareeb kar diya. Aur Musa aur un ke tamaam saathiyon ko bacha liya, phir doosre logon ko doob diya.”<sup>51</sup>

Allah Ta’ala ne un ki nijaat ke raaste mein aisi jagah se raasta khol diya jis ka tasawwur bhi nahi kiya ja sakta tha. Yeh mojiza Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke haath par zahir hua aur is ke dauran maghroor Fir’aun aur us ke pairokar doob gaye.<sup>52</sup>

Musalmanon ke liye yahan dars yeh hai ke Allah Ta’ala par tawakkul aisa hona chahiye ke agar tum us par sachcha imaan rakhte ho to yeh yaqeen bhi rakho ke woh ek asa ke zariye samandar ke pani ka rukh mominon ke haq mein badal sakta hai. Aakhir woh har shakhs aur har cheez ka Rab aur Khaaliq hai.

Zindagi mein kabhi kabhi humein aisi mushkil soorat-e-haal ka samna hota hai jo hamare imaan ki bunyaad ko aazmati hai. Aise lamhon mein sahi raaste se hat kar na-umeed hone ke bajaye humein un khayalat se door ho jana chahiye jo humein Shaytaan ki makkaari ke panjon mein phansa sakte hain.

Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aur Fir’aun ka qissa tawakkul ke mauzu par humein yaad dilata hai ke jab hal na-mumkin nazar aayen tab bhi Allah par tawakkul se nazar nahi hatani chahiye. Qisse mein jab log na-umeed hue to ek shakhs ke tawakkul ne sab ko bacha liya. Isi tarah zindagi ki na-mumkin lagne wali mushkilaat mein humein Allah par tawakkul qaim rakhna chahiye. Agar humein nijaat ki zarurat padi to Allah se behtar hifazat karne wala kaun ho sakta hai?

Is qisse ka Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki walida ke waqie se muqabla karne par tawakkul ka ek aur azeem dars saamne aata hai. Un ki walida ko khauf tha ke Fir’aun un ke bachche ko qatl kar dega. Allah Ta’ala ne unhein ilham kiya ke apne bachche se juda ho jao aur use pani mein daal do. Wahi bachcha bada ho kar samandaron ke darmiyan raasta banane wala bana aur apni poori qaum ko azaadi dilayi. Yeh sab is liye hua ke ek shakhs ne ikhlas ke saath Allah ki taraf rujoo kiya aur us ke hukm par sawal nahi uthaya, halaanke zahiri taur par hal asal masle jitna hi bura lag raha tha.

Agar hum bhi un aazmaishi lamhon mein sabr kar saken jab zahiri soch hamare rawayye par haavi hoti hai, to Allah ke hukm se hamare aamaal waqiaat ka aisa silsila paida kar sakte hain jo na sirf humein bachaye, balki ek poori qaum ki nijaat ka zariya ban jaye.

## Aazmaish Ke Din?

Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa tawakkul ke hamare mutale ki roshni mein Qur’ān ke is nihayat aham paigham ki tasdeeq karta hai ke is aarzi duniya mein insaan ko mushkilaat ka samna karna padta hai. Yeh paigham in aayaat mein bayan hua hai:

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

“To beshak mushkil ke saath aasani hai. Yaqeenan mushkil ke saath aasani hai.”<sup>53</sup>

Har insaan, chahe us ka samaji ya maali maqam kuch bhi ho, zindagi mein kabhi na kabhi mushkil waqt se guzarta hai. Maali, khandani ya jazbaati mushkil ho, duniya ka har shakhs pichhe mud kar keh sakta hai ke woh kabhi na kabhi mushkil se guzra hai ya is waqt guzar raha hai.

Jab yeh alfaaz likhe ja rahe the to duniya ek wabai marz (Covid-19) ka samna kar rahi thi aur is ki wajah se bahut se log duniya se ruksat ho chuke the. Is ke bawajood upar wali aayaat momin ke liye tasalli ka zariya hain. Allah Ta’ala batata hai ke aise waqt ke baad aasani aayegi aur humein sabr ke saath mushkilaat bardasht karni chahiye.

Qur’ān mein yeh ek nihayat anokha maqam hai jahan ek hi baat ko bilkul ek ke baad ek dohraya gaya hai. Is mein Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se hamare liye tasalli hai. Jab koi insaan kisi bade nuqsaan se guzarta hai to hum use tasalli dete hue kehte hain: “Sab theek ho jayega! Sab theek ho jayega!” Hum yeh baat us shakhs ko tasalli dene ke liye kehte hain.

Isi tarah Allah Ta’ala apne bandon ko yaqeen dila raha hai ke jab aazmaish ka waqt aaye to mutmain raho, kyunke sakht waqt ke baad behtareen waqt aayega.

Huzoor Musa <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa sabit karta hai ke ek aisi qaum jo ghulami mein thi aur kai saal tak sakht mushkilaat se guzri, Allah aur us ke Nabi par imaan aur bharosa rakhne ki wajah se apni dardnaak soorat-e-haal se nikal kar azaad ho gayi.

Isi tarah hamari apni zindagi mein bhi agar hum kisi mushkil ko bardasht kar rahe hon to Allah Ta’ala hamare sabr ko aazma raha hota hai aur humein yaad dila raha hota hai ke us ne mushkil ke baad aasani ka waada kiya hai. Jo log Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ke zariye hum tak pohanchne wale Allah ke paigham ki bina par us par tawakkul rakhte hain, woh zindagi ki mushkilaat ko Allah ke aasani ke waade ki umeed mein bardasht karte hain.

Yeh qissa humein yaad dilata hai ke Musalman Allah Ta’ala ki be-hadd qudrat se hamesha ba-khabar rahein, usi par tawakkul rakhein, aur yaqeen rakhein ke jab sab sahare khatam ho jaate hain to Allah ghalib rehta hai!
