# Aage Ki Tayari

Zindagi mein Musalman ko imaan mein mazboot hona chahiye, lekin saath hi sochne wala bhi hona chahiye, jo apne aamaal ke nateejon ko der hone se pehle samajh sake.

Tawakkul ko sabr ke samandar par thehri hui kashti samajhna chahiye, jise Allah ki wahdaniyyat par imaan ka dhaara raasta dikhata hai.

Zindagi mein humein apne aamaal ke nateejon ko samajhne ki salahiyat rakhni chahiye. Humein apni kamzori, apni be-sabri aur apne jhoote bharose ko pehchan kar aage ki tayyari karni chahiye. Tawakkul ka matlab yeh nahi ke insaan tadbeer chhod de. Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne baarish aane se pehle kashti banayi thi.

Qur’ān batata hai ke mominon ko aazmaya jayega. In aazmaishon ke dauran humein sabr karna, Allah ka shukr ada karna aur jo kuch hamare paas baqi hai us ki qadr karni hogi.

Tawakkul ka matlab yeh nahi ke momin samjhe ke Allah Ta’ala us par sone ki boonden barsa dega. Tawakkul ka matlab yeh hai ke imaan mazboot ho, tadbeer durust ho aur anjaam Allah ke hawale ho.

Musalman ko imaan mein mazboot hone ke saath apne aamaal ke nateejon ko samajhna chahiye. Tawakkul woh kashti hai jo sabr ke samandar par chalti hai aur Tauhīd par imaan us ke liye raasta banata hai.

Internet par aise hazaron qisse milte hain jahan log batate hain ke kisi achanak mulaqat ne un ki zindagi ka rukh badal diya. Kabhi woh apne ghar se kiye jaane wale kaam ke agle qadam ka mansuba bana rahe hote hain aur achanak apne aap ko kisi doosre mulk mein paate hain.

Allah par imaan mein mazboot Musalman jaanta hai ke hum ‘ittifaq’ ke aqeede ko nahi maante. Kya tum samajhte ho ke tumhare safar mein madad karne wale kuch log sirf ittifaq se tumhari zindagi mein aaye? Ya woh haadsa jise tum mushkil se bach gaye, sirf khush-qismati ki wajah se tha? Hargiz nahi! Allah ki qasam, hargiz nahi! Jo waqiaat pesh aaye aur jo log hamare raaste mein aaye, sab Allah ki marzi se hua.

Zindagi ke is safar mein humein yeh bhi nahi maloom ke din khatam hone tak hum zinda honge ya nahi. Is liye har musafir ki tarah humein apni manzil ki tayyari karni chahiye. Musalman ki manzil un logon ke liye Jannat ka waada kiya gaya ajr hai jo imaan aur nek aamaal rakhte hain.

Musalman ki zindagi ka har din isi tayyari ka hissa hai, bedar hone ke lamhe se le kar sone ke waqt tak. Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ne humein sikhaya ke bedar hote hi zindagi aur apne anjaam par ghaur karein aur Allah Ta’ala ka shukr ada karein ke us ne humein zindagi ki ne’mat di. Musalman ka din Fajr ki namaz se shuru hota hai aur Isha ki namaz par khatam, aur sone se pehle Allah ka naam liya jata hai.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa yeh bhi sikhata hai ke qudrat, daulat ya pairokar kitne hi zyada hon, kisi cheez par hamara poora ikhtiyar nahi. Un ki qaum ke sardar apni zindagi se khush aur mutma’in the. Unhon ne Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka mazaaq udaya aur paigham thukra diya. Aakhir un ke inkaar ki wajah se woh aane wali tabaahi ke liye tayyar nahi the.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa humein Allah par bharosa aur inhisar rakhte hue zindagi ki behtar tayyari ke dars deta hai. Yeh paigham khaas taur par un logon ke liye aham hai jo da‘wat-e-Islām ke kaam se jude hue hain. Is baat ko is aayat se samjha ja sakta hai:

وَاتْلُ عَلَيْهِمْ نَبَأَ نُوحٍ إِذْ قَالَ لِقَوْمِهِ يَا قَوْمِ إِن كَانَ كَبُرَ عَلَيْكُم مَّقَامِي وَتَذْكِيرِي بِآيَاتِ اللَّهِ فَعَلَى اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ ۖ فَأَجْمِعُوا أَمْرَكُمْ وَشُرَكَاءَكُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُنْ أَمْرُكُمْ عَلَيْكُمْ غُمَّةً ثُمَّ اقْضُوا إِلَيَّ وَلَا تُنظِرُونِ

“Aur unhein Nūh ka qissa suna do jab unhon ne apni qaum se kaha: ‘Aye meri qaum! Agar tum par mera rehna aur Allah ki aayaat ke zariye tumhein yaad dilana bhaari hai to main Allah hi par bharosa karta hoon. Tum apne jhoote maboodon ke saath mil kar apna mansuba pakka kar lo, phir tumhara maamla tum par posheeda na rahe. Phir mere khilaf jo karna hai kar guzro aur mujhe mohlat na do.’”<sup>68</sup>

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne 950 saal dawat di. Itni lambi mehnat ke baad bhi jab log unhein challenge karte to aap yeh iqraar karte ke saamne rakhi hui rukawaton se nikalne ka wahid raasta Allah par tawakkul aur bharosa hai.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka Allah par tawakkul us waqt bhi nazar aata hai jab kashti rawana hui:

وَقَالَ ارْكَبُوا فِيهَا بِسْمِ اللَّهِ مَجْرَاهَا وَمُرْسَاهَا ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَغَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ

“Aur Nūh ne kaha: ‘Is mein sawar ho jao. Allah hi ke naam se is ka chalna aur theharna hai. Beshak mera Rab yaqeenan bakhshne wala, meherban hai.’”<sup>69</sup>

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko apne paigham par aisa yaqeen tha ke woh ek kashti mein sawar ho rahe the aur unhein yeh maloom nahi tha ke woh kahan ja kar thehregi. Un ke paas aaj ki satellite navigation jaisi jadeed technology nahi thi. Toofan mein un ki rehnumai Allah ki taraf se milne wali hidayat ne ki. Isi liye unhon ne kaha ke kashti ka chalna aur us ka anjaam Allah Ta’ala ke ikhtiyar mein hai.

Aaj hum bhi ek safar par hain aur hamari simt kabhi kabhi ghair-yaqini hoti hai. Is jism ki rooh ka aakhri thikana kitna behtar hoga, yeh is baat par munhasir hai ke hum Allah par kitna imaan aur bharosa rakhte hain.

Upar wali aayat ka bunyadi ma’ni Musalman ke har safar ke liye rehnuma hona chahiye. Chahe naya karobar shuru karna ho, naya ghar talash karna ho ya kisi naye mauzu ka mutala karna ho, zindagi ke samandar mein yeh sab safar hain. Musalman ko Allah Ta’ala par be-larzish tawakkul aur bharosa rakhna chahiye taake woh is safar ke behtareen anjaam ki taraf hamari rehnumai kare.

Jab Musalman apne tamam maamlaat mein Allah ko shamil karna aur us par poora bharosa rakhna shuru karenge, tab InshaAllah un maamlaat mein sukoon aur itminan paida hoga.

Is liye Allah ke azaab ka toofan hum par aane se pehle maqsad ke saath aage ki tayyari karo. Us waqt koi buland pahaad bhi humein un paniyon ki gehraiyon se nahi bacha sakega jin ke neeche hum apne aap ko daba rahe honge.
