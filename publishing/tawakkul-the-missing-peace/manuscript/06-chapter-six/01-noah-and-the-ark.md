# Bab Shashum (Chapter Six)
# Allah Par Bharosa Rakho, Lekin Pehle Mansuba Banao (Trust in God, but Plan Ahead)

“Zindagi mein Musalman ko imaan mein mazboot hona chahiye, lekin saath hi sochne wala bhi hona chahiye, jo apne aamaal ke nateejon ko der hone se pehle samajhne ki salahiyat rakhta ho. Tawakkul ko phir sabr ke samandar par thehre hue kashti ki tarah samajhna chahiye, jise Allah ki wahdaniyyat par imaan ka dhaara raasta dikhata hai!”

Qadeem Mesopotamia mein sakht aazmaish ka daur tha. Jahan tak mujhe yaad hai, khushk-sali jaari thi aur sookhi zameen ne logon ko bechain kar diya tha. Lekin sardaron ne apni daulat aur qudrat ke zariye zindagi guzarne ke liye kafi zakhira jama kar liya tha. Woh humein, yani ghareebon ko, har waqt is baat ka ehsaas dilate rehte the. Log un ki izzat is liye nahi karte the ke woh izzat ka hukm dete, balki is liye ke woh izzat ka mutalaba karte the. Agar koi aisa na kare to un ki muhabbat se mehroom ho sakta tha, aur un mushkil dinon mein hum yeh khatra nahi le sakte the.

Is liye humein apne ba-asar sardaron ki itaat karni padti thi taake har din tez dhoop ke neeche, apne pyase jismon ke saath, zinda reh saken. Har roz dhoop ke urooj par sardar hamare saamne aate aur apne pani ke pyale dikha kar apni daulat ka izhar karte.

Ek din ek aadmi aage badha aur kehne laga ke us ke paas aisa khayal hai jo hamare shehar ko tabaahi se bacha sakta hai. Sardaron ne us se tafseel poochne ka kaha, lekin achanak sab ek saath hansne lage. Logon ne apne rehnumaon ko hanste dekha to wajah jaane baghair woh bhi hansne aur us aadmi ka mazaaq udaane lage.

Khushk-sali ke daur mein woh aadmi ek cheez bech raha tha jise ‘umbrella’ kehte hain. Us ka maqsad yeh tha ke jab bahut tez baarish ho to aadmi us ke neeche panah le sake.

“Pagal aadmi! Yeh pagal ho gaya hai!” log kehte. Phir bhi woh aadmi har roz dopahar ko aata aur kehta ke jo log us ke umbrella ke neeche panah lenge woh bach jayenge. Lekin har roz use wahi mazaaq aur zaleel-kun rawayya milta jis ka woh aadi ho chuka tha.

Kai saal baad use gaon ka pagal kaha jaane laga. Shehar ke log garmi ke mausam mein bhi roz jama hote, lekin us waqt woh aadmi apna paigham le kar nahi aata tha.

“Aakhir us ne apna be-ma’ni daawa chhod diya!” log kehte.

Achanak unhon ne dekha ke sooraj ki roshni ghane aur tareek baadalon se dhak gayi hai. Aasman ki garaj ne pareshan hujoom ko hila diya. Yeh wahi baarish thi jis ka woh itne saal intezar kar rahe the, lekin ab unhein samajh aa gaya ke yeh mamooli baarish nahi. Yeh aisa toofan tha jaisa pehle kabhi nahi aaya.

Log sardaron se munharif ho kar door door tak us ‘pagal’ aadmi ko dhoondne lage, kyunke ab unhein us ke umbrella ki zarurat thi. Lekin bahut der ho chuki thi. Aasman ki ek aakhri zor-dar garaj ke saath woh waada kiya gaya toofan aa gaya.

Ab humein samajh aata hai ke us din woh ‘pagal’ aadmi kyun nahi aaya tha. Kaash humne us ki pukaar maan li hoti, lekin ab hum sab ke liye bahut der ho chuki thi!

Yeh misaal batati hai ke insaan muashray ke ba-asar logon ke hukm ki pairwi kis tarah karta hai, jabke yeh log aksar wasail ki aksariyat par qabza rakhte hain. Jab Allah ki taraf se logon ko koi tanbeeh bheji jati hai to un ka fitri jawab ‘logic’ yani zahiri mantiq ki bina par inkaar hota hai.

Allah ke azeem Rasool Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka bhi yahi haal hua. Aap ne zindagi bhar logon ko Allah ke raaste ki taraf bulaya aur aane wale toofan se khabardaar kiya. Aksar logon ne aap ka mazaaq udaya aur paigham thukra diya, sirf chand log imaan laaye.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa humein ek nihayat aham dars deta hai: imaan ke liye hamesha zahiri saboot zaroori nahi hota. Jab imaan dil mein aa jaye to Allah par tawakkul aakhirkar us anjaam ko zahir karta hai jo pehle se muqarrar hai, aur humein zindagi ke na-maloom khatron se mehfooz karta hai.

Qur’ān ki 114 surahon mein se 28 surahon mein Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa bayan hua hai. Yeh aaj ki Ummat ke liye is qisse ki ahmiyat ka saboot hai. Lekin hum Qur’ān se tawakkul ke dars hasil karne ke bajaye social media par milne wali taaza afwahon par zyada tawajjoh dete hain.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ko Allah Ta’ala ne shirk ka muqabla karne aur khalis Tauhīd ka paigham dene ke liye bheja. Huzoor Ādam <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aur Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke darmiyan taqreeban das qarn yani sadiyon ya nasaloon ka fasla guzra. Is dauran Shaytaan ne logon ko dhoka de kar jhoote maboodon ki ibadat mein mubtala kar diya.

وَلَقَدْ أَرْسَلْنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوْمِهِ فَلَبِثَ فِيهِمْ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ إِلَّا خَمْسِينَ عَامًا فَأَخَذَهُمُ الطُّوفَانُ وَهُمْ ظَالِمُونَ

“Aur beshak humne Nūh ko un ki qaum ki taraf bheja. Woh un mein pachaas kam ek hazaar saal rahe, phir unhein toofan ne aa liya aur woh zalim the.”<sup>66</sup>

Qur’ān batata hai ke Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne 950 saal apni qaum ko dawat di. Itne saal ke mazaaq aur inkaar ke baad bhi aap ke saath sirf chand log reh gaye.

Zahiri soch ke mutabiq logon ka aap ke saath na aana samajh aata hai. Unhein lagta tha ke aap cheezon ke aam dhaare ke khilaf baat kar rahe hain. Aap ka bunyadi paigham goya khushk-sali ke dauran umbrella bechne jaisa tha.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke pairokar 950 saal tak kis qadr tawakkul rakhte rahe honge? Ek shakhs unhein waada de raha tha ke azeem toofan aayega, lekin saal dar saal guzarte rahe, din aur raat aate jaate rahe, aur koi toofan nahi aaya. Un ke dilon mein kitne sawal aur shak paida hue honge? Doosri taraf sardaron aur un ke pairokar in mominon ka mazaaq uda kar apne faisle par mutma’in hote gaye, kyunke toofan ka aana bilkul na-mumkin lag raha tha.

Aaj bhi jadeed daur mein log Allah ke waade ke poora hone ke baare mein shak karte hain. Woh sochte hain ke kya waqai maut ke baad zindagi hai? Kya Jannat aur Dozakh waqai maujood hain?

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka qissa, aap ka azeem sabr, bardasht, be-larzish imaan aur Allah, us ke Rusul aur un ke laaye hue paighamat par tawakkul humein aaj bhi dars deta hai. Hum Aakhirat ke apne aqeede ko zahiri taur par sabit nahi kar sakte, lekin agar Allah ki qudrat par bharosa rakhein to Aakhirat mein hamara ajr bahut zyada hoga.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ne apni qaum ko din raat, khule aam aur posheeda taur par bulaya, lekin woh takabbur karne wale log the aur itaat se inkaar karte rahe.

Lekin aaj hum un logon se kitne mukhtalif hain? Ummat ki haalat dekho to aksar Musalman Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ki peshgoyi ke mutabiq samandar ke jhaag ki tarah ho gaye hain.<sup>67</sup> Hum har lehar ke saath be-maqsad idhar udhar behte rehte hain aur jis lehar ka zor zyada ho usi ke saath chal padte hain.

Main yeh maanta hoon ke jab tak hum Qur’āni qisson ko Allah par apna tawakkul paida karne ke pehlu se nahi dekhenge, tab tak hum bhi zindagi ke aane wale toofan ke liye tayyar nahi honge.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ka mazaaq is liye udaya gaya ke aap ek Allah ki Tauhīd ka paigham de rahe the. Is ke ilawa log is baat par bhi aap ka mazaaq udate the ke aap 950 saal se aisi cheez ke aane ki khabar de rahe the jise sabit karna har din mushkil hota ja raha tha. Har guzarta din un ke paigham ko un ki nazar mein aur zyada shak ke qabil bana raha tha.

Lekin Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aam insaan nahi the. Woh Allah Ta’ala ke azm wale Rasool the, aur aap ki nubuwwat par imaan rakhne walon ka yaqeen aaj ke bahut se logon se bilkul mukhtalif tha.

Kafir log aap ka is liye bhi mazaaq udate ke aap hazaron kilometer andaroon-e-zameen mein kashti bana rahe the. Un ki nazar mein yeh ek na-munasib kaam tha aur roohani taur par andhe logon ko aap ka mazaaq udate ka ek aur mauqa mil gaya.

Qur’ān qari ko kitna azeem dars deta hai! Allah Ta’ala apni be-hadd qudrat se chand lamhon mein Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke liye kashti paida kar sakta tha aur un ke mukhalif foran imaan le aate. Lekin imaan ka taqaza yeh hai ke jab hum kisi paigham ki zahiri nishani ya wazeh saboot na dekh saken tab bhi us par yaqeen rakhein.

Huzoor Nūh <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ke qisse ka bunyadi dars yeh hai ke aage ki tayyari karo. Jab aap ne kashti banayi thi tab baarish nahi ho rahi thi. Baad mein baarish zaroor hui, aur agar aap ne pehle se tayyari na ki hoti to aap ki qaum tabaah ho jati.

Zindagi mein tawakkul ki ghalat samajh yeh hai ke insaan yeh samjhe ke sirf momin hone ki wajah se Allah Ta’ala us par sone ki baarish barsa dega. Haqeeqat is ke baraks hai. Qur’ān batata hai ke mominon ko aazmaya jayega, aur un aazmaishon ke baad humein sabr karna aur jo kuch abhi hamare paas hai us par Allah ka shukr ada karna hoga.

66. Sūrah Al-Ankabūt, 29:14
67. Huzoor Muhammad ﷺ ki is peshgoyi ka hawala *The Beginning of the End – An Eschatological Endeavour to Unravel the Mysteries of the Modern Age* aur Sunan Abū Dāūd, Hadīth 4297 mein milta hai.
