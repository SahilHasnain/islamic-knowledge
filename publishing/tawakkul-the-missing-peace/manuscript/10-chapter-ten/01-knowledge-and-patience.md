# Bab Dahum (Chapter Ten)
# Sab Raaste Usi Ki Taraf Jate Hain! (All Roads Lead to Him!)

“...kabhi aisa hoga ke cheezen hamare mansube ke mutabiq nahi chalengi. Lekin agar hum us haalat par sabr karein aur yaqeen rakhein ke Allah Ta’ala ke ilm ne hamare liye is raaste ko behtareen mumkin raasta pehle hi dekh liya hai, to jab us ka mansuba poora hoga, is ke nateeje is duniya ya Aakhirat mein be-aib honge.”

Tum ek raaste par chal rahe ho...lekin ab tumhein bilkul maloom hai ke yeh raasta kahan le jata hai. Sab raaste Allah Ta’ala ki taraf jate hain. To tum wahan kaise pohanchoge? Apni zindagi ke safar mein Allah ki nishaniyon ko dhoondo jo tumhari rehnumai karengi. Achhi tarah jaante raho ke agar tum us ke ahkaam par qaim raho aur us par bharosa karo, to agar koi tumhein kisi pur-khatar raaste par bhatkane ki koshish bhi kare, Allah tumhare bharose ki wajah se na-qabil-e-tasavvur zaraaye se tumhari madad bhej dega.

Tawakkul ko samajhne ki hamari ab tak ki koshish ne humein Allah ke barguzida Anbiya ki zindagiyon ke waqiat aur doosre nek bandon ki misaalon se guzara hai. In sab ka maqsad qari ke saamne chand bunyadi baatein wazeh karna hai.

**Pehli baat:** Hamara ilm mahdood hai. Is mahdoodiyat ka lazmi nateeja yeh hai ke hum sirf “abhi aur yahin” ko jaante hain, jabke Allah ka ilm waqt ke tasavvur se bhi pare hai. Is liye woh hamare har kiye hue, har kiye ja rahe aur aane wale har intikhab ke tamam asraat ko jaanta hai. Us ka ilm har zinda insaan, guzishta logon aur aane wali nasal ke har fard ko shamil hai, is lamhe bhi aur waqt ke har us lamhe mein bhi jo abhi nahi aaya.

Har ilm rakhne wala shakhs jaanta hai ke zindagi ke kisi bhi maamle mein an-jaane ilaqe mein qadam rakhne se pehle sab se aqalmandi ka kaam yeh hai ke kisi mahir ya us field mein hum se zyada tajurba rakhne wale shakhs se mashwara kiya jaye. Jab humein sarmaya lagana ho to banker se mashwara karte hain. Jab chhuttiyon ka mansuba banana ho to travel agent se mashwara karte hain. Jab bachon ko kisi khaas school mein dakhil karana ho to asatiza se mashwara lete hain.

Yahi mamool ka tareeqa hai. Kyun? Kyunke hum jaante hain ke jis maamle mein hum apna waqt, mehnat ya paisa lagana chahte hain us ki badi qeemat hai.

Phir jab imaan ke mustaqbil mein sarmaya lagane ki baat aati hai to hum itni kam ya bilkul koi koshish kyun nahi karte? Is nihayat aham maamle mein hum aqalmandi ka muzahira kar ke Us Allah se mashwara kyun nahi lete jo hamare mustaqbil ke har anjaam ka ilm rakhta hai?

Jab hum tasleem kar lete hain ke insaan ke Allah ke mansube par sawal uthane ki bunyadi wajah us ka mahdood ilm ya ilm ki kami hai, to hum hamesha is baat ka khayal rakhenge ke Allah ke mansube par sawal na uthayen. Humein ehsaas rahega ke hamara ilm mahdood hai aur Allah ka ilm la-mehdood. Is liye humein us par bharosa rakhna chahiye, yeh jaante hue ke anjaam hamare liye dhundla hai lekin Allah ke liye bilkul wazeh.

Yeh baat “Muqabla Karne Wala Kaun Hai?” wale bab mein samjhayi gayi thi. Abū Lahab ke mustaqbil ko sab ke saamne pesh kar diya gaya tha, taake log samjhein ke Allah Ta’ala ko mustaqbil ka aisa ilm hasil hai ke jab woh humein intikhabat ka mauqa bhi deta hai tab bhi woh jaanta hai ke hum kaunsa intikhab karenge. Hamare liye anjaam raaz ki dhund mein chhupa hua hai, lekin Allah ke liye bilkul wazeh. Is liye humein us par bharosa karna chahiye!

**Doosri baat:** Tawakkul ko samajhne ke liye humein har haalat mein sabr aur bardasht ka rawaiya ikhtiyar karna sikhaya jata hai. Beshak kabhi cheezen hamare mansube ke mutabiq nahi chalengi. Lekin agar hum us haalat par sabr karein aur yaqeen rakhein ke Allah Ta’ala ke ilm ne hamare liye is raaste ko behtareen mumkin raasta pehle hi dekh liya hai, to jab us ka mansuba poora hoga, is ke nateeje is duniya ya Aakhirat mein be-aib honge.

Sabr aur tawakkul apnane ki fazilat ki be-shumar misaalon ke saath is ki ek numaya misaal Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> aur “qurbani” ka mashhoor waqia hai.<sup>82</sup>

Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ki poori zindagi mukhtalif imtihanat se guzri, lekin aap ne har imtihan ko kamal ke saath paar kiya (2:124). Aap ne bete ki dua maangi (37:100), aur zindagi ke aakhri daur mein Allah Ta’ala ne aap ko Huzoor Ismā‘īl <sup>عليه السلام</sup> ata farmaye. Jab aap ke bete ki umr pukhtagi ko pohanchi to Allah Ta’ala ne unhein apne bete ko zabh karne ka hukm diya. Bete ne bhi is hukm ko qabool karte hue kaha ke aap mujhe sabr karne walon mein paenge (37:102).

Us nihayat sakht imtihan ke waqt, jab Huzoor Ibrāhīm <sup>عليه السلام</sup> apne bete ko zabh karne wale the, Allah Ta’ala ne elaan farmaya ke unhon ne imtihan kamyabi se paar kar liya (37:103-105). Allah Ta’ala par un ke azeem sabr aur tawakkul ki wajah se, ke woh aisa hukm milne par bhi us par amal ke liye tayyar ho gaye, aaj duniya bhar ke Musalman har saal is azeem qurbani ko yaad karte hain.

Is liye jab humein aazmaishon aur mushkilaat ka saamna ho to humein Allah Ta’ala par bharosa rakhna aur sabr karna chahiye, taake hum bhi imtihan mein surkhru ho saken. Agar hum sabr karein aur us par tawakkul rakhein to yaqeenan Allah Ta’ala jo nateeja hamare liye zahir karega us par humein khushi hogi.
