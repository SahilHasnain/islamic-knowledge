### Wuzu ke waswason se bachne ka tareeqa

Walahan ek Shaitan ka naam hai jo Wuzu mein waswase daalta hai. Is se bachne ke
liye Allah ki taraf ruju' kare, **أَعُوذُ بِاللهِ** aur **لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ
إِلَّا بِاللهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ** padhe. Surah Naas padhe. **آمَنْتُ بِاللهِ
وَرَسُولِهِ** padhe. **هُوَ الْأَوَّلُ وَالْآخِرُ وَالظَّاهِرُ وَالْبَاطِنُ وَهُوَ
بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ** padhe. **سُبْحَانَ الْمَلِكِ الْخَلَّاقِ إِنْ يَشَأْ

## Ghusl ke Masa'il

Ghusl Farz hone ke sabab baad mein bayan honge. Pehle Ghusl ki haqeeqat bayan ki jati
hai. Ghusl ke teen juz hain. Agar in mein se ek mein bhi kami reh gayi to Ghusl nahi
hoga. Yun bhi keh sakte hain ke Ghusl mein teen Farz hain.

**(1) Kulli:** Munh ke har hisse, har kone, honton se gale ki jad tak har jagah paani
bahna zaruri hai. Aksar log samajhte hain ke thoda sa paani munh mein lekar nikal dena
Kulli hai, chahe woh zaban ki jad aur gale ke kinare tak na pahunche. Is tarah Ghusl
nahi hoga. Ghusl ke baad Namaz bhi jaiz nahi hogi. Daanton ke peeche gaalon ki teh,
daanton ki jaden, daanton ke beech ki jagah, zaban ki har taraf aur gale ke kinare tak
paani pahunchana Farz hai.

**Mas'alah 1:** Daanton ki jaron ya daanton ke beech koi aisi cheez jami ho jo paani
bahne se roke, to agar use nikalne mein koi nuqsan ya mushkil na ho, use nikalna zaruri
hai. Misaal ke taur par supari ka dana ya gosht ka resha. Lekin agar use nikalne mein
nuqsan ya sakht mushkil ho, jaise bahut zyada paan khane se daanton ki jaron mein
Chuna jam gaya ho, ya auraton ke daanton mein Masi ki lakeeren jam gayi hon aur unhein
utarne se daant ya masooron ko nuqsan ka andesha ho, to maaf hai.

**Mas'alah 2:** Isi tarah hilte hue daant ko taar se ya ukhde hue daant ko kisi masale
waghera se jamaya gaya ho aur paani taar ya masale ke neeche na pahunche, to maaf hai.
Khana ya paan ka reza daant mein reh jaye aur us ki jaanch karte rehna mushkil ho, to
bhi maaf hai. Lekin baad mein maloom ho jaye ke woh paani pahunchne se rok raha tha,
to use nikalna aur daant dhona zaruri hai.

**(2) Naak mein paani dalna:** Dono nathnon ki naram jagah tak paani pahunchana aur
paani ko soongh kar upar chadhana zaruri hai. Baal ke barabar jagah bhi dhulne se na
reh jaye, warna Ghusl nahi hoga. Naak ke andar jami hui rint ko chhudana Farz hai.
Naak ke baal dhona bhi Farz hai.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 1, pages 439–440, 440–441 aur 452–453; Ad-Durr-ul-
Mukhtar ma'a Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, page 312.

**Mas'alah 3:** Naak ke andar bali ka suraakh band na ho to us mein paani pahunchana
zaruri hai. Agar suraakh tang ho to bali ko hila kar paani pahunchana zaruri hai,
warna nahi.

**(3) Poore zahiri badan ko dhona:** Sar ke baalon se paon ke talwon tak jism ke har
hisse aur har rongte par paani bahna zaruri hai. Aksar log sar par paani daal kar badan
par haath pher lete hain aur samajhte hain ke Ghusl ho gaya, halanke kuch aza aise hain
jo khaas ehtiyat ke baghair nahi dhulte. Isliye Ghusl nahi hota. Wuzu ke aza mein jin
maqamat ka khaas khayal rakhna zaruri hai, un ka hukm yahan bhi hai. Is ke alawa Ghusl
mein in jagahon ka khayal rakhna zaruri hai:

1. Sar ke baal khule hon to har baal par jad se nok tak paani bahna chahiye. Baal gunthi
   hui choti hon to mard par Farz hai ke use khol kar jad se nok tak paani bahaye. Aurat
   ke liye sirf jaden tar karna zaruri hai; choti kholna zaruri nahi. Haan, agar choti
   itni mazboot gunthi ho ke baghair khole jaden tar na hongi to use kholna zaruri hai.
2. Kaanon mein bali ya doosre zevar ke suraakh ka wahi hukm hai jo Wuzu mein naak ke
   Nathne ke suraakh ka bayan hua.
3. Bhawon, moonchon aur daarhi ke baalon ko jad se nok tak dhona aur un ke neeche ki
   jild ko dhona.
4. Kaan ka har hissa aur us ke suraakh ka munh dhona.
5. Kaanon ke peeche ke baal hata kar wahan paani bahana.
6. Thodi aur gale ke milne ki jagah. Munh uthaye baghair yeh jagah nahi dhulegi.
7. Baghalen. Haath uthaye baghair baghal nahi dhulegi.
8. Baazu ka har pehlu.
9. Peeth ka har zarra.
10. Pet ki silwaton ko utha kar dhona.
11. Naaf mein ungli daal kar dhona, jab paani pahunchne mein shak ho.
12. Jism ke har rongte ko jad se nok tak dhona.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 1, pages 443–445; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar ma'a
Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, page 312.

hai. Pehle padi hui Namaz ho gayi.

## Ghusl ki Sunnatein

1. Ghusl ki Niyyat kare. 2. Dono haath gatte tak teen baar dhoye. 3. Istinja ki
jagah dhoye, chahe wahan Najasat ho ya na ho. 4. Jism par jahan Najasat ho use
door kare. 5. Namaz jaisa Wuzu kare, lekin paon na dhoye. Agar chauki, takhte ya
pathar par nahaye to paon bhi dho le.

6. Jism par, khaas taur par sardiyon mein, tel lagane ki tarah paani mal le.
7. Pehle daahne kandhe par teen baar paani bahaye. 8. Phir baayen kandhe par teen
baar paani bahaye. 9. Phir sar aur poore badan par teen baar paani bahaye.
10. Ghusl ki jagah se alag ho jaye. Agar Wuzu mein paon nahi dhoye the to ab dho le.
11. Nahate waqt Qibla ki taraf rukh na kare. 12. Poore badan par haath phere.
13. Jism ko male. 14. Aisi jagah nahaye jahan koi na dekh sake. Agar yeh mumkin
na ho to naaf se ghutne tak ke hisson ka Satr dhakna zaruri hai.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 1, pages 455–461. Note: Jin Sunnaton ka bayan
“phir” ke saath hua hai, us cheez ka Sunnat hona aur us tartib se karna dono
Sunnat hain. Agar pehle baayen kandhe par paani dala aur phir daahne par, to
Sunnat-e-tarteeb ada nahi hui.

Agar Satr dhakne ki koi jagah bhi mumkin na ho to Tayammum kare, lekin yeh
imkaan bahut door hai. 15. Ghusl ke dauran kisi tarah ki baat na kare.
16. Koi Dua na padhe. Nahane ke baad rumal se badan ponchna jaiz hai.

**Mas'alah 1:** Ghusl-khane ki chhat na ho ya nange badan nahaye, lekin zaruri
jagah dhaki hui ho, to koi haraj nahi. Auraton ko bahut zyada ehtiyat karni
chahiye aur un ka baith kar nahana behtar hai. Nahane ke foran baad kapde pehen
le. Wuzu ki Sunnatein aur Mustahabbat Ghusl mein bhi Sunnatein aur Mustahabbat
hain. Lekin agar Satr khula ho to Qibla ki taraf rukh nahi karna chahiye. Agar
Tahband bandha ho to haraj nahi.

**Mas'alah 2:** Behte paani, jaise darya ya nahar, mein thodi der khade rehne se
teen baar dhona, tartib aur Wuzu ki Sunnatein sab ada ho jati hain. Aza ko teen
baar alag se hilaana zaruri nahi. Thehre hue paani, jaise talab, mein aza ko teen
baar hilaane ya jagah badalne se teen baar dhone ki Sunnat ada ho jati hai. Baarish
mein khade hona bhi behte paani mein khade hone ke hukm mein hai. Behte paani mein
Wuzu karte waqt uzv ko kuch der paani mein rakhna, aur thehre paani mein use hilaana,
teen baar dhone ke barabar hai.

**Mas'alah 3:** Har shakhs ke liye Ghusl ya Wuzu mein paani ki koi muqarrar miqdaar
nahi. Yeh baat jo aam logon mein mashhoor hai bilkul ghalat hai. Koi lamba-chauda
hai aur koi dubla-patla; kisi ke badan par bahut baal hain aur kisi ka badan saaf;
kisi ki daarhi ghani hai aur koi be-daarhi; kisi ke sar par lambe baal hain aur
kisi ka sar mundha hua hai. Isliye sab ke liye ek miqdaar kaise muqarrar ho sakti
hai?

**Mas'alah 4:** Aurat ka Hammam jana Makrooh hai. Mard ja sakta hai, lekin Satr
ka khayal rakhna zaruri hai. Logon ke saamne Satr khol kar nahana Haram hai.

**Mas'alah 5:** Baghair zarurat subah bahut sawere Hammam na jaye, kyunki is se
posheeda baat logon par zahir hoti hai.

## Ghusl Kin Cheezon Se Farz Hota Hai

1. Mani apni jagah se Shahwat ke saath alag hokar uzv se nikle, to Ghusl Farz
hota hai.

**Mas'alah 1:** Agar Mani Shahwat ke saath apni jagah se alag na hui, balki bojh
uthaane ya bulandi se girne ki wajah se nikli, to Ghusl Wajib nahi; Wuzu toot jayega.

**Mas'alah 2:** Mani apni jagah se Shahwat ke saath alag hui, lekin aadmi ne apne
uzv ko zor se pakad liya aur Mani bahar na aa saki. Shahwat khatam hone ke baad
use chhod diya aur Mani bahar aa gayi, to bahar nikalna Shahwat ke saath na hua
phir bhi Ghusl Wajib hai, kyunki Mani apni jagah se Shahwat ke saath alag ho chuki thi.

**Mas'alah 3:** Kuch Mani nikli. Peshab karne, sone ya chaalis qadam chalne se
pehle Ghusl karke Namaz padh li. Ab baqi Mani nikli to Ghusl kare, kyunki yeh usi
Mani ka hissa hai jo apni jagah se Shahwat ke saath alag hui thi. Pehle padhi hui
Namaz ho gayi, use dobara padhna zaruri nahi. Agar chaalis qadam chalne, peshab
karne ya sone ke baad Ghusl kiya aur phir Mani baghair Shahwat nikli, to Ghusl
zaruri nahi. Isay pehli Mani ka baqi hissa nahi kaha jayega.

**Mas'alah 4:** Mani patli ho gayi aur peshab ke waqt ya waise hi kuch qatre
baghair Shahwat nikle to Ghusl Wajib nahi, lekin Wuzu toot jayega.

2. Ehtilaam: Sone se uth kar badan ya kapde par tari mile aur us tari ke Mani ya
Mazi hone ka yaqeen ya imkaan ho to Ghusl Wajib hai, chahe khwab yaad na ho. Agar
yaqeen ho ke woh Mani ya Mazi nahi, balki paseena, peshab, Wadi ya koi aur cheez
hai, to khwab yaad hone aur inzaal ki lazzat mehsoos hone ke bawajood Ghusl Wajib
nahi. Agar Mani na hone ka yaqeen ho aur Mazi mein shak ho, to khwab mein Ehtilaam
yaad na ho to Ghusl nahi, aur yaad ho to Ghusl hai.

**Mas'alah 5:** Ehtilaam yaad ho lekin kapde waghera par koi asar na mile to Ghusl
Wajib nahi.

Hawalaat: Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar, jild 1, page 325; Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1,
pages 10, 14–15.

**Mas'alah 6:** Sone se pehle Shahwat thi aur uzv khada tha. Jaag kar tari mili,
Mazi hone ka ghalib gumaan hai aur Ehtilaam yaad nahi, to jab tak Mani hone ka
ghalib gumaan na ho Ghusl Wajib nahi. Agar sone se pehle Shahwat thi hi nahi, ya
thi magar sone se pehle khatam ho gayi thi aur jo cheez nikli thi use saaf kar
chuka tha, to Mani hone ke ghalib gumaan ki zarurat nahi; sirf Mani ka imkaan bhi
Ghusl Wajib kar dega. Yeh Mas'alah aksar pesh aata hai aur log is se ghafil hain;
is ka khayal rakhna chahiye.

**Mas'alah 7:** Bimari ya kisi aur wajah se behosh hua, ya nasha karke hosh mein
aya, aur kapde ya badan par Mazi lagi mili to Wuzu Wajib hoga, Ghusl nahi. Sone
ke baad aisa ho to Ghusl Wajib hai, lekin isi shart par ke sone se pehle Shahwat
na thi.

**Mas'alah 8:** Khwab dekha, Mani bahar nahi nikli thi ke aankh khul gayi. Uzv ko
pakad liya taake Mani bahar na aaye. Shahwat khatam hone ke baad chhod diya aur
Mani nikli to Ghusl Wajib ho gaya.

**Mas'alah 9:** Namaz mein Shahwat thi aur Mani utarti hui mehsoos hui, lekin
bahar nahi nikli thi ke Namaz poori kar li. Ab nikli to Ghusl Wajib hoga, lekin
Namaz ho gayi.

**Mas'alah 10:** Khade, baithe ya chalte hue so gaya. Aankh khuli to Mazi mili;
Ghusl Wajib hai.

**Mas'alah 11:** Raat ko Ehtilaam hua, jaaga to koi asar na mila. Wuzu karke Namaz
padh li. Baad mein Mani nikli to ab Ghusl Wajib hua aur woh Namaz ho gayi.

**Mas'alah 12:** Aurat ko khwab hua to jab tak andar ki sharmgah se Mani bahar na
nikle Ghusl Wajib nahi.

**Mas'alah 13:** Mard aur aurat ek charpai par soye. Jaagne ke baad bistar par Mani
mili aur dono mein se har ek Ehtilaam se inkar karta hai. Ehtiyat yeh hai ke dono
har haal mein Ghusl karein, aur yahi durust hai.

**Mas'alah 14:** Ladka Ehtilaam ke saath baligh hua to us par Ghusl Wajib hai.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 15–16; Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 1,
page 517; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, page 333.

3. Hashfah, yani uzv-e-tanasul ka sira, aurat ke aage ya peeche, ya mard ke peeche
dakhil ho jaye to dono par Ghusl Wajib hota hai, chahe Shahwat ke saath ho ya
baghair Shahwat, aur Inzaal ho ya na ho. Shart yeh hai ke dono Mukallaf hon.
Agar ek baligh ho to us baligh par Farz hai. Na-baligh par Ghusl Farz nahi, lekin
use Ghusl ka hukm diya jayega. Misal ke taur par mard baligh aur ladki na-baligh
ho to mard par Farz hai aur ladki ko bhi nahane ka hukm diya jayega. Agar ladka
na-baligh aur aurat baligha ho to aurat par Farz hai aur ladke ko bhi hukm diya jayega.

**Mas'alah 15:** Agar Hashfah kaat diya gaya ho aur baqi uzv-e-tanasul mein Hashfah
ke barabar hissa dakhil ho gaya ho to bhi wahi hukm hai jo Hashfah dakhil hone ka hai.

**Mas'alah 16:** Agar kisi janwar, murde, ya itni chhoti ladki se badkari ki jis
se aam taur par sohbat nahi ki ja sakti, to Inzaal hone tak Ghusl Wajib nahi.

**Mas'alah 17:** Aurat ki raan mein Jima' kiya aur Inzaal ke baad Mani andar ki
sharmgah mein chali gayi, ya kunwari se Jima' kiya aur Inzaal bhi hua lekin
bakaarat khatam na hui, to aurat par Ghusl Wajib nahi. Haan, agar aurat hamila
ho jaye to ab Ghusl Wajib hone ka hukm diya jayega aur Jima' ke waqt se Ghusl
karne tak ki tamam Namazon ko dobara padhna hoga.

**Mas'alah 18:** Aurat ne apni sharmgah mein ungli, janwar ya murde ka uzv, ya
rubber, mitti waghera ka uzv jaisi koi cheez dakhil ki, to Inzaal hone tak Ghusl
Wajib nahi. Agar Jinn insani shakal mein aakar aurat se Jima' kare to Hashfah ke
chhupte hi Ghusl Wajib ho jayega. Insani shakal mein na ho to aurat ko Inzaal
hone tak Ghusl Wajib nahi. Isi tarah mard Pari se Jima' kare aur woh us waqt
insani shakal mein na ho to baghair Inzaal Ghusl Wajib nahi; insani shakal mein ho
to sirf Hashfah ke chhupne se Ghusl Wajib ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 19:** Jima' ke baad aurat ke badan se mard ki baqi Mani nikli to is se
Ghusl Wajib nahi hoga, lekin Wuzu toot jayega.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 15; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-
Muhtar, jild 1, pages 328–335. Hashfah se murad uzv-e-tanasul ka sira chhup jana hai.

Fa'idah: In teen sabab se jis par nahana Farz ho use Junub kehte hain aur in
sababon ko Janabat kaha jata hai. 4. Haiz se farigh hona. 5. Nifas khatam hona.

**Mas'alah 20:** Bachcha paida hua aur bilkul khoon na aya, to sahih qaul ke
mutabiq Ghusl Wajib hai. Haiz aur Nifas ki tafseel, Insha-Allah, Haiz ke bayan
mein aayegi.

**Mas'alah 21:** Kafir mard ya aurat Junub ho, ya Haiz aur Nifas wali kafira aurat
Muslim ho jaye, to sahih qaul ke mutabiq us par Ghusl Wajib hai, chahe Islam
lane se pehle Haiz ya Nifas se farigh ho chuki ho. Haan, agar Islam lane se pehle
Ghusl kar chuki ho ya kisi tarah poore badan par paani bah chuka ho to sirf naak
ki naram haddi tak paani chadhana kaafi hai, kyunki Kafir se yahi cheez ada nahi
hoti. Paani ke bade ghoont peene se Kulli ka Farz ada ho jata hai; agar yeh bhi
baqi reh gaya ho to Kulli bhi kare.

Ghusl mein jin aza ko dhona Farz hai, Jima' waghera ke baad agar woh sab kufr ki
halat mein hi dhul chuke the to Islam ke baad Ghusl dobara zaruri nahi. Warna
jitna hissa baqi ho use dhona Farz hai. Mustahab yeh hai ke Islam ke baad poora
Ghusl kare.

**Mas'alah 22:** Musalman Mayyit ko nahlaana Musalmanon par Farz-e-Kifayah hai.
Agar ek shakhs ne nahla diya to sab ki taraf se ada ho gaya. Agar kisi ne na
nahlaaya to sab gunahgar honge.

**Mas'alah 23:** Paani mein Musalman ka murda mila to use bhi nahlaana Farz hai.
Agar nikaalne wale ne Ghusl ki Niyyat se nikaalte waqt use ek baar dubo diya to
Ghusl ho gaya, warna ab use nahlaayen.

**Mas'alah 24:** Jumu'ah, Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha aur Arafah ke din, aur Ihram
baandhte waqt nahana Sunnat hai. Wuquf-e-Arafat, Wuquf-e-Muzdalifah, Haram aur
Sarkar-e-A'zam ﷺ ki hazri, Tawaf, Mina mein dakhla, aur teenon din Jamarat par
kan-kariyan maarne ke liye bhi Ghusl Sunnat hai. Shab-e-Bara'at, Shab-e-Qadr,
Arafah ki raat, Milad Shareef aur doosri nek majlison mein hazri, Mayyit ko
nahlaane ke baad, junoon door hone, behoshi se hosh mein aane aur nasha utarne ke
baad bhi Ghusl Mustahab hai.

Hawalaat: Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar, jild 1, page 334; Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1,
page 16; Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, page 158.

Gunaah se Taubah karne, naya kapda pehenne, safar se lautne wale ke liye,
Istihaza ka khoon band hone ke baad, Namaz-e-Kusuf, Khusuf aur Istisqa ke liye,
khauf, andheri raat aur sakht andhepan ke liye, aur badan par Najasat lagi ho
magar jagah maloom na ho, in sab suratوں mein Ghusl Mustahab hai.

**Mas'alah 25:** Hajj karne wale par 10 Zil-Hijjah ko paanch Ghusl hain:
1. Wuquf-e-Muzdalifah. 2. Mina mein dakhla. 3. Jamarat par kan-kariyan maarna.
4. Makkah mein dakhla. 5. Tawaf, jab pichhli teen baatein bhi usi din kare.
Agar Jumu'ah ka din ho to Ghusl-e-Jumu'ah bhi kare. Isi tarah Arafah ya Eid
Jumu'ah ke din ho to yahan ke logon par do Ghusl honge.

**Mas'alah 26:** Jis par kai Ghusl hon, sab ki Niyyat se ek Ghusl kar liya to
sab ada ho jayenge aur sab ka Sawab milega.

**Mas'alah 27:** Aurat Junub hui aur Ghusl se pehle Haiz shuru ho gaya to chahe
abhi Ghusl kare ya Haiz khatam hone ke baad.

**Mas'alah 28:** Junub ne Jumu'ah ya Eid ke din Ghusl-e-Janabat kiya aur Jumu'ah,
Eid waghera ki Niyyat bhi kar li to sab ada ho gaya, jab isi Ghusl se Jumu'ah ya
Eid ki Namaz padhe.

**Mas'alah 29:** Aurat ko nahane ya Wuzu ke liye paani khareedna pade to us ki
qeemat shohar ke zimme hai, jab Ghusl ya Wuzu Wajib ho, ya badan ki mail door
karne ke liye nahaye.

**Mas'alah 30:** Jis par Ghusl Wajib ho use nahane mein der nahi karni chahiye.
Hadith mein hai ke jis ghar mein Junub ho us mein Rahmat ke Farishte nahi aate.
Agar itni der kar di ke Namaz ka aakhri waqt aa gaya, to ab foran nahana Farz hai.
Ab der karega to gunahgar hoga.

Hawalaat: Tanweer-ul-Absar wa Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar, jild 1, pages 339–342;
Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, page 343; Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 227.

Agar Junub khana khana ya biwi se Jima' karna chahe to Wuzu kar le, ya haath aur
munh dho kar Kulli kar le. Agar waise hi kha-pi liya to gunah nahi, lekin Makrooh
hai aur tangdasti laata hai. Baghair Ghusl ya Wuzu Jima' kar liya to bhi gunah
nahi, lekin jis ko Ehtilaam hua ho usay baghair Ghusl biwi ke paas nahi jana chahiye.

**Mas'alah 31:** Ramadan mein raat ko Junub ho to behtar yahi hai ke Subah Sadiq
se pehle nahaye, taake roze ka koi hissa Janabat ki halat mein na guzre. Agar na
nahaya tab bhi Roze ko nuqsan nahi, lekin behtar hai ke Gargara aur naak mein
jad tak paani chadhana Subah Sadiq se pehle kar le, kyunki roze mein yeh dono
kaam nahi kar sakega. Agar Ghusl mein itni der ki ke din nikal gaya aur Namaz
Qaza kar di, to yeh doosre dinon mein bhi gunah hai aur Ramadan mein aur zyada.

**Mas'alah 32:** Jis ko Ghusl ki zarurat ho us ke liye Masjid mein jana, Tawaf
karna, Quran-e-Majeed ko chhoona, chahe saada haashiya, jild ya choli chhue ya
na chhue, dekh kar ya zubani padhna, kisi Ayat ko likhna, Ayat ka Taweez likhna,
aise Taweez ko chhoona, ya aisi Angoothi chhoona ya pehenna jisme Muqatta'at hon,
Haram hai.

**Mas'alah 33:** Agar Quran-e-Azeem Juzdan mein ho to Juzdan ko chhoone mein
haraj nahi. Rumal ya aise kapde se pakadna jo na kapde ka apna tab'e ho na
Quran-e-Majeed ka, jaiz hai. Kameez ki aastin, dupatte ke aanchal, hatta ke
chadar ka ek kona kandhe par ho aur doosre kone se chhue, to Haram hai, kyunki
yeh sab us ke taabe hain, jaise Choli Quran ke taabe thi.

**Mas'alah 34:** Quran ki Ayat ko Dua, Tabarruk, Shukr ya Sana ki Niyyat se
padhne mein haraj nahi, jab Quran padhne ki Niyyat na ho. Misal ke taur par
“Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Raheem”, chheenk ke baad “Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil-
Aalameen”, pareshan khabar par “Inna lillahi wa inna ilaihi raji'oon”, poori
Surah Fatiha, Ayat-ul-Kursi, ya Surah Hashr ki aakhri teen Ayatein padhna.
Teenon Qul bhi “Qul” ke lafz ke baghair Sana ki Niyyat se padh sakta hai. “Qul”
ke lafz ke saath nahi padh sakta, chahe Sana hi ki Niyyat ho, kyunki us surat
mein Quran hona tay ho jata hai aur Niyyat ka koi asar nahi.

**Mas'alah 35:** Be-Wuzu shakhs ke liye Quran-e-Majeed ya us ki kisi Ayat ko
chhoona Haram hai. Baghair chhue zubani ya dekh kar padhne mein haraj nahi.

Hawalaat: Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, pages 343–348;
Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 1, pages 795, 819–820.

**Mas'alah 36:** Rupaye par Ayat likhi ho to Be-Wuzu, Junub aur Haiz-o-Nifas wali
aurat sab ke liye use chhoona Haram hai. Haan, agar rupaya theli mein ho to theli
uthana jaiz hai. Isi tarah jis bartan ya glass par Surah ya Ayat likhi ho use
chhoona bhi in sab ke liye Haram hai. Us bartan ka istemal sab ke liye Makrooh
hai, magar jab khaas Shifa ki Niyyat se ho.

**Mas'alah 37:** Quran ka tarjuma Farsi, Urdu ya kisi aur zaban mein ho to use
chhoone aur padhne ka hukm bhi Quran-e-Majeed jaisa hai.

**Mas'alah 38:** In sab logon ko Quran-e-Majeed dekhne mein haraj nahi, chahe
harfon par nazar pade, alfaaz samajh mein aayen aur dil mein padhte jayen.

**Mas'alah 39:** In sab ke liye Fiqh, Tafseer aur Hadith ki kitabon ko chhoona
Makrooh hai. Agar kisi kapde se chhue, chahe woh kapda pehna ya odha hua ho,
to haraj nahi. Lekin in kitabon mein jahan Ayat ho wahan haath rakhna Haram hai.

**Mas'alah 40:** In sab ke liye Taurat, Zaboor aur Injeel ko padhna aur chhoona
Makrooh hai.

**Mas'alah 41:** Durood Shareef aur Duayein padhne mein haraj nahi. Lekin behtar
hai ke Wuzu ya Kulli karke padhe.

**Mas'alah 42:** In sab ke liye Azaan ka jawab dena jaiz hai.

**Mas'alah 43:** Mushaf Shareef itna purana ho jaye ke padhne ke kaam na aaye,
to use kafan dekar Lahad khod kar aisi jagah dafn karein jahan paon padne ka
andesha na ho.

**Mas'alah 44:** Kafir ko Mushaf chhoone na diya jaye, balki har haal mein usay
Huroof se door rakha jaye.

**Mas'alah 45:** Quran ko sab kitabon ke upar rakhein, phir Tafseer, phir Hadith,
phir darje ke mutabiq baqi Deeni kitabein rakhein.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, page 38; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-
Muhtar, jild 1, page 354.
