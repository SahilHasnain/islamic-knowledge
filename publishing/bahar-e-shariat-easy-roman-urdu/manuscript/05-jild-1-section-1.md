# Wilayat ka Bayan

Wilayat ek khaas qurb hai jo Allah Ta'ala apne chune hue bandon ko apne fazl se
ata farmata hai.

### Mas'alah 1

Wilayat Allah ki taraf se diya hua inaam hai. Aadmi sirf mushkil ibaadat karke
use khud hasil nahi kar sakta. Haan, nek amal aksar is ilahi inaam ka zariya
ban jate hain aur baaz logon ko Wilayat shuru hi se mil jati hai.

### Mas'alah 2

Wilayat be-ilm shakhs ko nahi milti. Ilm zahiri taur par hasil ho ya Allah
Ta'ala is martabe tak pahunchne se pehle us par kuch ilm khol de, Wali ko ilm
zarur hota hai.

### Aqeedah 1

Tamam pehle aur baad ke Awliya ke muqable mein Ummat-e-Muhammadi ke Awliya
afzal hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=267 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 267]

Ummat-e-Muhammadi ke Awliya mein Allah ki Ma'rifat aur qurb ke aitibar se
Khulafa-e-Arba'ah رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم sab se afzal hain. Un mein bhi tarteeb
wahi hai: Abu Bakr, Umar, Usman aur Ali رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم.

Huzoor ﷺ ne Kamalaat-e-Nabuwat ki taraf Hazrat Abu Bakr aur Hazrat Umar ko qaim
farmaya aur Kamalaat-e-Wilayat ki taraf Hazrat Ali رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ko. Is liye
baad ke tamam Awliya ne Hazrat Ali رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ke zariye Wilayat ki
ne'mat pai hai.

### Aqeedah 2

Tariqat Shari'at ke khilaf nahi. Tariqat Shari'at ka baatini hissa hai. Jo log
kehte hain ke Tariqat alag hai aur Shari'at alag, woh gumrah hain. Apne aap ko
Shari'at se azaad samajhna saaf Kufr hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=268 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 268]

### Mas'alah 3

Koi Wali kitna hi bada ho, Shari'at ke ahkaam ki pabandi se azaad nahi ho sakta.
Kuch jahil kehte hain ke Shari'at sirf un logon ke liye raasta hai jo maqsood tak
nahi pahunche; hum to pahunch chuke. Hazrat Junaid Baghdadi رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ
ne farmaya: “Woh sach kehte hain ke pahunch gaye, magar kahan? Jahannam tak.”

Agar kisi Majzoob ki aqal bilkul zail ho jaye, jaise behoshi mein, to us par
Shari'at ke ahkaam ki zimmedari nahi rehti. Magar aisa shakhs kabhi Shari'at ka
muqabla nahi karega.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=269 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 269]

### Mas'alah 4

Allah Ta'ala ne Awliya ko bahut badi quwwat di hai. Baaz khidmat karne wale
Awliya ko duniya ke mamlaat mein tasarruf ka ikhtiyar diya jata hai. Woh Huzoor
ﷺ ke sachche Naib hote hain aur unhein yeh ikhtiyarat Huzoor ﷺ ki Niyabat mein
milte hain.

Un par Uloom-e-ghaibiyyah zahir hote hain. Baaz khaas Awliya ko Allah Ta'ala ne logon ki
tarbiyat aur rehnumai ka zariya banaya hai. Woh duniya mein tasarruf karte hain,
aur log apni mushkilat ka hal un se maangte aur paate hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=270 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 270]

Baaz Awliya ko `ma kan wa ma yakun` ka bahut ilm aur Lauh-e-Mahfooz par ittila di
jati hai. Magar yeh sab Huzoor ﷺ ke zariye aur Aap ﷺ ki ata se hota hai. Rasool
ke zariye ke baghair koi ghair-Nabi kisi ghayb par muttali nahi ho sakta.

### Aqeedah 3

Awliya ki Karamat haq hai. Is ka inkar karne wala gumrah hai.

### Mas'alah 5

Wali murde ko zinda kar sakta hai, maa-zad andhe aur kodhi ko shifa de sakta hai
aur ek qadam mein mashriq se maghrib tak pahunch sakta hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=271 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 271]

Yani tamam khilaaf-e-aadat kaam, jinhein Karamat kaha jata hai, Awliya se mumkin
hain; magar woh Mu'jiza mumkin nahi jo doosron ke liye mana hone par daleel se
sabit ho chuka ho.

Hazrat Shaykh Abdul Qadir Jilani رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ki Karamat ka zikr karte hue
riwayat hai ke Abu Ghalib فضل اللہ بن اسماعیل البغدادی ne aap ko apne ghar dawat
di. Aap ne ijazat milne ka intezar kiya, phir sawari par tashreef le gaye. Wahan
Baghdad ke mashayikh, ulama aur buzurg jama the. Ek badi moharband tokri laayi
gayi. Aap ne use khulwaaya to us mein Abu Ghalib ka paidaishi andha, mafluj aur
bars wala beta tha. Shaykh ne farmaya: “Allah ke hukm se uth, tandurust ho ja.”
Woh ladka foran daudne laga, dekhne laga aur us mein koi aib baqi na raha.

Ek martaba Rafiziyon ne do band aur mohar lagi tokriyan la kar poocha ke in mein
kya hai. Aap ne dono ke bare mein sahi bataya. Ek mein mafluj ladka tha, jise aap
ke hukm se shifa mil gayi, aur doosre mein ek tandurust ladka tha, jise aap ne
baithne ka hukm diya to woh baith gaya. Is par kai logon ne Rafziyat chhor di.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=272 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 272]

Lekin woh Karamat mumkin nahi jiske na-mumkin hone par daleel qayam ho chuki ho,
jaise Quran-e-Majid ki kisi Surah jaisi Surah le aana. Jo shakhs duniya mein
bedari ki halat mein Allah Ta'ala ke deedar ya haqeeqi Kalaam se musharraf hone
ka apne liye ya kisi Wali ke liye da'wa kare, woh kafir hai.

Awliya se hawa mein udna, paani par chalna, murdon ka bolna, janwaron ka baat
karna, bala ko door karna aur dushmanon ke muqable mein mushkil door kar dena
waqay' ho sakta hai. Hazrat Umar رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ka mimbar par Madinah mein
rehte hue Nihawand ke lashkar ko dekhna aur farmana, “Aye Sariyah! Pahad ki taraf
ho jao,” bhi isi qisam ki Karamat thi. Sariyah ne door hote hue bhi yeh baat suni.

Hazrat Khalid رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ka zehar peena aur nuqsan na hona, aur Hazrat
Umar رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ke khat se Neel darya ka jaari ho jana bhi isi qisam ke
waqiat hain. Nabi ke liye aisa khilaaf-e-aadat kaam Mu'jiza hai, aur Wali ke liye
Karamat. Wali apni taraf se mustaqil hone ka da'wa kare to Wali nahi rehta.

## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 273]

### Mas'alah 6

Awliya se madad talab karna aur un se istimdad wa iste'anat karna pasandeeda hai.
Woh madad maangne wale ki madad farmate hain.

Faqeeh aur buzurgon ne qabron ki ziyarat, Awliya se tawassul aur un se mushkilat
mein madad lene ko jaiz kaha hai. Hazrat Umar رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ qahat ke waqt
Hazrat Abbas رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ke zariye Allah se barish maangte the aur farmate:
“Aye Allah! Pehle hum Tere Nabi ke zariye Tujh se barish maangte the; ab Tere
Nabi ke chacha ke zariye maangte hain.”

Ziyarat karne wala pehle Huzoor ﷺ ke zariye Allah se tawassul kare, phir qabron
mein maujood nek logon ke zariye apni zarurat aur maghfirat maange. Un ke zariye
Allah se dua kare, kyun ke Allah ne unhein chuna, izzat di aur karam farmaya.
Duniya mein un ke zariye faida diya, Aakhirat mein aur zyada faida dega. Woh Allah
Ta'ala aur us ki makhlooq ke darmiyan waseela hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=274 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 274]

Mashaikh ne farmaya ke jo shakhs zindagi mein kisi buzurg se madad leta hai, us ki
wafaat ke baad bhi us se madad li ja sakti hai. Ek buzurg ne farmaya ke unhone
char mashaikh ko apni qabron mein zindagi ki tarah, ya us se bhi zyada, tasarruf
karte dekha: Shaykh Ma'ruf Karkhi, Shaykh Abdul Qadir Jilani aur do doosre
Awliya. Is ginati se tamam buzurg murad nahi, balki jo dekha gaya us ka zikr hai.

Sayyidi Ahmad bin Marzuq ne farmaya ke Sheikh Abu Abbas Hazrami ne poocha:
“Zinda ki madad zyada mazboot hai ya murde ki?” Main ne kaha: “Log kehte hain
zinda ki madad zyada mazboot hai, lekin main kehta hoon murde ki madad zyada
mazboot hai.” Shaykh ne farmaya: “Haan, kyun ke woh Allah Ta'ala ki bargah mein
hazir hai.”

Ruh baqi rehti hai aur ziyarat karne walon aur un ke haal ka ilm rakhti hai.
Kamilon ki roohon ko Allah ke huzoor qurb aur martaba hasil hai. Awliya ki Karamat
aur kainat mein tasarruf haq hai, magar haqeeqi tasarruf Allah hi ka hai aur sab
usi ki Qudrat se hota hai.

## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 275]

Sufi mashaikh aur baaz fuqaha ne Awliya se madad lena sabit aur jaiz qarar diya
hai. اہل-e-Kashf aur un ke kamileen ke nazdeek yeh pakka aqeedah hai. Imam Shafi'i
رحمہ اللہ تعالی علیہ ne farmaya: “Hazrat Musa Kazim ki qabr dua qabool hone ke
liye azmooda tiryaaq hai.” Imam Muhammad Ghazali رحمہ اللہ تعالی علیہ ne farmaya:
“Jis se zindagi mein madad lena jaiz hai, us se wafaat ke baad bhi madad lena jaiz
hai.”

Zindagi aur wafaat dono halaton mein haqeeqi fail aur tasarruf Allah ka hai.
Allah ke muqarrab bandon ke zariye kisi ko koi cheez milna zindagi ke baad bhi
na-mumkin nahi. Kisi Nabi ya Wali ke qareeb Masjid banana aur qabr ki ta'zeem ke
liye nahi, balki qabr ki barkat se ibadat ka sawab mukammal hone aur paak ruh ka
qurb hasil karne ki niyyat se namaz padhna jaiz hai.

## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 276]

Madad maangna kisi jaiz lafz ke saath ho to jaiz hai. Un buzurgon ko Allah se
mustaqil fa'il samajhna is mas'ale ka hissa nahi. Musalman unhein Allah ka
muqarrab banda, us ki bargah mein izzat wala aur Allah ke hukm se us ki ne'matein
baantne wala samajhta hai.

Imam Subki رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ne farmaya: “Nabi ﷺ ki taraf makhlooq hone aur
af'aal mein mustaqil hone ki nisbat murad nahi. Koi Musalman yeh irada nahi karta.
Is baat ko us taraf pherna aur iste'anat se rokna deen mein dhoka aur aam
Muwahhid Musalmanon ko pareshan karna hai.”

Ibn Hajar Makki قدس سرہ ne farmaya: “Rasoolullah ﷺ ya doosre Ambiya aur Awliya ki
taraf tawajjuh aur un se faryaad ka Musalmanon ke dil mein ek hi matlab hai. Haqeeqat
mein faryaad Allah ke huzoor hai aur Nabi ﷺ Allah aur faryaadi ke darmiyan waseela
hain. Allah murad ko paida karke faryaad rasi farmata hai, aur Nabi ﷺ sabab aur
apni rahmat se حاجت poori hone ka zariya bante hain.”

### Mas'alah 7

Awliya ke mazaraat par haziri Musalman ke liye sa'adat aur barkat ka sabab hai.

### Mas'alah 8

Awliya ko door aur nazdeek se pukarna Salaf-e-Saliheen ka tareeqa hai.

### Mas'alah 9

Awliya apni qabron mein hamesha ki zindagi ke saath zinda hain. Un ka ilm,
idraak, sunna aur dekhna pehle se bahut zyada mazboot hai.

Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Qabron ki ziyarat karo, kyun ke yeh tumhein duniya se
be-raghbat karti aur Aakhirat yaad dilati hain.” Khaas taur par Awliya ke mazaraat
ki ziyarat bohat si barkat aur sa'adat ka sabab hai. Ziyarat ke dauran jahil logon
ki ijad ki hui najaiz rasmein ziyarat ko najaiz nahi banatin.

Anbiya, Shuhada aur Awliya apne jismon ke saath zinda hain. Un ke jism aur kafan
qabron mein salamat rehte hain, unhein rizq diya jata hai. Wafaat ke baad ruh ki
baqa, us ke dekhne, sunne aur samajhne ki quwwatein baqi rehti hain, balki aur saaf
aur tez ho jati hain.

### Mas'alah 10

Awliya ko Isaal-e-Sawab karna bahut barkat wala aur Mustahab amal hai. Is ko
urf mein adab ki wajah se Nazr-o-Niyaz kaha jata hai; yeh fiqhi ma'ni wala
Shar'i nazr nahi, jaise badshah ko nazr pesh karna. Khaas taur par Gyarhween
Shareef ki Fatiha bahut badi barkat hai.

Wafaat ke baad bhi Awliya ko pesh ki jane wali hadiye ko urf ki wajah se nazr
kaha jata hai. Yeh Allah ke ilawa kisi ke liye fiqhi nazr nahi hoti. Agar kisi
shakhs ne kisi haajat ke poora hone par buzurgon ke liye kuch pesh karne ki mannat
maani ho to is ka matlab un ki qabr ke khadimon ko sadaqah dena hota hai; lafz ka
aitibar nahi, ma'ni ka aitibar hota hai.

### Mas'alah 11

Awliya ka Urs, yani Quran-khwani, Fatiha-khwani, Na't-khwani, wa'z aur Isaal-e-
Sawab, achha amal hai. Shar'i mana ki hui baatein har halat mein buri hain aur
mazaraat ke paas aur bhi zyada buri hain.

Muslimon ko Awliya se aqeedat aur mashaikh ke silsile mein shamil hone ko duniya
aur Aakhirat ki kamyabi samajhne ki wajah se aaj ke Wahabiyon ne logon ko gumrah
karne ke liye yeh jaal phaila rakha hai ke Peer-o-Mureedi bhi unhone shuru ki.
Haalan ke woh Awliya ke munkir hain. Is liye jab kisi ko Mureed hona ho to achhi
tarah tahqeeq kar le, warna agar Peer bad-mazhab hua to iman bhi haath se ja sakta
hai.

“Kabhi Iblees aadmi ki surat mein aata hai, is liye har haath mein haath nahi dena
chahiye.” Yani har kisi se Bai'at nahi karni chahiye.

## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 280]

Piri ke liye chaar shartein hain aur Bai'at se pehle un ka lihaz karna farz hai:

1. Peer Sunni aur sahih-ul-aqeedah ho.
2. Itna ilm rakhta ho ke apni zaruriyat ke masail kitabon se nikal sake.
3. Fasiq-e-Mu'lin na ho.
4. Us ka silsila Huzoor ﷺ tak muttasil ho.

“Hum Allah Ta'ala se deen, duniya aur Aakhirat mein maafi, aafiyat aur paak
Shari'at par istiqamat maangte hain. Meri taufeeq Allah hi se hai. Main usi par
bharosa karta hoon aur usi ki taraf ruju' karta hoon. Allah Ta'ala apne Mehboob ﷺ,
un ki Aal, Sahaba, bete aur giroh par hamesha hamesha durood bheje. Aur tamam
hamd Allah ke liye hai jo tamam jahanon ka Rab hai.”

Faqeer Amjad Ali A'zami عفی عنہ.
