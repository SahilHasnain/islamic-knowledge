# Aqaid Mutaliq Zaat-o-Sifaat-e-Ilahi

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=4 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 4]

Allah (عزوجل) ek hai. Us ka koi shareek nahi: na Zaat mein, na Sifaat mein, na
Af'aal mein, na Ahkaam mein aur na Asmaa mein.

Allah wajib-ul-wujood hai. Yani us ka hona zaruri hai aur us ka na hona mumkin
nahi. Woh Qadeem hai, yani hamesha se hai aur us se pehle koi cheez nahi thi.

Allah Ta'ala ki wahdaniyat ke bare mein Quran-e-Kareem mein irshad hai:

قل هو الله احد

و الهكم اله واحد-لا اله الا هو

Allah ki Zaat, Sifaat aur Af'aal mein kisi ko shareek na karna zaruri hai. Woh
apne faisle aur apni makhlooq ki tadbeer mein bilkul yakta hai. Us ke naam mein
bhi koi shareek nahi.

Allah Ta'ala apni Zaat se wajib-ul-wujood hai, jab ke Allah ke siwa har cheez
apni Zaat ke aitibar se mumkin-ul-wujood hai. Allah be-niyaz hai aur har mumkin
cheez apne wajood aur baqa ke liye us ki mohtaj hai.

Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

و الله الغنی و انتم الفقرآء

Qadeem hone ka matlab yeh hai ke Allah ke wajood se pehle kabhi adam nahi tha.
Allah ke haq mein Qadeem ka matlab sirf yeh hai ke us ka wajood hamesha se hai;
yeh zamane ke lamba hone ka naam nahi.

Is mawzu par tafseel ke liye *Sharh-ul-Fiqh-ul-Akbar* mein *Manh-ur-Rawd-ul-
Azhar*, *Hashiyah Sawi*, *Tafseer-e-Tabari*, *At-Tafseer-ul-Kabeer*, *Al-Mu'taqad
Al-Muntaqad* aur Imam Ahmad Raza Khan علیہ رحمۃ الرحمن ka risala *I'tiqad-ul-
Ahbab fil-Jameel wal-Mustafa wal-Aal wal-Ashab*, jise *Das Aqaid* bhi kaha jata
hai, dekhein.

Allah hamesha se hai aur hamesha rahega. Isi ko Azli aur Abadi kaha jata hai.
Sirf wahi ibadat aur bandagi ka haqdar hai.

Allah kisi ka mohtaj nahi aur poori kainaat us ki mohtaj hai. Quran-e-Kareem mein
hai:

كل شیء هالك الا وجهه

Allah ki Zaat ka aqal ke zariye poora ihata karna mumkin nahi. Aqal jis cheez ko
samajhti hai us ka ihata kar leti hai, jab ke Allah ki Zaat ka koi ihata nahi kar
sakta. Haan, Allah ke Af'aal se ijmali taur par us ki Sifaat ki pehchan hoti hai,
aur Sifaat ke zariye us ki Zaat ki ma'rifat hasil hoti hai.

Allah Ta'ala ki ibadat ka hukm Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

یایها الناس اعبدوا ربكم الذی خلقكم

ذلكم الله ربكم-لا اله الا هو-خالق كل شیء فاعبدوه

و قضى ربك الا تعبدوا الا ایاه

Allah ko *As-Samad* kaha gaya hai, yani woh sab se be-niyaz hai aur har koi us
ka mohtaj hai:

الله الصمد

Allah ki Zaat ko aankhen idraak nahi kar sakti. Idraak ka matlab yeh hai ke kisi
cheez ko us ki tamam hadon aur kinare ke saath apne ihate mein le liya jaye.
Allah ki ru'yat ke bare mein yeh zaruri hai ke us ki Zaat ka ihata nahi kiya ja
sakta.

Is tafseel ke liye *Al-Mu'taqad Al-Muntaqad*, *Al-Musamarah bi Sharh-il-
Musayarah*, *Sharh-ul-Fiqh-ul-Akbar*, *At-Tafseer-ul-Kabeer* aur doosri
ma'tabar kitaben dekhein.

### Aqeedah 4

Allah ki Sifaat na bilkul us ki Zaat ka naam hain aur na us se alag koi cheez.
Woh us ki Zaat ke saath qaim hain aur us ki Zaat ke liye lazim hain. Is baat ko
phool ki khushboo se samjhein: khushboo phool ki Sifat hai, phool ke saath hoti
hai, lekin khushboo ko phool nahi kaha jata aur na phool se alag paya ja sakta hai.

### Aqeedah 5

Jis tarah Allah ki Zaat Qadeem, Azli aur Abadi hai, isi tarah us ki Sifaat bhi
Qadeem, Azli aur Abadi hain. Allah ki koi Sifat baad mein paida nahi hui aur na
us ki koi Sifat kabhi khatam hogi, kyunki Allah apni Zaat aur Sifaat dono mein
kaamil hai.

### Aqeedah 6

Allah ki Sifaat na makhlooq hain aur na Allah ki qudrat ke neeche paida hone wali
cheezon mein shamil hain. *Al-Fiqh-ul-Akbar* mein hai ke Allah ki Sifaat Azal se
ghair-muhdath aur ghair-makhlooq hain.

### Aqeedah 7

Allah ki Zaat aur Sifaat ke ilawa har cheez haadith hai. Yani pehle maujood nahi
thi, phir Allah Ta'ala ne use wajood diya.

### Aqeedah 8

Jo shakhs Allah ki Sifaat ko makhlooq ya haadith kahe, ya in ke bare mein shak
rakhe aur Qadeem ya haadith hone ka faisla na kare, woh gumrah aur bad-deen hai.
*Al-Mu'taqad Al-Muntaqad* mein is aqeede ki wazahat ki gayi hai ke Allah ki
Sifaat Azli aur Abadi hain, un ki koi ibtida nahi aur koi intiha nahi.

In aqaid ki tafseel *Al-Musayarah*, *Sharh-ul-Aqaid An-Nasafiyyah*, *Sharh-ul-
Fiqh-ul-Akbar*, *Al-Mu'taqad Al-Muntaqad* aur *Al-Mu'tamad-ul-Mustanad* mein
dekhi ja sakti hai.

### Aqeedah 9

Jo shakhs kainaat ki kisi cheez ko Qadeem maane, ya us ke Haadith hone mein
shak kare, woh kafir hai. Allah ke siwa har cheez pehle maujood nahi thi, phir
Allah Ta'ala ne use paida kiya.

### Aqeedah 10

Allah na kisi ka baap hai, na beta, aur na us ki koi biwi hai. Jo Allah ke liye
baap, beta ya biwi sabit kare woh kafir hai. Balke jo is baat ko mumkin bhi
samjhe woh gumrah aur bad-deen hai.

Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

لم یلد و لم یولد

ما اتخذ صاحبة و لا ولدا

و ما ینبغی للرحمن ان یتخذ ولدا

Allah ki taraf kisi aisi sifat ko mansoob karna jo us ke laiq nahi, ya us ka
shareek, beta ya biwi banana, kufr hai. Is masle ki tafseel *Ash-Shifa*,
*Al-Mu'taqad Al-Muntaqad*, *Inba-ul-Hayy*, *Al-Fatawa Ar-Razaviyyah*,
*Al-Bahr-ur-Raiq*, *Majma-ul-Anhur* aur *At-Tatarkhaniyyah* mein dekhein.

### Aqeedah 11

Allah Hayy hai, yani woh khud zinda hai aur har makhlooq ki zindagi us ke qabze
mein hai. Jise chahe zindagi de aur jise chahe maut de.

### Aqeedah 12

Allah har mumkin cheez par Qadir hai. Koi mumkin us ki Qudrat se bahar nahi.

### Aqeedah 13

Jo cheez Muhaal hai, woh Allah (عزوجل) ki Qudrat se muta'alliq nahi hoti, kyunki
Muhaal woh hai jo ho hi nahi sakti. Allah Ta'ala ki Qudrat har mumkin cheez ko
shamil hai; jo cheez apni Zaat hi mein na-mumkin ho, us ka maujood hona tasawwur
mein nahi aa sakta.

Misal ke taur par doosra Khuda hona Muhaal hai. Agar doosra Khuda Allah ki
Qudrat mein aa sakta ho to us ka maujood hona mumkin ho jayega, aur phir woh
Muhaal nahi rahega. Isi tarah Allah ki fana bhi Muhaal hai. Agar fana Allah ki
Qudrat mein aa sakti ho to fana mumkin hogi, aur jis ki fana mumkin ho woh Khuda
nahi ho sakta.

Is liye Muhaal ko Qudrat ke ta'alluq mein lana durust nahi. Aisa samajhna Allah
Ta'ala ki Uluhiyyat ke khilaf hai.

### Aqeedah 14

Har mumkin cheez ka maujood ho jana zaruri nahi. Us ka mumkin hona zaruri hai,
chahe woh kabhi maujood na ho.

### Aqeedah 15

Allah har kamal aur khoobi ka Jaame' hai. Har aisi cheez se paak hai jo aib ya
nuqsan ho. Allah ke liye jhoot, dhoka, khiyanat, zulm, jahalat aur be-hayai
bilkul Muhaal hain.

Yeh kehna bhi ghalat hai ke Allah jhoot bolne par Qadir hai. Is ka matlab Muhaal
ko mumkin banana aur Allah ki taraf aib mansoob karna hoga. Isi tarah yeh sochna
ke Muhaal par Qudrat ka ta'alluq na hone se Allah ki Qudrat naaqis ho jayegi,
bilkul baatil hai. Nuqsan Allah ki Qudrat mein nahi, balke Muhaal cheez mein hai
jo Qudrat se muta'alliq hone ki salahiyat hi nahi rakhti.

Quran-e-Kareem mein Allah ki Hayaat aur Qudrat ka bayan hai:

هو الحی القیوم

و هو الذی یحی و یمیت

ان الله على كل شیء قدیر

Allah ke siwa kisi ko bhi Allah ki tarah Qadir samajhna durust nahi. Is aqeede
ki tafseel *Al-Musamarah bi Sharh-il-Musayarah* aur *Al-Fatawa Ar-Razaviyyah*
ke risale *Subhan-as-Subuh an Aib-e-Kazib-e-Maqbuh* mein hai.

### Aqeedah 16

Allah ki Zaati Sifaat mein Hayaat, Qudrat, Sama'at, Basarat, Kalaam, Ilm aur
Iradah shamil hain. Allah ka sunna kaan se, dekhna aankh se aur kalaam karna
zaban se nahi, kyunki kaan, aankh aur zaban jism hain aur Allah jism se paak hai.

Allah har halki se halki aawaz sunta hai. Woh har bareek se bareek cheez ko
dekhta hai, chahe insani nazar ya doorbeen use mehsoos na kar sake. Allah ka
sunna aur dekhna sirf in cheezon tak mahdood nahi: woh har maujood ko dekhta aur
har maujood ko sunta hai.

Allah ki Hayaat, Qudrat, Ilm, Kalaam, Sama'at, Basarat aur Iradah Azli aur Abadi
Sifaat hain. Us ki Sifaat makhlooq ki Sifaat ki tarah nahi. Allah ka sunna kisi
kaan ya hawa ke zariye nahi, aur us ka dekhna kisi aankh ya hadd ki zarurat nahi
rakhata.

Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

الله لا اله الا هو-الحی القیوم

و هو على كل شیء قدیر

ان الله هو السمیع البصیر

و كلم الله موسى تكلیما

ان الله قد احاط بكل شیء علما

ان الله یحكم ما یرید

ان ربك فعال لما یرید

Allah har purane aur naye, badi aur chhoti, zahir aur posheeda cheez ko poori
tarah jaanta, dekhta aur sunta hai. Us ka Kalaam us ki Zaat ke saath Azal se
qa'im hai aur hamesha rahega.

Is ki mazeed tafseel *Al-Fiqh-ul-Akbar*, *Al-Musamarah bi Sharh-il-Musayarah*,
*Al-Hadiqat-un-Nadiyyah* aur doosri ma'tabar kitabon mein hai.

### Aqeedah 17

Allah ka Kalaam bhi us ki doosri Sifaat ki tarah Qadeem hai. Woh haadith ya
makhlooq nahi. Jo Quran-e-Kareem ko makhlooq kahe, us ke bare mein Imam-e-Azam
aur doosre Imamon رضی الله تعالی عنہم ke aqwal mein sakht hukm aaya hai. Sahaba
aur buzurg ulama se bhi is qaul ki mazammat aur takfeer manqool hai.

Is masle mein ulama ne tafseel bayan ki hai. Allah ke Kalaam ka inkar, jo Quran
aur ijma se sabit hai, kufr hai. Quran ko makhlooq kehne ke bare mein fuqaha ka
sakt qaul manqool hai, jab ke aksar mutakallimeen zarooriyat-e-deen mein se kisi
cheez ke inkar par hi takfeer karne ko ehtiyat ke qareeb samajhte hain. Is liye
is masle mein ma'tabar ulama ki sharh aur tafseel ke baghair jaldi faisla nahi
karna chahiye.

### Aqeedah 18

Allah ka Kalaam awaaz aur huroof ki jins se paak hai. Quran-e-Kareem, jise hum
apni zaban se padhte aur mushaf mein likhte hain, Allah ka Qadeem Kalaam hai.
Hamari tilawat, hamari likhai, hamara sunna aur hamara hifz karna haadith hain;
lekin jis Quran ko hum padhte, likhte, sunte aur yaad karte hain, woh Allah ka
Qadeem Kalaam hai.

Qadeem Kalaam kisi awaaz, huroof, mushaf, zaban, kaan ya insani amal ke andar
sama nahi jata. Qadeem ek hai; us ka zahoor mukhtalif tareeqon se hota hai. Dil
mein ma'ni ki surat mein, zaban par lafz ki surat mein, mushaf mein naqsh ki
surat mein aur kaan mein suni hui surat mein. Zahoor ke badalne se asal Haqiqat
ek se zyada nahi ho jati.

Is aqeede ki tafseel *Al-Fiqh-ul-Akbar*, *Manh-ur-Rawd-ul-Azhar*, *Al-Hadiqat-
un-Nadiyyah*, *Al-Mu'tamad-ul-Mustanad* aur Imam Ahmad Raza Khan علیہ رحمۃ
الرحمن ke رسالہ *Anwar-ul-Mannan fi Tauheed-il-Quran* mein hai.

Quran-e-Kareem Allah ka ek haqiqi aur Azli Kalaam hai. Woh apni asal mein ek hi
hai, chahe woh hamare dilon mein ma'ni ki surat mein zahir ho, zabanon se padha
jaye, mushaf mein likha jaye ya kaanon se suna jaye.

Quran ko hamare hifz, tilawat, likhai aur sunne ko haadith kehna durust hai,
kyunki yeh hamare amal aur hamari sifat hain. Magar jis Quran ko hum hifz karte,
padhte, likhte aur sunte hain, woh haqiqatan wahi Qadeem Quran hai. Use Allah
Ta'ala se alag samajhna ya makhlooq kehna durust nahi.

Is baat ko is tarah samjhein: libaas badalne se libaas pehenne wale shakhs ki
Haqiqat nahi badalti. Isi tarah Quran ki tajalli dil, zaban, mushaf aur kaan ke
mutabiq zahir hoti hai, magar Quran ki Qidam par koi asar nahi padta.

Jo shakhs kahe ke Quran sirf likhe hue naqsh ya padhe hue lafz ka naam hai aur
Allah ka Qadeem Kalaam is ke alawa koi Haqiqat nahi, woh sahih aqeede se hat gaya.
Sahih baat yeh hai ke Quran Qadeem Allah ka Kalaam hai jo mukhtalif jagahon par
mukhtalif andaaz se zahir hota hai.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan علیہ رحمۃ الرحمن ne is mushkil masle ko *Anwar-ul-Mannan
fi Tauheed-il-Quran* mein tafseel se samjhaya hai. Is masle ko samajhne mein
agar mushkil ho to Allah ki Sifaat ki tarah is par bhi bila-kaif iman rakha jaye.

### Aqeedah 19

Allah ka Ilm har cheez ko muheet hai. Woh Azal mein bhi har badi aur chhoti
cheez ko jaanta tha, ab bhi jaanta hai aur hamesha jaanta rahega. Woh maujood,
ghair-maujood, mumkin aur Muhaal sab ko jaanta hai.

Cheezen badalti rehti hain, lekin Allah ke Ilm mein koi tabdeeli nahi aati. Woh
dilon ke khayalat aur posheeda waswason se bhi ba-khabar hai. Us ke Ilm ki koi
intiha nahi.

Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

یعلم ما فی السموت و الارض و یعلم ما تسرون و ما تعلنون-و الله علیم بذات الصدور

و عنده مفاتح الغیب لا یعلمها الا هو-و یعلم ما فی البر و البحر-و ما تسقط من ورقة الا یعلمها

و اسروا قولكم او اجهروا به-انه علیم بذات الصدور

Allah ka Ilm kisi naye ilm ke hasil hone, kisi asar qabool karne ya kisi tabdeeli
ke zariye paida nahi hua. Woh Azli Ilm hai aur kamal ke saath Allah ki Zaat mein
qa'im hai. Kainaat ki koi zarra-barabar cheez bhi us ke Ilm se bahar nahi.

### Aqeedah 20

Allah ghayb aur zahir, dono ko jaanta hai. Ghayb woh hai jo hamari nazar aur
hawas se posheeda ho, aur zahir woh jo hamare saamne ho. Allah ka Zaati Ilm us
ka khaas hai; kisi makhlooq ko Allah ki tarah Zaati Ilm hasil nahi.

Is bayan ki tafseel *At-Tafseer-ul-Kabeer*, *Manh-ur-Rawd-ul-Azhar* aur
*Al-Hadiqat-un-Nadiyyah* mein dekhein.

Allah ka Zaati Ilm-e-Ghaib Allah hi ke liye khaas hai. Jo shakhs Allah ke ilawa
kisi aur ke liye Zaati Ilm-e-Ghaib sabit kare, chahe bahut hi thodi miqdaar mein,
woh kufr aur shirk ka murtakib hota hai. Ilm-e-Zaati ka matlab yeh hai ke kisi
ko Allah ke diye baghair khud apne taur par ilm hasil ho.

### Aqeedah 21

Allah hi har cheez ka Khaliq hai. Zaat hon ya bandon ke Af'aal, sab Allah Ta'ala
hi ke paida kiye hue hain.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

الله خالق كل شیء

و الله خلقكم و ما تعملون

### Aqeedah 22

Haqiqi taur par Rizq pahunchane wala Allah hi hai. Farishte aur doosre zariye
aur wasile hain, magar Rizq dene wala asal mein Allah Ta'ala hi hai.

ان الله هو الرزاق ذو القوة المتین

Quran-e-Kareem mein farishton ko Allah ke hukm se kaam karne wale bataya gaya
hai. Kuch farishte baarish, paudon aur rizq ke intizam par muqarrar hain, kuch
roohon ko qabz karne par aur kuch Soor phoonkne par. Is se maloom hota hai ke
wasile apna kaam Allah ke hukm se karte hain.

### Aqeedah 23

Allah Ta'ala ne har bhale aur bure kaam ko apne Azli Ilm ke mutabiq muqaddar
farmaya. Allah jaanta tha ke kaun kya karega aur usi ko likha. Is ka matlab yeh
nahi ke Allah ke likhne ne humein majboor kar diya; balke Allah ne wahi likha jo
hum apne ikhtiyar se karne wale the.

Zaid ke bare mein burai likhi gayi kyunki Allah ke Azli Ilm mein tha ke Zaid
burai karega. Agar Zaid bhala kaam karne wala hota to us ke liye bhala hi likha
jata. Allah ka ilm kisi ko majboor nahi karta.

Allah Ta'ala Azal mein tamam cheezon ko un ke hone se pehle jaanta tha aur usi
ke mutabiq un ka taqdeeri faisla farmaya. Ahle-Haq ka aqeedah hai ke achhai aur
burai dono Allah ki paida ki hui hain; magar banday unhein apne irade aur amal
se kamate hain.

Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne taqdeer ka inkar karne walon ko is Ummat ke Majus farmaya:

القدریۃ مجوس ہذہ الامۃ

### Aqeedah 24

Qaza ki teen qismein hain:

1. **Qaza-e-Mubram-e-Haqiqi:** Allah ke Ilm mein kisi cheez par kisi aur cheez
   ki shart nahi. Is mein tabdeeli mumkin nahi.
2. **Woh Qaza jo farishton ke sahifon mein shart ke saath likhi gayi ho:**
   Farishton ke sahifon mein kisi cheez ka hona kisi shart ke saath dikhaya gaya
   ho.
3. **Woh Qaza jo zahiri taur par bina shart ho, lekin Allah ke Ilm mein shart
   ke saath ho:** Farishton ke sahifon mein shart ka zikr na ho, lekin Allah ke
   Ilm mein woh shart ke saath ho.

Qaza-e-Mubram-e-Haqiqi mein tabdeeli mumkin nahi. Allah ke khaas mehboob bande
agar is bare mein kisi cheez ki guzarish karein to unhein is faisle se wapas kar
diya jata hai.

### Ibrahim علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ka Waqia

Malak-ul-Maut aur doosre farishte Qoum-e-Loot par azaab lane aaye. Ibrahim
Khalilullah علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام nihayat shafeeq aur meherban the. Unhone
apne Rab se Qoum-e-Loot ke bare mein guftagu ki. Quran-e-Kareem mein Allah
Ta'ala farmata hai:

یجادلنا فی قوم لوط

Yani Ibrahim علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ne hamare saath Qoum-e-Loot ke bare mein guftagu
ki.

Is ayat se maloom hota hai ke Allah ke mehboob bandon ki Allah ki bargah mein
izzat hoti hai. Un ki shafqat aur guzarish ka zikr khud Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya.

### Musa علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ka Waqia

### Masla 1: Qaza-o-Qadr ko Samajhna

Qaza-o-Qadr ke masail aam aqal mein poori tarah nahi aa sakte. In mein hadd se
zyada ghour-o-fikr kabhi nuqsan ka sabab ban sakta hai. Sayyiduna Abu Bakr aur
Sayyiduna Umar رضی الله تعالی عنہما ko bhi is masle mein be-ja behas se roka gaya.

Itna samajh lena kafi hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne insaan ko pathar aur doosri be-hiss
jamadaat ki tarah be-harkat nahi banaya. Allah ne use ek qisam ka ikhtiyar diya
ke woh kisi kaam ko chahe kare aur chahe na kare. Usay aqal di, jis se woh achhai,
burai, faida aur nuqsan pehchan sake. Kaam ke liye zaruri asbab aur wasail bhi
ata kiye. Jab woh koi kaam karna chahta hai to us ke liye munasib asbab paida

Insaan ko na bilkul majboor samajhna durust hai aur na bilkul aisa mukhtar ke
Allah ki mashiyat aur Qudrat se be-niyaz ho. Dono soch ghalat hain.

Allah Ta'ala par kisi ko sawab ya azaab dena, ya banday ke liye woh kaam karna
jo us ke haq mein behtar ho, wajib nahi. Allah Ta'ala bilkul be-niyaz Malik hai;
jo chahe kare aur jo chahe hukm de.

Lekin Allah Ta'ala ne apne karam se wada farmaya hai ke Musalmanon ko Jannat mein
dakhil karega aur apne adl ke mutabiq kafiron ko Jahannum mein dakhil karega.
Allah ka wada aur waeed badalte nahi.

Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

لا یكلف الله نفسا الا وسعها

فعال لما یرید

لا تبدیل لكلمت الله

ما یبدل القول لدی

Insaan apne ikhtiyar se achha ya bura kaam karta hai. Allah Ta'ala us ke kaam ka
Khaliq hai, magar banday ke ikhtiyari amal ki nisbat banday hi ki taraf hoti hai.
Is liye achhe kaam par Allah ka shukr aur bure kaam par apne nafs ka muhasaba
karna chahiye.

Allah Ta'ala ke faisle mein na zulm hai na be-maqsadi. Us ka har hukm Ilm,
Hikmat aur adl par mabni hai.

### Aqeedah 29

Allah Ta'ala ke har kaam mein bahut si Hikmatein hoti hain, chahe humein un ka
ilm ho ya na ho. Allah ke kaam ke liye kisi aise maqsad ki zarurat nahi jo us ki
taraf laut kar use koi faida de. Na us ke kaam ke liye kisi aisi aakhri manzil
ki zarurat hai jo use kaam par majboor kare. Allah Ta'ala apni buland Hikmat se
asbab ki duniya mein sabab aur nateeje ke darmiyan talluq paida farmata hai.

Aankh dekhti hai, kaan sunta hai, aag jalati hai aur paani pyaas bujhata hai.
Allah Ta'ala chahe to aankh suna sakti hai, kaan dikha sakta hai, paani jala
sakta hai aur aag pyaas bujha sakti hai. Aur agar Allah Ta'ala na chahe to lakhon
aankhen hon, din mein pahaad nazar na aaye; croreon aag hon, ek tinke par daagh
na aaye.

Ibrahim علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ko kafiron ne kis qahr ki aag mein dala tha! Koi us
ke paas nahi ja sakta tha. Unhein gulel mein rakh kar aag ki taraf phenka gaya.
Jab woh aag ke saamne pahunche to Jibreel Amin علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام haazir hue aur
arz ki: “Aye Ibrahim! Koi zarurat hai?” Ibrahim علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ne farmaya:
“Haan, magar tum se nahi.”

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

ان الله لا یغفر ان یشرك به و یغفر ما دون ذلك لمن یشآء

Allah Ta'ala ke har kaam mein Hikmat hoti hai, chahe woh zahir ho ya hamari
samajh se posheeda. Is Hikmat ko Allah Ta'ala ke liye aise maqsad ke ma'ni mein
nahi lena chahiye jis se use koi faida pahunchta ho. Allah Ta'ala ke af'aal kisi
zarurat ya majboori ki wajah se nahi hote.

Allah Ta'ala ne duniya mein asbab aur nateejon ka ek nizam bana diya hai. Aankh
dekhne ka sabab hai aur kaan sunne ka sabab, lekin asar paida karna Allah
Ta'ala ke ikhtiyar mein hai. Asbab apne aap kuch nahi karte.
