# Malaika ka Bayan

Farishtay Noorani jism hain. Allah Ta'ala ne unhein qudrat di hai ke jis surat
mein chahein zahir ho jayein. Kabhi insaan ki surat mein aur kabhi kisi aur surat
mein nazar aate hain.

### Aqeedah 1

Farishtay wahi karte hain jo Allah Ta'ala ka hukm hota hai. Woh na jaan-boojh kar,
na bhool se aur na ghalti se Allah ke hukm ki mukhalifat karte hain. Woh Allah
عزوجل ke ma'soom banday hain aur tamam sagheerah-o-kabirah gunahon se paak hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=93 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 93]

### Aqeedah 2

Farishton ko mukhtalif khidmatein di gayi hain. Baaz Ambiya-e-Kiram علیہم السلام
ki khidmat mein Wahi laate hain, baaz paani barsate, hawa chalate aur rizq
pahunchate hain. Baaz maa ke pait mein bachche ki surat banate hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=94 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 94]

Baaz farishtay insaan ke badan mein tasarruf karte, dushmanon se hifazat karte,
zikr ki majlis dhoond kar us mein haazir hote aur insaan ke aamaal likhte hain.
Bahut se farishtay Darbar-e-Risalat mein haazir hote aur Musalmanon ka Salat-o-
Salam Huzoor ﷺ ki bargah mein pahunchate hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=95 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 95]

Baaz farishtay murdon se sawal karte, baaz rooh qabz karte, baaz azaab dete aur
baaz Soor phoonkte hain. Is ke ilawa bhi farishtay bahut se kaam anjaam dete hain.

### Aqeedah 3

Farishtay na mard hain na aurat.

### Aqeedah 4

Farishton ko Qadeem ya Khaliq maanna Kufr hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=96 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 96]

### Aqeedah 5

Farishton ki tadaad wahi jaanta hai jis ne unhein paida kiya, aur us ke batane
se us ka Rasool jaanta hai. Chaar farishtay sab se zyada mashhoor hain: Jibreel,
Mikaeel, Israfeel aur Izraeel علیہم السلام. Yeh sab farishton se afzal hain.

و ما یعلم جنود ربك الا هو

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=97 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 97]

### Aqeedah 6

Kisi farishte ke saath halki si bhi gustakhi Kufr hai. Jahil log apne dushman ya
na-pasand shakhs ko dekh kar kehte hain: “Malak-ul-Maut ya Izraeel aa gaya.” Yeh
baat Kufr ke qareeb hai.

### Aqeedah 7

Farishton ke wajood ka inkar karna, ya yeh kehna ke farishta sirf neki ki quwwat
ka naam hai aur kuch nahi, dono baatein Kufr hain.

ko Shaitan kehte hain. Woh insaan ki tarah aqal, rooh aur jism rakhte hain. Un
mein nikah aur aulaad hoti hai, khate peete, zinda rehte aur marte hain.

# Aalam-e-Barzakh ka Bayan

Duniya aur Aakhirat ke darmiyan ek aur aalam hai jise Barzakh kehte hain. Marne
ke baad aur Qiyamat se pehle tamam Ins-o-Jinn apne martabe ke mutabiq us mein
rehte hain. Yeh aalam duniya se bahut bada hai. Barzakh mein kisi ko aaraam hota
hai aur kisi ko takleef.

### Aqeedah 1

Har shakhs ki jo umr muqarrar hai, us mein na izafa ho sakta hai na kami. Jab
zindagi ka waqt poora ho jata hai to Izraeel علیہ السلام rooh qabz karne ke liye
aate hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=101 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 101]

Jab kisi ki rooh qabz ki jati hai to us ke daayein-baayein, jahan tak nigah jati
hai, farishtay nazar aate hain. Musalman ke aas-paas Rahmat ke farishtay hote
hain aur kafir ke aas-paas azaab ke farishtay.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=102 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 102]

Us waqt har shakhs par Islam ki haqqaniyat suraj se zyada roshan ho jati hai,
magar us waqt ka iman maqbool nahi hota, kyun ke iman bil-ghaib ka hukm hai aur
ab ghayb nahi raha, balke yeh cheezein saamne aa gayi hain.

### Aqeedah 2

Marne ke baad bhi rooh ka insani badan se ta'alluq baqi rehta hai. Agarche rooh
badan se juda ho gayi, lekin badan par jo guzarti hai rooh us se ba-khabar aur
mutasir hoti hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=103 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 103]

### Aqeedah 3

Musalman ki rooh apne martabe ke mutabiq mukhtalif maqamon par rehti hai. Baaz
ki rooh qabr par, baaz ki Zamzam Shareef ke kuen mein, baaz ki aasman-o-zameen
ke darmiyan, baaz ki pehle, doosre ya saatwein aasman tak, aur baaz ki aasmanon
se bhi buland hoti hai. Baaz roohen Arsh ke neeche qandilon mein aur baaz
Illiyyeen (bahut buland aur ba-izzat maqam) mein hoti hain. Magar jahan bhi hon, apne badan se un ka ta'alluq baqi
rehta hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=104 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 104]

Rooh apne badan se ta'alluq ki wajah se qabr par aane wale ko dekhti aur pehchanti
hai aur us ki baat sunti hai. Rooh ka dekhna sirf qabr ke qareeb hone tak mahdood
nahi. Hadith mein us ki misaal is tarah di gayi hai jaise koi parinda pehle qafas
(pinjre) mein band tha aur ab azaad kar diya gaya ho.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=105 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 105]

Hadith mein hai:

إذا مات المومن یخلی سربہ یسرح حیث شاء

“Jab Musalman ko wafat di jati hai to us ki raah khol di jati hai; woh jahan
chahe ja sakta hai.”

Kafiron ki napaak roohen baaz ke liye un ke qabrastan ya qabr par, baaz ke liye
Chaah-e-Barahoot mein, jo Yemen ki ek wadi hai, aur baaz ke liye pehli, doosri
ya saatwein zameen tak rehti hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=106 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 106]

### Aqeedah 5

Maut ka matlab rooh ka badan se juda ho jana hai, yeh nahi ke rooh khatam ho
jaye. Jo rooh ko fana maanay woh bad-mazhab hai.

### Aqeedah 6

Murdah baat bhi karta hai. Insaan aur Jinn ke ilawa tamam jaanwar us ki baat
sunte hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=107 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 107]

### Aqeedah 7

Jab murde ko qabr mein dafn kiya jata hai to qabr use dabati hai. Agar woh
Musalman ho to qabr ka dabana aisa hota hai jaise maa pyaar se apne bachche ko
zor se galay lagati hai. Agar woh kafir ho to qabr use itna zor se dabati hai ke
ek taraf ki pasliyan doosri taraf chali jati hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=108 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 108]

Dafn karne wale jab dafn karke wapas jate hain to murdah un ke jooton ki aahat
sunta hai. Us waqt us ke paas do farishtay aate hain. Un ki shaklein nihayat
khaufnaak hoti hain. Un ka rang siyah aur aankhen siyah-neeli hoti hain. Woh
use qabr mein bitha kar sawal karte hain.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=109 -->

ربي اللہ — “Mera Rab Allah عزوجل hai.”

دیني الإسلام — “Mera Deen Islam hai.”

ھو رسول اللہ — “Woh Allah ke Rasool hain.”

denge. Nek logon ke nek amal achchi aur pasandeeda surat mein ban kar unhein

### Aqeedah 9

Azaab-e-qabr haq hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=114 -->

Isi tarah qabr ki ne'matein bhi haq hain. Azaab aur ne'mat dono badan aur rooh
par hote hain. Agar badan gal jaye, jal jaye ya khaak ho jaye, phir bhi us ke
asli ajza Qiyamat tak baqi rehte hain. Unhi par azaab ya sawab hoga aur Qiyamat
ke din unhi ajza se dobara badan banaya jayega.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=115 -->

usi pehle badan mein bhejega. Is ka naam Hashr hai. Qabr ke azaab aur aaraam ka
inkar wahi karega jo gumrah hai.

### Aqeedah 10

Murdah agar qabr mein dafn na kiya jaye, balke jahan pada rahe ya phenk diya
jaye, wahan hi us se sawal honge aur wahin sawab ya azaab milega. Yahan tak ke
agar kisi ko sher kha jaye to sher ke pait mein bhi sawal, sawab aur azaab hoga.

# Jannat ka Bayan

Jannat woh maqam hai jo Allah Ta'ala ne Iman walon ke liye banaya hai. Is mein
aisi ne'matein hain jinhein na aankhon ne dekha, na kaanon ne suna aur na kisi
insaan ke dil mein un ka khayal aaya. Duniya ki behtareen cheez bhi Jannat ke
muqable mein kuch nahi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=155 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 155]

Jannat ki roshni suraj ki roshni ko aise mita degi jaise suraj sitaron ki roshni
ko mita deta hai. Jannat mein jitni jagah mein ek koora rakha ja sake, woh duniya
aur duniya ki tamam cheezon se behtar hai. Jannat mein ek sau darje hain aur har
do darjon ke darmiyan bahut lamba faasla hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=156 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 156]

Jannat mein ek darakht hai jis ke saaye mein tez ghode par sawar ek sau saal
chalta rahe to bhi saaya khatam na ho. Jannat ke darwaze itne wasee honge ke ek
baazu se doosre baazu tak tez ghode ki sattar saal ki raah hogi. Phir bhi logon
ki kasrat ki wajah se darwaze par kandhe se kandha chhilega.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=157 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 157]

Jannat ki imaratein sone aur chandi ki eenton se bani hongi. Un ka gaarah
(eenton ko jorne wala lep) mushk
ka hoga, ghaas ki jagah Zafran, kankariyan moti aur mitti Amber ki hogi. Jannat
mein moti ka ek khaima hoga jis ki bulandi saath meel hogi. Wahan pani, doodh,
shahad aur sharab ke chaar darya honge.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=158 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 158]

Jannat ki nehrein har shakhs ke ghar mein jari hongi. Wahan phal hamesha maujood
rahengay aur unhein tor kar khana aasaan hoga. Agar kisi parinde ka gosht khane
ki khwahish hogi to foran bhuna hua gosht aa jayega. Pani ya doodh ki khwahish
hogi to bartan khud haath mein aa jayenge.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=159 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 159]

Jannat walon ko khane, peene aur nikah ki quwwat di jayegi. Un ki zaban se
Tasbeeh aur Takbeer har waqt saans ki tarah jari rahegi. Kam se kam har shakhs ke
sarhane das hazaar khadim khade honge. Har khadim ke ek haath mein chandi aur
doosre mein sone ka pyala hoga.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=160 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 160]

Jannat ke bartan itne saaf honge ke sharab un mein laal nazar aayegi, jaise
surkh sharab safed sheeshe mein nazar aati hai. Jannati apna chehra Jannat ki
hoor ke gaal mein aaine se zyada saaf dekhega. Jannat ki ne'matein duniya ki kisi ne'mat ke
barabar nahi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=161 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 161]

Jannat ka mard jab apni hoor ke paas jayega to har baar use kunwari payega. Is
se mard aur aurat dono ko koi takleef nahi hogi. Jannat ki hoor agar samandar
mein thook de to us ki mithaas se samandar meetha ho jayega.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=162 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 162]

Adna darje ke Jannati ke liye assi hazaar khadim aur bahattar biwiyan hongi.
Unhein aise taaj milenge jin ka sab se chhota moti mashriq se maghrib tak roshni
dega. Agar koi Jannati aulaad ki khwahish kare to hamal, paidaish aur bachche ki
poori umr palak jhapakne ki der mein mukammal ho jayegi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=163 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 163]

Jannat mein ek shakhs doosre ke liye rukawat nahi banega. Allah Ta'ala har ek par
apni tajalli farmayega aur farmayega: “Aye falaan bin falaan! Kya tumhein yaad hai
jis din tum ne yeh kiya tha?” Banda kahega: “Aye Rab! Kya Tu ne mujhe maaf nahi
kardiya tha?” Allah Ta'ala farmayega: “Haan, meri maghfirat ki wajah se tum yahan
ho.”

# Dozakh ka Bayan

Dozakh Allah Ta'ala ke jalal aur qahr ka izhar hai. Jis tarah Allah ki Rahmat aur
ne'mat ki koi had nahi, isi tarah us ke ghazab aur azaab ki bhi koi had nahi.
Insaan ki soch jahan tak pahunch sakti hai, woh Dozakh ke azaab ka sirf ek chhota
sa hissa hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=166 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 166]

Dozakh ka indhan insaan aur pathar honge. Duniya ki aag, Dozakh ki aag ke sattar
hisson mein se sirf ek hissa hai. Sab se kam azaab wale ko aag ke jootay pehnaye
jayenge; un se us ka dimaagh aise khaulega jaise taambe ki handi khaulti hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=167 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 167]

Dozakh mein roshni ka naam-o-nishan nahi hoga. Jibreel علیہ السلام ne Huzoor ﷺ
se qasam kha kar arz kiya ke agar Dozakh se sui ke naake jitna hissa khol diya
jaye to zameen ke sab log us ki garmi se mar jayen. Agar us ka daroga duniya ke
logon ke saamne aa jaye to sab us ki haibat se behosh ho jayen.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=168 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 168]

Dozakh kitni gehri hai, Allah hi jaanta hai. Hadith mein hai ke agar pathar ki
chattaan Dozakh ke kinare se phenki jaye to sattar saal mein bhi us ki tah tak
nahi pahunchegi. Dozakh ke darwaze aur us ki deewarein bahut sakht aur buland
hongi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=169 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 169]

Dozakh mein bade-bade bichchhu aur Allah hi jaane kitne bade saanp honge. Un ka
ek kaatna itna dard aur jalan paida karega jo hazaar saal tak rahegi. Dozakhiyon
ko bahut garam pani pilaya jayega. Munh ke qareeb aate hi us ki garmi se chehre
ki khaal gir jayegi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=170 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 170]

Woh pani pait mein ja kar aantain kaat dega. Jab woh peene wale ke pait se niklega
to us ki haddiyan tak gala dega. Agar us ka ek qatra duniya mein aa jaye to us ki
badboo aur jalan se duniya walon ka guzara mushkil ho jaye.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=171 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 171]

Dozakh ke kafir hazaar saal tak Allah Ta'ala ko us ki Rahmat ke naamon se pukarenge,
magar koi jawab nahi milega. Phir farmaya jayega: “Door ho jao aur Dozakh mein
pade raho. Mujh se baat na karo.” Is waqt kafir har bhalai se mayoos ho jayenge.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=172 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 172]

Kafir ka ek daant Uhud pahad ke barabar hoga. Us ki khaal ki motai bayalis gaz
hogi. Us ki zaban ek ya do kos tak munh se bahar nikli hogi aur log use rondenge.
Us ke baithne ki jagah Makkah se Madina tak jitni hogi. Dozakh mein us ka munh
khula rahega aur upar ka hont sikud kar sar tak pahunch jayega.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=173 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 173]

Kafir samjhega ke us ke siwa ab koi Dozakh mein nahi raha. Yeh ek azaab ke upar
doosra azaab hoga aur hamesha us par azaab rahega. Jab sab Jannati Jannat mein
aur hamesha Dozakh mein rehne wale Dozakh mein chale jayenge, to Jannat aur Dozakh
ke darmiyan Maut ko mendhe ki surat mein la kar khatam kar diya jayega.

# Iman aur Kufr ka Bayan

Iman yeh hai ke dil se Deen ki un tamam zaruri baaton ko sach maana jaye jo
Islam mein mashhoor hain. Deen ki kisi ek zaruri baat ka inkar Kufr hai, chahe
baqi tamam zaruri baaton ko sach maanta ho. Zaruri Deeni baatein woh hain jinhein
har khaas-o-aam jaanta ho, jaise Allah عزوجل ki Wahdaniyat, Nabuwat aur Qiyamat.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=175 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 175]

Kuch log aise hote hain jo Kalima bhi theek se nahi padh sakte. Un ka zaruri
Deeni baaton se anjaan hona in baaton ko ghair-zaruri nahi banata. Haan, un ke
Musalman hone ke liye zaruri hai ke woh Deen ki kisi zaruri baat ka inkar na karein
aur yaqeen rakhein ke Islam mein jo kuch hai sab haq hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=176 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 176]

Jo shakhs baghair kisi shar'i majboori ke Kalima-e-Kufr bole, woh Musalman nahi
reh sakta. Iman dil se aisi pakki tasdeeq ka naam hai jis ke khilaf gunjaish na
ho. Sirf zaban se iman ka da'wa karna aur dil mein inkar rakhna Iman nahi.

### Mas'alah 1

Agar kisi se ghalti se ya zabardasti Kalima-e-Kufr nikal jaye to hukm alag hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=177 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 177]

### Mas'alah 2

Haath-pair ke amal Iman ka hissa nahi, lekin kuch aise kaam hain jo Iman ke
bilkul khilaf hote hain. Jaise but, chand ya suraj ko sajdah karna, Nabi ko qatl
karna ya un ki tauheen karna, Quran-e-Pak ya Ka'bah Shareef ki tauheen karna.

### Mas'alah 3

Kuch kaam Kufr ki nishani hote hain. Jaise kamar mein Zunnar baandhna, sar par
choti rakhna ya maathe par tilak lagana. Aise kaam karne wale ko Fuqaha-e-Kiram kafir
kehte hain. Is liye us par dobara Islam lana aur apna nikah dobara karna zaruri
hota hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=178 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 178]

Kisi Deeni baat ka inkar Kufr hota hai jab woh Deen ki zaruri baaton mein se ho,
ya inkar karne wala is qat'i hukm se waqif ho.

Aqaid ki bunyadi baaton mein taqleed (sirf kisi alim ki baat maan lena) jaiz nahi.
In baaton par dil se pakka yaqeen zaruri hai. Yeh yaqeen daleel se ho ya kisi
aur sahi tareeqe se, dono theek hain. Is ke liye khas taur par daleel se ilm
hasil karna zaruri nahi. Haan, aqeede ki kuch furooi baaton mein taqleed jaiz hai.

### Mas'alah 1

Usool-e-Aqaid mein taqleed jaiz nahi. Iman pakke yaqeen ke saath hona chahiye.
Ahle-Sunnat mein do mashhoor jamaatein hain: Maturidiyyah,
jo Imam Abu Mansoor Maturidi رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ki pairwi karti hai, aur Ash'ariyyah,
jo Imam Abul Hasan Ash'ari رحمہ اللہ تعالی ki pairwi karti hai. Dono Ahle-Sunnat
aur haq par hain.

## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 180]

Maturidi aur Ash'ari ka ikhtilaf Hanafi aur Shafi'i ke ikhtilaf ki tarah hai.
Dono Ahle-Haq hain; koi kisi ko gumrah ya fasiq nahi keh sakta. Iman mein kami
ya ziyadati nahi hoti, kyun ke kami-beshi us cheez mein hoti hai jis ki koi
miqdaar, lambai, chaudai, motai ya ginti ho.
