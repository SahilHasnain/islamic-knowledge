### Ahle-Sunnat ke Do Imam

Maturidiyyah, Imam Abu Mansoor Maturidi رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ke pairo hain, aur
Ash'ariyyah, Imam Abul Hasan Ash'ari رحمہ اللہ تعالی ke pairo hain. Yeh dono
jamaatein Ahle-Sunnat hain aur dono haq par hain. In dono ke darmiyan kuch
furooi aqeede mein ikhtilaf hai, lekin yeh ikhtilaf unhein ek doosre ko gumrah
ya fasiq kehne ka haq nahi deta.

Imam Abu Mansoor Maturidi رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ Samarkand ke mashhoor Imam,
Imam-ul-Mutakallimeen aur Imam-ul-Huda the. Unhon ne Musalmanon ke aqeedon ko
samjhane aur batil aqeedon ka rad karne par kai kitabein likhin, jaise
`Kitab-ut-Tauheed`, `Kitab-ul-Maqalaat`, `Kitab Radd Dalail-ul-Ka'bi` aur
`Taweelaat-ul-Quran`. Un ka wisal 333 Hijri mein hua aur un ka mazar Samarkand
mein hai.

Imam Abul Hasan Ash'ari رحمہ اللہ تعالی ka nasab Sahabi-e-Rasool Hazrat Abu
Moosa Ash'ari رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ tak pahunchta hai. Woh Ahle-Sunnat ke aksar
Mutakallimeen ke Imam the. Un ke pairo ko Ash'ariyyah kaha jata hai. Un ki
kitabon mein `Al-Fusool fir-Radd alal Mulhideen`, `Ar-Radd alal Mujassamah` aur
`Maqalaat-ul-Islamiyyeen` mashhoor hain.

Maturidiyyah aur Ash'ariyyah ka ikhtilaf Hanafi aur Shafi'i ke ikhtilaf jaisa
hai. Dono Ahle-Haq hain aur koi kisi ko gumrah ya fasiq nahi keh sakta.

### Mas'alah 2

Iman mein kami ya ziyadati nahi hoti, kyun ke kami-beshi us cheez mein hoti hai
jis ki miqdaar, lambai, chaudai, motai ya ginti ho. Iman dil ki tasdeeq aur
yaqeen ki ek khaas kaifiyat ka naam hai.

Quran ki baaz ayaat mein Iman ke badhne ka jo zikr hai, us se murad un cheezon
ka badhna hai jin par Iman laya gaya. Quran ke nazil hone ke zamane mein naye
ahkaam aate rahe aur har naye hukm par Iman lana zaruri hota raha. Is se yeh
murad nahi ke khud Iman ki haqeeqat badhti ya ghatti rahi. Haan, Iman ki mazbooti
aur kamzori ho sakti hai, kyun ke yeh dil ki kaifiyat se muta'alliq hai.

logon ke majmue ke Iman par bhaari hai.

### Aqeedah 4

Iman aur Kufr ke darmiyan koi teesri halat nahi. Aadmi ya Musalman hoga ya
kafir. Aisi koi teesri surat nahi ke woh na Musalman ho na kafir.

### Nifaq

Zaban se Islam ka da'wa karna aur dil mein Islam ko na maanna bhi khaalis Kufr
hai. Aise log Jahannam ke sab se neeche wale hisse mein honge. Huzoor ﷺ ke
zamane mein kuch log apne dil ke Kufr ki wajah se Munafiq ke naam se mashhoor
the. Quran ne un ke baatini Kufr ki khabar di aur Huzoor ﷺ ne apne wasee' ilm
se un mein se baaz ko pehchan kar Munafiq farmaya.

Ab kisi khaas shakhs ko yaqini taur par Munafiq nahi kaha ja sakta. Jo hamare
saamne Islam ka da'wa kare, hum use Musalman hi samjhenge jab tak us se Iman ke
khilaf koi baat ya kaam zahir na ho. Is zamane mein Nifaq ki ek shakh yeh bhi
hai ke kuch bad-mazhab apne aap ko Musalman kehte hain, magar Deen ki zaruri
baaton ka inkar karte hain.

### Aqeedah 5

Shirk ka matlab Allah ke ilawa kisi ko Wajib-ul-Wujood ya Ibadat ka mustahiq
maanna hai, yani Allah ki Uluhiyyat mein kisi doosre ko shareek maanna. Yeh Kufr
ki sab se badtareen qism hai. Is ke ilawa har sakht Kufr haqeeqi ma'ni mein Shirk
nahi hota.

Isi liye Shari'at ne Ahle-Kitab kafiron ke ahkaam Mushrikon se alag rakhe hain.
Ahle-Kitab ka zabiha khaya ja sakta hai aur un ki paak-daaman auraton se nikah
ho sakta hai, lekin Mushrik ka zabiha halal nahi aur Mushrik aurat se nikah jaiz
nahi.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=186 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 186]

Kabhi Shirk ka lafz aam Kufr ke ma'ni mein bhi bola jata hai. Quran mein jo
farmaya gaya hai ke “Allah Shirk ko nahi bakhshega,” is se murad yeh hai ke jo
shakhs Kufr par mare, us ka Kufr maaf nahi hoga. Baqi gunah Allah Ta'ala ki
marzi par hain; jis ko chahe maaf farmaye.

### Aqeedah 6

Bada gunah karne wala Musalman rehta hai aur Jannat mein jayega. Allah Ta'ala
apne fazl se us ki maghfirat farma sakta hai, Huzoor ﷺ ki Shafa'at se maaf kar
sakta hai, ya kuch saza dene ke baad Jannat mein dakhil farmayega. Woh hamesha
Dozakh mein nahi rahega.

### Mas'alah

Kisi kafir ke liye us ki maut ke baad maghfirat ki dua karna, kisi murtad ko
`marhoom` ya `maghfoor` kehna, ya kisi mare hue Hindu ko “Jannati” kehna Kufr hai.

### Aqeedah 7

Musalman ko Musalman aur kafir ko kafir maanna Deen ki zaruri baaton mein se hai.
Lekin kisi khaas shakhs ke bare mein yeh yaqeen nahi kiya ja sakta ke woh Iman
ya Kufr par mara, jab tak us ke anjaam ka haal shar'i daleel se sabit na ho.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=188 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 188]

Kisi zahiri kafir ke anjaam ka yaqini ilm Allah Ta'ala ko hai, lekin Shari'at ka
zahir hukm yeh hai ke use kafir hi jaana jaye. Us ki zindagi aur maut ke baad
usi ke saath woh muamlaat kiye jayen jo kafiron ke liye hain, jaise nikah,
Janazah, kafan aur dafn.

Jo shakhs zahiri taur par Musalman ho aur us se Iman ke khilaf koi baat ya kaam
zahir na ho, use Musalman hi maana jayega. Us ke anjaam ka ilm bhi Allah Ta'ala
ke supurd hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=189 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 189]

Kafir ko kafir kehna gaali nahi, balke Shari'at ke hukm ko bayan karna hai. Haan,
Islamic hukumat mein rehne wale Zimmi ko agar “Aye kafir!” kehna use bura lage to
aisa kehna mana hai. Kafir ko kafir na maanna ya us ke Kufr mein shak karna
durust nahi.

Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya ke Ummat tehattar firqon mein bat jayegi. Un mein ek firqa
Jannati hoga aur baqi Jahannami.

Sahaba ne arz ki: “Ya Rasool Allah! Najaat paane wala firqa kaun hai?” Huzoor ﷺ
ne farmaya:

ما انا علیہ واصحابي

“Woh jis par main aur mere Sahaba hain,” yani Sunnat ke pairo. Ek riwayat mein
hai:

ھم الجماعۃ

“Woh Jama'at hai,” yani Musalmanon ka bada giroh, jise Sawad-e-Azam kaha gaya.
Isi wajah se is Najaat paane wale firqe ka naam Ahle-Sunnat-o-Jama'at hai.

na aayein. Huzoor ﷺ ne aise logon se bachne ki hidayat farmayi hai. Jo firqe
Hindustan mein maujood hain, un ke aqeedon ka mukhtasar bayan kiya jata hai.

### Qadiyani

Qadiyani Mirza Ghulam Ahmad Qadiyani ke pairo hain. Is shakhs ne apni Nabuwat ka
da'wa kiya aur Ambiya-e-Kiram علیہم السلام, khaas taur par Hazrat Isa Roohullah
aur Kalimatullah علیہ السلام aur un ki paak walida ki shaan mein be-dharki se
gustakhiyan ki hain.

Us ne Khatm-e-Nabuwat ka inkar kiya aur apne liye Nabuwat ka da'wa kiya. Yeh
aqeedah Islam ke bilkul khilaf hai.

`ummati` bhi aur `Nabi` bhi kaha. Us ne apni kitabon ke hawale se apne liye
aisi baatein likhin jo Khatm-e-Nabuwat ko todti hain.

### Rafizi

Rafizi firqe ki tafseel dekhne ke liye `Tohfa-e-Isna Ashariyyah` kitab padhi ja
sakti hai. Yahan is firqe ki kuch baatein bayan ki jati hain. Rafizi Sahaba-e-
Kiram رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم ki shaan mein sakht gustakhi karte hain aur unhein
gaali dena un ka aam tareeqa hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=1 | page_number=208 -->
## [Jild 1 | Section 1 | Page 208]

Rafizi chand Sahaba ke ilawa aksar Sahaba-e-Kiram رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم ko,
ma'az Allah, kafir aur Munafiq qarar dete hain. Woh Hazrat Abu Bakr, Hazrat Umar
aur Hazrat Usman رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم ki Khilafat-e-Rashidah ko ghasibana
Khilafat kehte hain.

### Wahabi

Wahabi ek naya firqa hai jo 1209 Hijri mein zahir hua. Is ka baani Muhammad bin
Abdul Wahab Najdi tha. Us ne Arab, khaas taur par Haramayn Shareefayn mein sakht
fitne phailaye, Ulama ko qatl kiya aur Sahaba, Aimmah, Ulama aur Shuhada ki
qabrein khod dalin. Us ne Roza-e-Anwar ka naam, ma'az Allah, `Sanam-e-Akbar`
(bada but) rakha.

### Ghair Muqallideen

Ghair Muqallideen bhi Wahabiyat ki ek shakh hain. Wahabiyon ki baaz nayi aur
sakht baatein un se sabit nahi, lekin baqi aqeedon mein dono shareek hain. Woh
Wahabiyon ke bad-mazhab buzurgon ko kafir nahi maante, halanke un ke Kufr mein
shak karna bhi khatarnak hai.

Un ka ek alag aqeedah yeh hai ke woh chaaron fiqhi mazhabon se alag raasta
banate hain, Taqleed ko haram aur Bid'at kehte hain aur Aimmah-e-Deen ko bura
kehte hain. Woh asal mein kisi Imam ki Taqleed nahi karte, magar Shaytan ki
pairwi zaroor karte hain. Woh Qiyas ka bhi inkar karte hain, halanke Qiyas ka
bilkul inkar Kufr hai aur Taqleed ka bilkul inkar bhi Kufr hai.

### Mas'alah

Kisi na kisi surat mein Taqleed karna farz hai aur kisi khaas Imam ki Taqleed
karna wajib hai.

# Imamat ka Bayan

Imamat do qism ki hoti hai: Sughra aur Kubra. Imamat-e-Sughra namaz ki Imamat
hai, jis ka bayan Kitab-us-Salah mein aayega. Imamat-e-Kubra Huzoor ﷺ ki
Niyabat-e-Mutlaqah hai: jo Imam Huzoor ﷺ ki Niyabat mein Musalmanon ke Deeni aur
dunyawi kaamon mein Shari'at ke mutabiq aam tasarruf ka ikhtiyar rakhta ho aur
ghair-ma'siyat kaam mein us ki pairwi duniya bhar ke Musalmanon par farz ho.

Is Imam ke liye Musalman, azaad, aqalmand, baligh, qudrat rakhne wala aur Qurayshi
hona shart hai. Hashimi, Alawi ya ma'soom hona shart nahi. Inhein shart maanna
Rafiziyon ka aqeedah hai, taake Khulafa-e-Salasa ki Khilafat ko na maana ja sake.
