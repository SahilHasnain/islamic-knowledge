# Taharat ke Masail ka Bayan

Yeh Bahar-e-Shariat, Hissa Doem hai. Is mein aasaan ibarat aur takhreej shamil
hai. Is ke musannif Sadr-ush-Shari'ah Badr-ut-Tariqah Hazrat Allamah Maulana
Mufti Muhammad Amjad Ali A'zami علیہ رحمۃ اللہ الغنی hain. Isay Majlis Al-Madinah
Al-Ilmiyyah, Dawat-e-Islami, Shoba-e-Takhreej ne pesh kiya hai aur Maktabah
Al-Madinah, Bab-ul-Madinah Karachi ne shaya kiya hai.

بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم

Tamam hamd Allah ke liye hai. Woh Wahid, Ahad aur Samad hai. Woh apni Zaat aur
Sifaat mein yaktah hai. Na us ki koi misaal hai, na koi muqabil aur na koi us ka
humsar.

Durood-o-salam ho Allah ke Rasool aur Mehboob ﷺ par. Aap ﷺ insaanon aur jinnon
ke Sardar hain. Aap ﷺ par Quran nazil hua, jo logon ke liye hidayat aur hidayat
ki roshan daleelein hai. Aap ﷺ ki Aal aur Sahaba par bhi durood-o-salam ho. Un
logon par bhi ho jo Qiyamat tak achchai ke saath un ki pairwi karein.

Khaas taur par Mujtahid Imamon par, aur un mein sab se afzal Imam-e-Azam Abu
Hanifah Nu'man bin Sabit رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ par durood ho. Allah Ta'ala hamein
duniya aur Aakhirat mein un ke zariye qaul-e-sabit par qayam rakhe. Hamein achchai
aur apna mazeed fazl ata farmaye, aur hum par un ke zariye rahm farmaye. Tamam
hamd Allah ke liye hai jo tamam jahanon ka Rab hai.

## Tamheed

Ek zamane mein har Musalman apni zaruriyat jitna ilm rakhta tha. Allah ke fazl se
ulama bhi bahut the. Jo baat maloom na hoti, log ulama se aasani se pooch lete.
Hazrat Umar Farooq رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ne yahan tak hukm diya tha ke hamare bazaar
mein wahi khareed-o-farokht kare jo deen ka faqeeh ho.

Jitna zamana-e-Nabuwat se fasla badhta gaya, utna hi ilm kam hota gaya. Ab awam
to awam, bahut se ulama kehlane wale log bhi roz ke zaruri masail, hatta ke Faraiz
aur Wajibat se na-waqif hain. Jo kuch jaante hain, us par bhi amal nahi karte.

In ki wajah se awam ko seekhne aur amal karne ka mauqa nahi milta. Kam-ilmi aur
be-parwahi ka yeh natija hai ke log un masail ka inkar kar dete hain jinhein woh
nahi jaante. Na woh khud ilm rakhte hain, na ilm walon se poochne ka shauq, aur na
ulama ki sohbat mein hazir hote hain. Halanke ulama ki sohbat barkat bhi hai aur
masail seekhne ka zariya bhi.

Urdu mein ab tak kam padhe-likhe logon ke liye aisi aasaan, aam-fahm aur qabil-e-
aitimad kitab nahi thi. Is liye Musalmanon ki khair-khwahi ke liye Allah par
bharosa karke is aham kaam ki taraf mutawajjah hua.

Main jaanta hoon ke na mera yeh mansab hai, na main is kaam ke laiq hoon, aur na
mujhe itni fursat hai ke poora waqt de kar ise mukammal kar sakoon. Allah hamare
liye kaafi hai aur wahi behtareen kaarsaaz hai. Allah ke siwa kisi mein taqat aur
quwwat nahi.

Is kitab mein poori koshish hogi ke ibarat aasaan ho. Kam-ilm log, auratein aur
bachche bhi faida utha saken. Phir bhi ilm mushkil hai; har mushkil bilkul khatam
nahi ho sakti. Baaz maqamat par Ahl-e-Ilm se samajhna zaruri rahega. Lekin kitab
ka bayan unhein mutanabbah karega aur jo baat samajh na aaye, us par samajhne
walon ki taraf ruju' karne ki tawajjuh dilayega.

Is kitab mein masail ki daleelein nahi likhi jayengi. Daleelein samajhna har shakhs
ka kaam nahi. Aur daleelein kabhi kabhi khud mas'ala samajhna mushkil bana deti
hain. Is liye har mas'ale ka saaf aur chhanta hua hukm diya jayega. Jise daleelon
ka shauq ho woh Fatawa Razawiyyah Shareef padhe. Us mein har mas'ale ki aisi
tahqeeq hai jis ki misaal aaj duniya mein maujood nahi.

Is kitab mein mumkin had tak ikhtilafat nahi bataye jayenge. Awam ke samne do
mukhtalif baatein aayen to woh pareshan ho jate hain ke kis par amal karein.
Baaz log apne faide wali baat sirf khwahish ki wajah se le lete hain. Yeh Shari'at
ki pairwi nahi, nafs ki pairwi hai. Is liye har mas'ale mein Mufti-bihi, sahih,
asah aur rajih qaul diya jayega, taake har shakhs aasani se amal kare.

Allah Ta'ala taufeeq de, Musalmanon ko is se faida pahunchaye aur is be-samaan
koshish ko qabool farmaye. Main Maalik-e-Ma'bood ki taufeeq se maqsad shuru karta
hoon. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

و ما خلقت الجن و الانس الا لیعبدون

“Jinn aur aadmi, main ne unhein isi liye paida kiya ke woh meri ibadat karein.”

Hawala: Surah Adh-Dhariyat, para 27, ayat 56.

Har thodi aqal wala jaanta hai ke jis cheez ko jis kaam ke liye banaya jaye, agar
woh us kaam mein na aaye to bekaar hai. Isi tarah jo insaan apne Khaliq-o-Maalik
ko na pehchane aur us ki ibadat na kare, woh sirf naam ka aadmi hai. Haqeeqat mein
woh aadmi nahi, balki bekaar cheez hai.

Ibadat hi se insaan haqeeqi insaan banta hai. Duniya ki falah aur Aakhirat ki
nijat bhi ibadat hi se milti hai. Is liye har insaan ko ibadat ki qismein, Arkaan,
sharait aur ahkaam jaanna zaruri hai. Be-ilmi ke saath amal namumkin hai. Isi
wajah se ilm seekhna farz hai.

Ibadat ki bunyaad Iman hai. Iman ke baghair ibadat bekaar hai. Jaise darakht ki
jarr na ho to phal nahi aata, waise hi Iman ke baghair amal ka natija nahi hota.
Kafir bahut ibadat kare tab bhi us ka sara amal barbaad hai aur woh Jahannam ka
indhan hai.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

و قدمنا الى ما عملوا من عمل فجعلنه هبآء منثورا

“Aur kafiron ne jo kuch kiya, hum us ki taraf mutawajjah hue to use bikhre hue
zarron ki tarah kar diya.” Hawala: Surah Al-Furqan, para 19, ayat 23.

Musalman hone ke baad do qism ki ibadatein farz hoti hain. Ek ka ta'alluq badan
ke aaza se hai aur doosri ka dil se. Dil wali ibadat ke ahkaam ilm-e-Suluk mein
bayan hote hain. Badan wali ibadat ke ahkaam Fiqh mein bayan hote hain. Is kitab
mein badan wali ibadat bayan ki jayegi.

Badan se muta'alliq ibadat do qism ki hoti hai. Pehli woh jo bande aur us ke Rab
ke darmiyan hoti hai, jaise paanch waqt ki namaz aur roza. Doosri woh jis mein
bandon ke aapas ke ta'alluqaat ki islaah maqsood hoti hai, jaise nikah aur
khareed-o-farokht. Pehli ko Ibadat aur doosri ko Mu'amalat kehte hain.

Mu'amalat mein agar Allah aur Rasool ke hukm ke mutabiq amal kiya jaye to sawab
milta hai. Warna gunah aur azaab hota hai. Ibadat ki chaar qismein hain: Namaz,
Roza, Hajj aur Zakat. In mein Namaz sab se aham hai aur Allah Ta'ala ko bahut
mehboob hai. Namaz se pehle Taharat zaruri hai, kyun ke Taharat Namaz ki kunji
hai. Is liye pehle Taharat aur phir Namaz ke masail bayan honge.

# Kitab-ut-Taharat

Namaz ke liye Taharat zaruri hai. Taharat ke baghair Namaz hoti hi nahi. Jaan
boojh kar be-Taharat Namaz padhne ko ulama ne Kufr likha hai, kyun ke be-Wuzu ya
be-Ghusl Namaz padhne wala ibadat ki be-adabi aur tauheen karta hai.

Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jannat ki kunji Namaz hai aur Namaz ki kunji Taharat hai.”

Hazrat Jabir رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ se riwayat hai ke ek din Huzoor ﷺ Fajr ki Namaz
mein Surah Rum padh rahe the aur Qira'at mein shubha hua. Namaz ke baad farmaya:
“Un logon ka kya haal hai jo hamare saath Namaz padhte hain, magar achhi tarah
Taharat nahi karte? Un ki wajah se Imam ko Qira'at mein shubha padta hai.”

Jab mukammal Taharat ke baghair Namaz padhne ka yeh anjaam hai to bilkul be-Taharat
Namaz padhne ki nahusat ka kya kehna. Ek Hadith mein farmaya: “Taharat aadha Iman
hai.” Imam Tirmidhi ne is Hadith ko riwayat karke Hasan kaha hai.

Taharat ki do qismein hain:

1. Taharat-e-Sughra, yani Wuzu.
2. Taharat-e-Kubra, yani Ghusl.

Jin cheezon se sirf Wuzu lazim hota hai unhein Hadath-e-Asghar kehte hain. Jin
cheezon se Ghusl farz hota hai unhein Hadath-e-Akbar kehte hain. In sab ke aur
un se muta'alliq ahkaam ka tafseeli bayan aage hoga.

## Zaruri istilahat

**Farz-e-I'tiqadi:** Jo baat qat'i daleel se sabit ho, yani aisi daleel se jis
mein koi shubha na ho. Is ka inkar karne wala A'immah-e-Hanafiyyah ke nazdeek
bilkul kafir hai. Agar kisi farz ka deen mein aam aur bilkul wazeh hona bhi sabit
ho, to us ke munkir ke Kufr par qat'i ijma' hai. Jo shakhs is munkir ke Kufr mein
shak kare, woh khud kafir hai.

Jo shakhs kisi Farz-e-I'tiqadi ko baghair sahih Shar'i uzr ke jaan boojh kar ek
baar bhi chhor de, woh fasiq, gunah-e-kabirah ka murtakib aur azaab-e-Naar ka
mustahiq hai. Namaz mein Ruku' aur Sajdah is ki misaal hain.

**Farz-e-Amali:** Jis ka saboot qat'i na ho, lekin Mujtahid ke nazdeek Shar'i
daleelon ki bunyaad par yeh yaqeen ho ke us ke baghair aadmi zimmah se bari nahi
hoga. Agar woh kisi ibadat ka farz ho to us ke baghair woh ibadat batil hoti hai.
Is ka baghair uzr inkar fisq aur gumrahi hai.

Hawalaat: Al-Musnad lil-Imam Ahmad, Musnad Jabir, Hadith 14668; Sunan an-Nasa'i,
Hadith 944; Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3528.

Farz-e-Amali ka baghair uzr inkar karna fisq aur gumrahi hai. Haan, jo shakhs
Shar'i daleelon par ghour karne ka Ahl ho aur Shar'i daleel ki wajah se inkar kare,
woh aisa kar sakta hai.

Misaal ke taur par, Mujtahid Imamon ka ikhtilaf hai. Hanafiyon ke nazdeek Wuzu
mein sar ke chauthai hisse ka masah farz hai. Shafi'iyon ke nazdeek ek baal ka
masah farz hai aur Malikiyon ke nazdeek poore sar ka masah farz hai. Hanafiyon ke
nazdeek Wuzu mein Bismillah kehna aur niyyat Sunnat hai, jab ke Hanbaliyon aur
Shafi'iyon ke nazdeek farz hai.

Farz-e-Amali mein har shakhs usi Imam ki pairwi kare jis ka woh muqallid hai.
Baghair Shar'i zarurat ke apne Imam ke khilaf kisi doosre Imam ki pairwi jaiz nahi.

**Wajib-e-I'tiqadi:** Jis ki zarurat zaanni daleel se sabit ho.

**Wajib-e-Amali:** Jis ke bare mein yeh imkaan ho ke us ke baghair zimmah bari ho
jaye, lekin us ki zarurat ka ghalib gumaan ho. Agar kisi ibadat mein Wajib ho to
us ke baghair ibadat naaqis hoti hai, lekin ada ho jati hai. Mujtahid Shar'i daleel
ki bunyaad par Wajib ka inkar kar sakta hai. Wajib ko jaan boojh kar ek baar chhorna
gunah-e-saghirah aur kai baar chhorna gunah-e-kabirah hai.

**Sunnat-e-Mu'akkadah:** Jise Huzoor ﷺ ne hamesha kiya ho, lekin bayan-e-jawaz
ke liye kabhi tark bhi farmaya ho; ya jise karne ki takeed farmayi ho, magar tark
ka raasta bilkul band na farmaya ho. Is ka tark Isaa'at hai, karna sawab hai,
kabhi tark par itab aur aadat bana lene par azaab ka haqdar hota hai.

**Sunnat-e-Ghair Mu'akkadah:** Jise Shari'at ne pasand kiya ho, magar tark par
azaab ki wa'eed na ho. Is ka karna sawab hai. Aadat ke taur par na karne par itab
nahi.

**Mustahab:** Jise Shari'at ne pasand kiya ho, lekin tark par koi na-pasandgi na
ho. Is ka karna sawab hai aur na karne par kuch nahi.

**Mubah:** Jis ka karna aur na karna barabar ho.

**Haram-e-Qat'i:** Jise jaan boojh kar ek baar bhi karna gunah-e-kabirah aur fisq
hai. Is se bachna farz aur sawab hai.

**Makrooh-e-Tahrimi:** Yeh Wajib ke muqabil hai. Is ke karne se ibadat naaqis hoti
hai aur karne wala gunahgar hota hai. Is ka gunah Haram se kam hai, lekin kai baar
karna gunah-e-kabirah hai.

<!-- source_table=volume_one | jild=1 | section=2 | page_number=287 -->

**Isaa'at:** Jis ka karna bura ho. Kabhi karne wala itab ka mustahiq aur hamesha
karne wala azaab ka mustahiq hota hai. Yeh Sunnat-e-Mu'akkadah ke muqabil hai.

**Makrooh-e-Tanzeehi:** Jise karna Shari'at ko pasand na ho, magar azaab ki wa'eed
na ho. Yeh Sunnat-e-Ghair Mu'akkadah ke muqabil hai.

**Khilaf-e-Ula:** Jise na karna behtar ho. Karne par na haraj hai na itab. Yeh
Mustahab ke muqabil hai. In istilahon ki ibaratein mukhtalif milti hain, lekin
tahqeeq ka nichor yahi hai.

# Wuzu ka Bayan

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

یایها الذین امنوا اذا قمتم الى الصلوة فاغسلوا وجوهكم و ایدیكم الى المرافق و امسحوا برءوسكم و ارجلكم الى الكعبین

“Aye Iman walo! Jab tum Namaz ka irada karo aur Wuzu na ho to apne munh aur
kahniyon tak haath dho lo, sar ka masah karo aur takhnon tak paon dho lo.”

Wuzu ki fazilat mein pehle kuch Ahadith bayan karna munasib hai.

**Hadith 1:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din meri Ummat is halat mein
bulayi jayegi ke un ke munh, haath aur paon Wuzu ke asar se chamak rahe honge.
To jis se ho sake, woh apni chamak zyada kare.”

**Hadith 2:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Kya main tumhein aisi cheez na bata doon jis
ke sabab Allah Ta'ala khataein mita de aur darajaat buland kare? Sahaba ne arz ki:
‘Ya Rasoolallah! Zarur batayein.’ Farmaya: Jab Wuzu nagawar hone ke waqt mukammal
Wuzu karna, Masjidon ki taraf zyada qadam chalna aur ek namaz ke baad doosri
namaz ka intezar karna. Is ka sawab aisa hai jaise kafiron ki sarhad par Islam ki
himayat ke liye ghora baandhna.”

Hawala: Surah Al-Ma'idah, para 6, ayat 6.

Hawalaat: Sahih al-Bukhari, Kitab-ul-Wuzu, Hadith 136; Sahih Muslim, Kitab-ut-
Taharat, Hadith 251.

**Hadith 3:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Musalman banda jab Wuzu karta hai to kulli
karne se munh ke gunah gir jate hain. Naak mein paani daal kar saaf kare to naak
ke gunah nikal jate hain. Munh dhoye to chehre ke gunah, hatta ke palkon se nikal
jate hain. Haath dhoye to haathon ke gunah, hatta ke naakhunon se nikal jate hain.
Sar ka masah kare to sar ke gunah, hatta ke kaanon se nikal jate hain. Paon dhoye
to paon ki khataein, hatta ke naakhunon se nikal jati hain. Phir us ka Masjid jana
aur namaz mazeed sawab hai.”

**Hadith 4:** Hazrat Usman Ghani رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ne sardi ki raat Wuzu ke liye
paani mangwaya. Un ke ghulam ne arz kiya ke raat bahut thandi hai. Hazrat Usman
رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ ne farmaya: “Main ne Rasoolullah ﷺ se suna hai: Jo banda
mukammal Wuzu karta hai, Allah Ta'ala us ke agle aur pichhle gunah bakhsh deta
hai.”

**Hadith 5:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo sakht sardi mein mukammal Wuzu kare, us
ke liye doguna sawab hai.”

**Hadith 6:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo ek ek baar Wuzu kare, yeh zaruri amal hai;
jo do do baar kare, us ko doguna sawab hai; aur jo teen teen baar dhoye, yeh mera
aur pehle Ambiya ka Wuzu hai.”

**Hadith 7:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo Musalman Wuzu kare, achha Wuzu kare, phir
khada ho kar zahir aur baatin se mutawajjah hokar do rak'at padhe, us ke liye
Jannat wajib hoti hai.”

Hawalaat: Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 103; Musnad al-Bazzar, Hadith 422;
Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat, Hadith 5366; Musnad Ahmad, Hadith 5739; Sahih Muslim,
Hadith 234.

**Hadith 8:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Tum mein jo Wuzu kare aur mukammal Wuzu kare,
phir padhe: ‘Main gawahi deta hoon ke Allah ke siwa koi Ma'bood nahi, woh akela
hai, us ka koi shareek nahi, aur main gawahi deta hoon ke Muhammad ﷺ us ke bande
aur Rasool hain,’ us ke liye Jannat ke aath darwaze khol diye jate hain; jis darwaze
se chahe dakhil ho.”

**Hadith 9:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs ek Wuzu par doosra Wuzu kare, us
ke liye das nekiyan likhi jati hain.”

**Hadith 10:** Huzoor ﷺ ne Hazrat Bilal رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ se farmaya: “Aye Bilal!
Tum kis amal ki wajah se Jannat mein mujh se aage aage ja rahe the? Main raat
Jannat mein gaya to tumhare qadmon ki aahat apne aage suni.” Hazrat Bilal رضی
اللہ تعالی عنہ ne arz ki: “Ya Rasoolallah! Jab bhi main azaan ke baad do rak'at
padhta aur jab mera Wuzu toot jata to Wuzu kar leta.” Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Isi
ki wajah se.”

**Hadith 11:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Bismillah na padhi, us ka Wuzu nahi,”
yani mukammal Wuzu nahi. Doosri Hadith mein is ki tafseel hai.

**Hadith 12:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Bismillah keh kar Wuzu kiya, sar se
paon tak us ka sara badan paak ho gaya, aur jis ne Bismillah ke baghair Wuzu kiya,
utna hi badan paak hua jitne par paani guzra.”

**Hadith 13:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jab koi neend se bedar ho to Wuzu kare aur
teen martaba naak saaf kare, kyun ke Shaitan us ke nathne par raat guzarta hai.”

Hawalaat: Sahih Muslim, Hadith 234; Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 61; Sahih Ibn
Khuzaymah, Hadith 1209; Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 398; Sunan ad-Daraqutni,
Hadith 228; Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3295.

**Hadith 14:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Agar yeh baat na hoti ke meri Ummat par
mushkil hoga to main unhein har Wuzu ke saath Miswak karne ka hukm deta,” yani
use farz kar deta. Baaz riwayaton mein farz ka lafz bhi aaya hai.

**Hadith 15:** Huzoor ﷺ kisi namaz ke liye tashreef na le jate jab tak Miswak na
farma lete.

**Hadith 16:** Hazrat Aishah رضی اللہ تعالی عنہا se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ jab
bahar se ghar mein tashreef late to sab se pehle Miswak farmate.

**Hadith 17:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Miswak ko lazim pakdo, kyun ke yeh munh ki
safai aur Rab Ta'ala ki raza ka sabab hai.”

**Hadith 18:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Miswak karke padhi hui do rak'atein, baghair
Miswak padhi hui sattar rak'aton se afzal hain.”

**Hadith 19:** Ek riwayat mein hai: “Miswak karke padhi hui namaz, baghair Miswak
padhi hui namaz se sattar darje afzal hai.”

**Hadith 20:** Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Das cheezein fitrat se hain, yani har Shari'at
mein un ka hukm tha: moonchhein katarna, daadhi badhana, Miswak karna, naak mein
paani daalna, naakhun tarashna, ungliyon ke jor dhona, baghal ke baal door karna,
zer-e-naaf baal mundna, Istinja karna aur kulli karna.”

**Hadith 21:** Hazrat Ali رضی اللہ تعالی عنہ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne Miswak
ki fazilat bayan farmayi.
