## Paani ka Bayan

Allah عزوجل farmata hai: { و انزلنا من السمآء مآء طهورا }
Yani: “Aur hum ne aasman se paak karne wala paani utara.” Aur farmata hai:
{ و ینزل علیكم من السمآء مآء لیطهركم به و یذهب عنكم رجز الشیطن }
Yani: “Aur tum par aasman se paani utarta hai, taake tumhein us se paak kare aur
Shaitan ki gandagi tum se door kare.”

**Hadith 1:** Imam Muslim ne Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی الله تعالی عنہ se riwayat ki
ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Tum mein se koi Junub thehre hue paani mein na nahaye.”
Yani thode paani mein, jo das haath by das haath na ho. Logon ne poocha: “Aye Abu
Hurairah, phir kaise kare?” Unhone kaha: “Us mein se paani nikaal le.”

**Hadith 2:** Sunan Abi Dawud, Tirmizi aur Ibn Majah mein Hakim bin Amr رضی الله
تعالی عنہ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne mana farmaya ke mard aurat ki
Taharat ke baad bacha hua paani se Wuzu kare.

**Hadith 3:** Imam Malik, Abu Dawud aur Tirmizi ne Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی الله
تعالی عنہ se riwayat ki ke ek shakhs ne Rasoolullah ﷺ se poocha: “Hum samandar
ka safar karte hain aur thoda paani saath le jate hain. Agar us se Wuzu karein to
pyaase reh jayenge. Kya samandar ke paani se Wuzu karein?” Farmaya: “Us ka paani
paak hai aur us ka mara hua jaanwar halal hai.” Yani machhli.

Hawalaat: Surah Al-Furqan 48; Surah Al-Anfal 11; Sahih Muslim, Hadith 283;
Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 82.

## Kis Paani Se Wuzu Jaiz Hai

Jis paani se Wuzu jaiz hai us se Ghusl bhi jaiz hai, aur jis se Wuzu na-jaiz hai
us se Ghusl bhi na-jaiz hai.

**Mas'alah 1:** Baarish, nadi, naale, chashme, samandar, darya, kuaan, barf aur
ole ke paani se Wuzu jaiz hai.

**Mas'alah 2:** Paani mein koi cheez mil kar itni badal jaye ke aam bol-chal mein
use paani na kaha jaye, balki koi doosra naam ho, jaise Sharbat. Ya paani mein
aisi cheez pakaayi jaye jis ka maqsad mail kaatna na ho, jaise Shorba, Chai,
Gulab ya koi aur Arq, to us se Wuzu aur Ghusl jaiz nahi.

**Mas'alah 3:** Agar aisi cheez milayi ya pakaayi jaye jis ka maqsad mail kaatna
ho, jaise Sabun ya Beri ke patte, to jab tak paani ki rawani khatam na ho Wuzu
jaiz hai. Agar Sattu ki tarah gaadha ho jaye to Wuzu jaiz nahi.

**Mas'alah 4:** Agar koi paak cheez milne se paani ke rang, boo ya zaiqe mein farq
aa jaye, lekin patlapan baqi rahe, jaise Reta, Chuna ya thodi Zafran, to Wuzu
jaiz hai. Zafran ka rang itna ghalib ho jaye ke kapda rangne ke kaam aaye to Wuzu
jaiz nahi. Isi tarah Pudiya ka rang bhi. Agar itna Doodh mile ke doodh ka rang
ghalib na ho to Wuzu jaiz hai, warna nahi. Ghalib aur maghloob ki pehchan yeh hai
ke use “paani jis mein doodh mila hai” kaha jaye to Wuzu jaiz hai, aur “Lassi”
kaha jaye to jaiz nahi. Patte girne ya purane hone se paani badle to haraj nahi,
magar patte paani ko gaadha na kar dein.

Hawalaat: Jami' Tirmizi, Hadith 69; Sunan Darqutni, Hadith 85; Ad-Durr-ul-
Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, pages 357, 360, 369.

**Mas'alah 5:** Behte paani mein tinka daal dein to paani use baha le jaye; aisa
paani paak bhi hai aur paak karne wala bhi. Najasat girne se na-paak nahi hoga jab
tak Najasat us ke rang, boo ya zaiqe ko na badal de. Agar Najasat se rang, boo ya
zaiqa badal gaya to na-paak ho gaya. Ab paani tab paak hoga jab Najasat neeche
baith kar us ki sifat theek ho jaye, ya itna paak paani mil jaye ke Najasat baha
le jaye, ya rang, boo aur zaiqa theek ho jaye. Agar paak cheez se yeh sifat badle
to Wuzu aur Ghusl jaiz hai jab tak paani kisi aur cheez mein na badal jaye.

**Mas'alah 6:** Nahar ki chaudai mein mara hua jaanwar pada ho aur us ke upar se
paani beh raha ho, to har jagah se Wuzu jaiz hai, chahe jaanwar se mil kar behne
wala paani kam, barabar ya zyada ho. Najasat se koi sifat na badle to Najasat ki
jagah se bhi Wuzu jaiz hai. Yahi sahih hai aur isi par bharosa hai.

**Mas'alah 7:** Chhat ke Parnaale se baarish ka paani gire to paak hai, chahe chhat
par jagah jagah Najasat ho, hatta ke Parnaale ke munh par ho. Jab tak paani ki koi
sifat Najasat se na badle, paani paak rahega. Baarish ruk jaye aur paani behna band
ho jaye to ab thehra hua paani hai, aur chhat se tapakne wala paani na-paak hai.

**Mas'alah 8:** Naaliyon mein baarish ka behne wala paani paak hai jab tak Najasat
ka rang, boo ya zaiqa zahir na ho. Agar us mein Najasat ke dikhne wale zarre is
tarah beh rahe hon ke liya hua har chullu zarur un mein se kuch le aaye to haath
mein lete hi na-paak ho gaya aur Wuzu Haram hai. Warna jaiz hai, lekin bachna behtar.

**Mas'alah 9:** Baarish ke baad naali ka paani thehar jaye to agar Najasat ke zarre,
rang ya boo mehsoos ho to na-paak hai, warna paak hai.

Hawalaat: Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, pages 370–372;
Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 2, page 38.

**Mas'alah 10:** Das haath lamba aur das haath chauda Hauz, ya bada Hauz, kehlata
hai. Isi tarah bees haath lamba aur paanch haath chauda, ya pachees haath lamba
aur chaar haath chauda Hauz bhi bada hai; yani lambai aur chaudai ka majmua sau
haath ho. Gol Hauz ki golai taqreeban saade paintis haath ho. Agar sau haath na
ho to chhota Hauz hai, chahe kitna hi gehra ho.

Hauz ke bade ya chhote hone mein us ki poori banaawat nahi, balki paani ki uparli
satah dekhi jayegi. Bada Hauz ho magar paani kam hokar das by das na rahe to us
waqt bada Hauz nahi. Hauz sirf Masjid ya Eidgah ka bana hua talab nahi; har aisa
gaddha jis ki paimaish sau haath ho bada Hauz hai, aur is se kam chhota.

**Mas'alah 11:** Das by das Hauz mein itni jagah ka khula hona zaruri hai ke us
masahat mein zameen kahin se khuli na ho. Kitabon mein jo hai ke Lapp ya chullu
se paani lene par zameen na khule, yeh us ke Kaseer rehne ke liye hai. Agar paani
uthaane se zameen khul jaye to us waqt paani sau haath ki masahat mein nahi raha.
Aisa Hauz behte paani ke hukm mein hai aur Najasat se na-paak nahi hoga jab tak
rang, boo ya zaiqa na badle. Phir bhi jaan-boojh kar Najasat daalna mana hai.

**Mas'alah 12:** Bade Hauz ke na-paak na hone ki shart yeh hai ke us ka paani juda
juda na ho. Agar lakdi ke kundey ya shahteerein lagi hon to un ke alawa baqi jagah
agar sau haath ho to bada hai, warna nahi. Patli cheezein, jaise ghaas, narkul aur
fasal, paani ke jurne ko nahi roktin.

**Mas'alah 13:** Bade Hauz mein aisi Najasat pade jo dikhayi na de, jaise Sharaab
ya Peshab, to har taraf se Wuzu jaiz hai. Agar Najasat dikhayi de, jaise paikhana
ya mara hua jaanwar, to jis taraf ho us taraf Wuzu na karna behtar hai; doosri taraf
Wuzu kare.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 2, pages 274–287; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar,
jild 1, pages 375–380; Khulasat-ul-Fatawa, jild 1, page 4.

**Mas'alah 14:** Bade Hauz par bahut se log jama hokar Wuzu karein aur Wuzu ka
paani us mein gire tab bhi haraj nahi. Haan, us mein Kulli ya naak saaf na karein,

**Mas'alah 15:** Talab ya bada Hauz upar se jam gaya, lekin barf ke neeche paani
ki lambai aur chaudai das by das judi hui hai, to suraakh karke Wuzu jaiz hai,
chahe Najasat padi ho. Agar das by das na ho aur Najasat pade to na-paak hai.
Agar Najasat padne se pehle suraakh kar diya aur paani ubal pada, phir das by das
phail gaya, to Najasat padne par bhi paak rahega.

**Mas'alah 16:** Sukhe Talab mein Najasat padi thi. Baarish hui aur paak behne
wala paani itna aaya ke behna rukne se pehle das by das ho gaya, to paani paak hai.
Agar baarish se das by das se kam raha aur doosri baarish se das by das hua to sab
na-paak hai. Haan, bhar kar beh jaye to paak ho gaya, chahe ek ya do haath hi baha ho.

**Mas'alah 17:** Das by das paani mein Najasat padi, phir paani das by das se kam
ho gaya to ab bhi paak hai. Haan, agar Najasat baqi aur dikhayi deti ho to ab
na-paak hai; jab tak bhar kar beh na jaye paak nahi hoga.

**Mas'alah 18:** Chhota Hauz na-paak ho gaya, phir paani phail kar das by das ho
gaya to ab bhi na-paak hai. Paak paani use baha de to paak hoga.

**Mas'alah 19:** Hauz upar se tang aur neeche se chauda ho, yani upar das by das
na ho lekin neeche das by das ya zyada ho, to agar labrez ho aur Najasat pade to
na-paak hai. Phir paani ghat kar das by das ho gaya to paak ho jayega.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 19–20; Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 2,
pages 189, 272, 370.

**Mas'alah 20:** Huqqe ka paani paak hai. Rang, boo aur zaiqa badal jaye tab bhi
us se Wuzu jaiz hai. Jitni zarurat ka paani maujood ho us ke hote Tayammum jaiz
nahi. Haan, bila zarurat is se Wuzu na karna chahiye.

**Mas'alah 21:** Wuzu ya Ghusl karte waqt badan se gira hua paani paak hai, lekin
us se Wuzu aur Ghusl jaiz nahi. Be-Wuzu shakhs ka haath, ungli, poora, naakhun ya

Jis par nahana Farz ho us ka koi be-dhula hissa paani ko chhoo le to bhi yahi hukm
hai. Dhula hua haath ya badan ka hissa pad jaye to haraj nahi.

**Mas'alah 22:** Haath dhula hua ho, phir dobara dhone ki Niyyat se paani mein dala
aur yeh dhona Sawab ka kaam ho, jaise khane ya Wuzu ke liye, to paani Musta'mal
ho gaya, yani Wuzu ke kaam ka na raha. Use peena bhi Makrooh hai.

**Mas'alah 23:** Zarurat ki wajah se haath paani mein dala, jaise bada bartan jhuka
na sakta ho aur chhota bartan bhi na ho, to zarurat ke mutabiq haath daal kar paani

dhone ke liye doosra paani bhi na ho, to paon daal kar rassi aur Dol nikaalne se
paani Musta'mal nahi hoga. In masail se bahut kam log waqif hain, khayal rakhna chahiye.

zyada hai to Wuzu aur Ghusl ke kaam ka rahega, warna sab bekaar ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 25:** Paani mein haath padne se woh Musta'mal ho gaya. Use kaam ka
banana ho to us se zyada achha paani mila dein. Ya ek taraf se paani daalein ke

**Mas'alah 27:** Garam mulk mein garam mausam ke dauran sone-chandi ke alawa kisi
dhatu ke bartan mein dhoop se garam hua paani jab tak garam rahe us se Wuzu aur
Ghusl na karein, na use piyen, na badan tak pahunchne dein. Agar is se kapda bheeg
jaye to thanda hone tak na pehnen. Is paani ke istemal se Bars ka andesha hai.
Phir bhi Wuzu ya Ghusl kar liya to ada ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 28:** Chhote chhote gaddhon mein paani ho aur Najasat padna maloom na
ho to us se Wuzu jaiz hai.

**Mas'alah 29:** Kafir kahe ke paani paak hai ya na-paak, us ki khabar nahi mani
jayegi. Dono surat mein paani paak rahega, kyunki yahi us ki asal halat hai.

**Mas'alah 30:** Na-baligh ka bhara hua paani, jo Shariat ke mutabiq us ki milkiyat
ho gaya ho, usay peena, Wuzu, Ghusl ya kisi kaam mein lana us ke maa-baap ya jis ka
woh naukar ho un ke siwa kisi ko jaiz nahi, chahe bachcha ijazat de. Wuzu karega
to Wuzu ho jayega lekin gunahgar hoga. Muallimon ko is se sabaq lena chahiye,
kyunki woh aksar na-baligh bachon se paani bharwa kar apne kaam mein laate hain.

## Kuen ka Bayan

**Mas'alah 1:** Kuen mein aadmi ya kisi janwar ka peshab, behta hua khoon, Taadi,
Saindhi, kisi qism ki Sharaab ka qatra, na-paak lakdi, na-paak kapda ya koi aur
na-paak cheez gir jaye to poora paani nikaala jayega.

**Mas'alah 2:** Jin chaupaye ka gosht nahi khaya jata un ke paikhane-peshab se,
aur Murgi aur Batakh ki beet se, paani na-paak ho jayega. In tamam suratوں mein
poora paani nikaalna hoga.

**Mas'alah 3:** Meningni, Gobar aur Leid na-paak hain, lekin zarurat ki wajah se
Kuen mein un ki thodi miqdaar maaf hai aur paani ko na-paak nahi kaha jayega.
Uddne wale halal janwaron, jaise Kabootar aur Chirya, ya shikari parindon, jaise
Chil, Shikra aur Baaz, ki beet girne se paani na-paak nahi hoga. Chuha aur
Chamgadad ke peshab ka bhi yahi hukm hai.

**Mas'alah 4:** Peshab ki bahut bareek boondein, sui ki nok jaisi, ya na-paak
gard girne se paani na-paak nahi hoga.

**Mas'alah 5:** Jis Kuen ka paani na-paak ho gaya, us ka ek qatra bhi paak Kuen
mein gir jaye to woh bhi na-paak hai. Isi tarah Dol, rassi ya Ghada jis mein
na-paak Kuen ka paani laga ho, paak Kuen mein pade to paak Kuen bhi na-paak hoga.

**Mas'alah 6:** Kuen mein aadmi, bakri, kutta ya koi aur khoon rakhne wala janwar
apne barabar ya us se bada gir kar mar jaye to poora paani nikaalna hoga.

Hawalaat: Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar, jild 1, pages 407–409; Ghuniyat-ul-Mutamalli,
page 162; Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 19–20.

**Mas'alah 7:** Murga, Murgi, Billi, Chuha, Chipkali ya koi aur khoon rakhne wala
janwar Kuen mein mar kar phool jaye ya phat jaye to poora paani nikaalein.

**Mas'alah 8:** Yeh janwar bahar mar kar phir Kuen mein giren tab bhi yahi hukm hai.

**Mas'alah 9:** Chipkali ya Chuhe ki kati hui dum Kuen mein gire to, chahe phooli
ya phati na ho, poora paani nikaalna hoga. Lekin agar dum ki jad par mom lagi ho
to bees Dol nikaalna hoga.

**Mas'alah 10:** Billi ne Chuhe ko pakda aur woh zakhmi hua, phir chhoot kar Kuen
mein gir gaya to poora paani nikaalna hoga.

**Mas'alah 11:** Chuha, Chhachhundar, Chirya, Chipkali, Girgit, ya in ke barabar
ya in se chhota koi khoon rakhne wala janwar Kuen mein gir kar mar jaye to bees
se tees Dol tak nikaalein.

**Mas'alah 12:** Kabootar, Murgi ya Billi gir kar mare to chaalis se saath Dol tak
nikaalein.

**Mas'alah 13:** Zinda paida hone wala insani bachcha aadmi ke hukm mein hai aur
Bakri ka bachcha Bakri ke hukm mein.

**Mas'alah 14:** Kabootar se chhota janwar Chuhe ke hukm mein aur Bakri se chhota
janwar Murgi ke hukm mein hai.

**Mas'alah 15:** Do Chuhe gir kar mar jayein to bees se tees Dol tak nikaalein.
Teen, chaar ya paanch hon to chaalis se saath Dol tak, aur chhe hon to poora paani.

**Mas'alah 16:** Do Billiyan mar jayein to poora paani nikaala jayega.

**Mas'alah 17:** Musalman murda Ghusl ke baad Kuen mein gir jaye to asal mein paani
nikaalne ki zarurat nahi. Shaheed gire aur us ke badan par khoon na laga ho to bhi
zarurat nahi. Agar khoon laga ho magar behne ke qaabil na ho to bhi zarurat nahi.

Agar Shaheed ka behne wala khoon badan par laga ho, sookh gaya ho aur Kuen mein
girne par badan se juda hokar paani mein na mila ho to paani paak rahega, kyunki
Shaheed ka khoon badan par rehte hue kitna hi ho paak hai. Badan se juda hokar
paani mein mil jaye to paani na-paak ho jayega.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 19–20; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar,
jild 1, pages 414–417.

**Mas'alah 18:** Kafir ka murda, chahe sau baar dhoya gaya ho, Kuen mein gir jaye,
ya us ki ungli ya naakhun paani se lag jaye, to paani na-paak ho jayega aur poora
paani nikaalna hoga.

**Mas'alah 19:** Kacha bachcha ya murda paida hone wala bachcha Kuen mein gir jaye
to poora paani nikaalein, chahe girne se pehle use nahla diya gaya ho.

**Mas'alah 20:** Be-Wuzu shakhs ya jis par Ghusl Farz ho, bila zarurat Kuen mein
utar jaye aur badan par Najasat na lagi ho to bees Dol nikaalna hoga. Dol nikaalne

**Mas'alah 21:** Suwar Kuen mein gir jaye, chahe mare ya zinda rahe, paani na-paak
ho gaya; poora paani nikaalein.

**Mas'alah 22:** Suwar ke alawa koi janwar Kuen mein gir kar zinda nikal aaye aur
us ke badan par Najasat lagi hona yaqini na ho, aur us ka munh paani mein na pada
ho, to paani paak hai aur istemal jaiz. Ehtiyat ke liye bees Dol nikaalna behtar

Agar janwar ka munh paani mein pada ho to us ke L'aab aur Jhoothe ka jo hukm hai
woh is paani ka hai. Jhootha na-paak ya mashkook ho to poora paani nikaalein.
Makrooh ho to Chuhe waghera ki surat mein bees Dol aur chhoti Murgi ki surat mein
chaalis Dol nikaalein. Jis ka Jhootha paak ho us mein bhi bees Dol nikaalna behtar
hai, misal ke taur par Bakri giri aur zinda nikal aayi ho to bees Dol nikaal dein.

**Mas'alah 23:** Kuen mein aisa janwar gire jis ka Jhootha paak ya Makrooh ho,
aur paani na nikaala, phir Wuzu kar liya, to Wuzu ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 24:** Joota ya gend Kuen mein gir jaye aur yaqeen ho ke na-paak hai to

**Mas'alah 25:** Paani ka janwar, yani jo paani mein paida hota hai, Kuen mein
mar jaye ya murda gire to paani na-paak nahi hoga, chahe phool ya phat jaye.
Lekin phat kar us ke ajza paani mein mil jayein to us paani ka peena Haram hai.

**Mas'alah 26:** Khushki aur paani ke Mendak ka ek hi hukm hai. Us ke marne ya
sadne se paani na-paak nahi hota. Lekin jungle ka bada Mendak, jis mein behne
wala khoon hota hai, Chuhe jaisa hai. Paani ke Mendak ki ungliyon ke beech jhilli
hoti hai aur khushki ke Mendak ki ungliyon mein jhilli nahi hoti.

**Mas'alah 27:** Jo janwar paani mein paida na hua ho, lekin paani mein rehta ho,
jaise Batakh, us ke marne se paani na-paak ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 28:** Bachcha ya Kafir paani mein haath daale. Agar haath na-paak hona
maloom ho to paani na-paak hai. Warna paani na-paak nahi, lekin doosre paani se
Wuzu karna behtar hai.

**Mas'alah 29:** Jin janwaron mein behne wala khoon nahi, jaise Machhar aur Makkhi,
un ke marne se paani na-paak nahi hoga. Fa'idah: Makkhi Shorbe waghera mein gir
jaye to use dubo kar nikaal dein aur Shorba istemal karein.

**Mas'alah 30:** Murda jaanwar ki haddi par Gosht ya Charbi lagi ho aur paani mein
gire to paani na-paak hai; poora paani nikaalein. Agar Gosht ya Charbi na lagi ho
to paani paak hai. Suwar ki haddi se baharhaal paani na-paak ho jayega.

## Aadmi aur Janwaron ke Jhoothe ka Bayan

**Mas'alah 1:** Aadmi ka Jhootha paak hai, chahe Junub ho ya Haiz-o-Nifas wali
aurat. Kafir ka Jhootha bhi paak hai, lekin us se bachna chahiye. Thook, Rint aur
Khankar paak hain magar aadmi un se ghin karta hai; Kafir ke Jhoothe ko is se bhi
bad-tar samajhna chahiye.

**Mas'alah 2:** Kisi ke munh se itna khoon nikla ke thook surkh ho gaya aur us ne
foran paani piya to Jhootha na-paak hai. Surkhi khatam hone ke baad Kulli karke
munh paak kare. Agar Kulli na ki aur thook kai baar Najasat ki jagah se guzra,
chahe nigalne ya thookne mein, yahan tak ke Najasat ka asar na raha, to Taharat
ho gayi. Is ke baad paani piye to paak rahega, lekin us waqt thook nigalna sakht
na-paak aur gunah hai.

**Mas'alah 3:** Ma'az-Allah Sharaab peene ke foran baad paani piya to Jhootha
na-paak hai. Itni der thehar gaya ke Sharaab ke ajza thook mein mil kar gale se
utar gaye to na-paak nahi, lekin Sharaabi aur us ke Jhoothe se bachna hi chahiye.

**Mas'alah 4:** Sharaab peene wale ki moonchhein badi hon aur Sharaab un mein lag
gayi ho, to jab tak moonchhein paak na kare jo paani piyega woh paani aur bartan
dono na-paak ho jayenge.

**Mas'alah 5:** Mard ke liye ghair aurat ka aur aurat ke liye ghair mard ka Jhootha,
agar maloom ho ke falan ka hai aur lutf ke liye khaya-piya jaye, Makrooh hai.
Lekin us khane-paani mein doosri wajah se koi karaahat nahi. Agar maloom na ho ke
kis ka hai, ya lutf ke liye na khaya-piya gaya ho, to haraj nahi, balki kuch suratوں
mein behtar hai, jaise Shari'at ke alim ya Deendar Peer ka Jhootha Tabarruk samajh
kar khana-peena.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 23–24; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar,
jild 1, pages 424–425.

Teetar.

**Mas'alah 7:** Jo Murgi khuli phirti ho aur gandagi par munh maarti ho us ka Jhootha
Makrooh hai. Band rakhi jati ho to paak hai.

**Mas'alah 8:** Kuch Gaayein gandagi khane ki aadi hoti hain; un ka Jhootha Makrooh

ke liye sikhaya ho aur Chonch par Najasat na lagi ho to Jhootha paak hai.

## Tayammum ke Masa'il

**Mas'alah 1:** Jis ka Wuzu na ho ya jise nahane ki zarurat ho, aur paani par
qudrat na ho, woh Wuzu aur Ghusl ki jagah Tayammum kare. Paani par qudrat na hone
ki kuch suraten yeh hain:

1. Aisi bimari ho ke Wuzu ya Ghusl se bimari badhne ya der se theek hone ka sahi
andesha ho. Ya to us ne khud aazmaya ho ke Wuzu ya Ghusl se bimari badhti hai, ya
aisa Musalman, achha aur laayak Hakim, jo zahiri taur par Fasiq na ho, kahe ke
paani nuqsan karega.

**Mas'alah 2:** Sirf khayal ho ke bimari badhegi to Tayammum jaiz nahi. Kafir,
Fasiq ya mamooli Tabeeb ke kehne ka bhi aitibar nahi.

**Mas'alah 3:** Paani bimari ko nuqsan na deta ho, lekin Wuzu ya Ghusl ke liye
harkat nuqsan deti ho, ya khud Wuzu na kar sakta ho aur koi Wuzu karane wala bhi
na ho, to Tayammum kare. Isi tarah kisi ke haath phat gaye hon aur woh khud Wuzu
na kar sake aur koi Wuzu karane wala na ho to Tayammum kare.

**Mas'alah 4:** Be-Wuzu ke aksar aza-e-Wuzu mein, ya Junub ke badan ke aksar hisse
mein zakhm ya Chechak ho to Tayammum kare. Warna sehatmand hissa dhoye, zakhm ki
jagah aur nuqsan ke waqt us ke aas-paas Masah kare. Masah bhi nuqsan de to uzv par
kapda rakh kar us par Masah kare.

**Mas'alah 5:** Bimari mein thanda paani nuqsan karta ho aur garam paani nuqsan na
de to garam paani se Wuzu aur Ghusl zaruri hai. Agar aisi jagah ho jahan garam
paani na mil sake to Tayammum kare.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 28; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar wa
Radd-ul-Muhtar, jild 1, page 481; Sahih Bukhari, Hadith 337.

Thande waqt mein Wuzu ya Ghusl nuqsan karta ho aur garam waqt mein nuqsan na ho
to thande waqt Tayammum kare. Garam waqt aane par agli Namaz ke liye Wuzu karna
chahiye. Tayammum se jo Namaz padh li usay dobara padhna zaruri nahi.

**Mas'alah 6:** Sar par paani daalna nuqsan karta ho to gale se nahaye aur poore
sar ka Masah kare.

Tayammum karne wale ko apne charon taraf ek-ek meel tak paani ka pata na ho.

**Mas'alah 7:** Agar ek meel ke andar paani hone ka gumaan ho to talash karna
zaruri hai. Baghair talash Tayammum jaiz nahi. Baghair talash Tayammum karke Namaz
padh li aur talash karne par paani mil gaya to Wuzu karke Namaz dobara padhna lazim
hai. Paani na mila to Namaz ho gayi.

**Mas'alah 8:** Ghalib gumaan ho ke ek meel ke andar paani nahi hai to talash
zaruri nahi. Tayammum karke Namaz padh li, na talash ki aur na koi poochne wala
tha, phir baad mein maloom hua ke paani qareeb tha, to Namaz dobara nahi. Haan,
Tayammum ab toot gaya. Agar wahan koi maujood tha magar us se poocha nahi aur baad
mein maloom hua ke paani qareeb tha to Namaz dobara padhna hogi.

**Mas'alah 9:** Qareeb paani hone ya na hone ka koi gumaan na ho to talash karna
Mustahab hai. Baghair talash Tayammum karke Namaz padhi to Namaz ho gayi.

**Mas'alah 10:** Saath Zamzam Shareef ho, logon ke liye Tabarruk ke taur par le
ja raha ho, ya beemar ko pilane ke liye ho, aur itna ho ke Wuzu ho jaye, to Tayammum
jaiz nahi.

**Mas'alah 11:** Zamzam se Wuzu na karna chahe taake Tayammum jaiz ho, to kisi aise
shakhs ko jis par bharosa ho ke paani wapas de dega, paani Hiba kar de aur kuch
badla muqarrar kar le. Ab Tayammum jaiz ho jayega.

**Mas'alah 12:** Jo na Aabaadi mein ho na Aabaadi ke qareeb, us ke paas paani ho
magar yaad na raha aur Tayammum karke Namaz padh li, to agle bayan mein is ki
tafseel aayegi.

Hawalaat: Fatawa Hindiyyah, jild 1, pages 28–29; Fatawa Tatar Khaniyyah,
jild 1, page 234; Ad-Durr-ul-Mukhtar, jild 1, page 475.
