# Surah Al-Ikhlas — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 112:1

`Source: tafseerId=54498; ayatId=6333; surah=112; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌۚ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmaao: Woh Allah ek hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌ: Tum farmaao: Woh Allah ek hai.} Arab mein kafiron ki bahut si qismein thin: Dahriye, mushrik, Allah Ta'ala ki sifaat ke munkir aur Allah Ta'ala ke liye aulaad maanne wale waghera. Is Surah mein in sab ka radd hai. “هُوَ اللّٰهُ” farmaane mein Dahriyon ka radd hai, “اَحَدٌ” farmaane mein mushrikeen ka poora radd hai aur agli aayaat mein baqi kafiron ka radd hai. Irshad farmaya gaya ke woh Allah ek hai, yani Rububiyyat aur Uloohiyyat mein azmat aur kamaal ki sifaat ke saath mausoof hai. Us ki na koi misaal hai, na nazeer aur na shabeeh; us ka koi shareek nahin.

{اَللّٰهُ الصَّمَدُ: Allah be-niyaaz hai.} Irshad farmaya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala har cheez se be-niyaaz hai. Na khaaye, na piye, hamesha se hai aur hamesha rahega. Kisi kaam mein kisi ka mohtaaj nahin.

{لَمْ یَلِدْ ﳔ وَ لَمْ یُوْلَدْ: Na us ne kisi ko janam diya aur na woh kisi se paida hua.} Allah Ta'ala aulaad se paak hai, kyunke aulaad baap ki jins se hoti hai aur Allah Ta'ala is se paak hai. Isi tarah woh khud kisi se paida nahin hua, kyunke woh Qadeem hai, yani hamesha se hai. Paida hona us cheez ki sifat hai jo pehle na ho aur phir wajood mein aaye. Is mein mushrikeen aur Yahood-o-Nasaara sab ka radd hai. Mushrikeen farishton ko Allah Ta'ala ki betiyaan kehte the. Yahoodi Hazrat Uzair عَلَیْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام ko, jabke Isaai Hazrat Isa عَلَیْهِ الصَّلٰوةُ وَالسَّلَام ko Khuda ka beta maante the.

{وَ لَمْ یَكُنْ لَّهٗ كُفُوًا اَحَدٌ: Aur koi us ke barabar nahin.} Yani na zaat mein, na sifaat mein, kyunke woh Waajib hai, Khaaliq hai; baqi sab mumkin, makhlooq aur haadis hain. Us ki zaati sifaat Qadeem aur ghair-mehdood hain, jabke makhlooq ki sifaat ataai, haadis aur mehdood hain.
