# Surah An-Naas — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 114:1

`Source: tafseerId=54500; ayatId=6344; surah=114; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum kaho: Main tamam logon ke Rab ki panaah leta hoon.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ: Tum kaho: Main tamam logon ke Rab ki panaah leta hoon.} Allah Ta'ala saari makhlooq ka Rab hai, lekin insaan ashraf-ul-makhlooq hai, is liye yahan khaas taur par insaan ka zikr kiya gaya hai. (خازن، النّاس، تحت الآیۃ: ۱، ۴ / ۴۳۰)

### Insaan ki azmat aur sharaafat

Is se insaan ki azmat aur sharaafat bhi maloom hui ke Allah Ta'ala ne khaas taur par apni rububiyyat ka ta'alluq us ki taraf farmaya. Ulama ne yahan yeh hikmat bayan ki hai ke is Surah mein “اَلنَّاسِ” lafz paanch martaba aaya hai. Insaan bachpan mein sirf parwarish paata hai, is liye sab se pehle “رَبِّ النَّاسِ” yani Rab ki parwarish wali sifat ka zikr farmaya gaya. Jab insaan jawani mein mast ho kar be-raah ho jaata hai to us par qanooni giraft ki zaroorat hoti hai, is liye yahan “مَلِكِ النَّاسِ” yani logon ka Badshah farmaya gaya. Insaan budhape mein ibaadat mein mashghool hota hai, is liye teesri jagah Allah Ta'ala ki uluhiyyat aur ma'boodiyyat ka zikr farmaya gaya, yani “اِلٰهِ النَّاسِ”. Chauthi jagah “النَّاسِ” se saaliheen muraad ho sakte hain, kyunke Shaitaan aam taur par inhi ko waswason ke zariye ibaadat se hataane ki koshish karta hai. Paanchwi jagah “النَّاسِ” se shar-pasand aur fasaadi log muraad ho sakte hain, kyunke wahan logon ke shar se panaah maangi gayi hai.

{مَلِكِ النَّاسِ: Tamam logon ka Badshah.} Yani un ke kaamon ki tadbeer farmaane wala aur sab ka haqeeqi Haakim-o-Maalik hai. Duniya mein bhi kisi ko hukoomat aur milkiyyat mile to usi ki ata se milti hai.

{اِلٰهِ النَّاسِ: Tamam logon ka ma'bood.} Ma'bood hona sirf usi ke saath khaas hai aur saare logon ka haqeeqi ma'bood wahi hai.

{مِنْ شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ ﳔ الْخَنَّاسِ: Baar baar waswase daalne wale, chhup jaane wale ke shar se.} Is se Shaitaan muraad hai. Yeh us ki aadat hai ke jab insaan ghaafil hota hai to us ke dil mein waswase daalta hai aur jab insaan Allah Ta'ala ka zikr karta hai to Shaitaan dabak jaata hai aur hat jaata hai.

### Waswase aur ilhaam mein farq

Yaad rahe ke bure khayal ko waswasa kaha jaata hai, jabke achhe khayalat ko ilhaam kehte hain. Waswasa Shaitaan ki taraf se hota hai, is liye is par لَا حَوْل padhna chahiye. Ilhaam farishte ke zariye Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se hota hai, is liye is par Allah Ta'ala ka shukr karna chahiye. Nafs-e-ammaarah ke ghalabe mein waswase zyada hote hain, jabke nafs-e-mutma'innah ke ghalabe mein ilhaam zyada hota hai. Shaitaan hamara aisa dushman hai jo hamein nazar nahin aata; yani woh hamein dekhta hai aur hum use nahin dekhte. Is liye us taaqat wale Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki panaah maango jo use dekhta hai, aur woh Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko nahin dekhta. Qawi dushman se Qawi Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki panaah maango.

{اَلَّذِیْ یُوَسْوِسُ فِیْ صُدُوْرِ النَّاسِ: Woh jo logon ke dilon mein waswase daalta hai.} Yani Shaitaan zubaan aur awaaz se nahin behkaata, balki seedha dil mein asar daalta hai aur buri cheez ko achhi dikhaata hai. Woh khud dushman hai, lekin dosti ke libaas mein aata hai. Phir insaan jaisa hota hai, use waisa hi waswasa daalta hai.

{مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَ النَّاسِ: Jinnon aur insaanon mein se.} Yeh waswase daalne wale Shaitaan ka bayan hai ke woh jinnon mein se bhi hota hai aur insaanon mein se bhi. Jis tarah jinnon ke Shaitaan insaanon ko waswase mein daalte hain, isi tarah insaanon ke Shaitaan bhi naaseh ban kar aadmi ke dil mein waswase daalte hain. Phir agar aadmi in waswason ko maanta hai to yeh silsila badhta jaata hai aur woh khoob gumraah karte hain. Agar aadmi in se nafrat karta hai to woh hat jaate hain aur dabak kar rehte hain.

### Jinnon aur insaanon ke Shaitaanon se panaah maangein

Aadmi ko chahiye ke jinnon ke Shaitaanon ke shar se bhi panaah maange aur insaanon ke Shaitaanon ke shar se bhi. Is silsile mein yahan ek mufeed wazifa pesh-e-khidmat hai. Chunanche Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا farmati hain ke Rasoolullah ﷺ raat ke waqt jab bistar-e-mubarak par tashreef laate to apne dono dast-e-mubarak jama farma kar un mein dam karte. Phir Surah قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌ, قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ aur قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ padh kar apne mubarak haathon ko sar-e-mubarak se le kar tamaam jism-e-aqdas par pherte, jahan tak dast-e-mubarak pahunch sakte. Yeh amal teen martaba farmate. (بخاری، کتاب فضائل القرآن، باب فضل المعوّذات، ۳ / ۴۰۷، الحدیث: ۵۰۱۷)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Khubayb رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Hum par halki baarish hui aur us waqt andhera chhaya hua tha. Hum Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ka intezaar kar rahe the taake woh tashreef la kar hamein namaaz padhaayein. Phir Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ hamein namaaz padhaane ke liye tashreef laaye aur irshaad farmaya: “Padho!” Maine arz ki: “Kya padhoon?” Irshaad farmaya: “Subah-shaam teen teen baar قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌ, قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ aur قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ padho. In ki tilaawat tumhein har buri cheez se bachaayegi.” (سنن نسائی، کتاب الاستعاذۃ، ۱-باب، ص۸۶۲، الحدیث: ۵۴۳۸)

Allah Ta'ala se dua hai ke is tafseer ko awaam aur khawaas sabhi Musalmanon ke liye nafa-bakhsh aur faaidah-mand banaaye aur ise Musalmanon ki i'tiqaadi, ilmi aur amali islaah ka behtareen zariya banaaye. Is ke sadqe meri, mere waalidayn, mere azeez rishtedaaron, asaatiza-e-kiraam, dost-ahbaab, deegar muta'alliqeen aur mu'aawineen ki be-hisaab bakhshish-o-maghfirat farmaaye. Aameen summa Aameen.
