# Surah An-Nisa — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 4:1

`Source: tafseerId=50584; ayatId=498; surah=4; ayat=1; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا  النَّاسُ   اتَّقُوْا  رَبَّكُمُ  الَّذِیْ  خَلَقَكُمْ  مِّنْ  نَّفْسٍ  وَّاحِدَةٍ  وَّ  خَلَقَ  مِنْهَا  زَوْجَهَا  وَ  بَثَّ  مِنْهُمَا  رِجَالًا  كَثِیْرًا  وَّ  نِسَآءًۚ-وَ  اتَّقُوا  اللّٰهَ  الَّذِیْ  تَسَآءَلُوْنَ  بِهٖ  وَ  الْاَرْحَامَؕ-اِنَّ  اللّٰهَ  كَانَ  عَلَیْكُمْ  رَقِیْبًا(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aye logo! Apne Rab se daro jis ne tumhein ek jaan se paida kiya aur usi mein se us ka jora paida kiya aur un dono se bahut se mard aur auratein phaila dein aur Allah se daro jis ke naam par tum ek doosre se maangte ho aur rishton ko todne se bacho. Beshak Allah tum par nigahbaan hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein tamam Bani Adam ko khitaab karke sab ko taqwa ka hukm diya gaya hai. Kaafiron ke liye taqwa yeh hai ke woh imaan laayein aur nek amal karein. Musalmanon ke liye taqwa yeh hai ke imaan par mazboot rahen aur nek amal baja laayein. Har shakhs ko us ki haalat ke mutabiq taqwa ka hukm hai.

Is aayat mein chand baatein bayan hui hain:

1. Allah Ta'ala ne tamam insaanon ko ek jaan, yani Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se paida kiya.
2. Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke wajood se un ka jora, yani Hazrat Hawwa رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا, paida ki gayin.
3. In dono buzurgon se zameen mein nasl-dar-nasl bahut se mard aur auratein phailin.
4. Insani nasl ke phailne ke saath ek doosre par zulm aur haq talfi bhi shuru hui. Is liye Allah ke khauf ka hukm diya gaya taake zulm se bachein. Zulm ki ek badtareen surat rishtedaron se ta'alluq todna hai, is liye is se bhi bachne ka hukm diya gaya.

### Insaanon ki ibtida kis se hui?

Musalmanon ka aqeedah hai ke insaanon ki ibtida Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se hui. Isi liye aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Abul-Bashar, yani insaanon ka baap, kaha jata hai. Is baat par mazboot daleel hai. Duniya ki mardum-shumari se maloom hota hai ke aaj se sau saal pehle insaanon ki tadaad aaj se bahut kam thi aur us se sau saal pehle aur bhi kam thi. Maazi ki taraf chalte hue yeh kami aakhir ek shakhs par jaakar rukegi, aur woh Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام hain.

Qabeelon ki kasrat bhi ek shakhs par jaakar khatam hoti hai. Duniya mein Sayyid karoron hain, lekin un sab ka silsila Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki ek zaat tak pahunchta hai. Bani Israeel kitne hi zyada hon, un ka silsila Hazrat Yaqub عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام tak pahunchta hai. Isi tarah tamam insani khandanon aur qabeelon ka silsila ek zaat tak pahunchta hai jise tamam aasmani kitaabon mein Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام kaha gaya hai.

Yeh mumkin nahin ke Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aam paidaish ke tareeqe se, yani maa-baap se paida hue hon. Agar un ke liye baap maana jaye to maa kahan se aayegi, aur jis ko baap maana gaya woh khud kahan se aaya? Is liye zaroori hai ke un ki paidaish maa-baap ke baghair hui ho. Quran ne bataya ke Allah Ta'ala ne unhein mitti se paida kiya, jo duniya ka bunyadi juz hai.

Phir insani nasl ke jaari rehne ke liye doosra wajood chahiye tha. Hazrat Hawwa رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ko bhi mitti se alag paida karne ke bajaye Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke wajood se paida kiya gaya. Riwayat mein hai ke Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki baayen pasli se un ki biwi Hazrat Hawwa رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ko paida kiya gaya. Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne neend se jaagne ke baad unhein apne paas dekha. Un ke dil mein apni jins se muhabbat paida hui. Aap ne poocha: “Tum kaun ho?” Unhone arz ki: “Aurat.” Poocha: “Kis liye paida ki gayi ho?” Arz ki: “Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki taskeen ke liye.” Is par Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام un se maanus ho gaye. (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱، ۱ / ۳۴۰)

Yeh insani nasl ki ibtida ka samajh mein aane wala tareeqa hai. Is ke muqaable mein yeh kehna ke insaan bandar se bana hai, intehai na-ma'qul baat hai. Agar insaan bandar se bana hai to hazaaron saalon mein koi naya bandar insaan kyun na ban saka? Aaj poori duniya mil kar kisi bandar ko insaan kyun na bana saki? Bandar se insaan banne ka silsila kab shuru hua, kis ne dekha, us ka raavi kaun hai aur kis purani kitaab se yeh baat mili? Agar bandar insaan bana to dum ka kya hua? Kya insaan bante hi dum gir gayi, baad mein kaati gayi ya ghiste ghiste khatam hui? Kya dum wale insaan bhi kabhi paye gaye? Is baat ka koi seedha silsila, tareekh ya mazboot kadi nahin. Khayali farziye bana kar achhe-bhale insaan ko bandar se mila diya gaya.

**{ وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ الَّذِیْ تَسَآءَلُوْنَ بِهٖ وَ الْاَرْحَامَ : Aur Allah se daro jis ke naam par maangte ho aur rishton ka lihaz rakho.}**

Allah Ta'ala se daro, jis ke naam par ek doosre se kehte ho: “Allah ke waaste mujhe yeh de do.” Rishtedari todne ke maamle mein bhi Allah Ta'ala se daro.

### Rishtedari todne ki mazammat

Quran-e-Majeed aur ahadees mein rishtedari todne ki sakht mazammat bayan hui hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

وَ الَّذِیْنَ یَنْقُضُوْنَ عَهْدَ اللّٰهِ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مِیْثَاقِهٖ وَ یَقْطَعُوْنَ مَاۤ اَمَرَ اللّٰهُ بِهٖۤ اَنْ یُّوْصَلَ وَ یُفْسِدُوْنَ فِی الْاَرْضِۙ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ لَهُمُ اللَّعْنَةُ وَ لَهُمْ سُوْٓءُ الدَّارِ (رعد: ۲۵)

**Tarjumah:** Aur woh jo Allah ka ahad pakka karne ke baad tod dete hain, aur jis cheez ko jorne ka Allah ne hukm diya hai use kaat-te hain aur zameen mein fasad phailate hain, un ke liye laanat hai aur un ke liye bura ghar hai.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abi Aufa رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasool Allah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis qaum mein rishtedari todne wala ho, us par rahmat nahin utarti.” (شعب الایمان، السادس والخمسون من شعب الایمان، ۶ / ۲۲۳، الحدیث: ۷۹۶۲)

Hazrat Abu Bakrah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis gunah ki saza duniya mein bhi jaldi de di jaye aur Aakhirat mein bhi azaab rahe, woh baghawat aur qata-e-rahmi se badh kar nahin.” (ترمذی، کتاب صفۃ القیامۃ، ۵۷- باب، ۴ / ۲۲۹، الحدیث: ۲۵۱۹)

Har Musalman ko chahiye ke rishtedari todne se bache aur rishtedaron se ta'alluq jor kar rakhne ki poori koshish kare.

## Ayat 4:2

`Source: tafseerId=50585; ayatId=499; surah=4; ayat=2; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  اٰتُوا  الْیَتٰمٰۤى  اَمْوَالَهُمْ  وَ  لَا  تَتَبَدَّلُوا  الْخَبِیْثَ  بِالطَّیِّبِ   ۪-  وَ  لَا  تَاْكُلُوْۤا  اَمْوَالَهُمْ  اِلٰۤى  اَمْوَالِكُمْؕ-اِنَّهٗ  كَانَ  حُوْبًا  كَبِیْرًا(2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur yateemon ko un ke maal de do aur paak maal ke badle ganda maal na lo aur un ke maal apne maal mein mila kar na kha jao. Beshak yeh bada gunah hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke ek shakhs ki nigraani mein us ke yateem bhatije ka bahut maal tha. Jab yateem baalig hua aur us ne apna maal maanga to chacha ne dene se inkaar kar diya. Aayat nazil hui to us shakhs ne yateem ka maal us ke hawale kar diya aur kaha: “Hum Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki ita'at karte hain.” (بیضاوی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ۲ / ۱۴۱)

Ma'na yeh hai ke jab yateem apna maal maange aur doosre shar'i taqaaze poore ho jaayein to us ka maal us ke hawale kar do. Apne halaal maal ke badle yateem ka maal na lo. Apna ghatiya maal yateem ko de kar us ka umdah maal lena bhi isi mein shamil hai. Tumhara ghatiya maal tumhare liye paak hai kyun ke halaal hai, aur yateem ka umdah maal tumhare liye khabees hai kyun ke haraam hai. Jab ghatiya badla de kar yateem ka maal lena haraam hua to baghair badle ke kha lena aur bhi zyada haraam hai. Haan, Shari'at ne jahan yateemon ka maal apne maal ke saath mila kar istemal karne ki ijaazat di hai, woh alag masla hai; us ka bayan Surah Al-Baqarah, aayat 220 mein hai.

Yateem us na-baaligh ladke ya ladki ko kehte hain jis ka baap wafaat paa jaye. Is aayat ki roshni mein chand aham masail:

1. Yateem ko tohfa diya ja sakta hai, lekin us ka tohfa liya nahin ja sakta.
2. Agar kisi ke warison mein yateem bachche hon to us tarke se teja, chaleeswan, niyaz, fateha aur khairaat karna haraam hai. Yateemon ke maal wali niyaz ya fateha ka khaana bhi haraam hai. Yeh aam pesh aane wala masla hai, lekin log be-dhadak yateemon ka maal kha lete hain.
3. Jaiz niyaz ka tareeqa yeh hai ke baalig waris apne khaas maal se niyaz dilayein. Dafn ke baad ya teesre din pakai jaane wali daawat har surat najaiz hai, chahe apne maal se ho. Teja aur fateha ka isaale-sawab jaiz hai, lekin rishtedaron aur ahl-e-mohalla ki daawat najaiz hai; yeh khaana sirf fuqara ko khilaya ja sakta hai. Is ki tafseel ke liye Fatawa Razawiyyah ki jild 9 mein yeh do risaale dekhein: (1) اَلْحُجَّۃُ الْفَائِحہْ لِطِیْبِ التَّعْیِیْنِ وَالْفَاتِحَہْ، (2) جَلِیُّ الصَّوْتْ لِنَھْیِ الدَّعْوَۃِ اَمَامَ مَوْتْ۔

## Ayat 4:3

`Source: tafseerId=50586; ayatId=500; surah=4; ayat=3; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  اِنْ  خِفْتُمْ  اَلَّا  تُقْسِطُوْا  فِی  الْیَتٰمٰى  فَانْكِحُوْا  مَا  طَابَ  لَكُمْ  مِّنَ  النِّسَآءِ  مَثْنٰى  وَ  ثُلٰثَ  وَ  رُبٰعَۚ-فَاِنْ  خِفْتُمْ  اَلَّا  تَعْدِلُوْا  فَوَاحِدَةً  اَوْ  مَا  مَلَكَتْ  اَیْمَانُكُمْؕ-ذٰلِكَ  اَدْنٰۤى  اَلَّا  تَعُوْلُوْاﭤ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar tumhein andesha ho ke yateem ladkiyon mein insaaf na kar sako ge to un auraton se nikah karo jo tumhein pasand hon, do do, teen teen aur chaar chaar. Phir agar tumhein dar ho ke tum insaaf na kar sako ge to ek hi se nikah karo ya un laundiiyon par guzara karo jo tumhari milkiyat mein hain. Yeh is ke zyada qareeb hai ke tum se zulm na ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Is aayat ke ma'ni mein chand aqwaal hain. Imam Hasan Basri رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya ke pehle zamane mein Madine ke log apni nigraani mein rehne wali yateem ladkiyon se un ke maal ki wajah se nikah kar lete, halanke un ki taraf raghbat na hoti. Un ke huqooq ada na karte aur un ke maal ke waaris banne ke liye un ki wafaat ka intezaar karte. Aayat mein is se roka gaya. (صاوی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ۲ / ۳۵۹)

Ek qawl yeh hai ke log yateemon ki sarparasti mein na-insaafi se darte the lekin zina se nahin darte the. Unhein bataya gaya ke agar na-insaafi se bachne ke liye yateemon ki sarparasti se gurez karte ho to zina se bhi bacho aur halaal auraton se nikah karo. Doosra qawl yeh hai ke log yateemon ke haq mein na-insaafi se darte the lekin zyada nikah karne mein khatra mehsoos nahin karte the. Unhein bataya gaya ke zyada biwiyan hon to un ke huqooq mein bhi na-insaafi se daro aur utni hi biwiyan rakho jitnon ke huqooq ada kar sako. (تفسیر کبیر، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ۳ / ۴۸۵؛ مدارک، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ص ۲۰۹)

Hazrat Ikrimah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat kiya ke Quraish das ya us se zyada auratein nikah mein rakhte. Jab un ka bojh na utha sakte to apni sarparasti wali yateem ladkiyon ka maal kharch kar daalte. Is liye aayat mein apni maali haisiyat dekhne aur chaar se zyada nikah na karne ka hukm hai. (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ۱ / ۳۴۰)

Is aayat se maloom hua ke azaad mard ke liye ek waqt mein chaar auraton tak nikah jaiz hai. Ummat ka ijma' hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke siwa kisi ke liye chaar se zyada biwiyan jaiz nahin. Ek shakhs Islam laaya aur us ki aath biwiyan thin. Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “In mein se sirf chaar rakh lo.” (ابو داؤد، کتاب الطلاق، باب فی من اسلم وعندہ نساء۔۔۔ الخ، ۲ / ۳۹۶، الحدیث: ۲۲۴۱)

Agar ek se zyada nikah ki surat mein sab ke darmiyan adl ka dar ho to sirf ek nikah karo. Agar chaar mein adl na kar sako lekin teen mein kar sako to teen ki ijaazat hai; teen mein na kar sako lekin do mein kar sako to do ki. Biwiyon ke darmiyan adl farz hai. Nayi, purani, kunwari, doosre ki talaaq-shuda ya bewa sab ke saath kapron, khaane-peene, rehne ki jagah aur raat guzaarne mein barabar sulook zaroori hai.

## Ayat 4:4

`Source: tafseerId=50587; ayatId=501; surah=4; ayat=4; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  اٰتُوا  النِّسَآءَ  صَدُقٰتِهِنَّ  نِحْلَةًؕ-فَاِنْ  طِبْنَ  لَكُمْ  عَنْ  شَیْءٍ  مِّنْهُ  نَفْسًا  فَكُلُوْهُ  هَنِیْٓــٴًـا  مَّرِیْٓــٴًـا(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur auraton ko un ke mahr khushi se do, phir agar woh khushi-dili se mahr mein se tumhein kuchh de dein to use paakiza, khushgawar samajh kar khao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Allah Ta'ala ne shoharon ko hukm diya ke apni biwiyon ko mahr khushi se ada karein. Agar biwiyan khushi se mahr mein se kuchh tohfe ke taur par de dein to use paakiza aur khushgawar samajh kar lena jaiz hai; is mein duniya ya Aakhirat ka nuqsan nahin. (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۴، ۱ / ۳۴۴؛ جلالین مع صاوی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۴، ۲ / ۳۶۰، ملتقطاً)

1. Mahr ki haqdaar aurat hai, us ke sarparast nahin. Agar sarparast ne mahr le liya ho to use haqdaar aurat tak pahunchana zaroori hai.
2. Mahr ko bojh samajh kar nahin, balki aurat ka shar'i haq aur Allah Ta'ala ke hukm par amal samajh kar khushi se dena chahiye.
3. Mahr dene ke baad zabardasti ya tang karke wapas lena jaiz nahin.
4. Agar aurat khushi se poora ya kuchh mahr de de to woh halaal hai. Aaj kal kuchh log seedha majboor nahin karte, lekin apni chikni-chupdi baaton, kharab rawaiye, narazgi aur mel-jol ke badle hue andaaz se aurat ko mahr maaf karne ya lautane par majboor karte hain. Yeh sab mana hai, balki is mein aur zyada bad-diyaanti aur kaminiyat hai. Allah Ta'ala aise logon ko hidayat de.

## Ayat 4:5

`Source: tafseerId=50588; ayatId=502; surah=4; ayat=5; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  لَا  تُؤْتُوا  السُّفَهَآءَ  اَمْوَالَكُمُ  الَّتِیْ  جَعَلَ  اللّٰهُ  لَكُمْ  قِیٰمًا  وَّ  ارْزُقُوْهُمْ  فِیْهَا  وَ  اكْسُوْهُمْ  وَ  قُوْلُوْا  لَهُمْ  قَوْلًا  مَّعْرُوْفًا(5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur kam-aqlon ko un ke woh maal na do jise Allah ne tumhare liye guzara banaya hai aur unhein is maal mein se khilao aur pehnao aur un se achhi baat kaho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Jin bachchon ki parwarish tumhare zimmah hai aur jin ka maal tumhare paas hai, agar woh maal ka sahi istemal nahin jaante aur be-mahal kharch kar dete hain to jab tak unhein maal ki samajh na aa jaye, maal un ke hawale na karo. Un ki zarooriyat, jaise khaana-peena aur kapre, un ke maal se poore karte raho. Saath hi un se achhi baat kehte raho taake un ka dil mutmain rahe. Misal ke taur par kaho: “Bhai! Maal tumhara hi hai. Jab tum samajhdaar ho jaoge to yeh tumhare hawale kar diya jayega.” (مدارک، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۵، ص ۲۱۰)

Achhi baat mein yeh bhi shamil hai ke unhein zindagi ke aadaab sikhao aur khaane-peene, uthne-baithne aur baat-cheet ke tareeqon mein un ki tarbiyat karo.

## Ayat 4:6

`Source: tafseerId=50589; ayatId=503; surah=4; ayat=6; para=4; volume=2`

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Yateemon ki samajh ka imtihaan lete raho. Un ka maal de kar kabhi kabhi dekho ke woh kaise kharch karte hain. Jab woh baalig ho jaayein aur maal ke muamlaat theek tareeqe se karne ki samajh un mein nazar aaye to un ka maal un ke hawale kar do.

Yateem ka sarparast agar maaldaar ho to us ke maal se faida na uthaye. Agar zarooratmand ho to sirf zaroorat ke mutabiq, bahut kam miqdaar mein le sakta hai. (جمل، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۶، ۲ / ۱۳)

Jab yateemon ka maal un ke hawale karo to gawah bana lo taake baad mein jhagda na ho. Yeh hukm mustahab hai.

## Ayat 4:7

## Ayat 4:8

`Source: tafseerId=50591; ayatId=505; surah=4; ayat=8; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  اِذَا  حَضَرَ  الْقِسْمَةَ  اُولُوا  الْقُرْبٰى  وَ  الْیَتٰمٰى  وَ  الْمَسٰكِیْنُ  فَارْزُقُوْهُمْ  مِّنْهُ  وَ  قُوْلُوْا  لَهُمْ  قَوْلًا  مَّعْرُوْفًا(8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab taqseem ke waqt rishtedaar, yateem aur miskeen aa jaayein to unhein bhi us maal mein se kuchh de do aur un se achhi baat kaho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Jin logon ka virasat mein hissa hai un ka bayan baad ki aayaton mein hai. Phir farmaya: “Agar yeh aayat na hoti to main yeh kharch apne maal se karta.” (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۸، ۱ / ۳۴۸)

Tafseer Qurtubi mein hai ke Hazrat Ubaidah Sulmani aur Imam Muhammad bin Sirin رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہما dono ne aisa hi kiya. (قرطبی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۸، ۳ / ۳۶، الجزء الخامس)

Dar Mansoor mein hai ke Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Abu Bakr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne apne walid ki virasat taqseem ki to usi maal se bakri zibh karwa kar khaana pakwaya. Jab Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ki bargah mein yeh baat arz ki gayi to unhone farmaya: “Abdur Rahman رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Quran par amal kiya.” (در منثور، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۸، ۲ / ۴۴۰)

Yeh bachche pote hone ki wajah se aur apne chacha ki mojoodgi mein dada ki virasat se mehroom ho jaate hain. Dada ko chahiye ke un poton ke liye wasiyyat karke unhein maal ka haqdaar banaye. Agar dada ne aisa na kiya ho to waris apne hissa mein se unhein kuchh dein. Na-baaligh ya ghair-maujood waris ke hissa mein se dena jaiz nahin.

## Ayat 4:9

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Yateemon ke sarparaston se farmaya gaya hai ke Allah Ta'ala se daro aur yateemon ki parwarish is khayal se karo ke agar hamare bachche yateem reh jaayein aur koi doosra un ki parwarish kare to hum un ke liye kaisi parwarish pasand kareinge. Apne liye jo pasand ho wohi doosron ke liye pasand karo, aur jo apne liye pasand na ho woh doosron ke liye bhi pasand na karo. Yeh aayat akhlaaq ki behtareen taleem hai.

Hadees-e-mubarak mein hai: “Tum mein koi us waqt tak kaamil momin nahin ho sakta jab tak apne bhai ke liye wohi pasand na kare jo apne liye pasand karta hai.” (بخاری، کتاب الایمان، باب من الایمان ان یحبّ لاخیہ۔۔۔ الخ، ۱ / ۱۶، الحدیث: ۱۳)

Yateemon ke sarparaston ko chahiye ke Allah se darein aur un se achhi aur durust baat kahein, jaise: “Fikr na karo, hum bhi tumhare baap jaise hain. Tumhein pareshani nahin aane denge.” (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۹، ۱ / ۳۴۹؛ مدارک، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۹، ص ۲۱۲، ملتقطاً)

## Ayat 4:10

`Source: tafseerId=50593; ayatId=507; surah=4; ayat=10; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ  الَّذِیْنَ  یَاْكُلُوْنَ  اَمْوَالَ  الْیَتٰمٰى  ظُلْمًا  اِنَّمَا  یَاْكُلُوْنَ  فِیْ  بُطُوْنِهِمْ  نَارًاؕ-وَ  سَیَصْلَوْنَ  سَعِیْرًا۠    (10)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak woh log jo zulm karte hue yateemon ka maal khaate hain, woh apne peton mein sirf aag bharte hain aur jald hi bhadakti hui aag mein daakhil honge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Pichhli aayaton mein yateemon ka maal na-haq khaane se mana kiya gaya aur is aayat mein is par sakht wa'eed bayan hui. Yateem kamzor aur be-bas hote hain, is liye yeh un par Allah Ta'ala ki rahmat hai. “Woh apne pet mein aag bharte hain” ka matlab yeh hai ke yateem ka maal na-haq khaana goya aag khaana hai, kyun ke yeh Jahannam ki aag ke azaab ka sabab hai. (تفسیر کبیر، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۰، ۳ / ۵۰۶)

Yateem ka maal na-haq khaane par ahadees mein sakht wa'eedein hain:

1. Hazrat Buraidah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din ek qaum apni qabron se is haal mein uthai jaayegi ke un ke munh se aag nikal rahi hogi.” Arz ki gayi: “Ya Rasool Allah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, woh kaun log honge?” Farmaya: “Kya tumne Allah Ta'ala ka yeh farman nahin dekha: اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَاْكُلُوْنَ اَمْوَالَ الْیَتٰمٰى ظُلْمًا اِنَّمَا یَاْكُلُوْنَ فِیْ بُطُوْنِهِمْ نَارًا وَ سَیَصْلَوْنَ سَعِیْرًا۔” (کنز العمال، کتاب البیوع، قسم الاقوال، ۲ / ۹، الجزء الرابع، الحدیث: ۹۲۷۹)
2. Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Main ne Meraj ki raat aise log dekhe jin ke hont oonton ke honton jaise the. Un par aise log muqarrar the jo un ke hont pakarte aur un ke munh mein aag ke pathar daalte, jo un ke pichhe se nikal jaate.” Main ne poocha: “Aye Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, yeh kaun log hain?” Arz ki: “Yeh woh log hain jo yateemon ka maal zulm se khaate the.” (تہذیب الآثار، مسند عبد اللہ بن عباس، السفر الاول، ذکر من روی عن النبی صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم انہ رأی، ۲ / ۴۶۷، الحدیث: ۷۲۵)
3. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Chaar log aise hain jinhein Jannat mein daakhil na karna aur us ki ne'matein na chakhana Allah Ta'ala par haq hai: (1) sharaab ka aadi, (2) sood khaane wala, (3) na-haq yateem ka maal khaane wala, (4) walidain ka na-farman.” (مستدرک، کتاب البیوع، انّ اربی الربا عرض الرجل المسلم، ۲ / ۳۳۸، الحدیث: ۲۳۰۷)

Yateem ka maal na-haq khaana kabirah gunah aur sakht haraam hai. Is ka matlab sirf yeh nahin ke koi aadmi buri niyyat se khaaye; kai baar aadmi ko haraam ka ilm bhi nahin hota aur woh is gunah mein mubtala ho jata hai. Misal ke taur par agar marhoom ke warison mein yateem ho to yateem ke maal se ya mile-jule maal se fateha, teja waghera ka khaana haraam hai, kyun ke us mein yateem ka haq shamil hai. Aise khaane fuqara ke liye aur baalig mojood warison ke maal se tayyar kiye jaayein. Jo jaan-boojh kar yateem ka maal khaayega woh Jahannam ki aag khaayega.

### Yateem ki achhi parwarish ke fazaail

Jis ke saaye mein koi yateem ho, use yateem ki achhi parwarish karni chahiye. Ahadees mein is ke bahut fazaail hain:

1. Hazrat Sahl رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Main aur yateem ki kafaalat karne wala Jannat mein is tarah honge.” Phir aap ne shahadat wali aur darmiyani ungli se ishara kiya aur dono ko kushadah kiya. (بخاری، کتاب الطلاق، باب اللعان، ۳ / ۴۹۷، الحدیث: ۵۳۰۴)
2. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Imam-ul-Anbiya صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Musalmanon ke kisi yateem bachche ke khaane-peene ki zimmedari li, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ use Jannat mein daakhil farmayega, magar yeh ke woh aisa gunah kare jis ki maafi na ho.” (ترمذی، کتاب البر والصلۃ، باب ما جاء فی رحمۃ الیتیم، وکفالتہ، ۳ / ۳۶۸، الحدیث: ۱۹۲۴)
3. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Musalmanon ke gharon mein sab se achha ghar woh hai jahan yateem se achha sulook kiya jaye, aur sab se bura ghar woh hai jahan yateem se bura sulook kiya jaye.” (ابن ماجہ، کتاب الادب، باب حق الیتیم، ۴ / ۱۹۳، الحدیث: ۳۶۷۹)
4. Hazrat Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rahmat-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raza ke liye yateem ke sar par haath rakha, us ke liye har us baal ke badle neki hai jis par us ka haath guzra.” (مسند امام احمد، مسند الانصار، حدیث ابی امامۃ الباہلی، ۸ / ۳۰۰، الحدیث: ۲۲۳۴۷)

## Ayat 4:11

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah tumhein tumhari aulaad ke baare mein hukm deta hai: bete ka hissa do betiyon ke barabar hai. Phir agar sirf ladkiyan hon, agarche do se zyada hon, to un ke liye tarke ka do tihai hissa hoga, aur agar ek ladki ho to us ke liye aadha hissa hai. Aur agar mayyit ki aulaad ho to us ke maa-baap mein se har ek ke liye tarke se chhata hissa hoga. Phir agar mayyit ki aulaad na ho aur maa-baap chhore to maa ke liye tihai hissa hai. Phir agar us mayyit ke kai bhai-behen hon to maa ka chhata hissa hoga. Yeh sab ahkaam wasiyyat poori karne aur qarz ada karne ke baad hain. Tumhare baap aur tumhare bete, tum nahin jaante ke in mein kaun tumhein zyada nafa dega. Yeh Allah ki taraf se muqarrar kiya hua hissa hai. Beshak Allah bade ilm wala, hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne virasat ke ahkaam bayan farmaye hain. Virasat ka masla tafseeli hota hai aur sirf kitaab padh kar har surat ko hal karna mushkil ho sakta hai. Is liye in masail ko kisi maahir-e-ilm-e-miras ke paas baith kar mashq ke saath samjhein. Yahan un warison ki mukhtalif suratain bayan ki ja rahi hain jo in aayaat mein zikr hui hain. Virasat ka masla hal karte waqt in hisson ke saath doosre usool aur qawaid bhi dekhe jaate hain; mazeed tafseel ke liye ilm-e-miras ki kitaabein padhein.

### Warison mein virasat taqseem karne ki suratain

1. **Baap ki teen suratain:** Agar mayyit ka baap ho aur beta bhi ho to baap ko ek bata chhe milega. Agar beta na ho, sirf beti ho, to baap ko ek bata chhe milega aur doosre warison ke hisse dene ke baad jo maal bachega woh baap ko 'asabah ke taur par milega. Agar na beta ho na beti ho to baap 'asabah ke taur par maal paayega.

2. **Maa-shareek bhai ki teen suratain:** Agar maa-shareek bhai ek ho to use ek bata chhe milega. Agar do ya zyada maa-shareek bhai-behen hon, chahe bhai hon, behnein hon ya dono hon, to sab ko mila kar ek bata teen milega. Baap, dada, beta, beti, pota ya poti maujood hon to maa-shareek bhai mehroom ho jata hai. Maa-shareek behen ke bhi yahi ahkaam hain.

3. **Shohar ki do suratain:** Agar wafat paane wali aurat ki aulaad ho to shohar ko ek bata chaar milega. Agar us ki aulaad na ho to shohar ko aadha maal milega.

4. **Biwi ki do suratain:** Agar wafat paane wale mard ki aulaad ho to biwi ko ek bata aath milega. Agar us ki aulaad na ho to biwi ko ek bata chaar milega.

5. **Beti ki teen suratain:** Agar ek beti ho to use aadha maal milega. Agar do ya zyada betiyan hon to un sab ko mila kar do bata teen milega. Agar betiyon ke saath beta bhi ho to betiyan 'asabah ban jaayengi aur bete ko beti se do guna diya jaayega.

6. **Maa ki teen suratain:** Agar mayyit ka beta, beti, pota, poti ya kisi bhi qisam ke do bhai-behen hon to maa ko kul maal ka ek bata chhe milega. Agar mayyit ki aulaad na ho aur bhai-behen mein se do afraad bhi na hon, chahe ek bhai ya behen ho, to maa ko kul maal ka ek bata teen milega. Agar mayyit ne biwi aur maa-baap, ya shohar aur maa-baap chhore hon, to biwi ya shohar ka hissa dene ke baad jo maal bachega us ka ek bata teen maa ko diya jaayega.

### Do aham usool

- Bete ko beti se do guna milta hai. Jahan bhai 'asabah bante hon, wahan unhein behnon se do guna milta hai. Kai suratain aisi bhi hoti hain jahan behnein 'asabah ban kar faraiz walon ke hisse dene ke baad bacha hua maal le leti hain.
- Qareebi waris ke maujood hone par door ka waris mehroom ho jata hai. Misal ke taur par bete ke hote hue pota, baap ke hote hue dada, aur bhai ke hote hue bhai ki aulaad mehroom hoti hai.

Zina aur qazf ki saza ka bayan Surah An-Noor ki aayat 2 aur 4 mein hua hai.

### Zina ki mazammat

Is aayat mein zina karne walon ki saza se muta'alliq kuchh ahkaam bayan hue. Isi munasabat se yahan zina ki mazammat par chaar ahadees zikr ki jaati hain, taake Musalmanon par zina ki burai aur qabahat mazeed wazeh ho aur woh is bure amal se bachne ki koshish karein.

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo aurat kisi qaum mein aise shakhs ko daakhil kar de jo us qaum se na ho (yani zina karaya aur us se aulaad hui), use Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki rahmat ka hissa nahin milega aur Allah Ta'ala use Jannat mein daakhil na farmayega.” (Abu Dawood, Kitab-ut-Talaq, Bab-ut-Taghleez fil-Intifa, 2/406, hadees: 2263)

2. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis basti mein zina aur sood zahir ho jaaye to un logon ne apne liye Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke azaab ko halaal kar liya.” (Mustadrak, Kitab-ul-Buyoo, Iza Zahara-z-Zina war-Riba fi Qaryatin, 2/339, hadees: 2308)

3. Hazrat Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis qaum mein zina zahir hoga, woh qaht mein giraftaar hogi aur jis qaum mein rishwat ka zahoor hoga, woh rob mein giraftaar hogi.” (Mishkaat-ul-Masabeeh, Kitab-ul-Hudood, al-Fasl-us-Saalis, 1/656, hadees: 3582)

4. Hazrat Buraidah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Saaton aasman aur saton zameenein boorhe zaani par laanat karti hain aur zaniyon ki sharmgaah ki badboo Jahannam walon ko aziyyat degi.” (Majma'-uz-Zawaid, Kitab-ul-Hudood wad-Diyaat, Bab Zamm-uz-Zina, 6/389, hadees: 10541)

## Ayat 4:16

`Source: tafseerId=50597; ayatId=513; surah=4; ayat=16; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  الَّذٰنِ  یَاْتِیٰنِهَا  مِنْكُمْ  فَاٰذُوْهُمَاۚ-فَاِنْ  تَابَا  وَ  اَصْلَحَا  فَاَعْرِضُوْا  عَنْهُمَاؕ-اِنَّ  اللّٰهَ  كَانَ  تَوَّابًا  رَّحِیْمًا(16)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur tum mein jo mard aur aurat aisa kaam karein unhein takleef pahunchaao, phir agar woh taubah kar lein aur apni islaah kar lein to un ka peecha chhor do. Beshak Allah bara taubah qubool karne wala, Meherban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ فَاٰذُوْهُمَا: In dono ko takleef pahunchaao. }**

Be-hayai ka irtikaab karne walon ki saza bayan karte hue farmaya gaya ke unhein jhidak kar, bura-bhala keh kar, sharm dila kar, jootiyan waghera maar kar, zabaani aur jismani dono tarah se takleef do. Zina ki saza pehle takleef dena muqarrar ki gayi, phir qaid karna, phir kore maarna ya sangsaar karna. (Madaarik, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 16, safha 217)

Yeh aayat bhi zina ki hadd wali aayat se mansookh hai. Baaz ulama ne farmaya ke pichhli aayat mein “fahishah” se murad khud aurat ka aurat se be-hayai ka kaam karna hai aur **وَ الَّذٰنِ یَاْتِیٰنِهَا** se mard ka mard se liwaatat karna murad hai. Is surat mein yeh aayat mansookh nahin balki muhkam hai. Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke liwaatat aur musaahaqat (auraton ka auraton se be-hayai ka kaam) mein hadd muqarrar nahin, balki ta'zeer hai. Yani qazi ki soch aur faisle par hai ke woh jo saza chahe de. Yehi Imam-e-A'zam Abu Hanifah ka qawl hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 16, 3/528; Tafseeraat-e-Ahmadiyyah, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 16, safha 242, mulakhkhasan)

Isi wajah se liwaatat karne wale ko Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne mukhtalif sazaein dein. Agar liwaatat mein hadd hoti to ek hi saza di jaati aur is mein ikhtilaaf na hota. “Hadd” muqarrar hoti hai, jaise sau kore ya assi kore. Is ke muqaable mein ta'zeer wahan hoti hai jahan shar'i hadd muqarrar na ho, balki qazi ki soch par chhor di jaaye. Woh chahe to das kore maarne ka faisla kare, chahe to bees kore, aur chahe to koi aur saza de.

**{ فَاِنْ تَابَا وَ اَصْلَحَا: Phir agar woh taubah kar lein aur apni islaah kar lein. }**

Farmaya gaya ke be-hayai ka irtikaab karne wale agar pichhle gunahon par naadim ho jaayen aur aainda apni islaah kar lein to unhein chhor do. Is se maloom hua ke ta'zeer ke mustahiq mujrim ne ta'zeer se pehle sachchi taubah kar li to us par bila-wajah ta'zeer lagana zaroori nahin.

### Taubah ke ma'ni

Taubah ke ma'ni rujoo karna aur lautna hain. Agar yeh bande ki sifat ho to is ke ma'ni gunah ya gunah ke iraade se rujoo karna hain. Aur agar Rab Ta'ala ki sifat ho to is ke ma'ni bande ki taubah qubool farmana ya apni rahmat ko bande ki taraf mutawajjah karna hain.

**Arabic:** وَ  لَیْسَتِ  التَّوْبَةُ  لِلَّذِیْنَ  یَعْمَلُوْنَ  السَّیِّاٰتِۚ-حَتّٰۤى  اِذَا  حَضَرَ  اَحَدَهُمُ  الْمَوْتُ  قَالَ  اِنِّیْ  تُبْتُ  الْــٴٰـنَ وَ  لَا  الَّذِیْنَ  یَمُوْتُوْنَ  وَ  هُمْ  كُفَّارٌؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ  اَعْتَدْنَا  لَهُمْ  عَذَابًا  اَلِیْمًا(18)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un logon ki taubah nahin jo gunahon mein lage rehte hain yahan tak ke jab un mein kisi ko maut aaye to kehne lagte hain: ab main ne taubah ki, aur na un logon ki koi taubah hai jo kufr ki haalat mein marein. Un ke liye Hum ne dardnaak azaab tayyar kar rakha hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَیْسَتِ التَّوْبَةُ: Aur taubah qubool nahin.}** Pichhli aayat mein taubah qubool hone ka jo wa'da guzra tha, is aayat mein us ki wazahat ki gayi hai aur bataya ja raha hai ke kin logon ki taubah qubool nahin hogi.

Aayat mein “sayyi'aat” se murad gunah li jaaye to ma'na yeh hoga ke jo log kufr ke ilawa doosre gunahon mein mubtala rahe, yahan tak ke maut ke asaar zahir ho gaye, Allah Ta'ala ke azaab ka mushahadah kar liya aur rooh halaq tak aa pahunchi, ab taubah karein to woh qubool nahin hogi. Lekin maut ka yeh waqt aane se ek lamhah pehle bhi taubah kar li jaaye to qubool hai.

Agar in Musalmanon ki taubah qubool na bhi ho to bhi woh hamesha Jahannam mein nahin rahenge. Allah Ta'ala chahe to unhein bakhsh de aur chahe to azaab de, lekin azaab poora hone ke baad Jannat mein jaayenge. Is ke baraks jo log kufr ki haalat mein mare, Qiyamat ke din un ki taubah qubool nahin hogi. Woh kisi surat nijaat nahin paa sakenge aur hamesha Jahannam ke azaab mein mubtala rahenge.

Ek qawl yeh hai ke aayat mein “sayyi'aat” se murad kufr hai. Is surat mein ma'na yeh hoga ke woh kaafir jo maut ke asaar dekh kar, yani ghaib ka mushahadah karne ke baad, apne kufr se taubah karein aur imaan ka iqraar karein, un ki taubah aur iqraar-e-imaan qubool nahin. Aisi taubah to Fir'aun ne bhi ki thi. Isi tarah jo log kufr ki haalat mein mar gaye, yani maut ke waqt bhi taubah na ki, woh hamesha Jahannam ki saza paayenge. (تفسیر قرطبی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۸، ۳ / ۶۶، الجزء الخامس، تفسیر کبیر، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۸، ۴ / ۸، ملتقطاً)

### Kaafir ke liye dua-e-maghfirat karne ka shar'i hukm

Jo kisi kaafir ke inteqaal ke baad us ke kufr ka ilm hote hue us ke liye dua-e-maghfirat kare, ya kisi murdah murtad ko marhoom ya maghfoor kahe, ya kisi mare hue Hindu ko baikunth-baashi, yani Jannati, kahe, woh khud kaafir hai. (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 1, Imaan-o-Kufr ka bayan, 1/185)

Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

مَا كَانَ لِلنَّبِیِّ وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اَنْ یَّسْتَغْفِرُوْا لِلْمُشْرِكِیْنَ وَ لَوْ كَانُوْۤا اُولِیْ قُرْبٰى مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مَا تَبَیَّنَ لَهُمْ اَنَّهُمْ اَصْحٰبُ الْجَحِیْمِ(توبہ: ۱۱۳)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Nabi aur imaan walon ke laayiq nahin ke mushrikon ke liye maghfirat ki dua maangein agarche woh rishtedaar hon, jabke un ke liye wazeh ho chuka ho ke woh Dozakh wale hain.

## Ayat 4:19

`Source: tafseerId=50600; ayatId=516; surah=4; ayat=19; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا  الَّذِیْنَ  اٰمَنُوْا  لَا  یَحِلُّ  لَكُمْ  اَنْ  تَرِثُوا  النِّسَآءَ  كَرْهًاؕ-وَ  لَا  تَعْضُلُوْهُنَّ  لِتَذْهَبُوْا  بِبَعْضِ   مَاۤ  اٰتَیْتُمُوْهُنَّ  اِلَّاۤ  اَنْ  یَّاْتِیْنَ  بِفَاحِشَةٍ  مُّبَیِّنَةٍۚ-وَ  عَاشِرُوْهُنَّ  بِالْمَعْرُوْفِۚ-فَاِنْ  كَرِهْتُمُوْهُنَّ  فَعَسٰۤى  اَنْ  تَكْرَهُوْا  شَیْــٴًـا  وَّ  یَجْعَلَ  اللّٰهُ  فِیْهِ  خَیْرًا  كَثِیْرًا(19)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aye imaan walon! Tumhare liye halaal nahin ke tum zabardasti auraton ke waaris ban jao aur auraton ko is niyyat se roko nahin ke jo mahr tum ne unhein diya tha us mein se kuchh le lo, siwaye is surat ke woh khuli be-hayaai ka irtikaab karein, aur un ke saath achhe tareeqe se guzara karo. Phir agar tumhein woh na-pasand hon to mumkin hai koi cheez tumhein na-pasand ho aur Allah us mein bahut bhalai rakh de.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا یَحِلُّ لَكُمْ اَنْ تَرِثُوا النِّسَآءَ كَرْهًا: Tumhare liye halaal nahin ke tum zabardasti auraton ke waaris ban jao.}** Islam se pehle Arabon mein yeh rasm thi ke log maal ki tarah apne rishtedaron ki biwiyon ke bhi waaris ban jaate the. Phir agar chahte to bina mahr unhein apni biwiyon ke taur par rakhte, ya kisi aur se shaadi kar dete aur un ka mahr khud le lete. Kabhi unhein is liye bhi rokte ke woh unhein diya hua maal un logon ko de dein, phir un ki jaan chhor di jaaye, ya is liye rokte ke woh mar jaayen to yeh log un ke waaris ban jaayen. Gharz woh auratein bilkul majboor hoti thin aur apni marzi se kuchh nahin kar sakti thin. Is rasm ko mitaane ke liye yeh aayat naazil hui. (بخاری، کتاب التفسیر، باب لا یحلّ لکم ان ترثوا النساء کرہًا، ۳ / ۲۰۳، الحدیث: ۴۵۷۹، تفسیر قرطبی، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۹، ۳ / ۶۷، الجزء الخامس، ملتقطاً)

**{لِتَذْهَبُوْا بِبَعْضِ مَاۤ اٰتَیْتُمُوْهُنَّ: Taake jo mahr tum ne unhein diya tha us mein se kuchh le lo.}** Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُما ne farmaya: Yeh aayat us shakhs ke baare mein hai jo apni biwi se nafrat karta ho aur us se is liye bad-sulooki karta ho ke woh pareshaan hokar mahr wapas kar de ya mahr maaf kar de. Allah Ta'ala ne is se mana farma diya. Ek qawl yeh hai ke log aurat ko talaaq dete, phir rujoo kar lete, phir talaaq dete. Is tarah aurat ko latka hua rakhte the. Na woh un ke paas aaraam paa sakti thi aur na doosri jagah shaadi karke ghar basa sakti thi. Is se bhi mana farmaya gaya. (خازن، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۱۹، ۱ / ۳۶۰)

### Biwiyon par zulm karne wale ghaur karein

Jahaaliyyat ke zamane ke jo halaat yahan bayan kiye ja rahe hain, un par ghaur karein. Kya aaj hamara muashrah bhi inhi halaat par nahin chal raha? Biwiyon ko tang karna, zabardasti mahr maaf karwana, un ke huqooq ada na karna, zehni aziyyat dena, kabhi aurat ko us ke maa-baap ke ghar bitha dena aur kabhi apne ghar mein rakh kar baat-cheet band kar dena, doosron ke saamne daantna aur jhidakna, aur is tarah ki doosri sakhtiyan hamare gharon mein paayi ja rahi hain. Aurat bechaari shohar ke peechhe peechhe chal rahi hoti hai aur shohar Fir'aun bana aage aage ja raha hota hai. Aurat ke ghar walon se, ya biwi ke zariye, naye naye mutaalibaat kiye jaate hain. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ kare ke Quran ki yeh aayaat in logon ko samajh aa jaayen aur woh apni buri rawish se baaz aa jaayen.

In aayaat ki roshni mein woh log bhi ghaur karein jo Islam se sharminda rehte hain aur chhupe hue alfaaz mein kehte hain ke Islam mein auraton par bahut sakhtiyan hain. Woh dekhein ke Islam ne auraton par sakhti ki hai ya unhein sakhtiyon se nijaat dilaai hai.

**{فَعَسٰۤى اَنْ تَكْرَهُوْا شَیْــٴًـا: Mumkin hai koi cheez tumhein na-pasand ho.}** Gharon ko aman ka gahuara banane ke liye ek behtareen zehni tareeqa bataya ja raha hai. Agar bad-akhlaaqi ya khoobsurati na hone ki wajah se biwi pasand na ho to sabr karo aur talaaq dene mein jaldi na karo. Mumkin hai Allah Ta'ala isi biwi se tumhein aisi aulaad de jo nek aur farmabardaar ho aur budhaape ki be-basi mein tumhara sahaara bane.

### Musbat zehni soch ke fawaid

Yeh tareeqa sirf miyan-biwi ke ta'alluqaat mein nahin, balki zindagi ke hazaaron maamlaat mein kaam aata hai. Isay “musbat zehni soch” kehte hain. Yani agar kisi kaam ya cheez mein koi kharabi nazar aa rahi ho to us ke achhe pehluon par bhi ghaur karo. Mumkin hai achhe pehlu zyada hon ya koi ek achha pehlu zyada faidemand ho.

Misal ke taur par kisi ki biwi badsoorat hai, lekin usi se aadmi ko nek aulaad mili hai. Agar woh badsoorti ko bardaasht kar le to zindagi aman se guzregi. Lekin agar talaaq de de to biwi ke saath bachche bhi chhoot jaayenge aur saari zindagi talkhiyon, zehni aziyyat, aulaad ke liye jadd-o-jahad aur huqooq ki laraai mein guzregi. Isi tarah kisi idare mein ek aadmi kisi wajah se na-pasand hai, lekin usi ki wajah se nizaam bahut achha chal raha hai. Sirf na-pasand hone ki wajah se use nikaal dena poore nizaam ko tabah kar dega. Aisi jagah fawaid ko saamne rakh kar use bardaasht kar lena behtar hai.

Zindagi ke bahut se maamlaat in misaalon ko saamne rakh kar hal kiye ja sakte hain. Sirf “musbat zehni soch” paida karne ki zaroorat hai; zindagi ki bahut si talkhiyan khud-ba-khud khatam ho jaayengi. Is soch ka ek pehlu yeh bhi hai ke aadmi pareshaniyon ke bajaye ne'maton ko saamne rakhe. Agar aadmi ek takleef mein hai to usi waqt lakhon ne'maton aur saikron kaamyaabiyon mein bhi hai. Phir pareshani aur nakaami ko apna wazifa banane aur khushi aur kaamyaabi ko bilkul na sochne ki kya zaroorat hai? Is nuskhe par amal karke dekhein, InshaAllah عَزَّوَجَلَّ zindagi mein khushiyan hi khushiyan bhar jaayengi.

## Ayat 4:20

**Arabic:** وَ  اِنْ  اَرَدْتُّمُ  اسْتِبْدَالَ  زَوْجٍ  مَّكَانَ  زَوْجٍۙ-وَّ  اٰتَیْتُمْ  اِحْدٰىهُنَّ  قِنْطَارًا  فَلَا  تَاْخُذُوْا  مِنْهُ  شَیْــٴًـاؕ-اَتَاْخُذُوْنَهٗ  بُهْتَانًا  وَّ  اِثْمًا  مُّبِیْنًا(20)

Arabon mein yeh bhi rasm thi ke jab kisi aadmi ko apni biwi ke ilawa koi doosri aurat pasand aa jaati to woh apni biwi par jhooti tohmat lagaata taake woh pareshaan hokar diya hua maal wapas kar de aur talaaq le le. ### Zyada mahr muqarrar karna jaiz hai

Is aayat mein dheron maal dene ka zikr hai. Is se maloom hua ke zyada mahr muqarrar karna jaiz hai, agarche behtar kam mahr ya itna mahr hai jis ki adaigi aasaan ho.

Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne ek martaba mimbar par farmaya ke aurat ka mahr zyada muqarrar na karo. Ek aurat ne yahi aayat padh kar kaha: “Aye Ameer-ul-Momineen! Allah humein deta hai aur aap mana karte hain.” Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Aye Umar! Tum se har shakhs zyada samajhdaar hai. Aye logo! Tum jo chaho mahr muqarrar karo.” (مدارک، النساء، تحت الآیۃ: ۲۰، ص ۲۱۹)

SubhanAllah! Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki shaan-e-insaaf aur paaki-e-nafs kitni buland thi. Allah Ta'ala humein un ki pairwi ki taufeeq ata farmaye, Aameen.

## Ayat 4:22

`Source: tafseerId=50603; ayatId=519; surah=4; ayat=22; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ  لَا  تَنْكِحُوْا  مَا  نَكَحَ  اٰبَآؤُكُمْ  مِّنَ  النِّسَآءِ  اِلَّا  مَا  قَدْ  سَلَفَؕ-اِنَّهٗ  كَانَ  فَاحِشَةً  وَّ  مَقْتًاؕ-وَ  سَآءَ  سَبِیْلًا۠    (22)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَا تَنْكِحُوْا مَا نَكَحَ اٰبَآؤُكُمْ: Aur apne baap-dada ki mankoohah se nikah na karo.}** Zamana-e-Jaahiliyyat mein rasm thi ke baap ke inteqaal ke baad beta apni saggi maa ko chhor kar baap ki doosri biwi se shaadi kar leta tha. ## Ayat 4:23

`Source: tafseerId=50604; ayatId=520; surah=4; ayat=23; para=4; volume=2`

**Arabic:** حُرِّمَتْ  عَلَیْكُمْ  اُمَّهٰتُكُمْ  وَ  بَنٰتُكُمْ  وَ  اَخَوٰتُكُمْ  وَ  عَمّٰتُكُمْ  وَ  خٰلٰتُكُمْ  وَ  بَنٰتُ  الْاَخِ  وَ  بَنٰتُ  الْاُخْتِ  وَ  اُمَّهٰتُكُمُ  الّٰتِیْۤ  اَرْضَعْنَكُمْ  وَ  اَخَوٰتُكُمْ  مِّنَ  الرَّضَاعَةِ  وَ  اُمَّهٰتُ  نِسَآىٕكُمْ  وَ  رَبَآىٕبُكُمُ  الّٰتِیْ  فِیْ  حُجُوْرِكُمْ  مِّنْ  نِّسَآىٕكُمُ  الّٰتِیْ  دَخَلْتُمْ  بِهِنَّ٘-فَاِنْ  لَّمْ  تَكُوْنُوْا  دَخَلْتُمْ  بِهِنَّ  فَلَا  جُنَاحَ  عَلَیْكُمْ٘-وَ  حَلَآىٕلُ  اَبْنَآىٕكُمُ  الَّذِیْنَ  مِنْ  اَصْلَابِكُمْۙ-وَ  اَنْ  تَجْمَعُوْا  بَیْنَ  الْاُخْتَیْنِ  اِلَّا  مَا  قَدْ  سَلَفَؕ-اِنَّ  اللّٰهَ  كَانَ  غَفُوْرًا  رَّحِیْمًاۙ    (23)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum par haraam kar di gayin tumhari maaen aur tumhari betiyan aur tumhari behnein aur tumhari phoophiyaan aur tumhari khaalaen aur tumhari bhatijiyan aur tumhari bhanjiyan aur tumhari woh maaen jinhone tumhein doodh pilaya aur doodh ke rishte se tumhari behnein aur tumhari biwiyon ki maaen aur tumhari biwiyon ki woh betiyan jo tumhari god mein hain, un biwiyon se jin se tum sohbat kar chuke ho. Phir agar tum ne un biwiyon se sohbat na ki ho to un ki betiyon se nikah karne mein tum par koi haraj nahin. Aur tumhare un asli beton ki biwiyan jo tumhari pusht se hain aur do behnon ko ek saath nikah mein rakhna haraam hai, siwaye jo pehle guzar chuka. Beshak Allah bakhshne wala, Meherban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{حُرِّمَتْ عَلَیْكُمْ اُمَّهٰتُكُمْ: Tum par haraam kar di gayin tumhari maaen.}** Nasab ki wajah se saat auratein haraam hain:

1. Maa. Isi tarah baap ya maa ke zariye jis aurat se nasab banta ho, yani daadiyan aur naaniyan, chahe qareeb ki hon ya door ki, sab maaon ke hukm mein hain. Sauteli maaon ki hurmat ka zikr pehle guzar chuka hai.
2. Beti. Potiyaan aur naatiyaan kisi bhi darje ki hon, betiyon mein shamil hain.
3. Behen.
4. Phoophi.
5. Khaala.
6. Bhatiji.
7. Bhanji. Is mein bhatijiyon, bhanjiyon aur un ki aulaad bhi shamil hai.

Khulasa yeh hai ke apni aulaad aur apne usool, yani jin se insaan paida hua hai, haraam hain. Is ki tafseel isi aayat mein aage aa rahi hai.

**{وَ اُمَّهٰتُكُمُ الّٰتِیْۤ اَرْضَعْنَكُمْ: Aur tumhari woh maaen jinhone tumhein doodh pilaya.}** Doodh ke rishte ko raza'at kehte hain. Doodh ki maaon aur doodh ke behen-bhaiyon se bhi nikah haraam hai. Balki doodh ke bhatije, bhanje, khaala, maamoon waghera sab se nikah haraam hai. Hadees-e-mubarak mein farmaya gaya ke jo rishta nasab ki wajah se haraam hota hai, woh raza'at ki wajah se bhi haraam hota hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ush-Shahaadaat, Baab-ush-Shahaadah alan-ansaab... alkh, 2/191, Hadees: 2645)

**{وَ اُمَّهٰتُ نِسَآىٕكُمْ: Aur tumhari biwiyon ki maaen.}** Susraali rishton ki wajah se chaar tarah ki auratein haraam hoti hain:

1. Woh biwi jis se sohbat ki gayi ho, us ki betiyan.
2. Biwi ki maaen, daadiyan aur naaniyan.
3. Baap, dada waghera usool ki biwiyan.
4. Bete, pote waghera faroo' ki biwiyan.

**{وَ رَبَآىٕبُكُمْ: Aur tumhari sauteli betiyan.}** Jin biwiyon se sohbat ho chuki ho, un ke doosre shohar se paida hone wali beti se nikah haraam hai, agarche woh shohar ki parwarish mein na ho, kyun ke parwarish ki qaid aam taur par hoti hai. Yeh sauteli beti sirf us shohar par haraam hoti hai, us shohar ki aulaad ke liye halaal hoti hai. Shauhar par bhi us waqt haraam hoti hai jab biwi se sohbat kar chuka ho. Agar biwi ko sohbat se pehle talaaq di ya woh wafaat paa gayi to us ki beti halaal hai.

**{وَ حَلَآىٕلُ اَبْنَآىٕكُمْ: Aur tumhare haqeeqi beton ki biwiyan.}** Is se maloom hua ke munh-bole beton ki auraton se nikah jaiz hai. Doodh ke bete ki biwi bhi haraam hai, kyun ke woh nasabi bete ke hukm mein hai. Pote aur parpote bhi beton mein shamil hain.

**{وَ اَنْ تَجْمَعُوْا بَیْنَ الْاُخْتَیْنِ: Aur do behnon ko ek saath rakhna.}** Yani ek behen nikah mein maujood ho aur doosri se bhi nikah kar liya jaye, yeh haraam hai. Hadees Shareef mein phoophi aur bhatiji, aur khaala aur bhanji ko ek shakhs ke nikah mein jama karna bhi haraam farmaya gaya hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-un-Nikaah, Baab la tunkah-il-mar'atu ala ammatiha, 3/435, Hadees: 5109)

Tafseeli maloomat ke liye Fatawa Razawiyyah, jild 11, aur Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 7 mein “Muharramaat ka bayan” padhein.

## Ayat 4:24

`Source: tafseerId=50605; ayatId=521; surah=4; ayat=24; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَّ الْمُحْصَنٰتُ مِنَ النِّسَآءِ اِلَّا مَا مَلَكَتْ اَیْمَانُكُمْۚ-كِتٰبَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْۚ-وَ اُحِلَّ لَكُمْ مَّا وَرَآءَ ذٰلِكُمْ اَنْ تَبْتَغُوْا بِاَمْوَالِكُمْ مُّحْصِنِیْنَ غَیْرَ مُسٰفِحِیْنَؕ-فَمَا اسْتَمْتَعْتُمْ بِهٖ مِنْهُنَّ فَاٰتُوْهُنَّ اُجُوْرَهُنَّ فَرِیْضَةًؕ-وَ لَا جُنَاحَ عَلَیْكُمْ فِیْمَا تَرٰضَیْتُمْ بِهٖ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ الْفَرِیْضَةِؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَلِیْمًا حَكِیْمًا(24)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur shohar wali auratein tum par haraam hain, siwaye kaafiron ki un auraton ke jo tumhari milkiyat mein aa jaayen. Yeh tum par Allah ka likha hua hai. Aur in auraton ke ilawa sab tumhare liye halaal hain ke tum apne maal ke zariye unhein nikah mein talaash karo, na ke zina ke liye. To jin auraton se nikah karna chaho unhein un ke muqarrar mahr de do. Aur muqarrar mahr ke baad agar tum aapas mein kisi miqdaar par raazi ho jao to is mein tum par koi gunah nahin. Beshak Allah ilm wala, hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ الْمُحْصَنٰتُ مِنَ النِّسَآءِ: Aur shohar wali auratein.}** Jin auraton se nikah haraam hai, un ka bayan chal raha hai. Yahan bataya gaya ke jis aurat ka shohar ho, woh doosre mard par us waqt tak haraam hai jab tak pehla nikah ya us ki iddat baaqi ho. Albatta kaafiron ki woh auratein jin ke Musalman maalik ban jaayen, un ke liye halaal hain. Is ki surat yeh hai ke maidan-e-jang se kaafiron ki auratein qaid hon aur un ke shohar Daar-ul-Harb mein hon. To Islam ka baadshah ya lashkar ka muqarrar ameer un auraton ko mujahideen mein taqseem kar de. Jo qaid aurat jis mujahid ke hisse mein aaye, woh us ke liye halaal hai, kyun ke mukhtalif mulk mein hone ki wajah se un ka pehla nikah khatam ho gaya. Agar woh aurat hamila ho to bachcha paida hone ke baad, warna ek haiz aane ke baad us se sohbat ki ja sakti hai.

### Jangi qaidiyon se muta'alliq Islam ki taleem

Aaj ke zamane mein jangi qaidiyon ke saath jo wehshiyana sulook kiya jata hai, woh kisi se chhupa hua nahin. Un par hone wale zulm-o-sitam ka tasawwur bhi rooh ko hila deta hai. Islam ne jangi qaidiyon ke maamle mein aisa behtareen hal pesh kiya jis ki misaal kahin nahin milti. Jang mein qaid hone wale mardon ko ghulaam aur auraton ko laundiyan banaya jaata, lekin unhein bhooka-pyasa rakhne, tarah tarah ki aziyyat dene ya din-raat zabardasti kaam lene ke bajaye un se achha sulook karne ki hidayat di gayi. Unhein fidya liye baghair ya fidya lekar azaad karne ki targheeb di gayi aur azaad karne par be-shumar sawab ki basharatein sunayi gayin. Jangi qaidiyon ko ghulaam-laundi banana laazim nahin kiya gaya, balki us zamane ke rivaaj ki wajah se sirf ijaazat di gayi, jise upar bayan kiye hue tareeqon ke mutabiq dheere dheere khatam kiya gaya.

**{وَ اُحِلَّ لَكُمْ مَّا وَرَآءَ ذٰلِكُمْ: Aur in ke ilawa sab tumhare liye halaal hain.}** Yani jin auraton se nikah haraam hai, un ke ilawa baqi sab auraton se nikah halaal hai. Lekin yeh yaad rahe ke kuchh auratein aisi bhi hain jin ka zikr pichhli aayaat mein nahin hua, magar un se nikah haraam hai. Misal ke taur par chaar biwiyan nikah mein hote hue paanchwin se nikah, mushrik aurat se nikah, teen talaaq ke baad halaala se pehle usi aurat se dobara nikah, phoophi aur bhatiji ko ya khaala aur bhanji ko ek shakhs ke nikah mein jama karna, aur talaaq ya wafaat ki iddat mein nikah karna. Albatta in se hamesha ke liye nikah haraam nahin hota; nikah ki rukawat khatam hone ke baad nikah ho sakta hai.

**{اَنْ تَبْتَغُوْا بِاَمْوَالِكُمْ: Tum apne maal ke zariye talaash karo.}** Aurat se nikah mahr ke badle kiya jaye. Nikah ka maqsad sirf nafsani lutf aur shahwat poori karna na ho, balki aulaad paida karna, nasl baaqi rakhna aur apne nafs ko haraam se bachana ho. Yahan zaani ko tanbeeh ki ja rahi hai, kyun ke us ke saamne yeh maqsad nahin hote. Us ka maqsad sirf nafsani khwahish poori karna hota hai aur is tarah woh apna nutfa aur maal zaya karke deen-o-duniya ke nuqsan mein phans jata hai.

### Mahr ke chand zaroori masail

1. Mahr ki kam se kam miqdaar das dirham hai. Chandi mein is ka wazan do tole saade saat maashe hai. Us ki jo qeemat banti ho, woh mahr ki kam se kam miqdaar hai. Zyada ki koi had nahin; baahmi raza-mandi se jitna chahein muqarrar kar sakte hain, lekin mahr utna muqarrar karein jitna ada kar sakte hon.
2. Mahr ka maal hona zaroori hai. Jo cheez maal nahin, woh mahr nahin ban sakti. Misal ke taur par agar mahr yeh thehra ke shohar aurat ko Quran-e-Majeed ya ilm-e-deen padha dega to is surat mein mahr-e-misl waajib hoga.
3. Agar nikah mein mahr ka zikr hi na hua ya mahr ki nafi kar di gayi ke mahr ke baghair nikah kiya, to nikah ho jayega. Agar khalwat-e-saheeha ho gayi ya dono mein se koi wafaat paa gaya aur nikah ke baad miyan-biwi mein koi mahr tay nahin hua tha to mahr-e-misl waajib hoga, warna jo mahr tay hua tha woh waajib hoga.

Mahr ke baare mein tafseeli maloomat ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 7 ka mutaala karein.

**{فَمَا اسْتَمْتَعْتُمْ بِهٖ مِنْهُنَّ: To jin auraton se nikah karna chaho.}** Yani jin auraton se shar'i nikah karke sohbat waghera ka faida hasil karna chaho, unhein un ka muqarrar mahr ada karo.

### Aurat se faida uthane ki jaiz suratain

Yaad rahe ke Islam mein aurat se faida uthane ki sirf do suratain jaiz hain jo Quran-e-Pak mein bayan hui hain:

1. Shar'i nikah ke zariye.
2. Aurat jis surat mein laundi ban jaaye.

Is ke ilawa har surat haraam hai. Islam ke shuru ke zamane mein kuchh waqt ke liye nikah se milta-julta muahida karke faida uthane ki ijaazat thi, lekin baad mein Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne use Qiyamat tak ke liye haraam farma diya. Hazrat Saburah Juhani رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Aye logo! Main ne tumhein auraton se mut'ah ki surat mein faida uthane ki ijaazat di thi, ab Allah Ta'ala ne use Qiyamat tak ke liye haraam kar diya hai. Jis ke paas aisi koi aurat ho, woh use chhor de aur jo kuchh tum unhein de chuke ho us mein se kuchh wapas na lo.” (Muslim, Kitaab-un-Nikaah, Baab Nikaah-ul-Mut'ah wa bayaan annahu ubiha summa nusikha... alkh, safha 729, Hadees: 21 (1406))

Hazrat Ali Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم se riwayat hai ke Ghazwa-e-Khaibar ke mauqe par Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne mut'ah se mana farma diya. (Tirmizi, Kitaab-un-Nikaah, Baab ma jaa'a fi Nikaah-il-Mut'ah, 2/365, Hadees: 1124)

## Ayat 4:25

`Source: tafseerId=50606; ayatId=522; surah=4; ayat=25; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ لَّمْ یَسْتَطِعْ مِنْكُمْ طَوْلًا اَنْ یَّنْكِحَ الْمُحْصَنٰتِ الْمُؤْمِنٰتِ فَمِنْ مَّا مَلَكَتْ اَیْمَانُكُمْ مِّنْ فَتَیٰتِكُمُ الْمُؤْمِنٰتِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ اَعْلَمُ بِاِیْمَانِكُمْؕ-بَعْضُكُمْ مِّنْۢ بَعْضٍۚ-فَانْكِحُوْهُنَّ بِاِذْنِ اَهْلِهِنَّ وَ اٰتُوْهُنَّ اُجُوْرَهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوْفِ مُحْصَنٰتٍ غَیْرَ مُسٰفِحٰتٍ وَّ لَا مُتَّخِذٰتِ اَخْدَانٍۚ-فَاِذَاۤ اُحْصِنَّ فَاِنْ اَتَیْنَ بِفَاحِشَةٍ فَعَلَیْهِنَّ نِصْفُ مَا عَلَى الْمُحْصَنٰتِ مِنَ الْعَذَابِؕ-ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِیَ الْعَنَتَ مِنْكُمْؕ-وَ اَنْ تَصْبِرُوْا خَیْرٌ لَّكُمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ۠   (25)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur tum mein se jo koi itni qudrat na rakhta ho ke azaad Musalman auraton se nikah kar sake to un Musalman kanizon se nikah kar le jo tumhari milkiyat mein hain aur Allah tumhare imaan ko khoob jaanta hai. Tum sab aapas mein ek jaise ho. To un ke maalikon ki ijaazat se un se nikah kar lo aur achhe tareeqe se unhein un ke mahr de do, is haal mein ke woh nikah karne wali hon, na zina karne wali aur na posheeda aashna banane wali. Phir jab un ka nikah ho jaaye to agar woh kisi be-hayai ka irtikaab karein to un par azaad auraton ki nisbat aadhi saza hai. Yeh tum mein se us shakhs ke liye munasib hai jise badkaari mein padne ka andesha ho aur tumhara sabr karna tumhare liye behtar hai. Aur Allah bakhshne wala, Meherban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَنْ لَّمْ یَسْتَطِعْ مِنْكُمْ طَوْلًا: Aur tum mein se jo koi qudrat na rakhta ho.}** Jo shakhs azaad aurat se nikah ki qudrat aur gunjaish na rakhta ho, use chahiye ke kisi Musalman mominah kaniz se us ke maalik ki ijaazat ke saath nikah kar le. Apni kaniz se nikah nahin ho sakta, kyun ke woh maalik ke liye nikah ke baghair hi halaal hoti hai. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 1/367)

### Kaniz se nikah ke do shar'i masail

1. Jo shakhs azaad aurat se nikah ki qudrat rakhta ho, use bhi Musalman kaniz se nikah karna jaiz hai. Albatta agar azaad aurat nikah mein ho to ab kaniz se nikah nahin kar sakta. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 1/368)
2. Hanafiyon ke nazdeek Kitaabiyah laundi se bhi nikah kar sakta hai, jabke mominah kaniz se nikah karna mustahab hai. (Madaarik, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, safha 222)

**{وَ اللّٰهُ اَعْلَمُ بِاِیْمَانِكُمْ: Aur Allah tumhare imaan ko khoob jaanta hai.}** Arab apne nasab par fakhr karte aur kanizon se nikah ko sharm ki baat samajhte the. Un ke is khayal ki tardeed ki gayi ke nasab mein tum sab barabar ho, kyun ke sab Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad ho. Is liye kanizon se nikah karna sharm ki baat nahin. Is aayat ki tafseer mein ek qawl yeh hai ke tum sab imaan mein shareek ho, tumhara deen Islam hai. Imaan wala hona badi fazeelat hai, balki fazeelat ka daromadar imaan aur taqwa par hai. Is liye jab kanizon se nikah ki zaroorat ho to sharmao nahin; un ka imaan wala hona kaafi hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 4/49; Jamal, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 2/39, multaqatan)

**{فَاِنْ اَتَیْنَ بِفَاحِشَةٍ: To agar woh kisi be-hayai ka irtikaab karein.}** Nikah ke baad agar kaniz zina kare to azaad aurat ke muqaable mein us ki saza aadhi hai. Azaad kunwari aurat zina mein mubtala ho to us ki saza sau kore hai aur kaniz ki saza is se aadhi, yani pachaas kore hai. Kaniz kunwari ho ya shohar wali, us ki saza pachaas kore hi hai. Shohar wali kaniz ko azaad aurat ki tarah rajm nahin kiya jayega, kyun ke rajm ki saza ko aadha karna mumkin nahin. (Qurtubi, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 3/102, al-juz' ul-khaamis)

**{ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِیَ الْعَنَتَ مِنْكُمْ: Yeh us shakhs ke liye munasib hai jise zina ka andesha ho.}** Yani azaad aurat ke bajaye kaniz se nikah us shakhs ke liye munasib hai jise shahwat ke ghalbe ki wajah se zina mein padne ka dar ho. Agar woh is khauf ke bawajood sabr kare aur parhezgaar rahe to yeh behtar hai. Jahan tak mumkin ho kaniz se nikah na kare, kyun ke kaniz se nikah ki surat mein jo aulaad hogi woh us ke maalik ki ghulaam banegi aur kaniz apne maalik ki ijaazat ke baghair shohar ki khidmat ke liye bhi nahin aa sakegi. (Baizawi, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 25, 2/174)

Phir agar sabr na kar sake to kaniz se nikah kar le. Yaad rahe ke is zamane mein bain-ul-aqwami taur par mard ko ghulaam aur aurat ko kaniz banane ka qanoon khatam ho chuka hai.

### Nikah ka shar'i hukm

Mard ke liye nikah ka shar'i hukm yeh hai ke aitidaal ki haalat mein, yani na shahwat ka zyada ghalba ho aur na woh na-mard ho, aur mahr aur naano-nafaqah dene par qudrat rakhta ho, to us ke liye nikah karna sunnat-e-mu'akkadah hai. Lekin agar use zina mein padne ka andesha ho aur woh zawjiyat ke huqooq poore karne par qaadir ho to us ke liye nikah karna waajib hai. Agar use zina mein padne ka yaqeen ho to us par nikah karna farz hai. Agar use zawjiyat ke huqooq poore na kar sakne ka andesha ho to us ka nikah karna makrooh hai, aur agar huqooq poore na kar sakne ka yaqeen ho to us ke liye nikah karna haraam hai. (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 7, 2/4-5, mulakhkhasan)

Aurat ke liye nikah ka shar'i hukm yeh hai ke jis aurat ko apne nafs se yeh khauf ho ke ghaliban woh shohar ki ita'at na kar sakegi aur shohar ke waajib huqooq ada na kar sakegi, to us ke liye nikah karna mamnoo aur najaiz hai. Agar karegi to gunahgaar hogi. Agar use in cheezon ka yaqeen ho to us ke liye nikah karna haraam-e-qat'i hai. Jis aurat ko aisa khauf na ho aur use nikah ki sakht zaroorat ho ke nikah ke baghair, ma'aazAllah, gunah mein mubtala hone ka ghalib gumaan ho, to aisi aurat ke liye nikah karna waajib hai. Agar nikah ke baghair gunah mein padne ka poora yaqeen ho to us par nikah karna farz hai. Agar zaroorat aitidaal par ho, yani na nikah se bilkul be-parwai ho aur na itni shiddat ka shauq ho ke nikah ke baghair gunah mein padne ka ghalib gumaan ho, to aisi haalat mein us ke liye nikah karna sunnat hai, jabke use apne baare mein poora itminaan ho ke woh shohar ki ita'at nahin chhoregi aur shohar ke huqooq bilkul zaya nahin karegi. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 12/291-293, mulakhkhasan)

### Mishk ki khushboo mein base hue buzurg

Targheeb ke liye ek aise hi muttaqi buzurg ka waqia pesh hai.

Basrah mein ek buzurg “Miski”, yani “mishk ki khushboo mein basa hua”, ke naam se mashhoor the. Kisi ne israar karke is khushboo ka raaz poocha to unhone farmaya: “Main koi khushboo nahin lagata. Mera qissa bahut ajeeb hai. Main Baghdad-e-Mu'alla ka rehne wala hoon. Jawani mein bahut haseen-o-jameel tha aur haya wala bhi. Main ne kapre wale ki dukaan par naukri ki.

Ek din ek boodhi aurat aayi. Us ne kuchh qeemti kapre nikalwaye aur dukandaar se kaha: ‘Main in kapron ko ghar le jana chahti hoon. Is naujawan ko mere saath bhej dein. Jo kapre pasand aayenge woh rakh lenge, phir un ki qeemat aur baqi kapre is naujawan ke haath bhej denge.’

Dukandaar ke kehne par main us boodhi aurat ke saath chala gaya. Woh mujhe ek shandaar kothi mein le aayi aur ek kamre mein bitha diya. Kuchh der baad ek naujawan aurat kamre mein aayi aur us ne darwaza band kar diya. Phir woh mere qareeb baith gayi. Main ghabra kar nigah neeche karke foran wahan se hat gaya, lekin us par shahwat sawaar thi aur woh mere peeche pad gayi.

Main ne bahut kaha: ‘Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se daro, woh humein dekh raha hai.’ Lekin woh mere saath badkaari par israar karti rahi. Mere zehan mein is gunah se bachne ki ek tadbeer aayi. Main ne us se kaha: ‘Mujhe bait-ul-khala jaane do.’ Us ne ijazat de di.

Main bait-ul-khala mein gaya aur dil mazboot karke wahan ki gandagi apne haath, munh aur kapron par mal li. Jab main bahar aaya to meri aashiqah ghabra kar bhaag gayi aur kothi mein shor mach gaya: ‘Pagal, pagal!’ Main wahan se bhaag kar ek baagh mein chhup gaya, ghusl kiya, kapre paak kiye aur wahan se chal diya.

Raat ko jab main soya to khwab mein dekha ke koi aaya, mere chehre aur kapron par haath phera aur kaha: ‘Mujhe jaante ho main kaun hoon? Suno! Main Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام hoon.’ Jab meri aankh khuli to mere poore badan aur kapron se khushboo aa rahi thi. Woh khushboo aaj tak baqi hai. Yeh sab Hazrat Sayyiduna Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ke haath ki barkat hai.” (Raud-ur-Riyaheen, al-Hikayatus-Saabi'atu Asharata ba'dal Arba' Mi'ah, safha 334-335)

### Haraam maal kamaane ki mazammat

Haraam kamaana aur khaana Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki bargah mein sakht na-pasand hai. Ahadees mein is par sakht wa'eedein aayi hain. Un mein se chaar yeh hain:

1. Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas'ood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo banda haraam maal haasil karta hai, agar use sadaqah kare to qabool nahin hota, kharch kare to us mein barkat nahin hoti aur apne baad chhor kar mare to Jahannam mein jaane ka samaan hai. Allah Ta'ala burai se burai ko nahin mitaata, haan neki se burai ko mita deta hai. Beshak khabees ko khabees nahin mitaata.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad Abdullah bin Mas'ood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, 2/33, hadees: 3672)

2. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ne us badan par Jannat haraam farma di hai jo haraam ghiza se pala-badha ho.” (Kanz-ul-Ummal, Kitab-ul-Buyoo, Qism-ul-Aqwaal, 2/8, al-Juz-ur-Raabi, hadees: 9257)

3. Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Hazrat Sa'd رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se farmaya: “Aye Sa'd! Apni ghiza paak kar lo, mustajaab-ud-da'waat ho jaoge. Us Paak Zaat ki qasam jis ke qabze mein Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki jaan hai! Banda jab haraam luqma apne pait mein daalta hai to us ke chalis din ke amal qabool nahin hote aur jis bande ka gosht haraam se pala-badha ho us ke liye aag zyada behtar hai.” (Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, man ismuhu Muhammad, 5/34, hadees: 6495)

4. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne ek aise shakhs ka zikr farmaya jo lamba safar karta hai, us ke baal bikhre hue aur badan gard-aalood hai, woh haath aasman ki taraf utha kar “Ya Rab! Ya Rab!” pukarta hai, lekin us ka khaana haraam, peena haraam, libaas haraam aur ghiza haraam hai. Phir us ki dua kaise qabool hogi? (Muslim, Kitab-uz-Zakaat, Baab Qabool-us-Sadaqah min-al-Kasb-it-Tayyib wa Tarbiyyatiha, safha 506, hadees: 65 (1015))

**{اِلَّاۤ اَنْ تَكُوْنَ تِجَارَةً عَنْ تَرَاضٍ مِّنْكُمْ: Magar yeh ke tumhari baahmi raza-mandi se tijarat ho.}** Baahmi raza-mandi se ki gayi tijarat halaal hai. Is qaid se maloom hua ke khareed-o-farokht ki woh tamam suratain durust nahin jin mein dono taraf ki raza-mandi na ho, jaise zabt ki hui cheezon ki neelami khareedna, kyun ke asal maalik us par raazi nahin hota. Isi tarah kisi ki dukaan, makaan, zameen ya jaidad par zabardasti qabza karna haraam hai.

Maal ka maalik banne ke liye tijarat ke ilawa bhi jaiz zariye hain. Tohfe, wasiyyat aur virasat mein maal milna bhi jaiz hai. Tijarat ka khaas taur par zikr is liye kiya gaya ke yeh maalik banne ka ikhtiyaari zariya hai.

### Tijarat ke aadaab

Tijarat ke chaudah aadaab bayan kiye gaye hain. In mein aksar aise hain jin par har tajir ke liye shar'an amal karna zaroori hai:

1. Tajir ko har subah achhi niyyatain taza karni chahiye: bazaar is liye ja raha hoon ke halaal kamaai se apne ghar walon ki kifalat karoon, unhein logon se be-niyaaz karoon aur itni fursat paoon ke Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat aur Aakhirat ki tayyari karoon. Yeh niyyat bhi kare ke makhlooq par shafqat aur khuloos karunga, amanatdari karunga, neki ka hukm dunga, burai se mana karunga aur khayanat karne wale se pooch-gach karunga.
2. Tijarat karne wala asli aur naqli note pehchanne ka tareeqa seekhe. Na khud naqli note le aur na kisi doosre ko de, taake Musalmanon ka haq zaya na ho.
3. Agar koi naqli note de jaye aur dene wale ka pata na chale to use kisi aur ko na de. Agar dene wale ka pata chal jaye to use bhi woh naqli note wapas na de, balki phaad kar phenk de taake kisi aur ko dhoka na de sake.
4. Apne maal ki had se zyada tareef na kare, kyun ke yeh jhoot aur fareb hai. Agar khareedaar maal ki sifat pehle se jaanta ho to us ki jaiz tareef bhi na kare, kyun ke yeh fazool hai.
5. Aib wala maal na khareede. Agar khareed le to dil mein ahad kare ke khareedaar ko saare aib bata dega. Agar kisi ne use dhoka diya ho to nuqsan apni zaat tak rakhega aur doosron par nahin daalega.
6. Apne paas maujood durust maal mein koi aib paida ho jaye to grahak se na chhupaye, warna woh zaalim aur gunahgaar hoga.
7. Wazan aur naap mein dhoka na de, balki poora tole aur poora naap de.
8. Asal qeemat chhupa kar kisi ko qeemat mein dhoka na de.
9. Bahut zyada nafa na le, agarche khareedaar kisi majboori ki wajah se is zyada qeemat par raazi ho.
10. Mohtaajon ka maal zyada qeemat par khareede taake unhein bhi khushi mile, jaise bewa ka soota aur woh phal jo faqeer ke haath se wapas aaya ho. Aisi darguzar sadaqah se bhi zyada fazeelat rakhti hai.
11. Qarzkhwah ke taqaaze se pehle us ka qarz ada kare. Use apne paas bula kar dene ke bajaye khud us ke paas ja kar ada kare.
12. Jis shakhs se muamla kare, agar muamle ke baad woh pareshan ho to muamla khatam kar de.
13. Duniya ka bazaar use Aakhirat ke bazaar se na roke. Aakhirat ka bazaar masaajid hain.
14. Bazaar mein zyada der rehne ki koshish na kare, jaise sab se pehle jaye aur sab ke baad wapas aaye. (Keemiya-e-Sa'adat, Rukn-e-Dom dar Mu'amalaat, Asl-e-Som Adaab-e-Kasb, 1/326-340, multaqatan)

**{وَ لَا تَقْتُلُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْ: Aur apni jaanon ko qatl na karo.}** Yani aise kaam karke apni jaanon ko halaak na karo jo duniya aur Aakhirat mein halaakat ka sabab bante hain. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 29, 1/370)

### Khud ko halaak karne ki suratain

Khud ko halaak karne ki mukhtalif suratain hain:

1. Musalmanon ka ek doosre ko qatl karna khud ko halaak karna hai, kyun ke ahadees mein Musalmanon ko ek jism kaha gaya hai. Hazrat Nu'man bin Basheer رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Tum Musalmanon ko ek doosre par rahm, dosti aur shafqat mein ek jism ki tarah dekhoge. Jism ke kisi hisse ko takleef ho to poora jism bedari aur bukhaar mein us ka shareek hota hai.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ul-Adab, Baab Rahmat-in-Naas wal-Bahaa'im, 4/103, hadees: 6011)

Hazrat Nu'man bin Basheer رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ hi se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Musalman aapas mein ek shakhs ki tarah hain. Agar us ki aankh ko takleef ho to poore jism ko takleef hoti hai, aur agar us ke sar mein takleef ho to poore jism ko takleef hoti hai.” (Muslim, Kitab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Adaab, Baab Taraahum-il-Mu'mineen wa Ta'aatafihim wa Ta'aazudihim, safha 1396, hadees: 67 (2586))

Jab Musalman ek jism ki tarah hain to ek Musalman ka doosre Musalman ko qatl karna goya khud ko qatl karna hai.

2. Aisa kaam karna jis ki saza mein kaam karne wale ko qatl kar diya jaye bhi khud ko halaak karna hai. Misal ke taur par kisi Musalman ko qatl karna, shadi-shuda hone ke bawajood zina karna ya murtad hona. Zina aur na-haq Musalman ko qatl karna kabirah gunah hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

وَ لَا تَقْرَبُوا الزِّنٰۤى اِنَّهٗ كَانَ فَاحِشَةًؕ-وَ سَآءَ سَبِیْلًا(بنی اسرائیل:۳۲)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur badkaari ke paas na jao. Beshak woh be-hayai hai aur bahut bura raasta hai.

Na-haq Musalman ko qatl karne wale ke baare mein farmaya:

وَ مَنْ یَّقْتُلْ مُؤْمِنًا مُّتَعَمِّدًا فَجَزَآؤُهٗ جَهَنَّمُ خٰلِدًا فِیْهَا وَ غَضِبَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِ وَ لَعَنَهٗ وَ اَعَدَّ لَهٗ عَذَابًا عَظِیْمًا(النساء:۹۳)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jo kisi Musalman ko jaan-boojh kar qatl kare to us ka badla Jahannam hai, muddaton us mein rahega aur Allah ne us par ghazab kiya aur us par laanat ki aur us ke liye bada azaab tayyar kar rakha hai.

Murtad hone walon ke baare mein farmaya:

وَ مَنْ یَّرْتَدِدْ مِنْكُمْ عَنْ دِیْنِهٖ فَیَمُتْ وَ هُوَ كَافِرٌ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ حَبِطَتْ اَعْمَالُهُمْ فِی الدُّنْیَا وَ الْاٰخِرَةِۚ-وَ اُولٰٓىٕكَ اَصْحٰبُ النَّارِۚ-هُمْ فِیْهَا خٰلِدُوْنَ(بقرہ:۲۱۷)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur tum mein jo koi apne deen se murtad ho jaye, phir kaafir hi mare to un logon ke sab amal duniya aur Aakhirat mein barbaad ho gaye aur woh Dozakh wale hain, woh us mein hamesha rahenge.

3. Khudkushi karna bhi haraam hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis ne apna gala ghonta woh Jahannam ki aag mein apna gala ghonta rahega, aur jis ne apne aap ko neza maara woh Jahannam ki aag mein apne aap ko neza maarta rahega.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ul-Janaa'iz, Baab ma jaa'a fi Qaatil-in-Nafs, 4/460, hadees: 1365)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ hi se riwayat hai ke Sarwar-e-Aali-Waqaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo pahad se gir kar khudkushi karega woh Dozakh ki aag mein hamesha girta rahega. Jo zehar kha kar khudkushi karega woh Jahannam ki aag mein hamesha zehar khaata rahega. Jis ne lohe ke hathiyaar se khudkushi ki to Dozakh ki aag mein woh hathiyaar us ke haath mein hoga aur woh us se apne aap ko hamesha zakhmi karta rahega.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ut-Tibb, Baab Shurb-us-Summ wad-Dawa bihi, 4/43, hadees: 5778)

4. Aisa kaam karna jis ke natije mein kaam karne wala duniya ya Aakhirat mein halaakat mein pad jaye bhi khud ko halaak karna hai, jaise bhook hartaal karna ya baatil tareeqe se maal khaana.

Hazrat Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Ghazwa-e-Zaat-us-Salaasil ke mauqe par ek thandi raat mujhe ehtilaam ho gaya. Mujhe ghusl karne ki surat mein thand se halaak hone ka khauf hua, is liye main ne tayammum kiya aur apne saathiyon ke saath Fajr ki namaz padhi. Unhone Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se is ka zikr kiya. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Aye Amr! Tum ne apne saathiyon ke saath is haal mein namaz padh li ke tum junubi the?” Main ne ghusl na karne ka uzr bayan kiya aur arz ki: “Main ne Allah Ta'ala ka yeh farman suna hai: وَ لَا تَقْتُلُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ بِكُمْ رَحِیْمًا (النساء: ۲۹) — Aur apni jaanon ko qatl na karo, beshak Allah tum par meherban hai.” Yeh sun kar Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ muskara diye aur kuchh na farmaya. (Abu Dawood, Kitab-ut-Tahaarat, Baab Iza Khaaf-al-Junub-ul-Bard, 1/153, hadees: 334)

## Ayat 4:30

`Source: tafseerId=50610; ayatId=527; surah=4; ayat=30; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْ ذٰلِكَ عُدْوَانًا وَّ ظُلْمًا فَسَوْفَ نُصْلِیْهِ نَارًاؕ-وَ كَانَ ذٰلِكَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ یَسِیْرًا(30)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jo zulm-o-ziyadati se aisa karega to anqareeb Hum use aag mein daakhil karenge aur yeh Allah par bahut aasaan hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْ ذٰلِكَ عُدْوَانًا وَّ ظُلْمًا: Aur jo zulm-o-ziyadati se aisa karega.}** Yahan zulm-o-ziyadati ki qaid is liye lagayi gayi ke jin suratoun mein kisi momin ko qatl karna jaiz hai, un mein qatl karna jurm nahin. Jaise murtad ko saza ke taur par qatl karna, qaatil se qisaas lena, shadi-shuda zaani ko rajm karna, daaku ko muqable ya saza mein qatl karna aur baagiyon ko jang mein qatl karna. Yeh sab hukumat ke liye jaiz hain, balki hukumat ko in ka hukm hai. Qatl ke baare mein mazeed tafseel Surah Ma'idah ki mukhtalif aayaton ke tehat aayegi.

## Ayat 4:31

`Source: tafseerId=50611; ayatId=528; surah=4; ayat=31; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنْ تَجْتَنِبُوْا كَبَآىٕرَ مَا تُنْهَوْنَ عَنْهُ نُكَفِّرْ عَنْكُمْ سَیِّاٰتِكُمْ وَ نُدْخِلْكُمْ مُّدْخَلًا كَرِیْمًا(31)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Agar kabirah gunahon se bachte raho jin se tumhein mana kiya jata hai to Hum tumhare doosre gunah bakhsh denge aur tumhein izzat ki jagah daakhil karenge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنْ تَجْتَنِبُوْا كَبَآىٕرَ: Agar kabirah gunahon se bachte raho.}** Pichhli aayaton mein kuchh kabirah gunahon par wa'eed bayan hui aur is aayat mein kabirah gunahon se bachne par chhote gunah maaf karne aur izzat ki jagah daakhil karne ka wa'da diya gaya hai. (Al-Bahr-ul-Muheet, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 30, 3/243)

### Kabirah gunah ki ta'reef aur tadaad

Kabirah gunah woh hai jis ka murtakib Quran-o-Sunnat mein bayan ki gayi kisi khaas sakht wa'eed ka mustahiq ho. (Az-Zawaajir, Muqaddimah fi Ta'reef-il-Kabeerah, 1/12)

Kabirah gunahon ki tadaad mukhtalif bayan ki gayi hai. Kisi ne 7, kisi ne 10, kisi ne 17, kisi ne 40 aur kisi ne 700 tak bayan kiye hain.

### Gunahon se muta'alliq teen ahadees

1. Hazrat Abu Sa'laba Khushani رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ne kuchh faraiz muqarrar kiye hain, is liye unhein hargiz zaya na karo. Kuchh cheezein haraam ki hain, unhein hargiz halka na samjho. Kuchh hudood qayam ki hain, un se tajawuz na karo. Aur tum par rahm farmate hue kuchh cheezon ke baare mein jaan-boojh kar kuchh nahin farmaya, to un ki justuju na karo.” (Daar Qutni, Kitab-ur-Razaa', 4/217, hadees: 4350)

2. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jab banda koi gunah karta hai to us ke dil par ek siyah nuqta laga diya jata hai. Jab woh gunah se ruk jata hai aur taubah-o-istighfar karta hai to us ka dil saaf ho jata hai. Agar woh phir gunah kare to woh nuqta badhta rehta hai, yahan tak ke poora dil siyah ho jata hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitab-ut-Tafseer, Baab wa min Soorati Wail-ul-Mutaffifeen, 5/220, hadees: 3345)

3. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain: “Aye gunahgaar! Tu gunah ke bure anjaam se kyun be-khauf hai? Gunah ki talab mein rehna gunah karne se bhi bada gunah hai. Tera daayen-baayen ke farishton se haya na karna aur gunah par qayam rehna bhi bada gunah hai. Tera taubah kiye baghair gunah par qayam rehna is se bhi bada gunah hai. Gunah karne par khush hona aur qahqaha lagana bhi bahut bada gunah hai, halaanke tu nahin jaanta ke Allah Ta'ala tere saath kya muamla farmayega. Gunah karne mein nakaami par ghamgeen hona bhi bada gunah hai. Gunah karte waqt tez hawa se darwaze ka parda uth jaye to tu dar jata hai, lekin Allah Ta'ala ki us nigah se nahin darta jo tujh par hai.” (Az-Zawaajir, Muqaddimah fi Ta'reef-il-Kabeerah, 1/27)

### Kabirah gunahon ke baare mein mashhoor hadees

Hazrat Amr bin Hazm رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din Allah Ta'ala ke nazdeek sab se bade gunah yeh honge: (1) Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke saath shirk karna, (2) Musalman ko na-haq qatl karna, (3) jang ke din Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raah mein jihad se faraar hona, (4) walidain ki na-farmani karna, (5) paak-daaman auraton par tohmat lagana, (6) jaadu seekhna, (7) sood khaana, aur (8) yateem ka maal khaana.” (Sunan-ul-Kubra lil-Baihaqi, Kitab-uz-Zakaat, Baab Kaifa Faraz-as-Sadaqah, 4/149, hadees: 7255)

**{نُكَفِّرْ عَنْكُمْ سَیِّاٰتِكُمْ: Hum tum se doosre gunah mita denge.}** Agar tum kabirah gunahon se bachte raho aur doosri ibaadatein bhi baja laate raho to Hum apne fazl se tumhare chhote gunah maaf kar denge aur tumhein izzat ki jagah, yani Jannat, mein daakhil karenge.

Yaad rahe ke yeh bhi Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat aur raza par hai. Yeh bayan chhote gunahon ke baare mein hai. Kabirah gunah taubah hi se maaf hote hain, albatta maqbool Hajj ke baare mein bhi yeh bashaarat hai. Mazeed tahqeeq ke liye Fatawa Razawiyyah ki chaubeesween jild mein A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki kitaab “اَعْجَبُ الْاِمْدَاد فِیْ مُكَفِّرَاتِ حُقُوْقِ الْعِبَاد” (Bandon ke huqooq maaf karwane ke tareeqe) padhein. [2]

[2] A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki yeh kitaab tashil-o-takhreej ke saath “Huqooq-ul-Ibaad kaise maaf hon?” ke naam se Maktabat-ul-Madinah ne bhi shaya ki hai.

## Ayat 4:32

`Source: tafseerId=50612; ayatId=529; surah=4; ayat=32; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لَا تَتَمَنَّوْا مَا فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ بِهٖ بَعْضَكُمْ عَلٰى بَعْضٍؕ-لِلرِّجَالِ نَصِیْبٌ مِّمَّا اكْتَسَبُوْاؕ-وَ لِلنِّسَآءِ نَصِیْبٌ مِّمَّا اكْتَسَبْنَؕ-وَ سْــٴَـلُوا اللّٰهَ مِنْ فَضْلِهٖؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ بِكُلِّ شَیْءٍ عَلِیْمًا(32)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur tum us cheez ki tamanna na karo jis se Allah ne tum mein ek ko doosre par fazeelat di hai. Mardon ke liye un ke aamaal se hissa hai aur auraton ke liye un ke aamaal se hissa hai aur Allah se us ka fazl maango. Beshak Allah har shay ko jaanne wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَا تَتَمَنَّوْا مَا فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ بِهٖ: Aur us ki tamanna na karo jis se Allah ne fazeelat di.}** Jab insaan kisi doosre ke paas aisi ne'mat dekhta hai jo us ke paas nahin hoti to us ka dil pareshan ho jata hai. Is ki do suratain hain:

1. Woh tamanna kare ke doosre se ne'mat chhin jaye aur mujhe mil jaye. Isay hasad kehte hain. Hasad mazmoom aur haraam hai.
2. Woh yeh na chahe ke doosre se ne'mat chhin jaye, balki yeh chahe ke mujhe bhi waisi ne'mat mil jaye. Isay ghibtah kehte hain aur yeh mazmoom nahin. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 32, 4/65)

Is liye Allah Ta'ala ne jis bande ko deen ya duniya ke hawale se jo ne'mat di hai, use us par raazi rehna chahiye.

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Jab aayat-e-miraas mein “لِلذَّكَرِ مِثْلُ حَظِّ الْاُنْثَیَیْنِ” wala hissa naazil hua aur mayyit ke tarke mein mard ka hissa aurat se dugna muqarrar hua to mardon ne kaha: “Humein umeed hai ke Aakhirat mein nekiyon ka sawab bhi auraton se dugna milega.” Auraton ne kaha: “Humein umeed hai ke gunahon ka azaab mardon se aadha hoga.” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui aur bataya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala ne jis ko jo fazeelat di hai woh hikmat ke mutabiq hai. Bande ko Allah Ta'ala ke faisle par raazi rehna chahiye. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 32, 1/372)

### Dil ke sabr aur qarar ka nuskha

Dil ke sabr aur qarar ka nuskha Allah Ta'ala ki raza par raazi rehna hai. Duniya mein koi shakhs har ne'mat ki inteha tak nahin pahunch sakta. Agar koi ek ne'mat mein buland ho bhi to kisi doosri ne'mat mein kamtar hota hai. Agar dil ko inhi tamannaon ka markaz bana diya jaye to hazaaron ne'maton ka maalik hote hue bhi qarar nahin milta.

Misal ke taur par ek shakhs ek arab rupay ka maalik hai lekin khoobsurat nahin. Agar woh khoobsurti ki tamanna karta rahe to us ka jeena mushkil ho jayega. Agar koi khoobsurat ho lekin us ki jeb mein paisa na ho aur woh paison ka gham karta rahe to bhi be-qarar rahega. Aur jis ke paas na paisa ho na khoobsurti, lekin woh kahe ke main Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raza par raazi hoon, sabr kare aur Aakhirat ke sawab ko saamne rakhe to yaqeenan use dil ka sukoon mil jayega.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Sarwar-e-Aali-Waqaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Agar Ibn-e-Adam ke paas maal ki do waadiyan bhi hon to woh chahega ke us ke paas teesri waadi bhi ho. Us ka pait mitti hi bhar sakti hai. Allah Ta'ala us ki taubah qabool farmata hai jo taubah kare.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ur-Riqaaq, Baab ma Yattaqa min Fitnat-il-Maal, 4/228, hadees: 6436)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Apne se neeche wale ko dekho aur apne se upar wale ko na dekho. Yeh is se behtar hai ke tum Allah Ta'ala ki apne upar ne'mat ko haqeer samjho.” (Muslim, Kitab-uz-Zuhd war-Raqaa'iq, safha 1584, hadees: 9 (2963))

**{لِلرِّجَالِ نَصِیْبٌ مِّمَّا اكْتَسَبُوْا: Mardon ke liye un ki kamaai se hissa hai.}** Miyan-biwi mein se har ek ko apne nek aamaal ka sawab milega. Dono ka nek aur parhezgaar hona unhein apne aamaal se be-niyaaz nahin karega.

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Umm-e-Salamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ne arz ki: “Kaash hum bhi mard hote to jihad karte aur mardon ki tarah jaan qurban karne ka azeem sawab paate.” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui aur unhein tasalli di gayi ke mard jihad se sawab haasil kar sakte hain to auratein shoharon ki ita'at aur paak-daamani se sawab haasil kar sakti hain. (Jalalain, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 32, safha 27)

**{وَ سْــٴَـلُوا اللّٰهَ مِنْ فَضْلِهٖ: Aur Allah se us ka fazl maango.}** SubhanAllah! Dil ke qarar ka kitna pyara tareeqa bataya gaya ke Allah Kareem ka fazl-o-karam maango. Haqeeqat mein sab se badi cheez Allah Ta'ala ka fazl hai. Kisi ko doosre se laakh guna zyada sawab milta ho, phir bhi woh Allah Ta'ala ke fazl ka mohtaaj hai, kyun ke Jannat mein daakhla Allah Ta'ala ke fazl hi se hoga. Apne amal ke bal par koi Jannat mein nahin ja sakta. Is liye Allah Ta'ala se us ka fazl maangte rehna chahiye.

## Ayat 4:33

`Source: tafseerId=50613; ayatId=530; surah=4; ayat=33; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لِكُلٍّ جَعَلْنَا مَوَالِیَ مِمَّا تَرَكَ الْوَالِدٰنِ وَ الْاَقْرَبُوْنَؕ-وَ الَّذِیْنَ عَقَدَتْ اَیْمَانُكُمْ فَاٰتُوْهُمْ نَصِیْبَهُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ شَهِیْدًا۠    (33)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur maa-baap aur rishtedaar jo kuchh maal chhodein, Hum ne sab ke liye us maal mein mustahiq bana diye hain. Aur jin se tumhara muahida ho chuka hai unhein un ka hissa do. Beshak Allah har shay par gawah hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ الَّذِیْنَ عَقَدَتْ اَیْمَانُكُمْ: Aur jin se tumhara muahida ho chuka hai.}** Is se aqd-e-muwalaat murad hai. Is ki surat yeh hai ke aisa shakhs jis ka nasab maloom na ho, kisi doosre se kahe: “Tu mera mawla hai. Main wafaat pa jaaun to tu mera waaris hoga aur main koi jurm karoon to tujhe diyat deni hogi.” Doosra kahe: “Main ne qabool kiya.” Is surat mein yeh aqd sahih ho jata hai, qabool karne wala waaris ban jata hai aur diyat bhi us par aa jaati hai.

Agar doosra shakhs bhi isi tarah nasab se majhool ho aur aisa kahe aur pehla shakhs qabool kar le, to un mein se har ek doosre ka waaris aur us ki diyat ka zimmedaar hoga. Yeh aqd sabit hai aur Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم bhi is ke qaail hain. (Madarik, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 33, safha 225)

## Ayat 4:34

`Source: tafseerId=51246; ayatId=531; surah=4; ayat=34; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلرِّجَالُ قَوّٰمُوْنَ عَلَى النِّسَآءِ بِمَا فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلٰى بَعْضٍ وَّ بِمَاۤ اَنْفَقُوْا مِنْ اَمْوَالِهِمْؕ-فَالصّٰلِحٰتُ قٰنِتٰتٌ حٰفِظٰتٌ لِّلْغَیْبِ بِمَا حَفِظَ اللّٰهُؕ-وَ الّٰتِیْ تَخَافُوْنَ نُشُوْزَهُنَّ فَعِظُوْهُنَّ وَ اهْجُرُوْهُنَّ فِی الْمَضَاجِـعِ وَ اضْرِبُوْهُنَّۚ-فَاِنْ اَطَعْنَكُمْ فَلَا تَبْغُوْا عَلَیْهِنَّ سَبِیْلًاؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَلِیًّا كَبِیْرًا(34)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Mard auraton par nigran hain, is wajah se ke Allah ne un mein ek ko doosre par fazeelat di aur is wajah se ke mard auraton par apna maal kharch karte hain. To nek auratein shoharon ki ita'at karne wali aur un ki ghair-haazri mein Allah ki hifazat aur taufeeq se hifazat karne wali hoti hain. Aur jin auraton ki naafarmaani ka tumhein andesha ho to unhein samjhao aur na samjhein to un se apne bistar alag kar lo aur phir na samjhein to unhein maaro. Phir agar woh tumhari ita'at kar lein to ab un par ziyadti ka raasta na dhoondo. Beshak Allah bahut buland, bahut bada hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلرِّجَالُ قَوّٰمُوْنَ عَلَى النِّسَآءِ: Mard auraton par nigran hain.}** Aurat ki zarooriyat poori karne, us ki hifazat karne, use adab sikhane aur doosre kai umoor mein mard ko aurat par tasallut haasil hai. Goya aurat riaya aur mard baadshah hai. Is liye aurat par mard ki ita'at laazim hai. Is se maloom hua ke miyan-biwi ke huqooq ek jaise nahin, balki mard ke huqooq aurat se zyada hain. Yeh aurat par na-insafi ya zulm nahin, balki insaaf aur hikmat ka taqaaza hai.

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Hazrat Sa'd bin Rabi' رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne apni biwi Habibah ko kisi khata par ek tamaacha maara jis se un ke chehre par nishaan pad gaya. Woh apne walid ke saath Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki baargaah mein apne shohar ki shikayat karne haazir hui. Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne qisaas lene ka hukm diya. Isi waqt yeh aayat naazil hui to Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne qisaas lene se mana farma diya. Lekin yaad rahe ke aurat ko is tarah maarna najaiz hai. (Baghawi, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 34, 1/335)

**{بِمَا فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلٰى بَعْضٍ: Is wajah se ke Allah ne un mein ek ko doosre par fazeelat di.}** Mard ko aurat par jo hukmrani di gayi hai, us ki ek wajah yeh hai ke Rab Ta'ala ne mard ko aurat par fazeelat di hai. Mard ke aurat se afzal hone ki bohot si wajahain hain. In ka khulasa do cheezein hain: ilm aur qudrat. Aam taur par mard aqal aur ilm mein aurat se aage hote hain aur mushkil tareen kaam anjaam dene ki zyada qudrat rakhte hain.

Isi wajah se mard aqal, danish aur quwwat mein auraton se bartari rakhte hain. Jitne bhi Ambiya, khulafa aur aimma hue, sab mard hi the. Ghod-sawaari, teer-andaazi aur jihad mard karte hain. Badi hukumat aur saltanat, namaz ki imaamat, azaan, khutbah, hudood aur qisaas mein gawaahi ba-ijma' mardon ke zimmah hai. Nikaah, talaaq, ruju' aur ek waqt mein ek se zyada shaadiyan karne ka haq mard ke paas hai aur nasab bhi mardon ki taraf mansoob kiye jaate hain. Yeh sab baatein mard ke aurat se afzal hone par daleel hain.

Mard ki aurat par hukmrani ki doosri wajah yeh hai ke mard aurat par mahr aur naan-o-nafaqah ki surat mein apna maal kharch karte hain, is liye woh un par haakim hain. Yaad rahe ke majmuei taur par mardon ki jins auraton ki jins se afzal hai, na yeh ke har mard har aurat se afzal ho. Baaz auratein ilm-o-danish mein kai mardon se zyada hoti hain, jaise Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا. Hum jaise laakhon mard un ke na'lein ki khaak ke barabar bhi nahin. Isi tarah Sahabiyah auratein ghair-Sahabi bade bade buzurgon se afzal hain.

**{فَالصّٰلِحٰتُ: Nek auratein.}** Yahan nek aur parhezgaar auraton ke ausaaf bayan kiye ja rahe hain. Jab un ke shohar maujood hon to woh un ki ita'at karti hain, un ke huqooq ada karti hain aur shohar ki naafarmaani se bachti hain. Jab shohar maujood na ho to Allah Ta'ala ke fazl se us ke maal aur izzat ki hifazat karti hain.

### Nek biwi ke ausaaf aur fazaail

Bahut si ahadees mein nek aur parhezgaar biwiyon ke ausaaf aur fazaail bayan hue hain. Un mein se do ahadees yeh hain:

1. Hazrat Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Taqwa ke baad momin ke liye nek biwi se behtar koi cheez nahin. Agar woh use hukm de to ita'at kare, use dekhe to khush kar de, us par qasam kha baithe to qasam ko sach kar de aur kahin chala jaye to apne nafs aur shohar ke maal mein bhalai kare.” (Ibn Majah, Kitab-un-Nikaah, Baab afzal-in-nisaa, 2/414, hadees: 1857)

2. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jise chaar cheezein mil gayin, use duniya aur Aakhirat ki bhalai mil gayi: (1) shukr karne wala dil, (2) Allah ko yaad karne wali zaban, (3) museebat par sabr karne wala badan, (4) aisi biwi jo apne nafs aur shohar ke maal mein gunah ki talaash na kare, yani khayanat na kare.” (Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Talq bin Habib an Ibn Abbas, 11/109, hadees: 11275)

### Kis aurat se nikaah karna chahiye?

Nikaah ke liye aurat ka intikhaab karte waqt us ki deendari dekhi jaye aur deendar aurat ko tarjeeh di jaye. Jo log aurat mein sirf husn, maal-daari ya izzat-o-mansab dekhte hain, woh is hadees par ghaur karein. Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo kisi aurat se us ki izzat ki wajah se nikaah kare, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ us ki zillat badha dega. Jo us ke maal ki wajah se nikaah kare, Allah Ta'ala us ki mohtaaji hi badha dega. Aur jo us ke khandani martabe ki wajah se nikaah kare, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ us ke kamine-pan mein izafa kar dega.” (Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, man ismuhu Ibrahim, 2/18, hadees: 2342)

**{وَ الّٰتِیْ تَخَافُوْنَ نُشُوْزَهُنَّ: Aur jin auraton ki naafarmaani ka tumhein dar ho.}** Is aayat mein naafarman biwi ki islaah ka tareeqa nihayat achhe andaaz mein bayan hua hai.

### Naafarman biwi ki islaah ka tareeqa

Sab se pehle naafarman biwi ko shohar ki ita'at ke faide aur naafarmaani ke nuqsanaat samjhayein. Quran-o-hadees mein is silsile mein jo fazaail aur wa'eedein aayi hain, woh bhi batayein. Agar is ke baad bhi na maane to us se apne bistar alag kar lein. Phir bhi na maane to munasib andaaz mein use maarein. Is maar se murad yeh hai ke haath ya miswaak jaisi cheez se chehre aur naazuk azaa ke ilawa badan par ek-do halki zarb lagayi jaye.

Woh maar murad nahin jo hamare yahan jaahilon mein raaij hai: chehre aur saare badan par maarna, mukkon aur ghoonson se peetna, laatain maarna, danday ya jo cheez haath mein aaye us se maarna aur lahu-luhaan kar dena. Yeh sab haraam, najaiz, gunaah-e-kabeerah aur intehaai jahalat-o-kameenapan hai.

### Shohar aur biwi dono ek doosre ke huqooq ka lihaaz rakhein

Aurat aur mard dono ko chahiye ke ek doosre ke huqooq ka lihaaz rakhein. Is silsile mein paanch ahadees hain:

1. Hazrat Amr bin Ahwas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Main tumhein auraton ke haq mein bhalai ki wasiyyat karta hoon. Woh tumhare paas qaid hain. Tum un ki kisi cheez ke maalik nahin, siwaye is ke ke woh khuli badkaari karein. Agar woh aisa karein to unhein bistar mein alag chhor do aur agar na maanein to halki maar maaro. Phir agar woh tumhari baat maan lein to un ke khilaaf koi raasta na dhoondo. Tumhare auraton par aur auraton ke tum par kuchh huqooq hain. Tumhara haq yeh hai ke woh tumhare bistar par tumhare na-pasand logon ko na aane dein aur jinhein tum na-pasand karte ho unhein tumhare ghar mein daakhil na karein. Un ka haq tum par yeh hai ke tum un se bhalai karo, unhein achha libaas aur achhi ghiza do.” (Tirmizi, Kitab-ur-Riza', Baab ma jaa'a fi haqq-il-mar'ati ala zawjiha, 2/387, hadees: 1166)

2. Hazrat Mu'aaz bin Jabal رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jab aurat duniya mein apne shohar ko takleef deti hai to hoor-e-een kehti hain: Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ tujhe qatl kare, ise takleef na de. Yeh to tere paas mehmaan hai, jald hi tujh se juda ho kar hamare paas aa jayega.” (Tirmizi, Kitab-ur-Riza', 19-baab, 2/392, hadees: 1177)

3. Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Umm-e-Salamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Aali-Waqaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo aurat is haal mein wafaat paaye ke us ka shohar us se raazi ho, woh Jannat mein daakhil ho gayi.” (Tirmizi, Kitab-ur-Riza', Baab ma jaa'a fi haqq-iz-zauj alal-mar'ah, 2/386, hadees: 1164)

4. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Main tumhein auraton ke baare mein bhalai ki wasiyyat karta hoon, tum meri is wasiyyat ko qabool karo. Woh pasli se paida ki gayi hain aur pasliyon mein sab se zyada tedhi upar wali pasli hoti hai. Agar tum use seedha karne lago to tum use tod doge aur agar use isi tarah chhor do to tedhi hi rahegi.” (Bukhari, Kitab-un-Nikaah, Baab-ul-wasiyyati bin-nisaa, 3/457, hadees: 5185)

5. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Aurat pasli se paida ki gayi hai. Woh tumhare liye kabhi seedhi nahin ho sakti. Agar tum us se faida uthana chaho to isi haalat mein faida utha sakte ho aur agar use seedha karna chaho to tod doge. Aur todna talaaq dena hai.” (Muslim, Kitab-ur-Riza', Baab-ul-wasiyyati bin-nisaa, safha 775, hadees: 61 (1468))

**{فَاِنْ اَطَعْنَكُمْ: Phir agar woh tumhari ita'at kar lein.}** Yani jis tarah gunah ke baad taubah karne par Allah Ta'ala taubah qabool farma leta hai, isi tarah tumhein bhi chahiye ke tumhare zair-e-dast aurat jab apni khata ke baad maafi maange, naafarmaani chhor kar ita'at-guzaar ban jaye to us ki maazrat qabool kar lo aur taubah ke baad use tang na karo.

### Biwi jab apni ghalati ki maafi maange to use maaf kar diya jaye

Is aayat se un logon ko naseehat haasil karni chahiye jo aurat ke hazaar baar maazrat karne, gidgida kar pairon mein girne aur tarah tarah ke waaste dene ke bawajood apni naak neeche nahin karte. Woh nazuk jins ko sitam ka nishana bana kar apni buzdili ko bahaduri samajhte hain. Allah Ta'ala in bahaduron ko aajizi aur samajhne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye.

## Ayat 4:35

`Source: tafseerId=50614; ayatId=532; surah=4; ayat=35; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِنْ خِفْتُمْ شِقَاقَ بَیْنِهِمَا فَابْعَثُوْا حَكَمًا مِّنْ اَهْلِهٖ وَ حَكَمًا مِّنْ اَهْلِهَاۚ-اِنْ یُّرِیْدَاۤ اِصْلَاحًا یُّوَفِّقِ اللّٰهُ بَیْنَهُمَاؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَلِیْمًا خَبِیْرًا(35)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar tumhein miyan-biwi ke jhagde ka khauf ho to ek munsif mard ke ghar walon ki taraf se bhejo aur ek munsif aurat ke ghar walon ki taraf se bhejo. Yeh dono agar sulah karana chahein to Allah un ke darmiyan ittefaaq paida kar dega. Beshak Allah khoob jaanne wala, khabardaar hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِنْ خِفْتُمْ شِقَاقَ بَیْنِهِمَا: Aur agar tumhein miyan-biwi ke jhagde ka khauf ho.}** Jab biwi ko samjhane, alag rakhne aur maarne ke bawajood islaah ki surat na ban rahi ho to na mard talaaq dene mein jaldi kare aur na aurat khula ka mutaalaba karne par israar kare. Balki dono khandanon ke khaas qareebi rishtedaron mein se ek-ek shakhs ko munsif muqarrar kar liya jaye.

Is ka faida yeh hai ke rishtedaar ek doosre ke gharelu halaat se waaqif hote hain, dono fareeqon ko un par itminaan hota hai aur un se apne dil ki baat kehne mein jhijhak bhi nahin hoti. Yeh munsif munasib tareeqe se un ka masla hal kar lenge. Agar munsif miyan-biwi mein sulah karane ka iraada rakhein to Allah Ta'ala un dono mein ittefaaq paida farma dega. Is liye mumkin had tak sulah ke zariye is maamle ko hal karna chahiye.

Lekin yaad rakhein ke in munsifon ko miyan-biwi mein judaai karwane ka ikhtiyaar nahin. Agar woh judaai ka faisla karein to shar'an un dono mein judaai waaqe ho jaye, aisa nahin ho sakta.
