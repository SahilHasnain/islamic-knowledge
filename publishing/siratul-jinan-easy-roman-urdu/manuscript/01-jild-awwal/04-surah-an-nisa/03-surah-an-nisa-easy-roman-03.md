# Surah An-Nisa — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 4:84

`Source: tafseerId=50655; ayatId=581; surah=4; ayat=84; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَقَاتِلْ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِۚ-لَا تُكَلَّفُ اِلَّا نَفْسَكَ وَ حَرِّضِ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَۚ-عَسَى اللّٰهُ اَنْ یَّكُفَّ بَاْسَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ اَشَدُّ بَاْسًا وَّ اَشَدُّ تَنْكِیْلًا(84)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To Ae Habeeb! Allah ki raah mein jihaad karein. Aap ko Aap ki taaqat se zyada takleef nahin di jaayegi aur Musalmanon ko (jihaad ki) targheeb dete rahein. Qareeb hai ke Allah kaafiron ki taaqat rok dega aur Allah ki taaqat sab se zyada mazboot hai aur us ka azaab sab se zyada shadeed hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَقَاتِلْ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ: To Ae Habeeb! Allah ki raah mein jihaad karein.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Badr-e-Sughra, yani chhote Ghazwa-e-Badr ki jang Abu Sufyan se tay thi. Jab us ka waqt aaya to Sarkar-e-Aali Waqar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne wahan jaane ke liye logon ko bulaya. Kuch logon par yeh baat bhaari hui, to Allah Ta'ala ne yeh aayat naazil farmaayi aur apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko hukm diya ke Aap jihaad na chhorein, agarche Aap tanha hon. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ Aap ka madadgaar hai aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka waada sachcha hai. Yeh hukm paakar Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Badr-e-Sughra ki jang ke liye rawana hue aur Aap ke saath sirf sattar sawar the. (Madarik, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 84, safha 242)

Chunanche farmaya gaya ke Aap jihaad ke liye jaayein. Aap ko Aap ki taaqat se zyada takleef nahin di jaayegi, chahe koi Aap ka saath de ya na de aur chahe Aap akele reh jaayein. Haan, Musalmanon ko jihaad ki targheeb dete rahein. Phir Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaya ke Allah Kareem kaafiron ki sakhti ko rok dega. Aisa hi hua ke Musalmanon ke is chhote se lashkar se kaafir itne darr gaye ke Musalmanon ke muqaable mein maidaan mein na aa sake.

### Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki shuja'at

Is aayat se saabit hua ke Tajdaar-e-Madinah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ shuja'at mein sab se aala hain. Aap ko akele kaafiron ke muqaable mein jaane ka hukm hua aur Aap is ke liye tayyar ho gaye.

Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Habeeb-e-Khuda صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ tamaam logon se zyada shuja aur bahaadur the. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Fazail, Baab fi Shuja'atin-Nabiyyi alaihis-salaam wa taqaddumihi lil-harb, safha 1262, hadees: 48 (2307))

Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain: Maine Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se zyada bahaadur aur taaqatwar, sakhi aur pasandeeda kisi ko nahin dekha. (Ash-Shifa, al-qism-ul-awwal, al-baab-us-sāni, fasl wa amma ash-shuja'atu wan-najdah, safha 116, al-juz-ul-awwal)

Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki be-misaal shuja'at aur bahaaduri ka yeh haal tha ke Hazrat Ali al-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم jaise bahaadur Sahabi ka qaul hai: Jab ladaai khoob garam ho jaati thi aur jang ki shiddat dekh kar bade bade bahaaduron ki aankhen pathra kar surkh pad jaati thin, us waqt hum log Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke pehlu mein khade hokar apna bachaao karte the. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hum sab se zyada aage badh kar dushmanon ke bilkul qareeb pahunch kar jang farmate the. Hum mein sab se zyada bahaadur woh shakhs samjha jaata tha jo jang mein Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke qareeb reh kar dushmanon se ladta tha. (Ash-Shifa, al-qism-ul-awwal, al-baab-us-sāni, fasl wa amma ash-shuja'atu wan-najdah, safha 116, al-juz-ul-awwal)

Ghazwa-e-Hunain ke din jab shuru mein Musalman kaafiron ke hamle ko bardasht na kar sake aur maidaan-e-jang se bhaag gaye, to aise naazuk waqt mein Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ek qadam bhi peeche nahin hate, balki apne safed khacchar par sawar hokar dushmanon ki taraf aage badhte rahe. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab fi Ghazwati Hunain, safha 978, hadees: 76 (1775))

Ghazwa-e-Ahzaab ke mauqe par khandaq khodte hue ek aisi chattaan saamne aayi jo kisi se na toot saki. Sarkar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke waar se woh chattaan tukde tukde ho gayi. (Nasai, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad, Ghazwat-ut-Turk wal-Habashah, safha 517, hadees: 3173)

Ek raat Ahle-Madinah ne ek daraawni aawaaz sun kar ghabra gaye, to us aawaaz ki taraf sab se pehle Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ tashreef le gaye. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Adab, Baab Husn-ul-Khulqi was-Sakha, 4/108, hadees: 6033)

A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain:

Tum ho Hafeez
o Mugees, kya hai woh dushman khabees

Tum ho to phir khauf kya, tum pe karoron durood

## Ayat 4:85

`Source: tafseerId=50656; ayatId=582; surah=4; ayat=85; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** مَنْ یَّشْفَعْ شَفَاعَةً حَسَنَةً یَّكُنْ لَّهٗ نَصِیْبٌ مِّنْهَاۚ-وَ مَنْ یَّشْفَعْ شَفَاعَةً سَیِّئَةً یَّكُنْ لَّهٗ كِفْلٌ مِّنْهَاؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ مُّقِیْتًا(85)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jo achhi sifaarish kare us ke liye us ka ajr hai aur jo buri sifaarish kare us ke liye is mein se hissa hai aur Allah har cheez par qaadir hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مَنْ یَّشْفَعْ شَفَاعَةً حَسَنَةً: Jo achhi sifaarish kare.}** Achhi sifaarish woh hai jis mein kisi ko jaaiz faida pahunchaya jaaye ya takleef se bachaya jaaye. Misal ke taur par koi shakhs waqai naukri ka mustahiq ho aur kisi doosre ki haq-talfi na ho rahi ho, to us ke liye sifaarish karna jaaiz hai. Isi tarah koi mazloom ho aur police se insaaf dilaane mein madad ke liye sifaarish ki jaaye, to yeh bhi achhi sifaarish hai.

Buri sifaarish woh hai jis mein ghalat sifaarish ki jaaye, zaalim ko ghalat tareeqe se bachaya jaaye ya kisi ki haq-talfi ki jaaye. Misal ke taur par kisi na-mustahiq ko naukri dilaane ke liye sifaarish karna, ya kisi ko sharaab ya cinema ka license dilaane ki sifaarish karna. Aisi sifaarish haraam hai.

## Ayat 4:86

`Source: tafseerId=50657; ayatId=583; surah=4; ayat=86; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا حُیِّیْتُمْ بِتَحِیَّةٍ فَحَیُّوْا بِاَحْسَنَ مِنْهَاۤ اَوْ رُدُّوْهَاؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ حَسِیْبًاٜ      (86)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab tumhein kisi lafz se salaam kiya jaaye to tum is se behtar lafz se jawaab do ya wohi alfaaz keh do. Beshak Allah har cheez par hisaab lene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذَا حُیِّیْتُمْ بِتَحِیَّةٍ: Aur jab tumhein kisi lafz se salaam kiya jaaye.}** Islam se pehle Arab ki aadat thi ke jab woh ek doosre se milte to kehte: “حَیَّاکَ اللہُ” yani Allah Ta'ala tumhein zinda rakhe. Jab Islam aaya to is jumle ko “salaam” se badal diya gaya. Yeh lafz “حَیَّاکَ اللہُ” ke muqaable mein zyada mukammal hai, kyunke jo shakhs salaamat hoga woh zaroor zinda hoga, lekin sirf zinda shakhs zaroori nahin ke salaamat bhi ho, kyunke us ki zindagi museebaton aur aafaton se mili hui ho sakti hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 86, 4/161)

### Salaam se muta'alliq shar'i masail

Is aayat mein salaam ke baare mein bayan hua hai. Is munasabat se yahan salaam se muta'alliq kuch shar'i masail zikr kiye jaate hain:

1. Salaam karna sunnat hai aur jawaab dena farz hai. Jawaab mein afzal yeh hai ke salaam karne wale ke salaam par kuch badha diya jaaye. Misal ke taur par pehla shakhs “اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَیْکُمْ” kahe to doosra “وَعَلَیْکُمُ السَّلَامْ وَرَحْمَۃُ اللہِ” kahe. Agar pehle ne “وَرَحْمَۃُ اللہِ” bhi kaha tha to doosra “وَبَرَکَاتُہٗ” bhi badha de. Is se zyada salaam aur jawaab mein koi izaafa nahin hai.

2. Kaafir, gumraah, faasiq aur istinja karne wale Musalmanon ko salaam na karein. Isi tarah jo shakhs khutbe, tilawat-e-Quran, hadees, deeni ilm ki guftagu, azaan ya takbeer mein mashghool ho, us ko bhi us waqt salaam na kiya jaaye. Agar koi unhein salaam kar de to un par jawaab dena laazim nahin.

3. Jo shakhs shatranj, chaupar, taash, ganjafah ya koi aur najaaiz khel khel raha ho, ya gaane bajaane mein mashghool ho, ya paikhane ya ghusl-khane mein ho, ya barahna ho, us ko salaam na kiya jaaye.

4. Aadmi jab apne ghar mein daakhil ho to apni biwi ko salaam kare. Baaz jagahon par yeh badi ghalat rasm hai ke miyaan-biwi ke itne gehre ta'alluq ke bawajood dono ek doosre ko salaam karne se mehroom rehte hain, halaanke salaam us shakhs ke liye salaamati ki dua hai jise kiya jaata hai.

5. Behtar sawaari wala kamtar sawaari wale ko, kamtar sawaari wala paidal chalne wale ko, paidal chalne wala baithe hue ko, chhota bade ko aur kam log zyada logon ko salaam karein. Salaam se muta'alliq shar'i masail ki mazeed maloomat ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 16 ka mutala'ah kijiye.

## Ayat 4:87

`Source: tafseerId=50658; ayatId=584; surah=4; ayat=87; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَللّٰهُ لَاۤ اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَؕ-لَیَجْمَعَنَّكُمْ اِلٰى یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِ لَا رَیْبَ فِیْهِؕ-وَ مَنْ اَصْدَقُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ حَدِیْثًا۠    (87)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah hi hai jis ke siwa koi ibaadat ke laaiq nahin aur woh zaroor tumhein Qiyamat ke din ikattha karega jis mein koi shak nahin aur Allah se zyada kis ki baat sachchi.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَنْ اَصْدَقُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ حَدِیْثًا: Aur Allah se zyada kis ki baat sachchi.}** Farmaya gaya ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se zyada sachcha koi nahin, kyunke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka jhoot bolna mumkin aur muhaal hai. Jhoot aib hai aur har aib Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke liye muhaal hai. Allah Ta'ala tamaam aibon se paak hai.

### Imkaan-e-Kizb ka radd

Musalmanon ka aqeedah hai ke Allah Ta'ala ke kisi kalaam mein jhoot ka mumkin hona zaati taur par muhaal hai. Allah Ta'ala ki tamaam sifaat mukammal taur par sifaat-e-kamaal hain. Jis tarah kisi sifat-e-kamaal ki Allah Ta'ala se nafi muhaal hai, isi tarah kisi nuqs ya aib ki sifat ka Allah Ta'ala ke liye saabit hona bhi muhaal hai. Allah Ta'ala ka yahi farman hai:

وَ مَنْ اَصْدَقُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ حَدِیْثًا

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Allah se zyada kis ki baat sachchi.

Yeh aqeedah ki bahut badi daleel hai. Is aayat ke taht Allamah Abdullah bin Ahmad Nasafi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Is aayat mein istifhaam-e-inkaari hai, yani khabar, waade aur wa'eed kisi bhi baat mein koi shakhs Allah Ta'ala se zyada sachcha nahin. Allah Ta'ala ka jhoot zaati taur par muhaal hai, kyunke jhoot apne ma'na hi ke aitbaar se qabeeh hai. Jhoot waaqe ke khilaaf khabar dene ka naam hai. (Madarik, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 87, safha 243)

Allamah Baizawi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Allah Ta'ala is aayat mein is baat ka inkaar farmata hai ke koi shakhs Allah Ta'ala se zyada sachcha ho, kyunke Allah Ta'ala ki khabar tak kisi jhoot ki kisi tarah gunjaish nahin. Jhoot aib hai aur aib Allah Ta'ala par muhaal hai. (Baizawi, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 87, 2/229)

Allah Ta'ala mazeed irshaad farmata hai:

فَلَنْ یُّخْلِفَ اللّٰهُ عَهْدَهٗ (بقرہ:۸۰)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To Allah harگز waada-khilaafi nahin karega.

Is aayat ke taht Imam Fakhr-ud-Deen Razi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Allah Ta'ala ka yeh farman is baat par daleel hai ke Allah Ta'ala apne har waade aur wa'eed mein jhoot se paak hai. Hamare Ahl-e-Sunnat-o-Jama'at ke ulama is daleel se Allah Ta'ala ke jhoot ko na-mumkin jaante hain, kyunke jhoot sifat-e-naqs hai aur naqs Allah Ta'ala par muhaal hai. Mu'tazilah is daleel se Allah Ta'ala ke jhoot ko na-mumkin maante hain, kyunke jhoot apni zaat mein qabeeh hai aur Allah Ta'ala se iska zaahir hona muhaal hai. Gharz yeh saabit hua ke Allah Ta'ala ka jhoot bolna bilkul mumkin nahin. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Baqarah, taht-al-aayat: 80, 1/567, khulasa)

Shah Abdul Aziz Muhaddis Dehlawi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is aayat ke taht farmate hain: Allah Ta'ala ki khabar azali hai. Kalaam mein jhoot hona bada naqs hai, is liye Allah Ta'ala ki sifaat mein hargiz daakhil nahin ho sakta, kyunke Allah Ta'ala tamaam aibon aur nuqoos se paak hai. Allah Ta'ala ke haq mein khabar ke khilaaf hona saraasar naqs hai. (Tafseer-e-Azeezi, tarjuma, Al-Baqarah, taht-al-aayat: 80, 2/547, khulasa)

Allah Ta'ala mazeed irshaad farmata hai:

وَ تَمَّتْ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ صِدْقًا وَّ عَدْلًاؕ-لَا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمٰتِهٖۚ-وَ هُوَ السَّمِیْعُ الْعَلِیْمُ(انعام:۱۱۵)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur sach aur insaaf ke aitbaar se tere Rab ke kalimaat mukammal hain. Us ke kalimaat ko koi badalne wala nahin aur wahi sunne wala, jaanne wala hai.

Imam Fakhr-ud-Deen Razi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is aayat ke taht farmate hain: Yeh aayat is baat par daleel hai ke Allah Ta'ala ki baat bahut si sifaat ke saath mausoof hai. Un mein se ek sifat us ka sachcha hona hai. Is par daleel yeh hai ke jhoot aib hai aur aib Allah Ta'ala par muhaal hai. Mazeed farmate hain ke Quran-o-Hadees ke dalail ka sahih hona is baat par mauqoof hai ke Allah Ta'ala ke kizb ko muhaal maana jaaye. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-An'am, taht-al-aayat: 115, 5/125)

Jhoot fi-nafsihi do baaton se khaali nahin: ya to woh naqs hoga ya naqs nahin hoga. Yeh zaahir hai ke jhoot zaroor naqs hai. Jab woh naqs hai to ba-ijma' Allah Ta'ala ke liye muhaal ho gaya, kyunke Allah Ta'ala har naqs aur aib se paak hai. Doosri surat mein agar jhoot ko naqs aur aib na bhi maana jaaye, tab bhi Allah Ta'ala ke liye muhaal hai, kyunke agar jhoot naqs nahin to kamaal bhi nahin. Allah Ta'ala sirf naqs aur aib se paak nahin, balki har us cheez se bhi paak hai jo kamaal se khaali ho, chahe woh naqs aur aib mein shamil na ho. Allah Ta'ala ki har sifat sifat-e-kamaal hai aur jis mein koi kamaal hi na ho woh Allah Ta'ala ki sifat kaise ho sakti hai.

Yaad rahe ke logon ke jhoot bolne par qaadir hone aur Allah Ta'ala ke liye jhoot na-mumkin hone se yeh hargiz laazim nahin aata ke logon ki qudrat, ma'azAllah, Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat se badh gayi. Yeh kehna ke banda jhoot bol sakta hai aur Allah Ta'ala jhoot nahin bol sakta, is se yeh laazim nahin aata ke insaan ki qudrat, ma'azAllah, Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat se zyada ho gayi. Agar yeh baat sahih hoti ke aadmi jo kuch kar sakta hai Allah Ta'ala bhi kar sakta hai, to yeh bhi laazim aata ke jis tarah insaan nikah kar sakta aur biwi se hum-bistari kar sakta hai, Allah Ta'ala bhi, ma'azAllah, yeh kar sakta ho. Isi tarah jis tarah aadmi khaana kha sakta, paani pee sakta, apne aap ko darya mein dubo sakta, aag se jala sakta aur khaak aur kaanton par let sakta hai, Allah Ta'ala bhi apne liye yeh sab kar sakta ho. In sooraton mein insaan har tarah Khudaai se haath dho baithega, kyunke agar Allah Ta'ala yeh sab apne liye kar sakta ho to woh naaqis aur mohtaaj hua, aur naaqis aur mohtaaj Khuda nahin ho sakta. Aur agar na kar sake to aajiz thehrega aur kamaal-e-qudrat mein aadmi se kam ho jaayega, jabke aajiz Khuda nahin ho sakta.

Hamara sachcha Khuda sab aibon se aur muhaal par qudrat ki tohmat se paak aur munazzah hai. Koi mumkin us ki qudrat se baahar nahin, kisi ki qudrat us ki qudrat ke humsar nahin, aur apne liye kisi aib ya naqs par qaadir hona us ki quddoosi shaan ke laaiq nahin.

**Note:** Is mas'ale par tafseeli maloomat ke liye Fatawa Razawiyyah ki pandrahween jild mein maujood A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ke in rasaail ka mutala'ah karein: (1) سُبْحٰنُ السُّبُّوْحْ عَنْ عَیْبِ کِذْبٍ مَقْبُوْحْ (jhoot jaise badtareen aib se Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke paak hone ka bayan), (2) دَامَانِ بَاغِ سُبْحَانِ السُّبُّوْحْ (Risala Subhan-us-Subbooh ke baagh ka daaman), (3) اَلْقَمْعُ الْمُبِینْ لِآمَالِ الْمُکَذِّبِینْ (Allah Ta'ala ke liye jhoot mumkin maanne walon ke istidlaal ka radd).

## Ayat 4:88

`Source: tafseerId=50659; ayatId=585; surah=4; ayat=88; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَمَا لَكُمْ فِی الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ فِئَتَیْنِ وَ اللّٰهُ اَرْكَسَهُمْ بِمَا كَسَبُوْاؕ-اَتُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ تَهْدُوْا مَنْ اَضَلَّ اللّٰهُؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّضْلِلِ اللّٰهُ فَلَنْ تَجِدَ لَهٗ سَبِیْلًا(88)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To tumhein kya hua ke munaafiqon ke baare mein do giroh ho gaye, halaanke Allah ne un ke aamaal ke sabab un ke dilon ko ulat diya hai. Kya tum yeh chahte ho ke tum use raah dikha do jise Allah ne gumraah kar diya aur jise Allah gumraah kar de to hargiz tum us ke liye hidaayat ka raasta na paoge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَمَا لَكُمْ فِی الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ فِئَتَیْنِ: To tumhein kya hua ke munaafiqon ke baare mein do giroh ho gaye?}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke munaafiqon ki ek jama'at khul kar murtad ho gayi aur mushrikon se ja mili. Un ke baare mein Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke do giroh ho gaye. Ek giroh unhein qatl karne par israar kar raha tha aur ek giroh un ke qatl se inkaar kar raha tha. Is maamale mein yeh aayat naazil hui. (Madarik, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 88, safha 243)

Aur farmaya gaya: Ae imaan walo! Tumhein kya ho gaya ke tum munaafiqon ke baare mein do giroh ban gaye, halaanke Allah Ta'ala ne un ke dilon ko un ke murtad hone aur mushrikon se ja milne ki wajah se ulat diya hai. Kya tum chahte ho ke jise Allah Ta'ala ne gumraah kar diya hai use hidaayat ki raah dikha do? ## Ayat 4:89

`Source: tafseerId=50660; ayatId=586; surah=4; ayat=89; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَدُّوْا لَوْ تَكْفُرُوْنَ كَمَا كَفَرُوْا فَتَكُوْنُوْنَ سَوَآءً فَلَا تَتَّخِذُوْا مِنْهُمْ اَوْلِیَآءَ حَتّٰى یُهَاجِرُوْا فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِؕ-فَاِنْ تَوَلَّوْا فَخُذُوْهُمْ وَ اقْتُلُوْهُمْ حَیْثُ وَجَدْتُّمُوْهُمْ ۪- وَ لَا تَتَّخِذُوْا مِنْهُمْ وَلِیًّا وَّ لَا نَصِیْرًاۙ    (89)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh to yeh chahte hain ke jaise woh kaafir hue kaash ke tum bhi waise hi kaafir ho jao, phir tum sab ek jaise ho jao. To tum un mein se kisi ko apna dost na banao jab tak woh Allah ki raah mein hijrat na karein. Phir agar woh munh pherein to unhein pakro aur jahan pao qatl karo aur un mein kisi ko na dost banao aur na hi madadgaar.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَدُّوْا لَوْ تَكْفُرُوْنَ كَمَا كَفَرُوْا: Woh to yeh chahte hain ke jaise woh kaafir hue kaash ke tum bhi waise hi kaafir ho jao.}** Pehli aayaat mein munaafiqon ki apni sarkashi ka bayan hua aur is aayat mein un ke kufr aur sarkashi mein hadd se badhne ka bayan hai. Farmaya gaya: Ae Musalmano! Jo munaafiq imaan chhor kar kufr aur irtidaad ki taraf laut gaye, woh to yeh chahte hain ke jaise woh kaafir hue kaash tum bhi waise hi kaafir ho jao, phir tum sab kufr mein ek jaise ho jao. Jab un ka yeh haal hai to tum un mein se kisi ko us waqt tak apna dost na banao jab tak woh Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein hijrat na karein.

Hijrat se un ke imaan ka saboot mil jaayega ke un ka imaan Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki raza ke liye hai, kisi dunyawi maqsad ke liye nahin. Phir agar woh hijrat karne se munh pherein aur kufr par qaayam rehna pasand karein to Ae Musalmano! Tum unhein pakro aur jahan pao qatl karo. Aur agar woh tumhari dosti ka da'wa karein aur dushmanon ke khilaaf tumhari madad ke liye tayyar hon to un ki madad qabool na karo, kyunke woh bhi dushman hain. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 89, 2/256; Khazin, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 89, 1/411, mulaqqatan)

### Aayat se milne wale ahkaam

Is aayat se chand baatein maloom hui:

1. Doosre ko kaafir karne ki koshish karna kufr hai.

2. Kaafir, murtad aur badmazhab ko dost banana aur un se dili muhabbat rakhna haraam hai, agarche woh kalimah padhta ho aur apne aap ko Musalman kehta ho, jaise us zamane ke munaafiq the. A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Kuffar aur mushrikeen se ittehaad-o-wadaad haraam-e-qat'i hai. Quran-e-Azeem ki nusoos us ki hurmat ka elaan kar rahi hain. Aur agar kuch na ho to itna hi kaafi hai:

وَ مَنْ یَّتَوَلَّهُمْ مِّنْكُمْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنْهُمْ (مائدہ:۵۱)

Allah Wahid-o-Qahhar farmata hai ke tum mein jo koi un se dosti rakhega woh yaqeenan unhi mein se hai. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 21/229)

3. Deeni maamlaat mein mushrik se madad na li jaaye. Hazrat Abu Humaid Sa'idi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Hum mushrikeen se madad nahin lenge.” (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad, La nasta'eenu bil-mushrikeena alal-mushrikeen, 2/456, hadees: 2610)

## Ayat 4:90

`Source: tafseerId=50661; ayatId=587; surah=4; ayat=90; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ یَصِلُوْنَ اِلٰى قَوْمٍۭ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ بَیْنَهُمْ مِّیْثَاقٌ اَوْ جَآءُوْكُمْ حَصِرَتْ صُدُوْرُهُمْ اَنْ یُّقَاتِلُوْكُمْ اَوْ یُقَاتِلُوْا قَوْمَهُمْؕ-وَ لَوْ شَآءَ اللّٰهُ لَسَلَّطَهُمْ عَلَیْكُمْ فَلَقٰتَلُوْكُمْۚ-فَاِنِ اعْتَزَلُوْكُمْ فَلَمْ یُقَاتِلُوْكُمْ وَ اَلْقَوْا اِلَیْكُمُ السَّلَمَۙ-فَمَا جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ عَلَیْهِمْ سَبِیْلًا(90)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Magar un logon ko qatl na karo jo aisi qaum se ta'alluq rakhte hon ke tumhare aur un ke darmiyan aman ka mu'ahadah ho, ya tumhare paas is haal mein aayein ke un ke dil tum se laraai karne se tang aa chuke hon ya tumhare saath mil kar apni qaum se laraai karein. Aur Allah agar chahta to zaroor unhein tum par musallat kar deta, to woh yaqeenan tum se larte. Phir agar woh tum se door rahein aur na laren aur tumhari taraf sulah ka paighaam bhejein to sulah ki surat mein Allah ne tumhein un par laraai ka koi raasta nahin rakha.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ یَصِلُوْنَ اِلٰى قَوْمٍ: Magar jo aisi qaum se ta'alluq rakhte hon.}** Pichhli aayat mein qatl ka hukm diya gaya tha. Ab farmaya ja raha hai ke kuch log is hukm se alag hain. Woh yeh hain:

1. Woh log jin ka aisi qaum se ta'alluq ho jin se tumhara aman ka mu'ahadah ho chuka ho.

2. Woh log jo tum se laraai na karein.

3. Woh log jo tumhare saath mil kar apni qaum se laren.

In sab logon ko qatl karne ki ijaazat nahin. Phir Allah Ta'ala ne apna mazeed ehsaan bayan farmaya ke agar Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ chahta to zaroor unhein tum par musallat kar deta aur woh tum se larte, balki tum par ghaalib bhi aa jaate. Lekin Allah Ta'ala ne un ke dilon mein rob daal diya aur Musalmano ko un ke shar se mehfooz rakha.

**{فَاِنِ اعْتَزَلُوْكُمْ: Phir agar woh tum se door rahein.}** Agar kaafir tum se door rahein aur na laren, balki sulah ka paighaam bhejein to is surat mein tumhein un se jang karne ki ijaazat nahin. Baaz mufassireen farmate hain ke yeh aayat mansookh hai aur ab Islami sultan ko sulah karne ya na karne ka ikhtiyaar hai. (Jamal, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 90, 2/99; Khazin, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 90, 1/412, mulaqqatan)

## Ayat 4:91

`Source: tafseerId=50662; ayatId=588; surah=4; ayat=91; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** سَتَجِدُوْنَ اٰخَرِیْنَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ یَّاْمَنُوْكُمْ وَ یَاْمَنُوْا قَوْمَهُمْؕ-كُلَّمَا رُدُّوْۤا اِلَى الْفِتْنَةِ اُرْكِسُوْا فِیْهَاۚ-فَاِنْ لَّمْ یَعْتَزِلُوْكُمْ وَ یُلْقُوْۤا اِلَیْكُمُ السَّلَمَ وَ یَكُفُّوْۤا اَیْدِیَهُمْ فَخُذُوْهُمْ وَ اقْتُلُوْهُمْ حَیْثُ ثَقِفْتُمُوْهُمْؕ-وَ اُولٰٓىٕكُمْ جَعَلْنَا لَكُمْ عَلَیْهِمْ سُلْطٰنًا مُّبِیْنًا۠   (91)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Qareeb hai tum kuch doosron ko paoge jo chahte hain ke woh tum se bhi aman mein rahein aur apni qaum se bhi aman mein rahein. Lekin jab kabhi unhein fitne ki taraf phera jaata hai to woh us mein aundhe ja parte hain. Phir agar woh tum se kinaara-kashi na karein aur tumhare saath sulah na karein aur apne haath tum se laraai karne se na rokein to tum unhein pakro aur jahan pao unhein qatl karo. Aur yahi woh log hain jin ke khilaaf hum ne tumhein khula ikhtiyaar diya hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{سَتَجِدُوْنَ اٰخَرِیْنَ: Qareeb hai tum kuch doosron ko paoge.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Madinah Tayyibah mein Asad aur Ghatafaan do qabeelon ke log riyaakaari ke taur par kalimah padhte aur apne aap ko Musalman zahir karte the. Jab un mein se koi apni qaum ke paas jaata aur woh log us se kehte ke tum kis cheez par imaan laaye ho, to woh kehte: “Bandaron, bichchhuon waghera par”, yani Islam ka mazaaq udaate. Un ka maqsad yeh tha ke dono taraf ta'alluqaat rakhein aur kisi taraf se unhein nuqsaan na pahunche. Yeh log munaafiq the aur inhi ke baare mein yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khazin, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 91, 1/412)

Un ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke yeh log tum se bhi aman chahte hain aur apni qaum se bhi, lekin haqeeqat mein tumhare saath nahin hain. Jab unhein kisi fitne, masalan shirk ya Musalmano se jang, ki taraf bulaya jaata hai to yeh Musalmano ke dushmanon hi ke saath hote hain. Mazeed farmaya gaya ke agar yeh log tumhare saath jang karne se baaz aa kar ek taraf na ho jaayen aur tumhare saath sulah na karein to un ke kufr, ghaddari aur Musalmano ko nuqsaan pahunchane ki wajah se hum ne tumhein unhein qatl karne ka khula ikhtiyaar diya hai.

## Ayat 4:92

`Source: tafseerId=50663; ayatId=589; surah=4; ayat=92; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا كَانَ لِمُؤْمِنٍ اَنْ یَّقْتُلَ مُؤْمِنًا اِلَّا خَطَــٴًـاۚ-وَ مَنْ قَتَلَ مُؤْمِنًا خَطَــٴًـا فَتَحْرِیْرُ رَقَبَةٍ مُّؤْمِنَةٍ وَّ دِیَةٌ مُّسَلَّمَةٌ اِلٰۤى اَهْلِهٖۤ اِلَّاۤ اَنْ یَّصَّدَّقُوْاؕ-فَاِنْ كَانَ مِنْ قَوْمٍ عَدُوٍّ لَّكُمْ وَ هُوَ مُؤْمِنٌ فَتَحْرِیْرُ رَقَبَةٍ مُّؤْمِنَةٍؕ-وَ اِنْ كَانَ مِنْ قَوْمٍۭ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ بَیْنَهُمْ مِّیْثَاقٌ فَدِیَةٌ مُّسَلَّمَةٌ اِلٰۤى اَهْلِهٖ وَ تَحْرِیْرُ رَقَبَةٍ مُّؤْمِنَةٍۚ-فَمَنْ لَّمْ یَجِدْ فَصِیَامُ شَهْرَیْنِ مُتَتَابِعَیْنِ٘-تَوْبَةً مِّنَ اللّٰهِؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمًا حَكِیْمًا(92)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur kisi Musalman ke liye jaaiz nahin ke kisi Musalman ko qatl kare magar yeh ke ghalati se ho jaaye. Aur jo kisi Musalman ko ghalati se qatl kar de to ek Musalman ghulaam ko azaad karna aur diyat dena laazim hai jo maqtool ke ghar walon ke hawaale ki jaayegi, siwaye is ke ke woh maaf kar dein. Phir agar maqtool tumhari dushman qaum se ho aur woh khud Musalman ho to sirf ek Musalman ghulaam ko azaad karna laazim hai. Aur agar maqtool us qaum mein se ho ke tumhare aur un ke darmiyan mu'ahadah ho to us ke ghar walon ke hawaale diyat di jaaye aur ek Musalman ghulaam ya laundhi ko azaad kiya jaaye. Phir jise ghulaam na mile to do mahine ke lagataar roze laazim hain. Yeh Allah ki baargaah mein us ki taubah hai aur Allah jaanne wala, hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَا كَانَ لِمُؤْمِنٍ اَنْ یَّقْتُلَ مُؤْمِنًا: Aur kisi Musalman ke liye jaaiz nahin ke kisi Musalman ko qatl kare.}** Yeh aayat-e-mubarakah Ayyash bin Rabi'ah Makhzumi ke baare mein naazil hui. Hijrat se pehle woh Makkah Mukarramah mein Musalman ho gaye the aur ghar walon ke khauf se Madinah Tayyibah ja kar panaah-geer ho gaye. Un ki maa ko is se bahut beqaraari hui. Unhone Haris aur Abu Jahl, jo Ayyash ke sautelay bhai the, se kaha: “Khuda ki qasam! Na main saaye mein baithungi, na khaana chakhoongi aur na paani piyungi jab tak tum Ayyash ko mere paas na le aao.”

Woh dono Haris bin Zaid ko saath le kar Ayyash ki talaash mein nikle aur Madinah Tayyibah pahunch kar unhein paa liya. Unhone Ayyash ko un ki maa ke beqaraar hone aur khaana-peena chhor dene ki khabar di aur Allah Ta'ala ke naam par ahad kiya ke hum tum se deen ke baare mein kuch nahin kahenge, bas tum Makkah Mukarramah chalo. Is tarah woh Ayyash ko Madinah se nikaal laaye. Madinah se baahar aa kar unhein baandha aur har ek ne sau sau koore maare. Phir unhein maa ke paas laaye to maa ne kaha: “Main tumhari rassiyan nahin kholungi jab tak tum apna deen na chhor do.” Ayyash ko dhoop mein bandha hua daal diya gaya. In museebaton se majboor ho kar Ayyash ne un ki baat maan li aur apna deen chhor diya.

Is par Haris bin Zaid ne Ayyash ko malaamat ki aur kaha: “Tum Islam par the. Agar yeh haq tha to tum ne haq chhor diya aur agar baatil tha to tum baatil deen par rahe.” Yeh baat Ayyash ko bahut naagawaar guzri aur unhone kaha: “Jab main tumhein akela paaunga to Khuda ki qasam, zaroor tumhein qatl kar dunga.” Is ke baad Ayyash Islam laaye aur Madinah Tayyibah hijrat kar gaye. Un ke baad Haris bhi Islam laaye aur hijrat kar ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein pahunche, lekin us din Ayyash maujood na the aur unhein Haris ke Islam laane ki khabar na hui. Quba Shareef ke qareeb Ayyash ne Haris ko dekh kar qatl kar diya. Logon ne kaha: “Ae Ayyash! Tum ne bahut bura kiya, Haris Islam la chuke the.” Ayyash ko bahut afsos hua. Woh Rahmat-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat-e-aqdas mein haazir hue aur arz ki ke mujhe qatl ke waqt tak un ke Islam ki khabar hi na hui. Is par yeh aayat-e-karimah naazil hui aur kaffare ki surat bayan ki gayi. (Baghawi, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 92, 1/368)

**{وَ مَنْ قَتَلَ: Aur jo qatl kare.}** Is aayat mein qatl ki chaar sooratein bayan hui hain aur phir teen sooraton mein kaffare ka bayan hai.

Pehli surat yeh hai ke Musalman ka kisi doosre Musalman ko na-haq qatl karna haraam hai.

Doosri surat yeh hai ke koi Musalman kisi doosre Musalman ko ghalati se qatl kar de, jaise shikaar ko maar raha ho lekin goli Musalman ko lag jaaye, ya kisi ko jang karne wala kaafir samajh kar maar diya ho lekin qatl ke baad maloom ho ke maqtool Musalman tha. Is surat mein qaatil par ek ghulaam ya laundhi ko azaad karna laazim hai aur is ke saath diyat bhi ada karega jo maqtool ke waarison ko di jaayegi. Woh isay meeras ki tarah taqseem karenge. Diyat maqtool ke tarke ke hukm mein hai. Is se maqtool ka qarz bhi ada kiya jaayega aur wasiyyat bhi poori ki jaayegi. Haan, agar maqtool ke waaris diyat maaf kar dein to woh maaf ho jaayegi.

Teesri surat yeh hai ke agar maqtool dushman qaum se ho lekin woh khud Musalman ho to sirf ek Musalman ghulaam ko azaad karna laazim hai aur diyat waghera kuch laazim na hogi.

Chauthi surat yeh hai ke agar maqtool zimmi ho ya Muslim hukoomat ki ijaazat se Muslim mulk mein aaya ho, jise musta'man kehte hain, to usay qatl karne ki surat mein us ke ghar walon ko diyat di jaayegi aur ek Musalman ghulaam ya laundhi ko azaad kiya jaayega. Agar ghulaam ya laundhi na mile, jaise hamare zamane mein ghulaam aur laundiyan hain hi nahin, to do mahine ke lagataar roze rakhe jaayenge. Yaad rahe ke qatl-e-khata ke kaffare mein kaafir ghulaam azaad nahin kiya jaayega. Baqi kaffaaron mein Hanafi mazhab ke mutabiq har tarah ka ghulaam azaad kar sakte hain, jaise roze ya zihar ke kaffare mein.

## Ayat 4:93

`Source: tafseerId=50664; ayatId=590; surah=4; ayat=93; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ یَّقْتُلْ مُؤْمِنًا مُّتَعَمِّدًا فَجَزَآؤُهٗ جَهَنَّمُ خٰلِدًا فِیْهَا وَ غَضِبَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِ وَ لَعَنَهٗ وَ اَعَدَّ لَهٗ عَذَابًا عَظِیْمًا(93)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jo kisi Musalman ko jaan boojh kar qatl kar de to us ka badla Jahannam hai, arsa-e-daraaz tak us mein rahega aur Allah ne us par ghazab kiya aur us par la'nat ki aur us ke liye bada azaab tayyar kar rakha hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَنْ یَّقْتُلْ: Aur jo qatl kare.}** Pichhli aayat mein ghalati se kisi Musalman ko qatl kar dene ka hukm bayan hua aur is aayat mein jaan boojh kar kisi Musalman ko qatl karne ki ukhrawi wa'eed bayan hui hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 93, 4/182)

### Musalman ko na-haq qatl karne ki mazammat

Kisi Musalman ko jaan boojh kar qatl karna shadeed-tareen gunaah-e-kabeera hai aur bahut si ahadees mein is ki mazammat bayan hui hai. Un mein se chaar ahadees yeh hain:

1. Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Bade gunaah-e-kabeera mein se ek kisi jaan ko na-haq qatl karna hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ud-Diyaat, Baab Qaulullahi Ta'ala: Wa man ahyaaha, 4/358, hadees: 6871)

2. Kisi Musalman ko na-haq qatl karne wala Qiyamat ke din bade nuqsaan ka shikaar hoga. Hazrat Abu Bakrah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Agar zameen-o-aasmaan wale kisi Musalman ke qatl par jama ho jaayen to Allah Ta'ala sab ko aundhe munh Jahannam mein daal de.” (Mu'jam-e-Sagheer, Baab-ul-Ain, man ismuhu Ali, safha 205, al-juz-ul-awwal)

3. Hazrat Abu Bakrah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marwi hai, Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Jab do Musalman apni talwaron se laren to qaatil aur maqtool dono Jahannam mein jaayenge.” Raawi kehte hain: Maine arz ki: Maqtool Jahannam mein kyun jaayega? Irshaad farmaya: “Is liye ke woh apne saathi ko qatl karne par israar kar raha tha.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab Wa in taaaifataani minal-mu'mineena iqtataloo, 1/23, hadees: 31)

4. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Jis ne kisi momin ke qatl par ek harf jitni bhi madad ki to woh Qiyamat ke din Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein is haal mein aayega ke us ki dono aankhon ke darmiyan likha hoga: ‘Yeh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki rahmat se mayoos hai.’” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ud-Diyaat, Baab-ut-Taghleez fi Qatli Muslimin Zulman, 3/262, hadees: 2620)

Afsos ke aaj kal qatl karna bahut mamooli kaam ho gaya hai. Chhoti chhoti baaton par jaan se maar dena, gundagardi, dehshatgardi, daaka-zani, khandaani laraai aur ta'assub wali laraiyan aam hain. Musalmano ka khoon paani ki tarah bahaaya jaata hai. Giroh, jathe aur askari dasta bane hue hain jin ka kaam hi qatl-o-ghaaratgari karna hai.

### Musalmano ka baahmi ta'alluq kaisa hona chahiye?

Ek Musalman ka doosre Musalman se ta'alluq kaisa hona chahiye, is baare mein paanch ahadees yeh hain:

1. Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se marwi hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Musalman woh hai jis ke haath aur zabaan se doosre Musalman mehfooz rahein.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab-ul-Muslim man salimal-Muslimoon, 1/15, hadees: 10)

2. Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Tum mein se koi us waqt tak momin nahin ho sakta jab tak apne bhai ke liye bhi wohi pasand na kare jo apne liye pasand karta hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab min-al-imaan an yuhibba li-akheehi, 1/16, hadees: 13)

3. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Musalman Musalman ka bhai hai. Na us par zulm kare, na use haqeer jaane.” Aur Taqwa yahan hai aur apne seene ki taraf teen martaba ishaara farmaya. “Insaan ke liye yeh buraai kaafi hai ke apne Musalman bhai ko haqeer jaane. Musalman par Musalman ki har cheez haraam hai: us ka khoon, us ka maal aur us ki aabroo.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Aadaab, Baab Tahreem Zulmil-Muslim wa Khazlihi, safha 1386, hadees: 32 (2564))

4. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se marwi hai, Sarwar-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Woh hum mein se nahin jo hamare chhoton par rahm na kare, hamare badon ki ta'zeem na kare, achhi baaton ka hukm na de aur buri baaton se mana na kare.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah, Baab ma jaa'a fi rahmat-is-sibyaan, 3/369, hadees: 1928)

5. Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Musalman ko gaali dena fisq hai aur use qatl karna kufr hai.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab Bayaan Qaul-in-Nabiyyi ﷺ: Sibaabul-Muslim, safha 52, hadees: 116 (64))

### Musalman ko qatl karna kaisa hai?

Agar Musalmano ke qatl ko halaal samajh kar iska irtikaab kiya to yeh khud kufr hai aur aisa shakhs hamesha Jahannam mein rahega. Aur agar qatl ko haraam hi samjha lekin phir bhi iska irtikaab kiya to yeh gunaah-e-kabeera hai aur aisa shakhs arsa-e-daraaz tak Jahannam mein rahega. Aayat mein **خَالِدًا** ka lafz hai. Is ka ek ma'na hamesha hota hai aur doosra ma'na arsa-e-daraaz hota hai. Yahan doosre ma'na mein zikr hua hai.

## Ayat 4:94

`Source: tafseerId=50665; ayatId=591; surah=4; ayat=94; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا ضَرَبْتُمْ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ فَتَبَیَّنُوْا وَ لَا تَقُوْلُوْا لِمَنْ اَلْقٰۤى اِلَیْكُمُ السَّلٰمَ لَسْتَ مُؤْمِنًاۚ-تَبْتَغُوْنَ عَرَضَ الْحَیٰوةِ الدُّنْیَا٘-فَعِنْدَ اللّٰهِ مَغَانِمُ كَثِیْرَةٌؕ-كَذٰلِكَ كُنْتُمْ مِّنْ قَبْلُ فَمَنَّ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْكُمْ فَتَبَیَّنُوْاؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعْمَلُوْنَ خَبِیْرًا(94)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Jab tum Allah ke raaste mein chalo to khoob tahqeeq kar liya karo aur jo tumhein salaam kare use yeh na kaho ke tu Musalman nahin. Tum dunyawi zindagi ka saamaan chahte ho, pas Allah ke paas bahut se ghanimat ke maal hain. Pehle tum bhi aise hi the to Allah ne tum par ehsaan kiya, to khoob tahqeeq kar liya karo. Beshak Allah tumhare tamam aamaal se khabardar hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِذَا ضَرَبْتُمْ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ فَتَبَیَّنُوْا: Jab tum Allah ke raaste mein chalo to khoob tahqeeq kar liya karo.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Fadak ke rehne wale Mirdas bin Nahik ki qaum mein un ke siwa koi Islam nahin laya tha. Jab un logon ko khabar mili ke Islam ka lashkar un ki taraf aa raha hai to sab log bhaag gaye, lekin Mirdas wahin thehre rahe. Jab unhone door se lashkar ko dekha to yeh soch kar ke kahin koi ghair-Muslim jamaat na ho, apni bakriyan lekar pahaad ki choti par chadh gaye. Lashkar aaya aur unhone Allahu Akbar ke naare sune to khud bhi takbeer padhte hue neeche aaye aur kehne lage: لَا اِلٰـہَ اِلَّا اللہُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَّسُوْلُ اللہِ، السّلام علیکم۔ Musalmano ne socha ke Fadak ke sab log kaafir hain aur yeh shakhs dhoka dene ke liye iman ka izhaar kar raha hai. Is khayal se Hazrat Usamah bin Zaid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne unhein qatl kar diya aur bakriyan le aaye.

Jab Tajdaar-e-Madinah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein haazir hokar poora waaqia arz kiya gaya to Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko bahut ranj hua aur farmaya: “Tum ne is ke saamaan ki wajah se use qatl kar diya.” Phir Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Hazrat Usamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko hukm diya ke maqtool ki bakriyan us ke ghar walon ko wapas kar do. (Khazin, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 94, 1/417)

Yeh riwayat kuch lafzi farq ke saath Bukhari aur doosri kutub-e-hadees mein bhi maujood hai. Is aayat mein farmaya ja raha hai ke ae iman walo! Jab tum Allah Ta'ala ke raaste mein jao to khoob tahqeeq kar liya karo. Jo tumhein salaam kare ya jis mein Islam ki koi alamat pao, us se haath rok lo. Jab tak us ka kufr saabit na ho, us par haath na uthao aur use yeh na kaho ke tu Musalman nahin. Abu Daud aur Tirmizi ki hadees mein hai ke Nabi-e-Rahmat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ jab koi lashkar rawana farmate to hukm dete ke agar tum koi masjid dekho ya azaan suno to qatl na karna. (Abu Daud, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad, Baab fi Dua-il-Mushrikeen, 3/60, hadees: 2635; Tirmizi, Kitaab-us-Siyar, 2-baab, 3/194, hadees: 1554)

**{كَذٰلِكَ كُنْتُمْ مِّنْ قَبْلُ: Pehle tum bhi aise hi the.}** Musalmano ko samjhane ke liye mazeed farmaya gaya ke pehle tum bhi aise hi the. Yani jab tum Islam mein daakhil hue the to tumhari zabaan se kalima-e-shahaadat sun kar tumhare jaan-o-maal mehfooz kar diye gaye the aur tumhare izhaar-e-iman ko be-aitbaar nahin samjha gaya tha. Islam mein daakhil hone walon ke saath tumhein bhi aisa hi sulook karna chahiye. Yeh tum par Allah Ta'ala ka ehsaan hai ke tumhein Islam par isteqaamat di aur tumhara momin hona mashhoor kiya. Is liye khoob tahqeeq kar liya karo, kahin tumhare haathon koi iman wala qatl na ho. Is se maloom hua ke jo Musalman kaafiron ke darmiyan rehta ho aur Musalmano ko us ke iman ki khabar na ho, us ke qatl se na kaffara waajib hoga aur na diyat.

Yaad rahe ke pichhli aayat mein woh surat bayan hui thi jahan Musalman ka Islam sab ko maloom ho, lekin andhere waghera ki wajah se pata na chale aur Musalmano ke haath se woh maara jaye. Is aayat mein woh surat bayan hui hai jismein Musalman ka iman kisi ko maloom nahin. Is liye in dono aayaat mein koi ta'aruz nahin.

## Ayat 4:95

`Source: tafseerId=50666; ayatId=592; surah=4; ayat=95; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** لَا یَسْتَوِی الْقٰعِدُوْنَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ غَیْرُ اُولِی الضَّرَرِ وَ الْمُجٰهِدُوْنَ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ بِاَمْوَالِهِمْ وَ اَنْفُسِهِمْؕ-فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ الْمُجٰهِدِیْنَ بِاَمْوَالِهِمْ وَ اَنْفُسِهِمْ عَلَى الْقٰعِدِیْنَ دَرَجَةًؕ-وَ كُلًّا وَّعَدَ اللّٰهُ الْحُسْنٰىؕ-وَ فَضَّلَ اللّٰهُ الْمُجٰهِدِیْنَ عَلَى الْقٰعِدِیْنَ اَجْرًا عَظِیْمًاۙ      (95)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Uzr walon ke ilawa jo Musalman jihaad se baithe rahe woh aur Allah ki raah mein apne maal aur jaan ke saath jihaad karne wale barabar nahin. Apni jaan aur maal ke saath jihaad karne walon ko baithe rehne walon par Allah ne darjon ke aitbaar se fazeelat di hai. Allah ne sab se bhalaai ka waada farmaya hai aur jihaad karne walon ko baithe rehne walon par bahut bade ajr ki fazeelat di hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا یَسْتَوِی الْقٰعِدُوْنَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ غَیْرُ اُولِی الضَّرَرِ: Uzr walon ke ilawa jo Musalman jihaad se baithe rahe woh barabar nahin.}** Is aayat mein jihaad ki targheeb hai ke baithe rehne wale aur jihaad karne wale barabar nahin, balki mujahideen ke liye bade darje aur sawab hain. Yeh mas'alah bhi saabit hota hai ke jo log beemari, budhape, na-taaqat, na-beenaai, haath-paaon ke na-kaara hone ya kisi uzr ki wajah se jihaad mein haazir na hon, woh fazeelat se mehroom nahin kiye jaayenge jab un ki niyyat achhi ho.

Bukhari Shareef ki riwayat mein hai ke jab aayat ka pehla hissa naazil hua ke mujahideen aur ghair-mujahideen barabar nahin, to Hazrat Abdullah bin Umm-e-Maktum رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, jo na-beena Sahabi the, arz karne lage: “Ya RasoolAllah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Main na-beena hoon, jihaad mein kis tarah jaaun?” Is par **غَیْرُ اُولِی الضَّرَرِ** naazil hua, yani ma'zooron ko mustasna kar diya gaya. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab Qaulullahi: La yastawil-qa'idun, 2/263, hadees: 2832)

Bukhari Shareef hi mein Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Ghazwa-e-Tabook se wapsi par farmaya: “Kuch log Madinah mein reh gaye hain. Hum kisi ghaati ya aabaadi mein nahin chalte magar woh hamare saath hote hain. Unhein uzr ne rok liya hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab man habasah-ul-uzru an-il-ghazw, 2/265, hadees: 2839)

### Niyyat ki azmat aur jihaad ka sawab

Is se maloom hua ke niyyat bahut azeem amal hai. Sachchi niyyat ki surat mein haqeeqi amal kiye baghair bhi sawab mil jaata hai. Haan, uzr ki wajah se jihaad mein haazir na hone wale niyyat ka sawab paayenge, lekin jihaad karne walon ko amal ki fazeelat is se zyada haasil hai. Allah ki raah mein jaan-o-maal kharch karne ki fazeelat ke baare mein yeh chaar ahadees mulahiza hon:

1. Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein arz ki gayi: “Ya RasoolAllah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Logon mein sab se afzal shakhs kaun hai?” Farmaya: “Jo Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein apne maal aur jaan ke saath jihaad kare.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab afzal-un-naas mominun yujaahidu binafsihi, 2/249, hadees: 2786)

2. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se arz ki gayi: “Kya Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein jihaad ke barabar bhi koi ibaadat hai?” Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Tum is ki iste'ta'at nahin rakhte.” Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ne phir sawaal kiya, hatta ke teen martaba poocha, aur Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne har baar farmaya ke tum is ki taaqat nahin rakhte. Teesri baar farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein jihaad karne wala jihaad se wapsi tak us shakhs ki tarah hai jo rozedaar, qiyaam karne wala aur Allah Ta'ala ki aayaat par amal karne wala ho; na woh roze se thakta hai aur na namaaz se uktaata hai.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaarah, Baab Fadl-ush-Shahaadah fi Sabeelillah Ta'ala, safha 1044, hadees: 110 (1878))

3. Hazrat Khuraym bin Faatik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein kuch kharch kiya, us ke liye saat sau guna sawab likha jaata hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab Faza'il-ul-Jihaad, Baab ma jaa'a fi fazl-in-nafaqah fi sabeelillah, 3/233, hadees: 1631)

4. Hazrat Mu'aaz رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Beshak Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein nikal kar zikr karne ka sawab maal kharch karne se saat lakh guna zyada hai.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Makkiyeen, Hadees Mu'aaz bin Anas al-Juhani, 5/314, hadees: 15647)

## Ayat 4:96

`Source: tafseerId=50667; ayatId=593; surah=4; ayat=96; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** دَرَجٰتٍ مِّنْهُ وَ مَغْفِرَةً وَّ رَحْمَةًؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرًا رَّحِیْمًا۠    (96)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah ki taraf se bahut se darje, bakhshish aur rehmat hai aur Allah bakhshne wala, meherban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{دَرَجٰتٍ مِّنْهُ: Allah ki taraf se bahut se darje.}** Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne mujahideen ka ajr bayan farmaya ke un ke liye Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se Jannat ke bahut se darje, un ke gunahon ki bakhshish aur Jannat ki ne'matein hain. Allah Ta'ala jihaad karne walon ko bakhshne wala aur un par meherban hai. (Tafseer-e-Samarqandi, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 96, 1/380)

### Jannat mein mujahideen ke darje aur maghfirat

Ahadees mein mujahideen ke Jannati darjon ki tafseel bayan hui hai. Is silsile mein yeh teen ahadees hain:

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ne mujahideen ke liye Jannat mein sau darje tayyar farmaye hain. Har do darjon ke darmiyan itna faasla hoga jitna aasman aur zameen ke darmiyan hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab darajaat-il-mujaahideen fi sabeelillah, 2/250, hadees: 2790)

2. Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Ae Abu Saeed رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ! Jo shakhs Allah ke Rab hone, Islam ke deen hone aur Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Nabi hone par raazi ho gaya, us ke liye Jannat waajib ho gayi.” Hazrat Abu Saeed رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko yeh baat achhi lagi, to arz ki: “Ya RasoolAllah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Is baat ko dobara irshaad farma dein.” Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne dobara isi tarah farmaya. Phir farmaya: “Ek aur baat hai jis ki wajah se bande ke sau darje buland hote hain aur har do darjon ke darmiyan zameen-o-aasman jitna faasla hai.” Maine arz ki: “Ya RasoolAllah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Woh darja kis cheez se milta hai?” Farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein jihaad karne se, Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein jihaad karne se.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaarah, Baab bayaan ma a'addallahu lil-mujaahid fil-jannah min-ad-darajaat, safha 1045, hadees: 116 (1884))

3. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs Allah ki raah mein jihaad kare aur ghar se us ka nikalna sirf Allah ki raah mein jihaad aur us ke deen ki tasdeeq ke liye ho, to Allah Ta'ala is baat ka zaamin ho jaata hai ke agar woh shaheed ho gaya to use Jannat mein daakhil karega, ya ajr aur ghanimat ke saath us ke ghar wapas bhej dega, jahan se woh rawana hua tha.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaarah, Baab fadl-ul-jihaad wal-khurooj fi sabeelillah, safha 1042, hadees: 104 (1876))

## Ayat 4:97

`Source: tafseerId=50668; ayatId=594; surah=4; ayat=97; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ تَوَفّٰىهُمُ الْمَلٰٓىٕكَةُ ظَالِمِیْۤ اَنْفُسِهِمْ قَالُوْا فِیْمَ كُنْتُمْؕ-قَالُوْا كُنَّا مُسْتَضْعَفِیْنَ فِی الْاَرْضِؕ-قَالُوْۤا اَلَمْ تَكُنْ اَرْضُ اللّٰهِ وَاسِعَةً فَتُهَاجِرُوْا فِیْهَاؕ-فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ مَاْوٰىهُمْ جَهَنَّمُؕ-وَ سَآءَتْ مَصِیْرًاۙ    (97)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak jin logon ki jaan farishte is haal mein qabz karte hain ke woh apni jaan par zulm karne wale hote hain, un se farishte kehte hain: Tum kis haal mein the? Woh kehte hain: Hum zameen mein kamzor the. To farishte kehte hain: Kya Allah ki zameen kushadah na thi ke tum us mein hijrat kar jaate? To yeh woh log hain jin ka thikana Jahannam hai aur woh kitni buri lautne ki jagah hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ظَالِمِیْ أَنْفُسِهِمْ: Apni jaan par zulm karne wale.}** Yeh aayat un logon ke baare mein naazil hui jinhone zabaan se kalima-e-Islam to padha, lekin jab hijrat farz thi us waqt hijrat na ki. Jab mushrikeen Ghazwa-e-Badr mein Musalmano ke muqaable ke liye gaye to yeh log bhi un ke saath ho liye aur kaafiron ke saath hi maare gaye. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab innal-lazeena tawaffaahumul-malaaikah, 3/209, hadees: 4596; Sunan-ul-Kubra lil-Bayhaqi, Kitaab-us-Siyar, Baab fard-ul-hijrah, 9/22, hadees: 17749)

Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke jin logon ki rooh farishte is haal mein qabz karte hain ke woh farz hijrat chhor kar aur kaafiron ka saath de kar apni jaan par zulm kar rahe hote hain, maut ke waqt farishte unhein jhirakte hue kehte hain: Tum apne deen ke maamle mein kis haal mein the? Woh uzr pesh karte hue kehte hain ke hum zameen mein kamzor log the aur kaafiron ki sarzameen mein rehne ki wajah se deen ke ahkaam par amal karne se aajiz the. Farishte un ka uzr radd karte aur daantte hue kehte hain: Kya Allah Ta'ala ki zameen kushadah na thi ke tum kufr ki sarzameen se aisi jagah hijrat kar jaate jahan deen ke ahkaam par amal kar sakte? Jin logon ke bure haal yahan bayan hue, Aakhirat mein un ka thikana Jahannam hai aur woh kitni buri lautne ki jagah hai. (Jalalain, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 97, safha 85; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 97, 2/268-269, multaqatan)

### Hijrat kab waajib hai?

Is aayat se saabit hua ke jo shakhs kisi shehar mein apne deen par qaayam na reh sakta ho aur use maloom ho ke doosri jagah ja kar apne deeni faraiz ada kar sakega, us par hijrat waajib ho jaati hai. Is hukm ko saamne rakh kar kaafiron ke darmiyan rehne wale bahut se Musalmano ko ghaur karne ki zaroorat hai. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ taufeeq ata farmaye. Hadees mein hai ke jo shakhs apne deen ki hifaazat ke liye ek jagah se doosri jagah muntaqil hua, agarche ek baalisht hi kyun na ho, us ke liye Jannat waajib hui aur use Hazrat Ibrahim aur Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki rafaqat naseeb hogi. (Tafseer-e-Samarqandi, Al-Ankaboot, taht-al-aayat: 56, 2/542)

### Hijrat ki qismein aur un ke ahkaam

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne hijrat ki qismein bayan farmayi hain. Un mein se ek qism Dar-ul-Islam se hijrat hai. Is ke baare mein farmate hain: Dar-ul-Islam se hijrat-e-aamma haraam hai, kyun ke is se masaajid ki veerani aur be-hurmati, Musalmano ki qabron ki barbaadi, aur aurton, bachon aur kamzor logon ki tabaahi hogi. Hijrat-e-khaassa ki teen suratein hain:

1. Agar koi shakhs kisi khaas wajah se kisi khaas jagah apne deeni faraiz ada na kar sakta ho aur doosri jagah unhein ada karna mumkin ho, to agar majboori isi ghar mein ho to us par farz hai ke yeh ghar chhor kar doosre ghar chala jaye. Agar mohalle mein majboor ho to doosre mohalle mein chala jaye aur agar shehar mein majboor ho to doosre shehar mein chala jaye.

2. Agar yahan apne deeni faraiz ada karne se aajiz na ho, lekin us ke boodhe maa-baap, biwi ya bachay jin ka nafaqah us par farz hai, na ja sakte hon ya na jaayen, aur us ke chale jaane se woh be-sahaara ho jaayen, to us ke liye Dar-ul-Islam se hijrat karna haraam hai. Hadees mein hai: “Kisi aadmi ke gunahgaar hone ke liye itna kaafi hai ke woh use zaya kar de jis ka nafaqah us ke zimme tha.” Isi tarah woh aalim bhi wahan se hijrat nahin kar sakta jis se badh kar us shehar mein koi aalim na ho.

3. Na woh deeni faraiz ada karne se aajiz ho aur na hi wahan us ki khaas zaroorat ho, to use ikhtiyaar hai ke wahin rahe ya chala jaye. Jo us ki maslahat mein ho woh kar sakta hai. Yeh tafseel Dar-ul-Islam ke baare mein hai. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 14/131-132, mulakhkhasan)

## Ayat 4:98

`Source: tafseerId=50669; ayatId=595; surah=4; ayat=98; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِلَّا الْمُسْتَضْعَفِیْنَ مِنَ الرِّجَالِ وَ النِّسَآءِ وَ الْوِلْدَانِ لَا یَسْتَطِیْعُوْنَ حِیْلَةً وَّ لَا یَهْتَدُوْنَ سَبِیْلًاۙ    (98)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Magar woh majboor mard, aurtein aur bachay jo na koi tadbeer karne ki taaqat rakhte hon aur na raasta jaante hon.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِلَّا الْمُسْتَضْعَفِیْنَ مِنَ الرِّجَالِ وَالنِّسَاءِ وَالْوِلْدَانِ: Magar woh majboor mard, aurtein aur bachay.}** Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke woh majboor mard, aurtein aur bachay jo na hijrat karne ki taaqat rakhte hon, na un ke paas kharch ho aur na hijrat ki jagah ka raasta jaante hon, to aise be-bas aur majboor log hijrat na karne par pakar ke mustahiq nahin. Qareeb hai ke Allah Ta'ala un se dar-guzar farmaye, aur Allah Ta'ala bakhshne wala, maaf farmane wala hai. (Jalalain, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 98, safha 85; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, taht-al-aayat: 97, 2/269, multaqatan)
