# Surah An-Nisa — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 4:131

`Source: tafseerId=50694; ayatId=628; surah=4; ayat=131; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِؕ-وَ لَقَدْ وَصَّیْنَا الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ وَ اِیَّاكُمْ اَنِ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-وَ اِنْ تَكْفُرُوْا فَاِنَّ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ غَنِیًّا حَمِیْدًا(131)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Allah hi ka hai jo kuchh aasmanon mein hai aur jo kuchh zameen mein aur beshak hum ne un logon ko jinhein tum se pehle Kitaab di gayi aur tumhein bhi takeed farma di hai ke Allah se darte raho aur agar na maano to beshak Allah hi ka hai jo kuchh aasmanon mein hai aur jo kuchh zameen mein hai aur Allah be-niyaaz hai, khoobiyon ka maalik hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنْ یَّشَاْ یُذْهِبْكُمْ اَیُّهَا النَّاسُ: Ae logo! Agar woh chahe to tumhein le jaaye.}** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ kainaat se be-niyaaz hai. Agar saari kainaat us ki ibaadat karne lage to us ki shaan mein koi izaafa nahin hota, aur agar saari duniya us ki na-farmaan ho jaaye to us ki shaan mein koi kami nahin aati. Woh Ghani aur be-parwaah hai. Woh chahe to tum sab ko fana kar de aur doosre logon ko le aaye. Tumhein maut de kar kisi doosri qaum ko yahan basa de, jaise Fir'aun ke mulk ka maalik doosron ko bana diya. Us ki shaan buland hai aur woh har cheez par qaadir hai.

Hazrat Abu Zar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya ke Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: "Ae mere bando! Tum sab gumraah ho, siwaaye us ke jise main hidayat doon. Is liye mujh se hidayat maango, main tumhein hidayat doonga. Tum sab faqeer ho, siwaaye us ke jise main Ghani kar doon. Is liye mujh se maango, main tumhein rizq doonga. Tum sab mujrim ho, siwaaye us ke jise main salaamat rakhun. Tum mein se jo yeh jaan le ke main bakhshne par qaadir hoon, phir mujh se maghfirat maange to main use bakhsh doonga aur parwaah bhi nahin karunga.

"Agar tumhare agle-pichhle, zinda-murde, tar-o-khushk sab mere bando mein sab se nek bande ke dil jaise ho jaayen, yani saare insan us nek aadmi ki tarah ho jaayen, to un ki neki meri baadshahi mein machhar ke par ke barabar bhi izaafa nahin karegi. Aur agar tumhare agle-pichhle, zinda-murde, tar-o-khushk sab mere bando mein sab se badbakht aadmi ke dil jaise ho jaayen to un ke gunaah meri baadshahi se machhar ke par ke barabar bhi kam nahin karenge.

"Agar tumhare pichhle aur agle, zinda aur murde, tar-o-khushk ek maidaan mein jama ho jaayen aur tum mein se har shakhs apni intehaai tamanna aur aarzoo mujh se maange, phir main har maangne wale ko de doon, to meri baadshahi ke muqaable mein itna hi hoga jaise tum mein se koi dariya ke paas se guzre aur us mein sui dubo kar phir use utha le, yani kuchh bhi kam nahin hoga. Yeh is liye hai ke main ata karne wala, bahut dene wala hoon. Jo chahta hoon karta hoon. Meri ata ke liye sirf mera farma dena kaafi hai aur mere azaab ke liye bhi sirf mera farma dena kaafi hai. Kisi cheez ke baare mein mera hukm yeh hai ke jab main kisi cheez ka iraada karta hoon to sirf itna farmata hoon: 'Ho ja', to woh ho jaati hai." (Tirmizi, Kitaab Sifat-ul-Qiyaamat, 48waan Baab, 4/222, hadees: 2503)

## Ayat 4:134

`Source: tafseerId=50695; ayatId=631; surah=4; ayat=134; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** مَنْ كَانَ یُرِیْدُ ثَوَابَ الدُّنْیَا فَعِنْدَ اللّٰهِ ثَوَابُ الدُّنْیَا وَ الْاٰخِرَةِؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ سَمِیْعًۢا بَصِیْرًا۠    (134)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jo duniya ka inaam chahta hai to duniya aur Aakhirat ka inaam Allah hi ke paas hai aur Allah hi sunta, dekhta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مَنْ كَانَ یُرِیْدُ ثَوَابَ الدُّنْیَا: Jo duniya ka inaam chahe.}** Is ka matlab yeh hai ke jo shakhs apne amal se sirf duniya chahega, use duniya hi mil sakti hai, lekin woh Aakhirat ke sawab se mehroom rahega. Aur jis ne Allah Ta'ala ki raza aur Aakhirat ke sawab ke liye amal kiya ho, to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ use duniya aur Aakhirat dono mein sawab dene wala hai.

Jo shakhs Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se sirf duniya maange, woh naadaan, kanjoos aur kam-himmat hai. Jab Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke paas duniya aur Aakhirat ki har bhalai hai to us se duniya aur Aakhirat dono ki bhalai maango. Maangne wale mein himmat honi chahiye. Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke duniya ko apna asal maqsad na banao ke Aakhirat ko bhool jao, aur na hi bilkul duniya chhor dena chahiye.

## Ayat 4:135

`Source: tafseerId=50696; ayatId=632; surah=4; ayat=135; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا كُوْنُوْا قَوّٰمِیْنَ بِالْقِسْطِ شُهَدَآءَ لِلّٰهِ وَ لَوْ عَلٰۤى اَنْفُسِكُمْ اَوِ الْوَالِدَیْنِ وَ الْاَقْرَبِیْنَۚ-اِنْ یَّكُنْ غَنِیًّا اَوْ فَقِیْرًا فَاللّٰهُ اَوْلٰى بِهِمَا- فَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا الْهَوٰۤى اَنْ تَعْدِلُوْاۚ-وَ اِنْ تَلْوٗۤا اَوْ تُعْرِضُوْا فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ بِمَا تَعْمَلُوْنَ خَبِیْرًا(135)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Allah ke liye gawahi dete hue insaaf par khoob qaim ho jao, chahe tumhare apne ya walidain ya rishtedaaron ke khilaf hi gawahi ho. Jis ke khilaf gawahi do, woh ameer ho ya ghareeb, baharhaal Allah un dono ke zyada qareeb hai. To nafs ki khwahish ke peeche na chalo ke insaaf na karo. Aur agar tum baat ko pher do ya munh pher lo to beshak Allah tumhare kaamon se khabar rakhta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{كُوْنُوْا قَوّٰمِیْنَ بِالْقِسْطِ: Insaaf par khoob qaim ho jao.}** Is aayat mein adl-o-insaaf ke taqaze poore karne ka aham hukm diya gaya hai. Jo cheezein aadmi ko na-insaafi ki taraf le ja sakti hain, unhein bhi wazeh kar diya gaya hai taake woh insaaf ke raaste mein rukawat na banen.

Rishtedaaron ko tarjeeh dena, apne logon ki tarafdaari karna, ta'alluq rakhne walon ka lihaz karna, kisi ki ameeri ki wajah se us ka saath dena, ya kisi ki ghurbat par taras kha kar doosre fareeq par zyadti karna, yeh sab insaaf ke taqaze poore karne mein rukawat hain. In sab ka zikr karne ke baad Allah Ta'ala ne hukm diya ke faisla karte aur gawahi dete waqt jo sahi hukm hai us ke mutabiq chalo aur kisi ta'alluq ka lihaz na karo. Yahan tak ke agar tumhara faisla ya tumhari gawahi tumhare sage maa-baap ke khilaf bhi ho, tab bhi adl se na hato.

**Haq faisle ki azeem tareen misaal:** Is ki azeem tareen misaal is hadees ki roshni mein dekhiye. Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا farmati hain: "Quraish ke ek khandan ki aurat ne chori ki. Us ke khandan walon ne Hazrat Usamah bin Zaid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se kaha ke woh Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein us ki sifarish karein. Hazrat Usamah bin Zaid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne sifarish ki to Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: 'Kya tum Allah Ta'ala ki hudood mein se ek hadd ke baare mein sifarish karte ho?' Phir Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ kharay hue aur khutbah irshad farmaya. Phir farmaya: 'Tum se pehle logon ko is baat ne halaak kiya ke jab un mein se koi moazziz shakhs chori karta to use chhor dete, aur jab koi kamzor chori karta to us par hadd qaim kar dete. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam! Agar Fatimah bint Muhammad رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا bhi chori kar leti to main us ka bhi haath kaat deta.'" (Bukhari, Kitaab Ahadees-ul-Anbiya, 56waan Baab, 2/468, hadees: 3475)

## Ayat 4:136

`Source: tafseerId=50697; ayatId=633; surah=4; ayat=136; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اٰمِنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ رَسُوْلِهٖ وَ الْكِتٰبِ الَّذِیْ نَزَّلَ عَلٰى رَسُوْلِهٖ وَ الْكِتٰبِ الَّذِیْۤ اَنْزَلَ مِنْ قَبْلُؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّكْفُرْ بِاللّٰهِ وَ مَلٰٓىٕكَتِهٖ وَ كُتُبِهٖ وَ رُسُلِهٖ وَ الْیَوْمِ الْاٰخِرِ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ ضَلٰلًۢا بَعِیْدًا(136)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Allah aur us ke Rasool par aur us kitaab par jo us ne apne Rasool par utaari aur us kitaab par jo is se pehle naazil ki gayi, in sab par hamesha iman rakho. Aur jo Allah, us ke farishton, us ki kitaabon, us ke Rasoolon aur Qiyamat ko na maane to woh zaroor door ki gumraahi mein ja pada.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اٰمِنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ رَسُوْلِهٖ: Allah aur us ke Rasool par iman rakho.}** Is aayat mein ahl-e-iman ko mukhatab karke farmaya gaya: **اٰمِنُوْا**, yani iman lao. Agar yeh khitab haqeeqi Musalmanon se ho to iska matlab hai ke iman par mazbooti se qaim raho. Agar Yahood-o-Nasara se ho to matlab yeh hai: ae kuchh kitaabon aur kuchh Rasoolon par iman lane walo! Tum tamam kitaabon aur tamam Rasoolon par, jin mein Quran aur Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ bhi shamil hain, poora iman lao. Aur agar yeh khitab munafiqon se ho to matlab yeh hai: ae iman ka zahiri da'wa karne walo! Ikhlas ke saath iman lao.

Yahan Rasool se Sayyid-ul-Anbiya صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Kitaab se Quran-e-Paak murad hai. Yaad rahe ke ahl-e-iman ka lafz haqeeqi ma'ni ke lihaz se is zamane mein sirf Musalmanon ke liye bola ja sakta hai. Kisi aur mazhab wale, chahe woh Yahoodi ho ya Isaai, ke liye yeh lafz nahin bola ja sakta.

Aayat mein farmaya gaya ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, us ke Rasool Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, Quran, aur is se pehle ki har kitaab aur Rasool par iman lao. Is se maloom hua ke tamam kitaabon par iman lana zaroori hai, lekin amal sirf Quran Shareef par hoga. Un kitaabon ke baare mein yeh aqeedah rakhna chahiye ke woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki bheji hui kitaabein hain.

## Ayat 4:137

`Source: tafseerId=50698; ayatId=634; surah=4; ayat=137; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا ثُمَّ كَفَرُوْا ثُمَّ اٰمَنُوْا ثُمَّ كَفَرُوْا ثُمَّ ازْدَادُوْا كُفْرًا لَّمْ یَكُنِ اللّٰهُ لِیَغْفِرَ لَهُمْ وَ لَا لِیَهْدِیَهُمْ سَبِیْلًاﭤ(137)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak woh log jo iman laaye, phir kafir ho gaye, phir iman laaye, phir kafir ho gaye, phir kufr mein aur barh gaye, to Allah hargiz na unhein bakhshega aur na unhein raah dikhayega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: Beshak jo iman laaye.}** **Sha'n-e-Nuzool:** Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُما ne farmaya ke yeh aayat un Yahoodiyon ke baare mein naazil hui jo Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman laaye, phir bachhre ki pooja karke kafir hue. Is ke baad phir iman laaye, phir Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Injeel ka inkaar karke kafir ho gaye. Phir Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Quran ka inkaar karke kufr mein aur barh gaye.

Ek qawl yeh hai ke yeh aayat munafiqon ke baare mein naazil hui. Woh iman laaye, phir iman ke baad kafir ho gaye, phir iman laaye, yani unhone apne iman ka izhar kiya taake un par mominon ke ahkaam jaari hon, phir kufr mein barh gaye, yani kufr hi par un ki maut hui. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 137, 1/440)

Un ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ unhein nahin bakhshega, yani jab tak woh kufr par qaim rahen aur kufr par maren. Kufr bakhsha nahin jata. Haan, jab kafir taubah karke iman le aaye to bakhshish ka mustahiq ho jata hai. Jaisa ke ek aur maqam par farmaya:

**قُلْ لِّلَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْۤا اِنْ یَّنْتَهُوْا یُغْفَرْ لَهُمْ مَّا قَدْ سَلَفَ**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kafiron se farma do: agar woh baaz aa jaayen to jo pehle ho chuka woh unhein bakhsh diya jayega. Hadees Shareef mein hai: "Islam pichhle tamam gunaahon ko mita deta hai." (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Iman, Baab: Kaun-ul-Islam Yahdim Ma Qablahu..., safha 74, hadees: 192 (121))

## Ayat 4:138

`Source: tafseerId=50699; ayatId=635; surah=4; ayat=138; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** بَشِّرِ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ بِاَنَّ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا اَلِیْمَاﰳۙ      (138)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Munafiqon ko khushkhabri do ke un ke liye dardnaak azaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ یَتَّخِذُوْنَ الْكٰفِرِیْنَ اَوْلِیَآءَ مِنْ دُوْنِ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ: Woh jo Musalmanon ko chhor kar kafiron ko dost banate hain.}** Is se pehli aayat mein munafiqon ke liye dardnaak azaab ki dhamki bayan ki gayi thi. Phir munafiqon ki ek buri harkat bhi bayan ki gayi ke woh Musalmanon ko chhor kar kafiron ko dost banate hain. Un ka khayal tha ke Islam ghalib nahin hoga. Is liye woh kafiron ko taaqat aur shaukat wala samajh kar un se dosti karte aur un se milne mein izzat samajhte the.

Haalanke kafiron se dosti mana hai aur un se milne mein izzat samajhna baatil hai. Is se maloom hua ke kafiron se mohabbat aur dosti rakhna, khaas taur par Musalmanon ke muqaable mein, munafiqon ki alamat hai. Aise sab logon ke baare mein farmaya ja raha hai ke kya yeh logon ke paas ja kar izzat dhoondte hain, jabke tamam izzaton ka Maalik Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ hai. Wahi Kareem Maula izzat wala hai. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ata se hi Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur momin izzat wale bante hain, jinhein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ izzat deta hai.

Munafiqon ke is rawaiye ko saamne rakh kar aaj duniya ke halaat dekhiye to maloom hoga ke yeh bimaari aaj bahut aam hai. Apnon ko chhor kar beganon se dosti, Musalmanon ko chhor kar kafiron se pyaar, aur baahmi ittehad se izzat paane ke bajaye kafiron ke qadmon mein baith kar izzat haasil karne ki koshish Musalman qaum mein kis tarah phaili hui hai. Allah Ta'ala hum sab ko aqal-e-saleem ata farmaye.

## Ayat 4:139

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=636; surah=4; ayat=139; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْنَ یَتَّخِذُوْنَ الْكٰفِرِیْنَ اَوْلِیَآءَ مِنْ دُوْنِ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَؕ-اَیَبْتَغُوْنَ عِنْدَهُمُ الْعِزَّةَ فَاِنَّ الْعِزَّةَ لِلّٰهِ جَمِیْعًاﭤ(139)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh jo Musalmanon ko chhor kar kafiron ko dost banate hain. Kya yeh un ke paas izzat dhoondte hain? To tamam izzaton ka Maalik Allah hai.

## Ayat 4:140

`Source: tafseerId=50700; ayatId=637; surah=4; ayat=140; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ قَدْ نَزَّلَ عَلَیْكُمْ فِی الْكِتٰبِ اَنْ اِذَا سَمِعْتُمْ اٰیٰتِ اللّٰهِ یُكْفَرُ بِهَا وَ یُسْتَهْزَاُ بِهَا فَلَا تَقْعُدُوْا مَعَهُمْ حَتّٰى یَخُوْضُوْا فِیْ حَدِیْثٍ غَیْرِهٖۤ ﳲ اِنَّكُمْ اِذًا مِّثْلُهُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ جَامِعُ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ وَ الْكٰفِرِیْنَ فِیْ جَهَنَّمَ جَمِیْعَاﰳۙ      (140)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak Allah tum par Kitaab mein yeh hukm naazil farma chuka hai ke jab tum suno ke Allah ki aayaat ka inkaar kiya ja raha hai aur un ka mazaaq udaaya ja raha hai to un logon ke saath na baitho jab tak woh kisi doosri baat mein mashghool na ho jaayen, warna tum bhi un jaise ho jaoge. Beshak Allah munafiqon aur kafiron sab ko Jahannam mein ikattha karne wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَدْ نَزَّلَ عَلَیْكُمْ فِی الْكِتٰبِ: Aur beshak Allah tum par Kitaab mein yeh hukm naazil farma chuka hai.}** Is aayat mein saaf farma diya gaya ke jo log Allah Ta'ala ki aayaat ka inkaar karte aur un ka mazaaq udaate hain, jab woh is bure kaam mein lage hon to un ke paas na baitho. Balki aisi jagah jao hi nahin. Agar jaana zaroori ho to pehle haath se roko, agar yeh mumkin na ho to zaban se roko, aur agar yeh bhi na kar sako to dil mein us harkat se nafrat karte hue wahan se uth jao. Un ki sohbat hargiz na ikhtiyar karo.

Quran, Shariat ya deen ka mazaaq udaaya ja raha ho aur koi shakhs phir bhi wahan baitha rahe to ya to woh khud is kaam mein mubtala ho jayega, ya buri sohbat ki nahusat se mutasir hoga, ya kam az kam yeh baat zaroor saamne aa jayegi ke us ke dil mein deen ki qadr nahin. Agar Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, Quran-e-Majeed aur deen se mohabbat hoti to jahan in ka mazaaq udaaya ja raha ho wahan hargiz na baithta. Insani fitrat hai ke jahan kisi ke pyare ko bura kaha jaaye wahan baithna gawara nahin hota. Jahan kisi ke maa-baap ko gaali di ja rahi ho wahan aadmi baith nahin sakta. To jahan Allah Ta'ala, Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Quran-o-deen ka mazaaq udaaya ja raha ho, wahan Musalman kaise baith sakta hai? Kya مَعَاذَاللہ Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur us ke pyare Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki qadr maa-baap ke barabar bhi nahin?

**Buri sohbat ki mazammat:** Is aayat se woh log sabaq lein jo film, drama, gaana, theatre, doston ki gap-shap aur badmazhabon ki sohbat mein deen ka mazaaq udaaya jaata dekhte hain, phir bhi wahan baithe rehte hain, balki مَعَاذَ اللہ un ki haan mein haan mila rahe hote hain. Buri sohbat ke baare mein ahadith bahut hain. Un mein se paanch ahadith yeh hain:

(1) Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Bure saathi se bach, kyunki log tumhein usi ke saath pehchaanenge." Yani jin logon ke paas aadmi uthta-baithta hai, log use waisa hi samajhte hain. (Ibn Asakir, Al-Husain bin Ja'far bin Muhammad bin Hamdan... alkh, 14/46)

(2) Hazrat Abu Musa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Nek aur bure hum-nasheen ki misaal us shakhs jaisi hai jis ke paas mushk ho aur doosra dhonkni chala raha ho. Mushk wala ya to tumhein mushk de dega ya tum us se khareed loge, aur agar kuchh na bhi ho to khushboo zaroor aayegi. Doosra tumhare kapde jala dega ya tumhein us se badboo aayegi." (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Buyoo', Baab fi al-attar wa bai' al-misk, 2/20, hadees: 2101)

(3) Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Jo kisi badmazhab ko salaam kare, ya us se khule chehre ke saath mile, ya aisi baat kare jis se us ka dil khush ho, us ne us cheez ki tahqeer ki jo Allah Ta'ala ne Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par utaari." (Tareekh-e-Baghdad, 5378 - Abdul Rahman bin Nafi', Abu Ziyad al-Mukharrami... alkh, 10/262)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rahmat-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Tum un se door raho aur woh tum se door rahen. Kahin woh tumhein gumraah na kar dein aur fitne mein na daal dein." (Sahih Muslim, Baab al-nahi an al-riwayah an al-du'afa wal-ihtiyat fi tahammuliha, safha 9, hadees: 7 (7))

(4) Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka farman-e-aalishan hai: "Aadmi apne dost ke deen par hota hai. Is liye tum mein se har shakhs dekhe ke kis se dosti kar raha hai." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, 45waan Baab, 4/167, hadees: 2385)

Maulana Ma'navi قُدِّسَ سِرُّہٗ farmate hain:

صحبت صالح تُرا صالح کُنَد

صحبت طالح تُرا طالح کند

Yani achhe aadmi ki sohbat tumhein achha bana degi aur bure aadmi ki sohbat tumhein bura bana degi.[1]

1… Achhi sohbat aur nek mahaul paane ke liye Dawat-e-Islami ke saath wabasta ho jaiye.

## Ayat 4:141

`Source: tafseerId=50701; ayatId=638; surah=4; ayat=141; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْنَ یَتَرَبَّصُوْنَ بِكُمْۚ-فَاِنْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فَتْحٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ قَالُوْۤا اَلَمْ نَكُنْ مَّعَكُمْ ﳲ وَ اِنْ كَانَ لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ نَصِیْبٌۙ-قَالُوْۤا اَلَمْ نَسْتَحْوِذْ عَلَیْكُمْ وَ نَمْنَعْكُمْ مِّنَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَؕ-فَاللّٰهُ یَحْكُمُ بَیْنَكُمْ یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِؕ-وَ لَنْ یَّجْعَلَ اللّٰهُ لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ سَبِیْلًا۠    (141)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh jo tumhare upar gardish-e-zamana ka intezaar karte rehte hain. Phir agar Allah ki taraf se tumhein fatah mile to kehte hain: Kya hum tumhare saath na the? Aur agar kafiron ke liye fatah ka hissa ho to un se kehte hain: Kya hum tum par ghaalib na the aur kya hum ne Musalmanon ko tum se roke na rakha? To Allah tumhare darmiyan Qiyamat ke din faisla karega aur Allah kafiron ko Musalmanon par koi raah na dega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ یَتَرَبَّصُوْنَ بِكُمْ: Woh jo tum par intezaar karte hain.}** Yahan munafiqon ki haalat bayan ki gayi hai. Ae Musalmano! Yeh munafiq tum par gardish-e-zamana ka intezaar karte hain. Agar Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se tumhein fatah mil jaaye to Musalmanon se kehte hain: Kya hum tumhare saath na the? Is liye humein bhi maal-e-ghanimat do. Aur agar kafiron ko fatah mil jaaye to un se kehte hain: Kya hum tum par ghaalib na the ke tumhein pakad sakte the, lekin hum ne tumhein na pakad kar tumhari madad ki? Aur hum ne Musalmanon ko tum se roke rakha, is liye humein bhi apna hissa do. Gharz munafiqon ki zindagi sirf apne faide ke gird ghoomti hai. Woh kisi ke saath bhi haqeeqi taur par mukhlis nahin hote.

## Ayat 4:142

`Source: tafseerId=50702; ayatId=639; surah=4; ayat=142; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ یُخٰدِعُوْنَ اللّٰهَ وَ هُوَ خَادِعُهُمْۚ-وَ اِذَا قَامُوْۤا اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ قَامُوْا كُسَالٰىۙ-یُرَآءُوْنَ النَّاسَ وَ لَا یَذْكُرُوْنَ اللّٰهَ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا٘ۙ   (142)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak munafiq log apne gumaan mein Allah ko fareb dena chahte hain aur wahi unhein ghaafil karke maar dega. Aur jab namaz ke liye khade hote hain to bade sust hokar logon ke saamne riyaakaari karte hue khade hote hain aur Allah ko bahut thoda yaad karte hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ یُخٰدِعُوْنَ اللّٰهَ: Beshak munafiq log apne gumaan mein Allah ko fareb dena chahte hain.}** Yahan munafiqon ki ek aur buri aadat bayan ki gayi hai. Woh apne gumaan mein Allah Ta'ala ko dhoka dena chahte hain, halaanke asal mein woh Musalmanon ko dhoka dena chahte hain, kyunki Allah Ta'ala ko haqeeqatan fareb dena mumkin hi nahin. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ un ke is fareb ka badla unhein ghaafil karke dega, duniya mein ruswa karega aur Qiyamat mein azaab mein mubtala karega.

Munafiqon ki nishani yeh hai ke jab mominon ke saath namaz ke liye khade hote hain to mare dil aur susti ke saath khade hote hain. Un ke dilon mein iman nahin hota, is liye unhein ibaadat ka shauq aur bandagi ka lutf haasil nahin hota. Woh sirf logon ko dikhane ke liye namaz padhte hain.

**Namaz mein susti karna munafiqon ki alamat hai:** Is se maloom hua ke namaz mein susti karna munafiqon ki alamat hai. Namaz na padhna, ya sirf logon ke saamne padhna aur tanhaai mein na padhna, ya logon ke saamne khushu-o-khuzu ke saath aur tanhaai mein jaldi-jaldi padhna, ya namaz mein idhar-udhar ke khayalat laana aur dil jama karne ki koshish na karna, yeh sab susti ki alamat hain.

**Namaz mein khushu-o-khuzu paida karne ka aasaan nuskha:** Kisi ne Hazrat Hatim Asamm رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ se un ki namaz ke baare mein poocha to unhone farmaya: Jab namaz ka waqt qareeb aata hai to main mukammal wuzu karta hoon. Phir jis jagah namaz ada karni hoti hai wahan aa kar itni der baithta hoon ke mere azaa jama ho jaayen. Is ke baad yeh tasawwur karke namaz ke liye khada hota hoon ke Ka'bah Muazzamah mere saamne hai, Pul-Siraat mere qadmon ke neeche hai, Jannat mere daahine taraf aur Jahannam baayen taraf hai, Malak-ul-Maut عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام mere peechhe khade hain, aur mujhe yeh khayal hota hai ke yeh meri aakhri namaz hai. Phir main umeed aur khauf ke darmiyan qiyaam karta hoon, theek tarah takbeer kehta hoon, thehar-thehar kar qiraat karta hoon, aajizi ke saath ruku karta hoon, darte hue sajda karta hoon, baayen pindli par baith kar apne qadmon ka pichhla hissa bichha deta hoon aur daahina qadam anguthe par khada kar deta hoon. Phir ikhlaas ke saath baqi af'aal ada karta hoon. Ab main nahin jaanta ke meri namaz qabool bhi hui ya nahin. (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab Asraar-us-Salaat wa Muhimmaatuha, Al-Baab-ul-Awwal, Fazilat-ul-Khushu', 1/206)

## Ayat 4:143

`Source: tafseerId=50703; ayatId=640; surah=4; ayat=143; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** مُّذَبْذَبِیْنَ بَیْنَ ذٰلِكَ ﳓ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِ وَ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّضْلِلِ اللّٰهُ فَلَنْ تَجِدَ لَهٗ سَبِیْلًا(143)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Darmiyan mein dagmaga rahe hain, na in ki taraf hain na un ki taraf, aur jise Allah gumraah kare to tum us ke liye koi raasta na paoge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مُذَبْذَبِیْنَ بَیْنَ ذٰلِكَ: Darmiyan mein dagmaga rahe hain.}** Yani munafiq kufr aur iman ke darmiyan dagmaga rahe hain. Woh na haqeeqi taur par mominon aur mukhlis iman walon ke saath hain, na saaf taur par kafiron aur khule shirk karne walon ke saath. Ae Habeeb! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, aap in munafiqon ke raah-e-raast par aane ki umeed na rakhein, kyunki jise Allah Ta'ala us ki hidayat aur taufeeq ki ahliyat na hone ki wajah se gumraah kar de, aap us ke liye aisa raasta nahin paa sakenge jis par chal kar woh haq tak pahunch sake. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 143, 1/442; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 143, 2/308, multaqatan)

## Ayat 4:144

`Source: tafseerId=50704; ayatId=641; surah=4; ayat=144; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوا الْكٰفِرِیْنَ اَوْلِیَآءَ مِنْ دُوْنِ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَؕ-اَتُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ تَجْعَلُوْا لِلّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ سُلْطٰنًا مُّبِیْنًا(144)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Musalmanon ko chhor kar kafiron ko dost na banao. Kya tum yeh chahte ho ke apne upar Allah ke liye sareeh hujjat qaim kar lo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَوْلِیَآءَ: Dost.}** Is aayat mein Musalmanon ko bataya gaya ke kafiron ko dost banana munafiqon ki aadat hai, is liye tum is se bacho. Kya tum yeh chahte ho ke kafiron ko dost bana kar munafiqat ka raasta ikhtiyar karo aur is tarah apne khilaf Allah Ta'ala ki hujjat qaim kar lo?

## Ayat 4:145

`Source: tafseerId=50705; ayatId=642; surah=4; ayat=145; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ فِی الدَّرْكِ الْاَسْفَلِ مِنَ النَّارِۚ-وَ لَنْ تَجِدَ لَهُمْ نَصِیْرًاۙ   (145)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak munafiq Dozakh ke sab se nichle tabqe mein hain aur tum hargiz un ka koi madadgaar na paaoge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ فِی الدَّرْكِ الْاَسْفَلِ مِنَ النَّارِ: Beshak munafiq Dozakh ke sab se nichle tabqe mein hain.}** Irshaad farmaya ke beshak munafiq Dozakh ke sab se nichle tabqe mein honge. Aur tum hargiz un ka koi aisa madadgaar na paaoge jo unhein azaab se bacha sake aur Jahannam ke sab se nichle tabqe se bahar nikaal sake. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 145, 2/309)

Yaad rahe ke munafiq ka azaab kafir se bhi zyada hai, kyunki woh duniya mein apne aap ko Musalman keh kar mujahidon ke haathon se bacha raha tha. Kafir hone ke bawajood Musalmanon ko dhoka dena aur Islam ka mazaaq udaana us ka tareeqa raha hai.

## Ayat 4:146

`Source: tafseerId=50706; ayatId=643; surah=4; ayat=146; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ تَابُوْا وَ اَصْلَحُوْا وَ اعْتَصَمُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ اَخْلَصُوْا دِیْنَهُمْ لِلّٰهِ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ مَعَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَؕ-وَ سَوْفَ یُؤْتِ اللّٰهُ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ اَجْرًا عَظِیْمًا(146)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Magar woh log jinhon ne taubah ki aur apni islaah kar li aur Allah ki rassi ko mazbooti se thaam liya aur apna deen khaalis Allah ke liye kar liya, to yeh Musalmanon ke saath hain aur qareeb hai ke Allah Musalmanon ko bada sawaab dega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ تَابُوْا: Magar woh log jinhon ne taubah ki.}** Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke jin logon ne apni munafiqat se taubah kar li, apne bigde hue haal ki islaah kar li, Allah Ta'ala ke deen ki rassi ko mazbooti se thaam liya aur apna deen Allah Ta'ala ke liye khaalis kar liya, sirf us ki ita'at mein us ki raza chaahi, to aise log Jannat ke buland darajaat mein Musalmanon ke saath honge aur un ki pehli munafiqat unhein koi nuqsan na degi.

Aur qareeb hai ke Allah Ta'ala Musalmanon ko bada sawaab dega, jis mein munafiqat se sachchi taubah karne wale bhi shareek honge. Ae logo! Agar tum Allah Ta'ala ke shukr guzaar bande ban jao aur us par iman lao to Allah Ta'ala tumhein azaab de kar kya karega? Allah Ta'ala ki shaan yeh hai ke woh shukr guzaar Musalmanon ki qadr karne wala aur unhein jaanne wala hai. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayaat: 146-147, 2/309-311)

## Ayat 4:147

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=644; surah=4; ayat=147; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** مَا یَفْعَلُ اللّٰهُ بِعَذَابِكُمْ اِنْ شَكَرْتُمْ وَ اٰمَنْتُمْؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ شَاكِرًا عَلِیْمًا(147)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar tum shukr guzaar ban jao aur iman lao to Allah tumhein azaab de kar kya karega? Aur Allah qadr karne wala, jaanne wala hai.

## Ayat 4:148

`Source: tafseerId=50707; ayatId=645; surah=4; ayat=148; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** لَا یُحِبُّ اللّٰهُ الْجَهْرَ بِالسُّوْٓءِ مِنَ الْقَوْلِ اِلَّا مَنْ ظُلِمَؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ سَمِیْعًا عَلِیْمًا(148)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah buri baat ka elaan pasand nahin karta magar mazloom se, aur Allah sunne wala, jaanne wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا یُحِبُّ اللّٰهُ الْجَهْرَ بِالسُّوْٓءِ مِنَ الْقَوْلِ: Allah buri baat ka elaan pasand nahin karta.}** Ek qawl yeh hai ke buri baat ke elaan se murad kisi ke posheeda maamlaat ko zaahir karna hai, jaise kisi ki gheebat karna ya kisi ki chughli khaana. (Jamal, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 148, 2/144)

Yani Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ is baat ko pasand nahin farmata ke koi shakhs kisi ke posheeda maamlaat zaahir kare. Doosra qawl yeh hai ke buri baat ke elaan se murad gaali dena hai. (Madarik, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 148, safha 261) Yani Allah Ta'ala is baat ko pasand nahin karta ke koi kisi ko gaali de.

**Ek doosre ko gaali dene ki mazammat:** Gaali dena gunaah hai aur Musalman ki shaan se door hai. Is ke baare mein 3 ahadees yeh hain:

(1) Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Musalman Musalman ka bhai hai. Woh us par zulm nahin karta aur na use gaali deta hai." (Sharah-us-Sunnah, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah, Baab-us-Satr, 6/489, hadees: 3412)

(2) Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Musalman ko gaali dena fisq hai aur use qatl karna kufr hai." (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Fitan, Baab: Qaul-un-Nabi ﷺ: La Tarji'u Ba'di Kuffaaran... alkh, 4/434, hadees: 7076)

(3) Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: "Aapas mein gaali dene wale do aadmi jo kuchh kahen, us ka wabaal shuru karne wale par hai, jab tak mazloom hadd se na barhe." (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Aadaab, Baab-un-Nahyi an-is-Sibaab, safha 1396, hadees: 68 (2587))

Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko buri baat ka elaan pasand nahin, lekin mazloom ko ijaazat hai ke woh zaalim ke zulm ko bayan kare. Is liye woh chor ya ghaasib ke baare mein keh sakta hai ke us ne mera maal churaaya ya ghasb kiya. (Jamal, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 148, 2/145)

**Sha'n-e-Nuzool:** Is aayat ka ek sha'n-e-nuzool yeh hai ke ek shakhs ek qaum ka mehmaan hua. Un logon ne achhi tarah us ki mehmaan-nawaazi na ki. Jab woh wahan se nikla to un ki shikaayat karta hua nikla. (Baizawi, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 148, 2/272) Isi waqiye ke baare mein yeh aayat naazil hui.

Baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke yeh aayat Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke baare mein naazil hui. Ek shakhs Sarwar-e-Kaainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke saamne Hazrat Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki shaan mein bad-zabani karta raha. Hazrat Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ khaamosh rahe, lekin woh baaz na aaya. Ek martaba aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne use jawaab de diya to Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ khade ho gaye.

Hazrat Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne arz ki: "Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Yeh shakhs mujhe bura-bhala kehta raha to Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne kuchh na farmaya, aur main ne ek martaba jawaab diya to Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ khade ho gaye." Irshaad farmaya: "Ek farishta tumhari taraf se jawaab de raha tha. Jab tum ne jawaab diya to farishta chala gaya aur Shaitaan aa gaya." Isi ke baare mein yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 148, 1/444)

**Mehmaan-nawaazi se khush na hone walon ko naseehat:** Pehle, yani mehmaan-nawaazi wale sha'n-e-nuzool ko saamne rakhein to is se un logon ko ibrat haasil karni chahiye jo mezbaan ki mehmaan-nawaazi se khush nahin hote, chahe ghar wale ne kitni hi tangi ke saath khaane ka intezaam kiya ho.

Khaas taur par rishtedaaron aur bil-khusoos sasuraali rishtedaaron mein mehmaan-nawaazi par shikwa-shikaayat aam hai. Ek khaana banaya to aitiraaz hota hai ke do kyun nahin banaye? Do banaye to aitiraaz hota hai ke teen kyun nahin banaye? Namkeen banaya to aitiraaz hota hai ke meetha kyun nahin banaya? Meetha banaya to aitiraaz hota hai ke falan meetha kyun nahin banaya? Gharz bahut se mehmaan zulm, zyadati aur takleef dene se baaz nahin aate. Aise rishtedaaron ko dekh kar ghar walon ki tabiyat kharaab hona shuru ho jaati hai.

Hadees-e-Mubarak mein mehmaan ko hukm diya gaya hai ke kisi Musalman ke liye halaal nahin ke woh apne Musalman bhai ke paas itni der thehre ke use gunaah mein mubtala kar de. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne arz ki: "Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Woh use gunaah mein kaise mubtala karega?" Irshaad farmaya: "Woh apne bhai ke paas thehra hoga aur haal yeh hoga ke us ke paas koi aisi cheez na hogi jis se woh us ki mehmaan-nawaazi kar sake." (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Luqatah, Baab-ud-Diyaafah wa Nahwaha, safha 951, hadees: 15 (1726))

**Zaalim ke zulm ko bayan karna jaaiz hai:** Aayat mein mazloom ko zulm bayan karne ki ijaazat di gayi. Is se maloom hua ke mazloom haakim ke saamne zaalim ki buraai bayan kar sakta hai, yeh gheebat mein daakhil nahin. Is se hazaarha masail maloom ho sakte hain. Hadees ke raawiyon ka fisq ya aib bayan karna, chor ya ghaasib ki shikaayat karna aur mulk ke ghaddaaron ki khabar hukoomat ko dena sab jaaiz hai. Gheebat ke jaaiz hone ki jitni bhi sooratein hain, woh sab kisi na kisi bade faayde ki wajah se hain.

## Ayat 4:149

`Source: tafseerId=50708; ayatId=646; surah=4; ayat=149; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنْ تُبْدُوْا خَیْرًا اَوْ تُخْفُوْهُ اَوْ تَعْفُوْا عَنْ سُوْٓءٍ فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَفُوًّا قَدِیْرًا(149)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Agar tum koi bhalai elaniya karo ya chhup kar ya kisi ki burai se darguzar karo to beshak Allah maaf karne wala, qudrat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنْ تُبْدُوْا خَیْرًا: Agar tum koi bhalai elaniya karo.}** Irshaad farmaya ke agar tum koi nek kaam elaniya karo ya chhup kar, ya kisi ki burai se darguzar karo, to yeh behtar hai. Is liye ke Allah Ta'ala saza dene par har tarah se qudrat rakhne ke bawajood apne bando ke gunaahon se darguzar karta aur unhein maaf farmata hai. Is liye tum bhi apne upar zulm karne walon ko maaf karo aur logon ki ghaltiyon se darguzar karo. (Tafseer-e-Samarqandi, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 149, 1/401; Rooh-ul-Bayan, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 149, 2/312, multaqatan)

**Maaf karne ke fazaail:** Is aayat se maloom hua ke zaalim se badla lena agarche jaaiz hai, lekin us se badla lene par qudrat rakhne ke bawajood us ke zulm par sabr karna aur use maaf kar dena behtar hai aur ajr-o-sawab ka sabab hai.

Allah Ta'ala ek aur maqam par irshaad farmata hai:

**وَ اِنْ عَاقَبْتُمْ فَعَاقِبُوْا بِمِثْلِ مَا عُوْقِبْتُمْ بِهٖؕ-وَ لَىٕنْ صَبَرْتُمْ لَهُوَ خَیْرٌ لِّلصّٰبِرِیْنَ (نحل: 126)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar tum kisi ko saza dene lago to aisi hi saza do jaisi tumhein takleef pahunchai gayi ho aur agar tum sabr karo to beshak sabr walon ke liye sabr sab se behtar hai.

Aur irshaad farmata hai:

**وَ لَمَنْ صَبَرَ وَ غَفَرَ اِنَّ ذٰلِكَ لَمِنْ عَزْمِ الْاُمُوْرِ (Shura: 43)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak jis ne sabr kiya aur maaf kar diya to yeh zaroor himmat wale kaamon mein se hai.

Aur irshaad farmata hai:

**وَ جَزٰٓؤُا سَیِّئَةٍ سَیِّئَةٌ مِّثْلُهَاۚ-فَمَنْ عَفَا وَ اَصْلَحَ فَاَجْرُهٗ عَلَى اللّٰهِؕ-اِنَّهٗ لَا یُحِبُّ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ (Shura: 40)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur burai ka badla us ke barabar burai hai, to jis ne maaf kiya aur kaam sanwaar diya to us ka ajr Allah ke zimme karam par hai. Beshak woh zalimon ko pasand nahin karta.

Aur irshaad farmata hai:

**وَ لْیَعْفُوْا وَ لْیَصْفَحُوْاؕ-اَلَا تُحِبُّوْنَ اَنْ یَّغْفِرَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ (Noor: 22)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur unhein chahiye ke maaf kar dein aur darguzar karein. Kya tum is baat ko pasand nahin karte ke Allah tumhari bakhshish farma de? Aur Allah bakhshne wala, mehrban hai.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Beshak Allah Ta'ala darguzar farmane wala hai aur darguzar karne ko pasand farmata hai.” (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ul-Hudood, 5/546, hadees: 8216)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Pur-Noor ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne arz ki: ‘Ae mere Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Tere bando mein se teri baar-gah mein zyada izzat wala kaun hai?’ Allah Ta'ala ne irshaad farmaya: ‘Woh banda jo badla lene par qudrat rakhne ke bawajood maaf kar de.’” (Shu'ab-ul-Iman, 6/319, hadees: 8327)

**Makhlooq-e-Khuda par shafqat ke fazaail:** Is aayat se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko Allah ki makhlooq par shafqat, rahm aur meherbani karna bahut pasand hai. Ahadees mein logon par shafqat aur rahm karne ke bahut fazaail bayan hue hain. Targheeb ke liye chaar ahadees darj hain:

**(1)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Rahm karne walon par Rahman rahm farmata hai. Tum zameen walon par rahm karo, aasmanon ki baadshahi ka Maalik tum par rahm karega.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah, 3/371, hadees: 1931)

**(2)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Musalman Musalman ka bhai hai. Na us par zulm kare, na us ki madad chhode. Jo shakhs apne bhai ki haajat poori karne ki koshish mein ho, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ us ki haajat poori farma deta hai. Jo shakhs kisi Musalman ki ek takleef door kare, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ Qiyamat ki takleefon mein se us ki ek takleef door karega. Jo shakhs kisi Musalman ki pardah-poshi kare, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ Qiyamat ke din us ki pardah-poshi karega.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Mazalim wal-Ghasb, 2/126, hadees: 2442)

**(3)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Woh hum mein se nahin jo hamare chhote par rahm na kare, hamare bade ki ta'zeem na kare, achhi baat ka hukm na de aur buri baat se mana na kare.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah, 3/369, hadees: 1928)

**(4)** Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Tum mein behtar woh hai jis se bhalai ki umeed ho aur jis ke shar se aman ho. Aur tum mein bura woh hai jis se bhalai ki umeed na ho aur jis ke shar se aman na ho.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Fitan, 4/116, hadees: 2270)

## Ayat 4:150

`Source: tafseerId=50709; ayatId=647; surah=4; ayat=150; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَكْفُرُوْنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ وَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ یُّفَرِّقُوْا بَیْنَ اللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ وَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ نُؤْمِنُ بِبَعْضٍ وَّ نَكْفُرُ بِبَعْضٍۙ-وَّ یُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ یَّتَّخِذُوْا بَیْنَ ذٰلِكَ سَبِیْلًاۙ    (150)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh log jo Allah aur us ke Rasoolon ko nahin maante aur chahte hain ke Allah aur us ke Rasoolon mein farq karein aur kehte hain: Hum kisi par iman laate hain aur kisi ka inkar karte hain, aur chahte hain ke iman aur kufr ke beech mein koi raah nikaal lein.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ یُّفَرِّقُوْا بَیْنَ اللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ: Aur chahte hain ke Allah aur us ke Rasoolon mein farq karein.}** Yeh mubarak aayat Yahood aur Nasara ke baare mein nazil hui. Yahood Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman laaye, lekin Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Imam-ul-Ambiya ﷺ ke saath unhone kufr kiya. Nasara Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman laaye, lekin Sarwar-e-Kaainaat ﷺ ke saath kufr kiya. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 150, 1/444)

Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne in ke baare mein farmaya ke yeh log Allah aur us ke Rasoolon par iman lane mein farq karte hain: Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ par iman laate hain lekin us ke Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman nahin laate. Isi wajah se inhein pakke kafir farmaya gaya, kyun ke sirf kuchh Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman lana kufr se nahin bachata. Sab par iman lana zaroori hai. Ek Nabi ka inkar bhi tamam Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke inkar ke barabar hai.

## Ayat 4:151

**Arabic:** اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْكٰفِرُوْنَ حَقًّاۚ-وَ اَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ عَذَابًا مُّهِیْنًا(151)

## Ayat 4:152

`Source: tafseerId=50710; ayatId=649; surah=4; ayat=152; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ وَ لَمْ یُفَرِّقُوْا بَیْنَ اَحَدٍ مِّنْهُمْ اُولٰٓىٕكَ سَوْفَ یُؤْتِیْهِمْ اُجُوْرَهُمْؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرًا رَّحِیْمًا۠    (152)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur woh jo Allah aur us ke sab Rasoolon par iman laaye aur un mein se kisi par iman lane mein farq na karein, to qareeb hai ke Allah unhein un ke ajr ata farmayega, aur Allah bakhshne wala, mehrban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ: Aur woh jo Allah aur us ke sab Rasoolon par iman laaye.}** Is aayat mein iman walon se ajr-o-sawab ka waada farmaya gaya hai. Is mein kabira gunaah karne wala bhi shamil hai, kyun ke woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur us ke sab Rasoolon par iman rakhta hai.

**Kabira gunaah karne wala kafir nahin:** Mu'tazilah ka aqeeda hai ke kabira gunaah karne wale hamesha Jahannam ke azaab mein rahenge. Is aayat se un ke is aqeede ka ghalat hona sabit hota hai. Allah Ta'ala ek aur maqam par irshaad farmata hai:

**وَ اِنْ طَآىٕفَتٰنِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ اقْتَتَلُوْا (Hujuraat: 9)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar Musalmanon ke do giroh aapas mein lad padein.

Is se bhi maloom hua ke kabira gunaah karna kufr nahin, kyun ke jang aur jhagda gunaah hai, phir bhi dono girohon ko momin farmaya gaya.

Sahih Bukhari mein Hazrat Abu Zar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “Main Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki baar-gah mein haazir hua. Aap ﷺ safed kapde pehne hue aaraam farma rahe the. Phir main dobara haazir hua to Aap ﷺ bedaar ho chuke the. Aap ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: ‘Jo banda yeh kahe ke Allah Ta'ala ke siwa koi mabood nahin aur isi waade par us ka inteqaal ho jaye to woh Jannat mein dakhil hua.’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Agarche us ne zina ya chori ki ho?’ Irshaad farmaya: ‘Agarche us ne zina ya chori ki ho.’ Main ne phir arz ki: ‘Agarche woh zina ya chori kare?’ Irshaad farmaya: ‘Agarche woh zina ya chori kare.’ Main ne phir arz ki: ‘Agarche us ne zina ya chori ki ho?’ Irshaad farmaya: ‘Agarche woh zina ya chori kare, Abu Zar ki naak khaak-aalood ho.’” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Libaas, 4/57, hadees: 5827)

Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Meri shafaat meri ummat ke un logon ke liye hai jo kabira gunaahon ke murtakib honge.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab Sifat-ul-Qiyamat, 4/198, hadees: 2444)

In ahadees se bhi maloom hua ke kabira gunaah karne wala kafir nahin hota. Kafir na kabhi Jannat mein jayega aur na Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ us ki shafaat farmayenge. Jannat mein sirf Musalman jayenge aur Tajdaar-e-Risalat ﷺ ki shafaat bhi sirf Musalmanon ko milegi, agarche woh kabira gunaahon ke murtakib hon.

Yaad rahe ke Ahl-e-Sunnat ka ijma hai ke momin kisi gunaah ki wajah se kafir nahin hota. Sharh-e-Aqaid-e-Nasafiyyah mein hai: “Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ke zamane se aaj tak ummat ka is baat par ijma hai ke Ahl-e-Qibla mein se jo shakhs baghair taubah ke mar gaya, us ki namaz-e-janazah padhi jayegi aur us ke liye dua aur istighfar bhi kiya jayega, agarche us ka kabira gunaah karna maloom ho. Is liye ke pehle hi ummat ka is baat par ittefaq hai ke momin ke siwa kisi ke liye namaz-e-janazah, dua aur istighfar jaaiz nahin.” (Sharh Aqaid Nasafiyyah, Mabhas-ul-Kabeerah, safha 110)

Sharh Fiqh-e-Akbar mein hai: “Hum Khawarij ki tarah kisi gunaah ke sabab kisi Musalman ki takfeer nahin karenge, agarche woh kabira gunaah ho. Haan, agar woh kisi aise gunaah ko halaal jaane jis ki hurmat qat'i daleel se sabit ho to woh kafir hai. Hum Mu'tazilah ki tarah kabira gunaah karne wale se iman ka wasf bhi saaqit nahin karenge, balki use haqeeqi momin kahenge. Ahl-e-Sunnat wal-Jamaat ke nazdeek iman dil se tasdeeq aur zaban se iqraar ka naam hai, jabke amal ka ta'alluq kamal-e-iman se hai.” (Sharh Fiqh-e-Akbar, Al-Kabeerah la tukhrij-ul-mu'min anil-iman, safha 71, 74)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Aadmi haqeeqatan kisi baat se mushrik us waqt tak nahin hota jab tak ghair-e-Khuda ko mabood ya mustaqil-bizzat aur wajib-ul-wujood na maane. Baaz nusoos mein baaz af'aal par tashbeeh, sakhti zahir karne, ya tawheed ke khilaaf kisi irade aur aqeede ki wajah se shirk ka lafz aaya hai. Baaz amal par kufr ka lafz aana aisa kufr nahin jo aadmi ko mutlaqan Islam se nikaal de aur baghair taubah ke maghfirat se mehroom kar de. Ahl-e-Sunnat ka ijma hai ke momin kisi kabira gunaah ki wajah se Islam se baahar nahin hota. Nusoos ko bila tafseel istilahi kufr-o-shirk par mahmool karna Khawarij ka mardood mazhab hai.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 21/131)

Is ibarat ka khulasa yeh hai ke aadmi sirf do cheezon se mushrik hota hai: (1) ghair-e-Khuda ko mabood maanne se, (2) Allah ke siwa kisi ko mustaqil-bizzat maanne se. In do cheezon ke siwa kisi teesri cheez se aadmi haqeeqatan mushrik nahin hota.

Baaz ahadees mein kuchh kaamon ko kisi qaid ke baghair shirk ya kufr kaha gaya hai. Ulama ne un ki tawjeeh bayan ki hai: ya to wahan kufr-o-shirk se mushabahat murad hoti hai, ya shariat ne us kaam par sakhti zahir karne ke liye shirk ka lafz istemal kiya hota hai, ya us waqt shirk murad hota hai jab us amal ke saath tawheed ke khilaaf koi irada ya aqeeda bhi shamil ho. Misal ke taur par ghair-e-Khuda ko sajdah karna har surat mein haqeeqi shirk nahin, lekin agar us ke saath shirk ka irada ho to yaqeenan shirk hai.

Is liye jahan ghair-haqeeqi shirk ko shirk kaha gaya ho, wahan woh haqeeqi kufr-o-shirk murad nahin jis ki wajah se aadmi Islam se baahar ho jaye aur baghair taubah ke hamesha Jahannam mein rahe. Ahl-e-Sunnat ka ijma hai ke Musalman kabira gunaah ki wajah se Islam se baahar nahin hota. Quran-o-Hadees ki is qisam ki wazahaton ko tafseel samjhe baghair haqeeqi kufr-o-shirk qarar dena Khawarij ka mardood mazhab hai.

## Ayat 4:153

`Source: tafseerId=50711; ayatId=650; surah=4; ayat=153; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یَسْــٴَـلُكَ اَهْلُ الْكِتٰبِ اَنْ تُنَزِّلَ عَلَیْهِمْ كِتٰبًا مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ فَقَدْ سَاَلُوْا مُوْسٰۤى اَكْبَرَ مِنْ ذٰلِكَ فَقَالُوْۤا اَرِنَا اللّٰهَ جَهْرَةً فَاَخَذَتْهُمُ الصّٰعِقَةُ بِظُلْمِهِمْۚ-ثُمَّ اتَّخَذُوا الْعِجْلَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مَا جَآءَتْهُمُ الْبَیِّنٰتُ فَعَفَوْنَا عَنْ ذٰلِكَۚ-وَ اٰتَیْنَا مُوْسٰى سُلْطٰنًا مُّبِیْنًا(153)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** (Ae Habeeb!) Ahl-e-Kitaab aap se sawal karte hain ke aap un par aasman se ek kitaab utaar dein. To yeh log Musa se is se bhi bada sawal kar chuke hain, jo unhone kaha tha: (Ae Musa!) Allah humein elaniya dikha do. To un ke zulm ki wajah se unhein kadak ne pakad liya. Phir un ke paas roshan nishaniyan aa jaane ke bawajood woh bachhde ko mabood bana baithe. Phir hum ne yeh maaf kar diya aur hum ne Musa ko roshan ghalba ata farmaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یَسْــٴَـلُكَ اَهْلُ الْكِتٰبِ: Ahl-e-Kitaab aap se sawal karte hain.}** Is aayat ka shan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Yahood mein se Ka'b bin Ashraf aur Fanhas bin Azura ne Sarkar-e-Madinah ﷺ se arz ki: “Agar aap Nabi hain to hamare paas aasman se ek hi baar mein kitaab laiye, jaise Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Taurat laaye the.” Un ka yeh sawal hidayat paane ke liye nahin tha, balki sarkashi aur baghawat ki wajah se tha. Is par yeh aayat nazil hui. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 153, 1/445)

Sarkar-e-Doalam ﷺ ko tasalli dete hue farmaya gaya ke aap ﷺ un ke sawalon par ta'ajjub na karein. Yeh log aap ﷺ se Quran ek hi baar mein nazil karwane ka sawal kar rahe hain. Un ka yeh sawal un ki intehaai jahalat ki wajah se hai aur aisi jahalat mein un ke baap-dada bhi mubtala rahe. Agar un ka sawal hidayat ke liye hota to un ki baat dekhi jaati, lekin woh kisi haal mein iman lane wale na the.

Un ke baap-dada ke isi tarz-e-amal ko samjhane ke liye un ki do harkatein bayan ki ja rahi hain. Ek yeh ke Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke Koh-e-Toor par tashreef le jaane ke baad unhone bachhde ko mabood bana liya. Doosri yeh ke unhone Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se kaha: “Hum aap par us waqt tak yaqeen nahin karenge jab tak aap humein Allah ko elaniya na dikha dein.”

Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ se un ka mutalabah yeh tha ke aap ﷺ ek hi baar mein kitaab nazil karwa dein, phir woh aap ﷺ ki ita'at karenge. Lekin jab Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par ek hi baar Taurat nazil hui to ita'at karne ke bajaye unhone Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko dekhne ka mutalabah kar diya. Asal baat yeh hai ke na karne ke sau bahaane hote hain.

**وَ اٰتَیْنَا مُوْسٰى سُلْطٰنًا مُّبِیْنًا: Aur hum ne Musa ko roshan ghalba ata farmaya.** Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko roshan ghalba aur tasallut ata farmaya gaya. Jab aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne Bani Israeel ko taubah ke liye apne hi logon ko qatl karne ka hukm diya to woh inkar na kar sake aur unhone aap عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ki ita'at ki.

## Ayat 4:154

`Source: tafseerId=50712; ayatId=651; surah=4; ayat=154; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ رَفَعْنَا فَوْقَهُمُ الطُّوْرَ بِمِیْثَاقِهِمْ وَ قُلْنَا لَهُمُ ادْخُلُوا الْبَابَ سُجَّدًا وَّ قُلْنَا لَهُمْ لَا تَعْدُوْا فِی السَّبْتِ وَ اَخَذْنَا مِنْهُمْ مِّیْثَاقًا غَلِیْظًا(154)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir hum ne un se ahd lene ke liye un par Koh-e-Toor ko buland kar diya aur un se farmaya ke darwaze mein sajdah karte hue dakhil ho aur un se farmaya ke Haftah ke din hadd se na barho aur hum ne un se mazboot ahd liya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ رَفَعْنَا فَوْقَهُمُ الطُّوْرَ: Phir hum ne un par Koh-e-Toor ko buland kar diya.}** Yahoodiyon ke bare mein mazeed teen baatein bayan ki ja rahi hain. Pehli baat yeh hai ke un se Taurat par amal karne ka ahd lene ke liye Koh-e-Toor ko un ke saron par latka diya gaya. Doosri baat yeh hai ke unhein Bait-ul-Muqaddas ya Ariha naam ki basti ke darwaze se sajdah karte hue guzarne ka hukm diya gaya, lekin unhone is ki na-farmani ki. Teesri baat yeh hai ke unhein Haftah ke din shikar karne se mana farmaya gaya.

Lekin unhone in teeno baaton mein khilaf-warzi ki aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se mazboot ahd karke use tod diya.

## Ayat 4:155

`Source: tafseerId=50713; ayatId=652; surah=4; ayat=155; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَبِمَا نَقْضِهِمْ مِّیْثَاقَهُمْ وَ كُفْرِهِمْ بِاٰیٰتِ اللّٰهِ وَ قَتْلِهِمُ الْاَنْۢبِیَآءَ بِغَیْرِ حَقٍّ وَّ قَوْلِهِمْ قُلُوْبُنَا غُلْفٌؕ-بَلْ طَبَعَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهَا بِكُفْرِهِمْ فَلَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا۪     (155)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To hum ne un par laanat ki, un ke ahd todne, Allah ki aayaat ke saath kufr karne, Anbiya ko na-haq shaheed karne aur un ke yeh kehne ki wajah se ke hamare dilon par ghilaaf hain. Balki Allah ne un ke kufr ke sabab un ke dilon par muhar laga di hai, to un mein se bahut thore iman late hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَبِمَا نَقْضِهِمْ مِّیْثَاقَهُمْ: To un ke ahd todne ki wajah se.}** Yahan se Ahl-e-Kitaab ke jurm aur un par Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke ghazab ka bayan shuru hota hai. Yahoodiyon ke jurm ki fehrist bahut lambi hai. Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayaat mein un ke aadhe darjan se zyada jurm bayan kiye gaye hain:

**(1)** Yahoodiyon ne Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se kiya hua ahd tod diya. Is ki tafseel pichhli aayat mein guzar chuki hai.

**(2)** Yahoodiyon ne Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki un nishaniyon ka inkar kiya jo Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki sachchai par daleel thin, jaise Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke mu'jizaat.

**(3)** Yahoodiyon ne Ambiya-e-Kiram عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko shaheed kiya, aur Yahoodi khud bhi samajhte the ke un ka Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko shaheed karna na-haq tha.

**(4)** Yahoodiyon ne Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se kaha tha ke hamare dilon par ghilaaf chadhe hue hain. Yeh bhi un ka jhoot aur Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki dawat ko thukrana tha, is liye yeh bhi azaab ka sabab bana. Paanchwa aur chhata jurm agli aayat mein bayan kiya gaya hai.

**{بَلْ طَبَعَ اللّٰهُ: Balki Allah ne un ke dilon par muhar laga di.}** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaya ke Yahoodi kehte hain: Hamare dilon par parde pade hue hain. Balki haqeeqat yeh hai ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne un ke kufr ki wajah se un ke dilon par muhar laga di hai. Is liye koi waaz-o-naseehat un ke dilon par asar nahin karti. Is se maloom hua ke kufr aur badkaariyan dil par muhar lagne ka sabab ban jati hain.

## Ayat 4:156

`Source: tafseerId=50714; ayatId=653; surah=4; ayat=156; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَّ بِكُفْرِهِمْ وَ قَوْلِهِمْ عَلٰى مَرْیَمَ بُهْتَانًا عَظِیْمًاۙ    (156)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un par laanat ki, un ke kufr aur Maryam par bara bohtan lagane ki wajah se.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ بِكُفْرِهِمْ: Aur un ke kufr ki wajah se.}** Yahoodiyon ka paanchwa jurm yeh tha ke unhone Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke saath kufr kiya. Un ka chhata jurm yeh tha ke unhone Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا par bohtan lagaya.

Is se maloom hua ke paak-daaman aurat par bohtan lagana sakht gunah hai. Kisi muqaddas aurat par, aur khaas taur par muqaddas nisbat rakhne wali par bohtan lagana aur bhi zyada sangin hai. Isi liye Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا par bohtan lagane walon ki mazammat zyada bayan ki gayi.

## Ayat 4:157

`Source: tafseerId=50715; ayatId=654; surah=4; ayat=157; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَّ قَوْلِهِمْ اِنَّا قَتَلْنَا الْمَسِیْحَ عِیْسَى ابْنَ مَرْیَمَ رَسُوْلَ اللّٰهِۚ-وَ مَا قَتَلُوْهُ وَ مَا صَلَبُوْهُ وَ لٰـكِنْ شُبِّهَ لَهُمْؕ-وَ اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اخْتَلَفُوْا فِیْهِ لَفِیْ شَكٍّ مِّنْهُؕ-مَا لَهُمْ بِهٖ مِنْ عِلْمٍ اِلَّا اتِّبَاعَ الظَّنِّۚ-وَ مَا قَتَلُوْهُ یَقِیْنًۢاۙ      (157)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un ke is kehne ki wajah se ke hum ne Maseeh Isa bin Maryam Allah ke Rasool ko shaheed kiya, halanke unhone na to use qatl kiya aur na use sooli di, balki un Yahoodiyon ke liye Isa se milta-julta ek aadmi bana diya gaya. Aur beshak yeh Yahoodi jo is Isa ke bare mein ikhtilaf kar rahe hain, zaroor is ki taraf se shubhe mein pade hue hain. Haqeeqat yeh hai ke guman ki pairwi ke siwa unhein is ki kuch bhi khabar nahin, aur beshak unhone use qatl nahin kiya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَوْلِهِمْ: Aur un ke is kehne ki wajah se.}** Is aayat mein Yahoodiyon ke saatwen sangin jurm ka bayan hai. Yahoodiyon ne dawa kiya ke unhone Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko qatl kar diya hai aur Nasara ne is ki tasdeeq ki. Allah Ta'ala ne dono ki takzeeb farma di.

Waqia yeh hua ke ek munafiq shakhs Yahoodiyon ko Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka pata dene ke liye aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke ghar mein dakhil hua. Woh Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka hum-shakal bana diya gaya aur aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aasman par tashreef le gaye. Yahoodiyon ne isi munafiq ko Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام samajh kar sooli de di.

Lekin phir woh khud bhi hairan the ke hamara aadmi kahan gaya. Us ka chehra Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام jaisa tha, lekin haath aur paon mukhtalif the. (Madarik, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 157, safha 263-264)

Isi ka zikr is aayat-e-karimah mein ho raha hai. Isi wajah se woh shubhe mein pad gaye aur yaqini taur par nahin keh sakte the ke maqtool kaun hai. Baaz kehte hain ke yeh maqtool Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام hain. Baaz kehte hain ke chehra to Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka hai, lekin jism Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka nahin, is liye yeh woh nahin.

Yahoodiyon ki pairwi mein aaj kal Qadiani bhi isi jahalat mein giraftar hain.

## Ayat 4:159

`Source: tafseerId=50717; ayatId=656; surah=4; ayat=159; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِنْ مِّنْ اَهْلِ الْكِتٰبِ اِلَّا لَیُؤْمِنَنَّ بِهٖ قَبْلَ مَوْتِهٖۚ-وَ یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِ یَكُوْنُ عَلَیْهِمْ شَهِیْدًاۚ    (159)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Koi kitabi aisa nahin jo us ki maut se pehle us par imaan na le aayega aur Qiyamat ke din woh (Isa) un par gawah honge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِلَّا لَیُؤْمِنَنَّ بِهٖ: Magar woh us par imaan laayega.}** Is aayat ki tafseer mein chand aqwaal hain.

Ek qawl yeh hai ke Yahood-o-Nasara ko apni maut ke waqt jab azaab ke farishte nazar aate hain to woh Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan le aate hain, lekin us waqt ka imaan maqbool aur mo'tabar nahin hota. (Qurtubi, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, 3/298, al-juz' al-khamis; Jalalain, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, safha 91, multaqatan)

Lekin yeh qawl zaeef hai. Doosra qawl yeh hai ke aayat ka matlab yeh hai ke har kitabi apni maut se pehle Allah Ta'ala ya Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan le aayega, lekin maut ke waqt ka imaan maqbool nahin hoga aur us se koi faida nahin hoga. (Baghawi, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, 1/397)

Teesra qawl yeh hai ke Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki wafat se pehle har Yahoodi, Nasrani aur woh log jo ghair-e-Khuda ki ibaadat karte hain, Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan le aayenge, yahan tak ke us waqt sirf ek hi deen, Islam, baqi rahega. Yeh us waqt hoga jab aakhiri zamane mein Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aasman se zameen par nuzool farmayenge.

Us waqt Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Shariat-e-Muhammadiyah عَلٰی صَاحِبِہَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke mutabiq hukm karenge aur Deen-e-Muhammadi ke imaamon mein se ek imam ki haisiyat se honge. Woh Isaaiyon ke un ghalat gumaan ko baatil farmayenge jo unhone Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke bare mein bana rakhe hain aur Deen-e-Muhammadi ki ishaat karenge. Us waqt Yahood-o-Nasara ko ya to Islam qabool karna hoga ya qatl kar diya jayega. Jizya qabool karne ka hukm Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke nuzool karne tak hai. (Baghawi, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, 1/397; Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, 1/448-449; Sawai, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 159, 1/456, multaqatan)

Is qawl se maloom hua ke abhi Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki wafat waqi nahin hui, kyunke aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki wafat se pehle tamam Ahl-e-Kitab aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan le aayenge, jabke abhi Yahoodi aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan nahin laaye. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Qiyamat ke qareeb zameen par tashreef laayenge.

Yeh bhi maloom hua ke aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki is aamad par tamam Yahoodi aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan le aayenge. Woh sab aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka banda aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se ek kalimah hone ka iqraar karke Musalman ho jayenge.

**{یَكُوْنُ عَلَیْهِمْ شَهِیْدًا: Woh un par gawah honge.}** Yani Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Qiyamat ke din Yahoodiyon par yeh gawahi denge ke unhone aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki takzeeb ki aur aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke haq mein zaban-e-ta'n daraaz ki. Aur Nasara par yeh gawahi denge ke unhone aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Rabb thehraya aur Khuda عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek jaana. Ahl-e-Kitab mein se jo log imaan le aayenge, Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام un ke imaan ki bhi gawahi denge.

## Ayat 4:160

`Source: tafseerId=50718; ayatId=657; surah=4; ayat=160; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَبِظُلْمٍ مِّنَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا حَرَّمْنَا عَلَیْهِمْ طَیِّبٰتٍ اُحِلَّتْ لَهُمْ وَ بِصَدِّهِمْ عَنْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ كَثِیْرًاۙ    (160)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To Yahoodiyon ke bade zulm ki wajah se aur un ke bahut se logon ko Allah ke raste se rokne ki wajah se hum ne un par woh baaz paakiza cheezen haraam kar din jo un ke liye halaal thin.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَبِظُلْمٍ مِّنَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا: To Yahoodiyon ke bade zulm ki wajah se.}** Yahoodiyon ki kartooten upar bayan ki gayin aur is aayat mein un ke jurm ki saza ki ek surat yeh bayan farmayi gayi ke un ki zyadtiyon ki wajah se un par kai halaal cheezen bhi haraam kar di gayin.

## Ayat 4:161

`Source: tafseerId=50719; ayatId=658; surah=4; ayat=161; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَّ اَخْذِهِمُ الرِّبٰوا وَ قَدْ نُهُوْا عَنْهُ وَ اَكْلِهِمْ اَمْوَالَ النَّاسِ بِالْبَاطِلِؕ-وَ اَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ عَذَابًا اَلِیْمًا(161)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur is liye (haraam kin) ke woh sood lete halanke unhein is se mana kiya gaya tha aur woh baatil tareeqe se logon ka maal kha jaate the aur un mein se kafiron ke liye hum ne dardnaak azaab tayyar kar rakha hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اَخْذِهِمُ الرِّبٰوا: Aur un ke sood lene ki wajah se.}** Yahoodiyon mein aqeede ki kharabiyon ke saath amali buraiyan bhi maujood thin. Chunanche sood khana aur rishwat lena un mein aam tha. Faisla karte waqt rishwat lete, yahan tak ke rishwat ki khaatir shar'i ahkaam bhi badal dete.

**Sood aur rishwat ki mazammat:** Is aayat se sood ki hurmat aur rishwat ki qabahat aur gandagi bhi maloom hui. Sood lena shadeed haraam hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: "Beshak sood sattar gunahon ka majmua hai. In mein sab se chhota gunah yeh hai ke aadmi apni maa se zina kare." (Ibn Majah, Kitab-ut-Tijaraat, Baab-ut-Taghleez fir-Riba, 3/72, hadees: 2274)

Sood ke bare mein mazeed kalam Surah Baqarah, aayat 275 se 278 aur Surah Aal-e-Imran, aayat 130 ke tehat guzar chuka hai. Rishwat ke bare mein Hazrat Sauban رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne rishwat lene wale, dene wale aur un dono ke darmiyan len-den mein madad karne wale par laanat farmayi. (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Ansar, wa min hadees-e-Sauban radiyallahu anhu, 8/327, hadees: 22462)

## Ayat 4:162

`Source: tafseerId=50720; ayatId=659; surah=4; ayat=162; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** لٰـكِنِ الرّٰسِخُوْنَ فِی الْعِلْمِ مِنْهُمْ وَ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِمَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكَ وَ مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ مِنْ قَبْلِكَ وَ الْمُقِیْمِیْنَ الصَّلٰوةَ وَ الْمُؤْتُوْنَ الزَّكٰوةَ وَ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَ الْیَوْمِ الْاٰخِرِؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ سَنُؤْتِیْهِمْ اَجْرًا عَظِیْمًا۠      (162)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Lekin un mein ilm mein pukhtagi wale aur imaan wale imaan laate hain us par jo, aye Habeeb! tumhari taraf nazil kiya gaya aur jo tum se pehle nazil kiya gaya aur namaz qayam rakhne wale aur zakat dene wale aur Allah aur Qiyamat par imaan laane wale, aison ko anqareeb hum bada sawab denge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لٰكِنِ الرّٰسِخُوْنَ فِی الْعِلْمِ مِنْهُمْ: Lekin un mein ilm mein pukhtagi wale.}** Yahoodiyon ki aksariyat gumrah aur badkirdar thi, lekin un mein kuch log ache bhi the, jaise Hazrat Abdullah bin Salam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur un ke saathi. Woh pichle Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par imaan, mazboot ilm, saaf aqal aur mukammal baseerat rakhte the. Unhone apne ilm se Islam ki haqqaniyat ko pehchana aur Sayyid-ul-Anbiya صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan laaye.

**Raaskh fil-ilm ki ta'reef:** Raaskh fil-ilm woh aalim hai jis ka ilm us ke dil mein utar gaya ho, jaise mazboot darakht woh hota hai jis ki jarrain zameen mein jagah pakad chuki hon. Is se murad ache aqeede wale aur amal karne wale ulama hain. Is se maloom hua ke amal karne wale aalim ka sawab doosron se zyada hai, kyunke woh khud bhi nek hota hai aur doosron ko bhi nek banata hai. Chahiye ke aalim ka amal Sunnat-e-Nabawi ka namoona ho aur us ka har andaaz tableegh kare.

Is se ishare ke taur par yeh bhi maloom hua ke be-deen ya be-amal aalim ka azaab bhi doosron se zyada hai, kyunke woh khud bhi gumrah hai aur doosron ko bhi gumrah karta hai. Us ki bad-amali doosron ko bhi bad-amal bana degi.

## Ayat 4:163

`Source: tafseerId=50721; ayatId=660; surah=4; ayat=163; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّاۤ اَوْحَیْنَاۤ اِلَیْكَ كَمَاۤ اَوْحَیْنَاۤ اِلٰى نُوْحٍ وَّ النَّبِیّٖنَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِهٖۚ-وَ اَوْحَیْنَاۤ اِلٰۤى اِبْرٰهِیْمَ وَ اِسْمٰعِیْلَ وَ اِسْحٰقَ وَ یَعْقُوْبَ وَ الْاَسْبَاطِ وَ عِیْسٰى وَ اَیُّوْبَ وَ یُوْنُسَ وَ هٰرُوْنَ وَ سُلَیْمٰنَۚ-وَ اٰتَیْنَا دَاوٗدَ زَبُوْرًاۚ   (163)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak aye Habeeb! hum ne tumhari taraf wahi bheji jaise hum ne Nooh aur us ke baad paighambaron ki taraf bheji aur hum ne Ibrahim aur Ismail aur Ishaq aur Yaqub aur un ke beton aur Isa aur Ayyub aur Yunus aur Haroon aur Sulaiman ki taraf wahi farmayi aur hum ne Dawood ko Zaboor ata farmayi.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّاۤ اَوْحَیْنَاۤ اِلَیْكَ: Beshak hum ne tumhari taraf wahi bheji.}** Is aayat ka shan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Yahood-o-Nasara ne Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se sawal kiya tha ke un ke liye aasman se ek hi baar poori kitab nazil ki jaye to woh aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat par imaan le aayenge. Is par yeh aayat-e-karimah nazil hui aur un par hujjat qayam ki gayi ke Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke siwa bahut se Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام hain jin mein se gyarah ke mubarak naam yahan aayat mein bayan farmaye gaye.

Ahl-e-Kitab un sab ki nubuwwat ko maante hain. Jab un mein se kai Ambiya ki nubuwwat tasleem karne mein Ahl-e-Kitab ko koi hichkichahat nahin hui to Imam-ul-Anbiya, Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat tasleem karne mein kya uzr hai?

Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko bhejne ka maqsad makhlooq ki hidayat karna, unhein Allah Ta'ala ki tauheed aur ma'rifat ka dars dena, imaan mukammal karna aur ibaadat ke tareeqe sikhana hai. Kitab ke alag alag waqt par nazil hone se yeh maqsad zyada mukammal tareeqe se hasil hota hai, kyunke thoda thoda karke baat aasani se dil mein utarti chali jaati hai. Is hikmat ko na samajhna aur aitiraaz karna intehai darje ki bewaqoofi hai. SubhanAllah! Kaisa dil-nasheen aur pyara jawab hai.

## Ayat 4:164

`Source: tafseerId=50722; ayatId=661; surah=4; ayat=164; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ رُسُلًا قَدْ قَصَصْنٰهُمْ عَلَیْكَ مِنْ قَبْلُ وَ رُسُلًا لَّمْ نَقْصُصْهُمْ عَلَیْكَؕ-وَ كَلَّمَ اللّٰهُ مُوْسٰى تَكْلِیْمًاۚ    (164)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur (hum ne bheje) bahut se aise Rasool jin ka zikr hum tum se pehle farma chuke aur bahut se woh Rasool jin ka zikr tum se na farmaya aur Allah ne Moosa se haqeeqatan kalaam farmaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ رُسُلًا: Aur bahut se Rasool.}** Allah Ta'ala ne bataya ke bahut se Rasool woh hain jin ka Quran-e-Majeed mein naam lekar zikr ho chuka hai aur bahut se woh hain jin ka ab tak Quran-e-Pak mein un ke naam ki tafseel ke saath zikr nahin farmaya gaya. In sab Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام mein se kitne aise hain jin par ek hi baar poori kitab nazil hui? Jab sab Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par ek hi baar kitab nazil nahin hui to Aakhri Nabi صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par ek hi baar kitab nazil na hona Yahoodiyon ke liye aitraaz ka sabab kyun bana hua hai?

**{وَ كَلَّمَ اللّٰهُ مُوْسٰى تَكْلِیْمًا: Aur Allah ne Moosa se kalaam farmaya.}** Yeh bhi Yahoodiyon ke aitraaz ka jawab hai. Jis tarah Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se bila-waasta kalaam farmana un Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki nubuwwat ke inkaar ka sabab nahin ban sakta jin se is tarah kalaam nahin farmaya gaya, isi tarah Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par kitab ka ek hi baar nazil hona bhi doosre Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki nubuwwat ke inkaar ka sabab nahin ban sakta. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 164, 1/452)

Is aayat ke is hisse se do masail maloom hue. Ek yeh ke Hazrat Moosa عَلٰی نَبِیِّنَا وَعَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Bani Israeel ke Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام mein bahut buland shaan rakhte hain, kyunke un ka zikr khaas taur par alag kiya gaya. Doosra yeh ke Allah Ta'ala ne baaz Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko khaas fazaail aur buzurgiyan ata farmayi hain. Kisi ek Nabi ki khaasiyat tamam Ambiya mein dhoondhna ghalati hai, jaise har Nabi Kaleemullah nahin hota.

## Ayat 4:165

`Source: tafseerId=50723; ayatId=662; surah=4; ayat=165; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** رُسُلًا مُّبَشِّرِیْنَ وَ مُنْذِرِیْنَ لِئَلَّا یَكُوْنَ لِلنَّاسِ عَلَى اللّٰهِ حُجَّةٌۢ بَعْدَ الرُّسُلِؕ-وَ كَانَ اللّٰهُ عَزِیْزًا حَكِیْمًا(165)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** (Hum ne) Rasool khushkhabri dete aur dar sunate bheje, taake Rasoolon (ko bhejne) ke baad Allah ke yahan logon ke liye koi uzr baqi na rahe aur Allah Zabar-dast hai, Hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{رُسُلًا مُّبَشِّرِیْنَ وَ مُنْذِرِیْنَ: Rasool bheje, khushkhabri dete aur dar sunate.}** Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki tashreef-aawari ka maqsad nek aamaal par sawab ki khushkhabri dena aur bure aamaal par azaab se darana hai. Is mein ek hikmat yeh bhi hai ke Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke tashreef laane ke baad logon ko yeh kehne ka mauqa na mile ke agar hamare paas Rasool aate to hum zaroor un ka hukm maante aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke farmabardaar ho jaate.

Is aayat se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala Rasoolon ki ba'sat se pehle makhlooq par azaab nahin farmata, jaisa ke doosri jagah irshad hua:

وَ مَا كُنَّا مُعَذِّبِیْنَ حَتّٰى نَبْعَثَ رَسُوْلًا( ۱۵ ) ( بنی اسرائیل: ۱۵ )

## Ayat 4:166

`Source: tafseerId=50724; ayatId=663; surah=4; ayat=166; para=6; volume=2`

## Ayat 4:171

`Source: tafseerId=50728; ayatId=668; surah=4; ayat=171; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ لَا تَغْلُوْا فِیْ دِیْنِكُمْ وَ لَا تَقُوْلُوْا عَلَى اللّٰهِ اِلَّا الْحَقَّؕ-اِنَّمَا الْمَسِیْحُ عِیْسَى ابْنُ مَرْیَمَ رَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ وَ كَلِمَتُهٗۚ-اَلْقٰىهَاۤ اِلٰى مَرْیَمَ وَ رُوْحٌ مِّنْهُ٘-فَاٰمِنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ رُسُلِهٖ ۚ۫-وَ لَا تَقُوْلُوْا ثَلٰثَةٌؕ-اِنْتَهُوْا خَیْرًا لَّكُمْؕ-اِنَّمَا اللّٰهُ اِلٰهٌ وَّاحِدٌؕ-سُبْحٰنَهٗۤ اَنْ یَّكُوْنَ لَهٗ وَلَدٌۘ-لَهٗ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِؕ-وَ كَفٰى بِاللّٰهِ وَكِیْلًا۠    (171)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aye Kitaab walo! Apne deen mein had se na badho aur Allah par sach ke siwa koi baat na kaho. Beshak Maseeh, Maryam ka beta Eesa sirf Allah ka Rasool aur us ka ek kalma hai jo us ne Maryam ki taraf bheja aur us ki taraf se ek khaas rooh hai to Allah aur us ke Rasoolon par imaan lao aur na kaho (ke mabood) teen hain. (Is se) baaz raho, (yeh) tumhare liye behtar hai. Sirf Allah hi ek mabood hai, woh paak hai is se ke us ki koi aulaad ho. Usi ka hai jo kuch aasmanon mein hai aur jo kuch zameen mein hai aur Allah kaafi kaarsaaz hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ: Aye Kitaab walo!}** Pehle wali aayaat mein Yahoodiyon ki deen mein hadd se badhne aur un ke jurm bayan kiye gaye. Ab Isaaiyon ke deen mein ghuluww aur hadd se badhne ka bayan hai.

### Isaaiyon ke firqe aur un ke aqeede

Isaai chaar bade firqon mein bat gaye the: (1) Ya'qoobiyah, (2) Malkaniyah, (3) Nasturiyah, (4) Marqosiyah. In mein se har ek Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baare mein alag kufri aqeeda rakhta tha. Ya'qoobiyah aur Malkaniyah Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Khuda kehte the. Nasturiyah Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Khuda ka beta kehte the. Marqosiyah firqe ka aqeeda tha ke Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام teen mein se teesre hain.

Is jumle ka matlab kya hai, is mein bhi un ke darmiyan ikhtilaf tha. Baaz teen uqnoom, yani wajood, maante the aur kehte the ke Baap, Beta aur Rooh-ul-Qudus teen hain. Baap se zaat, Bete se Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Rooh-ul-Qudus se un mein hulool karne wali hayaat murad lete the. Goya un ke nazdeek mabood teen the aur woh in teenon ko ek kehte the.

Baaz kehte the ke Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام naasootiyat, yani insaaniyat, aur uloohiyat dono ke jaame' hain. Maa ki taraf se un mein naasootiyat aayi aur Baap ki taraf se uloohiyat. **تَعَالَى اللّٰهُ عَمَّا يَقُوْلُوْنَ عُلُوًّا كَبِيْرًا** Allah Ta'ala un ki baat se paak aur bahut buland-o-baala hai. Isaaiyon mein yeh firqebandi ek Yahoodi ne paida ki thi jis ka naam Boulos tha. Us ne unhein gumraah karne ke liye is tarah ke aqeedon ki taleem di. (Khazin, An-Nisa, tehat-ul-aayat: 171, 1/454)

Is aayat mein Ahl-e-Kitaab ko hidayat di gayi hai ke Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baare mein ifraat-o-tafreet se baaz rahein. Unhein Khuda ya Khuda ka beta na kahen aur hulool-o-ittehaad ka aqeeda rakh kar un ki tauheen bhi na karein. Balke yeh aqeeda rakhein ke Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ke bete hain, un ka is ke siwa koi nasab nahin. Woh sirf Allah Ta'ala ke Rasool, us ka ek kalma jo Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ki taraf bheja, aur Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se ek khaas rooh hain. Is liye unhein chahiye ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur us ke Rasoolon par imaan laayein, tasdeeq karein ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ek hai aur beta aur aulaad se paak hai, aur tasdeeq karein ke Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke Rasoolon mein se hain.

**{وَ لَا تَقُوْلُوْا ثَلٰثَةٌ: Aur na kaho ke mabood teen hain.}** Baaz Isaai Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Khuda ka beta kehte the, baaz unhein teesra Khuda maante the aur baaz unhein hi Khuda maante the. In teenon firqon ki tardeed ke liye yeh aayat-e-kareema nazil hui. Lafz **اللّٰهُ** mein ek firqe ki tardeed hai, **وَاحِدٌ** mein doosre ki aur **سُبْحٰنَهٗۤ اَنْ یَّكُوْنَ لَهٗ وَلَدٌ** mein teesre ki.

Aqalmand insaan khud gaur kare ke aasman-o-zameen mein jo kuch hai sab Allah Ta'ala ki makhlooq hai. Jitne insaan hain sab usi ke bande aur mamluk hain. Un mein Hazrat Eesa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا bhi shamil hain. Jab yeh dono bhi Allah ke bande aur mamluk hain to Allah ka beta ya biwi hona kaise tasawwur kiya ja sakta hai? Beshak Allah Ta'ala in sab behooda baaton se paak aur munazzah hai.

## Ayat 4:174

`Source: tafseerId=50731; ayatId=671; surah=4; ayat=174; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ قَدْ جَآءَكُمْ بُرْهَانٌ مِّنْ رَّبِّكُمْ وَ اَنْزَلْنَاۤ اِلَیْكُمْ نُوْرًا مُّبِیْنًا(174)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aye logo! Beshak tumhare paas tumhare Rab ki taraf se wazeh daleel aa gayi aur hum ne tumhari taraf roshan noor utaara.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ: Aye logo!}** Yahan tamam insano se khitaab hai, woh kahin ke hon aur kisi bhi zamane ke hon.

### Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki shaan ka bayan

Is se maloom hua ke Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat kisi zamane, kisi jagah aur kisi qaum ke saath khaas nahin. Aam elaan farma diya gaya: Aye logo! Tumhare paas woh tashreef laaye jo sarapa Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ma'rifat ki daleel hain. Un ki sachchai par un ke mojizaat gawah hain aur woh munkiron ki aqalon ko hairan kar dete hain.

Jitne mojizaat pehle Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko diye gaye, un se zyada mojizaat Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko ata hue. Balke haq yeh hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ sar se qadmon tak khud Allah Ta'ala ki wahdaniyyat aur us ki zaat-o-sifaat ki daleel hain.

Chunanche Sarkar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka mubarak baal bhi mojiza hai. Jab Hazrat Khalid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki topi mein raha to unhein hamesha dushmanon par fatah hoti rahi. Jab woh Qaisar-e-Room Heraclius ki pagri mein raha to us ke sar ka dard door raha. Hazrat Sayyiduna Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne wasiyyat ki thi ke mere kafan mein Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke mubarak baal rakh diye jaayen taa-ke qabr ki mushkil aasaan ho. Hazrat Ameer Muawiyah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne wasiyyat ki ke mujhe ghusl de kar meri aankhon aur labon par Sultan-e-Do Jahan صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke naakhun aur mubarak baal rakh diye jaayen taa-ke qabr ke hisaab mein aasaani ho. Is se maloom hua ke mubarak baal qabr ki mushkil aasaan karte hain. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم mareezon ko mubarak baalon ka dhula hua paani pilaya karte the. Hazrat Talhah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke ghar ek martaba mubarak baal pohnch gaye to unhone saari raat malaaikah ki tasbeeh-o-tahleel suni.

Sookhe dhaanon pe hamare bhi karam ho jaaye
Chhaaye rehmat ki ghata ban ke tumhare gesu

Hum siyahkaron pe ya Rab tapish-e-mahshar mein
Saaya-afgan hon tere pyaare ke pyaare gesu

Mubarak aankh ka mojiza yeh hai ke Qiyamat tak ke waaqiaat dekhe, Jannat-o-Dozakh, Arsh-o-Kursi ka mushahida farmaya, balke khud Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko dekha. Namaz-e-Kusoof mein Jannat-o-Dozakh ko masjid ki deewar mein dekha. Peechhe muqtadi jo kuch karte hon use bhi mulahiza farma dete. Mubarak naak ka mojiza yeh hai ke jis ne mohabbat ki khushboo Yemen se aati hui soonghi. Zubaan ka mojiza yeh hai ke un ki har baat Wahy-e-Khuda hai aur woh zubaan hai jo kun ki kunji hai.

Munh ka luaab bhi mojiza hai. Hazrat Jabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke ghar haandi mein daala gaya to haandi ki tarkari mein barkat hui. Aate mein daala gaya to chaar seer aata hazaaron aadmiyon ne khaaya, phir bhi utna hi raha. Khyber mein Hazrat Ali کَرَّمَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم ki dukhti aankh mein laga diya gaya to aankh ko aaraam ho gaya. Hazrat Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke paon mein ghaar mein saanp ne kaata, us par laga diya gaya to aaraam ho gaya. Khaari kuen mein daala gaya to us ka paani meetha ho gaya.

Mubarak haath bhi daleel hai. Badr ke din ek muthi kankariyan kafiron ki taraf phenki to Rab Ta'ala ne farmaya: **مَا رَمَیْتَ اِذْ رَمَیْتَ وَ لٰكِنَّ اللّٰهَ رَمٰى** yani aap ne nahin phenka balke hum ne phenka. Isi haath mein aa kar kankariyon ne kalima padha. Isi haath se bai'at li gayi to Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaya ke un ke haathon par hamara haath hai. Ungliyan bhi mojiza hain: ek pyale paani mein ungliyan mubarak rakh di to paani ke chashme jaari ho gaye. Ungli ke ishaare se chaand cheer diya. Mubarak paon bhi mojiza hai: pathar par chale to pathar ne un ka asar le liya, farsh par bhi chale aur Arsh par bhi.

Gharaz un ka har har uzv-e-paak aur har har mubarak baal Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko pehchanne ki daleel hai. Mubarak paseena bhi mojiza hai jis mein gulaab se be-misaal khushboo hoti hai. Jaagna aur sona bhi mojiza hai: har shakhs ki neend wuzu tod deti hai, magar Sarkar-e-Aali Waqar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki neend wuzu nahin todti. Aap ka poora mubarak jism saaye se mehfooz raha, yani saaya bhi kisi ke qadmon ke neeche nahin aata tha. Gharaz Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka har wasf mojiza aur Rab Ta'ala ki qudrat ki daleel hai.

**{وَ اَنْزَلْنَاۤ اِلَیْكُمْ نُوْرًا مُّبِیْنًا: Aur hum ne tumhari taraf roshan noor utaara.}** Roshan noor se muraad Quran-e-Paak hai jo Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke zariye hamein mila.

## Ayat 4:175

`Source: tafseerId=50732; ayatId=672; surah=4; ayat=175; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَاَمَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَ اعْتَصَمُوْا بِهٖ فَسَیُدْخِلُهُمْ فِیْ رَحْمَةٍ مِّنْهُ وَ فَضْلٍۙ-وَّ یَهْدِیْهِمْ اِلَیْهِ صِرَاطًا مُّسْتَقِیْمًاﭤ(175)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To woh jo Allah par imaan laaye aur unhone us ki rassi mazbooti se thaam li to anqareeb Allah unhein apni rehmat aur apne fazl mein daakhil karega aur unhein apni taraf seedhi raah dikhaayega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَسَیُدْخِلُهُمْ فِیْ رَحْمَةٍ مِّنْهُ وَ فَضْلٍ: To anqareeb Allah unhein apni rehmat aur apne fazl mein daakhil karega.}** Imaan walon ko rehmat, fazl aur seedhe raaste ki basharat di gayi hai. Rehmat se muraad Jannat hai, fazl se muraad Jannat mein mazeed karam wale umoor hain, aur seedha raasta Deen-e-Islam hai jo seedha qurb-e-Ilahi tak le jaata hai.

## Ayat 4:176

`Source: tafseerId=50733; ayatId=673; surah=4; ayat=176; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یَسْتَفْتُوْنَكَؕ-قُلِ اللّٰهُ یُفْتِیْكُمْ فِی الْكَلٰلَةِؕ-اِنِ امْرُؤٌا هَلَكَ لَیْسَ لَهٗ وَلَدٌ وَّ لَهٗۤ اُخْتٌ فَلَهَا نِصْفُ مَا تَرَكَۚ-وَ هُوَ یَرِثُهَاۤ اِنْ لَّمْ یَكُنْ لَّهَا وَلَدٌؕ-فَاِنْ كَانَتَا اثْنَتَیْنِ فَلَهُمَا الثُّلُثٰنِ مِمَّا تَرَكَؕ-وَ اِنْ كَانُوْۤا اِخْوَةً رِّجَالًا وَّ نِسَآءً فَلِلذَّكَرِ مِثْلُ حَظِّ الْاُنْثَیَیْنِؕ-یُبَیِّنُ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ اَنْ تَضِلُّوْاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ بِكُلِّ شَیْءٍ عَلِیْمٌ۠    (176)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aye Habeeb! Tum se fatwa poochte hain, tum farma do ke Allah tumhein kalaalah ke baare mein fatwa deta hai. Agar kisi mard ka inteqaal ho jis ki aulaad na ho aur us ki ek behen ho to tarke mein us ki behen ka aadha hai aur mard apni behen ka waaris hoga agar behen ki aulaad na ho. Phir agar do bahnein hon to tarke mein un ka do-tihaai hissa hoga aur agar bhai behen hon, jin mein mard bhi hon aur auratein bhi, to mard ka hissa do auraton ke barabar hoga. Allah tumhare liye saaf bayan farmata hai taa-ke tum bhatak na jao aur Allah har cheez jaanta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یَسْتَفْتُوْنَكَ: Tum se fatwa poochte hain.}** Is mubarak aayat mein kalaalah ki miraas ka bayan hai. Kalaalah us shakhs ko kehte hain jo apne baad na baap chhode aur na aulaad.

Is aayat ke shaan-e-nuzool ke baare mein Bukhari aur Muslim mein hai ke Hazrat Jabir bin Abdullah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ beemar the. Sarkar-e-Madina صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Hazrat Siddiq-e-Akbar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke saath un ki ayaadat ke liye tashreef laaye. Hazrat Jabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ behosh the. Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne wuzu farma kar us ka paani un par daala to unhein ifaaqah hua. Unhone aankh khol kar dekha to Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ tashreef farma the. Unhone arz ki: Ya RasoolAllah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Main apne maal ka kya intizaam karun? Is par yeh aayat-e-kareema naazil hui. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Faraaiz, Baab: Qaulullahi Ta'ala: Yuseekumullahu..., 4/312, hadees: 6723; Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Faraaiz, Baab: Miras-ul-Kalaalah, s. 872, hadees: 5 (1616))

Abu Dawood ki riwayat mein yeh bhi hai ke Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Hazrat Jabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se farmaya: Aye Jabir! Mere ilm mein tumhari wafaat is beemari se nahin hai. (Abu Dawood, Kitaab-ul-Faraaiz, Baab: Man Kaana Laisa Lahu Waladun Wa Lahu Akhawaat, 3/165, hadees: 2887)

Is hadees se chand masail maloom hue: (1) Buzurgon ke wuzu ka paani tabarruk hai aur use shifa haasil karne ke liye istemaal karna sunnat hai. (2) Mareezon ki ayaadat sunnat hai. (3) Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko Allah Ta'ala ne uloom-e-ghaib ata farmaye hain. Isi liye Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko maloom tha ke Hazrat Jabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki wafaat is marz mein nahin hai.

### Kalaalah ki miraas ke ahkaam

Aayat mein jo masail bayan hue un ka khulasa aur wazahat yeh hai:

(1) Agar koi shakhs wafaat paaye aur us ke waarison mein baap aur aulaad na ho to sagi behen ya baap-shareek behen ko miraas ke maal ka aadha hissa milega, jabke woh sirf ek ho. Aur agar do ya do se zyada bahnein hon to unhein do-tihaai hissa milega.

(2) Agar koi behen wafaat paaye aur waarison mein na baap ho na aulaad, to bhai us ke poore maal ka waaris hoga.

(3) Agar wafaat paane wala bhai aur behen dono chhode to bhai ko behen se dugna hissa milega.

### Aham tanbeeh

Miraas ke masail mein bahut wus'at aur qaidain hoti hain. Aayat mein jo sooratein maujood thin unhein bayan kar diya gaya hai, lekin agar miraas ka koi mas'alah darpesh ho to kisi maahir-e-miraas Aalim ke baghair khud us ka hal na nikaalein.
