## Ayat 4:36

`Source: tafseerId=50615; ayatId=533; surah=4; ayat=36; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اعْبُدُوا اللّٰهَ وَ لَا تُشْرِكُوْا بِهٖ شَیْــٴًـا وَّ بِالْوَالِدَیْنِ اِحْسَانًا وَّ بِذِی الْقُرْبٰى وَ الْیَتٰمٰى وَ الْمَسٰكِیْنِ وَ الْجَارِ ذِی الْقُرْبٰى وَ الْجَارِ الْجُنُبِ وَ الصَّاحِبِ بِالْجَنْۢبِ وَ ابْنِ السَّبِیْلِۙ-وَ مَا مَلَكَتْ اَیْمَانُكُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یُحِبُّ مَنْ كَانَ مُخْتَالًا فَخُوْرَاۙ    ﰳ  (36)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Allah ki ibaadat karo aur us ke saath kisi ko shareek na thehrao aur maa-baap se achha sulook karo aur rishtedaron, yateemon, mohtaajon, qareebi padosi, door ke padosi, paas baithne wale saathi, musaafir aur apne ghulaam-londiyon ke saath achha sulook karo. Beshak Allah aise shakhs ko pasand nahin karta jo mutakabbir, fakhr karne wala ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اعْبُدُوا اللّٰهَ: Aur Allah ki ibaadat karo.}** Is aayat-e-kareema mein Allah Ta'ala aur bandon dono ke huqooq ki taleem di gayi hai. Allah Ta'ala ka bandon par haq yeh hai ke sirf usi ki ibaadat ki jaye aur us ke saath kisi ko shareek na thehraya jaye. Bandon ke aapas ke huqooq yeh hain:

### Bandon ke baahmi huqooq

**1. Walidain ke saath ihsaan:** Un ke saath ihsaan yeh hai ke walidain ka adab aur ita'at ki jaye, naafarmaani se bacha jaye, har waqt un ki khidmat ke liye tayyar raha jaye aur apni haisiyat ke mutabiq un par kharch karne mein kami na ki jaye.

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Kaainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne teen martaba farmaya: “Us ki naak khaak-aalood ho.” Kisi ne poocha: “Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Kaun?” Farmaya: “Jis ne maa-baap dono ya un mein se kisi ek ko budhape mein paaya aur Jannat mein daakhil na hua.” (Muslim, Kitab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Adaab, Baab Ragham man adraka abawaihi, safha 1381, hadees: 9 (2551))

**2. Rishtedaron se husn-e-sulook:** Is ka matlab yeh hai ke rishtedaron ke saath silah-rahmi ki jaye aur qata'-e-ta'alluq se bacha jaye. Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jise pasand ho ke us ke rizq mein wus'at ho aur us ki umr lambi ho, use chahiye ke apne rishtedaron ke saath achha sulook kare.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ul-Buyoo, Baab man ahabbal-basta fir-rizq, 2/10, hadees: 2067)

Hazrat Jubair bin Mut'im رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Rishtah kaatne wala Jannat mein nahin jayega.” (Muslim, Kitab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Adaab, Baab Silat-ir-Rahim wa tahreem qatee'atiha, safha 1383, hadees: 18 (2556))

Silah-rahmi ka matlab bayan karte hue Sadr-ush-Shariah Maulana Amjad Ali A'zami رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Silah-e-rahm ke ma'ni rishtah jorna hain, yani rishtedaron ke saath neki aur sulook karna. Poori ummat ka is par ittefaaq hai ke silah-rahmi waajib hai aur qata'-e-rahm, yani rishtah kaatna, haraam hai.” (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa shashum, sulook karne ka bayan, 3/558)

**3, 4. Yateemon aur mohtaajon se husn-e-sulook:** Yateem ke saath husn-e-sulook yeh hai ke us ki parwarish ki jaye, us se narmi se pesh aaya jaye aur shafqat se us ke sar par haath phera jaye. Hazrat Sahl bin Sa'd رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs yateem ki kafaalat kare, main aur woh Jannat mein is tarah honge.” Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne shahadat ki ungli aur darmiyani ungli se ishaara kiya aur dono ungliyon ke darmiyan thoda sa faasla rakha. (Bukhari, Kitab-ut-Talaaq, Baab-ul-Li'aan, 3/497, hadees: 5304)

Miskeen ke saath husn-e-sulook yeh hai ke us ki madad ki jaye aur use khaali haath na lautaya jaye. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Bewah aur miskeen ki madad aur khabar-geeri karne wala Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raah mein jihad karne wale ki tarah hai.” (Bukhari, Kitab-un-Nafaqaat, Baab fazl-un-nafaqati alal-ahl, 3/511, hadees: 5353)

**5. Padosiyon se husn-e-sulook:** Qareebi padosi se murad woh hai jis ka ghar apne ghar se mila hua ho. Door ke padosi se murad woh hai jo mohallay mein rehta ho lekin us ka ghar apne ghar se mila hua na ho. Jo padosi bhi ho aur rishtedaar bhi, woh qareebi padosi hai; jo sirf padosi ho aur rishtedaar na ho, woh door ka padosi hai. Isi tarah jo padosi aur Musalman dono ho, woh qareebi padosi hai aur jo sirf padosi ho, Musalman na ho, woh door ka padosi hai. (Tafseeraat-e-Ahmadiyyah, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 36, safha 275)

Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا se riwayat hai, Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام mujhe padosi ke baare mein musalsal wasiyyat karte rahe, yahan tak ke mujhe gumaan hua ke woh padosi ko waaris bana denge.” (Bukhari, Kitab-ul-Adaab, Baab-ul-Wasiyyati bil-Jaar, 4/104, hadees: 6014)

**6. Paas baithne walon se husn-e-sulook:** Is se murad biwi hai ya woh shakhs jo sohbat mein rahe, jaise safar ka saathi, saath padhne wala ya majlis aur masjid mein saath baithne wala. Yahan tak ke jo shakhs ek lamhe ke liye bhi paas baithe, us ke saath husn-e-sulook ka hukm hai.

**7. Musaafir se husn-e-sulook:** Is mein mehmaan bhi daakhil hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs Allah Ta'ala aur Roz-e-Qiyamat par imaan rakhta ho, woh mehmaan ka ikraam kare.” (Muslim, Kitab-ul-Imaan wan-Nuzoor, Baab-ul-Hass ala ikraam-il-jaar, safha 43, hadees: 74 (47))

**8. Ghulam aur laundi se husn-e-sulook:** Un se husn-e-sulook yeh hai ke unhein un ki taaqat se zyada takleef na di jaye, sakht kalami na ki jaye aur zaroorat ke mutabiq khaana, kapra waghera diya jaye. Hadees mein hai, Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Ghulam tumhare bhai hain. Allah Ta'ala ne unhein tumhare zair-e-dast kiya hai. Jo tum khaate ho us mein se unhein khilao, jo libaas tum pehante ho waisa hi unhein pehnao aur un ki taaqat se zyada un par bojh na daalo. Agar aisa ho to tum khud bhi un ki madad karo.” (Muslim, Kitab, Baab It'aam-ul-Mamlook mimma yaakul, safha 906, hadees: 38 (1661))

**{اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یُحِبُّ مَنْ كَانَ مُخْتَالًا فَخُوْرًا: Beshak Allah aise shakhs ko pasand nahin karta jo mutakabbir, fakhr karne wala ho.}** Kisi ko apne se haqeer samajhna aur haq baat qabool na karna takabbur hai. Yeh nihayat mazmoom sifat aur gunaah-e-kabeerah hai. Hadees mein hai ke Qiyamat ke din mutakabbireen ko insani shakal mein cheentiyon ki tarah uthaya jayega. Har taraf se un par zillat chha jayegi, unhein Jahannam ke “Boolas” naam ke qaid-khaane ki taraf haanka jayega aur badi aag unhein apni lapet mein le legi. Unhein “Teenat-ul-Khabaal”, yani Jahannamiyon ki peep pilayi jayegi. (Tirmizi, Kitab Sifat-il-Qiyamah, 47-baab, 4/221, hadees: 2500)

[1] Takabbur ki aqsaam, un ke ahkaam aur us ke ilaaj se muta'alliq maloomaat haasil karne ke liye Maktabat-ul-Madinah ki kitaab “Takabbur” ka mutaala karein.

## Ayat 4:37

`Source: tafseerId=50616; ayatId=534; surah=4; ayat=37; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْنَ یَبْخَلُوْنَ وَ یَاْمُرُوْنَ النَّاسَ بِالْبُخْلِ وَ یَكْتُمُوْنَ مَاۤ اٰتٰىهُمُ اللّٰهُ مِنْ فَضْلِهٖؕ-وَ اَعْتَدْنَا لِلْكٰفِرِیْنَ عَذَابًا مُّهِیْنًاۚ    (37)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh log jo khud bukhl karte hain aur doosre logon ko bukhl ka kehte hain aur Allah ne jo unhein apne fazl se diya hai use chhupate hain. Aur kafiron ke liye Hum ne zillat ka azaab tayyar kar rakha hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ یَبْخَلُوْنَ: Woh log jo bukhl karte hain.}** Arabi lughat mein bukhl se murad yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne fazl se jo maal diya ho, us mein se maangne wale ko na dena. Bukhl ki shar'i ta'reef yeh hai ke jo cheez zimmah par waajib ho use ada na karna. (Baghawi, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 37, 1/339)

Sadr-ul-Afaazil Maulana Naeem-ud-Deen Muradabadi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Bukhl yeh hai ke khud khaaye aur doosre ko na de. Shuh yeh hai ke na khud khaaye aur na doosre ko khilaye. Sakhaawat yeh hai ke khud bhi khaaye aur doosron ko bhi khilaye. Jood yeh hai ke khud na khaaye aur doosre ko khilaye.” (Khazain-ul-Irfan, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 37, safha 166; Madarik, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 37, safha 227)

Yahan bukhl se murad yeh hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Taurat mein bayan kiye gaye ausaaf batane mein bukhl karna aur doosron ko bhi is se rokna. Yeh aayat un Yahoodiyon ke baare mein naazil hui jo Taurat mein zikr kiye gaye Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ausaaf bayan karne mein bukhl karte aur unhein chhupate the. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 37, 1/379)

Ek qaul yeh hai ke is se maal kharch karne mein bukhl karna murad hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 37, 4/78)

Hazrat Abu Sa'eed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarwar-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Do khaslatein kisi momin mein jama nahin ho saktiin: bukhl aur bad-akhlaaqi.” (Tirmizi, Kitab-ul-Birr was-Silah, Baab ma jaa'a fil-bukhl, 3/387, hadees: 1969)

### Tanbeeh

Is se hamare zamane ke un ulama ko ibrat haasil karni chahiye jo Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ausaaf-e-hameedah khud bhi bayan nahin karte aur bayan karne walon ko bhi tarah tarah ke bahaane bana kar rokne ki koshish karte hain. A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain:

Zikr roke fazl kaate naqs ka
jooyaan rahe

Phir kahe mardak ke hoon
Ummat-e-Rasoolullah ki

Bukhl ke baare mein tafseel Surah Aal-e-Imran, aayat 180 ke tehat guzar chuki hai.

**{وَ یَكْتُمُوْنَ مَاۤ اٰتٰىهُمُ اللّٰهُ مِنْ فَضْلِهٖ: Aur Allah ne jo unhein apne fazl se diya hai use chhupate hain.}** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ jo ne'mat ata farmaye, use achhi niyyat ke saath, mauqa-mahal ki munaasibat aur hikmat ko saamne rakhte hue zaahir karna chahiye.

Hazrat Abu-ul-Ahwas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Mere walid paraganda baalon aur na-pasandeedah ha'iyat mein Sarkar-e-Aali-Waqaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki baargaah mein haazir hue. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne poocha: “Kya tumhare paas maal nahin?” Unhone arz ki: “Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Mujhe Allah Ta'ala ne har tarah ka maal ata farmaya hai.” Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala jab bande ko koi ne'mat ata farmata hai to us ka asar bande par dekhna pasand farmata hai.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Makkiyeen, hadees Malik bin Nadlah Abi-ul-Ahwas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, 5/384, hadees: 15892)

**Mas'alah:** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ne'mat ka izhaar ikhlaas ke saath ho to yeh bhi shukr hai. Is liye aadmi ko apni haisiyat ke mutabiq jaaiz libaas mein behtar libaas pehnna mustahab hai.

## Ayat 4:38

`Source: tafseerId=50617; ayatId=535; surah=4; ayat=38; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ الَّذِیْنَ یُنْفِقُوْنَ اَمْوَالَهُمْ رِئَآءَ النَّاسِ وَ لَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَ لَا بِالْیَوْمِ الْاٰخِرِؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّكُنِ الشَّیْطٰنُ لَهٗ قَرِیْنًا فَسَآءَ قَرِیْنًا(38)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur woh log jo apne maal logon ke dikhawe ke liye kharch karte hain aur na Allah par imaan laate hain aur na hi Aakhirat ke din par (to un ke liye shadeed wa'eed hai.) Aur jis ka saathi Shaitan ban jaye to kitna bura saathi ho gaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ الَّذِیْنَ یُنْفِقُوْنَ اَمْوَالَهُمْ رِئَآءَ النَّاسِ: Aur woh log jo apne maal logon ke dikhawe ke liye kharch karte hain.}** Bukhl ki burai bayan karne ke baad ab un logon ke baare mein bataya ja raha hai jo sirf dikhawa aur shohrat ke liye maal kharch karte hain aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raza haasil karna un ka maqsad nahin hota. Yeh log bhi usi hukm mein daakhil hain jo upar guzra.

### Riya-kaari ki mazammat

Is se un logon ko ibrat pakarni chahiye jo neki ke kaamon mein laakhon rupay kharch karte hain, lekin maqsad sirf wah-wah karwana hota hai. Woh bahut zyada khairaat karte hain, lekin saath hi yeh shart rakhte hain ke akhbaar mein khabar aur tasveer zaroor aani chahiye. Isi tarah shaadiyon ki fazool rasmon mein laakhon rupay ura dene wale bhi ibrat haasil karein. Woh sirf is liye rasmein karte hain ke agar yeh rasmein achhe tareeqe se na ki gayin to log kya kahenge, falan ne itna kharch kiya tha, main kyun peeche rahun, waghera.

Hazrat Mahmood bin Lubayd رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Mujhe tum par sab se zyada shirk-e-asghar, yani dikhawe mein mubtala hone ka khauf hai. Allah Ta'ala Qiyamat ke din kuch logon ko un ke hisaab ke waqt irshaad farmayega ke un logon ke paas jao jin ke liye tum duniya mein dikhawa karte the aur dekho ke kya tum un ke paas koi jaza paate ho?” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, hadees Mahmood bin Lubayd رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, 9/161, hadees: 23697)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Beshak Jahannam mein ek waadi hai jis se Jahannam rozana chaar sau martaba panaah maangti hai. Allah Ta'ala ne yeh waadi Ummat-e-Muhammadiyyah ke un riya-kaaron ke liye tayyar ki hai jo Quran-e-Paak ke haafiz, raah-e-Khuda mein sadqah karne wale, Allah Ta'ala ke ghar ke haaji aur raah-e-Khuda عَزَّوَجَلَّ mein nikalne wale honge, lekin yeh saare kaam sirf riya-kaari ke liye kar rahe honge.” (Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, al-Hasan an Ibn Abbas, 12/136, hadees: 12803) ([1])

**{وَ مَنْ یَّكُنِ الشَّیْطَانُ لَهٗ قَرِیْنًا: Aur jis ka saathi Shaitan ban jaye.}** Duniya mein Shaitan ka saathi is tarah hota hai ke woh Shaitani kaam karke use khush karta hai. Jo Shaitan ko khush karta hai, Shaitan us ke saath rehta hai. Yahan tak ke khaane-peene, raat guzaarne aur kai doosre maamlaat mein bhi us ke saath shareek ho jata hai. Isi liye hukm hai ke har jaaiz kaam BISMILLAH padh kar shuru kiya jaye, taake Shaitan ke liye rukawat ho. Aakhirat mein Shaitan ka saathi hona yeh hoga ke woh ek Shaitan ke saath aatishi zanjeer mein jakra hua hoga. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat: 38, 1/379)

Yeh wa'eed khaas taur par us shakhs ke baare mein hai jo gunaahon ke zariye Shaitan ka saathi banta hai. Jis ka saathi Shaitan ho, woh apne anjaam par khud hi ghaur kar le ke us ka anjaam kaisa hoga.

### Shaitan ke behkaane ka andaaz

Shaitan insaan ko mukhtalif tareeqon se behkata hai aur Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat aur farmabardaari se rokta hai. Sab se pehle Shaitan insaan ko Allah Ta'ala ki ita'at aur ibaadat se rokta hai. Agar Allah Ta'ala insaan ko Shaitan se mehfooz rakh le to insaan Shaitan ko yeh keh kar door kar deta hai: “Mujhe Allah Ta'ala ki ita'at aur ibaadat ki bahut sakht zaroorat hai, kyunke mujhe is duniya se Aakhirat ke liye tosha aur zade-raah tayyar karna bahut zaroori hai. Is ke baghair Aakhirat ka safar mumkin nahin.”

Jab insaan Shaitan ke is makr se bach jata hai to Shaitan use is tarah behkaane aur gumraah karne ki koshish karta hai ke woh use ibaadat mein susti aur kaahili ka hukm deta hai aur kehta hai: “Aaj rehne do, kal kar lena.” Agar Allah Ta'ala insaan ko is se bhi mehfooz rakh le to woh Shaitan ko yeh keh kar thukra deta hai: “Meri maut mere qabze mein nahin. Agar main aaj ka kaam kal par chhor doon to kal ka kaam kis din karunga? Har din ke liye ek kaam hai.”

Jab Shaitan is chaal se bhi mayoos ho jata hai to kehta hai: “Ae insaan! Tum Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat jaldi-jaldi karo taake falan-falan kaam ke liye faarigh ho sako.” Agar Allah Ta'ala insaan ko is chaal se bhi bacha le to insaan Shaitan ko yeh keh kar door kar deta hai: “Thodi aur mukammal ibaadat, zyada lekin naaqis ibaadat se kahin behtar hai.”

Agar Shaitan is chaal mein bhi nakaam aur na-muraad ho jata hai to woh insaan ko riya-kaari ke saath ibaadat karne ki targheeb deta hai aur use riya-kaari mein mubtala karne ki poori koshish karta hai. Agar insaan Allah Ta'ala ki madad se is chaal se mehfooz ho jaye to woh riya-kaari ke waswase ko yeh keh kar thukra deta hai: “Main kisi aur ko dikhane ke liye ibaadat kyun karun? Kya Allah Ta'ala ka dekh lena mere liye kaafi nahin?”

Jab Shaitan is hathkande se bhi nakaam ho jata hai to woh insaan ko khud-pasandi mein mubtala karne ki koshish karta hai aur kehta hai: “Tum ne kitna umdah kaam kiya aur tum ne kitni zyada shab-bedari ki.” Agar insaan Allah Ta'ala ke fazl-o-karam se is baar bhi mehfooz rahe aur khud-pasandi mein mubtala hone se bach jaye to woh Shaitan ke waswase ko yeh keh kar rad kar deta hai: “Mujh mein koi khoobi aur buzurgi nahin. Yeh to sab Allah Ta'ala ka azeem ehsaan hai ke Us ne mujh jaise gunaahgaar ko khaas taufeeq ata farmayi. Yeh bhi Us hi ka fazl-o-karam hai ke Us ne meri haqeer aur naaqis ibaadat ko sharf-e-qubooliyat ata farmaya. Agar Us ka fazl-o-karam shaamil-e-haal na hota to mere gunaahon ke muqaable mein meri in ibaadaton ki haisiyat hi kya thi.”

Jab mal'oon Shaitan in sab tadbeeron se nakaam ho jata hai to phir woh sab se zyada khatarnaak hathkanda istemaal karta hai. Is hathkande se bahut aqalmand aur hoshyaar dil shakhs ke siwa koi nahin bach sakta. Shaitan kehta hai: “Ae nek-bakht insaan! Tum logon se chhup-chhup kar nekiyan karne mein koshish kar rahe ho aur Allah Ta'ala tumhari in nekiyon ko jald hi sab logon mein mashhoor kar dega. Log tumhein Allah Ta'ala ka maqarrab banda keh kar yaad kiya karenge.” Is tarah Shaitan use riya-kaari mein mubtala karna chahta hai.

Agar Allah Ta'ala apni rahmat aur inaayat se insaan ko Shaitan ke is hathkande se bhi mehfooz farma de to insaan Shaitan ko yeh keh kar zaleel aur khaari karta hai: “Ae mal'oon! Abhi tak tu mere paas mere aamaal ko faasid aur bekaar karne aata tha aur ab un aamaal ki islaah aur durusti ke liye aata hai, taake mere aamaal ko bilkul khatam kar de. Door ho ja! Main Allah Ta'ala ka banda hoon aur wohi mera Aaqa-o-Maula hai. Main apni nekiyon ki shohrat ka mushtaaq aur talabgaar nahin hoon. Mera Parwardigaar chahe mere aamaal zaahir aur mashhoor kar de, chahe posheeda rakhe, chahe mujhe izzat aur martaba ata farmaye aur chahe mujhe zaleel aur ruswa kar de. Sab kuch usi ke qabze-e-qudrat mein hai. Mujhe is ki koi parwah nahin ke woh logon ke saamne mere aamaal ka izhaar farmaye ya na farmaye. Insaanon ke qabze mein koi cheez nahin hai.”

Agar insaan Shaitan ke is waar se bhi bach jaye to Shaitan us ke paas aakar kehta hai: “Tujhe aamaal karne ki kya zaroorat hai? Insaan ke nek aur bad hone ka faisla to roz-e-awwal hi mein ho chuka hai. Us din jo bura ho gaya woh bura hi rahega aur jo achha aur nek ho gaya woh nek hi rahega. Is liye agar tujhe nek-bakht paida kiya gaya hai to aamaal chhorna tere liye nuqsaan-deh nahin, aur agar tujhe bad-bakht aur shaqi paida kiya gaya hai to tera amal tujhe koi faidah nahin pahuncha sakta.”

Agar Allah Ta'ala apne fazl-o-karam se insaan ko Shaitan ke is waar se bacha le to insaan Shaitan-e-mardood se is tarah mukhatib hota hai: “Main Allah Ta'ala ka banda hoon aur bande ka kaam yeh hai ke woh apne Aaqa-o-Maula ke ahkaam baja laaye. Allah Ta'ala saare jahaan ka Parwardigaar hai. Woh jo chahta hai hukm karta hai aur jo chahta hai karta hai. Yaqeenan aamaal mere liye faidahmand hain, kisi soorat mein bhi nuqsaan-deh nahin ho sakte. Agar Allah Ta'ala ke ilm mein main nek-bakht hoon to Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein zyada sawaab ka mohtaaj hoon. Aur agar khuda-nakhwasta ilm-e-ilahi mein mera naam bad-bakhton mein hai to bhi ibaadat karne par main apne aap ko malamat nahin karunga, kyunke Allah Ta'ala mujhe ita'at aur ibaadat karne par saza nahin dega. Kam az kam itna to zaroor hai ke naafarmaan hokar Dozakh mein jaana, farmabardaar hokar Dozakh mein jaane se behtar hai.”

Phir yeh sab sirf ehtimaal hain, warna Allah Ta'ala ka waada bilkul haq hai aur Us ka farmaan bilkul sach hai. Allah Ta'ala ne bahut se maqamaat par ita'at aur ibaadat par sawaab ata farmane ka waada farmaya hai. Jo shakhs Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein imaan aur ita'at ke saath haazir hoga woh hargiz hargiz Jahannam mein daakhil na hoga, balki Allah Ta'ala ke fazl-o-karam aur Us ke sachhe aur muqaddas waade ki wajah se Jannat mein jayega.

Is liye ae logo! Khwab-e-ghaflat se bedaar ho jao, Shaitan ke hile se hoshyaar raho, har waqt Allah Ta'ala se madad maangte raho aur Shaitan-e-mardood se Allah Ta'ala hi ki panaah maangte raho. Tamam maamlaat usi ke qabze-e-qudrat mein hain. Gunahon se bachne aur ita'at aur ibaadat karne ki taaqat Allah Ta'ala ki taufeeq se hi milti hai. (Minhaaj-ul-Aabideen, al-Uqbah-us-Saalisah, al-Aaiq-us-Saalis: ash-Shaytaan, safha 61-62)

[1] Riya-kaari ke baare mein mufeed maloomaat haasil karne ke liye Maktabat-ul-Madinah ki kitaab “Riya-kaari” ka mutaala karein.

### Allah Ta'ala ke azaab se har ek ko darna chahiye

Yeh aayat kafiron ke baare mein naazil hui, lekin baharhaal duniya mein har aadmi ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke azaab se darna chahiye. Isi wajah se Qiyamat ki holnaaki aur azaab-e-Jahannam ki shiddat ke pesh-e-nazar hamare akaabir aslaaf aur buzurgan-e-deen bhi tamanna karte the ke kaash woh paida hi na hue hote.

Ameer-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne ek baar parinde ko dekh kar irshaad farmaya: “Ae parinde! Kaash main tumhari tarah hota aur mujhe insaan na banaya jata.” Ameer-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka farmaan hai: “Meri tamanna hai ke main ek mendha hota jise mere ghar wale apne mehmaanon ke liye zibah kar dete.” Hazrat Abu Zar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka qaul hai: “Kaash main ek darakht hota jo kaat diya jata.” Ameer-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Usman-e-Ghani رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmaya karte: “Main is baat ko pasand karta hoon ke mujhe wafaat ke baad na uthaya jaye.” Hazrat Talhah aur Hazrat Zubair رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmaya karte: “Kaash hum paida hi na hue hote.” Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا farmaya kartin: “Kaash main koi bhooli-bisri cheez hoti.” Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmaya karte: “Kaash main raakh hota.” (Qoot-ul-Quloob, al-Fasl-us-Saani was-Salaasoon, Sharh Maqaam-il-Khauf wa Wasf-il-Khaa'ifeen ... al-kh, 1/459-460, mulakhkhasan)

Yeh kalimaat un buzurg hastiyon ke hain jo zubaan-e-Risaalat se qat'i Jannati hone ki bashaarat se behra-mand hue, jabke aaj ke logon ki haalat yeh hai ke amal naam ki koi cheez paas nahin aur be-hisaab maghfirat ka yaqeen dil mein saja kar baithe hain. Ae kaash! Hamein bhi haqeeqi ma'non mein imaan par khaatime ki fikr, qabr aur Hashr ke pur-haul lamhaat ki tayyari, azaab-e-Jahannam ka dar aur Jabbaar-o-Qahhaar Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka khauf naseeb ho jaye.

## Ayat 4:43

`Source: tafseerId=50622; ayatId=540; surah=4; ayat=43; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَقْرَبُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اَنْتُمْ سُكٰرٰى حَتّٰى تَعْلَمُوْا مَا تَقُوْلُوْنَ وَ لَا جُنُبًا اِلَّا عَابِرِیْ سَبِیْلٍ حَتّٰى تَغْتَسِلُوْاؕ-وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ مَّرْضٰۤى اَوْ عَلٰى سَفَرٍ اَوْ جَآءَ اَحَدٌ مِّنْكُمْ مِّنَ الْغَآىٕطِ اَوْ لٰمَسْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ فَلَمْ تَجِدُوْا مَآءً فَتَیَمَّمُوْا صَعِیْدًا طَیِّبًا فَامْسَحُوْا بِوُجُوْهِكُمْ وَ اَیْدِیْكُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ عَفُوًّا غَفُوْرًا(43)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan waalo! Beshak Allah maaf karne wala, bakhshne wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: Ae imaan waalo!}** Shaan-e-nuzool: Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kuch Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki daawat ki. Khane ke baad sharaab pesh ki gayi. Us waqt tak sharaab haraam nahin hui thi, is liye kuch hazraat ne sharaab pee li. Phir Maghrib ki namaaz padhi gayi. Chunanche Musalmanon ne namaaz ke auqaat mein sharaab chhor di. Is ke baad Surah Ma'idah mein sharaab ko bilkul haraam kar diya gaya.

### Nashe ki haalat mein kufriya kalima bolne ka hukm

Is waaqe'ah se maloom hua ke agar nashe ki haalat mein koi shakhs kufriya kalima bol de to woh kaafir nahin hota. “قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْكٰفِرُوْنَ” mein dono jagah “لَا” ka tark kufr hai, kyunke is se ma'ni yeh banega: “Ae kaafiro! Jin buton ki tum ibaadat karte ho, main bhi un ki ibaadat karta hoon.” Yeh yaqeenan kufriya kalima hai. Lekin yahan nashe ki haalat thi, is liye Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne us par kufr ka hukm na diya, balki Quran-e-Paak mein unhein “یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا” keh kar khitaab farmaya gaya. ([1])

**{وَ لَا جُنُبًا: Aur na janaabat ki haalat mein.}** Pehla hukm yeh tha ke nashe ki haalat mein namaaz ke qareeb na jao. Doosra hukm yeh diya gaya ke jab tum janaabat ki haalat mein ho to jab tak ghusl na kar lo, namaaz ke qareeb na jao. Yani pehle ghusl karna farz hai. Haan, agar safar ki haalat mein ho aur paani na mile to tayammum karke namaaz padh lo.

Yahan safar ki qaid is liye hai ke paani na milna aksar safar hi mein hota hai. Warna na safar mein tayammum ki har surat mein ijaazat hai aur na tayammum ki ijaazat sirf safar ke saath khaas hai. Agar safar mein paani maujood ho to tayammum ki ijaazat nahin hogi. Isi tarah agar safar na ho lekin beemaari waghera ho jis mein paani ka istemaal nuqsaan-deh ho to tayammum ki ijaazat hai.

**{وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ مَّرْضٰى: Aur agar tum beemaar ho.}** Is aayat mein tayammum ke hukm ki tafseel bayan ki gayi hai. Tayammum ki ijaazat jis tarah be-ghusl hone ki surat mein hai, isi tarah be-wuzu hone ki surat mein bhi hai. Agar tum beemaar ho ya safar mein ho aur tumhein wuzu ya ghusl ki zaroorat ho, ya tum bait-ul-khala se qaza-e-haajat karke aaye ho aur tumhein wuzu ki zaroorat ho, ya tum ne auraton se humbistari ki ho aur tum par ghusl farz ho gaya ho, to in sab suraton mein agar tum paani ke istemaal par qaadir na ho to tayammum kar sakte ho.

Paani par qaadir na hone ka matlab yeh hai ke paani maujood na ho, ya door ho, ya use haasil karne ka saamaan na ho, ya saanp, darinde, dushman waghera ka khauf ho. Yaad rahe ke jab aurat haiz ya nifaas se paak hone ke baad ghusl ki mohtaaj ho aur us waqt paani par qudrat na paaye to use bhi tayammum ki ijaazat hai, jaisa ke hadees shareef mein aaya hai.

**{فَتَیَمَّمُوْا صَعِیْدًا طَیِّبًا: To paak mitti se tayammum karo.}** Aayat ke aakhir mein tayammum karne ka tareeqa bhi bataya gaya hai. Tayammum karne wala paaki haasil karne ki niyyat kare. Jo cheez mitti ki jins se ho, jaise gard, rait, patthar ya mitti ka farsh, us par do martaba haath maare. Ek martaba haath maar kar chehre par pher le aur doosri martaba zameen par haath pher kar kohniyon samet dono haathon par pher le.

### Tayammum ke do ahkaam

**(1)** Ek tayammum se bahut se faraaiz aur nawafil padhe ja sakte hain.

**(2)** Tayammum karne wale ke peeche ghusl aur wuzu karne wale ki iqtida durust hai.

**Note:** Tayammum ke mazeed ahkaam jaanne ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, jild 1, hissa number 2, “Tayammum ka bayan” ka mutaala karein.

Aayat-e-mubarakah ke aakhri hisse ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Ghazwa-e-Bani Mustaliq mein Lashkar-e-Islam raat ke waqt ek veeraan maidaan mein thehra. Wahan paani nahin tha aur subah wahan se kooch karna tha. Umm-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ka haar gum ho gaya. Us ki talaash ke liye Sayyid-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne wahan qiyaam farmaya. Subah hui to paani maujood nahin tha.

Is par Allah Ta'ala ne tayammum ki aayat naazil farmayi. Yeh dekh kar Hazrat Usaid bin Huzair رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Ae Aal-e-Abu Bakr! Yeh tumhari pehli barkat nahin hai.” Yani tumhari barkat se Musalmanon ko pehle bhi bahut aasaaniyan aur faide pahunche hain. Phir jab oont uthaya gaya to us ke neeche haar mil gaya. (Bukhari, Kitab-ut-Tayammum, Baab-ut-Tayammum, 1/133, hadees: 334)

Haar gum hone aur Rahmat-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka is ke baare mein na batane mein bahut si hikmatein thin. Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ke haar ki wajah se Nabi-e-Rahmat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka wahan qiyaam farmana Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ki fazeelat aur martabe ko zaahir karta hai. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ka haar talaash karna is baat ki hidaayat hai ke Huzoor Tajdaar-e-Anbiya صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki Azwaj-e-Mutahharaat ki khidmat momineen ke liye sa'adat hai. Is waaqe'ah se tayammum ka hukm bhi maloom hua, jis se Qiyamat tak Musalman faida uthate rahenge. سُبْحَانَ اللہ۔

[1] Kufriya jumlon aur alfaaz ke baare mein maloomaat haasil karne ke liye Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَتْ بَرَکَاتُہُمُ الْعَالِیَہ ki tasneef “Kufriya Kalimaat ke Baare Mein Sawaal Jawaab” ka mutaala zaroor karein.

## Ayat 4:44

`Source: tafseerId=50623; ayatId=541; surah=4; ayat=44; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوْا نَصِیْبًا مِّنَ الْكِتٰبِ یَشْتَرُوْنَ الضَّلٰلَةَ وَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ اَنْ تَضِلُّوا السَّبِیْلَﭤ(44)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya tum ne un logon ko na dekha jinhein Kitaab se ek hissa mila ke woh gumraahi khareedte hain aur chahte hain ke tum bhi raaste se bhatak jao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَمْ تَرَ: Kya tum ne na dekha.}** Yahan Yahoodiyon ke baare mein farmaya gaya hai ke un logon ko Allah Ta'ala ki Kitaab, yani Tauraat mili. Unhone Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ki nubuwwat ko to pehchana, lekin Imam-ul-Anbiya صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke baare mein Tauraat mein jo bayan tha, us hissa ko chhupa diya aur Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat ke munkir ho gaye. Isi liye farmaya gaya ke unhein Kitaab ka ek hissa mila.

Goya Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki Kitaab rakhne ke bawajood woh hidaayat ke bajaye gumraahi ke pairokaar hue. Is ke saath woh Musalmanon ko bhi gumraah karne ki koshish karte rahe. Is se maloom hua ke hidaayat ka daromadaar Huzoor Sayyid-e-Kaainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par kaamil imaan laane par hai.

## Ayat 4:45

`Source: tafseerId=50624; ayatId=542; surah=4; ayat=45; para=5; volume=2`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Allah tumhare dushmanon ko khoob jaanta hai aur hifaazat ke liye Allah hi kaafi hai aur Allah hi kaafi madadgaar hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اللّٰهُ اَعْلَمُ بِاَعْدَآىٕكُمْ: Aur Allah tumhare dushmanon ko khoob jaanta hai.}** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ tumhare dushmanon ko khoob jaanta hai aur Us ne tumhein bhi un ki dushmani se khabardaar kar diya hai. Is liye tumhein chahiye ke un dushmanon se bachte raho. Yaqeenan Allah Ta'ala hum se zyada hamare dushmanon ko jaanta hai. Is liye jise Allah Ta'ala dushman farma de, woh yaqeenan hamara dushman hai, jaise Shaitan, kafir aur munaafiq.

## Ayat 4:46

`Source: tafseerId=50625; ayatId=543; surah=4; ayat=46; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا یُحَرِّفُوْنَ الْكَلِمَ عَنْ مَّوَاضِعِهٖ وَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ سَمِعْنَا وَ عَصَیْنَا وَ اسْمَعْ غَیْرَ مُسْمَعٍ وَّ رَاعِنَا لَیًّۢا بِاَلْسِنَتِهِمْ وَ طَعْنًا فِی الدِّیْنِؕ-وَ لَوْ اَنَّهُمْ قَالُوْا سَمِعْنَا وَ اَطَعْنَا وَ اسْمَعْ وَ انْظُرْنَا لَكَانَ خَیْرًا لَّهُمْ وَ اَقْوَمَۙ-وَ لٰـكِنْ لَّعَنَهُمُ اللّٰهُ بِكُفْرِهِمْ فَلَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا(46)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Yahoodiyon mein kuch woh hain jo kalimaat ko un ki jagah se badal dete hain aur kehte hain: Hum ne suna aur maana nahin, aur aap sunein, aap ko na sunaya jaye, aur “Raa'ina” kehte hain zabaanen maror kar aur deen mein ta'na dene ke liye. Aur agar woh kehte ke hum ne suna aur maana aur Huzoor hamari baat sunein aur hum par nazar farmaein to yeh un ke liye behtar aur zyada durust hota, lekin un par to Allah ne un ke kufr ki wajah se la'nat kar di, to woh bahut thoda yaqeen rakhte hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا: Yahoodiyon mein kuch woh hain.}** Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke bahut se Yahoodiyon ki aadat nihayat buri thi. Un mein kai buri aadatein thin:

**Pehli baat:** Tauraat Shareef mein Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہِ وَسَلَّمَ ki shaan mein jo bayan farmaya tha, Yahoodi un kalimaat ko badal dete the.

**Doosri baat:** Jab Tajdaar-e-Madinah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ unhein koi hukm dete to zubaan se kehte ke hum ne maan liya, lekin dil se kehte ke hum ne qabool nahin kiya.

**Teesri baat:** Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein woh kai tarah se munaafiqat karte the. Is ki ek surat yeh bhi thi ke Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke saamne aise jumle bolte jin ke do ma'ni bante hon, ek achhe aur doosre bure.

Un ka tareeqa yeh tha ke zahir mein achhe ma'ni ka asar dete, lekin dil mein wahi bure ma'ni muraad lete. Jaise kehte: “Suniye aur Aap ko na sunaya jaye.” “Aap ko na sunaya jaye” ka zahiri ma'ni yeh tha ke koi na-gawaar baat Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke sunne mein na aaye, lekin un ki muraad yeh thi ke Aap ko sunna naseeb na ho.

Isi ki doosri misaal “رَاعِنَا” ka kalima hai. Is ka zahiri ma'ni “hamari ri'aayat farmaiye” hai, lekin Yahoodi is ka aisa ma'ni lete the jo shaan-e-mubarak ke laayiq na tha, halaanke is lafz ke istemaal se mana farma diya gaya tha. Phir un ke saathi Yahoodi apne logon se kehte: “Hum to Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko bura bhala kehte hain. Agar Aap Nabi hote to Aap is ko jaan lete.” Allah Ta'ala ne un ke andar ki napaaki yahan zaahir farma di.

Is se maloom hua ke Sarwar-e-Kaainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ilm par ta'na karna dar-haqeeqat Islam ke deen par ta'na karna hai, aur yeh Yahoodiyon ka tareeqa hai. Phir Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaya ke agar yeh log bhi ahl-e-adab ka tareeqa ikhtiyaar karte aur un ki tarah kehte: “Ya Rasoolallah! Hum ne suna aur dil-o-jaan se tasleem kiya. Huzoor! Hamari baat suniye aur hum par nazar-e-karam farmaiye,” to yeh un ke liye duniya aur Aakhirat, dono ke lihaaz se behtar hota. Lekin kyunke un par la'nat ki gayi hai, is liye unhein adab ki taufeeq nahin milegi.

## Ayat 4:47

`Source: tafseerId=50626; ayatId=544; surah=4; ayat=47; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ اٰمِنُوْا بِمَا نَزَّلْنَا مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا مَعَكُمْ مِّنْ قَبْلِ اَنْ نَّطْمِسَ وُجُوْهًا فَنَرُدَّهَا عَلٰۤى اَدْبَارِهَاۤ اَوْ نَلْعَنَهُمْ كَمَا لَعَنَّاۤ اَصْحٰبَ السَّبْتِؕ-وَ كَانَ اَمْرُ اللّٰهِ مَفْعُوْلًا(47)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Kitaab waalo! Jo hum ne tumhare paas maujood Kitaab ki tasdeeq karne wala Quran utaara hai, us par imaan le aao, is se pehle ke hum chehre bigaadh dein, phir unhein un ki peeth ki surat pher dein, ya un par bhi aisi hi la'nat karein jaise Haftay walon par la'nat ki thi, aur Allah ka hukm hokar hi rehta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ: Ae Kitaab waalo!}** Yahan Yahoodiyon se khitaab hai ke Ae Ahl-e-Kitaab! Hum ne Quran utaara hai jo tumhare paas maujood Kitaab, yani Tauraat, ki tasdeeq karta hai. Is par imaan le aao, warna aisa na ho ke Hum tumhare chehron se aankhen, naak, kaan, abroo waghera mita kar tumhari shaklein bigaadh dein. Tumhare chehre ko saamne se aisi khaal bana dein jaisi sar ka pichhla hissa hota hai, jis mein na aankhen rahen, na naak aur na munh.

Ya Hum in Yahoodiyon par bhi aisi hi la'nat karein jaise Haftay ke din naafarmaani karne wale Yahoodiyon ke giroh par la'nat ki gayi thi. Yahoodiyon par la'nat aisi padi ke duniya unhein mal'oon kehti hai.

Is aayat ke baare mein mufassireen ke chand aqwaal hain. Baaz kehte hain ke yeh wa'eed duniya ke lihaaz se hai. Baaz ise Aakhirat ke lihaaz se qarar dete hain. Baaz kehte hain ke la'nat ho chuki aur wa'eed poori ho gayi, jabke baaz kehte hain ke abhi is ka intezaar hai. Ek qaul yeh hai ke chehre bigaadne ki wa'eed us waqt thi jab Yahoodiyon mein se koi imaan na laata. Chunke bahut se Yahoodi imaan le aaye, is liye shart poori na hui aur wa'eed uth gayi.

### Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka Islaam qabool karna

Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ bahut bade Yahoodi aalim the. Woh Shaam se wapas aate hue raaste mein the ke unhone yeh aayat suni. (Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 47, 1/390-391)

Hazrat Ka'b Ahbaar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ Yahoodi ulama mein badi qadr-o-manzilat rakhte the. Unhone ek raat kisi shakhs se yahi aayat suni. Woh khaufzada hue aur Ameer-ul-Mu'mineen Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki baargaah mein haazir hokar Musalman ho gaye. (Futooh-us-Shaam, Zikr-e-Fath-e-Madinat-e-Bait-ul-Muqaddas, safha 234-235, al-juz al-awwal)

## Ayat 4:48

`Source: tafseerId=50627; ayatId=545; surah=4; ayat=48; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَغْفِرُ اَنْ یُّشْرَكَ بِهٖ وَ یَغْفِرُ مَا دُوْنَ ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ یَّشَآءُۚ-وَ مَنْ یُّشْرِكْ بِاللّٰهِ فَقَدِ افْتَرٰۤى اِثْمًا عَظِیْمًا(48)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak Allah is baat ko nahin bakhshta ke Us ke saath shirk kiya jaye aur is se neeche jo kuch hai jise chahta hai maaf farma deta hai aur jis ne Allah ka shareek thehraya to beshak us ne bahut bade gunah ka bohtaan baandha.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَغْفِرُ اَنْ یُّشْرَكَ بِهٖ: Beshak Allah is baat ko nahin bakhshta ke Us ke saath shirk kiya jaye.}** Aayat ka ma'ni yeh hai ke jo shakhs kufr ki haalat mein mare, Allah Ta'ala us ki maghfirat nahin farmayega, balki us ke liye hamesha ka azaab hai. Aur jis ne kufr na kiya ho, chahe woh kitna hi gunahgaar ho, kabirah gunahon mein mubtala ho aur baghair taubah ke mar jaye, phir bhi woh Jahannam mein hamesha nahin rahega. Us ki maghfirat Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat, yani Allah ke chahne, par hai. Allah Ta'ala chahe to use karam se maaf farma de aur chahe to us ke gunahon par azaab dene ke baad apni rahmat se Jannat mein daakhil farma de. Is aayat mein Yahoodiyon ko imaan lane ki targheeb di gayi hai.

### Maghfirat ki ummeed par gunah karna bahut khatarnaak hai

Yaad rahe ke kufr ke siwa Qiyamat ke din har gunah ke bakhshe jaane ka imkaan zaroor hai. Lekin is imkaan ki ummeed par gunahon mein padna bahut khatarnaak hai. Baaz sooraton mein gunah ko halka samajhne ki wajah se khud kufr ho jayega. Woh Allah Ta'ala kitna Kareem hai jo lakhon gunah karne wale bande ko maafi ki ummeed dila raha hai, aur woh banda kitna past hai jo aise Kareem ke karam aur rahmat par dil-o-jaan se qurbaan hokar us ki bandagi mein lagne ke bajaye us ki naafarmani par kamar baandhe hue hai.

### Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka Islam qabool karna

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, jinhone Hazrat Hamzah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko shaheed kiya tha, Sultan-e-Do Jahan صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki baargaah mein haazir hue aur arz ki: “Mujhe amaan dijiye taa-ke main Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se Allah ka Kalaam sun sakun. Is mein meri maghfirat aur najaat hai.”

Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Mujhe yeh pasand tha ke meri nazar tum par is tarah padti ke tum amaan na maang rahe hote. Lekin ab tum ne amaan maangi hai, to main tumhein amaan deta hoon taa-ke tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka Kalaam sun sako.” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui:

**وَ الَّذِیْنَ لَا یَدْعُوْنَ مَعَ اللّٰهِ اِلٰهًا اٰخَرَ(الفرقان :۶۸)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur woh jo Allah ke saath kisi doosre mabood ko nahin poojte.

Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Main shirk mein mubtala raha hoon, na-haq khoon bhi kiya hai aur zina ka bhi murtakib hua hoon. Kya in gunahon ke hote hue Allah Ta'ala mujhe bakhsh dega?” Is par Sarkar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne khamoshi ikhtiyaar farmayi aur koi kalaam na farmaya. Phir yeh aayat naazil hui:

**اِلَّا مَنْ تَابَ وَ اٰمَنَ وَ عَمِلَ عَمَلًا صَالِحًا(الفرقان:۷۰)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Magar jo taubah kare, imaan laye aur achha kaam kare.

Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Is aayat mein shart rakhi gayi hai ke gunahon se maghfirat usi ko milegi jo taubah kare aur nek amal kare. Main nek amal na kar saka, to mera kya hoga?” Tab yeh aayat tilaawat farmayi:

**اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَغْفِرُ اَنْ یُّشْرَكَ بِهٖ وَ یَغْفِرُ مَا دُوْنَ ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ یَّشَآءُ(النساء:۴۸)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak Allah is baat ko nahin bakhshta ke Us ke saath kufr kiya jaye aur kufr se neeche jo kuch hai jise chahe maaf farma deta hai.

Ab Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Is aayat mein maghfirat Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat ke saath waabasta hai. Ho sakta hai main un logon mein se hoon jin ke saath Allah Ta'ala ki maghfirat ki mashiyyat waabasta na ho.” Is ke baad yeh aayat naazil hui:

**قُلْ یٰعِبَادِیَ الَّذِیْنَ اَسْرَفُوْا عَلٰۤى اَنْفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوْا مِنْ رَّحْمَةِ اللّٰهِ(الزمر :۵۳)**

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmao: Ae mere woh bando jinhone apni jaanon par zyadati ki, Allah ki rahmat se na-umeed na ho.

Yeh aayat sunkar Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Ab main koi qaid aur shart nahin dekhta.” Aur usi waqt Islam qabool kar liya. (Madarij-un-Nubuwwah, Qism Som, Baab Haftum: Zikr-e-Saal Haftum wa Fath-e-Makkah, 3/302)

Madarij ke ilawa baaqi kitabon mein yeh zikr hai ke yeh arz Huzoor Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki baargaah mein nahin hui, balki kisi doosre zariye se hui.

## Ayat 4:49

`Source: tafseerId=50628; ayatId=546; surah=4; ayat=49; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الَّذِیْنَ یُزَكُّوْنَ اَنْفُسَهُمْؕ-بَلِ اللّٰهُ یُزَكِّیْ مَنْ یَّشَآءُ وَ لَا یُظْلَمُوْنَ فَتِیْلًا(49)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya tum ne un logon ko nahin dekha jo khud apni paakeezgi bayan karte hain? Balki Allah jise chahta hai paakeeza bana deta hai, aur un par khajoor ke andar ki jhilli ke baraabar bhi zulm nahin kiya jayega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ یُزَكُّوْنَ اَنْفُسَهُمْ: Jo khud apni paakeezgi bayan karte hain.}** Yeh aayat Yahood aur Nasara ke baare mein naazil hui jo apne aap ko Allah Ta'ala ka beta aur us ka pyaara batate the. Woh kehte the ke Yahood aur Nasara ke siwa koi Jannat mein daakhil nahin hoga. Is tarah woh jhooti tareef ke saath apni paakeezgi bayan karte the.

### Khud-pasandi ki mazammat

Is aayat-e-Kareema mein khud-pasandi ki mazammat bayan hui hai. Khud-pasandi yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne fazl-o-karam se kisi ko deeni ya dunyawi ne'mat di ho, lekin woh yeh samjhe ke yeh ne'mat milna meri apni koshish ka nateeja hai, aur phir us par naaz karne lage. (Keemiya-e-Sa'adat, Rukn Som: Muhlikaat, Asl Nohum, Haqiqat-e-Ujb-o-Dalaal, 2/725)

Khud-pasandi ek mazmoom baatini marz hai aur aaj kal Musalmanon ki aksariyat is mein mubtala nazar aati hai. Apne ilm aur amal par naaz karna, zyada ibaadat par itraana, izzat, mansab aur daulat par naazaan hona, apni fanni mahaarat par kisi ki tanqeed bardasht na karna aur kisi doosre ko koi ahmiyat na dena bahut aam hai. Aise logon ko chahiye ke in riwayaat ka gaur se mutaala'a karein:

**(1)** Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Jis ne kisi nek amal par apni tareef ki, us ka shukr zaaya hua aur amal barbaad ho gaya.” (Kanz-ul-Umaal, Kitaab-ul-Akhlaaq, Qism-ul-Aqwaal, Al-Ujb, 2/206, Al-Juz-us-Saalis, Hadees: 7674)

**(2)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Aali Waqar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Teen cheezen halaakat mein daalne wali hain: (1) woh lalach jis ki pairwi ki jaye, (2) woh khwahish jis ki pairwi ki jaye, (3) bande ka apne amal ko pasand karna, yani khud-pasandi.” (Al-Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, Man Ismuhu Muhammad, 4/212, Hadees: 5754)

**(3)** Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshaad farmaya: “Ae Suraaqah! Kya main tumhein Jannati aur Jahannami logon ke baare mein na bataun?” Arz ki: “Ya Rasoolallah! Zaroor bataiye.” Irshaad farmaya: “Har sakhti karne wala, itra kar chalne wala aur apni badaai chaahne wala Jahannami hai, jabke kamzor aur maghloob log Jannati hain.” (Al-Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Ali bin Rabaah an Suraaqah bin Maalik, 7/129, Hadees: 6589)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Dekho yeh Allah par kaise jhoot baandh rahe hain, aur khule gunah ke liye yahi jhoot kaafi hai.

## Ayat 4:51

`Source: tafseerId=50630; ayatId=548; surah=4; ayat=51; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوْا نَصِیْبًا مِّنَ الْكِتٰبِ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِالْجِبْتِ وَ الطَّاغُوْتِ وَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ لِلَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا هٰۤؤُلَآءِ اَهْدٰى مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا سَبِیْلًا(51)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوْا نَصِیْبًا مِّنَ الْكِتٰبِ: Kya tum ne un logon ko nahin dekha jinhein Kitaab ka ek hissa mila?}** Ka'b bin Ashraf aur us ke saath mazeed sattar Yahoodi mushrikon-e-Makkah ke paas gaye aur unhein Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se jang karne par uksaaya. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 51, 4/101; Khazin, An-Nisa, zer-e-aayat 51, 1/392, multaqatan)

Allah Ta'ala ne un par la'nat farmayi ke unhone Huzoor Tajdaar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki adawat mein mushrikon ke buton tak ki pooja ki.

### Taaghoot ka ma'ni

Is aayat mein “Taaghoot” ka lafz aaya hai. Yeh “طَغٰی” se bana hai, jis ka ma'ni “sarkashi” hai. Jo Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se sarkash ho aur doosron ko sarkash banaye, woh Taaghoot hai, chahe Shaitaan ho ya insaan. Quran-e-Kareem ne kufr ke sardaron ko bhi Taaghoot kaha hai. Taaghoot ke lafz mein sarkashi ka maadda maujood hai, is liye Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah ke maqarrabeen ke liye yeh lafz hargiz istemaal nahin ho sakta. Jo un ke liye yeh lafz istemaal kare, woh khud “Taaghoot” hai.

## Ayat 4:54

`Source: tafseerId=50632; ayatId=551; surah=4; ayat=54; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَمْ یَحْسُدُوْنَ النَّاسَ عَلٰى مَاۤ اٰتٰىهُمُ اللّٰهُ مِنْ فَضْلِهٖۚ-فَقَدْ اٰتَیْنَاۤ اٰلَ اِبْرٰهِیْمَ الْكِتٰبَ وَ الْحِكْمَةَ وَ اٰتَیْنٰهُمْ مُّلْكًا عَظِیْمًا(54)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَمْ یَحْسُدُوْنَ النَّاسَ: Balkay yeh logon se hasad karte hain.}** Is aayat mein Yahoodiyon ke asal marz ko bayan farmaya gaya ke asal baat yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko jo nubuwwat ata farmayi aur un ke saath un ke ghulamon ko jo nusrat, ghalba, izzat aur doosri nematein ata farmayi hain, yeh log Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Ahl-e-Imaan se un par hasad karte hain.

Yahoodiyon ka yeh kaam bilkul jahalat aur bewaqoofi hai. Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad par fazl farmaya tha: kisi ko Kitaab di, kisi ko nubuwwat aur kisi ko hukoomat di, aur kisi ko kai cheezen ikatthi ata farmayin. Jaise Hazrat Yusuf, Hazrat Dawood aur Hazrat Sulaiman عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko nawaza gaya.

To jab Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ apne Habeeb, Imam-ul-Ambiya, Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko apne karam se Kitaab aur nubuwwat-o-risaalat ata farmaye to aye Yahoodiyo! tum is par kyun jalte aur hasad karte ho?

## Ayat 4:57

## Ayat 4:58

`Source: tafseerId=50636; ayatId=555; surah=4; ayat=58; para=5; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَاْمُرُكُمْ اَنْ تُؤَدُّوا الْاَمٰنٰتِ اِلٰۤى اَهْلِهَاۙ-وَ اِذَا حَكَمْتُمْ بَیْنَ النَّاسِ اَنْ تَحْكُمُوْا بِالْعَدْلِؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ نِعِمَّا یَعِظُكُمْ بِهٖؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ كَانَ سَمِیْعًۢا بَصِیْرًا(58)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak Allah tumhein hukm deta hai ke amaanatein jin ki hain un ke supurd karo aur yeh ke jab tum logon mein faisla karo to insaaf ke saath faisla karo. Beshak Allah tumhein kya hi achhi naseehat farmata hai. Beshak Allah sunne wala, dekhne wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَاْمُرُكُمْ: Beshak Allah tumhein hukm deta hai.}** Yahan do hukm bayan kiye gaye hain. Pehla hukm yeh hai ke amaanatein un logon ke hawale karo jin ki woh hain. Doosra hukm yeh hai ke jab faisla karo to insaaf ke saath karo.

### Islami taleemaat ke shahkaar

Yeh dono hukm Islami taleemaat ke shahkaar hain aur aman-o-amaan qayam karne aur huqooq ada karne mein markazi haisiyat rakhte hain. Dono ki kuch tafseel yeh hai:

**(1) Amaanat ada karna:** Amaanat ada karne mein bunyadi baat yeh hai ke maali maamlaat mein haqdaar ko us ka haq de diya jaye. Is ke saath aur bhi bohat si cheezen amaanat ada karne mein daakhil hain. Jaise Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُما se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo Musalmanon ka haakim bana, phir us ne un par kisi aise shakhs ko haakim muqarrar kiya jis ke baare mein woh khud jaanta hai ke is se behtar aur is se zyada Kitaab-o-Sunnat ka aalim Musalmanon mein maujood hai, to us ne Allah Ta'ala, us ke Rasool aur tamam Musalmanon se khayanat ki.” (Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Amr bin Dinar an Ibn Abbas, 11/94, hadees: 11216)

**(2) Insaaf ke saath faisla karna:** Nizaam-e-adl-o-adalat ki rooh hi yeh hai ke insaaf ke saath faisla kiya jaye. Dono fareeqon mein se kisi ki bhi asal mein riayat na ki jaye. Ulama ne farmaya ke haakim ko paanch baaton mein dono fareeqon ke saath barabar sulook karna chahiye: (1) apne paas aane ka ek ko jaisa mauqa de, doosre ko bhi de; (2) dono ko ek jaisi nashist de; (3) dono ki taraf barabar tawajjuh rakhe; (4) dono ki baat sunne mein ek hi tareeqa rakhe; (5) faisla dete waqt haq ka lihaz kare aur jis ka doosre par haq ho, woh poora poora dilaye.

Hadees-e-Paak mein hai ke insaaf karne walon ko qurb-e-Ilahi mein noor ke mimbar ata kiye jaayenge. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaarah, Baab fazeelat-ul-imaam-il-aadil, hadees: 18 (1827))

### Qaazi Shuraih رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka adilana faisla

Musalman qaaziyon ne Islam ke adilana nizaam aur bar-haq faislon ki aisi azeem misaalein qayam ki hain ke duniya un ki nazir pesh nahin kar sakti. Is mauqe par ek waqia mulahiza kijiye.

Jang-e-Siffeen ke mauqe par Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم ki ek zarah gum ho gayi. Baad mein jab aap کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم Kufa tashreef laaye to woh zarah ek Yahoodi ke paas mili. Aap ne us se farmaya: “Yeh zarah meri hai. Main ne tumhein na bechi hai aur na tohfe mein di hai.” Yahoodi ne kaha: “Yeh zarah meri hai kyunke mere qabze mein hai.” Aap ne farmaya: “Hum Qaazi Sahib se faisla karwate hain.”

Chunancha dono Qaazi Shuraih رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki adalat mein pahunche. Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم un ke saath tashreef farma hue. Qaazi Shuraih رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Aye Ameer-ul-Momineen! Irshad farmaiye.” Aap ne farmaya: “Is Yahoodi ke qabze mein jo zarah hai woh meri hai. Main ne ise na becha hai aur na tohfe mein di hai.”

Qaazi Shuraih رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Yahoodi se farmaya: “Aye Yahoodi! Tum kya kehte ho?” Yahoodi bola: “Yeh zarah meri hai kyunke mere qabze mein hai.” Qaazi Sahib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم se kaha: “Aye Ameer-ul-Momineen! Kya aap ke paas koi daleel hai?” Aap ne farmaya: “Haan, Qanbar aur Hasan dono is baat ke gawah hain.”

Qaazi Sahib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Hasan aap ke bete hain aur shar'i usool yeh hai ke bete ki gawahi baap ke haq mein jaiz nahin.” Jab Yahoodi ne Qaazi Sahib ka adilana faisla suna to hairat-zada ho kar kehne laga: “Aye Ameer-ul-Momineen! Aap mujhe Qaazi Sahib ke paas laaye aur Qaazi Sahib ne aap hi ke khilaaf faisla kar diya! Main gawahi deta hoon ke yahi mazhab-e-haq hai aur main gawahi deta hoon ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke siwa koi mabood nahin aur beshak Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Allah Ta'ala ke Rasool hain. Yeh zarah aap hi ki hai.”

Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم us ke Islam qabool karne se bohat khush hue. Aap ne woh zarah aur ek ghora use tohfe mein de diya. (Tareekh-ul-Khulafa, Abu-us-Sibtain: Ali bin Abi Talib, Fasl fi nabzin min akhbaari Ali, s. 184-185; Al-Kaamil fit-Taareekh, san 40, zikr ba'zi seeratihi, 3/265)
