# Surah Ad-Duha — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 93:1

`Source: tafseerId=54425; ayatId=6172; surah=93; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ الضُّحٰىۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Chadhte din ke waqt ki qasam.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَالضُّحٰى: Chadhte din ke waqt ki qasam.} Is Surah ka sabab-e-nuzool yeh hai ke ek baar chand din tak wahi na aayi. Kuffar ne aitraaz karte hue kaha ke Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ ko un ke Rab عزوجل ne chhor diya aur na-pasand farmaya hai. Is par Surah Ad-Duha nazil hui.

Baaz mufassireen ke nazdeek is aayat mein “Duha” se woh waqt muraad hai jab suraj buland hota hai. Allah Ta'ala ne is waqt ki qasam is liye irshad farmayi ke isi waqt Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apne kalaam se musharraf farmaya aur isi waqt jaadugar sajde mein gir gaye. Baaz mufassireen ke nazdeek yahan “Duha” se poora din muraad hai. (Tafseer Baghawi, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 1, 4/465; Madaarik, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 1, s. 1356, mulakhkhas)

### Chaasht ki namaaz ke 3 fazaail

Is aayat mein Chaasht ki namaaz ka zikr hai. Is munasabat se Chaasht ki namaaz ke 3 fazaail mulahiza hon.

1. Hazrat Abu Zar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Aadmi ke har jor ke badle sadaqah hai, aur kul 360 jor hain. Har tasbeeh sadaqah hai, har hamd sadaqah hai, لَا اِلٰـہَ اِلَّا اللّٰہ kehna sadaqah hai, اَللّٰہُ اَکْبَرْ kehna sadaqah hai, achhi baat ka hukm dena sadaqah hai aur buri baat se mana karna sadaqah hai. In sab ki taraf se Chaasht ki do rak'atain kaafi ho jaati hain.” (Muslim, Kitaab Salaat-ul-Musaafireen wa Qasriha, Baab Istihbaab Salaat-ud-Duha, s. 363, hadees: 84 (720))

2. Hazrat Anas bin Maalik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne Chaasht ki baarah rak'atain parhin, Allah Ta'ala us ke liye Jannat mein sone ka mahal bana dega.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Witr, Baab Ma Ja'a fi Salaat-id-Duha, 2/17, hadees: 472)

3. Hazrat Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne Chaasht ki do rak'atain parhin, woh ghaafil logon mein nahin likha jayega. Jo chaar rak'at parhega, woh ibaadat guzaar logon mein likha jayega. Jo chhe rak'at parhega, us din Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se us ki kifayat ki jayegi. Jo aath rak'at parhega, Allah Ta'ala use farmabardaar logon mein likhega. Jo baarah rak'at parhega, Allah Ta'ala us ke liye Jannat mein ek mahal banayega. Koi din ya raat aisa nahin jis mein Allah Ta'ala bandon par ehsaan aur sadaqah na farmaye, aur us bande se barh kar kisi par ehsaan nahin kiya gaya jise Allah Ta'ala ne apna zikr ilham farmaya.” (At-Targheeb wat-Tarheeb, Kitaab-un-Nawaafil, At-Targheeb fi Salaat-id-Duha, 1/318, hadees: 1011)

### Chaasht ki namaaz ke 2 shar'i masail

1. Chaasht ki namaaz mustahab hai. Is ki kam az kam do aur zyada se zyada baarah rak'atain hain.

2. Is ka waqt suraj buland hone se zawal, yani shar'i dopahar, tak hota hai. Behtar yeh hai ke din ka chautha hissa guzarne par parhi jaye. (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa chahaarum, 1/675–676)

{وَ الَّیْلِ اِذَا سَجٰى: Aur raat ki qasam jab woh dhaanp de.} Yani us raat ki qasam jo apni tareeki se har cheez ko dhaanp de. Imam Ja'far Saadiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Duha se woh Chaasht muraad hai jis mein Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se kalaam farmaya aur raat se Meraj ki raat muraad hai. Baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke Duha se Mustafa ﷺ ke husn ke noor aur raat se Aap ﷺ ke ambarin zulfain muraad hain. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 2, 10/453)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain:

Hai kalaam-e-Ilaahi mein Shams-o-Duha tere chehra-e-noor-fiza ki qasam
Qasam-e-shab-e-taar mein raaz yeh tha ke Habeeb ki zulf-e-dota ki qasam

## Ayat 93:3

`Source: tafseerId=54426; ayatId=6174; surah=93; ayat=3; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَ مَا قَلٰىﭤ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tumhare Rab ne na tumhein chhora aur na na-pasand kiya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَ مَا قَلٰى: Tumhare Rab ne na tumhein chhora aur na na-pasand kiya.} Kuffar ke aitraaz ka jawab dete hue A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain ke Haqq Jalla wa Ala ne farmaya: “Din chadhne ki qasam aur raat ki qasam jab woh tareeki dhaanp de; ya Ae Mehboob! Tumhare roshan chehre aur tumhari un zulfon ki qasam jo chamakte hue rukhsaron par bikhar aayen: na tumhein tumhare Rab ne chhora aur na tumhein dushman banaya.” Yeh badbakht log apne dil mein achhi tarah jaante hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne tum par kaisi rehmat farmayi hai. Isi rehmat ko dekh kar jalte hain, hasad aur dushmani ki wajah se yeh toofan uthate hain aur apne jale hue dil ke phaphole phorte hain.

Lekin unhein yeh khabar nahin ke “Aur beshak aakhirat tumhare liye pehli se behtar hai.” Yaqeenan aakhirat tumhare liye duniya se behtar hai. Wahan tumhein woh ne'matein milengi jinhein na aankhon ne dekha, na kaanon ne suna aur na kisi insaan ya farishte ke khayal mein aayi. Un ka khulasa yeh hai: “Aur beshak qareeb hai ke tumhara Rab tumhein itna dega ke tum raazi ho jaoge.” Us din dost aur dushman sab par khul jayega ke tumhare barabar koi Mehboob na tha.

Bahrahaal, agar aaj yeh andhe log aakhirat par yaqeen nahin rakhte to Ae Pyare Habeeb! Allah Ta'ala ki tum par azeem, jaleel, kaseer aur be-panah ne'matein aur rahmatein sirf aaj ki nahin, balke qadim se hain. Kya unhone tumhare pehle haal nahin dekhe aur is se yaqeen nahin kiya ke tum par Allah Ta'ala ki nazar-e-inaayat kabhi badalne wali nahin? “Kya Us ne tumhein yateem na paaya phir jagah di, aur tumhein apni muhabbat mein gum paaya to apni taraf raah di, aur tumhein haajatmand paaya to ghani kar diya. To yateem par dabao na daalo, mangte ko na jhidko aur apne Rab ki ne'mat ka khoob charcha karo.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/165–166, mulakhkhas)

Kuffar ke is aitraaz se maloom hua ke woh Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ko Allah Ta'ala ka Rasool jaante the, kyunke agar unhein yeh maloom na hota to aisa aitraaz na karte. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke Quran-e-Majeed Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ka apni taraf se banaya hua kalaam nahin, balke Allah Ta'ala ka kalaam hai jo Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ par nazil hua. Agar Quran-e-Majeed Aap ﷺ ka apna kalaam hota to Aap ka kalaam musalsal jaari rehta aur waqfa na aata, aur kuffar ko yeh aitraaz karne ka mauqa na milta ke Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ ko un ke Rab ne chhor diya aur na-pasand kiya hai.

## Ayat 93:4

`Source: tafseerId=54427; ayatId=6175; surah=93; ayat=4; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ لَلْاٰخِرَةُ خَیْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْاُوْلٰىﭤ(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak tumhare liye har pichhli ghari pehli se behtar hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ لَلْاٰخِرَةُ خَیْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْاُوْلٰى: Aur beshak tumhare liye har pichhli ghari pehli se behtar hai.} Mufassireen ne ek ma'na yeh bayan kiya hai ke Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Yaqeenan aakhirat tumhare liye duniya se behtar hai, kyunke wahan Maqaam-e-Mahmood, Hauz-e-Kausar aur woh bhalai hai jis ka wa'dah kiya gaya hai. Aap ﷺ ko tamam Ambiya aur Rusul عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par muqaddam kiya jayega, Aap ﷺ ki Ummat tamam ummaton par gawaah hogi, Aap ﷺ ki shafa'at se mominon ke martabe buland honge aur be-shumaar izzatein aur karaamatein milengi jin ka bayan mumkin nahin.

Baaz mufassireen ne yeh bhi farmaya ke aane wale haalaat aap ﷺ ke liye guzre hue haalaat se behtar aur buland hain. Goya Allah Ta'ala ka wa'dah hai ke Woh roz-ba-roz Aap ﷺ ke darje buland farmayega, izzat par izzat aur mansab par mansab zyada farmayega aur har aane wali ghari mein Aap ﷺ ke martabe taraqqi karte rahenge. (Madaarik, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 4, s. 1356; Tafseer Kabeer, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 4, 11/193, mulakhkhas)

## Ayat 93:5

`Source: tafseerId=54428; ayatId=6176; surah=93; ayat=5; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ لَسَوْفَ یُعْطِیْكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضٰىﭤ(5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak qareeb hai ke tumhara Rab tumhein itna dega ke tum raazi ho jaoge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَلَسَوْفَ یُعْطِیْكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضٰى: Aur beshak qareeb hai ke tumhara Rab tumhein itna dega ke tum raazi ho jaoge.} Yani Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Yaqeenan qareeb hai ke Allah Ta'ala tumhein duniya aur aakhirat mein itna ata farmayega ke tum raazi ho jaoge.

Allah Ta'ala ka apne Habeeb ﷺ se yeh wa'dah un ne'maton ko bhi shaamil hai jo Aap ﷺ ko duniya mein ata hui: kamaal-e-nafs, awwaleen-o-aakhireen ke uloom, deen ki sarbulandi, Aap ﷺ ke ahd-e-mubarak aur Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ke zamane ki fatah, Qayamat tak Musalmanon ko milne wali fatah, Aap ﷺ ki da'wat ka aam hona, Islam ka mashriq-o-maghrib mein phailna, Aap ﷺ ki Ummat ka tamam ummaton se behtar hona aur woh karaamatein aur kamaalaat jin ka ilm sirf Allah Ta'ala ko hai. Yeh wa'dah aakhirat ki izzat aur takreem ko bhi shaamil hai, jahan Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko shafa'at-e-aammah, shafa'at-e-khaassah, Maqaam-e-Mahmood aur doosri azeem ne'matein ata farmayi hain. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 5, 10/455; Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 5, 4/386, mulakhkhas)

### Allah Ta'ala Muhammad ﷺ ki raza chahta hai

Hazrat Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne Quran-e-Majeed mein Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki yeh dua parhi: “رَبِّ اِنَّهُنَّ اَضْلَلْنَ كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ النَّاسِۚ-فَمَنْ تَبِعَنِیْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنِّیْۚ-وَ مَنْ عَصَانِیْ فَاِنَّكَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ” (Ibrahim: 36). Is ka tarjuma hai: “Ae mere Rab! Beshak buton ne bahut se logon ko gumrah kar diya, to jo mera pairwi kare beshak woh mera hai aur jo meri na-farmani kare to beshak Tu bakhshne wala, meherban hai.”

Phir Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki yeh dua parhi: “اِنْ تُعَذِّبْهُمْ فَاِنَّهُمْ عِبَادُكَۚ-وَ اِنْ تَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ فَاِنَّكَ اَنْتَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ” (Ma'idah: 118). Is ka tarjuma hai: “Agar Tu unhein azaab de to woh Tere bande hain aur agar Tu unhein bakhsh de to beshak Tu hi ghalib, hikmat wala hai.”

Phir Aap ﷺ ne dono dast-e-mubarak utha kar Ummat ke liye ro kar dua ki aur arz ki: “اَللّٰھُمَّ اُمَّتِیْ اُمَّتِیْ” yani “Ae Allah! Meri Ummat, meri Ummat.” Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ko hukm diya ke Mere Habeeb ﷺ ke paas jao. Tumhara Rab khoob jaanta hai, lekin un se poochho ke un ke rone ka sabab kya hai. Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne ja kar poocha. Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne poora haal bataya aur gham-e-Ummat ka izhaar kiya. Hazrat Jibreel Ameen عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne baargaah-e-Ilaahi mein arz ki: “Ae Allah عزوجل! Tere Habeeb ﷺ yeh farmate hain, aur Allah Ta'ala khoob jaanta hai.” Allah Ta'ala ne hukm diya: “Jao aur Mere Habeeb ﷺ se kaho ke Hum jald tumhein tumhari Ummat ke baare mein raazi kar denge aur tumhare qalb-e-mubarak ko ranjida nahin hone denge.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab Dua-in-Nabi li-Ummatihi, s. 130, hadees: 346 (202))

Abu-ul-Barakaat Abdullah bin Ahmad Nasafi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain ke jab yeh aayat nazil hui to Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jab tak meri Ummat ka ek fard bhi Dozakh mein rahega, main raazi nahin hounga.” (Madaarik, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 5, s. 1356)

Mufti Na'eem-ud-Deen Muraadabadi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Yeh aayat saaf batati hai ke Allah Ta'ala wahi karega jis mein Rasool ﷺ raazi honge. Ahadees-e-Shafa'at se sabit hai ke Rasool ﷺ ki raza is mein hai ke Ummat ke sab gunahgaar bakhsh diye jayen. Is liye aayat aur ahadees se yaqini taur par natija nikalta hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ki shafa'at maqbool hogi aur Aap ﷺ ki raza ke mutabiq Ummat ke gunahgaar bakhsh diye jayenge. SubhanAllah! Kya buland martabah hai ke jis Allah عزوجل ko raazi karne ke liye tamam muqarrabeen mushkilaat aur mehnatein uthate hain, Woh is Habeeb-e-Akram ﷺ ko raazi karne ke liye aam ata farmata hai.” (Khazain-ul-Irfan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 5, s. 1109)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain:

Khuda ki raza chahte hain do aalam
Khuda chahta hai raza-e-Muhammad

## Ayat 93:6

`Source: tafseerId=54429; ayatId=6177; surah=93; ayat=6; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ یَجِدْكَ یَتِیْمًا فَاٰوٰى۪    (6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya Us ne tumhein yateem na paaya phir jagah di.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اَلَمْ یَجِدْكَ یَتِیْمًا فَاٰوٰى: Kya Us ne tumhein yateem na paaya phir jagah di.} Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ abhi apni walidah ke shikam mein the aur hamal do mahine ka tha ke Aap ﷺ ke walid-e-mohtaram ne Madinah Munawwarah mein wisal farmaya. Na maal chhora na ghar. Un ke baad Aap ﷺ ki kafaalat ki zimmedari dada Abdul-Muttalib ne sambhali. Jab Aap ﷺ chaar ya chhe saal ke hue to walidah-e-mohtaramah ka bhi wisal ho gaya. Jab Aap ﷺ aath saal ke hue to dada Abdul-Muttalib bhi wisal farma gaye. Unhone wisal se pehle apne farzand Abu Talib ko, jo Aap ﷺ ke haqeeqi chacha the, Aap ﷺ ki khidmat aur nigraani ki wasiyyat ki. Abu Talib Aap ﷺ ki khidmat mein lage rahe, yahan tak ke Aap ﷺ ne Allah Ta'ala ke hukm se nubuwwat ka elaan farmaya.

Mufassireen ne is aayat ka ek ma'na yeh bhi bayan kiya hai ke yateem ka matlab yakta aur be-misaal bhi hota hai, jaise “Durr-e-Yateem” kaha jaata hai. Is surat mein aayat ka ma'na yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko izzat aur sharafat mein yakta aur be-misaal paaya, Aap ﷺ ko qurb ke maqam mein jagah di, dushmanon ke darmiyan apni hifazat mein parwarish farmayi aur nubuwwat, risaalat aur apna chuna hua banda hone ka sharaf ata farmaya. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 6, 4/386; Jaml, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 6, 8/347; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 6, 10/456–457, mulakhkhas)

## Ayat 93:7

`Source: tafseerId=54430; ayatId=6178; surah=93; ayat=7; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ وَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدٰى۪    (7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Us ne tumhein apni muhabbat mein gum paaya to apni taraf raah di.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ وَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدٰى: Aur Us ne tumhein apni muhabbat mein gum paaya to apni taraf raah di.} Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya: Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko apni muhabbat mein gum paaya, to apni taraf raah di, ghaib ke raaz Aap ﷺ par khol diye, ma-kaana wa ma-yakoon ke uloom ata farmaye aur apni zaat-o-sifaat ki ma'rifat mein sab se buland martabah diya.

Mufassireen ne ek ma'na yeh bhi bayan kiya hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko aisa waruftah paaya ke Aap ﷺ apne nafs aur apne martabon ki khabar bhi na rakhte the, to Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko apni zaat-o-sifaat aur martabon-o-darajaat ki ma'rifat ata farmayi.

Yahan yeh baat yaad rakhein ke Allah Ta'ala ke sab Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام nubuwwat se pehle aur nubuwwat ke baad shirk, kufr aur har gunah se ma'soom hote hain aur hamesha se Allah Ta'ala ki tauheed aur us ki sifaat ki ma'rifat rakhte hain. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 7, 4/386–387; Khazain-ul-Irfan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 7, s. 1109)

## Ayat 93:8

`Source: tafseerId=54431; ayatId=6179; surah=93; ayat=8; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ وَجَدَكَ عَآىٕلًا فَاَغْنٰىﭤ(8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Us ne tumhein haajatmand paaya to ghani kar diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ وَجَدَكَ عَآىٕلًا فَاَغْنٰى: Aur Us ne tumhein haajatmand paaya to ghani kar diya.} Is ki ek tafseer yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko haajatmand paaya to Hazrat Khadijah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا ke maal, phir Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke maal, phir Hazrat Usman Ghani رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke maal aur phir maal-e-ghanimeet ke zariye Aap ﷺ ko ghani kar diya. Doosri tafseer yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko qana'at ki daulat ata farma kar ghani kar diya. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 8, 4/387)

### 3 khush-naseeb hazraat

Is aayat ki pehli tafseer se maloom hua ke Hazrat Khadijah, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq aur Hazrat Usman Ghani رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ nihayat khush-naseeb hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne unhein apne Habeeb ﷺ ki khidmat ka mauqa diya.

### Haqeeqi taur par maaldaar kaun hai?

Doosri tafseer se maloom hua ke haqeeqi maaldaar woh hai jise Allah Ta'ala ne qana'at ki daulat se nawaza ho. Is silsile mein do ahadees mulahiza hon:

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Maaldari zyada maal rakhne se haasil nahin hoti, balke haqeeqi maaldari nafs ka be-niyaaz hona hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, Baab-ul-Ghina Ghina-un-Nafs, 4/233, hadees: 6446)

2. Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwaayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jis ne Islam qabool kiya, use us ki zaroorat ke mutabiq rizq diya gaya aur Allah Ta'ala ne use un cheezon par qana'at karne wala bana diya jo use di gayin, us ne kamyaabi pa li.” (Muslim, Kitaab-uz-Zakaat, Baab fil-Kafaaf wal-Qanaa'ah, s. 524, hadees: 125 (1054))

Allah Ta'ala hamein bhi qana'at ki azeem daulat se maala-maal farmaye. Aameen.

## Ayat 93:9

`Source: tafseerId=54432; ayatId=6180; surah=93; ayat=9; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَاَمَّا الْیَتِیْمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْﭤ(9)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To kisi bhi surat yateem par sakhti na karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {فَاَمَّا الْیَتِیْمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ: To kisi bhi surat yateem par sakhti na karo.} Zamana-e-Jahiliyyat mein Arab yateemon ke maal par qabza kar lete, un par dabao daalte aur un ke huqooq ke maamle mein zulm karte the. Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ se irshad farmaya ke Ae Pyare Habeeb ﷺ! Kisi bhi surat yateem par sakhti na farmana. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 9, 4/387)

### Yateemon ke baare mein Islam ka aizaz

Islam ko yeh aizaz haasil hai ke us ne yateemon ke huqooq wazeh kiye, un ke cheene hue huqooq unhein wapas dilaye aur arse se yateemon par jaari zulm-o-sitam ka khaatma kiya. Yateemon ke baare mein Islam ki kuch achhi taleemaat yeh hain:

1. Yateemon ke maal ke baare mein hukm: Allah Ta'ala ne irshad farmaya: “Aur kam-aqlon ko un ke woh maal na do jise Allah ne tumhare liye guzara banaya hai, balke unhein us maal mein se khilao, pehnao aur un se achhi baat kaho. Yateemon ko nikah ki umar tak aazmate raho. Phir agar un mein samajhdaari dekho to un ke maal unhein de do. Un ke bade hone ke khauf se unhein fazool kharchi aur jaldi mein na kha jao. Jo maaldaar ho woh bachay aur jo haajatmand ho woh munasib taur par kha sakta hai. Jab un ke maal un ke hawale karo to un par gawaah bana lo, aur hisaab lene ke liye Allah kaafi hai.” (An-Nisa: 5–6)

Aur Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya: “Aur yateemon ko un ke maal de do, paak maal ke badle napaak maal na lo aur un ke maal apne maal ke saath mila kar na khao. Beshak yeh bada gunah hai.” (An-Nisa: 2)

Aur farmaya: “Beshak jo log zulm karte hue yateemon ka maal khaate hain, woh apne paiton mein sirf aag bharte hain aur jald bhadakti hui aag mein daakhil honge.” (An-Nisa: 10)

2. Yateemon ke saath sulook ke baare mein hukm: Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Musalmanon ke gharon mein sab se achha ghar woh hai jahan yateem ke saath achha sulook kiya jaata ho, aur sab se bura ghar woh hai jahan yateem ke saath bura bartao kiya jaata ho.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ul-Adab, Baab Haqq-il-Yateem, 4/193, hadees: 3679)

3. Yateem ki kafaalat ki targheeb: Hazrat Sahl bin Sa'd رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne farmaya: “Main aur yateem ko paalne wala, chahe woh yateem us ka apna ho ya kisi aur ka, Jannat mein is tarah honge.” Aap ﷺ ne yeh keh kar shahadat aur darmiyani ungli se ishara kiya aur dono ke darmiyan thoda faasla rakha. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ut-Talaaq, Baab-ul-Li'aan, 3/497, hadees: 5304)

Yateemon ke baare mein Islam ki yeh teen taleemaat yahan mukhtasar bayan ki gayi hain. Yateemon ke mazeed ahkaam jaanne ke liye Surah An-Nisa ki ibtidaai aayaat ki tafseer mulahiza karein.

## Ayat 93:10

`Source: tafseerId=54433; ayatId=6181; surah=93; ayat=10; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ اَمَّا السَّآىٕلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْﭤ(10)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur kisi bhi surat maangne wale ko na jhidko.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ اَمَّا السَّآىٕلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ: Aur kisi bhi surat maangne wale ko na jhidko.} Yani Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Jab Aap ke dar-e-daulat par koi saail aa kar kuch maange to use kisi bhi surat na jhidkein. Balke use kuch de dein ya husn-e-akhlaq aur narmi ke saath na dene ka uzr bayan kar dein. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 10, 4/388; Madaarik, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 10, s. 1357, mulakhkhas)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ isi aayat ki taraf ishara karte hue farmate hain:

Momin hoon mominon pe Rauf-o-Raheem ho
Saail hoon saailon ko khushi laane hi ki hai

Aur farmate hain:

Maangen ge maange jaayen ge munh-maangi paayen ge
Sarkar mein na “La” hai na haajat “agar” ki hai

Maangta ka haath uthte hi Daata ki den thi
Duri-e-qubool-o-arz mein bas haath bhar ki hai

Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ki is shaan ki kuch jhalkein mulahiza hon:

1. Ghaar-e-Saur mein Hazrat Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne arz ki ke mujhe saanp ne kaat liya hai. Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ne apna lu'aab-e-dahan laga kar zehar ka asar door kar diya.

2. Ghazwa-e-Badr ke mauqe par Hazrat Mu'awwaz رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ apna kata hua bazu le kar haazir hue to Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ne apne dahan-e-mubarak ka lu'aab laga kar use jor diya.

3. Ghazwa-e-Uhud mein teer lagne se Hazrat Qatadah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki aankh nikal gayi. Woh apni nikli hui aankh le kar baargaah-e-Risaalat ﷺ mein haazir hue to Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ ne lu'aab-e-dahan laga kar un ki aankh durust kar di.

4. Ghazwa-e-Khaibar ke mauqe par Hazrat Ali Murtaza کَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم ne aankh ki takleef ki shikayat ki. Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne apna lu'aab-e-dahan laga kar un ki beemari door kar di.

5. Isi ghazwe mein Hazrat Salmah bin Akwa' رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ zakhmi pindli le kar haazir hue. Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne apne lu'aab-e-dahan se use durust kar diya.

6. Hazrat Abdullah bin Ateek رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ tooti hui taang le kar baargaah-e-Risaalat ﷺ mein haazir hue. Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne apna dast-e-mubarak pher kar un ki taang durust kar di.

7. Ek mauqe par Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ne paani khatam ho jaane par faryaad ki to Aap ﷺ ki ungliyon se paani ke chashme jaari ho gaye.

Yeh dunyawi ata ki chand misaalein thin. Ab ukhrawi ata ki misaalein suniye:

8. Hazrat Rabee'ah aur Hazrat Ukaashah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne Jannat maangi to Allah Ta'ala ke Habeeb ﷺ ne unhein Jannat ata farma di.

9. Khajoor ke ek tane ne arz ki ke mujhe Jannat mein bo diya jaye, to Sarkar-e-Aali Waqar ﷺ ne use Jannat mein bo diya.

Sirf insaan hi Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ki baargaah mein faryaad nahin karte the, balke jaanwar bhi apni faryaad arz kar ke daad-rasi paate the.

10. Ek oont ne zyada kaam aur kam chaare ki faryaad ki to Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne us ki daad-rasi farma di.

11. Shikaari ki qaid mein maujood ek hirni ne bachon ko doodh pilaane ke liye jaane ki iltija ki to Huzoor-e-Anwar ﷺ ne us ki iltija poori farma di.

Gharz, Tajdaar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ se maang kar apni munh-maangi muraadein paane walon ki itni misaalein hain ke sab ko tafseel se bayan kiya jaye to ek moti kitaab tayyar ho sakti hai. A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ kya khoob farmate hain:

Maalik-e-Kaunain hain go paas kuch rakhte nahin
Do-jahan ki ne'matein hain un ke khaali haath mein

Aur farmate hain:

Maangta ka haath uthte hi Daata ki den thi
Duri-e-qubool-o-arz mein bas haath bhar ki hai

Baaz mufassireen ke nazdeek is aayat mein saail se taalib-e-ilm muraad hai. Is liye us ka ikraam karna chahiye, us ki zaroorat poori karni chahiye aur us ke saath tursh-ru'i aur badkhulqi se pesh nahin aana chahiye. (Khaazin, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 10, 4/388)

## Ayat 93:11

`Source: tafseerId=54434; ayatId=6182; surah=93; ayat=11; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ اَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ۠    (11)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur apne Rab ki ne'mat ka khoob charcha karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ اَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ: Aur apne Rab ki ne'mat ka khoob charcha karo.} Yahan ne'mat se woh ne'matein muraad hain jo Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko ata farmayi aur woh ne'matein bhi muraad hain jin ka Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ se wa'dah farmaya. Ne'maton ka charcha karne ka hukm is liye diya gaya ke ne'mat ka bayan karna shukr-guzaari hai. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Ad-Duha, taht-al-aayat: 11, 10/459)

### Aayat “Aur apne Rab ki ne'mat ka khoob charcha karo” se haasil hone wali maloomat

Is aayat se teen baatein maloom hui:

1. Musalmanon ko apni surat aur seerat Islami rakhni chahiye, kyunke is mein Allah Ta'ala ki azeem ne'mat yani Islam ka izhaar hai.

2. Milad Shareef, Gyarhween Shareef aur buzurgan-e-deen ka Urs manana behtareen aamaal hain, kyunke yeh buzurg Allah Ta'ala ki ne'matein hain aur Milad-o-Urs mein Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ki wiladat aur Allah Ta'ala ke Auliya ka charcha hota hai.

3. Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ki na'at-goi behtareen ibaadat hai, kyunke Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ke mubarak ausaaf hamare liye bhi Allah Ta'ala ki ne'matein hain. Huzoor ﷺ ki shaan buland hone se Ummat ki shaan bhi buland hoti hai. Is liye Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ke ausaaf-o-kamaalaat ka zikr karna Allah Ta'ala ki ne'maton ka charcha karna hai.
