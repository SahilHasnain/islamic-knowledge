# Surah Al-Jumu'ah — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 62:1

`Source: tafseerId=54027; ayatId=5239; surah=62; ayat=1; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** یُسَبِّحُ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوْسِ الْعَزِیْزِ الْحَكِیْمِ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jo kuchh aasmanon mein hai aur jo kuchh zameen mein hai, sab us Allah ki paaki bayan karte hain jo Badshah, nihayat paaki wala, bahut izzat wala, bada hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {یُسَبِّحُ لِلّٰهِ: Allah ki paaki bayan karte hain.} Yaani aasmanon aur zameen mein maujood tamam cheezein Allah Ta'ala ko har nuqs aur aib se paak batati hain. Allah haqeeqi Badshah, nihayat paak, izzat wala aur hikmat wala hai.

### Tasbeeh ki teen qismein

Tasbeeh teen tarah ki hai:

1. **Khilqat ki tasbeeh:** Har cheez ki zaat aur us ki paidaish Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat, hikmat, wahdaniyyat aur har nuqs se paak hone par daleel hai.
2. **Ma'rifat ki tasbeeh:** Allah Ta'ala apne fazl-o-karam se makhlooq mein apni pehchan paida farma de aur woh Allah Ta'ala ki paaki bayan kare.
3. **Zaroori tasbeeh:** Allah Ta'ala har jauhar par apni tasbeeh jaari farmaata hai aur har jauhar ma'rifat ke baghair bhi yeh tasbeeh karta hai. (Madaarik, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 1, s. 1239, khulasa)

## Ayat 62:2

`Source: tafseerId=54028; ayatId=5240; surah=62; ayat=2; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** هُوَ الَّذِیْ بَعَثَ فِی الْاُمِّیّٖنَ رَسُوْلًا مِّنْهُمْ یَتْلُوْا عَلَیْهِمْ اٰیٰتِهٖ وَ یُزَكِّیْهِمْ وَ یُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ وَ الْحِكْمَةَۗ-وَ اِنْ كَانُوْا مِنْ قَبْلُ لَفِیْ ضَلٰلٍ مُّبِیْنٍۙ   (2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Wohi hai jis ne anparhon mein unhi mein se ek Rasool bheja jo un ke saamne Allah ki aayatein tilawat farmaata hai, unhein paak karta hai aur unhein Kitaab aur hikmat ka ilm ata farmaata hai, aur beshak woh is se pehle zaroor khuli gumraahi mein the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {هُوَ الَّذِیْ بَعَثَ فِی الْاُمِّیّٖنَ رَسُوْلًا مِّنْهُمْ: Wohi hai jis ne anparhon mein unhi mein se ek Rasool bheja.} Allah Ta'ala ne anparhon mein unhi ke nasab aur sharafat ko pehchanne wale ek Rasool bheje. Aap ka naam-e-mubarak Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ hai. Aap ﷺ un ke saamne Quran-e-Majeed ki aayatein tilawat farmaate hain, jin mein risalat, halal-o-haram aur haq-o-baatil ka bayan hai. Aap ﷺ unhein baatil aqeedon, bure akhlaaq, daur-e-jaahiliyyat ki buraiyon aur bad-amaliyon se paak karte hain. Aap ﷺ unhein Kitaab aur hikmat, yaani Quran, Sunnat, fiqh, Shari'at ke ahkaam aur tareeqat ke asraar ka ilm ata farmaate hain. Beshak log Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki tashreef-aawari se pehle shirk, baatil aqeedon aur bure aamaal ki wajah se khuli gumraahi mein the aur unhein ek kaamil murshid ki sakht zaroorat thi. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 2, 4/264; Madaarik, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 2, s. 1239, mulaqqatan)

### Nabi-e-Ummi ﷺ ki sifat ki teen wajah

Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ki ek sifat “Nabi-e-Ummi” hai. Is ki bahut si wajah hain, jin mein se teen yeh hain:

1. Aap ﷺ Ummat-e-Ummiyyah ki taraf mab'oos hue. Kitaab-e-Shi'yaa mein hai ke Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: “Main ummiyon mein ek ummi bhejunga aur us par nubuwwat khatm kar dunga.”
2. Aap ﷺ ki ba'sat Umm-ul-Qura, yaani Makkah Mukarramah, mein hui.
3. Huzoor-e-Anwar ﷺ likhte aur kitaab se kuchh padhte nahin the. Yeh aap ﷺ ki fazeelat thi, kyunke ilm poori tarah yaad hone ki wajah se aap ﷺ ko is ki zaroorat na thi. Khat ek zehni hunar hai jo jismani aale se zahir hota hai. Jis zaat ke farman ke neeche Qalam-e-A'la ho, use is likhai ki kya zaroorat? Huzoor-e-Akram ﷺ ka likhna na jaanna aur phir bhi likhai ka maahir hona azeem mu'jiza hai. Aap ﷺ likhne walon ko likhai aur likhne ke tareeqe sikhaate, pesha-war logon ko un ke hunar sikhaate aur duniya-o-Aakhirat ke har kamaal mein Allah Ta'ala ne aap ﷺ ko tamam makhlooq se zyada ilm ata kiya.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رحمۃ اللہ علیہ ne kya khoob kaha:

**Farsh ta arsh sab aaina-e-zamaair haazir**

**Bas qasam khaiye Ummi teri danaai ki**

### Is aayat se milne wali paanch baatein

1. Dil ki paaki Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ki nigah-e-karam se milti hai; imaan aur aamaal bhi paaki ke sabab hain.
2. Quran-o-Hadees itne aasaan nahin ke har shakhs sirf apni aqal se samajh le, warna un ki taleem ke liye Huzoor ﷺ na bheje jaate.
3. Hidayat ke liye Hadees ki bhi zaroorat hai.
4. Quran-e-Majeed ko sirf apni aqal se na samjha jaaye, balki Huzoor-e-Akram ﷺ ki taleem se samjha jaaye, warna aadmi gumraah ho sakta hai.
5. Taajdaar-e-Risalat ﷺ duniya mein kisi ke shagird nahin, kyunke aap ﷺ ki tashreef-aawari ke waqt aam log jaahil the.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رحمۃ اللہ علیہ ne farmaya:

**Aisa Ummi kis liye منت کش استاد ho**

**Kya kifayat us ko اِقْرَاْ رَبُّکَ الْاَکْرَمْ nahin**

## Ayat 62:3

`Source: tafseerId=54029; ayatId=5241; surah=62; ayat=3; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَّ اٰخَرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ لَمَّا یَلْحَقُوْا بِهِمْؕ-وَ هُوَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un se baad wale doosre logon ko bhi yeh Rasool paak karte aur ilm dete hain jo in maujood logon se abhi nahin mile aur wohi bahut izzat wala, bada hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ اٰخَرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ: Aur un se baad wale doosre logon ko.} Is aayat ka ta'alluq pichhli aayat se hai. Is mein un logon ka zikr hai jinhein Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ paak karte aur ilm ata farmaate hain. “Doosre logon” se ya to Ajam ke log muraad hain, ya Qiyamat tak Islam mein daakhil hone wale tamam log. “Pehle logon se na milne” ka matlab yeh hai ke woh un ke zamane mein nahin the, balki un ke baad aaye. (Madaarik, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 3, s. 1239; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/264)

Is baat par Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ ki yeh Hadees daleel hai. Woh farmate hain: Jab Surah Al-Jumu'ah naazil hui to hum Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki baargaah mein haazir the. Jab aap ﷺ ne yeh aayat tilawat farmaai: “وَ اٰخَرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ لَمَّا یَلْحَقُوْا بِهِمْ” to ek shakhs ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Jo log pehle logon se abhi nahin mile, woh kaun hain? Aap ﷺ ne koi jawab na diya. Us ne do ya teen baar poocha. Hum mein Hazrat Salman Farsi رضی اللہ عنہ bhi the. Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne Hazrat Salman رضی اللہ عنہ par haath rakh kar farmaya: “Us Zaat ki qasam jis ke dast-e-qudrat mein meri jaan hai! Agar deen Surayya sitare ke paas bhi ho to Fars ke farzand us tak jaakar deen haasil kar lenge.” (Muslim, Kitaab Fazaail-us-Sahaabah, Baab Fazl-e-Faris, s. 1378, hadees: 230-231 (2546))

Is se maloom hua ke Huzoor ﷺ ka faiz sirf Sahaabah-e-Kiraam رضی اللہ عنہم tak mahdood nahin, balki Qiyamat tak jaari rahega. Log aap ﷺ ki nigah-e-karam se paak hote rahenge aur ilm seekhte rahenge.

{ذٰلِكَ فَضْلُ اللّٰهِ: Yeh Allah ka fazl hai.} Yaani Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ aur aap ﷺ ki ummat ki fazeelat ke baare mein jo bayan hua, yeh Allah Ta'ala ka fazl hai. Woh jis ko chahe yeh fazl ata farmaaye. Allah Ta'ala apni makhlooq par bada fazl farmaane wala hai; us ne hidayat ke liye apne Habeeb Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ ko mab'oos farmaya. (Saawi, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 4, 6/2163; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/265, mulaqqatan)

## Ayat 62:4

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=5242; surah=62; ayat=4; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** ذٰلِكَ فَضْلُ اللّٰهِ یُؤْتِیْهِ مَنْ یَّشَآءُؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ ذُو الْفَضْلِ الْعَظِیْمِ(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Yeh Allah ka fazl hai, woh use jisay chahe de aur Allah bahut bade fazl wala hai.

## Ayat 62:5

`Source: tafseerId=54030; ayatId=5243; surah=62; ayat=5; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** مَثَلُ الَّذِیْنَ حُمِّلُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ ثُمَّ لَمْ یَحْمِلُوْهَا كَمَثَلِ الْحِمَارِ یَحْمِلُ اَسْفَارًاؕ-بِئْسَ مَثَلُ الْقَوْمِ الَّذِیْنَ كَذَّبُوْا بِاٰیٰتِ اللّٰهِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jin par Taurat ka bojh rakha gaya phir unhone us ka bojh na uthaya, un logon ki misaal gadhe ki misaal jaisi hai jo kitaabein uthaye ho. Un logon ki kya hi buri misaal hai jinhon ne Allah ki aayaton ko jhutlaya, aur Allah zaalim logon ko hidayat nahin deta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Pichhli aayaat mein bayan hua ke Huzoor ﷺ ummiyon ki taraf bheje gaye. Yahood ne yeh shubah pesh kiya ke aap ﷺ sirf Arab walon ki taraf mab'oos hue hain, hamari taraf nahin. Allah Ta'ala ne yahan un ki misaal bayan farmaai. Jin par Taurat ke ahkaam par amal karna laazim kiya gaya, phir unhon ne Taurat par amal na karke is zimmedari ka bojh na uthaya, aur us mein Huzoor ﷺ ki na't-o-sifat dekhne ke bawajood aap ﷺ par imaan na laaye, un ki misaal us gadhe jaisi hai jo peeth par kitaabein uthaye aur un se bojh ke siwa koi faida na paaye. Yeh yahood ka haal hai: woh Taurat ke alfaaz yaad karte hain magar us se faida nahin uthate aur us ke mutabiq amal nahin karte. Un ki misaal buri hai, kyunke unhon ne Allah ki aayaton ko jhutlaya. Allah un logon ko hidayat nahin deta jo us ke ilm mein zaalim hain. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 5, 10/539; Khazin, 4/265; Madaarik, s. 1240, mulaqqatan)

### Yahood ko gadhe se tashbeeh dene ki wajah

Gadhe par ghore aur khachar ki nisbat zyada bojh laad diya jaata hai. Doosri wajah yeh hai ke gadhe mein jahalat aur himaqat ka ma'na doosre jaanwaron se zyada paaya jaata hai. Teesri wajah yeh hai ke urf mein bhi doosre jaanwaron ke muqaable mein gadhe ko haqeer samjha jaata hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Jumu'ah, 10/540, mulakhkhasan)

### Quran samajh kar amal na karne walon ki misaal

Allamah Ali bin Muhammad Khazin رحمۃ اللہ علیہ farmate hain ke Taurat par amal na karne wale yahood ki misaal un logon par bhi saadiq aati hai jo Quran-e-Kareem ke ma'ni na samjhein, us par amal na karein aur us se munharif ho jaayein. Is liye har Musalman ko Quran-e-Majeed samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye aur us ke diye hue ahkaam par amal karna chahiye, taake yeh misaal us par saadiq na aaye. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/265)

### Ilm par amal na karne ki paanch wa'eedein

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din sab se sakht azaab paane wala woh aalim hoga jis ke ilm ne use koi faida na diya.” (Mu'jam-e-Sagheer, Baab-ut-Ta, Tahir, s. 182, juz 1)
2. Hazrat Waleed bin Uqbah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jannat wale Jahannam walon ko dekh kar kahenge: Tum Jahannam mein kyun daakhil hue, halanke hum usi ilm ke zariye Jannat mein daakhil hue jo hum ne tum se seekha tha? Woh kahenge: Hum jo kehte the, khud nahin karte the.” (Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, Baab-ul-Alif, Ahmad, 1/41, hadees: 99)
3. Hazrat Abu Darda رضی اللہ عنہ farmate hain ke Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne mujh se farmaya: “Ae Uwaimir! Qiyamat ke din tumhara kya haal hoga jab tum se kaha jaayega: Kya tum ne ilm haasil kiya tha ya jaahil rahe? Agar tum kaho ke ilm haasil kiya tha to poocha jaayega: Apne ilm par kitna amal kiya? Aur agar kaho ke jaahil raha to poocha jaayega: Jaahil rehne mein tumhara uzr kya tha?” (Ibn Asaakir, Harf-ul-Meem, 8793, Abu Muhammad Kalbi, 67/181)
4. Hazrat Huzaifah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Taajdaar-e-Risalat ﷺ ne farmaya: “Us shakhs ke liye halaakat hai jo ilm haasil na kare, aur us ke liye bhi halaakat hai jo ilm haasil kare phir us par amal na kare.” (Kanz-ul-Ummal, Harf-ul-Ain, Kitaab-ul-Ilm, 10/86, hadees: 29036)
5. Hazrat Umar Farooq رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Mujhe tum par har ilm wale munaafiq ka khauf hai jo hikmat wali baat karega magar gunaahon par amal karega.” (Kanz-ul-Ummal, Harf-ul-Ain, Kitaab-ul-Ilm, 10/86, hadees: 29040)

## Ayat 62:6

`Source: tafseerId=54031; ayatId=5244; surah=62; ayat=6; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْۤا اِنْ زَعَمْتُمْ اَنَّكُمْ اَوْلِیَآءُ لِلّٰهِ مِنْ دُوْنِ النَّاسِ فَتَمَنَّوُا الْمَوْتَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِیْنَ(6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmaao: Ae Yahoodiyo! Agar tumhein yeh gumaan hai ke sirf tum Allah ke dost ho, doosre log nahin, to zara marne ki tamanna karo agar tum sachche ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا: Tum farmaao: Ae Yahoodiyo!} Yahood kehte the ke hum Allah Ta'ala ke bete aur us ke pyaare hain, Aakhirat ka ghar khaas hamare liye hai aur Jannat mein sirf yahoodi jaayenge. Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko hukm diya ke un ka jhoot zahir karne ke liye farma dein: Tumhein gumaan hai ke doosre logon ko chhor kar sirf tum Allah ke dost ho. Agar tum is da'we mein sachche ho to maut ki tamanna karo, taake woh tumhein Allah Ta'ala tak pahuncha de, kyunke Allah ke doston ke liye Aakhirat duniya se behtar hai. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Jumu'ah, 9/518; Khazin, 4/265, mulaqqatan)

## Ayat 62:7

`Source: tafseerId=54032; ayatId=5245; surah=62; ayat=7; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ لَا یَتَمَنَّوْنَهٗۤ اَبَدًۢا بِمَا قَدَّمَتْ اَیْدِیْهِمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمٌۢ بِالظّٰلِمِیْنَ(7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur woh kabhi maut ki tamanna nahin karenge un aamaal ke sabab jo un ke haath aage bhej chuke hain, aur Allah zaalim logon ko khoob jaanta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Yahood ne jo kufr kiya aur Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ko jhutlaya, us ki wajah se woh kabhi maut ki tamanna nahin karenge. Yeh Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se ghaib ki khabar thi jo sachchi saabit hui: jin yahood ke baare mein farmaya gaya tha ke woh kabhi maut ki tamanna nahin karenge, unhon ne waqai kabhi aisi tamanna na ki.

### Maut ki tamanna ka shar'i hukm

Ahadees mein maut ki tamanna se mana kiya gaya hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Tum mein se koi maut ki tamanna na kare. Nek shakhs is liye ke shayad mazeed neki kar le aur gunahgaar is liye ke shayad taubah kar le.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Marda, Baab Tamanni-ul-Mareez-il-Maut, 4/13, hadees: 5673)

Aur farmaya: “Tum mein se koi maut ki tamanna na kare aur na us ke aane se pehle us ki dua kare, kyunke jab woh mar jaayega to us ka amal khatm ho jaayega aur Momin ki umar us ki bhalai hi badhaati hai.” (Muslim, Kitaab-uz-Zikr wad-Dua, Baab Karaahat-e-Tamanni-il-Maut li-Durrin Nazala bih, s. 1441, hadees: 13 (2682))

Majboori mein agar maut ki tamanna karni pade to Hazrat Anas رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Koi aayi hui museebat ki wajah se maut ki tamanna na kare. Agar karni hi pade to kahe: Ae Allah! Jab tak mere liye zindagi behtar ho mujhe zinda rakh, aur jab mere liye maut behtar ho to mujhe maut de.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Marda, 4/13, hadees: 5671)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Naeemi رحمۃ اللہ علیہ farmate hain: Yeh Hadees pehli dono Ahadees ki sharh hai. Bimari aur takleef se ghabra kar maut na maange. Jo tareeqa dua ke liye bataya gaya hai, woh bahut pyara hai, kyunke is mein deen-o-duniya ki khair aur shar shamil hai; goya maut ki tamanna bhi kar li aur mana kiye gaye tareeqe se bhi bach gaya. (Miraat-ul-Manaajih, Janaazon ka Baab, 2/421)

Mazeed farmate hain: Maut ki tamanna kabhi achhi aur kabhi buri hoti hai. Huzoor ﷺ ke deedaar ya dunyawi fitnon se bachne ke liye ho to achhi hai, aur dunyawi takleef se ghabra kar ho to buri hai. (Miraat-ul-Manaajih, 2/420)

{وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمٌۢ بِالظّٰلِمِیْنَ: Allah zaalim logon ko khoob jaanta hai.} Yahan zaalim se kaafir muraad hai. Allah Ta'ala kaafiron ko khoob jaanta hai aur unhein sakht saza dega.

## Ayat 62:8

`Source: tafseerId=54033; ayatId=5246; surah=62; ayat=8; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** قُلْ اِنَّ الْمَوْتَ الَّذِیْ تَفِرُّوْنَ مِنْهُ فَاِنَّهٗ مُلٰقِیْكُمْ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّوْنَ اِلٰى عٰلِمِ الْغَیْبِ وَ الشَّهَادَةِ فَیُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُوْنَ۠    (8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmaao: Beshak woh maut jis se tum bhaagte ho, pas woh zaroor tumhein milne wali hai, phir tum us ki taraf phere jaaoge jo har ghaib aur zaahir ka jaanne wala hai, phir woh tumhein tumhare aamaal bata dega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {قُلْ اِنَّ الْمَوْتَ الَّذِیْ تَفِرُّوْنَ مِنْهُ: Tum farmaao: Beshak woh maut jis se tum bhaagte ho.} Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Aap un yahood se farma dein: Apne kufr ke wabaal ki wajah se jis maut se tum bhaagte ho, us se kisi tarah nahin bach sakte. Woh zaroor tumhein aane wali hai aur tumhara bhaagna tumhein koi faida na dega. Marne ke baad tum us Allah ki taraf lautaaye jaaoge jo har ghaib aur zaahir ko jaanta hai; tumhara koi haal us se chhupa nahin. Phir woh tumhein tumhare aamaal bata dega aur un ki saza dega. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Jumu'ah, 10/541; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Jumu'ah, 9/519-520, mulaqqatan)

### Qiyamat ke din aamaal bataye jaane ki teen sooratein

1. Aamaal-naame dikha kar aamaal bataaye jaayenge: **’’وَ وُضِعَ الْكِتٰبُ فَتَرَى الْمُجْرِمِیْنَ مُشْفِقِیْنَ مِمَّا فِیْهِ وَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ یٰوَیْلَتَنَا مَالِ هٰذَا الْكِتٰبِ لَا یُغَادِرُ صَغِیْرَةً وَّ لَا كَبِیْرَةً اِلَّاۤ اَحْصٰىهَاۚ-وَ وَجَدُوْا مَا عَمِلُوْا حَاضِرًاؕ-وَ لَا یَظْلِمُ رَبُّكَ اَحَدًا‘‘ (Kahf: 49)** Yaani aamaal-naama rakha jaayega, mujrim us se darenge, kahenge: Haaye hamari kharaabi! Is ne har chhote aur bade gunah ko gin rakha hai. Woh apne tamam aamaal saamne maujood paayenge aur tumhara Rab kisi par zulm na karega.
2. Insaan ke aaza us ke aamaal ki gawaahi denge: **’’یَوْمَ تَشْهَدُ عَلَیْهِمْ اَلْسِنَتُهُمْ وَ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ اَرْجُلُهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَعْمَلُوْنَ‘‘ (An-Noor: 24)** Yaani jis din un ki zubanein, haath aur paon un ke aamaal ki gawaahi denge. Aur farmaya: **’’اَلْیَوْمَ نَخْتِمُ عَلٰۤى اَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَ تُكَلِّمُنَاۤ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ تَشْهَدُ اَرْجُلُهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَكْسِبُوْنَ‘‘ (Yaaseen: 65)** Yaani aaj hum un ke munhon par mohar laga denge, un ke haath hum se baat karenge aur un ke paon gawaahi denge.
3. Zameen logon ke aamaal bayan karegi: **’’یَوْمَىٕذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَاۙ(۴) بِاَنَّ رَبَّكَ اَوْحٰى لَهَا‘‘ (Zilzaal: 4, 5)** Yaani us din zameen apni khabrein bayan karegi, kyunke tumhare Rab ne use hukm diya hoga.

## Ayat 62:9

`Source: tafseerId=54034; ayatId=5247; surah=62; ayat=9; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا نُوْدِیَ لِلصَّلٰوةِ مِنْ یَّوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا اِلٰى ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِ وَ ذَرُوا الْبَیْعَؕ-ذٰلِكُمْ خَیْرٌ لَّكُمْ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُوْنَ(9)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Jab Jumu'ah ke din namaz ke liye azaan di jaaye to Allah ke zikr ki taraf daudo aur khareed-o-farokht chhor do. Agar tum jaano to yeh tumhare liye behtar hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** Is aayat se Namaz-e-Jumu'ah ke ahkaam bayan kiye ja rahe hain.

1. Yahan azaan se pehli azaan muraad hai, doosri azaan nahin jo khutbe se mili hoti hai. Pehli azaan Hazrat Usman Ghani رضی اللہ عنہ ke zamane mein badhaai gayi, lekin namaz ki tayyari wajib hone aur khareed-o-farokht chhorne ka ta'alluq isi se hai.
2. “Daudne” se bhaagna muraad nahin, balki namaz ki tayyari shuru karna muraad hai. Jumhoor ulama ke nazdeek Allah ke zikr se khutba muraad hai.
3. Is aayat se Namaz-e-Jumu'ah ki farziyyat, khareed-o-farokht aur dunyawi mashaghil ki hurmat, namaz ka ehtimaam wajib hona aur khutbe ka saboot milta hai. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, Al-Jumu'ah, taht-al-aayah: 9, s. 1025, mulakhkhasan)

### Jumu'ah naam rakhne ki wajah

Arabi zubaan mein is din ka naam Aroobah tha. Baad mein Jumu'ah rakha gaya. Sab se pehle Ka'b bin Luway ne is din ko Jumu'ah kaha. Ek qaul yeh hai ke is din namaz ke liye jamaaton ka ijtima hota hai, is liye ise Jumu'ah kaha gaya. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/265)

### Huzoor ﷺ ka pehla Jumu'ah

Seerat bayan karne wale ulama kehte hain ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ Hijrat karke Madinah Tayyibah tashreef laaye to 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal, Peer ke din, chaasht ke waqt Quba ke maqam par thehre. Peer se Jumeraat tak wahan qiyaam farmaaya aur masjid ki bunyaad rakhi. Jumu'ah ke din Madinah Tayyibah jaane ka irada farmaya. Bani Salim bin Auf ki waadi ke darmiyan Jumu'ah ka waqt aa gaya. Logon ne wahan masjid banaai aur Sarwar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne wahan Jumu'ah padhaya aur khutba diya. Yeh pehla Jumu'ah tha jo Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne apne ashaab رضی اللہ عنہم ke saath padha. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/266)

### Roz-e-Jumu'ah ke chaar fazaail

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Taajdaar-e-Risalat ﷺ ne farmaya: “Behtareen din jis par sooraj tuloo hua, Jumu'ah ka din hai. Isi mein Hazrat Adam علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام paida kiye gaye, isi mein Jannat mein daakhil kiye gaye, isi mein Jannat se utarne ka hukm hua aur Qiyamat bhi Jumu'ah ke din qayam hogi.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jumu'ah, Baab Fazl-e-Yaum-il-Jumu'ah, s. 425, hadees: 18 (854))
2. Hazrat Abu Darda رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jumu'ah ke din mujh par durood ki kasrat karo. Yeh din mashhood hai, is mein farishte haazir hote hain aur jo mujh par durood padhta hai woh mere saamne pesh kiya jaata hai.” Unhon ne arz ki: Maut ke baad bhi? Farmaya: “Beshak! Allah Ta'ala ne zameen par Ambiya-e-Kiraam علیہم الصلاۃ والسلام ke jism khaana haraam kar diya hai. Allah ka Nabi zinda hai, use rizq diya jaata hai.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ul-Janaiz, Baab Zikr-e-Wafaatihi wa Dafnihi ﷺ, 2/291, hadees: 1637)
3. Hazrat Abu Lubabah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jumu'ah tamam dinon ka sardaar aur Allah ke nazdeek sab se bada din hai. Yeh Eid-ul-Adha aur Eid-ul-Fitr se bhi bada hai. Is mein paanch khaslatein hain: Allah ne isi mein Adam علیہ الصلاۃ والسلام ko paida kiya, isi mein zameen par utara, isi mein unhein wisal diya, is mein ek ghari hai ke banda haraam cheez na maange to Allah use ata karta hai, aur isi din Qiyamat qayam hogi. Koi muqarrab farishta, aasman-o-zameen, hawa, pahaad aur darya aisa nahin jo Jumu'ah ke din se na darta ho.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab Iqaamat-is-Salaat, Baab Fazl-il-Jumu'ah, 2/8, hadees: 1084)
4. Hazrat Jabir رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo Jumu'ah ke din ya Jumu'ah ki raat wafaat paaye, use azaab-e-qabr se bacha liya jaayega aur Qiyamat ke din is tarah aayega ke us par shuhada ki mohar hogi.” (Hilyat-ul-Auliya, Muhammad bin Munkadir, 3/181, hadees: 3629)

### Jumu'ah ke din dua qabool hone ki ghari

Jumu'ah ke din ek ghari aisi hai jismein Allah Ta'ala khaas taur par dua qabool farmaata hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Is mein ek ghari hai, jo Musalman banda use paaye aur namaz padh raha ho, woh Allah se jo maangega use ata kiya jaayega.” Aap ﷺ ne haath ke ishare se bataya ke woh waqt bahut thoda hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jumu'ah, Baab-us-Saa'ah, 1/321, hadees: 935)

Is waqt ke baare mein bahut riwayatein hain. Do qawi qaul yeh hain: pehla, jab Imam khutbe ke liye baithta hai us se namaz khatm hone tak; doosra, Jumu'ah ke din ki aakhri ghari. (Bahar-e-Shari'at, Hissa 4, Jumu'ah ka bayan, 1/754, mulaqqhasan)

### Namaz-e-Jumu'ah ke do fazaail

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jis ne achhi tarah wuzu kiya, phir Jumu'ah ko aaya, khutba suna aur khamosh raha, us ke is Jumu'ah aur agle Jumu'ah ke darmiyan ke gunah aur mazeed teen din ke gunah bakhsh diye jaayenge. Aur jis ne kankari chhui us ne laghw kiya.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jumu'ah, Baab Fazl-e-Man Istama'a, s. 427, hadees: 27 (857))
2. Hazrat Abu Sa'eed رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs ek din mein paanch kaam karega Allah use Jannati likh dega: mareez ki iyadat kare, janaaze mein haazir ho, roza rakhe, Jumu'ah ko jaaye aur ghulaam azaad kare.” (Al-Ihsaan bi-Tarteeb Sahih Ibn Hibban, Kitaab-us-Salaat, 3/191, hadees: 2760)

### Namaz-e-Jumu'ah chhorne ki wa'eedein

1. Hazrat Abu Hurairah aur Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رضی اللہ عنہما se riwayat hai ke Huzoor ﷺ ne farmaya: “Log Jumu'ah chhorne se baaz aa jaayen, warna Allah un ke dilon par mohar laga dega aur woh ghaafilon mein ho jaayenge.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jumu'ah, Baab Taghleez fi Tark-il-Jumu'ah, s. 430, hadees: 40 (865))
2. Hazrat Usamah رضی اللہ عنہ se riwayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jis ne baghair uzr teen Jumu'ah chhore, woh munaafiqon mein likh diya gaya.” (Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Musnad-uz-Zubair bin Al-Awwaam, 1/170, hadees: 422)

Namaz-e-Jumu'ah ki farziyyat ki mazeed tafseel ke liye Bahar-e-Shari'at, Jild 1, Hissa 4, “Jumu'ah ka bayan” ka mutaala karein.

### Namaz-e-Jumu'ah ki farziyyat ke teen shar'i masail

1. Jumu'ah farz-e-ain hai aur is ki farziyyat Zuhr se zyada mu'akkad hai. Is ka munkir kaafir hai. (Bahar-e-Shari'at, Hissa 4, 1/762)
2. Jumu'ah padhne ke liye chhe shartein hain. Ek bhi na paai jaaye to Jumu'ah ada nahin hota: (1) jagah shehar ya fana-e-shehar ho, (2) Jumu'ah padhane wala Sultan-e-Islam ya us ka muqarrar kardah naib ho, (3) Zuhr ka waqt ho aur usi waqt namaz poori ho, (4) khutba ho, (5) jamaat mein Imam ke ilawa kam az kam teen mard hon, (6) izn-e-aam ho, yaani masjid ka darwaza khula ho aur kisi Musalman ko roka na jaaye.
3. Jumu'ah farz hone ke liye gyarah shartein hain. Ek bhi na ho to Jumu'ah farz nahin, lekin padhne se ada ho jaayega: shehar mein muqeem hona, sehatmand hona, azaad hona, mard hona, aqalmand hona, baaligh hona, aankhon wala hona, chalne par qaadir hona, qaid mein na hona, baadshah ya chor jaise zaalim ka khauf na hona, aur itni baarish, aandhi, ole ya sardi na hona jis se nuqsan ka sahih khauf ho.

{ذٰلِكُمْ خَیْرٌ لَّكُمْ: Yeh tumhare liye behtar hai.} Yahan behtari se lughwi behtari muraad hai: Jumu'ah ki namaz aur khutba dunyawi karobaar se behtar hain. Is ka matlab yeh nahin ke haazri wajib nahin, sirf mustahab hai.

## Ayat 62:10

`Source: tafseerId=54035; ayatId=5248; surah=62; ayat=10; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَاِذَا قُضِیَتِ الصَّلٰوةُ فَانْتَشِرُوْا فِی الْاَرْضِ وَ ابْتَغُوْا مِنْ فَضْلِ اللّٰهِ وَ اذْكُرُوا اللّٰهَ كَثِیْرًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ(10)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir jab namaz poori ho jaaye to zameen mein phail jaao, Allah ka fazl talaash karo aur Allah ko bahut yaad karo, is umeed par ke tum kaamyaab ho jaao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {فَاِذَا قُضِیَتِ الصَّلٰوةُ: Phir jab namaz poori ho jaaye.} Jab namaz poori ho jaaye to tumhare liye jaiz hai ke rozgaar ke kaamon mein mashghool ho jaao, ya ilm haasil karo, mareez ki iyadat karo, janaaze mein shirkat karo, ulama ki ziyaarat karo aur is tarah ke doosre nek kaam karke nekiyan haasil karo. Namaz ke ilawa bhi har haal mein Allah Ta'ala ko yaad karte raho, taake tumhein kaamyaabi mile. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, 4/268; Madaarik, s. 1241, mulaqqatan)

## Ayat 62:11

`Source: tafseerId=54036; ayatId=5249; surah=62; ayat=11; para=28; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا رَاَوْا تِجَارَةً اَوْ لَهْوَاﰳ انْفَضُّوْۤا اِلَیْهَا وَ تَرَكُوْكَ قَآىٕمًاؕ-قُلْ مَا عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ خَیْرٌ مِّنَ اللَّهْوِ وَ مِنَ التِّجَارَةِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ خَیْرُ الرّٰزِقِیْنَ۠    (11)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab unhon ne koi tijarat ya khel dekha to us ki taraf chal diye aur tumhein khara chhor gaye. Tum farmaao: Jo Allah ke paas hai woh khel aur tijarat se behtar hai aur Allah behtareen rozi dene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ اِذَا رَاَوْا تِجَارَةً اَوْ لَهْوًا: Aur jab unhon ne koi tijarat ya khel dekha.} Hazrat Jabir bin Abdullah رضی اللہ عنہما farmate hain: Ek martaba Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ Jumu'ah ke din kharay khutba farma rahe the ke Madinah Tayyibah mein ek tijarati qaafila aa gaya. Elan ke liye dhol bajaya gaya, to Rasoolullah ﷺ ke ashaab us ki taraf chal diye, hatta ke masjid mein barah aadmiyon ke siwa koi na raha. Main, Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq aur Hazrat Umar Farooq رضی اللہ عنہما bhi un barah mein shamil the. Us waqt yeh aayat naazil hui. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jumu'ah, Baab fi Qaulihi Ta'ala: Wa iza ra'au Tijaaratan, s. 429, hadees: 38 (863))

Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai: Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Jab unhon ne tijarat ke aane ka ilm paaya ya khel ka elan suna to us ki taraf chal diye aur aap ﷺ ko khutbe ki haalat mein mimbar par khara chhor gaye. Aap ﷺ un se farma dein: Namaz ka ajr-o-sawab aur Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki khidmat mein haazir rehne ki barkat jo Allah ke paas hai, woh khel aur tijarat se behtar hai. Allah behtareen rozi dene wala hai, is liye tum usi ki taraf aao aur usi se rizq talab karo. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Jumu'ah, 9/528)

Yaad rahe ke us waqt bahut tangi aur mehngai thi. Sahaabah-e-Kiraam رضی اللہ عنہم is khayal se chale gaye the ke kahin ashya khatm na ho jaayen aur woh unhein haasil karne se reh na jaayen. Aise haalaat mein yeh fitri baat thi. Is aayat se pehle is amal se mana bhi nahin kiya gaya tha, is liye hukm naazil hone se pehle aisa karna gunah na tha. Allah Ta'ala ne un ki mazammat nahin ki, balki tarbiyat farmaai ke yeh kaam un ki shaan ke laayiq nahin. Is liye un ke is amal par aitiraaz nahin kiya ja sakta.

Is se yeh bhi saabit hua ke khateeb ko kharay hokar khutba padhna chahiye.
