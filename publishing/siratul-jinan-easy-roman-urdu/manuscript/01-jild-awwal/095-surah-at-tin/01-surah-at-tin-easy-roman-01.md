# Surah At-Tin — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 95:1

`Source: tafseerId=54442; ayatId=6193; surah=95; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ التِّیْنِ وَ الزَّیْتُوْنِۙ   (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Anjeer ki qasam aur zaitoon ki.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَالتِّیْنِ وَالزَّیْتُوْنِ: Anjeer ki qasam aur zaitoon ki.} Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne anjeer aur zaitoon ki qasam irshad farmai, kyunki in cheezon mein aise faide aur munaafe maujood hain jo in ke Khaaliq, Rab Ta'ala ki qudrat par daleel hain. Anjeer nihayat umdah mewa hai. Is mein fazla nahin hota. Yeh bahut jald hazm hone wala, zyada faidemand, qabz door karne wala, masana mein maujood rait aur pathri nikaalne wala, jigar aur tilli mein phanse hue gande mawad ki ganth kholne wala, badan ko mota karne wala aur balgham ko chhaantne wala hai. Zaitoon ek barkat wala darakht hai. Is ka tel roshni ke kaam aata hai aur salan ki tarah khaya bhi jaata hai. Yeh khoobi duniya ke kisi aur tel mein nahin. Is ka darakht khushk pahaadon mein ugta hai, jahan chiknai ka naam-o-nishan nahin hota. Yeh baghair kisi dekh-bhaal ke parwarish paata aur hazaaron baras baaqi rehta hai. (Khazin, Wat-Teen, taht-al-aayat: 1, 4/390; Ruh-ul-Bayan, At-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 1, 10/466-467, multaqatan)

### Anjeer aur zaitoon ke baare mein ahadees

Anjeer ke baare mein Hazrat Abu Zar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Anjeer khao. Agar main kahun ke yeh woh phal hai jo Jannat se naazil hua hai to keh sakta hoon, kyunki Jannat ke phal mein guthli nahin hoti. To ise khao, kyunki yeh bawaseer ko khatam karta aur gathiya ke dard mein faida deta hai.” (Musnad Firdous, Baab-ul-Kaaf, 3/243, hadees: 4716)

Aur zaitoon ke baare mein ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala ne irshad farmaya: “وَ شَجَرَةً تَخْرُ جُ مِنْ طُوْرِ سَیْنَآءَ تَنْۢبُتُ بِالدُّهْنِ وَ صِبْغٍ لِّلْاٰكِلِیْنَ” (Mo'minoon: 20)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur hum ne ek darakht paida kiya jo Toor-e-Seena pahaad se nikalta hai, tel aur khaane walon ke liye salan lekar ugta hai.

Aur Hazrat Mu'az bin Jabal رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Barkat wale darakht zaitoon ki miswaak bahut achhi hai, kyunki yeh munh ko khushbudaar karti aur is ki badboo door karti hai. Yeh meri aur mujh se pehle Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki miswaak hai.” (Mu'jam-ul-Ausat, Baab-ul-Alif, man ismuhu: Ahmad, 1/201, hadees: 678)

{وَطُوْرِ سِیْنِیْنَ: Aur Toor-e-Seena ki.} Toor woh pahaad hai jis par Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apne saath kalaam karne ka sharaf ata farmaya. Seena us jagah ka naam hai jahan yeh pahaad waaqe hai. Is jagah ko Seena is ke khushnuma ya mubarak hone ki wajah se kehte hain. Baaz mufassireen ke nazdeek Toor-e-Seena se muraad khushnuma ya mubarak pahaad hai, aur baaz ke nazdeek har us pahaad ko Toor-e-Seena kehte hain jahan kasrat se phaldaar darakht hon. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, At-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 2, 11/211-212)

Is se maloom hua ke jis jagah aur maqam ko Allah Ta'ala ke maqbool bando ke saath nisbat haasil ho jaaye, woh jagah bhi Allah Ta'ala ki baargah mein azmat wali ho jaati hai.

## Ayat 95:4

`Source: tafseerId=54443; ayatId=6196; surah=95; ayat=4; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْاِنْسَانَ فِیْۤ اَحْسَنِ تَقْوِیْمٍ٘    (4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak yaqeenan hum ne aadmi ko sab se achhi soorat mein paida kiya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْاِنْسَانَ فِیْۤ اَحْسَنِ تَقْوِیْمٍ: Beshak yaqeenan hum ne aadmi ko sab se achhi soorat mein paida kiya.} Allah Ta'ala ne anjeer, zaitoon, Toor-e-Seena aur shahar-e-Makkah ki qasam zikr karke irshad farmaya ke beshak hum ne aadmi ko sab se achhi shakal-o-soorat mein paida kiya. Is ke aaza mein munasibat rakhi. Use jaanwaron ki tarah jhuka hua nahin, balki seedhi qamat wala banaya. Use jaanwaron ki tarah munh se pakad kar nahin, balki apne haath se pakad kar khaane wala banaya. Use ilm, fahm, aql, tameez aur baat karne ki salahiyat se aaraasta kiya. (Khazin, Wat-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 4, 4/391; Madaarik, At-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 4, safha 1360, multaqatan)

### Allah Ta'ala ki ma'rifat haasil karne ka zariya

Insaan agar Allah Ta'ala ki doosri makhlooq ko saamne rakh kar apni paidaish mein ghaur kare to us par roshan din ki tarah wazeh ho jaayega ke Allah Ta'ala ne use husn-e-soori aur husn-e-ma'navi ki kitni azeem ne'matein ata ki hain. Is cheez mein jitna zyada ghaur kiya jaaye, utni hi zyada Allah Ta'ala ki azmat aur qudrat ki ma'rifat haasil hoti jaayegi aur woh is azeem ne'mat ko bahut achhi tarah samajh jaayega.

## Ayat 95:5

`Source: tafseerId=54444; ayatId=6197; surah=95; ayat=5; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَۙ    (5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir use har neechi se neechi haalat ki taraf pher diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَ: Phir use har neechi se neechi haalat ki taraf pher diya.} Is ka ek ma'na yeh hai ke insaan ko sab se achhi soorat par paida karne ke baad use budhaape ki taraf pher diya. Us waqt badan kamzor, aaza nakaara, aql naaqis, peeth jhuki aur baal safed ho jaate hain, jild mein jhurriyan pad jaati hain aur woh apni zarooriyaat poori karne mein majboor ho jaata hai. Doosra ma'na yeh hai ke jab us ne achhi shakal-o-soorat ki shukr-guzaari na ki, Allah Ta'ala ki na-farmaani par jama raha aur imaan na laaya, to us ka anjaam yeh hua ke hum ne Jahannam ke sab se neeche ke darajaat ko us ka thikaana kar diya. (Khazin, Wat-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 5, 4/391; Madaarik, At-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 5, safha 1360-1361, multaqatan)

### Aayat ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَ se haasil hone wali maloomat

Is se teen baatein maloom hui:

1. Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat par kamar-basta hone ke liye budhaape ko muntakhib karna aqalmandi nahin, kyunki budhaape mein ibaadat ke liye aaza mein woh taaqat baaqi nahin rehti jo jawaani mein hoti hai.
2. Allah Ta'ala ne hamein insaani shakal-o-soorat ki jo ne'mat ata ki hai, us ka shukr karte hue hamein us ki na-farmaani se bachna chahiye.
3. Paidaish ke baad taaqat aur quwwat dena aur us ke baad kamzori ki taraf lauta dena is baat ki daleel hai ke jo Zaat is cheez par qaadir hai, woh hamari wafaat ke baad hamein dobara zinda karne par bhi qaadir hai. Isi cheez ko bayan karte hue ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala ne irshad farmaya: “وَ مَنْ نُّعَمِّرْهُ نُنَكِّسْهُ فِی الْخَلْقِؕ-اَفَلَا یَعْقِلُوْنَ” (Ya-Seen: 68)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur jise hum lambi umr dete hain to khalqat-o-banaawat mein hum use ulta pher dete hain, to kya woh samajhte nahin?

Aur irshad farmaya: “یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ فِیْ رَیْبٍ مِّنَ الْبَعْثِ فَاِنَّا خَلَقْنٰكُمْ مِّنْ تُرَابٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ نُّطْفَةٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ عَلَقَةٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ مُّضْغَةٍ مُّخَلَّقَةٍ وَّ غَیْرِ مُخَلَّقَةٍ لِّنُبَیِّنَ لَكُمْؕ-وَ نُقِرُّ فِی الْاَرْحَامِ مَا نَشَآءُ اِلٰۤى اَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى ثُمَّ نُخْرِجُكُمْ طِفْلًا ثُمَّ لِتَبْلُغُوْۤا اَشُدَّكُمْۚ-وَ مِنْكُمْ مَّنْ یُّتَوَفّٰى وَ مِنْكُمْ مَّنْ یُّرَدُّ اِلٰۤى اَرْذَلِ الْعُمُرِ لِكَیْلَا یَعْلَمَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ عِلْمٍ شَیْــٴًـا” (Hajj: 5)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Ae logo! Agar tumhein Qiyamat ke din uthne ke baare mein kuch shak ho to is baat par ghaur kar lo ke hum ne tumhein mitti se paida kiya, phir paani ki ek boond se, phir jame hue khoon se, phir gosht ki booti se jis ki shakal ban chuki hoti hai aur adhoori bhi hoti hai, taake hum tumhare liye apni qudrat zaahir farmaayen. Aur hum maaon ke peton mein jise chahte hain use ek muqarrar muddat tak thehraaye rakhte hain, phir tumhein bachche ki soorat mein nikaalte hain, phir tumhein jawaani tak pahunchate hain. Aur tum mein koi pehle hi wafaat paa jaata hai aur koi sab se nikammi umr ki taraf lauta diya jaata hai taake jaanne ke baad kuch na jaane.

## Ayat 95:6

`Source: tafseerId=54445; ayatId=6198; surah=95; ayat=6; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ فَلَهُمْ اَجْرٌ غَیْرُ مَمْنُوْنٍﭤ(6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Magar woh log jo imaan laaye aur unhone achhe kaam kiye to un ke liye be-intiha sawaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: Magar woh log jo imaan laaye.} Yani jo log imaan laaye aur unhone achhe kaam kiye, un ke liye be-intiha sawaab hai. Agarche budhaape ki kamzori ki wajah se woh jawaani ki tarah zyada ibaadatein ada na kar saken aur un ke amal kam ho jaayen, lekin Allah Ta'ala ke karam se unhein wahi ajr milega jo jawaani aur quwwat ke zamaane mein amal karne se milta tha. Un ke utne hi amal likhe jaayenge jitne jawaani mein likhe jaate the aur Jahannam ke sab se neeche ke darajaat un ka thikaana nahin hoga. (Khazin, Wat-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 6, 4/391; Madaarik, At-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 6, safha 1361, multaqatan)

Isi tarah ka maamla ek hadees-e-paak mein bhi bayan kiya gaya hai. Hazrat Abu Moosa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Musalman banda jab beemaar ho jaaye ya safar mein ho to us ke liye un aamaal ka sawaab likha jaata hai jo woh tandurust aur muqeem hone ki haalat mein kiya karta tha, lekin beemaari ya safar ki wajah se na kar saka.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Koofiyyeen, hadees-e-Abi Moosa Ash'ari, 7/177, hadees: 19774)

### Aayat اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا se haasil hone wali maloomat

Is aayat se do baatein maloom hui:

1. Imaan, aamaal par muqaddam hai aur imaan ke baghair koi neki durust nahin.
2. Lambi umr milna aur aamaal ka nek hona bahut badi ne'mat hai. Hazrat Abu Bakrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “Ek dehaati ne baargah-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ mein arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Logon mein sab se behtar kaun hai? Irshad farmaya: ‘Woh shakhs sab se behtar hai jis ki umr lambi ho aur amal nek hon.’ Us ne arz ki: Logon mein sab se bura kaun hai? Irshad farmaya: ‘Woh shakhs sab se bura hai jis ki umr lambi ho aur amal bure hon.’” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, 22-baab minhu, 4/148, hadees: 2337)

## Ayat 95:7

`Source: tafseerId=54446; ayatId=6199; surah=95; ayat=7; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَمَا یُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّیْنِﭤ(7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To ab kaun si cheez tujhe insaaf ke jhutlaane par aamaada karti hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {فَمَا یُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّیْنِ: To ab kaun si cheez tujhe insaaf ke jhutlaane par aamaada karti hai.} Yani ae kaafir insaan! Kya tu ne apni soorat, apni paidaish ki ibtida, apni jawaani aur budhaape mein ghaur nahin kiya, taake tu yeh kehta ke jo Zaat in cheezon par qaadir hai, woh is baat par bhi qaadir hai ke mujhe wafaat ke baad dobara zinda kare, mujh se mere aamaal ka hisaab le. Is qatai daleel aur roshan hujjat ke baad ab kaun si cheez tujhe insaaf ke din ko jhutlaane par aamaada karti hai? Tu Allah Ta'ala ki yeh qudratein dekhne ke bawajood wafaat ke baad uthaye jaane, Qiyamat ke din hisaab hone aur aamaal ki jaza milne ka inkaar kyun karta hai? (Khazin, Wat-Tin, taht-al-aayat: 7, 4/391, mulakhkhasan)

{اَلَیْسَ اللّٰهُ بِاَحْكَمِ الْحٰكِمِیْنَ: Kya Allah sab haakimon se badh kar haakim nahin?} Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo Surah وَالتِّیْنِ وَالزَّیْتُوْنِ padhte hue اَلَیْسَ اللّٰهُ بِاَحْكَمِ الْحٰكِمِیْنَ padhe, use chahiye ke woh kahe: ‘بَلٰی وَاَنَا عَلٰی ذَالِکَ مِنَ الشَّاہِدِیْن’ yani kyun nahin, yaqeenan hai aur main is baat par gawaahon mein se hoon.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, baab wa min Surati Wat-Tin, 5/230, hadees: 3358)
