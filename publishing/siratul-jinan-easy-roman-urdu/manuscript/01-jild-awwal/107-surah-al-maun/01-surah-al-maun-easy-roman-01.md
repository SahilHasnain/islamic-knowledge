# Surah Al-Ma'un — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 107:1

`Source: tafseerId=54487; ayatId=6304; surah=107; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اَرَءَیْتَ الَّذِیْ یُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّیْنِﭤ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya tum ne us shakhs ko dekha jo deen ko jhutlata hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اَرَءَیْتَ الَّذِیْ یُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّیْنِ: Kya tum ne us shakhs ko dekha jo deen ko jhutlata hai.} Shan-e-Nuzool: Is aayat ke shan-e-nuzool ke baare mein ek qaul yeh hai ke yeh Aas bin Waa'il Sahmi ke baare mein naazil hui. Woh Qiyamat ke din ka inkaar karta tha aur bure kaam bhi karta tha.

Doosra qaul yeh hai ke yeh aayat Waleed bin Mugheerah ke baare mein naazil hui.

Teesra qaul yeh hai ke yeh aayat Abu Jahl ke baare mein naazil hui. Riwayat hai ke Abu Jahl ne ek yateem ki parwarish ki zimmedari li. Ek din woh yateem nange badan us ke paas aaya aur apne maal mein se kuchh maanga, lekin Abu Jahl ne use dhakke de kar nikaal diya. Quraish ke sardaron ne us se kaha: Tum Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ke paas jao, woh tumhari sifarish kar denge. Un logon ka maqsad Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ka mazaaq udaana tha, lekin yateem ko is baat ka ilm na tha.

Chunanche woh yateem Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ki baargaah mein haazir hua aur apni fariyaad pesh ki. Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ kabhi kisi mohtaaj ko khaali haath na lautate the. Aap صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ us yateem ke saath Abu Jahl ke paas gaye. Abu Jahl ne Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ko dekh kar khush-aamdeed kaha aur foran yateem ka maal us ke hawaale kar diya. Qabeela-e-Quraish ke logon ne yeh dekha to use taane diye aur kaha: Tu apne deen se phir gaya hai. Abu Jahl ne jawaab diya: Khuda ki qasam! Main apne deen se nahin phira. Asal baat yeh hai ke main ne un ki daayin aur baayin taraf ek neza dekha tha. Mujhe darr laga ke agar main ne un ki baat na maani to yeh neza mujhe phaad daalega.

Chautha qaul yeh hai ke yeh aayat kisi khaas aadmi ke baare mein naazil nahin hui, balki is mein har woh shakhs shaamil hai jo Qiyamat ke din ka inkaar karta hai. Is ki wajah yeh hai ke insaan nekiyan sawaab ki ragbat aur azaab ke darr ki wajah se karta aur mana ki hui cheezon se rukta hai. Jab woh Qiyamat ka hi munkir ho to nafs ki khwahishon aur lazzaton mein se kuchh bhi nahin chhodega. Is se maloom hua ke Qiyamat ka inkaar kufr aur gunahon ki tamaam qismon ki bunyaad ki tarah hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 1-3, 11/301-303; Khazin, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 1-3, 4/412, mulaqqatan)

Is aayat aur is ke baad wali do aayaat ka khulasa yeh hai: Ae insaan! Kya tum us shakhs ko pehchaante ho jo wazeh daleelain saamne aa jaane ke baad bhi hisaab aur badle ka inkaar karta hai? Agar nahin pehchaante to suno: yeh woh shakhs hai jo apne kufr ki wajah se yateem ko dhakke deta, daantta aur maarta hai. Woh us par zulm karte hue us ka haq aur us ka maal use nahin deta. Apni intehaai kanjoosi, dil ki sakhti aur kaminepan ki wajah se miskeen ko khana nahin deta aur na kisi doosre ko yateem ko khana dene ki targheeb deta hai, kyunke use is kaam par sawaab milne ka yaqeen nahin. Agar woh badle par imaan laata aur wa'eed par yaqeen rakhta to yeh kaam us se kabhi na hote.

## Ayat 107:2

`Source: tafseerId=54488; ayatId=6305; surah=107; ayat=2; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَذٰلِكَ الَّذِیْ یَدُعُّ الْیَتِیْمَۙ    (2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir woh aisa hai jo yateem ko dhakke deta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {فَذٰلِكَ الَّذِیْ یَدُعُّ الْیَتِیْمَ: Phir woh aisa hai jo yateem ko dhakke deta hai.} Yaani deen ko jhutlane wale ka akhlaaqi haal yeh hai ke woh yateem ko dhakke deta hai.

### Yateemon ke saath kaafiron ka sulook aur Islam ki taleemaat

Is aayat mein kaafiron ka yateemon ke saath sulook bayan kiya gaya hai. Is ke muqaable mein yateemon ke baare mein Islam ki taleemaat dekhiye. Allah Ta'ala yateemon ke sarparaston se farmaata hai:

’’وَ اٰتُوا الْیَتٰمٰۤى اَمْوَالَهُمْ وَ لَا تَتَبَدَّلُوا الْخَبِیْثَ بِالطَّیِّبِ ۪- وَ لَا تَاْكُلُوْۤا اَمْوَالَهُمْ اِلٰۤى اَمْوَالِكُمْؕ-اِنَّهٗ كَانَ حُوْبًا كَبِیْرًا‘‘ (النساء:۲)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur yateemon ko un ke maal de do aur paak maal ke badle ganda maal na lo aur un ke maal ko apne maal mein mila kar na kha jao. Beshak yeh bada gunaah hai.

Aur farmaaya:

’’وَ لَا تُؤْتُوا السُّفَهَآءَ اَمْوَالَكُمُ الَّتِیْ جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ قِیٰمًا وَّ ارْزُقُوْهُمْ فِیْهَا وَ اكْسُوْهُمْ وَ قُوْلُوْا لَهُمْ قَوْلًا مَّعْرُوْفًا‘‘ (النساء:۵)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur kam-aqlon ko un ke woh maal na do jise Allah ne tumhare liye guzara-basar ka zariya banaya hai, aur unhein is maal mein se khilao aur pehnao aur un se achhi baat kaho.

Aur farmaaya:

’’وَ لْیَخْشَ الَّذِیْنَ لَوْ تَرَكُوْا مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّیَّةً ضِعٰفًا خَافُوْا عَلَیْهِمْ ۪- فَلْیَتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ وَ لْیَقُوْلُوْا قَوْلًا سَدِیْدًا‘‘ (النساء:۹)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur woh log darein jo agar apne peechhe kamzor aulaad chhorte to un ke baare mein kaise andeshon ka shikaar hote. To unhein chahiye ke Allah se darein aur durust baat kahen.

Aur farmaaya:

’’اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَاْكُلُوْنَ اَمْوَالَ الْیَتٰمٰى ظُلْمًا اِنَّمَا یَاْكُلُوْنَ فِیْ بُطُوْنِهِمْ نَارًا ۚ-وَ سَیَصْلَوْنَ سَعِیْرًا‘‘ (النساء:۱۰)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Beshak woh log jo zulm karte hue yateemon ka maal khaate hain, woh apne pait mein bilkul aag bharte hain aur anqareeb yeh log bhadakti hui aag mein jaayenge.

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: “Musalmanon mein behtareen ghar woh hai jis mein yateem ho aur us ke saath achha sulook kiya jaata ho, aur Musalmanon mein badtareen ghar woh hai jis mein yateem ho aur us ke saath bura sulook kiya jaata ho.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ul-Adab, Baab Haqq-il-Yateem, 4/193, hadees: 3679)

Hazrat Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: “Jo kisi yateem ke sar par Allah Ta'ala ki raza ke liye haath phere to us ke liye har us baal ke badle nekiyan hongi jis par us ka haath phire. Aur jo apne paas rehne wale yateem ladke ya yateem ladki se bhalaai kare to Jannat mein main aur woh in do ungliyon ki tarah honge.” Aur Aap صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne apni do ungliyan milaayin. (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Ansaar, hadees Abi Umamah Al-Baahili, 8/300, hadees: 22347)

Kaafiron ke tareeqe aur Islam ki taleemaat ko saamne rakh kar har shakhs achhi tarah samajh sakta hai ke bachchon ke huqooq ki hifaazat ke liye Islam ne jo iqdamaat kiye aur jo ahkaam diye, un ki misaal kisi aur deen mein nahin mil sakti.

## Ayat 107:3

`Source: tafseerId=54489; ayatId=6306; surah=107; ayat=3; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ لَا یَحُضُّ عَلٰى طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِیْنِﭤ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur miskeen ko khana dene ki targheeb nahin deta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ لَا یَحُضُّ عَلٰى طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِیْنِ: Aur miskeen ko khana dene ki targheeb nahin deta.} Yaani deen ko jhutlane wale ka haal yeh hai ke woh apne ghar walon aur doosre maal-daar logon ko miskeen ko khana dene ki targheeb nahin deta. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 3, 10/522)

### Miskeen ke saath kaafiron ka rawayya aur Islam ki taleemaat

Is aayat mein miskeen ke saath kaafiron ka sulook bayan hua. Ab miskeen ke baare mein Islam ki taleemaat dekhiye. Allah Ta'ala farmaata hai:

’’فَاٰتِ ذَا الْقُرْبٰى حَقَّهٗ وَ الْمِسْكِیْنَ وَ ابْنَ السَّبِیْلِؕ-ذٰلِكَ خَیْرٌ لِّلَّذِیْنَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ وَجْهَ اللّٰهِ٘-وَ اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ‘‘ (الروم:۳۸)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: To rishtedaar ko us ka haq do aur miskeen aur musaafir ko bhi. Yeh un logon ke liye behtar hai jo Allah ki raza chaahte hain aur wohi log kaamyaab hone wale hain.

Aur Allah Ta'ala ne apne nek bandon ka wasf bayan karte hue farmaaya:

’’وَ یُطْعِمُوْنَ الطَّعَامَ عَلٰى حُبِّهٖ مِسْكِیْنًا وَّ یَتِیْمًا وَّ اَسِیْرًا(۸)اِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللّٰهِ لَا نُرِیْدُ مِنْكُمْ جَزَآءً وَّ لَا شُكُوْرًا‘‘ (الدہر:۸،۹)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur woh Allah ki muhabbat mein miskeen aur yateem aur qaidi ko khana khilaate hain. Hum tumhein khaas Allah ki raza ke liye khana khilaate hain. Hum tum se na koi badla chaahte hain aur na shukriya.

Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: “Miskeen log Jannat mein maal-daaron se chaalees saal pehle jaayenge. Ae Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا! Miskeen ko khaali na lautaao, agarche khajoor ka tukra hi ho, use de do. Ae Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا! Miskeenon se muhabbat karo aur unhein apne qareeb rakho taake Allah Ta'ala Qiyamat mein tumhein apne qareeb kar de.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab Ma Ja'a Anna Fuqara-ul-Muhaajireen Yadkhuloon-al-Jannah Qabla Aghniyaa'ihim, 4/157, hadees: 2359)

## Ayat 107:4

`Source: tafseerId=54490; ayatId=6307; surah=107; ayat=4; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَوَیْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّیْنَۙ    (4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To un namaziyon ke liye kharabi hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {فَوَیْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّیْنَ: To un namaziyon ke liye kharabi hai.} Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayat mein farmaaya gaya ke un namaziyon ke liye kharabi hai jo apni namaz se ghaafil hain. Is se muraad munaafiq hain: jab woh tanha hote hain to namaz nahin padhte, kyunke unhein namaz ke farz hone ka aqeedah nahin hota; aur jab logon ke saamne hote hain to namazi ban kar dikhawa karte hain aur unhein dikhane ke liye uthte-baithte hain. Haqeeqat mein yeh log namaz se ghaafil hain. (Madaarik, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayaat: 4-5, s. 1377)

### Namaz se ghaafil rehne walon ka anjaam

Namaz se ghaafil rehne walon ke baare mein ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala farmaata hai:

’’فَخَلَفَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِهِمْ خَلْفٌ اَضَاعُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اتَّبَعُوا الشَّهَوٰتِ فَسَوْفَ یَلْقَوْنَ غَیًّا‘‘ (مریم:۵۹)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: To un ke baad woh na-laaiq log un ki jagah aaye jinhon ne namazon ko zaya kiya aur apni khwahishon ki pairwi ki, to anqareeb woh Jahannam ki khaufnaak waadi Ghayy se ja milenge.

Aur farmaaya:

’’اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ یُخٰدِعُوْنَ اللّٰهَ وَ هُوَ خَادِعُهُمْۚ-وَ اِذَا قَامُوْۤا اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ قَامُوْا كُسَالٰىۙ-یُرَآءُوْنَ النَّاسَ وَ لَا یَذْكُرُوْنَ اللّٰهَ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا٘ۙ(۱۴۲) مُّذَبْذَبِیْنَ بَیْنَ ذٰلِكَ ﳓ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِ وَ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّضْلِلِ اللّٰهُ فَلَنْ تَجِدَ لَهٗ سَبِیْلًا‘‘ (النساء:۱۴۲،۱۴۳)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Beshak munaafiq log apne gumaan mein Allah ko fareb dena chaahte hain aur wahi unhein ghaafil karke maar dega. Aur jab namaz ke liye khade hote hain to bahut sust hokar logon ke saamne riyaakaari karte hue khade hote hain aur Allah ko bahut thoda yaad karte hain. Darmiyaan mein dagmaga rahe hain, na in ki taraf hain na un ki taraf. Aur jise Allah gumraah kare to tum us ke liye koi raasta na paaoge.

Yaad rahe ke namaz se ghaafil hone, yaani kabhi namaz padhne aur kabhi chhod dene se bachna zaroori hai. Yeh khaas munaafiqon ka wasf hai. Namaz mein ghaaflat, yaani namaz ke dauraan doosre kaamon ke baare mein sochne lag jaana ya Shaitaan ke waswason ko qabool kar lena, is se bhi bachne ki koshish karni chahiye, agarche is ki buraai kam hai.

{اَلَّذِیْنَ هُمْ عَنْ صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُوْنَ: Jo apni namaz se ghaafil hain.} Namaz se ghaaflat ki kuchh sooratein yeh hain: pabandi se namaz na padhna, sahi waqt par na padhna, faraaiz aur waajibaat durust tareeqe se ada na karna, shar'i uzr ke baghair ba-jamaat na padhna, namaz ki parwaah na karna, tanhaai mein qaza kar dena aur logon ke saamne padh lena. Yeh sab sooratein wa'eed mein shaamil hain. Shauq se na padhna, samajh-boojh kar ada na karna aur tawajjuh se na padhna bhi namaz se ghaaflat mein shaamil hai, lekin yeh sooratein pichhli aayat mein bayan ki gayi wa'eed mein shaamil nahin.

{اَلَّذِیْنَ هُمْ یُرَآءُوْنَ: Woh jo dikhawa karte hain.} Yaani munaafiq faraaiz Allah Ta'ala ki raza haasil karne ke liye nahin, balki logon ko dikhane ke liye ada karte hain. (Madaarik, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 6, s. 1377)

### Riyaakaari ki ta'reef aur mazammat

Riyaakaari yeh hai ke apne amal ko is niyyat se zaahir kiya jaaye ke log use dekh kar us ki ibaadat-guzaari ki ta'reef karein. (Qurtubi, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 6, 10/154, al-juz' al-ishroon)

Bahut si ahadees mein riyaakaari ki mazammat bayan hui hai. Un mein se do ahadees yahan zikr hain.

(1) Hazrat Abu Sa'eed Khudri رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmaate hain: Hum Maseeh-e-Dajjaal ka zikr kar rahe the ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ tashreef laaye aur farmaaya: “Kya main tumhein aisi cheez na bata doon jis ka mujhe tum par Maseeh-e-Dajjaal se bhi zyada darr hai?” Hum ne arz ki: Haan, Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ. Aap ne farmaaya: “Woh shirk-e-khafi hai. Aadmi namaz ke liye is wajah se use lamba karta hai ke koi doosra shakhs use namaz padhte dekh raha hota hai.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab-ur-Riyaa wa as-Sum'ah, 4/470, hadees: 4204)

(2) Hazrat Abu Sa'eed bin Abi Fazaalah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ne farmaaya: “Jab Allah Ta'ala tamaam pehle aur baad walon ko us din jama farmaayega jis mein koi shak nahin, to ek munaadi nida karega: Jis ne Allah عزوجل ke liye koi kaam kiya aur us mein kisi ko shareek kar liya, woh apne amal ka sawaab usi shareek se talab kare, kyunke Allah Ta'ala shirk se bilkul be-niyaaz hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab Wa Min Soorat-il-Kahf, 5/105, hadees: 3165)

Yaad rahe ke niyyat durust rakhte hue farz ibaadaat elaaniya ada karni chahiye taake log farz ibaadaat chhodne ka ilzaam na lagaayen. Nafl ibaadaat posheeda karni chahiye kyunke un mein ilzaam lagne ka andesha nahin hota.

## Ayat 107:6

## Ayat 107:7

`Source: tafseerId=54492; ayatId=6310; surah=107; ayat=7; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ یَمْنَعُوْنَ الْمَاعُوْنَ۠    (7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur bartne ki mamooli cheezein bhi nahin dete.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ یَمْنَعُوْنَ الْمَاعُوْنَ: Aur bartne ki mamooli cheezein bhi nahin dete.} Pichhli aayaat mein Khaliq ke saath munaafiqon ka maamla bayan hua, aur yahan se makhlooq ke saath un ka rawayya bayan kiya ja raha hai. Agar un se istemaal ki koi mamooli cheez, jaise sui, handi ya pyaala waghera maanga jaaye to woh kanjoosi karte hue use bhi nahin dete. (Jalaalain, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 7, s. 507)

### Gharon mein zaroorat se zyada mamooli cheezein rakhna

Ulama farmaate hain ke aadmi ke liye mustahab hai ke apne ghar mein aisi cheezein apni zaroorat se zyada rakhe jin ki padosiyon ko zaroorat hoti ho, aur unhein aarzi taur par de diya kare. (Khazin, Al-Ma'un, taht-al-aayah: 7, 4/413)

Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا farmaati hain: Main ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ! Kaunsi cheez hai jis ka rokna halaal nahin? Aap ne farmaaya: “Paani, namak aur aag.” Main ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ! Paani ko to hum samajh gaye, lekin namak aur aag ka yeh hukm kyun hai? Aap ne farmaaya: “Ae Humaira رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا! Jis ne kisi ko aag di, us ne goya us aag par pakaya hua poora khana sadaqa kiya. Jis ne kisi ko namak diya, us ne goya woh poora khana sadaqa kiya jis ko us namak ne lazeez banaya. Aur jis ne kisi Musalman ko wahan ek ghont paani pilaaya jahan paani aam milta ho, us ne goya ghulaam azaad kiya. Aur jis ne kisi Musalman ko wahan ek ghont paani pilaaya jahan paani na milta ho, us ne goya use zindagi de di.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ur-Rahoon, Baab Al-Muslimoon Shurakaa fi Sulaas, 3/177, hadees: 2474)
