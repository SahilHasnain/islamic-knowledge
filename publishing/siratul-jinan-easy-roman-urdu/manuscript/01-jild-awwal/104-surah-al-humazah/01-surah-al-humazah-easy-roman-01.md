# Surah Al-Humazah — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 104:1

`Source: tafseerId=54482; ayatId=6283; surah=104; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ ﹰۙ   (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Us ke liye kharabi hai jo logon ke munh par aib nikaale, peeth peechhe buraai kare.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ: Us ke liye kharabi hai jo logon ke munh par aib nikaale, peeth peechhe buraai kare.} Yeh aayaat un kafiron ke baare mein naazil huin jo Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ aur Aap ﷺ ke Sahabah-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ par aitraaz karte aur un buzurgon ki gheebat karte the. Jaise Akhnas bin Shuraiq, Umayyah bin Khalf aur Waleed bin Mugheerah waghera. Aur is aayat mein bayan kiya gaya hukm har gheebat karne wale ke liye aam hai. (Jalalain, Al-Humazah, taht-al-aayah: 1, safha 506; Madaarik, Al-Humazah, taht-al-aayah: 1, safha 1373, multaqatan)

### Gheebat aur aib-joi ki mazammat

Ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا اجْتَنِبُوْا كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ الظَّنِّ٘-اِنَّ بَعْضَ الظَّنِّ اِثْمٌ وَّ لَا تَجَسَّسُوْا وَ لَا یَغْتَبْ بَّعْضُكُمْ بَعْضًاؕ-اَیُحِبُّ اَحَدُكُمْ اَنْ یَّاْكُلَ لَحْمَ اَخِیْهِ مَیْتًا فَكَرِهْتُمُوْهُؕ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ تَوَّابٌ رَّحِیْمٌ‘‘ (Hujuraat: 12)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Bahut zyada gumaan karne se bacho, beshak koi gumaan gunah ho jaata hai aur (posheeda baaton ki) jastaju na karo aur ek doosre ki gheebat na karo. Kya tum mein koi pasand karega ke apne mare hue bhai ka gosht khaaye? To yeh tumhein naapasand hoga aur Allah se daro, beshak Allah bahut taubah qubool karne wala, meherbaan hai.

Aur Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Main Meraj ki raat aisi qaum ke paas se guzra jo apne chehron aur seenon ko taambe ke naakhunon se noch rahe the. Main ne poocha: Ae Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, yeh kaun log hain? Unhone arz ki: Yeh logon ka gosht khaate, yaani gheebat karte, aur un ki izzat kharab karte the.” (Abu Daud, Kitaab-ul-Adab, baab fi-l-gheebah, 4/353, hadees: 4878)

Hazrat Raashid bin Sa'd رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Meraj ki raat main aisi aurton aur mardon ke paas se guzra jo apni chhaatiyon ke saath latak rahe the. Main ne poocha: Ae Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, yeh kaun log hain? Unhone arz ki: Yeh munh par aib lagaane wale aur peeth peechhe buraai karne wale hain. Aur in ke baare mein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ irshad farmata hai: ’’وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ‘‘ Us ke liye kharabi hai jo logon ke munh par aib nikaale, peeth peechhe buraai kare.” (Shu'ab-ul-Imaan, ar-Raabi' wal-Arba'oon min Shu'ab-il-Imaan..., fasl fima warada min-al-akhbaar fit-tashdeed..., 5/309, hadees: 6750)

Allah Ta'ala hamein gheebat aur aib-joi jaise mazmoom aamaal se bachne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye, Aameen.

## Ayat 104:2

`Source: tafseerId=54483; ayatId=6284; surah=104; ayat=2; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْ جَمَعَ مَالًا وَّ عَدَّدَهٗۙ   (2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jis ne maal joda aur use gin gin kar rakha.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اَلَّذِیْ جَمَعَ مَالًا وَّ عَدَّدَهٗ: Jis ne maal joda aur use gin gin kar rakha.} Is se maloom hua ke maal jodna aur gin gin kar rakhna logon ke munh par aib nikaalne aur peeth peechhe buraai karne ke mazmoom ausaaf paida hone ka ek sabab hai. Hamare muashray mein maal-daar logon ki ek tadaad is marz mein buri tarah mubtala hai. Allah Ta'ala unhein hidayat ata farmaaye.

### Maal jama karne aur gin gin kar rakhne ki mazmoom sooratein

Yaad rahe ke maal jama karna aur gin gin kar rakhna chand sooraton mein bura hai: (1) Haraam zaraaye se maal jama kiya jaaye. (2) Jama kiye hue maal se sharai huqooq ada na kiye jaayen. (3) Maal jama karne mein itna mashghool ho jaaye ke Allah Ta'ala ko bhool jaaye. (4) Allah Ta'ala par tawakkul karne ke bajaaye sirf maal ko aafaton ko door karne ka zariya samjha jaaye.

## Ayat 104:3

`Source: tafseerId=54484; ayatId=6285; surah=104; ayat=3; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** یَحْسَبُ اَنَّ مَالَهٗۤ اَخْلَدَهٗۚ    (3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh samajhta hai ke us ka maal use (duniya mein) hamesha rakhega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {یَحْسَبُ اَنَّ مَالَهٗۤ اَخْلَدَهٗ: Woh samajhta hai ke us ka maal use (duniya mein) hamesha rakhega.} Is aayat aur is ke baad wali 6 aayaat ka khulasa yeh hai ke logon ke munh par aib nikaalne, peeth peechhe buraai karne, maal jodne aur gin gin kar rakhne wala yeh samajhta hai ke us ka maal use duniya mein hamesha rakhega aur marne nahin dega. Isi wajah se woh maal ki muhabbat mein mast hota hai aur nek amal ki taraf maail nahin hota. Aisa hargiz nahin hoga. Balki woh zaroor zaroor Jahannam ke chura-chura kar dene wale tabqay mein phenka jaayega. Wahan aag us ki haddiyan aur pasliyan tod daalegi. Aur tumhein kya maaloom ke woh chura-chura kar dene wali kya hai? Woh Allah ki bhadkaai hui aag hai jo kabhi thandi nahin hoti. Us ka wasf yeh hai ke woh jism ke zaahiri hisse ko bhi jalaayegi, jism ke andar bhi pahunchegi aur dilon ko bhi jalaayegi. Beshak unhein aag mein daal kar darwaaze band kar diye jaayenge aur darwaazon ki bandish aatisheen lohe ke sutoon se mazboot kar di jaayegi, taake kabhi darwaaza na khule. Baaz mufassireen ne is ke yeh maani bayan kiye hain ke darwaaze band karke aatisheen sutoon se un ke haath-paaon baandh diye jaayenge. (Khazin, Al-Humazah, taht-al-aayaat: 3-9, 4/406, khulasa)

### Jahannam ki aag doosri aag ki tarah nahin

Surah Humazah ki aayat number 6 se maloom hua ke Jahannam ki aag doosri aag ki tarah nahin hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jahannam ki aag ek hazaar baras bhadkaai gayi, yahan tak ke woh surkh ho gayi. Phir ek hazaar baras bhadkaai gayi, yahan tak ke woh safed ho gayi. Phir ek hazaar baras bhadkaai gayi, yahan tak ke woh siyaah ho gayi. Ab woh siyaah aur andheri hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab Sifat-e-Jahannam, baab 8, 4/266, hadees: 2600)

{اَلَّتِیْ تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى الْاَفْـٕدَةِ: Woh jo dilon par chadh jaayegi.} Dil aisi cheezein hain jin mein halki si garmi bardasht karne ki bhi taaqat nahin hoti. To jab Jahannam ki aag un par chadh jaayegi aur maut bhi nahin aayegi, us waqt kya haal hoga! Aur dilon ko jalaana is liye hai ke woh kufr, baatil aqaaid aur faasid niyyaton ka maqam hain. (Khazin, Al-Humazah, taht-al-aayah: 7, 4/406-407; Madaarik, Al-Humazah, taht-al-aayah: 7, safha 1373, multaqatan)
