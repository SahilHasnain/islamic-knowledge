## Ayat 5:87

`Source: tafseerId=50811; ayatId=761; surah=5; ayat=87; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُحَرِّمُوْا طَیِّبٰتِ مَاۤ اَحَلَّ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ وَ لَا تَعْتَدُوْاؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یُحِبُّ الْمُعْتَدِیْنَ(87)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Un paak cheezon ko haraam na thehrao jo Allah ne tumhare liye halal ki hain aur hadd se na badho. Beshak Allah hadd se badhne walon ko pasand nahin karta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا تُحَرِّمُوْا طَیِّبٰتِ: Paak cheezon ko haraam na thehrao.}** Is aayat ke nuzool ka sabab yeh hai ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki ek jamaat Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ka waaz sun kar ek din Hazrat Usman bin Maz'un رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke ghar jama hui. Unhone aapas mein duniya se kinara karne ka ahad kiya. Unka faisla hua ke woh taat pehnenge, hamesha din mein roza rakhenge, saari raat ibaadat mein guzaarenge, bistar par nahin letenge, gosht aur charbi nahin khaayenge, aurton se alag rahenge aur khushboo nahin lagaayenge. Is par yeh aayat-e-kareema naazil hui aur unhein is irade se rok diya gaya. (Tafseer Qurtubi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 87, 3/156, al-juz-us-saadis)

### Aamaal mein aitidaal ka hukm

Ahadees-e-mubarakah mein is tarah ke bahut se waaqi'aat milte hain jin mein Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne aitidaal ka hukm diya aur ibaadat mein khud ko bahut zyada takleef dene se mana farmaya. Is silsile mein 3 ahadees mulahiza hon:

(1) Umm-ul-Momineen Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا farmati hain: Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ un ke paas tashreef farma the. Us waqt Hazrat Haula bint-e-Tuwayt رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا wahan se guzrin. Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ne Tajdar-e-Risalat ﷺ se arz ki: “Yeh Haula bint-e-Tuwayt hain. Log kehte hain ke yeh raat bhar nahin soti.” Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Raat bhar nahin soti! Itna amal kiya karo jitna aasani se kar sako. Khuda ki qasam! Allah Ta'ala nahin uktaayega, lekin tum ukta jaoge.” (Muslim, Kitaab Salaat-il-Musaafireen wa Qasruha, Baab fazeelat-ul-amal-id-daaim min qiyaam-il-lail wagherah, s. 394, hadees: 220 (785))

(2) Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Rahmat-e-Aalam ﷺ masjid mein tashreef laaye. Masjid ke do sutoon ke darmiyan ek rassi bandhi hui thi. Aap ﷺ ne poocha: “Yeh kya hai?” Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne arz ki: “Yeh Hazrat Zainab رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ki rassi hai. Woh namaz padhti hain aur jab un par thakan ya susti hoti hai to is rassi ko pakad leti hain.” Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Is rassi ko khol do. Tum mein se har shakhs us waqt tak namaz padhe jab tak aasani se padh sake. Jab thakan ya susti ho to baith jaaye.” (Muslim, Kitaab Salaat-il-Musaafireen wa Qasruha, Baab fazeelat-ul-amal-id-daaim min qiyaam-il-lail wagherah, s. 394, hadees: 219 (784))

(3) Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُما farmate hain: “Main hamesha roze rakhta tha aur har raat Quran-e-Majeed ki tilaawat karta tha. Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ke saamne mera zikr hua to Aap ﷺ ne mujhe bulaya aur irshad farmaya: ‘Mujhe khabar mili hai ke tum hamesha roze rakhte ho aur har raat Quran-e-Majeed padhte ho?’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Ji haan, Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Main ne is ibaadat se sirf bhalaai ka irada kiya hai.’ Sarkar-e-Risalat ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Tumhare liye kaafi hai ke mahine mein sirf 3 din roze rakh liya karo.’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Main is se zyada ki taaqat rakhta hoon.’ Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Tumhari biwi ka tum par haq hai, tumhare mehmaan ka tum par haq hai aur tumhare jism ka tum par haq hai. Allah Ta'ala ke Nabi Hazrat Dawood عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ke roze rakho, kyunki woh logon mein sab se zyada ibaadat-guzar the.’ Main ne poocha: ‘Ya Allah Ta'ala ke pyaare Nabi! Hazrat Dawood عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ke roze kis tarah the?’ Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Woh ek din roza rakhte aur ek din iftaar karte the.’ Phir farmaya: ‘Har mahine ek Quran-e-Pak khatm kiya karo.’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Main is se zyada ki taaqat rakhta hoon.’ Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Phir 20 din mein ek Quran-e-Pak khatm kar lo.’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Main is se zyada ki taaqat rakhta hoon.’ Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Phir 10 din mein ek Quran-e-Pak khatm kar lo.’ Main ne arz ki: ‘Main is se zyada ki taaqat rakhta hoon.’ Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: ‘Phir 7 din mein Quran-e-Pak khatm kar lo aur is se zyada apne aap ko mashakkat mein na daalo, kyunki tumhari biwi ka bhi tum par haq hai, tumhare mehmaan ka bhi tum par haq hai aur tumhare jism ka bhi tum par haq hai.’” (Muslim, Kitaab-us-Siyaam, Baab-un-nahi an saum-id-dahr, s. 585, hadees: 182 (1159))

### Halal cheezon ko chhorne ka shar'i hukm

Halal cheezon ko chhorna jaiz hota hai, kyunki unhein karna farz ya wajib nahin hota. Lekin jis tarah haraam samajh kar haraam cheez ko chhoda jaata hai, usi tarah halal cheez ko chhorne ki ijaazat nahin. Kisi halal cheez ke baare mein mubalaghe ke taur par yeh kehna bhi jaiz nahin ke “ham ne ise apne upar haraam kar liya hai.” Sufiya-e-Kiraam رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِم se bahut si cheezein chhorne ke waaqi'aat milte hain. Yeh ilaaj ke taur par hota hai. Jaise beemaar aadmi bahut si ghizaon ko halal samajhne ke bawajood sehat ke liye parhez karta hai aur kai cheezein chhor deta hai, isi tarah Sufiya-e-Kiraam رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِم nafs ke ilaaj ke liye baaz halal cheezein chhor dete hain. Lekin yeh zaroori hai ke halal cheezon ke saath haraam jaisa sulook na kiya jaaye.

### Halal cheez ko haraam thehrane ka aham mas'alah

Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein paak cheezon ko haraam thehrane se mana farmaya gaya. Is se un logon ko bhi ibrat leni chahiye jo Allah Ta'ala ke maqbool bandon ki taraf mansub har cheez par haraam ka fatwa lagane par tule rehte hain aur har cheez mein shirk hi dekhte hain.

## Ayat 5:88

## Ayat 5:89

`Source: tafseerId=50812; ayatId=763; surah=5; ayat=89; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** لَا یُؤَاخِذُكُمُ اللّٰهُ بِاللَّغْوِ فِیْۤ اَیْمَانِكُمْ وَ لٰـكِنْ یُّؤَاخِذُكُمْ بِمَا عَقَّدْتُّمُ الْاَیْمَانَۚ-فَكَفَّارَتُهٗۤ اِطْعَامُ عَشَرَةِ مَسٰكِیْنَ مِنْ اَوْسَطِ مَا تُطْعِمُوْنَ اَهْلِیْكُمْ اَوْ كِسْوَتُهُمْ اَوْ تَحْرِیْرُ رَقَبَةٍؕ-فَمَنْ لَّمْ یَجِدْ فَصِیَامُ ثَلٰثَةِ اَیَّامٍؕ-ذٰلِكَ كَفَّارَةُ اَیْمَانِكُمْ اِذَا حَلَفْتُمْؕ-وَ احْفَظُوْۤا اَیْمَانَكُمْؕ-كَذٰلِكَ یُبَیِّنُ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ اٰیٰتِهٖ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَشْكُرُوْنَ(89)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah tumhein tumhari bekaar qasmon par nahin pakdega, lekin un qasmon par pakdega jinhein tum mazboot kar lo. To aisi qasam ka kaffarah 10 miskeenon ko us ausat darje ka khaana khilana hai jo tum apne ghar walon ko khilate ho, ya unhein kapde dena hai, ya ek ghulaam azaad karna hai. Phir jo yeh na paaye to 3 din ke roze rakhe. Yeh tumhari qasmon ka kaffarah hai jab tum qasam khao aur apni qasmon ki hifazat karo. Allah isi tarah tumhare liye apni aayaat bayan karta hai taake tum shukr ada karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا یُؤَاخِذُكُمُ اللّٰهُ بِاللَّغْوِ فِیْۤ اَیْمَانِكُمْ: Allah tumhein bekaar qasmon par nahin pakdega.}** Pichhli aayat mein bayan hua ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki ek jamaat ne khaane peene ki kuchh halal cheezein aur kuchh libaas apne upar haraam kar liye aur duniya se kinara kar liya. Unhone is par qasmein bhi kha li thin. Jab Allah Ta'ala ne unhein is se mana farmaya to unhone arz ki: “Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Ab ham apni qasmon ka kya karein?” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui, jis mein qasam ke ahkaam bayan kiye gaye. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 89, 4/418-419)

### Qasam ki qismein

Qasam ki 3 qismein hain:

(1) **Yameen-e-laghw**, yani ghalat fehmi ki qasam. Yeh woh qasam hai ke aadmi kisi waaqi'e ko apne khayal mein durust samajh kar qasam kha le, haalaanke haqeeqat mein woh aisa na ho. Aisi qasam par kaffarah nahin.

(2) **Yameen-e-ghamoos**, yani jhooti qasam. Kisi guzishte waaqi'e ke baare mein jaan boojh kar jhooti qasam khaana haraam hai.

(3) **Yameen-e-mun'aqidah**, jo kisi aane wale maamle ko karne ya na karne ke liye khaai jaaye. Kisi durust maamle par khaai hui aisi qasam todna mana hai aur is par kaffarah bhi laazim hai. Qasam ki isi teesri surat par kaffarah laazim hota hai.

### Qasam ka kaffarah

Yahan aayat-e-mubarakah mein qasam ka kaffarah bayan hua hai. Agar koi qasam tode to ek ghulaam azaad kare, ya 10 miskeenon ko do waqt pet bhar kar darmiyaane darje ka khaana khilaaye, ya 10 miskeenon ko kapde pehnaaye. In teenon mein se koi bhi tareeqa ikhtiyar karne ki ijaazat hai. Agar in mein se kisi ki bhi taaqat na ho to lagataar 3 roze rakhna kaffarah hai.

### Qasam ke kaffare ke chand masail

(1) Miskeenon ko khaana khilaane ke bajaaye unhein sadaqah-e-fitr ki miqdaar dena bhi jaiz hai.

(2) Yeh bhi jaiz hai ke ek miskeen ko 10 din tak roz khaana diya ya khilaya jaaye.

(3) Bahut ghatiya qisam ka khaana khilaane ki ijaazat nahin. Khaana darmiyaane darje ka hona chahiye.

(4) Miskeenon ko kapde pehnaaye jaayen to woh bhi darmiyaane darje ke hon. Aise kapde hon jin se badan ka aksar hissa dhak sake aur jinhein darmiyaane darje ke log pehente hon. Suit bahut ghatiya na ho aur kam az kam 3 mahine chal sakta ho.

(5) Roze se kaffarah us waqt ada hota hai jab khaana khilaane, kapda dene aur ghulaam azaad karne ki qudrat na ho.

(6) Roze rakhne ki surat mein zaroori hai ke ye roze lagataar rakhe jaayen.

(7) Qasam ka kaffarah qasam todne se pehle dena durust nahin.

**Mashwarah:** Qasam ke baare mein kuchh guftagu Surah Baqarah ki aayat 224 aur 225 ke tehat tafseer mein guzar chuki hai. Wahan se iska mutaala'a karein. Qasam aur us ke kaffare ki mazeed tafseel ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 9 se “Qasam ka bayan” padhein.

**{وَ احْفَظُوْۤا اَیْمَانَكُمْ: Aur apni qasmon ki hifazat karo.}** Qasmon ki hifazat ka hukm yeh hai ke agar shar'an koi harj na ho to unhein poora karo. Yeh bhi hifazat hai ke qasam khaane ki aadat chhor di jaaye. (Tafseer Baghaawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 89, 2/51) [1]

[1] Qasam ke baare mein maloomaat ke liye risaala “Qasam ke baare mein Madani Phool” (matboo'ah Maktabat-ul-Madinah) ka mutaala'a bhi mufeed hai.

## Ayat 5:90

`Source: tafseerId=51248; ayatId=764; surah=5; ayat=90; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِنَّمَا الْخَمْرُ وَ الْمَیْسِرُ وَ الْاَنْصَابُ وَ الْاَزْلَامُ رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ الشَّیْطٰنِ فَاجْتَنِبُوْهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ(90)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Sharaab aur jua aur but aur qismat jaanne ke teer napaak shaitani kaam hi hain, to in se bachte raho taake tum kaamyaab ho jao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ الشَّیْطٰنِ: Napaak shaitani kaam hain.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein 4 cheezon ki napaaki aur khabaasat, aur un ke shaitani kaam hone ka bayan farmaya gaya. In se bachne ka hukm diya gaya hai. Yeh 4 cheezein hain: (1) sharaab, (2) jua, (3) ansaab yani but, (4) azlaam yani paanse daalna. Ab in chaaron ke baare mein tafseel bayan hoti hai.

### (1) Sharaab

Sadr-ush-Shariah Mufti Amjad Ali A'zami رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Sharaab peena haraam hai aur is ki wajah se bahut se gunah paida hote hain. Is liye agar ise gunaahon aur behayaai ki asal kaha jaaye to durust hai.” (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 9, Sharaab peene ki hadd ka bayan, 2/385)

Hazrat Mu'az رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Sharaab hargiz na piyo, kyunki yeh har badkaari ki asal hai.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Ansaar, hadees Mu'az bin Jabal, 8/249, hadees: 22136)

### Sharaab peene ki wa'eedein

Ahadees mein sharaab peene ki nihayat sakht wa'eedein bayan hui hain. In mein se 3 ahadees yeh hain:

(1) Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne sharaab ke baare mein 10 afraad par la'nat farmayi: (1) sharaab banaane wale par, (2) sharaab banwaane wale par, (3) sharaab peene wale par, (4) sharaab uthaane wale par, (5) jis ke paas sharaab utha kar laaye jaane par, (6) sharaab pilaane wale par, (7) sharaab bechne wale par, (8) sharaab ki qeemat khaane wale par, (9) sharaab khareedne wale par, (10) jis ke liye sharaab khareedi gayi us par. (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Buyoo', Baab-un-nahi an yattakhiz-al-khamra khallan, 3/47, hadees: 1299)

(2) Hazrat Abu Maalik Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Meri ummat ke kuchh log sharaab piyenge aur us ka naam badal kar koi aur rakh lenge. Un ke saron par baaje bajaaye jaayenge aur gaane waliyan gaayengi. Allah Ta'ala unhein zameen mein dhansa dega aur un mein se kuchh logon ko bandar aur suar bana dega.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ul-Fitan, Baab-ul-Uqoobaat, 4/368, hadees: 4020)

(3) Hazrat Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: Meri izzat ki qasam! Jo banda sharaab ka ek ghoont bhi piyega, main use utni hi peep pilaunga. Aur jo banda mere khauf se use chhor dega, main use Hauz-e-Quds se pilaunga.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Ansaar, hadees Abi Umamah Al-Baahili, 8/286, hadees: 22281)

### Sharaab haraam hone ka 10 andaaz mein bayan

Is aayat aur is ke baad wali aayat mein sharaab ke haraam hone ko 10 alag andaaz mein bayan kiya gaya hai:

(1) Sharaab ko jue ke saath milaaya gaya hai.

(2) Sharaab ko buton ke saath milaaya gaya hai.

(3) Sharaab ko napaak qarar diya gaya hai.

(4) Sharaab ko shaitani kaam qarar diya gaya hai.

(5) Sharaab se bachne ka hukm diya gaya hai.

(6) Kaamyaabi ko sharaab se bachne par mauqoof rakha gaya hai.

(7) Sharaab ko dushmani aur bughz ka sabab qarar diya gaya hai.

(8, 9) Sharaab ko Allah ke zikr aur namaz se rokne wali cheez bataya gaya hai.

(10) Sharaab se baaz rehne ka taakeedi hukm diya gaya hai. (Tafseeraat-e-Ahmadiyyah, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, s. 370)

### Sharaab noshi ke nataij

Sharaab noshi ke chand nataij poori duniya mein nazar aa rahe hain. Unhein bayan karne ka maqsad yeh hai ke Musalman ibrat haasil karein aur jo Musalman is buri aadat mein mubtala hain woh baaz aa jaayen.

(1) Sharaab noshi ki wajah se karoron afraad mukhtalif jaanleva aur khatarnaak amraaz ka shikaar ho rahe hain.

(2) Laakhon afraad sharaab noshi ki wajah se halaak ho rahe hain.

(3) Aksar sadak ke haadse sharaab pee kar gaari chalaane ki wajah se ho rahe hain.

(4) Hazaaron afraad sharaabiyon ke haathon be-gunah qatl-o-ghaarat ka nishana ban rahe hain.

(5) Laakhon auratein sharaabi shoharon ke zulm-o-sitam ka nishana banti hain.

(6) Laakhon auratein sharaabi mardon ki taraf se jinsi hamlon ka shikaar ho rahi hain.

(7) Walidain ki sharaab noshi ki wajah se zindagi ki taaqat se khaali aur mukhtalif amraaz mein mubtala bachche paida ho rahe hain.

(8) Laakhon bachche sharaabi walidain ki wajah se yateemi aur aseeri ki zindagi guzaarne par majboor hain.

(9) Sharaabi shakhs ke ghar wale aur ahl-o-ayaal us ki hamdardi, pyaar aur muhabbat se mehroom ho rahe hain.

(10) In nuqsanaat ke ilawa sharaab ke maashi nuqsanaat bhi bahut hain. Agar sharaab ki khareed-o-farokht aur daraamad-o-baraamad se haasil hone wali raqam ka un akhrajaat se muqaabla kiya jaaye jo sharaab ke bure asraat rokne par hote hain, to wazeh ho jaayega ke sharaab se haasil hone wali aamdani un akhrajaat ke muqaable mein kuchh bhi nahin. Sharaab noshi ki wajah se hone wali zehni aur doosri beemariyon ka ilaaj, nashe ki haalat mein driving se hone wale haadse, police ki giraftariyan, sharaabiyon ki aulaad ke liye parwarish-gaahen aur aspataal, sharaab se muta'alliq jurm par adaalaton ki masroofiyat aur sharaabiyon ke liye qaid-khaane, in sab par hone wale akhrajaat sharaab ki aamdani se kahin zyada hain. Is ke ilawa kuchh nuqsanaat aise hain jin ka muqaabla maal-o-daulat se nahin kiya ja sakta, jaise paak nasal ki tabaahi, susti, be-raah-rawi, saqaafat-o-tamaddun ki pasmaandgi, ehsaasaat ki maut, gharon ki tabaahi, aarzooon ki barbaadi aur sahib-e-fikr afraad ki dimaghi salahiyat ka nuqsaan. In nuqsanaat ki talaafi rupay paise se kisi tarah mumkin nahin. Allah Ta'ala Musalmanon ko aqal-e-saleem aur hidaayat ata farmaaye aur sharaab noshi ki aafat se nijaat de.

### (2) Jua

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Jue ka paisa qat'i haraam hai.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 19/646)

### Jue ki mazammat mein 2 ahadees

Ahadees mein jue ki shadeed mazammat bayan hui hai. Jue ke ek khel ke baare mein Hazrat Buraidah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne nard-sheer, yani jue ka ek khel, khela to goya us ne apna haath suar ke gosht aur khoon mein duboya.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ush-Sha'r, Baab tahreem-ul-la'ib bin-nard-sheer, s. 1240, hadees: 10 (2260))

Hazrat Abu Abdur-Rahman Khatmi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo shakhs nard khelta hai, phir namaz padhne ke liye khada hota hai, us ki misaal us shakhs ki tarah hai jo peep aur suar ke khoon se wuzu kar ke namaz padhne khada hota hai.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Ahaadees Rijaal min Ashaab-in-Nabi ﷺ, 9/50, hadees: 23199)

### Jue ke dunyawi nuqsanaat

Deen-e-Islam ne apne maan-ne walon ko har us kaam aur aadat se roka hai jis se un ka maali ya jismani nuqsaan ho aur jo unhein Allah Ta'ala ke zikr se ghaafil kar de. Aisi cheezon mein jua bhi hai. Jua samaaji aman-o-sukoon aur baahmi muhabbat-o-yagaanat ke liye zehr-e-qaatil se bhi zyada nuqsaan-deh hai. Quran-o-Hadees mein mukhtalif andaaz se Musalmanon ko is shaitani amal se roka gaya hai. Lekin afsos ke aaj Musalmanon ka ek hissa is khabees amal mein mubtala nazar aata hai. Woh duniya aur aakhirat ke liye mufeed kaam chhor kar apna din-raat isi mein laga rahe hain. Is rawish ka nateeja hai ke na un ki dunyawi pasmaandgi door ho rahi hai aur na woh ukhrawi kaamyaabi ke liye kuchh kar pa rahe hain. Jue ke 3 dunyawi nuqsanaat yeh hain:

(1) Jue ki wajah se juebaazon mein bughz, dushmani aur adaawat paida ho jaati hai aur baaz auqaat baat qatl-o-ghaarat tak pahunch jaati hai.

(2) Juebaazi ki wajah se maaldaar aadmi chand lamhon mein ghurbat aur iflaas ka shikaar ho jaata hai. Khushhaal ghar badhaali ka manzar pesh karne lagte hain. Achha khaasa aadmi khaane peene tak ka mohtaaj ho jaata hai, samaaj mein us ka waqaar khatm ho jaata hai aur us ki qadr, qeemat aur izzat baqi nahin rehti.

(3) Juebaaz nafa ke laalach mein kasrat se qarz lene, kabhi kabhi soodi qarz lene par majboor ho jaata hai. Jab woh qarz ada nahin kar paata ya use qarz nahin milta to daaka-zani aur chori waghera mein mubtala ho jaata hai. Yahan tak ke woh chaaron taraf se museebaton mein is tarah ghir jaata hai ke aakhirkaar khudkushi par majboor ho jaata hai. Allah Ta'ala Musalmanon ko hidaayat de aur is shaitani amal se bachne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye.

### (3) Ansaab

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se marwi hai ke ansaab se woh pathar murad hain jin ke paas kafir apne jaanwar zibah karte the. (Ibn Katheer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, 3/161)

Imam Abdullah bin Ahmad Nasafi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Ansaab se but murad hain, kyunki unhein khada karke un ki pooja ki jaati thi.” (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, s. 302)

Allamah Abu Hayyan Muhammad bin Yusuf Andalusi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Agar ansaab se woh pathar murad hon jin ke paas kafir apne jaanwar zibah ya nahr karte the to un patharon ko napaak is liye kaha gaya ke kamzor iman walon ke dil mein agar un ki koi azmat baqi ho to woh bhi nikal jaaye. Aur agar ansaab se woh but murad hon jin ki Allah Ta'ala ke ilawa ibaadat ki jaati hai, chahe un ke paas jaanwar zibah kiye jaate hon ya nahin, to unhein napaak is liye kaha gaya ke sab par wazeh ho jaaye ke jis tarah asnaam se bachna wajib hai, isi tarah ansaab se bachna bhi wajib hai.” (Al-Bahr-ul-Muheet, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, 4/16)

### (4) Azlaam

Zamana-e-Jahiliyyat mein kafiron ne 3 teer bana rakhe the. Ek par “haan” likha tha, doosre par “nahin” aur teesra khaali tha. Woh log in teeron ki bahut ta'zeem karte the. Yeh teer kaahino ke paas aur Ka'bah Mu'azzamah mein Quraysh ke paas hote the. Jab unhein koi safar ya aham kaam darpesh hota to in teeron se paanse daalte aur jis teer par jo likha hota us ke mutabiq amal karte. Parindon aur wehshi jaanwaron se bad-shagoon lena, kitaabon se faal nikaalna aur is qism ke kaam bhi azlaam mein shamil hain. (Al-Bahr-ul-Muheet, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, 4/16)

### Kaahino aur najmiyon ke paas jaane ki mazammat

Ahadees mein kaahino aur najmiyon ke paas jaane ki shadeed mazammat bayan hui hai. In mein se 3 ahadees yeh hain:

(1) Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo kisi najmi ya kaahin ke paas gaya aur us ki baat ki tasdeeq ki to goya us ne us ka inkaar kar diya jo Muhammad ﷺ par naazil kiya gaya.” (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ul-Iman, At-tashdeed fi ityaan-il-kaahin wa tasdeeqih, 1/153, hadees: 15)

(2) Hazrat Waasilah bin Asqa' رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marwi hai ke Sarwar-e-Aalam ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo kaahin ke paas gaya aur us se kisi cheez ke baare mein poocha, us ki 40 raat tak taubah rok di jaati hai. Aur agar us ne us ki tasdeeq ki to us ne kufr kiya.” (Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Abu Bakr bin Basheer an Waasilah, 22/69, hadees: 169)

(3) Hazrat Qabeesah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Lakeer kheenchna, faal nikaalna aur parinday uda kar bad-shagoon lena jibt, yani shaitani kaamon, mein se hai.” (Abu Dawood, Kitaab-ut-Tibb, Baab fil-khatt wa zajr-it-tair, 4/22, hadees: 3907)

### Aayat **فَاجْتَنِبُوْهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ** se maloom hone wale masail

Is aayat se 2 masail maloom hue:

(1) Sirf nek amal karne se kaamyaabi haasil nahin hoti, balki bure aamaal se bachna bhi zaroori hai. Yeh dono taqwa ke do par hain. Parinda ek par se nahin udta.

(2) Nekiyan karna aur buraaiyon se bachna duniya aur dikhaave ke liye nahin, balki Allah Ta'ala ki raza aur kaamyaabi haasil karne ke liye hona chahiye.

## Ayat 5:92

`Source: tafseerId=50814; ayatId=766; surah=5; ayat=92; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ اَطِیْعُوا اللّٰهَ وَ اَطِیْعُوا الرَّسُوْلَ وَ احْذَرُوْاۚ-فَاِنْ تَوَلَّیْتُمْ فَاعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّمَا عَلٰى رَسُوْلِنَا الْبَلٰغُ الْمُبِیْنُ(92)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Allah ka hukm maano aur Rasool ka hukm maano aur hoshiyar raho. Phir agar tum phir jao to jaan lo ke hamare Rasool par to sirf wazeh taur par tableegh farma dena laazim hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اَطِیْعُوا اللّٰهَ وَ اَطِیْعُوا الرَّسُوْلَ: Aur Allah ka hukm maano aur Rasool ka hukm maano.}** Yani Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne jis kaam ka hukm diya aur jis kaam se mana kiya, us mein un ka hukm maano. Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ahkaam aur mana ki hui baaton mein mukhalafat karne se daro.

Phir agar tum us hukm se munh pher lo jo tumhein diya gaya aur us kaam se bhi baaz na aao jis se mana kiya gaya, to jaan lo ke is se hamare Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka koi nuqsaan nahin. Hamare Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par to sirf wazeh taur par tableegh farma dena laazim hai. Balki tumhara apna nuqsaan hai ke tum apne munh pherne ki wajah se Allah Ta'ala ki naaraazi aur us ke azaab ke mustahiq thehroge. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 92, 1/525; Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 92, s. 302, multaqatan)

## Ayat 5:93

`Source: tafseerId=50815; ayatId=767; surah=5; ayat=93; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** لَیْسَ عَلَى الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ جُنَاحٌ فِیْمَا طَعِمُوْۤا اِذَا مَا اتَّقَوْا وَّ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ ثُمَّ اتَّقَوْا وَّ اٰمَنُوْا ثُمَّ اتَّقَوْا وَّ اَحْسَنُوْاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ یُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِیْنَ۠    (93)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jo iman laaye aur unhone nek amal kiye, un par khaane mein koi gunah nahin jab ke darte rahein, iman rakhein aur achhe amal karein, phir darte rahein aur iman rakhein, phir darte rahein aur nekiyan karein. Aur Allah neki karne walon se muhabbat farmata hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَیْسَ عَلَى الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ جُنَاحٌ: Jo iman laaye aur unhone nek amal kiye, un par koi gunah nahin.}** Yeh aayat-e-mubarakah un Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke baare mein naazil hui jo sharaab haraam hone se pehle duniya se tashreef le ja chuke the. Us waqt sharaab haraam nahin thi, is liye woh use piya karte the. Jab un ke baad sharaab ki hurmat ka hukm naazil hua to Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ko un ki fikr hui ke un se sharaab ke baare mein pooch-gachh hogi ya nahin? (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab “Wa min Suratil-Maidah”, 5/38, hadees: 3062)

Isi tarah jo Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم doosre shehron mein maujood the aur unhein abhi yeh maloom nahin hua tha ke Allah Ta'ala ne sharaab haraam farma di hai, agar woh is be-ilmi ke dauran sharaab pi lete to un ke baare mein kya hukm hai? Un ke haq mein yeh aayat naazil hui aur bataya gaya ke hurmat ka hukm naazil hone se pehle jin nek aur iman walon ne kuchh khaaya ya piya, woh gunahgaar nahin. Isi tarah jin logon ko hurmat ka hukm naazil hone ka ilm nahin, un par hukm ki maloomat hone se pehle sharaab pee lene ki surat mein koi gunah nahin.

Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein lafz “اِتَّقَوْا” aaya hai, jis ke ma'ni darne aur parhez karne ke hain. Yeh lafz teen martabah aaya hai. Pehle lafz se murad shirk se bachna, doosre se tamam haraam kaamon aur gunaahon se bachna, aur teesre se shubhaat ko chhor dena hai. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, s. 302)

Baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke pehle se murad tamam haraam cheezon se bachna, doosre se us par qaaim rehna, aur teesre se wahi naazil hone ke zamaane mein ya us ke baad jin cheezon se mana kiya jaaye unhein chhor dena hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, 1/525; Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, s. 303; Jaml, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, 2/273)

Baaz mufassireen ne yeh ma'ni bayan kiye ke pehle bhi gunaahon se bachte rahe hon, ab bhi bachte rahein aur aainda bhi bachte rahein. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, 4/427)

## Ayat 5:94

`Source: tafseerId=50816; ayatId=768; surah=5; ayat=94; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَیَبْلُوَنَّكُمُ اللّٰهُ بِشَیْءٍ مِّنَ الصَّیْدِ تَنَالُهٗۤ اَیْدِیْكُمْ وَ رِمَاحُكُمْ لِیَعْلَمَ اللّٰهُ مَنْ یَّخَافُهٗ بِالْغَیْبِۚ-فَمَنِ اعْتَدٰى بَعْدَ ذٰلِكَ فَلَهٗ عَذَابٌ اَلِیْمٌ(94)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Zaroor Allah un shikaaron ke zariye jin tak tumhare haath aur neze pahunch sakein, tumhara imtihaan karega taake Allah un logon ki pehchaan kara de jo us se bin dekhe darte hain. Phir is mana ke baad jo hadd se badhe to us ke liye dardnaak azaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَیَبْلُوَنَّكُمُ اللّٰهُ: Zaroor Allah tumhein aazmaayega.}** 6 Hijri mein, jis saal Sulah-e-Hudaybiyah ka waaqi'ah pesh aaya, Musalman ihram ki haalat mein the. Us haalat mein unhein is tarah aazmaaya gaya ke shikaar kiye jaane wale jaanwar aur parinde bahut zyada aa gaye aur un ki sawaariyon par chha gaye. Itni kasrat thi ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke liye unhein hathyaar se shikaar kar lena, balki haath se pakad lena bhi bilkul aasaan tha. Is ke baare mein Allah Ta'ala ne yeh aayat naazil farmaayi. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 94, 1/525)

Lekin Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم Allah Ta'ala ke hukm par mazbooti se qaaim rahe aur ihram ki haalat mein shikaar nahin kiya. Is se Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki azmat bhi zahir hoti hai. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke kisi gunah ke sabab aur mauqe jitni zyada kasrat se maujood hon, un se bachne mein utna hi zyada sawaab hota hai. Jaise kisi naujawan ke liye taqwa, parhezgaari aur paarsaai ka sawaab boodhe ki nisbat zyada hai. Isi tarah jo shakhs bure logon ke darmiyan reh kar bhi nek rahe, woh un logon se behtar hai jo nek logon ke darmiyan nek rahte hain. Hazrat Sayyiduna Yusuf عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Zulaikha ka waaqi'ah bhi is baat ki mazboot daleel hai.

Lekin yaad rahe ke is ka yeh matlab nahin ke koi bure doston ki sohbat mein rahe ya gunah ki jagah ja kar nek banne ki koshish kare taake zyada bada muttaqi ban jaaye. Balkeh jitna mumkin ho, aisi sohbat aur jagah se bachna chahiye. Kahin aisa na ho ke zyada taqwa ki umeed mein asal taqwa hi haath se nikal jaaye.

## Ayat 5:95

`Source: tafseerId=50817; ayatId=769; surah=5; ayat=95; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَقْتُلُوا الصَّیْدَ وَ اَنْتُمْ حُرُمٌؕ-وَ مَنْ قَتَلَهٗ مِنْكُمْ مُّتَعَمِّدًا فَجَزَآءٌ مِّثْلُ مَا قَتَلَ مِنَ النَّعَمِ یَحْكُمُ بِهٖ ذَوَا عَدْلٍ مِّنْكُمْ هَدْیًۢا بٰلِغَ الْكَعْبَةِ اَوْ كَفَّارَةٌ طَعَامُ مَسٰكِیْنَ اَوْ عَدْلُ ذٰلِكَ صِیَامًا لِّیَذُوْقَ وَ بَالَ اَمْرِهٖؕ-عَفَا اللّٰهُ عَمَّا سَلَفَؕ-وَ مَنْ عَادَ فَیَنْتَقِمُ اللّٰهُ مِنْهُؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَزِیْزٌ ذُو انْتِقَامٍ(95)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar ko qatl na karo. Aur tum mein jo use qasdan qatl kare to us ka badla yeh hai ke maweshiyon mein se isi tarah ka woh jaanwar de jis ke shikaar ki misl hone ka tum mein se do mo'tabar aadmi faisla karein. Yeh Ka'bah ko pahunchti hui qurbani ho, ya chand miskeenon ka khaana kaffare mein de, ya us ke barabar roze, taake woh apne kaam ka wabaal chakhe. Allah ne pehle jo kuchh guzra use maaf farma diya aur jo dobara karega to Allah us se inteqaam lega. Aur Allah Ghaalib hai, badla lene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا تَقْتُلُوا الصَّیْدَ وَ اَنْتُمْ حُرُمٌ: Haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar ko qatl na karo.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar karne se mana farmaya gaya hai. Yahan is ke chand masail bayan kiye jaate hain.

### Haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar karne ke shar'i masail

(1) Muhrim, yani ihram wale par shikaar karna haraam hai. Shikaar se murad khushki ka koi wehshi jaanwar hai.

(2) Jaanwar ki taraf shikaar karne ke liye ishaara karna ya kisi tarah us ka pata batana bhi shikaar mein shamil aur mana hai.

(3) Haalat-e-ihram mein har wehshi jaanwar ka shikaar mana hai, chahe woh halaal ho ya haraam.

(4) Kaatne wala kutta, kawwa, bichchhu, cheel, chooha, bheriya aur saanp. In jaanwaron ko ahadees mein فَوَاسِق kaha gaya hai aur inhein maarne ki ijaazat di gayi hai.

(5) Machchhar, pissu, cheenti, makkhi, zameen par rengne wale keede aur hamla karne wale darindon ko maarna maaf hai. (Tafseer-e-Ahmadi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 95, s. 372-377)

(6) Haalat-e-ihram mein jin jaanwaron ko maarna mana hai, unhein har haalat mein maarna mana hai, chahe jaan boojh kar maara ho ya ghalti se. Jaan boojh kar maarne ka hukm is aayat mein hai aur ghalti se maarne ka hukm hadees-e-shareef se saabit hai. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 95, s. 303)

### Haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar ke kaffare ki tafseel

Haalat-e-ihram mein shikaar ke kaffare ki tafseel yeh hai:

(1) Khushki ka wehshi jaanwar shikaar karna, us ki taraf shikaar karne ka ishaara karna ya kisi aur tarah us ka pata batana, yeh sab kaam haraam hain aur sab mein kaffara wajib hai, agarche bhook ki wajah se shikaar ka gosht khaana majboori hi kyun na ho. Kaffara us jaanwar ki qeemat hai. Wahan ke do aadil log jo qeemat bata dein, woh deni hogi. Agar wahan us ki koi qeemat na ho to wahan se qareeb jagah ki qeemat maani jaayegi. Agar sirf ek aadil ne qeemat bata di to bhi kaafi hai.

(2) Shikaar ki qeemat mein ikhtiyaar hai. Agar us qeemat se bher, bakri waghera khareed sakta ho to khareed kar Haram mein zabah kare aur fuqara ko taqseem kar de. Ya us qeemat ka anaaj khareed kar miskeenon ko sadaqah kare. Itna anaaj de ke har miskeen ko sadaqah-e-fitr ki miqdaar pahunch jaaye. Yeh bhi ho sakta hai ke us qeemat ke anaaj mein jitne sadaqe ho sakte hon, har sadaqah ke badle ek roza rakhe.

Agar kuchh anaaj bach jaaye jo ek poore sadaqah ke barabar na ho to ikhtiyaar hai ke woh kisi miskeen ko de de ya us ke badle ek roza rakh le. Agar poori qeemat ek sadaqah ke laayak bhi na ho to bhi ikhtiyaar hai ke itni qeemat ka anaaj khareed kar ek miskeen ko de ya us ke badle ek roza rakhe.

(3) Kaffare ka jaanwar Haram ke baahar zabah kiya to kaffara ada nahin hoga. Aur agar us mein se khud bhi kha liya to jitna khaaya hai us ka taawaan dena hoga. (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa chhahum, Jurm aur un ke kaffare ka bayan, 1/1179-1180)

## Ayat 5:96

`Source: tafseerId=50818; ayatId=770; surah=5; ayat=96; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اُحِلَّ لَكُمْ صَیْدُ الْبَحْرِ وَ طَعَامُهٗ مَتَاعًا لَّكُمْ وَ لِلسَّیَّارَةِۚ-وَ حُرِّمَ عَلَیْكُمْ صَیْدُ الْبَرِّ مَا دُمْتُمْ حُرُمًاؕ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ الَّذِیْۤ اِلَیْهِ تُحْشَرُوْنَ(96)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tumhare aur musafiron ke faide ke liye tumhare liye samandar ka shikaar aur us ka khaana halal kar diya gaya aur jab tak tum ihram ki haalat mein ho tab tak tum par khushki ka shikaar haraam kar diya gaya. Aur Allah se darte raho jis ki taraf tum sab jama kiye jaoge.

### Haraam shikaar ka bayan

Yaad rahe ke do shikaar haraam hain: muhrim ka kiya hua shikaar aur Haram ka shikaar. Haram Shareef mein rehne wale shikaar kiye jaane wale jaanwar ko na ihram wala shakhs shikaar kar sakta hai aur na woh shakhs jo ihram mein na ho. Yeh Allah Ta'ala ki amaan mein hain.

Yahan ihram ke shikaar ki hurmat ka zikr hai, jo ihram khatam hone par khatam ho jaati hai. Lekin Haram ka shikaar hamesha har shakhs ke liye haraam hai, chahe woh ihram mein ho ya ihram se farigh. Balki Haram ke shikaar ko us ki jagah se uthaana bhi mana hai.

## Ayat 5:100

`Source: tafseerId=50822; ayatId=774; surah=5; ayat=100; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ لَّا یَسْتَوِی الْخَبِیْثُ وَ الطَّیِّبُ وَ لَوْ اَعْجَبَكَ كَثْرَةُ الْخَبِیْثِۚ-فَاتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ یٰۤاُولِی الْاَلْبَابِ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ۠    (100)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farma do ke ganda aur paakeeza barabar nahin hain, agarche gande logon ki kasrat tumhein ta'ajjub mein daale. To ae aqal walo! Tum Allah se darte raho taake tum kaamyaab ho jao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ لَّا یَسْتَوِی الْخَبِیْثُ وَ الطَّیِّبُ: Tum farma do ke ganda aur paakeeza barabar nahin hain.}** Is aayat mein bataya gaya ke halal aur haraam, nek aur bad, Musalman aur kaafir, khara aur khota ek darje ke nahin ho sakte. Haraam ki jagah halal, bad ki jagah nek, kaafir ki jagah Musalman aur khote ki jagah khara hi maqbool hota hai.

**{وَ لَوْ اَعْجَبَكَ كَثْرَةُ الْخَبِیْثِ: Agarche gande ki kasrat tumhein ta'ajjub mein daale.}** Is ka matlab yeh hai ke duniya-daar log maal-o-daulat ki kasrat aur duniya ki zeenat ko pasand karte hain. Haalanki Allah Ta'ala ke paas jo nematein hain woh sab se behtar aur zyada baqi rehne wali hain. Duniya ki zeenat aur nematein khatam ho jaayengi, jabke Allah Ta'ala ke paas wali nematein hamesha baqi rahengi. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 100, 1/530)

### Duniya ki mazammat

Is se maloom hua ke duniya ke maal-o-daulat ki chaahat, us ki nematon aur aaraam ki khwahish, us ki ranginiyon se lutf lene ki tamanna aur Aakhirat ki tayyari se ghaafil rehna nihayat mazmoom hai. Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

**’’زُیِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ حُبُّ الشَّهَوٰتِ مِنَ النِّسَآءِ وَ الْبَنِیْنَ وَ الْقَنَاطِیْرِ الْمُقَنْطَرَةِ مِنَ الذَّهَبِ وَ الْفِضَّةِ وَ الْخَیْلِ الْمُسَوَّمَةِ وَ الْاَنْعَامِ وَ الْحَرْثِؕ-ذٰلِكَ مَتَاعُ الْحَیٰوةِ الدُّنْیَاۚ-وَ اللّٰهُ عِنْدَهٗ حُسْنُ الْمَاٰبِ‘‘** (Aal-e-Imran: 14)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Logon ke liye un ki khwahishon ki muhabbat ko aarasta kar diya gaya, yani aurton, beton, sone aur chaandi ke jama kiye hue dheron, nishaan lagaaye hue ghodon, maveshiyon aur khetiyon ko un ke liye aarasta kar diya gaya. Yeh sab dunyawi zindagi ka saamaan hai aur sirf Allah ke paas achha thikaana hai.

Aur Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

**’’وَ مَنْ یُّرِدْ ثَوَابَ الدُّنْیَا نُؤْتِهٖ مِنْهَاۚ-وَ مَنْ یُّرِدْ ثَوَابَ الْاٰخِرَةِ نُؤْتِهٖ مِنْهَاؕ-وَ سَنَجْزِی الشّٰكِرِیْنَ‘‘** (Aal-e-Imran: 145)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur jo shakhs duniya ka inaam chahta hai hum use duniya ka kuchh inaam denge aur jo Aakhirat ka inaam chahta hai hum use Aakhirat ka inaam ata farmaayenge aur jald hi hum shukr ada karne walon ko sila ata karenge.

Hazrat Zaid bin Saabit رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo shakhs hamesha duniya ki fikr mein mubtala rahega aur deen ki parwaah nahin karega, Allah Ta'ala us ke tamam kaam pareshaan kar dega. Us ki muflisi hamesha us ke saamne rahegi aur use duniya utni hi milegi jitni us ki taqdeer mein likhi hui hai. Aur jis ki niyyat Aakhirat ki taraf hogi, Allah Ta'ala us ki dil-jami ke liye us ke tamam kaam durust farma dega, us ke dil mein duniya se be-raghbati daal dega aur duniya us ke paas khud aayegi.” (Ibn Maajah, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab-ul-hamm bid-dunya, 4/424, hadees: 4105)

Umm-ul-Momineen Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Duniya us ka ghar hai jis ka Aakhirat mein koi ghar nahin, aur yeh us ka maal hai jis ka koi doosra maal nahin. Duniya ke liye wohi shakhs jama karta hai jis ke paas aqal nahin.” (Shu'ab-ul-Iman, Al-Haadi wal-Sab'oona min Shu'ab-il-Iman, Fasl fima balaghana an-is-Sahaabah, 7/375, hadees: 10638)

Hazrat Abu Musa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne apni duniya se muhabbat ki us ne apni Aakhirat ko nuqsaan pahunchaya, aur jis ne apni Aakhirat se muhabbat ki us ne apni duniya ko nuqsaan pahunchaya. Pas tum fana hone wali duniya par baqi rehne wali Aakhirat ko tarjeeh do.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ul-Koofiyyeen, Hadees Abi Musa Al-Ash'ari, 7/165, hadees: 19717)

Allah Ta'ala tamam Musalmanon ko apni dunyawi behtari ke saath apni ukhrawi tayyari ki taraf bhi tawajjuh karne aur is ke liye bharpoor koshish karne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye. Aameen.

## Ayat 5:101

`Source: tafseerId=50823; ayatId=775; surah=5; ayat=101; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَسْــٴَـلُوْا عَنْ اَشْیَآءَ اِنْ تُبْدَ لَكُمْ تَسُؤْكُمْۚ-وَ اِنْ تَسْــٴَـلُوْا عَنْهَا حِیْنَ یُنَزَّلُ الْقُرْاٰنُ تُبْدَ لَكُمْؕ-عَفَا اللّٰهُ عَنْهَاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرٌ حَلِیْمٌ(101)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Aisi baatein na poochho jo tum par zaahir ki jaayen to tumhein buri lagen aur agar tum unhein us waqt poochhoge jab Quran naazil kiya ja raha hai to tum par woh cheezen zaahir kar di jaayengi aur Allah unhein maaf kar chuka hai aur Allah bakhshne wala, hilm wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا تَسْــٴَـلُوْا عَنْ اَشْیَآءَ اِنْ تُبْدَ لَكُمْ تَسُؤْكُمْ: Aisi baatein na poochho jo tum par zaahir ki jaayen to tumhein buri lagen.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah ka shan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke ek din Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ khutbah dete hue farma rahe the: “Jis ko jo poochhna ho, poochhe.” Hazrat Abdullah bin Huzafah Sahmi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ khade hue aur poochha: “Mera baap kaun hai?” Irshad farmaya: “Huzafah.” Phir farmaya: “Aur poochho.” Is par Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ khade hue, iman aur risalat ka iqraar kiya aur maazrat pesh ki. (Bukhari, Kitaab Mawaqeet-is-Salaah, Baab Waqt-uz-Zuhr ind-az-Zawaal, 1/200, hadees: 540)

Imam Ibn Shahab Zuhri رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki riwayat hai ke Hazrat Abdullah bin Huzafah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki walidah ne un se shikayat ki aur kaha: “Tum bahut na-laaiq bete ho. Tumhein kya maloom ke zamana-e-Jahiliyyat ki aurton ka kya haal tha? Khuda na khwasta agar tumhari maa se koi qasoor ho gaya hota to aaj woh kis qadar ruswa hoti!” Is par Hazrat Abdullah bin Huzafah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Agar Sarkar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ kisi Habshi ghulam ko mera baap bata dete to main yaqeen ke saath maan leta.” (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 101, 2/57)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se marwi Bukhari Shareef ki hadees mein hai ke log mazaaq ke taur par is qisam ke sawaal kiya karte the. Koi kehta: “Mera baap kaun hai?” Koi poochhta: “Meri oontni gum ho gayi hai, woh kahan hai?” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab La Tas'aloo an Ashyaa'in in Tubda Lakum Tasoo'kum, 3/218, hadees: 4622)

Muslim Shareef ki hadees mein hai ke Sarkar-e-Aali Waqaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne khutbah mein Hajj farz hone ka bayaan farmaya. Ek shakhs ne kaha: “Kya har saal farz hai?” Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَسَلَّمَ khamosh rahe. Sawaal karne wale ne phir poochha to irshad farmaya: “Jis baat ko main bayaan na karun, us ke peeche na pado. Agar main haan keh deta to har saal Hajj karna farz ho jaata aur tum na kar sakte.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Hajj, Baab Fard-ul-Hajj Marratan fil-Umr, ص 698, hadees: 412 (1337))

### Is aayat aur riwayaat se milne wali aham baatein

Is aayat aur is ki tafseer mein zikr ki gayi riwayaat se chaar aham baatein maloom hoti hain:

1. **Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka ilm-e-ghaib:** In riwayaat se maloom hua ke Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ghaib ka ilm rakhte hain, kyunki kisi ka haqeeqi baap kaun hai, is ka ta'alluq ghaib se hai. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko kulli ilm ata farmaya gaya. Warna Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ yeh na farmate ke jo chaho poochho, balki farmate ke falan falan shobay ke baare mein poochho ya sirf shariat ke muta'alliq sawaal karo. Sarwar-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka baghair kisi qaid ke farmana ke jo poochhna hai poochho, aur poochhne walon ka har qisam ki baat pooch lena, is baat ki daleel hai ke Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ sab kuch jaante hain. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ka bhi yahi aqeedah tha.

2. **Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ikhtiyaraat:** Aakhri riwayat se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ne Sarkar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko ikhtiyar diya hai ke Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ jis cheez ko farz farma dein, woh farz ho jaati hai.

3. **Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki ummat par shafqat:** Aayat se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ apni ummat par nihayat shafeeq hain. Agar Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ek martaba haan farma dete to har saal Hajj farz ho jaata, lekin Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne ummat par aasani farmaayi aur haan nahin farmayi.

**Note:** Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ilm-e-ghaib ke muta'alliq Fatawa Razawiyyah ki 29veen jild mein maujood A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki yeh kitaabein padhna nihayat mufeed hai: (1) **Khaalis-ul-I'tiqaad**: ilm-e-ghaib se muta'alliq 120 dalaail par mushtamil azeem kitaab. (2) **Anbaa-ul-Mustafa bi Haali Sirrin wa Akhfa**: Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko ma kaana wa ma yakoon ka ilm diye jaane ka saboot. (3) **Izaahat-ul-Aib bi Saif-il-Ghaib**: ilm-e-ghaib ke mas'ale se muta'alliq dalaail aur badmazhabon ka radd. Aur Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke kainaat aur shariat dono ke muta'alliq ikhtiyaraat jaanne ke liye Fatawa Razawiyyah ki 30veen jild mein maujood A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki azeem tasneef **Al-Amn wal-Ula li Na'ati-il-Mustafa bi Daafi-il-Bala** ka muta'ala karein. Is mein Mustafa Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko Daafi-ul-Bala, yani balaayein door karne wala kehne walon ke liye inaamaat bayan kiye gaye hain.

4. **Halal aur haraam ka aham usool:** Is aayat se yeh bhi sabit hua ke jis kaam ki shariat mein mana na aayi ho, woh mubah aur jaaiz hai. Hazrat Salman رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marwi hadees mein hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Halal woh hai jise Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne apni kitaab mein halal farmaya, haraam woh hai jise Us ne apni kitaab mein haraam farmaya, aur jis ke baare mein khamoshi farmayi woh maaf hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Libaas, Baab Ma Jaa'a fi Labs-il-Furaa, 3/280, hadees: 1732)

## Ayat 5:102

`Source: tafseerId=50824; ayatId=776; surah=5; ayat=102; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قَدْ سَاَلَهَا قَوْمٌ مِّنْ قَبْلِكُمْ ثُمَّ اَصْبَحُوْا بِهَا كٰفِرِیْنَ(102)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak tum se pehle ek qaum ne in ashya ke baare mein sawaal kiya tha, phir us ka inkaar karne wale ho gaye.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قَدْ سَاَلَهَا قَوْمٌ مِّنْ قَبْلِكُمْ: Beshak tum se pehle ek qaum ne in ashya ke baare mein sawaal kiya tha.}** Musalmanon ko ek hukm dene ke baad pehli ummaton ke waqiaat se samjhaya gaya ke tum se pehle ki qaumon ne bhi apne Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se be-zaroorat sawaalaat kiye. Jab Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne ahkaam bayaan farma diye to woh un par amal na kar sake. Is liye tum sawaalaat karne se bacho, kyunki agar tumhare har sawaal ka jawaab de diya gaya to mumkin hai ke kisi sawaal ka jawaab tumhein bura lage.

### Be-zaroorat sawaalaat ki mazammat

Ahadees mein be-zaroorat sawaalaat karne ki mazammat bayan hui hai. Is silsile mein teen ahadees hain:

1. Hazrat Sa'd bin Abi Waqqaas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Musalmanon mein sab se bada mujrim woh hai jis ne aisi cheez ke baare mein sawaal kiya jo haraam nahin ki gayi thi, lekin us ke sawaal karne ki wajah se haraam kar di gayi.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-I'tisaam bil-Kitaab was-Sunnah, Baab Ma Yukrah min Kasrat-is-Su'aal, 4/502, hadees: 7289)

2. Hazrat Abu Tha'labah Khushani رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ne kuchh hudood muqarrar ki hain, un se aage na badho. Kuchh faraiz laazim farmaye hain, unhein zaya na karo. Kuchh cheezein haraam ki hain, un ki hurmat na todo. Aur tum par rahmat farmate hue kuchh cheezon se baghair bhool ke khamoshi farmayi hai, to un ke baare mein behas na karo.” (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ul-At'imah, Shaan-e-Nuzool Ma Ahallallahu fahuwa Halaal, 5/157, hadees: 7196)

3. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Main tumhein jis kaam se rok doon, us se bacho aur jis kaam ka tumhein hukm doon, use apni taaqat ke mutabiq karo. Tum se pehle log zyada sawaalaat karne aur apne Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se ikhtilaaf karne ki wajah se halaak ho gaye.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Hajj, Baab Fard-ul-Hajj Marratan fil-Umr, ص 698, hadees: 412 (1337))

## Ayat 5:103

`Source: tafseerId=50825; ayatId=777; surah=5; ayat=103; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** مَا جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ مِنْۢ بَحِیْرَةٍ وَّ لَا سَآىٕبَةٍ وَّ لَا وَصِیْلَةٍ وَّ لَا حَامٍۙ-وَّ لٰكِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا یَفْتَرُوْنَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ الْكَذِبَؕ-وَ اَكْثَرُهُمْ لَا یَعْقِلُوْنَ(103)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah ne baheera, saaibah, waseelah aur haam muqarrar nahin kiya, lekin kaafir log Allah par jhoota bohtaan lagate hain aur un mein aksar be-aqal hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مَا جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ: Allah ne muqarrar nahin kiya.}** Zamana-e-Jahiliyyat mein kaafiron ka yeh tareeqa tha ke jo oontni paanch martaba bachche janti aur aakhri martaba us ka nar bachcha hota, us ka kaan cheer dete. Phir na us par sawaari karte, na use zibah karte aur na use paani ya chaare se hataate. Is oontni ko **baheera** kehte the.

Jab safar darpesh hota ya koi beemar hota to nazr maante ke agar main safar se khairiyat se wapas aa gaya ya tandurust ho gaya to meri oontni **saaibah** hai. Is oontni se bhi faida uthana baheera ki tarah haraam samajhte aur use azaad chhor dete.

Bakri jab saat martaba bachche deti to agar saatwan bachcha nar hota to use mard khaate aur agar maada hoti to use bakriyon mein chhor dete. Isi tarah agar nar aur maada dono hote to kehte ke yeh apne bhai se mil gayi, is ko **waseelah** kehte.

Jab nar oont se das martaba oontni ko gabhin karwa liya jaata to use bhi chhor dete. Na us par sawaari karte, na us se kaam lete aur na use chaare paani se rokte. Is ko **haam** kehte the. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 103, ص 306)

Bukhari aur Muslim ki hadees mein hai ke baheera woh jaanwar tha jis ka doodh buton ke liye rok dete the; koi us ka doodh na nikaalta. Aur saaibah woh jaanwar tha jise apne buton ke liye chhor dete the; koi us se kaam na leta. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Manaaqib, Baab Qissatu Khuzaa'ah, 2/480, hadees: 3521; Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jannat wa Sifati Na'eemiha wa Ahliha, Baab-un-Naar Yadkhuluhal-Jabbaaroon, ص 1528, hadees: 51 (2856))

Yeh rasmein zamana-e-Jahiliyyat se Islam ke ibtidaai daur tak chali aa rahi thin. Is aayat mein inhein baatil qarar diya gaya aur farmaya gaya ke yeh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne muqarrar nahin kiya, balki kaafir Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ par jhoot baandhte hain. Allah Ta'ala ne in jaanwaron ko haraam nahin kiya, is liye in ki hurmat Allah ki taraf mansub karna ghalat hai. Yeh log be-aqal hain ke apne sardaron ke kehne par in cheezon ko haraam samajhte hain aur itna shaoor nahin rakhte ke jis cheez ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur Us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne haraam na kiya ho, use koi haraam nahin kar sakta.

### Jaanwar par kisi ka naam pukarne ka mas'alah

Aayat-e-mubarakah se yeh bhi maloom hua ke jaanwar ki zindagi mein us par kisi ka naam pukarna use haraam nahin karta. Haan, zibah ke waqt ghair-e-Khuda ka naam pukarna use haraam kar deta hai. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke jo jaanwar halal ho, use bila wajah haraam kehna mushrikeen ka tareeqa aur bilkul jahalat hai.

## Ayat 5:104

`Source: tafseerId=50826; ayatId=778; surah=5; ayat=104; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا قِیْلَ لَهُمْ تَعَالَوْا اِلٰى مَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ وَ اِلَى الرَّسُوْلِ قَالُوْا حَسْبُنَا مَا وَجَدْنَا عَلَیْهِ اٰبَآءَنَاؕ-اَوَ لَوْ كَانَ اٰبَآؤُهُمْ لَا یَعْلَمُوْنَ شَیْــٴًـا وَّ لَا یَهْتَدُوْنَ(104)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab un se kaha jaaye ke jo Allah ne naazil farmaya hai us ki taraf aur Rasool ki taraf aao to kehte hain: Hamein wohi kaafi hai jis par hum ne apne baap dada ko paaya hai. Kya agarche un ke baap dada na kuch jaante hon aur na unhein hidayat ho?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذَا قِیْلَ لَهُمْ: Aur jab unhein kaha jaaye.}** Mushrikon se jab kaha jaata hai ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur Us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki pairwi karo to woh kehte hain: “Hamare liye hamare baap dada ka deen kaafi hai.” Is par farmaya gaya ke baap dada ki pairwi us waqt durust hoti jab un ke paas ilm hota aur woh seedhi raah par hote.

### Baap dada ki najaaiz rasmein poori karne ki mazammat

Allah Ta'ala aur Us ke Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki pairwi ki dawat milne par kaafiron ne jo jawaab diya, us se maloom hua ke shariat ke muqaable mein jaahil baap dada ki rasmein ikhtiyar karna kaafiron ka tareeqa hai. Un logon ko naseehat haasil karni chahiye jo khushi ki taqreebon aur gham ke mauqe par najaaiz aur haraam rasmein karte hain, un rasmon mein shareek na hone wale ko bura bhala kehte hain aur mana karne wale se kehte hain: “Hamare khandan mein yeh rasmein arse se chali aa rahi hain, hum inhein nahin chhor sakte.” Allah Ta'ala aise Musalmanon ko hidayat ata farmaaye. Aameen.

### Nek logon ki pairwi zaroori hai

Aayat ke aakhri hisse se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ke nek bandon ki pairwi karna zaroori hai. Allah Ta'ala ek aur maqam par irshad farmata hai:

**’’یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ وَ كُوْنُوْا مَعَ الصّٰدِقِیْنَ‘‘** (Taubah: 119)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Allah se daro aur sachchon ke saath ho jao.

Hazrat Sufyan bin Uyainah رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain ke Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka irshad hai: “Aise logon ki sohbat ikhtiyar karo jin ki soorat dekh kar tumhein Khuda yaad aaye, jin ki guftagu tumhare ilm mein izafa kare aur jin ka amal tumhein Aakhirat ka shauq dilaye.” (Jaami' Bayaan-il-Ilm wa Fadlihi, Baab Jaami' fi Aadaab-il-Aalim wal-Muta'allim, ص 172)

## Ayat 5:105

`Source: tafseerId=50827; ayatId=779; surah=5; ayat=105; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا عَلَیْكُمْ اَنْفُسَكُمْۚ-لَا یَضُرُّكُمْ مَّنْ ضَلَّ اِذَا اهْتَدَیْتُمْؕ-اِلَى اللّٰهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ جَمِیْعًا فَیُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُوْنَ(105)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Tum apni jaanon ki fikr karo. Jab tum hidayat par ho to gumraah hone wala tumhara kuchh na bigaadega. Allah hi ki taraf tum sab ka lautna hai, phir woh tumhein bata dega jo tum karte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{عَلَیْكُمْ اَنْفُسَكُمْ: Tum apni fikr karo.}** Musalman kaafiron ke Islam se mehroom rehne par afsos karte aur ranj mehsoos karte the ke kaafir zid ki wajah se daulat-e-Islam se mehroom rahe. Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne unhein tasalli di ke is mein tumhara koi nuqsaan nahin. Tum ne **amr bil-ma'roof aur nahi anil-munkar** ka farz ada karke apni zimmedari poori kar di hai. Ab tum apni neki ka badla paoge.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Mubarak رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya: “Is aayat mein amr bil-ma'roof aur nahi anil-munkar ke wajib hone par bahut zor diya gaya hai. Apni fikr rakhne ka matlab yeh hai ke ek doosre ki khabar-geeri karo, nekion ki raghib karo aur buraiyon se roko.” (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 105, 1/534)

Mufti Ahmad Yaar Khan Naeemi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne bahut pyari baat farmayi, jis ka khulasa yeh hai: “Tum apni fikr karo” yani aqeede durust karke aur nek amal karke apni fikr karo. Aamaal mein tableegh bhi shamil hai, is liye jo qudrat ke bawajood tableegh na kare woh raah par nahin. (Noor-ul-Irfan, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 105, ص 198)

### Neki ka hukm dene aur burai se rokne ke baare mein ahadees

Yahan neki ka hukm dene aur burai se rokne ka zikr hua hai. Is munasibat se teen ahadees pesh hain:

1. Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Ae logo! Tum yeh aayat padhte ho: **’’یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا عَلَیْكُمْ اَنْفُسَكُمْۚ-لَا یَضُرُّكُمْ مَّنْ ضَلَّ اِذَا اهْتَدَیْتُمْ‘‘** aur main ne Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se suna hai ke jab log zaalim ko zulm karte dekhein aur use zulm se na rokein to qareeb hai ke Allah Ta'ala un sab ko azaab mein mubtala farma de.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Fitan, Baab Ma Jaa'a fi Nuzool-il-Azaab Iza Lam Yugayyir-il-Munkar, 4/69, hadees: 2175)

2. Ek martaba Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Ae logo! Tum Allah Ta'ala ke is farmaan **عَلَیْكُمْ اَنْفُسَكُمْ** ko padh kar dhoke mein na padna ke tum mein se koi kehne lage: ‘Main bas apni jaan ki fikr karunga.’ Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam! Tum zaroor neki ka hukm doge aur burai se rokoge, warna tum par tumhare badkaar log hukmaran bana diye jaayenge. Woh tumhein sakht takleef denge. Phir tumhare nek log dua karenge, lekin un ki dua qubool na ki jaayegi.” (Kanz-ul-Ummal, Kitaab-ul-Akhlaaq, Qism-ul-Af'aal, Al-Amr bil-Ma'roof wan-Nahi anil-Munkar, 2/271, Al-Juz-us-Saalis, hadees: 8442; Tafseer Tabari, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 105, 5/98-99)

3. Hazrat Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “Tum par zaroor neki ka hukm dena aur burai se rokna laazim hai, warna Allah Ta'ala tum par aisa zaalim hukmaran musallat kar dega jo tumhare buzurgon ki buzurgi ka khayal nahin rakhega aur tumhare chhoton par rahm nahin karega. Tumhare nek log us ke khilaaf dua maangenge, lekin un ki dua qubool na hogi. Tum madad maangoge, lekin tumhein madad na milegi.” (Ihya-ul-Uloom, Kitaab-ul-Amr bil-Ma'roof wan-Nahi anil-Munkar, Al-Baab-ul-Awwal fi Wujub-il-Amr bil-Ma'roof, 2/383)

**[1]** Neki ki dawat dene aur burai se rokne ki targheeb, jazbah aur mauqa paane ke liye Dawat-e-Islami se wabastagi aur Madani qaafilon mein safar bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 5:106

`Source: tafseerId=50828; ayatId=780; surah=5; ayat=106; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا شَهَادَةُ بَیْنِكُمْ اِذَا حَضَرَ اَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ حِیْنَ الْوَصِیَّةِ اثْنٰنِ ذَوَا عَدْلٍ مِّنْكُمْ اَوْ اٰخَرٰنِ مِنْ غَیْرِكُمْ اِنْ اَنْتُمْ ضَرَبْتُمْ فِی الْاَرْضِ فَاَصَابَتْكُمْ مُّصِیْبَةُ الْمَوْتِؕ-تَحْبِسُوْنَهُمَا مِنْۢ بَعْدِ الصَّلٰوةِ فَیُقْسِمٰنِ بِاللّٰهِ اِنِ ارْتَبْتُمْ لَا نَشْتَرِیْ بِهٖ ثَمَنًا وَّ لَوْ كَانَ ذَا قُرْبٰىۙ-وَ لَا نَكْتُمُ شَهَادَةَۙ-اللّٰهِ اِنَّاۤ اِذًا لَّمِنَ الْاٰثِمِیْنَ(106)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Jab tum mein kisi ko maut aane lage to wasiyyat karte waqt tumhari aapas ki gawahi (dene wale) tum mein se do mo'tabar shakhs hon ya agar tum zameen mein safar kar rahe ho phir tumhein maut ka haadisa aa pahunche to tumhare ghairon mein se do aadmi (gawah hon). Tum in dono gawahon ko namaz ke baad rok lo phir agar tumhein kuch shak ho to woh dono Allah ki qasam khaayen ke hum qasam ke badle koi maal na lenge agarche qareebi rishtedaar ho aur hum Allah ki gawahi na chhupaayenge. (Agar hum aisa karein to) us waqt hum zaroor gunahgaron mein honge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{شَهَادَةُ بَیْنِكُمْ: Tumhari aapas ki gawahi.}** Is aayat ke nazool ka sabab yeh hai ke Muhajireen mein se Budail naam ke ek sahabi tijarat ke iraade se do Isaaiyon, Tamim bin Aus Dari aur Adi bin Bada, ke saath mulk-e-Shaam ki taraf rawana hue. Shaam pahunchte hi Budail beemar ho gaye. Unhone apne tamam samaan ki fehrist likh kar samaan mein rakh di aur apne saathiyon ko is ki khabar na di.

Jab marz zyada barh gaya to Budail ne Tamim aur Adi dono ko wasiyyat ki ke un ka tamam maal Madinah Sharif pahunch kar un ke ghar walon ke hawale kar dein. Phir Budail is duniya se chale gaye. Un dono ne un ki wafat ke baad samaan dekha to us mein ek chaandi ka jaam tha jis par sone ka kaam hua tha aur us mein teen sau misqaal chaandi thi. Budail yeh jaam baadshah ko nazr karne ke iraade se laaye the. Un dono saathiyon ne jaam chhupa liya.

Jab woh apna kaam poora karke Madinah Tayyibah pahunche to Budail ka samaan un ke ghar walon ke hawale kar diya. Samaan kholne par fehrist mili. Fehrist ke mutabiq tamam samaan mil gaya lekin jaam na mila. Ghar walon ne Tamim aur Adi se poocha: “Kya Budail ne kuch samaan becha tha?” Unhone kaha: “Nahin.” Phir poocha: “Kya koi tijarati muamla kiya tha?” Unhone kaha: “Nahin.” Phir poocha: “Kya Budail zyada arse beemar rahe aur unhone ilaaj par kuch kharch kiya tha?” Unhone kaha: “Nahin. Woh shehar pahunchte hi beemar hue aur jaldi hi is duniya se chale gaye.”

Ghar walon ne kaha: “Un ke samaan mein ek fehrist mili hai jis mein sone se naqsh kiye hue chaandi ke jaam ka bhi zikr hai. Us mein teen sau misqaal chaandi hai, lekin woh maujood nahin.” Tamim aur Adi ne kaha: “Hamein is ka ilm nahin. Hum ne jo wasiyyat ki gayi thi us ke mutabiq samaan tumhare hawale kar diya. Hamein jaam ki koi khabar nahin.” Yeh muqaddamah Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein pesh hua. Tamim aur Adi wahan bhi inkar par qaim rahe aur qasam kha li. Is par yeh aayat nazil hui. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 106, 1/534)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ki riwayat mein hai ke phir woh jaam Makkah Mukarramah mein pakra gaya. Jis shakhs ke paas tha us ne kaha: “Main ne yeh jaam Tamim aur Adi se khareeda hai.” Jaam ke maalik ke ghar walon mein se do aadmi kharay hue aur unhone qasam khai: “Hamari gawahi un dono ki gawahi se zyada qabool kiye jaane ki haqdaar hai. Yeh jaam hamare marhoom ka hai.” Is ke baare mein yeh aayat nazil hui. (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab: Wa min Suratil-Maidah, 5/44, hadees: 3071)

Is aayat mein hukm diya gaya ke jab kisi Musalman ki maut ka waqt qareeb aa jaye, zindagi ki umeed na rahe aur maut ki nishaniyan zahir hon to apne logon mein se do aadmiyon ko wasiyyat ka gawah bana lo. Agar safar mein ho aur apne log, yani Musalman, na milen to ghair-Muslimon ko gawah bana sakte ho.

**{تَحْبِسُوْنَهُمَا مِنْۢ بَعْدِ الصَّلٰوةِ: Tum dono gawahon ko namaz ke baad rok lo.}** Pehle wasiyyat par gawah banane ka tareeqa bataya gaya, phir yeh bataya gaya ke agar qareenon aur nishaniyon se gawahi mein jhoot ka andesha ho aur marhoom ke waris ko shak ho to gawahon se kis tarah gawahi li jaye. Dono gawah Asr ki namaz ke baad Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam khaayen ke hum gawahi ke badle kisi se koi maal nahin lenge, agarche woh qareebi rishtedaar hi kyun na ho, aur hum gawahi nahin chhupaayenge. Yani hum jhooti qasam nahin khaayenge aur kisi ki khaatir aisa nahin karenge. Agar hum aisa karein to us waqt hum zaroor gunahgar honge.

Aayat mein namaz se murad Asr ki namaz hai. Har deen aur mazhab ke log us waqt ki ta'zeem karte the aur us waqt jhooti qasam khaane se bachte the. (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 106, 2/60-61)

## Ayat 5:107

`Source: tafseerId=50829; ayatId=781; surah=5; ayat=107; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** فَاِنْ عُثِرَ عَلٰۤى اَنَّهُمَا اسْتَحَقَّاۤ اِثْمًا فَاٰخَرٰنِ یَقُوْمٰنِ مَقَامَهُمَا مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ اسْتَحَقَّ عَلَیْهِمُ الْاَوْلَیٰنِ فَیُقْسِمٰنِ بِاللّٰهِ لَشَهَادَتُنَاۤ اَحَقُّ مِنْ شَهَادَتِهِمَا وَ مَا اعْتَدَیْنَاۤ ﳲ اِنَّاۤ اِذًا لَّمِنَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(107)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir agar is baat par ittila mile ke woh dono gawah (gawahi mein jhoot bol kar) kisi gunah ke mustahiq hue hain to un ki jagah un logon mein se jin ka haq dabaya gaya, mayyit ke zyada qareebi do aadmi (qasam khaane ke liye) kharay ho jaayen. Phir woh Allah ki qasam khaayen ke hamari gawahi, yani hamari qasam, un ki gawahi se zyada durust hai aur hum hadd se nahin barhe. (Aur agar aisa karein to) us waqt hum zalimon mein honge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَاِنْ عُثِرَ عَلٰۤى اَنَّهُمَا اسْتَحَقَّاۤ اِثْمًا: Phir agar pata chale ke woh dono kisi gunah ke mustahiq hue.}** Agar wasiyyat ke dono gawahon ka jhoot sabit ho jaye, jaise Tamim aur Adi ka jhoot sabit hua ke jaam Makkah Muazzamah mein pakra gaya, to marhoom ke warison mein se do aadmi qasam kha kar kahen ke yeh dono amanatdaar jhoote hain. Hamari gawahi, yani qasam, un dono ki gawahi se zyada durust hai aur hum hadd se nahin barhe. Agar hum aisa karein to us waqt hum zalimon mein honge.

Budail ke waqie mein jab us ke dono saathiyon ki khayanat zahir hui to Budail ke warison mein se do shakhs kharay hue. Unhone qasam kha kar kaha ke yeh jaam hamare moorisi ka hai aur hamari gawahi un dono ki gawahi se zyada theek hai. Is ke baad jaam ka faisla un ke haq mein kar diya gaya.

## Ayat 5:108

`Source: tafseerId=50830; ayatId=782; surah=5; ayat=108; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** ذٰلِكَ اَدْنٰۤى اَنْ یَّاْتُوْا بِالشَّهَادَةِ عَلٰى وَجْهِهَاۤ اَوْ یَخَافُوْۤا اَنْ تُرَدَّ اَیْمَانٌۢ بَعْدَ اَیْمَانِهِمْؕ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ وَ اسْمَعُوْاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الْفٰسِقِیْنَ۠   (108)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Yeh is ke zyada qareeb hai ke woh gawah sahi tareeqe se gawahi ada karein ya woh is baat se darein ke un ki qasmon ke baad qasmon ko waris ki taraf lauta diya jaayega. Aur Allah se daro aur hukm suno aur Allah nafarmaanon ko hidayat nahin deta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ذٰلِكَ اَدْنٰى: Yeh qareeb-tar hai.}** Adi aur Tamim ke waqie mein gawahi aur qasam ka jo qanoon bayan hua, us ki hikmat yeh hai ke log is waqie se ibrat lein, gawahi mein haq aur durusti ka raasta na chhorain aur jhooti gawahi ke us anjaam se darte rahen jo ruswai aur sharmindagi ke siwa kuch nahin.

## Jhooti qasam aur jhooti gawahi ki mazammat

Aaj logon ki haalat itni kharab ho chuki hai ke un ke nazdeek jhooti qasam khaana, jhooti gawahi dena, jhoote muqadme mein phansa kar apne Musalman bhai ki izzat barbaad karna, use lohe ki salakhon ke peeche majboori ki zindagi guzaarne par majboor karna aur apne Musalman bhai ka na-haq maal kha jaana goya jurm hi nahin. Is duniya ki fani zindagi ko aakhri manzil samajh lena aqalmandi nahin, balki nadani aur bewakoofi ki inteha hai. Unhein chahiye ke in Qurani aayaat aur in ahadees ko ghaur se parh kar ibrat haasil karein.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarkar-e-Aali Waqar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne jhooti qasam khaayi taa-ke us ke zariye apne Musalman bhai ka maal kha jaye, woh Allah Ta'ala se is haal mein milega ke Allah Ta'ala us par sakht naraaz hoga.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Iman wan-Nuzoor, Baab: Ahdullah عَزَّوَجَلَّ, 4/290, hadees: 6659)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jhoote gawah ke qadam hatne bhi na paayenge ke Allah Ta'ala us ke liye Jahannam wajib kar dega.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ul-Ahkaam, Baab: Shahadat-uz-Zoor, 3/123, hadees: 2373)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Jis ne aisi gawahi di jis se kisi Musalman mard ka maal barbaad ho jaye ya kisi ka khoon bahaaya jaye to us ne apne upar Jahannam wajib kar liya.” (Al-Mu'jam-ul-Kabeer, Ikrimah an Ibn Abbas, 11/172, hadees: 11541)

## Ayat 5:109

## Ayat 5:110

`Source: tafseerId=50832; ayatId=784; surah=5; ayat=110; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اِذْ قَالَ اللّٰهُ یٰعِیْسَى ابْنَ مَرْیَمَ اذْكُرْ نِعْمَتِیْ عَلَیْكَ وَ عَلٰى وَ الِدَتِكَۘ-اِذْ اَیَّدْتُّكَ بِرُوْحِ الْقُدُسِ- تُكَلِّمُ النَّاسَ فِی الْمَهْدِ وَ كَهْلًاۚ-وَ اِذْ عَلَّمْتُكَ الْكِتٰبَ وَ الْحِكْمَةَ وَ التَّوْرٰىةَ وَ الْاِنْجِیْلَۚ-وَ اِذْ تَخْلُقُ مِنَ الطِّیْنِ كَهَیْــٴَـةِ الطَّیْرِ بِاِذْنِیْ فَتَنْفُخُ فِیْهَا فَتَكُوْنُ طَیْرًۢا بِاِذْنِیْ وَ تُبْرِئُ الْاَ   كْمَهَ وَ الْاَبْرَصَ بِاِذْنِیْۚ-وَ اِذْ تُخْرِ جُ الْمَوْتٰى بِاِذْنِیْۚ-وَ اِذْ كَفَفْتُ بَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ عَنْكَ اِذْ جِئْتَهُمْ بِالْبَیِّنٰتِ فَقَالَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْهُمْ اِنْ هٰذَاۤ اِلَّا سِحْرٌ مُّبِیْنٌ(110)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jab Allah farmayega: Ae Maryam ke bete Isa! Apne upar aur apni walidah par mera woh ehsan yaad kar, jab main ne paak rooh se teri madad ki. Tu gahware mein aur badi umar mein logon se baatein karta tha aur jab main ne tujhe Kitaab aur hikmat aur Taurat aur Injeel sikhaayi aur jab tu mere hukm se mitti se parinde jaisi soorat bana kar us mein phoonk maarta tha to woh mere hukm se parinda ban jaati aur tu mere hukm se paidaishi naabina aur safed daag ke mareez ko shifa deta tha aur jab tu mere hukm se murdon ko zinda karke nikaalta aur jab main ne Bani Israeel ko tum se rok diya. Jab tu un ke paas roshan nishaniyan le kar aaya to un mein se kaafiron ne kaha: Yeh to khula jadoo hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِذْ قَالَ اللّٰهُ: Jab Allah farmayega.}** Is aayat mein bhi Qiyamat ke din ka ek maamla bayan kiya gaya hai. Goya farmaya gaya: “Aap us din ko yaad karein jis din Allah Ta'ala Rasoolon ko jama farmayega aur Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se is tarah farmayega.” (Qurtubi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 110, 3/224, Juz 6)

Yaad rahe ke is aayat-e-mubarakah mein Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par bayan kiye gaye ehsanaat ki tafseeli tafseer Surah Aal-e-Imran ki aayat 37 se 49 ke tehat guzar chuki hai.
