# Surah Al-Maidah — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 5:1

`Source: tafseerId=50734; ayatId=675; surah=5; ayat=1; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اَوْفُوْا بِالْعُقُوْدِ۬ؕ-اُحِلَّتْ لَكُمْ بَهِیْمَةُ الْاَنْعَامِ اِلَّا مَا یُتْلٰى عَلَیْكُمْ غَیْرَ مُحِـلِّی الصَّیْدِ وَ اَنْتُمْ حُرُمٌؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَحْكُمُ مَا یُرِیْدُ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Tamam ahad poore kiya karo. Tumhare liye chopaye jaanwar halal kar diye gaye, siwaye un ke jo aage tumhare saamne bayan kiye jaayenge, lekin ihram ki haalat mein shikaar ko halal na samjho. Beshak Allah jo chahta hai hukm farmata hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَوْفُوْا بِالْعُقُودِ: Tamam ahad poore karo.}** Uqood ka matlab ahad hai. Inhein poora karne ka hukm diya gaya hai. Is se murad kaun se ahad hain, is baare mein mufassireen ke chand aqwaal hain:

(1) Imam Ibn Jurej رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya ke yahan Ahl-e-Kitaab ko mukhatab kiya gaya hai. Ma'ni yeh hain: Ae Ahl-e-Kitaab ke momino! Guzishta kitaabon mein Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par iman lane aur Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki itaat karne ke baare mein tum se jo ahad liye gaye hain, unhein poora karo.

(2) Baaz mufassireen ka qawl hai ke is aayat mein momino ko mukhatab kiya gaya hai aur unhein apne ahad poore karne ka hukm diya gaya hai.

(3) Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke in uqud, yani ahad se iman aur woh ahad murad hain jo halal aur haram ke baare mein Quran-e-Paak mein liye gaye.

(4) Baaz mufassireen ka qawl hai ke is se momino ke aapas ke muahide murad hain. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 1, 1/458)

**{اُحِلَّتْ لَكُمْ: Tumhare liye halal kar diye gaye.}** Yahan halal jaanwaron ka bayan hai. Farmaya gaya ke jin jaanwaron ki hurmat Shariat mein bayan hui hai, un ke siwa tamam chopaye tumhare liye halal kiye gaye. Is mein un kafiron ka radd hai jo buton ke naam par chhode hue jaanwar, baheerah, saaibah waghera ko haram samajhte the.

Is se maloom hua ke haram sirf wahi hai jise Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ haram farma dein. Halal hone ke liye khaas daleel zaroori nahin; kisi cheez ka haram na hona hi us ke halal hone ki daleel hai, jaisa ke is aayat mein saaf farma diya gaya. Un logon ko is se ibrat leni chahiye jo Musalmanon ke paak khane ko bahane bana kar haram, balkeh shirk qaraar dete rehte hain.

**{وَ اَنْتُمْ حُرُمٌ: Aur tum ihram ki haalat mein ho.}** Ihram ki haalat mein khushki ka shikaar karna haram hai, jabke dariyai shikaar jaiz hai. (Bada'i-us-Sana'i, Kitaab-ul-Hajj, Fasl wa amma bayan anwaa'ihi, 2/427)

Yaad rahe ke muhrim, yani ihram wale, ka kiya hua shikaar na muhrim ke liye halal hai na ghair ke liye. Ihram chahe Hajj ka ho ya Umrah ka. Is Surah ke aakhir mein bhi muhrim ke shikaar ke masail bayan honge.

## Ayat 5:2

`Source: tafseerId=50735; ayatId=676; surah=5; ayat=2; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُحِلُّوْا شَعَآىٕرَ اللّٰهِ وَ لَا الشَّهْرَ الْحَرَامَ وَ لَا الْهَدْیَ وَ لَا الْقَلَآىٕدَ وَ لَاۤ   ﰰمِّیْنَ الْبَیْتَ الْحَرَامَ یَبْتَغُوْنَ فَضْلًا مِّنْ رَّبِّهِمْ وَ رِضْوَانًاؕ-وَ اِذَا حَلَلْتُمْ فَاصْطَادُوْاؕ-وَ لَا یَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَاٰنُ قَوْمٍ اَنْ صَدُّوْكُمْ عَنِ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ اَنْ تَعْتَدُوْاۘ-وَ تَعَاوَنُوْا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَ التَّقْوٰى ۪-وَ لَا تَعَاوَنُوْا عَلَى الْاِثْمِ وَ الْعُدْوَانِ۪- وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ شَدِیْدُ الْعِقَابِٛ     (2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Allah ki nishaniyon ko halal na thehrao, na hurmat wale mahino ko, na Haram ko bheji hui qurbaniyon ko, na un jaanwaron ko jin ke gale mein alaamati patte hon, aur na hurmat wale ghar ka qasd karke aane walon ke maal aur izzat ko, jo apne Rab ka fazl aur us ki raza dhoondte hain. Aur jab ihram se bahar aa jao to shikaar kar sakte ho. Tumhein kisi qaum ki dushmani is wajah se zyadti par na ubhare ke unhone tumhein Masjid-e-Haram se roka tha. Neki aur parhezgari par ek doosre ki madad karo aur gunah aur zyadti par baahmi madad na karo. Allah se darte raho. Beshak Allah sakht azaab dene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا تُحِلُّوْا شَعَآىٕرَ اللّٰهِ: Allah ki nishaniyon ko halal na thehrao.}** Is aayat mein deen ki nishaniyon ki qadr karne ka hukm hai. Yani Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne jo cheezein farz ki hain aur jin se mana farmaya hai, un sab ki hurmat ka lihaz rakho. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki nishaniyan qarar paane wali cheezon ka ehtiram bhi bahut zaroori hai. Is liye deen ki azmat wali cheezon ka ehtiram kiya jaaye. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ farmata hai:

وَ مَنْ یُّعَظِّمْ شَعَآىٕرَ اللّٰهِ فَاِنَّهَا مِنْ تَقْوَى الْقُلُوْبِ (الحج:۳۲)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur jo Allah ki nishaniyon ki ta'zeem kare to yeh dilon ka taqwa hai.

Sha'aairullah, yani Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki nishaniyon mein Khanah-e-Ka'bah, Quran-e-Paak, masajid, azaan aur buzurgon ke mazaar waghera sab shamil hain. Balki jis cheez ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke maqbool bando se nisbat ho jaaye, woh bhi Sha'aairullah ban jaati hai. Jaise Hazrat Hajrah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ke qadam Safa aur Marwah pahadon par pade to woh pahad Sha'aairullah ban gaye aur Rab-e-Kareem عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaya:

اِنَّ الصَّفَا وَ الْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَآىٕرِ اللّٰهِ (البقرہ:۱۵۸)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Beshak Safa aur Marwah Allah ki nishaniyon mein se hain.

**{وَ لَا الشَّهْرَ الْحَرَامَ: Aur na hurmat wale mahino ko.}** Hurmat wale mahino ko halal na thehrao. Mohtaram mahine chaar hain: Rajab, Zil-Qa'dah, Zil-Hijjah aur Muharram. Zamana-e-Jahiliyyat mein bhi kafir in ka adab karte the aur Islam ne bhi in ka ehtiram baqi rakha. Pehle Islam mein in mahino mein jang haram thi. Ab har waqt jihad ho sakta hai, lekin in mahino ka ehtiram badastoor baqi hai. Is ki tafseel Surah Taubah, aayat 36 mein aayegi.

**{وَ لَا الْهَدْیَ وَ لَا الْقَلَآىٕدَ: Aur na Haram ki qurbaniyon ko aur na alaamati patte wali qurbaniyon ko.}** Arab log qurbaniyon ke gale mein Haram Shareef ke darakhton ki chaal waghera ke haar daalte the taake log jaan lein ke yeh Haram ko bheji hui qurbaniyan hain aur un se chhed-chhad na karein. Haram Shareef ki un qurbaniyon ke ehtiram ka hukm diya gaya hai.

**{وَ لَاۤ ﰰمِّیْنَ الْبَیْتَ الْحَرَامَ: Aur na hurmat wale ghar ka qasd karke aane walon ke maal aur izzat ko.}** Is se Hajj aur Umrah ke liye aane wale murad hain. Shaan-e-Nuzool yeh hai ke Shuraih bin Hind ek mashhoor badbakht tha. Woh Madinah Tayyibah aaya aur Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein haazir ho kar poochhne laga ke Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Allah ki makhlooq ko kis cheez ki dawat dete hain. Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: Apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ par iman lane, meri risalat ki tasdeeq karne, namaz qaim rakhne aur zakat dene ki dawat deta hoon.

Woh bola: Bahut achhi dawat hai. Main apne sardaron se mashwara kar loon to main bhi Islam launga aur unhein bhi launga. Yeh keh kar chala gaya. Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne us ke aane se pehle hi apne Sahaba ko bata diya tha ke Rabi'ah qabeelay ka ek shakhs aane wala hai jo shaitani zaban bolega. Us ke chale jaane ke baad Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: Kafir ka chehra le kar aaya aur dagha-baaz aur bad-ahd ki tarah peeth pher kar gaya; yeh Islam lane wala nahin.

Us ne dhoka kiya aur Madinah Shareef se nikalte hue wahan ke mawashi aur maal le gaya. Agle saal woh Yamamah ke hajiyon ke saath tijarat ka bahut saamaan aur qilaadah-posh, yani khaas haar wali qurbaniyan le kar Hajj ke irade se nikla. Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ apne Ashab رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke saath tashreef le ja rahe the. Raaste mein Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne Shuraih ko dekha aur chaha ke mawashi us se wapas le lein, lekin Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne mana farma diya. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, 1/459)

Hukm diya gaya ke jo Hajj ke irade se nikla ho, us ke saath kuchh na kiya jaaye.

**{وَ اِذَا حَلَلْتُمْ: Aur jab ihram se farigh ho jao.}** Ihram se farigh hone ke baad Haram Shareef se bahar shikaar ki ijaazat hai. Yeh asal mein ijaazat hai, lekin iska jaiz hona itna qat'i hai ke jo is ka inkaar kare woh kafir hai.

**{وَ لَا یَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ: Aur tumhein ubhaare na.}** Ahle Makkah ne Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Ashab رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ko Hudaybiyah ke din Umrah karne se roka tha. Lekin tum un ke is dushmani wale amal ka badla na lo. Haan, ab kafir ko Masjid-e-Haram se roka jaayega, kyunki baad mein mana karne ka hukm naazil ho gaya tha. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

اِنَّمَا الْمُشْرِكُوْنَ نَجَسٌ فَلَا یَقْرَبُوا الْمَسْجِدَ الْحَرَامَ(التوبہ:۲۸)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Mushrik nare napaak hain, to is baras ke baad woh Masjid-e-Haram ke paas na aane paayen.

**{وَ تَعَاوَنُوْا: Aur ek doosre ki madad karo.}** Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne do baaton ka hukm diya: (1) Neki aur parhezgari par ek doosre ki madad karna. (2) Gunah aur zyadti par baahmi ta'awun na karna. Bir se har woh nek kaam murad hai jis ka Shariat ne hukm diya. Taqwa ka matlab har us kaam se bachna hai jis se Shariat ne roka. Ism se gunah aur udwan se Allah Ta'ala ki hudood se badhna murad hai. (Jalalain, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, safha 94)

Ek qawl yeh hai ke ism se kufr aur udwan se zulm ya bid'at murad hai. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, 1/461)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain ke neki se Sunnat ki pairwi murad hai. (Sawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, 2/469)

Hazrat Nawas bin Sam'an رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: "Main ne Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se neki aur gunah ke baare mein poocha. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: Neki husn-e-akhlaq hai aur gunah woh hai jo tumhare dil mein khatke aur logon ka us se waaqif hona tumhein na-pasand ho." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab ma jaa'a fil-birri wal-ism, 4/173, hadees: 2396)

### Neki ke kaamo mein madad aur gunah mein madad na karne ka hukm

Yeh nihayat jaame' aayat hai. Neki aur taqwa ki tamam qismein is mein shamil hain, aur ism aur udwan mein har woh cheez shamil hai jo gunah aur zyadti ke daayre mein aati ho. Ilm-e-deen phelane mein waqt, maal, dars-o-tadrees aur likhne waghera se ek doosre ki madad karna; Islam ki dawat aur us ki taleemaat duniya ke har kone tak pahunchane ke liye baahmi ta'awun karna; apni aur doosron ki amali haalat sudharna; neki ki dawat dena aur burai se rokna; mulk aur qaum ke mushtarka faidon mein ek doosre ka saath dena; social work aur samaji khidmaat, sab is mein shamil hain.

Gunah aur zulm mein kisi ki madad na karne ka hukm hai. Kisi ka haq maarne mein doosron ka saath dena, rishwat le kar faisle badalna, jhooti gawahi dena, bila wajah kisi Musalman ko phansana, zalim ka us ke zulm mein saath dena, haram aur najaiz karobar karne wali company mein kisi tarah shareek hona, aur burai ke adday mein naukri karna, sab burai mein ta'awun ki sooratein hain aur najaiz hain.

سُبْحَانَ اللہ! Quran-e-Paak ki taleemaat kitni umdah hain. Is ka har hukm dil ki gehraai mein utarne wala aur har aayat gumrah logon ke liye roshni ka minaar hai. In taleemaat se sahi faida us waqt milega jab in par amal bhi kiya jaaye. Afsoos ke aaj Musalmanon ki ek tadaad amali taur par Quran ki taleemaat se bahut door ho gayi hai. Allah Ta'ala sab Musalmanon ko Quran ke ahkaam par amal ki taufeeq ata farmaye.

## Ayat 5:3

`Source: tafseerId=50736; ayatId=677; surah=5; ayat=3; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** حُرِّمَتْ عَلَیْكُمُ الْمَیْتَةُ وَ الدَّمُ وَ لَحْمُ الْخِنْزِیْرِ وَ مَاۤ اُهِلَّ لِغَیْرِ اللّٰهِ بِهٖ وَ الْمُنْخَنِقَةُ وَ الْمَوْقُوْذَةُ وَ الْمُتَرَدِّیَةُ وَ النَّطِیْحَةُ وَ مَاۤ اَكَلَ السَّبُعُ اِلَّا مَا ذَكَّیْتُمْ- وَ مَا ذُبِحَ عَلَى النُّصُبِ وَ اَنْ تَسْتَقْسِمُوْا بِالْاَزْلَامِؕ-ذٰلِكُمْ فِسْقٌؕ-اَلْیَوْمَ یَىٕسَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْ دِیْنِكُمْ فَلَا تَخْشَوْهُمْ وَ اخْشَوْنِؕ-اَلْیَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِیْنَكُمْ وَ اَتْمَمْتُ عَلَیْكُمْ نِعْمَتِیْ وَ رَضِیْتُ لَكُمُ الْاِسْلَامَ دِیْنًاؕ-فَمَنِ اضْطُرَّ فِیْ مَخْمَصَةٍ غَیْرَ مُتَجَانِفٍ لِّاِثْمٍۙ-فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum par haram kiya gaya hai murdaar, khoon, suar ka gosht, woh jaanwar jis ke zabh ke waqt ghairullah ka naam pukara gaya ho, gala ghontne se marne wala, bina dhaar wali cheez ki chot se marne wala, bulandi se gir kar marne wala, kisi jaanwar ke seeng maarne se marne wala, aur woh jise kisi darinde ne kha liya ho, siwaye un ke jinhein tum ne zinda paa kar zabh kar liya ho. Aur jo but ke aastane par zabh kiya gaya ho, aur paanse daal kar qismat maloom karna bhi haram hai. Yeh gunah ka kaam hai. Aaj kafir tumhare deen se na-umeed ho gaye, to un se na daro aur mujh se daro. Aaj main ne tumhare liye tumhara deen mukammal kar diya, tum par apni nemat poori kar di aur tumhare liye Islam ko deen pasand kiya. To jo bhook ki shiddat mein majboor ho, is haal mein ke gunah ki taraf mail na ho, to beshak Allah bakhshne wala, mehrban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{حُرِّمَتْ عَلَیْكُمُ: Tum par haram kar diya gaya hai.}** Pehli aayat mein farmaya gaya tha ke tum par chopaye halal hain, siwaye un jaanwaron ke jo aage bayan kiye jaayenge. Yahan unhi ka bayan hai aur gyarah cheezein haram hone ka zikr hai:

(1) Murdaar, yani jis jaanwar ke liye Shariat mein zabh ka hukm ho aur woh baghair zabh ke mar jaaye.

(2) Behta hua khoon.

(3) Suar ka gosht aur us ke tamam ajzaa.

(4) Woh jaanwar jis ke zabh ke waqt ghair-e-Khuda ka naam liya gaya ho, jaise zamana-e-Jahiliyyat mein buton ke naam par zabh kiya jaata tha. Agar jaanwar zabh ke waqt sirf Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke naam par zabh kiya gaya ho, lekin doosre waqt us ki nisbat ghair-e-Khuda ki taraf ki gayi ho, to woh haram nahin. Jaise Abdullah ki gaaye, aqeeqe ka bakra, walime ka jaanwar, ya woh jaanwar jis ka sawab Auliya رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِمْ ki arwah ko pahunchana maqsad ho. Inhein zabh ke waqt ke ilawa Auliya ke naam se yaad kiya jaaye, lekin zabh sirf Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke naam par ho aur us waqt kisi doosre ka naam na liya jaaye, to woh halal aur tayyib hain. Is aayat mein sirf us jaanwar ko haram kiya gaya hai jis ke zabh ke waqt ghair-e-Khuda ka naam liya gaya ho. Jo log zabh ki yeh qaid nahin lagate, woh aayat ke ma'ni mein ghalati karte hain aur un ka qawl tamam mo'tabar tafaseer aur Quran ke mafhoom ke khilaf hai.

(5) Gala ghont kar maara hua jaanwar.

(6) Laathi, pathar, dhele, goli ya chharre, yani baghair dhaar wali cheez se maara gaya jaanwar.

(7) Gir kar mara hua jaanwar, chahe pahad se gira ho ya kuen waghera mein.

(8) Woh jaanwar jis ko doosre jaanwar ne seeng maara aur woh is chot se mar gaya.

(9) Woh jaanwar jise kisi darinde ne thoda sa kha liya aur woh zakhm ki takleef se mar gaya. Lekin agar woh mara na ho aur is waqia ke baad zinda bach jaaye, phir tum use baqaida zabh kar lo to woh halal hai.

(10) Woh jaanwar jo kisi but ke than par ibaadat ke taur par zabh kiya gaya ho. Ahl-e-Jahiliyyat ne Ka'bah Shareef ke gird teen sau saath pathar nasb kiye the, jin ki woh ibaadat karte aur un ke liye zabh karte the. Is zabh se un ki ta'zeem aur qurb hasil karna maqsad hota tha. Is soorat ka khaas zikr is liye kiya gaya ke yeh tareeqa un mein aam tha.

(11) Kisi kaam ka hukm maloom karne ke liye paansa daalna. Zamana-e-Jahiliyyat ke log safar, jang, tijarat ya nikah waghera kaam darpesh hone par teen teeron se paanse daalte. Jo haan ya na nikalta, us ke mutabiq amal karte aur use hukm-e-Ilahi samajhte. In sab ki mana ki gayi.

**{اَلْیَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِیْنَكُمْ: Aaj main ne tumhare liye tumhara deen mukammal kar diya.}** Yeh aayat Hajjat-ul-Wida mein Yaum-e-Arafah, Jumu'ah ke din Asr ke baad naazil hui. Bata diya gaya ke halal aur haram ke ahkaam aur qiyas ka qanoon sab mukammal kar diya gaya. Is liye is aayat ke baad halal aur haram ke bayan ki koi aayat naazil nahin hui, agarche **وَ اتَّقُوْا یَوْمًا تُرْجَعُوْنَ فِیْهِ اِلَى اللّٰهِ** naazil hui jo wa'z-o-naseehat par mushtamil hai.

Baaz mufassireen ka qawl hai ke deen mukammal karne ka matlab Islam ko ghalib karna hai. Isi ka asar tha ke Hajjat-ul-Wida mein is aayat ke naazil hone par koi mushrik Musalmanon ke saath Hajj mein shareek na ho saka. Ek qawl yeh hai ke ma'ni yeh hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne tumhein dushman se aman diya. Ek qawl yeh hai ke deen ka ikmaal yeh hai ke yeh pichhli Shari'aton ki tarah mansookh nahin hoga aur Qiyamat tak baqi rahega. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 3, 1/464)

### Deeni kaamyabi ke din khushi manana jaiz hai

Is aayat ke baare mein Bukhari aur Muslim ki hadees mein hai ke Hazrat Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke paas ek Yahoodi aaya aur bola: Ae Ameer-ul-Momineen رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ! Aap ki kitaab mein ek aayat hai. Agar woh hum Yahoodiyon par naazil hui hoti to hum us ke naazil hone ke din Eid manate. Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne poocha: Kaun si aayat? Us Yahoodi ne **اَلْیَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ** wali aayat padhi. Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Main us din ko jaanta hoon jis din yeh naazil hui aur is ke naazil hone ki jagah ko bhi pehchanta hoon. Woh jagah Arafaat thi aur din Jumu'ah ka tha. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Iman, Baab Ziyadat-ul-Iman wa Nuqsanuhu, 1/28, hadees: 45; Muslim, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, safha 1609, hadees: 5 (3017))

Hazrat Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki murad yeh thi ke hamare liye woh din Eid hai. Tirmizi Shareef mein Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke ek Yahoodi ne Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se isi tarah kaha to Aap ne farmaya: Jis din yeh aayat naazil hui, us din do Eidein thin: Jumu'ah aur Arafah. (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab wa min Suratil-Maidah, 5/33, hadees: 3055)

Is se maloom hua ke kisi deeni kaamyabi ke din khushi manana jaiz hai aur Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم se sabit hai. Warna Hazrat Umar aur Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم saaf farma dete ke khushi ke kisi waqia ki yaadgaar banana aur us din Eid manana bid'at hai. Is se sabit hua ke Eid-e-Milad manana jaiz hai, kyunki woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki sab se azeem nemat ki yaadgaar aur shukr guzari hai.

**{وَ اَتْمَمْتُ عَلَیْكُمْ نِعْمَتِیْ: Aur main ne tum par apni nemat poori kar di.}** Is se murad hai ke Makkah Mukarramah fatah farma kar Allah Ta'ala ne tum par apni nemat poori kar di. Makkah Mukarramah ki fatah Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki azeem nemat thi.

**{وَ رَضِیْتُ لَكُمُ الْاِسْلَامَ دِیْنًا: Aur tumhare liye Islam ko deen pasand kiya.}** Yani Allah Ta'ala ne tumhare liye Islam ko deen ke taur par pasand farma liya aur is ke siwa koi doosra deen qabool nahin.

### Is aayat se maloom hone wale ahkaam

Is aayat se kai ahkaam maloom hue:

Pehla: Sirf Islam Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko pasand hai. Ab jo deen Muhammad-e-Arabi صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke deen ki soorat mein hai, us ke siwa baqi sab deen na-qabil-e-qabool hain.

Doosra: Is aayat ke naazil hone ke baad Qiyamat tak Islam ka koi hukm mansookh nahin ho sakta.

Teesra: Usool-e-deen mein na izaafa ho sakta hai na kami. Ijtehadi furooi masail hamesha nikalte rahenge.

Chautha: Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke baad koi Nabi nahin ban sakta, kyunki deen mukammal ho chuka. Suraj nikalne ke baad chiragh ki zaroorat nahin hoti. Is liye Qadiyani jhoote aur be-deen hain aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke kalaam aur deen ko naaqis samajhne wale hain.

Paanchwa: Islam chhor kar koi shakhs lakhon nekiyan kare, phir bhi Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko pyara nahin ho sakta. Islam jad hai aur amal shaakhein aur patte. Jad kaat dene ke baad shaakhon aur patton ko paani dena bekaar hai.

**{فَمَنِ اضْطُرَّ فِیْ مَخْمَصَةٍ: To jo bhook ki shiddat mein majboor ho.}** Upar haram cheezon ka bayan ho gaya. Lekin jab khane peene ke liye koi halal cheez milna mumkin na ho aur bhook ki shiddat se jaan par ban jaaye, to jaan bachane ke liye zaroorat ke mutabiq khane peene ki ijaazat hai. Gunah ki taraf mail na ho, yani zaroorat se zyada na khaaye. Itna hi khaaye jis se jaan ka khatra door ho jaaye.

## Ayat 5:4

`Source: tafseerId=50737; ayatId=678; surah=5; ayat=4; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یَسْــٴَـلُوْنَكَ مَا ذَاۤ اُحِلَّ لَهُمْؕ-قُلْ اُحِلَّ لَكُمُ الطَّیِّبٰتُۙ-وَ مَا عَلَّمْتُمْ مِّنَ الْجَوَارِ حِ مُكَلِّبِیْنَ تُعَلِّمُوْنَهُنَّ مِمَّا عَلَّمَكُمُ اللّٰهُ٘-فَكُلُوْا مِمَّاۤ اَمْسَكْنَ عَلَیْكُمْ وَ اذْكُرُوا اسْمَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْهِ۪-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ سَرِیْعُ الْحِسَابِ(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Habeeb! Tum se poochhte hain ke un ke liye kya halal hua. Tum farma do ke tumhare liye paak cheezein halal ki gayi hain aur woh shikari jaanwar bhi jinhein tum ne shikaar par daudate hue shikaar karna sikha diya hai. Tum unhein woh sikhate ho jo Allah ne tumhein sikhaya hai. To us mein se khao jo woh tumhare liye shikaar karke rok dein aur shikari jaanwar ko chhorte waqt us par Allah ka naam lo. Allah se darte raho. Beshak Allah bahut jald hisaab lene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مَا ذَاۤ اُحِلَّ لَهُمْ: Un ke liye kya halal hua?}** Yeh aayat Hazrat Adi bin Hatim aur Hazrat Zaid bin Mahlahal رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ke haq mein naazil hui. Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Hazrat Zaid ka naam "Zaid-ul-Khair" rakha tha. Dono hazraat ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Hum kutte aur baaz ke zariye shikaar karte hain, kya yeh hamare liye halal hai? Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Baghawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 4, 2/8)

Aayat mein **طَیِّبٰتُ** ko halal farmaya gaya hai. Tayyibaat woh cheezein hain jin ki hurmat Quran, Hadees, ijma' ya qiyas mein se kisi se sabit na ho. Ek qawl yeh bhi hai ke tayyibaat woh cheezein hain jinhein saheeh tabiyat wale log pasand karte hain aur khabees woh cheezein hain jin se saheeh tabiyat nafrat karti hai. (Baizawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 4, 2/295)

Is se maloom hua ke kisi cheez ke haram hone par daleel na milna bhi us ke halal hone ke liye kaafi hai.

**{اَلْجَوَارِحِ: Shikari jaanwar.}** Shikari jaanwaron ka kiya hua shikaar bhi halal hai, chahe woh kutte aur cheete jaise darinde hon ya shikra, baaz aur shaheen jaise parinde. Jab unhein is tarah sikhaya jaaye ke jo shikaar karein us mein se khud na khaayen, shikari ke chhorne par shikaar ki taraf jaayen aur bulaane par wapas aa jaayen, to aise jaanwar mu'allam, yani sikhaye hue, kehlaate hain.

**{مِمَّاۤ اَمْسَكْنَ عَلَیْكُمْ: Jo woh tumhare liye rok dein.}** Tumhare sikhaye hue kutte ya doosre shikari jaanwar jab shikaar kar ke laayein aur us mein se khud kuchh na khaayen, to jaanwar mar gaya ho tab bhi halal hai. Agar kutte ne us mein se kuchh kha liya ho to haram hai, kyunki us ne apne liye shikaar kiya, tumhare liye nahin.

### Shikari jaanwar ke shikaar ke halal hone ki shartein

(1) Shikari jaanwar Musalman ya Kitaabi ka ho aur sikhaya hua ho.

(2) Us ne shikaar ko zakhm laga kar maara ho.

(3) Shikari jaanwar ko بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ keh kar chhoda gaya ho.

(4) Agar shikaar shikari ke paas zinda pahunche to use بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ keh kar zabh kare.

In mein se koi shart na paayi jaaye to shikaar halal nahin hoga. Misal ke taur par shikari jaanwar mu'allam na ho, us ne zakhm na kiya ho, shikaar par chhorte waqt jaan-boojh kar بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ na padha ho, shikaar zinda pahuncha ho aur use zabh na kiya gaya ho, mu'allam jaanwar ke saath ghair-mu'allam jaanwar bhi shikaar mein shareek ho gaya ho, aisa shikari jaanwar shareek ho gaya ho jis ko chhorte waqt بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ na padha gaya ho, ya shikari jaanwar Majusi kafir ka ho, to in tamam sooraton mein shikaar haram hai.

### Teer se shikaar ka shar'i hukm

Teer se shikaar ka bhi yahi hukm hai. Agar بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ keh kar teer maara aur shikaar zakhmi ho kar mar gaya to halal hai. Agar na mare to dobara بِسْمِ اللہِ اللہُ اَکْبَرْ padh kar use zabh kare. Agar us par بِسْمِ اللہْ na padhi, teer ka zakhm use na laga, ya zinda milne ke baad use zabh na kiya, to in sab sooraton mein shikaar haram hai.

**Note:** Shikaar ke masail ki mazeed tafseel ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 17 ka mutala'ah karein.

## Ayat 5:5

`Source: tafseerId=50738; ayatId=679; surah=5; ayat=5; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلْیَوْمَ اُحِلَّ لَكُمُ الطَّیِّبٰتُؕ-وَ طَعَامُ الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ حِلٌّ لَّكُمْ۪-وَ طَعَامُكُمْ حِلٌّ لَّهُمْ٘-وَ الْمُحْصَنٰتُ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنٰتِ وَ الْمُحْصَنٰتُ مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ اِذَاۤ اٰتَیْتُمُوْهُنَّ اُجُوْرَهُنَّ مُحْصِنِیْنَ غَیْرَ مُسٰفِحِیْنَ وَ لَا مُتَّخِذِیْۤ اَخْدَانٍؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّكْفُرْ بِالْاِیْمَانِ فَقَدْ حَبِطَ عَمَلُهٗ٘-وَ هُوَ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ مِنَ الْخٰسِرِیْنَ۠    (5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aaj tumhare liye paak cheezein halal kar di gayi hain. Ahl-e-Kitaab ka khana tumhare liye halal hai aur tumhara khana un ke liye halal hai. Aur paak-daaman Musalman auratein aur un logon ki paak-daaman auratein bhi tumhare liye halal hain jinhein tum se pehle Kitaab di gayi, jab tum unhein un ke mahr do, nikah karne wale ho, na khuli badkaari karne wale aur na chhupi dosti banane wale. Aur jo iman se phir kar kafir ho gaya to us ka amal barbad ho gaya aur woh Aakhirat mein nuqsan uthane walon mein se hoga.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلْیَوْمَ اُحِلَّ لَكُمُ الطَّیِّبٰتُ: Aaj tumhare liye paak cheezein halal kar di gayi hain.}** Ahl-e-Kitaab ka zabh kiya hua jaanwar bhi Musalmanon ke liye halal hai, chahe Yahoodi zabh kare ya Isaai, aur chahe mard zabh kare, aurat ya samajhdaar bachcha. Lekin yeh bahut zaroori hai ke woh log haqeeqi Ahl-e-Kitaab hon. Aaj kal Isaaiyon ki bahut badi tadaad dahriya aur Khuda ki munkir ho chuki hai. Is liye na un ka zabiha halal hai aur na un ki auratein.

### Ahl-e-Kitaab se nikah ke chand aham masail

(1) Ahl-e-Kitaab ki aurat se nikah halal hai, lekin shart yeh hai ke woh haqeeqi Ahl-e-Kitaab ho, dahriya na ho. Aaj kal aise log bahut hain.

(2) Yeh ijaazat Dar-ul-Islam mein rehne wali zimmi Ahl-e-Kitaab aurat ke saath hai. Aaj ke zamane mein jo Ahl-e-Kitaab hain, woh harbi hain. Harbiyyah Ahl-e-Kitaab se nikah karna makrooh-e-tahrimi hai.

(3) Yeh ijaazat sirf Musalman mardon ke liye hai. Musalman aurat ka nikah Kitaabi mard se qat'an haram hai.

(4) Ahl-e-Kitaab auraton mein se achhe kirdar wali se nikah kiya jaaye. Yeh mustahab hai.

(5) Ahl-e-Kitaab aurat se jinsi ta'alluq sirf nikah ke zariye qaim kiya jaaye. Poshida dosti lagana, ya poshida aur elaniya badkaari karna, un ke saath bhi haram hai.

(6) Ahl-e-Kitaab aurat ko bhi mahr diya jaayega.

**{غَیْرَ مُسٰفِحِیْنَ: Na khuli badkaari karte hue.}** Najaiz tareeqe par khuli badkaari se be-dhadhak zina karna aur poshida aashna banane se poshida zina murad hai.

**{وَ مَنْ یَّكْفُرْ بِالْاِیْمَانِ: Aur jo iman se phir kar kafir ho gaya.}** Aayat ke aakhir mein murtad ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke us ke tamam nek amal barbad ho jaate hain aur Aakhirat mein us ke liye koi ajr-o-sawab baqi nahin rehta.

## Ayat 5:6

`Source: tafseerId=50739; ayatId=680; surah=5; ayat=6; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا قُمْتُمْ اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ فَاغْسِلُوْا وُجُوْهَكُمْ وَ اَیْدِیَكُمْ اِلَى الْمَرَافِقِ وَ امْسَحُوْا بِرُءُوْسِكُمْ وَ اَرْجُلَكُمْ اِلَى الْكَعْبَیْنِؕ-وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ جُنُبًا فَاطَّهَّرُوْاؕ-وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ مَّرْضٰۤى اَوْ عَلٰى سَفَرٍ اَوْ جَآءَ اَحَدٌ مِّنْكُمْ مِّنَ الْغَآىٕطِ اَوْ لٰمَسْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ فَلَمْ تَجِدُوْا مَآءً فَتَیَمَّمُوْا صَعِیْدًا طَیِّبًا فَامْسَحُوْا بِوُجُوْهِكُمْ وَ اَیْدِیْكُمْ مِّنْهُؕ-مَا یُرِیْدُ اللّٰهُ لِیَجْعَلَ عَلَیْكُمْ مِّنْ حَرَجٍ وَّ لٰـكِنْ یُّرِیْدُ لِیُطَهِّرَكُمْ وَ لِیُتِمَّ نِعْمَتَهٗ عَلَیْكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَشْكُرُوْنَ(6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Jab tum namaz ki taraf kharay hone lago to apne chehron ko aur apne haath kohniyon tak dho lo aur saron ka masah karo aur takhnon tak paon dho lo. Aur agar tum be-ghusl ho to khoob paak ho jao. Aur agar tum beemar ho ya safar mein ho ya tum mein se koi bait-ul-khala se aaya ho ya tum ne auraton se sohbat ki ho aur in sooraton mein paani na pao to paak mitti se tayammum kar lo, to apne chehron aur haathon ka us se masah kar lo. Allah nahin chahta ke tum par kuchh tangi rakhe, lekin woh yeh chahta hai ke tumhein khoob paak kar de aur apni nemat tum par poori kar de taake tum shukr ada karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِذَا قُمْتُمْ اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ: Jab namaz ki taraf kharay hone lago.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein wuzu aur tayammum ka tareeqa aur un ki zaroorat kab hoti hai, yeh bayan kiya gaya hai.

### Wuzu ke faraiz

Wuzu ke chaar farz hain: (1) Chehra dhona. (2) Kohniyon samet dono haath dhona. (3) Chauthai sar ka masah karna. (4) Takhnon samet dono paon dhona.

### Wuzu ke chand ahkaam

(1) Jitna dhone ka hukm hai us se kuchh zyada dho lena mustahab hai, kyunki jahan tak wuzu ke azaa dhoye jaayenge, Qiyamat ke din wahan tak azaa roshan honge. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Wuzu, Baab Fazl-ul-Wuzu wal-Ghurr wal-Muhajjaloon, 1/71, hadees: 136)

(2) Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur baaz Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم har namaz ke liye naya wuzu farmaya karte, jabke aksar Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم jab tak wuzu na toot jaata, usi wuzu se ek se zyada namazain ada farmate. Ek wuzu se zyada namazain ada karna Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se bhi sabit hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Wuzu, Baab-ul-Wuzu min ghair-e-hadath, 1/95, hadees: 214-215; Umdat-ul-Qaari, Kitaab-ul-Wuzu, Baab-ul-Wuzu min ghair-e-hadath, 2/590, tehat-ul-hadees: 214)

(3) Agarche ek wuzu se bhi bahut si farz aur nafl namazain durust hain, lekin har namaz ke liye alag wuzu karna zyada barkat aur sawab ka sabab hai. Baaz mufassireen ka qawl hai ke Islam ke ibtidaai zamane mein har namaz ke liye alag wuzu farz tha. Baad mein yeh hukm mansookh kar diya gaya aur jab tak wuzu todne wali koi cheez na ho, ek hi wuzu se farz aur nafl sab ada karna jaiz ho gaya. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 6, safha 274)

(4) Yaad rahe ke jahan dhone ka hukm hai wahan dhona hi zaroori hai, wahan masah nahin kar sakte. Jaise paon ko dhona hi zaroori hai, masah karne ki ijaazat nahin. Haan, agar moze pehne hon to un ki sharaait paayi jaane ki soorat mein mozon par masah kar sakte hain, kyunki yeh mashhoor ahadees se sabit hai.

**{وَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ جُنُبًا: Aur agar tum janabat ki haalat mein ho.}** Janabat ka aam fahm matlab yeh hai ke shahwat ke saath mani ka kharij hona.

### Janabat ke asbaab aur un ka shar'i hukm

Janabat ke kai asbaab hain: (1) Jaagte mein shahwat ke saath uchhal kar mani ka kharij hona. (2) Sote mein ehtilaam ho jaana. (3) Humbistari karna, agarche mani kharij na ho. Is ka hukm yeh hai ke ghusl kiye baghair namaz padhna, tilawat-e-Quran karna, Quran-e-Paak ko chhoona aur masjid mein daakhil hona najaiz hai. Jo kaam janabat ki haalat mein mana hain, haiz aur nifaas ki haalat mein bhi mana honge. Lekin jab tak aurat haiz ya nifaas ki haalat mein hai, ghusl karne se paak nahin hogi, jabke junubi ghusl karne se paak ho jaata hai. Isi tarah haiz aur nifaas ki haalat mein biwi se sohbat karna bhi mana hai, jabke janabat ki haalat mein sohbat karna mana nahin. (Ahkaam-ul-Quran, Surah Al-Maidah, Baab-ul-Ghusl min-al-Janabah, 2/457)

Haiz aur nifaas se bhi ghusl lazim ho jaata hai. Haiz ka mas'alah Surah Al-Baqarah, aayat number 222 mein guzar chuka hai aur nifaas se ghusl lazim hona ijma' se sabit hai. Tayammum ka bayan Surah An-Nisa, aayat number 43 mein tafseel se guzar chuka hai. Mazeed tafseel jaanne ke liye fiqhi kitaabon ka mutala'ah karein. [1]

[1] Wuzu, ghusl aur tayammum ke baare mein shar'i masail jaanne ke liye Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دامَتْ بَرَکاتُہُمُ الْعَالِیَہ ki tasneef "Namaz ke Ahkaam" ka mutala'ah karna bhi bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 5:7

`Source: tafseerId=50740; ayatId=681; surah=5; ayat=7; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَةَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ وَ مِیْثَاقَهُ الَّذِیْ وَاثَقَكُمْ بِهٖۤۙ-اِذْ قُلْتُمْ سَمِعْنَا وَ اَطَعْنَا٘-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ عَلِیْمٌۢ بِذَاتِ الصُّدُوْرِ(7)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَةَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ: Aur apne upar Allah ka ehsaan yaad karo.}** Is aayat mein Bai'at-e-Aqabah ya Bai'at-e-Rizwan ki taraf ishara hai. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 7, safha 276)

Majmuei taur par aayat-e-mubarakah ka khulasa yeh hai ke ae Sahaba! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka apne upar ehsaan yaad karo ke us ne tumhein Musalman banaya aur tumhare liye aasaan ahkaam bheje. Us ne saari zameen ko masjid aur paak karne wali banaya. Is ke saath us ahad aur muahide ko bhi yaad karo jo tum ne Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se bai'at karte waqt Bai'at-e-Aqabah ki raat aur Bai'at-e-Rizwan mein kiya tha. Is muahide mein Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne arz kiya tha ke hum Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka har hukm har haal mein sunenge aur maanenge.

### Aayat وَ اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَةَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ se maloom hone wale masail

Is aayat se chand masail maloom hue:

(1) Insaan har neki Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taufeeq se karta hai. Is liye us par fakhr na kare, balki Rab-e-Kareem عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shukr ada kare.

(2) Bai'at-e-Aqabah aur Bai'at-e-Rizwan wale saare Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke pyaare aur maqbool bande hain, jinhein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne us bai'at ka sharaf bakhsha. Usi bai'at ko yahan Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki nemat qarar diya gaya hai.

(3) Un tamam Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ne in bai'aton ke saare waade poore kiye aur Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم waade ke sachche the, kyunki Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne yahan un ke waade baghair tardeed zikr farmaye.

## Ayat 5:8

`Source: tafseerId=50741; ayatId=682; surah=5; ayat=8; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا كُوْنُوْا قَوّٰمِیْنَ لِلّٰهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِالْقِسْطِ٘-وَ لَا یَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَاٰنُ قَوْمٍ عَلٰۤى اَلَّا تَعْدِلُوْاؕ-اِعْدِلُوْا- هُوَ اَقْرَبُ لِلتَّقْوٰى٘-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ خَبِیْرٌۢ بِمَا تَعْمَلُوْنَ(8)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{كُوْنُوْا قَوّٰمِیْنَ لِلّٰهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِالْقِسْطِ: Insaaf ke saath gawahi dete hue Allah ke hukm par khoob qaim ho jao.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah mein adl-o-insaaf ka hukm diya gaya hai aur saaf bata diya gaya ke kisi bhi qism ki qaraabat ya adaawat tumhein adl se na hata sake.

### Adl-o-insaaf ke do aala namoone

Yahan adl-o-insaaf ke do aala namoone pesh kiye ja rahe hain jin se Islam ki taleemaat ka naqsha saamne aata hai.

(1) Mulk Ghassaan ka badshah Jablah bin Ayham apne kuchh saathiyon ke saath Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki bargah mein haazir ho kar Musalman ho gaya. Kuchh din baad Ameer-ul-Momineen Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ Hajj ke irade se nikle to Jablah bin Ayham bhi is qafile mein shareek ho gaya.

Makkah Mukarramah pahunchne ke baad ek din tawaaf ke dauran kisi dehaati Musalman ka paon us ki chaadar par pad gaya aur chaadar kandhe se utar gayi. Jablah bin Ayham ne poocha: Tum ne meri chaadar par qadam kyun rakha? Us ne kaha: Main ne jaan-boojh kar qadam nahin rakha, ghalati se pad gaya tha. Yeh sun kar Jablah ne us ke chehre par zor daar thappad maar diya. Thappad ki wajah se us ke do daant toot gaye aur naak bhi zakhmi ho gayi.

Woh dehaati Musalman Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki bargah mein haazir hua aur Jablah bin Ayham ke sulook ki shikayat ki. Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Jablah bin Ayham ko talab kiya aur poocha: Kya tum ne is dehaati ko thappad maara hai? Jablah ne kaha: Haan, main ne thappad maara hai. Agar is Haram ki hurmat ka khayal na hota to main use qatl kar deta.

Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Ae Jablah! Tum ne apne jurm ka iqraar kar liya hai. Ab ya to tum is dehaati se maafi maango ya main tum se is ka qisaas loonga. Jablah ne hairat se kaha: Kya aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ is ghareeb dehaati ki wajah se mujh se qisaas lenge, halaanke main badshah hoon?

Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Islam qabool karne ke baad huqooq mein tum dono barabar ho. Jablah ne arz ki: Mujhe ek din ki mohlat de dijiye, phir mujh se qisaas le lijiye. Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne us dehaati se poocha: Kya tum ise mohlat dete ho? Dehaati ne arz ki: Ji haan. Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne use mohlat de di. Mohlat milne ke baad raaton-raat Jablah bin Ayham Ghassani Mulk-e-Shaam ki taraf bhaag gaya aur us ne Isaai mazhab ikhtiyar kar liya. (Futuh-ush-Shaam, Zikr-e-Fath-e-Hims, safha 100, al-juz-ul-awwal)

(2) Ek martaba Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur Hazrat Ubai bin Ka'b رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka kisi baat par ikhtilaaf hua. Dono ne tay kiya ke un ke muamale ka faisla Hazrat Zaid bin Saabit رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ karein. Chunanche dono faisle ke liye Hazrat Zaid bin Saabit رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke ghar pahunche.

Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Hum tumhare paas is liye aaye hain taake tum hamare muamale ka faisla kar do. Hazrat Zaid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne bistar ke darmiyan se jagah khaali karte hue arz ki: Ae Ameer-ul-Momineen! Yahan tashreef rakhiye. Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Yeh tumhara pehla zulm hai jo tum ne faisle ke liye muqarrar hone ke baad kiya. Main to apne fareeq ke saath hi baithunga. Yeh keh kar Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ Hazrat Ubai bin Ka'b رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke saath Hazrat Zaid رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke saamne baith gaye.

Muqadame ki karwai shuru hui. Hazrat Ubai bin Ka'b رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne daawa kiya aur Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne us ka inkaar kiya. Hazrat Ubai bin Ka'b رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ apne dawe ke saboot mein gawah pesh na kar sake, to shar'i usool ke mutabiq Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ par qasam khana lazim aata tha. Hazrat Zaid bin Saabit رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki shakhsiyat aur rutbe ka lihaz karte hue Hazrat Ubai bin Ka'b رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se kaha: Aap Ameer-ul-Momineen رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se qasam lene se darguzar kijiye.

Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne foran halaf utha liya aur qasam kha kar farmaya: Zaid us waqt tak mansab-e-qaza, yani judge banne, ka ahl nahin ho sakta jab tak Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur ek aam shakhs us ke nazdeek muqadame ke maamale mein barabar na ho jaayen. (Ibn Asaakir, Zikr man ismuhu Zaid, Zaid bin Saabit bin Ad-Dahhaak, 19/319)

## Ayat 5:9

`Source: tafseerId=50742; ayatId=683; surah=5; ayat=9; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَعَدَ اللّٰهُ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِۙ-لَهُمْ مَّغْفِرَةٌ وَّ اَجْرٌ عَظِیْمٌ(9)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ: Aur unhone achhe amal kiye.}** Achhe aamaal se murad har woh amal hai jo Allah Ta'ala ki raza ka sabab bane. Is mein faraiz-o-wajibat, sunnatain, mustahabbaat, jaani aur maali ibaadatain, Huqooqullah, Huqooq-ul-Ibaad waghera sab shaamil hain.

### Nek aamaal ki targheeb

Targheeb ke liye ek hadees-e-mubarakah pesh ki jaati hai. Hazrat Muaz bin Jabal رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: "Main ek safar mein Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke humraah tha. Ek roz chalte chalte main Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke qareeb ho gaya aur arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Mujhe aisa amal bataiye jo mujhe Jannat mein daakhil kare aur Jahannam se door rakhe.

"Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: Tum ne mujh se ek bahut badi baat ka sawaal kiya. Albatta jis ke liye Allah Ta'ala aasaan farma de, us ke liye aasaan hai. Tum Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat karo aur us ke saath kisi ko shareek na thehrao, namaz qaim karo, zakat ada karo, Ramazan ke roze rakho aur Baitullah Shareef ka Hajj karo.

"Phir irshad farmaya: Kya main tumhein neki ke darwaaze na bataun? Roza dhaal hai aur sadaqah gunahon ko is tarah bujha deta hai, jaise paani aag ko bujhata hai. Raat ke darmiyani hisse mein insaan ka namaz padhna bhi gunahon ko mita deta hai." Phir yeh aayat **تَتَجَافٰى جُنُوْبُهُمْ عَنِ الْمَضَاجِعِ** (tarjumah: un ki karwatein bistaron se alag rehti hain) se le kar **یَعْمَلُوْنَ** tak tilawat farmayi.

"Phir irshad farmaya: Kya main tumhein har cheez ke sar, sutoon aur kohan ki bulandi na bata doon? Main ne arz ki: Ji haan, Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Irshad farmaya: Tamam cheezon ka sar Islam hai, us ka sutoon namaz hai aur kohan ki bulandi jihad hai.

"Phir irshad farmaya: Kya main tumhein in sab ki asal ki khabar na de doon? Main ne arz ki: Kyun nahin, Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! To Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne apni zabaan-e-mubarak pakar kar irshad farmaya: Ise roko.

"Main ne arz ki: Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke pyaare Nabi صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Kya zabaani guftagu par bhi hamara muakhazah hoga? Irshad farmaya: Teri maa tujhe roye! Logon ko aundhe munh Jahannam mein un ki zabanon ki kaati hui kheti, yani guftagu, giraati hai." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Iman, Baab ma jaa'a fi hurmat-is-Salaat, 4/280, hadees: 2625) [1]

**{وَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا: Aur jinhon ne kufr kiya.}** Is aayat se maloom hua ke hamesha Jahannami sirf kafir hain, jabke Musalman hamesha ke liye Jahannam mein nahin rahenge.

[1] Nek aamaal ki ragbat aur zaban ki hifazat ka jazba paida karne ke liye Dawat-e-Islami ke saath wabasta ho jaana chahiye.

## Ayat 5:11

`Source: tafseerId=50743; ayatId=685; surah=5; ayat=11; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَتَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ اِذْ هَمَّ قَوْمٌ اَنْ یَّبْسُطُوْۤا اِلَیْكُمْ اَیْدِیَهُمْ فَكَفَّ اَیْدِیَهُمْ عَنْكُمْۚ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-وَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ فَلْیَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ۠    (11)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Apne upar Allah ka ehsaan yaad karo jab ek qaum ne irada kiya ke tumhari taraf apne haath badhaayein, to Allah ne un ke haath tum par se rok diye. Allah se darte raho aur Musalmanon ko Allah hi par bharosa karna chahiye.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِذْ هَمَّ قَوْمٌ: Jab ek qaum ne irada kiya.}** Is aayat-e-mubarakah ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke ek martaba Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne ek jagah qiyam farmaya. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم alag alag darakhton ke saaye mein aaraam karne lage. Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne apni talwar ek darakht par latka di.

Ek a'raabi mauqa pa kar aaya. Us ne chhup kar talwar li, use kheench kar Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se kaha: “Aap ko mujh se kaun bachayega?” Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah.” Us ne do ya teen baar yahi sawaal kiya aur har baar jawab mila: “Allah.” Phir Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne us ke haath se talwar gira di.

Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne talwar pakar kar farmaya: “Ab tujhe mujh se kaun bachayega?” Us ne arz ki: “Mujhe koi nahin bacha sakta.” Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ko bulaya aur apne paas baithe hue us a'raabi ki harkat unhein batai. Phir Rahmat-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne use maaf farma diya. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 11, 4/322)


## Ayat 5:12

`Source: tafseerId=50744; ayatId=686; surah=5; ayat=12; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لَقَدْ اَخَذَ اللّٰهُ مِیْثَاقَ بَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَۚ-وَ بَعَثْنَا مِنْهُمُ اثْنَیْ عَشَرَ نَقِیْبًاؕ-وَ قَالَ اللّٰهُ اِنِّیْ مَعَكُمْؕ-لَىٕنْ اَقَمْتُمُ الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اٰتَیْتُمُ الزَّكٰوةَ وَ اٰمَنْتُمْ بِرُسُلِیْ وَ عَزَّرْتُمُوْهُمْ وَ اَقْرَضْتُمُ اللّٰهَ قَرْضًا حَسَنًا لَّاُكَفِّرَنَّ عَنْكُمْ سَیِّاٰتِكُمْ وَ لَاُدْخِلَنَّكُمْ جَنّٰتٍ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْاَنْهٰرُۚ-فَمَنْ كَفَرَ بَعْدَ ذٰلِكَ مِنْكُمْ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ السَّبِیْلِ(12)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak Allah ne Bani Israeel se ahad liya aur hum ne un mein baarah sardaar muqarrar kiye aur Allah ne farmaya: Beshak main tumhare saath hoon. Agar tum namaz qaim rakho, zakat dete raho, mere Rasoolon par iman lao, un ki ta'zeem karo aur Allah ko achha qarz do to beshak main tum se tumhare gunah mita doonga aur zaroor tumhein un baaghon mein daakhil karunga jin ke neeche nahrein jaari hain. To is ahad ke baad tum mein se jis ne kufr kiya to woh zaroor seedhi raah se bhatak gaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَقَدْ اَخَذَ اللّٰهُ مِیْثَاقَ بَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ: Aur beshak Allah ne Bani Israeel se ahad liya.}** Is aayat ka mafhoom samajhne ke liye yeh waqia yaad rakhein. Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se wada farmaya tha ke unhein aur un ki qaum ko muqaddas zameen yani Shaam ka waaris banayega, jahan Kan'ani jabbaar log rehte the.

Fir'aun ke halaak hone ke baad Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Allah ka hukm hua ke Bani Israeel ko Arz-e-Muqaddasah, yani Bait-ul-Muqaddas, ki taraf le jaayen. Farmaya gaya: Main ne ise tumhare liye rehne ki jagah banaya hai, to wahan jao aur wahan ke dushmanon se jihad karo; main tumhari madad karunga. Aur ae Musa! Apni qaum ke har giroh mein se ek sardaar banao. Is tarah baarah sardaar muqarrar karo; har sardaar apni qaum ke hukm maanne aur ahad poora karne ka zimmedaar ho.

Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne baarah sardaar muntakhab kiye aur Bani Israeel ko le kar rawana hue. Jab Ariha ke qareeb pahunche to un sardaron ko dushmanon ke haalaat maloom karne bheja. Unhone dekha ke woh log bahut bade qad-o-qamat, nihayat taaqatwar aur haibat wale hain. Yeh sardaar un se dar kar laut aaye aur apni qaum ko sab haal bata diya, halaanke unhein aisa karne se mana kiya gaya tha.

Sab ne ahad tod diya, siwaye do aadmiyon ke: ek Kaalib bin Yuqna aur doosre Yusha' bin Noon. Yeh dono apne ahad par qaim rahe. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 12, safha 277)

Is pasmanzar mein aayat ka mafhoom yeh hai ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne Bani Israeel se ahad liya ke woh Allah ki ibaadat karein, us ke saath kisi ko shareek na karein aur Taurait ke ahkaam par amal karein. Fir jabbaar qaum se jihad ke liye un mein baarah sardaar banaye gaye. Allah Ta'ala ne unhein farmaya: Main tumhare saath hoon aur tumhari madad karunga.

Agar tum namaz qaim rakho, zakat dete raho, mere Rasoolon par iman lao, un ki ta'zeem karo aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko qarz-e-hasan do, yani us ki raah mein kharch karo, to main tumhare gunah maaf kar dunga aur tumhein Jannat mein daakhil karunga. Aayat mein Rasoolon par iman lane ke saath un ki ta'zeem ka bhi hukm diya gaya hai. Is se maloom hua ke Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki ta'zeem sab se aham faraiz mein se hai.

## Ayat 5:13

`Source: tafseerId=50745; ayatId=687; surah=5; ayat=13; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَبِمَا نَقْضِهِمْ مِّیْثَاقَهُمْ لَعَنّٰهُمْ وَ جَعَلْنَا قُلُوْبَهُمْ قٰسِیَةًۚ-یُحَرِّفُوْنَ الْكَلِمَ عَنْ مَّوَاضِعِهٖۙ-وَ نَسُوْا حَظًّا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُوْا بِهٖۚ-وَ لَا تَزَالُ تَطَّلِعُ عَلٰى خَآىٕنَةٍ مِّنْهُمْ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا مِّنْهُمْ فَاعْفُ عَنْهُمْ وَ اصْفَحْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِیْنَ(13)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To un ke ahad todne ki wajah se hum ne un par laanat ki aur un ke dil sakht kar diye. Woh Allah ki baaton ko un ke maqamat se badal dete hain aur unhone un nasihaton ka bada hissa bhula diya jo unhein ki gayi thin. Aur tum un mein se chand ek ke siwa sab ki kisi na kisi khayanat par muttali hote rahoge. To unhein maaf kar do aur un se darguzar karo. Beshak Allah ehsaan karne walon se muhabbat farmata hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَبِمَا نَقْضِهِمْ: To un ke ahad todne ki wajah se.}** Bani Israeel ne Allah ka ahad toda, Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baad aane wale Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko jhutlaya, unhein shaheed kiya aur Taurait ke ahkaam ki mukhalifat ki. Unhone un aayaat ko bhi badal diya jin mein Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki na't-o-sifat ka zikr tha. Unhone Taurait ki bahut si hidayaat bhi bhula di thin ke Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki pairwi karein aur Aap ﷺ par iman laayein. In harkaton ke natije mein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne un par laanat ki aur un ke dil sakht kar diye.

### Gunahon ki wajah se dil sakht ho jaate hain

Is se maloom hua ke bad-amaliyon ki wajah se bhi dil sakht ho jaate hain. Hazrat Yahya bin Mu'aaz رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Aansu dilon ki sakhti ki wajah se khushk hote hain. Dilon ki sakhti gunahon ki kasrat se hoti hai aur gunah aibon ki zyadaati ki wajah se bahut ho jaate hain.” (Shu'ab-ul-Iman, As-Saabi' wal-Arba'oon min Shu'ab-ul-Iman, fasl fi at-tab'i alal-qalb aw ar-rain, 5/446, hadees: 7221)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Sakht dil aadmi Allah Ta'ala se bahut door hota hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, 62-baab minhu, 4/184, hadees: 2419)

Allah Ta'ala humein dil ki sakhti se mehfooz farmaye. Aameen.

**{وَ لَا تَزَالُ تَطَّلِعُ: Aur aap hamesha muttali hote rahenge.}** Sarwar-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko bataya gaya ke Aap hamesha un logon ki khayanaton se muttali hote rahenge. Daghabaazi, khayanat, ahad todna aur Rasoolon se bad-ahdi un ki aur un ke baap dada ki purani aadat hai. Haan, un mein se jo thodi si tadaad iman laayi, woh khayanat karne wali nahin. Un se jo pehle ho chuka us par giraft na farmayein. (Baizawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 13, 2/306)

Baaz mufassireen ka qawl hai ke yeh aayat us qaum ke baare mein naazil hui jis ne pehle Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se ahad kiya, fir use tod diya. Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko un ki is khayanat se muttali farmaya aur yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 13, 1/476)

Is soorat mein ma'ni yeh hain ke un ki ahad-shikni se darguzar kijiye jab tak woh jang se baaz rahein aur jizyah dene se mana na karein.

## Ayat 5:14

`Source: tafseerId=50746; ayatId=688; surah=5; ayat=14; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ قَالُوْۤا اِنَّا نَصٰرٰۤى اَخَذْنَا مِیْثَاقَهُمْ فَنَسُوْا حَظًّا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُوْا بِهٖ۪-فَاَغْرَیْنَا بَیْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةَ وَ الْبَغْضَآءَ اِلٰى یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِؕ-وَ سَوْفَ یُنَبِّئُهُمُ اللّٰهُ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَصْنَعُوْنَ(14)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jinhone da'wa kiya ke hum Nasraani hain, un se hum ne ahad liya to woh un nasihaton ka bada hissa bhula baithe jo unhein ki gayi thin. To hum ne un ke darmiyan Qiyamat ke din tak ke liye dushmani aur bughz daal diya aur qareeb hai ke Allah unhein bata dega jo kuchh woh karte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ قَالُوْۤا اِنَّا نَصٰرٰى: Aur jinhone da'wa kiya ke hum Nasraani hain.}** Yahoodiyon ke baad ab Isaaiyon ka zikr kiya ja raha hai. Farmaya gaya ke jinhone da'wa kiya ke hum Nasraani, yani Allah ke deen ke madadgaar hain, un se bhi Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par iman lane ka ahad liya gaya tha. Lekin woh bhi Injeel ki nasihaton ka bada hissa bhula baithe aur unhone ahad tod diya.

Is par Allah Ta'ala ne Qiyamat tak un ke darmiyan dushmani aur bughz daal diya. Hazrat Qatadah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya ke jab Nasraaniyon ne Allah ki kitaab, yani Injeel, par amal chhor diya, Rasoolon ki na-farmaani ki, faraiz ada na kiye aur Allah ki hudood ki parwah na ki to Allah Ta'ala ne un ke darmiyan dushmani daal di. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 14, 1/477)

Is ka natija yeh hua ke woh girohon mein bat gaye aur ek doosre ko tabah karne lage. Do azeem aalami jangein aur un ki tabahiyan bhi unhi logon ki bad-amaliyon ka natija hain.

## Ayat 5:15

`Source: tafseerId=50747; ayatId=689; surah=5; ayat=15; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ قَدْ جَآءَكُمْ رَسُوْلُنَا یُبَیِّنُ لَكُمْ كَثِیْرًا مِّمَّا كُنْتُمْ تُخْفُوْنَ مِنَ الْكِتٰبِ وَ یَعْفُوْا عَنْ كَثِیْرٍ ﱟ قَدْ جَآءَكُمْ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ نُوْرٌ وَّ كِتٰبٌ مُّبِیْنٌۙ    (15)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Ahl-e-Kitaab! Beshak tumhare paas hamare Rasool tashreef laaye. Woh tum par bahut si woh cheezein zaahir farmate hain jo tum ne Allah ki kitaab se chhupa di thin aur bahut si maaf farma dete hain. Beshak tumhare paas Allah ki taraf se ek Noor aaya aur ek roshan Kitaab.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ: Ae Ahl-e-Kitaab!}** Yahan Yahoodiyon aur Isaaiyon dono se khitaab hai. Farmaya gaya ke ae Ahl-e-Kitaab! Beshak tumhare paas hamare Rasool Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ tashreef laaye. Woh tum par Allah ki kitaab se chhupayi hui bahut si baatein zaahir farmate hain, jaise rajm ki aayaat aur Sarkar-e-Kaainat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke woh ausaaf jo tum ne chhupa diye the. Huzoor Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne unhein bayan farmaya. Yeh Aap ﷺ ka mu'jiza hai.

Tumhari chhupayi hui baatein zaahir karne ke saath Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ bahut si baatein maaf bhi farma dete hain. Aap ﷺ un ka zikr nahin farmate aur na un par muakhazah farmate hain, kyunki Aap ﷺ sirf us baat ka zikr farmate hain jis mein maslihat ho. Yeh sab Huzoor Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki azmat aur shaan ka bayan hai.

**{قَدْ جَآءَكُمْ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ نُوْرٌ: Beshak tumhare paas Allah ki taraf se Noor aaya.}** Is aayat mein Noor se kya murad hai, is baare mein mufassireen ke mukhtalif aqwaal hain. Ek qawl yeh hai ke is se Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki zaat murad hai.

Faqeeh Abu Al-Lais Samarqandi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “وَھُوَ مُحَمَّدٌ صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ وَالْقُرْآنُ” yani Noor se murad Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Quran hain. (Samarqandi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 1/424)

Imam Abu Muhammad Husain bin Mas'ood Baghawi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ likhte hain: “یَعْنِیْ مُحَمَّدًا صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ وَقِیْلَ اَلْاِسْلَامُ” yani Noor se murad Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hain aur ek qawl yeh hai ke is se Islam murad hai. (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 2/17)

Allamah Khazin رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “یَعْنِیْ مُحَمَّدًا صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ اِنَّمَا سَمَّاہُ اللہُ نُوْرًا لِاَنَّہٗ یُھْتَدٰی بِہٖ کَمَا یُھْتَدٰی بِالنُّوْرِ فِی الظُّلَامِ” yani Noor se murad Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hain. Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko Noor is liye farmaya ke jis tarah andhere mein Noor ke zariye hidayat milti hai, isi tarah Aap ﷺ ke zariye bhi hidayat milti hai. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 1/477)

Allamah Jalaal-ud-Deen Suyuti رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne lafz “نُوْرْ” ki tafseer karte hue farmaya: “وَھُوَ النَّبِیُّ صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ” yani Noor se murad Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hain. (Jalalain, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, safha 97)

Allamah Sawi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “وَ سُمِّیَ نُوْرًا لِاَنَّہٗ یُنَوِّرُ الْبَصَائِرَ وَ یَھْدِیْھَا لِلرَّشَادِ وَ لِاَنَّہٗ اَصْلُ کُلِّ نُوْرٍ حِسِّیٍّ وَ مَعْنَوِیٍّ” yani Huzoor Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko is aayat mein Noor is liye kaha gaya ke Aap ﷺ basiraton ko roshan karte hain, unhein raah-e-rushd aur hidayat dete hain, aur is liye bhi ke Aap ﷺ har zahiri Noor aur ma'nawi Noor, jaise ilm aur hidayat, ki asal hain. (Tafseer Sawi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 2/486)

Imam Fakhr-ud-Deen Razi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “اَلنُّوْرُ وَالْکِتَابُ ھُوَ الْقُرْآنُ، وَھٰذَا ضَعِیْفٌ لِاَنَّ الْعَطْفَ یُوْجِبُ الْمُغَایَرَۃَ بَیْنَ الْمَعْطُوْفِ وَالْمَعْطُوْفِ عَلَیْہِ” yani yeh qawl ke Noor aur Kitaab dono se Quran murad hai, kamzor hai, kyunki harf-e-atf ma'toof aur ma'toof alaih ke alag hone ko zaroori karta hai. (Tafseer Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 4/327)

Allamah Sayyid Mahmood Aalusi Baghdadi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “وَھُوَ نُوْرُ الْاَنْوَارِ وَالنَّبِیُّ الْمُخْتَارُ صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ” yani is Noor se murad tamam Noor ka Noor, Nabi-e-Mukhtaar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki zaat hai. (Rooh-ul-Ma'ani, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 15, 5/367)

Allamah Mulla Ali Qaari رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “وَ اَیُّ مَانِعٍ مِنْ اَنْ یُجْعَلَ النَّعْتَانِ لِلرَّسُوْلِ صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ فَاِنَّہٗ نُوْرٌعَظِیْمٌ لِکَمَالِ ظُھُوْرِہٖ بَیْنَ الْاَنْوَارِ وَ کِتَابٌ مُّبِیْنٌ حَیْثُ اَنَّہٗ جَامِعٌ لِجَمِیْعِ الْاَسْرَارِ وَمُظْھِرٌ لِلْاَحْکَامِ وَالْاَحْوَالِ وَالْاَخْبَارِ” yani is baat mein kya rukawat hai ke Noor aur Kitaab-e-Mubeen dono Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki na'at hon? Beshak Huzoor ﷺ anwaar mein apne kamal-e-zuhoor ki wajah se azeem Noor hain aur is haisiyat se Kitaab-e-Mubeen hain ke Aap ﷺ tamam asraar ke jaame' aur ahkaam, ahwaal aur akhbaar ko zaahir farmane wale hain. (Sharh-e-Shifa, Al-Qism-ul-Awwal, Al-Baab-ul-Awwal fi Sana'illahi Ta'ala alaih, Al-Fasl-ul-Awwal, 1/51)

Balki khud Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne apna Noor hona bayan farmaya. Imam Abdul Razzaaq رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ Hazrat Jaabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat karte hain: “قَالَ سَاَلْتُ رَسُوْلَ اللہِ صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ عَنْ اَوَّلِ شَیْئٍ خَلَقَہُ اللہُ تَعَالٰی؟ فَقَالَ ھُوَ نُوْرُ نَبِیِّکَ یَا جَابِرُ خَلَقَہُ اللہُ” yani Hazrat Jaabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Main ne Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se poocha ke Allah Ta'ala ne sab se pehle kya paida farmaya? To Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: “Ae Jaabir! Woh tumhare Nabi ka Noor hai jise Allah Ta'ala ne paida farmaya.” (Al-Juz-ul-Mafqood min Al-Musannaf Abdul Razzaaq, Kitaab-ul-Iman, Baab fi Takhleeq Noor-e-Muhammad ﷺ, safha 63, hadees: 18)

## Ayat 5:16

`Source: tafseerId=50748; ayatId=690; surah=5; ayat=16; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یَّهْدِیْ بِهِ اللّٰهُ مَنِ اتَّبَعَ رِضْوَانَهٗ سُبُلَ السَّلٰمِ وَ یُخْرِجُهُمْ مِّنَ الظُّلُمٰتِ اِلَى النُّوْرِ بِاِذْنِهٖ وَ یَهْدِیْهِمْ اِلٰى صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِیْمٍ(16)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Allah us ke zariye use salaamati ke raaston ki hidayat deta hai jo Allah ki marzi ka taabe' ho jaaye aur unhein apne hukm se tareekiyon se roshni ki taraf le jaata hai aur unhein seedhi raah ki taraf hidayat deta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یَهْدِیْ بِهِ اللّٰهُ: Allah is ke zariye hidayat deta hai.}** Yahan Quran-e-Majeed ki shaan bayan ki gayi hai ke Allah Ta'ala Quran ke zariye us shakhs ko hidayat ata farmata hai jo Allah Ta'ala ki marzi ka taabe' ho jaata hai aur apne tamam aamaal Allah Ta'ala ki raza mein laga deta hai. Allah Ta'ala use kufr, shirk aur gunaahon ki tareekiyon se nikaal kar iman aur nek aamaal ke Noor mein daakhil farma deta hai.

Baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke **بِهٖ** ki zamir se Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ murad hain. Is ma'ni ke mutabiq Allah Ta'ala Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke zariye hidayat ata farmata hai. Ma'nawi taur par yeh baat bilkul durust hai.

## Ayat 5:17

`Source: tafseerId=50749; ayatId=691; surah=5; ayat=17; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** لَقَدْ كَفَرَ الَّذِیْنَ قَالُوْۤا اِنَّ اللّٰهَ هُوَ الْمَسِیْحُ ابْنُ مَرْیَمَؕ-قُلْ فَمَنْ یَّمْلِكُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ شَیْــٴًـا اِنْ اَرَادَ اَنْ یُّهْلِكَ الْمَسِیْحَ ابْنَ مَرْیَمَ وَ اُمَّهٗ وَ مَنْ فِی الْاَرْضِ جَمِیْعًاؕ-وَ لِلّٰهِ مُلْكُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِ وَ مَا بَیْنَهُمَاؕ-یَخْلُقُ مَا یَشَآءُؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ(17)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak woh log kafir ho gaye jinhon ne kaha ke Allah hi Maseeh bin Maryam hai. Tum farma do: Agar Allah Maseeh bin Maryam ko aur us ki maa aur tamaam zameen walon ko halaak karne ka irada farma le to kaun hai jo Allah se bachane ki taaqat rakhta hai? Aur aasmaanon aur zameen ki aur jo kuchh un ke darmiyan hai sab ki baadshahat Allah hi ke liye hai. Woh jo chahta hai paida karta hai aur Allah har shay par Qaadir hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَقَدْ كَفَرَ الَّذِیْنَ قَالُوْا: Beshak woh log kafir ho gaye jinhon ne kaha.}** Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke Najran ke Isaaiyon ne yeh baat kahi thi. Nasraaniyon ke Ya'qubiyyah aur Malakaniyyah firqon ka bhi yahi aqeeda tha ke Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Allah batate the. Woh hulool ke qaail the aur un ka batil aqeeda tha ke Allah Ta'ala Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام mein is tarah hulool kiye hue hai jaise phool mein khushboo aur aag mein garmi. مَعَاذَ اللہ ثُمَّ مَعَاذَاللہ۔ Allah Ta'ala is se paak hai. Allah Ta'ala ne is aayat mein un ke kufr ka hukm diya aur phir un ke aqeede ke fasad ko bayan farmaya.

### Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki uloohiyyat ki tardeed

Is aayat mein Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki uloohiyyat ki kai tareeqon se tardeed hai:

(1) Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko wafaat aa sakti hai. Jise wafaat aa sakti ho, woh Khuda nahin ho sakta.

(2) Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام maa ke pait se paida hue. Jis mein yeh sifat ho, woh Allah nahin ho sakta.

(3) Allah Ta'ala aasmaan aur zameen ki tamam cheezon ka Maalik hai aur har cheez Allah Ta'ala ki bandi hai. Agar kisi mein Allah Ta'ala hulool kiye hue hota to woh Allah Ta'ala ka banda na hota, halaanke Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام khud iqraar farmate hain ke woh Allah Ta'ala ke bande hain.

(4) Allah Ta'ala apni zaat se Khaaliq hai. Agar Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام mein uloohiyyat hoti to Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام bhi apni zaat se Khaaliq hote.

## Ayat 5:18

`Source: tafseerId=50750; ayatId=692; surah=5; ayat=18; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالَتِ الْیَهُوْدُ وَ النَّصٰرٰى نَحْنُ اَبْنٰٓؤُا اللّٰهِ وَ اَحِبَّآؤُهٗؕ-قُلْ فَلِمَ یُعَذِّبُكُمْ بِذُنُوْبِكُمْؕ-بَلْ اَنْتُمْ بَشَرٌ مِّمَّنْ خَلَقَؕ-یَغْفِرُ لِمَنْ یَّشَآءُ وَ یُعَذِّبُ مَنْ یَّشَآءُؕ-وَ لِلّٰهِ مُلْكُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِ وَ مَا بَیْنَهُمَا٘-وَ اِلَیْهِ الْمَصِیْرُ(18)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Yahoodiyon aur Isaaiyon ne kaha: Hum Allah ke bete aur us ke pyaare hain. Ae Habeeb! Tum farma do: Agar aisa hai to phir woh tumhein tumhare gunaahon par azaab kyun deta hai? Balki tum bhi us ki makhlooq mein se aam aadmi ho. Woh jise chahta hai bakhsh deta hai aur jise chahta hai saza deta hai aur aasmaanon aur zameen aur jo kuchh us ke darmiyan hai sab ki saltanat Allah hi ke liye hai aur usi ki taraf phirna hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَالَتِ الْیَهُوْدُ وَ النَّصٰرٰى: Aur Yahoodiyon aur Isaaiyon ne kaha.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Ahl-e-Kitaab Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein aaye aur deen ke maamle mein guftagu karne lage. Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne unhein Islam ki dawat di aur Allah Ta'ala ki na-farmaani par us ke azaab se daraaya. Woh kehne lage: Ae Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Aap humein kis baat se dara rahe hain? Hum to Allah Ta'ala ke bete aur us ke pyaare hain. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 18, 1/478)

Yahoodiyon ke is qawl ka matlab yeh tha ke hum Allah Ta'ala ko aise pyaare hain jaise beta baap ko pyaara hota hai, kyunki beta kitna hi bura ho baap ko pyaara hota hai. Yahan bete se haqeeqi aulaad murad nahin, kyunki woh apne aap ko is ma'ni mein Allah Ta'ala ka beta nahin kehte the.

### Khud ko aamaal se be-niyaaz samajhna Isaaiyon ka aqeeda hai

Is aayat se maloom hua ke apne aap ko aamaal se be-niyaaz samajhna Isaaiyon ka aqeeda hai. Aaj kal baaz Ahl-e-Bait se muhabbat ka da'wa karne wale aur baaz jaahil faqeer bhi yahi aqeeda rakhte hain. Aisa aqeeda kufr hai, kyunki Quran-e-Kareem ne har jagah iman ke saath nek aamaal ka bhi zikr farmaya hai.

**{فَلِمَ یُعَذِّبُكُمْ بِذُنُوْبِكُمْ: Phir woh tumhein tumhare gunaahon par azaab kyun deta hai?}** Yahoodiyon ka aqeeda tha ke hum chalis din Dozakh mein rahenge, yani bachhde ki pooja ki muddat ke barabar. Aayat mein farmaya ja raha hai ke agar tum beton ki tarah Allah Ta'ala ko pyaare ho to tumhein yeh saza bhi kyun milegi? Tum khud iqraar karte ho ke chand ginte hue din Jahannam mein rahoge. Socho, kya koi baap apne bete ko ya koi shakhs apne pyaare ko aag mein jalaata hai? Jab aisa nahin hota to tumhare da'we ka jhoota aur batil hona tumhare apne iqraar se saabit hai.

## Ayat 5:19

`Source: tafseerId=50751; ayatId=693; surah=5; ayat=19; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ قَدْ جَآءَكُمْ رَسُوْلُنَا یُبَیِّنُ لَكُمْ عَلٰى فَتْرَةٍ مِّنَ الرُّسُلِ اَنْ تَقُوْلُوْا مَا جَآءَنَا مِنْۢ بَشِیْرٍ وَّ لَا نَذِیْرٍ٘-فَقَدْ جَآءَكُمْ بَشِیْرٌ وَّ نَذِیْرٌؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ۠    (19)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Kitaab walo! Beshak tumhare paas hamare Rasool tashreef laaye, woh Rasoolon ki tashreef aawari band ho jaane ke arse ke baad tum par hamare ahkaam zaahir farma rahe hain taake tum yeh na kaho ke hamare paas to koi khushkhabri dene wala aur daraane wala aaya hi nahin. To beshak tumhare paas khushkhabri dene wala aur daraane wala tashreef la chuka aur Allah har shay par Qaadir hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ: Ae Ahl-e-Kitaab!}** Allah Ta'ala Ahl-e-Kitaab ko apne ek bahut bade ehsaan ki taraf tawajjuh dila raha hai. Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baad Huzoor Sarwar-e-Kaainat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki tashreef-aawari tak paanch sau unahattar (569) saal tak koi Nabi tashreef nahin laaye. Phir Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki tashreef-aawari aisi azeem nemat hai jaise sakht pyaas mein khushgawaar, jaan-bakhsh thanda paani ya sakht garmi aur ghutan mein khushgawaar baarish.

Is nihayat zaroorat ke waqt Allah Ta'ala ki yeh azeem nemat tum par bheji gayi, is liye tumhein is ki qadr karni chahiye. Ab tumhare paas yeh kehne ka mauqa nahin raha ke hamare paas koi tanbeeh karne wala tashreef nahin laaye.

### Zamana-e-Fitrat se kya murad hai?

Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke darmiyani zamane ko zamana-e-fatrat kehte hain. Is zamane ke logon ke liye sirf aqeeda-e-tauheed kaafi tha, jaise Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke walidain-e-kareem.

## Ayat 5:20

`Source: tafseerId=50752; ayatId=694; surah=5; ayat=20; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى لِقَوْمِهٖ یٰقَوْمِ اذْكُرُوْا نِعْمَةَ اللّٰهِ عَلَیْكُمْ اِذْ جَعَلَ فِیْكُمْ اَنْۢبِیَآءَ وَ جَعَلَكُمْ مُّلُوْكًا ﳓ وَّ اٰتٰىكُمْ مَّا لَمْ یُؤْتِ اَحَدًا مِّنَ الْعٰلَمِیْنَ(20)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur yaad karo jab Moosa ne apni qaum se farmaya: Ae meri qaum! Allah ka ehsaan apne upar yaad karo jab us ne tum mein se Ambiya paida farmaye aur tumhein baadshah banaya aur tumhein woh kuchh ata farmaya jo saare jahan mein kisi ko na diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى لِقَوْمِهٖ: Aur jab Moosa ne apni qaum se farmaya.}** Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne apni qaum ko Allah Ta'ala ka shukr ada karne ki targheeb di aur Allah Ta'ala ki azeem nematon ka zikr farmaya. Khaas taur par teen nematein bayan farmaayin:

(1) Bani Israeel mein Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام tashreef laaye.

(2) Bani Israeel ko hukoomat aur saltanat ata ki gayi. Woh azaad hue aur Fir'aun ke haathon qaid rehne ke baad us ki ghulami se nijaat paayi. Doosra qawl yeh hai ke **مُلُوْكًا** se muraad khadimon aur sawaariyon ka maalik hona hai. Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: Bani Israeel mein jo shakhs khadim, aurat aur sawaari rakhta, use malik kaha jaata tha. (Durr-e-Mansoor, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 20, 3/46)

(3) Bani Israeel ko woh nematein di gayin jo kisi doosri qaum ko nahin di gayin, jaise Mann-o-Salwa ka utarna, darya ka phat jaana aur paani se chashmon ka jaari ho jaana waghera.

### Milad manane ka saboot

Is aayat mein bayan ki gayi pehli nemat se maloom hua ke Ambiya ki tashreef-aawari nemat hai. Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne apni qaum ko is nemat ko yaad karne ka hukm diya, kyunki yeh barkat aur phalon ka sabab hai. Is se Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہِ وَسَلَّمَ ka Milad-e-Mubarak manane aur us ka zikr karne ki bhi wazeh daleel milti hai. Jab Bani Israeel ke Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki tashreef-aawari nemat hai aur use yaad karne ka hukm diya gaya, to Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki tashreef-aawari to is se bhi badi nemat hai. Allah Ta'ala ne khud farmaya:

لَقَدْ مَنَّ اللّٰهُ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ اِذْ بَعَثَ فِیْهِمْ رَسُوْلًا(آل عمران:۱۶۴)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Beshak Allah ne mominon par ehsaan farmaya jab un mein azeem Rasool mab'ous farmaya.

Is liye Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki tashreef-aawari ko yaad karne ka hukm badarja-e-oola hoga.

### Iqtidaar milne par Allah Ta'ala ka shukr ada karne ka behtareen tareeqa

Is aayat mein bayan ki gayi doosri nemat se maloom hua ke hukoomat, saltanat aur iqtidaar bhi Allah Ta'ala ki nemat hain aur in ka shukr ada karna chahiye. Is shukr ka behtareen tareeqa yeh hai ke hukoomat Allah Ta'ala ke hukm ke mutabiq chalaayi jaaye, ghareebon ki madad ki jaaye, logon ke huqooq ada kiye jaayen, zulm khatam kiya jaaye aur mulk ke bashindon ko aman-o-sukoon ki zindagi guzaarne ke mauqe diye jaayen.

### Hukmaranon ke liye naseehat-aamez chaar ahadees

(1) Hazrat Ma'qil bin Yasar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jis shakhs ko Allah Ta'ala ne kisi ri'aya ka hukmaran banaya ho aur woh khair-khwahi ke saath un ki nigraani ka farz ada na kare to woh Jannat ki khushboo tak na paa sakega.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Ahkaam, Baab man istar'a ri'yyatan فلم ينصح, 4/456, hadees: 7150)

(2) Hazrat Ma'qil bin Yasar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ hi se riwayat hai ke Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jin Musalmanon ko kisi wali ki ri'aya banaya jaaye, phir woh wali is haal mein mare ke us ne Musalmanon ke huqooq ghasab kiye hon to Allah Ta'ala us par Jannat haraam farma deta hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Ahkaam, Baab man istar'a ri'yyatan فلم ينصح, 4/456, hadees: 7151)

(3) Hazrat Aaishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا farmati hain: Main ne Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko yeh dua karte hue suna: “Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Meri ummat ka jo shakhs bhi kisi par wali aur haakim ho aur woh un par sakhti kare to Tu bhi us par sakhti farma, aur agar woh un par narmi kare to Tu bhi us par narmi farma.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaarah, Baab fazeelat-ul-imaam-ul-aadil wa uqobat-ul-jaa'ir, 1016, hadees: 19 (1828))

(4) Hazrat Abu Maryam Azdi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jise Allah Ta'ala Musalmanon ke kisi kaam ka wali banaye aur woh un ki zaroorat, be-kasi aur ghurbat mein un se kinara-kashi kare to Allah Ta'ala us ki zaroorat, be-kasi aur ghurbat mein use chhor dega.” (Abu Dawood, Kitaab-ul-Kharaaj wal-Fai wal-Imaarah, Baab feemaa yalzam-ul-imaam min amr-ir-ra'iyyah, 3/188, hadees: 2948)

### Iqtidaar ke bojh se aansu bahaana

Hazrat Umar bin Abdul Aziz رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki zawja-e-mohtarma farmati hain: Jab Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ khilaafat ke mansab par faa'iz hue to ghar aa kar musalle par baith gaye aur rone lage. Itna roye ke Aap ki daadhi mubarak aansuon se tar ho gayi. Main ne arz ki: Ae Ameer-ul-Momineen رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ! Aap kyun ro rahe hain? Aap ne farmaya: “Meri gardan par Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki ummat ka bojh daal diya gaya hai. Jab main ne bhooke faqeeron, mareezon, mazloom qaidiyon, musafiron, boodhon, bachchon aur aulaad-daaron, balki poori saltanat ke museebat-zadon ki khabar-giri ke baare mein socha aur mujhe maloom hai ke mera Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ Qiyamat ke din in ke baare mein mujh se poochhega, to mujhe dar hai ke kahin main un ke baare mein jawab na de sakun. Isi bhaari zimmedari aur us ke baare mein poochh-gachh ki fikr ki wajah se main ro raha hoon.” (Tareekh-ul-Khulafa, Umar bin Abdul Aziz رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, safha 189)

## Ayat 5:23

## Ayat 5:24

`Source: tafseerId=50756; ayatId=698; surah=5; ayat=24; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** قَالُوْا یٰمُوْسٰۤى اِنَّا لَنْ نَّدْخُلَهَاۤ اَبَدًا مَّا دَامُوْا فِیْهَا فَاذْهَبْ اَنْتَ وَ رَبُّكَ فَقَاتِلَاۤ اِنَّا هٰهُنَا قٰعِدُوْنَ(24)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** (Phir qaum ne) kaha: Ae Moosa! Beshak hum to wahan hargiz kabhi nahin jaayenge jab tak woh wahan hain. To aap aur aap ka Rab dono jao aur lado, hum to yahin baithe hue hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّا لَنْ نَّدْخُلَهَاۤ اَبَدًا: Beshak hum wahan hargiz kabhi nahin jaayenge.}** Bani Israeel ne Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke saath jihad mein jaane se saaf inkar kar diya.

### Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki fazeelat

Is waqia se maloom hua ke Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke saathiyon se kahin afzal hain. Un buzurgon ne kisi mushkil mauqe par bhi Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka saath nahin chhoda aur aisa rukha jawab nahin diya. Balki apna sab kuch Huzoor-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par qurban kar diya.

Jis tarah Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ tamam Nabiyon ke sardaar hain, isi tarah Huzoor Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم tamam Ambiya ke Sahaba ke sardaar hain. Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki jaanisaari ke baare mein yeh waqia mulahaza karein.

Jang-e-Badr ke mauqe par Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم se mashwara farmaya. Hazrat Sa'd bin Ubadah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ khade hue aur arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hamari raye maloom karna chahte hain. Us Zaat ki qasam jis ke qabze-e-qudrat mein meri jaan hai, agar Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hamein samandar mein kood jaane ka hukm dein to hum us mein kood jaayenge. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad was-Siyar, Baab Ghazwat-ul-Badr, safha 981, hadees: 83 (1779))

Ansar ke ek buzurg sardaar Hazrat Miqdad bin Aswad رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, hum Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki qaum ki tarah yeh nahin kahenge ke aap aur aap ka Khuda ja kar lado. Balki hum Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke daayen se, baayen se, aage se aur peeche se ladenge. Yeh sun kar Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka chehra-e-anwar khushi se chamak utha. ## Ayat 5:26

## Ayat 5:27

### Haabeel aur Qaabeel ka waqia

Tareekh ke ulama ka bayan hai ke Hazrat Hawwa رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ke har hamal mein ek ladka aur ek ladki paida hote the. Ek hamal ke ladke ka doosre hamal ki ladki ke saath nikah kiya jaata tha. Us waqt insaan sirf Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad mein mahdood the, is liye aapas mein nikah ke siwa koi doosri soorat na thi.

Isi dastoor ke mutabiq Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Qaabeel ka nikah Liwda se karna chahte the, jo Haabeel ke saath paida hui thi, aur Haabeel ka nikah Aqleema se, jo Qaabeel ke saath paida hui thi. Qaabeel is par raazi na hua. Aqleema zyada khoobsurat thi, is liye woh us ka talabgaar tha. Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne farmaya: Woh tumhare saath paida hui hai, is liye tumhari behen hai; tumhara us ke saath nikah halal nahin. Qaabeel ne kaha: Yeh aap ki raye hai, Allah Ta'ala ne yeh hukm nahin diya.

Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne farmaya: Agar tum aisa samajhte ho to tum dono qurbani lao. Jis ki qurbani qabool ho jaaye, wahi Aqleema ka haqdaar hoga. Us zamane mein jo qurbani qabool hoti, aasman se ek aag utar kar use kha leti thi. Qaabeel ne gandum ka ek dher pesh kiya aur Haabeel ne ek bakri qurbani ke liye pesh ki. Aasmani aag ne Haabeel ki qurbani qabool kar li aur Qaabeel ki gandum ko chhor diya.

Is par Qaabeel ke dil mein bahut bughz aur hasad paida hua. Jab Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Hajj ke liye Makkah Mukarramah tashreef le gaye to Qaabeel ne Haabeel se kaha: Main tujhe qatl kar dunga. Haabeel ne poocha: Kyun? Qaabeel ne kaha: Is liye ke teri qurbani qabool hui aur meri qabool na hui, aur tu Aqleema ka haqdaar thehra. Is mein meri zillat hai.

Haabeel ne jawab diya: Allah Ta'ala sirf darne walon ki qurbani qabool farmata hai. Is baat ka matlab yeh tha ke qurbani qabool karna Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka kaam hai. Woh muttaqi logon ki qurbani qabool farmata hai. Tu muttaqi hota to teri qurbani qabool hoti. Yeh khud tere aamaal ka natija hai, is mein mera kya qasoor hai.

Agar tu mujhe qatl karne ke liye meri taraf haath badhaayega to main tujhe qatl karne ke liye apna haath teri taraf nahin badhaunga, kyunki main nahin chahta ke meri taraf se ibtida ho, halaanke main tujh se zyada taaqatwar hoon. Yeh sirf is liye hai ke main Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se darta hoon. Main chahta hoon ke mera, yani mere qatl ka gunah, aur tera gunah, yani jo tu ne pehle kiya ke walid ki na-farmaani ki, hasad kiya aur Allah ke faisle ko na maana, dono tere hi upar aa jaayen aur tu Dozakhi ho jaaye.

### Haabeel aur Qaabeel ke waqia se milne wale sabaq

Yeh waqia bahut si ibrat aur nasihat par mushtamil hai. In mein se ek yeh hai ke insaan ne jo sab se pehle jurm kiye, un mein ek qatl tha. Doosra yeh ke hasad bahut buri cheez hai. Hasad hi ne Shaitaan ko barbaad kiya aur hasad hi ne duniya mein Qaabeel ko tabah kiya.

### Hasad, qatl aur husn-parasti ki muzammat

Is waqia se teen cheezon ki muzammat bhi zahir hoti hai:

(1) **Hasad:** Hazrat Zubair رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Tum mein pichhli ummaton ki beemari sarayat kar gayi: hasad aur bughz. Yeh mund dene wali hai. Main yeh nahin kehta ke baal mundti hai, lekin yeh deen ko mund deti hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab Sifat-ul-Qiyamat, 56-baab, 4/228, hadees: 2518)

(2) **Qatl:** Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain: “Na-haq haraam khoon bahaana halaak kar dene wale un umoor mein se hai jin se nikalne ki koi raah nahin.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ud-Diyaat, Baab qawlullahi Ta'ala: Wa man yaqtul mu'minan, 4/356, hadees: 6863)

(3) **Husn-parasti:** Hazrat Abu Umaamah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Auraton ke mahaasin ki taraf nazar karna Iblees ke zehar mein bujhe hue teeron mein se ek teer hai.” (Nawaadir-ul-Usool, Al-Asl-ur-Raabi' was-Salaasoon, 1/147, hadees: 213)

### Na-haq qatl ki do wa'eedain

(1) Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: "Kisi momin ko qatl karne mein agar zameen-o-aasman wale shareek ho jaayen to Allah Ta'ala un sab ko Jahannam mein dhakel de." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ud-Diyaat, Baab-ul-Hukm fid-Dimaa, 3/100, hadees: 1403)

(2) Hazrat Bara bin Aazib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai, Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: "Allah Ta'ala ke nazdeek duniya ka khatam ho jaana ek Musalman ke zulman qatl se zyada aasaan hai." (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-ud-Diyaat, Baab-ut-Taghleez fi Qatl-i-Muslimin Zulman, 3/261, hadees: 2619)

### Aman-o-salaamati ka mazhab

Yeh aayat-e-mubarakah Islam ki asal taleemaat ko wazeh karti hai ke Islam kis qadar aman-o-salaamati ka mazhab hai aur Islam ki nazar mein insani jaan ki kitni ahmiyat hai. Un logon ko ibrat haasil karni chahiye jo Islam ki asal taleemaat ko peechhe daal kar Islam ke daaman par qatl-o-ghaaratgari ki himayat ka badnuma daagh lagaate hain. Aur un logon ko bhi naseehat haasil karni chahiye jo Musalman kehla kar be-qasoor logon ko bam dhamakon aur khudkush hamlon ke zariye maut ki neend sula kar yeh gumaan karte hain ke unhone Islam ki bahut badi khidmat anjaam de di.

### Qatl ki jaiz sooratein

Qatl ki shadeed manaahi ke saath chand sooraton ko is se alag rakha gaya hai. Aayat-e-mubarakah mein bayan ki gayi sooratein yeh hain:

(1) Qatil ko qisaas mein qatl karna jaiz hai.

(2) Zameen mein fasad phailane wale ko qatl karna jaiz hai. Is ki tafseel agli aayat mein maujood hai.

Is ke ilawa bhi chand sooraton mein Shariat ne qatl ki ijaazat di hai: (1) Shadi-shuda mard ya aurat ko zina karne par hadd ke taur par rajm karna, (2) murtad ko qatl karna, (3) baaghi ko qatl karna.

## Ayat 5:33

`Source: tafseerId=50762; ayatId=707; surah=5; ayat=33; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّمَا جَزٰٓؤُا الَّذِیْنَ یُحَارِبُوْنَ اللّٰهَ وَ رَسُوْلَهٗ وَ یَسْعَوْنَ فِی الْاَرْضِ فَسَادًا اَنْ یُّقَتَّلُوْۤا اَوْ یُصَلَّبُوْۤا اَوْ تُقَطَّعَ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ اَرْجُلُهُمْ مِّنْ خِلَافٍ اَوْ یُنْفَوْا مِنَ الْاَرْضِؕ-ذٰلِكَ لَهُمْ خِزْیٌ فِی الدُّنْیَا وَ لَهُمْ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِیْمٌۙ    (33)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak jo log Allah aur us ke Rasool se ladte hain aur zameen mein fasad barpa karne ki koshish karte hain, un ki saza yahi hai ke unhein khoob qatl kiya jaaye ya unhein sooli de di jaaye ya un ke ek taraf ke haath aur doosri taraf ke paon kaat diye jaayen ya mulk ki sarzameen se jila-watan kar ke door kar diye jaayen. Yeh un ke liye duniya mein ruswai hai aur Aakhirat mein un ke liye bada azaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ یُحَارِبُوْنَ اللّٰهَ وَ رَسُوْلَهٗ: Jo log Allah aur us ke Rasool se ladte hain.}** Pichhli aayaat mein qatl ki ek qisam, yani na-jaiz qatl ka zikr hua. Ab doosri qisam, yani jaiz qatl ka zikr kiya ja raha hai.

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Uraynah qabeelay ke kuchh log Madinah Munawwarah mein Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki bargah mein haazir hue. Wahan ki aab-o-hawa unhein raas na aayi. Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne un se farmaya: Sadaqah ki oontniyon ki charagaah mein jao aur un ka doodh aur peshab piyo. Unhone aisa hi kiya to tandurust ho gaye. Phir woh murtad ho gaye, charwahon par hamla kar ke unhein qatl kar diya aur oont le kar bhaag gaye.

Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko is waqie ki khabar pahunche to Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne un ke peechhe log bheje. Woh unhein giraftaar kar ke le aaye. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne un ke haath paon katwa diye, un ki aankhon mein garam salaiyan phirwain, phir unhein tapte hue maidaan mein chhor diya, yahan tak ke woh mar gaye. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Maghaazi, Baab Qissatu Ukl wa Uraynah, 3/78, hadees: 4192; Tafseeraat-e-Ahmadiyyah, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 33, safha 350)

### Daaku ki saza ki sharaait

Is aayat-e-karimah mein raahzan, yani daaku ki saza ka bayan hai. Raahzan ke liye Shariat ki taraf se saza muqarrar hone ki chand sharaait hain:

(1) Un mein itni taaqat ho ke raahgir un ka muqaabla na kar saken, chahe hathiyar, laathi ya pathar waghera se daaka daala ho.

(2) Shehar se baahar raahzani ki ho, ya shehar mein raat ke waqt hathiyar se daaka daala ho.

(3) Yeh jurm Daar-ul-Islam mein hua ho.

(4) Chori ki tamam sharaait paayi jaati hon.

(5) Taubah karne aur maal wapas karne se pehle Badshah-e-Islam ne unhein giraftaar kar liya ho. (Aalamgiri, Kitaab-us-Sariqah, Al-Baab-ur-Raabi' fi Quttaa-it-Tareeq, 2/186)

### Daaku ki chaar sazayein

Jin logon mein yeh tamam sharaait paayi jaayen, un ke liye Quran-e-Paak mein chaar sazayein bayan ki gayi hain:

(1) Unhein qatl kar diya jaaye.

(2) Sooli par chadha diya jaaye.

(3) Daayan haath aur baayan paon kaat diya jaaye.

(4) Jila-watan kar diya jaaye. Hamare yahan is se murad qaid kar lena hai.

Is saza ki tafseel yeh hai ke daakuon ne kisi Musalman ya zimmi ko qatl kiya aur maal na liya to unhein qatl kiya jaaye. Agar qatl bhi kiya aur maal bhi loota to Badshah-e-Islam ko ikhtiyar hai ke haath paon kaat kar qatl kar de, ya sooli de, ya haath paon kaat kar qatl kare aur phir laash ko sooli par chadha de, ya sirf qatl kar de, ya qatl kar ke sooli par chadha de, ya sirf sooli de. Agar qatl nahin kiya, sirf maal loota to daayan haath aur baayan paon kaat diya jaaye. Agar na maal loota na qatl kiya, sirf daraaya dhamkaya to unhein qaid kar liya jaaye, yahan tak ke sachchi taubah kar lein. (Aalamgiri, Kitaab-us-Sariqah, Al-Baab-ur-Raabi' fi Quttaa-it-Tareeq, 2/186; Durr-e-Mukhtar, Kitaab-us-Sariqah, Baab Qat'-it-Tareeq, 6/181-183, khulasa)

### Islami sazayon ki hikmat

Islam ne har jurm ki saza us ki no'iyat ke mutabiq mukhtalif rakhi hai. Chhote jurm ki saza halki aur bade jurm ki us ki haisiyat ke mutabiq sakht saza rakhi hai, taake zameen mein aman qaim ho aur log be-khauf sukoon aur chain ki zindagi guzaar saken. Is ke ilawa bhi be-shumar hikmatein hain.

Daaka-zani ki saza hi ko le lijiye. Jab tak is par amal raha, tijarati qaafile apne qeemti saaz-o-saamaan ke saath be-khauf-o-khatar safar karte the. Is wajah se tijarat ko bahut farogh mila aur log maashi taur par mazboot ho gaye. Jab se is saza par amal nahin ho raha, tijarati sargarmiyan sab ke saamne hain. Jis mulk mein tijarati saaz-o-saamaan ki naql-o-hamal ki hifazat ka munasib intizam nahin hota, wahan ki baraamad aur daraamad nihayat kam hoti hai aur maeeshat par bahut bura asar padta hai.

Ab haalaat itne naazuk ho chuke hain ke koi bank se paise le kar nikle to raaste mein lut jaata hai, paidal jaane wale ki naqdi aur mobile chheen liya jaata hai, bus ka musaafir bhi mehfooz nahin, aur apni sawaari par jaane wala khud ko zyada khatre mein mehsoos karta hai. Sarkari aur ghair-sarkari amlaak daakuon ki dast-burd se mehfooz nahin. Agar daaka-zani ki bayan ki hui saza par sahi tareeqe se amal ho to in sab ka dimaagh chand dinon mein theek ho jaayega aur har insaan pur-aman mahaul mein zindagi guzaarna shuru kar dega.

## Ayat 5:34

`Source: tafseerId=50763; ayatId=708; surah=5; ayat=34; para=6; volume=2`

## Ayat 5:35

`Source: tafseerId=50764; ayatId=709; surah=5; ayat=35; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ وَ ابْتَغُوْۤا اِلَیْهِ الْوَسِیْلَةَ وَ جَاهِدُوْا فِیْ سَبِیْلِهٖ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ(35)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae iman walo! Allah se daro aur us ki taraf waseela dhoondo aur us ki raah mein jihad karo, is umeed par ke tum falaah pao.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ ابْتَغُوْۤا اِلَیْهِ الْوَسِیْلَةَ: Aur us ki taraf waseela dhoondo.}** Aayat mein waseela ka ma'ni yeh hai: "Jis ke zariye Allah Ta'ala ka qurb haasil ho." Yani Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat, chahe farz ho ya nafl, ada karne ke zariye Allah Ta'ala ka qurb haasil karo.

### Nek bando ko waseela banana jaiz hai

Yaad rakhiye! Rab Ta'ala ki bargah mein us ke nek bando ko waseela banana, un ke waseele se duaein karna aur un ke tawassul se Rab-e-Qadeer عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki bargah mein apni jaiz haajaton ki takmeel ke liye iltija karna na sirf jaiz balki Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ka tareeqa bhi raha hai. Is se muta'alliq teen riwayat dekhiye:

(1) Sahih Bukhari mein Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke jab log qahat mein mubtala hote to Hazrat Umar bin Khattab رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ Hazrat Abbas bin Abdul Muttalib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke waseele se baarish ki dua karte aur arz karte: "اَللّٰہُمَّ اِنَّا کُنَّا نَتَوَسَّلُ اِلَیْکَ بِنَبِیِّنَا فَتَسْقِیْنَا وَاِنَّا نَتَوَسَّلُ اِلَیْکَ بِعَمِّ نَبِیِّنَا فَاسْقِنَا" Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Hum teri bargah mein apne Nabi صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka waseela pakda karte the to Tu hum par baarish barsa deta tha, aur ab hum teri bargah mein apne Nabi صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke chacha jaan رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko waseela banate hain, pas hum par baarish barsa. To log seraab kiye jaate the. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Istisqa, Baab Su'aal-un-Naas-il-Imaam-al-Istisqa, iza Qahat, 1/346, hadees: 1010)

(2) Hazrat Aus bin Abdullah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Madinah Munawwarah ke log sakht qahat mein mubtala ho gaye. Unhone Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا se is ki shikayat ki. Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ne farmaya: Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki qabr-e-anwar ki taraf gaur karo aur us ki chhat mein aasmaan ki taraf ek taaq bana do, yahan tak ke qabr-e-anwar aur aasmaan ke darmiyan chhat na rahe. Logon ne aisa kiya to hum par itni baarish hui ke chara ug aaya aur oont mote ho gaye, yahan tak ke charbi se goya phat pade. Is saal ka naam Aam-ul-Fatq, yani phatne ka saal rakha gaya. (Sunan Daarimi, Baab Ma Akramallahu Ta'ala Nabiyyahu, 1/56, hadees: 92)

(3) Balki khud Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne apne waseele se Allah Ta'ala ki bargah mein dua karne ki taleem ek Sahabi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko di. Hazrat Usman bin Hunaif رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke ek naabeena shakhs bargah-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ mein haazir hua aur dua ka talabgaar hua. To Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne use yeh dua sikhaayi: "اَللّٰہُمَّ اِنِّی اَسْاَلُکَ وَاَتَوَجَّہُ اِلَیْکَ بِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِیِّ الرَّحْمَۃِ یَا مُحَمَّدُ اِنِّی قَدْ تَوَجَّہْتُ بِکَ اِلٰی رَبِّیْ فِیْ حَاجَتِیْ ہَذِہٖ لِتُقْضٰی اَللّٰہُمَّ فَشَفِّعْہُ فِیَّ" Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Main tujh se sawaal karta hoon aur teri taraf Nabi-e-Rahmat Hazrat Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke saath mutawajjih hota hoon. Ae Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Main ne Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke waseele se apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf apni is haajat mein tawajjuh ki hai taake meri haajat poori kar di jaaye. Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Mere liye Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki shafaa'at qubool farma. (Ibn Majah, Kitaab Iqaamat-is-Salaat was-Sunnatu feeha, Baab Ma Jaa'a fi Salaatil-Haajah, 2/156, hadees: 1385)

**Note:** Jo shakhs is hadees-e-paak mein bayan ki hui dua padhna chahe, use chahiye ke dua mein "یَا مُحَمَّدُ" ki jagah "یَا نَبِیَّ اللہ" ya "یَا رَسُوْلَ اللہ" padhe. Is silsile mein mazeed tafseel ke liye Surah Fatiha ki aayat number 4 ki tafseer mein bayan ki hui guftagu dekhiye.

## Ayat 5:36

`Source: tafseerId=50765; ayatId=710; surah=5; ayat=36; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا لَوْ اَنَّ لَهُمْ مَّا فِی الْاَرْضِ جَمِیْعًا وَّ مِثْلَهٗ مَعَهٗ لِیَفْتَدُوْا بِهٖ مِنْ عَذَابِ یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِ مَا تُقُبِّلَ مِنْهُمْۚ-وَ لَهُمْ عَذَابٌ اَلِیْمٌ(36)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak agar kaafir log zameen mein jo kuchh hai woh sab aur us ke barabar itna hi aur us ke saath mila kar Qiyamat ke din ke azaab se chhutkaare ke liye dein to un se qubool nahin kiya jaayega aur un ke liye dardnaak azaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لِیَفْتَدُوْا بِهٖ: Taake fidya de kar jaan chhudaayen.}** Yani agar kaafir duniya ke tamaam maal ke maalik hon aur us ke barabar doosri duniya ke maal ke bhi maalik hon, aur yeh sab kuchh apni jaan ko Qiyamat ke din ke azaab se chhudaane ke liye fidya mein de dein, tab bhi un ka yeh fidya qubool nahin kiya jaayega. Qiyamat ke din kaafiron ko azaab zaroor hoga aur us din un ke paas azaab se bachne ki koi soorat nahin hogi. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 36, 1/491)

Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din jab kaafir ko pesh kiya jaayega to us se kaha jaayega: Agar tere paas itna sona ho ke us se zameen bhar jaaye to kya tu use apne badle mein dene ko tayyar ho jaata? Woh haan mein jawab dega. To us se kaha jaayega: Tum se is se bhi bahut aasaan cheez ka mutaalabah kiya gaya tha, yani imaan ka.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, Baab Man Nuqish-al-Hisaab Uzzib, 4/257, hadees: 6538)

Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se hi riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala Qiyamat ke din us shakhs se, jise Jahannam mein sab se kam azaab hoga, farmayega: Agar tere paas zameen ki tamaam cheezein hon to kya tu unhein apne badle mein de deta? Woh arz karega: Ji haan. Allah Ta'ala farmayega: Main ne is se bhi aasaan cheez tujh se maangi thi jab tu Adam عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ki pusht mein tha ke mere saath kisi ko shareek na thehraana, lekin tu ne inkaar kiya aur mere saath shirk karta raha.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, Baab Sifat-ul-Jannat wan-Naar, 4/261, hadees: 6557)

### Imaan ki hifaazat ki fikr karna zaroori hai

Is aayat se maloom hua ke jab imaan hoga tabhi Qiyamat ke din aamaal ka ajr milega, shafaa'at ka faida hoga, Allah Ta'ala ki rahmat mutawajjih hogi aur Jahannam se chhutkaara milega. Is liye imaan ki hifaazat ki fikr karna nihayat zaroori hai. Hamare buzurgan-e-deen nek aamaal ki kasrat ke bawajood hamesha bure khaatime se darte rehte the. Chunancha:

Jab Hazrat Sufyan Sauri رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki wafaat ka waqt qareeb aaya to aap bahut beqaraar aur muẓtarib hue aur zaar-o-qataar rone lage. Logon ne arz ki: Huzoor! Itni girya-o-zaari na karein. Allah Ta'ala ki bakhshish aur maghfirat aap ke gunahon se kahin zyada hai. Aap رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne jawab diya: Mujhe is baat ka yaqeen nahin ke mera khaatima bilkhair hoga. Agar yeh pata chal jaaye ke mera khaatima imaan par hoga to mujhe pahaadon ke barabar gunahon ki bhi parwah na hogi.

Hazrat Imam Hasan Basri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se poocha gaya: Aap ka kya haal hai? Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Jis shakhs ki kashti darya ke beech ja kar toot jaaye, us ke takhte bikhar jaayen aur har shakhs hichkole khaate hue takhton par nazar aaye, us ka kya haal hoga?” Arz ki gayi: Be-had pareshaan-kun. Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Mera bhi yahi haal hai.”

Ek baar aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ itne dil-girifta hue ke kai saal tak hansi na aayi. Log aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko is tarah dekhte jaise koi qaid-e-tanhaai mein ho aur use saza-e-maut sunaayi jaane wali ho. Aap se is gham aur ranj ka sabab poocha gaya ke itni ibaadat, riyaazat aur mujaahidon ke bawajood aap fikrmand kyun rehte hain? Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: “Mujhe har waqt yeh khatra rehta hai ke kahin mujh se koi aisa kaam na ho jaaye jis ki wajah se Allah Ta'ala naaraaz ho jaaye aur woh farma de: ‘Tum jo chaho karo, lekin meri rahmat tumhare shaamil-e-haal na hogi.’ Bas isi wajah se main apni jaan ghula raha hoon.” (Keemiya-e-Sa'adat, Rukn Chahaarum: Munjiyaat, Asl-e-Seem dar Khauf-o-Raja, 2/825-832) ([1])
