## Ayat 5:38

`Source: tafseerId=50766; ayatId=712; surah=5; ayat=38; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ السَّارِقُ وَ السَّارِقَةُ فَاقْطَعُوْۤا اَیْدِیَهُمَا جَزَآءًۢ بِمَا كَسَبَا نَكَالًا مِّنَ اللّٰهِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَزِیْزٌ حَكِیْمٌ(38)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jo mard ya aurat chor ho to Allah ki taraf se saza ke taur par un ke amal ke badle mein un ke haath kaat do aur Allah Ghalib, Hikmat wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَاقْطَعُوْۤا اَیْدِیَهُمَا: To un dono ke haath kaat do.}** Is aayat mein chor ki saza bayan ki gayi hai ke jab shar'i taur par chori saabit ho jaaye to chor ka haath kaat diya jaayega.

### Chori ka shar'i hukm aur us ki wa'eedain

Chori gunaah-e-kabeerah hai aur chor ke liye Shariat mein sakht wa'eedain hain. Chunancha:

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Chor chori karte waqt momin nahin rehta.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Imaan, Baab Bayaan Nuqsaan-il-Imaan bil-Ma'aasi, safha 48, hadees: 100 (57))

Unhi se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Agar us ne aisa kiya, yani chori ki, to beshak us ne Islam ka patta apni gardan se utaar diya. Phir agar us ne taubah ki to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ us ki taubah qubool farma lega.” (Nasaai, Kitaab Qat'-us-Saariq, Ta'zeem-us-Sariqah, safha 783, hadees: 4882)

### Chori ki ta'reef

Sariqah yani chori ka lughwi ma'ni hai: Chhup kar kisi doosre ki cheez utha lena. (Hidaayah, Kitaab-us-Sariqah, 1/362)

Shar'i ta'reef yeh hai ke aqil-baaligh shakhs kisi aisi mehfooz jagah se, jis ki hifaazat ka intizam kiya gaya ho, das dirham ya itni maal ki qeemat ya us se zyada ki koi aisi cheez jo jaldi kharaab hone wali na ho, chhup kar, kisi shubah aur taweel ke baghair utha le. (Fath-ul-Qadeer, Kitaab-us-Sariqah, 5/120)

### Chori se muta'alliq do shar'i masail

(1) Chori saabit hone ke do tareeqe hain: (1) Chor khud iqraar kar le, agarche ek baar hi kare. (2) Do mard gawahi dein. Agar ek mard aur do auraton ne gawahi di to haath nahin kaata jaayega.

(2) Qaazi gawaahon se chand baatein pooche: Chori kis tarah ki, kahan ki, kitne maal ki aur kis ki cheez churaayi? Jab gawaah in baaton ka jawab dein aur haath kaatne ki tamaam sharaait paayi jaayen to haath kaatne ka hukm diya jaayega.

### Tanbeeh

Hudood aur ta'zeer ke masail mein awaam-un-naas ko qanoon apne haath mein lene ki shar'an ijaazat nahin. Chori ke masail ki tafseeli maloomat ke liye Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa 9 ka mutaala'a kijiye.

## Ayat 5:39

`Source: tafseerId=50767; ayatId=713; surah=5; ayat=39; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَمَنْ تَابَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ ظُلْمِهٖ وَ اَصْلَحَ فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ یَتُوْبُ عَلَیْهِؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(39)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To jo apne zulm ke baad taubah kar le aur apni islaah kar le to Allah apni mehrbani se us par rujoo farmayega. Beshak Allah Bakhshne wala, Mehrban hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَمَنْ تَابَ: To jo taubah kar le.}** Taubah nihayat qeemti cheez hai. Kitna hi bada gunaah ho, agar us se taubah kar li jaaye to Allah Ta'ala apna haq maaf farma deta hai aur taubah karne wale ko Aakhirat ke azaab se najaat de deta hai. Lekin yeh yaad rahe ke jis gunaah mein kisi bande ka haq bhi shaamil ho, wahan taubah ke liye zaroori hai ke us bande ka haq bhi ada kiya jaaye.

## Ayat 5:40

`Source: tafseerId=50768; ayatId=714; surah=5; ayat=40; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ تَعْلَمْ اَنَّ اللّٰهَ لَهٗ مُلْكُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِؕ-یُعَذِّبُ مَنْ یَّشَآءُ وَ یَغْفِرُ لِمَنْ یَّشَآءُؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ(40)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya tujhe maloom nahin ke aasmanon aur zameen ki baadshahi Allah hi ke liye hai. Woh jise chahta hai saza deta hai aur jise chahta hai bakhsh deta hai aur Allah har shay par Qaadir hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یُعَذِّبُ مَنْ یَّشَآءُ: Jise chahe saza deta hai.}** Is aayat se maloom hua ke azaab dena aur rahmat farmana Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat par hai. Woh Maalik hai, jo chahe kare. Kisi ko etiraaz karne ki gunjaish nahin.

Is se Qadariyyah, yani taqdeer ke munkiron, aur Mu'tazilah ka radd hota hai jo nek shakhs par rahmat aur gunaahgaar par azaab dena Allah Ta'ala par waajib kehte hain, kyunki waajib hona Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat ke munaafi hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 40, 1/494)

## Ayat 5:41

`Source: tafseerId=50769; ayatId=715; surah=5; ayat=41; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الرَّسُوْلُ لَا یَحْزُنْكَ الَّذِیْنَ یُسَارِعُوْنَ فِی الْكُفْرِ مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ قَالُـوْۤا اٰمَنَّا بِاَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَ لَمْ تُؤْمِنْ قُلُوْبُهُمْۚۛ-وَ مِنَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْاۚۛ-سَمّٰعُوْنَ لِلْكَذِبِ سَمّٰعُوْنَ لِقَوْمٍ اٰخَرِیْنَۙ-لَمْ یَاْتُوْكَؕ-یُحَرِّفُوْنَ الْكَلِمَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مَوَاضِعِهٖۚ-یَقُوْلُوْنَ اِنْ اُوْتِیْتُمْ هٰذَا فَخُذُوْهُ وَ اِنْ لَّمْ تُؤْتَوْهُ فَاحْذَرُوْاؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّرِدِ اللّٰهُ فِتْنَتَهٗ فَلَنْ تَمْلِكَ لَهٗ مِنَ اللّٰهِ شَیْــٴًـاؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ الَّذِیْنَ لَمْ یُرِدِ اللّٰهُ اَنْ یُّطَهِّرَ قُلُوْبَهُمْؕ-لَهُمْ فِی الدُّنْیَا خِزْیٌ ۚۖ-وَّ لَهُمْ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِیْمٌ(41)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Rasool! Jo log kufr mein daude ja rahe hain tumhein ghamgeen na karein. Ye woh hain jo apne munh se kehte hain ke hum imaan laaye, halaanke un ke dil Musalman nahin. Aur kuch Yahoodi bahut jhoot sunne wale hain aur un doosre logon ki bhi khoob sunte hain jo aap ki bargah mein nahin aaye. Ye Allah ke kalaam ko us ke maqamaat ke baad badal dete hain. Ye aapas mein kehte hain: Agar tumhein ye tehreef wala hukm mile to ise le lena aur agar ye na mile to bachna. Aur jise Allah gumraah karna chahe to ae mukhatab! Tu hargiz use Allah se bachane ka kuch ikhtiyaar nahin rakhta. Yehi woh log hain jin ke dilon ko paak karne ka Allah ne iraadah nahin farmaya. Un ke liye duniya mein ruswaai hai aur un ke liye Aakhirat mein bada azaab hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَا یَحْزُنْكَ الَّذِیْنَ یُسَارِعُوْنَ فِی الْكُفْرِ: Jo kufr mein daude ja rahe hain tumhein ghamgeen na karein.}** Yahan se munaafiqon ki harkaton ka bayan shuru hota hai. Sab se pehle Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko “یٰۤاَیُّهَا الرَّسُوْلُ” ke mubarak khitaab se izzat di aur Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke dil ko tasalli di ke ae Habeeb! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, main aap ka naasir-o-madadgaar hoon. Munaafiq kufr ko jaldi zahir karne lagein aur kaafiron se dosti kar lein to aap ranj na karein. Ye log sirf zubaan se kalima padhte hain, lekin dil se imaan nahin laate.

**{سَمّٰعُوْنَ لِلْكَذِبِ: Bahut jhoot sunne wale.}** Pehle yahoodi awaam murad thi jo paadriyon aur sardaron ka jhoot sun kar us par amal karti thi. Yahan yahoodi hukmraan aur paadri murad hain jo rishwat le kar haraam ko halaal karte aur Shariat ke ahkaam badal dete the. Ye apne sardaron ke jhoot aur un ke bohtaan qabool karte the aur Khaibar ke yahoodiyon ki baat bhi maante the.

**{یُحَرِّفُوْنَ الْكَلِمَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مَوَاضِعِهٖ: Ye Allah ke kalaam ko us ke maqamaat ke baad badal dete hain.}** Is aayat ka sabab-e-nuzool ye hai ke Khaibar ke ba-izzat yahoodiyon mein se ek shaadi-shuda mard aur ek shaadi-shuda aurat ne zina kiya. Taurat mein un dono ki saza sangsaar karna thi, lekin unhein ye pasand na tha. Is liye unhone socha ke is muqaddame ka faisla Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se karwaya jaaye.

Un dono mujrim ko ek jamaat ke saath Madinah Tayyibah bheja gaya aur kaha gaya: Agar Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ hadd, yani kode maarne ka hukm dein to maan lena, lekin agar sangsaar karne ka hukm dein to na maanna. Ye log Bani Quraizah aur Bani Nazeer ke yahoodiyon ke paas gaye aur samjhe ke ye Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke hum-watan hain aur un ke saath aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka sulh ka muahida bhi hai, is liye un ki sifaarish kaam aa jaayegi.

Yahoodi sardaron mein se Ka'b bin Ashraf, Ka'b bin Asad, Sa'eed bin Amr, Maalik bin Saif aur Kinaanah bin Abil-Huqaiq waghera unhein le kar Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein haazir hue aur mas'alah poocha. Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Kya tum mera faisla maanoge?” Unhone iqraar kiya. Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne rajm, yani sangsaar karne ka hukm diya. Yahoodiyon ne ye hukm maanne se inkaar kar diya.

Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Tum mein ek naujawaan Ibn-e-Suriya hai, kya tum use jaante ho?” Unhone kaha: Haan. Farmaya: “Woh kaisa aadmi hai?” Yahoodiyon ne kaha: Aaj poori duniya mein yahoodiyon ke darmiyan us jaisa koi aalim nahin; woh Taurat ka yakta maahir hai. Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Use bulao.”

Jab Ibn-e-Suriya haazir hua to Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne poocha: “Kya tum Ibn-e-Suriya ho?” Us ne kaha: Ji haan. Farmaya: “Kya yahoodiyon mein sab se bada aalim tum hi ho?” Us ne kaha: Log aisa hi kehte hain. Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne yahoodiyon se farmaya: “Kya tum is maamale mein is ki baat maanoge?” Sab ne iqraar kiya.

Phir Sarkar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Ibn-e-Suriya se us Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam de kar poocha jis ke siwa koi ma'bood nahin, jis ne Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par Taurat naazil ki, unhein Misr se nikaala, un ke liye samandar mein raaste banaye, unhein nijaat di, Fir'auniyon ko gharq kiya, baadal ko saayabaan banaya, “mann-o-salwa” naazil kiya aur halaal-o-haraam wali kitaab naazil ki: “Kya tumhari kitaab mein shaadi-shuda mard aur aurat ke liye sangsaar karne ka hukm hai?” Ibn-e-Suriya ne arz ki: Beshak Taurat mein ye hukm hai.

Us ne kaha: Agar mujhe azaab naazil hone ka darr na hota to main iqraar na karta aur jhoot bol deta. Lekin aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki kitaab mein iska kya hukm hai? Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jab chaar aadil aur mo'tabar gawaahon ki gawaahi se zina saaf taur par saabit ho jaaye to sangsaar karna waajib ho jaata hai.” Ibn-e-Suriya ne kaha: Khuda ki qasam, Taurat mein bhi bilkul aisa hi hai.

Phir Ibn-e-Suriya se poocha gaya ke Allah ke hukm mein tabdeeli kaise hui? Us ne kaha: Hamara tareeqa ye tha ke jab kisi ameer ko pakadte to chhod dete aur ghareeb par hadd jaari karte. Is se ameeron mein zina bahut badh gaya. Ek martaba baadshah ke chacha-zad bhai ne zina kiya to humne use sangsaar na kiya. Phir ek aur shakhs ne apni qaum ki aurat se zina kiya. Baadshah ne use sangsaar karna chaha to us ki qaum ne kaha: Jab tak baadshah ke bhai ko sangsaar na kiya jaaye, ise bhi sangsaar nahin kiya jaayega.

Tab hum sab jama hue aur ameer-ghareeb sab ke liye sangsaar ke badle ye saza bana di ke chaalis kode maare jaayen, munh kaala kiya jaaye aur gadhe par ulta bitha kar shehar mein ghumaya jaaye. Ye sun kar yahoodi Ibn-e-Suriya par bigadne lage aur kehne lage: Tumne Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko bahut jaldi bata diya; humne tumhari jitni tareef ki thi tum us ke mustahiq nahin.

Ibn-e-Suriya ne kaha: Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne mujhe Taurat ki qasam di thi. Agar mujhe azaab ke naazil hone ka darr na hota to main aap ko khabar na deta. Is ke baad Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke hukm se dono zaaniyon ko sangsaar kiya gaya aur ye aayat naazil hui. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 41, 1/494-495)

## Ayat 5:42

`Source: tafseerId=50770; ayatId=716; surah=5; ayat=42; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** سَمّٰعُوْنَ لِلْكَذِبِ اَكّٰلُوْنَ لِلسُّحْتِؕ-فَاِنْ جَآءُوْكَ فَاحْكُمْ بَیْنَهُمْ اَوْ اَعْرِضْ عَنْهُمْۚ-وَ اِنْ تُعْرِضْ عَنْهُمْ فَلَنْ یَّضُرُّوْكَ شَیْــٴًـاؕ-وَ اِنْ حَكَمْتَ فَاحْكُمْ بَیْنَهُمْ بِالْقِسْطِؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ الْمُقْسِطِیْنَ(42)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Bahut jhoot sunne wale, bade haraam-khor hain. To agar ye tumhare huzoor haazir hon to in mein faisla farmaao ya in se munh pher lo; dono ka aap ko ikhtiyaar hai. Aur agar aap in se munh pher lenge to woh aap ka kuch na bigaad sakenge. Aur agar aap in mein faisla farmaayen to insaaf ke saath faisla karein. Beshak Allah insaaf karne walon ko pasand karta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَكّٰلُوْنَ لِلسُّحْتِ: Bade haraam-khor.}** Pichhli aayat mein jhoot sunne walon se yahoodi awaam murad thi jo paadriyon aur sardaron ka jhoot sun kar us par amal karte the. Is aayat mein yahoodi hukmraan aur paadri murad hain jo rishwat le kar haraam ko halaal karte aur Shariat ke ahkaam badalte the.

### Rishwat ka shar'i hukm aur us ki wa'eedain

Rishwat lena aur dena dono haraam hain aur lene-dene wale dono Jahannami hain. A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Rishwat lena mutlaqan haraam hai. Kisi doosre ka haq dabane ke liye diya jaane wala maal rishwat hai. Apna kaam banwane ke liye haakim ko diya jaane wala maal bhi rishwat hai. Lekin apne upar se zulm door karne ke liye jo maal diya jaaye, woh dene wale ke haq mein rishwat nahin; woh de sakta hai. Lene wale ke haq mein woh bhi rishwat hai aur us ka lena haraam hai.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 23/597)

Ek aur maqam par farmate hain: “Rishwat lena mutlaqan gunaah-e-kabeerah hai. Lene wala haraam-khor aur sakht azaab-e-naar ka mustahiq hai. Agar majboori ki wajah se apne upar se zulm door karne ke liye diya jaaye to haraj nahin, lekin apna aata wasool karne ke liye dena haraam hai. Lene-dene wale dono Jahannami hain. Kisi ka haq dabane ya kisi aur zulm ke liye dena aur bhi zyada haraam hai aur sakht tareen ghazab-o-intiqaam ka mustahiq banata hai.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 18/469)

Ahadees mein rishwat lene aur dene wale ke liye sakht wa'eedain aayi hain:

**(1)** Hazrat Abu Humaid Saa'idi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Bani Sulaim se zakaat wasool karne ke liye Ibn-e-Lutbiyah naam ke ek shakhs ko aamil banaya. Jab woh hisaab le kar aaya to kehne laga: Ye aap ka maal hai aur ye mera tohfa hai.

Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Tum apne maa-baap ke ghar baithe rehte aur dekhte ke tumhare liye kitne tohfe aate? Phir tum apni baat mein kitne sachche hote.” Phir aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Allah Ta'ala ki hamd-o-sana ke baad farmaya: “Jab main tum mein se kisi ko kisi jagah ka aamil banata hoon jis ka Allah Ta'ala ne mujhe ikhtiyaar diya hai, to woh mere paas aa kar kehta hai: Ye aap ka maal hai aur ye mujhe tohfe mein diya gaya hai. Woh apne maa-baap ke ghar kyun na baitha raha, yahan tak ke us ke paas tohfe aate? Khuda ki qasam! Tum mein se jo shakhs na-haq koi cheez lega, woh use uthaye hue Allah Ta'ala ki bargah mein haazir hoga.” Phir aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne haath buland kiya yahan tak ke baghal ki safedi nazar aane lagi aur farmaya: “Ae Allah! Kya main ne tera hukm pahuncha diya?” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Hiyal, Baab Ihtiyaal-ul-Aamil li-yuhda lah, 4/398, hadees: 6979)

**(2)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Rishwat lene aur dene wale dono Jahannami hain.” (Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, Baab-ul-Alif, man ismuhu Ahmad, 1/550, hadees: 2026)

**(3)** Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Jo gosht suht se pala-badha ho, aag us ki zyada haqdaar hai.” Arz ki gayi: Suht se kya murad hai? Farmaya: “Faisla karne mein rishwat lena.” (Jam'-ul-Jawami', Qism-ul-Aqwaal, Harf-ul-Kaaf, 5/391, hadees: 15904)

### Rishwat se haasil kiye hue maal ka shar'i hukm

Jis ne rishwat ke zariye koi maal haasil kiya ho, us par farz hai ke jis jis se woh maal liya tha unhein wapas kare. Agar woh log zinda na rahe hon to un ke waarison ko de. Agar dene walon ya un ke waarison ka pata na chale to woh maal faqeeron par sadaqah kare. Kharid-o-farokht waghera mein is maal ko lagana haraam-e-qatai hai. Is ke siwa rishwat ke maal ke wabaal se bachne ka koi aur tareeqa nahin. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 23/551, khulaasa)

Rishwat sirf us maal ka naam nahin jo sarkaari daftar mein diya jaaye ya ghalat kaam karwane ke liye diya jaaye. Kisi bhi naam se diya gaya aisa maal jo is maqsad ke liye ho, rishwat mein daakhil hai. Sirf woh soorat mustasna hai jo zulm door karne ke liye majboori mein di jaaye aur dene wale ke liye rishwat na ho.

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain ke jis shakhs ke paas apne ohde ya haakim ki taraf se logon par kisi qism ka dabao ho, chahe woh qati ya ghair-qati faisla karne ka ikhtiyaar na rakhta ho, jaise kotwal, thanedaar, jamadaar, zameendaar, patwaari, panchayat ka chaudhri ya kisi peshe ke logon ka sardar, us ke liye kisi qism ka tohfa lena ya khaas daawat qabool karna jaaiz nahin.

Is ki teen sooratein mustasna hain: Pehli, apne us afsar se tohfa ya daawat lena jis par is ka koi dabao na ho aur ye gumaan bhi na ho ke woh apne kaam mein ri'aayat karwane ke liye tohfa de raha hai. Doosri, aise shakhs se lena jo ohda milne se pehle bhi tohfa deta ya daawat karta tha, magar ab bhi utni hi miqdaar ho; agar qeemat, tadaad ya baar-baar dene mein izaafa ho to jaaiz nahin. Teesri, apne qareebi mahram rishtedaron, jaise maa-baap, aulaad, behen-bhai, chacha, mamu, khaala aur phuphi se lena. Phuphi ke bete aur khaala ke bete mahram nahin hote, agarche urf mein unhein bhai kaha jaata ho.

Qareebi mahram se mutlaqan tohfa lene ki ijaazat Zahiri Ibaarat-e-Qudoori se samajh aati hai. Lekin Imam Sughnaaqi ne Nihaayah mein aur Imam Muhaqqiq Ali-ul-Itlaaq ne Fath-ul-Qadeer mein is ko bhi doosri soorat mein daakhil kiya hai: Qareebi mahram se bhi tohfa ya daawat usi waqt qabool ki ja sakti hai jab ohda milne se pehle bhi woh aisa bartao karte rahe hon. Agar ohda milne se pehle shakhs ghareeb tha aur baad mein maal-daar ho gaya ho to pehle tohfa na dena ghurbat ki wajah se maana jaayega. Daleel ke lihaz se bhi zyada wazeh baat ye hai ke agar ohda milne se pehle qudrat ke bawajood tohfa kam ya bilkul na milta ho aur ohda milne ke baad zyada milne lage to ye ziyaadati ohde ki wajah se samjhi jaayegi. Is surat mein sirf do sooratein mustasna rahengi.

Baharhaal, mustasna soorat mein bhi us waqt tohfa lena jaaiz nahin jab dene wale se apna koi kaam muta'alliq ho, chahe woh afsar ho, bhai ho ya pehle se tohfa dene wala ho. Aise waqt aam daawat mein shareek hona bhi munasib nahin, khaas daawat ka to sawaal hi nahin. Jahan tohfa lene ki manaahi hai, wahan wajah sirf ye nahin ke lene wala waqai apne faisle mein tabdeeli laayega; asal wajah ye bhi hai ke tohmat aur ri'aayat ka andesha paida hota hai. Duniya ke kaam umeed par chalte hain. Log kahenge ke is baar muft maal ka asar na hua to agli baar hoga, aur phir kabhi hoga.

Jahan mana kiya gaya hai wahan wajah sirf tohmat aur ri'aayat ka andesha hai. Ye zaroori nahin ke lene wala waqai apne faisle mein tabdeeli laaye. Jab tohfe aur daawatein qabool ki jaayengi to logon ko zaroor khayaal hoga ke shayad is martaba tohfa asar kare. Ye kehna bhi durust nahin ke tohfa ohde ki wajah se nahin balki insaani akhlaaq ki wajah se hai, kyunki Huzoor-e-Aqdas, Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Ibn-e-Lutbiyah ke waaqe'e se is ki tardid farma di.

**{فَاِنْ جَآءُوْكَ: To agar woh tumhare paas aayein.}** Is mein Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko ikhtiyaar diya gaya ke Ahl-e-Kitaab koi muqaddamah laayein to aap faisla farmaayein ya na farmaayein. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 18/170-171)

## Ayat 5:43

`Source: tafseerId=50771; ayatId=717; surah=5; ayat=43; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ كَیْفَ یُحَكِّمُوْنَكَ وَ عِنْدَهُمُ التَّوْرٰىةُ فِیْهَا حُكْمُ اللّٰهِ ثُمَّ یَتَوَلَّوْنَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ ذٰلِكَؕ-وَ مَاۤ اُولٰٓىٕكَ بِالْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ۠    (43)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur ye aap ko kaise haakim banaayenge, halaanke un ke paas Taurat maujood hai jis mein Allah ka hukm maujood hai. Is ke bawajood ye munh pherte hain aur ye imaan lane wale nahin hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ كَیْفَ یُحَكِّمُوْنَكَ: Aur ye aap ko kaise haakim banaayenge.}** Shaadi-shuda mard aur aurat ke zina ki saza rajm, yani sangsaar karna hai aur ye hukm Taurat mein maujood hai. Ye log Taurat par imaan ka da'wa bhi karte hain aur jaante bhi hain ke Taurat mein rajm ka hukm hai. Is hukm ko na maanna, aur saath hi Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat ka inkaar karte hue aap se faisla chaahna nihayat ta'ajjub ki baat hai.

## Ayat 5:44

`Source: tafseerId=50772; ayatId=718; surah=5; ayat=44; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّاۤ اَنْزَلْنَا التَّوْرٰىةَ فِیْهَا هُدًى وَّ نُوْرٌۚ-یَحْكُمُ بِهَا النَّبِیُّوْنَ الَّذِیْنَ اَسْلَمُوْا لِلَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا وَ الرَّبّٰنِیُّوْنَ وَ الْاَحْبَارُ بِمَا اسْتُحْفِظُوْا مِنْ كِتٰبِ اللّٰهِ وَ كَانُوْا عَلَیْهِ شُهَدَآءَۚ-فَلَا تَخْشَوُا النَّاسَ وَ اخْشَوْنِ وَ لَا تَشْتَرُوْا بِاٰیٰتِیْ ثَمَنًا قَلِیْلًاؕ-وَ مَنْ لَّمْ یَحْكُمْ بِمَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْكٰفِرُوْنَ(44)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak humne Taurat naazil farmayi jis mein hidaayat aur noor hai. Farmanbardaar Nabi aur Rabbani ulama aur fuqaha yahoodiyon ko isi ke mutabiq hukm dete the, kyunki unhein Allah ki is kitaab ka muhafiz banaya gaya tha aur woh us ke khud gawaah the. To logon se khauf na karo aur mujh se daro aur meri aayaat ke badle thodi zaleel qeemat na lo. Aur jo us ke mutabiq faisla na kare jo Allah ne naazil kiya to wohi log kaafir hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّاۤ اَنْزَلْنَا التَّوْرٰىةَ: Beshak humne Taurat naazil farmayi.}** Is aayat mein Taurat Shareef ki azmat aur us ke mutabiq Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur sachche ulama-o-fuqaha ke faisle bayan kiye gaye hain. Taurat ke baad yahoodiyon ko asli Taurat par amal karte hue Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan lane ki da'wat di gayi.

Allah Ta'ala ne Taurat naazil farmayi. Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, un ke baad aane wale bahut se Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur ulama-o-fuqaha Taurat ke mutabiq faisle karte aur us ki taleemaat par amal karte the. Is ki wajah ye thi ke un se Taurat ki hifaazat ka ahd liya gaya tha: Woh use apne seenon mein mehfooz rakhein aur us ki taleem mein mashghool rahein, taake kitaab faramosh na ho aur us ke ahkaam zaya na hon. (Abu Saud, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 44, 2/45; Khaazin, 1/498, khulaasa)

Ae yahoodiyo! Taurat mein maujood Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki na'at-o-sifat aur rajm ka hukm zahir karne mein logon se na daro, sirf Allah Ta'ala se daro. Allah ke ahkaam ko logon ke khauf, un ki naaraazgi, maal-o-jaah ya rishwat ki laalach ki wajah se badalna har haal mein mana hai. Is mein ulama ke liye bhi hukm hai ke Allah ki kitaab ki hifaazat karein, us ki aayaat ke badle duniya ki zaleel daulat na lein aur logon ke bajaye Allah Ta'ala se darein.

### Pehli shari'aton ke ahkaam ka aham mas'alah

Is aayat se maloom hua ke hum se pehle ki shari'aton ke woh ahkaam jinhein Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne bayan farmaya ho, jin ke tark ka humein hukm na diya gaya ho aur jo mansookh na hue hon, woh hum par bhi laazim hote hain. (Abu Saud, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 44, 2/45)

## Ayat 5:45

`Source: tafseerId=50773; ayatId=719; surah=5; ayat=45; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ كَتَبْنَا عَلَیْهِمْ فِیْهَاۤ اَنَّ النَّفْسَ بِالنَّفْسِۙ-وَ الْعَیْنَ بِالْعَیْنِ وَ الْاَنْفَ بِالْاَنْفِ وَ الْاُذُنَ بِالْاُذُنِ وَ السِّنَّ بِالسِّنِّۙ-وَ الْجُرُوْحَ قِصَاصٌؕ-فَمَنْ تَصَدَّقَ بِهٖ فَهُوَ كَفَّارَةٌ لَّهٗؕ-وَ مَنْ لَّمْ یَحْكُمْ بِمَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الظّٰلِمُوْنَ(45)`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur humne Taurat mein un par laazim kar diya tha ke jaan ke badle jaan, aankh ke badle aankh, naak ke badle naak, kaan ke badle kaan aur daant ke badle daant ka qisaas liya jaayega aur tamaam zakhmon ka qisaas hoga. Phir jo dil ki khushi se khud ko qisaas ke liye pesh kar de to ye us ka kaffaarah ban jaayega. Aur jo us ke mutabiq faisla na kare jo Allah ne naazil kiya to wohi log zaalim hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ كَتَبْنَا عَلَیْهِمْ: Aur humne un par laazim kar diya.}** Is aayat mein agarche Taurat mein yahoodiyon par qisaas ke ahkaam ka bayan hai, lekin kyunki humein un ahkaam ko tark karne ka hukm nahin diya gaya, is liye ye hum par bhi laazim hain. Pehli shari'aton ke jo ahkaam Allah Ta'ala aur Rasool-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke bayan se hum tak pahunche aur mansookh na hue, woh hum par laazim hote hain.

Aayat mein jaan, aaza aur zakhmon ke qisaas ka hukm bayan hua. Aaza aur zakhmon ke qisaas mein tafseel hai, jis ke liye fiqhi kitaabon ka mutaala'a zaroori hai. Jaan ke qisaas ka hukm ye hai ke agar kisi ne kisi ko qatl kiya to us ki jaan maqtool ke badle li jaayegi, chahe maqtool mard ho ya aurat, azaad ho ya ghulaam, Musalman ho ya zimmi. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke log aurat ke badle mard ko qatl nahin karte the, is par ye aayat naazil hui. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 45, safha 287)

**{فَمَنْ تَصَدَّقَ بِهٖ: To jo khud ko qisaas ke liye pesh kar de.}** Yani jo qaatil ya mujrim apne jurm par naadim ho kar gunaah ke wabaal se bachne ke liye khushi se apne upar hukm-e-shar'i jaari karwaaye, to qisaas us ke jurm ka kaffaarah ban jaayega aur Aakhirat mein us par azaab na hoga. (Jamal ma'a Jalaalain, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 45, 2/228)

Baaz mufassireen ne is ke ma'ni ye bayan kiye hain ke jo haqdaar qisaas maaf kar de, us ki ye maafi us ke liye kaffaarah hai. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 45, safha 287) Dono tafseeron ke mutabiq tarjume mein farq hota hai. Tafseer-e-Ahmadi mein hai ke ye qisaas us waqt waajib hoga jab haqdaar maaf na kare; agar woh maaf kar de to qisaas saaqit ho jaayega. (Tafseer-e-Ahmadi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 45, safha 359)

## Ayat 5:46

`Source: tafseerId=50774; ayatId=720; surah=5; ayat=46; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ قَفَّیْنَا عَلٰۤى اٰثَارِهِمْ بِعِیْسَى ابْنِ مَرْیَمَ مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَیْنَ یَدَیْهِ مِنَ التَّوْرٰىةِ۪-وَ اٰتَیْنٰهُ الْاِنْجِیْلَ فِیْهِ هُدًى وَّ نُوْرٌۙ-وَّ مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَیْنَ یَدَیْهِ مِنَ التَّوْرٰىةِ وَ هُدًى وَّ مَوْعِظَةً لِّلْمُتَّقِیْنَﭤ(46)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur humne in nabiyon ke peeche un ke naqsh-e-qadam par Isa bin Maryam ko bhi bheja, us Taurat ki tasdeeq karte hue jo is se pehle maujood thi aur humne use Injeel ata ki jis mein hidaayat aur noor tha aur woh (Injeel) is se pehle maujood Taurat ki tasdeeq farmane wali thi aur parhezgaaron ke liye hidaayat aur naseehat thi.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَفَّیْنَا عَلٰۤى اٰثَارِهِمْ: Aur humne un nabiyon ke peeche un ke naqsh-e-qadam par bheja.}** Taurat ke ahkaam bayan karne ke baad ab Injeel ke ahkaam ka zikr shuru kiya gaya hai.

Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Taurat ki tasdeeq farmane wale the. Woh gawahi dete the ke Taurat Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki naazil ki hui kitaab hai aur Taurat ke mansookh hone se pehle us par amal karna waajib tha. Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki Shariat mein Taurat ke kuch ahkaam mansookh kar diye gaye the.

Is ke baad Injeel ki shaan bayan hui ke us mein hidaayat aur noor tha aur woh hidaayat aur naseehat thi. Pehli jagah hidaayat se murad gumraahi aur jahalat se bachane ke liye rehnumaai karna hai. Doosri jagah hidaayat se murad سیدُ الانبیاء، حبیبِ کبریا صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki tashreef-aawari ki bashaarat hai, jo Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat ki taraf logon ki rehnumaai ka sabab hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 46, 1/500)

## Ayat 5:47

`Source: tafseerId=50775; ayatId=721; surah=5; ayat=47; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لْیَحْكُمْ اَهْلُ الْاِنْجِیْلِ بِمَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ فِیْهِؕ-وَ مَنْ لَّمْ یَحْكُمْ بِمَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْفٰسِقُوْنَ(47)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Injeel walon ko bhi usi ke mutabiq hukm karna chahiye jo Allah ne us mein naazil farmaya hai aur jo us ke mutabiq faisla na kare jo Allah ne naazil kiya to wahi log naafarmaan hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لْیَحْكُمْ اَهْلُ الْاِنْجِیْلِ: Aur Injeel walon ko hukm karna chahiye.}** Is aayat ka ek ma'ni ye hai ke Injeel walon ko bhi us ke mutabiq faisla karna chahiye jo Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne Injeel mein naazil farmaya. Yani unhein سیدُ الانبیاء صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par imaan lana aur aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat ki tasdeeq karni chahiye, kyunki Injeel mein isi ka hukm diya gaya hai.

Doosra ma'ni ye hai ke jab Allah Ta'ala ne Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Injeel ata ki to us waqt unhein hukm diya gaya tha ke Injeel mein bayan kiye gaye ahkaam par amal karein. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 47, 1/500, khulaasa)

### Injeel par amal karne se muta'alliq ek etiraaz ka jawab

Agar ye etiraaz kiya jaaye ke Quran-e-Majeed ke naazil hone ke baad Injeel par amal karne ke hukm ki kya wajah hogi, to is ke chand jawab hain:

(1) Injeel mein Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki nubuwwat ke jo daleel maujood hain, Ahl-e-Injeel ko chahiye ke un daleelon ki roshni mein imaan le aayein.

(2) Ahl-e-Injeel un ahkaam par amal karein jinhein Quran ne mansookh nahin kiya.

(3) Injeel ke ahkaam par amal karne se murad ye hai ke Injeel mein tehreef na karein, jaisa ke Yahoodiyon ne Taurat mein tehreef kar di thi. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 47, 4/371)

Lekin tahqeeq ye hai ke ye hukm us waqt diya gaya tha jab Allah Ta'ala ne Injeel naazil farmayi thi. Quran-e-Majeed ke naazil hone ke baad Quran-e-Majeed ke siwa kisi aasmani kitaab par amal jaaiz nahin aur Islam ke siwa koi aur deen maqbool nahin.

## Ayat 5:48

`Source: tafseerId=50776; ayatId=722; surah=5; ayat=48; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اَنْزَلْنَاۤ اِلَیْكَ الْكِتٰبَ بِالْحَقِّ مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَیْنَ یَدَیْهِ مِنَ الْكِتٰبِ وَ مُهَیْمِنًا عَلَیْهِ فَاحْكُمْ بَیْنَهُمْ بِمَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ وَ لَا تَتَّبِـعْ اَهْوَآءَهُمْ عَمَّا جَآءَكَ مِنَ الْحَقِّؕ-لِكُلٍّ جَعَلْنَا مِنْكُمْ شِرْعَةً وَّ مِنْهَاجًاؕ-وَ لَوْ شَآءَ اللّٰهُ لَجَعَلَكُمْ اُمَّةً وَّاحِدَةً وَّ لٰـكِنْ لِّیَبْلُوَكُمْ فِیْ مَاۤ اٰتٰىكُمْ فَاسْتَبِقُوا الْخَیْرٰتِؕ-اِلَى اللّٰهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ جَمِیْعًا فَیُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ فِیْهِ تَخْتَلِفُوْنَۙ      (48)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur ae Habeeb! Humne tumhari taraf sachchi kitaab utaari jo pehli kitabon ki tasdeeq farmane wali aur un par nigraan hai. To un Ahl-e-Kitaab mein Allah ke naazil kiye hue hukm ke mutabiq faisla karo aur ae sunne wale! Apne paas aaye hue haq ko chhor kar un ki khwahishon ki pairwi na karna. Humne tum sab ke liye ek ek Shariat aur raasta banaya hai aur agar Allah chahta to tum sab ko ek hi ummat bana deta, magar (us ne aisa nahin kiya) taake jo Shariatein us ne tumhein di hain un mein tumhein aazmaaye. To nekiyon ki taraf doosron se aage badho. Tum sab ko Allah hi ki taraf lautna hai, to woh tumhein bata dega woh baat jis mein tum jhagadte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اَنْزَلْنَاۤ اِلَیْكَ الْكِتٰبَ: Aur humne aap ki taraf kitaab naazil farmayi.}** Taurat aur Injeel ka zikr karne ke baad ab Quran-e-Azeem ka zikr kiya ja raha hai. Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki taraf sachchi kitaab naazil farmayi jo pehle Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par naazil hone wali kitabon ki tasdeeq karti hai aur un ki nigraan hai. Is liye jab Ahl-e-Kitaab apne muqaddame le kar Huzoor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki taraf ruju karein to aap Quran-e-Paak ke mutabiq un ke darmiyan faisla farma dein.

**{لِكُلٍّ جَعَلْنَا مِنْكُمْ شِرْعَةً وَّ مِنْهَاجًا: Humne tum sab ke liye ek ek Shariat aur raasta banaya hai.}** Allah Ta'ala ne har ummat ke liye alag Shariat aur raasta banaya. Furoo'i aamaal har ummat ke liye khaas aur alag the, jaise namazon aur rozon ki tadaad aur isi tarah doosre ahkaam. Lekin asal deen sab ka ek tha: Tauheed-o-Risaalat, Aakhirat ka aqeeda aur bunyaadi akhlaaq sab mein mushtarak the.

Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtaza كَرَّمَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم ne farmaya ke Hazrat Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke zamaane se imaan yahi hai ke “لَآاِلٰہَ اِلَّااللہُ” ki gawaahi di jaaye aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se jo kuch aaya us ka iqraar kiya jaaye, jabke har ummat ki Shariat alag hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 48, 1/501)

**{وَ لَوْ شَآءَ اللّٰهُ لَجَعَلَكُمْ اُمَّةً وَّاحِدَةً: Aur agar Allah chahta to tum sab ko ek hi ummat bana deta.}** Allah Ta'ala ne chaha to sab logon ko ek hi ummat bana sakta tha, lekin us ne aisa nahin kiya taake jin Shariaton ka hukm us ne tumhein diya un ke zariye tumhara imtihaan le. Is se zahir ho ke har zamaane ke munasib diye gaye ahkaam par tum yaqeen aur aqeede ke saath amal karte ho, kyunki ahkaam ka farq Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat se hai aur is mein bahut si hikmatein aur duniya-o-Aakhirat ke faayde hain, ya tum haq ko chhor kar apni nafsaani khwahishon ki pairwi karte ho. (Abu Saud, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 48, 2/51)

**{فَاسْتَبِقُوا الْخَیْرٰتِ: To nekiyon ki taraf doosron se aage badho.}** Quran-e-Paak ka hikmat bhara tareeqa ye hai ke jin maamlaat se insaan ki duniya aur Aakhirat ka koi qaabil-e-qabool faayda nahin hota, un mein behas aur muqabla karne ke bajaye Allah ki raza aur bhalaai ke kaamon mein ek doosre se aage badhne ki da'wat deta hai.

Yahan bhi Shariaton ke ikhtilaaf ki wajah par falsafiyana behas aur baal ki khaal utaarne ke bajaye nekiyon ki taraf aane ki da'wat di gayi. Is mein hamari bahut si islaah hai. Aaj kal har jagah fazool baaton par behas aur paani se makkhan nikaalne ki koshish jaari rehti hai, jabke karne ke kaamon ki taraf tawajjoh bahut kam hoti hai. Be-wajah bareekiyan nikaalne ko mahaarat aur qaabiliyat samjha jaata hai, agarche amali zindagi mein aise shakhs ki haalat bahut gir chuki hoti hai.

Behas wahan karni chahiye jahan us se koi faayda nazar aaye. Sirf waqt guzaarne, logon ko apni taraf mutawajjah rakhne, shohrat paane aur apni qaabiliyat dikhane ke liye apna aur doosron ka waqt barbaad karna, aur amali zindagi mein tinka tak na todna, aqal, deen aur Islam sab ke khilaaf hai. Bahut se logon ko is naseehat ki roshni mein apne tareeqe par nazar karne ki zaroorat hai.

Is baat ko samajhne ke liye Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka ye farmaan kaafi hai: “مِنْ حُسْنِ اِسْلَامِ الْمَرْءِ تَرْکُہٗ مَالَایَعْنِیْہِ” Yani aadmi ke Islam ke achha hone ki alamat ye hai ke woh fazool cheezon ko chhor de. (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, 11-baab, 4/142, hadees: 2324)

Is baat se bhi bachna chahiye ke ye log tumhein kisi ghalat kaam mein mubtala kar dein.

## Ayat 5:50

`Source: tafseerId=50778; ayatId=724; surah=5; ayat=50; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اَفَحُكْمَ الْجَاهِلِیَّةِ یَبْغُوْنَؕ-وَ مَنْ اَحْسَنُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ حُكْمًا لِّقَوْمٍ یُّوْقِنُوْنَ۠    (50)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To kya ye log jaahiliyyat ka hukm chahte hain aur yaqeen walon ke liye Allah se behtar kis ka hukm ho sakta hai?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَفَحُكْمَ الْجَاهِلِیَّةِ یَبْغُوْنَ: To kya ye log jaahiliyyat ka hukm chahte hain.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool ye hai ke Bani Nazeer aur Bani Quraizah Yahoodiyon ke do qabeele the. Aur farmaya gaya ke kya ye log jaahiliyyat ki gumraahi aur zulm ka hukm chahte hain? Huzoor-e-Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne jo hukm diya woh Allah Ta'ala ka hukm hai. Allah Ta'ala ke hukm se behtar kis ka hukm ho sakta hai?

## Ayat 5:51

`Source: tafseerId=50779; ayatId=725; surah=5; ayat=51; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوا الْیَهُوْدَ وَ النَّصٰرٰۤى اَوْلِیَآءَ ﳕ بَعْضُهُمْ اَوْلِیَآءُ بَعْضٍؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَوَلَّهُمْ مِّنْكُمْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنْهُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(51)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Yahood-o-Nasaara ko dost na banao. Woh sirf aapas mein ek doosre ke dost hain. Aur tum mein jo koi un se dosti rakhega to woh unhi mein se hai. Beshak Allah zaalim logon ko hidaayat nahin deta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوا الْیَهُوْدَ وَ النَّصٰرٰۤى اَوْلِیَآءَ: Ae imaan walo! Yahood-o-Nasaara ko dost na banao.}** Yeh aayat mashhoor Sahabi Hazrat Ubadah bin Saamit رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur mashhoor munaafiq Abdullah bin Ubay ke baare mein naazil hui. Abdullah bin Ubay munaafiqon ka sardaar tha. Hazrat Ubadah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne arz kiya ke Yahoodiyon mein mere bahut se dost hain jo badi shaukat aur quwwat rakhte hain. Ab main un ki dosti se bezaar hoon aur Allah Ta'ala aur us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke siwa mere dil mein kisi ki muhabbat ki gunjaish nahin. Is par Abdullah bin Ubay ne kaha ke main Yahoodiyon ki dosti se bezaar nahin ho sakta. Mujhe aane wale waaqiat ka darr hai aur mere liye un se ta'alluq rakhna zaroori hai. Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: “Yahoodiyon ki dosti ka da'wa karna tera hi kaam hai, Ubadah ka yeh kaam nahin.” Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 51, 1/503)

### Kafiron se dosti aur madad ka shar'i hukm

Is aayat mein Yahood-o-Nasaara ke saath dosti aur muwalaat, yani un ki madad karna, un se madad lena aur un ke saath muhabbat ke ta'alluq rakhna mana kiya gaya hai. Yeh hukm aam hai, agarche aayat kisi khaas waaqe'e ke mauqe par naazil hui. Is liye bila qaid farmaya gaya ke Yahoodiyon aur Isaaiyon ko dost na banao. Woh Musalmanon ke muqable mein aapas mein ek doosre ke dost hain, tumhare dost nahin. Kafir koi bhi hon aur aapas mein un ke kitne hi ikhtilaaf hon, Musalmanon ke muqable mein woh sab ek hain: **’’اَلْکُفْرُ مِلَّۃٌ وَّاحِدۃٌ‘‘** Kafir ek hi millat hain. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 51, safha 289)

Is liye Musalmanon ko kafiron ki dosti se bachne ka hukm dene ke saath bahut sakht wa'eed bayan ki gayi ke jo un se dosti karega woh unhi mein se hai. Is mein sakhti aur taakeed hai ke Musalmanon par Yahood-o-Nasaara aur Islam ke har mukhaalif se alag rehna waajib hai. (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 51, safha 289; Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 51, 1/503, multaqatan)

Jo log kafiron se dosti karte hain woh apni jaanon par zulm karte hain. Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Islami hukoomat mein kafiron ko aham aur faisla-kun ohde na diye jaayen. Yeh aayat Musalmanon ke hazaaron maamlaat mein rehnumaai karti hai aur is ki haqaaniyat bilkul zaahir hai. Duniya ke haalaat par nazar daalein to samajh aata hai ke Musalmanon ki zillat aur barbaadi us waqt shuru hui jab woh aapas mein nafrat, dushmani aur toot-phoot ka shikaar hue, ghair Muslimon ko apna khairkhwaah aur hamdard samajh kar un se dostiyan lagaane lage aur unhein apnon par tarjeeh dene lage. Allah Ta'ala hamein aqal-e-saleem ata farmaye.

## Ayat 5:52

`Source: tafseerId=50780; ayatId=726; surah=5; ayat=52; para=6; volume=2`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To jin ke dilon mein marz hai tum unhein dekhoge ke Yahood-o-Nasaara ki taraf daude jaate hain. Kehte hain ke hamein apne upar gardish aane ka darr hai. To qareeb hai ke Allah fatah ya apni taraf se koi khaas hukm le aaye, phir yeh log us par pachtaayenge jo apne dilon mein chhupaate the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَتَرَى الَّذِیْنَ فِیْ قُلُوْبِهِمْ مَّرَضٌ: To jin ke dilon mein marz hai tum unhein dekhoge.}** Musalmanon ko to Yahood-o-Nasaara se dosti na karne ka hukm diya gaya, lekin munaafiqon ke baare mein bataya gaya ke un ki Yahood-o-Nasaara se dostiyan mazboot hain, kyunki un ke dil beemaar hain. Woh un se dosti karne mein daude jaate hain aur kehte hain ke agar kabhi haalaat badal gaye, Musalman maghloob aur kafir ghaalib ho gaye, to kafiron se dosti us waqt un ke kaam aayegi. Yeh sab un ki munaafiqat ki wajah se tha. Unhein Rasool-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Islam ke ghaalib hone ki baaton par yaqeen nahin tha. Agar yaqeen hota to woh mazbooti se Islam ki himaayat karte.

Allah Ta'ala bashaarat deta hai ke qareeb hai woh Musalmanon ko fatah ata farmaye, apne Rasool Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko kaamyaab aur kaamraan farmaye, aap ke deen ko tamaam dinon par ghaalib kar de aur Musalmanon ko un ke dushman Yahood-o-Nasaara aur doosre kafiron par ghaalib kar de. Yeh khabar sach saabit hui. Allah Ta'ala ke karam se Makkah Mukarramah aur Yahoodiyon ke ilaake fatah hue. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 52, 1/503)

Is aayat mein pehle farmaya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala fatah le aaye. Doosri baat yeh farmayi gayi ke Allah Ta'ala apni taraf se koi khaas hukm le aaye. Is se murad Hijaz ki sarzameen ko Yahoodiyon se paak karna aur wahan un ka naam-o-nishaan baaqi na rehne dena, ya munaafiqon ke raaz khol kar unhein ruswa karna ho sakta hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 52, 1/503-504; Jalaalain, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 52, safha 102, multaqatan)

Jab Allah Ta'ala apna waada poora farmayega to munaafiq apni munaafiqat par, ya is khayaal par, pachtaayenge ke Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ kafiron ke muqable mein kaamyaab nahin honge.

## Ayat 5:53

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur imaan wale kahenge: Kya yahi woh log hain jinhon ne Allah ki badi pakki qasmein khaayi thin ke woh tumhare saath hain? To un ke tamaam aamaal barbaad ho gaye, pas woh nuqsaan uthaane walon mein se ho gaye.

**{حَبِطَتْ اَعْمَالُهُمْ: To un ke tamaam aamaal barbaad ho gaye.}** Yani un ke nifaak aur Yahoodiyon se dosti ki wajah se un ke tamaam nek aamaal barbaad ho gaye. Duniya mein zillat aur ruswaai ki wajah se unhein nuqsaan uthana pada. Aakhirat mein apne aamaal ke sawaab se mehroom hone aur Jahannam ka hamesha ka azaab paane ki wajah se bhi woh nuqsaan uthaayenge. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 53, 1/504)

## Ayat 5:54

`Source: tafseerId=50782; ayatId=728; surah=5; ayat=54; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا مَنْ یَّرْتَدَّ مِنْكُمْ عَنْ دِیْنِهٖ فَسَوْفَ یَاْتِی اللّٰهُ بِقَوْمٍ یُّحِبُّهُمْ وَ یُحِبُّوْنَهٗۤۙ-اَذِلَّةٍ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ اَعِزَّةٍ عَلَى الْكٰفِرِیْنَ٘-یُجَاهِدُوْنَ فِیْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِ وَ لَا یَخَافُوْنَ لَوْمَةَ لَآىٕمٍؕ-ذٰلِكَ فَضْلُ اللّٰهِ یُؤْتِیْهِ مَنْ یَّشَآءُؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ وَاسِعٌ عَلِیْمٌ(54)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Tum mein jo koi apne deen se phirega to qareeb hai ke Allah aisi qaum le aayega jin se Allah muhabbat farmata hai aur woh Allah se muhabbat karte hain. Musalmanon par narm aur kafiron par sakht hain. Allah ki raah mein jihaad karte hain aur kisi malaamat karne wale ki malaamat se nahin darte. Yeh achchi seerat Allah ka fazl hai, jise chahta hai ata farma deta hai aur Allah wus'at wala, ilm wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{مَنْ یَّرْتَدَّ مِنْكُمْ عَنْ دِیْنِهٖ: Tum mein jo koi apne deen se phirega.}** Kafiron ke saath dosti, yaari aur dil ka ta'alluq kabhi be-deeni aur irtidaad ka sabab ban jaata hai. Isi liye kafiron se dosti ki manaahi ke baad murtaddon ka zikr kiya gaya aur un ke murtad hone se pehle hi is ki khabar de di gayi. Yeh khabar sach saabit hui aur bahut se log murtad hue.

**{فَسَوْفَ یَاْتِی اللّٰهُ بِقَوْمٍ یُّحِبُّهُمْ: To qareeb hai ke Allah aisi qaum le aayega jin se Allah muhabbat farmata hai.}** Farmaya gaya ke agar tum mein se kuch log murtad bhi ho jaayen to Allah Ta'ala ke paakiza sifat bande phir bhi maujood rahenge. Woh badi azeem sifat rakhte honge. Is aayat mein un ki chand sifat bayan hui hain:

(1) Woh Allah Ta'ala ke mehboob hain.

(2) Woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ se muhabbat karte hain.

(3) Woh Musalmanon ke saath narmi aur shafqat ka bartao karte hain.

(4) Woh kafiron ke saath sakhti se pesh aate hain.

(5) Woh Allah ki raah ke mujaahid hain.

(6) Woh haq bayan karne mein kisi ki malaamat ki parwaah nahin karte. Woh haq-go aur haq-goi mein bebaak hain.

Jin buzurgon mein yeh sifat hain, un ke baare mein kai aqwaal hain:

(1) Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtaza, Sher-e-Khuda كَرَّمَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم, Imam Hasan Basri aur Hazrat Qataadah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke is se murad Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur un ke woh saathi hain jinhon ne Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke baad murtad hone walon aur zakaat ke munkiron se jihaad kiya.

(2) Hazrat Ayaaz bin Ghanam Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke jab yeh aayat naazil hui to Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہِ وَسَلَّمَ ne Hazrat Abu Moosa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki qaum ke baare mein farmaya: “Yeh un ki qaum hai.”

(3) Ek qawl yeh hai ke is se murad Ahle Yemen hain jin ki tareef Bukhari aur Muslim ki ahadees mein aayi hai.

(4) Mufassir Suddi ka qawl hai ke is se murad Ansaar hain jinhon ne Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat ki.

In tamaam aqwaal mein koi ikhtilaaf nahin, kyunki bayan kiye gaye tamaam buzurgon mein in sifat ka paaya jaana durust hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 54, 1/504-505)

### Kaamil Musalman ka namoona

Is aayat mein Musalmanon ke saamne kaamil Musalman ka namoona bhi pesh kar diya gaya ke kaamil Musalman kaisa hota hai. Hamein bhi in sifat ki roshni mein apna jaaiza lena chahiye. Musalmanon ki khairkhwaahi ke baare mein yeh waaqe'a badi azeem misaal hai:

Hazrat Sheikh Abu Abdullah Khayyaat رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ke paas ek aatish-parast kapde silwaata tha aur har baar ujrat mein khota sikka de jaata tha. Aap رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ use le lete the. Ek baar aap ki ghair-haazri mein shaagird ne aatish-parast se khota sikka lene se inkaar kar diya. Jab Hazrat Sheikh Abu Abdullah Khayyaat رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ waapas aaye aur unhein yeh maloom hua to shaagird se farmaya: Tum ne khota dirham kyun nahin liya? Kai saal se woh mujhe khota sikka hi deta raha hai aur main bhi khaamoshi se le leta hoon taake woh kisi doosre Musalman ko na de aaye. (Ihya-ul-Uloom, Kitaab Riyaazat-un-Nafs wa Tahzeeb-ul-Akhlaaq, Bayaan Alamaat Husn-ul-Khuluq, 3/87-88)

Yeh Musalmanon ke saath narmi hai. Hadees-e-Mubarak mein Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala ke nazdeek behtareen saathi woh hain jo apne humraahiyon ke liye behtar hon aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke nazdeek behtareen hamsaaya woh hain jo apne hamsaaye ke liye achche hon.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah, Baab Ma Ja'a fi Haqq-il-Jiwaar, 3/379, hadees: 1951)

Haq-goi mein kisi ki malaamat ki parwaah na karne ke baare mein yeh hikayat dekhiye: Qaazi Abu Haazim رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ insaaf ke maamale mein bahut sakht the. Aap hamesha haq baat kehte aur durust faisle karte the. Ek baar waqt ke Khalifa Mu'tazid-billah ne aap ki taraf paighaam bheja: Falaan taajir ne hum se maal khareeda hai aur naqad raqam ada nahin ki. Woh mere ilawa doosron ka bhi qarzdaar hai. Mujhe khabar mili hai ke doosre qarzkhwaahon ne aap ke paas gawaah pesh kiye to aap ne us taajir ka maal un mein taqseem kar diya. Mujhe us maal mein se kuch nahin mila, halaanke jis tarah woh doosron ka qarzdaar tha, isi tarah mera bhi tha. Is liye mera hissa bhi diya jaaye.

Paighaam milne par Qaazi Abu Haazim رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne qaasid se kaha: Khalifa se kehna ke Allah Ta'ala aap ki umr daraaz farmaye. Woh waqt yaad karein jab aap ne mujh se kaha tha ke main ne faislon ki zimmedaari ka bojh apni gardan se utaar kar tumhare gale mein daal diya hai. Ae Khalifa! Ab main faisla karne ka mukhtaar hoon aur mere liye jaaiz nahin ke gawaahon ke baghair kisi da'wedaar ke haq mein faisla karoon.

Qaasid ne Qaazi Sahib ka paighaam sunaaya to Khalifa ne kaha: Jaao, Qaazi Sahib se kehna ke mere paas bahut mo'tabar aur muazzaz gawaah maujood hain. Jab yeh paighaam Qaazi Sahib ko mila to farmaya: Gawaah mere saamne aa kar gawaahi dein. Main un se pooch-gachh karunga. Agar woh gawaahi ki sharaait poori karte honge to un ki gawaahi qubool kar lunga, warna wahi faisla qaabil-e-amal rahega jo main kar chuka hoon.

Jab gawaahon ko Qaazi Sahib ka yeh paighaam pahuncha to unhon ne aap se khauf khaate hue adaalat aane se inkaar kar diya. Is liye Qaazi Sahib ne Khalifa Mu'tazid-billah ka da'wa rad kar diya aur use kuch bhi na bheja. (Uyun-ul-Hikayaat, Al-Hikayat-us-Saadisah wal-Isruna ba'd-al-Mi'ah, safha 261-262)

## Ayat 5:55

`Source: tafseerId=50783; ayatId=729; surah=5; ayat=55; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّمَا وَلِیُّكُمُ اللّٰهُ وَ رَسُوْلُهٗ وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا الَّذِیْنَ یُقِیْمُوْنَ الصَّلٰوةَ وَ یُؤْتُوْنَ الزَّكٰوةَ وَ هُمْ رٰكِعُوْنَ(55)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tumhare dost sirf Allah aur us ka Rasool aur imaan wale hain jo namaaz qaayam karte hain aur zakaat dete hain aur Allah ke huzoor jhuke hue hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّمَا وَلِیُّكُمُ اللّٰهُ وَ رَسُوْلُهٗ وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا: Tumhare dost sirf Allah aur us ka Rasool aur imaan wale hain.}** Guzishta aayaat mein un logon ka bayan hua jin se dil ki dosti haraam hai. Un ka zikr karne ke baad ab un logon ka bayan kiya gaya jin ke saath muwalaat waajib hai.

Is aayat ke baare mein Hazrat Jaabir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya ke yeh Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke haq mein naazil hui. Woh Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein haazir hue aur arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Hamari qaum ne hamein chhor diya aur qasmein khaayi hain ke woh hamare paas nahin baithenge. Doori ki wajah se hum aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke ashaab ki sohbat mein bhi nahin baith sakte. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui ke Allah Ta'ala, us ka Rasool aur imaan wale tumhare dost hain. Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne kaha: “Hum Allah Ta'ala ke Rab hone, us ke Rasool صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Nabi hone aur momineen ke dost hone par raazi hain.” (Qurtubi, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 3/131, Al-Juz-us-Saadis)

Aayat-e-Mubarakah mein bayan kiya gaya hukm tamaam Musalmanon ke liye aam hai. Sab ek doosre ke dost aur muhabbat karne wale hain.

**{وَ هُمْ رٰكِعُوْنَ: Aur Allah ke huzoor jhuke hue hain.}** Arabi grammar ke aitbaar se is jumle ke chaar ma'ni bayan kiye gaye hain:

(1) Pehla ma'ni yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein jhukna mominon ki ek mazeed sifat hai. (Jamal, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 2/242)

(2) Doosra ma'ni yeh hai ke momin namaaz qaayam karne aur zakaat dene dono kaam khushu aur tawazu ke saath karte hain. (Abu Saud, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 2/59)

(3) Teesra ma'ni yeh hai ke woh tawazu aur aajizi ke saath zakaat dete hain. (Jamal, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 2/242)

(4) Chautha ma'ni yeh hai ke woh ruku ki haalat mein Allah ki raah mein dete hain.

Pehla ma'ni sab se zyada qawi hai aur chautha ma'ni sab se kamzor hai. Balki Imam Fakhruddin Raazi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne Tafseer-e-Kabeer mein is ma'ni ka bahut zor ke saath radd kiya hai aur is ke baatil hone par bahut se daleel qaayam kiye hain.

## Ayat 5:57

`Source: tafseerId=50784; ayatId=731; surah=5; ayat=57; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوا الَّذِیْنَ اتَّخَذُوْا دِیْنَكُمْ هُزُوًا وَّ لَعِبًا مِّنَ الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ وَ الْكُفَّارَ اَوْلِیَآءَۚ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ مُّؤْمِنِیْنَ(57)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Jin logon ko tum se pehle kitaab di gayi, un mein se un logon ko jinhon ne tumhare deen ko mazaaq aur khel bana liya hai, aur kafiron ko apna dost na banao aur agar imaan rakhte ho to Allah se darte raho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{الَّذِیْنَ اتَّخَذُوْا دِیْنَكُمْ هُزُوًا وَّ لَعِبًا: Woh log jinhon ne tumhare deen ko mazaaq aur khel bana liya hai.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Rifaa'ah bin Zaid aur Suwaid bin Haaris naam ke do aadmi Islam zahir karne ke baad munaafiq ho gaye. Baaz Musalman un se muhabbat rakhte the. Allah Ta'ala ne yeh aayat naazil farma kar bataya ke zubaan se Islam ka izhaar karna aur dil mein kufr chhupa kar rakhna deen ko hansi aur khel banana hai.

Aise logon aur un ke ilawa mushrikon aur kafiron ko dost banane se bhi mana kar diya gaya, kyunki Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke dushmanon se dosti karna imaan wale ka kaam nahin. Is ki mazeed tafseel agli aayat ke taht maujood hai.

## Ayat 5:58

`Source: tafseerId=50785; ayatId=732; surah=5; ayat=58; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا نَادَیْتُمْ اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ اتَّخَذُوْهَا هُزُوًا وَّ لَعِبًاؕ-ذٰلِكَ بِاَنَّهُمْ قَوْمٌ لَّا یَعْقِلُوْنَ(58)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab tum namaaz ke liye azaan dete ho to yeh us ko hansi mazaaq aur khel bana lete hain. Yeh is liye hai ke woh bilkul be-aqal log hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذَا نَادَیْتُمْ اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ: Aur jab tum namaaz ke liye azaan dete ho.}** Is aayat ke baare mein Kalbi ka qaul hai ke jab Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka mo'azzin namaaz ke liye azaan kehta aur Musalman uthte to Yahoodi hanste aur mazaaq karte. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui.

Mufassir Suddi ne bayan kiya ke Madinah Tayyibah mein jab mo'azzin azaan mein “اَشْهَدُ اَنْ لَّا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللهُ” aur “اَشْهَدُ اَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَّسُوْلُ اللهِ” kehta to ek Nasraani kaha karta: “Jal jaaye jhoota.” Ek raat us ka khaadim aag laaya. Woh aur us ke ghar wale so rahe the. Aag se ek sharaara uda aur us Nasraani, us ke ghar walon aur poore ghar ko jala gaya. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 58, 1/507)

### Aayat “وَ اِذَا نَادَیْتُمْ اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ” se maloom hone wale masail

Is aayat se 3 mas'ale maloom hue:

(1) Panch waqt ki namaaz ke liye azaan honi chahiye. Azaan ka saboot is aayat se bhi milta hai.

(2) Deen ki kisi cheez ka mazaaq udaana kufr hai, jaisa ke Allah Ta'ala ne azaan ka mazaaq udaane walon ko kafir qarar diya. Isi tarah aalim, masjid, Khanah-e-Ka'bah, namaaz, roza aur doosri deeni cheezon mein se kisi ka mazaaq udaana bhi kufr hai.

(3) Deeni cheezon ka mazaaq udaane wale ahmaq aur be-aqal hain jo aisi bewaqoofi aur jaahilana harkatein karte hain.

### Deeni cheezon ka mazaaq udaane walon ka radd

Is aayat mein deeni cheezon ka mazaaq udaane walon ka kitna shadeed radd hai. Afsos ke jo kaam Yahoodi aur munaafiq kiya karte the, wahi kaam Musalman kehlane walon mein aate ja rahe hain. Namaaz, roza, Hajj, zakaat, farishte, Jannat, hoorain, Dozakh aur us ka azaab, Qurani aayaat, Ahadees-e-Nabawi, deeni kitaabein, deeni shaa'ir, imaamah, daarhi, masjid, madrasa, deendaar aadmi, deeni libaas aur deeni jumle, garz woh kaunsi mazhabi cheez hai jis ka is zamaane mein khule aam filmon, dramaon, khaas taur par mazaahiya dramaon, aam bol-chaal, doston ki majlison, dunyawi taqreeron, hansi mazaaq ki nashiston aur baahmi guftagu mein mazaaq nahin udaaya jaata. Afsos ke Musalman kehlane wale Islam ka mazaaq udaate hain. Musalman kehlane walon ko daarhi, imaamah aur mazhabi libaas se nafrat hai. Musalman kehlane wale ko azaan sun kar takleef hoti hai. Quran-o-Hadees ki baatein use puraani baatein lagti hain. Yaad rakhein ke deeni shaa'ir ka mazaaq udaana kufr hai. Deen ka mazaaq udaane walon ke baare mein Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

وَ اِذَا عَلِمَ مِنْ اٰیٰتِنَا شَیْــٴَـاﰳ اتَّخَذَهَا هُزُوًاؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ لَهُمْ عَذَابٌ مُّهِیْنٌ(الجاثیہ :۹)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur jab hamari aayaton mein se kisi par ittila paaye to use mazaaq banaata hai. Un ke liye zillat ka azaab hai.

Aur farmata hai:

وَ لَىٕنْ سَاَلْتَهُمْ لَیَقُوْلُنَّ اِنَّمَا كُنَّا نَخُوْضُ وَ نَلْعَبُؕ-قُلْ اَبِاللّٰهِ وَ اٰیٰتِهٖ وَ رَسُوْلِهٖ كُنْتُمْ تَسْتَهْزِءُوْنَ(۶۵)لَا تَعْتَذِرُوْا قَدْ كَفَرْتُمْ بَعْدَ اِیْمَانِكُمْ(التوبہ:۶۵، ۶۶)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur ae Mehboob! Agar tum un se poochho to kahenge: Hum to yunhi hansi khel mein the. Tum farmaao: Kya Allah aur us ki aayaton aur us ke Rasool se hanste ho? Bahane na banao, tum Musalman ho kar kafir ho chuke.

Aur farmata hai:

وَ ذَرِ الَّذِیْنَ اتَّخَذُوْا دِیْنَهُمْ لَعِبًا وَّ لَهْوًا وَّ غَرَّتْهُمُ الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَا( انعام:۷۰)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur un ko chhor de jinhon ne apna deen hansi khel bana liya aur unhein duniya ki zindagi ne dhoke mein daal diya.

Allah Ta'ala Musalmanon ko aql-e-saleem ata farmaaye aur in aayaat ko saamne rakh kar apni haalat par ghaur karne aur apni is rawish ko badalne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye. Aameen.

## Ayat 5:62

`Source: tafseerId=50789; ayatId=736; surah=5; ayat=62; para=6; volume=2`

## Ayat 5:63

`Source: tafseerId=50790; ayatId=737; surah=5; ayat=63; para=6; volume=2`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Un ke darvesh aur ulama unhein gunaah ki baat kehne aur haraam khaane se kyun nahin rokte? Beshak yeh bahut hi bure kaam kar rahe hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{لَوْ لَا یَنْهٰىهُمُ الرَّبّٰنِیُّوْنَ: Unhein kyun na roka un ke paadriyon ne?}** Hazrat Hasan Basri رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Is aayat mein **“ar-Rabbaniyyoon”** se Isaaiyon ke ulama aur **“al-Ahbaar”** se Yahoodiyon ke ulama murad hain. Ek lafz se Yahoodiyon ke darvesh aur doosre lafz se Yahoodiyon ke ulama murad hain.

### Ulama par burai se rokna zaroori hai

Yahan Yahoodi darveshon aur ulama ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke unhone apni qaum ko gunaah ki baat kehne aur haraam khaane se kyun na roka. Is se maloom hua ke aalim-e-deen bhi is baat par pakda jaayega ke woh gunaah hota dekhe aur qudrat ke bawajood mana na kare, kyunki aisa aalim gunaah karne wale ki tarah hai. Imam Fakhruddin Raazi رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Gunaah roohani marz hai aur iska ilaaj Allah Ta'ala, us ki sifaat aur us ke ahkaam ki ma'rifat hai. Agar yeh ilm haasil hone ke bawajood gunaah khatam na hon to yeh aise marz ki tarah hai jo dawa khaane ke bawajood khatam na ho. Aalim ka gunaah karna is baat ki daleel hai ke us ka dil ka marz nihayat shadeed hai.” (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 63, 4/393)

Aalim par waajib hai ke khud bhi sambhle aur doosron ko bhi sambhaale. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain: “Quran-e-Paak mein ulama ke liye is aayat se zyada daant-dapat wali koi aayat nahin.” (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 63, 1/509)

Aur farmate hain: “Quran-e-Paak mein yeh aayat ulama ke baare mein bahut sakht hai, kyunki Allah Ta'ala ne burai se rokna chhor dene wale ko burai karne wale ki wa'eed mein shaamil farma diya hai.” (Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 63, safha 292-293)

Imam Dahhaak رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “Mere nazdeek Quran-e-Paak mein is aayat se zyada khauf dilaane wali koi aayat nahin. Afsos hai ke hum buraiyon se nahin rokte.” (Tafseer Tabari, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 63, 4/638)

## Ayat 5:64

`Source: tafseerId=50791; ayatId=738; surah=5; ayat=64; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالَتِ الْیَهُوْدُ یَدُ اللّٰهِ مَغْلُوْلَةٌؕ-غُلَّتْ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ لُعِنُوْا بِمَا قَالُوْاۘ-بَلْ یَدٰهُ مَبْسُوْطَتٰنِۙ-یُنْفِقُ كَیْفَ یَشَآءُؕ-وَ لَیَزِیْدَنَّ كَثِیْرًا مِّنْهُمْ مَّاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكَ مِنْ رَّبِّكَ طُغْیَانًا وَّ كُفْرًاؕ-وَ اَلْقَیْنَا بَیْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةَ وَ الْبَغْضَآءَ اِلٰى یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِؕ-كُلَّمَاۤ اَوْقَدُوْا نَارًا لِّلْحَرْبِ اَطْفَاَهَا اللّٰهُۙ-وَ یَسْعَوْنَ فِی الْاَرْضِ فَسَادًاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یُحِبُّ الْمُفْسِدِیْنَ(64)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Yahoodiyon ne kaha: Allah ka haath bandha hua hai. Un ke haath baandhe jaayen aur un par is kehne ki wajah se laanat hai. Balki Allah ke haath kushaada hain, jaise chahta hai kharch farmata hai. Aur ae Habeeb! Yeh jo tumhari taraf tumhare Rab ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai, yeh un mein se bahut se logon ki sarkashi aur kufr mein izafa karega. Aur humne Qiyamat tak un mein dushmani aur bughz daal diya. Jab kabhi yeh ladaai ki aag bhadkaate hain to Allah use bujha deta hai. Aur yeh zameen mein fasaad phailaane ke liye koshish karte hain aur Allah fasaad phailaane walon ko pasand nahin karta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَالَتِ الْیَهُوْدُ: Aur Yahoodiyon ne kaha.}** Is aayat ke shaan-e-nuzool ke baare mein Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke Yahoodi bahut khush-haal aur nihayat daulatmand the. Jab unhone Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki takzeeb aur mukhaalifat ki to un ka rizq kam ho gaya. Us waqt Finhaas Yahoodi ne kaha: “Allah Ta'ala ka haath bandha hua hai,” yani ma'aazAllah Allah Ta'ala rizq dene aur kharch karne mein bakheel hai. Us ke is qaul par kisi Yahoodi ne use mana na kiya, balki raazi rahe. Isi liye yeh sab ka qaul qarar diya gaya aur un ke baare mein yeh aayat naazil hui. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 64, 1/509; Madaarik, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 64, safha 293)

Farmaya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala to Jowaad-o-Kareem hai. Haan, in Yahoodiyon ke haath baandhe jaayen. Is irshaad ka ek asar yeh hua ke Yahoodi duniya mein sab se zyada bakheel ho gaye. Ya is jumle ka ma'ni yeh hai ke un ki behuda-goi aur gustakhi ki saza mein un ke haath Jahannam mein baandhe jaayen aur is tarah unhein Dozakh ki aag mein daala jaayega.

Aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ke haath kushaada hone se murad be-had karam aur mehrbaani hai ke Allah Ta'ala doston ko bhi nawazta hai aur dushmanon ko bhi mehroom nahin karta. Allah Ta'ala jism ke haath aur haath ke khulne se paak hai. Allah Ta'ala apni hikmat ke mutabiq jise aur jaise chahta hai ata farmata hai. Is par kisi ko etiraaz karne ki gunjaish nahin. Woh kisi ko ameer aur kisi ko ghareeb karta hai, lekin is ki wajah us ke khazaane mein kami ya karam mein nuqsaan nahin, balki bandon ke halaat ka taqaaza aur hazaaron hikmatein hain.

**{وَ لَیَزِیْدَنَّ كَثِیْرًا مِّنْهُمْ: Aur zaroor un mein se bahut se logon ki sarkashi aur kufr mein izafa karega.}** Jitna Quran-e-Paak naazil hota jaayega, utna hi Yahoodiyon ka hasad aur zid badhti jaayegi aur woh kufr aur sarkashi mein barhte rahenge. Yeh aise hai jaise taaqat dene wali ghiza kamzor maide wale ko beemaar kar deti hai; is mein ghiza ka qasoor nahin, mareez ke maide ka qasoor hai. Ya jaise sooraj ki roshni chamgadad ko andha kar deti hai; is mein sooraj ka qasoor nahin, chamgadad ki aankh ka qasoor hai.

### Aayat “وَ لَیَزِیْدَنَّ كَثِیْرًا مِّنْهُمْ” se maloom hone wale masail

Is se do baatein maloom hui:

(1) Jis ke dil mein Sarwar-e-Kainaat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki azmat na ho, us ke liye Quran-o-Hadees kufr mein izafe ka sabab ban sakte hain, jaise aaj bahut se be-deen logon ko dekha ja raha hai. Yaad rahe ke deen ki azmat deen laane wale Mehboob صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki azmat se hoti hai.

(2) Kufr mein kami-beshi hoti hai. Koi kam shadeed kaafir hota hai aur koi zyada shadeed. Yeh kami-beshi kisi miqdaar ke aitbaar se nahin hoti. Isi tarah imaan mein bhi kami-beshi hoti hai: koi zyada mazboot imaan wala hota hai aur koi kamzor imaan wala.

**{وَ اَلْقَیْنَا بَیْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةَ وَ الْبَغْضَآءَ اِلٰى یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِ: Aur humne Qiyamat tak un mein dushmani aur bughz daal diya.}** Yani woh hamesha aapas mein ikhtilaaf rakhte rahenge aur un ke dil kabhi na milenge, agarche kabhi zahiri taur par Musalmanon ke khilaaf muttahid ho jaayen.

**{كُلَّمَاۤ اَوْقَدُوْا نَارًا لِّلْحَرْبِ: Jab kabhi yeh ladaai ki aag bhadkaate hain.}** Jab bhi Yahoodiyon ne fasaad, shar-angezi aur Allah Ta'ala ke hukm ki mukhaalifat ki to Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne un par kisi aise shakhs ko musallat kar diya jis ne unhein halaakat aur barbaadi se do-chaar kar diya. Pehle jab unhone fitna-fasaad shuru kiya aur Taurat ke ahkaam ki mukhaalifat ki to Allah Ta'ala ne Bakht Nasr ko un ki taraf bheja, jis ne unhein tabaah kar diya. Kuch arse baad jab unhone phir sar uthaya to Taitus Roomi ne un ki eent se eent baja di. Phir jab unhone shar-angezi shuru ki to Farsi Majusiyon ne un ka hashr kar diya. Kuch arse baad jab unhone fasaad ka baazaar garam kiya to Allah Ta'ala ne Musalmanon ko un par tasallut aur ghalaba ata farmaya. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 64, 1/510-511)

Ek qawl yeh hai ke jab bhi Yahoodi Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke khilaaf jang ka iraadah kar ke us ke asbaab tayyar karenge to Allah Ta'ala un ke mansoobe nakaam bana dega. (Abu Saud, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 64, 2/66)

## Ayat 5:65

`Source: tafseerId=50792; ayatId=739; surah=5; ayat=65; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لَوْ اَنَّ اَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ اٰمَنُوْا وَ اتَّقَوْا لَكَفَّرْنَا عَنْهُمْ سَیِّاٰتِهِمْ وَ لَاَدْخَلْنٰهُمْ جَنّٰتِ النَّعِیْمِ(65)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar Ahl-e-Kitaab imaan laaye aur parhezgaari ikhtiyaar karte to zaroor hum un ke gunaah mita dete aur zaroor unhein naimaton ke baaghon mein daakhil karte.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَوْ اَنَّ اَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ اٰمَنُوْا: Aur agar Ahl-e-Kitaab imaan laaye.}** Ahl-e-Kitaab ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke agar yeh imaan le aate to un ke gunaah bakhsh diye jaate aur yeh Jannat ke mustahiq qarar paate. Is aayat mein imaan laane ki ukhrawi jaza ka bayan hai aur agli aayat mein imaan laane ki dunyawi jaza ka bayan kiya gaya hai.

## Ayat 5:66

`Source: tafseerId=50793; ayatId=740; surah=5; ayat=66; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ لَوْ اَنَّهُمْ اَقَامُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ وَ الْاِنْجِیْلَ وَ مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْهِمْ مِّنْ رَّبِّهِمْ لَاَكَلُوْا مِنْ فَوْقِهِمْ وَ مِنْ تَحْتِ اَرْجُلِهِمْؕ-مِنْهُمْ اُمَّةٌ مُّقْتَصِدَةٌؕ-وَ كَثِیْرٌ مِّنْهُمْ سَآءَ مَا یَعْمَلُوْنَ۠   (66)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar woh Taurat aur Injeel aur jo kuch un ke Rab ki taraf se un ki taraf naazil kiya gaya tha, use qaayam karte to unhein un ke upar se aur un ke qadmon ke neeche se rizq milta. Un mein ek giroh seedhi aur darmiyani raah wala hai aur un mein aksar log bahut hi bure kaam kar rahe hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَوْ اَنَّهُمْ اَقَامُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ وَ الْاِنْجِیْلَ: Aur agar woh Taurat aur Injeel ko qaayam karte.}** Farmaya gaya ke agar Ahl-e-Kitaab Taurat, Injeel aur doosri aasmani kitaabon par is tarah amal karte ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ par imaan laate aur Aap ﷺ ki pairwi karte, kyunki Taurat aur Injeel mein bhi is ka hukm diya gaya hai, aur Allah Ta'ala ne apne Rasoolon par jo doosri kitaabein naazil farmaeen un sab mein Imam-ul-Ambiya ﷺ ka zikr aur Aap ﷺ par imaan laane ka hukm hai, to unhein un ke upar se aur un ke qadmon ke neeche se rizq milta. Yani un ke rizq mein bahut wus'at hoti aur har taraf se un tak rizq pahunchta.

### Deen ki pabandi aur Allah Ta'ala ki ita'at rizq mein wus'at ka zariya hai

Is aayat se maloom hua ke deen ki pabandi aur Allah Ta'ala ki ita'at aur farmabardaari se rizq mein wus'at hoti hai. Hazrat Abu Ishaq Hamdani رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Jise apni umr mein izafa aur rizq mein ziyadati pasand ho, use chahiye ke Allah Ta'ala se dare aur rishtedaron ke saath silah-rahmi kare.” (Shu'ab-ul-Imaan, as-saadis wal-khamsun min shu'ab-il-imaan, 6/219, hadees: 7947)

Aur Hazrat Abdullah bin Masood رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Ae logo! Jo cheez tumhein Jannat ke qareeb aur Jahannam se door kar sakti hai, main ne tumhein us ka hukm de diya hai, aur jo cheez tumhein Jahannam ke qareeb aur Jannat se door kar sakti hai, main ne tumhein us se mana kar diya hai. Beshak Rooh-ul-Ameen عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne mere dil mein yeh baat daali hai ke koi jaan us waqt tak wafaat nahin paayegi jab tak apna rizq poora na kar le. Is liye Allah Ta'ala se daro aur achhe tareeqe se rizq talab karo. Rizq der se milna tumhein is baat par na ubhaare ke tum Allah Ta'ala ki na-farmaani ke zariye rizq talab karne lago, kyunki Allah Ta'ala ke paas jo kuch hai, woh us ki ita'at ke zariye hi haasil kiya ja sakta hai.” (Sharh-us-Sunnah, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, baab-ut-tawakkul alallah عزوجل، 7/329, hadees: 4006)

**{مِنْهُمْ اُمَّةٌ مُّقْتَصِدَةٌ: Un mein ek giroh darmiyani raah wala hai.}** Farmaya gaya ke saare Ahl-e-Kitaab ek jaise nahin. Un mein kuch log darmiyani raah ikhtiyaar karne wale hain aur hadd se nahin badhte. Yahoodiyon mein se woh log is mein shaamil hain jo Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ par imaan laaye, jaise Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ aur doosre log. Baqi aksar na-farmaan hain aur kufr par qaaim hain.

## Ayat 5:67

`Source: tafseerId=50794; ayatId=741; surah=5; ayat=67; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الرَّسُوْلُ بَلِّغْ مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكَ مِنْ رَّبِّكَؕ-وَ اِنْ لَّمْ تَفْعَلْ فَمَا بَلَّغْتَ رِسَالَتَهٗؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ یَعْصِمُكَ مِنَ النَّاسِؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الْكٰفِرِیْنَ(67)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Rasool! Jo kuch Aap ki taraf Aap ke Rab ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai, use pahunchaa dein aur agar Aap ne aisa na kiya to Aap ne us ka koi paighaam bhi nahin pahunchaya, aur Allah logon se Aap ki hifaazat farmayega. Beshak Allah kaafiron ko hidaayat nahin deta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الرَّسُوْلُ: Ae Rasool!}** Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko Rasool ke laqab se khitaab farmaya. Yeh Sarkar-e-Do-Aalam ﷺ ki khaas fazeelat hai. Doosre Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko un ke mubarak naam se khitaab farmaya gaya, lekin Aap ﷺ ko Rasool keh kar pukara gaya.

Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ se farmaya gaya ke Aap Allah Ta'ala ke paighaam logon tak pahunchate rahein aur kisi qisam ka khatra mehsoos na karein. Allah Ta'ala un kaafiron se Aap ki hifaazat farmayega jo Aap ko shaheed karna chahte hain. Is khatre ki wajah se safar ke dauraan raat ko Sarkar-e-Do-Aalam ﷺ ki hifaazat ke liye pehra diya jaata tha. Jab yeh aayat-e-mubarakah naazil hui to pehra hata diya gaya. Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne pehredaron se farmaya: “Tum log chale jao, Allah Ta'ala ne meri hifaazat ka zimmah le liya hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, baab wa min Suratil-Maaidah, 5/35, hadees: 3057)

Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ki hifaazat ke liye pehra dene ka sharaf sab se pehle Hazrat Sa'd bin Abi Waqqaas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko haasil hua. Hazrat Ayesha Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا farmati hain: “Rasoolullah ﷺ Madinah aate waqt ek raat be-khwaab rahe, phir farmaya: ‘Kaash koi nek shakhs hamari hifaazat karta.’ Achanak hum ne hathiyaaron ki aawaaz suni. Aap ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: ‘Yeh kaun hai?’ Unhone arz ki: ‘Main Sa'd hoon.’ Aap ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: ‘Tumhein kya cheez yahan laayi hai?’ Unhone arz ki: ‘Mere dil mein Rasoolullah ﷺ ke baare mein khatra guzra, is liye main Aap ki hifaazat karne aaya hoon.’ Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne un ke liye dua ki, phir Aap ﷺ so gaye.” (Muslim, Kitaab Fazaail-us-Sahaabah, baab fi fazli Sa'd bin Abi Waqqaas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, safha 1314, hadees: 40 (2410))

## Ayat 5:68

`Source: tafseerId=50795; ayatId=742; surah=5; ayat=68; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** قُلْ یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ لَسْتُمْ عَلٰى شَیْءٍ حَتّٰى تُقِیْمُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ وَ الْاِنْجِیْلَ وَ مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكُمْ مِّنْ رَّبِّكُمْؕ-وَ لَیَزِیْدَنَّ كَثِیْرًا مِّنْهُمْ مَّاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكَ مِنْ رَّبِّكَ طُغْیَانًا وَّ كُفْرًاۚ-فَلَا تَاْسَ عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكٰفِرِیْنَ(68)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farma do: Ae Kitabiyo! Jab tak tum Taurat, Injeel aur jo kuch tumhari taraf tumhare Rab ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai, use qaayam nahin kar lete, tum kisi cheez par nahin ho. Aur ae Habeeb! Yeh jo tumhari taraf tumhare Rab ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai, yeh un mein se bahut se logon ki sarkashi aur kufr mein zaroor izafa karega. To tum kaafir qaum par gham na karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ یٰۤاَهْلَ الْكِتٰبِ: Tum farma do, ae Kitabiyo!}** Ahl-e-Kitaab se farmaya gaya ke jab tak tum Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ par imaan nahin laate, tum kisi deen-o-millat par nahin ho. Agar tum haqeeqi taur par Taurat aur Injeel par amal karo to Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ par bhi imaan lao, kyunki Aap ﷺ par imaan laane ka hukm Taurat aur Injeel mein maujood hai.

**{مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَیْكَ مِنْ رَّبِّكَ: Ae Habeeb! Yeh jo Aap ki taraf Aap ke Rab ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai.}** Yani ae Habeeb ﷺ, jo Quran Aap ki taraf Aap ke Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se naazil kiya gaya hai, yeh Ahl-e-Kitaab ke ulama aur sardaron ki puraani sarkashi aur kufr mein izafa karega. Jab Quran-e-Majeed ki koi aayat naazil hoti hai to woh us ka inkaar kar dete hain aur is tarah apne kufr aur sarkashi mein aur sakht ho jaate hain. Is liye ae Habeeb ﷺ, jo Yahoodi Aap ki nubuwwat ka inkaar kar rahe hain aur Aap par imaan nahin la rahe, un ki wajah se ghamgeen na hon, kyunki un ke kufr ka wabaal unhi par padega. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 68, 2/419; Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 68, 1/511-513, multaqatan)

## Ayat 5:69

`Source: tafseerId=50796; ayatId=743; surah=5; ayat=69; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا وَ الصّٰبِــٴُـوْنَ وَ النَّصٰرٰى مَنْ اٰمَنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَ الْیَوْمِ الْاٰخِرِ وَ عَمِلَ صَالِحًا فَلَا خَوْفٌ عَلَیْهِمْ وَ لَا هُمْ یَحْزَنُوْنَ(69)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak jo apne aap ko Musalman kehte hain, aur Yahoodi, sitaron ki pooja karne wale aur Isaai, in mein se jo Allah aur Qiyamat par sachche dil se imaan laaye aur nek amal kare, to un par na koi khauf hoga aur na woh ghamgeen honge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: Beshak woh jo apne aap ko Musalman kehte hain.}** Pichhli aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne bayan farmaya ke Ahl-e-Kitaab us waqt tak kisi deen-o-millat par nahin jab tak woh imaan nahin laate. Is aayat mein bayan farmaya gaya ke yeh hukm sirf Ahl-e-Kitaab ke liye khaas nahin, balki har millat wala is mein shaamil hai. Kisi ko us waqt tak koi fazeelat aur khoobi haasil nahin ho sakti jab tak woh sachche dil se Allah Ta'ala aur Qiyamat ke din par imaan na laaye aur aise nek amal na kare jin se Allah Ta'ala raazi ho.

### Auliya-e-Kiraam aur un ke mazaaraat ke baare mein ghulu

Auliya-e-Kiraam ki ta'zeem karna aur faiz-o-barkaatein haasil karne ke liye un ke mazaaraat par haazir hona jaaiz aur pasandeeda amal hai, kyunki Auliya-e-Kiraam Allah Ta'ala ke maqbool bande hain aur un ke mazaaraat rahmat-e-Ilaahi utarne ke maqamaat hain. Lekin aaj ke zamaane mein Auliya-e-Kiraam aur un ke mazaaraat ke baare mein intehaai ghulu kiya jaata hai. Baaz log un ki jaaiz ta'zeem ko najaaiz aur haraam kehte hain aur un ke mazaaraat par haaziri ko shirk aur but-parasti kehte hain. Doosri taraf baaz naadaan log un ki ta'zeem mein shar'i hadd paar kar jaate hain aur mazaaraat par aise kaam karte hain jo Shariat mein najaaiz aur haraam hain.

Misal ke taur par ta'zeem ke liye mazar ka tawaaf karna, mazaar ke saahib ko sajdah-e-ta'zeemi karna, mazaaraat par baaja baja kar qawwaliyan padhna, auraton ka mardon ke saath mil kar mazaaraat par jaana aur urs waghera ke mauqe par lahu-o-la'ib ka intezaam karna. Auliya ki ta'zeem ko najaaiz aur haraam kehne walon aur mazaaraat par haaziri ko shirk aur but-parasti samajhne walon ko chahiye ke woh is aayat ko saamne rakh kar apni haalat par ghaur karein. Shar'an jaaiz amal ko apni taraf se najaaiz aur haraam keh kar deen mein ziyaadti na karein, balki haq ki pairwi karein. Aur mazaaraat par najaaiz aur haraam kaam karne wale bhi apne af'aal se baaz aa jaayen, taake Auliya ke dushman un ki naadaniyon ki wajah se logon ko Allah Ta'ala ke maqbool bandon se door karne ki koshish na kar sakein.

Jab unhone is hukm ki mukhaalifat ki aur shikaar karne se baaz na aaye to Hazrat Daawood عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne un par laanat ki aur un ke khilaaf dua farmayi. Aur Ashaab-e-Maa'idah ne jab naazil kiye hue dastarkhwaan ki nematein khaane ke baad mana kiye jaane ke bawajood unhein zakhira kiya aur imaan na laaye to Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne un ke khilaaf dua farmayi. Woh khinzeer aur bandar ban gaye. Us waqt un ki tadaad paanch hazaar thi. (Jamal, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 78, 2/260-261)

Baaz mufassireen ka qaul hai ke Yahoodi apne baap-daada par fakhr kiya karte the aur kehte the: Hum Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad hain. Is aayat mein unhein bata diya gaya ke un Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne to un par laanat ki hai. Ek qaul ye hai ke Hazrat Daawood aur Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ki tashreef-aawari ki bashaarat di aur Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ par imaan na laane aur kufr karne walon par laanat ki thi. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 78, 1/516)

Is se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ke nek bandon ke munh se nikli hui nuqsaan ki dua duniya aur Aakhirat mein ruswaai aur barbaadi ka sabab ban sakti hai. Is liye un kaam se bachte rehna chahiye jo un ki naaraazgi ka sabab banen.

**{ذٰلِكَ بِمَا عَصَوْا وَّ كَانُوْا یَعْتَدُوْنَ: Ye laanat un ki naafarmaani aur sar-kashi ka badla hai.}** Is mein Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ko tasalli di gayi hai ke aap ﷺ un Yahoodiyon ki sar-kashi se ghamgeen na hon. Ye log aadati mujrim aur puraane sar-kash hain. Yahan tak ke sar-kashi ki saza mein bandar aur khinzeer bhi ban chuke hain. Is waqt in ka aman mein rehna sirf is liye hai ke aap ﷺ tamaam jahaanon ke liye rahmat hain, aap ﷺ ki maujoodgi mein azaab nahin aayega.

## Ayat 5:79

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{كَانُوْا لَا یَتَنَاهَوْنَ عَنْ مُّنْكَرٍ: Woh ek doosre ko kisi bure kaam se mana na karte the.}** Yahoodiyon ki ek sar-kashi ye thi ke unhone burai hoti dekh kar ek doosre ko us se rokna chhor diya tha. ### Gunaah se rokna waajib aur rokne se baaz rehna gunaah hai

Is se maloom hua ke logon ko burai se rokna waajib hai aur gunaah se mana karne se baaz rehna sakht gunaah hai. Woh ghulu karne walon ki tehreefein, ahl-e-baatil ke jhoote da'we aur jaahilon ki ghalat ta'weel aur tashreeh ko deen se door karte rahenge.” (Sunan-ul-Kubra lil-Baihaqi, Kitaab-ush-Shahaadaat, Baab-ur-Rajul min Ahlil-Fiqh..., 10/253, hadees: 20911)

Woh maqbool jamaat in sab cheezon ko door karegi. Alhamdulillah! Aaj tak aisa ho raha hai aur aainda bhi hoga. Dekho, ulama-e-deen ki sarparasti na hukoomat karti hai na qaum, lekin phir bhi ye jamaat paida ho rahi hai aur deen ki khidmat barabar kar rahi hai.” (Miraat-ul-Manaajih, Kitaab-ul-Ilm, al-fasl-us-saani, 1/201, tehat-ul-hadees: 230)

## Ayat 5:80

`Source: tafseerId=50806; ayatId=754; surah=5; ayat=80; para=6; volume=2`

**Arabic:** تَرٰى كَثِیْرًا مِّنْهُمْ یَتَوَلَّوْنَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْاؕ-لَبِئْسَ مَا قَدَّمَتْ لَهُمْ اَنْفُسُهُمْ اَنْ سَخِطَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِمْ وَ فِی الْعَذَابِ هُمْ خٰلِدُوْنَ(80)

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Ka'b bin Ashraf Yahoodi aur us ke saathi Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ se bughz aur dushmani ki wajah se Mushrikeen-e-Makkah ke paas gaye aur unhein Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ se jang karne par ubhaara. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 80, 1/517)

Kaafiron se dosti ka nateeja ye hua ke Allah Ta'ala ka ghazab un par naazil hua aur Aakhirat mein woh hamesha ke azaab ke mustahiq thehre.

### Kaafiron se dosti karne walon ke liye ibrat

Is se maloom hua ke kaafiron se dosti aur muwaalaat haraam hai aur Jahannam mein le jaane wala kaam hai. Ye aayat un Musalmanon ke liye ibrat ka chabuk hai jo kaafiron ki Musalmanon se khuli dushmani apni aankhon se dekhne ke bawajood sirf apne mansab ko bachaane ke liye un ki taraf dosti ka haath badhaate, un ki haan mein haan milaate aur un ki naaraazgi se darte hain. Allah Ta'ala hamein samajh aur amal ki taufeeq ata farmaye.

### Shaan-e-nuzool

Islam ke ibtidaai zamaane mein jab Quraysh ke kaafiron ne Musalmanon ko bahut takleefein dein to Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم mein se gyarah mard aur chaar auraton ne Huzoor ﷺ ke hukm se Habsha ki taraf hijrat ki. Ye sab log elaan-e-nubuwwat ke paanchwein saal Rajab mein samundari safar karke Habsha pahunche. Is hijrat ko Hijrat-e-Ula kehte hain.

In ke baad Hazrat Ja'far bin Abi Talib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ bhi gaye. Phir aur Musalman rawana hote rahe, yahan tak ke bachchon aur auraton ke ilawa muhajireen ki tadaad bayasi mardon tak pahunch gayi. Jab Quraysh ko is hijrat ka ilm hua to unhone tohfe de kar kuch logon ko Najashi badshah ke paas bheja.

Un logon ne badshah ke darbar mein ja kar kaha: Hamare mulk mein ek shakhs ne nubuwwat ka da'wa kiya hai aur logon ko naadaan bana diya hai. Jo jamaat aap ke mulk mein aayi hai woh yahan fasaad phailaayegi aur aap ki ri'aya ko baaghi bana degi. Ham aap ko khabar dene aaye hain aur hamari qaum darkhwast karti hai ke aap unhein hamare hawaale kar dein.

Najashi ne kaha: Pehle ham in logon se guftagu kar lein, baaqi baat baad mein dekhenge. Us ne Musalmanon ko bulaya aur poocha: Tum Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur un ki walidah ke baare mein kya aqeeda rakhte ho?

Hazrat Ja'far bin Abi Talib رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Allah Ta'ala ke bande aur us ke Rasool, Kalimatullah aur Ruhullah hain, aur Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا paak daaman kunwaari hain. Ye sun kar Najashi ne zameen se lakri ka ek tukra uthaya aur kaha: Khuda عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam! Tumhare Aaqa ﷺ ne Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baare mein is lakri jitna bhi zyada nahin kaha. Yani Huzoor ﷺ ka irshad Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke kalam ke bilkul mutabiq hai. Ye dekh kar Makkah ke mushrikon ke chehre utar gaye.

Phir Najashi ne Quran-e-Pak sunne ki khwahish ki. Hazrat Ja'far رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Surah Maryam ki tilaawat ki. Darbar mein maujood Nasrani aalim aur darvesh Quran-e-Pak sun kar be-ikhtiyaar rone lage. Najashi ne Musalmanon se kaha: Tum meri saltanat mein bilkul aman mein ho. Makkah ke mushrik apne maqsad mein naakaam ho kar laut gaye aur Musalman Najashi ke paas izzat aur aaraam ke saath rahe. Allah Ta'ala ke fazl se Najashi ko bhi imaan ki daulat naseeb hui. Is waaqe'e ke baare mein ye aayat naazil hui. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 82, 1/518)

## Ayat 5:83

`Source: tafseerId=50809; ayatId=757; surah=5; ayat=83; para=7; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا سَمِعُوْا مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَى الرَّسُوْلِ تَرٰۤى اَعْیُنَهُمْ تَفِیْضُ مِنَ الدَّمْعِ مِمَّا عَرَفُوْا مِنَ الْحَقِّۚ-یَقُوْلُوْنَ رَبَّنَاۤ اٰمَنَّا فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشّٰهِدِیْنَ(83)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab ye sunte hain woh jo Rasool ki taraf naazil kiya gaya to tum dekhoge ke un ki aankhein aansuon se ubal padti hain, is liye ke woh haq ko pehchan gaye. Kehte hain: Ae hamare Rab! Ham imaan laaye, pas Tu hamein haq ke gawaahon ke saath likh de.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذَا سَمِعُوْا مَاۤ اُنْزِلَ اِلَى الرَّسُوْلِ: Aur jab ye sunte hain jo Rasool ki taraf naazil kiya gaya.}** Jab Habsha ki taraf hijrat karne wale Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم Najashi ke darbar mein jama the aur Makkah ke mushrikon ka wafd bhi wahan maujood tha, to Najashi ne Hazrat Ja'far Tayyari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se poocha: Kya tumhari kitaab mein Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ka zikr hai? Hazrat Ja'far رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Quran-e-Pak mein Hazrat Maryam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا ke naam par ek poori Surah hai. Isi tarah jab Habsha ka ek wafd Huzoor ﷺ ki khidmat mein haazir hua aur us mein sattar afraad the, to Huzoor ﷺ ne un ke saamne Surah Yaseen ki tilaawat farmayi. In sab ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke jab ye log us kalam ko sunte hain jo Rasool ﷺ ki taraf naazil kiya gaya, to tum dekhoge ke un ki aankhein aansuon se ubal padti hain, kyunki unhone haq ko pehchan liya. Is aayat se maloom hua ke zikr-e-Ilaahi ke waqt ishq-o-muhabbat mein rona badi ibaadat hai. Isi tarah Allah Ta'ala ke azaab ke khauf aur us ki rahmat ki umeed mein rona bhi ibaadat hai. Bahut se Quran se muhabbat rakhne wale Quran-e-Kareem ki tilaawat karte hue jhoomte hain. Ye Quran-e-Kareem se lutf aur khushi milne ki wajah se hota hai. Jaise subah ki khushgawaar hawa se narm shaakhein hilti hain, waise hi tilaawat karne wala Allah Ta'ala ki rahmat ki hawa se hilta hai.

### Tilaawat-e-Quran ke 2 fazaail

Hazrat Abu Musa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo Momin Quran padhta hai us ki misaal turanj ki si hai ke khushboo bhi achhi hai aur zaiqa bhi achha hai. Jo Momin Quran nahin padhta, woh khajoor ki tarah hai ke us mein khushboo nahin magar zaiqa meetha hai. Jo munaafiq Quran padhta hai, woh phool ki tarah hai ke us mein khushboo hai magar zaiqa kadwa hai. Aur jo munaafiq Quran nahin padhta, woh andarain ki tarah hai ke us mein khushboo bhi nahin aur zaiqa bhi kadwa hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-At'imah, Baab zikr-ut-ta'aam, 3/535, hadees: 5427)

Hazrat Aishah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہا se marwi hai, Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jo Quran padhne mein maahir hai, woh Kiraaman Kaatibeen ke saath hai. Aur jo shakhs ruk-ruk kar Quran padhta hai aur ye us par mushkil hai, yani us ki zubaan aasaani se nahin chalti aur woh takleef ke saath padhta hai, us ke liye do ajr hain.” (Muslim, Kitaab Salaat-il-Musaafireen wa Qasruha, Baab fazl-ul-maahir bil-Quran..., s. 400, hadees: 244 (798)) [1]

### Tilaawat-e-Quran ke waqt rone ki targheeb

Tilaawat-e-Quran ke waqt rona mustahab hai. Ye Allah Ta'ala ke buzurg bandon ka tareeqa hai ke buland martabon par pahunchne ke bawajood jab un ke saamne Allah Ta'ala ke kalam ki aayaat padhi jaati hain to woh Allah Ta'ala ke khauf se rone lagte hain. Jaisa ke Surah Maryam ki aayat 58 mein bayan hua. Allah Ta'ala un ki ek aur kaifiyat bayan farmata hai:

“اَللّٰهُ نَزَّلَ اَحْسَنَ الْحَدِیْثِ كِتٰبًا مُّتَشَابِهًا مَّثَانِیَ ﳓ تَقْشَعِرُّ مِنْهُ جُلُوْدُ الَّذِیْنَ یَخْشَوْنَ رَبَّهُمْۚ-ثُمَّ تَلِیْنُ جُلُوْدُهُمْ وَ قُلُوْبُهُمْ اِلٰى ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِ” (Zumar: 23)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Allah ne sab se achhi kitaab utaari ke saari ek jaisi hai aur baar-baar dohraayi jaati hai. Is se un logon ke badan par baal khade ho jaate hain jo apne Rab se darte hain, phir un ki khaalein aur dil Allah ki yaad ki taraf narm pad jaate hain.

Hazrat Sa'd bin Abi Waqqaas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Ye Quran gham ke saath naazil hua tha. Jab tum ise padho to ro-o aur agar na ro sako to rone ki shakal bana lo.” (Ibn Majah, Kitaab Iqaamat-is-Salaat was-Sunnatu feeha, Baab fi husn-is-saut bil-Quran, 2/129, hadees: 1337)

Hazrat Buraidah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai, Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Quran ko gham ke saath padho, kyunki ye gham ke saath naazil hua hai.” (Mu'jam-ul-Awsat, Baab-ul-Alif, man ismuhu Ibrahim, 2/166, hadees: 2902)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا farmate hain: “Jab tum Surah Subhan mein sajde ki aayat padho to sajdah karne mein jaldi na karo, yahan tak ke tum ro-o. Agar tum mein se kisi ki aankh na roye to dil ko rona chahiye.” Dil mein gham aur afsos ko haazir karne se rona paida hota hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Maryam, tehat-ul-aayat: 58, 7/551)

Imam Muhammad Ghazali رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Gham zahir karne ka tareeqa ye hai ke Quran ki dhamkiyon, waadon aur ahd-o-paimaaan ko yaad kare. Phir soche ke us ne Allah Ta'ala ke ahkaam aur mana ki hui cheezon ke baare mein kitni kotahi ki hai. Is tarah woh zaroor ghamgeen hoga aur royega. Agar saaf dil logon ki tarah gham aur rona zahir na ho to gham aur rone ke na hone par roye, kyunki ye sab se badi museebat hai.” (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab Aadaab Tilaawat-il-Quran, al-baab-us-saani fi zaahir Aadaab-it-Tilaawah, 1/368)

[1] Tilaawat-e-Quran ke mazeed fazaail jaanne ke liye Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَتْ بَرَکَاتُہُمُ الْعَالِیَہ ka risaala “Tilaawat ki Fazilat” (matboo'ah Maktabat-ul-Madinah) ka mutaala'a farmayein.

## Ayat 5:84

`Source: tafseerId=50810; ayatId=758; surah=5; ayat=84; para=7; volume=2`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا لَنَا لَا نُؤْمِنُ بِاللّٰهِ وَ مَا جَآءَنَا مِنَ الْحَقِّۙ-وَ نَطْمَعُ اَنْ یُّدْخِلَنَا رَبُّنَا مَعَ الْقَوْمِ الصّٰلِحِیْنَ(84)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur hamein kya hai ke ham Allah par aur us haq par imaan na laayein jo hamare paas aaya aur ham umeed rakhte hain ke hamara Rab hamein nek logon ke saath Jannat mein daakhil kar de.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَا لَنَا لَا نُؤْمِنُ بِاللّٰهِ: Aur hamein kya hai ke ham Allah par imaan na laayein.}** Jab Habsha ka wafd Islam se musharraf ho kar wapas gaya to Yahoodiyon ne unhein is par malamat ki. Is ke jawab mein unhone kaha: Jab haq wazeh ho gaya to ham imaan kyun na laayein? Aisi haalat mein imaan na lana malamat ke laaiq hai, imaan lana nahin, kyunki imaan dono jahaan ki kaamyaabi ka sabab hai. (Khaazin, Al-Maidah, tehat-ul-aayat: 84, 1/520)

## Ayat 5:85

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=759; surah=5; ayat=85; para=7; volume=2`

**Arabic:** فَاَثَابَهُمُ اللّٰهُ بِمَا قَالُوْا جَنّٰتٍ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْاَنْهٰرُ خٰلِدِیْنَ فِیْهَاؕ-وَ ذٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ الْمُحْسِنِیْنَ(85)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To Allah ne un ke is kehne ke badle unhein woh baagaat ata farmaye jin ke neeche nahrein jaari hain, hamesha un mein rahenge aur ye nek logon ki jaza hai.
