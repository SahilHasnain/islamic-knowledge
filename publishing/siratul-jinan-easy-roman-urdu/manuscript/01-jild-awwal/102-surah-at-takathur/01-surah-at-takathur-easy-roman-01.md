# Surah At-Takathur — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 102:1

`Source: tafseerId=54479; ayatId=6270; surah=102; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Zyada maal jama karne ki talab ne tumhein ghaafil kar diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ: Zyada maal jama karne ki talab ne tumhein ghaafil kar diya.} Is ka matlab yeh hai ke zyada maal jama karne aur apne maal aur aulaad par fakhr karne ne tumhein Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat se ghaafil kar diya. (Jalalain, At-Takathur, taht-al-aayah: 1, safha 505)

### Maal ki kasrat ki lalach ki mazammat

Is se maloom hua ke zyada maal ki lalach karna aur maal aur aulaad par fakhr karna mazmoom hai. Jo shakhs is mein mubtala ho jaata hai, woh Aakhirat ki sa'adaton se mehroom reh jaata hai. Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

“اِعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّمَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَا لَعِبٌ وَّ لَهْوٌ وَّ زِیْنَةٌ وَّ تَفَاخُرٌۢ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ تَكَاثُرٌ فِی الْاَمْوَالِ وَ الْاَوْلَادِؕ-كَمَثَلِ غَیْثٍ اَعْجَبَ الْكُفَّارَ نَبَاتُهٗ ثُمَّ یَهِیْجُ فَتَرٰىهُ مُصْفَرًّا ثُمَّ یَكُوْنُ حُطَامًاؕ-وَ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ شَدِیْدٌۙ-وَّ مَغْفِرَةٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ وَ رِضْوَانٌؕ-وَ مَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَاۤ اِلَّا مَتَاعُ الْغُرُوْرِ‘‘ (Hadeed: 20)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Jaan lo ke duniya ki zindagi to sirf khel-kood, zeenat, aapas mein fakhr-o-ghuroor karna aur maal aur aulaad mein ek doosre se badhne ki chaahat hai. Duniya ki zindagi us baarish ki tarah hai jis ka ugaaya hua sabzah kaafiron ko achha laga, phir woh sabzah sookh jaata hai aur tum use peela dekhte ho, phir woh kuchla hua bekaar ho jaata hai. Aur Aakhirat mein sakht azaab hai aur Allah ki taraf se bakhshish aur raza bhi hai. Aur duniya ki zindagi to sirf dhoke ka saamaan hai.

Aur Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ اَمْوَالُكُمْ وَ لَاۤ اَوْلَادُكُمْ عَنْ ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِۚ-وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْ ذٰلِكَ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْخٰسِرُوْنَ‘‘ (Munafiqoon: 9)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Tumhare maal aur tumhari aulaad tumhein Allah ke zikr se ghaafil na kar dein aur jo aisa karega to wahi log nuqsaan uthane wale hain.

Aur irshad farmaya:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِنَّ مِنْ اَزْوَاجِكُمْ وَ اَوْلَادِكُمْ عَدُوًّا لَّكُمْ فَاحْذَرُوْهُمْۚ-وَ اِنْ تَعْفُوْا وَ تَصْفَحُوْا وَ تَغْفِرُوْا فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(۱۴) اِنَّمَاۤ اَمْوَالُكُمْ وَ اَوْلَادُكُمْ فِتْنَةٌؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عِنْدَهٗۤ اَجْرٌ عَظِیْمٌ(۱۵) فَاتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ وَ اسْمَعُوْا وَ اَطِیْعُوْا وَ اَنْفِقُوْا خَیْرًا لِّاَنْفُسِكُمْؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّوْقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهٖ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ‘‘ (Taghaabun: 14-16)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae imaan walo! Beshak tumhari biwiyon aur tumhari aulaad mein se kuch tumhare dushman hain, to un se ehtiyaat rakho. Aur agar tum maaf karo, darguzar karo aur bakhsh do to beshak Allah bakhshne wala, meherbaan hai. Tumhare maal aur tumhari aulaad ek aazmaish hi hain aur Allah ke paas bahut bada sawaab hai. To Allah se daro jahan tak tum se ho sake, suno, hukm maano aur Allah ki raah mein kharch karo; yeh tumhari jaanon ke liye behtar hoga. Aur jise us ke nafs ke laalchi-pan se bacha liya gaya to wahi log falaah paane wale hain.

Hazrat Mutarrif رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ apne walid se riwayat karte hain: Main Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ki khidmat mein haazir hua. Us waqt Aap ﷺ “اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ” ki tilaawat farma rahe the. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: “Ibn-e-Aadam kehta hai: mera maal, mera maal. Ae Ibn-e-Aadam! Tera maal wahi hai jo tu ne kha kar khatam kar diya, ya pehen kar puraana kar diya, ya sadaqah karke Aakhirat ke liye aage bhej diya.” (Muslim, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd war-Raqaa'iq, safha 1582, hadees: 3-(2958))

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Banda kehta hai: mera maal, mera maal. Us ke maal mein se us ke liye sirf teen cheezein hain: (1) jo us ne kha kar khatam kar diya, (2) jo us ne pehen kar puraana kar diya, (3) jo us ne kisi ko de kar Aakhirat ke liye jama kar liya. Is ke siwa jo kuch hai, woh jaane wala hai aur woh use logon ke liye chhod jaayega.” (Muslim, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd war-Raqaa'iq, safha 1582, hadees: 4-(2959))

Hazrat Amr bin Auf رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Khuda ki qasam! Mujhe tumhare ghareeb ho jaane ka dar nahin. Mujhe to is baat ka dar hai ke duniya tum par khol di jaaye, jaise tum se pehle logon par kholi gayi thi. Phir tum is mein waise hi raghib ho jao jaise woh raghib hue aur yeh tumhein halaak kar de jaise unhein halaak kar diya.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Jizya wal-Muwaada'ah, 2/363, hadees: 3158)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne farmaya: “Maal-o-asbaab ki kasrat se maaldaari nahin hoti, balki asal maaldaari dil ka ghani hona hai. Khuda ki qasam! Mujhe tumhare mohtaaj hone ka khauf nahin, lekin mujhe tumhare baare mein yeh khauf hai ke tum zyada maal ki hawas mein mubtala ho jaoge.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad Abi Hurairah, 3/645, hadees: 10958)

Hazrat Ka'b bin Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se marwi hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne farmaya: “Do bhooke bhediye jinhein bakriyon ke rewar mein chhod diya jaayein, woh bakriyon ko itna nuqsaan nahin pahunchate jitna maal aur izzat ki lalach insaan ke deen ko nuqsaan pahunchati hai.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, baab 43, 4/166, hadees: 2383)

Allah Ta'ala sab Musalmanon ko maal ki lalach aur hawas se mehfooz farmaaye, Aameen.

{حَتّٰى زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ: Yahan tak ke tum ne qabron ka munh dekh liya.} Yaani zyada maal ki lalach tumhare dil mein rahi, yahan tak ke tumhein maut aa gayi aur tum qabron mein dafn kar diye gaye. (Khazin, At-Takathur, taht-al-aayah: 2, 4/404)

### Maal aur aulaad ki haqeeqat

Yaad rakho, jis maal ke zyada hone ki lalach ki jaati hai aur jis aulaad par fakhr-o-ghuroor kiya jaata hai, un ki haqeeqat yeh hai ke woh us waqt tak insaan ke saath rehte hain jab tak us ke jism mein rooh baaqi hoti hai. Jaise hi rooh jism se juda hoti hai aur use qabr mein dafn kar diya jaata hai, maal aur aulaad us ka saath chhod dete hain. Qabr mein us ke saath sirf us ka amal jaata hai. Is liye har aqalmand ko chahiye ke maal zyada hone ki lalach aur aulaad par fakhr karne ke bajaaye nek aamaal zyada karne ki koshish kare, taake woh qabr mein us ke behtareen saathi hon.

Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne farmaya: “Mayyit ke saath teen cheezein jaati hain. Un mein se do laut aati hain aur ek us ke saath reh jaati hai. Us ke ghar wale, maal aur amal us ke saath jaate hain. Ghar wale aur maal laut aate hain aur amal us ke saath baaqi rehta hai.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, baab Sakaraat-ul-Maut, 4/250, hadees: 6514)

Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا farmati hain: Ek din Rasoolullah ﷺ ne Sahabah-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ se poocha: “Kya tum jaante ho ke tumhare ghar walon, maal aur aamaal ki misaal kaisi hai?” Sahabah ne arz ki: Allah Ta'ala aur us ka Rasool ﷺ behtar jaante hain. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: “Tumhare ghar walon, maal aur aamaal ki misaal us shakhs jaisi hai jis ke teen bhai hon. Jab us ki maut ka waqt qareeb aaye to woh teenon ko bulaaye aur ek se kahe: Tum meri haalat dekh rahe ho, batao tum mere liye kya kar sakte ho? Woh jawaab de: Main filhaal tumhari teemaardaari kar sakta hoon, tumhare saath reh kar tumhari zarooratein poori kar sakta hoon. Phir tumhara inteqaal ho jaaye to tumhein ghusl de kar kafan pehnaunga, logon ke saath mil kar tumhara janaazah uthaunga, kabhi main kandha dunga aur kabhi koi aur. Tumhein dafn karke wapas aaoon to jo tumhare baare mein poochega, us ke saamne tumhari bhalaai hi bayan karunga.” Yeh bhai asal mein us shakhs ke ghar wale hain.

Phir Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne Sahabah se poocha: “Is ke baare mein tumhara kya khayaal hai?” Unhone arz ki: Hum is mein koi bhalaai nahin paate. Phir Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: “Woh apne doosre bhai se kahe: Tum bhi meri haalat dekh rahe ho, tum mere liye kya kar sakte ho? Woh jawaab de: Main us waqt tak tumhara saath dunga jab tak tum zinda ho. Jab tum duniya se rukhsat ho jaoge to hamare raaste juda ho jaayenge, kyunke tum qabr mein pahunch jaoge aur main duniya mein reh jaunga.” Yeh bhai us shakhs ka maal hai. Phir Aap ﷺ ne poocha: “Is ke baare mein tumhara kya khayaal hai?” Sahabah ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Hum ise bhi achha nahin samajhte.

Phir Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Woh apne teesre bhai se kahe: Tum meri haalat dekh rahe ho aur mere ghar walon aur maal ka jawaab bhi sun chuke ho. Batao tum mere liye kya kar sakte ho? Woh tasalli dete hue kahe: Mere bhai! Main qabr mein bhi tumhare saath rahunga, tumhein wehshat se bachaunga. Jab hisaab ka din aayega to tumhare meezan mein ja baithunga aur use bhaari kar dunga.” Yeh us ka amal hai. Sahabah ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Yeh to bahut achha dost hai. Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Haqeeqat yahi hai.” (Kanz-ul-Umaal, harf-ul-meem, Kitaab-ul-Maut, 8/318, al-juz'ul-khaamis-ashar, hadees: 42974)

## Ayat 102:3

`Source: tafseerId=54480; ayatId=6272; surah=102; ayat=3; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُوْنَۙ    (3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Haan, haan, ab jald jaan jaoge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُوْنَ: Haan, haan, ab jald jaan jaoge.} Is aayat aur is ke baad wali paanch aayaat ka khulasa yeh hai: Ae logo! Haan, haan, ab tum naza' ke waqt zyada maal ki lalach aur aulaad par fakhr-o-ghuroor ke bure nateeje ko jald jaan jaoge. Phir tum qabron mein ja kar bhi yaqeenan jald jaan jaoge. Agar tum maal ki lalach ka anjaam yaqini ilm ke saath jaante hote to maal ki lalach mein mubtala ho kar Aakhirat se ghaafil na hote. Beshak tum marne ke baad zaroor Jahannam ko dekhoge. Phir tum yaqeen ki aankh se use zaroor dekhoge. Phir Qiyamat ke din tum se un ne'maton ke baare mein zaroor poocha jaayega jo Allah Ta'ala ne tumhein ata farmaayi thin, jaise sehat, fursat, aman, aish aur maal. Tum se poocha jaayega ke in cheezon ko kis kaam mein kharch kiya aur in ka kya shukr ada kiya. In ne'maton ka shukr chhodne par tumhein azaab diya jaayega. (Khazin, At-Takathur, taht-al-aayaat: 3-8, 4/404; Madaarik, At-Takathur, taht-al-aayaat: 3-8, safha 1371; Jalalain, At-Takathur, taht-al-aayaat: 3-8, safha 506, multaqatan)

### Qiyamat ke din har ne'mat ke baare mein poocha jaayega

Surah At-Takathur ki aayat 8 se maloom hua ke Qiyamat ke din har ne'mat ke baare mein sawaal hoga, chahe woh jismani ho ya roohani, zaroorat ki ho ya aish-o-raahat ki. Yahan tak ke thande paani, darakht ke saaye aur aaraam ki neend ke baare mein bhi poocha jaayega. Allah Ta'ala irshad farmata hai:

“وَ لَا تَقْفُ مَا لَیْسَ لَكَ بِهٖ عِلْمٌؕ-اِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَ الْبَصَرَ وَ الْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ اُولٰٓىٕكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـٴُـوْلًا” (Bani Isra'eel: 36)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur us baat ke peechhe na pad jis ka tujhe ilm nahin. Beshak kaan, aankh aur dil, in sab ke baare mein sawaal kiya jaayega.

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Jab yeh aayat “ثُمَّ لَتُسْــٴَـلُنَّ یَوْمَىٕذٍ عَنِ النَّعِیْمِ” naazil hui to Sahabah-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ne arz ki: Ya Rasoolallah ﷺ! Hum se kis ne'mat ke baare mein poocha jaayega? Hamare paas to sirf yeh do kaali cheezein, yaani khajoor aur paani, hain; dushman saamne hai aur talwarein hamare kandhon par hain. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: “Anqareeb aisa hi hoga.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, baab wa min Suratil-Haakumut-Takathur, 5/235, hadees: 3368)

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din bande se sab se pehle ne'mat ke baare mein poocha jaayega. Us se poocha jaayega: Kya Hum ne tumhein jismani sehat nahin di aur thande paani se sairab nahin kiya?” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, baab wa min Suratil-Haakumut-Takathur, 5/236, hadees: 3369)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas'ood رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Qiyamat ke din insaan ke qadam nahin hatenge jab tak us se paanch cheezon ke baare mein pooch na liya jaayega: (1) us ki umr ke baare mein ke kis cheez mein kharch ki, (2) us ki jawaani ke baare mein ke kis kaam mein guzri, (3, 4) us ke maal ke baare mein ke kahan se kamaaya aur kahan kharch kiya, (5) us ke ilm ke baare mein ke us par kitna amal kiya.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-e-Sifat-il-Qiyamah war-Raqaa'iq wal-Wara', baab fil-Qiyamah, 4/188, hadees: 2424)

Allah Ta'ala har Musalman ko apni ne'maton ka shukr ada karne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye. Har Musalman ko chahiye ke yeh dua maanga kare:

“رَبِّ اَوْزِعْنِیْۤ اَنْ اَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِیْۤ اَنْعَمْتَ عَلَیَّ وَ عَلٰى وَالِدَیَّ وَ اَنْ اَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضٰىهُ ﱂاِنِّیْ تُبْتُ اِلَیْكَ وَ اِنِّیْ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِیْنَ” (Ahqaaf: 15)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae mere Rab! Mujhe taufeeq de ke main teri ne'mat ka shukr ada karun jo tu ne mujh par aur mere maa-baap par farmaayi hai aur main woh kaam karun jis se tu raazi ho jaaye. Aur mere liye meri aulaad mein neki rakh. Main ne teri taraf ruju' kiya aur main Musalmanon mein se hoon.
