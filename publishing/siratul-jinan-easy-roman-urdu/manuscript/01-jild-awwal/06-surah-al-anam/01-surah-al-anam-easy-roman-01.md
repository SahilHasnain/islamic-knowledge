# Surah Al-Anam — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 6:1

`Source: tafseerId=50843; ayatId=796; surah=6; ayat=1; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِیْ خَلَقَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضَ وَ جَعَلَ الظُّلُمٰتِ وَ النُّوْرَ۬ؕ-ثُمَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا بِرَبِّهِمْ یَعْدِلُوْنَ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tamam tareefein Allah ke liye hain jis ne aasman aur zameen paida kiye aur andheron aur noor ko paida kiya, phir bhi kafir log apne Rab ke barabar thehraate hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ: Tamam tareefein Allah ke liye hain.}** Is aayat mein bandon ko Allah Ta'ala ki hamd karne ki taleem di gayi hai. Jab woh hamd karein to “اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ” kahein. Aasman aur zameen ki paidaish ka zikr is liye kiya gaya hai ke in mein dekhne walon ke liye Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki hikmat aur qudrat ke bahut se ajeeb nishan, ibrat aur faayde hain. Hazrat Ka'b Ahbar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya ke Taurat mein sab se pehle yahi aayat hai. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 1, 2/2)

**{وَ جَعَلَ الظُّلُمٰتِ وَ النُّوْرَ: Aur andheron aur noor ko paida kiya.}** Yani har andhera Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ hi ne paida farmaya hai, chahe woh raat ka andhera ho, kufr ka, jahalat ka ya Jahannam ka. Isi tarah har roshni bhi Usi ne paida farmayi hai, chahe woh din ki roshni ho, iman aur hidayat ki, ilm ki ya Jannat ki.

### Bura kaam Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat ki taraf mansub na karein

Yahan yeh baat yaad rakhein ke agarche har achhi aur buri cheez ka paida karne wala Rab Ta'ala hai, lekin bura kaam karke use taqdeer aur Allah Ta'ala ki mashiyyat ki taraf mansub karna buri baat hai. Hukm yeh hai ke jo achha kaam kare, use Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se kahe; aur jo burai ho jaaye, use apne nafs ki badbakhti samjhe.

Is aayat mein zulumat, yani andheron, ko jama ke sighay mein aur noor ko wahid ke sighay mein bayan karne mein is taraf ishara hai ke batil ki rahein bahut hain, jabke Haq ki raah sirf ek hai, aur woh Islam ka deen hai.

**{ثُمَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا بِرَبِّهِمْ یَعْدِلُوْنَ: Phir bhi kafir log apne Rab ke barabar thehraate hain.}** Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat ke in daleelon aur nishaniyon ko dekhne ke bawajood kafir Allah Ta'ala ke siwa doosron, hatta ke pattharon ko poojte hain. Halanke woh is baat ka iqraar karte hain ke aasmanon aur zameen ko paida karne wala Allah Ta'ala hai. Is jagah kafiron ke shirk ka zikr hua, is munasabat se shirk ki ta'reef bayan hoti hai.

### Shirk ki ta'reef

Shirk yeh hai ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke siwa kisi aur ko wajib-ul-wujood ya ibaadat ke laaiq samjha jaaye. Hazrat Allamah Sa'd-ud-Deen Taftazani رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne shirk ki haqeeqat in alfaaz mein bayan farmayi:

“اَلْاِشْتِرَا کُ ھُوَاِثْبَاتُ الشَّرِیْکِ فِی الْاُلُوْہِیَّۃِبِمَعْنٰی وُجُوْبِ الْوُجُوْدِکَمَا لِلْمَجُوْسِ اَوْ بِمَعْنٰی اِسْتِحْقَاقِ الْعِبَادَۃِ کَمَا لِعَبَدَۃِ الْاَصْنَامِ”

Yani shirk yeh hai ke Khuda ki uloohiyat, yani Ma'bood hone, mein kisi ko shareek karna. Yeh is tarah ho ke kisi ko wajib-ul-wujood maan liya jaaye, jaisa Majus ka aqeedah hai; ya Khuda ke siwa kisi ko ibaadat ka haqdaar maan liya jaaye, jaisa but-paraston ka khayal hai. (Sharh-e-Aqaid Nasafiyyah, Mabhas-ul-af'aal kulluha bi-khalqillah Ta'ala wad-daleelu alaiha, s. 78)

Imam-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan عَلَیْہِ رَحْمَۃُ الرَّحْمٰنْ farmate hain: “Aadmi haqeeqatan kisi baat se mushrik nahin hota jab tak ghair-e-Khuda ko ma'bood ya mustaqil-biz-zaat aur wajib-ul-wujood na jaane.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 21/131)

Sadr-ush-Shariah Badr-ut-Tareeqah Maulana Mufti Amjad Ali A'zami رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Shirk ke ma'ni ghair-e-Khuda ko wajib-ul-wujood ya mustahiq-e-ibaadat jaanna, yani uloohiyat mein doosre ko shareek karna hain. Yeh kufr ki sab se badtar qism hai. Is ke siwa koi baat kitni hi shadeed kufr ho, haqeeqatan shirk nahin.” (Bahar-e-Shariat, hissa awwal, Iman-o-Kufr ka bayan, 1/183)

## Ayat 6:2

`Source: tafseerId=50844; ayatId=797; surah=6; ayat=2; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** هُوَ الَّذِیْ خَلَقَكُمْ مِّنْ طِیْنٍ ثُمَّ قَضٰۤى اَجَلًاؕ-وَ اَجَلٌ مُّسَمًّى عِنْدَهٗ ثُمَّ اَنْتُمْ تَمْتَرُوْنَ(2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Wohi hai jis ne tumhein mitti se paida kiya, phir ek muddat ka faisla farmaya aur ek muqarrarah muddat Usi ke paas hai, phir tum log shak karte ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{هُوَ الَّذِیْ خَلَقَكُمْ مِّنْ طِیْنٍ: Wohi hai jis ne tumhein mitti se paida kiya.}** Is aayat mein un mushrikin ka radd kiya gaya hai jo kehte the ke jab hum gal kar mitti ho jaayenge to phir kaise zinda kiye jaayenge? Unhein bataya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala ne hi tumhare baap Hazrat Aadam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko mitti se paida farmaya aur un ki nasal se log paida hue. Phir Allah Ta'ala ne ek muddat ka faisla farmaya, jis ke poora hone par tum mar jaoge. Marne ke baad uthaye jaane ki ek muqarrarah muddat bhi Usi ke paas hai. Phir ae kafiro! Tum marne ke baad dobara zinda kiye jaane mein shak karte ho, halanke tum jaante ho ke tumhari takhleeq shuru karne wala Allah Ta'ala hai. Jo pehli baar paida karne par qaadir hai, woh dobara zinda karne par aur bhi zyada qaadir hai. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, 2/3; Jalalain, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, s. 112, multaqatan)

## Ayat 6:3

`Source: tafseerId=50845; ayatId=798; surah=6; ayat=3; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ اللّٰهُ فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ فِی الْاَرْضِؕ-یَعْلَمُ سِرَّكُمْ وَ جَهْرَكُمْ وَ یَعْلَمُ مَا تَكْسِبُوْنَ(3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur wohi Allah aasmanon mein aur zameen mein laaiq-e-ibaadat hai. Woh tumhari har posheeda aur zaahir baat jaanta hai aur tumhare sab kaam jaanta hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ هُوَ اللّٰهُ فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ فِی الْاَرْضِ: Aur wohi Allah aasmanon mein aur zameen mein laaiq-e-ibaadat hai.}** Pichhli aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ki kaamil qudrat ka zikr hua aur is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ke kaamil ilm ka zikr kiya ja raha hai. Yaad rahe ke is aayat ka matlab yeh nahin ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aasmanon aur zameen mein rehta hai. Is ka matlab yeh hai ke har jagah Usi ki ibaadat ki ja rahi hai, har jagah wahi haqeeqi Ma'bood hai aur har jagah Usi ki saltanat aur hukumat hai. Jaisa ke ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْ فِی السَّمَآءِ اِلٰهٌ وَّ فِی الْاَرْضِ اِلٰهٌؕ-وَ هُوَ الْحَكِیْمُ الْعَلِیْمُ (زخرف:۸۴)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur wohi aasman walon ka Ma'bood hai aur zameen walon ka Ma'bood hai, aur wohi hikmat wala, ilm wala hai.

**{یَعْلَمُ سِرَّكُمْ وَ جَهْرَكُمْ: Woh tumhari har posheeda aur zaahir baat jaanta hai.}** Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Allah Ta'ala tamam maloomaat ka ilm rakhta hai. Zameen ki teh se aasmanon ki bulandi tak jo kuchh hota hai, sab ka ihata farmane wala hai. Us ke ilm se zameen aur aasman ka koi zarra bhi bahar nahin ja sakta. Balki woh sakht andheri raat mein saaf chattaan par chalne wali siyah cheenti ke chalne ki aawaaz ko bhi jaanta hai. Woh hawa mein ek zarre ki harkat bhi jaanta hai. Woh posheeda umoor, dilon ke waswason, khayalon aur chhupi hui baaton ka ilm rakhta hai. Us ka ilm qadeem aur azali hai. Woh hamesha se isi ilm ke saath moosuf raha hai. Us ka ilm naya nahin aur na hi us ki zaat mein aane ki wajah se haasil hua hai.” (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab Qawa'id-ul-Aqaid, Al-Fasl-ul-Awwal fi tarjimati aqeedati Ahl-is-Sunnat fi kalimat-ish-Shahadatain, 1/126)

## Ayat 6:4

`Source: tafseerId=50846; ayatId=799; surah=6; ayat=4; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا تَاْتِیْهِمْ مِّنْ اٰیَةٍ مِّنْ اٰیٰتِ رَبِّهِمْ اِلَّا كَانُوْا عَنْهَا مُعْرِضِیْنَ(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un ke paas un ke Rab ki nishaniyon mein se koi bhi nishani nahin aati magar woh us se munh pher lete hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَا تَاْتِیْهِمْ مِّنْ اٰیَةٍ: Aur un ke paas koi bhi nishani nahin aati.}** Pichhli aayaat mein mushrikin ke us kufr ka bayan hua jo woh Allah Ta'ala ke saath karte the. Is aayat mein un ke us kufr ka bayan hai jo woh Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke saath karte the. Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke kafir-e-Makkah ke kufr aur sarkashi ka haal yeh hai ke jab bhi un ke paas Quran-e-Majeed ki aayaat aati hain ya woh Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke mu'jizaat dekhte hain to us se munh pher lete hain. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 4, 2/4, multaqatan)

## Ayat 6:5

`Source: tafseerId=50847; ayatId=800; surah=6; ayat=5; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** فَقَدْ كَذَّبُوْا بِالْحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمْؕ-فَسَوْفَ یَاْتِیْهِمْ اَنْۢبٰٓؤُا مَا كَانُوْا بِهٖ یَسْتَهْزِءُوْنَ(5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To beshak unhone haq ko jhutlaya jab woh un ke paas aaya, to anqareeb un ke paas us ki khabrein aane wali hain jis ka woh mazaaq udaate the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَقَدْ كَذَّبُوْا بِالْحَقِّ: To beshak unhone haq ko jhutlaya.}** Yahan haq se ya to Quran-e-Majeed ki aayaat murad hain, ya Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ aur Aap ﷺ ke mu'jizaat murad hain. Jab bhi Quran ki aayaat ya Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke mu'jizaat kafir-e-Makkah ke saamne aate, ya Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ unhein kuchh samjhate, to woh Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko jhutlate. Is par farmaya gaya ke anqareeb un ke paas us cheez ki khabrein aane wali hain jis ka woh mazaaq udaate the. Unhein maloom ho jaayega ke woh kitni azeem khabar thi aur us ka mazaaq udaane ka anjaam kitna khaufnaak hai.

## Ayat 6:6

`Source: tafseerId=50848; ayatId=801; surah=6; ayat=6; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ یَرَوْا كَمْ اَهْلَكْنَا مِنْ قَبْلِهِمْ مِّنْ قَرْنٍ مَّكَّنّٰهُمْ فِی الْاَرْضِ مَا لَمْ نُمَكِّنْ لَّكُمْ وَ اَرْسَلْنَا السَّمَآءَ عَلَیْهِمْ مِّدْرَارًا۪-وَّ جَعَلْنَا الْاَنْهٰرَ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهِمْ فَاَهْلَكْنٰهُمْ بِذُنُوْبِهِمْ وَ اَنْشَاْنَا مِنْۢ بَعْدِهِمْ قَرْنًا اٰخَرِیْنَ(6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya unhone nahin dekha ke hum ne un se pehle kitni qaumo ko halaak kar diya? Unhein hum ne zameen mein woh quwwat aur taaqat di thi jo tumhein nahin di aur hum ne un par musaladhar baarish bheji aur un ke neeche nahrein baha dein, phir un ke gunaho ki wajah se unhein halaak kar diya aur un ke baad doosri qaumein paida kar dein.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَمْ یَرَوْا: Kya unhone nahin dekha.}** Pichhli aayaat mein kafir-e-Makkah ko Allah Ta'ala ki nishaniyon se munh modne, haq ko jhutlane aur Allah Ta'ala ki nishaniyon ka mazaaq udaane se sarzanish aur darawe ke zariye mana kiya gaya. Is aayat mein pichhli qaumo ka ibratnaak anjaam bayan karke unhein naseehat ki ja rahi hai.

Is liye farmaya gaya: Kya Shaam aur doosre mumalik ki taraf safar karte hue kafir-e-Makkah ne nahin dekha ke hum ne un se pehle kitni qaumo ko halaak kar diya? Ae Ahle-Makkah! Hum ne unhein zameen mein woh quwwat aur taaqat di thi jo tumhein nahin di. Jab unhein zaroorat hoti to hum un par musaladhar baarish bhejte. Is se un ki khetiyan hari-bhari ho jatin aur un ke darakhto, gharon aur mahalo ke qareeb nahrein baha dete. Is se baagh parwan chadhte, phal bahut hote aur duniya ki zindagi ke liye aish aur aaraam ke saaman paida hote.

Lekin jab unhone Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko jhutlaya aur un ke ahkaam ko na maana to hum ne un ke gunaho ki wajah se unhein halaak kar diya. Un ki izzat, shaan-o-shaukat aur saaman unhein halaakat se na bacha saka. Un ke baad hum ne doosri qaumein paida kar dein aur doosre logo ko un ka jaanasheen bana diya. Tum bhi un ke ibratnaak anjaam ko dekh kar naseehat haasil karo aur pichhle kafiro wala tareeqa na apnao, warna tum par bhi waisa hi azaab aa sakta hai.

### Pichhli ummato ke anjaam se ibrat haasil karein

Is aayat mein guzri hui ummato ka jo haal aur anjaam bayan hua ke woh log quwwat, daulat aur maal-o-ayal ki kasrat ke bawajood kufr, sarkashi aur Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke ahkaam ki mukhalifat ki wajah se halaak kar diye gaye, is mein khaas taur par kafiro aur aam taur par har Musalman ke liye ibrat aur naseehat hai. Is liye sab ko chahiye ke un ke haal se ibrat haasil karke khwab-e-ghaflat se bedaar ho jaayein aur kufr, sarkashi aur gunaho ko chhor kar imaan, ita'at, ibaadat aur nek kaamo mein lag jaayein.

### Kafiro ki dunyawi taraqqi Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein maqbooliyat ki daleel nahin

Is aayat se maloom hua ke dunyawi khushhaali, maal-o-daulat aur sahuliyato ki kasrat Allah Ta'ala ki raza ki alamat nahin. Warna Qaroon Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein bahut maqbool hota. Un logo ko is se sabaq seekhna chahiye jo Musalmano ke muqable mein kafiro ki dunyawi taraqqi, saainsi maharat, bahut si sahuliyat, kasrat se ijaad aur maal-o-daulat ki farawani dekh kar unhein Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein maqbool aur Musalmano ko mardood samajhte hain. Woh akhlaaq aur kirdar mein Musalmano ko kafiro ki naqal karne ka mashwara dete hain.

Kafiro ki yeh dunyawi kaamyaabi maqbooliyat ki nahin balki mohlat milne ki daleel hai. Allah Ta'ala ka qaida yeh hai ke Woh kafiro ko foran nahin pakadta, balki unhein mohlat deta aur aish ki cheezein ata farmata hai, phir unhein apne azaab mein mubtala karta hai. Naam-nihaad danishwaro ka Musalmano ko Europe ki andhi naqal ka mashwara na dena hi behtar hai; unhein apni Qaroon jaisi soch apne paas rakhni chahiye.

## Ayat 6:7

`Source: tafseerId=50849; ayatId=802; surah=6; ayat=7; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَوْ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَیْكَ كِتٰبًا فِیْ قِرْطَاسٍ فَلَمَسُوْهُ بِاَیْدِیْهِمْ لَقَالَ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْۤا اِنْ هٰذَاۤ اِلَّا سِحْرٌ مُّبِیْنٌ(7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar hum kaghaz mein kuchh likha hua Aap par utaar dete, phir yeh use chhoo lete to bhi kafir kehte ke yeh to khula jaadu hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَوْ نَزَّلْنَا عَلَیْكَ: Aur agar hum Aap par utaar dete.}** Yeh aayat Nadr bin Haris, Abdullah bin Umayyah aur Naufal bin Khuwaylid ke baare mein naazil hui. Unhone kaha tha: Ae Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ! Hum tum par hargiz imaan nahin laayenge jab tak tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se hamare paas aisi kitaab na laao jis ke saath chaar farishte hon. Woh gawahi dein ke yeh Allah Ta'ala ki kitaab hai aur tum us ke Rasool ho. Is par yeh aayat-e-Kareema naazil hui. (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 7, 2/70)

Is aayat mein bataya gaya ke yeh sab bahane hain. Agar kaghaz par likhi hui kitaab bhi utaar di jaati aur woh use apne haatho se chhoo kar aur jaanch kar dekh lete, tab bhi kehte ke un ki nazarbandi kar di gayi hai. Kitaab utarti hui nazar to aayi thi lekin haqeeqat mein kuchh bhi nahin tha. Jis tarah unhone Shaqq-ul-Qamar, yani chaand ke do tukde hone ke mu'jize ko jaadu kaha aur use dekh kar bhi imaan na laaye, isi tarah is par bhi imaan na laate. Jo log zid ki wajah se inkaar karte hain woh aayaat aur mu'jizaat se faayda nahin utha sakte.

## Ayat 6:8

`Source: tafseerId=50850; ayatId=803; surah=6; ayat=8; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالُوْا لَوْ لَاۤ اُنْزِلَ عَلَیْهِ مَلَكٌؕ-وَ لَوْ اَنْزَلْنَا مَلَكًا لَّقُضِیَ الْاَمْرُ ثُمَّ لَا یُنْظَرُوْنَ(8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur kafiro ne kaha: In par aasman se koi farishta kyun nahin utaar diya jaata? Haalanke agar hum koi farishta utaar dete to faisla kar diya jaata, phir unhein mohlat na di jaati.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَالُوْا: Aur unhone kaha.}** Mushriko ne mazeed kaha ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par aasman se koi farishta kyun nahin utaar diya jaata jise woh dekhte? Is par farmaya gaya ke agar Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ farishta utaar deta aur kafir phir bhi imaan na laate to Allah Ta'ala ka azaab un par laazim ho jaata.

Allah Ta'ala ka tareeqa yeh hai ke jab kafir mil kar koi nishani maangein aur woh nishani zahir ho jaane ke baad bhi imaan na laayein to azaab wajib ho jaata aur woh halaak kar diye jaate hain. Is liye agar kafiro ka mutalaba poora kar diya jaata aur woh phir bhi imaan na laate to unhein ek lamhe ki bhi mohlat na milti aur azaab un se mu'akhkhar na kiya jaata.

### Nishaniyan poori hone ke bawajood kafir-e-Makkah par azaab kyun na aaya?

Yaad rahe ke kafiro ne aise farishte ke utarne ka mutalaba kiya tha jo unhein bhi nazar aaye. Isi mutalabe ka radd kiya gaya hai. Warna Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ par ek nahin, bahut se farishte naazil hote the aur kai martaba insani shakal mein haazir hote the. Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم bhi unhein dekhte the. Kafiro ka mutalaba yeh tha ke farishta apni asli shakal mein aaye aur woh use usi shakal mein dekhein.

Yahan ek sawal hai: Kafiro ne bahut si nishaniyan maangi jo poori bhi hui, jaise chaand ke do tukde hona, lekin is ke baad woh imaan na laaye. Aisi surat mein aayat ke bayan kiye hue hukm ke mutabiq sab ko halaak kar dena chahiye tha, lekin aisa nahin hua. Is ka jawab yeh hai ke nabuwat par daleel banne wali nishani do tarah ki hoti hai: (1) aam nishani, (2) khaas nishani.

Aam nishani woh hai jis ka sab log mutalaba karein, ya sab mutalaba na bhi karein lekin sab log us ka mushahida kar lein. Khaas nishani woh hai jis ka mutalaba kuchh khaas logo ne kiya ho aur sab log us ka mushahida na kar sakein. Pichhli ummato mein Allah Ta'ala ka tareeqa yeh raha ke aam nishani, yani jis mein sab aam-o-khaas log shareek ho jaayein, poori hone ke baad agar woh imaan na laayein to azaab naazil farmaata. Khaas nishani poori hone ke baad imaan na laane par azaab naazil nahin farmata.

Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se chaand ke do tukde hone ka mutalaba kuchh khaas logo ne kiya tha aur woh poora hua, lekin yeh aam nishani nahin balki khaas nishani thi. Jab chaand do tukde hua to aksar log so rahe the. Kai jagah matla' ke ikhtilaaf ya baadal ki wajah se chaand ka do tukde hona nazar na aaya. Isi liye mutalaba karne walo par azaab naazil na hua. (Sharh-us-Sunnah lil-Baghawi, Kitaab-ul-Fazaa'il, Baab Alamaat-in-Nubuwwat, 7/66, tehat-ul-hadees: 3605, khulasa)

Mirqaat-ul-Mafateeh mein bhi yahi ibaarat Sharh-us-Sunnah se naqal ki gayi hai. Is ki roshni mein Abu Jahl aur doosre kafiro ke mutalabat ko dekha jaaye to yeh khaas mutalabat khaas fard ke liye poore hue the. Is liye in par azaab naazil na hua.

## Ayat 6:9

`Source: tafseerId=50851; ayatId=804; surah=6; ayat=9; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَوْ جَعَلْنٰهُ مَلَكًا لَّجَعَلْنٰهُ رَجُلًا وَّ لَلَبَسْنَا عَلَیْهِمْ مَّا یَلْبِسُوْنَ(9)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar hum Nabi ko farishta bana dete to bhi use mard hi banate aur in par wahi shubah daal dete jis mein ab pade hue hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَوْ جَعَلْنٰهُ مَلَكًا: Aur agar hum Nabi ko farishta bana dete.}** Yeh un kafiro ka jawab hai jo kehte the: Yeh hamari tarah insaan hain. Isi ghalat soch ki wajah se woh imaan se mehroom rahe. Unhein insano mein se Rasool bhejne ki hikmat batayi ja rahi hai ke un se faayda haasil karne aur Nabi ki taleem se faayda uthaane ki yahi surat hai ke Nabi insani shakal mein zahir ho.

Farishte ko us ki asli shakal mein dekhne ki to yeh log taaqat hi na rakhte. Use dekhte hi haibat se behosh ho jaate ya mar jaate. Is liye agar farishta hi Rasool banaya jaata to use bhi mard hi banaya jaata aur insani shakal mein bheja jaata, taake yeh log use dekh sakein, us ka kalaam sun sakein aur us se deen ke ahkaam jaan sakein. Lekin agar farishta insani shakal mein aata to unhein phir yahi kehne ka mauqa milta ke yeh to insaan hai. Phir farishte ko Nabi banane ka kya faayda hota? (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 9, 2/5)

## Ayat 6:10

`Source: tafseerId=50852; ayatId=805; surah=6; ayat=10; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَقَدِ اسْتُهْزِئَ بِرُسُلٍ مِّنْ قَبْلِكَ فَحَاقَ بِالَّذِیْنَ سَخِرُوْا مِنْهُمْ مَّا كَانُوْا بِهٖ یَسْتَهْزِءُوْنَ۠    (10)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur ae Habeeb! Beshak tum se pehle Rasoolo ke saath bhi mazaaq kiya gaya, to jo un mein se Rasoolo ka mazaaq udaate the un par un ka mazaaq hi aa gaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَقَدِ اسْتُهْزِئَ بِرُسُلٍ مِّنْ قَبْلِكَ: Aur ae Mehboob! Beshak tum se pehle Rasoolo ke saath bhi mazaaq kiya gaya.}** Kafir Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka mazaaq udaate the aur is se Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ dil chhota karte the. Is par Sarwar-e-Do-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ se farmaya gaya ke Aap se pehle Rasoolo ka bhi mazaaq udaaya gaya, phir mazaaq udaane walo ka nihayat daraawna anjaam hua aur woh azaab mein mubtala hue.

Is mein Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke liye tasalli hai ke Aap dil-giriftah na hon. Kafiro ka pehle Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke saath bhi yahi rawayya raha hai aur is ka wabaal un kafiro ko uthana pada. Is mein mushriko ko bhi tanbeeh hai ke woh pichhli ummato ke haal se ibrat lein aur Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke saath adab ka tareeqa ikhtiyar karein, taake pichhle logo ki tarah azaab mein mubtala na hon.

### Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ka mazaaq udaane walo ka anjaam

Kafiro-Quraish ke paanch sardaar Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko bahut takleef dete aur Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ka mazaaq udaaya karte the: (1) Aas bin Waa'il Sahmi, (2) Aswad bin Muttalib, (3) Aswad bin Abd Yaghus, (4) Haris bin Qais aur in sab ka afsar (5) Waleed bin Mughirah Makhzoomi.

Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne Aswad bin Muttalib ke khilaaf dua ki thi: Ya Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Is ko andha kar de. Ek din Tajdar-e-Risalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Masjid-e-Haraam mein tashreef farma the ke yeh paancho aaye. Unhone apne mamool ke mutabiq taane aur mazaaq ki baatein ki aur tawaaf mein mashghool ho gaye.

Isi haal mein Hazrat Jibreel-e-Ameen عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام Huzoor-e-Anwar صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki khidmat mein aaye. Unhone Waleed bin Mughirah ki pindli ki taraf, Aas ke qadmo ki taraf, Aswad bin Muttalib ki aankho ki taraf, Aswad bin Abd Yaghus ke pait ki taraf aur Haris bin Qais ke sar ki taraf ishara karke kaha: Main in ka shar door karunga.

Chand hi arse mein yeh sab halaak ho gaye. Waleed bin Mughirah teer bechne wale ki dukaan ke paas se guzra to us ke tehband mein ek teer ki nok chubh gayi. Takabbur ki wajah se us ne use nikaalne ke liye sar neecha na kiya. Is se us ki pindli mein zakhm hua aur isi mein mar gaya. Aas bin Waa'il ke paao mein kaanta laga jo use nazar na aaya. Us ka paao sooj gaya aur woh bhi mar gaya.

Aswad bin Muttalib ki aankho mein aisa dard hua ke woh deewar mein sar maarta tha aur isi haal mein mar gaya. Woh kehta hua mara: Mujhe Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne qatl kiya. Aswad bin Abd Yaghus ko istisqa ki beemari lag gayi. Kalbi ki riwayat mein hai ke use lu lag gayi aur us ka munh itna kaala ho gaya ke ghar walo ne use pehchana nahin aur ghar se nikaal diya. Isi haal mein woh kehta hua mara: Mujhe Muhammad صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ke Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne qatl kiya.

Haris bin Qais ki naak se khoon aur peep jaari hui aur woh isi haal mein halaak ho gaya. (Khazin, Al-Hijr, tehat-ul-aayat: 95, 3/111, khulasa)

## Ayat 6:11

`Source: tafseerId=50853; ayatId=806; surah=6; ayat=11; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ سِیْرُوْا فِی الْاَرْضِ ثُمَّ انْظُرُوْا كَیْفَ كَانَ عَاقِبَةُ الْمُكَذِّبِیْنَ(11)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farma do: Zameen mein sair karo, phir dekho ke jhutlane walo ka kaisa anjaam hua?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ سِیْرُوْا فِی الْاَرْضِ: Tum farma do: Zameen mein sair karo.}** Yahan Sarkar-e-Do-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko farmaya gaya hai ke ae Habeeb! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ azaab ka mazaaq udaane walo se farma dein ke jao aur zameen mein sair karke dekho ke jhutlane walo ka kaisa anjaam hua aur unhone kufr-o-takzeeb ka kya phal paaya? Yahan zameen se murad woh jagah hai jahan pichhli qaumo par azaab aaya aur ab tak wahan un ujri hui bastiyo ke asaar maujood hain. Sair ka yeh hukm targheeb ke liye hai, wajib hone ke liye nahin.

### Allah Ta'ala ka khauf aur us ki muhabbat paida karne ka zariya

Is se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ka khauf paida karne ke liye azaab wali jagah ja kar dekhna behtar hai, kyunki khabar ke muqable mein aankho se dekhi hui cheez ka asar zyada hota hai. Jis tarah azaab ki jagah dekhne se khauf paida hota hai, isi tarah rahmat ki jagah dekhne se ibaadat ki ragbat aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki muhabbat paida hoti hai. Is liye Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki rahmat dekhne ke liye buzurgo ke aastane, jahan Allah Ta'ala ki rahmatein barasti hain, safar karke dekhna bhi behtar hai, taake Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ita'at ka shauq paida ho.

### Safar karke mazaraat-e-Auliya par jaana jaaiz hai

Is aayat se yeh bhi maloom hua ke imaani quwwat haasil karne ke liye safar karna ba'is-e-rahmat hai. Is aayat se un logo ka bhi radd hota hai jo sirf teen masjido ke ilawa kisi aur taraf safar ko mutlaqan najaaiz kehte hain aur is ki daleel mein yeh hadees pesh karte hain: Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marwi hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne irshad farmaya: “In teen masjido ke siwa kisi ki taraf kaajawe na bandhe jaayen: (1) Masjid-e-Haraam. (2) Rasoolullah صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ki masjid. (3) Masjid-e-Aqsa.” (Bukhari, Kitaab Fazl-us-Salaat fi Masjid-e-Makkah wal-Madinah, Baab Masjid Bait-ul-Maqdis, 1/403, hadees: 1197)

Is hadees ka matlab yeh hai ke in masjido ke ilawa kisi aur masjid ki taraf is liye safar karke jaana ke wahan namaaz ka sawab zyada hai, mana hai, kyunki in ke ilawa sab masjido mein namaaz padhne ka sawab barabar hai. Agar is hadees ka matlab yeh liya jaaye ke in teen masjido ke ilawa kisi aur masjid ki taraf safar karna haraam hai ya in teen masjido ke ilawa kahin aur safar karna jaaiz nahin, to yeh hadees Quran-e-Majeed ki is aayat aur doosri ahadees ke bhi khilaaf hogi. Is matlab ke mutabiq to kisi maqsad ke liye koi safar jaaiz nahin rahega, jaise jihaad, talab-e-ilm, tableegh-e-deen, tijaarat aur sair waghera ke liye safar bhi jaaiz na hoga, aur yeh ummat ke ijma ke khilaaf hai. Is se saabit hua ke safar karke Allah Ta'ala ke mehboob bando ke mazaraat par jaana mana aur haraam nahin, balki jaaiz aur mustahsan hai.

## Ayat 6:12

`Source: tafseerId=50854; ayatId=807; surah=6; ayat=12; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ لِّمَنْ مَّا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِؕ-قُلْ لِّلّٰهِؕ-كَتَبَ عَلٰى نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَةَؕ-لَیَجْمَعَنَّكُمْ اِلٰى یَوْمِ الْقِیٰمَةِ لَا رَیْبَ فِیْهِؕ-اَلَّذِیْنَ خَسِرُوْۤا اَنْفُسَهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ(12)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmao kis ka hai jo kuchh aasmano aur zameen mein hai? Farma do: Allah hi ka hai. Us ne apne zimme-e-karam par rahmat likh li hai. Beshak woh zaroor tumhein Qiyamat ke din jama karega jis mein kuchh shak nahin. Woh jinhone apni jaano ko nuqsan mein daala hua hai to woh imaan nahin laate.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ: Tum farmao.}** Mazeed farmaya gaya ke ae Habeeb! صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ Aap un se poochhein ke jo kuchh aasmano aur zameen mein hai, us ka Maalik kaun hai? Pehle to woh khud hi kahenge ke yeh sab kuchh Allah Ta'ala ka hai, kyunki woh bhi is baat ko maante hain. Agar woh yeh na kahen to Aap khud yeh jawab dein ke sab ka Maalik Allah Ta'ala hai, kyunki is ke siwa koi aur jawab ho hi nahin sakta aur woh is jawab ki mukhalifat kar hi nahin sakte. Jin buto ko yeh mushrik poojte hain woh bejaan hain. Woh kisi cheez ke Maalik banne ki salahiyat nahin rakhte, balki khud doosro ke mamlook hain. Aasman-o-zameen ka Maalik wahi ho sakta hai jo Hayy-o-Qayyum, Azali-o-Abadi, Qaadir-e-Mutlaq, har cheez par tasarruf rakhne wala aur hukmaran ho. Har cheez us ke paida karne se wajood mein aayi ho. Allah Ta'ala ke siwa aisa koi nahin. Is liye poori aasmani aur zameeni kainaat ka Maalik us ke siwa koi nahin ho sakta.

**{كَتَبَ عَلٰى نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَةَ: Us ne apne zimme-e-karam par rahmat likh li hai.}** Yani Allah Ta'ala ne rahmat ka waada farma liya hai. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke waade ki khilaaf-warzi karna aur ma'azAllah jhoot bolna muhaal hai. Us ne rahmat ka waada farma liya aur us ki rahmat aam hai, deeni ho ya dunyawi. Apni pehchan, tauheed aur ilm ki taraf hidayat farmana bhi isi rahmat mein daakhil hai. Isi tarah kafiro ko mohlat dena aur unhein saza dene mein jaldi na farmana bhi rahmat mein daakhil hai, kyunki is se unhein tauba aur rujoo ka mauqa milta hai. (Jamal, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 12, 2/322-323)

### Allah Ta'ala ki rahmat dekh kar gunaho par bebaak nahin hona chahiye

Is baat mein koi shak nahin ke Allah Ta'ala sab se zyada Raheem aur sab se badh kar Kareem hai. Us ke rahm-o-karam ke khazano ki koi inteha nahin. Woh chahe to umr bhar ke gunahgaar ko ek pal mein bakhsh de aur us ki saari khataein maaf farma de. Lekin us ki wasee rahmat ko dekh kar aisa nahin hona chahiye ke banda gunaho par bebaak ho jaaye aur us ki na-farmaani ki parwah na kare.

Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain, jis ka khulasa yeh hai: “Gunahgaar momin is tarah dhoke mein mubtala hain ke woh kehte hain: Allah Ta'ala Kareem hai aur mujhe us ke afw-o-darguzar ki umeed hai. Phir is baat par bharosa karke aamaal se ghaafil ho jaate hain. Un ka khayal hai ke Allah Ta'ala ki ne'mat wasee aur rahmat-o-karam aam hai. Us ki rahmat ke samandar ke muqable mein bando ke gunaho ki kya haisiyat hai? Hum tauheed ko maanne wale aur momin hain aur imaan ke zariye us ki rahmat ke umeedwaar hain.”

Baaz auqaat un ke paas is umeed ki daleel yeh hoti hai ke hamare baap-dada nek log the aur un ka darja buland tha. Kai log apne nasab ki wajah se dhoke ka shikaar hote hain. Haalanke woh khauf-e-Khuda, taqwa aur parhezgaari ke maamle mein apne baap-dada ki seerat ke khilaaf chalte hain. Goya un ka gumaan yeh hai ke woh Allah Ta'ala ke nazdeek apne baap-dada se bhi zyada mo'azzaz hain. Baap-dada intehaai darje ke taqwa ke bawajood khaufzada rehte the, jabke yeh log intehaai darje ke fisq-o-fujoor ke bawajood be-khauf hain. Yeh Allah Ta'ala ki zaat par bahut bada dhoka hai.

Isi tarah Shaitaan buland nasab walo ke dilo mein yeh baat daalta hai ke jo kisi insaan se muhabbat karta hai woh us ki aulaad se bhi muhabbat karta hai. Allah Ta'ala ne tumhare baap-dada se muhabbat ki hai, is liye woh tum se bhi muhabbat karta hai. Is liye tumhein ibaadat karne ki zaroorat nahin. Yeh bhi Allah Ta'ala ki zaat par dhoka hai, kyunki Allah Ta'ala farmabardaar ko pasand aur gunahgaar ko na-pasand farmata hai. Jis tarah gunahgaar bete ko bura samajhne ki wajah se us ke farmabardaar baap ko bura nahin samjha jaata, isi tarah farmabardaar baap ki ita'at ki wajah se us ke gunahgaar bete se muhabbat bhi nahin ki jaati. Agar muhabbat baap se bete ki taraf chali jaati to qareeb tha ke nafrat bhi phail jaati.

Haqeeqat yeh hai ke koi bojh uthaane wala doosre ka bojh nahin uthata. Jis shakhs ka yeh khayal ho ke woh apne baap ki parhezgaari ki wajah se najaat paa lega, woh us shakhs ki tarah hai jo yeh samjhe ke baap ke khaane se woh bhi sair ho jaayega, baap ke peene se beta bhi sairaab ho jaayega, baap ke seekhne se beta bhi aalim ban jaayega aur baap Ka'bah Shareef mein pahunch jaaye to us ke wahan pahunchne se yeh bhi wahan pahunch kar Ka'bah Shareef ko dekh lega. Yaad rakhein ke taqwa aur parhezgaari ikhtiyar karna har ek par laazim hai. Is mein baap apne bete ki taraf se kaafi nahin hota aur is ke baraks bhi yahi hukm hai. (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab Zamm-ul-Ghuroor, Bayan Zamm-ul-Ghuroor wa Haqeeqatuhu wa Amtsilatuhu, 3/471-472, mulakhkhasan)[1]

[1] Aaj ke zamaane mein apne dilo mein Allah Ta'ala ka khauf paida karne aur gunaho par bebaaki se bachne ke liye Dawat-e-Islami ke saath wabastagi bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 6:19

### Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki gawaahi dena sunnat-e-Khuda hai

Allah Ta'ala ne Huzoor ﷺ ki gawaahi kai tarah di hai. Ek yeh ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne khaas bandon se Huzoor ﷺ ki gawaahi dilwa di. Doosre yeh ke Huzoor ﷺ par jo kalaam naazil farmaya, us mein Huzoor ﷺ ki nubuwwat ka elaan farmaya. Teesre yeh ke Huzoor ﷺ ko bahut se mu'jizaat ata farmaye. Yeh sab Allah Ta'ala ki gawaahiyaan hain.

Is se maloom hua ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki gawaahi dena Rasoolullah ﷺ ki sunnat hai aur Huzoor ﷺ ki gawaahi dena Allah Ta'ala ki sunnat hai. Hamare Huzoor ﷺ ke gawaah khud Rabb-ul-Aalameen عَزَّوَجَلَّ hain aur Kalima-e-Shahaadat mein dono gawaahiyaan jama farma di gayi hain. سُبْحَانَ اللہ۔

**{وَ اُوْحِیَ اِلَیَّ هٰذَا الْقُرْاٰنُ: Aur meri taraf is Quran ki wahi ki gayi hai.}** Yani Allah Ta'ala meri nubuwwat ki gawaahi deta hai, kyunki Us ne meri taraf is Quran ki wahi farmaayi. Yeh aisa azeem mu'jiza hai ke tum fasih-o-baligh aur zabaan ke maahir hone ke bawajood is ka muqaabla karne se aajiz rahe. Is se saabit hua ke Quran-e-Paak aajiz kar dene wala mu'jiza hai. Jab yeh aajiz kar dene wala hai to is Kitaab ka mujh par naazil hona Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se mere Rasool hone ki yaqini gawaahi hai.

Allah Ta'ala ke is farmaan **لِاُنْذِرَكُمْ بِهٖ** ka matlab yeh hai ke meri taraf Quran ki wahi farmaayi gayi taa-ke main is ke zariye tumhein Allah Ta'ala ke hukm ki mukhaalifat se daraaun. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 19, 2/8)

**{وَ مَنْۢ بَلَغَ: Aur jin ko yeh pahunche.}** Yani mere baad Qiyamat tak jin logon tak yeh Quran-e-Paak pahunche, chahe woh insaan hon ya jinn, main un sab ko Allah Ta'ala ke hukm ki mukhaalifat se daraaun. Hadees Shareef mein hai ke jis shakhs ko Quran-e-Paak pahuncha aur us ne Quran samajh liya to goya us ne Nabi ﷺ ko dekha aur Aap ﷺ ka kalaam-e-mubarak suna. (Durr-e-Mansoor, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 19, 3/257)

Hazrat Anas bin Malik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya ke jab yeh aayat naazil hui to Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne Kisra, Qaisar aur doosre baadshahon ko Islam ki da'wat ke khat bheje. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 19, 2/8)

Is aayat ki tafseer mein ek qawl yeh bhi hai ke **مَنْۢ بَلَغَ** fa'il ke ma'ni mein hai. Is surat mein ma'ni yeh honge: Main tumhein is Quran se daraaun aur jin logon tak yeh Quran pahunche woh bhi daraayen. (Jamal, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 19, 2/327)

Tirmizi ki hadees mein hai: "Allah Ta'ala us shakhs ko tar-o-taaza rakhe jis ne hamara kalaam suna aur jaisa suna waisa hi aage pahunchaaya. Bahut se log jin tak kalaam pahunchaaya jaata hai, sunne wale se zyada ahl hote hain." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Ilm, Baab: Ma Jaa'a fil Hassi ala Tabligh-is-Simaa', 4/299, hadees: 2666)

Ek riwaayat mein hai: "Sunne wale se zyada samajh rakhne wale hote hain." (Tirmizi, Kitaab-ul-Ilm, Baab: Ma Jaa'a fil Hassi ala Tabligh-is-Simaa', 4/298, hadees: 2665)

Is se fuqaha ki qadr-o-manzilat maloom hoti hai. Yeh bhi maloom hua ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki nubuwwat aur Quran ki hidayat kisi khaas zamane, jagah ya qaum ke saath khaas nahin.

**{اَىٕنَّكُمْ لَتَشْهَدُوْنَ: Kya tum gawaahi dete ho?}** Yahan mushrikon se khitaab hai. Yani Aye Habeeb ﷺ! Aap in kafiron se farma dein: Aye mushriko! Kya tum yeh gawaahi dete ho ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke saath doosre mabood bhi hain? Aye Habeeb-e-Akram ﷺ! Tum farmao ke main yeh gawaahi nahin deta. Balki tum farmao ke woh to ek hi mabood hai aur main un jhoote khudaon se bezaar hoon jinhein tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek thehraate ho.

### Islam qabool karne wale ko kya karna chahiye?

Is aayat se saabit hua ke jo shakhs Islam laaye, use chahiye ke Tauheed-o-Risaalat ki gawaahi ke saath Islam ke har mukhaalif aqeede aur deen se bezaari ka izhaar kare. Balki tamam be-deenon se door rahe aur kufr, shirk aur gunah se bezaar rahe. Momin ko chahiye ke apni soorat, seerat, raftaar aur guftagu se apne imaan ka elaan kare. [1]

[1] Aaj ke zamaane mein apne dilon mein Allah Ta'ala ka khauf paida karne aur gunahon par bebaaki se bachne ke liye Dawat-e-Islami ke saath wabastagi bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 6:20

`Source: tafseerId=50859; ayatId=815; surah=6; ayat=20; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اَلَّذِیْنَ اٰتَیْنٰهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ یَعْرِفُوْنَهٗ كَمَا یَعْرِفُوْنَ اَبْنَآءَهُمْۘ-اَلَّذِیْنَ خَسِرُوْۤا اَنْفُسَهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ۠    (20)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Woh log jinhein hum ne Kitaab ata farmaayi woh is Nabi ko aise pehchaante hain jaise apne beton ko pehchaante hain, lekin jo apni jaanon ko nuqsan mein daalne wale hain to woh imaan nahin laate.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَّذِیْنَ اٰتَیْنٰهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ: Jinhein hum ne Kitaab ata farmaayi.}** Yani Yahoodiyon aur Isaaiyon ke woh ulama jinhein Taurat aur Injeel mili, apni kitaabon mein mazkoor Huzoor ﷺ ke mubarak huliye, na'at aur sifaat ki wajah se Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ko aise pehchaante hain jaise apne beton ko pehchaante hain. Jaise kisi shakhs ko apni aulaad ke us ki aulaad hone mein koi shak nahin hota, isi tarah unhein Huzoor ﷺ ke Nabi hone mein bhi shak nahin.

Is ki tasdeeq is riwaayat se bhi hoti hai ke Islam qabool karne ke baad Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne yeh aayat Hazrat Abdullah bin Salaam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke saamne padh kar Huzoor ﷺ ko pehchanne ki surat poochhi. Main is baat ki gawaahi de sakta hoon ke Rasoolullah ﷺ sachche Rasool hain, lekin auraton ne kya kiya, main nahin jaanta. Ho sakta hai unhone mere peechhe koi khayaanat ki ho aur yeh beta mera na ho, lekin Rasoolullah ﷺ ke sachche Rasool hone mein mujhe kisi qisam ka shak nahin ho sakta."

Tafseer-e-Baghawi mein pehchanne ki yeh surat likhi hai: "Jaise aadmi hazaaron bachchon ke darmiyan apne bete ko bila-taraddud pehchaan leta hai, isi tarah yeh log Sarwar-e-Kaayenaat ﷺ ko pehchaante hain." Lekin in mein jo apni jaanon ko nuqsaan mein daalne wale hain, woh imaan nahin laate. Yahoodiyon ka imaan na laana hasad ki wajah se tha. Is se maloom hua ke hasad ke hote hue kisi ki khoobi tasleem karna bahut mushkil hota hai.

Is aayat se yeh aham mas'alah bhi maloom hua ke Sarkar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ko jaanna aur pehchaanna imaan nahin, balki Huzoor ﷺ ko sachcha Nabi maan-na imaan hai.

## Ayat 6:21

`Source: tafseerId=50860; ayatId=816; surah=6; ayat=21; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَى اللّٰهِ كَذِبًا اَوْ كَذَّبَ بِاٰیٰتِهٖؕ-اِنَّهٗ لَا یُفْلِحُ الظّٰلِمُوْنَ(21)

Is liye jo baat Allah Ta'ala ki shaan ke laayiq na ho, use Allah Ta'ala ki taraf mansoob karna yaqeenan sab se bara zulm hai.

### Zalim ki wa'eed mein shaamil log

Is wa'eed mein mushrik bhi shaamil hain, kyunki woh Tauheed ko us ki jagah se hata dete hain. Doosre kafir bhi is mein shaamil hain. Isi tarah jo log filmon, dramaon ya kisi bhi zariye se kufriyaat seekh kar bolte ya khushi se sunte hain, woh bhi is mein shaamil hain. Woh bhi Allah Ta'ala ki taraf aisi baatein mansoob karte hain jo Us ki shaan ke laayiq nahin.

Is mein guzre hue zamaane aur khaas taur par aaj ke zamaane ke woh ulama, danishwar aur mufakkir bhi shaamil hain jo jaan-boojh kar Quran ki ghalat tafseerein karte hain, ya na-ahil hote hue Quran ki tafseer karte hain. Yeh bhi Allah Ta'ala par jhoot baandhne ke zumeray mein aata hai.

## Ayat 6:22

`Source: tafseerId=50861; ayatId=817; surah=6; ayat=22; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ یَوْمَ نَحْشُرُهُمْ جَمِیْعًا ثُمَّ نَقُوْلُ لِلَّذِیْنَ اَشْرَكُوْۤا اَیْنَ شُرَكَآؤُكُمُ الَّذِیْنَ كُنْتُمْ تَزْعُمُوْنَ(22)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jis din Hum sab ko uthaayenge phir mushrikon se kahenge, tumhare woh shareek kahan hain jinhein tum (Khuda ka shareek) gumaan karte the?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ثُمَّ لَمْ تَكُنْ فِتْنَتُهُمْ: Phir un ki koi ma'zirat na hogi.}** Qiyamat ke din kafiron ke paas apne kufr aur shirk se ma'zirat karne ki koi surat na hogi. Sirf itna hoga ke woh shirk se saaf inkar kar denge aur kahenge: Hum to mushrik the hi nahin.

In ke baare mein agli aayat mein farmaya gaya: Aye Habeeb ﷺ! Dekhein, unhone apne upar kaisa jhoot baandha, kyunki woh umr bhar ke shirk hi se mukar gaye. Mushrik shuru mein apne gunaahon ka inkar karenge, phir doosre waqt iqraar karenge. Phir ek doosre par ilzaam lagaayenge aur kahenge ke hamein to hamare baron ne gumraah kiya tha.

## Ayat 6:25

`Source: tafseerId=50862; ayatId=820; surah=6; ayat=25; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مِنْهُمْ مَّنْ یَّسْتَمِـعُ اِلَیْكَۚ-وَ جَعَلْنَا عَلٰى قُلُوْبِهِمْ اَكِنَّةً اَنْ یَّفْقَهُوْهُ وَ فِیْۤ اٰذَانِهِمْ وَقْرًاؕ-وَ اِنْ یَّرَوْا كُلَّ اٰیَةٍ لَّا یُؤْمِنُوْا بِهَاؕ-حَتّٰۤى اِذَا جَآءُوْكَ یُجَادِلُوْنَكَ یَقُوْلُ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْۤا اِنْ هٰذَاۤ اِلَّاۤ اَسَاطِیْرُ الْاَوَّلِیْنَ(25)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un mein se koi woh hai jo tumhari taraf kaan laga kar sunta hai aur Hum ne un ke dilon par ghilaaf kar diye hain ke is ko na samjhein aur un ke kaanon mein bojh daal diya hai aur agar saari nishaaniyaan (bhi) dekh lein to un par imaan na laayein ge hatta ke jab tumhare paas tum se jhagrte hue aate hain to kafir kehte hain yeh to pehle logon ki daastaanen hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مِنْهُمْ مَّنْ یَّسْتَمِـعُ اِلَیْكَ: Aur un mein se koi woh hai jo tumhari taraf kaan laga kar sunta hai.}** Ek martaba Abu Sufyan, Waleed, Nadr aur Abu Jahl waghera jama ho kar Rasoolullah ﷺ ki tilawat-e-Quran-e-Paak sunne lage. Nadr ke saathiyon ne us se kaha: Muhammad ﷺ kya kehte hain? Us ne kaha: Main nahin jaanta. Woh zaban hilaate hain aur pehle logon ke qisse suna rahe hain, jaise main tumhein sunaya karta hoon.

Abu Sufyan ne kaha: Un ki baatein mujhe haq maloom hoti hain. Abu Jahl ne kaha: Un ki baaton ko maan lene ka iqraar karne se mar jaana behtar hai. Is par yeh aayat-e-kareema naazil hui. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 25, 2/10)

Kafiron ka Quran-e-Paak ko purane qisse kehne ka maqsad Kalaam-e-Paak ke wahi-e-ilaahi hone ka inkar karna hai.

## Ayat 6:26

`Source: tafseerId=50863; ayatId=821; surah=6; ayat=26; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُمْ یَنْهَوْنَ عَنْهُ وَ یَنْــٴَـوْنَ عَنْهُۚ-وَ اِنْ یُّهْلِكُوْنَ اِلَّاۤ اَنْفُسَهُمْ وَ مَا یَشْعُرُوْنَ(26)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur woh (doosron ko) is se rokte aur khud is se door bhaagte hain aur woh apne aap hi ko halaakat mein daalte hain aur unhein shaoor nahin.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ هُمْ یَنْهَوْنَ عَنْهُ: Aur woh (doosron ko) is se rokte hain.}** Yani mushrik logon ko Quran-e-Shareef se, Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ se, Aap ﷺ par imaan laane aur Aap ﷺ ki pairwi karne se rokte hain aur khud bhi door rehte hain. Yeh aayat Makkah ke un kafiron ke baare mein naazil hui jo logon ko Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ par imaan laane, Aap ﷺ ki mehfil mein haazir hone aur Quran-e-Kareem sunne se rokte the. Woh khud bhi is liye door rehte the ke kahin Kalaam-e-Mubarak un ke dil par asar na kar jaaye. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 26, 2/10)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke yeh aayat Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ke chacha Abu Talib ke baare mein naazil hui. Woh mushrikon ko Huzoor ﷺ ko takleef dene se rokte the, lekin khud imaan laane se bachte the. (Qurtubi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 26, 3/251, al-juz'us-saadis.) Lekin zahiri taur par pehla qawl hi durust hai, kyunki yahan jama' ka seega istemaal hua hai.

## Ayat 6:27

`Source: tafseerId=50864; ayatId=822; surah=6; ayat=27; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَوْ تَرٰۤى اِذْ وُقِفُوْا عَلَى النَّارِ فَقَالُوْا یٰلَیْتَنَا نُرَدُّ وَ لَا نُكَذِّبَ بِاٰیٰتِ رَبِّنَا وَ نَكُوْنَ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ(27)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar Aap dekhein jab unhein aag par khara kiya jaayega phir yeh kahenge: Aye kaash ke hamein wapas bhej diya jaaye aur hum apne Rab ki aayatein na jhutlayen aur Musalman ho jaayen.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَوْ تَرٰى: Aur agar Aap dekhein.}** Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke agar Aap ﷺ kafiron ki haalat dekhein jab unhein aag ke saamne khara kiya jaayega to Aap ﷺ ek nihayat khaufnaak manzar dekhenge. Us waqt kafir kahenge: Aye kaash! Kisi tarah hamein duniya mein wapas bhej diya jaaye. Hum apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki aayatein na jhutlayen aur Musalman ho jaayen, taa-ke is haulnaak azaab se bach jaayen.

## Ayat 6:28

`Source: tafseerId=50865; ayatId=823; surah=6; ayat=28; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** بَلْ بَدَا لَهُمْ مَّا كَانُوْا یُخْفُوْنَ مِنْ قَبْلُؕ-وَ لَوْ رُدُّوْا لَعَادُوْا لِمَا نُهُوْا عَنْهُ وَ اِنَّهُمْ لَكٰذِبُوْنَ(28)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Balki pehle jo yeh chhupa rahe the woh un par khul gaya hai aur agar unhein lauta diya jaaye to phir wahi karenge jis se unhein mana kiya gaya tha aur beshak yeh zaroor jhoote hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{بَلْ بَدَا لَهُمْ: Balki un par zaahir ho gaya.}** Jaisa ke isi ruku' mein pehle guzra hai, mushrikon se jab poocha jaayega ke tumhare shareek kahan hain to woh apne kufr ko chhupa lenge aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki qasam kha kar kahenge: Hum mushrik na the.

Is aayat mein bataya gaya ke phir jab un par woh cheez zaahir ho jaayegi jo woh chhupa rahe the, yani un ka kufr is tarah zaahir hoga ke un ke aaza-o-jawarih un ke kufr aur shirk ki gawaahi denge, to woh duniya mein wapas jaane ki tamanna karenge. Kafir agarche duniya mein lautaye jaane aur imaan laane ki tamanna zahir kar rahe honge, lekin un ki yeh tamanna sachchi nahin hogi. Asal baat yeh hai ke pehle jo woh apna mushrik hona chhupa rahe the, woh un par khul gaya hai. Agar unhein duniya mein lauta diya jaaye to phir wahi karenge jis se unhein mana kiya gaya tha aur beshak yeh zaroor jhoote hain.

## Ayat 6:29

`Source: tafseerId=50866; ayatId=824; surah=6; ayat=29; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالُوْۤا اِنْ هِیَ اِلَّا حَیَاتُنَا الدُّنْیَا وَ مَا نَحْنُ بِمَبْعُوْثِیْنَ(29)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur unhone kaha tha ke zindagi to sirf dunyawi zindagi hi hai aur hamein uthaya nahin jaayega.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ قَالُوْا: Aur unhone kaha tha.}** Yeh un kafiron ka qawl hai jo Qiyamat ke munkir the. Is ka waqia yeh tha ke jab Sarwar-e-Kaayenaat ﷺ ne kafiron ke saamne Qiyamat ke ahwaal, Aakhirat ki zindagi, imaan walon aur farmabardaron ke sawaab aur kafiron aur na-farmanon ke azaab ka zikr farmaya to kafir kehne lage: Zindagi to bas duniya ki zindagi hai. Jab hum marne ke baad is duniya se chale jaayenge to hamein dobara zinda nahin kiya jaayega. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 29, 2/11)

Kafiron ka aqeeda hi yeh tha ke zindagi sirf duniya ki zindagi hai aur marne ke baad logon ko dobara nahin uthaya jaayega. Isi aqeede ki wajah se un ki zindagi ghaflat mein guzarti thi. Lekin Musalmanon par bhi afsos hai ke un ka yaqini aqeeda hai ke marne ke baad log uthaye jaayenge aur aamaal ka hisaab dena hoga, is ke bawajood woh ghaflat mein pade hue hain. Yeh bahut afsosnaak haalat hai. Isi ma'ni ke muta'alliq buzurgon ne bahut si baatein irshad farmaayi hain.

Hazrat Abu Ja'far رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: "Us shakhs par badi hairat hai jo hamesha rehne wale ghar, yani Jannat, ki tasdeeq karta hai, lekin koshish dhokhe ke ghar, yani duniya ko haasil karne aur use sajaane sanwaarne, ke liye karta hai." (Kitaab-uz-Zuhd li-Ibn Abi-d-Dunya, safha 28, riwaayat: 14)

Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Ek buzurg رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya: "Us shakhs par ta'ajjub hai jo ehsaan karne wale ki ma'rifat haasil karne ke baad Us ki na-farmaani karta hai aur Shaitaan-e-la'een ki sarkashi ko jaante hue us ki ita'at karta hai." (Ihya-ul-Uloom, Kitaab: Sharh Ajaaib-ul-Qalb, Baab: Bayaan tafseel-e-madaakhil-ush-Shaitaan ilal-qalb, 3/47)

Hazrat Fuzail رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: "Mujhe us shakhs par ta'ajjub nahin hota jo imaarat bana kar chhod jaata hai, balki mujhe us par ta'ajjub hota hai jo us imaarat ko dekh kar ibrat haasil nahin karta." (Ihya-ul-Uloom, Kitaab-ul-Faqr-uz-Zuhd, Baab: Bayaan tafseel-uz-zuhd fima huwa min zaruriyaat-il-hayaat, 4/292)

Hazrat Wuhaib Makki رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: "Kisi shakhs ke liye yeh kaise munasib hai ke woh duniya mein hanse, haalanke woh jaanta hai ke us ke saamne Qiyamat ke din ki faryaadein, pareshaniyan aur haulnaak manazir hain? Qareeb hai ke shadeed ro'ab aur khauf se us ke jism ke jod kaat diye jaayen." (Tanbeeh-ul-Mughtarrin, al-baab-us-sa-sani fi jumlatin ukhra minal-akhlaaq, wa min akhlaaqihim zannuhum bi-anfusihim-il-halaak, safha 109)

## Ayat 6:30

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=825; surah=6; ayat=30; para=7; volume=3`

## Ayat 6:31

`Source: tafseerId=50867; ayatId=826; surah=6; ayat=31; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قَدْ خَسِرَ الَّذِیْنَ كَذَّبُوْا بِلِقَآءِ اللّٰهِؕ-حَتّٰۤى اِذَا جَآءَتْهُمُ السَّاعَةُ بَغْتَةً قَالُوْا یٰحَسْرَتَنَا عَلٰى مَا فَرَّطْنَا فِیْهَاۙ-وَ هُمْ یَحْمِلُوْنَ اَوْزَارَهُمْ عَلٰى ظُهُوْرِهِمْؕ-اَلَا سَآءَ مَا یَزِرُوْنَ(31)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak un logon ne nuqsan uthaya jinhon ne apne Rab se milne ko jhutlaya. Yahan tak ke jab un par achanak Qiyamat aa jaayegi to kahenge: Haaye afsos us par jo hum ne is ke maan-ne mein kotahi ki. Aur woh apne gunahon ke bojh apni peeth par laadte hue honge. Khabardar! Woh kitna bura bojh utha rahe hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قَدْ خَسِرَ الَّذِیْنَ كَذَّبُوْا بِلِقَآءِ اللّٰهِ: Beshak un logon ne nuqsan uthaya jinhon ne apne Rab se milne ko jhutlaya.}** Yani jin kafiron ne marne ke baad dobara zinda kiye jaane aur Qiyamat ke din aamaal ka hisaab dene ke liye Allah Ta'ala ki bargah mein haazir hone ko jhutlaya, unhone apna hi nuqsan kiya. Woh hamesha rehne wali nematon ke ghar Jannat se mehroom honge aur Jahannam ke darjon mein dardnaak azaab mein mubtala honge. Jab Qiyamat achanak un par aa jaayegi aur woh apni zillat aur ruswaai dekhenge to kahenge: Haaye afsos! Hamein is baat par bahut nadamat hai ke hum ne duniya mein Qiyamat ko maan-ne mein kotahi ki, us din par imaan laa kar us ki tayyari na ki aur nek aamaal se door rahe. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 31, 2/12; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 31, 3/22, multaqatan.)

**{وَ هُمْ یَحْمِلُوْنَ اَوْزَارَهُمْ: Aur woh apne gunahon ke bojh uthaaye honge.}** Hadees Shareef mein hai ke jab kafir apni qabr se niklega to us ke saamne nihayat badsoorat, daraawni aur bahut badbudaar shakal aayegi. Woh kafir se kahegi: Kya tu mujhe pehchanta hai? Kafir kahega: Nahin. Woh kahegi: Main tera badkaar amal hoon. Duniya mein tu mujh par sawaar raha tha aur aaj main tujh par sawaar houngi aur tujhe tamaam makhlooq mein ruswa karungi. Phir woh us par sawaar ho jaayegi. (Tafseer Tabari, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 31, 5/178; Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 31, 2/12.)

### Musalman ke Ukhrawi Nuqsan ka Sabab

Qiyamat ke din kafir ka yeh haal hoga, lekin duniya mein kiye gaye bure aamaal Musalman ke liye bhi ukhrawi nuqsan ka sabab ban sakte hain. Hadees mein hai ke Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: "Chaar aadmi aise hain jo Jahannam walon ki takleef mein izafa karenge. Woh kholte hue paani aur aag ke darmiyan daudte hue halaakat aur tabaahi maang rahe honge. Un mein se ek ke gale mein angaron ka sanduq latak raha hoga, doosra apni aantain kheench raha hoga, teesre ke munh se peep aur khoon beh raha hoga aur chautha apna gosht kha raha hoga."

Angaron wale ke baare mein Jahannam wale ek doosre se kahenge: "Is badbakht ko kya hua? Is ne to hamari takleef aur badha di." Sanduq wala jawab dega: "Main is haal mein mara ke meri gardan par logon ke maal ka bojh, yani qarz, tha." Phir apni aantain kheenchne wale ke baare mein kahenge: "Is badbakht ka kya maamla hai jis ne hamari takleef aur badha di?" Woh jawab dega: "Main kapron ko peshab se bachaane ki parwah nahin karta tha."

Phir jis ke munh se khoon aur peep beh rahi hogi, us ke baare mein kahenge: "Is badnaseeb ka kya maamla hai jis ne hamari takleef aur badha di?" Woh kahega: "Main badnaseeb buri baat ki taraf mutawajjih ho kar us se aisi lazzat haasil karta tha jaise jinsi baaton se." Phir jo shakhs apna gosht kha raha hoga, us ke baare mein Jahannam wale kahenge: "Is mardood ko kya hua jis ne hamari takleef mein mazeed izafa kar diya?" Woh jawab dega: "Main badbakht gheebat karke logon ka gosht khaata aur chughli karta tha." (Rasaa'il Ibn Abi-d-Dunya, As-Samt wa Aadaab-ul-Lisaan, Baab-ul-Gheebat wa Zamm-uha, 7/132, riwaayat: 187; Hilyat-ul-Auliya, Shafi bin Maati' al-Asbahi, 5/190, riwaayat: 6786; Az-Zawaajir an Iqtiraaf-il-Kabaa'ir, al-baab-us-sa-nee fil-kabaa'ir-iz-zaahirah, al-kabeerat-us-saaminat wat-taasi'at ba'd-al-mia'tain, 2/18-19.)

Nafs yeh kya zulm hai jab dekho taaza jurm hai

Natawaan ke sar par itna bojh bhaari waah waah

## Ayat 6:32

`Source: tafseerId=50868; ayatId=827; surah=6; ayat=32; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَاۤ اِلَّا لَعِبٌ وَّ لَهْوٌؕ-وَ لَلدَّارُ الْاٰخِرَةُ خَیْرٌ لِّلَّذِیْنَ یَتَّقُوْنَؕ-اَفَلَا تَعْقِلُوْنَ(32)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur duniya ki zindagi sirf khel-kood hai aur beshak Aakhirat wala ghar darne walon ke liye behtar hai to kya tum samajhte nahin?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَاۤ اِلَّا لَعِبٌ وَّ لَهْوٌ: Aur duniya ki zindagi sirf khel-kood hai.}** Irshad farmaya ke duniya ki zindagi sirf khel-kood hai. Is ko baqa nahin, yeh bahut jald guzar jaati hai. Mominon se duniya hi mein hone wali nekiyan aur ibaadat agarche duniya mein hoti hain, lekin woh Aakhirat ke aamaal mein se hain.

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Naeemi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne is aayat ki tafseer mein bahut pyari baat farmayi: "Duniya ki zindagi woh hai jo nafs ki khwahishat mein guzar jaaye. Jo zindagi Aakhirat ke liye zakhira jama karne mein kharch ho, woh duniya mein zindagi to hai lekin duniya ki zindagi nahin. Is liye Ambiya aur saleheen ki zindagi duniya ki nahin balki deen ki hai. Ghaafil aur aqalmand ki zindagiyon mein bahut bada farq hai." (Noor-ul-Irfan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 32, safha 208.)

### Aakhirat Bhula Dene Wale Duniya Daaron ki Misaal

Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne is baat ko bahut pyare andaaz mein samjhaya hai. Kimya-e-Sa'adat mein farmate hain: Duniya daaron ke dunyawi kaam mein mashghool ho kar Aakhirat bhula dene ki misaal aisi hai jaise ek giroh kashti mein sawaar hua aur kashti ek jazeere par ja kar ruk gayi. Log is mauqe ko ghanimat samajh kar apni zaroori haajat se farigh hone ke liye jazeere par utar gaye. Mallah ne elaan kiya: "Hum yahan zyada der nahin rukenge. Waqt zaya kiye baghair sirf tahaarat waghera se farigh ho kar jaldi wapas aa jao."

Jazeere mein utarne ke baad log teen girohon mein taqseem ho gaye:

**(1)** Kuch log jazeere ki sair, us ki khoobiyan aur hairatangez cheezen dekhne mein aise mashghool hue ke unhein kashti mein wapas aana yaad hi na raha. Yahan tak ke woh wahin bhook aur pyaas se tadap kar mar gaye aur darindon ki ghiza ban gaye.

**(2)** Aqalmand log apni haajat se jaldi farigh ho kar kashti mein apni pasand ki jagah par aa kar baith gaye.

**(3)** Kuch log jazeere ki roshniyan, ajeeb-o-ghareeb phool, kaliyan aur khile hue phool dekhne, wahan ke parindon ke meethe naghme sunne aur qeemti patthar dekhne mein lag gaye. Un mein kuch ne sirf dekhne par iktifa na kiya balki bahut si cheezen apne saath utha laaye. Jab kashti mein jagah tang hui aur in cheezon ko rakhne ki jagah na mili to unhein majbooran apne sar par uthana pada. Abhi do din hi guzre the ke un cheezon ka rang badalne laga aur khushboo ki jagah badboo aane lagi. Ab unhein phenkne ki jagah bhi na thi. Woh nadim aur sharminda ho kar unhein apne sar par uthaane par majboor rahe.

Pehle giroh ki misaal kafiron, mushrikon aur bad-aqeedah logon ki hai. Unhone apne aap ko poori tarah duniya ke hawale kar diya aur duniya hi ke ho kar reh gaye. Allah Ta'ala aur Yawm-e-Aakhirat ko bhula diya aur Aakhirat ke muqable mein duniya ki zindagi ko pasand kiya.

Doosre giroh se murad woh aqalmand log hain jinhein momin kaha gaya. Woh tahaarat se farigh hote hi kashti mein sawaar hue aur achhi jagah paa li, yani Jannat ke haqdaar ho gaye.

Teesre giroh ki misaal khatakaaron ki hai. Unhone imaan to mehfooz rakha lekin duniya mein mulawwis hone se bach na sake. (Kimya-e-Sa'adat, Unwaan سوم: Ma'rifat-e-Duniya, Fasl Chahaarum, 1/95-96, mulakhkhasan.)

## Ayat 6:37

`Source: tafseerId=50873; ayatId=832; surah=6; ayat=37; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ قَالُوْا لَوْ لَا نُزِّلَ عَلَیْهِ اٰیَةٌ مِّنْ رَّبِّهٖؕ-قُلْ اِنَّ اللّٰهَ قَادِرٌ عَلٰۤى اَنْ یُّنَزِّلَ اٰیَةً وَّ لٰكِنَّ اَكْثَرَهُمْ لَا یَعْلَمُوْنَ(37)

## Ayat 6:38

`Source: tafseerId=50874; ayatId=833; surah=6; ayat=38; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا مِنْ دَآبَّةٍ فِی الْاَرْضِ وَ لَا طٰٓىٕرٍ یَّطِیْرُ بِجَنَاحَیْهِ اِلَّاۤ اُمَمٌ اَمْثَالُكُمْؕ-مَا فَرَّطْنَا فِی الْكِتٰبِ مِنْ شَیْءٍ ثُمَّ اِلٰى رَبِّهِمْ یُحْشَرُوْنَ(38)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur zameen mein chalne wala koi jaandaar nahin hai aur na hi apne paron ke saath udne wala koi parinda hai magar woh tumhari jaisi ummatein hain. Hum ne is Kitaab mein kisi shay ki koi kami nahin chhodi. Phir yeh apne Rab ki taraf hi uthaaye jaayenge.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِلَّاۤ اُمَمٌ اَمْثَالُكُمْ: Magar woh tumhari jaisi ummatein hain.}** Is ka matlab yeh hai ke tamam jaandaar, chahe woh chaupaaye hon, darinde hon ya parinde, sab tumhari tarah ummatein hain. Yeh mushabahat har pehlu se nahin, balki kuch pehluon se hai.

Is mushabahat ki wajah bayan karte hue baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke jaanwar tumhari tarah Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko pehchaante hain, use Wahid-o-Yakta jaante hain, us ki tasbeeh padhte aur us ki ibaadat karte hain. Baaz ne kaha ke makhlooq hone mein woh tumhari tarah hain. Baaz ne kaha ke woh insaan ki tarah ek doosre se ulfat rakhte hain aur ek doosre ko samajhte aur samjhate hain.

Baaz ka qoul hai ke rozi talaash karne, halaakat se bachne aur nar-o-maada mein farq rakhne mein woh tumhari tarah hain. Baaz ne kaha ke paida hone, marne aur marne ke baad hisaab ke liye uthaaye jaane mein woh tumhari tarah hain. (Khazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 38, 2/15.)

**{مَا فَرَّطْنَا فِی الْكِتٰبِ مِنْ شَیْءٍ: Hum ne is Kitaab mein kisi shay ki koi kami nahin chhodi.}** Yani is mein tamam uloom aur jo kuch ho chuka aur jo kuch hone wala hai, sab ka bayan hai aur sab cheezon ka ilm is mein hai. Is Kitaab se ya to Quran-e-Kareem murad hai ya Lauh-e-Mahfooz. (Jamal, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 38, 2/345.)

Is se Rasoolullah ﷺ ka ilm-e-kulli sabit hota hai, kyunki tamam uloom Lauh-e-Mahfooz ya Quran mein hain aur yeh Kitaabein Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ke ilm mein hain.

**{ثُمَّ اِلٰى رَبِّهِمْ یُحْشَرُوْنَ: Phir yeh apne Rab ki taraf uthaaye jaayenge.}** Qiyamat ke din tamam insaan, jaanwar aur parinde uthaaye jaayenge. Insaan Jannat ya Jahannam mein jaayenge, jabke jaanwaron aur parindon ka hisaab liya jaayega. Is ke baad unhein mitti kar diya jaayega. (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 38, 2/78.)

### Qiyamat ke din jaanwaron ka bhi hisaab hoga

Is se maloom hua ke Qiyamat ke din insaanon aur jinnon ke alawa jaanwaron aur parindon ka bhi hisaab hoga. Baaz ahadees mein bhi yeh baat bayan hui hai.

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: "Qiyamat ke din tum log zaroor haqdaaron ko un ke huqooq de doge, yahan tak ke be-seeng bher ka badla seeng wali bher se liya jaayega." (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Birr was-Silah wal-Aadaab, Baab: Tahreem-uz-Zulm, safha 1394, hadees: 60 (2582).)

Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: "Qiyamat ke din zameen kheench kar chamde ki tarah phaila di jaayegi. Allah Ta'ala insaanon, jinnon, chaupaayon aur wehshi jaanwaron, yani tamam makhlooq ko jama farmayega. Us din Allah Ta'ala jaanwaron ke darmiyan bhi qisaas rakhega, yahan tak ke be-seeng bher seeng wali bher se badla legi. Phir unhein kaha jaayega: Tum sab mitti ho jao. Us waqt kafir tamanna karega: Kaash main bhi mitti ho jaata!" (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ul-Ahwaal, Baab: Ja'alallahu al-Qisaasa bainad-Dawaab, 5/794, hadees: 8756.)

## Ayat 6:39

### Allah Ta'ala ki nemat par khush hone ka hukm

Yaad rahe ke Allah Ta'ala ki nemat par khush hona agar fakhr, takabbur aur shekhi ke taur par ho to bura hai aur kafiron ka tareeqa hai. Agar shukr ke taur par ho to behtar hai aur saaliheen ka tareeqa, balki Allah Ta'ala ka hukm hai. Jaisa ke Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

وَ اَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ  (والضحی: ۱۱)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur apne Rab ki nemat ka khoob charcha karo.

Aur farmata hai:

قُلْ بِفَضْلِ اللّٰهِ وَ بِرَحْمَتِهٖ فَبِذٰلِكَ فَلْیَفْرَحُوْا  (یونس: ۵۸)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Tum farma do: Allah ke fazl aur us ki rahmat par hi khushi manani chahiye.

### Kufr aur gunaahon ke bawajood dunyawi khushhaali ka asal sabab

Is aayat se maloom hua ke kufr aur gunaahon ke bawajood duniya ki rahat milna asal mein Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se mohlat aur us ka ghazab-o-azaab hai. Is se insaan aur zyada ghafil hokar gunaah par diler ho jaata hai. Kabhi woh yeh samajhne lagta hai ke gunaah achhi cheez hai, warna mujhe yeh nematein na miltin. Yeh kufr hai.

Hazrat Uqbah bin Aamir رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshad farmaya: “Jab tum dekho ke Allah Ta'ala bandon ko un ke gunaahon ke bawajood un ki pasand ke mutabiq ata farma raha hai to yeh un ke liye Allah Ta'ala ki taraf se sirf istidraaj aur mohlat hai.” Phir Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne yeh aayat tilawat farmayi:

فَلَمَّا نَسُوْا مَا ذُكِّرُوْا بِهٖ فَتَحْنَا عَلَیْهِمْ اَبْوَابَ كُلِّ شَیْءٍؕ-حَتّٰۤى اِذَا فَرِحُوْا بِمَاۤ اُوْتُوْۤا اَخَذْنٰهُمْ بَغْتَةً فَاِذَا هُمْ مُّبْلِسُوْنَ

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Phir jab unhone un naseehaton ko bhula diya jo unhein ki gayi thin to Hum ne un par har cheez ke darwaaze khol diye, yahan tak ke jab woh us par khush ho gaye jo unhein di gayi to Hum ne achanak unhein pakad liya, ab woh mayoos hain. (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad-ush-Shaamiyyeen, hadees Uqbah bin Aamir Al-Juhani, 6/122, hadees: 17313)

Hazrat Hasan Basri رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: “Khuda ki qasam! Jis shakhs ko Allah Ta'ala ne duniya mein wus'at ata ki aur use yeh khauf na ho ke kahin is mein us ke liye Allah Ta'ala ki koi posheeda tadbeer na ho, to yaqeenan us ka amal naaqis aur us ki fikr kamzor hai. Aur jis shakhs se Allah Ta'ala ne wus'at rok li aur us ne yeh gumaan na kiya ke wus'at rokne mein us ke liye koi bhalai hogi, to yaqeenan us ka amal naaqis aur us ki fikr kamzor hai.” (Tafseer Qurtubi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 44, 3/265, al-juz-us-saadis.)

Is se un naam-nihaad daanishwaron ko bhi sabaq lena chahiye jo kafiron ki taraqqi dekh kar Islam se hi naraaz ho jaate hain. Woh Musalmanon ki maeeshat ka rona rote hue unhein kafiron ki andhi taqleed ka dars dete hain aur Islami sharm-o-haya aur tijaarat ke shar'i qawaneen ko thukraane ka mashwara dete hain.

Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne Yamaamah ki fatah aur Musailimah Kazzab ke halaak hone ki khabar milne par sajdah-e-shukr kiya. ### Ukhrawi najaat ke liye kya cheez zaroori hai?

Is se maloom hua ke ukhrawi najaat ke liye imaan aur nek aamaal dono zaroori hain. Imaan laane ke baad apne aap ko nek aamaal se be-niyaaz samajhne wale aur imaan qabool kiye baghair achhe aamaal ko apni najaat ke liye kaafi samajhne wale, dono duniya ke ahmaq log hain. Albatta in dono sooraton mein sahib-e-imaan yaqeenan be-imaan se behtar hai.

Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Jab banda imaan laata hai aur us ke baad achhe aamaal karta, buraiyon se baaz rehta aur umeed-o-khauf ke darmiyan rehta hai, to use apne amal ke qabool na hone ka dar hota hai. Woh is baat se bhi darta hai ke shayad hamesha nek amal na kar sake aur us ka khaatima achha na ho. Saath hi woh Allah Ta'ala se umeed rakhta hai ke Allah use mazboot aur qaul-e-saabit par qaayam rakhega, us ke deen ko maut ki sakhtiyon se bachaayega, yahan tak ke woh tauheed par duniya se rukhsat ho jayega, aur baaqi zindagi us ke dil ko khwahishon se mehfooz rakhega taake woh gunaah ki taraf mail na ho. Aisa aadmi aqalmand hai. Is ke ilawa log dhoke mein hain. Woh jald azaab dekhenge aur jaan lenge ke sab se zyada bhatka hua kaun tha. Kuch waqt ke baad tumhein zaroor is ki khabar mil jayegi. Us waqt woh kahenge:

رَبَّنَاۤ اَبْصَرْنَا وَ سَمِعْنَا فَارْجِعْنَا نَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا اِنَّا مُوْقِنُوْنَ (سجدہ: ۱۲)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Ae Hamare Rab! Hum ne dekha aur suna, to humein wapas bhej de taake hum nek kaam karein. Beshak ab hum yaqeen karne wale hain.

Yani woh kahenge: Humein maloom ho gaya hai ke jis tarah zameen mein beej daale aur hal chalaaye baghair fasal paida nahi hoti, isi tarah Aakhirat mein achhe amal ke baghair ajr-o-sawaab nahi milta. Ae Allah! Humein wapas bhej de, hum achhe amal kareinge. Ab humein teri is baat ki sachchai maloom ho gayi hai:

وَ اَنْ لَّیْسَ لِلْاِنْسَانِ اِلَّا مَا سَعٰىۙ(۳۹) وَ اَنَّ سَعْیَهٗ سَوْفَ یُرٰى (نجم: ۳۹،۴۰)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur yeh ke insaan ke liye wahi hoga jis ki us ne koshish ki, aur yeh ke us ki koshish jald dekhi jayegi.

Lekin us waqt un ki yeh baatein unhein koi faida nahi dengi aur na hi unhein wapas bheja jayega. (Ihya-ul-Uloom-id-Deen, Kitaab Zamm-ul-Ghuroor, Bayan Zamm-ul-Ghuroor wa Haqeeqatuhu wa Amtsilatuhu, 3/472-473.)

## Ayat 6:50

`Source: tafseerId=50883; ayatId=845; surah=6; ayat=50; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ لَّاۤ اَقُوْلُ لَكُمْ عِنْدِیْ خَزَآىٕنُ اللّٰهِ وَ لَاۤ اَعْلَمُ الْغَیْبَ وَ لَاۤ اَقُوْلُ لَكُمْ اِنِّیْ مَلَكٌۚ-اِنْ اَتَّبِـعُ اِلَّا مَا یُوْحٰۤى اِلَیَّؕ-قُلْ هَلْ یَسْتَوِی الْاَعْمٰى وَ الْبَصِیْرُؕ-اَفَلَا تَتَفَكَّرُوْنَ۠   (50)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** (Ae Habeeb!) Tum farma do: Main tum se nahi kehta ke mere paas Allah ke khazaane hain aur na yeh kehta hoon ke main khud ghaib jaan leta hoon aur na tum se yeh kehta hoon ke main farishta hoon. Main to sirf us wahi ka pairvi karne wala hoon jo meri taraf aati hai. Tum farmao: Kya andha aur dekhne wala barabar ho sakte hain? To kya tum ghaur nahi karte?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ: Tum farmao.}** Kaafiron ka tareeqa tha ke woh Rasoolullah ﷺ se tarah tarah ke sawaal karte rehte the. Kabhi kehte ke agar Aap Rasool hain to humein bahut sa daulat aur maal de dijiye taake hum kabhi mohtaaj na hon aur Hamare liye pahaadon ko sona bana dijiye. Kabhi kehte ke guzri hui aur aane wali khabrein suna dijiye aur humein Hamare mustaqbil ki khabar de dijiye ke kya kya pesh aayega, taake hum munaafa haasil karne aur nuqsaan se bachne ke liye pehle se intezaam kar lein. Kabhi kehte ke humein Qiyamat ka waqt bata dijiye ke woh kab aayegi. Kabhi kehte ke Aap ﷺ kaise Rasool hain jo khaana-peena bhi karte hain aur nikaah bhi karte hain.

Un ki in sab baaton ka is aayat mein jawab diya gaya hai ke tumhari yeh baat nihayat be-mahal aur jaahilana hai. Jo shakhs kisi cheez ka da'wa kare, us se wahi baatein poochhi ja sakti hain jo us ke da'we se ta'alluq rakhti hon. Ghair-mutalliq baat poochhna aur use us ke da'we ke khilaaf daleel banana inteha darje ki jaahaalat hai.

Is liye irshaad hua ke Aap ﷺ farma dein: Mera da'wa yeh to nahi ke mere paas Allah Ta'ala ke khazaane hain, jin ka tum mujh se maal aur daulat ka sawaal karo aur agar main tumhari marzi ke mutabiq tumhara sawaal poora na karoon to tum Risaalat ke munkir ho jao. Na mera da'wa zaati ghaib-daani ka hai ke agar main tumhein guzri hui ya aane wali khabrein na bataoon to tum meri nubuwwat maanne mein bahaana bana sako. Na main ne farishta hone ka da'wa kiya hai ke mera khaana-peena aur nikaah karna tumhare nazdeek aitraaz ban jaye. Jin cheezon ka da'wa hi nahi kiya gaya, un ka sawaal karna be-mahal hai aur aise sawaal ko poora karna mujh par laazim nahi. Mera da'wa to nubuwwat-o-risaalat ka hai. Jab is par zabardast daleelein aur mazboot burhaan qaayam ho chuki hain, to ghair-mutalliq baatein pesh karne ka kya matlab hai?

### Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ke ilm-e-ghaib ka inkaar karne walon ka rad

Is se saaf wazeh ho gaya ke is aayat-e-kareema ko Tajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ko ghaib par muttali' kiye jaane ki nafi ke liye daleel banana bhi utna hi be-mahal hai jitna kaafiron ka in sawaalaat ko inkaar-e-nubuwwat ki daleel banana be-mahal tha. Upar ka kalaam padhne ke baad ab dobara aayat ka tarjuma pesh kiya jaata hai. Ise padhiye aur ghaur kijiye ke kya waaqe'i aayat mein yahi bayan nahi kiya gaya:

“(Ae Habeeb!) Tum farma do: Main tum se nahi kehta ke mere paas Allah ke khazaane hain aur na yeh kehta hoon ke main khud ghaib jaan leta hoon aur na tum se yeh kehta hoon ke main farishta hoon. Main to sirf us wahi ka pairvi karne wala hoon jo meri taraf aati hai.”

Yahi Nabi ka kaam hai. Is liye main tumhein wahi cheez dunga jis ki mujhe ijaazat hogi, wahi bataunga jis ki mujhe ijaazat hogi aur wahi karunga jis ka mujhe hukm diya gaya ho. Is aayat se Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ke ilm-e-ghaib-e-ataai ki nafi kisi tarah murad nahi ho sakti, kyunki is soorat mein aayaat mein ta'aruz ko maanna padega aur yeh bilkul baatil hai. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 50, 2/17; Madaarik, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 50, safha 322; Jaml, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 50, 2/353, mulattqatan.)

Allamah Nizaam-ud-Deen Hasan bin Muhammad Naishapoori رَحْمَۃُاللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is aayat ki tafseer mein farmate hain: “Irshaad hua ke ae Nabi! Farma do ke main tum se nahi kehta ke mere paas Allah Ta'ala ke khazaane hain. Yahan yeh nahi farmaya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala ke khazaane mere paas nahi, balki yeh farmaya gaya hai ke main tum se yeh nahi kehta ke mere paas Allah Ta'ala ke khazaane hain. Is se maloom hota hai ke Allah Ta'ala ke khazaane Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ke paas hain, lekin Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ logon se un ki samajh ke mutabiq baat farmate hain. Woh khazaane tamaam cheezon ki haqeeqat aur maahiyyat ka ilm hain. Huzoor-e-Akram ﷺ ne isi ilm ke milne ki dua ki aur Allah Ta'ala ne use qabool farmaya.

Phir farmaya: ‘Main nahi jaanta’, yani main tum se yeh nahi kehta ke mujhe zaati ghaib ka ilm hai. Warna Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ khud farmate hain: Mujhe maa kaana wa maa yakoon ka ilm diya gaya, yani jo kuch guzar chuka aur jo kuch Qiyamat tak hone wala hai, sab ka ilm mujhe ata kiya gaya.” (Tafseer Naishapoori, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 50, 3/83.)

Aayat ke aakhir mein farmaya gaya ke kya andha aur dekhne wala barabar hain? Is se muraad yeh hai ke kya momin aur kaafir, aur aalim aur jaahil barabar hain? Hargiz barabar nahi hain.

## Ayat 6:51

`Source: tafseerId=50884; ayatId=846; surah=6; ayat=51; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ اَنْذِرْ بِهِ الَّذِیْنَ یَخَافُوْنَ اَنْ یُّحْشَرُوْۤا اِلٰى رَبِّهِمْ لَیْسَ لَهُمْ مِّنْ دُوْنِهٖ وَلِیٌّ وَّ لَا شَفِیْعٌ لَّعَلَّهُمْ یَتَّقُوْنَ(51)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur is Quran se un logon ko darao jo is baat se darte hain ke unhein un ke Rab ki taraf yun uthaya jaayega ke Allah ke siwa na un ka koi himaayati hoga aur na koi sifaarishi. (Unhein) is umeed par (darao) ke yeh parhezgaar ho jaayein.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اَنْذِرْ بِهِ: Aur is Quran se darao.}** Pehle bayan hua tha ke Allah Ta'ala ke Rasoolon عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka mansab khushkhabri dena aur daraana hai. Is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko hukm diya ke woh bhi logon ko daraayein. Irshaad hua: Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Aap is Quran mein bayan kiye gaye azaab se un logon ko daraayein jo is baat se darte hain ke unhein un ke Rab ki taraf yun uthaya jaayega ke Allah Ta'ala ke muqaable mein un ka koi himaayati aur sifaarishi nahi hoga. Aap logon ko is umeed par daraayein ke woh kufr aur gunahon ko chhor kar parhezgaar ban jaayein. ### Qiyamat ke din shafa'at

Yaad rakhein ke Qiyamat ke din Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke muqaable mein koi kisi ka himaayati aur sifaarishi nahi hoga. Haan, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ijaazat se himaayati aur sifaarishi honge, jaise Ambiya, Auliya, shuhada, sulaha, ulama aur Hajiyon-e-Kiraam. Yeh sab Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke izn aur ijaazat se logon ki himaayat aur sifaarish karenge.

Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Auliya-e-Izaam رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِمْ ki shafa'at to wazeh hai. Is liye barkat ke liye doosre buzurgon ki shafa'at se muta'alliq chaar ahadees pesh ki jaati hain.

**(1)** Hazrat Usman bin Affan رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: Qiyamat ke din teen jamaatein shafa'at karengi: pehle Ambiya, phir ulama, phir shaheed log. (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab Zikr-ush-Shafa'ah, 4/526, hadees: 4313)

**(2)** Hazrat Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Rasool-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Shaheed ke sattar ghar walon ke haq mein us ki shafa'at qabool ki jaayegi.” (Abu Dawood, Kitaab-ul-Jihaad, Baab fis-Shaheed Yashfa', 3/22, hadees: 2522)

**(3)** Hazrat Jabir bin Abdullah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: Aalim aur aabid dono ko Qiyamat ke din dobara zinda kiya jaayega. Phir aabid se kaha jaayega ke Jannat mein daakhil ho jao, aur aalim ko hukm hoga ke tum abhi thehro aur logon ki shafa'at karo. (Shu'ab-ul-Imaan, As-Saabi' Ashar min Shu'ab-il-Imaan... al-kh, Fasl fi Fazl-il-Ilm wa Sharaf Miqdaaruhu, 2/268, hadees: 1717)

**(4)** Hazrat Abu Musa Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se marfoo'an riwaayat hai ke Haaji ki shafa'at us ke khandaan ke chaar sau afraad ke haq mein qabool ki jaayegi. (Musnad-ul-Bazzaar, Musnad Abi Musa رضی اللہ عنہ, 8/169, hadees: 3196)
