# Surah Al-Anam — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 6:52

`Source: tafseerId=50885; ayatId=847; surah=6; ayat=52; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَا تَطْرُدِ الَّذِیْنَ یَدْعُوْنَ رَبَّهُمْ بِالْغَدٰوةِ وَ الْعَشِیِّ یُرِیْدُوْنَ وَجْهَهٗؕ-مَا عَلَیْكَ مِنْ حِسَابِهِمْ مِّنْ شَیْءٍ وَّ مَا مِنْ حِسَابِكَ عَلَیْهِمْ مِّنْ شَیْءٍ فَتَطْرُدَهُمْ فَتَكُوْنَ مِنَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(52)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un logon ko door na karo jo subah-o-shaam apne Rab ko Us ki raza chaahte hue pukaarte hain. Aap par un ke hisaab se kuchh nahi aur un par aap ke hisaab se kuchh nahi. Phir agar aap unhein door karein to yeh kaam insaaf se door hoga.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَا تَطْرُدِ الَّذِیْنَ یَدْعُوْنَ رَبَّهُمْ بِالْغَدٰوةِ وَ الْعَشِیِّ: Aur un logon ko door na karo jo subah-o-shaam apne Rab ko pukaarte hain.}** Is aayat ka sabab-e-nuzool yeh hai ke kaafiron ki ek jamaat Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki baargaah mein aayi. Unhone dekha ke Tajdar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ke ird-gird ghareeb Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki ek jamaat baithi hui hai aur unhone saadah libaas pehne hue hain. Yeh dekh kar woh kehne lage: Humein in logon ke paas baithte hue sharm aati hai. Agar Aap inhein apni majlis se nikaal dein to hum Aap par imaan le aayenge aur Aap ki khidmat mein haazir raha karenge. Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne un ki baat manzoor na farmayi. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. (Ibn Majah, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, Baab Majaalisat-ul-Fuqara, 4/435, hadees: 4127; Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 52, 2/81.)

Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya ke in mukhlis aur ghareeb Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ko apni baargaah se door na karo. Woh subah-o-shaam Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko Us ki raza chaahte hue pukaarte hain. In ghareeb Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ka rizq Aap ﷺ ke zimme nahi ke ghurbat ki wajah se unhein door kar diya jaaye, aur na Aap ﷺ ki zimmedaari un ke hisaab ki hai. Sab ka hisaab Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke zimme hai. Wahi saari makhlooq ko rizq dene wala hai aur Us ke siwa kisi ke zimme kisi ka hisaab nahi.

Is aayat ka ek doosra ma'ni yeh bayan kiya gaya hai ke kaafiron ne ghareeb Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم par ta'n kiya tha ke yeh log ghurbat ki wajah se Aap ﷺ ke paas baithe hain taake yahan khaane-peene ka intezaam ho jaaye. Yeh mukhlis nahi hain. Is par pehle Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke ikhlaas ka bayan farmaya gaya ke woh Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki raza chaahte hue din-raat Us ki ibaadat karte hain. Phir farmaya gaya: Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Aap par in ke haal ki jaanch zaroori nahi ke yeh Aap ﷺ ke saath mukhlis hain ya nahi. Balki Aap ﷺ inhein apni sohbat ke faiz se nawazte rahein. Khulasa yeh hai ke woh zaeef aur ghareeb log jo pehle bayan hue, Aap ﷺ ke darbaar mein qurb paane ke mustahiq hain. Unhein door na karna hi durust hai. (Tafseer Kabeer, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 52, 4/542, multaqatan.)

## Ayat 6:53

`Source: tafseerId=50886; ayatId=848; surah=6; ayat=53; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ كَذٰلِكَ فَتَنَّا بَعْضَهُمْ بِبَعْضٍ لِّیَقُوْلُوْۤا اَهٰۤؤُلَآءِ مَنَّ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِمْ مِّنْۢ بَیْنِنَاؕ-اَلَیْسَ اللّٰهُ بِاَعْلَمَ بِالشّٰكِرِیْنَ(53)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur isi tarah hum ne un mein baaz ki doosron ke zariye aazmaish ki taake yeh kahen: Kya yeh log hain jin par hamare darmiyaan mein se Allah ne ehsaan kiya? Kya Allah shukr guzaaron ko khoob nahi jaanta?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ كَذٰلِكَ فَتَنَّا بَعْضَهُمْ بِبَعْضٍ: Aur isi tarah hum ne un mein baaz ki doosron ke zariye aazmaish ki.}** Pichhli aayat ke hawale se yahan farmaya gaya ke ghareebon ke zariye ameeron ki aazmaish hoti rehti hai. Guzre hue Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke Sahaba ke saath bhi yahi hota tha. Maaldaar kaafir ghareeb Musalmanon ko haqaarat ki nazar se dekhte, un ka mazaaq udaate aur Islam ki haqqaniyat ka inkaar karte. Woh kehte: Agar Islam sachcha aur kufr jhoota hai to Musalman ghareeb aur kaafir maaldaar kyun hain?

### Ghareebon ke saath achha sulook

Is se maloom hua ke ameer ya ghareeb hona Haq ka paimaana nahi ban sakta. Pichhli aayat ke sabab-e-nuzool aur is aayat ke dars se bahut se deendar logon aur khud ameeron ko sabaq lena chahiye. Hamare zamane mein bhi yeh rawayya paaya jaata hai ke ameer aaye to us ki ta'zeem ki jaati hai, jabke ghareeb ko haqaarat ki nazar se dekha jaata hai. Ghareeb ka dil dukhaaya jaata hai aur ameer ke aage bichhe jaate hain.

Khud baaz ameer bhi yeh chaahte hain ke un se alag tarah ka sulook kiya jaaye, un ke liye khaas waqt nikaala jaaye aur un ke aane par Maulvi apni saari masroofiyat chhor kar un ke peechhe phirta rahe. Ghareeb aadmi paas baith jaaye to ameer ise apne rutbe ke khilaaf samajhta hai. Use ghareeb ke kapron se boo aati hai, ghareeb ka paas baithna us ki tabiyat kharaab kar deta hai aur ghareeb se haath milaane ko woh apne haath par jaraseem chadh jaana samajhta hai. Yeh sab ghamand aur takabbur ki baatein hain. In se bachna laazim aur zaroori hai.

### Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ aur ek maghroor ameer

Ek sahib Imam-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki khidmat mein aaya karte the aur Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ bhi kabhi kabhi un ke ghar tashreef le jaate the. Ek martaba Aap رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ un ke yahan tashreef farma the ke un ke mohalle ka ek bechara ghareeb Musalman sahn ke kinaare rakhi hui tooti purani chaarpai par jhijhakte hue baithne laga. Ghar ke maalik ne nihayat talkh nazron se us ki taraf dekhna shuru kar diya. Woh sharminda hokar sar jhukaaye uth kar chala gaya.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ko is maghroorana rawayye se bahut takleef hui, lekin Aap ne kuchh na farmaya. Kuchh din baad woh sahib Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ke yahan aaye to Aap ne unhein apni chaarpai par jagah di. Woh abhi baithe hi the ke Kareem Bakhsh Hajjam, Aap رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ka khat banaane ke liye aaye. Woh soch rahe the ke kahan baithoon. Aap رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya: Bhai Kareem Bakhsh! Kyun khade ho? Musalman aapas mein bhai-bhai hain. Phir Aap ne un sahib ke qareeb baithne ka ishaara farmaya. Kareem Bakhsh Hajjam un ke saath baith gaye. Un sahib ko itna gussa aaya jaise saanp phunkare maar raha ho. Woh foran uth kar chale gaye aur phir kabhi na aaye. (Hayat-e-A'la Hazrat, 1/108, mulakhkhasan.)

Apni aadaton par ghaur karke ek baar phir is aayat ka tarjuma dekhiye: “Aur isi tarah hum ne un mein baaz ki doosron ke zariye aazmaish ki taake maaldaar kaafir ghareeb Musalmanon ko dekh kar kahen: Kya yeh log hain jin par hamare darmiyaan mein se Allah ne ehsaan kiya? Kya Allah shukr guzaaron ko khoob nahi jaanta?”

## Ayat 6:54

`Source: tafseerId=50887; ayatId=849; surah=6; ayat=54; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا جَآءَكَ الَّذِیْنَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِاٰیٰتِنَا فَقُلْ سَلٰمٌ عَلَیْكُمْ كَتَبَ رَبُّكُمْ عَلٰى نَفْسِهِ الرَّحْمَةَۙ-اَنَّهٗ مَنْ عَمِلَ مِنْكُمْ سُوْٓءًۢا بِجَهَالَةٍ ثُمَّ تَابَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِهٖ وَ اَصْلَحَ فَاَنَّهٗ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(54)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur jab Aap ki baargaah mein woh log haazir hon jo Hamari aayaat par imaan laate hain to un se farmaayein: “Tum par salaam.” Tumhare Rab ne apne zimme karam par rahmat laazim kar li hai ke tum mein se jo koi naadaani se koi burai kar le, phir us ke baad tauba kare aur apni islaah kar le to beshak Allah bakhshne wala, meherbaan hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِذَا جَآءَكَ الَّذِیْنَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِاٰیٰتِنَا: Aur jab Aap ki baargaah mein woh log haazir hon jo Hamari aayaat par imaan laate hain.}** Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ se farmaya ke jab Aap ﷺ ki baargaah mein imaan wale log haazir hon to Aap un ki izzat-afzaai karte hue pehle unhein salaam farmaayein aur yeh khushkhabri dein ke tumhare Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne fazl-o-karam se apne zimme rahmat laazim kar li hai. Tum mein se jo koi naadaani se koi burai kar le, phir us ke baad tauba kare aur apni islaah kar le to beshak Allah Ta'ala us ke gunaah bakhshne wala aur us par meherbaani farmane wala hai. (Madaarik, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 54, safha 323; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 54, 3/38-39, multaqatan.)

### Nek Musalmanon ka ehteraam karna chahiye

Is se maloom hua ke nek Musalmanon ka ehteraam aur ta'zeem karni chahiye. Har aisi baat se bachna chahiye jo un ki naaraazgi ka sabab bane, kyunki unhein naaraaz karna Allah Ta'ala ki naaraazgi ka sabab hai.

Hazrat Aa'iz bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Hazrat Salman, Hazrat Suhaib aur Hazrat Bilal رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ke saamne Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne un ke mizaaj ke khilaaf koi baat keh di. Phir Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ki baargaah mein haazir hue aur Aap ﷺ ko is ki khabar di. Aap ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Ae Abu Bakr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ! Shayad tum ne unhein naaraaz kar diya. Agar tum ne unhein naaraaz kar diya to apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko naaraaz kar diya.” Phir Hazrat Abu Bakr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ un ke paas gaye aur kaha: Ae mere bhaiyo! Kya main ne tumhein naaraaz kar diya? Unhone kaha: Nahi, ae bhai! Allah Ta'ala Aap ki maghfirat farmaaye. (Muslim, Kitaab Faza'il-us-Sahaabah رضی اللہ تعالی عنہم, Baab min Faza'il Salmaan wa Suhaib wa Bilaal, safha 1359, hadees: 170 (2504).)

**[1]** Musalmanon ke ehteraam se muta'alliq mufeed maloomat haasil karne ke liye Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَتْ بَرَکَاتُہُمُ الْعَالِیَہ ka risaalah “Ehteraam-e-Muslim” (matboo'a Maktabat-ul-Madinah) mutaala'a farmaayein.

## Ayat 6:55

`Source: tafseerId=50888; ayatId=850; surah=6; ayat=55; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ كَذٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ الْاٰیٰتِ وَ لِتَسْتَبِیْنَ سَبِیْلُ الْمُجْرِمِیْنَ۠   (55)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur isi tarah hum aayaat ko tafseel se bayan farmate hain aur is liye ke mujrim logon ka raasta wazeh ho jaaye.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ كَذٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ الْاٰیٰتِ: Aur isi tarah hum aayaat ko tafseel se bayan farmate hain.}** Yani ae Habeeb ﷺ! Jis tarah hum ne is surah mein apni wahdaaniyat ki daleelein tafseel ke saath bayan ki hain, isi tarah hum apni hujjatein aur daleelein bhi tafseel se bayan farmate hain. Quran-e-Majeed mein hum ita'at guzaar logon ke ausaaf, gunaah ke baad tauba karne walon ki sifaat aur gunaah par ade rehne walon ki khubiyan bayan karte hain, taake Haq zaahir ho aur us par amal kiya jaaye. Aur hum yeh cheezein is liye bayan karte hain ke mujrim logon ka raasta aur un ka tareeqa wazeh ho jaaye aur us se bacha ja sake. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 2/20; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 55, 3/39, multaqatan.)

### Aakhirat ki kaamyaabi tak pahunchane wale raaste par chalne ki targheeb

Is se maloom hua ke aadmi ko chahiye ke woh falaah aur kaamyaabi ke raaste par chale aur wahan pahunche jahan nek log pahunche. Is ka behtareen raasta yeh hai ke foran apne guzre hue gunahon se tauba aur istighfaar kare aur aainda nek aamaal kare.

Imam Muhammad Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: “Zindagi ka har lamha, balki har saans ek anmol moti hai jis ka koi badal nahi. Woh tumhein hamesha ki sa'aadat tak pahuncha sakta hai aur hamesha ki badbakhti se bacha sakta hai. Is se zyada anmol moti aur kya ho sakta hai? Agar tum ise ghaflat mein zaya kar doge to khula nuqsaan uthaaoge, aur agar ise gunaah mein lagaoge to saaf taur par halaak ho jaaoge. Agar tum is museebat par nahi rote to yeh tumhari jaahaalat hai, aur jaahaalat ki museebat sab museebaton se badh kar hai. Afsoos! Log ghaflat ki neend soye hue hain. Jab maut aayegi to woh jaag jaayenge. Us waqt har muflis ko apni muflisi aur har museebat-zadah ko apni museebat ka ilm ho jaayega, lekin us waqt iska azaala nahi ho sakega.” (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab-ut-Taubah, Ar-Rukn-ul-Awwal fi Nafs-it-Taubah, Bayan Anna Wujub-ut-Taubah Aam fil Ashkhaas..., 4/15.)

Imam Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ mazeed farmate hain: “Jab tum in teen dehshatnaak baaton ko hamesha yaad rakhoge aur raat-din ke har hisse mein inhein taza karte rahoge to tumhein gunahon se zaroor sachchi aur khalis tauba naseeb hogi:

**(1)** Gunahon ki intehaai buraai bayan karna.

**(2)** Allah Ta'ala ki saza ki shiddat, dardnaak azaab, Us ki naaraazgi, Us ke ghazab aur jalal ka zikr karna.

**(3)** Allah Ta'ala ke ghazab aur azaab ki sakhti ko saamne rakh kar apni kamzori aur na-tawaani yaad karna. Jo shakhs suraj ki tapish, sipahi ke tamaanche aur cheenti ke dank ko bardaasht nahi kar sakta, woh Jahannam ki aag ki tapish, azaab ke farishton ke kooron ki maar, lambe gardan wale Bakhti oonton jaise lambe aur zehreelay saanpon ke dank aur khachar jaise bichchhuon ke dank kaise bardaasht kar sakega?” (Minhaaj-ul-Aabideen, Al-Uqbat-us-Saaniyah: Uqbat-ut-Taubah, safha 33.)

## Ayat 6:56

`Source: tafseerId=50889; ayatId=851; surah=6; ayat=56; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ اِنِّیْ نُهِیْتُ اَنْ اَعْبُدَ الَّذِیْنَ تَدْعُوْنَ مِنْ دُوْنِ اللّٰهِؕ-قُلْ لَّاۤ اَتَّبِـعُ اَهْوَآءَكُمْۙ-قَدْ ضَلَلْتُ اِذًا وَّ مَاۤ اَنَا مِنَ الْمُهْتَدِیْنَ(56)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmao: Mujhe mana kiya gaya hai ke main un ki ibaadat karoon jin ki tum Allah ke siwa ibaadat karte ho. Tum farmao: Main tumhari khwahishon par nahi chalta. (Agar yun hota) to main bhatak jaata aur hidaayat yafta logon mein se na hota.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ اِنِّیْ نُهِیْتُ: Tum farmao: Mujhe mana kiya gaya hai.}** Pichhli aayat mein bayan hua ke Allah Ta'ala tafseel ke saath aayaat bayan farmata hai taake Haq zaahir ho jaaye aur mujrim logon ka raasta wazeh ho jaaye. Is aayat mein farmaya gaya ke Allah Ta'ala ne mujrim logon ke raaste par chalne se mana farmaya hai.

Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ se farmaya ke Aap kaafiron se farma dein: Mujhe mana kiya gaya hai ke main un ki ibaadat karoon jin ki tum Allah Ta'ala ke siwa ibaadat karte ho. Tumhara un ki ibaadat karna kisi daleel ki bunyaad par nahi, balki sirf apni khwahish aur baap-dada ki andhi pairvi ki wajah se hai. Jin ki tum ibaadat karte ho woh be-jaan cheezein aur patthar hain. Un ka martaba insaan se bahut kam hai. Buland martabe wale ka kam martabe wale ki ibaadat karna aisa kaam hai jise aqal bhi ghalat qarar deti hai.

Agar main tumhari khwahishon ki pairvi karta to Haq ke raaste se bhatak jaata aur hidaayat yafta logon mein se na hota. (Tafseer Kabeer, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 57, 5/8.)

## Ayat 6:57

`Source: tafseerId=50890; ayatId=852; surah=6; ayat=57; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ اِنِّیْ عَلٰى بَیِّنَةٍ مِّنْ رَّبِّیْ وَ كَذَّبْتُمْ بِهٖؕ-مَا عِنْدِیْ مَا تَسْتَعْجِلُوْنَ بِهٖؕ-اِنِ الْحُكْمُ اِلَّا لِلّٰهِؕ-یَقُصُّ الْحَقَّ وَ هُوَ خَیْرُ الْفٰصِلِیْنَ(57)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmao: Main to apne Rab ki taraf se roshan daleel par hoon aur tum ne use jhutlaya hai. Jis azaab ke aane ki tum jaldi macha rahe ho woh mere paas nahi, hukm sirf Allah hi ka hai. Woh Haq bayan farmata hai aur woh sab se behtar faisla farmane wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{قُلْ: Tum farmao.}** Yani Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Aap farmaayein: Main apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki taraf se roshan daleel par hoon, mujhe Allah Ta'ala ki ma'rifat haasil hai aur main jaanta hoon ke Us ke siwa koi ibaadat ke laaiq nahi. Tum Allah Ta'ala ke saath doosron ko shareek karke Us ka inkaar karte ho. Yahan roshan daleel mein Quran-e-Majeed, mu'jizaat aur tauheed ke wazeh daleel sab shaamil hain.

**{مَا عِنْدِیْ مَا تَسْتَعْجِلُوْنَ بِهٖ: Jis azaab ke aane ki tum jaldi macha rahe ho woh mere paas nahi.}** Kaafir mazaaq udaane ke liye Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ se kehte the: Hum par jaldi azaab naazil karaaiye. Is aayat mein unhein jawab diya gaya aur wazeh kar diya gaya ke Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ se yeh sawaal karna bilkul ghalat hai. Azaab naazil karna Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka kaam hai, Tajdar-e-Risaalat ﷺ ka nahi. Haan, agar Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ is ke liye dua farma dein to baat alag hai. Jaise Hazrat Nooh عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki dua se Qaum-e-Nooh tabaah hui, Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki dua se Fir'aun aur us ki qaum tabaah hui, aur doosre Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki duaon se un ki qaumein tabaah hui. Isi tarah Habeeb-e-Kareem ﷺ ki dua se Kuffaar-e-Makkah bhi barbaad ho jaate.

Agli aayat mein mazeed farmaya gaya ke Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Aap farma dein: Agar woh azaab mere paas hota jis ki tum jaldi macha rahe ho to mere aur tumhare darmiyaan faisla ho chuka hota. Main tumhein ek lamhe ki bhi mohlat na deta aur tumhein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka mukhaalif dekh kar be-dharek halaak kar deta. Lekin Allah Ta'ala Haleem-o-Kareem hai. Woh saza dene mein jaldi nahi farmata. Is liye Us ki hilm-o-karam ki wajah se jari na ho jao, balki Us ki baargaah mein ruju karo.

## Ayat 6:58

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=853; surah=6; ayat=58; para=7; volume=3`

## Ayat 6:59

`Source: tafseerId=50891; ayatId=854; surah=6; ayat=59; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ عِنْدَهٗ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَیْبِ لَا یَعْلَمُهَاۤ اِلَّا هُوَؕ-وَ یَعْلَمُ مَا فِی الْبَرِّ وَ الْبَحْرِؕ-وَ مَا تَسْقُطُ مِنْ وَّرَقَةٍ اِلَّا یَعْلَمُهَا وَ لَا حَبَّةٍ فِیْ ظُلُمٰتِ الْاَرْضِ وَ لَا رَطْبٍ وَّ لَا یَابِسٍ اِلَّا فِیْ كِتٰبٍ مُّبِیْنٍ(59)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur ghaib ki kunjiyaan Usi ke paas hain. Unhein sirf Wahi jaanta hai aur jo kuchh khushki aur tari mein hai woh sab jaanta hai. Koi patta nahi girta aur na zameen ki tareekiyon mein koi daana hai magar woh in sab ko jaanta hai. Aur koi tar cheez nahi aur na koi khushk cheez magar woh ek roshan Kitaab mein hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ عِنْدَهٗ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَیْبِ: Aur ghaib ki kunjiyaan Usi ke paas hain.}** Tafseer-e-Inaayat-ul-Qaazi mein hai ke is aayat mein ghaib ki kunjiyon ka Allah Ta'ala ke paas hone ka matlab yeh hai ke ghaib ki cheezon ki haqeeqat doosro par Allah Ta'ala ke bataaye baghair zaahir nahi hoti. (Inaayat-ul-Qaazi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 59, 4/73.)

Tafseer-e-Saawi mein hai ke yeh aayat is baat ke khilaaf nahi ke Allah Ta'ala ne baaz Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام aur Auliya رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِمْ ko baaz cheezon ka ilm-e-ghaib ata farmaya hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai:

عٰلِمُ الْغَیْبِ فَلَا یُظْهِرُ عَلٰى غَیْبِهٖۤ اَحَدًاۙ(۲۶) اِلَّا مَنِ ارْتَضٰى مِنْ رَّسُوْلٍ

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Ghaib ka jaanne wala to apne ghaib par kisi ko muttali' nahi karta, siwaaye apne pasandeeda Rasoolon ke. (Tafseer-e-Saawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 59, 2/586.)

Ilm-e-ghaib se muta'alliq tafseeli maloomat ke liye A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ki kitaab “Ad-Daulah-tul-Makkiyyah bil-Maaddatil-Ghaibiyyah” (ilm-e-ghaib ke masale ke daleel ke saath tafseeli bayan) aur Fatawa Razawiyyah ki 29veen jild mein maujood in rasail ka mutaala'a karein: (1) “Izaahat-ul-Aib bi Saif-il-Ghaib” (ilm-e-ghaib ke masale se muta'alliq daleel aur badmazhabon ka rad), (2) “Khaalis-ul-I'tiqaad” (ilm-e-ghaib se muta'alliq 120 daleelon par mushtamil ek azeem kitaab).

## Ayat 6:60

`Source: tafseerId=50892; ayatId=855; surah=6; ayat=60; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْ یَتَوَفّٰىكُمْ بِالَّیْلِ وَ یَعْلَمُ مَا جَرَحْتُمْ بِالنَّهَارِ ثُمَّ یَبْعَثُكُمْ فِیْهِ لِیُقْضٰۤى اَجَلٌ مُّسَمًّىۚ-ثُمَّ اِلَیْهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ ثُمَّ یُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُوْنَ۠    (60)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Wahi hai jo raat ko tumhari rooh qabz kar leta hai aur jo kuchh tum din mein kamaate ho use jaanta hai. Phir tumhein din ke waqt uthaata hai taake muqarrarah muddat poori ho jaaye. Phir Us ki taraf tumhara lautna hai, phir Woh tumhein bata dega jo kuchh tum karte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْ یَتَوَفّٰىكُمْ بِالَّیْلِ: Aur Wahi hai jo raat ko tumhari roohein qabz kar leta hai.}** Pichhli aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ke kamaal-e-ilm ka bayan hua aur is aayat mein Allah Ta'ala ki kamaal-e-qudrat ka bayan kiya ja raha hai. Allah Ta'ala wahi hai jo raat ke waqt tumhari roohein qabz kar leta hai, tumhare ehsaas ki taaqat khatam karke tumhein murde ki tarah kar deta hai. Is se tum par neend musallat ho jaati hai aur tumhare ikhtiyaari tasarruf apni haalat par baaqi nahi rehte.

Phir Allah Ta'ala tumhein din ke waqt uthaata hai taake tumhari zindagi ki muqarrarah muddat poori ho jaaye aur tumhari umr apni inteha ko pahunch jaaye. Maut ke baad Aakhirat mein tumhara lautna Us hi ki taraf hai. Phir Woh tumhein un aamaal ki jaza dega jo tum din-raat kiya karte the.

Is aayat mein pehle neend laane aur us ke baad bedaar karne ka bayan hua. Is mein Qiyamat qayam hone aur maut ke baad dobara zinda kiye jaane par roshan daleel hai. Jo Rab roz sone ke waqt tum par ek tarah ki maut waarid karta hai, jis se tumhare hawaas aur un ke zaahiri kaam, jaise dekhna, sunna, bolna, chalna-phirna aur pakarna, sab ruk jaate hain, phir bedari ke waqt wahi Rab tamaam azaa ko un ke tasarrufaat de deta hai aur woh dekhna, sunna, bolna aur chalna-phirna shuru kar dete hain, to woh Rab makhlooq ko haqeeqi maut ke baad zindagi ke tasarrufaat ata karne par bhi qaadir hai.

## Ayat 6:61

`Source: tafseerId=50893; ayatId=856; surah=6; ayat=61; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ الْقَاهِرُ فَوْقَ عِبَادِهٖ وَ یُرْسِلُ عَلَیْكُمْ حَفَظَةًؕ-حَتّٰۤى اِذَا جَآءَ اَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ تَوَفَّتْهُ رُسُلُنَا وَ هُمْ لَا یُفَرِّطُوْنَ(61)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Wahi apne bandon par ghalib hai aur tum par nigehbaan bhejta hai, yahan tak ke jab tum mein kisi ko maut aati hai to Hamare farishte us ki rooh qabz karte hain aur woh koi kotahi nahi karte.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ هُوَ الْقَاهِرُ فَوْقَ عِبَادِهٖ: Aur Wahi apne bandon par ghalib hai.}** Yani Allah Ta'ala bandon ke tamaam maamlaat mein har tarah tasarruf karne ki qudrat rakhta hai. Wahi deta hai aur Wahi apni ata rokta hai. Wahi milaata hai aur Wahi juda karta hai. Wahi nafa aur nuqsaan pahunchata hai. Wahi izzat aur zillat deta hai. Wahi zindagi aur maut deta hai. Us ke faisle ko radd karne wala koi nahi aur Us ke siwa koi panaah ki jagah nahi. (Saawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 61, 2/588.) Ek aur maqam par Allah Ta'ala irshaad farmata hai:

’’ وَ اِنْ یَّمْسَسْكَ اللّٰهُ بِضُرٍّ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهٗۤ اِلَّا هُوَۚ-وَ اِنْ یُّرِدْكَ بِخَیْرٍ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضْلِهٖؕ-یُصِیْبُ بِهٖ مَنْ یَّشَآءُ مِنْ عِبَادِهٖؕ-وَ هُوَ الْغَفُوْرُ الرَّحِیْمُ‘‘(Yunus: 107)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur agar Allah tujhe koi takleef pahunchaye to Us ke siwa koi takleef door karne wala nahi aur agar Woh tere saath bhalaai ka iraada farmaye to Us ke fazl ko koi radd karne wala nahi. Apne bandon mein se jis ko chahta hai apna fazl pahunchata hai aur Wahi bakhshne wala, meherbaan hai.

**{وَ یُرْسِلُ عَلَیْكُمْ حَفَظَةً: Aur tum par nigehbaan bhejta hai.}** Is se muraad woh farishte hain jo Bani Adam ki neki aur badhi likhte rehte hain. Inhein Kiraaman Kaatibeen kehte hain. Har aadmi ke saath do farishte hote hain: ek daahni taraf aur ek baayen taraf. Daahni taraf ka farishta nekiyaan likhta hai aur baayen taraf ka farishta buraaiyaan likhta hai.

### Gunaah karne wale ghaur karein

Bandon ko chahiye ke ghaur karein aur buraaiyon aur gunaahon se bachein, kyunki har amal likha jaata hai. Roz-e-Qiyamat woh naama-e-a'maal tamaam makhlooq ke saamne padha jaayega. Gunaah kitni ruswaai ka sabab honge! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ panaah de. Isi ke pesh-e-nazar Imam Muhammad Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne zabaan ki hifaazat ke muta'alliq farmaya:

Hazrat Ata bin Abi Rabaah رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Tum se pehle log fazool kalaam ko naapasand karte the. Woh log Kitaabullah, Sunnat-e-Rasool, neki ki dawat dene, buraai se rokne aur apni aisi haajat jis ke baghair koi chaara na ho, ke ilawa har kalaam ko fazool samajhte the. Kya tum is baat ka inkaar karte ho ke tumhare daahne-baayen do muhafiz farishte Kiraaman Kaatibeen baithe hain?

’’ مَا یَلْفِظُ مِنْ قَوْلٍ اِلَّا لَدَیْهِ رَقِیْبٌ عَتِیْدٌ ‘‘(Qaaf: 18)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Koi baat woh zabaan se nahi nikaalta magar Us ke paas ek muhafiz tayyar baitha hota hai.

Kya tumhein is baat se haya nahi aati ke tumhara naama-e-a'maal jab khola jaayega to woh din bhar ki baaton se bhara hoga aur un mein aksar woh baatein hongi jin ka na tumhare deen se koi ta'alluq hoga na duniya se? Ek Sahabi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Mere saath koi shakhs baat karta hai. Use jawaab dena shadeed pyaas ke waqt thande paani se zyada meri khwahish hoti hai, lekin main is khauf se use jawaab nahi deta ke kahin yeh fazool kalaam na ho jaaye. (Ihya Ulum-id-Deen, Kitaab Aafaat-ul-Lisaan, Bayaan Azeem Khatar-ul-Lisaan wa Fazilat-us-Samt, Al-Aafat-us-Saaniyah: Fazool-ul-Kalaam, 3/141.) **[1]**

**{تَوَفَّتْهُ رُسُلُنَا: Hamare farishte us ki rooh qabz karte hain.}** In farishton se muraad ya to tanha Hazrat Malak-ul-Maut عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام hain, is surat mein jama ka lafz ta'zeem ke liye hai, ya Hazrat Malak-ul-Maut عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام aur woh farishte muraad hain jo un ke madadgaar hain. Jab kisi ki maut ka waqt aata hai to Hazrat Malak-ul-Maut عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام Allah Ta'ala ke hukm se apne madadgaaron ko us ki rooh qabz karne ka hukm dete hain. Jab rooh halq tak pahunchti hai to khud qabz farmate hain. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 61, 2/23.) In sab farishton ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke woh koi kotahi nahi karte. Yani hukm ki ta'meel mein un se kotahi nahi hoti, un ke amal mein susti aur taakhir raah nahi paati, balki woh apne faraaiz theek waqt par ada karte hain.

**[1]** Zabaan ki hifaazat se muta'alliq mazeed maloomat ke liye kitaab “Jannat ki Do Chaabiyan” (matboo'a Maktabat-ul-Madinah) ka mutaala'a bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 6:62

`Source: tafseerId=50894; ayatId=857; surah=6; ayat=62; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** ثُمَّ رُدُّوْۤا اِلَى اللّٰهِ مَوْلٰىهُمُ الْحَقِّؕ-اَلَا لَهُ الْحُكْمُ- وَ هُوَ اَسْرَعُ الْحٰسِبِیْنَ(62)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir unhein Allah ki taraf lautaaya jaayega jo un ka haqeeqi Maalik hai. Sun lo, hukm Us hi ka hai aur Woh sab se jald hisaab lene wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ثُمَّ رُدُّوْۤا اِلَى اللّٰهِ: Phir unhein Allah ki taraf lautaaya jaayega.}** Yani jab log marne ke baad dobara zinda kiye jaayenge to farishte unhein hisaab ki jagah mein us Allah Ta'ala ke hukm aur Us ki jaza ki taraf lautaayenge jo un ke tamaam maamlaat ka haqeeqi Maalik hai. Ae logo! Sun lo, Qiyamat ke din bandon ke darmiyaan Us hi ka faisla naafiz hoga. Kisi aur ka koi faisla kisi tarah naafiz nahi ho sakta aur Woh bahut kam muddat mein tamaam makhlooq ka hisaab lene wala hai. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 62, 3/46.)

### Hisaab hone se pehle apna muhaasaba kar lo

Allama Ismaeel Haqqi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is aayat ke tehat farmate hain: Jab marne ke baad dobara zinda kiya jaana aur a'maal ka hisaab hona hai, aur Allah Ta'ala tamaam makhlooq se un ke a'maal ka hisaab lega, to aqalmand insaan par laazim hai ke hisaab ke maamle mein poochh-gachh hone se pehle hi apne nafs ka muhaasaba kar le. Insaan Aakhirat ki raah mein taajir hai. Us ki umr us ka maal-o-mata hai. Us ka nafa apni zindagi ko ibaadat aur nek a'maal mein kharch karna hai, aur us ka nuqsaan gunaahon aur ma'aasi mein zindagi guzaarna hai. Us ka nafs is tijaarat mein us ka shareek hai. Nafs neki aur buraai dono kar sakta hai, lekin neki ke muqaable mein gunaahon aur nafsaanee khwahishon ki taraf zyada ma'il hota hai, is liye us ka muhaasaba karna intehaai zaroori hai. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 62, 3/46.) **[1]**

Imam Muhammad Ghazali رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Insaan ko chahiye ke raat sote waqt ek ghadi muqarrar kare taake woh apne nafs se is din ka saara hisaab le. Jis tarah tijaarat mein shareek shakhs se hisaab lete waqt nihayat ehtiyaat aur mubalagha kiya jaata hai, isi tarah apne nafs ka hisaab lete hue bahut ehtiyaat karni chahiye. Nafs bada makkaar aur hilaasaaz hai. Woh apni khwahish ko insaan ke saamne ita'at ki shakl mein pesh karta hai taake insaan use bhi nafa samajh le, halaanki woh nuqsaan hota hai. Insaan ko chahiye ke woh apne nafs se mubaahaat ka bhi hisaab le: Yeh tune kyun kiya? Kis ke liye kiya? Agar is mein koi aib dekhe to apne nafs ko us ka zimmedaar thehraaye.

Lekin afsos hai ke aaj insaan apne nafs se hisaab lene ke liye kitna ghafil hai. Agar insaan har gunaah par apne ghar mein ek patthar rakhta jaaye to thode din mein ghar pattharon se bhar jaaye. Agar Kiraaman Kaatibeen is insaan se likhne ki mazdoori maangte to us ke paas kuchh baaqi na rehta. Agar insaan kabhi ghaflat mein chand baar سُبْحَانَ اللہ padhta hai to tasbeeh haath mein lekar baith jaata hai aur kehta hai ke main ne yeh sau martaba padh liya, halaanki saara din behooda bakwaas karta phirta hai. Woh inhein shumaar nahi karta aur na hi shumaar karne ke liye koi cheez haath mein leta hai taake use maloom ho ke main ne saare din mein kitne gunaah kiye. Is surat-e-haal ke bawajood yeh sochna ke mere nekiyon ka palra bhaari ho jaayega, kitni be-aqli hai! Isi liye Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya: Apne a'maal tole jaane se pehle khud apne a'maal ka jaaiza le lo.

Apna muhaasaba karne mein Aap رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki kaifiyat yeh thi ke jab raat hoti to apne paon par durre lagaate aur kehte: Bata, tune aaj kya kiya? Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmate hain: Main ne Hazrat Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ko ek deewaar ke peechhe yeh kehte suna: Waah waah! Log tujhe Ameer-ul-Momineen kehte hain, lekin Khuda ki qasam! Tu Allah Ta'ala se nahi darta aur Us ke azaab mein mubtala hone ko tayyar rehta hai. (Keemiya-e-Sa'aadat, Rukn Chahaarum, Asl Shashum dar Muhaasaba wa Muraaqaba, Maqaam سوم dar Muhaasabaat, 2/891-892.)

**[1]** A'maal ke muhaasabe ki fazeelat, targheeb aur is se muta'alliq deegar maloomat ke liye kitaab “Fikr-e-Madinah” (matboo'a Maktabat-ul-Madinah) ka mutaala'a karein.

## Ayat 6:63

`Source: tafseerId=50895; ayatId=858; surah=6; ayat=63; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** قُلْ مَنْ یُّنَجِّیْكُمْ مِّنْ ظُلُمٰتِ الْبَرِّ وَ الْبَحْرِ تَدْعُوْنَهٗ تَضَرُّعًا وَّ خُفْیَةًۚ-لَىٕنْ اَنْجٰىنَا مِنْ هٰذِهٖ لَنَكُوْنَنَّ مِنَ الشّٰكِرِیْنَ(63)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmaao: Woh kaun hai jo tumhein khushki aur samandar ki holnaakiyon se najaat deta hai? Tum Use gidgida kar aur posheeda taur par pukaarte ho aur kehte ho ke agar Woh humein is se najaat de de to hum zaroor shukr guzaaron mein se ho jaayenge.

### Musalmanon ki baahmi ladaai ka ek sabab

Yaad rahe ke yeh Allah Ta'ala ki Sunnat-e-Jaariyah hai ke Woh apni na-farmaani ki saza mein Musalmanon ko kaafiron se aur kaafiron ko Musalmanon se ladwa deta hai. Isi tarah kaafiron ko kaafiron se aur Musalmanon ko Musalmanon se ladwa deta hai, jaisa ke hamare zamaane mein is ki misaalein dekhi ja sakti hain.

Sahih Bukhari ki hadees mein hai ke jab aayat ka yeh hissa naazil hua: “Woh qaadir hai ke tum par tumhare upar se azaab bheje,” to Sarkar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَ اٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ne farmaya: Main Teri hi panaah maangta hoon. Jab yeh hissa naazil hua: “Ya tumhare paon ke neeche se,” to farmaya: Main Teri hi panaah maangta hoon. Aur jab yeh hissa naazil hua: “Ya tumhein mukhtalif giroh bana kar lada de aur ek ko doosre ki sakhti chakhaye,” to farmaya: Yeh aasaan hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, Baab Qul Huwal-Qaadiru ala an yab'asa alaikum..., 3/221, hadees: 4628.)

Sahih Muslim ki hadees-e-shareef mein hai ke ek din Rahmat-e-Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَسَلَّمَ ne Masjid-e-Bani Muawiyah mein do rak'at namaaz ada farmayi aur us ke baad lambi dua ki. Phir Sahaba رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم ki taraf mutawajjah hokar farmaya: Main ne apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ se teen sawaal kiye, un mein se sirf do qabool hue. Ek sawaal yeh tha ke Meri ummat ko qaht-e-aam se halaak na farmaye; yeh qabool hua. Doosra yeh tha ke unhein gharq karke azaab na de; yeh bhi qabool hua. Teesra sawaal yeh tha ke un mein baahmi jang-o-jidaal na ho; yeh qabool nahi hua. (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Fitan wa Ashraat-us-Saa'ah, Baab Halaak Haazihil-Ummat Ba'zuhum Bi-Ba'z, safha 1544, hadees: 20 (2890).)

### Gunaahon par israar na kiya jaaye

Is se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ne Quran-e-Majeed mein kaafiron aur gunaahgaron ke liye azaabon ki jo khabrein di hain, un ka apne waqt par waqea hona yaqini hai. Yeh elaan sunne ke baad bhi kufr par ade rehna aur gunaahon mein mashghool rehna intehaai nadaani hai. Is liye har shakhs ko chahiye ke aqalmandi se kaam le, kufr aur gunaahon par israar chhor de, Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein sachchi tauba kare, gunaahon ki maghfirat maange aur khud ko halaakat se bacha le.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwaayat hai, Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Gunaahon par israar karne walon ke liye halaakat hai, jo apne gunaahon par qaayam hain aur is ke bawajood tauba-o-istighfaar nahi karte, halaanki woh jaante hain ke un ka yeh kaam gunaah hai.” (Shu'ab-ul-Imaan, As-Saabi'u wal-Arba'una min Shu'ab-il-Imaan, Fasl fit-Tab'i alal-Qalb, 5/449, hadees: 7236.) **[1]**

**[1]** Gunaahon se bachne aur nekiyon ka jazba paane ke liye Dawat-e-Islami ke saath wabastagi bahut faaidemand hai.

### Badmazhabon ki mehfilon mein jaane aur un ki taqreerein sunne ka shar'i hukm

Is aayat se maloom hua ke bad-deen logon ki jis majlis mein deen ka ehteraam na kiya jaata ho, Musalman ka wahan baithna jaaiz nahi. Is se yeh bhi saabit hua ke kaafiron aur bad-deen logon ke woh jalse jin mein deen ke khilaaf taqreerein hoti hain, un mein jaana aur shirkat karna jaaiz nahi.

Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai, Rasoolullah ﷺ ne irshaad farmaya: “Aakhri zamaane mein jhoote Dajjaal honge jo tumhare paas aisi ahadees laayenge jo na tum ne suni hongi aur na tumhare baap-dada ne. Un se apne aap ko door rakho aur unhein apne se door rakho, kahin woh tumhein gumraah na kar dein aur fitne mein na daal dein.” (Muslim, Baab-un-Nahyi anir-Riwaayati anid-Du'afaa wal-Ihtiyaati fi Tahammuliha, safha 9, hadees: 7 (7).)

Albatta jo Ulama in badmazhabon ka rad karne ke liye jaate hain, woh is hukm mein shaamil nahi. Yaad rahe ke badmazhabon ki mehfil mein jaana aur un ki taqreer sunna najaaiz-o-haraam hai. Yeh apne aap ko badmazhabi aur gumraahi ke saamne pesh karna hai. Un ki taqreerein Quranic aayaat ya ahadees-e-mubarakah par mabni hon, tab bhi achhi baatein chunne ke khayaal se unhein sunna hargiz jaaiz nahi. Aksar aisa hota hai ke gumraah shakhs Quran-o-hadees ki sharh ki aad mein apni badmazhabi ki baat bhi mila deta hai. Sunne wale ke zehan mein woh baat baith jaati hai aur dil mein ghar kar leti hai. Isi wajah se gumraah aur bad-deen ki taqreer sunne wala aam taur par khud bhi gumraah ho jaata hai.

Hamare aslaaf apne imaan ke baare mein bahut ehtiyaat karte the. Aqeedah mein nihayat mazboot hone ke bawajood woh kisi badmazhab ki baat sunna pasand nahi karte the, chahe woh sau baar yaqeen dilaata ke main sirf Quran-o-hadees bayan karunga. A'la Hazrat, Imam-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Shah Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne aslaaf ka amal naqal karte hue farmaya: “Sayyiduna Sa'eed bin Jubair, Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ke shagird, ko raaste mein ek badmazhab mila. Us ne kaha: Main kuchh arz karna chahta hoon. Aap ne farmaya: Main sunna nahi chahta. Us ne arz ki: Sirf ek kalimah. Aap ne apna angutha chhoti ungli ke sire par rakh kar farmaya: ‘وَ لَا نِصْفَ کَلِمَۃٍ’ Aadha lafz bhi nahi. Logon ne poochha: Is ki wajah kya hai? Farmaya: Yeh unhi mein se hai, yani gumraahon mein se hai.”

Imam Muhammad bin Seereen, Hazrat Anas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke shagird, ke paas do badmazhab aaye. Unhone arz ki: Hum aap ko Allah ki Kitaab ki kuchh aayaat suna dein. Aap ne farmaya: Main sunna nahi chahta. Unhone arz ki: Nabi ﷺ ki kuchh ahadees suna dein. Aap ne farmaya: Main sunna nahi chahta. Unhone israar kiya to farmaya: Tum dono chale jao ya main chala jaata hoon. Aakhir woh mayoos hokar chale gaye. Logon ne arz ki: Imam! Agar woh kuchh aayat ya hadees suna dete to aap ka kya nuqsaan tha? Farmaya: Mujhe khauf tha ke woh aayaat aur ahadees ke saath apni koi taweel mila dein aur woh mere dil mein baith jaaye, phir main halaak ho jaaun.

Phir Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne farmaya: “A'immah ko to yeh khauf hai aur aaj awaam ko yeh jur'at hai. وَلَا حَوْلَ وَ لَا قُوَّۃَ اِلَّا بِاللہْ. Dekho! Amaan ki raah wahi hai jo tumhein tumhare pyaare Nabi ﷺ ne bataai: ‘اِیَّاکُمْ وَ اِیَّا ھُمْ لَا یُضِلُّوْنَکُمْ وَلَا یَفْتِنُوْنَکُمْ’ Un se door raho aur unhein apne se door karo, kahin woh tumhein gumraah na kar dein aur fitne mein na daal dein. Naja’at ki raah wahi hai jo tumhare Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne bataai: ‘فَلَا تَقْعُدْ بَعْدَ الذِّكْرٰى مَعَ الْقَوْمِ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ’ Yaad aane ke baad zaalimon ke paas na baitho.”

Agar bhool se un mein se kisi ke paas baith gaye ho to yaad aate hi foran uth jao. (Fataawa Razawiyyah, 15/106-107, mulakhkhasan.)

### Gunaahon par israar kufr ki haalat mein wafaat ka sabab ban sakta hai

Allama Ismaeel Haqqi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is aayat ke tehat farmate hain: “Yaad rakhein ke jis tarah Allah Ta'ala ki aayaat ka inkaar karna aur un ka mazaaq udaana kufr hai aur kufr ki saza Jahannam ka hamesha ka dardnaak azaab hai, isi tarah kisi Musalman ka gunaahon par israar karna bhi aisa amal hai jis se us ki wafaat kufr ki haalat mein ho sakti hai.

Hazrat Abu Ishaq Fazaari رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Ek shakhs aksar hamare paas baitha karta aur apna aadha chehra dhaanp kar rakhta tha. Ek din main ne us se kaha: Tum hamare paas bahut baithte ho aur apna aadha chehra dhaanp kar rakhte ho, is ki wajah batao. Us ne kaha: Main kafan-chor tha. Ek din ek aurat ko dafn kiya gaya. Main us ki qabr par aaya. Jab main ne qabr khod kar kafan kheenchna chaha to us ne apna haath utha kar mere chehre par tamaacha maara. Phir us shakhs ne apna chehra dikhaaya to us par paanch ungliyon ke nishaan the. Main ne poochha: Is ke baad kya hua? Us ne kaha: Main ne us ka kafan chhor diya, qabr band karke us par mitti daal di aur dil mein pakka iraada kar liya ke jab tak zinda rahunga kisi ki qabr nahi khodunga.

Hazrat Abu Ishaq Fazaari رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain: Main ne yeh waaqia Imam Awzaa'i رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ko likh kar bheja. Unhone mujhe likha: Is se poochho ke jin Musalmanon ko dafn kiya gaya tha, kya un ke chehre qiblah ki taraf the? Main ne kafan-chor se poochha to us ne jawaab diya: Un mein se zyada tar logon ke chehre qiblah se phire hue the. Main ne yeh jawaab Imam Awzaa'i رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ko likh kar bheja. Unhone mujhe ek tehreer bheji jis par teen martaba “اِنَّا لِلّٰہِ وَاِنَّا اِلَیْہِ رَاجِعُونْ” likha tha aur saath likha tha: “Jis ka chehra qiblah se phira hua tha, us ki wafaat Islam ke deen par nahi hui. Tum Allah Ta'ala se us ke afw, maghfirat aur Us ki raza talab karo.” (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 70, 3/51.)

### Aazar Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke chacha the ya baap

Aayat mein Aazar ke liye “اَبْ” ka lafz zikr kiya gaya hai. Is ka ek ma'ni baap hai aur doosra ma'ni chacha. Yahan is se muraad chacha hai, jaisa ke Qaamoos mein hai: Aazar Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke chacha ka naam hai. (Al-Qaamoos-ul-Muheet, Baab-ur-Ra, Fasl-ul-Hamzah, 1/491, tehat-ul-lafz: al-Aazar.)

Imam Jalaal-ud-Deen Suyooti رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne bhi “Masaalik-ul-Hunafa” mein isi tarah likha hai. Chacha ko baap kehna tamam mulkon mein aam hai, khaas taur par Arab mein. Quran aur hadees mein bhi chacha ko baap kehne ki misaal milti hai. Quran-e-Kareem mein hai:

“نَعْبُدُ اِلٰهَكَ وَ اِلٰهَ اٰبَآىٕكَ اِبْرٰهٖمَ وَ اِسْمٰعِیْلَ وَ اِسْحٰقَ اِلٰهًا وَّاحِدًا”(Baqarah: 133)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Hum aap ke ma'bood aur aap ke aaba-o-ajdaad Ibrahim, Isma'eel aur Ishaaq ke ma'bood ki ibaadat kareinge jo ek ma'bood hai.

Is aayat mein Hazrat Isma'eel عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko Hazrat Ya'qoob عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke aaba mein zikr kiya gaya hai, halaanke Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Hazrat Ya'qoob عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke chacha hain.

Hadees Shareef mein hai ke Nabi-e-Akram ﷺ ne irshaad farmaaya: “رُدُّوْاعَلَیَّ اَبِیْ” Mere baap ko mere paas lauta do. (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaibah, Kitaab-ul-Maghaazi, Hadees-e-Fath-e-Makkah, 8/530, hadees: 3.) Yahan “اَبِیْ” se Hazrat Abbas رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ muraad hain jo Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ke chacha hain.

## Ayat 6:75

## Ayat 6:76

`Source: tafseerId=50906; ayatId=871; surah=6; ayat=76; para=7; volume=3`

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir jab un par raat ka andhera chhaaya to ek taara dekha. Farmaaya: Kya ise mera Rab thaharaate ho? Phir jab woh doob gaya to farmaaya: Main doobne walon ko pasand nahi karta.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَلَمَّا جَنَّ عَلَیْهِ الَّیْلُ: To jab un par raat ka andhera chhaaya.}** Mufassireen aur tareekh likhne walon ka bayan hai ke Namrood bin Kan'aan bahut jabr karne wala baadshah tha. ### Islami aqeeda aur Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka waqia

Yeh baat yaqini hai ke Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام har haal mein ma'soom hote hain aur woh ibtida hi se har waqt Allah Ta'ala ki ma'rifat se waaqif hote hain. Is aqeede ko saamne rakh kar is aayat aur is ke baad wali do aayaat ki tafseer samajhne ke liye Quran-e-Paak mein bayan kiya gaya waqia tafseel se padhiye.

Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne ibtida hi se tauheed ki himaayat aur kufriya aqeede rad karna shuru farma diya tha. Phir jab Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne raat ke waqt ek suraakh se Zuhrah ya Mushtari sitara dekha to logon ke saamne Allah Ta'ala ki tauheed ki daleel bayan karna shuru ki. Us zamaane ke log buton aur sitaron ki pooja karte the. Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne nihayat khoobsurat aur dil-nasheen andaaz mein unhein ghaur-o-fikr ki taraf rehnumaayi ki.

Is se woh is nateeje par pahunch sakte the ke poori duniya pehle maujood na thi, phir wajood mein aayi aur aakhir khatam hone wali hai. Is liye yeh ma'bood nahi ho sakti. Balki poori duniya khud use wajood mein laane wali Zaat ki mohtaaj hai, jis ki qudrat aur ikhtiyaar se is mein tabdeeliyan hoti rehti hain.

Chunanche pehle Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne sitara dekha to farmaaya: Kya tum ise mera Rab kehte ho? Phir jab woh doob gaya to farmaaya: Main doobne walon ko pasand nahi karta. Yani jis mein aisi tabdeeliyan hoti hain woh Khuda nahi ho sakta.

Is ke baad Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne chaand ko chamakta hua dekha to farmaaya: Kya tum ise mera Rab kehte ho? Phir jab woh doob gaya to farmaaya: Agar mere Rab ne mujhe hidaayat na di hoti to main bhi gumraah logon mein se hota. Is mein un logon ko tanbeeh hai jo chaand ko ma'bood maante the. Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne unhein gumraah qarar diya aur khud ko hidaayat par bataya. Is se bhi maloom hua ke Aap ki yeh baatein un ka rad karne ke liye thin.

Chaand ke ma'bood na hone par bhi Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne yahi daleel di ke us ka ek haal se doosre haal ki taraf muntaqil hona fana hone ki daleel hai. Phir Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne sooraj ko jagmagaata hua dekha to farmaaya: Kya tum ise mera Rab kehte ho? Yeh to in sab se bada hai. Phir jab woh bhi doob gaya to farmaaya: Ae meri qaum! Main un cheezon se bezaar hoon jinhein tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek thaharaate ho.

Is tarah Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne saabit kar diya ke sitaron mein chhote se bade tak koi bhi Rab hone ki salahiyat nahi rakhta. Un ka ma'bood hona baatil hai. Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne apni qaum ke shirk se bezaari ka izhaar kar diya aur is ke baad haq deen ka bayan farmaaya jo agli aayaat mein aa raha hai.

Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke sitare, chaand aur sooraj ke baare mein farmaan logon ko samjhaane ke liye the, apne baare mein nahi the. Is ki bahut wazeh daleel yeh hai ke jab Aap عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne sitare, chaand aur sooraj ke baare mein yeh farmaaya to kya Aap ne is se pehle din aur raat ka farq aur sooraj, chaand ke ghuroob hone ko kabhi nahi dekha tha? Aisa hargiz nahi ho sakta. Is se maloom hua ke sooraj, chaand aur sitare ke hawale se Aap ka kalaam sirf qaum ko samjhaane ke liye tha. Is baat ka zyada sareeh bayan aayat number 83 mein bhi maujood hai.

## Ayat 6:79

`Source: tafseerId=50907; ayatId=874; surah=6; ayat=79; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اِنِّیْ وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِیَ لِلَّذِیْ فَطَرَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضَ حَنِیْفًا وَّ مَاۤ اَنَا مِنَ الْمُشْرِكِیْنَۚ   (79)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Maine har baatil se juda hokar apna munh Us ki taraf kiya jis ne aasman aur zameen banaaye aur main mushrikon mein se nahi hoon.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنِّیْ وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِیَ: Maine apna munh Us ki taraf kiya.}** Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne jhoote ma'boodon se bezaari ka izhaar karne ke baad apne aqeede aur haq deen ka elaan farma diya. Aap ne farmaaya: Maine har baatil se juda hokar us Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki baargaah ki taraf apna munh kiya jis ne aasman aur zameen banaaye aur main mushrikon mein se nahi hoon. Yani Islam ke siwa tamaam deeno se alag hokar main Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke saamne jhukne wala hoon.

### Haneef ke ma'ni

Is aayat mein Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne apne liye Haneef ka lafz istemaal farmaaya. Haneef ke ma'ni hain: Tamaam jhoote deeno se paak aur har baatil se juda.

### Haq deen ke mazboot hone ki surat

Is se maloom hua ke haq deen ka qiyaam aur mazbooti us waqt hi ho sakti hai jab tamaam baatil deeno se bezaari ho aur haq deen par mazbooti ho. Deen ke maamle mein dheela-pan dikhaane, sab ko apni apni jagah durust maanne aur sab mazaahib mein ittehaad paida karne ki koshish se haq deen mazboot nahi ho sakta.

### Namaaz se pehle padha jaane wala wazifa

Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ namaaz shuru karne se pehle ek wazifa padha karte the. Is ke baare mein Hazrat Ali-ul-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللہ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم farmaate hain: Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ jab namaaz shuru karne ke liye khade hote to yeh padha karte the:

“وَجَّہْتُ وَجْہِیَ لِلَّذِیْ فَطَرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْاَرْضَحَنِیْفًا وَمَا اَنَا مِنَ الْمُشْرِکِیْنَ اِنَّ صَلَا تِیْ وَنُسُکِیْ وَمَحْیَایَ وَمَمَاتِیْ لِلّٰہِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِیْنَ لَا شَرِیْکَ لَہُ وَبِذٰلِکَ اُمِرْتُ وَاَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِیْنََ”

Maine har baatil se juda hokar apna munh Us ki taraf kiya jis ne aasman aur zameen banaaye aur main mushrikon mein se nahi hoon. Beshak meri namaaz, meri qurbaaniyan, mera jeena aur mera marna sab Allah ke liye hai jo saare jahaanon ka Rab hai. Us ka koi shareek nahi. Isi ka mujhe hukm diya gaya hai aur main Musalman hoon. (Muslim, Kitaab Salaat-ul-Musaafireen wa Qasruha, Baab-ud-Dua fi Salaatil-Lail wa Qiyaamihi, s. 390, hadees: 201 (771).)

## Ayat 6:80

`Source: tafseerId=50908; ayatId=875; surah=6; ayat=80; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ حَآجَّهٗ قَوْمُهٗؕ-قَالَ اَتُحَآجُّوْٓنِّیْ فِی اللّٰهِ وَ قَدْ هَدٰىنِؕ-وَ لَاۤ اَخَافُ مَا تُشْرِكُوْنَ بِهٖۤ اِلَّاۤ اَنْ یَّشَآءَ رَبِّیْ شَیْــٴًـاؕ-وَسِعَ رَبِّیْ كُلَّ شَیْءٍ عِلْمًاؕ-اَفَلَا تَتَذَكَّرُوْنَ(80)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur un ki qaum un se jhagarne lagi. Ibrahim ne farmaaya: Kya tum Allah ke baare mein mujh se jhagadte ho, haalanke Woh mujhe hidaayat ata farma chuka hai? Aur mujhe un ka koi dar nahi jinhein tum shareek bataate ho, magar yeh ke mera Rab koi baat chaahe. Mere Rab ka ilm har cheez ko muheet hai. To kya tum naseehat nahi maante?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ حَآجَّهٗ قَوْمُهٗ: Aur un ki qaum un se jhagarne lagi.}** Jab Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne haq ka elaan farmaaya, jhoote ma'boodon ka rad kiya aur Allah Ta'ala ki tauheed bayan karna shuru ki to qaum Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se jhagarne lagi.

Woh kehne lage: Ae Ibrahim! Buton se daro aur unhein bura kehne se khauf karo. Kahin aap ko koi nuqsaan na pahunch jaaye. Is ke jawaab mein Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne farmaaya: Kya tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke baare mein mujh se jhagar rahe ho? Haalanke Woh mujhe apni tauheed aur ma'rifat ki hidaayat ata farma chuka hai. Mujhe un buton ka koi dar nahi jinhein tum Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek bataate ho, kyunki woh be-jaan hain. Na nuqsaan de sakte hain aur na nafa pahuncha sakte hain. Un se kaisa darna?

Mujhe koi nuqsaan nahi pahunch sakta, siwaaye is ke ke mera Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ koi baat chaahe to woh ho sakti hai, kyunki mera Rab Qadir-e-Mutlaq hai. Yeh nahi ke tumhare buton ke chaahne se kuchh ho jaaye. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 80, 2/31-32; Madaarik, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 80, s. 330, multaqatan.)

SubhanAllah! Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne aise khatarnak mauqe par bhi imaan nahi chhupaaya, balki apne imaan ka elaan farma diya. Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke kisi Nabi عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke dil mein makhlooq ki aisi haibat nahi aa sakti jo unhein faraaiz ada karne se rok de.

## Ayat 6:81

`Source: tafseerId=50909; ayatId=876; surah=6; ayat=81; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ كَیْفَ اَخَافُ مَاۤ اَشْرَكْتُمْ وَ لَا تَخَافُوْنَ اَنَّكُمْ اَشْرَكْتُمْ بِاللّٰهِ مَا لَمْ یُنَزِّلْ بِهٖ عَلَیْكُمْ سُلْطٰنًاؕ-فَاَیُّ الْفَرِیْقَیْنِ اَحَقُّ بِالْاَمْنِۚ-اِنْ كُنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُوْنَﭥ(81)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur main tumhare shareekon se kyun daroon? Aur tum is baat se nahi darte ke tum ne Allah ka shareek us ko thaharaaya jis ki koi daleel Allah ne tum par nahi utaari. To dono girohon mein aman ka zyaada haqdaar kaun hai? Agar tum jaante ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ كَیْفَ اَخَافُ مَاۤ اَشْرَكْتُمْ: Aur main tumhare shareekon se kyun daroon?}** Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne mazeed farmaaya: Main tumhare shareekon se kyun daroon, jo bejaan, jamadaat aur bilkul aajiz aur be-bas hain? Mujhe daraane ke bajaaye tumhein darna chahiye, kyunki tum ne in buton ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek thaharaaya hai aur in ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke shareek hone ki tumhare paas koi daleel nahi.

Is baat ko saamne rakh kar ghaur karo ke aman ka mustahiq kaun hai: woh momin jis ke paas apne aqeede ki haqqaniyat ke daleelein hain, ya woh mushrik jis ke paas apne aqeede ki koi ma'qul aur qaabil-e-qabool daleel nahi? Yaqeenan aman ka mustahiq momin hi hai.

## Ayat 6:82

Kya tum ne Hazrat Luqman رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ki woh naseehat nahi suni jo unhone apne bete se kahi thi: Ae mere bete! ## Ayat 6:83

`Source: tafseerId=50911; ayatId=878; surah=6; ayat=83; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ تِلْكَ حُجَّتُنَاۤ اٰتَیْنٰهَاۤ اِبْرٰهِیْمَ عَلٰى قَوْمِهٖؕ-نَرْفَعُ دَرَجٰتٍ مَّنْ نَّشَآءُؕ-اِنَّ رَبَّكَ حَكِیْمٌ عَلِیْمٌ(83)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur yeh hamari mazboot daleel hai jo hum ne Ibrahim ko us ki qaum ke muqaable mein ata farmaayi. Hum jis ke chaahte hain darajaat buland kar dete hain. Beshak tumhara Rab hikmat wala, ilm wala hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ تِلْكَ حُجَّتُنَا: Aur yeh hamari mazboot daleel hai.}** Is rukoo mein Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki shaan aur doosre Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki azmat bayan ki gayi hai. Is poore bayan ka maqsad tamaam Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke Aaqa aur Imam-ul-Anbiya ﷺ ki taskeen, taleem aur tarbiyat hai, jaisa ke rukoo ke aakhir mein bhi bayan hoga.

Is se maloom hua ke Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka kasrat se zikr karna aur mehfilon aur majlison ko un ke zikr-e-paak se sajaana Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ko bahut pasand hai. Yeh imaan ki taaqat aur aqeeda-e-tauheed ko mazboot karne ka behtareen zariya hai.

Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne apni qaum ke saamne sooraj, chaand aur sitaron ke zariye jo daleelein bayan farmaayin, woh sab Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne Aap ko ata farmaayi thin. Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka sooraj, chaand waghera ko Rab kehna, ma'azAllah shirk ke taur par nahi tha, balki qaum ke saamne daleel pesh karne ke liye tha, kyunki Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne farmaaya ke yeh baatein hum ne unhein daleel ke taur par ata ki thin.

**{نَرْفَعُ دَرَجٰتٍ: Hum darajaat buland karte hain.}** Allah Ta'ala ne farmaaya ke hum jis ke chaahte hain us ke ilm, aqal, samajh aur fazeelat ke zariye darajaat buland kar dete hain. Jaisa ke Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke duniya mein ilm, hikmat aur nubuwwat ke zariye aur aakhirat mein qurb aur sawaab ke zariye darajaat buland farmaaye.

## Ayat 6:84

`Source: tafseerId=50912; ayatId=879; surah=6; ayat=84; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ وَهَبْنَا لَهٗۤ اِسْحٰقَ وَ یَعْقُوْبَؕ-كُلًّا هَدَیْنَاۚ-وَ نُوْحًا هَدَیْنَا مِنْ قَبْلُ وَ مِنْ ذُرِّیَّتِهٖ دَاوٗدَ وَ سُلَیْمٰنَ وَ اَیُّوْبَ وَ یُوْسُفَ وَ مُوْسٰى وَ هٰرُوْنَؕ-وَ كَذٰلِكَ نَجْزِی الْمُحْسِنِیْنَۙ   (84)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur hum ne unhein Ishaq aur Ya'qub ata kiye. In sab ko hum ne hidaayat di aur in se pehle Nuh ko hidaayat di aur us ki aulaad mein se Daood aur Sulaiman aur Ayyub aur Yusuf aur Musa aur Haroon ko hidaayat ata farmaayi aur aisa hi hum nek logon ko badla dete hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ وَهَبْنَا لَهٗ: Aur hum ne unhein ata farmaaye.}** Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se pehle Hazrat Nooh عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki hidaayat ka zikr kiya gaya aur is ke saath Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad-e-mubaarak ka zikr bhi kiya gaya. Aayat mein jin buzurgon ke naam bayan hue, woh sab Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad mein se the aur sab Nabi hue.

### Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka maqam

Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko yeh maqam aur martaba ata farmaaya ke Aap ke baad jitne bhi Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام mab'ous hue, sab Aap hi ki aulaad se the. Allah Ta'ala irshaad farmaata hai:

“وَ جَعَلْنَا فِیْ ذُرِّیَّتِهِ النُّبُوَّةَ وَ الْكِتٰبَ” (Ankaboot: 27)

Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan: Aur hum ne us ki aulaad mein nubuwwat aur kitaab rakhi.

Tafseer-e-Baghawi aur Tafseer-e-Khaazin mein hai: “یُقَالُ اِنَّ اللہَ لَمْ یَبْعَثْ نَبِیًّا بَعْدَ اِبْرَاہِیْمَ اِلَّا مِنْ نَسْلِہٖ” Yani kaha jaata hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baad jo Nabi mab'ous farmaaya, woh un ki nasl se tha. (Baghawi, Al-Ankaboot, tehat-ul-aayat: 27, 3/399-400; Khaazin, Al-Ankaboot, tehat-ul-aayat: 27, 3/449.)

Tafseer-e-Qurtubi mein hai: “لَمْ یَبْعَثِ اللہُ نَبِیًّا مِنْ بَعْدِ اِبْرَاہِیْمَ اِلَّا مِنْ صُلْبِہٖ” Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baad tamaam Ambiya un ke sulb se mab'ous farmaaye. (Qurtubi, Al-Ankaboot, tehat-ul-aayat: 27, 7/255, al-juz' us-saalis ashar.)

Tafseer-e-Jalaalain mein hai: “فَکُلُّ الْانبیاءِ بَعَدَ اِبْرَاہِیْمَ مِنْ ذُرِّیَّتِہٖ” Pas Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baad tamaam Ambiya un ki aulaad mein se the. (Jalaalain ma'a Saawi, Al-Ankaboot, tehat-ul-aayat: 27, 4/1561.)

Yaad rahe ke Surah Hadeed ki aayat number 26 mein hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Nooh aur Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام dono ki aulaad mein nubuwwat rakhi. Is ki tafseer mein Abu Hayyan Muhammad bin Yusuf Andalusi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmaate hain: Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام kyunki Hazrat Nooh عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad mein se hain, is liye sharf-e-nubuwwat ka in dono ki aulaad mein hona durust hua. (Al-Bahr-ul-Muheet, Al-Hadeed, tehat-ul-aayat: 26, 8/226.) Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Qadiani Nabi hargiz nahi, kyunki agar Qadiani Nabi hota to Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki aulaad mein hota.

## Ayat 6:86

`Source: tafseerId=50913; ayatId=881; surah=6; ayat=86; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ اِسْمٰعِیْلَ وَ الْیَسَعَ وَ یُوْنُسَ وَ لُوْطًاؕ-وَ كُلًّا فَضَّلْنَا عَلَى الْعٰلَمِیْنَۙ    (86)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Ismaeel aur Yasa aur Yunus aur Loot ko (hidaayat di) aur hum ne sab ko tamaam jahan walon par fazeelat ata farmaayi.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِسْمٰعِیْلَ وَ الْیَسَعَ: Aur Ismaeel aur Yasa.}** Is aayat aur is se upar wali do aayaat mein Allah Ta'ala ne athaarah Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr farmaaya. Aayat mein in ke naam jis tarteeb se aaye hain, woh na zamane ke aitbaar se hai aur na fazeelat ke aitbaar se. Lekin jis shaan se Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke naam zikr kiye gaye hain, us mein ek ajeeb bareek baat hai. Allah Ta'ala ne Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke har giroh ko ek khaas karam aur fazeelat ke saath mumtaaz farmaaya.

Misal ke taur par, pehle Hazrat Nooh, Hazrat Ibrahim, Hazrat Ishaq aur Hazrat Ya'qub عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr kiya gaya, kyunki yeh Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke asal buzurg hain. In ki aulaad mein bahut se Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام hue, jin ka nasab inhi ki taraf laut-ta hai.

Phir nubuwwat ke baad buland maqamon aur martabon mein se mulk, ikhtiyaar, saltanat aur qudrat ka martaba hai. Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Daood aur Hazrat Sulaiman عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko is ka bahut bada hissa ata farmaaya. Is martabe ka zyaada ta'alluq shukr ke maqam se hai.

Is ke baad Hazrat Ayyub عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr hai, kyunki buland martabon mein museebat aur aazmaish par sabr karna bhi hai. Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Ayyub عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko is martabe ke saath mumtaaz farmaaya. Phir mulk aur sabr dono martabe Hazrat Yusuf عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko ata kiye gaye. Aap عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne sakhti aur museebat par muddat tak sabr kiya, phir Allah Ta'ala ne nubuwwat ke saath Misr ki saltanat bhi ata farmaayi.

Phir Hazrat Moosa aur Hazrat Haroon عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr hai, kyunki mojizaat ki kasrat aur daleelon aur burhaanon ki quwwat bhi aham martabon mein se hai. Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Moosa aur Hazrat Haroon عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko is fazeelat se nawaza.

Phir duniya se be-raghbati aur duniya ko chhor dena bhi aham martabon mein se hai. Hazrat Zakariya, Hazrat Yahya, Hazrat Isa aur Hazrat Ilyas عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko is fazeelat ke saath khaas farmaaya gaya. In buzurgon ke baad Allah Ta'ala ne un Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr farmaaya jin ke na pairavi karne wale baqi rahe aur na un ki shariat baqi rahi, jaise Hazrat Ismaeel, Hazrat Yasa, Hazrat Yunus aur Hazrat Loot عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 86, 2/33.)

Is tarah Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr farmaane mein un ki karaamat aur khaas khoobiyon ki ek ajeeb bareek baat nazar aati hai.

**{وَ كُلًّا فَضَّلْنَا عَلَى الْعٰلَمِیْنَ: Aur hum ne sab ko tamaam jahan walon par fazeelat ata farmaayi.}** Is aayat mein daleel hai ke Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام farishton se afzal hain. Aalam yani jahan mein Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke siwa tamaam makhlooq daakhil hai, is liye farishte bhi is mein daakhil hain. Jab Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko tamaam jahan walon par fazeelat di gayi to farishton par bhi un ki fazeelat saabit ho gayi. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 86, 2/33.)

## Ayat 6:88

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{ذٰلِكَ هُدَى اللّٰهِ: Yeh Allah ki hidaayat hai.}** Yahan hidaayat se muraad is baat ki pehchaan hai ke Allah Ta'ala Wahid-o-Yakta hai aur us ka koi shareek nahi. Jab buland fazeelat aur martabe wale Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke baare mein Allah Ta'ala ne farma diya ke agar woh bhi shirk karte to un ke tamaam aamaal zaroor zaya ho jaate, to doosre logon ka haal kya hoga? (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 88, 3/62.)

## Ayat 6:89

`Source: tafseerId=50915; ayatId=884; surah=6; ayat=89; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اُولٰٓىٕكَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰتَیْنٰهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ وَ الْحُكْمَ وَ النُّبُوَّةَۚ-فَاِنْ یَّكْفُرْ بِهَا هٰۤؤُلَآءِ فَقَدْ وَ كَّلْنَا بِهَا قَوْمًا لَّیْسُوْا بِهَا بِكٰفِرِیْنَ(89)

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اُولٰٓىٕكَ: Yahi woh hastiyan hain.}** Jin Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka zikr kiya gaya, yahi woh buzurg hastiyan hain jinhein Allah Ta'ala ne kitaab, hikmat aur nubuwwat ata farmaayi. Agar yeh kaafir, yani Makkah ke kaafir, kitaab, hikmat aur nubuwwat ka inkaar karte hain to Allah Ta'ala ne in cheezon ke huqooq ada karne ke liye aisi qaum muqarrar kar rakhi hai jo in ka inkaar karne wali nahi.

Is qaum se ya to Ansaar muraad hain, ya Muhaajireen, ya tamaam Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُم, ya Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم par imaan laane wale woh tamaam Musalman muraad hain jinhein Allah Ta'ala deen ki khidmat ki taufeeq ata farmaaye, jaise muballigh, Ulama, Auliya aur sultaan waghera.

## Ayat 6:90

`Source: tafseerId=50916; ayatId=885; surah=6; ayat=90; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اُولٰٓىٕكَ الَّذِیْنَ هَدَى اللّٰهُ فَبِهُدٰىهُمُ اقْتَدِهْؕ-قُلْ لَّاۤ اَسْــٴَـلُكُمْ عَلَیْهِ اَجْرًاؕ-اِنْ هُوَ اِلَّا ذِكْرٰى لِلْعٰلَمِیْنَ۠      (90)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Yahi woh (muqaddas) hastiyan hain jinhein Allah ne hidaayat di to tum un ki hidaayat ki pairavi karo. Tum farmaao: Main is par tum se koi ujrat nahi maangta. Yeh to sirf saare jahan walon ke liye naseehat hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَبِهُدٰىهُمُ اقْتَدِهْ: To tum un ki hidaayat ki pairavi karo.}** Jaleel-ul-qadr Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke zikr ke baad Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم se farmaaya: Aap un Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki hidaayat ki pairavi farmaayein.

Ulama-e-Deen ne is aayat se yeh mas'alah saabit kiya hai ke Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم tamaam Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se afzal hain. Is liye ke jo sharf, kamaal, khaas khoobiyan aur sifaat alag alag Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko ata ki gayi thin, Allah Ta'ala ne woh sab Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ke liye jama farma di hain. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ko hukm diya gaya: “فَبِهُدٰىهُمُ اقْتَدِهْ”. Jab Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم tamaam Ambiya-e-Kiraam عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki kamaali sifaat ke jaame' hain to yaqeenan sab se afzal hue. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, 2/34.)

**{ذِكْرٰى لِلْعٰلَمِیْنَ: Saare jahan walon ke liye naseehat.}** Is aayat se saabit hua ke hamare Aaqa-o-Maula صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم tamaam makhlooq ki taraf mab'ous hain. Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ki da'wat tamaam makhlooq ke liye aam hai aur poora jahan Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ki ummat hai. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 90, 2/35.)

## Ayat 6:91

`Source: tafseerId=50917; ayatId=886; surah=6; ayat=91; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَا قَدَرُوا اللّٰهَ حَقَّ قَدْرِهٖۤ اِذْ قَالُوْا مَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى بَشَرٍ مِّنْ شَیْءٍؕ-قُلْ مَنْ اَنْزَلَ الْكِتٰبَ الَّذِیْ جَآءَ بِهٖ مُوْسٰى نُوْرًا وَّ هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ تَجْعَلُوْنَهٗ قَرَاطِیْسَ تُبْدُوْنَهَا وَ تُخْفُوْنَ كَثِیْرًاۚ-وَ عُلِّمْتُمْ مَّا لَمْ تَعْلَمُوْۤا اَنْتُمْ وَ لَاۤ اٰبَآؤُكُمْؕ-قُلِ اللّٰهُۙ-ثُمَّ ذَرْهُمْ فِیْ خَوْضِهِمْ یَلْعَبُوْنَ(91)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur Yahudiyon ne Allah ki qadr na ki jaisi us ki qadr karne ka haq tha jab unhone kaha: Allah ne kisi insaan par koi cheez nahi utaari. Tum farmaao: Woh kitaab kis ne utaari thi jise Moosa le kar aaye the? Noor aur logon ke liye hidaayat thi, jis ke tumne alag alag kaaghaz bana liye the, kuch zaahir karte ho aur bahut kuch chhupa lete ho aur tumhein woh sikhaya jaata hai jo na tumhein maloom tha aur na tumhare baap dada ko. Tum kaho: “Allah.” Phir unhein un ki behoodagi mein khelte hue chhor do.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَا قَدَرُوا اللّٰهَ حَقَّ قَدْرِهٖ: Aur unhone Allah ki qadr na ki jaisi us ki qadr karne ka haq tha.}** Is aayat ka shaan-e-nuzool yeh hai ke Yahudiyon ki ek jamaat apne sab se bade aalim Maalik bin Saif ko saath le kar Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم se behas karne aayi. Tajdaar-e-Risaalat صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ne us se farmaya: Main tujhe us Parwardigaar ki qasam deta hoon jis ne Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام par Taurat naazil farmaayi, kya Taurat mein tumne yeh dekha hai: “Allah Ta'ala ko mota aalim na-pasand hai?” Us ne kaha: “Haan, yeh Taurat mein hai.” Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ne farmaya: “Tu mota aalim hi to hai.” Woh gusse mein aa gaya aur kehne laga ke Allah Ta'ala ne kisi aadmi par kuch nahi utaara. Is par yeh aayat naazil hui. Woh lajawab ho gaya, Yahudiyon ne use jhidka aur us ke ohde se hata diya. (Tafseer Baghawi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 91, 2/94; Madaarik, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 91, s. 331-332, multaqatan.)

Mufti Ahmad Yaar Khan Naeemi رَحْمَۃُ اللہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain ke mote paadri se muraad woh paadri hain jo haraam khori kar ke bahut mote taaze ho jaate the.

Is aayat ka khulasa yeh hai ke Yahudiyon ne Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki waisi qadr na ki jaisi qadr karne ka haq tha. Woh Allah Ta'ala ki ma'rifat se mehroom rahe aur bandon par Allah Ta'ala ke rahm-o-karam ko na samajh sake. Unhone kaha ke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne kisi insaan par koi cheez naazil nahi ki. Ae Habeeb صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم! Aap unhein jawaab dein ke agar Allah Ta'ala ne koi kitaab naazil nahi ki to woh kitaab kis ne utaari thi jise Hazrat Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام le kar aaye the? Woh kitaab sarasar noor aur logon ke liye hidaayat thi.

Yahudiyon ne us kitaab ke alag alag kaaghaz bana liye the. Apni khwahish ke mutabiq kuch baatein zaahir karte aur Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم aur Aap ke deen ke baare mein bahut kuch chhupa lete. Unhein Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ki taleem aur Quran-e-Kareem ke zariye woh kuch sikhaya ja raha tha jo na unhein maloom tha aur na un ke baap dada ko.

Phir agar Yahudi Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ke sawaal ka jawaab na de saken to Aap khud jawaab de dein ke Taurat Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne naazil farmaayi thi aur jis tarah Taurat Allah Ta'ala ne naazil farmaayi, usi tarah Quran bhi Allah Ta'ala ne naazil farmaaya hai. Jawaab dene ke baad unhein un ki behoodagi mein khelte hue chhor dein, kyunki Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ne hujjat qaayam kar di, naseehat ka haq ada kar diya aur un ke liye koi uzr aur bahaana baqi na chhora. Is ke baad bhi woh baaz na aayein to unhein un ki behoodagi mein chhor dena kaafiron ke haq mein dhamki hai.

## Ayat 6:92

`Source: tafseerId=50918; ayatId=887; surah=6; ayat=92; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هٰذَا كِتٰبٌ اَنْزَلْنٰهُ مُبٰرَكٌ مُّصَدِّقُ الَّذِیْ بَیْنَ یَدَیْهِ وَ لِتُنْذِرَ اُمَّ الْقُرٰى وَ مَنْ حَوْلَهَاؕ-وَ الَّذِیْنَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِالْاٰخِرَةِ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِهٖ وَ هُمْ عَلٰى صَلَاتِهِمْ یُحَافِظُوْنَ(92)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur yeh barkat wali kitaab hai jise hum ne naazil farmaaya hai, pehli kitaabon ki tasdeeq karne wali hai aur is liye (utri) taake tum is ke zariye markazi shahar aur is ke gird-o-nawaah walon ko daraao aur jo aakhirat par imaan laate hain wohi is kitaab par imaan laate hain aur woh apni namaaz ki hifaazat karte hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ هٰذَا كِتٰبٌ: Aur yeh kitaab hai.}** Yahan Quran-e-Paak ke baare mein farmaaya gaya ke yeh barkat wali kitaab hai jise Allah Ta'ala ne naazil farmaaya. Yeh pehli kitaabon ki tasdeeq karti hai aur Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne ise is liye naazil farmaaya ke Aap صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم is ke zariye markazi shahar, yani Makkah Mukarramah, aur us ke gird-o-nawaah ke logon ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke azaab se daraayein.

**{وَ الَّذِیْنَ یُؤْمِنُوْنَ بِالْاٰخِرَةِ: Aur jo aakhirat par imaan laate hain.}** Jo log aakhirat aur us mein maujood tarah tarah ke azaabon par imaan laate hain, wohi Quran par imaan laate hain. Woh bure anjaam se darte hain aur yahi khauf unhein ghaur-o-fikr par ubhaarta rehta hai, yahan tak ke woh Quran par imaan le aate hain. Quran-e-Majeed par imaan laane wale apni namaaz ki hifaazat karte hain. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 92, 3/64.)

Namaaz ko khaas taur par is liye zikr kiya gaya ke yeh imaan ke baad sab se afzal ibaadat hai. Jab banda is ke tamaam arkaan aur sharaait ke saath pabandi karta hai to doosri ibaadat aur nek kaamon ki bhi pabandi shuru kar deta hai. Is se maloom hua ke Quran-e-Majeed par imaan laane ka ek taqaaza yeh hai ke paanchon namaazein apne tamaam arkaan aur sharaait ke saath pabandi se ada ki jaayein aur in mein susti na ki jaaye. Allah Ta'ala sab Musalmanon ko is taqaaze par poora utarne ki taufeeq ata farmaaye. [1]

[1] Namaaz ke arkaan, sharaait aur is se muta'alliq mazeed maloomat ke liye Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat دَامَتْ بَرَکَاتُہُمُ الْعَالِیَہ ki kitaab “Namaaz ke Ahkaam” aur “Islami Behnon ki Namaaz” ka mutaala karein.

## Ayat 6:93

`Source: tafseerId=50919; ayatId=888; surah=6; ayat=93; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَى اللّٰهِ كَذِبًا اَوْ قَالَ اُوْحِیَ اِلَیَّ وَ لَمْ یُوْحَ اِلَیْهِ شَیْءٌ وَّ مَنْ قَالَ سَاُنْزِلُ مِثْلَ مَاۤ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُؕ-وَ لَوْ تَرٰۤى اِذِ الظّٰلِمُوْنَ فِیْ غَمَرٰتِ الْمَوْتِ وَ الْمَلٰٓىٕكَةُ بَاسِطُوْۤا اَیْدِیْهِمْۚ-اَخْرِجُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْؕ-اَلْیَوْمَ تُجْزَوْنَ عَذَابَ الْهُوْنِ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَقُوْلُوْنَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ غَیْرَ الْحَقِّ وَ كُنْتُمْ عَنْ اٰیٰتِهٖ تَسْتَكْبِرُوْنَ(93)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur us se barh kar zaalim kaun? Jo Allah par jhoot baandhe ya kahe: Meri taraf wahi ki gayi, haalanke us ki taraf kisi shay ki wahi nahi bheji gayi, aur jo kahe: Main bhi abhi aisa utaar doonga jaisa Allah ne utaara hai. Aur agar tum dekho jab zaalim maut ki sakhtiyon mein hote hain aur farishte haath phailaate hue kehte hain: Apni jaanein nikaalo. Aaj tumhein zillat ka azaab diya jaayega us ke badle jo tum Allah par na-haq baatein kehte the aur us ki aayaat se takabbur karte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ مَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَى اللّٰهِ كَذِبًا: Aur us se barh kar zaalim kaun jo Allah par jhoot baandhe?}** Yeh aayat Musailimah Kazzab ke baare mein naazil hui jis ne Yemen ke ilaqe Yamaamah mein nubuwwat ka jhoota da'wa kiya. Bani Haneefah ke kuch log us ke dhoke mein aa gaye. Yeh jhoota shakhs Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke zamana-e-khilaafat mein Hazrat Wahshi رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ke haath se maara gaya. (Khaazin, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, 2/37.)

Is mein farmaya gaya ke us se barh kar zaalim kaun jo Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ par jhoot baandhe, nubuwwat ka jhoota da'wa kare aur kahe ke meri taraf wahi ki gayi, haalanke us ki taraf kisi shay ki wahi nahi bheji gayi. Yeh aayat Mirza Qaadiyani ka bhi saaf radd karti hai, kyunki us ne bhi apni taraf wahi aane ka da'wa kiya tha. Aaj kal Qaadiyani log mukhtalif tareeqon se logon ko dhoka dete hain. Koi kehta hai ke Mirza Ghulam Ahmad ne nubuwwat ka nahi balki mujaddid hone ka da'wa kiya, aur koi kehta hai ke us ne mutlaq nubuwwat-o-risaalat ka da'wa nahi kiya balki khaas qisam ki nubuwwat ka da'wa kiya. Haalanke us ki kitaabon mein mutlaq nubuwwat-o-risaalat ka da'wa maujood hai. Zilli aur burozi nubuwwat ka da'wa bhi nubuwwat hi ka da'wa hai aur bilkul kufr hai. Is liye kisi Musalman ko un ke dhoke mein nahi aana chahiye.

**{وَمَنْ قَالَ: Aur jis ne kaha.}** Aayat ka yeh hissa Abdullah bin Abi Sarh ke baare mein naazil hua jo kaatib-e-wahi tha. Jab “وَ لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْاِنْسَانَ” naazil hui to us ne use likha. Aayat ke aakhir tak insaan ki paidaish ki tafseel par use ta'ajjub hua aur “فَتَبٰرَكَ اللّٰهُ اَحْسَنُ الْخَالِقِیْنَ” be-ikhtiyaar us ki zubaan par jaari ho gaya. Us ne samjha ke mujh par bhi wahi aane lagi hai aur murtad ho gaya. Us ne yeh na samjha ke wahi ke noor aur kalam ki quwwat ki wajah se aayat ka aakhiri jumla zubaan par aa gaya tha aur is mein us ki apni qaabiliyat ka koi dakhl na tha.

Kalam ki taaqat kabhi kabhi apna qaafiya khud bata deti hai. Koi shayar umdah mazmoon padhe to sunne wale us se pehle qaafiya keh dete hain, haalanke woh waisa sher kehne par qaadir nahi hote. Yahan to wahi ka noor tha. Majlis-e-Mubaarak se alag ho kar murtad hone ke baad woh phir Quran ke nizaam se milta-julta ek jumla bhi na bana saka. Aakhir Fatah-e-Makkah se pehle zamana-e-Aqdas mein phir Islam se musharraf ho gaya. (Tafseer Qurtubi, Al-Anam, tehat-ul-aayat: 93, 4/30, al-juz us-saabi'.)

Is ke baare mein farmaya gaya ke us se barh kar zaalim kaun jo kahe: Main bhi jald aisi kitaab utaar doonga jaisi Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne naazil ki hai.

**{وَ لَوْ تَرٰى: Aur agar tum dekho.}** Yahan kaafiron ki maut ke waqt ke haalaat bayan hue hain. Agar tum zaalim kaafiron ko dekho jab woh maut ki sakhtiyon mein hote hain to tum ek nihayat khaufnaak manzar dekhoge. Farishte un ki rooh qabz karne ke liye haath phailaate aur kehte hain: Apni jaanein nikaalo. Aaj tumhein zillat ka azaab diya jaayega, kyunki tum nubuwwat aur wahi ke jhoote da'we karte, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke liye shareek aur biwi bachche banaate aur us par na-haq baatein kehte the.

### Maut ke waqt Musalman aur kaafir ke haalaat

Is aayat mein kaafir ki maut ke waqt ka haal bayan hua. Hadees-e-Paak mein Musalman aur kaafir dono ke haalaat bayan hue hain. Hazrat Abu Hurairah رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Nabi-e-Akram صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ne irshaad farmaaya: Jab Musalman ki maut qareeb hoti hai to rahmat ke farishte safed reshmi kapre le kar aate hain aur kehte hain: Ae rooh! Allah Ta'ala tum se raazi hai, is haal mein khushi khushi apne Parwardigaar ki taraf nikal, jo tum se naaraaz nahi. Phir rooh achhi khushboo wale mushk ki tarah nikalti hai. Farishte use aasmaan ke darwaaze par le jaate hain aur kehte hain: Yeh zameen se aane wali kitni achhi khushboo hai. Phir use Musalmanon ki roohon ke paas le jaate hain. Woh rooh us shakhs se bhi zyada khush hoti hain jo kisi door gaye hue shakhs ke lautne par hota hai. Woh poochti hain ke duniya mein falan shakhs kaise kaam karta hai. Phir kehti hain: Abhi use chhor do, yeh duniya ke gham mein hai. Woh rooh poochti hai: Kya woh tumhare paas nahi tha? Roohain kehti hain: Agar woh duniya se aa gaya aur hamare paas nahi hai to woh Jahannam mein gaya hoga.

Jab kaafir ki maut aati hai to azaab ke farishte boriye ke ek tukde ke saath aate hain aur kehte hain: Ae rooh! Allah Ta'ala ke azaab ki taraf nikal, kyunki tu Allah Ta'ala se naaraaz hai aur Allah Ta'ala tujh se naaraaz hai. Phir rooh jale hue badboodaar murdaar ki tarah nikalti hai. Farishte use zameen ke darwaaze par laate hain to poochte hain: Yeh kya badboo hai? Phir use kaafiron ki roohon mein le jaate hain. (Sunan Nasai, Kitaab-ul-Janaa'iz, Baab ma yalqa bihil-mu'min minal-karaamah..., s. 313, hadees: 1830.)

## Ayat 6:94

`Source: tafseerId=50920; ayatId=889; surah=6; ayat=94; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ لَقَدْ جِئْتُمُوْنَا فُرَادٰى كَمَا خَلَقْنٰكُمْ اَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍ وَّ تَرَكْتُمْ مَّا خَوَّلْنٰكُمْ وَرَآءَ ظُهُوْرِكُمْۚ-وَ مَا نَرٰى مَعَكُمْ شُفَعَآءَكُمُ الَّذِیْنَ زَعَمْتُمْ اَنَّهُمْ فِیْكُمْ شُرَكٰٓؤُاؕ-لَقَدْ تَّقَطَّعَ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ ضَلَّ عَنْكُمْ مَّا كُنْتُمْ تَزْعُمُوْنَ۠   (94)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak tum hamare paas akele aaye jaise hum ne tumhein pehli martaba paida kiya tha aur tum apne peechhe woh sab maal-o-mataa chhor aaye jo hum ne tumhein diya tha aur aaj hum tumhare saath tumhare un sifaarishiyon ko nahi dekhte jinhein tum gumaan karte the ke woh tum mein hamare shareek hain. Beshak tumhare darmiyan judaai ho gayi aur tum se woh ghaayab ho gaye jin ke mabood hone ka tum da'wa karte the.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ لَقَدْ جِئْتُمُوْنَا فُرَادٰى: Aur tum hamare paas akele aaye.}** Pichhli aayat mein kaafiron ki maut ke haalaat bayan hue aur is aayat mein Qiyamat ke haalaat bayan kiye gaye hain. Qiyamat ke din kaafiron se kaha jaayega: Tum hisaab aur jaza ke liye hamare paas bilkul akele aaye, jaise hum ne tumhein pehli baar paida kiya tha. Tumhare saath na maal hai, na jaah-o-martabah, na woh aulaad jis ki muhabbat mein tum umr bhar lage rahe aur na woh but jinhein tum poojte rahe. Aaj in mein se koi tumhare kaam nahi aaya.

Tum woh sab maal-o-mataa apne peechhe chhor aaye jo hum ne tumhein diya tha. Hum tumhare saath tumhare un sifaarishiyon ko nahi dekhte jinhein tum apni ibaadat mein Allah Ta'ala ka shareek samajhte the. Tumhare darmiyan judaai ho gayi aur tum se woh but ghaayab ho gaye jin ke mabood hone ka tum da'wa karte the.

### Qabr aur Hashr ki tanhaai ke saathi banana danishmandi hai

Yaad rahe ke marne ke baad insaan qabr mein akela hoga. Duniya ka maal-o-mataa, ahl-o-ayaal, azeez rishtedaar aur dost-ahbaab mein se koi bhi us ke saath qabr mein nahi jaayega. Yeh sab use qabr ke andhere gaddhe mein tanha chhor kar chale jaayenge. Isi tarah maidaan-e-Hashr mein bhi insaan akela hoga. Us waqt har shakhs apne anjaam ki fikr mein hoga aur kisi doosre ka saath nahi dega. Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein apne aamaal ka hisaab dene ke liye bhi insaan tanha haazir hoga.

Is liye aqalmandi yahi hai ke duniya ki zindagi mein aise saathi bana liye jaayein jo qabr ki wehshatnaak tanhaai mein unsiyat aur ghamkhwaari ka sabab ban sakein aur Qiyamat ke din ke haulnaak haal mein taskeen dein. Yeh saathi nek aamaal hain. Hazrat Anas bin Maalik رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwaayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم ne irshaad farmaaya: Mayyit ke saath teen cheezein jaati hain, do laut aati hain aur ek us ke saath baqi rehti hai. Us ke ahl-o-ayaal, maal aur amal us ke saath jaate hain. Us ke ahl-o-ayaal aur maal laut aate hain aur us ka amal baqi rehta hai. (Bukhari, Kitaab-ur-Riqaaq, Baab Sakraat-ul-Maut, 4/250, hadees: 6514.) [1]

[1] Qabr ke saathi banaane ki ahmiyat aur targheeb ke liye kitaab “Qabr mein Aane Wala Dost” ka mutaala bahut mufeed hai.

## Ayat 6:95

`Source: tafseerId=50921; ayatId=890; surah=6; ayat=95; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ اللّٰهَ فَالِقُ الْحَبِّ وَ النَّوٰىؕ-یُخْرِ جُ الْحَیَّ مِنَ الْمَیِّتِ وَ مُخْرِ جُ الْمَیِّتِ مِنَ الْحَیِّؕ-ذٰلِكُمُ اللّٰهُ فَاَنّٰى تُؤْفَكُوْنَ(95)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak Allah daane aur guthli ko cheerne wala hai, zinda ko murdah se nikaalne aur murdah ko zinda se nikaalne wala hai. Yeh Allah hai to tum kahan phire jaate ho?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَالِقُ الْحَبِّ: Daane ko cheerne wala.}** Tauheed-o-nubuwwat ke bayan ke baad Allah Ta'ala ne apni kamaal ki qudrat, ilm aur hikmat ki daleelein bayan farmaayi hain, kyunki asal maqsad Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ aur us ki tamaam sifaat-o-af'aal ki ma'rifat haasil karna hai. Jo tamaam cheezon ka paida karne wala ho, wahi ibaadat ka mustahiq hai, na ke woh but jinhein mushrik poojte hain.

Khushk daane aur guthli ko cheer kar un se sabzah aur darakht paida karna aur sakht zameen mein un ki narm jarron ko jaari kar dena Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat ke ajeeb karishme hain. Wahi Kareem Rab daane aur guthli ko cheer kar sabzah aur darakht bana deta hai aur zinda ko murdah se nikaalta hai, jaise jandaar sabzah ko bejaan daane aur guthli se, insaan aur jaanwar ko nutfe se aur parinde ko ande se.

Isi tarah wahi Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ murdah ko zinda se nikaalne wala hai, jaise jandaar darakht se bejaan guthli aur daana, insaan aur jaanwar se nutfah aur parinde se anda nikaalta hai. Yeh sab Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat aur hikmat ke ajeeb nishaan hain. Ae kaafiro! Yeh Allah hai, to tum kahan ulte phire jaate ho? Itni roshan daleelein qaayam hone ke baad imaan kyun nahi laate aur maut ke baad dobara uthne ka yaqeen kyun nahi karte? Ghaur karo ke jo bejaan nutfe se jandaar jaanwar paida karta hai, us ki qudrat se murdah ko zinda karna kya mushkil hai?

## Ayat 6:96

`Source: tafseerId=50922; ayatId=891; surah=6; ayat=96; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** فَالِقُ الْاِصْبَاحِۚ-وَ جَعَلَ الَّیْلَ سَكَنًا وَّ الشَّمْسَ وَ الْقَمَرَ حُسْبَانًاؕ-ذٰلِكَ تَقْدِیْرُ الْعَزِیْزِ الْعَلِیْمِ(96)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur wahi tareeki ko cheer kar subah nikaalne wala hai aur us ne raat ko aaraam ka zariya banaya aur Suraj aur Chaand ko auqaat ke hisaab ka zariya banaya. Yeh zabardast, ilm wale ka muqarrar kiya hua andaazah hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَالِقُ الْاِصْبَاحِ: Tareeki ko cheer kar subah nikaalne wala.}** Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ apni mazeed azmatein bayan farmaata hai. Chunanche farmaaya ke woh tareeki ko cheer kar subah nikaalne wala hai. Subah ke waqt mashriq ki taraf roshni dhaage ki tarah numoodaar hoti hai aur aisa maaloom hota hai ke is roshni ne tareeki ko cheer diya. Yeh bhi Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat hai.

Us ne raat ko aaraam ka zariya banaya, taake makhlooq us mein chain paaye, din ki thakaawat aur maandi ko aaraam se door kare aur shab-bedaar zaahid tanhaai mein apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ki ibaadat se sukoon paaye.

Us ne Suraj aur Chaand ko auqaat ke hisaab ka zariya banaya, kyunki un ke dauron aur gardish se ibaadat aur muamlaat ke auqaat maaloom hote hain. Misal ke taur par Chaand se Qamari mahine aur Suraj se Shamsi mahine bante hain. Chaand se Islami ibaadat aur Suraj se mausamon aur namaazon ka hisaab lagta hai. Gharz yeh ke in mein Allah Ta'ala ki qudrat ke ajeeb karishme hain. Yeh sab zabardast, ilm wale ka muqarrar kiya hua andaazah hai.

Is aayat se maaloom hua ke ilm-e-riyaazi, ilm-e-nabaataat, ilm-e-falkiyaat aur ilm-ul-haiwanaat bhi bahut aala uloom hain, kyunki in se Rab Ta'ala ki kaamil qudrat zaahir hoti hai. Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne aasmaani aur zameeni cheezon ko apni qudrat ka namoona banaaya hai.

### Faqr aur mohtaaji door hone ki dua

Hazrat Muslim bin Yasaar رَضِیَ اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ farmaate hain ke Nabi-e-Kareem صَلَّی اللہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّم yeh dua maanga karte the: “اَللّٰہُمَّ فَالِقَ الْاِصْبَاحِ وَجَاعِلَ اللَّیْلِ سَکَنًا وَالشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ حُسْبَانًا، اِقْضِ عَنِّی الدَّیْنَ وَاغْنِنِی مِنَ الْفَقْرِ وََ مَتِّعْنِی بِسَمْعِیْ وَبَصَرِیْ وَقُوَّتِیْ فِیْ سَبِیْلِکَ” Ae Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ! Ae tareeki ko cheer kar subah nikaalne wale, raat ko aaraam ka zariya banaane wale aur Suraj-o-Chaand ko auqaat ke hisaab ka zariya banaane wale! Mera qarz ada farma de, mujhe mohtaaji se be-niyaaz farma de aur meri samaat, basaarat aur quwwat se apni raah mein faaidah uthaane wala bana de. (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaibah, Kitaab-ud-Dua, Man kaana yad'u bil-ghina, 7/27, hadees: 3.)

## Ayat 6:97

`Source: tafseerId=50923; ayatId=892; surah=6; ayat=97; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ النُّجُوْمَ لِتَهْتَدُوْا بِهَا فِیْ ظُلُمٰتِ الْبَرِّ وَ الْبَحْرِؕ-قَدْ فَصَّلْنَا الْاٰیٰتِ لِقَوْمٍ یَّعْلَمُوْنَ(97)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur wahi hai jis ne tumhare liye sitaare banaaye taake tum un ke zariye khushki aur samandar ki tareekiyon mein raasta pao. Beshak hum ne ilm walon ke liye tafseel se nishaaniyan bayan kar di hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَهُوَ الَّذِيْۤ اَنْشَاَكُمْ مِّنْ نَّفْسٍ وَّاحِدَةٍ: Aur wahi hai jis ne tumhein ek jaan se paida kiya.}** Is aayat ka khulaasa yeh hai ke wahi Allah hai jis ne tumhein ek jaan, yaani Hazrat Aadam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se paida kiya. Phir maa ke reham mein ya zameen ke upar tumhara thikaana banaaya aur baap ki peeth ya zameen ke andar tumhare liye amaanat rakhe jaane ki jagah banaayi hai. Beshak hum ne samajhne walon ke liye apni qudrat ki nishaaniyan tafseel se bayan kar di hain.

## Ayat 6:98

`Source: tafseerId=null; ayatId=893; surah=6; ayat=98; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْۤ اَنْشَاَكُمْ مِّنْ نَّفْسٍ وَّاحِدَةٍ فَمُسْتَقَرٌّ وَّ مُسْتَوْدَعٌؕ-قَدْ فَصَّلْنَا الْاٰیٰتِ لِقَوْمٍ یَّفْقَهُوْنَ(98)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur wahi hai jis ne tumhein ek jaan se paida kiya, phir ek thikaana hai aur ek amaanat rakhne ki jagah. Beshak hum ne samajh rakhne walon ke liye nishaaniyan tafseel se bayan kar di hain.

## Ayat 6:99

`Source: tafseerId=50924; ayatId=894; surah=6; ayat=99; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْۤ اَنْزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَآءِ مَآءًۚ-فَاَخْرَجْنَا بِهٖ نَبَاتَ كُلِّ شَیْءٍ فَاَخْرَجْنَا مِنْهُ خَضِرًا نُّخْرِ جُ مِنْهُ حَبًّا مُّتَرَاكِبًاۚ-وَ مِنَ النَّخْلِ مِنْ طَلْعِهَا قِنْوَانٌ دَانِیَةٌ وَّ جَنّٰتٍ مِّنْ اَعْنَابٍ وَّ الزَّیْتُوْنَ وَ الرُّمَّانَ مُشْتَبِهًا وَّ غَیْرَ مُتَشَابِهٍؕ-اُنْظُرُوْۤا اِلٰى ثَمَرِهٖۤ اِذَاۤ اَثْمَرَ وَ یَنْعِهٖؕ-اِنَّ فِیْ ذٰلِكُمْ لَاٰیٰتٍ لِّقَوْمٍ یُّؤْمِنُوْنَ(99)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur wahi hai jis ne aasmaan se paani utaara. Phir hum ne us ke zariye har ugne wali cheez nikaali. Phir hum ne us se sabz kheti nikaali jis mein se hum ek doosre par chadhe hue daane nikaalte hain. Aur khajoor ke ibtidaai kachche shagoofon se khajoor ke khoshe nikaalte hain jo phalon ki kasrat se latke hue hote hain. Aur angur ke baagh aur zaitoon aur anar nikaalte hain jo kisi sifat mein ek doosre se milte hote hain aur kisi sifat mein alag hote hain. Darakht ke phal aur us ke pakne ki taraf dekho jab woh phal de. Beshak is mein imaan walon ke liye nishaaniyan hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَهُوَ الَّذِيْۤ اَنْزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَآءِ مَآءً: Aur wahi hai jis ne aasmaan se paani utaara.}** SubhanAllah! Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne apni qudrat ki kitni azeem daleel bayan farmaayi ke dekho, paani ek hai aur woh zameen bhi ek hai jis se sab kuch ugta hai, lekin Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ne us se jo cheezein ugaayi hain woh qism qism aur rang birangi hain.

Jo Rab-e-Azeem عَزَّوَجَلَّ ek paani se itni qism ki sabziyaan paida karne par qaadir hai, woh ek soor ki phoonk se saare aalam ko maarne aur zinda karne par bhi qaadir hai. Is liye Qiyamat haq hai.

## Ayat 6:100

`Source: tafseerId=50925; ayatId=895; surah=6; ayat=100; para=7; volume=3`

**Arabic:** وَ جَعَلُوْا لِلّٰهِ شُرَكَآءَ الْجِنَّ وَ خَلَقَهُمْ وَ خَرَقُوْا لَهٗ بَنِیْنَ وَ بَنٰتٍۭ بِغَیْرِ عِلْمٍؕ-سُبْحٰنَهٗ وَ تَعٰلٰى عَمَّا یَصِفُوْنَ۠    (100)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur logon ne jinnon ko Allah ka shareek bana liya, haalanke Allah ne to un jinnon ko paida kiya hai. Aur logon ne Allah ke liye jahalat se bete aur betiyaan gadh leen, haalanke Allah un ki bayan ki hui cheezon se paak aur buland hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَجَعَلُوْا لِلّٰهِ شُرَكَآءَ الْجِنَّ: Aur logon ne jinnon ko Allah ka shareek gadh liya.}** Pichhli aayaat mein bayan kiye gaye qudrat ke daleel, hikmat ke ajeeb namoone aur in inaamaat aur nematon ka taqaaza yeh tha ke log is Kareem Kaar-saaz par imaan laate. Lekin is ke bajaaye but-paraston ne yeh zulm kiya ke jinnon ko Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka shareek qarar diya aur un ki ita'at karke but-parast ho gaye.

Unhon ne Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ ke liye, ma'azAllah, bete aur betiyaan gadh leen, haalanke Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ un ki bayan ki hui baaton se paak aur buland hai. Yeh baatein us ki shaan ke bilkul laayiq nahi.
