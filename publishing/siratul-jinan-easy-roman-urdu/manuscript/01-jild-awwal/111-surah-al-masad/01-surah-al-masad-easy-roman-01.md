# Surah Al-Masad — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 111:1

`Source: tafseerId=54495; ayatId=6327; surah=111; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** تَبَّتْ یَدَاۤ اَبِیْ لَهَبٍ وَّ تَبَّﭤ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Abu Lahab ke dono haath tabaah ho jaayen aur woh tabaah ho hi gaya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {تَبَّتْ یَدَاۤ اَبِیْ لَهَبٍ وَّ تَبَّ: Abu Lahab ke dono haath tabaah ho jaayen aur woh tabaah ho hi gaya.} Abu Lahab ka naam Abdul Uzza hai. Woh Abdul Muttalib ka beta aur Sarwar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ka chacha tha. Woh bahut gora aur khoobsurat aadmi tha, isi liye us ki kuniyat Abu Lahab hai aur isi kuniyat se woh mashhoor tha.

Us ke beton Utbah aur Utaibah ke nikaah mein Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ki sahibzadiyan Hazrat Ruqayyah aur Hazrat Umm-e-Kulsum رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا thin. Is Surah ke nazil hone ke baad Abu Lahab ne un sahibzadiyon ko talaaq dilwa di. Utbah ka waqi'a bhi bada ibratnaak hai. Us ne Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ki shehzadi ko talaaq dene ke saath Huzoor ﷺ ki gustakhi bhi ki. Is par Sarwar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ ne us ke liye halaakat ki dua ki. Chunanche ek safar mein bahut hifaazati intezaam karne ke bawajood ek sher ne use phaad daala.

Aayat mein Abu Lahab ke dono haath halaak hone se muraad us ki zaat ki halaakat hai. Mubarak aayat mein Abu Lahab ki halaakat ki peshgoi ki gayi. Chunanche woh badtareen maut mara. Woh Jang-e-Badr ke ek hafte baad kaale daane ki bimaari se mara, jise Arab mein Udsa kehte hain. Arab ke log use muta'addi bimaari samajh kar us se bahut bachte the. Is liye teen din tak is mardood ki laash padi rahi. Phir us ka jism phool kar phat gaya aur badboo dene laga. Tab ujrat de kar mazdooron se use phinkwaaya gaya. (روح البیان، المسد، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ۱۰ / ۵۳۴)

## Ayat 111:2

`Source: tafseerId=54496; ayatId=6328; surah=111; ayat=2; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** مَاۤ اَغْنٰى عَنْهُ مَالُهٗ وَ مَا كَسَبَﭤ(2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Us ka maal aur us ki kamaai us ke kuch kaam na aayi.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {مَاۤ اَغْنٰى عَنْهُ مَالُهٗ وَ مَا كَسَبَ: Us ka maal aur us ki kamaai us ke kuch kaam na aayi.} Riwayat hai ke jab Abu Lahab ne pehli aayat suni to kehne laga: “Mere bhatije jo kuch kehte hain, agar sach hai to main apni jaan ke liye apne maal aur aulaad ko fidya kar dunga.” Is aayat mein us ka radd farmaya gaya ke yeh khayal ghalat hai. Us waqt koi cheez kaam aane wali nahin.

(مدارک، المسد، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ص۱۳۸۱، خزائن العرفان، اللّہب، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ص۱۱۲۴)

{سَیَصْلٰى نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ: Ab woh sholon wali aag mein daakhil hoga.} Yani Abu Lahab Qiyamat ke baad Dozakh mein daakhil ho kar aag ka azaab paayega. Is se maloom hua ke Abu Lahab ka dozakhi hona yaqini hai.

## Ayat 111:4

`Source: tafseerId=54497; ayatId=6330; surah=111; ayat=4; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَّ امْرَاَتُهٗؕ-حَمَّالَةَ الْحَطَبِۚ    (4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur us ki biwi lakdiyon ka gathha uthaane wali hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {وَ امْرَاَتُهٗؕ-حَمَّالَةَ الْحَطَبِ: Aur us ki biwi lakdiyon ka gathha uthaane wali hai.} Umm-e-Jameel bint-e-Harb bin Umayyah, Abu Sufyan ki behen thi. Woh Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ se nihayat kina aur dushmani rakhti thi. Bahut daulatmand aur bade gharane ki aurat hone ke bawajood Rasoolullah ﷺ ki dushmani mein is inteha tak pahunchi hui thi ke khud apne sar par kaanton ka gathha la kar Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ke raaste mein daalti, taake Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ aur Aap ﷺ ke ashaab رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ko aziyat aur takleef ho. Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ko takleef dena use itna pasand tha ke woh is kaam mein kisi doosre se madad lena bhi gawara nahin karti thi. (بیضاوی، المسد، تحت الآیۃ: ۴، ۵ / ۵۴۵، خازن، ابولہب، تحت الآیۃ: ۴، ۴ / ۴۲۵، ملتقطاً)

{فِیْ جِیْدِهَا حَبْلٌ مِّنْ مَّسَدٍ: Us ke gale mein khajoor ki chhaal ki rassi hai.} Umm-e-Jameel ke gale mein khajoor ki chhaal se bani hui rassi hoti thi, jis se woh kaanton ka gathha baandhti thi. Ek din woh yeh bojh utha kar la rahi thi ke thak kar aaraam karne ke liye ek patthar par baith gayi. Ek farishte ne Allah Ta'ala ke hukm se us ke peeche se is gathhe ko kheench liya. Woh gir gaya, Umm-e-Jameel ko rassi se gale mein phansi lag gayi aur woh mar gayi. (خازن، ابو لہب، تحت الآیۃ: ۵، ۴ / ۴۲۵)

Is gustakh, khabees ne duniya mein bhi azaab ka maza chakha aur Aakhirat mein bhi azaab mein jaayegi. Aakhirat mein aag ki zanjeerein us ke gale mein hongi aur Jahannam ki lakdiyon ka gathha us ki peeth par lada hua hoga.
