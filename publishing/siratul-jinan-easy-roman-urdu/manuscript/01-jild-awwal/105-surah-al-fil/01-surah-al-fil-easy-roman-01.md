# Surah Al-Fil — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 105:1

`Source: tafseerId=54485; ayatId=6293; surah=105; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ تَرَ كَیْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِاَصْحٰبِ الْفِیْلِﭤ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya tum ne na dekha ke tumhare Rab ne un haathi walon ka kya haal kiya?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اَلَمْ تَرَ كَیْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِاَصْحٰبِ الْفِیْلِ: Kya tum ne na dekha ke tumhare Rab ne un haathi walon ka kya haal kiya?} Is Surah mein jo waqi'a bayan hua hai, us ka khulasa yeh hai ke Yemen aur Habasha ke baadshah Abraha ne dekha ke Hajj ke mausam mein log Baitullah ka Hajj karne ki tayyari kar rahe hain. Us ne Sana'a mein ek kanisa (ibaadat-khana) banwaya, taake Hajj karne wale Makkah Mukarramah jaane ke bajaye wahin aayen aur us kanise ka tawaaf karein.

Arab ke logon ko yeh baat bahut na-gawaar guzri. Qabeela Bani Kinana ke ek aadmi ne mauqa pa kar kanise mein qaza-e-haajat ki aur use napaaki se aalooda kar diya. Abraha ko jab yeh maaloom hua to use bahut ghussa aaya. Us ne qasam khai ke woh Ka'bah Mu'azzamah ko gira dega. Phir woh isi iraade se apna lashkar le kar chal pada. Is lashkar mein bahut se haathi the. Un ka sab se aage chalne wala ek bahut bade jism ka pahaad jaisa haathi tha, jis ka naam Mahmood tha.

Jab Abraha Makkah Mukarramah ke qareeb pahuncha to us ne Ahle-Makkah ke jaanwar qaid kar liye. Un mein Hazrat Abdul Muttalib ke do sau oont bhi the. Hazrat Abdul Muttalib Abraha ke paas aaye. Abraha ne un ki ta'zeem ki, unhein apne paas bithaya aur poocha: Aap kis maqsad se aaye hain aur aap ka kya mutaalabah hai? Aap ne farmaya: Mera mutaalabah yeh hai ke mere oont mujhe wapas kar diye jaayen.

Abraha ne kaha: Mujhe aap ki baat sun kar bahut ta'ajjub hua. Main us Khanah-e-Ka'bah ko dhaane ke liye yahan aaya hoon jo aap ka aur aap ke baap-dada ka mu'azzam aur mohtaram maqam hai, lekin aap us ke baare mein kuchh nahin keh rahe aur apne oonton ki wapsi ka mutaalabah kar rahe hain! Aap ne farmaya: Main oonton ka maalik hoon, is liye unhi ke baare mein keh raha hoon. Ka'bah ka jo Maalik hai, woh khud us ki hifaazat farmayega.

Yeh sun kar Abraha ne aap ke oont wapas kar diye. Hazrat Abdul Muttalib wapas aaye, Quraish ko poori surat-e-haal batayi aur mashwarah diya ke woh pahaadon ki ghaatiyon aur chotiyon mein panaah le lein. Quraish ne aisa hi kiya. Hazrat Abdul Muttalib Ka'bah ke darwaaze par aaye aur Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein Ka'bah ki hifaazat ki dua ki. Dua se faarigh ho kar aap bhi apni qaum ki taraf chale gaye.

Abraha ne subah sawere apne lashkar ko tayyari ka hukm diya. Us waqt Mahmood naam ke haathi ka haal yeh tha ke jab use kisi aur taraf le jaate to woh chal padta, lekin jab Ka'bah ki taraf us ka rukh karte to woh baith jaata. Allah Ta'ala ne Abraha ke lashkar par samandar ki taraf se parindon ke giroh bheje. Har parinde ke paas teen kankariyan thin: do dono panjon mein aur ek chonch mein. Woh parinde aaye aur kankar ke patharon se un logon ko maarne lage.

Jis aadmi par parinda kankari giraata, woh kankari us ki topi ko tod kar sar se nikalti, jism ko cheer kar haathi ke jism se guzarti hui zameen tak pahunch jaati. Har kankari par us aadmi ka naam likha hua tha jis ke liye woh kankari muqarrar ki gayi thi. Is tarah un parindon ne Abraha ke lashkariyon ko jaanwaron ke khaaye hue bhoose ki tarah kar diya. Jis saal yeh waqi'a pesh aaya, usi saal Sarwar-e-Do Aalam صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ ki wiladat hui. (خازن، الفیل، تحت الآیۃ: ۱-۵، ۴ / ۴۰۷-۴۱۰، ملخصاً)

## Ayat 105:2
