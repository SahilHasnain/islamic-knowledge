# Surah Al-Kausar — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 108:1

`Source: tafseerId=54491; ayatId=6312; surah=108; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَﭤ(1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Ae Mehboob! Beshak hum ne tumhein be-shumaar khoobiyan ata farmaayin.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ: Ae Mehboob! Beshak hum ne tumhein be-shumaar khoobiyan ata farmaayin.} Kausar ki tafseer mein mufassireen ke mukhtalif aqwaal hain. In sab aqwaal ka khulasa yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko be-shumaar khoobiyan aur bahut se fazaail ata farma kar tamaam makhlooq par afzal kiya. Aap ko husn-e-zaahir bhi diya aur husn-e-baatin bhi. Aap ko aala nasab, nubuwwat, kitaab, hikmat, ilm, shafa'at, Hauz-e-Kausar, Maqaam-e-Mahmood, ummat ki kasrat, deen ke dushmano par ghalaba, fatahaat ki kasrat aur be-shumaar aisi ne'matein aur fazeelatein ata farmaayin jin ki koi inteha nahin. (خازن، الکوثر، تحت الآیۃ: ۱، ۴ / ۴۱۳-۴۱۴، ملتقطاً)

### Ayat “اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ” se haasil hone wali baatein

Is aayat se paanch baatein maloom hoti hain:

1. Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko Kausar ata farma di hai. Yahan yeh nahin farmaya gaya ke hum aap ko Kausar ata karenge, balki yeh farmaya gaya ke hum ne aap ko Kausar ata kar di.

2. Allah Ta'ala ki apne Habeeb ﷺ par yeh ata un ke Nabi aur Rasool hone ki wajah se nahin, balki un ki zaat ki wajah se hai jo shaan-e-mahboobiyat ki surat hai. Yahan “اَعْطَیْنٰكَ” yani “hum ne aap ko ata farmaayi” kaha gaya hai; “اَعْطَیْنَا الرَّسُوْلَ” ya “اَعْطَیْنَا النَّبِیَّ” nahin farmaya gaya.

3. Taajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ par yeh ata kisi ibaadat aur riyaazat ki wajah se nahin, balki Allah Ta'ala ke azeem fazl aur ehsaan ki wajah se hai, kyunke yahan ata ka zikr pehle hua aur ibaadat ka zikr baad mein.

4. Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ﷺ ko Kausar ka maalik bana diya hai, is liye Aap jis ko chahein aur jo chahein ata farma sakte hain.

5. Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ki madhat khud Rab Ta'ala farmata hai. A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ farmate hain:

اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ

Saari kasrat paate yeh hain
Thanda thanda meetha meetha
Peete hum hain pilaate yeh hain

Aur farmate hain:

Ae Raza khud Sahib-e-Quran hai maddah-e-Huzoor
Tujh se kab mumkin hai phir madhat Rasoolullah ki

{فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَ انْحَرْ: To tum apne Rab ke liye namaaz padho aur qurbani karo.} Yani Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Allah Ta'ala ne Aap ko dono jahaan ki be-shumaar bhalaiyan ata ki hain aur Aap ko woh khaas rutba diya hai jo Aap ke siwa kisi aur ko ata nahin kiya. Is liye Aap apne us Rab عزوجل ke liye namaaz padhte rahein jis ne Aap ﷺ ko Kausar ata karke izzat aur sharaafat di, taake buton ke pujari zaleel hon. Buton ke naam par zabh karne walon ki mukhaalifat karte hue apne Rab عزوجل ke liye aur us ke naam par qurbani karein. Is aayat ki tafseer mein ek qaul yeh bhi hai ke namaaz se namaaz-e-Eid muraad hai. (مدارک، الکوثر، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ص۱۳۷۸، خازن، الکوثر، تحت الآیۃ: ۲، ۴ / ۴۱۶-۴۱۷، ملتقطاً)

{اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ: Beshak jo tumhara dushman hai wohi har khair se mehroom hai.} Shan-e-Nuzool: Jab Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ke farzand Hazrat Qasim رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ka wisal hua to kaafiron ne Aap ko “abtar”, yani jis ki nasl khatam ho gayi ho, kaha. Unhone kaha ke ab un ki nasl nahin rahi, un ke baad un ka zikr bhi nahin rahega aur yeh saara charcha khatam ho jaayega. Is par yeh Surah-e-Kareema naazil hui aur Allah Ta'ala ne un kaafiron ka zabardast jawab diya. Aayat mein farmaya gaya: Ae Habeeb ﷺ! Beshak tumhara dushman hi har bhalai se mehroom hai, tum nahin. Aap ka silsila Qiyamat tak jaari rahega, Aap ki aulaad mein bhi kasrat hogi, Aap ki pairwi karne walon se duniya bhar jaayegi, Aap ka zikr mimbaron par buland hoga aur Qiyamat tak paida hone wale aalim aur wa'iz Allah Ta'ala ke zikr ke saath Aap ka zikr karte rahenge. Aakhirat mein Aap ke liye woh kuchh hai jis ka koi wasf bayan nahin kar sakta. Jis ki yeh shaan ho, woh abtar, be-naam-o-nishaan aur har bhalai se mehroom kaise ho sakta hai? Aap ke dushman hi aise hain. (مدارک، الکوثر، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ص۱۳۷۸، خازن، الکوثر، تحت الآیۃ: ۳، ۴ / ۴۱۷، ملتقطاً)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ is Surah ki tafseer bayan karte hue farmate hain ke Aas bin Waa'il ne Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen ﷺ ke farzand ke pur-malaal wisal par Huzoor ko abtar, yani nasl-bureeda, kaha. Haq جلَّ وَعَلا ne farmaya: “اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ” Beshak hum ne tumhein khair-e-kaseer ata farmaayi. Aulaad se naam chalne ko tumhari rif'at-e-zikr se kya nisbat! Karoron aulaad wale guzre jin ka naam tak koi nahin jaanta, jabke tumhari sana ka danka Qiyamat tak duniya ke har kone mein bajega aur tumhare naam ka khutbah hamesha aasmaan aur zameen ke aafaaq mein padha jaayega.

Phir Allah Ta'ala tumhein aisi nafees aur paak aulaad bhi ata farmaayega jin ki baqa se duniya ki baqa judi rahegi. Is ke ilawa tamaam Musalman tumhare baal-bachche hain aur tum se zyada un par koi meherbaan nahin. Haqeeqat par nazar karein to tamaam aalam tumhari ma'nawi aulaad hai; agar tum na hote to kuchh bhi na hota aur tumhare hi noor se sab ki aafreenish hui. Isi liye jab Abu-ul-Bashar Adam علیہ السلام tumhein yaad karte to kehte: “یَا اِبْنِیْ صُوْرَۃً وَاَبَایَ مَعْنًی” Ae zahir mein mere bete aur haqeeqat mein mere baap! Aakhirat mein tumhein jo kuchh milega us ka haal to Khuda hi jaanta hai. Jab Allah Ta'ala ki yeh be-intiha inayat tum par hai to tum un badbakhton ki zabaan daraazi par kyun ranjida ho? Balki “فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَ انْحَرْ” apne Rab ke shukrane mein us ke liye namaaz padho aur qurbani karo.

“اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ” Tumhara dushman hi nasl-bureeda hai. Jin beton par use naaz hai, yani Amr aur Hishaam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا, wohi us ke dushman ho jaayenge. Tumhare deen-e-haq mein aakar deen ke ikhtilaaf ki wajah se us ki nasl se alag ho kar tumhare deeni beton mein shumaar honge. Agar koi aadmi be-nasl hota to yeh baat itni buri na hoti ke us ka naam na chalta; lekin us ka bura naam baqi rehna hazaar darje badtar hai. Tumhare dushman ka napaak naam hamesha buraai aur la'nat ke saath liya jaayega aur Roz-e-Qiyamat use apni gustakhiyon ki poori saza milegi. وَ الْعِیَاذُ بِاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی۔ (فتاویٰ رضویہ، ۳۰ / ۱۶۷-۱۶۸)

Is se maloom hua ke Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ka Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein maqam itna buland hai ke Aap ke gustakh ko us ki gustakhi ka jawab khud Rab Ta'ala deta hai.
