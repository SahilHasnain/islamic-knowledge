# Surah Al-Inshirah — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 94:1

`Source: tafseerId=54435; ayatId=6184; surah=94; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Kya hum ne tumhari khaatir tumhara seena kushadah na kar diya?

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ: Kya hum ne tumhari khaatir tumhara seena kushadah na kar diya?}** Is Surah ke nuzool ka sabab yeh bayan kiya gaya hai ke ek din Sarwar-e-Aalam ﷺ ne Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein arz ki: “Ae Allah! Aap ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apna khaleel banne ka sharaf diya, Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apne saath kalaam karne ka sharaf diya aur doosre Ambiya ko khaas fazeelatein ata farmayin; mujhe kis sharaf aur karam ke saath khaas farmaya hai?” Is par yeh Surah naazil hui.

Yani: “Ae Habib ﷺ! Agar hum ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko apna khaleel banaya hai to aap ki khaatir aap ka seena ilm-o-hikmat aur ma'rifat ke noor se khol diya, taake munajaat ki lazzat, ummat ka gham, hamari baargaah mein haazri ka shauq aur aakhirat ke ghar ki chaahat aap ke dil mein sama jaaye. Aasmani wahi ko uthaana aap ke dil par aasaan ho jaaye aur Allah Ta'ala ki taraf bulaane mein aane wali museebatein aap bardaasht kar saken.” In kamalaat ki wajah se aap ko aisa maqam mila ke Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ke khaleel hone ko is se kuchh nisbat na rahi.

Aur agar Hazrat Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko hum ne apne saath ham-kalaam hone ka sharaf diya aur Hazrat Idrees عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko buland maqam tak rasai di, to aap ko us gham se nijaat di jo aap ki peeth par bahut bhaari tha. Aap ko Laa-Makaan mein bula kar apne deedaar se musharraf kiya; yahan tak ke hamari shaan ke laaiq do kamaanon ke barabar, balki us se bhi kam faasla reh gaya. Aasmaanon ki poori saltanat mein aap ki qurbat aur manzilat ka charcha ho gaya.

Aur agar Hazrat Daud aur Hazrat Sulaiman عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ko duniya ki kuchh cheezon par hukumat di, to aap ko aalam-e-ulwi, yani aasmaanon par qudrat di. Wahan ke farishte khaadimon ki tarah aap ki baargaah mein haazir rehte hain aur aap ke dushmanon se aap ke sipahiyon ki tarah ladte hain. Aasmaanon mein koi cheez aisi nahin jo aap ki nubuwwat-o-risaalat se waaqif na ho ya aap ke hukm se inhiraf kare. (Rooh-ul-Bayaan, Al-Inshirah, tehat-ul-aayat: 1, 10/465; Al-Kalaam-ul-Awdah, s. 14, multaqatan)

### Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ka seena-e-mubarak kholna

Mufti Naeem-ud-Deen Muradabadi رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ likhte hain ke Allah Ta'ala ne Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ka seena hidayat, ma'rifat, naseehat, nubuwwat aur ilm-o-hikmat ke liye itna kushadah kar diya ke aalam-e-ghaib aur aalam-e-shahaadat us ki wus'at mein sama gaye. Jismani ta'alluqaat roohani anwaar ke liye rukawat na rahe aur ilme-ladunni, hukm-e-ilahi, ma'arif-e-rabbani aur haqeeqat-e-rahmani aap ke paak seene mein jalwahgar ho gaye. Baaz mufassireen ne is aayat mein zaahiri taur par seena-e-mubarak ka khulna bhi muraad liya hai. Ahadees mein hai ke bachpan mein, nuzool-e-wahi ke aaghaz par aur Shab-e-Mi'raaj mein Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne aap ka seena chaaq kiya, qalb-e-mubarak nikaala, sone ke tasht mein Aab-e-Zamzam se ghusl diya, noor-o-hikmat se bhara aur apni jagah rakh diya. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, Al-Inshirah, tehat-ul-aayat: 1, s. 1110; Khazin, 4/388; Rooh-ul-Bayaan, 10/461–462, multaqatan)

### Aala Hazrat ki naat

Aala Hazrat رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne Allah Ta'ala ke Habib ﷺ ki shaan bayan karte hue farmaya:

Asaalat-e-kul, imaamat-e-kul,  
Siyaadat-e-kul, amaarat-e-kul  
Hukoomat-e-kul, wilaayat-e-kul,  
Khuda ke yahan tumhare liye

Farishtay khidam, Rasool hashm,  
Tamaam-e-umam ghulaam-e-karam  
Wujood-o-adam, hudus-o-qidam,  
Jahan mein ayaan tumhare liye

Yeh Toor kahan, sipahr to kya,  
Ke Arsh-e-Ula bhi door raha  
Jihat se wara, wisaal mila,  
Yeh rif'at-e-shaan tumhare liye

Is aayat se maloom hua ke duniya ki haqeeqat aur aakhirat ke kamaal ka ilm seene ka khul jaana Allah Ta'ala ki bahut badi ne'mat hai. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **فَمَنْ یُّرِدِ اللّٰهُ اَنْ یَّهْدِیَهٗ یَشْرَحْ صَدْرَهٗ لِلْاِسْلَامِۚ- وَ مَنْ یُّرِدْ اَنْ یُّضِلَّهٗ یَجْعَلْ صَدْرَهٗ ضَیِّقًا حَرَجًا كَاَنَّمَا یَصَّعَّدُ فِی السَّمَآءِ** (Al-An'aam: 125). Tarjumah: Allah jis ko hidayat dena chahta hai us ka seena Islam ke liye khol deta hai, aur jis ko gumraah karna chahta hai us ka seena bahut tang kar deta hai.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas'ood رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ne poocha: “Seena kholne se kya muraad hai?” Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Woh noor jo momin ke dil mein daala jaata hai aur jis se us ka dil khul jaata hai.” Poocha gaya: “Is ki nishani kya hai?” Farmaya: “Aakhirat ki taraf ragbat, duniya se nafrat aur maut se pehle aakhirat ki tayyari.” (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, 8/126, hadees: 14)

Is se yeh bhi maloom hua ke Huzoor ﷺ Allah Ta'ala ke aise Habib hain jin ka muqaddas seena Allah Ta'ala ne baghair maange hidayat aur ma'rifat ke liye khol diya.

## Ayat 94:2

`Source: tafseerId=54436; ayatId=6185; surah=94; ayat=2; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ وَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ    (2)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur hum ne tumhare upar se tumhara bojh utaar diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ وَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَ: Aur hum ne tumhare upar se tumhara bojh utaar diya.}** Is bojh se ek qaul ke mutabiq woh gham muraad hai jo kafiron ke iman na laane ki wajah se Huzoor ﷺ ko hota tha. Doosre qaul ke mutabiq ummat ke gunahon ka gham muraad hai jis mein aap ka qalb-e-mubarak mashghool rehta tha. Muraad yeh hai ke ae Habib ﷺ! Hum ne aap ko maqbool-ush-shafaa'at bana kar gham ka woh bojh door kar diya jo aap ki peeth par bahut bhaari tha. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, 11/207–208; Khazin, 4/388–389; Khazain-ul-Irfan, s. 1110, multaqatan)

### Huzoor ﷺ ka gham-e-ummat

Kafiron ke iman na laane se Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ko kitna gham hota tha, iska andaaza is aayat se hota hai: **فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ عَلٰۤى اٰثَارِهِمْ اِنْ لَّمْ یُؤْمِنُوْا بِهٰذَا الْحَدِیْثِ اَسَفًا** (Al-Kahf: 6). Tarjumah: Agar woh is baat par iman na laayein to mumkin hai ke tum un ke peeche gham ke maare apni jaan ko khatam kar do.

Ummat ke liye aap ﷺ ki shafqat ka haal is aayat se bhi maloom hota hai: **لَقَدْ جَآءَكُمْ رَسُوْلٌ مِّنْ اَنْفُسِكُمْ عَزِیْزٌ عَلَیْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ حَرِیْصٌ عَلَیْكُمْ بِالْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ رَءُوْفٌ رَّحِیْمٌ** (At-Tawbah: 128). Tarjumah: Beshak tumhare paas tum mein se woh azeem Rasool tashreef laaye jin par tumhari mushakkat mein padna bhaari guzarta hai, woh tumhari bhalaai ke nihayat chaahne wale aur Musalmanon par bahut mehrbaan hain.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Amr bin Aas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا se riwayat hai ke Nabi ﷺ ne Hazrat Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ki yeh dua padhi: **رَبِّ اِنَّهُنَّ اَضْلَلْنَ كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ النَّاسِۚ فَمَنْ تَبِعَنِیْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنِّیْۚ- وَ مَنْ عَصَانِیْ فَاِنَّكَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ** (Ibrahim: 36), aur Hazrat Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ka yeh qaul padha: **اِنْ تُعَذِّبْهُمْ فَاِنَّهُمْ عِبَادُكَۚ- وَ اِنْ تَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ فَاِنَّكَ اَنْتَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ** (Al-Maaidah: 118). Phir Huzoor ﷺ par giryah taari ho gaya aur aap ne dua ki: “Ae Allah! Meri ummat, meri ummat.” Allah Ta'ala ne Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ko bheja aur farmaya ke mere Habib ﷺ se poochho unhein kya cheez rula rahi hai. Jab unhone arz pesh ki to Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya: “Hum aap ko aap ki ummat ke maamle mein raazi kar denge aur aap ko ghamgeen na karenge.” (Muslim, Kitaab-ul-Iman, hadees: 346)

Aala Hazrat ne is shafqat ko yun bayan kiya: “Ae jaan-e-bhai! Kya tum ne kabhi suna hai ke jo tum se sachchi mohabbat kare, woh tumhari buri haalat dekh kar bhi tum se raazi rahe? Woh mehboob kaisa hai jise Allah Ta'ala ne saare jahan ke liye rahmat bana kar bheja, jis ne apni zaat par ek aalam ka bojh utha liya aur tumhare gham mein din ka khaana aur raat ka sona chhor diya. Tum us ki na-farmaniyon mein mashghool ho aur woh tumhari bakhshish ke liye raat-din giryaan-o-malool hai.”

Woh raat ki tanhaai mein ummat ke liye dua karte, qabr-e-anwar mein “Ummati, Ummati” farmate aur Qiyamat ke din bhi “Ya Rabb! Ummati” keh kar shafaa'at farmayenge. Aise ghamkhwaar Aaqa ki fazeelatein mitaana aur un ki ita'at se munh modna kitni badi na-insaafi hai. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/716–717)

## Ayat 94:4

`Source: tafseerId=54437; ayatId=6187; surah=94; ayat=4; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَﭤ(4)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur hum ne tumhari khaatir tumhara zikr buland kar diya.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ: Aur hum ne tumhari khaatir tumhara zikr buland kar diya.}** Mufassireen ne is ki kai sooratein bayan ki hain. Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habib ﷺ par iman laana aur aap ki ita'at karna makhlooq par laazim kar diya. Kisi ka Allah Ta'ala par iman aur us ki ibaadat us waqt tak maqbool nahin hoti jab tak woh Nabi ﷺ par iman na laaye aur aap ki ita'at na kare. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **مَنْ یُّطِعِ الرَّسُوْلَ فَقَدْ اَطَاعَ اللّٰهَ** (An-Nisa: 80), yani jis ne Rasool ka hukm maana us ne Allah ka hukm maana.

Aur farmaya: **فَلَا وَ رَبِّكَ لَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ حَتّٰى یُحَكِّمُوْكَ فِیْمَا شَجَرَ بَیْنَهُمْ ثُمَّ لَا یَجِدُوْا فِیْۤ اَنْفُسِهِمْ حَرَجًا مِّمَّا قَضَیْتَ وَ یُسَلِّمُوْا تَسْلِیْمًا** (An-Nisa: 65). Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke jo shakhs Allah ki ibaadat kare magar Rasool ﷺ ki risaalat ki gawahi na de, us ka amal bekaar hai aur woh kaafir hi rahega.

Allah Ta'ala ke zikr ke saath Huzoor ﷺ ka zikr azaan, iqaamat, namaaz, tashahhud, khutbe aur bahut se maqamaat par kiya jaata hai. Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne farmaya: “Jab mera zikr kiya jaaye to mere saath aap ka bhi zikr kiya jaaye.” Hazrat Qatadah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne farmaya ke duniya aur aakhirat mein aap ka zikr buland kiya gaya; har khateeb aur har tashahhud padhne wala **اَشْهَدُ اَنْ لَّا ٓاِلٰـهَ اِلَّا اللّٰه** ke saath **اَشْهَدُ اَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَّسُوْلُ اللّٰه** kehta hai.

Allah Ta'ala ne Ambiya عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام se bhi aap ﷺ par iman laane ka ahd liya. Aala Hazrat farmate hain ke jahan Allah ka zikr hota hai wahan Habib ﷺ ka bhi charcha hota hai; aasmaan aur zameen aap ke naam se goonj rahe hain. Azaan dene wale, khateeb aur zikr karne wale Allah ke zikr ke saath aap ka naam lete hain. Dushman aap ke fazaail mitaane ki koshish karte rahe, lekin woh khud mit gaye aur aap ki shaan roz-ba-roz buland hoti rahi. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/718–719)

Rif'at-e-zikr hai tera hissa,  
Dono aalam mein hai tera charcha  
Murg-e-Firdous pas az hamd-e-Khuda,  
Teri hi madh-o-sana karte hain

**وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ** ka hai saaya tujh par,  
Bol baala hai tera, zikr hai ooncha tera  
Mit gaye, mitte hain, mit jaayenge a'da tere,  
Na mita hai na mitega kabhi charcha tera  
Tu ghataane se kisi ke na ghata hai na ghate,  
Jab badhaaye tujhe Allah Ta'ala tera

## Ayat 94:5

`Source: tafseerId=54438; ayatId=6188; surah=94; ayat=5; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًاۙ    (5)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًا: To beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai.}** Ae Habib ﷺ! Kuffar ke muqaable mein aap jo sakhti bardaasht farma rahe hain, us ke saath aasaani bhi hai: hum aap ko un par ghalba ata farmayenge. Baaz mufassireen ne kaha ke mushrikeen Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ aur Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ko faqr ki wajah se aib dilaate the. Allah Ta'ala ne tasalli di ke jald hi tangdasti ki yeh dushwaari door ho jaayegi. (Madarik, Al-Inshirah, tehat-ul-aayat: 6; Khazin, 4/389, multaqatan)

Is aayat se maloom hua ke mushkil, museebat ya dushwaari aane par ghabraana nahin chahiye. Allah Ta'ala se dua karte hue aasaani ki umeed rakhni chahiye. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **لَا یُكَلِّفُ اللّٰهُ نَفْسًا اِلَّا مَاۤ اٰتٰىهَاؕ-سَیَجْعَلُ اللّٰهُ بَعْدَ عُسْرٍ یُّسْرًا** (At-Talaaq: 7), yani Allah kisi jaan par us se zyada bojh nahin rakhta jitna use diya hai; jald Allah dushwaari ke baad aasaani ata farma dega.

## Ayat 94:6

`Source: tafseerId=54439; ayatId=6189; surah=94; ayat=6; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًاﭤ(6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًا: Beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai.}** Is aayat ko dobara zikr karne se maloom hua ke ek tangi ke baad do aasaaniyan hain. Hazrat Hasan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ muskurate hue tashreef laaye aur farmaya: “Ek tangi do aasaaniyon par hargiz ghaalib nahin aa sakti: to beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai, beshak dushwaari ke saath aasaani hai.” (Mustadrak, Kitaab-ut-Tafseer, hadees: 4004)

## Ayat 94:7

`Source: tafseerId=54440; ayatId=6190; surah=94; ayat=7; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْۙ    (7)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** To jab tum farigh ho to khoob koshish karo.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْ: To jab tum farigh ho to khoob koshish karo.}** Ek tafseer yeh hai ke ae Habib ﷺ! Jab namaaz se farigh hon to aakhirat ke liye dua karne mein mehnat karein, kyunke namaaz ke baad dua maqbool hoti hai. Is dua se muraad namaaz ke aakhir mein namaaz ke andar maangi jaane wali dua bhi ho sakti hai aur salaam pherne ke baad ki dua bhi.

Doosri tafseer yeh hai ke jab aap makhlooq ko deen ki daawat dene se farigh hon to apne Rab ki ibaadat mein mashghool ho jaayein. (Madarik, Al-Inshirah, tehat-ul-aayat: 7, s. 1359)

Is aayat se teen baatein maloom hoti hain: (1) Namaaz ke baad khaas taur par Allah Ta'ala se dua karni chahiye, kyunke Allah Ta'ala namaaz ke baad ki duaein qabool farmata hai. (2) Bande ko bekaar farigh nahin rehna chahiye aur na aise kaam mein lagna chahiye jis ka koi deeni ya dunyawi faaidah na ho. Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Insaan ke Islam ki khoobiyon mein se ek khoobi yeh hai ke woh us cheez ko chhor de jo use faaidah na de.” (Tirmizi, Kitaab-uz-Zuhd, hadees: 2324)

(3) Jo khateeb, waiz aur muballigh raat der tak logon ko naseehat karte hain, unhein is ke baad Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat ki koshish karni chahiye. Afsos ke baaz log raat ke aakhir tak taqreer mein chust rehte hain, magar apni namaaz mein susti karte hain. Unhein apni haalat par ghaur karna chahiye. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لِمَ تَقُوْلُوْنَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ(۲) كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ اَنْ تَقُوْلُوْا مَا لَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ** (As-Saff: 2–3), yani ae iman walo! Woh baat kyun kehte ho jo karte nahin? Allah ke nazdeek yeh bahut na-pasandeedah hai.

Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **اَتَاْمُرُوْنَ النَّاسَ بِالْبِرِّ وَ تَنْسَوْنَ اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ اَنْتُمْ تَتْلُوْنَ الْكِتٰبَؕ-اَفَلَا تَعْقِلُوْنَ** (Al-Baqarah: 44), yani kya tum logon ko neki ka hukm dete ho aur apne aap ko bhoolte ho, halaanke tum Kitaab padhte ho?

Hazrat Usaamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Qiyamat ke din ek shakhs ko Jahannam mein daala jaayega. Us ki aantain nikal kar bikhri hongi aur woh chaki ke gird ghoomte gadhe ki tarah ghoomega. Jahannam ke log poochhenge: “Tum to hamein neki ka hukm dete aur burai se rokte the!” Woh kahega: “Main tumhein neki ka hukm deta tha magar khud nahin karta tha, aur tumhein burai se rokta tha magar khud karta tha.” (Bukhari, Kitaab-ul-Bad-il-Khalq, hadees: 3267)

Hazrat Anas بن Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Shab-e-Mi'raaj kuchh aise log dikhaye gaye jin ke hont aag ki qainchiyon se kaate ja rahe the. Hazrat Jibreel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام ne bataya ke yeh woh khateeb hain jo logon ko neki ki daawat dete the magar apne aap ko bhool jaate the. (Musnad Abi Ya'la, hadees: 3979)

## Ayat 94:8

`Source: tafseerId=54441; ayatId=6191; surah=94; ayat=8; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ اِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ۠    (8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur apne Rab hi ki taraf ragbat rakho.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ: Aur apne Rab hi ki taraf ragbat rakho.}** Ae Habib ﷺ! Khaas taur par apne Rab ki taraf ragbat rakhein, Usi ke fazl ke talabgaar rahen aur Usi par tawakkul karein. Is aayat se maloom hua ke har Musalman ko Allah Ta'ala par bharosa karna aur Us se Us ka fazl maangna chahiye. Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: **وَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ فَلْیَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ** (Ibrahim: 11), yani Musalmanon ko Allah hi par bharosa karna chahiye.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas'ood رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ se riwayat hai ke Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Allah Ta'ala se Us ka fazl maango, kyunke Allah Ta'ala se maangna pasand farmata hai.” (Tirmizi, Ahadees-e-Shatta, baab fi intizaar-il-faraj, 5/333, hadees: 3582)
