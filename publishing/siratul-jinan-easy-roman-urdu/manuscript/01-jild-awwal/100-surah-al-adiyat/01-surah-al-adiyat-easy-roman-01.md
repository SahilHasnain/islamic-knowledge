# Surah Al-Adiyat — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 100:1

`Source: tafseerId=54471; ayatId=6246; surah=100; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ الْعٰدِیٰتِ ضَبْحًاۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** In ghodon ki qasam jo haanpte hue daurte hain.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَالْعٰدِیٰتِ ضَبْحًا: Un ghodon ki qasam jo haanpte hue daurte hain.}** Is aayat mein jin ghodon ki qasam bayan hui, un se muraad un mujahidon ke ghode hain jo jihad mein daurte hain aur un ke seene se awaaz nikalti hai. (Abu Saud, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 1, 5/896)

### “Un ghodon ki qasam jo haanpte hue daurte hain” se milne wale sabaq

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Naeemi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ ne is aayat se kuchh pyare nuqte bayan farmaye:

1. Is se maloom hua ke mujahidon ka martabah bahut buland hai, kyunke Allah Ta'ala ne un ke ghodon ki qasam bayan farmayi.

2. Jab mujahid ka ghoda apni peeth par mujahid ko bithata hai to us ghode ki shaan buland ho jaati hai. To jab Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ ne hijrat ki raat Sayyid-ul-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ko apne kandhe par uthaya, Hazrat Ali al-Murtazaٰ کَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم ne Sahba ke maqam par Huzoor Pur-Noor صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ko apne zaanu par sulaya, aur Hazrat Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا ne Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ke wisal ke waqt aap ka sar-e-mubarak apne seene par rakha, to un Aaminah khatoon aur Haleemah daai ki kya shaan hogi jinhone Huzoor-e-Aqdas صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ ko apni god mein khilaya.

3. Jab mujahid ke ghode ki saans barkat wali hai, kyunke Allah Ta'ala ne us ki qasam bayan farmayi, to Allah Ta'ala ka zikr karne wale ki saans bhi barkat wali hai aur us se shifa milti hai.

**{فَالْمُوْرِیٰتِ قَدْحًا: Phir apne sumon se patharon par maar kar chingariyan nikaalne walon ki.}** Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya: “Is se woh ghode muraad hain jo pathreeli zameen par chalte hain aur un ke sumon ki ragad se aag ki chingariyan nikalti hain.” (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 2, 11/259)

### Maqbool logon se door ki nisbat bhi izzat ka sabab hai

Yahan ek qabil-e-zikr nuqta hai ke jab ghazi ke ghode ke sum se pathar aur shola muta'alliq hue to Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein itne pyare ho gaye ke Allah Ta'ala ne qasam mein un ka bhi zikr farmaya. Is se maloom hua ke maqbool logon se door ki nisbat bhi izzat ka sabab banti hai.

## Ayat 100:3

`Source: tafseerId=54472; ayatId=6248; surah=100; ayat=3; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** فَالْمُغِیْرٰتِ صُبْحًاۙ    (3)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Phir subah ke waqt ghaarat kar dene walon ki.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{فَالْمُغِیرٰتِ صُبْحًا: Phir subah ke waqt hamla karne walon ki.}** Is aayat aur is ke baad wali do aayaat ka khulasa yeh hai ke un ghodon ki qasam jo subah ke waqt Islam ke dushmanon par hamla karte hain, phir daurte hue gard udaate hain aur usi waqt dushman ke lashkar mein be-khauf daakhil ho jaate hain.

Jab mujahidin Islam ke kisi dushman par hamla karne ka irada karte to raat bhar safar karte aur subah hamla kar dete. Is ka faidah yeh hota ke raat ke andhere mein woh nazar nahin aate the aur jab hamla karte to log ghafil aur jang ke liye tayyar nahin hote the. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 3, 11/260; Qurtubi, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 3, 10/114, Juz 20, multaqatan)

### Surah Al-Adiyat ki aayaat 3 se 5 se milne wale sabaq

In aayaat se chand baatein maloom hui:

1. Subah ke waqt aam taur par jihad ba-barkat hota hai. Balki us waqt kiya jaane wala har deeni aur dunyawi kaam barkat wala hota hai.

2. Jihad ke waqt ghodon ke daurne se jo gard udti hai woh bhi Allah Ta'ala ko pasand hai, kyunke woh Allah Ta'ala ki raah mein udne wali gard hai.

3. Dushman ke lashkar mein be-khauf daakhil hona bhi Allah Ta'ala ko pasand hai.

## Ayat 100:6

`Source: tafseerId=54473; ayatId=6251; surah=100; ayat=6; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الْاِنْسَانَ لِرَبِّهٖ لَكَنُوْدٌۚ    (6)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Beshak insaan zaroor apne Rab ka bada na-shukra hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{اِنَّ الْاِنْسَانَ لِرَبِّهٖ لَكَنُوْدٌ: Beshak insaan zaroor apne Rab ka bada na-shukra hai.}** Allah Ta'ala ne mujahidon ke ghodon ki qasmein bayan karne ke baad farmaya ke beshak insaan apne Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ ka bada na-shukra hai. Hazrat Abdullah bin Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا ne farmaya ke na-shukre se muraad woh insaan hai jo Allah Ta'ala ki ne'maton ka inkaar karta hai. Baaz mufassireen ne farmaya ke na-shukre se muraad gunahgaar insaan hai aur baaz ne farmaya ke is se woh insaan muraad hai jo museebaton ko yaad rakhta aur ne'maton ko bhool jaata hai. **{وَ اِنَّهٗ عَلٰى ذٰلِكَ لَشَهِیْدٌ: Aur beshak woh is baat par zaroor khud gawaah hai.}** Is aayat ka ek matlab yeh hai ke insaan apne amal se khud apne na-shukra hone par gawaahi deta hai. Doosra matlab yeh hai ke Allah Ta'ala insaan ke na-shukra hone par khud gawaah hai. (Khazin, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 7, 4/402)

## Ayat 100:8

`Source: tafseerId=54474; ayatId=6253; surah=100; ayat=8; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** وَ اِنَّهٗ لِحُبِّ الْخَیْرِ لَشَدِیْدٌﭤ(8)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Aur beshak woh maal ki mohabbat mein zaroor bahut shadeed hai.

**Easy Roman Urdu:** **{وَ اِنَّهٗ لِحُبِّ الْخَیْرِ لَشَدِیْدٌ: Aur beshak woh maal ki mohabbat mein zaroor bahut shadeed hai.}** Is aayat aur is ke baad wali teen aayaat ka khulasa yeh hai ke insaan maal ki mohabbat aur us ki talab mein to bahut mazboot aur taaqatwar hai, lekin Allah Ta'ala ki ibaadat karne aur Us ki ne'maton ka shukr ada karne mein kamzor hai. To kya maal ki mohabbat mein mubtala insaan nahin jaanta ke jab qabron mein jo log hain unhein uthaya jaayega aur Allah Ta'ala ki baargaah mein pesh kiya jaayega, aur jo kuchh seenon mein hai woh khol diya jaayega, to us waqt Allah Ta'ala unhein un ke nek aur bad aamaal ka badla dega? Beshak un ka Rab عَزَّوَجَلَّ Qiyamat ke din, jo faisle ka din hai, un ke aamaal, niyyaton, ita'at aur na-farmani ki khoob khabar rakhne wala hai, jaise ke woh hamesha se hai. (Tafseer-e-Kabeer, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayat: 8, 11/262; Samarqandi, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayaat: 8–11, 3/503–504; Madarik, Al-Adiyat, tehat-ul-aayaat: 8–11, s. 1369, multaqatan)

### Ghafil insaan ka sakht dil

Noor-ul-Irfan mein hai ke ghafil insaan maal ki mohabbat ki wajah se sakht dil hota hai, kyunke maal ki mohabbat dil ko sakht kar deti hai, jabke Huzoor صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ki mohabbat dil ki narmi ka sabab hai. Dekho, Yazeed, Fir'aun aur Shaddad jaanwaron se bhi zyada sakht dil the; un ki sakhti ka sabab maal ki mohabbat thi. Ya ghafil insaan maal ki mohabbat mein sakht dil hota hai aur deen ke maamle mein narm, yani deen ki mushkil bardasht nahin karta. Isi liye aam taur par log duniya ke liye woh mushqatein jhel lete hain jo deen ke liye nahin jhelte.

### Maal ki mohabbat ki chaar sooratein

Yaad rakhein ke maal ki mohabbat chaar tarah ki hoti hai:

1. **Hubb-e-imani:** Hajj waghera ke liye maal ki chaahat.
2. **Hubb-e-nafsani:** Apne aaraam aur rahat ke liye maal ki ragbat.
3. **Hubb-e-tughyaani:** Sirf maal jama karne aur peeche chhor jaane ke liye maal se mohabbat.
4. **Hubb-e-shaitani:** Gunah aur sarkashi ke liye maal ki mohabbat.

Yahan aakhri do mohabbatein muraad hain. Pehli qism ki mohabbat ibaadat hai. Hazrat Sulaiman عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام ne farmaya tha: **اِنِّیْۤ اَحْبَبْتُ حُبَّ الْخَیْرِ**۔ Huzoor صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ ko jihad ke ghodon se bahut mohabbat thi. Chunke maal khair ka bada zariya hai, is liye use khair kaha gaya.

Sufiya ke nazdeek kisi ne'mat se aisi mohabbat buri hai jo dil ko is tarah bhar de ke ne'mat dene wale ki mohabbat ke liye jagah na rahe. Yahi baat yahan muraad hai: dil ke andar sirf Yaar ki mohabbat ho aur ghair ki jagah na ho; baaqi mohabbatein dil ke baahar rahen. Kashti paani mein rahe to mehfooz hoti hai, lekin jab paani kashti mein aa jaaye to woh doob jaati hai.
