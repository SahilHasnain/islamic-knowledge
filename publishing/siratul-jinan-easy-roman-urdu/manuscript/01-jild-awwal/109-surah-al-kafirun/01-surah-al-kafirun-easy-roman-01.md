# Surah Al-Kafirun — Easy Roman Urdu

## Ayat 109:1

`Source: tafseerId=54493; ayatId=6316; surah=109; ayat=1; para=30; volume=10`

**Arabic:** قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْكٰفِرُوْنَۙ    (1)

**Tarjumah-e-Kanz-ul-Irfan:** Tum farmaao! Ae kaafiro!

**Easy Roman Urdu:** {قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْكٰفِرُوْنَ: Tum farmaao! Ae kaafiro!} Shaan-e-Nuzool: Quraish ke ek giroh ne Taajdaar-e-Risaalat ﷺ se kaha: Aap hamare deen ki pairwi karein, hum aap ke deen ki pairwi karein ge. Ek saal aap hamare maboodo ki ibaadat karein aur ek saal hum aap ke Mabood ki ibaadat karein ge. Rasool-e-Kareem ﷺ ne un se farmaya: “Is baat se Allah Ta'ala ki panaah ke main us ke saath kisi ghair ko shareek karun.” Kaafir kehne lage: To aap aisa karein ke hamare kisi mabood ko haath hi laga dein, hum aap ki tasdeeq kar dein ge aur aap ke Mabood ki ibaadat karein ge. Is par yeh Surah-e-Mubarakah naazil hui aur Sarkar-e-Do Aalam ﷺ Masjid-e-Haraam mein tashreef le gaye. Wahan Quraish ka woh giroh maujood tha jis ne Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ se yeh guftagu ki thi. Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ne yeh Surah unhein padh kar sunaayi to woh mayoos ho gaye. Phir unhone Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ aur Huzoor Pur-Noor ﷺ ke Sahaba-e-Kiraam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ ko aziyatein dena shuru kar dein. Yaad rahe ke is aayat mein woh kaafir muraad hain jo Allah Ta'ala ke ilm mein imaan se mehroom the aur kufr hi par marne wale the. (خازن، قل یا ایّہا الکافرون، ۴ / ۴۱۷-۴۱۸)

### Surah Al-Kafirun ke Shaan-e-Nuzool se haasil hone wali baatein

Is se chand baatein maloom hui:

1. Kaafiro se deeni sulah haraam hai, balki kai soorato mein yeh kufr hai.

2. Kaafiro ke buto aur un ke mazhabi ayyaam ki izzat samajh kar un ki ta'zeem karna kufr hai.

3. Momin ke dil mein kaafiro ki haibat nahin honi chahiye.

4. Kaafiro ko shar'i uzr ke baghair achhe alqaab se yaad na kiya jaaye.

5. Kaafir ko zaroorat ke waqt aur mauqe ki munaasibat se kaafir kehna durust, balki Qurani andaaz ke mutabiq hai.

{وَ لَاۤ اَنَا عَابِدٌ مَّا عَبَدْتُّمْ: Aur na main us ki ibaadat karunga jise tum ne pooja.} Is se chand baatein maloom hui:

1. Insaan dunyawi muamlaat mein narm ho, lekin deen mein intehaai mazboot ho, taake kaafir us se mayoos ho jaayein.

2. Huzoor-e-Aqdas ﷺ ko apne mustaqbil ka ilm tha ke Aap kabhi kufr, shirk aur fisq ka irtikaab nahin farmaate.

3. Musalman ko chahiye ke apne baare mein kaafiro ko mayoos kar de ke woh use deen se pher sakte hain.

{وَ لَاۤ اَنْتُمْ عٰبِدُوْنَ مَاۤ اَعْبُدُ: Aur na tum us ki ibaadat karne wale ho jis ki main ibaadat karta hoon.} Is se maloom hua ke Allah Ta'ala ne apne Habeeb ﷺ ko logon ke achhe aur bure khaatime ki khabar di hai ke kaun kufr par mare ga aur kise imaan par maut aayegi, kyunke yahan un kaafiro se kalaam ho raha hai jo kufr par marne wale the.

{لَكُمْ دِیْنُكُمْ وَ لِیَ دِیْنِ: Tumhare liye tumhara deen hai aur mere liye mera deen hai.} Yani tumhare liye tumhara kufr hai aur mere liye meri tauheed aur mera ikhlaas hai. Is kalaam ka maqsad kaafiro ko daraana hai, na ke un ke kufr se raazi hona. (خازن، الکافرون، تحت الآیۃ: ۶، ۴ / ۴۱۸)
