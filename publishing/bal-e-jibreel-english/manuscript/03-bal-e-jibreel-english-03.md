## Kalam 061: khudi-ho-ilm-se-mohkam-to-ghairat-e-jibreel

**Title:** If the Self Is Made Firm by Knowledge

**JSON title:** Khudi Ho Ilm Se Muhkam To Ghairat-e-Jibreel

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khudi-ho-ilm-se-mohkam-to-ghairat-e-jibreel/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

If the self is firm through knowledge, it is Gabriel's honor;
If firm through love, it is Israfeel's trumpet-call.

I know the torment of present-day knowledge,
For I was cast into this fire like Khalil.

The caravan is deceived by the destination; otherwise,
The joy of departure surpasses the comfort of arrival.

If you lack vision, do not sit in my circle of speech;
For the subtleties of selfhood are like a true-forged sword.

Those lessons of the West return to me today:
Where is the bliss of Presence, where the veil of proof?

It is a dark night; you are parted from your caravan;
For you, my song's flame is a lantern.

The tale of the Sanctuary is poor, simple, and colorful;
Its end is Husayn, its beginning Ismail.

## Kalam 062: maktabon-mein-kahin-raanai-e-afkar-bhi-hai

**Title:** The Beauty of Thought in the Schools

**JSON title:** Maktabon Mein Kahin Raanayi-e-Afkaar Bhi Hai?

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/maktabon-mein-kahin-raanai-e-afkar-bhi-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Is there anywhere in the schools a beauty of thought?
Is there anywhere in the cloisters a delight of secrets?

The wayfarer's destination is far, and hard as well;
Is there a caravan-leader in this caravan?

This struggle of faith and homeland rises beyond Khayber;
Is there any Haidar the Daring in this age?

Beyond knowledge's boundary, for the believing servant,
There is the joy of longing, and the blessing of vision.

The tavern elder says: Europe's palace
Is weak in foundation, and a mirror-wall as well!

## Kalam 063: hadsa-woh-jo-abhi-pardaay-aflak-mein-hai

**Title:** The Event Behind the Veil of the Heavens

**JSON title:** Hadsa Woh Jo Abhi Parda-e-Aflak Mein Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/hadsa-woh-jo-abhi-pardaay-aflak-mein-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

The event still hidden behind the veils of the heavens
Has its reflection in my mirror of perception.

Not in the star, nor in the turning heavens;
Your destiny lies in my fearless cry.

Either my sigh contains no living spark,
Or your dry grass and refuse still hold a little moisture.

What wonder if, through my dawn-hour song,
The fire within your dust should come alive?

This very dust will shatter the spell of night and day,
Though it lies tangled in the twists of destiny.

## Kalam 064: raha-na-halqa-e-sufi-mein-soz-e-mushtaqi

**Title:** The Passionate Heat Remained No More in the Sufi Circle

**JSON title:** Raha Na Halqa-e-Sufi Mein Souz-e-Mushtaqi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/raha-na-halqa-e-sufi-mein-soz-e-mushtaqi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

No heat remained in the Sufi circle of longing;
Only tales of miracles were left behind.

The Sultan's palace lies ruined, the dervish's cloister too;
Alas, the throne and prayer-mat: the height of trickery.

One day the Judge of Resurrection will be shamed
By the plain leaves of the Sufi's and mullah's book.

Not Chinese, nor Arab; neither Roman nor Syrian;
The man of horizons could fit in neither world.

The intoxication of the night-drinking is past, but
The Saqi's enchantment still pricks within hearts.

Bear my bitter speech in the garden,
For even poison sometimes performs the work of an antidote.

Dearer than the wealth of prince and sultan
Is the verse in which lightning's heat and brilliance burn.

## Kalam 065: huwa-na-zor-se-uss-ke-koi-gireban-chaak

**Title:** No Collar Was Torn by Its Force

**JSON title:** Huwa Na Zor Se Uss Ke Koi Greban Chaak

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/huwa-na-zor-se-uss-ke-koi-gireban-chaak/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

No collar was torn by its force,
Though the madness of the West was cunning too.

With wine of certainty, life's conscience is aflame;
O Lord, let this fire-water be the seminary's lot.

All these await the rise of earth-born Adam:
This Milky Way, these stars, these blue heavens.

Is this the whole universe of the present age--
A bright mind, a darkened heart, and a fearless gaze?

If you are sightless, this too blocks the gaze;
Otherwise the believer is fire, and the world dry grass and refuse.

The age has taken reason for its guiding torch;
Who knows that madness too possesses understanding?

The whole world is the believing man's inheritance;
The point of "Were it not for you" bears witness to my verse.

## Kalam 066: yun-hath-nahin-ata-vo-gohar-e-yak-dana

**Title:** Thus the Single Pearl Does Not Come to Hand

**JSON title:** Yun Hath Nahin Ata Woh Gohar-e-Yak Dana

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/yun-hath-nahin-ata-vo-gohar-e-yak-dana/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

Thus the single pearl does not come to hand:
O manly courage, one-heartedness and freedom!

Either the world-conquering law of Sanjar and Tughral,
Or the kingly manner of the dervish-man.

Either Farabi's wonder, or Rumi's fire and force,
Either the sage's thought, or the passion of Kaleem.

Either the fox-cunning of reason, or love's hand of God,
Either Europe's stratagem, or the Turkic assault.

Either Muslim law, or guardianship of the temple,
Or the drunken cry, whether Kaaba or idol-house.

In intoxication, poverty, kingship, or slavery,
Nothing succeeds without the boldness of a free spirit.

## Kalam 067: na-takht-o-taj-mein-ne-lashkar-o-sipah-mein-hai

**Title:** Neither in Throne and Crown nor in Army and Force

**JSON title:** Na Takht-o-Taaj Mein Ne Lashkar-o-Sipah Mein Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/na-takht-o-taj-mein-ne-lashkar-o-sipah-mein-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Neither in throne and crown, nor in army and force,
Is found the secret that lives in the dervish's court.

The world is an idol-house, and the man of truth is Khalil;
This is the point hidden in "There is no god but Allah."

That world is yours which you bring into being;
These stones and bricks are not the world within your sight.

Beyond the moon and stars lies the station that is his;
That handful of dust still wanders the roads.

From the gods of land and sea I have received this news:
Europe is caught in a flood without shore.

Seek your fortune in its open skies;
The new world is present in my dawn-born sigh.

Count my little gourd a treasure, for pure wine
Is found no longer in the seminary, nor in the dervish lodge.

## Kalam 068: fitrat-ne-na-bakhsha-mujhe-andeshaay-chalak

**Title:** Nature Granted Me No Cunning Thought

**JSON title:** Fitrat Ne Na Bakhsha Mujhe Andesha’ay Chalak

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/fitrat-ne-na-bakhsha-mujhe-andeshaay-chalak/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 4 stanzas, 8 lines

Nature granted me no cunning thought,
Yet my dust bears the power of flight.

That dust whose madness sharpens perception,
That dust from which Gabriel's robe is torn.

That dust which cares nothing for a nest,
Which gathers no chaff from the garden's wide floor.

Allah has granted this dust such tears
That their gleam makes the stars glisten with sweat.

## Kalam 069: karain-ge-ahl-e-nazar-taza-bastiyan-abad

**Title:** The People of Vision Will Build New Abodes

**JSON title:** Karain Ge Ahl-e-Nazar Taza Bastiyan Abad

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/karain-ge-ahl-e-nazar-taza-bastiyan-abad/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The people of vision will build new abodes;
My gaze does not turn toward Kufa or Baghdad.

This seminary, these young men, this joy and grace:
Their very breath keeps Europe's tavern flourishing.

I have no concern with the philosopher or the mullah;
One is the death of the heart, the other corruption of thought and sight.

To slight the city's jurist--what power have I?
Yet this remains: I seek the heart's opening.

The world's delights of Parviz can be bought;
The wealth of Farhad's grief is a gift from Allah.

I have revealed the mysteries of the dervish's way,
So that the seminary's and cloister's thought may be free.

The sage's fasts did not break the Brahmin's spell;
Without the staff, the work of Moses is built on nothing.

## Kalam 070: ki-haq-se-farishton-ne-iqbal-ki-ghamazi

The angels have informed God of Iqbal:
He is insolent; he adorns Nature with henna.

Earth-born he may be, but his ways are heavenly;
Neither Roman nor Syrian, Kashan nor Samarqand.

He taught the angels Adam's yearning,
And teaches Adam the manners of his Lord.

## Kalam 071: ne-muhrah-baqi-ne-muhrah-bazi

**Title:** No Piece Remains, No Game of Pieces

**JSON title:** Ne Muhra Baqi, Ne Muhra Bazi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ne-muhrah-baqi-ne-muhrah-bazi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

No piece remains, no game of pieces remains;
Rumi has won, and Razi has lost.

Jamshid's cup still shines with light;
Kingship is not secured by playing with glass.

The heart is Muslim, neither mine nor yours:
You too are at prayer, and I too am at prayer!

I know how that battle ends,
The battle in which I have joined the victorious.

Turkic is sweet, and Arabic is sweet;
Love's word is neither Turkic nor Arabic.

Azar's craft is stone-carving;
The work of Khalil is to soften stone.

You are life, you are abiding life;
All else that remains is only dust at play.

## Kalam 072: garam-e-faghan-hai-jaras-uth-ke-gya-qafla

**Title:** The Bell Is Hot with Lament; Rise, the Caravan Has Gone

**JSON title:** Garam-e-Gaghan Hai Jaras, Uth Ke Gya Qafla

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

The bell is hot with lament; rise, the caravan has gone!
Woe to the traveler who waits for the mount!

Your nature is one thing, your age another;
The cloister's order is not made for you.

Whether the heart is reason's slave or reason's imam,
Wayfarer, beware: this is a difficult stage.

His self is still captive between evening and dawn,
While his tongue complains of the turning of time.

Your breath has made the rose's fire burn brighter;
O garden bird, this is the reward of your song.

## Kalam 073: meri-nawa-se-huay-zinda-arif-o-aami

**Title:** Through My Song, the Gnostic and the Commoner Were Revived

**JSON title:** Meri Nawa Se Huwe Zinda Arif-o-Aami

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/meri-nawa-se-huay-zinda-arif-o-aami/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

Through my song the gnostic and the commoner came alive;
I gave them a taste for drinking fire.

Near the Sanctuary, some Persian sings a melody,
And the garments of ihram have been torn thread by thread.

The eternal truth is the station of Shabbir;
The ways of Kufa and Syria keep changing.

I fear the gamblers are seasoned players;
May your hand's failing not go unrewarded.

It would be no wonder if they granted the Muslim once more
Sanjar's grandeur and Junayd and Bastami's poverty.

The robe of knowledge and art is a special grace; otherwise,
Your gaze had seen my wretched disfigurement.

## Kalam 074: har-ek-maqam-se-agay-guzar-gya-mah-e-nau

**Title:** The New Moon Passed Beyond Every Station

**JSON title:** Har Ek Maqam Se Agay Guzar Gya Mah-e-Nau

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/har-ek-maqam-se-agay-guzar-gya-mah-e-nau/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

The new moon passed beyond every station;
Who has attained perfection without striving?

What if a bud opened through the force of breath,
When it was never granted a ray of the sun?

If your gaze is pure, then your heart is pure as well,
For God made the heart follow the gaze.

The burning tulip could not flourish in the garden,
For this world of wheat and barley is not its element.

Though the battles of Aibak and Ghori have vanished,
The song of Khusrau remains forever fresh and sweet.

## Kalam 075: kho-na-ja-iss-sehar-o-sham-mein-ae-sahib-e-hosh

**Title:** Do Not Lose Yourself in This Dawn and Dusk, O Wise One!

**JSON title:** Kho Na Ja Iss Sehar-o-Sham Mein Ae Sahib-e-Hosh!

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/kho-na-ja-iss-sehar-o-sham-mein-ae-sahib-e-hosh/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Do not lose yourself in this dawn and dusk, O wise one!
There is another world where neither tomorrow nor yesterday exists.

Who knows the station of tomorrow's tumult?
The mosque, the school, and the tavern have long been silent.

I found Him in the tears of the dawn watch,
The pure pearl for which the shell's embrace is empty.

The new civilization is nothing but affectation;
If the face is bright, what need is there for the seller of rouge?

The master of the instrument must not remain heedless;
Now and then even Sarosh strikes a wrong note.

## Kalam 076: tha-jahan-madrasa-e-sheri-o-shahanshahi

**Title:** Where the School of Lions and Sovereignty Once Stood

**JSON title:** Tha Jahan Madrasa-e-Sheri-o-Shehanshahi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/tha-jahan-madrasa-e-sheri-o-shahanshahi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Where once stood a school of lions and sovereignty,
Today, in those cloisters, there is only fox-like cunning.

I found no shepherding among the caravan leaders,
That shepherding which is the prelude to the speech of Kaleem Allah.

Where is the delight of melody for the sweet-voiced bird?
Alas, in this garden the breath falls short.

One intoxication and wonder is wholly dark,
One intoxication and wonder is complete awareness.

My lofty thought flashes like lightning,
So travellers may not wander through the darkness of night.

## Kalam 077: hai-yaad-mujhe-nuktaay-salman-e-khush-ahang

## Kalam 078: faqar-ke-hain-maujazat-taj-o-sareer-o-sipah

**Title:** The Miracles of Faqr Are Crown, Throne, and Army

**JSON title:** Phir Bad-e-Bahar Ayi, Iqbal Ghazal Khawan Ho

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/faqar-ke-hain-maujazat-taj-o-sareer-o-sipah/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The miracles of Faqr are crown, throne, and army;
Faqr is the lord of lords, Faqr is the king of kings.

The aim of knowledge is the purity of intellect and reason;
The aim of Faqr is the chastity of heart and sight.

Knowledge is jurist and sage; Faqr is Messiah and Kaleem;
Knowledge seeks the way; Faqr knows the way.

Faqr is the station of vision, knowledge the station of report;
In Faqr, ecstasy is merit; in knowledge, ecstasy is sin.

Knowledge has one "Being," and Faqr has another "Being":
"I bear witness that there is no god but Allah," "I bear witness that there is no god but Allah."

When the sword of selfhood rises upon Faqr's whetstone,
One soldier's blow accomplishes the work of an army.

If the heart is living and awake in this dust,
Your gaze will shatter the mirror of sun and moon.

## Kalam 079: kamal-e-josh-e-junoon-main-raha-garam-e-tawaf

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

In the height of passionate madness, I remained warm with circumambulation;
Praise be to Allah, the covering of the Sanctuary remained safe.

May this blessed accord be for the believers:
The jurists of my city are united in opposition to me.

Plato is restless between the Unseen and the Seen;
Since eternity, the station of the people of reason has been Araf.

Until the Book descends upon your conscience,
Neither Razi nor the author of the Kashshaf can untie the knot.

It is impermanent in delight and burning heat; otherwise,
Even the dregs of Europe's wine are not impure.

## Kalam 080: shaur-o-hosh-o-khird-ka-maamla-hai-ajeeb

**Title:** The Matter of Consciousness, Awareness, and Reason Is Strange

**JSON title:** Shaur-o-Hosh-o-Khirad Ka Maamla Hai Ajeeb

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/shaur-o-hosh-o-khird-ka-maamla-hai-ajeeb/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

The matter of consciousness, awareness, and reason is strange:
In the station of longing, all hearts and eyes are rivals.

I know what the gathering's fate will be;
The preacher is entangled in theoretical questions.

Though the bird of the garden circles my nest,
The bird of the garden has no share in my song.

I have heard that the Ottoman Turk is skilled in speech;
Who will recite to him this strange verse of Iqbal?

They think Europe is their neighbour,
Though the stars are nearer to their nests.
