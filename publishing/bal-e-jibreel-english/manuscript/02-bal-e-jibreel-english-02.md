# Bal-e-Jibreel: Poetic English Translation

## Kalam 031: hazar-khof-ho-lekin-zuban-ho-dil-ki-rafeeq-2

**Title:** Thousands of Fears, Yet the Tongue Is the Heart's Companion

**JSON title:** Hazar Khof Ho Lekin Zuban Ho Dil Ki Rafeeq

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/hazar-khof-ho-lekin-zuban-ho-dil-ki-rafeeq-2/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Though a thousand fears surround me, my tongue keeps faith with my heart;
This has been the qalandars' way since the Dawn of Eternity.

Why is the crowd so thick within the tavern?
For this alone: the Pir of the Magians is a gracious man.

They cannot cure the weakness of faith with their subtle arguments,
Though Razi's fine points may be poor, they are still precise.

The simple disciple has wept himself into repentance;
May God grant the Sheikh this same grace.

Adam remains captive in that ancient enchantment;
In his embrace still lie the idols of an age long past.

For me, even a confession spoken by the tongue is enough;
A thousand thanks that the mullahs are men who affirm the truth.

When there is love, even disbelief becomes Muslim faith;
When there is none, the Muslim man is a kafir and a heretic.

## Kalam 032: pooch-iss-se-ke-maqbool-hai-fitrat-ki-gawahi

**Title:** Ask Whether Nature Bears Witness to Your Acceptance

**JSON title:** Pooch Iss Se Ke Maqbool Hai Fitrat Ki Gawahi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/pooch-iss-se-ke-maqbool-hai-fitrat-ki-gawahi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Ask it: does nature bear witness that you are accepted?
Are you one who has reached the destination, or a traveler gone astray?

If the Muslim is a kafir, he is neither sovereign nor dervish;
If he is a believer, even in dervishhood he exercises sovereignty.

If you are a kafir, you place your trust in the sword;
If a believer, you fight as a warrior even without a sword.

If you are a kafir, you are subject to destiny, O Muslim;
If you are a believer, you yourself are the Divine decree.

I have torn open even the veil of secrets;
Your illness of blindness is ancient.

## Kalam 033: ye-hooriyan-e-farangi

**Title:** These European Houris, the Veil of Heart and Sight

**JSON title:** Ye Hooriyan-E-Farangi, Dil-O-Nazar Ka Hijab

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ye-hooriyan-e-farangi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

These European houris are a veil over heart and sight,
A Western paradise, its visions with their feet in the stirrups.

Carry the vessel of heart and sight with care;
The moon and stars are whirlpools in the sea of being.

It cannot be contained within the world of sound and speech;
The eternal subtlety is the lament of harp and rebab.

The Sufi has taught the city's jurist the ways of the khanqah,
And the jurist has been ruined by the Sufi.

That prostration before which the soul of earth would tremble,
The pulpit and the mihrab now yearn for it.

I did not hear in Egypt or Palestine that call to prayer
Which once gave the mountains a shiver like quicksilver.

O breeze of Cordoba, perhaps this is your effect:
My song carries the fire and delight of the age of youth.

## Kalam 034: dil-e-baidar-farooqi

**Title:** The Farooqi Heart Awake, the Karari Heart Awake

**JSON title:** Dil Baidar Farooqi, Dil Baidar Karari

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/dil-e-baidar-farooqi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

For Adam's copper, the heart's awakening is alchemy;
Awakeness of heart is the true philosopher's stone.

Create an awakened heart, for while the heart lies asleep,
Neither your blow is keen, nor is mine.

Its trace in the desert is found by a keen scent;
Conjecture and guesswork cannot bring the Tatar gazelle to hand.

How long shall I restrain my sigh from this fear,
That the Magian-born may carry away the spark of your fortune?

O God, where shall these simple-hearted servants of Yours go?
Dervishhood too is cunning, and sovereignty too is cunning.

Modern civilization has granted me such freedom
That outwardly I am free, but inwardly imprisoned.

O Master of Yathrib, bring healing to my affliction;
My wisdom is European, and my faith wears the Zunnari belt.

## Kalam 035: khudi-ki-shokhi-o-tundi-mein-kubr-o-naaz-nahin

**Title:** In the Boldness and Fierceness of the Self, No Pride or Vanity

**JSON title:** Khudi Ki Shokhi-O-Tundi Mein Kubr-O-Naaz Nahin

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khudi-ki-shokhi-o-tundi-mein-kubr-o-naaz-nahin/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

In the boldness and fierceness of the self there is neither pride nor vanity;
Even when there is pride, it is not a joyless, submissive need.

The gaze of love seeks a heart alive;
A dead quarry is not worthy of the royal falcon.

My song bears none of the beloved's grace,
For the trumpet-call of Israfil is not a caress to the heart.

I shall not ask wine from the Saqi of Europe,
For this is not the way of the pure and fearless lovers.

Love's dominion has never become common in the world;
The reason is that love does not shape itself to the age.

One ceaseless restlessness, whether absence or presence;
If I say it myself, my story is not long.

If you have the taste, read the Zabur of Ajam in solitude;
The midnight lament is not voiceless before the secret.

## Kalam 036: mir-e-sipah-na-saza-lashkar-yaan-shakistah-saf

**Title:** An Unworthy Commander, a Broken-Ranked Army

**JSON title:** Mir-e-Sipah Na Saza, Lashkariyan Shakista Saf

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/mir-e-sipah-na-saza-lashkar-yaan-shakistah-saf/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

An unworthy commander, an army in broken ranks—
Ah, that half-drawn arrow with no mark for its target!

In Your ocean there is no pearl of life anywhere;
Wave after wave I have searched, shell after shell I have seen.

Raise your hands from love of idols, plunge into your self;
Do not waste the heart's blood on the temple's forms and colors.

How can I speak plainly of the secret place of death and love?
Love is an honorable death; a life of dishonorable death.

The companionship of the Roman Master made this secret plain to me:
A hundred thousand sages hide their hands in their collars; one Moses stands head in hand.

If one who enters the battle were like Moses the Word-bearer,
Even now a cry of “Fear not!” rises from the tree of Sinai.

The splendor of Western wisdom could not dazzle me;
The dust of Madinah and Najaf is kohl for my eye.

## Kalam 037: zamistani-hawa-mein-garcha-thi-shamsheer-ki-taizi

**Title:** Though Winter's Wind Held the Sword's Sharpness

**JSON title:** Zmastani Hawa Mein Garcha Thi Shamsheer Ki Taizi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/zamistani-hawa-mein-garcha-thi-shamsheer-ki-taizi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Though winter's wind held the sword's sharpness,
Even in London I did not abandon the manners of rising before dawn.

Sometimes my warm speech was the gathering's capital;
Sometimes my reserve left everyone bewildered.

If the worker's hands held the reins of affairs, what then?
The path of the mountain-hewer holds those same stratagems of Parviz.

Whether it is the majesty of kingship or the democratic spectacle,
When faith is severed from politics, only Chingez remains.

In the realm of Great Rome, Delhi comes back to me:
The same lesson, the same grandeur, the same captivating glory.

## Kalam 038: ye-dair-e-kuhan-hai-anbar-e-khas-o-khashaak

**Title:** What Is This Ancient Temple but a Heap of Straw?

**JSON title:** Ye Dair-E-Kuhan Kya Hai, Anbaar-E-Khas-O-Khashaak

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ye-dair-e-kuhan-hai-anbar-e-khas-o-khashaak/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

What is this ancient temple but a heap of straw and chaff?
It is hard to pass through it without a cry of fire in the heart.

The tale of love's quarry is not a long one:
The pleasure is the arrow's sting, not the ease of the saddle-strap.

What was lost in the meaning of the seventy-two sects?
You will not understand until perception is stripped of color.

One is the law of Islam, and one is the passion of Islam;
The passion of Islam is the secret of the highest heavens.

O wise traveler, without the passion of Islam,
There is neither a road of action nor a branch of faith made moist.

Love's secrets are boldness and fearless daring;
Not every longing is bold, nor every passion fearless.

My madness will not sit idle on the Day of Resurrection:
Either my own collar torn, or the hem of Yazdan torn.

## Kalam 039: kamal-e-tark-nahin-aab-o-gill-se-mahjoori

**Title:** Perfection Is Not Abandoning Water and Clay

**JSON title:** Kamal-E-Tark Nahin Aab-O-Gil Se Mehjoori

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/kamal-e-tark-nahin-aab-o-gill-se-mahjoori/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Perfection is not to renounce water and clay;
Perfection is to master the earthly and the luminous.

O people of the circle, I have turned away from such poverty:
Your poverty is destitution and sickness.

Neither fit for poverty nor fit for sovereignty is that people
Who have squandered the treasure of Timur.

If the moon-faced Saqi does not listen, all the better;
The plea of excuse is the measure of companionship's warmth.

The sage, the knower, and the Sufi are all drunk on manifestation;
Who knows that revelation itself is the essence of concealment?

If they turn their attention to you, even the cage's corner is freedom;
If they do not, even the garden court is a place of compulsion.

Do not be offended—test it once and see:
Europe's heart is diseased, while its intellect is full to overflowing.

## Kalam 040: aqal-go-aastan-se-door-nahin

**Title:** Reason Is Not Far from the Threshold

**JSON title:** Aqal Go Astan Se Door Nahin

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/aqal-go-aastan-se-door-nahin/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 9 stanzas, 18 lines

Reason, they say, is not far from the threshold;
Yet presence is not written in its destiny.

Ask God also for a seeing heart;
The light of the eye is not the light of the heart.

There is rapture in knowledge too, but this is a Paradise
In which no houri dwells.

What a wonder that in this age
There is not even one possessor of rapture.

One madness is conscious as well;
Another madness is not conscious.

The heart's life is impatience—
Ah, that heart which knows no impatience!

Your death's secret is absence;
If you are alive, you are not without presence.

Every pearl has broken open its shell;
You alone are not ready to reveal yourself.

I say “Show me” as well, but this is not
The discourse of Moses and the Mount.

## Kalam 041: khudi-woh-beher-hai-jis-ka-koi-kinara-nahin

**Title:** The Self Is a Sea Whose Shore Is Nowhere

**JSON title:** Khudi Woh Behar Hai Jis Ka Koi Kinara Nahin

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khudi-woh-beher-hai-jis-ka-koi-kinara-nahin/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The Self is a sea whose shore is nowhere to be found;
Take it for a stream, and no remedy can be found.

You can shatter the spell of the sky's revolving dome;
This is a glass-built edifice, not a rugged stone.

We plunge into the Self, then rise again;
But such courage is not for the man who counts for nothing.

How can a star-reader know your station?
You are living dust, not a star's obedient one.

Here are Paradise, the houris, and Gabriel too;
Your gaze has yet to learn the boldness of seeing.

My madness knew the age through and through;
It gave me a garment that is not torn to shreds.

What a wonder: nature is miserly in the very heart of grace;
The pure ruby holds fire, but it holds no spark.

## Kalam 042: ye-peyam-de-gayi-hai-mujhe-bad-e-subah-gahi

**Title:** The Dawn Wind Has Brought Me This Message

**JSON title:** Ye Peyam De Gyi Hai Mujhe Bad-e-Subahgahi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ye-peyam-de-gayi-hai-mujhe-bad-e-subah-gahi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The dawn wind has brought me this message:
For those who know the Self, kingship is their station.

Your life comes from it, your honor comes from it;
If the Self remains, there is royalty; if it goes, disgrace.

You gave me no sign of the destination, O sage;
Why should I reproach you? You are neither guide nor traveler.

Within my circle of speech are still under instruction
Those beggars who know the ways of the crooked crown.

These matters are delicate; if it is Your will, do it;
For I found no pleasure in this monastic way.

You are the hunter of Huma; your beginning has only begun;
This world of bird and fish is not without wisdom.

Whether you are Arab or Ajam, your La ilaha illallah
Is a strange tongue until your heart bears witness.

## Kalam 043: teri-nigah-firo-maya-hath-hai-kotah

**Title:** Your Gaze Is Mean, Your Hand Is Short

**JSON title:** Teri Nigah Firomaya, Hath Hai Kotah

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/teri-nigah-firo-maya-hath-hai-kotah/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Your gaze is mean, your hand is short;
Your fault is the fault of a lofty palm.

The schoolmen have strangled your cry;
How can the call of La ilaha illallah arise?

The Divine is lost within the Self; seek it, heedless one;
This alone is now the counsel for your work.

Ask a bare-cloaked dervish about the heart's speech;
May God make you aware of your station.

You stand bareheaded: bring forth a lofty resolve;
Here a cap is only for the head of the Shaheen.

Neither the turning of the stars nor the play of the heavens:
The death of the Self is your fall from fortune and rank.

I rose grief-stricken from school and lodge;
There was no life, no love, no gnosis, no vision.

## Kalam 044: kirad-ke-paas-khabar-ke-siwa-kuch-aur-nahin

**Title:** Reason Has Knowledge and Nothing More

**JSON title:** Khird Ke Paas Khabar Ke Siwa Kuch Aur Nahin

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/kirad-ke-paas-khabar-ke-siwa-kuch-aur-nahin/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Reason has knowledge, and nothing more;
Your cure is vision, and nothing more.

Every station has another station beyond it;
Your life is nothing but the joy of journeying.

You are precious through guarding the Self; otherwise,
Within the pearl is nothing but the pearl's own water.

If blood is circulating in your veins, what is gained?
Life is nothing but the fire of the liver.

O bride of the tulip, veiling yourself from me is not fitting;
For I am nothing but the morning breeze.

What the merchants of the West deem a dead stock,
That thing is nothing but the treasure of art.

Iqbal is most generous, though bereft;
His gift is nothing but a flame's spark.

## Kalam 045: nigah-e-faqar-mein-shan-e-sikandari-kya-hai

**Title:** What Is Alexander's Glory to the Gaze of Faqr?

**JSON title:** Nigah-e-Faqr Mein Shan-e-Sikandari Kya Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/nigah-e-faqar-mein-shan-e-sikandari-kya-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

What is Alexander's glory to the gaze of faqr?
What is Caesarhood when its tribute is a beggar's due?

You place your hopes in idols and despair of God;
Tell me, what is kufr if not this?

Heaven has granted lordship to those
Who know nothing of the manner of serving a servant.

The heart's verdict comes through the gaze alone;
If the gaze lacks boldness, what is beauty?

For this fault the wrath of kings falls upon me:
I know what remains after Alexander's glory.

Who does not yearn for sovereignty? Yet
What sovereignty is that in which the Self must die?

The world has welcomed my qalandari;
Otherwise, what are my verses, what is poetry?

## Kalam 046: na-tu-zameen-ke-liye-hai-na-asman-ke-liye

**Title:** Neither for Earth nor Sky Are You

**JSON title:** Na Tu Zameen Ke Liye Hai Na Asman Ke Liye

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/na-tu-zameen-ke-liye-hai-na-asman-ke-liye/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 8 stanzas, 16 lines

You are not for earth, nor are you for the sky;
The world is for you, but you are not for the world.

Reason and heart are sparks from Love's flame;
That is for thorn and chaff, this for the reedbed.

This garden is the place where sigh and lament are nurtured;
It is neither for strolling among roses nor for a nest.

How long will your vessel remain in Ravi, Nile, and Euphrates,
When it is meant for the shoreless sea!

Those who once showed the stars the road
Now yearn for some man who knows the way.

A lofty gaze, heart-winning speech, a soul aflame—
This is the caravan-master's provision for the journey.

It was a little matter, but Ajam's anxiety
Made it larger, merely to adorn the tale.

In my throat is a song that troubles Gabriel,
Carefully kept for the placeless realm.

## Kalam 047: tu-ae-aseer-e-makan

**Title:** O Captive of Place, You Are Not Far from La-Makan

**JSON title:** Tu Ae Aseer-E-Makan! La-Makan Se Door Nahin

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/tu-ae-aseer-e-makan/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 9 lines

O captive of place, you are not far from La-Makan;
That place of manifestation is not far from your dust-vessel.

That meadow knows no fear of autumn;
Do not grieve: it is not far from your nest.

This is the essence of the dervish's knowledge: life
Is a leaping arrow, yet not far from the bow.

Your expanse lies just beyond the moon and Pleiades.

Rise: this station is not far from the sky.
Do not say to the guide, “Leave me alone”;

## Kalam 048: kirad-ne-mujh-ko-atta-ki-nazar-hakeemana

**Title:** Reason Gave Me a Philosophic Gaze

**JSON title:** Khirad Ne Mujh Ko Atta Ki Nazar Hakeemana

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/kirad-ne-mujh-ko-atta-ki-nazar-hakeemana/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Reason gave me a philosophic gaze;
Love taught me the tavern-keeper's discourse.

There is no wine, no flask, no measured round of cups;
The beloved's gathering is made radiant by the gaze alone.

Do not mistake my troubled song for poetry;
I am the confidant of the tavern's inward secret.

See the bud: it thirsts for the dawn breeze;
Within this lies the whole tale of my heart.

Tell me: is this absence, or is it presence?
All are familiar here; I alone am a stranger.

Let me stay another day in Farang,
If this wilderness can hold my madness.

Iqbal passed with ease beyond the station of reason;
In the station of longing, that wise one was lost.

## Kalam 049: aflak-se-ata-hai-nalon-ka-jawab-akhir

**Title:** At Last the Heavens Answer the Cries

**JSON title:** Aflak Se Ata Hai Nalon Ka Jawab Akhir

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/aflak-se-ata-hai-nalon-ka-jawab-akhir/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

From the heavens comes the answer to cries at last;
At last they address us, at last the veils rise.

In love's condition there is no difference at all:
Burning, heat, and fever at first; burning, heat, and fever at last.

I tell you what the destiny of nations is:
Sword and spear at first; peacock and rebab at last.

The customs of Europe's tavern are strange:
They bring ecstasy first, they give wine at last.

What is Nadir's pomp, what is Timurid splendor?
All their records drown in pure wine at last.

The hour of seclusion has passed; the hour of manifestation has come;
The cloud's embrace is about to be split by lightning at last.

Restraint was very hard before this flood of meaning;
At last the Qalandar spoke the secrets of the Book.

## Kalam 050: har-cheez-hai-mehew-e-khud-numai

**Title:** Everything Is Absorbed in Self-Display

**JSON title:** Har Cheez Hai Mehew-e-Khud Numai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/har-cheez-hai-mehew-e-khud-numai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 8 stanzas, 16 lines

Everything is absorbed in self-display;
Every particle is a martyr to Divine Majesty.

Life without the relish of self-revelation is death;
In building the self lies godhood.

By the strength of the self, a grain of sand becomes a mountain;
By the weakness of the self, a mountain becomes a grain of sand.

The stars wander, with little fellowship among them;
Separation is the destiny of existence.

This yellow-faced moon of the late watch of night
Knows neither secret nor the intimacy of a lover.

Your heart is your lamp;
You yourself are your own light.

You alone are the Truth in this world;
All else is the display of alchemy.

These desert thorns untie the knots;
Lessen your complaint of going barefoot.

## Kalam 051: ejaz-hai-kissi-ka-ya-gardish-e-zamana

**Title:** Is It Someone's Miracle, or the Turning of Time?

**JSON title:** Ejaz Hai Kisi Ka Ya Gardish-e-Zamana!

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ejaz-hai-kissi-ka-ya-gardish-e-zamana/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Is it someone's miracle, or the turning of time?
Asia's enchanted Western spell has broken.

From building a nest I learned this secret:
For the people of song, the nest itself is lightning.

This is servitude to God; that is servitude to beggary:
Become a servant of God, or a servant of the age!

Do not be heedless of the self; stand guard over it—
Perhaps you too are the threshold of some Sanctuary.

O heir of La ilaha! Nothing remains in you
Of the lover's speech, the conqueror's character.

Hearts once trembled in their breasts at your gaze;
Your qalandar-like passion has been lost.

Perhaps Iqbal knows the secret of the Sanctuary;
The manner of his speech bears the intimacy of its keepers.

## Kalam 052: khirad-mandon-se-kya-poochun-ke-meri-ibtada-kya-hai

**Title:** What Should I Ask the Wise About My Beginning?

**JSON title:** Khirad Mandon Se Kya Poochun

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khirad-mandon-se-kya-poochun-ke-meri-ibtada-kya-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

What should I ask the wise of wit: what was my beginning?
For I remain absorbed in thought: what will be my end?

Raise the self so high that, before each destiny,
God Himself asks the servant: tell Me, what is your desire?

What is the station of speech if I am an alchemist?
This very burning breath is my alchemy; what is my alchemy?

Let the depths of destiny appear to me within it;
Ask me not, O companion, what that kohl-dark eye may be.

Had that European mystic lived in this age,
Iqbal would have taught him what the station of Divine Majesty is.

The dawn-song has made my liver bleed;
O God, what is the sin for which this is the punishment?

## Kalam 053: jab-ishq-sikhata-hai-adab-e-khud-agahi

**Title:** When Love Teaches the Manners of Self-Awareness

**JSON title:** Jab Ishq Sikhata Hai Adab-e-Khud Agahi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/jab-ishq-sikhata-hai-adab-e-khud-agahi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

When love teaches the manners of self-awareness,
The secrets of imperial sovereignty open before slaves.

Whether Attar, Rumi, Razi, or Ghazali,
Nothing comes to hand without a dawn-splitting sigh.

Do not despair of them, O wise guide!
They strive little, but the travelers are not without taste.

O bird of the heavenly realm! Better death than that sustenance
By which your flight grows weak.

Better than Dara and Alexander is that dervish man
Whose dervishhood bears the scent of Asadullah.

The code of young men is truth-speaking and fearlessness;
Allah's lions know no fox-like cowardice.

## Kalam 054: mujhe-aah-o-fighan-e-neem-shab-ka-phir-peyam-aya

**Title:** The Message of Midnight Sighs Has Come Again

**JSON title:** Mujhe Aah-o-Faghan-e-Neem Shab Ka Phir Peyam Aya

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/mujhe-aah-o-fighan-e-neem-shab-ka-phir-peyam-aya/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

The message of midnight sigh and lament has come to me again;
Halt, O traveler, perhaps another difficult station has arrived.

Dive yourself a little into destiny's depths;
For from this field of war I have come forth a naked sword.

Who wrote this line upon the mosque's mihrab?
These simpletons fell into prostration when the hour of standing came.

Come, O watcher who came to see the spectacle of my poverty;
That gathering rose the moment the distant cup reached me.

Iqbal gave the Muslims of India his own burning pain;
He was a comfort-loving man, yet he came to serve the comfort-lovers.

I kept seeking Iqbal for years;
After a long while, at last that Shaheen came beneath the snare.

## Kalam 055: na-ho-tughyan-e-mushtaqi-to-main-rehta-nahin-baqi

**Title:** Without the Uprising of Longing, I Do Not Remain

**JSON title:** Na Ho Tughyan-e-Mushtaqi To Main Rehta Nahin Baqi

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/na-ho-tughyan-e-mushtaqi-to-main-rehta-nahin-baqi/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Without the uprising of longing, I do not remain;
What is my life? This very uprising of longing.

Nature keeps compelling me, again and again, to sing;
Perhaps someone who knows pain still remains in the gathering.

That fire can still burn your nest today;
If your longing is not sincere, then what complaint against the Saqi?

Do not judge the West by its brilliance;
The brilliance of this jewel comes from electric lamps.

No surges of world-conquering zeal rise in hearts
If a world-embracing manner does not arise in the eyes.

Even in autumn, how could I have fallen within the hunter's reach?
The sparse leaves of my nest betrayed me.

Plans will be overturned, destinies will change;
It is reality, not the creative power of my imagination.

## Kalam 056: fitrat-ko-khirad-ke-ru-ba-ru-kar

**Title:** Set Nature Before Wisdom

**JSON title:** Fitrat Ko Khird Ke Ru-Ba-Ru Kar

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/fitrat-ko-khirad-ke-ru-ba-ru-kar/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Set nature face to face with wisdom;
Conquer the realm of color and fragrance.

You have lost your selfhood—
Seek out the thing that you have lost.

The stars' expanse is boundless;
Make this station, too, the object of your longing.

The houris of your garden stand unveiled;
Stitch up the torn robes of rose and tulip.

Nature is not without taste, though—
Do what it could not do!

## Kalam 057: ye-piran-e-kaleesa-o-haram-ae-waye-majboori

**Title:** These Elders of Church and Sanctuary, Alas, This Helplessness

**JSON title:** Ye Peeran-e-Kalisa-o-Haram, Ae Waye Majboori!

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ye-piran-e-kaleesa-o-haram-ae-waye-majboori/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

These elders of church and Sanctuary—alas, this helplessness!
The fruit of their bitter toil is the darkness of hearts.

Awaken certainty, O heedless one! Through certainty comes
That dervishhood before which the emperor bows.

Now wonder, now rapture, now the dawn's sigh—
My pain of separation changes into a thousand hues.

The matters of love and rapture lie beyond the reach of reason;
This much I understand: distance is the heart's death.

They are compelled to manifest by the intoxication of their beauty;
Within the sight of my eyes lie the causes of concealment.

No one could understand the logic of destiny, otherwise
The Ottomans would not be lesser Turks than Timur's Turks.

How did Iqbal come into the hands of the dervishes of the Sanctuary?
The camphor Shaheen is not granted to kings and sultans.

## Kalam 058: taza-phir-danish-e-hazir-ne-kiya-sehar-e-qadeem

**Title:** Once More the Present Wisdom Has Wrought the Ancient Spell

**JSON title:** Taza Phir Danish-e-Hazir Ne Kiya Sahar-e-Qadeem

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/taza-phir-danish-e-hazir-ne-kiya-sehar-e-qadeem/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

Once more the wisdom of the present has wrought the ancient spell;
In this age, no passing beyond is possible without Moses' staff.

Reason is cunning—it can assume a hundred forms;
Poor love is neither mullah, ascetic, nor sage.

The ease of the destination is forbidden to love's poor;
All are travelers, though outwardly they seem settled.

You move heavily, burdened by grief for provisions and the journey;
You can cross mountain and river like the breeze.

The dervish man's treasure is freedom and death;
Is this treasury of gold and silver for someone else's sake?

## Kalam 059: sitaron-se-agay-jahan-aur-bhi-hain

**Title:** Beyond the Stars Lie Other Worlds as Well

**JSON title:** Sitaron Se Agay Jahan Aur Bhi Hain

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/sitaron-se-agay-jahan-aur-bhi-hain/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Beyond the stars lie other worlds as well;
There are still more trials of love to come.

These expanses are not empty of life;
Here are hundreds of caravans as well.

Do not be content with the world of color and fragrance;
There are other gardens, other nests as well.

If one nest has been lost, what grief?
There are other stations of sigh and lament as well.

You are a Shaheen; flight is your vocation;
There are other skies before you as well.

Do not remain entangled in this day and night,
For you have other times and spaces as well.

The days are gone when I stood alone in the gathering;
Now there are other keepers of my secrets here as well.

## Kalam 060: dhoond-raha-hai-farang-aysh-e-jahan-ka-dawam

**Title:** The West Seeks the Perpetuity of Worldly Pleasure

**JSON title:** (فرانس میں لِکھّے گئے)Dhoond Raha Hai Farang Aysh-e-Jahan Ka Dawam

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/dhoond-raha-hai-farang-aysh-e-jahan-ka-dawam/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The West seeks the perpetuity of worldly pleasure—
Alas, the raw desire, alas, the raw desire!

The elder of the Sanctuary heard my account and said:
Your lament is ripe now; no longer hold it in your heart.

Though Moses said, “Show me,” I do not say, “Show me”;
The demand was fitting for him, but forbidden for me.

Though the cry of those who see unveils the secret,
The way of the reveller can never become common.

In the Sufi circle there is remembrance, dry and without fire or music;
I too remained thirsty, and you too remained thirsty.

Love is your limit, love is my limit;
You are still unfinished, and I too am still unfinished.

Alas, you have lost the secret of dervishhood;
Otherwise, the dervish's wealth is the kingdom of Rome and Syria.
