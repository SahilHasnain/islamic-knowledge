## Kalam 101: khudi-ke-zor-se-dunya-pe-cha-ja

**Title:** By the Strength of Self, Rise Over the World

**JSON title:** Khudi Ke Zor Se Dunya Pe Chha Ja

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khudi-ke-zor-se-dunya-pe-cha-ja/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 2 stanzas, 4 lines

By the strength of self, rise over the world;
Grasp the secret of the realm of colour and scent.

Like the sea, remain acquainted with the shore;
Keep drawing your hem away from the shore's edge.

## Kalam 102: chaman-mein-rakht-e-gul-shabnam-se-tar-hai

**Title:** The Garden's Flower-Dress Is Wet with Dew

**JSON title:** Chaman Mein Rakht-e-Gul Se Tar Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/chaman-mein-rakht-e-gul-shabnam-se-tar-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 2 stanzas, 4 lines

The garden's flower-dress is wet with dew;
Jasmine, green grass, and the dawn wind are here.

Yet no tumult can grow warm here;
This garden's poppy is without the fire of the heart.

## Kalam 103: khirad-se-rahru-roshan-basar-hai

**Title:** Through Reason, the Wayfarer Sees Clearly

**JSON title:** Khirad Se Rahru Roshan Basar Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/khirad-se-rahru-roshan-basar-hai/

**Source trace:** JSON `urdu` and `transliteration`; 2 stanzas, 4 lines

## Kalam 121: iqbal-ne-kal-ahl-e-khayaban-ko-sunaya
