Kalam 001: My Song of Longing

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/meri-nawa-e-shauk/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

My song of longing shakes the Sanctuary of the Essence;
In the idol-house of attributes, cries of refuge rise.
Houris and angels are captives in my imaginings;
My gaze disturbs the revelations of You.
Though my quest has traced the forms of temple and Sanctuary,
My lament brings resurrection to Kaaba and Somnath.
At times my piercing gaze cleaves the heart of Being;
At times, entangled, it is stranded in my conjectures.
What wonder have You wrought? You have laid me bare as well;
I alone was a secret in the breast of the universe!

Kalam 002: If the Stars Have Strayed

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/agar-kaj-ro-hain-anjum-asman-tera-hai-ya-mera/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

If stars have strayed awry, is the sky Yours or mine?
Why should I trouble over the world: is the world Yours or mine?
If the placeless realm is empty of the tumult of longing,
Whose fault is it, O Lord? Is the placeless realm Yours or mine?
How did he dare deny at the Dawn of Eternity?
What do I know: is that keeper of secrets Yours or mine?
Muhammad ﷺ is Yours, Gabriel is, Quran is Yours;
But this sweet word, this interpreter: is it Yours or mine?
By the radiance of this one star Your world shines bright;
The downfall of earth-born Adam: is it loss Yours or mine?

Kalam 003: There Is No Wine Left in Your Glass

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/tere-shishe-mein-mai-baqi-nahin-hai/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 2 stanzas, 4 lines

There is no wine left in Your glass;
Tell me, are You not my Saqi?
To the thirsty, from an ocean, a dewdrop:
Is this generosity, or is it miserliness?

Kalam 004: Make the Radiant Tresses Yet More Radiant

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/gaisu-e-tabdar-ko-aur-bhi-tabdar-kar/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Make the radiant tresses yet more radiant;
Make reason and understanding your prey, make heart and vision your prey.
Let love be veiled, let beauty too be veiled;
Either reveal Yourself, or unveil me.
You encompass the shoreless; I am but a little stream;
Either bring me to Your shore, or make me shoreless.
I am a shell; in Your hands rests the honor of my pearl;
If I am mere potsherd, make me a royal pearl.
If the new song of spring is not within my fate,
Make this half-burning breath a little bird of spring.
Why did You command me to journey from the Garden of Paradise?
The world's task stretches long; now wait for me.
When my book of deeds is brought forth on Judgment Day,
Be ashamed Yourself, and make me ashamed as well.

Kalam 005: Whether It Moves You or Not, Hear My Cry

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/asar-kare-na-kare-sun-to-le-meri-faryad/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Whether it moves You or not, at least hear my cry;
This free servant seeks no reward.
This handful of dust, this desert wind, this vastness of skies:
Is it grace, or is it cruelty, Your delight in creation?
The flower's tent could not stand in the garden wind;
Is this the springtime? Is this the wind of fulfillment?
I am guilty, a stranger far from home, yet
Your ruined realm could not be made habitable even by angels.
He blesses my hunger for hardship:
That simple wilderness, that groundless world of Yours.
A nature that loves danger finds no ease
In a garden where no hunter lies in wait.
The station of longing is not for Your holy ones;
It belongs to those whose courage runs high.

Kalam 006: What Is Love to a Borrowed Life?

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/kya-ishq-aik-zindagi-e-mastaar-ka/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 5 stanzas, 10 lines

What is love to a life on loan,
What is love of the lasting for the fleeting?
The love whose candle death can snuff with one breath
Has no delight of burning, no delight of waiting.
What is my poor measure? The heat and brilliance of one breath;
Why should a spark wrestle with a flame where it does not belong?
First grant me life everlasting;
Then watch the longing and delight of this restless heart.
Give me a thorn whose prick shall never fade;
O Lord, a pain whose ache shall never fade!

Kalam 007: Make Our Hearts the Home of Love and Mercy

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 2 stanzas, 4 lines

Make our hearts the center of mercy and fidelity;

To the one You have given bread of barley,
Grant also the arm of Haider.

Kalam 008: Lest My Dust, Distressed, Become a Heart

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/preshaan-ho-ke-meri-khaak/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

Distressed, may my dust at last become a heart;
What is difficult now, O Lord, may not become difficult once more.
Do not let the houris of Paradise compel me into song;
May my inward burning not become the warmth of the gathering.
The wayfarer sometimes remembers the destination he left behind;
May this ache within my breast not become the grief of the destination.
Love has made me a shoreless sea;
May this watchfulness over myself not become my shore.
Even in this colorless, scentless world, may my seeking not become
That same old tale of trailing after the camel-litter.
The ascent of earth-born Adam makes the stars tremble:
Lest this broken star become the perfect moon.

Kalam 009: The World Is Turned Upside Down, O Saqi

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/digargoon-hai-jahan-taron-ki-gardish-taez-hai-saqi/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The world is turned upside down; the stars whirl fast, O Saqi;
In every atom, the tumult of resurrection roars, O Saqi.
The treasure of faith and knowledge has been looted from Allah's people;
Whose infidel glance is this, blood-shedding, O Saqi?
The same old sickness, the same old weakness of the heart;
The same water of joy-awakening is its cure, O Saqi.
No fire of longing rises in the heart of the Sanctuary;
Your appearing still comes clothed in the veil, O Saqi.
No Rumi has risen again from Ajam's tulip fields;
The same Iranian clay and water, the same Tabriz, O Saqi.
Iqbal is not without hope for his ruined field:
Let it receive a little rain, and this soil is rich, O Saqi.
The wayfaring faqir has been granted secrets of sovereignty;
Let the wealth of Parviz flow through my song, O Saqi.

Kalam 010: Bring Once More That Wine and Cup, O Saqi

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/la-phir-aik-bar-wohi-badah-o-jaam-ae-saqi/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Bring once more that wine and cup, O Saqi;
Let my true station come into my hands, O Saqi.
For three hundred years the wine-houses of Hind have been shut;
Now it is fitting that Your grace flow freely, O Saqi.
Only a little remained in my wine-cup of ghazal;
The Sheikh declares that even this is forbidden, O Saqi.
The lion-hearted have deserted the wilderness of inquiry;
Only the slaves of Sufi and Mullah remain, O Saqi.
Who has carried off love's liver-piercing sword?
In the hand of knowledge, only an empty sheath, O Saqi.
When the breast is lit, the burning word is life itself;
When it is not lit, speech is everlasting death, O Saqi.
Do not leave my night bereft of moonlight;
Your cup holds the full moon, O Saqi.

Kalam 011: My Saqi Erased the World of I and You

JSON title: Mita Diya Mere Saqi Ne Alam-e-Maan-o-Tu(This cup divine has set me free from the illusive world of sense)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/mita-diya-mere-saqi-ne-alam-e-maan-o-tu/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

My Saqi erased the world of I and you,
Pouring me the wine of ’لاَ اِلٰہَ اِلاَّ ھُو‘.
No wine, no verse, no Saqi, no clamor of string and drum—
Only the mountain's silence, the stream's lip, the wild tulip.
See the fearless dignity of the tavern's beggar:
Reaching the Fountain of Life, he smashes his little cup.
My little cup is precious in this age,
For the Sufis' gourds lie empty in the monastery.
I am newly come; concealment is better for me,
For my gaze is wilder still than my heart.
Though his place is in the waves of the sea,
The pearl's ablution is the purity of its nature.
More beautiful are rose and tulip through his grace;
In the colorful-voiced poet's gaze, there is magic.

Kalam 012: Priceless Treasure Is the Pain and Burning of Longing

JSON title: Mata-e-Bebaha Hai Dard-o-Souz-e-Arzoo Mandi (Slow fire of longing, wealth beyond compare)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/mata-e-bebaha-hai-dard-o-souz-e-arzoo-mandi/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Pain and the burning of longing are treasure beyond price;
Give me the station of servitude—I will not take the majesty of lordship.
For Your free servants, neither this world nor that:
Here, the bondage of dying; there, the bondage of living.
The veil is an elixir for the wanderer in love's lane;
Your long withdrawal fans the fire within me.
He can pass his days among mountain and wilderness,
For nest-building is humiliation to the falcon.
Was this the grace of a gaze, or the miracle of a school?
Who taught Ismaeel the manners of a son?
My grave is a place of pilgrimage for the people of resolve;
I taught the dust of the road the secret of Alwand.
What need has beauty of meaning for my adornment?
Nature itself applies henna to the tulip.

Kalam 013: Have You Forgotten That Age of My Heart?

JSON title: Tujhe Yaad Kya Nahin Hai Mere Dil Ka Woh Zamana (Have You forgotten then my heart of old)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/tujhe-yaad-kya-nahin-hai-mere-dil-ka-woh-zamana/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

Have You forgotten that age of my heart,
That sanctuary of love, that whip of the gaze?
These idols of the present age, fashioned in the school:
Neither the unbeliever's grace nor Azar's art of carving.
There is no quiet corner in this open expanse;
What a strange world this is—neither cage nor nest.
The vine's vein waits for the rain of Your grace,
For no Magian wine remains in Ajam's taverns.
My fellow birds took this for the touch of spring;
What do they know of this lover's song?
From my dust and blood You brought this world to birth;
What is a martyr's recompense? Everlasting ardor and radiance.
By Your nurture of Your servant, my days pass on;
I have no complaint against friends, no grievance against the age.

Kalam 014: The Poppy's Heart Is Brimming with Red Wine

JSON title: Zameer-e-Lala Mai-e-Laal Se Huwa Labraiz(By dint of Spring the poppy‐cup, With vintage red is over‐flown)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/zameer-e-lala-mai-e-laal-se-huwa-labraiz/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The poppy's heart is brimming with ruby wine;
At the first sign, the Sufi broke his abstinence.
Wherever Love has spread its board,
It has made the faqirs heirs to Parviz.
These stars are ancient, and the sky is worn;
I want a world that is newborn still.
Who knows what the tumult of resurrection is?
The turning of Your gaze is my resurrection.
Do not snatch from me the delight of the dawn prayer's sigh;
Do not make neglect in Your gaze seem a form of favor.
The season of flowers does not suit the sorrowful heart;
The garden bird's cry is far too joy-awakening.
It is the speech of the heedless: make peace with the age;
If the age will not make peace with you, then fight the age.

Kalam 015: The Same Ill Fortune, the Same Divine Indifference

JSON title: Wohi Meri Kam Naseebi, Wohi Teri Be-Niazi(Mine ill luck the same and same, O Lord, the coldness on Your part)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/wohi-meri-kam-naseebi-wohi-teri-be-niazi/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

The same ill fortune of mine, the same indifference of Yours;
This mastery of song has brought me nothing.
Where am I, and where are You? Is this place or the placeless?
Is this world mine, or is it Your wonder-working?
In this struggle passed the nights of my life:
Now Rumi's burning music, now Razi's tortuous thought.
That deceived falcon, raised among vultures—
What can it know of the ways and rites of falconry?
I have no tongue for ghazal, nor am I versed in speech;
Let there be some heart-winning cry, Persian or Arabic.
In poverty and kingship there is no such distinction:
Here the army's sword-play, there the gaze's sword-play.
One broke away from the caravan, one grew distrustful of the Sanctuary;
For the caravan leader has no habit of winning hearts.

Kalam 016: I Thought My Racecourse Lay Beneath the Sky

JSON title: Dilon Ko Markaz-e-MApni Jolangah Zair-e-Asman Samjha Tha Main(Methought my racing field lay under the skies)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/apni-jolangah-zair-e-asman-samjha-tha-main/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

I thought my racecourse lay beneath the sky;
I thought the play of dust and water was my world thereby.
When Your unveiling broke the spell upon my gaze,
I had thought the blue mantle was the sky's own blaze.
The caravan grew weary in the twists of air;
I thought sun, moon, and Jupiter companions there.
One leap of love has brought the tale to its end;
I thought this earth and sky had no bound or bend.
The veiled ones of longing told love's secret true;
The cry I thought restrained was a cry of longing too.
It was a weary traveler's cry of pain I heard,
Which I had thought the caravan's departure word.

Kalam 017: One Luminous Wisdom, One Demonstrative Wisdom

JSON title: Ek Danish-e-Noorani,….(Reason is either luminous,…)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ek-danish-e-noorani-ek-danish-e-burhani/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 7 stanzas, 14 lines

One wisdom is luminous, another wisdom demonstrative;
Demonstrative wisdom multiplies wonder beyond measure.
Within this earthen form there is one thing, and that is Yours;
For me, guarding that one thing is a difficult endeavor.
What now, if my cry has reached the stars on high?
You Yourself had taught me this art of song to cry.
If the design is false, what profit can repetition gain?
Does the cheapness of Adam bring You pleasure again?
The West has taught me denial, left me faithless and astray;
Why are the scholars of this age a shame to Islam today?
The power to shatter fate still remains within this clay;
The fool they call a prisoner of fate has strength to break away.
You have your idol-houses, and I have mine as well;
Both sets of idols are of dust, both are doomed to fall.

Kalam 018: O Lord, This Passing World Is Lovely, Yet

JSON title: Ya Rab! Ye Jahan-e-Guzran Khoob Hai Lekin(Lovely, oh Lord, this fleeting world; but)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/ya-rab-ye-jahan-e-guzran-khoob-hai-lekin/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 16 stanzas, 32 lines

O Lord, this passing world is lovely, yet why are the pure and skilled brought low?
Why are men of clean hearts and craft made objects of woe?
Though the moneylender shares a hand in its dominion and sway,
The world still calls the Western master its lord today.
You give the wise a blade of grass, while the flower field is granted to the few;
The garden of roses and poppies they receive in handfuls, but what is given to you?
The church has kebab and crimson wine displayed;
What has the mosque besides sermons and counsel laid?
Your commands are true, but their interpreters, by their art,
Could turn the Quran, through twisting, into a dead book's part.
No one has seen the Paradise that is Yours and bright;
Every village of the West resembles Paradise to the sight.
For long my thought has wandered through the spheres above;
Now imprison it in the moon's caves, where it cannot rove.
Nature has granted me celestial jewels in generous measure;
I am of dust, yet keep no bond with dust as my treasure.
God-intoxicated dervish, neither Eastern nor Western am I;
My home is neither Delhi, Isfahan, nor Samarqand beneath the sky.
I say only what I understand and know to be true;
I am no fool of the mosque, nor a child of culture's view.
My own are displeased with me, and strangers are displeased as well;
I could never call the poison of Halahil sugar's sweet spell.
It is hard for a truth-seeing, truth-thinking servant to say
That a heap of straw is Mount Damavand, whatever others may say.
Even in Nimrod's fire my silence remains;
I am a believing servant, not a seed that crackles in flames.
Burning, far-seeing, discerning, and gentle in deed;
Free and bound, empty-pursed and content, I remain freed.
In every state my unchained heart is joyful and bright;
What can steal from a bud its delight in a smiling light?
Even before the Lord of Yazdan Iqbal could not stay mute;
Would anyone come and seal this bold servant's lips shut?

Kalam 019: All Nature's Vastness Cannot Contain My Longing

JSON title: Sama Sakta Nahin Pehna’ay Fitrat Mein Mera Soda(All Nature’s vastness cannot contain you, oh)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/sama-sakta-nahin-pehnaay-fitrat-mein-mera-soda/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 6 stanzas, 12 lines

All nature's vastness cannot contain my longing;
O madness, perhaps your measure of the wilderness was wrong.
Through the self's power we can break this spell of color and scent;
This was the oneness that neither you nor I understood or meant.
Awaken vision, heedless one: revelation is nature's very face;
The river cannot live estranged from the wave it must embrace.
The pulpit's rivalry of knowledge and gnosis is mistaken sight;
It thinks the scaffold of Hallaj is its rival in the fight.
For God's pure servants, in rule or in slavery's chain,
If anything guards their armor, it is freedom from desire and gain.
O Gabriel, do not imitate my ecstasy and wine;
For ease-loving heavenly beings, remembrance, praise, and circling are enough and fine.

Kalam 020: I Have Seen Many a Wine-Shop East and West

JSON title: Bohat Dekhe Hain Main Ne Mashriq-o-Maghrib Ke May Khane(I have seen many a wine‐shop East and West)

Source URL: https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/bohat-dekhe-hain-main-ne-mashriq-o-maghrib-ke-maikhane/

Source trace: JSON urdu and transliteration; 8 stanzas, 16 lines

I have seen many a wine-shop in East and West;
Here no cupbearer is born, there the wine lacks all zest.
Neither Iran nor Turan retains that band of the poor and free,
Those servants of poverty who brought Caesar and Chosroes to their knee.
This is the sanctuary's shaykh, who steals and sells for gain
The cloak of Bu Zar, the mantle of Awais, and Zahra's shawl again.
In the presence of Truth, Israfil has brought my case:
This servant may raise the Resurrection before its appointed place.
A voice came: what is this beside the world's upheaval and alarm?
"The Chinese seized the ihram, while Mecca slept in Bat'ha's calm."
The glass of present-day culture brims with the wine of "No";
