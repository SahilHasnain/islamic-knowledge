# Editorial Decisions

- This is a dedicated English project and must remain independent of `publishing/bal-e-jibreel-easy-roman-urdu/`.
- Kalams are included in exact JSON order; Batch 01 covers 001 through 005 and Batch 02 covers 006 through 010.
- The Urdu field governs meaning; the transliteration field is the line and stanza traceability aid.
- Titles are rendered in poetic English where useful, while the JSON slug remains the stable identifier.
- Every source stanza is represented by exactly two English lines. Blank lines preserve stanza boundaries.
- The repeated question `tera hai ya mera` is rendered consistently as `Yours or mine?` and remains a question in every occurrence.
- `Saqi` is retained as a poetic Islamic term rather than flattened into an explanatory phrase.
- `Muhammadؐ` in the source is rendered as `Muhammad ﷺ` in accordance with the project's adab rule.
- The final Prophet's honorific is always rendered as `ﷺ`, never as `S.A.W.` or another Latin
  abbreviation. Honorifics for other people are omitted in the English translation.
- Divine direct address uses capitalized `You` and `Your`; the translation does not make Allah's address casual.
- No commentary, explanation, translator note, HTML, or source transliteration is placed inside the poem.
- English poetic freedom may alter syntax and rhyme, but not source coverage, order, imagery, force, or philosophical direction.
- Each source stanza remains exactly two English lines, with blank lines preserving stanza boundaries.
- In Kalam 078, the Urdu source abbreviates the testimony as `اَشْھَدُ اَنْ لَّا اِلٰہ` twice and omits `إِلَّا الله`. The English renders the intended complete testimony as “I bear witness that there is no god but Allah” to avoid a misleading or irreverent standalone denial of Allah.
