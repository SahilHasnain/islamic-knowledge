# Surah Al-Kafirun: English Translation

## Ayat 109:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54493
**Metadata:** surah=109; ayat=1; ayatId=6316; tafseerId=54493; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْكٰفِرُوْنَۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Say you (O Beloved), 'O disbelievers!'

{قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْكٰفِرُوْنَ: Say you (O Beloved), 'O disbelievers!'}. **Occasion of revelation:** A group of Quraysh said to the Crown of Messengership صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, “Follow our religion and we will follow your religion. Worship our gods for one year, and we will worship your God for one year.” The Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ said to them, “May Allah Almighty protect me from associating anyone with Him.” The disbelievers said, “Then just touch one of our gods, and we will affirm you and worship your God.” Upon this, this blessed Surah was revealed, and the Master of both worlds صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ went to the Sacred Mosque. The group of Quraysh who had held this conversation with the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ was present there. The Illuminated Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ recited this Surah to them, so they became disappointed and began causing harm to the Esteemed Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ and to his Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ. Remember that the disbelievers intended by this verse are those who, in the knowledge of Allah Almighty, were deprived of faith and were destined to remain upon disbelief. (Khazin, Qul ya ayyuhal-kafirun, 4/417–418.)

### Information Derived from the Occasion of Revelation of Surah Al-Kafirun

Several matters are learned from this:

1. Making a religious compromise with disbelievers is unlawful; rather, in several cases it is disbelief.

2. Revering the idols of disbelievers and their religious festivals while considering them worthy of respect is disbelief.

3. A believer's heart should not contain awe of disbelievers.

4. Disbelievers should not be referred to with good titles without a lawful justification.

5. Calling a disbeliever a disbeliever when necessary and appropriate to the circumstances is permissible; indeed, it accords with the Quranic style.

{وَ لَاۤ اَنَا عَابِدٌ مَّا عَبَدْتُّمْ: And I shall not worship whom you have worshipped.} Several matters are learned from this:

1. A person should be gentle in worldly dealings but extremely firm in religion, so that disbelievers lose hope of turning him away.

2. The Esteemed Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ knew about his future: he could never commit disbelief, polytheism, or sin.

3. A Muslim should make disbelievers lose hope concerning him, so that they cannot turn him away from religion.

{وَ لَاۤ اَنْتُمْ عٰبِدُوْنَ مَاۤ اَعْبُدُ: And nor do you worship Whom I worship.} It is learned from this that Allah Almighty informed His Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ about the good and bad ends of people: who would die upon disbelief and who would come to faith, because the discourse here is with those disbelievers who were destined to die upon disbelief.

{لَكُمْ دِیْنُكُمْ وَ لِیَ دِیْنِ: For you is your religion, and for me is my religion.} This means that your disbelief is for you, while my monotheism and sincerity are for me. The purpose of these words is to warn the disbelievers, not to approve of their disbelief. (Khazin, Al-Kafirun, under verse 6, 4/418.)
