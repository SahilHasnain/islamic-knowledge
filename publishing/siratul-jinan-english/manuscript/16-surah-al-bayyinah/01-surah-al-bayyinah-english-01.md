# Surah Al-Bayyinah: English Translation

## Ayat 98:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54459

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=1; ayatId=6228; tafseerId=54459; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** لَمْ یَكُنِ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْ اَهْلِ الْكِتٰبِ وَ الْمُشْرِكِیْنَ مُنْفَكِّیْنَ حَتّٰى تَاْتِیَهُمُ الْبَیِّنَةُۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The disbelieving People of the Book and the polytheists were not going to leave their religion until clear proof comes to them.

{لَمْ یَكُنِ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْ اَهْلِ الْكِتٰبِ وَ الْمُشْرِكِیْنَ مُنْفَكِّیْنَ: The disbelieving People of the Book and the polytheists were not going to leave their religion.} That is, the disbelieving People of the Book, the Jews and Christians, and the polytheists, the idol-worshippers, were not going to leave their religions until the Leader of the Messengers, Sayyiduna Muhammad ﷺ, appeared before them. This was because, before the arrival of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, all these people used to say, “We will not leave our religion until that Prophet appears whose mention is found in the Torah and the Gospel.” When that Prophet appeared and announced his prophethood, some of them believed in him and some disbelieved in him. (Khazin, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 1, 4/398; Sawi, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 1, 6/2403–2404, excerpted.)

### Lessons from Ayat 1 of Surah Al-Bayyinah

Three matters are learned from this verse:

1. Although the People of the Book and the polytheists are all disbelievers, the rulings concerning the People of the Book are lenient because they have an association with a Prophet and a Book. If they accept faith, they receive double reward. If association with a Book and a Prophet benefits disbelievers so greatly, then what will be the state of the believer who gains a special association with the Illuminated Prophet ﷺ and the Quran?

2. The Noble Prophet ﷺ corrected a nation whose reform appeared impossible.

3. Acting upon the heavenly Books was guidance before their abrogation, but after their abrogation, acting upon them became misguidance.

## Ayat 98:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54460

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=2; ayatId=6229; tafseerId=54460; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** رَسُوْلٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ یَتْلُوْا صُحُفًا مُّطَهَّرَةًۙ    (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Who is that (clear proof)? He is the Messenger of Allah (i.e. the Beloved Prophet Muhammad), that he recites the sacred Scriptures.

{رَسُوْلٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ: (That clear proof is) the Messenger of Allah.} That is, the clear proof is the most magnificent Messenger of Allah Almighty, who recites the Holy Quran, the pure Book that encompasses the subjects of all the Scriptures. (Khazin, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 2, 4/399.)

The Holy Quran is pure in every respect: it came from a pure place through pure angels to a pure Prophet; it has always remained, and will always remain, in pure tongues, pure breasts, and pure hands; and it is protected from mixture and alteration. It should also be remembered that the recitation of the Noble Prophet ﷺ is a miracle, because he ﷺ recited and taught the Quran, and understood and explained it, without learning it from anyone among creation. Our recitation is not a miracle, because we learn the recitation and pronunciation of the Holy Quran, as well as its rulings and secrets, from a memoriser, reciter, scholar, and others.

{فِیْهَا كُتُبٌ قَیِّمَةٌ: In those Scriptures are upright words.} That is, those Scriptures contain straight words of truth and justice which guide towards correctness and reform. (Madarik, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 3, p. 1366; Samarqandi, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 3, 3/499, excerpted.)

## Ayat 98:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54461

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=4; ayatId=6231; tafseerId=54461; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ مَا تَفَرَّقَ الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ اِلَّا مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مَا جَآءَتْهُمُ الْبَیِّنَةُﭤ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And the People of the Book did not dispute but after this that the clear proof (i.e. the Beloved Prophet Muhammad) came to them.

{وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ الَّذِیْنَ اُوْتُوا الْكِتٰبَ: And the People of the Book did not dispute.} The meaning of this verse is that, before this, all of them agreed that when the Prophet whose glad tidings had been given appeared, they would believe in him. However, when that Noble Prophet ﷺ appeared, division arose among them. Some of them believed in the Noble Prophet ﷺ, while some adopted disbelief because of envy and obstinacy. (Khazin, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 4, 4/399; Madarik, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 4, p. 1366, excerpted.)

## Ayat 98:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54462

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=5; ayatId=6232; tafseerId=54462; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ مَاۤ اُمِرُوْۤا اِلَّا لِیَعْبُدُوا اللّٰهَ مُخْلِصِیْنَ لَهُ الدِّیْنَ ﳔ حُنَفَآءَ وَ یُقِیْمُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ یُؤْتُوا الزَّكٰوةَ وَ ذٰلِكَ دِیْنُ الْقَیِّمَةِﭤ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And those people (i.e of the Book) were ordered to worship Allah only, believing solely in Him, being on one side purely (i.e. towards Islam only), and to establish Salah, and to pay Zakah, and this is the straight religion.

{وَ مَاۤ اُمِرُوْۤا اِلَّا لِیَعْبُدُوا اللّٰهَ: And those people were ordered only to worship Allah.} This explains what the Jews and Christians were commanded in the Torah and the Gospel. They were commanded in the Torah and the Gospel to leave all religions, become sincere followers of Islam, worship Allah Almighty alone with sincerity, remain distant from polytheism and hypocrisy, establish the obligatory prayer at its appointed times, and pay the Zakah made obligatory upon their wealth. This is the straight religion. (Khazin, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 5, 4/399; Madarik, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 5, p. 1366, excerpted.)

### Lessons from the verse “وَ مَاۤ اُمِرُوْۤا اِلَّا لِیَعْبُدُوا اللّٰهَ”

Three matters are learned from this verse:

1. Disbelievers are obligated to accept Islam and worship Allah Almighty.

2. Both beliefs and deeds are necessary in religion.

3. Only the deed in which the intention is to gain the pleasure of Allah Almighty alone is accepted.

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Leader of the Messengers ﷺ said: “Indeed, Allah Almighty does not look at your forms or your wealth; rather, He looks at your hearts and your deeds.” (Muslim, Book of Righteousness, Maintaining Ties and Manners, chapter on the prohibition of oppressing and abandoning a Muslim, etc., p. 1387, hadith 34 [2564].)

## Ayat 98:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54463

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=6; ayatId=6233; tafseerId=54463; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْ اَهْلِ الْكِتٰبِ وَ الْمُشْرِكِیْنَ فِیْ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خٰلِدِیْنَ فِیْهَاؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمْ شَرُّ الْبَرِیَّةِﭤ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, all the disbelievers;  the People of the Book and the polytheists, are in the fire of Hell, they will remain in it forever; it is they who are the worst of the entire creation.

{اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا مِنْ اَهْلِ الْكِتٰبِ: Indeed, those among the People of the Book who disbelieved.} Previously, the worldly state of the disbelievers was described. From here, their state in the Hereafter is described. The polytheists are mentioned along with the People of the Book so that they do not imagine that the ruling described in this verse is specific to the People of the Book. (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 6, 10/489.)

### Lessons from the verse “اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا”

Four matters are learned from this verse:

1. Among the People of the Book were people who believed in Allah Almighty and worshipped Him, but did not believe in the prophethood of the Beloved of Allah Almighty ﷺ and did not honour and revere him ﷺ. Allah Almighty declared them disbelievers; indeed, this verse mentions their punishment before mentioning the punishment of the polytheists.

2. A disbeliever, whether from the People of the Book or a polytheist, will remain in Hell forever, even though the nature of the punishment may differ according to the type of disbelief.

3. Disbelief is a certain cause of entering Hell.

4. Even if a disbeliever is performing some extremely great service, he remains the worst of creation.

## Ayat 98:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54464

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=7; ayatId=6234; tafseerId=54464; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِۙ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمْ خَیْرُ الْبَرِیَّةِﭤ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, those who believed and did good deeds; it is they who are the best of the entire creation.

{اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمْ خَیْرُ الْبَرِیَّةِ: It is they who are the best of the entire creation.} This verse shows that those who believe and perform righteous deeds are superior even to the angels, because the angels are also included among all creation. There is, however, detail in this matter: among human beings, those who were granted the rank of prophethood and messengership are superior to all angels. Among the angels, those who are messengers are superior to saints and scholars, while saints and scholars are superior to ordinary angels. Ordinary angels are superior to sinful believers because angels are protected from sins. (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 7, 10/490; Sharh Fiqh Akbar, p. 118, excerpted.)

## Ayat 98:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54465

**Metadata:** surah=98; ayat=8; ayatId=6235; tafseerId=54465; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** جَزَآؤُهُمْ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنّٰتُ عَدْنٍ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْاَنْهٰرُ خٰلِدِیْنَ فِیْهَاۤ اَبَدًاؕ-رَضِیَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهُمْ وَ رَضُوْا عَنْهُؕ-ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِیَ رَبَّهٗ۠    (8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Their reward with their Lord is Gardens for dwelling, beneath which streams flow, in which they will abide forever and ever. Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him; this (reward) is for the one who fears his Lord.

{جَزَآؤُهُمْ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ: Their reward with their Lord.} That is, the reward of those who believe and perform good deeds is Gardens of eternal dwelling with their Lord Almighty, beneath which streams flow. They will remain in them forever and ever. Allah Almighty is pleased with them because of their obedience and sincerity, and they are pleased with Him because of His grace and gifts. This great glad tiding is for the one who feared his Lord Almighty in the world and avoided disobeying Him. (Khazin, Al-Bayyinah, under verse 8, 4/400, excerpted.)

### Lessons from the verse “جَزَآؤُهُمْ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ”

Four matters are learned from this verse:

1. The blessings of this world are not the true reward of righteous people, even though Allah Almighty may grant them these blessings because of their good deeds.

2. This world is a place of passage, while Paradise is the true abode.

3. After entering Paradise as a reward, there will be neither departure from it nor death.

4. It is permissible to say رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ for every saint and spiritually accomplished person; this expression is not exclusive to the Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ. This meaning is clearly present in the verse.
