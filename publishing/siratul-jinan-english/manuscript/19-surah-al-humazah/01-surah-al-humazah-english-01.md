# Surah Al-Humazah: English Translation

## Ayat 104:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54482

**Metadata:** surah=104; ayat=1; ayatId=6283; tafseerId=54482; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ ﹰۙ   (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Destruction is for every open slanderer, backbiter.

{وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ: Destruction is for every open slanderer, backbiter.} These verses were revealed concerning those disbelievers who used to raise objections against the Leader of Both Worlds ﷺ and his Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ and backbite them, such as Akhnas ibn Shurayq, Umayyah ibn Khalaf and Walid ibn Mughirah, among others. The ruling mentioned in this verse is general for everyone who backbites. (Jalalayn, Al-Humazah, under verse 1, p. 506; Madarik, Al-Humazah, under verse 1, p. 1373, excerpted.)

### Condemnation of Backbiting and Fault-Finding

Elsewhere, Allah Almighty states:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا اجْتَنِبُوْا كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ الظَّنِّ٘-اِنَّ بَعْضَ الظَّنِّ اِثْمٌ وَّ لَا تَجَسَّسُوْا وَ لَا یَغْتَبْ بَّعْضُكُمْ بَعْضًاؕ-اَیُحِبُّ اَحَدُكُمْ اَنْ یَّاْكُلَ لَحْمَ اَخِیْهِ مَیْتًا فَكَرِهْتُمُوْهُؕ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ تَوَّابٌ رَّحِیْمٌ‘‘ (Al-Hujurat: 12)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: O believers! Refrain from excessive assumptions; indeed, some assumptions are sins. Do not spy, and do not backbite one another. Would any of you like to eat the flesh of his dead brother? You would surely detest it. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is Most Relenting, Most Merciful.

Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Holy Prophet ﷺ said: “On the night of the Ascension, I passed by a people who were scratching their faces and chests with copper claws. I asked, ‘O Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, who are these people?’ He replied, ‘They used to eat the flesh of people,’ meaning they used to backbite, ‘and they used to disgrace their honour.’” (Abu Dawud, Book of Manners, chapter on backbiting, 4/353, hadith 4878.)

Sayyiduna Rashid ibn Sa'd رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “On the night of the Ascension, I passed by women and men who were hanging by their breasts. I asked, ‘O Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, who are these people?’ He replied, ‘They are those who find fault with people to their faces and speak ill of them behind their backs.’ Regarding them, Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ states: ‘وَیْلٌ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٍ لُّمَزَةِ’ — ‘Destruction is for every open slanderer, backbiter.’” (Shu'ab al-Iman, the forty-fourth branch of faith, chapter concerning reports that mention severity, 5/309, hadith 6750.)

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to avoid reprehensible deeds such as backbiting and fault-finding. Ameen.

## Ayat 104:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54483

**Metadata:** surah=104; ayat=2; ayatId=6284; tafseerId=54483; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** الَّذِیْ جَمَعَ مَالًا وَّ عَدَّدَهٗۙ   (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Who accumulated wealth, counting it over and over.

{اَلَّذِیْ جَمَعَ مَالًا وَّ عَدَّدَهٗ: Who accumulated wealth, counting it over and over.} It is learned from this that accumulating wealth and repeatedly counting it is one cause of developing the reprehensible qualities of finding fault with people to their faces and speaking ill of them behind their backs. In our society, a number of wealthy people are severely afflicted by this illness. May Allah Almighty guide them.

### Reprehensible Forms of Accumulating and Repeatedly Counting Wealth

Remember that accumulating wealth and repeatedly counting it is bad in several circumstances:

1. Wealth is accumulated through unlawful means.
2. The religious rights due from the accumulated wealth are not fulfilled.
3. A person becomes so occupied with accumulating wealth that he forgets Allah Almighty.
4. Instead of relying upon Allah Almighty, a person regards wealth alone as the means of removing afflictions.

## Ayat 104:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54484

**Metadata:** surah=104; ayat=3; ayatId=6285; tafseerId=54484; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** یَحْسَبُ اَنَّ مَالَهٗۤ اَخْلَدَهٗۚ    (3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Does he think that his wealth will keep him on earth forever?

{یَحْسَبُ اَنَّ مَالَهٗۤ اَخْلَدَهٗ: Does he think that his wealth will keep him on earth forever?} The summary of this verse and the six verses after it is that the person who finds fault with people to their faces, speaks ill of them behind their backs, accumulates wealth and repeatedly counts it thinks that his wealth will keep him in this world forever and will not let him pass away. Because of this, he is intoxicated with love for wealth and does not turn towards righteous deeds. This can never be so. Rather, he will certainly be thrown into the crushing compartment of Hell, where the Fire will break his bones and ribs. And what do you know what the crushing one is? It is the Fire kindled by Allah, which will never become cold. Its nature is that it will burn the outer parts of the body, reach into the inside of the body, and burn the hearts as well. Indeed, after they are cast into the Fire, its doors will be closed, and the doors will be reinforced with fiery iron pillars so that they never open. Some exegetes have explained this to mean that, after the doors are closed, their hands and feet will be bound with fiery pillars. (Khazin, Al-Humazah, under verses 3–9, 4/406, excerpted.)

### The Fire of Hell Is Not Like Other Fires

It is learned from verse 6 of Surah Al-Humazah that the Fire of Hell is not like other fires. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “The Fire of Hell was kindled for a thousand years until it became red. Then it was kindled for another thousand years until it became white. Then it was kindled for another thousand years until it became black. Thus, it is now black and dark.” (Tirmidhi, Book of the Description of Hell, chapter 8, 4/266, hadith 2600.)

{اَلَّتِیْ تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى الْاَفْـٕدَةِ: Which will climb on to the hearts.} Hearts are such things that cannot bear even a little heat. So when the Fire of Hell climbs upon them and there is no death, what will their condition be at that time? The hearts are burned because they are the places of disbelief, false beliefs and corrupt intentions. (Khazin, Al-Humazah, under verse 7, 4/406–407; Madarik, Al-Humazah, under verse 7, p. 1373, excerpted.)
