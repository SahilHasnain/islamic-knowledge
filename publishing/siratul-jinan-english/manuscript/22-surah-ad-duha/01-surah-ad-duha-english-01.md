# Surah Ad-Duha: English Translation

## Ayat 93:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54425

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=1; ayatId=6172; tafseerId=54425; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ الضُّحٰىۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of the forenoon.

{وَالضُّحٰى: By the time of the forenoon.} The occasion of revelation of this Surah is that, once, revelation did not come for several days. The disbelievers objected, saying that Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ had been abandoned and disliked by his Lord عزوجل. Upon this, Surah Ad-Duha was revealed.

According to some exegetes, “Duha” in this verse refers to the time when the sun rises high. Allah Almighty swore by this time because it was the time when Allah Almighty honoured Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام with His speech and the magicians fell in prostration. According to other exegetes, “Duha” here refers to the entire day. (Tafsir Baghawi, Ad-Duha, under verse 1, 4/465; Madarik, Ad-Duha, under verse 1, p. 1356, excerpted.)

### Three virtues of the Chasht prayer

This verse mentions the Chasht prayer. Therefore, three virtues of the Chasht prayer are noted here.

1. Sayyiduna Abu Dharr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “There is a charity due from a person for every joint of his body; there are three hundred and sixty joints. Every glorification is charity, every praise is charity, saying لَا اِلٰـہَ اِلَّا اللّٰہ is charity, saying اَللّٰہُ اَکْبَرْ is charity, enjoining good is charity and forbidding evil is charity; and two rak'ats of Chasht suffice for all of this.” (Muslim, Book of the Prayer of Travellers and its Shortening, chapter on the recommendation of the Duha prayer, p. 363, hadith 84 (720))

2. Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Radiant Presence ﷺ said: “Whoever offers twelve rak'ats of Chasht, Allah Almighty will build a palace of gold for him in Paradise.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Witr, chapter on what has been reported concerning the Duha prayer, 2/17, hadith 472.)

3. Sayyiduna Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Whoever offers two rak'ats of Chasht will not be recorded among the heedless. Whoever offers four rak'ats will be recorded among the worshippers. Whoever offers six rak'ats will be granted sufficiency for that day by Allah Almighty. Whoever offers eight rak'ats will be recorded by Allah Almighty among the obedient. Whoever offers twelve rak'ats, Allah Almighty will build a palace for him in Paradise. There is no day or night in which Allah Almighty does not bestow favour and charity upon His servants, and no one has received a greater favour than the servant to whom He inspired His remembrance.” (At-Targhib wat-Tarhib, Book of Voluntary Prayers, encouragement towards the Duha prayer, 1/318, hadith 1011)

### Two religious rulings concerning the Chasht prayer

Two religious rulings concerning the Chasht prayer are also noted here.

1. The Chasht prayer is recommended. Its minimum is two rak'ats and its maximum is twelve rak'ats.

2. Its time begins when the sun has risen high and continues until zawal, meaning the legally defined midday. It is better to offer it when one quarter of the day has passed. (Bahar-e-Shariat, Part Four, 1/675–676)

{وَ الَّیْلِ اِذَا سَجٰى: And by the night when it covers.} That is, by the night when it covers everything with its darkness. Imam Ja'far Sadiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ said that Duha refers to the forenoon in which Allah Almighty spoke to Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, while “night” refers to the night of the Ascension. Some exegetes have said that Duha alludes to the light of the beauty of Mustafa ﷺ, while night alludes to his amber-scented, curling locks. (Ruh al-Bayan, Ad-Duha, under verse 2, 10/453)

The Exalted Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says:

In the Word Divine, the sun and the forenoon are sworn,
By your light-increasing face, where radiant beauty is born.

By the dark night's oath, this secret was made clear:
By the beloved's twofold locks, falling on cheeks so dear.

## Ayat 93:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54426

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=3; ayatId=6174; tafseerId=54426; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَ مَا قَلٰىﭤ(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** That your Lord has neither abandoned you (O Beloved), nor did He dislike you.

{مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَ مَا قَلٰى: That your Lord has neither abandoned you nor did He dislike you.} In response to the disbelievers' objection, the Exalted Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says that the Almighty declared: “By the forenoon and by the night when it casts its darkness; or, O Beloved, by your radiant countenance and by your locks when they spread over your shining cheeks: your Lord has neither abandoned you nor made you an enemy.” These wretched people also understand well in their hearts what mercy Allah has bestowed upon you. They burn when they see that mercy, and out of envy and enmity they construct this storm and burst the blisters of their burning hearts. Yet they do not know that “And indeed, the latter is better for you than the former”: certainly, the Hereafter is better for you than the world. There, you will receive blessings which no eye has seen, no ear has heard and which have not occurred to the imagination of any human or angel. Their brief description is: “And indeed, it is close that your Lord will give you so much that you will be pleased.” Soon it will be clear to friends and enemies alike that no one was as beloved as you.

In any case, if these blind people do not believe in the Hereafter today, then, O Beloved, the magnificent, majestic, abundant and immense blessings and mercies of Allah upon you are not only of today; they have existed from ancient times. Did they not see your earlier states and gain certainty from them that the gracious attention upon you is not of a kind that can ever change? “Did He not find you (O Beloved as) an orphan, then provided you with a place? And He found you profoundly engrossed in His love, so He gave you a way towards Himself. And found you in need, then made you prosperous. Therefore, do not put pressure on an orphan. And do not scold a beggar. And publicise well the bounty of your Lord.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/165–166, abridged.)

This objection of the disbelievers shows that they knew the Noble Prophet ﷺ to be Allah Almighty's Messenger, for if they had not known this, they would not have made such an objection. It also shows that the Holy Quran is not speech composed by the Noble Prophet ﷺ himself; rather, it is the Word of Allah Almighty, revealed by Him to the Crown of Messengership ﷺ. If the Holy Quran had been his own speech, his speech would have continued without interruption and there would have been no pause in it, leaving the disbelievers no opportunity to object that Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ had been abandoned and disliked by his Lord عزوجل.

## Ayat 93:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54427

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=4; ayatId=6175; tafseerId=54427; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ لَلْاٰخِرَةُ خَیْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْاُوْلٰىﭤ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And indeed, the latter is better for you than the former.

{وَ لَلْاٰخِرَةُ خَیْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْاُوْلٰى: And indeed, the latter is better for you than the former.} One meaning given by the exegetes is: O Beloved ﷺ, certainly the Hereafter is better for you than the world, because there you will have the Praiseworthy Station, the Pool of Kawthar and the good that has been promised. You will be given precedence over all the Prophets and Messengers عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, your Ummah will bear witness over all other communities, and through your intercession the ranks and degrees of believers will be raised. There will also be honours and miracles beyond description.

The exegetes have also given the meaning that the conditions to come will be better and greater for you than those that have passed. It is as though the promise of the Exalted Truth is that He will raise your ranks day by day, add honour to honour and station to station, and keep your rank increasing with every coming moment. (Madarik, Ad-Duha, under verse 4, p. 1356; Tafsir Kabir, Ad-Duha, under verse 4, 11/193, excerpted.)

## Ayat 93:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54428

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=5; ayatId=6176; tafseerId=54428; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ لَسَوْفَ یُعْطِیْكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضٰىﭤ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And indeed, it is close that your Lord will give you so much that you will be pleased.

{وَلَسَوْفَ یُعْطِیْكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضٰى: And indeed, it is close that your Lord will give you so much that you will be pleased.} This means: O Beloved ﷺ, certainly your Lord عزوجل will soon give you so much in this world and the Hereafter that you will be pleased.

Allah Almighty's gracious promise to His Beloved ﷺ includes the blessings He bestowed upon him in this world: perfection of the soul, knowledge of the first and the last, the manifestation of the command, the supremacy of the religion, the victories achieved during his blessed era and those achieved during the era of the Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ and which will continue to come to Muslims until the Day of Judgement, the universality of his invitation, the spread of Islam in the east and west, the superiority of his Ummah over all communities, and such miracles and perfections of his that Allah Almighty alone knows. The promise also includes the honour and veneration of the Hereafter: Allah Almighty granted him general and particular intercession, the Praiseworthy Station and other magnificent blessings. (Ruh al-Bayan, Ad-Duha, under verse 5, 10/455; Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 5, 4/386, excerpted.)

### Allah Almighty desires the pleasure of Muhammad ﷺ

Sayyiduna Amr ibn al-As رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ says that the Noble Messenger ﷺ recited this supplication of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام from the Holy Quran:

“رَبِّ اِنَّهُنَّ اَضْلَلْنَ كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ النَّاسِۚ-فَمَنْ تَبِعَنِیْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنِّیْۚ-وَ مَنْ عَصَانِیْ فَاِنَّكَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ” (Ibrahim: 36)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O my Lord! Indeed, the idols have led many people astray. So whoever follows me is surely of me, and whoever disobeys me, then surely You are Most Forgiving, Most Merciful.”

He also recited this supplication of Sayyiduna Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام:

“اِنْ تُعَذِّبْهُمْ فَاِنَّهُمْ عِبَادُكَۚ-وَ اِنْ تَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ فَاِنَّكَ اَنْتَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ” (Al-Ma'idah: 118)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “If You punish them, then they are Your servants; and if You forgive them, then surely You alone are the Most Honourable, the Wise.”

Then he raised both blessed hands, wept and supplicated for his Ummah, saying: “اَللّٰھُمَّ اُمَّتِیْ اُمَّتِیْ” (“O Allah, my Ummah, my Ummah!”). Allah Almighty commanded Sayyiduna Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام to go to His Beloved ﷺ. He said: “Your Lord knows best, but ask him what has caused him to weep.” Sayyiduna Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام presented himself as commanded and asked. The Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ told him the whole matter and expressed his concern for his Ummah. Sayyiduna Jibril Amin عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام presented this to the Divine Court: “O Allah عزوجل, Your Beloved ﷺ says this, and Allah Almighty knows best.” Allah Almighty commanded Sayyiduna Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام to go and tell His Beloved ﷺ: “We shall soon make you pleased concerning your Ummah, and We shall not let your blessed heart grieve.” (Muslim, Book of Faith, chapter on the Prophet's supplication for his Ummah, p. 130, hadith 346 (202))

Abu al-Barakat Abdullah ibn Ahmad Nasafi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says that when this verse was revealed, the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “I will not be pleased while even one of my Ummah remains in Hell.” (Madarik, Ad-Duha, under verse 5, p. 1356)

Mufti Na'im al-Din Muradabadi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: “This blessed verse clearly indicates that Allah Almighty will do only that with which the Messenger is pleased. The hadith of intercession establishes that the pleasure of the Messenger ﷺ lies in all the sinners of his Ummah being forgiven. Thus, the verse and hadith lead with certainty to the conclusion that the intercession of the Noble Messenger ﷺ will be accepted and the sinners of his Ummah will be forgiven according to his blessed wish. Subhan Allah! What an exalted rank: the Lord عزوجل whom all the close ones endure hardships and labour to please grants universal bounty in order to please this most beloved Messenger ﷺ.” (Khazain al-Irfan, Ad-Duha, under verse 5, p. 1109)

The Exalted Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says:

The two worlds seek the pleasure of God above;
God seeks Muhammad's pleasure and love.

## Ayat 93:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54429

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=6; ayatId=6177; tafseerId=54429; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ یَجِدْكَ یَتِیْمًا فَاٰوٰى۪    (6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Did He not find you (O Beloved as) an orphan, then provided you with a place?

{اَلَمْ یَجِدْكَ یَتِیْمًا فَاٰوٰى: Did He not find you as an orphan, then provided you with a place?} The father of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ passed away in Madinah Tayyibah when he was still in his mother's blessed womb and the pregnancy was two months along. His father left neither wealth nor a home. After his passing, his grandfather Abdul-Muttalib took responsibility for him. When he was four or six years old, his mother also passed away. When he was eight, his grandfather Abdul-Muttalib passed away as well. Before his passing, he instructed his son Abu Talib, who was the Noble Prophet's ﷺ paternal uncle, to care for and supervise him. Abu Talib continued to serve him until he announced his Prophethood by the command of Allah Almighty.

Another meaning given by the exegetes is that “orphan” can mean unique and matchless, as in the phrase “the unique pearl.” On this interpretation, the verse means that Allah Almighty found the Noble Prophet ﷺ unique and matchless in honour and nobility, gave him a place in nearness, raised him under His protection among his enemies, and honoured him with Prophethood, Messengership and being His chosen bondsman. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 6, 4/386; Jaml, Ad-Duha, under verse 6, 8/347; Ruh al-Bayan, Ad-Duha, under verse 6, 10/456–457, excerpted.)

## Ayat 93:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54430

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=7; ayatId=6178; tafseerId=54430; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ وَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدٰى۪    (7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And He found you profoundly engrossed in His love, so He gave you a way towards Himself (i.e. towards His closeness).

{وَ وَجَدَكَ ضَآلًّا فَهَدٰى: And He found you profoundly engrossed in His love, so He gave you a way towards Himself.} Allah Almighty says: O Beloved ﷺ, He found you engrossed in His love, so He gave you a way towards Himself, opened the secrets of the unseen to you, granted you knowledge of what was and what will be, and gave you the highest rank in knowing His Essence and attributes.

Another meaning given by the exegetes is that Allah Almighty found you so absorbed in Him that you did not concern yourself with your own self or your ranks, so He granted you knowledge of your essence, attributes, ranks and degrees.

Keep this matter in mind: all the Prophets of Allah عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام are protected from polytheism, disbelief and every sin both before and after Prophethood, and they have always possessed knowledge of the Oneness of Allah Almighty and His attributes. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 7, 4/386–387; Khazain al-Irfan, Ad-Duha, under verse 7, p. 1109)

## Ayat 93:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54431

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=8; ayatId=6179; tafseerId=54431; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ وَجَدَكَ عَآىٕلًا فَاَغْنٰىﭤ(8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And found you in need, then made you prosperous.

{وَ وَجَدَكَ عَآىٕلًا فَاَغْنٰى: And found you in need, then made you prosperous.} One interpretation is that Allah Almighty found the Noble Prophet ﷺ in need and made him prosperous through the wealth of Sayyidatuna Khadijah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, then through the wealth of Sayyiduna Abu Bakr al-Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, then through the wealth of Sayyiduna Uthman al-Ghani رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, and then through the spoils of war. A second interpretation is that Allah Almighty found him in need and made him prosperous by granting him the wealth of contentment. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 8, 4/387)

### Three fortunate personalities

The first interpretation of this verse shows that Sayyidatuna Khadijah, Sayyiduna Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and Sayyiduna Uthman al-Ghani رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ are extremely fortunate, because Allah Almighty gave them the opportunity to serve His Beloved ﷺ.

### Who is truly wealthy?

The second interpretation shows that the truly wealthy person is the one whom Allah Almighty has blessed with the wealth of contentment. Two hadith concerning this are noted here.

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “Wealth is not attained through possessing much property; true wealth is the independence of the soul.” (Bukhari, Book of Softening of the Hearts, chapter on true wealth being the wealth of the soul, 4/233, hadith 6446)

2. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Amr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Whoever accepts Islam, is given provision sufficient for his need, and Allah Almighty makes him content with what he has been given has achieved success.” (Muslim, Book of Zakat, chapter on sufficiency and contentment, p. 524, hadith 125 (1054))

May Allah Almighty also fill us with the great wealth of contentment. Amin.

## Ayat 93:9

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54432

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=9; ayatId=6180; tafseerId=54432; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاَمَّا الْیَتِیْمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْﭤ(9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Therefore, do not put pressure on an orphan.

{فَاَمَّا الْیَتِیْمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ: Therefore, do not put pressure on an orphan.} In the age of ignorance, the Arabs would seize the property of orphans, put pressure on them and wrong them concerning their rights. Allah Almighty says to His Beloved ﷺ: O Beloved, do not put pressure on an orphan in any way. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 9, 4/387)

### Islam's honour concerning orphans

Islam has the distinction of clarifying the rights of orphans, returning their seized rights to them and ending the long-standing oppression and persecution inflicted upon them. A glimpse of the excellent teachings Islam gave Muslims concerning orphans follows.

1. The command concerning the property of orphans: Allah Almighty says:

“وَ لَا تُؤْتُوا السُّفَهَآءَ اَمْوَالَكُمُ الَّتِیْ جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ قِیٰمًا وَّ ارْزُقُوْهُمْ فِیْهَا وَ اكْسُوْهُمْ وَ قُوْلُوْا لَهُمْ قَوْلًا مَّعْرُوْفًا(۵)وَ ابْتَلُوا الْیَتٰمٰى حَتّٰۤى اِذَا بَلَغُوا النِّكَاحَۚ-فَاِنْ اٰنَسْتُمْ مِّنْهُمْ رُشْدًا فَادْفَعُوْۤا اِلَیْهِمْ اَمْوَالَهُمْۚ-وَ لَا تَاْكُلُوْهَاۤ اِسْرَافًا وَّ بِدَارًا اَنْ یَّكْبَرُوْاؕ-وَ مَنْ كَانَ غَنِیًّا فَلْیَسْتَعْفِفْۚ-وَ مَنْ كَانَ فَقِیْرًا فَلْیَاْكُلْ بِالْمَعْرُوْفِؕ-فَاِذَا دَفَعْتُمْ اِلَیْهِمْ اَمْوَالَهُمْ فَاَشْهِدُوْا عَلَیْهِمْؕ-وَ كَفٰى بِاللّٰهِ حَسِیْبًا” (An-Nisa: 5–6)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And do not give the foolish their wealth, which Allah has made a means of support for you; provide them food and clothing from it and speak good words to them. Keep testing the orphans until they reach the age of marriage. Then, if you find sound judgement in them, hand over their wealth to them. Do not consume it extravagantly and hastily, fearing that they will grow up. Whoever is wealthy should abstain, and whoever is poor may eat according to what is fair. When you hand over their wealth to them, take witnesses over them; and Allah is sufficient as a Reckoner.”

Allah Almighty also says:

“وَ اٰتُوا الْیَتَامٰۤى اَمْوَالَهُمْ وَ لَا تَتَبَدَّلُوا الْخَبِیْثَ بِالطَّیِّبِ ۪- وَ لَا تَاْكُلُوْۤا اَمْوَالَهُمْ اِلٰۤى اَمْوَالِكُمْؕ- اِنَّهٗ كَانَ حُوْبًا كَبِیْرًا” (An-Nisa: 2)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And give the orphans their wealth, do not exchange the impure for the pure, and do not consume their wealth by mixing it with your wealth. Indeed, this is a great sin.”

And He says:

“اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَاْكُلُوْنَ اَمْوَالَ الْیَتَامٰى ظُلْمًا اِنَّمَا یَاْكُلُوْنَ فِیْ بُطُوْنِهِمْ نَارًاۚ- وَ سَیَصْلَوْنَ سَعِیْرًا” (An-Nisa: 10)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Indeed, those who consume the property of orphans unjustly are only filling their bellies with fire, and soon they will enter the blazing Fire.”

2. The command concerning treatment of orphans: Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Radiant Presence ﷺ said: “Among the homes of Muslims, the best home is the one in which an orphan is treated well, and the worst home is the one in which an orphan is treated badly.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Etiquette, chapter on the right of an orphan, 4/193, hadith 3679)

3. Encouragement to support an orphan: Sayyiduna Sahl ibn Sa'd رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “I and the one who cares for an orphan, whether the orphan is his own or another's, will be like this in Paradise.” He then indicated with his index and middle fingers and kept a slight space between them. (Bukhari, Book of Divorce, chapter on Li'an, 3/497, hadith 5304)

These three teachings of Islam concerning orphans have been mentioned briefly. For further commands concerning orphans, see the commentary of the opening verses of Surah An-Nisa.

## Ayat 93:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54433

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=10; ayatId=6181; tafseerId=54433; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اَمَّا السَّآىٕلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْﭤ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And do not scold a beggar.

{وَ اَمَّا السَّآىٕلَ فَلَا تَنْهَرْ: And do not scold a beggar.} O Beloved ﷺ, when a beggar comes to your generous door and asks for something, do not scold him in any way. Rather, give him something, or explain your excuse for not giving with good manners and gentleness. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 10, 4/388; Madarik, Ad-Duha, under verse 10, p. 1357, excerpted.)

The Exalted Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ alludes to this verse:

Momin hoon mominon pe Rauf-o-Raheem ho;
Sa'il hoon sa'ilon ko khushi laane hi ki hai.

He also says:

Maangen ge, maange jaayen ge, munh-maangi paayen ge;
Sarkar mein na “La” hai, na haajat “agar” ki hai.

In the Sovereign's court there is no “No,”
Nor is there need for an “If” to show.

The beggar's hand had scarcely risen high,
When the Giver's gift came from the sky.

Some glimpses of the rank of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ follow.

1. In the Cave of Thawr, Sayyiduna Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ said, “A snake has bitten me.” The Crown of Messengership ﷺ applied his blessed saliva and removed the effect of the poison.

2. During the Battle of Badr, Sayyiduna Mu'awwidh رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ came with his severed arm. The Master of the Messengers ﷺ applied blessed saliva from his sacred mouth and joined it.

3. At Uhud, an arrow caused Sayyiduna Qatadah's رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ eye to come out. He came to the Prophetic Court ﷺ carrying his eye. The Noble Messenger ﷺ applied his blessed saliva and restored the eye.

4. During the Battle of Khaybar, Sayyiduna Ali al-Murtada كَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم complained of an eye ailment. The Noble Prophet ﷺ applied his blessed saliva and removed the illness.

5. During the same battle, Sayyiduna Salamah ibn al-Akwa' رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ came with an injured leg. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ healed it with his blessed saliva.

6. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Atiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ came to the Prophetic Court ﷺ with a broken leg. The Noble Prophet ﷺ passed his blessed hand over it and restored the leg.

7. On one occasion, the Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ cried out when their water had run out, so springs of water flowed from his fingers.

These were a few examples of worldly gifts. Now listen to examples of gifts in the Hereafter.

8. Sayyiduna Rabi'ah and Sayyiduna Ukashah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا asked for Paradise, so the Beloved of Allah Almighty ﷺ gave them Paradise.

9. A date-palm trunk requested that it be planted in Paradise, so the Noble and Exalted Presence ﷺ planted it in Paradise.

It was not only human beings who presented their pleas to the Radiant Presence ﷺ and received relief; animals also presented their pleas and received justice.

10. A camel complained of excessive work and insufficient fodder, so the Noble Prophet ﷺ relieved it.

11. A doe held captive by a hunter requested permission to go and feed her young. The Radiant Presence ﷺ fulfilled her request.

In short, there are so many examples of people asking the Crown of both worlds ﷺ for what they desired and receiving it that, if all were described in detail, a substantial book could be compiled. The Exalted Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says beautifully:

Maalik-e-Kaunain hain,
Go paas kuch rakhte nahin;
Do-jahan ki ne'matein
Hain un ke khaali haath mein.

He also says:

Mangta ka haath uthte hi
Data ki den thi;
Doori qubool-o-arz mein
Bas haath bhar ki hai.

According to some exegetes, “beggar” in this verse refers to a seeker of knowledge. Therefore, he should be honoured, his need should be fulfilled and he should not be treated harshly or rudely. (Khazin, Ad-Duha, under verse 10, 4/388)

## Ayat 93:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54434

**Metadata:** surah=93; ayat=11; ayatId=6182; tafseerId=54434; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ۠    (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And publicise well the bounty of your Lord.

{وَ اَمَّا بِنِعْمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثْ: And publicise well the bounty of your Lord.} “Bounty” here refers to the blessings Allah Almighty bestowed upon His Beloved ﷺ and also to those blessings which Allah Almighty promised him. He commanded that these blessings be publicised because describing a blessing is gratitude. (Ruh al-Bayan, Ad-Duha, under verse 11, 10/459)

### Lessons from the verse “And publicise well the bounty of your Lord”

Three matters are learned from this verse.

1. Muslims should keep their appearance and conduct Islamic, because this displays the great blessing of Islam bestowed by Allah Almighty.

2. Celebrating the blessed Mawlid, Giyarhwin Sharif and the Urs of the righteous elders are excellent deeds, because these personalities are blessings of Allah Almighty, and the Mawlid and Urs publicise the birth of the Radiant Presence ﷺ and the lives of Allah Almighty's saints.

3. Praising the Noble Prophet ﷺ is an excellent act of worship, because his blessed qualities are also blessings of Allah Almighty for us. When the rank of the Radiant Presence ﷺ is elevated, the rank of the Ummah is elevated as well. Therefore, mentioning the qualities and perfections of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ is publicising the bounties of Allah Almighty.
