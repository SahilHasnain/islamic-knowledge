# Surah Quraysh: English Translation

## Ayat 106:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54486
**Metadata:** surah=106; ayat=1; ayatId=6299; tafseerId=54486; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** لِاِیْلٰفِ قُرَیْشٍۙ   (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** In order to give inclination to the Quraysh.

{لِاِیْلٰفِ قُرَیْشٍ: In order to give inclination to the Quraysh.} The summary of this Surah is that the blessings of Allah Almighty are countless. One of these evident blessings is that He inspired the Quraysh every year to make two journeys and placed love for them in the hearts of people. Thus, the Quraysh would travel to Yemen in winter for trade and to Syria in summer. In every place, people would call them the people of the Sacred Precinct and would honour and respect them. They conducted their trade safely, benefited from it, and continually brought capital to Makkah Mukarramah, where there is neither agriculture nor the means of livelihood, in order to maintain their residence there. The blessing of Allah Almighty upon them is evident, and these people benefit from it. Therefore, they should, in gratitude for this blessing, worship the Lord of the Glorious Ka'bah, Who gave them food through these journeys when they had suffered hunger in their homeland because there was no agriculture before these journeys. He also granted them security from fear because of the Sacred Precinct and because they were residents of Makkah: no one opposed them, even though killing and plunder continually took place around them and in their surrounding areas, caravans were looted, and travellers were killed. Some exegetes have said that granting security from fear means that they were protected from leprosy, so that this disease would not occur in their city. Other exegetes have said that it means that, through the blessing of the Crown of Messengership صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, they were granted safety from the greatest fear. (Khazin, Quraysh, under verses 1–4, 4/410–412.)

### An Introduction to the Quraysh

Quraysh is the name of the tribe in which the blessed birth of the Leader of the Messengers صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ took place. Various reasons have been stated for this tribe's name. One of them is that among the forefathers of the Beloved Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ was the accomplished personality Fahr ibn Malik. His title was “Quraysh,” and his descendants were called “Qurayshi” or “Quraysh.” Fahr ibn Malik was called “Quraysh” because “Quraysh” is the name of a very powerful sea animal that devours other sea animals. This animal always remains dominant over all animals and is never overcome. Since Fahr ibn Malik was dominant over all the tribes of Arabia because of his courage and God-given strength, all the Arabs began calling him by the title “Quraysh.” (Zarqani 'ala al-Mawahib, al-Maqsad al-Awwal fi Tashrif Allah Ta'ala Lahu 'Alayhis-Salatu was-Salam, 1/143–145; Sirat-e-Mustafa, pp. 51–52.)

### Hadiths Concerning the Quraysh

Here are three hadiths concerning the Quraysh:

1. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Wathilah ibn al-Asqa' رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ said: “Allah Almighty chose Kinanah from among the descendants of Sayyiduna Isma'il عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, chose Quraysh from Kinanah, chose Banu Hashim from Quraysh, and chose me from Banu Hashim.” (Muslim, Book of Virtues, chapter on the excellence of the lineage of the Prophet, etc., p. 1249, hadith 1 (2276).)

2. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Leader of the Messengers صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ said: “In the matter of governance, people follow the Quraysh: the Muslims among them follow their Muslims, and the disbelievers among them follow their disbelievers.” (Bukhari, Book of Virtues, chapter on the statement of Allah Almighty, “O mankind,” etc., 2/473, hadith 3495.)

3. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Mu'awiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ said: “The caliphate will remain among the Quraysh as long as they remain protectors of the religion, and whoever bears enmity towards them, Allah Almighty will throw him down upon his face.” (Bukhari, Book of Virtues, chapter on the virtues of Quraysh, 2/474, hadith 3500.)

### Information Derived from Verse 3 of Surah Quraysh

Several matters are learned from this verse:

1. Disbelievers are also bound by the commands of the Sacred Law: they must believe and worship.

2. No good deed or valid worship is accepted in the state of disbelief, because the disbelievers of Makkah performed tawaf, Hajj, and Umrah and served the pilgrims, yet these deeds were declared void.

3. The Glorious Ka'bah is a manifestation of the Being of the Lord Almighty, the Possessor of greatness and majesty.

4. Although Allah Almighty is the Lord of every lowly and exalted being, His Lordship should be attributed to His most exalted creation. Thus, one should say, “O Lord of Muhammad Mustafa صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ!” and “O Lord of the Glorious Ka'bah!”

### Providing Ease to People and Establishing Peace in Society Are Fundamental Priorities and Distinguishing Features of Islam

Verse 4 mentions being given food in a state of hunger and being granted security in a state of fear. It should be remembered that hunger and fear are two things that play an extremely important and central role in increasing sins and immoral acts in society, raising the crime rate, spreading disorder, and causing unrest. On the other hand, removing hunger and dispelling fear greatly help establish a pure environment and an atmosphere of peace and security in society. In other words, wherever people are provided with facilities and sufficient arrangements are made to meet their necessities of life, the rate of sins and immoral acts will be lower. Wherever peace and security are established, the number of crimes will be negligible and people will live peaceful lives.

If the conditions of people throughout the world today are examined, it will become clear that the basic reason for the widespread occurrence of adultery, theft, robbery, looting, plunder, murder, bloodshed, disorder, unrest, anxiety, and various other crimes, sins, and fears is the lack of valuable means capable of eliminating hunger, deprivation of the basic facilities required for life, and the absence of necessary arrangements for establishing peace and security. If the commands and teachings of Islam are considered, this reality will appear clearer than daylight: eliminating people's hunger, providing them with facilities, establishing a pure society, and establishing peace are among the fundamental priorities and distinguishing features of Islam. For example, zakat has been made obligatory upon wealthy Muslims in Islam, and giving charity for certain deeds has been commanded. Those who are extremely needy and live in poverty and destitution have been made entitled to receive it, so that there is an organised means of fulfilling their needs and removing poverty and destitution.

Even today, if people begin properly acting upon the commands of Islam and implement its teachings in their lives, they will attain the basic and essential facilities of life in this world as well, begin living in a pure and peaceful society, and, by the grace of Allah Almighty, live forever in comfort, tranquillity, relief, blessings, and ease in the Hereafter too. May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to properly act upon the commands and teachings of Islam. Ameen.
