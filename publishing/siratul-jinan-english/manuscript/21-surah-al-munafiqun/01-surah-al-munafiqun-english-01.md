# Surah Al-Munafiqun: English Translation

## Ayat 63:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54037

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=1; ayatId=5251; tafseerId=54037; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِذَا جَآءَكَ الْمُنٰفِقُوْنَ قَالُوْا نَشْهَدُ اِنَّكَ لَرَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِۘ-وَ اللّٰهُ یَعْلَمُ اِنَّكَ لَرَسُوْلُهٗؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ یَشْهَدُ اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ لَكٰذِبُوْنَۚ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** When the hypocrites present themselves in your august court (O Beloved), they say; that 'We testify that indeed your respected self are certainly Allah's Messenger.' And Allah knows that you are His Messenger, and Allah testifies that the hypocrites are definitely liars (in that they do not believe in what they say).

{اِذَا جَآءَكَ الْمُنٰفِقُوْنَ: When the hypocrites present themselves before you.} This blessed Surah describes various conditions of the hypocrites. The summary of this verse is that, O Beloved! ﷺ, when the hypocrites present themselves in your august court, they say, contrary to their inner condition: “We testify that indeed you are certainly Allah Almighty’s Messenger.” In response to their statement, Allah Almighty says: O Beloved! ﷺ, Allah Almighty knows that you are His Messenger, and what has come from the mouths of these hypocrites is completely true; but Allah Almighty also testifies that the hypocrites are certainly liars in giving this testimony, because their inner state does not agree with their outward appearance and they hold a belief contrary to what they say. (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 1, 4/270; Madarik, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 1, p. 1242, excerpted.)

## Ayat 63:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54038

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=2; ayatId=5252; tafseerId=54038; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِتَّخَذُوْۤا اَیْمَانَهُمْ جُنَّةً فَصَدُّوْا عَنْ سَبِیْلِ اللّٰهِؕ-اِنَّهُمْ سَآءَ مَا كَانُوْا یَعْمَلُوْنَ(2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** They have made their oaths as a shield (for their hypocrisy and misdeeds), thus prevented (other people) from the path of Allah; indeed, they commit extremely evil deeds.

{اِتَّخَذُوْۤا اَیْمَانَهُمْ جُنَّةً: They made their oaths a shield.} The hypocrites made their oaths a shield so that, through them, they would remain safe from being killed or imprisoned. They swear with their tongues, but their action is that they prevent people, by creating various doubts and suspicions in their minds, from believing in the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ and striving in the way of Allah Almighty. Indeed, they commit extremely evil deeds: they come to the august court with hypocrisy, outwardly display faith in order to deceive, prevent people from the way of Allah Almighty, and choose disbelief over faith. (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 2, 4/271; Madarik, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 2, p. 1242, excerpted.)

{ذٰلِكَ بِاَنَّهُمْ اٰمَنُوْا ثُمَّ كَفَرُوْا: This is because they believed and then disbelieved.} The reason for these evil deeds of the hypocrites is that they professed faith with their tongues and then became disbelievers in their hearts. The disbelief in their hearts became apparent to people, and because of these actions a seal was set upon their hearts. How, then, can faith enter their hearts now?

## Ayat 63:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54039

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=4; ayatId=5254; tafseerId=54039; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا رَاَیْتَهُمْ تُعْجِبُكَ اَجْسَامُهُمْؕ-وَ اِنْ یَّقُوْلُوْا تَسْمَعْ لِقَوْلِهِمْؕ-كَاَنَّهُمْ خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌؕ-یَحْسَبُوْنَ كُلَّ صَیْحَةٍ عَلَیْهِمْؕ-هُمُ الْعَدُوُّ فَاحْذَرْهُمْؕ-قٰتَلَهُمُ اللّٰهُ٘-اَنّٰى یُؤْفَكُوْنَ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And when you see them, their (outward) bodies would please you; and if they speak, so you would listen carefully to their speech, as if they are wooden panels propped up against a wall (unable to stand on their own); they think every shout is against them; they are the (worst) enemies, so beware of them; may Allah slay them, where are they returning to?

{وَ اِذَا رَاَیْتَهُمْ تُعْجِبُكَ اَجْسَامُهُمْ: And when you see them, their bodies please you.} Abdullah ibn Ubayy was a healthy, handsome and eloquent man, and the hypocrites accompanying him were much the same. When these people attended the Noble Prophet’s ﷺ blessed assembly, they spoke very attractively, in a way that pleased the listener. Therefore, this verse reveals their reality to Muslims: O Muslims! When you see hypocrites such as Abdullah ibn Ubayy and others, their bodies please you, and if they speak, you listen carefully to their speech. In reality, however, they are like pieces of wood propped against a wall: lifeless forms, with neither the spirit of faith nor the intellect that considers the outcome of matters. They regard every loud voice as being directed against them. When someone calls another person, searches for something lost, or raises his voice in the army for some purpose, they think, because of the foulness of their souls and their evil suspicion, that something has been said about them. They fear that a passage may be revealed concerning them which exposes their secrets. They are enemies, harbouring intense enmity in their hearts, and they convey information to the disbelievers and spy for them. So beware of them and do not be deceived by their outward condition. May Allah destroy them; where are they being turned away, and how do they turn away from the truth despite clear proofs having been established? (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 4, 4/271; Madarik, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 4, p. 1243, excerpted.)

Two hadith related to this verse concerning people whose tongues and hearts differ are noted here.

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “In the latter days, there will be people who will earn worldly benefit through religion by means of deception and fraud. To show gentleness to people, they will wear the skin of sheep; their tongues will be sweeter than sugar, while their hearts will be the hearts of wolves. Allah Almighty will say to them: ‘Are you deceiving Me or being bold against Me? I swear by Myself that I shall certainly send upon those people a trial from among themselves that will leave even their intelligent ones bewildered and distressed.’” (Tirmidhi, Book of Asceticism, chapter 60, 4/181, hadith 2412.)

2. Sayyidatuna Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “Allah Almighty says: ‘Among My servants there will be people who will wear the skin of sheep before people, while their hearts will be more bitter than aloes, an herb by that name, and their tongues will be sweeter than honey. They will deceive people through their religion. Are they deceiving Me or being bold against Me? I swear by Myself that I shall send among them a trial that will leave even a wise person among them bewildered.’” (Ibn Asakir, mention of those whose fathers were named Sulayman, 6416—Muhammad ibn Sulayman ibn Abi Dawud …, 53/121.)

## Ayat 63:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54040

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=5; ayatId=5255; tafseerId=54040; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا قِیْلَ لَهُمْ تَعَالَوْا یَسْتَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ رَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ لَوَّوْا رُءُوْسَهُمْ وَ رَاَیْتَهُمْ یَصُدُّوْنَ وَ هُمْ مُّسْتَكْبِرُوْنَ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And when it is said to them that 'Come! Allah's Messenger may seek forgiveness for you.' So, they turn their heads sideways and you see them turn their faces away in arrogance.

{وَ اِذَا قِیْلَ لَهُمْ تَعَالَوْا: And when it is said to them, “Come.”} Occasion of revelation: When the Noble Prophet ﷺ stopped along the way after returning from the Battle of Muraysi, an incident took place there. Jihjah al-Ghifari, the employee of Sayyiduna Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, and Sinan ibn Dabr al-Juhani, an ally of Abdullah ibn Ubayy, quarrelled. Jihjah called the Muhajirun and Sinan called the Ansar. At that time, the hypocrite Abdullah ibn Ubayy spoke extremely insolent and indecent words concerning the Holy Prophet ﷺ. He said, “When we reach Madinah Tayyibah, those of us who possess honour will expel the humiliated ones,” and he told his people, “If you do not give them your leftover food, they will not ride upon your necks, so do not spend anything on them now, so that they flee Madinah.”

When Sayyiduna Zayd ibn Arqam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ heard this disgraceful speech, he could not bear it and said to him, “By Allah! You are the humiliated one and the one who sows enmity among his people, whereas Allah Almighty has granted the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ honour and strength, and he is beloved to the Muslims.” Abdullah ibn Ubayy said, “Be quiet. I was only saying this jokingly.” Sayyiduna Zayd ibn Arqam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ conveyed the report to the Presence of Light ﷺ. Sayyiduna Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ asked permission to kill Abdullah ibn Ubayy, but the Noble Prophet ﷺ forbade him and said, “People will say that Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ kills his own companions.” The Noble Prophet ﷺ asked Abdullah ibn Ubayy whether he had said these things. He denied it and swore that he had said nothing. His companions who were present in the blessed assembly began saying that Abdullah ibn Ubayy was an old man and that what he said must be correct; perhaps Sayyiduna Zayd ibn Arqam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ had been mistaken and had not remembered correctly.

Then, when the verses above were revealed and Abdullah ibn Ubayy’s lie became clear, he was told, “Go and ask the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ to seek Allah Almighty’s forgiveness for you.” On hearing this, he turned his neck and said, “You said, ‘Believe,’ so I believed; you said, ‘Give zakat,’ so I gave zakat; now all that remains is that I prostrate to Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ.” Upon this, this noble verse was revealed.

The summary of the verse is that, after the hypocrites’ lie became apparent, when they are admonished and told, “Come, so that Allah’s Messenger may supplicate to Allah Almighty for you, that He may forgive your sins by His grace and conceal your faults,” they turn away and turn their heads to another side. And, O Muslims, you will see them turn away from attending the august court of the Noble Messenger ﷺ out of arrogance. (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 5, 4/271; Madarik, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 5, p. 1244; Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 5, 9/535, excerpted.)

## Ayat 63:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54041

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=6; ayatId=5256; tafseerId=54041; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** سَوَآءٌ عَلَیْهِمْ اَسْتَغْفَرْتَ لَهُمْ اَمْ لَمْ تَسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْؕ-لَنْ یَّغْفِرَ اللّٰهُ لَهُمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الْفٰسِقِیْنَ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** It is the same for them, whether you seek or do not seek forgiveness for them; Allah will never forgive them; indeed, Allah does not guide the transgressing people.

{سَوَآءٌ عَلَیْهِمْ اَسْتَغْفَرْتَ لَهُمْ اَمْ لَمْ تَسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ: It is the same for them whether you seek forgiveness for them or do not seek forgiveness for them.} O Beloved! ﷺ, your seeking forgiveness for them and not seeking forgiveness for them is the same for them. Allah Almighty will never forgive them because they have become firmly rooted in hypocrisy. Indeed, Allah Almighty does not guide those whom He knows to be disobedient.

This command applied when seeking forgiveness for the hypocrites had not yet been prohibited. It was later forbidden. Therefore, it is now prohibited to pray for forgiveness for hypocrites and disbelievers; indeed, praying for forgiveness for a disbeliever is disbelief.

## Ayat 63:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54042

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=7; ayatId=5257; tafseerId=54042; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** هُمُ الَّذِیْنَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ لَا تُنْفِقُوْا عَلٰى مَنْ عِنْدَ رَسُوْلِ اللّٰهِ حَتّٰى یَنْفَضُّوْاؕ-وَ لِلّٰهِ خَزَآىٕنُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِ وَ لٰكِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ لَا یَفْقَهُوْنَ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** They are the ones who say, 'Do not spend upon those who are with Allah's Messenger until they become distressed.' And only to Allah belong the treasures of the heavens and the earth, but the hypocrites do not have sense.

{هُمُ الَّذِیْنَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ: They are the ones who say.} The hypocrites tell people, “Do not spend on the Muhajir Companions present with Allah’s Messenger ﷺ, so that poverty distresses them and they leave of their own accord and no longer remain with you ﷺ.” Yet all the treasures of the heavens and earth belong to Allah Almighty alone. In reality, He alone gives sustenance to everyone. If people stop spending on them, what of it? Allah Almighty will grant them sustenance. But the hypocrites do not understand this, which is why they utter such absurdities. Nor do they yet know the firmness of the Companions’ رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ faith: they can never leave the company of the Presence of Light ﷺ under any circumstances. They know that their sustenance depends not on servants, but on the generosity of their Lord, and they place complete trust in their Lord عزوجل.

Many misguided people today hold a similar belief, claiming that the Presence of Light ﷺ owns nothing, whereas many hadith indicate that, by Allah Almighty’s granting, the Presence of Light ﷺ is the owner of all things. Two of these hadith are noted here:

1. Sayyiduna Muawiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crown of Messengership ﷺ said: “I distribute, and Allah Almighty grants.” (Bukhari, Book of Knowledge, chapter on the one for whom Allah intends good and grants understanding of religion, 1/42, hadith 71.)

2. Sayyiduna Uqbah ibn Amir رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “I have been given the keys to the treasures of the earth.” (Bukhari, Book of Funerals, chapter on praying over a martyr, 1/452, hadith 1344.)

## Ayat 63:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54043

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=8; ayatId=5258; tafseerId=54043; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یَقُوْلُوْنَ لَىٕنْ رَّجَعْنَاۤ اِلَى الْمَدِیْنَةِ لَیُخْرِجَنَّ الْاَعَزُّ مِنْهَا الْاَذَلَّؕ-وَ لِلّٰهِ الْعِزَّةُ وَ لِرَسُوْلِهٖ وَ لِلْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ وَ لٰكِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ لَا یَعْلَمُوْنَ۠   (8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** They say, 'If we return to Madinah, then indeed the honourable one will soon banish the humble one from it.' Whereas, honour (truly) belongs to Allah and to His Messenger and to the Muslims, but the hypocrites know not.

{یَقُوْلُوْنَ: They say.} The hypocrites say, “If we return to Madinah after completing this battle, then surely the one who possesses great honour will expel from it the one who is extremely humiliated.” The hypocrites called themselves honourable and the Muslims humiliated. Refuting them, Allah Almighty states that honour belongs only to Allah, His Messenger and the Muslims, but the hypocrites do not know. If they knew this, they would never say such a thing. It is transmitted that Abdullah ibn Ubayy the hypocrite passed away in his state of hypocrisy only a few days after this verse was revealed. (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 8, 4/274.)

### The Love of the Messenger ﷺ Shown by the Son of Abdullah ibn Ubayy

The son of Abdullah ibn Ubayy was also named Abdullah. He was a firm Muslim and a true lover of the Messenger ﷺ. On the return from the battle, he stood outside illuminated Madinah with his sword drawn and said to his father, “I will not let you enter Madinah until you acknowledge that you are humiliated and that Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ is honourable.” The son had always treated his father well, so his father was greatly astonished. Yet, when it came to the father opposing the Presence of Light ﷺ, no honour or love for his father remained in the son’s heart. At last, his father was compelled to acknowledge, “By Allah, I am humiliated and Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ is honourable.” Only then was he able to enter Madinah. (Sirat Halabiyyah, chapter on his campaigns ﷺ, the expedition of Banu al-Mustaliq, 2/393; Madarij al-Nubuwwah, section three, chapter five, 2/157, excerpted.)

### Lessons Learned from “And Honour Belongs to Allah, His Messenger and the Believers”

Four matters are learned from this verse:

1. Every believer possesses honour. It is unlawful to consider a Muslim nation humiliated or to call it lowly.
2. A believer’s honour comes from faith and righteous deeds, not from money.
3. A believer’s honour is everlasting, not temporary. This is why honour is shown even to a believer’s body and grave.
4. Whoever considers a believer humiliated is himself humiliated in the sight of Allah Almighty. A poor, needy believer possesses honour, while a wealthy disbeliever is worse.

### Types of Hypocrisy and the Signs of Practical Hypocrites

The discussion of the hypocrites has ended. Three hadith concerning the types of hypocrisy and the signs of practical hypocrites are now presented. Sadr al-Shariah Mufti Amjad Ali Azmi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ states concerning hypocrisy:

Hypocrisy means claiming Islam with the tongue while denying Islam in the heart. This too is pure disbelief, and such people will be in the lowest level of Hell. In the blessed era of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, some people became known by this name because the Quran openly testified to their inward disbelief. The Prophet ﷺ recognised each one through his vast knowledge and declared that he was a hypocrite. In the present age, however, no particular person may be called a hypocrite with certainty. Whoever professes Islam before us will be regarded as a Muslim until a statement or action contrary to faith issues from him. A branch of hypocrisy is nevertheless found in this age: many misguided people call themselves Muslims, yet alongside their claim to Islam they deny essentials of religion. (Bahar-e-Shariat, Part One, The Discussion of Faith and Disbelief, 1/182.)

Practical hypocrisy means doing something unworthy of Muslims and characteristic of hypocrites. Two hadith are presented here:

1. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Amr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever has four faults is a complete hypocrite, and whoever has one of these four faults has a trait of hypocrisy until he abandons it: (1) when entrusted with something, he betrays the trust; (2) when he speaks, he lies; (3) when he makes a promise, he breaks it; and (4) when he quarrels, he uses abusive language.” (Bukhari, Book of Faith, chapter on the sign of a hypocrite, 1/25, hadith 34.)

2. Sayyiduna Abd al-Rahman ibn Harmalah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “The distinction between us and the hypocrites is attendance at the Isha and Fajr prayers. The hypocrites are unable to attend these two prayers.” (Al-Sunan al-Kubra of Bayhaqi, Book of Prayer, chapter on what has been reported concerning the stern warning for abandoning congregational prayer without an excuse, 3/83, hadith 4953.)

May Allah Almighty protect us from hypocrisy and from doing the deeds of hypocrites. Ameen.

## Ayat 63:9

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54044

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=9; ayatId=5259; tafseerId=54044; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ اَمْوَالُكُمْ وَ لَاۤ اَوْلَادُكُمْ عَنْ ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِۚ-وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْ ذٰلِكَ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْخٰسِرُوْنَ(9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Let not your wealth or your children (or) anything cause you to neglect the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does this, so it is they who are in loss.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: O believers!} The preceding verses described the conditions of the hypocrites. From here, the believers are advised: O believers! Do not let your wealth and children, like those of the hypocrites, make you heedless of Allah Almighty’s remembrance. Whoever becomes so occupied with the world that he forgets religion, neglects his own condition out of love for wealth, and remains heedless of the comfort of the Hereafter for the sake of his children’s happiness is among those who suffer loss, because such people preferred the perishing world to the everlasting blessings of the home of the Hereafter. (Khazin, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 9, 4/274; Madarik, Al-Munafiqun, under verse 9, p. 1245, excerpted.)

A blessed hadith concerning worldly wealth is noted here. Sayyiduna Hakim ibn Hizam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: I asked the Noble Messenger ﷺ for some wealth and pleaded repeatedly. He said: “O Hakim! Why do you ask so excessively? O Hakim! Indeed, this wealth is green and sweet, yet it is also the dirt from people’s hands. Allah Almighty’s hand is above the hand of the giver; the giver’s hand is above the hand of the recipient; and the hand of the recipient is the lowest.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad of the Makkans, Musnad of Hakim ibn Hizam, 5/228, hadith 15321.)

**Arabic:** وَ اَنْفِقُوْا مِنْ مَّا رَزَقْنٰكُمْ مِّنْ قَبْلِ اَنْ یَّاْتِیَ اَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ فَیَقُوْلَ رَبِّ لَوْ لَاۤ اَخَّرْتَنِیْۤ اِلٰۤى اَجَلٍ قَرِیْبٍۙ-فَاَصَّدَّقَ وَ اَكُنْ مِّنَ الصّٰلِحِیْنَ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And from what We have provided you spend some in Our path before this that death comes upon any one of you then he says, 'O my Lord! Why did you not give me respite for a little while, that I would have given charity and become of the virtuous?'

{وَ اَنْفِقُوْا مِنْ مَّا رَزَقْنٰكُمْ: And spend some of what We have provided you.} O believers! Pay the obligatory charities due from the sustenance We have given you, and do so before the signs of death appear and the tongue becomes unable to speak, lest someone among you reaches the time of death and says inwardly, “O my Lord! Why did You not give me respite for a little while, so that I might give charity and become among the righteous?”

## Ayat 63:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54046

**Metadata:** surah=63; ayat=11; ayatId=5261; tafseerId=54046; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ لَنْ یُّؤَخِّرَ اللّٰهُ نَفْسًا اِذَا جَآءَ اَجَلُهَاؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ خَبِیْرٌۢ بِمَا تَعْمَلُوْنَ۠    (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And Allah will never give respite to any soul when its promise arrives. And Allah is Aware of your deeds.

{وَ لَنْ یُّؤَخِّرَ اللّٰهُ نَفْسًا اِذَا جَآءَ اَجَلُهَا: Allah will never give a soul respite when its appointed time arrives.} Remember that when Allah Almighty’s promise arrives, He will never delay any soul. Allah Almighty is Aware of all your deeds and will recompense you for them.

It should be remembered that the promise mentioned here means a decree that has been finalised, known as irrevocable divine decree (قضاءِ مُبْرَم). Concerning it, Allah Almighty states:

“اِذَا جَآءَ اَجَلُهُمْ فَلَا یَسْتَاْخِرُوْنَ سَاعَةً وَّ لَا یَسْتَقْدِمُوْنَ” (Yunus: 49)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “When their appointed time arrives, they will be unable to move back from it even for an hour, nor will they be able to move ahead.”

However, change can occur in conditional divine decree (قضاءِ مُعَلَّق): an impending death is averted and lifespans are extended. Concerning this, Allah Almighty states:

“یَمْحُوا اللّٰهُ مَا یَشَآءُ وَ یُثْبِتُ ۚۖ-وَ عِنْدَهٗۤ اُمُّ الْكِتٰبِ” (Ar-Ra'd: 39)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Allah erases and maintains whatever He wills, and the original record is with Him.”

This also includes what Satan requested:

“رَبِّ فَاَنْظِرْنِیْۤ اِلٰى یَوْمِ یُبْعَثُوْنَ” (Al-Hijr: 36)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O my Lord! Grant me respite until the day when people will be raised.”

Allah Almighty had said:

“فَاِنَّكَ مِنَ الْمُنْظَرِیْنَ” (Al-Hijr: 37)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “So indeed, you are among those who have been granted respite.”

This too falls under conditional divine decree. It is therefore clear that every verse is correct according to its particular context.
