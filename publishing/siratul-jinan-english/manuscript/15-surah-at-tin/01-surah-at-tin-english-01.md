# Surah At-Tin: English Translation

## Ayat 95:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54442

**Metadata:** surah=95; ayat=1; ayatId=6193; tafseerId=54442; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ التِّیْنِ وَ الزَّیْتُوْنِۙ   (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of the fig and the olive.

{وَالتِّیْنِ وَالزَّیْتُوْنِ: By the oath of the fig and the olive.} In this verse, Allah Almighty has sworn by the fig and the olive because these things contain benefits and advantages that point to the power of their Creator, the Lord Almighty. The fig is an extremely excellent fruit. It has no waste, is digested very quickly, is highly beneficial, removes constipation, expels sand and stones from the bladder, dissolves the knots of harmful matter trapped in the liver and spleen, makes the body stout, and clears phlegm. The olive, meanwhile, is a blessed tree. Its oil is used for lighting and is also eaten like a curry; no other oil possesses this quality. Its tree grows on dry mountains in which there is no trace of moisture, flourishes without cultivation, and remains for thousands of years. (Khazin, At-Tin, under verse 1, 4/390; Ruh al-Bayan, At-Tin, under verse 1, 10/466–467, excerpted.)

### Hadiths concerning the fig and the olive

#### The fig

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Dharr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “Eat figs. If I were to say that it is a fruit descended from Paradise, I could say so, because the fruit of Paradise has no stone. Therefore, eat it, for it removes piles and benefits the pain of gout.” (Musnad al-Firdaws, Book of the Letter Kaf, 3/243, hadith 4716.)

#### The olive

Allah Almighty said at another place:

“وَ شَجَرَةً تَخْرُ جُ مِنْ طُوْرِ سَیْنَآءَ تَنْۢبُتُ بِالدُّهْنِ وَ صِبْغٍ لِّلْاٰكِلِیْنَ” (Al-Mu'minun: 20)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And (We created) a tree that emerges from Mount Sinai, growing oil and a relish for those who eat.”

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Mu'adh ibn Jabal رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “The miswak of the blessed olive tree is very good, because it makes the mouth fragrant and removes its bad odour. It is my miswak and the miswak of the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام before me.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat, Book of the Letter Alif, the one whose name is Ahmad, 1/201, hadith 678.)

{وَطُوْرِ سِیْنِیْنَ: And by Mount Sinai.} Tur is the mountain upon which Allah Almighty honoured Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام by speaking with him, and Sina is the name of the place where this mountain is situated. This place is called Sina because it is beautiful or blessed. According to some exegetes, Tur Sina means a beautiful or blessed mountain. According to others, every mountain where fruit-bearing trees are abundant is called Tur Sina. (Tafsir Kabir, At-Tin, under verse 2, 11/211–212.)

It is learned from this that when a place or location becomes associated with the accepted servants of Allah Almighty, that place also becomes honoured in the court of Allah Almighty.

{وَهٰذَاالْبَلَدِالْاَمِیْنِ: And by this secure city.} That is, and by the secure city of Makkah Mukarramah. Imam Abdullah ibn Ahmad Nasafi رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ states: “Mentioning the fig, the olive, Mount Sinai, and Makkah Mukarramah reveals the greatness and nobility of these blessed places, and makes clear the blessings that appeared because the Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and saints رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِمْ lived there. Thus, the place where figs and olives grow is the place to which Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام migrated, and Sayyiduna Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was born and raised there as well. Tur is the place where Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was called. The Crowned One of Messengership ﷺ was born in Makkah Mukarramah, announced his prophethood and messengership in this city, and the Ka'bah is also here, towards which Muslims throughout the world turn their faces in prayer.” (Madarik, At-Tin, under verse 3, p. 1360.)

## Ayat 95:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54443

**Metadata:** surah=95; ayat=4; ayatId=6196; tafseerId=54443; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْاِنْسَانَ فِیْۤ اَحْسَنِ تَقْوِیْمٍ٘    (4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** We have indeed created man in the best form.

{لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْاِنْسَانَ فِیْۤ اَحْسَنِ تَقْوِیْمٍ: We have indeed created man in the best form.} After mentioning the oath by the fig, the olive, Mount Sinai, and the city of Makkah, Allah Almighty declared that He has indeed created man in the best form. He gave proportion and harmony to his limbs. He made him upright in stature, rather than bent over like animals; He made him eat by taking food with his hands, rather than seizing it with his mouth like animals; and He adorned him with knowledge, understanding, intellect, discernment, and the ability to speak. (Khazin, At-Tin, under verse 4, 4/391; Madarik, At-Tin, under verse 4, p. 1360, excerpted.)

### A means of attaining knowledge of Allah Almighty

If a person reflects upon his own creation while considering the other creations of Allah Almighty, it will become as clear to him as daylight what tremendous blessings of outward and inward beauty Allah Almighty has bestowed upon him. The more deeply he reflects upon this, the more knowledge he will gain of the greatness and power of Allah Almighty, and he will understand this great blessing very well.

## Ayat 95:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54444

**Metadata:** surah=95; ayat=5; ayatId=6197; tafseerId=54444; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَۙ    (5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** We then turned him towards the lowest of the low state (i.e. old age).

{ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَ: We then turned him towards the lowest of the low state.} One meaning is that after creating man in the best form, Allah Almighty turns him towards old age. At that time the body becomes weak, the limbs become ineffective, the intellect becomes deficient, the back bends, the hair turns white, wrinkles appear in the skin, and he becomes dependent in fulfilling his needs. A second meaning is that when he did not give thanks for his excellent form, persisted in disobeying Allah Almighty, and did not believe, his end was that the lowest depths of Hell were made his dwelling. (Khazin, At-Tin, under verse 5, 4/391; Madarik, At-Tin, under verse 5, pp. 1360–1361, excerpted.)

### Lessons from the verse “ثُمَّ رَدَدْنٰهُ اَسْفَلَ سٰفِلِیْنَ”

Three matters are learned from this:

1. It is not wise to choose old age for dedicating oneself to the worship of Allah Almighty, because the limbs no longer retain in old age the strength for worship that they possess in youth.

2. We should give thanks for the blessing of the human form that Allah Almighty has granted us by protecting ourselves from disobeying Him.

3. Giving strength and power after creation and then returning a person to weakness is evidence that the Being capable of this is also capable of bringing us back to life after our passing. Allah Almighty said at another place:

“وَ مَنْ نُّعَمِّرْهُ نُنَكِّسْهُ فِی الْخَلْقِؕ-اَفَلَا یَعْقِلُوْنَ” (Ya-Sin: 68)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And whoever We grant a long life, We reverse him in creation, so do they not understand?”

He also said:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ فِیْ رَیْبٍ مِّنَ الْبَعْثِ فَاِنَّا خَلَقْنٰكُمْ مِّنْ تُرَابٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ نُّطْفَةٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ عَلَقَةٍ ثُمَّ مِنْ مُّضْغَةٍ مُّخَلَّقَةٍ وَّ غَیْرِ مُخَلَّقَةٍ لِّنُبَیِّنَ لَكُمْؕ-وَ نُقِرُّ فِی الْاَرْحَامِ مَا نَشَآءُ اِلٰۤى اَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى ثُمَّ نُخْرِجُكُمْ طِفْلًا ثُمَّ لِتَبْلُغُوْۤا اَشُدَّكُمْۚ-وَ مِنْكُمْ مَّنْ یُّتَوَفّٰى وَ مِنْكُمْ مَّنْ یُّرَدُّ اِلٰۤى اَرْذَلِ الْعُمُرِ لِكَیْلَا یَعْلَمَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ عِلْمٍ شَیْــٴًـا” (Al-Hajj: 5)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O people! If you have any doubt about being raised on the Day of Judgement, then reflect that We created you from dust, then from a drop of water, then from a clot of blood, then from a lump of flesh, formed and unformed, so that We may manifest Our power to you. We keep in the wombs whatever We wish for a specified term, then We bring you out as a child, then (grant you life) so that you may reach your full strength. Among you are those who are called back, and among you are those who are returned to the most worthless age, so that after having knowledge they know nothing.”

## Ayat 95:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54445

**Metadata:** surah=95; ayat=6; ayatId=6198; tafseerId=54445; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ عَمِلُوا الصّٰلِحٰتِ فَلَهُمْ اَجْرٌ غَیْرُ مَمْنُوْنٍﭤ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Except those who believed and did good deeds, that for them is limitless reward.

{اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: Except those who believed.} That is, those who believed and performed good deeds shall have limitless reward. Even if, because of the weakness of old age, they cannot perform as many acts of worship as they did in youth, and their deeds decrease, by the grace of Allah Almighty they will receive the same reward that they received for deeds performed during youth and strength, and the same number of deeds will be recorded for them as were recorded in their youth. The lowest depths of Hell will not be their dwelling. (Khazin, At-Tin, under verse 6, 4/391; Madarik, At-Tin, under verse 6, p. 1361, excerpted.)

A similar matter is also mentioned in a noble hadith. Sayyiduna Abu Musa al-Ash'ari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “When a Muslim servant becomes ill or is travelling, the reward of the deeds that he used to perform while healthy and resident is written for him, though he could not perform them because of illness or travel.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad of the Kufans, hadith of Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, 7/177, hadith 19774.)

### Lessons from the verse “اِلَّا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا”

Two matters are learned from this verse:

1. Faith precedes deeds, and no good deed is valid without faith.

2. Being granted a long life and having righteous deeds are great blessings. Sayyiduna Abu Bakrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: “A desert Arab asked in the court of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who is the best among people?’ He said: ‘The best person is the one whose life is long and whose deeds are righteous.’ He asked, ‘Who is the worst among people?’ He said: ‘The worst person is the one whose life is long and whose deeds are evil.’” (Tirmidhi, Book of Asceticism, chapter 22, 4/148, hadith 2337.)

## Ayat 95:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54446

**Metadata:** surah=95; ayat=7; ayatId=6199; tafseerId=54446; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَمَا یُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّیْنِﭤ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Now (after all these proofs), what causes you to belie the Judgement (Day)?

{فَمَا یُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّیْنِ: Now (after all these proofs), what causes you to belie the Judgement (Day)?} That is, O disbelieving man! Have you not reflected upon your form, the beginning of your creation, your youth, and your old age, so that you would say that the Being capable of these things is also capable of bringing me back to life after my passing, taking account of my deeds, and recompensing me for them? After this decisive proof and clear argument, what causes you to belie the Day of Judgement, and why do you deny being raised after passing, reckoning on the Day of Judgement, and recompense for deeds despite seeing these powers of Allah Almighty? (Khazin, At-Tin, under verse 7, 4/391, abridged.)

{اَلَیْسَ اللّٰهُ بِاَحْكَمِ الْحٰكِمِیْنَ: Is Allah not the Greatest Ruler of all rulers?} It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Illuminated Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever recites ‘اَلَیْسَ اللّٰهُ بِاَحْكَمِ الْحٰكِمِیْنَ’ while reciting the Surah ‘وَالتِّیْنِ وَالزَّیْتُوْنِ’ should say: ‘بَلٰی وَاَنَا عَلٰی ذَالِکَ مِنَ الشَّاہِدِیْن’, meaning, ‘Why not? Indeed, He is, and I am among those who bear witness to this.’” (Tirmidhi, Book of Tafsir, chapter on Surah At-Tin, 5/230, hadith 3358.)
