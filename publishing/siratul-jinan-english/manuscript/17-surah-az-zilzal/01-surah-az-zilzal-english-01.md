# Surah Az-Zilzal: English Translation

## Ayat 99:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54466

**Metadata:** surah=99; ayat=1; ayatId=6237; tafseerId=54466; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْاَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَاۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** When the earth is shaken violently with its destined tremor.

{اِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْاَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا: When the earth is shaken violently with its destined tremor.} Allah Almighty states that when the earth is shaken with its earthquake, no tree, building, or mountain will remain upon it. Indeed, because of the earthquake's intensity, everything will be broken into pieces. This trembling of the earth will occur either when the Day of Judgement draws near or on the Day of Judgement itself. (Khazin, Az-Zilzal, under verse 1, 4/400.)

The terror of the earthquake of the Day of Judgement is described by Allah Almighty at another place:

“يٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوْا رَبَّكُمْۚ-اِنَّ زَلْزَلَةَ السَّاعَةِ شَیْءٌ عَظِیْمٌ(۱)یَوْمَ تَرَوْنَهَا تَذْهَلُ كُلُّ مُرْضِعَةٍ عَمَّاۤ اَرْضَعَتْ وَ تَضَعُ كُلُّ ذَاتِ حَمْلٍ حَمْلَهَا وَ تَرَى النَّاسَ سُكٰرٰى وَ مَا هُمْ بِسُكٰرٰى وَ لٰكِنَّ عَذَابَ اللّٰهِ شَدِیْدٌ‘‘(حج:۱،۲)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O people! Fear your Lord. Indeed, the earthquake of the Day of Judgement is a very great thing. On the Day when you see it, every nursing mother will forget the child she is nursing, every pregnant woman will drop her burden, and you will see people as though they are intoxicated, although they will not be intoxicated; but the punishment of Allah is severe.”

## Ayat 99:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54467

**Metadata:** surah=99; ayat=2; ayatId=6238; tafseerId=54467; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اَخْرَجَتِ الْاَرْضُ اَثْقَالَهَاۙ    (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And (when) the earth casts out (all of) its burdens.

{وَ اَخْرَجَتِ الْاَرْضُ اَثْقَالَهَا: And (when) the earth casts out (all of) its burdens.} That is, when the earth will cast out all the treasures and the dead present within it. It should be remembered that human beings and jinn are weight-bearing beings. As long as they are upon the earth, they are a burden upon it, and when they are inside the earth, they are a burden for it. For this reason, human beings and jinn are called ثَقَلَین, because whether they are dead or alive, the earth carries their burden. (Madarik, Az-Zilzal, under verse 2, p. 1368; Khazin, Az-Zilzal, under verse 2, 4/400–401, excerpted.)

Allah Almighty describes this action of the earth at another place:

“وَ اِذَا الْاَرْضُ مُدَّتْۙ(۳)وَاَلْقَتْ مَا فِیْهَا وَ تَخَلَّتْۙ(۴)وَ اَذِنَتْ لِرَبِّهَا وَ حُقَّتْؕ(۵) یٰۤاَیُّهَا الْاِنْسَانُ اِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ اِلٰى رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلٰقِیْهِ ‘‘(انشقاق:۳-۶)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And when the earth is stretched out, and it casts out whatever is in it and becomes empty, and it listens to the command of its Lord, and it is worthy of that. O man! Indeed, you are striving towards your Lord with great effort, then you will meet Him.”

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “The earth will disgorge its pieces of the heart like pillars of gold and silver. The murderer will see them and say, ‘It was because of this wealth that I killed.’ The one who severed family ties will say, ‘It was because of this that I severed family ties.’ The thief will see it and say, ‘It was because of this wealth that my hand was cut off.’ Then everyone will leave that wealth, and no one will take anything from it.” (Muslim, Book of Zakah, chapter on encouraging charity before there is no one to accept it, p. 505, hadith 62 [1013].)

## Ayat 99:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54468

**Metadata:** surah=99; ayat=4; ayatId=6240; tafseerId=54468; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** یَوْمَىٕذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَاؕ    (4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** On that Day, the earth will narrate (all) its news.

{یَوْمَىٕذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَا: On that Day, the earth will narrate (all) its news.} The summary of this verse and the following verse is that when these matters occur, the earth will, by the command of Allah Almighty, tell creation its news on that Day and will describe every good and evil deed performed upon it. The purpose of this will be that the earth may complain about the disobedient and thank the obedient. Thus, it will say: “So-and-so performed prayer upon me; so-and-so paid Zakah; so-and-so fasted; and so-and-so performed Hajj. Whereas so-and-so disbelieved, so-and-so committed adultery, so-and-so stole, and so-and-so committed oppression.” Indeed, upon hearing this, the disbeliever will wish that he had been thrown into Hell. (Khazin, Az-Zilzal, under verses 4–5, 4/401; Tafsir Kabir, Az-Zilzal, under verse 4, 11/255, excerpted.)

### The earth is a witness to our deeds

It is learned from this that the earth is a witness to our deeds, and on the Day of Judgement it will describe our deeds before us. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recited this verse, “یَوْمَىٕذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَا”, and then said: “Do you know what its news is?” The Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ submitted: “Allah Almighty and His Messenger ﷺ know best.” The Holy Prophet ﷺ said: “Its news is that it will testify concerning the deeds that every man and woman performed upon its back. It will say: ‘This person performed this deed on such-and-such day, and that person performed that deed on such-and-such day.’ This is its news.” (Tirmidhi, Book of the Description of the Day of Judgement, Softening of Hearts and Piety, chapter 7, 4/194, hadith 2437.)

Therefore, every person should be cautious of the earth when committing a sin. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Rabi'ah Jurashi رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Be cautious of the earth, for it is your origin. Whoever performs a good or bad deed upon it, it will report it.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Kabir, chapter on the letter Ra, Rabi'ah ibn al-Ghaz al-Jurashi … etc., 5/65, hadith 4596.)

Similarly, the limbs with which we commit sins are also witnesses to our deeds. On the Day of Judgement, they will describe all the deeds that were performed through them. Allah Almighty states:

“اِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَ الْبَصَرَ وَ الْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ اُولٰٓىٕكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـٴُـوْلًا” (بنی اسرائیل:۳۶)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Indeed, the ear, the eye, and the heart—all of these will be questioned.”

He also states:

“یَّوْمَ تَشْهَدُ عَلَیْهِمْ اَلْسِنَتُهُمْ وَ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ اَرْجُلُهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَعْمَلُوْنَ” (نور:۲۴)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “On the Day when their tongues, their hands, and their feet will testify against them concerning their deeds.”

Therefore, whenever a person is about to commit a sin with his ears, eyes, heart, tongue, hands, or feet, he should keep in mind that on the Day of Judgement these very limbs will testify concerning that sin.

## Ayat 99:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54469

**Metadata:** surah=99; ayat=6; ayatId=6242; tafseerId=54469; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** یَوْمَىٕذٍ یَّصْدُرُ النَّاسُ اَشْتَاتًا ﳔ لِّیُرَوْا اَعْمَالَهُمْﭤ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** On that Day, people will return towards their Lord, in different paths, in order to be shown their deeds.

{یَوْمَىٕذٍ یَّصْدُرُ النَّاسُ اَشْتَاتًا: On that Day, people will return in different groups.} One meaning of this verse is that on the Day of Judgement, after appearing at the place of reckoning, people will return from there by different routes. Some will go through the right side towards Paradise, while others will go through the left side towards Hell, so that they may be shown the recompense for their deeds. A second meaning is that on the day when the matters mentioned earlier occur, people will return from their graves towards the place of reckoning in different conditions. Some will have white faces and some will have black faces; some will be riding, while others will walk bound in chains and fetters; some will be in a state of safety, while others will be terrified, so that they may be shown their deeds. (Khazin, Az-Zilzal, under verse 6, 4/401; Ruh al-Bayan, Az-Zilzal, under verse 6, 10/493, excerpted.)

## Ayat 99:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54470

**Metadata:** surah=99; ayat=7; ayatId=6243; tafseerId=54470; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَمَنْ یَّعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَیْرًا یَّرَهٗﭤ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So, whosoever does a good deed equal to the weight of an atom, (he) shall see it.

{وَ مَنْ یَّعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا یَّرَهٗ: And whoever does an evil deed equal to the weight of an atom, (he) shall see it.} Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا states that every believer and disbeliever will be shown his good and evil deeds on the Day of Judgement. The believer will be shown his good deeds and evil deeds; Allah Almighty will forgive his evil deeds and grant him reward for his good deeds. The good deeds of the disbeliever will be rejected because they will have been destroyed on account of his disbelief, and he will be punished for his evil deeds.

Sayyiduna Muhammad ibn Ka'b al-Qurazi رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that if a disbeliever performed even an atom's weight of good, he would see its recompense in this world, until, when he leaves the world, he has no good deed remaining. A believer will receive the punishment for his evil deeds in this world, so that no evil deed will remain with him in the Hereafter. Some exegetes have said that the preceding verse concerns believers, while this verse concerns disbelievers. (Khazin, Az-Zilzal, under verse 8, 4/401; Madarik, Az-Zilzal, under verse 8, p. 1368, excerpted.)

### Even a little good is beneficial, and even a small sin is a burden

It is learned from this verse that even a little good is beneficial, and even a small sin is a burden. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “A servant sometimes says a word that pleases Allah Almighty, without paying attention to it”—that is, some words seem extremely insignificant to a person—“and Allah Almighty raises him many degrees because of it. And sometimes a person says a word that angers Allah Almighty, without considering it, and because of it he falls into Hell.” (Bukhari, Book of Softening of Hearts, chapter on guarding the tongue, 4/241, hadith 6478.)
