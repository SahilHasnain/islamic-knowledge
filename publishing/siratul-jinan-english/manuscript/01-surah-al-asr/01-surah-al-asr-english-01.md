# Surah Al-'Asr: English Translation

## Ayat 103:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54481
**Metadata:** surah=103; ayat=1; ayatId=6279; tafseerId=54481; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ الْعَصْرِۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of that era of the Beloved (Prophet Muhammad).

{وَالْعَصْرِ: By the oath of the era.} One opinion of the exegetes regarding the word “Asr” mentioned in this verse is that it means an era. Since an era contains extraordinary events, and the changing conditions within it become a source of admonition for the observer, and since these things point towards Allah Almighty’s power, wisdom, and Oneness, it is possible that the oath of an era is intended here.

The second opinion is that “Asr” also refers to the time before sunset. It is therefore possible that an oath was sworn by that time concerning the one who suffers loss, just as the oath of “Duha”, meaning the forenoon, was mentioned concerning the one who gains profit.

The third opinion is that “Asr” may refer to the Asr prayer, which is the last of the daytime acts of worship. The oath may have been sworn by it here because of its excellence.

The fourth opinion is this, and it is the one towards which the heart inclines: the era intended here is the particular era of the Crown of Prophethood صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, an era of great goodness and blessings. Thus, just as Allah Almighty swore by the dwelling and place of the Radiant Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ in “لَاۤ اُقْسِمُ بِهٰذَا الْبَلَدِ”, and just as He swore by the blessed life of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ by saying “لَعَمْرُكَ”, so here, by saying “وَالْعَصْرِ”, He swore by the sacred era of His Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ.

It is learned from this that the era of the Leader of the Messengers صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ is superior to all eras, the city of the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ is superior to all cities, and the blessed life of the Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ is superior to all lives. (Khazin, Surah Al-'Asr, under verse 1, 4/405; Sawi, Surah Al-'Asr, under verse 1, 6/2419, excerpted.)

### A'la Hazrat رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says:

God has bestowed on you a rank sublime,
Such as none received before, nor shall receive in time;
The Glorious Quran has sworn by your city and your speech,
O King, and by your blessed life, beyond all reach.

{اِنَّ الْاِنْسَانَ لَفِیْ خُسْرٍ: Indeed, mankind is surely in loss.} The summary of this verse and the verse after it is that Allah Almighty mentioned an oath and then declared that mankind is surely in loss: the lifespan that is his capital and principal wealth is decreasing at every moment. However, those who believed, performed good deeds, advised one another to have faith and perform righteous deeds, and advised one another to remain patient over the troubles and hardships that came to them in the path of religion are not in loss, by the grace of Allah Almighty. Rather, they are the ones who gain profit, because every part of their lives that passed was spent in goodness and obedience. (Ruh al-Bayan, Surah Al-'Asr, under verses 2–3, 10/505–506; Khazin, Surah Al-'Asr, under verses 2–3, 4/405, excerpted.)

Similarly, Allah Almighty states at another place:

“اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَتْلُوْنَ كِتٰبَ اللّٰهِ وَ اَقَامُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اَنْفَقُوْا مِمَّا رَزَقْنٰهُمْ سِرًّا وَّ عَلَانِیَةً یَّرْجُوْنَ تِجَارَةً لَّنْ تَبُوْرَۙ(۲۹) لِیُوَفِّیَهُمْ اُجُوْرَهُمْ وَ یَزِیْدَهُمْ مِّنْ فَضْلِهٖؕ-اِنَّهٗ غَفُوْرٌ شَكُوْرٌ” (Faatir: 29–30)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: Indeed, those who recite the Book of Allah, establish prayer, and spend secretly and openly in Our way from the sustenance We have given them hope for a trade that will never perish, so that Allah may give them their rewards in full and grant them even more from His grace. Indeed, He is Most Forgiving, Most Appreciative.

### Information derived from verses 2 and 3 of Surah Al-'Asr

Three matters are learned from these verses:

1. A person’s life is his most valuable capital, and he can benefit from this capital only when he spends it in obedience and submission to Allah Almighty. If he continues spending this capital in disbelief in Allah Almighty, disobedience to Him, and sins, he will gain no benefit; rather, he will suffer a very great loss. Therefore, every person should consider his life a valuable opportunity and become engaged in obedience and worship of Allah Almighty.

2. The portion of a person’s life that passes in worship of Allah Almighty is the best portion.

3. Turning away from the world and becoming occupied with seeking the Hereafter and loving it is a cause of happiness for a person. Allah Almighty states:

“وَ مَنْ اَرَادَ الْاٰخِرَةَ وَ سَعٰى لَهَا سَعْیَهَا وَ هُوَ مُؤْمِنٌ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ كَانَ سَعْیُهُمْ مَّشْكُوْرًا” (Bani Isra'il: 19)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: And whoever desires the Hereafter and strives for it as one should, while being a believer, then such people are those whose effort will be appreciated.

### Issues learned from verse 3 of Surah Al-'Asr

Nur-ul-Irfan states that several issues are learned from this verse:

1. One should first become righteous oneself and then guide others, as this order is stated in the verse.

2. One should always convey the message, as is understood from the unrestricted wording of “وَ تَوَاصَوْا”.

3. Every Muslim should be one who conveys the message. Whatever matter a person knows correctly, he should tell it to the people. Conveying the message is not restricted to scholars, as is evident from the generality of the subject of “وَ تَوَاصَوْا”.

4. One should convey the message in every circumstance; it is not restricted to gatherings or stages.

5. Conveying the message is obligatory just like prayer and fasting.

6. The general public should convey the message with their hearts and tongues, scholars with their tongues and pens, and rulers through their power and authority. The basic principle is that everyone should strive, according to his ability, to spread the invitation towards goodness.

## Ayat 103:3
