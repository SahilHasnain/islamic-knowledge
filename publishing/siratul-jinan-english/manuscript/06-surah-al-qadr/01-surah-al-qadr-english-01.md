# Surah Al-Qadr: English Translation

## Ayat 97:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54456
**Metadata:** surah=97; ayat=1; ayatId=6222; tafseerId=54456; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّاۤ اَنْزَلْنٰهُ فِیْ لَیْلَةِ الْقَدْرِﭕ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** We have indeed sent down it (i.e. the Qur'aan) on the Night of Decree.

{اِنَّاۤ اَنْزَلْنٰهُ فِیْ لَیْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ: We have indeed sent down it (i.e. the Qur'aan) on the Night of Decree.} That is, indeed We sent down this Holy Quran all at once from the Preserved Tablet to the heaven of the world on the Night of Decree.

### The Virtues of the Night of Decree

The Night of Decree is a night of honour and blessing. It is called the Night of Decree because the commands for the entire year are enforced on this night, and the angels are appointed to the tasks and services of the entire year. It has also been said that it is called the Night of Decree because of its honour and rank above other nights. It is also transmitted that good deeds are accepted on this night and valued in the Divine Court, and for this reason it is called the Night of Decree. (Khazin, Al-Qadr, under verse 1, 4/395.)

Many virtues of this night have been mentioned in hadiths. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Whoever spends this night in worship, standing in prayer with faith and sincerity, Allah Almighty forgives his previous minor sins.” (Bukhari, Book of Faith, chapter on standing in prayer on the Night of Decree being part of faith, 1/25, hadith 35.)

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that when the month of Ramadan arrived, the Illuminated Presence ﷺ said: “Indeed, this month has come to you, and in it there is a night which is better than a thousand months. Whoever is deprived of this night is deprived of all goodness, and only the one whose lot is deprivation remains deprived.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Fasting, chapter on what has been reported concerning the virtue of the month of Ramadan, 2/298, hadith 1644.)

Therefore, every Muslim should spend this night in worship and seek forgiveness abundantly during it, as Sayyidatuna Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا says: “I asked: ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, if I come to know which night is the Night of Decree, what should I say during it?’ He said: ‘Say: اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنَّکَ عَفُوٌّ کَرِیْمٌ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّیْ “O Allah, indeed You are Most Pardoning and Most Generous; You love to pardon, so pardon my sins too.”’” (Tirmidhi, Book of Supplications, chapter 84, 5/306, hadith 3524.)

She also says: “If I came to know which night is the Night of Decree, I would supplicate abundantly on that night: ‘O Allah, I ask You for forgiveness and well-being.’” (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah, Book of Supplication, supplication for well-being, 7/27, hadith 8.)

### The Night of Decree Comes Once a Year

It should be remembered that the Night of Decree comes once in the entire year. Numerous narrations establish that it occurs in the last ten days of Ramadan, and most often it is one of the odd nights among them. According to some scholars, the Night of Decree is the twenty-seventh night of Ramadan, and this is also narrated from Imam-e-A'zam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. (Madarik, Al-Qadr, under verse 1, p. 1364.)

### Reasons for Keeping the Night of Decree Hidden

Imam Fakhr al-Din al-Razi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says that Allah عزَّوَجَلَّ has kept the Night of Decree hidden for several reasons.

1. Just as He kept other things hidden, Allah عزَّوَجَلَّ kept His pleasure hidden among acts of obedience so that servants would desire every act of obedience. He kept His displeasure hidden among sins so that they would continue avoiding every sin. He kept His saint hidden among people so that people would honour everyone. He kept the acceptance of supplication hidden among supplications so that they would exert themselves in all supplications. He kept the Greatest Name hidden among the Names so that they would honour all the Names. He kept the middle prayer hidden among the prayers so that they would observe all the prayers regularly. He kept the acceptance of repentance hidden so that a servant would persist in all forms of repentance, and He kept the time of death hidden so that a servant would remain fearful. In the same way, He kept the Night of Decree hidden so that people would honour all the nights of Ramadan.

2. It is as though Allah Almighty says: “If I had specified the Night of Decree, while I also know your boldness in sin, then if desire ever brought you to the edge of sin on that night and you fell into sin, your sin despite knowing that it was the Night of Decree would be more severe than sin committed unknowingly. Therefore, I have kept it hidden.”

3. It is as though He says: “I kept this night hidden so that the servant bound by the commands of the Sacred Law would strive to seek it and earn the reward for that effort.”

4. When a servant is not certain about the Night of Decree, he will strive in obedience to Allah عزَّوَجَلَّ on every night of Ramadan, hoping that this might be the Night of Decree. (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Qadr, under verse 1, 11/229–230.)

## Ayat 97:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54457
**Metadata:** surah=97; ayat=2; ayatId=6223; tafseerId=54457; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ مَاۤ اَدْرٰىكَ مَا لَیْلَةُ الْقَدْرِﭤ(2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And what have you come to know what the Night of Decree is?

{لَیْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ ﳔ خَیْرٌ مِّنْ اَلْفِ شَهْرٍ: The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.} The magnificent virtues of the Night of Decree are being described from here. One virtue of the Night of Decree is that it is better than those thousand months which are without the Night of Decree, and performing a good deed on this one night is better than performing deeds for a thousand nights. (Khazin, Al-Qadr, under verse 3, 4/397; Madarik, Al-Qadr, under verse 3, p. 1364, abridged.)

### One Night Better Than a Thousand Months

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Mujahid رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Prophet ﷺ mentioned a man from the Children of Israel who performed jihad in the path of Allah عزَّوَجَلَّ for a thousand months. The Muslims were amazed by this, so Allah Almighty revealed these verses:

“اِنَّاۤ اَنْزَلْنٰهُ فِیْ لَیْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ ۖ(۱) وَ مَاۤ اَدْرٰىكَ مَا لَیْلَةُ الْقَدْرِؕ(۲) لَیْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ ﳔ خَیْرٌ مِّنْ اَلْفِ شَهْرٍ”

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: We have indeed sent down it (i.e. the Qur'aan) on the Night of Decree. And what have you come to know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. (Sunan al-Kubra of Bayhaqi, Book of Fasting, chapter on the virtue of the Night of Decree, 4/505, hadith 8522.)

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “Allah Almighty granted the gift of the Night of Decree to my Ummah, and He did not grant this night to anyone before them.” (Musnad al-Firdaws, chapter alif, 1/173, hadith 647.)

Mufti Na'im al-Din Muradabadi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: “It is the generosity of Allah Almighty upon His Beloved (صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ) that if the followers of the Beloved (صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَسَلَّمَ) worship on one night of the Night of Decree, their reward is greater than that of those who worshipped for a thousand months among the previous nations.” (Khazain al-Irfan, Al-Qadr, under verse 3, p. 1113.)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Na'imi رَحْمَۃَ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: “Two benefits are gained from this verse. The first is that association with exalted things is greatly beneficial: the virtue of the Night of Decree is due to its association with the Quran. The dog of the Companions of the Cave received eternal life and honour by being associated with those exalted people. The second is that the Quran is superior to all the heavenly books, because the date of revelation of the Torah and the Gospel was not granted this distinction.” (Nur al-Irfan, Al-Qadr, under verse 3, p. 990.)

## Ayat 97:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54458
**Metadata:** surah=97; ayat=4; ayatId=6225; tafseerId=54458; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلٰٓىٕكَةُ وَ الرُّوْحُ فِیْهَا بِاِذْنِ رَبِّهِمْۚ-مِنْ كُلِّ اَمْرٍۙۛ    (4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Therein descend the angels and Jibra'eel, by the command of their Lord, for all tasks.

{تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلٰٓىٕكَةُ وَ الرُّوْحُ فِیْهَا: Therein descend the angels and Jibra'eel.} The second virtue of the Night of Decree is that on this night the angels and Sayyiduna Jibra'eel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام descend from the heavens towards the earth, by the command of their Lord, for every matter which Allah Almighty has decreed for this year. They greet every servant who is occupied in the remembrance of Allah Almighty, whether standing or sitting, and supplicate and seek forgiveness for him. (Khazin, Al-Qadr, under verse 4, 4/397–398, abridged.)

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “When the Night of Decree occurs, Sayyiduna Jibra'eel عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام descends among the company of angels and supplicates for every servant, standing or sitting, who is remembering Allah Almighty.” (Shu'ab al-Iman, the twenty-third branch of faith, etc., concerning the night of Eid and its day, 3/343, hadith 3717.)

{سَلٰمٌ ﱡ هِیَ حَتّٰى مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ: That (night) is (full of) peace until the rising of the dawn.} The third virtue of the Night of Decree is that this night is entirely a night of safety from calamities and afflictions until the dawn rises. (Khazin, Al-Qadr, under verse 5, 4/398; Madarik, Al-Qadr, under verse 5, p. 1364, abridged.)
