# Surah Al-Kawthar: English Translation

## Ayat 108:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54491
**Metadata:** surah=108; ayat=1; ayatId=6312; tafseerId=54491; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَﭤ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O Beloved, We have indeed bestowed upon you countless excellences.

{اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ: O Beloved, We have indeed bestowed upon you countless excellences.} The exegetes have expressed various opinions concerning the exegesis of Al-Kawthar. The summary of all these opinions is that Allah Almighty says: O Beloved, We have indeed bestowed upon you countless excellences, made you superior to all creation by granting you abundant virtues, and given you both outward beauty and inward beauty, noble lineage, Prophethood, the Book, wisdom, knowledge, intercession, the Pool of Kawthar, the Station of Praise, a great number of followers, victory over the enemies of the religion, abundant conquests, and countless blessings and excellences whose end cannot be reached. (Khazin, Al-Kawthar, under verse 1, 4/413–414, excerpted.)

### Information derived from the verse “اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ”

Five matters are learned from this verse:

1. Allah Almighty has already granted Kawthar to His Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, because it is not stated here, “We shall grant you Kawthar”; rather, it is stated, “Indeed, We have granted you Kawthar.”

2. Allah Almighty's granting this gift to His Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ is not on account of His Beloved being a Prophet and Messenger; rather, it is on account of the very person of the Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, which is the manifestation of the rank of the Beloved. This is because He said, “اَعْطَیْنٰكَ” — “We have granted you”; He did not say, “اَعْطَیْنَا الرَّسُوْلَ” — “We have granted the Messenger,” or “اَعْطَیْنَا النَّبِیَّ” — “We have granted the Prophet.”

3. This gift bestowed upon the Crown of Messengership صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ was not granted because of any worship or spiritual exertion. Rather, it was granted through the immense grace and favour of Allah Almighty, because the granting of the gift is mentioned first here, while worship is mentioned afterwards.

4. Allah Almighty has made him the owner of Kawthar, so he can grant whomever he wishes whatever he wishes.

5. The Lord Almighty Himself praises the Leader of the Messengers صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ. A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says:

اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ

He receives all abundance;

Cool and sweet,

We drink, while he gives us to drink.

And he says:

O Raza, the Lord of the Quran Himself
is the praiser of the Beloved;

How could praise of the Messenger of Allah
ever be possible through you?

{فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَ انْحَرْ: So, you should offer Salah for your Lord, and sacrifice.} This means: O Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, Allah Almighty has granted you countless blessings of both worlds and given you a special rank that He has not granted to anyone besides you. Therefore, continue offering Salah for your Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ, Who granted you Kawthar and honoured you with dignity and nobility, so that the idol-worshippers may be disgraced. Opposing those who sacrifice in the name of idols, sacrifice for your Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ and in His name. One opinion concerning the exegesis of this verse is also that “Salah” refers to the Eid prayer. (Madarik, Al-Kawthar, under verse 2, p. 1378; Khazin, Al-Kawthar, under verse 2, 4/416–417, excerpted.)

{اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ: Indeed, he who is your enemy, only he is deprived of every goodness.} **Occasion of revelation:** When Sayyid al-Mursaleen's صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ son, Sayyiduna Qasim رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, passed away, the disbelievers called him “Abtar”, meaning one whose lineage had been cut off. They said that his lineage no longer remained, that after him his mention would not remain, and that all this talk would come to an end. Upon this, this blessed Surah was revealed, and Allah Almighty gave a powerful refutation of those disbelievers. In this verse, He declared: O Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, indeed your enemy alone is deprived of every goodness, not you. Your chain will continue until the Day of Judgement, your offspring will also be numerous, and the world will be filled with those who follow you. Your mention will be raised upon the pulpits. The scholars and preachers born until the Day of Judgement will continue mentioning you along with the mention of Allah Almighty. In the Hereafter, you have that which no description can express. One possessing such a rank cannot be abtar. The nameless, unknown one deprived of every goodness is your enemy. (Madarik, Al-Kawthar, under verse 3, p. 1378; Khazin, Al-Kawthar, under verse 3, 4/417, excerpted.)

A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ, explaining the exegesis of this Surah, says that the wretched As ibn Wa'il called the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ “Abtar”, meaning one whose lineage had been cut off, upon the sorrowful passing of the son of Sayyid al-Mursaleen صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ. Allah Almighty said:

“اِنَّاۤ اَعْطَیْنٰكَ الْكَوْثَرَ”

Indeed, We have bestowed abundant goodness upon you. What comparison is there between having one's name continue through one's children and the elevation of your mention? Millions of people with children have passed whose names no one even knows, whereas the drum of praise for you will resound throughout the corners of the world and the regions of the earth until the establishment of the Day of Judgement. The sermon in which your honoured name is mentioned will be delivered throughout the spheres of the heavens and the horizons of the earth forever and ever.

You will also be granted children who are noble and pure, through whose survival the survival of the world will remain connected. Besides them, all Muslims are your children, and no one is as merciful to them as you are. Indeed, if the reality of the matter is considered, the entire world is your spiritual progeny: had you not existed, nothing would have existed, and everything was created through your light. This is why, when the father of humanity, Adam, remembered you, he would say:

“یَا اِبْنِیْ صُوْرَۃً وَاَبَایَ مَعْنًی”

“O my son in outward form, and my father in reality.”

As for what you will receive in the Hereafter, only Allah knows its reality. When this limitless favour is being showered upon you, why should you be saddened by the insolence of these wretched people? Rather:

“فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَ انْحَرْ”

Offer Salah for Him in gratitude to your Lord and sacrifice.

“اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ”

Your enemy is the one whose lineage has been cut off. The very sons of whom he is proud, namely Amr and Hisham رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا, will become his enemies. By entering your true religion, they will separate from his lineage because of the difference in religion and will be counted among your religious sons. Even if a person had no descendants, that alone would merely mean that his name did not continue. It is a thousand times worse for an evil name to remain. The impure name of your enemy will always be mentioned with evil and condemnation, and on the Day of Judgement he will receive the full punishment for these insolences. وَ الْعِیَاذُ بِاللّٰہ تعالٰی. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/167–168)

It is learned from this that the rank of the Radiant Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ in the court of Allah Almighty is so lofty that the Lord Almighty Himself responds to the insolence of those who show insolence towards him.
