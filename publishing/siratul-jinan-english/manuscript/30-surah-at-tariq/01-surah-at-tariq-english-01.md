# Surah At-Tariq: English Translation

## Ayat 86:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54356

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=1; ayatId=6017; tafseerId=54356; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** وَ السَّمَآءِ وَ الطَّارِقِۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of the sky, and by the one that comes at night.

{وَ السَّمَآءِ وَ الطَّارِقِ: By the oath of the sky, and by the one that comes at night.} One night, Abu Talib brought some food as a gift to the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ. While the Radiant Prophet ﷺ was consuming it, a star fell and the entire atmosphere filled with fire. Abu Talib became frightened and asked what it was. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said, “It is a star by which devils are struck, and it is among the signs of Allah Almighty’s power.” Abu Talib was amazed by this, so Allah Almighty revealed these verses in confirmation of His Beloved ﷺ.

The summary of this verse and the three verses after it is that Allah Almighty mentioned an oath by the sky and by the brightly shining star that appears at night, and stated that every soul has a guardian appointed by its Lord عزَّوَجَلَّ. He guards its deeds and records all its good and bad deeds. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا said that the guardian here refers to angels. (Khazin, At-Tariq, under verses 1–4, 4/368.)

Allah Almighty states concerning these angels elsewhere:

“وَ یُرْسِلُ عَلَیْكُمْ حَفَظَةً” (Al-An'am: 61)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And He sends guardians over you.”

### Two matters learned from “There is no soul that does not have a guardian over it”

1. Although Allah Almighty has power to protect everyone in every way Himself, the established practice is that this work is carried out by the angels appointed by Him.
2. Some of Allah Almighty’s names may be given to His servants. One of Allah Almighty’s names is Hafiz, meaning Guardian, and the angels have been called guardians in this verse. Therefore, we may say that Allah Almighty and His Beloved ﷺ are our guardians and helpers.

## Ayat 86:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54357

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=5; ayatId=6021; tafseerId=54357; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** فَلْیَنْظُرِ الْاِنْسَانُ مِمَّ خُلِقَﭤ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So, man should ponder what thing he has been created from.

{فَلْیَنْظُرِ الْاِنْسَانُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ: So, man should ponder what thing he has been created from.} In the preceding verses, Allah Almighty mentioned an oath and stated that a guardian is appointed over every soul, who guards and records its deeds. In this verse, man is commanded to ponder his beginning of creation, so that he may know that the One Who created him the first time is also able to resurrect him after his passing away in order to recompense him. Therefore, man should perform deeds for the day on which he will be resurrected and recompensed. (Madarik, At-Tariq, under verse 5, p. 1338.)

## Ayat 86:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54358

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=6; ayatId=6022; tafseerId=54358; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** خُلِقَ مِنْ مَّآءٍ دَافِقٍۙ    (6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Created from a spurting (drop of) fluid.

{خُلِقَ مِنْ مَّآءٍ دَافِقٍ: Created from a spurting (drop of) fluid.} This verse explains the substance from which Allah Almighty created man. The summary of this verse and the one after it is that Allah Almighty created man from spurting fluid, meaning the fluids of the man and woman which combine in the woman’s womb and become one. This fluid emerges from between the man’s backbone and the woman’s chest.

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا said that this fluid emerges from the place on the woman’s chest where a necklace is worn. It is also narrated from him that it emerges from between the woman’s two breasts. It has also been said that semen emerges from all of man’s organs: most of it comes from the brain through the man’s back, while a great deal comes from the many veins in the front part of the woman’s body that are located at the chest. This is why both places were mentioned specifically here. (Madarik, At-Tariq, under verses 6–7, p. 1338; Khazin, At-Tariq, under verses 6–7, 4/368, excerpted.)

{اِنَّهٗ عَلٰى رَجْعِهٖ لَقَادِرٌ: Indeed, Allah is Able to return him (back to life).} The result of man pondering his creation is that the Lord Who created man from a drop of fluid the first time is especially able to return him to life after his passing away. (Sawi, At-Tariq, under verse 8, 6/2346; Madarik, At-Tariq, under verse 8, p. 1338, excerpted.)

## Ayat 86:9

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54359

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=9; ayatId=6025; tafseerId=54359; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** یَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَآىٕرُۙ    (9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The Day when secrets will be examined.

{یَوْمَ تُبْلَى السَّرَآىٕرُ: The Day when secrets will be examined.} The summary of this verse and the one after it is that, on the day when hidden matters are exposed, the person who denies resurrection after passing away will possess neither such strength as could repel the punishment nor such a helper as could save him from it. Hidden matters means beliefs, intentions and deeds which a person conceals; Allah Almighty will expose all of them on the Day of Judgement. (Madarik, At-Tariq, under verses 9–10, pp. 1338–1339; Khazin, At-Tariq, under verses 9–10, 4/369, excerpted.)

### Hidden deeds will be exposed on the Day of Judgement

It is known that although a servant’s beliefs, intentions and deeds may not become apparent to anyone in the world, everything he did will come before everyone on the Day of Judgement. Allah Almighty states elsewhere:

“هُنَالِكَ تَبْلُوْا كُلُّ نَفْسٍ مَّاۤ اَسْلَفَتْ وَ رُدُّوْۤا اِلَى اللّٰهِ مَوْلٰىهُمُ الْحَقِّ” (Yunus: 30)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “There, every person will examine his former deeds, and they will be returned to Allah, their True Master.”

He also states:

“یُنَبَّؤُا الْاِنْسَانُ یَوْمَىٕذٍۭ بِمَا قَدَّمَ وَ اَخَّرَؕ(۱۳) بَلِ الْاِنْسَانُ عَلٰى نَفْسِهٖ بَصِیْرَةٌ” (Al-Qiyamah: 13–14)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “On that day, man will be informed of all that he sent ahead and left behind. Rather, man himself will be fully aware of his own state.”

Therefore, everyone should correct his outward deeds as well as his inward and hidden deeds, so that he may be saved from disgrace before people on the Day of Judgement.

## Ayat 86:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54360

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=11; ayatId=6027; tafseerId=54360; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** وَ السَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ الرَّجْعِۙ    (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of the sky from which comes down the rain.

{وَ السَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ الرَّجْعِ: By the oath of the sky from which comes down the rain.} After presenting proofs of Divine Oneness and resurrection, Allah Almighty mentions an oath by the earth and sky and explains the reality of the Holy Quran. The summary of this verse and the three verses after it is that, by the sky from which rain descends repeatedly and by the earth which is split so that vegetation may emerge, the Holy Quran is certainly a decisive Word. It distinguishes truth from falsehood, and the Quran is not an amusement or a useless, meaningless matter. (Tafsir Kabir, At-Tariq, under verses 11–14, 11/122–123; Khazin, At-Tariq, under verses 11–14, 4/369, excerpted.)

These verses also indicate that the sky from which rain repeatedly descends is like a father to the earth’s produce, vegetation and trees, while the earth that is split is like a mother to vegetation. Both are among Allah Almighty’s wondrous blessings, and countless signs of His power appear in them. By pondering over them, a person finds many proofs of being resurrected after passing away. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, At-Tariq, under verse 12, 10/400, abridged.)

### The Quran is a decisive Word

Regarding this quality of the Holy Quran and its other qualities, Sayyiduna Ali al-Murtada كَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم said: I heard the Noble Messenger ﷺ say, “A tribulation will soon arise.” I asked, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, what will be the way to remain safe from it?” He ﷺ said, “The Book of Allah Almighty, in which are the accounts of those before you and those after you, and the judgments between you. The Quran is decisive, and it is not an amusement. Whoever abandons it while being unjust, Allah Almighty will destroy him; and whoever seeks guidance in other than it, Allah Almighty will misguide him. It is the strong rope of Allah Almighty and the wise reminder. It is the straight path.

“The Quran is that through whose blessing desires do not become corrupt, and with which other tongues cannot make it doubtful and ambiguous. Scholars never become satiated with it, it does not grow old through repeated recitation, and its wonders never end. When the jinn heard it, they could not help saying, ‘We have heard a wondrous Quran which guides towards goodness, so we believed in it.’ Whoever speaks in accordance with the Quran is truthful; whoever acts upon it will receive reward; whoever judges according to it will be just; and whoever calls towards it calls towards the straight path.” (Tirmidhi, Book of the Virtues of the Quran, chapter on what has been narrated concerning the excellence of the Quran, 4/414, hadith 2915.)

## Ayat 86:15

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54361

**Metadata:** surah=86; ayat=15; ayatId=6031; tafseerId=54361; para=30; volume=10; page=591

**Arabic:** اِنَّهُمْ یَكِیْدُوْنَ كَیْدًاۙ    (15)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, the disbelievers carry out their (evil) scheme.

{اِنَّهُمْ یَكِیْدُوْنَ كَیْدًا: Indeed, the disbelievers carry out their (evil) scheme.} The summary of this verse and the two verses after it is that the disbelievers of Makkah are making various schemes to erase the religion of Allah Almighty, extinguish the light of truth and cause pain to the Crown of Messengership ﷺ. Allah Almighty is making a hidden plan of His own, of which they have no knowledge. Therefore, O Beloved ﷺ, do not supplicate for the destruction of the disbelievers; rather, give them respite and allow them a little time, because they will soon be destroyed. This is exactly what happened: at the Battle of Badr, Allah Almighty’s punishment seized them. (Khazin, At-Tariq, under verses 15–17, 4/369; Madarik, At-Tariq, under verses 15–17, p. 1339, excerpted.)
