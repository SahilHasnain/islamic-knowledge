# Surah Al-Maun: English Translation

## Ayat 107:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54487

**Metadata:** surah=107; ayat=1; ayatId=6304; tafseerId=54487; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَرَءَیْتَ الَّذِیْ یُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّیْنِﭤ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Do you see the one who belies the religion?

{اَرَءَیْتَ الَّذِیْ یُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّیْنِ: Do you see the one who belies the religion?} Concerning the occasion of revelation of this verse, one opinion is that it was revealed about ‘As ibn Wa’il Sahmi, who denied the Day of Judgement and also used to commit evil deeds.

The second opinion is that this verse was revealed about Walid ibn Mughirah.

The third opinion is that it was revealed about Abu Jahl. It is narrated that Abu Jahl had accepted responsibility for bringing up an orphan. One day, that orphan came to him unclothed and asked him for some of his property, but Abu Jahl pushed him out. The leaders of Quraysh told him, “Go to Muhammad (Mustafa) ﷺ; he will intercede for you.” Their purpose was to mock the Noble Prophet ﷺ, but the orphan did not know this. Thus, the orphan came to the court of the Crown of Messengership ﷺ and presented his complaint. The Leader of the Messengers ﷺ never sent a needy person away empty-handed, so he ﷺ went with the orphan to Abu Jahl.

When Abu Jahl saw the Noble Messenger ﷺ, he welcomed him and immediately handed the orphan his property. When the people of Quraysh saw this, they reproached him and said, “You have abandoned your religion.” Abu Jahl replied, “By God, I have not abandoned my religion. The real matter is that I saw a spear to his right and left, and I feared that if I did not obey him, that spear would tear me apart.”

The fourth opinion is that this verse was not revealed about any particular person; rather, it includes every person who denies the Day of Judgement. The reason is that a person performs righteous deeds and refrains from forbidden things because of desire for reward and fear of punishment. When he denies the Day of Judgement itself, he will not give up any of his carnal desires and pleasures. This proves that denial of the Day of Judgement is like the foundation of disbelief and all types of sins.

The summary of this verse and the two verses after it is: O man! Do you recognise the person who denies reckoning and recompense despite the clear proofs? If you do not recognise him, then listen: he is the one who, because of his disbelief, pushes away, rebukes, and beats the orphan; while wronging him, he does not give him his right or his property. Because of his extreme miserliness, hardness of heart, and meanness, he does not feed the needy, nor does he encourage anyone else to feed the orphan, because he does not believe that this deed earns reward. If he believed in recompense and had conviction in the warning of punishment, these deeds would not proceed from him. (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Maun, under verses 1–3, 11/301–303; Khazin, Al-Maun, under verses 1–3, 4/412, excerpted.)

## Ayat 107:2

**Metadata:** surah=107; ayat=2; ayatId=6305; tafseerId=54488; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَذٰلِكَ الَّذِیْ یَدُعُّ الْیَتِیْمَۙ    (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:**  Then it is him who pushes away the orphan.

{فَذٰلِكَ الَّذِیْ یَدُعُّ الْیَتِیْمَ: Then it is him who pushes away the orphan.} That is, the moral condition of the person who belies the religion is that he pushes away the orphan.

### The Disbelievers’ Treatment of Orphans and Islam’s Teachings Concerning Them

This verse describes the disbelievers’ treatment of orphans. In contrast, consider Islam’s teachings concerning orphans. Allah Almighty says to the guardians of orphans:

“وَ اٰتُوا الْیَتٰمٰۤى اَمْوَالَهُمْ وَ لَا تَتَبَدَّلُوا الْخَبِیْثَ بِالطَّیِّبِ ۪- وَ لَا تَاْكُلُوْۤا اَمْوَالَهُمْ اِلٰۤى اَمْوَالِكُمْؕ-اِنَّهٗ كَانَ حُوْبًا كَبِیْرًا” (An-Nisa: 2)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And give the orphans their property, and do not exchange the impure for the pure, and do not consume their property by mixing it with your property. Indeed, this is a great sin.”

And He says:

“وَ لَا تُؤْتُوا السُّفَهَآءَ اَمْوَالَكُمُ الَّتِیْ جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ قِیٰمًا وَّ ارْزُقُوْهُمْ فِیْهَا وَ اكْسُوْهُمْ وَ قُوْلُوْا لَهُمْ قَوْلًا مَّعْرُوْفًا” (An-Nisa: 5)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And do not give the foolish their property which Allah has made a means of support for you, and feed them from it, clothe them, and speak good words to them.”

And He says:

“وَ لْیَخْشَ الَّذِیْنَ لَوْ تَرَكُوْا مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّیَّةً ضِعٰفًا خَافُوْا عَلَیْهِمْ ۪- فَلْیَتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ وَ لْیَقُوْلُوْا قَوْلًا سَدِیْدًا” (An-Nisa: 9)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And let those fear who, if they left behind weak offspring, would fear for them. So let them fear Allah and speak the right words.”

And He says:

“اِنَّ الَّذِیْنَ یَاْكُلُوْنَ اَمْوَالَ الْیَتٰمٰى ظُلْمًا اِنَّمَا یَاْكُلُوْنَ فِیْ بُطُوْنِهِمْ نَارًا ؕ- وَ سَیَصْلَوْنَ سَعِیْرًا” (An-Nisa: 10)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Indeed, those who consume the property of orphans unjustly are only filling their bellies with fire, and soon they will enter a blazing fire.”

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said, “The best house among Muslims is the house in which there is an orphan and he is treated well, and the worst house among Muslims is the house in which there is an orphan and he is treated badly.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Manners, chapter on the right of the orphan, 4/193, hadith 3679.)

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, “Whoever strokes the head of an orphan for the sake of Allah Almighty’s pleasure will receive a good deed for every hair over which his hand passes. Whoever treats the orphan boy or orphan girl living with him well, I and he will be like these two in Paradise.” The Messenger of Allah ﷺ joined two of his fingers. (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad al-Ansar, hadith of Abu Umamah al-Bahili ... etc., 8/300, hadith 22347.)

In view of the disbelievers’ conduct and Islam’s teachings, everyone can clearly understand that no other religion has examples comparable to the measures Islam has taken and the commands it has given to protect children’s rights.

## Ayat 107:3

**Metadata:** surah=107; ayat=3; ayatId=6306; tafseerId=54489; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ لَا یَحُضُّ عَلٰى طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِیْنِﭤ(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And does not encourage (others) to feed the needy.

{وَ لَا یَحُضُّ عَلٰى طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِیْنِ: And does not encourage (others) to feed the needy.} That is, the condition of the person who belies the religion is that he does not encourage his family and other wealthy people to feed the needy. (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Maun, under verse 3, 10/522.)

### The Disbelievers’ Treatment of the Needy and Islam’s Teachings

This verse describes the disbelievers’ treatment of the needy. Now consider Islam’s teachings concerning the needy. Allah Almighty says:

“فَاٰتِ ذَا الْقُرْبٰى حَقَّهٗ وَ الْمِسْكِیْنَ وَ ابْنَ السَّبِیْلِؕ-ذٰلِكَ خَیْرٌ لِّلَّذِیْنَ یُرِیْدُوْنَ وَجْهَ اللّٰهِ٘-وَ اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ” (Ar-Rum: 38)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “So give the relative his right, and the needy and the traveller as well. This is better for those who seek Allah’s pleasure, and it is they who are successful.”

While describing the qualities of His righteous servants, Allah Almighty says:

“وَ یُطْعِمُوْنَ الطَّعَامَ عَلٰى حُبِّهٖ مِسْكِیْنًا وَّ یَتِیْمًا وَّ اَسِیْرًا (۸) اِنَّمَا نُطْعِمُكُمْ لِوَجْهِ اللّٰهِ لَا نُرِیْدُ مِنْكُمْ جَزَآءً وَّ لَا شُكُوْرًا” (Ad-Dahr: 8–9)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And they feed the needy, the orphan, and the captive, despite their own love for the food. ‘We feed you only for the sake of Allah; we do not desire any recompense or thanks from you.’”

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said, “The needy will enter Paradise forty years before the wealthy. O Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, do not send away a needy person empty-handed; even if it is only a slice of date, give it to him. O Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, love the needy and keep them close, so that Allah Almighty may bring you close on the Day of Judgement.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Asceticism, chapter on what has been narrated concerning the poor emigrants entering Paradise before their wealthy people, 4/157, hadith 2359.)

## Ayat 107:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54490

**Metadata:** surah=107; ayat=4; ayatId=6307; tafseerId=54490; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَوَیْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّیْنَۙ    (4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So, destruction is for those performers of Salah.

{فَوَیْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّیْنَ: So, destruction is for those performers of Salah.} This verse and the verse after it state that destruction is for those performers of Salah who are heedless of their Salah. This refers to hypocrites: when they are alone, they do not offer Salah because they do not believe that it is obligatory; but when they are before people, they act like performers of Salah, present themselves as worshippers, and stand and sit for people to see them. In reality, these people are heedless of Salah. (Madarik, Al-Maun, under verses 4–5, p. 1377.)

### The End of Those Who Are Heedless of Salah

Concerning those who are heedless of Salah, Allah Almighty says elsewhere:

“فَخَلَفَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِهِمْ خَلْفٌ اَضَاعُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اتَّبَعُوا الشَّهَوَاتِ فَسَوْفَ یَلْقَوْنَ غَیًّا” (Maryam: 59)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Then after them came unworthy people who abandoned Salah and followed their desires, so soon they will encounter the dreadful valley of Ghayy in Hell.”

And He says:

“اِنَّ الْمُنٰفِقِیْنَ یُخٰدِعُوْنَ اللّٰهَ وَ هُوَ خَادِعُهُمْۚ-وَ اِذَا قَامُوْۤا اِلَى الصَّلٰوةِ قَامُوْا كُسَالٰىۙ-یُرَآءُوْنَ النَّاسَ وَ لَا یَذْكُرُوْنَ اللّٰهَ اِلَّا قَلِیْلًا٘ۙ(۱۴۲) مُّذَبْذَبِیْنَ بَیْنَ ذٰلِكَ ﳓ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِ وَ لَاۤ اِلٰى هٰۤؤُلَآءِؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّضْلِلِ اللّٰهُ فَلَنْ تَجِدَ لَهٗ سَبِیْلًا” (An-Nisa: 142–143)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Indeed, the hypocrites seek to deceive Allah in their own supposition, and He will repay them for their deception. When they stand for Salah, they stand lazily, showing off before people, and they remember Allah only a little. They waver between the two; they are neither with these nor with those. And whoever Allah sends astray, you will never find a way for him.”

Remember that it is necessary to avoid being heedless of Salah in the sense of sometimes offering it and sometimes abandoning it; this is a particular quality of hypocrites. One should also strive to avoid heedlessness within Salah, such as beginning to think about other matters during Salah or accepting Satan’s whispers, although this form of heedlessness is less reprehensible.

{اَلَّذِیْنَ هُمْ عَنْ صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُوْنَ: Those who are heedless of their Salah.} Heedlessness of Salah has several forms, such as not offering it regularly, not offering it at the correct time, not properly performing the obligations and necessary acts, not offering it in congregation without a valid Shar‘i excuse, not caring about Salah, making it up in private and offering it before people, and so on. All these forms are included in the warning. Failing to offer it eagerly, not performing it with understanding, and not offering it attentively are also included in heedlessness of Salah; however, these forms are not included in the particular warning mentioned in the preceding verse.

{اَلَّذِیْنَ هُمْ یُرَآءُوْنَ: Those who show off.} That is, the hypocrites perform the obligatory acts not to attain the pleasure of Allah Almighty, but to show people. (Madarik, Al-Maun, under verse 6, p. 1377.)

### The Definition and Condemnation of Showing Off

Showing off means displaying one’s deed with the intention that people will see it and praise one’s worship. (Qurtubi, Al-Maun, under verse 6, 10/154, twentieth part.)

Many hadith condemn showing off. Two of them are mentioned here.

1. Sayyiduna Abu Sa‘id al-Khudri رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: We were discussing the Antichrist when the Noble Messenger ﷺ came and said, “Shall I not tell you of something which, in my view, is more feared for you than the Antichrist?” We said, “Yes, O Messenger of Allah ﷺ.” He ﷺ said, “It is hidden polytheism: a person stands to offer Salah and prolongs it because another person is watching him offer Salah.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Asceticism, chapter on showing off and seeking to be heard, 4/470, hadith 4204.)

2. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abu Sa‘id ibn Abu Fadalah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, “When Allah Almighty gathers all the earlier and later people on the Day concerning which there is no doubt, a caller will announce: ‘Whoever performed a deed for Allah عَزَّوَجَلَّ and associated someone else with Him in it should seek the reward of his deed from that associate, because Allah Almighty is completely independent of polytheism.’” (Tirmidhi, Book of Tafsir, chapter on Surah Al-Kahf, 5/105, hadith 3165.)

Remember that, while keeping one’s intention correct, obligatory acts of worship should be performed openly, so that people do not accuse a person of abandoning obligatory acts of worship. Voluntary acts of worship should be performed secretly, because there is no fear of such an accusation concerning them.

## Ayat 107:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54492

**Metadata:** surah=107; ayat=7; ayatId=6310; tafseerId=54492; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ یَمْنَعُوْنَ الْمَاعُوْنَ۠    (7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And refuse to give (small) things of use when asked.

{وَ یَمْنَعُوْنَ الْمَاعُوْنَ: And refuse to give (small) things of use when asked.} The earlier verses described the hypocrites’ dealings with the Creator. From here, their conduct with creation is being described: if they are asked for an ordinary item used in daily life, such as a needle, cooking pot, or bowl, they refuse to give it out of miserliness. (Jalalayn, Al-Maun, under verse 7, p. 507.)

### Keeping Ordinary Household Items Beyond One’s Need

The scholars say that it is recommended for a person to keep in his home, beyond his own need, items which his neighbours need and to lend those items to them. (Khazin, Al-Maun, under verse 7, 4/413.)

Sayyidatuna Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا states: I asked, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, which thing is it unlawful to withhold?” He ﷺ said, “Water, salt, and fire.” I asked, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, we understand the ruling concerning water, but why is this ruling given for salt and fire?” He ﷺ said, “O Humayra رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, whoever gives someone fire has, as if, given in charity all the food cooked with that fire; whoever gives someone salt has, as if, given in charity all the food made tasty by that salt; whoever gives a Muslim one sip of water where water is readily available has, as if, freed a slave; and whoever gives a Muslim one sip of water where water is unavailable has, as if, given him life.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Pawning, chapter on Muslims sharing three things, 3/177, hadith 2474.)
