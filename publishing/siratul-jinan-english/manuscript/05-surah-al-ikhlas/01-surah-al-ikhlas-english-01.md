# Surah Al-Ikhlas: English Translation

## Ayat 112:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54498
**Metadata:** surah=112; ayat=1; ayatId=6333; tafseerId=54498; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌۚ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Say you (O Beloved), 'He is Allah, He is One.'

{قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ اَحَدٌ: Say you (O Beloved), He is Allah, He is One.} There were many kinds of disbelievers in Arabia: atheists, polytheists, those who denied the attributes of Allah Almighty, and those who attributed offspring to Allah Almighty, among others. This Surah refutes all of them. The words “هُوَاللّٰهُ” refute the atheists. The word “اَحَدٌ” completely refutes the polytheists, while the remaining disbelievers are refuted in the subsequent verses. Allah Almighty states that He is One, meaning that He is described with attributes of greatness and perfection in Lordship and Divinity. He has no equal, counterpart, or likeness, and He has no partner.

{اَللّٰهُ الصَّمَدُ: Allah is the Independent.} Allah Almighty states that He is independent of everything. He neither eats nor drinks; He has always existed and will always remain. He is not dependent upon anyone for any matter.

{لَمْ یَلِدْ ﳔ وَ لَمْ یُوْلَدْ: He has no offspring, nor is He born from anyone.} Allah Almighty is pure of offspring, because offspring belongs to the same kind as the father, while Allah Almighty is pure of this. Likewise, He Himself was not born from anyone, because He is Eternal, meaning that He has always existed. Being born is an attribute of something that did not exist before and then came into existence. This verse refutes the polytheists, the Jews, and the Christians alike. The polytheists used to call the angels the daughters of Allah Almighty. The Jews believed that Sayyiduna Uzair عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was the son of God, while the Christians believed that Sayyiduna Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was the son of God.

{وَ لَمْ یَكُنْ لَّهٗ كُفُوًا اَحَدٌ: And there is no one equal to Him.} That is, no one is equal to Him either in Being or in attributes, because He is Necessary in existence and is the Creator, whereas everything else is possible in existence, created, and originated. His essential attributes are Eternal and unlimited, whereas the attributes of created beings are bestowed, originated, and limited.
