# Surah Al-Mumtahanah: English Translation

## Ayat 60:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54006

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=1; ayatId=5210; tafseerId=54006; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوْا عَدُوِّیْ وَ عَدُوَّكُمْ اَوْلِیَآءَ تُلْقُوْنَ اِلَیْهِمْ بِالْمَوَدَّةِ وَ قَدْ كَفَرُوْا بِمَا جَآءَكُمْ مِّنَ الْحَقِّۚ-یُخْرِجُوْنَ الرَّسُوْلَ وَ اِیَّاكُمْ اَنْ تُؤْمِنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ رَبِّكُمْؕ-اِنْ كُنْتُمْ خَرَجْتُمْ جِهَادًا فِیْ سَبِیْلِیْ وَ ابْتِغَآءَ مَرْضَاتِیْ ﳓ تُسِرُّوْنَ اِلَیْهِمْ بِالْمَوَدَّةِ ﳓ وَ اَنَا اَعْلَمُ بِمَاۤ اَخْفَیْتُمْ وَ مَاۤ اَعْلَنْتُمْؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْهُ مِنْكُمْ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ سَوَآءَ السَّبِیْلِ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Do not befriend My enemies and your enemies, you inform them (about your secrets) out of friendship; whereas, they deny that truth which has come to you. It is they who separate the Messenger and you from your homes on this basis that you believed in your Lord Allah. If you have come out to fight in My path and to gain My contentment, so do not befriend them. You send them secret messages of friendship, and I know well what you hide and what you disclose. And whoever amongst you does this, he has indeed gone astray from the straight path.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَّخِذُوْا عَدُوِّیْ وَ عَدُوَّكُمْ اَوْلِیَآءَ: O believers! Do not befriend My enemies and your enemies.} Occasion of revelation: A slave woman named Sarah, from the family of Banu Hashim, came to Madinah when the Noble Prophet ﷺ was preparing for the conquest of Makkah. The Noble Prophet ﷺ asked her, “Have you come as a Muslim?” She replied, “No.” He asked, “Have you come as an emigrant?” She replied, “No.” He asked, “Then why have you come?” She replied, “I have come because hardship has exhausted me.” The children of Sayyiduna Abd al-Muttalib helped her, providing clothes and provisions. Sayyiduna Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ met her, gave her ten dinars and a cloak, and through her sent a letter to the people of Makkah saying: “The Noble Prophet ﷺ intends to attack you, so take whatever measures you can to protect yourselves.” Sarah set out with this letter.

Allah Almighty informed His Beloved ﷺ of this. The Noble Prophet ﷺ sent several Companions, including Sayyiduna Ali al-Murtada کَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم, on horseback and said, “You will meet a travelling woman at Rawdah Khakh. She has a letter from Hatib ibn Abi Balta'ah addressed to the people of Makkah. Take the letter from her and let her go; if she refuses to give it, strike her neck.” They went and found the woman at exactly the place described. When they asked for the letter, she denied having it and swore an oath. The Companions intended to return, but Sayyiduna Ali al-Murtada کَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم swore that the report of the Noble Prophet ﷺ could not be contrary to reality. He drew his sword and said to her, “Bring out the letter, or I shall strike your neck.” When she saw that he was fully prepared to kill her, she took the letter from her plait and handed it over.

When they returned with the letter, the Noble Prophet ﷺ summoned Sayyiduna Hatib رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ and asked, “O Hatib, what made you write this letter?” He replied, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, since I accepted Islam I have never disbelieved, and since I have had the honour of your company I have never betrayed you. Since leaving the people of Makkah, I have never held love for them in my heart. However, I lived among Quraysh without being one of their people. Unlike the other Muhajirun, I have no relatives in Makkah to protect my family. I feared for my household, so I wished to place a favour upon the people of Makkah so that they would not harm my family. I know with certainty that Allah Almighty will send punishment upon the people of Makkah; my letter cannot save them.” The Crown of Messengership ﷺ accepted his excuse and confirmed his truthfulness.

Sayyiduna Umar Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ said, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, permit me to strike the neck of this hypocrite.” The Radiant Prophet ﷺ said, “O Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, Allah Almighty is fully aware. This is why He said concerning the people of Badr: ‘Do whatever you wish; I have forgiven you.’” On hearing this, tears flowed from Sayyiduna Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ’ s eyes, and these verses were revealed.

The summary of the verse is: O believers! Do not befriend the disbelievers who are My enemies and your enemies. Because of friendship, you convey the hidden news of the Noble Messenger ﷺ to them, although they deny the truth that has come to you, namely Islam and the Holy Quran. They expel the Noble Messenger ﷺ and you from Makkah because you believe in your Lord, Allah Almighty. If you left your homeland to strive in My path and seek My pleasure, then do not befriend these disbelievers. You send them secret messages of love, although you know well that I know everything you conceal and everything you reveal. Remember that whoever among you befriends them has certainly strayed from the straight path. (Madarik, Surah Al-Mumtahanah, p. 1230; Khazin, under verse 1, 4/255–256, excerpted.)

### Lessons from “O believers! Do not befriend My enemies and your enemies”

Five matters are learned from this verse:

1. The disbelievers of Makkah were enemies of the Muslims, but Allah Almighty also called them His enemies. This shows that the enemy of Allah Almighty’s righteous servants is also the enemy of Allah Almighty.
2. Informing disbelievers of the Muslims’ secrets is treachery and rebellion against the religion and the nation.
3. A sin was committed by Sayyiduna Hatib رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, yet Allah Almighty called him a believer. This shows that committing a major sin does not make a person a disbeliever.
4. The enemy of faith is more dangerous than the enemy of one’s life.
5. Striving in the way of Allah Almighty is possible only when the heart of the fighter is filled with love for believers and enmity for disbelievers. If even a little inclination towards a disbeliever enters the fighter’s heart, remaining a fighter in the way of Allah becomes difficult.

### The virtues of friendship and enmity for Allah Almighty’s pleasure

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “On the Day of Judgement, Allah Almighty will say: ‘Where are those who loved one another for the sake of My Majesty? Today I shall place them under My shade, on the day when there is no shade besides the shade of My Throne.’” (Muslim, Book of Righteousness, Maintaining Relations and Good Manners, chapter on the virtue of love for Allah, p. 1388, hadith 37 (2566).)
2. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “A man went to another region to meet his brother. Allah Almighty placed an angel on his path. When he came to the angel, he asked, ‘Where do you intend to go?’ He replied, ‘I have a brother in this region whom I am going to visit.’ The angel asked, ‘Do you have a favour over him which you are going to collect?’ He replied, ‘No. I only love him for Allah Almighty’s sake.’ The angel said, ‘Allah Almighty sent me to you to inform you that He loves you just as you loved him for Allah Almighty’s sake.’” (Muslim, Book of Righteousness, Maintaining Relations and Good Manners, chapter on the virtue of love for Allah, p. 1388, hadith 38 (2567).)
3. Sayyiduna Abu Umamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever loves someone for Allah Almighty, bears enmity towards someone for Allah Almighty, gives for Allah Almighty and withholds for Allah Almighty has perfected his faith.” (Abu Dawud, Book of Sunnah, chapter on evidence for increase and decrease of faith, 4/290, hadith 4681.)
4. Sayyiduna Abu Dharr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “Do you know which deed is most beloved to Allah Almighty?” Someone said, “Prayer and zakat,” and someone said, “Striving.” The Radiant Prophet ﷺ said: “The deed most beloved to Allah Almighty is love and hatred for Allah Almighty.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad of the Ansar, hadith of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, 8/68, hadith 21361.)

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to love, oppose and hate for the sake of His pleasure. Ameen.

## Ayat 60:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54007

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=2; ayatId=5211; tafseerId=54007; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنْ یَّثْقَفُوْكُمْ یَكُوْنُوْا لَكُمْ اَعْدَآءً وَّ یَبْسُطُوْۤا اِلَیْكُمْ اَیْدِیَهُمْ وَ اَلْسِنَتَهُمْ بِالسُّوْٓءِ وَ وَدُّوْا لَوْ تَكْفُرُوْنَﭤ(2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** If they find you, so they will be your (open) enemies and will extend their hands and their tongues towards you with evil, and their wish is that somehow you become disbelievers.

{اِنْ یَّثْقَفُوْكُمْ یَكُوْنُوْا لَكُمْ اَعْدَآءً: If they find you, they will be your enemies.} The enmity of the disbelievers is such that no matter how you deal with them, whenever they find an opportunity they will not reduce their hostility towards you. They will extend their hands towards you to beat and kill you, and extend their tongues by abusing and insulting you. Their wish is that somehow you become disbelievers. Therefore, such people should never be befriended, goodness should not be expected from them, and their enmity should not be overlooked. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 2, 4/256; Madarik, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 2, p. 1231, excerpted.)

## Ayat 60:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54008

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=3; ayatId=5212; tafseerId=54008; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** لَنْ تَنْفَعَكُمْ اَرْحَامُكُمْ وَ لَاۤ اَوْلَادُكُمْۚۛ-یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِۚۛ-یَفْصِلُ بَیْنَكُمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُوْنَ بَصِیْرٌ(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Neither your relations nor your offspring (left behind in Makkah because of whose safety, you keep friendship with the disbelievers) will ever benefit you, on the Day of Judgement. (Allah) will separate you from them; and Allah is seeing your deeds.

{لَنْ تَنْفَعَكُمْ اَرْحَامُكُمْ وَ لَاۤ اَوْلَادُكُمْۚۛ-یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِ: Neither your relations nor your offspring will benefit you on the Day of Judgement.} O believers! The relatives and children because of whom you befriend the disbelievers and maintain relations of affection will never benefit you on the Day of Judgement. Allah Almighty will separate you from them on that day: the obedient will be in Paradise and the disbelieving disobedient in Hell. Remember that Allah Almighty sees your deeds and will reward you according to them. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 3, 4/257; Madarik, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 3, p. 1231–1232, excerpted.)

It should be remembered that a Muslim’s disbelieving relatives and children will not benefit him on the Day of Judgement, whereas believing relatives and children will certainly benefit him by Allah Almighty’s command. Allah Almighty states:

“اَلْاَخِلَّآءُ یَوْمَىٕذٍۭ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍ عَدُوٌّ اِلَّا الْمُتَّقِیْنَ” (Az-Zukhruf: 67)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “On that day, close friends will be enemies of one another except the pious.”

And He states:

“وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا وَ اتَّبَعَتْهُمْ ذُرِّیَّتُهُمْ بِاِیْمَانٍ اَلْحَقْنَا بِهِمْ ذُرِّیَّتَهُمْ وَ مَاۤ اَلَتْنٰهُمْ مِّنْ عَمَلِهِمْ مِنْ شَيْءٍ” (At-Tur: 21)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And those who believed and whose descendants followed them in faith, We shall join their descendants with them and We shall not reduce anything from their deeds.”

In summary, believers will benefit one another, whereas disbelievers will benefit no one.

## Ayat 60:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54009

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=4; ayatId=5213; tafseerId=54009; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ اُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ فِیْۤ اِبْرٰهِیْمَ وَ الَّذِیْنَ مَعَهٗۚ-اِذْ قَالُوْا لِقَوْمِهِمْ اِنَّا بُرَءٰٓؤُا مِنْكُمْ وَ مِمَّا تَعْبُدُوْنَ مِنْ دُوْنِ اللّٰهِ٘-كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَ بَدَا بَیْنَنَا وَ بَیْنَكُمُ الْعَدَاوَةُ وَ الْبَغْضَآءُ اَبَدًا حَتّٰى تُؤْمِنُوْا بِاللّٰهِ وَحْدَهٗۤ اِلَّا قَوْلَ اِبْرٰهِیْمَ لِاَبِیْهِ لَاَسْتَغْفِرَنَّ لَكَ وَ مَاۤ اَمْلِكُ لَكَ مِنَ اللّٰهِ مِنْ شَیْءٍؕ-رَبَّنَا عَلَیْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَ اِلَیْكَ اَنَبْنَا وَ اِلَیْكَ الْمَصِیْرُ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, there was a good example for you to follow in Ibrahim and those with him, when they said to their people 'Indeed, we are disgusted with you and what you worship other than Allah. We have rejected you, and enmity and hatred has surfaced between us and you forever until you accept faith in the One Allah.' Except for the saying of Ibrahim to his (figurative) father, 'I will definitely seek forgiveness for you. And I do not possess any power to benefit you against Allah.' (Ibrahim and his followers said) 'O our Lord, we have relied upon only You, and only towards You we have turned and only towards You is the (final) return.'

{قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ اُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ فِیْۤ اِبْرٰهِیْمَ وَ الَّذِیْنَ مَعَهٗ: Indeed, there was a good example for you to follow in Ibrahim and those with him.} This verse addresses Sayyiduna Hatib رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ and the other believers, commanding all of them to follow Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام in matters of religion, including his manner with relatives. The meaning is that the life of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and those who believed in him contained an excellent example: they said to their polytheist people, “We are free from you and from the idols you worship besides Allah Almighty. We reject your religion, and enmity and hatred have appeared between us and you forever until you believe in Allah Almighty alone.”

However, the statement of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام to his figurative father, “I shall certainly seek forgiveness for you,” is not an example to follow. It was based on a promise; when it became clear to him that his father would remain upon disbelief, he expressed his disassociation from him. Therefore, it is not permissible for anyone to seek forgiveness for a disbelieving relative. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 4, 4/257; Madarik, under verse 4, p. 1232, excerpted.)

{رَبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا: O our Lord, we have relied upon only You.} This is also the supplication of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and the believers who were with him. It is connected to the words before the exception, so believers should follow Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام in this supplication as well. (Madarik, under verse 4, p. 1232, summarised.)

Note: Muslims must follow the Noble Prophet ﷺ absolutely, whereas following the other noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام concerns particular deeds, because many rulings of the laws of earlier Prophets were abrogated. Thus this verse does not oppose the verse of Surah Al-Ahzab, “لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِیْ رَسُوْلِ اللّٰهِ اُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ”. Here, following is commanded in particular circumstances and particular matters, while the verse of Surah Al-Ahzab commands absolute following.

### Lessons from “Indeed, there was a good example for you to follow in Ibrahim”

Three matters are learned from this verse:

1. The practice of the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام is to make their faith apparent through their words and deeds.
2. Enmity towards disbelievers is as necessary as love for Muslims.
3. By Allah Almighty’s permission, the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام will remove punishment from sinful believers, and punishment will be removed through their intercession. Therefore, this verse cannot be used as evidence that there is no intercession for believers.

{رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِّلَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوْا: O our Lord, do not make us a trial for the disbelievers.} Muslims should also follow them in making this supplication: “O our Lord! Do not give the disbelievers dominance over us and thereby make us a trial for them,” because they would then consider themselves upon truth and the Muslims upon falsehood, and their disbelief would increase further. “O Allah, forgive us. O our Lord, indeed You alone are the Honourable, the Wise.”

## Ayat 60:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54011

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=7; ayatId=5216; tafseerId=54011; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** عَسَى اللّٰهُ اَنْ یَّجْعَلَ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ بَیْنَ الَّذِیْنَ عَادَیْتُمْ مِّنْهُمْ مَّوَدَّةًؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ قَدِیْرٌؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** It is near that Allah may create friendship between you and those amongst them who are your enemies (by making them Muslims); and Allah is All-Powerful; and Allah is Most Forgiving, Ever Merciful.

{عَسَى اللّٰهُ اَنْ یَّجْعَلَ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ بَیْنَ الَّذِیْنَ عَادَیْتُمْ مِّنْهُمْ مَّوَدَّةً: It is near that Allah may create friendship between you and those amongst them who are your enemies.} Occasion of revelation: When the preceding verses were revealed, the Companions became very severe in their enmity towards their relatives and dear ones and became completely disassociated from them. Allah Almighty then revealed this verse, giving them hope that the condition of those disbelievers would change.

The summary is: O believers! It is near that Allah Almighty will create such love between you and those people among the disbelievers of Makkah with whom you have become enemies that He will grant them the ability to accept faith. Allah Almighty is All-Powerful over changing hearts, changing conditions and making the causes of love easy. He forgives and shows mercy to those among the polytheists who accept faith. Allah Almighty did exactly this: after the conquest of Makkah, a great number of them accepted faith, became friends and brothers of the believers, and their love for one another increased. (Madarik, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 7, p. 1233; Khazin, under verse 7, 4/257, excerpted.)

## Ayat 60:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54012

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=8; ayatId=5217; tafseerId=54012; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** لَا یَنْهٰىكُمُ اللّٰهُ عَنِ الَّذِیْنَ لَمْ یُقَاتِلُوْكُمْ فِی الدِّیْنِ وَ لَمْ یُخْرِجُوْكُمْ مِّنْ دِیَارِكُمْ اَنْ تَبَرُّوْهُمْ وَ تُقْسِطُوْۤا اِلَیْهِمْؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ الْمُقْسِطِیْنَ(8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Allah does not forbid you from those who did not fight against you on account of (your) religion and (they) did not drive you out from your homes, that you may be favourable towards them and deal with them fairly; indeed, the just ones are the beloveds of Allah.

{لَا یَنْهٰىكُمُ اللّٰهُ عَنِ الَّذِیْنَ لَمْ یُقَاتِلُوْكُمْ فِی الدِّیْنِ: Allah does not forbid you from those who did not fight against you on account of your religion.} There are many statements and differences of opinion in the exegesis of this verse, and more than a hundred practical cases to which it applies. Therefore, only one preferred summary is presented here. For details, study the treatise of Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ in the fourteenth volume of Fatawa Razawiyyah, entitled “اَلْمَحَجَّۃُ الْمُؤْتَمِنَۃ فِی اٰیَۃِ الْمُمْتَحِنَہ”.

The summary of the verses is that doing good and dealing fairly with disbelievers with whom Muslims have a peace treaty, or with protected disbelievers, is permitted. With others, it is prohibited. The meaning of qist and the details of birr and qist follow.

### What does birr, meaning goodness and good treatment, entail?

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ described three forms of birr and kind treatment towards disbelievers:

1. The highest form is to intentionally benefit and do good to a disbeliever without any valid personal purpose. This is forbidden even with a musta'min, a disbeliever who enters an Islamic state under a guarantee of safety, and with a mu'ahid, a disbeliever with whom a treaty exists, because a guarantee and treaty are intended to prevent harm, not to knowingly benefit the enemies of Allah Almighty.
2. The middle form is to act for a personal benefit, such as giving a disbeliever something in return for what he gave, or dealing financially with him while considering family ties. This is permissible with a disbeliever who has a treaty with the Muslims, but prohibited with one who has no treaty.
3. The lowest form is to give something as a military stratagem for the benefit of Islam and the Muslims. This is permissible even with a harbi disbeliever, one with whom there is no treaty.

The middle form of birr is intended in the noble verse “لَا یَنْهٰىكُمْ”, because the highest form is forbidden even with a treaty-holder, while the lowest form is permissible even with one who has no treaty. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, treatise “Al-Mahajjah al-Mu'taminah fi Ayat al-Mumtahanah”, 14/465, 468, 469, summarised.)

### The meaning of qist

Exegetes have mentioned three meanings of qist, meaning justice. The first is not to oppress them. In this meaning, the command applies to every disbeliever, whether a harbi or a treaty-holder: it is not permissible to oppress even a harbi. This is an obligation, not merely a concession.

The second is to fulfil a treaty made with disbelievers. This too is obligatory, not merely permitted. However, completing the treaty period is not obligatory: if there is a benefit, it is permissible to announce the matter and terminate the treaty before its expiry.

The third meaning is to give them some of one’s wealth, which is the same as birr, goodness. Thus birr and qist would be one matter in this meaning.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan عَلَیْہِ الرَّحْمَۃ explained that a distinction may be made between the two words: qist means giving the disbeliever the equivalent of what he gave, such as giving an item worth one thousand rupees in return for an item worth one thousand. If he gave nothing and a Muslim gives him an item worth one thousand because of kinship or another benefit, or if he gave an item worth one thousand and the Muslim gives him something worth more, this is birr: doing good and showing favour. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 14/471, summarised.)

### Forms and rulings of friendship with disbelievers

Verse 9 prohibits friendship with disbelievers. The summary of the words of Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ concerning friendship with them is as follows.

There are two forms of muwalat, meaning friendship with disbelievers:

1. Real muwalat: its lowest form is an inclination of the heart. This is absolutely forbidden with every disbeliever in every situation. An involuntary natural inclination, such as towards parents, children or a beautiful wife, is not included in this ruling. Nevertheless, one must suppress this inclination as far as possible, to the point of eliminating it if necessary, while remembering that they are enemies of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ and that friendship with them is forbidden. The arrival of the inclination was involuntary, but keeping it is within one’s control; retaining it would therefore amount to choosing friendship, which is categorically forbidden. This is why a person has no acceptable excuse for an involuntary matter whose initial causes he created by choice: losing one’s reason after drinking wine is not within one’s control, but because drinking was chosen, the loss of reason and everything resulting from it is also attributed to his choice.
2. Outward muwalat: the heart has no inclination towards a disbeliever, but one behaves in a way that outwardly indicates love and inclination. In necessity and compulsion, this is permitted only to the extent of the necessity. If it is enough merely not to display enmity, one should stop at that. If expressing love is necessary, one should speak indirectly as far as possible; explicit expression is not permitted. If there is no escape without it and the heart is firm upon faith, explicit expression is permitted as a concession, although the proper course remains not to do so. (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 14/465–467, summarised.)

The summary of the two verses under discussion is: Allah Almighty does not forbid you from doing good and dealing fairly with those disbelievers who did not fight you on account of religion, did not expel you from your homes, and did not support your opponents in expelling you. Indeed, Allah Almighty loves those who deal fairly. He only forbids you from befriending those disbelievers who fought you on account of religion, expelled you from your homes, and supported your opponents in expelling you. Whoever befriends them is among the wrongdoers. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verses 8–9, 4/258.)

## Ayat 60:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54013

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=10; ayatId=5219; tafseerId=54013; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا جَآءَكُمُ الْمُؤْمِنٰتُ مُهٰجِرٰتٍ فَامْتَحِنُوْهُنَّؕ-اَللّٰهُ اَعْلَمُ بِاِیْمَانِهِنَّۚ-فَاِنْ عَلِمْتُمُوْهُنَّ مُؤْمِنٰتٍ فَلَا تَرْجِعُوْهُنَّ اِلَى الْكُفَّارِؕ-لَا هُنَّ حِلٌّ لَّهُمْ وَ لَا هُمْ یَحِلُّوْنَ لَهُنَّؕ-وَ اٰتُوْهُمْ مَّاۤ اَنْفَقُوْاؕ-وَ لَا جُنَاحَ عَلَیْكُمْ اَنْ تَنْكِحُوْهُنَّ اِذَاۤ اٰتَیْتُمُوْهُنَّ اُجُوْرَهُنَّؕ-وَ لَا تُمْسِكُوْا بِعِصَمِ الْكَوَافِرِ وَ سْــٴَـلُوْا مَاۤ اَنْفَقْتُمْ وَ لْیَسْــٴَـلُوْا مَاۤ اَنْفَقُوْاؕ-ذٰلِكُمْ حُكْمُ اللّٰهِؕ-یَحْكُمُ بَیْنَكُمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمٌ حَكِیْمٌ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! When Muslim women leave the land of disbelief and migrate towards you, so examine them (by taking an oath from them); Allah knows more about their faith; so if you find the women to be believers, do not therefore send them back to the disbelievers; neither are they (i.e. the believing women) lawful for the disbelieving (men), nor are the disbelieving (men) lawful for them (i.e. the believing women); and give back to their disbelieving husbands what they had spent (on these believing women e.g. dowry); and there is no sin upon you to marry them (i.e. these believing women) when you give them their dowry; and (O Muslims) do not continue the marriage with disbelieving women, and ask back (from the disbelievers) what you had spent, and the disbelievers may ask for what they had spent (on the believing women); this is Allah's command, He judges between you, and Allah is All Knowing, the Wise.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا جَآءَكُمُ الْمُؤْمِنٰتُ مُهٰجِرٰتٍ: O believers! When Muslim women leave their homes and come to you.} Seven rulings concerning Muslim women who migrate are given in this verse.

1. When Muslim women leave the land of disbelief and come to you, examine them to determine whether their migration is purely for religion, rather than because of enmity towards their husbands or another worldly reason. The examination is for your knowledge; Allah Almighty knows their faith better than you. Their examination is carried out by making them swear that they did not leave because of enmity towards their husbands or any worldly reason, but migrated only for their religion and faith. This is reported from Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا.
2. If, after examination, they appear to be believers, do not return them to their disbelieving husbands. These Muslim women are not lawful for those disbelieving men, nor are those disbelieving men lawful for them. This shows that when a disbelieving man’s disbelieving wife accepts faith and migrates, she leaves his marriage.
3. Give their disbelieving husbands the dowries they paid to those women. Occasion of revelation: This verse was revealed after the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah. The treaty contained the condition that any man from Makkah who accepted faith and came to the Noble Prophet ﷺ could be taken back by the people of Makkah. This verse clarified that the condition concerned men only; women were not specified in the treaty and could not be included in it, because a Muslim woman is not lawful for a disbeliever. Some exegetes said that this part abrogated the earlier command not to return them to their disbelieving husbands.

That view would be correct if women were included in the treaty, but they were not, because the words of the treaty reported in Sahih Bukhari are: “لَایَاتِیْکَ مِنَّا رَجُلٌ وَاِنْ کَانَ عَلٰی دِیْنِکَ اِلَّا رَدَدْتَہٗ” — “No man from us will come to you, even if he is upon your religion, except that you will return him.” (Bukhari, Book of Conditions, chapter on conditions in jihad and reconciliation with the people of war, 2/223, hadith 2731, 2732.) No woman is mentioned.

Two legal matters concerning this dowry are worth noting: the dowry is given when the disbelieving husband demands it; if he does not demand it, nothing is given to him. Likewise, if the disbeliever had not paid a dowry to the emigrant woman, he receives nothing.

4. There is no sin in marrying these emigrant women after giving them their dowries, even if they have husbands in the land of war, because accepting Islam made them unlawful for those husbands and removed them from their marriages. Giving the dowry here means making it obligatory upon oneself, even if it is not paid immediately. This also proves that a new dowry is obligatory when marrying these women; the amount paid to their former husbands is not counted as it. Thus two amounts are involved: one for the former disbelieving husband and another as the woman’s dowry.
5. Do not continue marriage with disbelieving women. This means that women who remained in the land of war, or who became apostates and went to the land of war, should not remain wives. After this verse was revealed, the Companions of the Noble Prophet ﷺ divorced the disbelieving women who were in Makkah. If, God forbid, a Muslim’s wife becomes an apostate, she does not leave his marriage immediately; however, after she returns to Islam, a new marriage contract with the same husband is required.
6. Recover from the disbelievers the dowries that you paid to the women who left you.
7. The disbelievers may ask the Muslims who married women who migrated to the Islamic land to pay what they had spent on those women.

At the end of the verse, Allah Almighty states that these rulings are His command; He judges between you, and Allah Almighty is All-Knowing, All-Wise. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 10, 4/259; Madarik, under verse 10, p. 1233–1234.)

### The allegiance of Sayyidatuna Hind bint Utbah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا and other women

On the day of the conquest of Makkah, when the Noble Prophet ﷺ had finished taking the men’s allegiance, he began taking the women’s allegiance on Mount Safa. Sayyiduna Umar Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ stood below and conveyed the Noble Prophet’s ﷺ blessed words to the women. During this, the wife of Sayyiduna Abu Sufyan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, Sayyidatuna Hind bint Utbah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, came fearfully wearing a veil so that she would not be recognised.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said, “I take your allegiance that you will not associate anything with Allah Almighty.” Sayyidatuna Hind raised her head and said, “You are taking from us a pledge that we did not see you take from the men.” On that day, the men had pledged only Islam and striving. The Noble Prophet ﷺ then said, “And you will not steal.” Sayyidatuna Hind رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا replied, “Sayyiduna Abu Sufyan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ is a miserly man, and I have certainly taken some of his wealth. I do not know whether it was lawful for me.” Sayyiduna Abu Sufyan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ was present and said, “Whatever you took before and whatever you take in future is lawful.” The Noble Prophet ﷺ smiled and said, “You are Hind bint Utbah.” She replied, “Yes. Please forgive whatever wrongs I committed.”

The Noble Prophet ﷺ then said, “And you will not commit adultery.” Sayyidatuna Hind رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا said, “Does a free woman commit adultery?” He then said, “You will not kill your children.” She replied, “We raised small children, and when they became grown, you killed them; now you know and they know.” She said this because her son Hanzalah ibn Abu Sufyan had been killed at Badr. Sayyiduna Umar Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ laughed greatly on hearing this conversation.

The Radiant Prophet ﷺ then said, “You will not fabricate any false accusation between your hands and feet.” Sayyidatuna Hind رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا said, “By Allah, false accusation is a very evil thing, and the Noble Prophet ﷺ commands us to do good and adopt good character.” The Noble Prophet ﷺ then said, “You will not disobey the Noble Messenger ﷺ in any righteous matter.” Sayyidatuna Hind رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا replied, “We have not come to this gathering intending to allow the thought of disobeying you into our hearts.” The women affirmed all these matters, and 457 women pledged allegiance. (Khazin, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 12, 4/260; Madarik, under verse 12, p. 1234–1235; Khazain-ul-Irfan, under verse 12, p. 1019, excerpted.)

### How the women pledged allegiance

When the Noble Prophet ﷺ took the women’s allegiance, he did not shake hands with them and did not allow the women to touch his blessed hand. Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا said: “By Allah, the hand of the Noble Prophet ﷺ did not touch the hand of any woman during the allegiance. He took their allegiance only through his words.” (Sahih Bukhari, Book of Tafsir, Surah Al-Mumtahanah, chapter on when believing women who emigrated come to you, 3/350, hadith 4891.)

It has also been reported that the Chief of the Messengers ﷺ placed his blessed hand into a large vessel of water, after which the women placed their hands into it. It has also been said that the allegiance was taken through a cloth. It is not unlikely that both methods were practised.

### Lessons from “O Prophet! When Muslim women are present before you”

Four matters are learned from this verse:

1. When a spiritual guide makes someone a disciple, he should make him repent not only generally but also from the particular sins in which he is involved. For example, he should make one who does not pray specifically repent from abandoning prayer, and one who consumes interest repent specifically from consuming interest, and command him to remain firm upon this in future.
2. After taking allegiance, the spiritual guide should supplicate for his disciple’s forgiveness: “O Allah Almighty, forgive his previous sins.”
3. The command to repent oneself is one matter, and repenting at the hand of one of Allah Almighty’s accepted servants is another matter.
4. It is Sunnah for Muslims to pledge allegiance at the hands of spiritual guides, because these believing women pledged to the Noble Prophet ﷺ that they would avoid sins in future, and this is the purpose of allegiance to spiritual guides. There are four types of allegiance: the allegiance of Islam, the allegiance of the Caliphate, the allegiance of piety, and the allegiance of repentance. The allegiance taken today is the allegiance of repentance or piety. This verse and similar verses are its basis.

## Ayat 60:13

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54016

**Metadata:** surah=60; ayat=13; ayatId=5222; tafseerId=54016; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَوَلَّوْا قَوْمًا غَضِبَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِمْ قَدْ یَىٕسُوْا مِنَ الْاٰخِرَةِ كَمَا یَىٕسَ الْكُفَّارُ مِنْ اَصْحٰبِ الْقُبُوْرِ۠   (13)ٜ

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Do not befriend the people upon whom is the wrath of Allah; they have lost hope in the Hereafter just like the disbelievers have lost hope in the people of the graves (ever returning back to life on the Day of Judgement).

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تَتَوَلَّوْا قَوْمًا غَضِبَ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِمْ: O believers! Do not befriend the people upon whom Allah’s wrath has descended.} One interpretation of this verse is: O believers! Do not befriend the polytheists. Because they deny the Hereafter, they have lost hope of its reward just as they have lost hope that the people in the graves will return to the world. A second interpretation is: O believers! Do not befriend the Jews. Because they deny the Noble Prophet ﷺ despite knowing that he is a true Prophet, they have lost hope of the reward of the Hereafter just as the disbelievers have become hopeless that the dead will return to the world. (Madarik, Al-Mumtahanah, under verse 13, p. 1235, summarised.)
