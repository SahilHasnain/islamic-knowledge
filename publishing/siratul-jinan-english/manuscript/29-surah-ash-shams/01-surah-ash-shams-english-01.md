# Surah Ash Shams: English Translation

## Ayat 91:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54412

**Metadata:** surah=91; ayat=1; ayatId=6134; tafseerId=54412; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ الشَّمْسِ وَ ضُحٰىهَاﭪ     (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** By the oath of the sun and its light.

{وَ الشَّمْسِ وَ ضُحٰىهَا: By the oath of the sun and its light.} That is, by the sun when its light becomes manifest. In this Surah, Allah Almighty has sworn by several things in order to manifest the greatness of His Power and His Oneness in being worthy of worship. These are things upon which great benefits for creation depend, and by pondering over them, every person can learn about the Power and Oneness of Allah Almighty. (Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verse 1, 4/381; Sawi, Ash-Shams, under verse 1, 6/2369; Tafsir Kabir, Ash-Shams, under verse 1, 11/173, excerpted)

{وَ الْقَمَرِ اِذَا تَلٰىهَا: And by the moon, when it follows it (i.e. follows the sun).} That is, by the moon when it rises after the sun sets.

## Ayat 91:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54413

**Metadata:** surah=91; ayat=3; ayatId=6136; tafseerId=54413; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ النَّهَارِ اِذَا جَلّٰىهَاﭪ     (3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And by the day, when it brightens it.

{وَ النَّهَارِ اِذَا جَلّٰىهَا: And by the day, when it brightens it.} One meaning of this verse is: by the day when it makes the sun clearly visible. Since day is the name of the sun's light, the brighter the day, the more manifest the sun becomes, because the strength and perfection of an effect indicate the strength and perfection of the one producing the effect. Thus, the day makes the sun manifest. The second meaning is: by the day when it illuminates the world or the earth, or removes the darkness of night. (Tafsir Kabir, Ash-Shams, under verse 3, 11/174–175; Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verse 3, 4/381, excerpted)

{وَ الَّیْلِ اِذَا یَغْشٰىهَا: And by the night when it hides it (i.e. hides the sun).} One meaning of this verse is: by the night when it hides the sun and the edges of the sky become filled with darkness and gloom. The second meaning is: by the night when it covers the world.

The four things mentioned up to this point are, in reality, four qualities of the sun. Day exists because of the sun, and the light becomes clearly visible; night occurs when the sun sets, and the moon rises after it. Whoever ponders over the sun even a little and observes, with the eye of the heart, its structure, creation and other matters will come to know the greatness of its Creator. (Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verse 4, 4/381–382; Tafsir Kabir, Ash-Shams, under verse 4, 11/175, excerpted)

## Ayat 91:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54415

**Metadata:** surah=91; ayat=7; ayatId=6140; tafseerId=54415; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ نَفْسٍ وَّ مَا سَوّٰىهَاﭪ     (7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And by the soul and by Him Who made it proper.

{وَ نَفْسٍ وَّ مَا سَوّٰىهَا: And by the soul and by Him Who made it proper.} The summary of this verse and the verse after it is that Allah Almighty swore by the soul and by Him Who made it proper and granted it many powers, such as the power to speak, hear and see, and granted it thought, imagination, knowledge and understanding. Then He placed in its heart the understanding of disobedience and piety, made it aware of good and evil, righteousness and sin, and informed it about right and wrong. (Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verses 7–8, 4/382)

### A supplication of the Noble Prophet ﷺ

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ reached these verses while reciting, “وَ نَفْسٍ وَّ مَا سَوّٰىهَاﭪ(۷) فَاَلْهَمَهَا فُجُوْرَهَا وَ تَقْوٰىهَا”, he would stop and then say:

“اَللّٰهُمَّ آتِ نَفْسِیْ تَقْوَاهَا وَزَکِّهَا اَنْتَ خَیْرٌ مَّنْ زَکَّاهَا اَنْتَ وَلِیُّهَا وَمَوْلَاهَا”

That is: “O Allah, grant my soul its piety and purify it. You are the best of those who purify it; You alone are its Guardian and Master.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Kabir, Amr ibn Dinar from Ibn Abbas, 11/87, Hadith 11191; Ruh al-Bayan, Ash-Shams, under verse 8, 10/443)

## Ayat 91:9

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54416

**Metadata:** surah=91; ayat=9; ayatId=6142; tafseerId=54416; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** قَدْ اَفْلَحَ مَنْ زَكّٰىهَاﭪ      (9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, successful is the one who made it (i.e. his soul) pure.

{قَدْ اَفْلَحَ مَنْ زَكّٰىهَا: Indeed, successful is the one who made it (i.e. his soul) pure.} After mentioning the oaths by several things in the previous verses, Allah Almighty states in this verse and the verse after it that indeed the one who purified his soul from evils has succeeded, and indeed the one who concealed his soul beneath sins has failed. (Jalalayn with Sawi, Ash-Shams, under verses 9–10, 6/2370)

### Purifying the soul from evils is a means of success

This shows that purifying one's soul from evils is a means of attaining success, while concealing one's soul beneath sins is a cause of failure. The soul can only be purified from evils when one obeys Allah Almighty and His Beloved ﷺ. Regarding those who obey, Allah Almighty states:

“وَ مَنْ یُّطِعِ اللّٰهَ وَ رَسُوْلَهٗ وَ یَخْشَ اللّٰهَ وَ یَتَّقْهِ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْفَآىٕزُوْنَ” (An-Nur: 52)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger, fears Allah and remains cautious of disobedience to Him, it is they who are successful.”

And He states:

“وَ مَنْ یُّطِعِ اللّٰهَ وَ رَسُوْلَهٗ فَقَدْ فَازَ فَوْزًا عَظِیْمًا” (Al-Ahzab: 71)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has indeed attained a great success.”

Therefore, whoever wishes to attain true success and be saved from failure should purify his soul from evils by obeying Allah Almighty and His Beloved ﷺ.

## Ayat 91:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54417

**Metadata:** surah=91; ayat=11; ayatId=6144; tafseerId=54417; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُوْدُ بِطَغْوٰىهَاﭪ     (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The (nation of) Thamood denied (their Prophet Saalih) with their rebelliousness.

{كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُوْدُ بِطَغْوٰىهَا: The (nation of) Thamood denied (their Prophet Saalih) with their rebelliousness.} In the previous verses, the success of the obedient and the failure of the disobedient were described through several oaths. Here, Allah Almighty describes the state of one of His Messengers and those who disobeyed him, so that it would become clear to the disbelievers of Makkah that just as the nation of Sayyiduna Salih عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was destroyed because of disobeying him, they too could be destroyed because of disobeying the Noble Messenger ﷺ. The summary of this verse and the four verses after it is that the nation of Thamood, in its rebellion, denied their Messenger, Sayyiduna Salih عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, when their most wretched man, Qudar ibn Salif, rose with the agreement of all of them to cut the camel's legs. Allah's Messenger, Sayyiduna Salih عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, said to them: “Beware of pursuing the she-camel of Allah, and do not take water on the day appointed for her to drink, so that punishment does not befall you.” They denied Sayyiduna Salih عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and then cut the camel's legs. Their Lord, عزَّوَجَلَّ, therefore caused destruction to them because of their sin and levelled their settlement, killing them all; not one of them remained. Allah Almighty does not fear pursuing them, as kings do, because He is the King of all kings. He does whatever He wills, and no one has the power to oppose Him. (Sawi, Ash-Shams, under verses 11–15, 6/2370–2371; Abu Saud, Ash-Shams, under verses 11–15, 5/875–876; Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verses 11–15, 4/382–383, excerpted)

{وَ لَا یَخَافُ عُقْبٰهَا: And He has no fear (at all) going after (them, to take revenge).} Some exegetes have also explained this verse to mean that Sayyiduna Salih عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام had no fear that any of them could harm him after the punishment descended. (Khazin, Ash-Shams, under verse 15, 4/383)
