# Surah At-Takathur: English Translation

## Ayat 102:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54479

**Metadata:** surah=102; ayat=1; ayatId=6270; tafseerId=54479; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The desire to seek more wealth has kept you neglectful (from the remembrance of Allah).

{اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ: The desire to seek more wealth has kept you neglectful.} Allah Almighty states that the desire to seek more wealth, and boasting about one's wealth and children, has made you neglectful of the worship of Allah Almighty. (Jalalayn, At-Takathur, under verse 1, p. 505.)

### Condemnation of greed for abundant wealth

It is learned from this that greed for abundant wealth, and displaying pride in it and in one's children, is blameworthy. When a person becomes involved in this, he is deprived of the blessings of the Hereafter. Allah Almighty states:

“اِعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّمَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَا لَعِبٌ وَّ لَهْوٌ وَّ زِیْنَةٌ وَّ تَفَاخُرٌۢ بَیْنَكُمْ وَ تَكَاثُرٌ فِی الْاَمْوَالِ وَ الْاَوْلَادِؕ-كَمَثَلِ غَیْثٍ اَعْجَبَ الْكُفَّارَ نَبَاتُهٗ ثُمَّ یَهِیْجُ فَتَرٰىهُ مُصْفَرًّا ثُمَّ یَكُوْنُ حُطَامًاؕ-وَ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ شَدِیْدٌۙ-وَّ مَغْفِرَةٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ وَ رِضْوَانٌؕ-وَ مَا الْحَیٰوةُ الدُّنْیَاۤ اِلَّا مَتَاعُ الْغُرُوْرِ‘‘(حدید:۲۰)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: Know that the life of this world is only play, amusement, adornment, boasting among yourselves, and seeking to surpass one another in wealth and children. It is like rain whose vegetation pleases the farmers; then that vegetation dries, and you see it yellow; then it becomes crushed and useless. And in the Hereafter there is severe punishment, and forgiveness and pleasure from Allah. And the life of this world is only the provision of deception.

And Allah Almighty states:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ اَمْوَالُكُمْ وَ لَاۤ اَوْلَادُكُمْ عَنْ ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِۚ-وَ مَنْ یَّفْعَلْ ذٰلِكَ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْخٰسِرُوْنَ‘‘(منافقون:۹)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: O believers! Let neither your wealth nor your children make you neglectful of the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that, it is they who are the losers.

And Allah Almighty states:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِنَّ مِنْ اَزْوَاجِكُمْ وَ اَوْلَادِكُمْ عَدُوًّا لَّكُمْ فَاحْذَرُوْهُمْۚ-وَ اِنْ تَعْفُوْا وَ تَصْفَحُوْا وَ تَغْفِرُوْا فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(۱۴)اِنَّمَاۤ اَمْوَالُكُمْ وَ اَوْلَادُكُمْ فِتْنَةٌؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عِنْدَهٗۤ اَجْرٌ عَظِیْمٌ(۱۵)فَاتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ وَ اسْمَعُوْا وَ اَطِیْعُوْا وَ اَنْفِقُوْا خَیْرًا لِّاَنْفُسِكُمْؕ-وَ مَنْ یُّوْقَ شُحَّ نَفْسِهٖ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ‘‘(تغابن:۱۴-۱۶)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: O believers! Indeed, among your wives and children there are some who are your enemies, so beware of them. And if you pardon, overlook, and forgive, then indeed Allah is Most Forgiving, Most Merciful. Your wealth and your children are only a trial, and with Allah is a great reward. So fear Allah as much as you are able, listen, obey, and spend in the way of Allah; this is better for your souls. And whoever is protected from the greed of his own soul, it is they who are successful.

Sayyiduna Mutarrif رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates from his father that he came into the presence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ while he ﷺ was reciting “اَلْهٰىكُمُ التَّكَاثُرُ”. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “The son of Adam says, ‘My wealth, my wealth.’ O son of Adam! Your wealth is only what you have eaten and consumed, what you have worn and worn out, or what you have given in charity and sent ahead.” (Muslim, Book of Asceticism and Heart-Softening Matters, p. 1582, hadith 3 [2958].)

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “The servant says, ‘My wealth, my wealth,’ but he has only three things from his wealth: (1) what he ate and consumed; (2) what he wore and wore out; and (3) what he gave to someone and stored for the Hereafter. Everything besides this will depart, and he will leave it for the people.” (Muslim, Book of Asceticism and Heart-Softening Matters, p. 1582, hadith 4 [2959].)

Sayyiduna Amr ibn Awf رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “By Allah, I do not fear that you will become poor. Rather, I fear that the world will be expanded for you as it was expanded for those before you. Then you will desire it as they desired it, and it will destroy you as it destroyed them.” (Bukhari, Book of Jizyah and Truces, chapter on jizyah and truces with the People of Protection and the warriors, 2/363, hadith 3158.)

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Leader of the Worlds ﷺ said: “Richness does not come from having abundant wealth and possessions; rather, true richness is the richness of the heart. By Allah, I do not fear poverty for you, but I fear that you will become afflicted with greed for abundant wealth.” (Musnad Imam Ahmad, Musnad Abu Hurayrah, 3/645, hadith 10958.)

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Ka'b ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا that the Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “Two hungry wolves released among sheep do not harm those sheep more than the greed for wealth and honour harms a person's religion.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Asceticism, chapter 43, 4/166, hadith 2383.)

May Allah Almighty protect all Muslims from greed and avarice for wealth. Ameen.

{حَتّٰى زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ: Until you (died and) confronted the graves.} That is, the greed for abundant wealth remained in your hearts until death came to you and you were buried in graves. (Khazin, At-Takathur, under verse 2, 4/404.)

### The reality of wealth and children

Remember that the wealth whose increase people desire, and the children about whom they display pride and arrogance, remain with a person only as long as the soul remains in his body. As soon as the soul separates from his body and he is buried in the grave, that wealth and those children leave him. Only his deeds go with him into the grave. Therefore, instead of striving to increase wealth and boasting about one's children, every intelligent person should strive to increase righteous deeds, so that they may be his best companions in the grave.

Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “Three things accompany the deceased; two return and one remains with him. His family, wealth, and deeds accompany him. His family and wealth return, while his deeds remain with him.” (Bukhari, Book of Heart-Softening Matters, chapter on the agonies of death, 4/250, hadith 6514.)

Sayyidatuna Aishah Siddiqah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا says that one day the Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked the Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ: “Do you know what the example of you, your family, your wealth, and your deeds is?” The Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ submitted: “Allah Almighty and His Messenger ﷺ know best.” He ﷺ said: “The example of you, your family, your wealth, and your deeds is like that of a man who has three brothers. When the time of his death draws near, he calls all three brothers and says to one of them, ‘You see my condition; tell me, what can you do for me?’ He replies, ‘I can care for you now, remain with you, fulfil your needs and necessities, and then, when you pass away, wash you, shroud you, and carry your funeral with the people, sometimes carrying one side and sometimes letting another person carry it. When I return after burying you, I will mention your good qualities to anyone who asks about you.’ This brother is in fact the man's family. After saying this, the Radiant Prophet ﷺ asked the Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ, ‘What do you think of this?’ They submitted, ‘We find no good in it.’

“Then he says to his second brother, ‘You also see my condition; what can you do for me?’ He replies, ‘I will remain with you as long as you are alive. As soon as you depart from this world, our paths will separate, because you will reach the grave while I will remain here in the world.’ This brother is in fact the man's wealth. ‘What do you think of this?’ The Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ submitted, ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, we do not consider this good either.’

“The Revered Prophet ﷺ further said: ‘Then the man says to his third brother, “Certainly, you also see my condition and you have heard the answers concerning my family and wealth. Tell me, what can you do for me?” He comforts him and says, “My brother, I will remain with you even in the grave, protect you from its loneliness, and when the Day of Reckoning comes, I will sit in your scale and make it heavy.”’ This is his deed. ‘What do you think of this?’ The Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ submitted, ‘O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, this is a very good companion.’ The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: ‘This is the reality.’” (Kanz-ul-Ummal, letter Meem, Book of Death, section of deeds, supplement to Death, 8/318, Part 15, hadith 42974.)

## Ayat 102:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54480

**Metadata:** surah=102; ayat=3; ayatId=6272; tafseerId=54480; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُوْنَۙ    (3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Yes, certainly! You will soon come to know.

{كَلَّا سَوْفَ تَعْلَمُوْنَ: Yes, certainly! You will soon come to know.} The summary of this verse and the five verses after it is: O people! Yes, certainly, at the time of the agony of death you will soon learn the evil result of greed for abundant wealth and pride and arrogance regarding children. Then, certainly, you will soon learn it in the graves. Indeed, if you knew the consequence of greed for wealth with certain knowledge, you would not become involved in greed for wealth and thereby become neglectful of the Hereafter. Certainly, after your passing away you will surely see Hell; then you will surely see it with the sight of certainty. Then, on the Day of Judgement, you will surely be questioned about the blessings Allah Almighty granted you, such as health, leisure, security, comfort, and wealth, through which you enjoyed pleasures in the world. You will be asked: For what purpose did you spend these things? How did you show gratitude for them? And you will be punished for abandoning gratitude for these blessings. (Khazin, At-Takathur, under verses 3–8, 4/404; Madarik, At-Takathur, under verses 3–8, p. 1371; Jalalayn, At-Takathur, under verses 3–8, p. 506, excerpted.)

### Every blessing will be questioned about on the Day of Judgement

It is learned from verse 8 of this surah that every blessing will be questioned about on the Day of Judgement, whether the blessing is physical or spiritual, necessary or one of luxury and comfort. A person will even be questioned about cool water, the shade of a tree, and comfortable sleep. Allah Almighty states:

“وَ لَا تَقْفُ مَا لَیْسَ لَكَ بِهٖ عِلْمٌؕ-اِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَ الْبَصَرَ وَ الْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ اُولٰٓىٕكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـٴُـوْلًا” (Bani Isra'il: 36)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the ear, the eye, and the heart—all of these will be questioned.

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ says that when this verse was revealed, “ثُمَّ لَتُسْــٴَـلُنَّ یَوْمَىٕذٍ عَنِ النَّعِیْمِ”, the Noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ submitted: “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, about which blessing will we be questioned, when we possess only these two black things, meaning dates and water, while the enemy is present and our swords are upon our shoulders?” He ﷺ said: “It will soon be so.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Tafsir, chapter concerning Surah At-Takathur, 5/235, hadith 3368.)

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “On the Day of Judgement, the servant will first be questioned about a blessing. He will be asked: Did We not give you physical health and provide you with cool water to drink?” (Tirmidhi, Book of Tafsir, chapter concerning Surah At-Takathur, 5/236, hadith 3369.)

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Mas'ud رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “A person's feet will not move on the Day of Judgement until he is questioned about five things: (1) his life—what he spent it on; (2) his youth—what work it passed in; (3 and 4) his wealth—where he earned it and where he spent it; and (5) his knowledge—how much he acted upon it.” (Tirmidhi, Book of the Description of the Day of Judgement, Heart-Softening Matters and Piety, chapter on the Day of Judgement, 4/188, hadith 2424.)

May Allah Almighty grant every Muslim the ability to give thanks for His blessings. Every Muslim should regularly make this supplication:

“رَبِّ اَوْزِعْنِیْۤ اَنْ اَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِیْۤ اَنْعَمْتَ عَلَیَّ وَ عَلٰى وَالِدَیَّ وَ اَنْ اَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضٰىهُ وَ اَصْلِحْ لِیْ فِیْ ذُرِّیَّتِیْ ﱂاِنِّیْ تُبْتُ اِلَیْكَ وَ اِنِّیْ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِیْنَ” (Ahqaf: 15)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: O my Lord! Grant me the ability to give thanks for Your blessing which You have bestowed upon me and my parents, and to perform such a good deed as pleases You, and make righteousness prevail in my offspring. I have turned towards You in repentance, and I am among the Muslims.
