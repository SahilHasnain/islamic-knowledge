# Surah Al-Qariah: English Translation

## Ayat 101:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54475

**Metadata:** surah=101; ayat=1; ayatId=6258; tafseerId=54475; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَلْقَارِعَةُۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** (The event) which causes the heart to tremble.

{اَلْقَارِعَةُ: (The event) which causes the heart to tremble.} Qariah is one of the names of the Day of Judgement. It was given this name because, due to its terror, dread and severity, the hearts of all people will tremble. Some exegetes say that the Day of Judgement is called Qariah because of the voice of Sayyiduna Israfil عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. When he blows the Trumpet, all creation will perish from the intensity of the sound of his blowing. (Khazin, Al-Qariah, under verse 1, 4/403)

{وَ مَاۤ اَدْرٰىكَ مَا الْقَارِعَةُ: And what have you understood what is (the event) which causes the heart to tremble?} Allamah Ahmad Sawi رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ states that the meaning of this verse is: O Beloved ﷺ, you can know the terror, severity and dread of the Day of Judgement only through the revelation coming from Us. Thus, this verse negates knowledge of the terror of the Day of Judgement without revelation; it does not negate knowledge of it absolutely. (Sawi, Al-Qariah, under verse 3, 6/2413)

## Ayat 101:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54476

**Metadata:** surah=101; ayat=4; ayatId=6261; tafseerId=54476; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** یَوْمَ یَكُوْنُ النَّاسُ كَالْفَرَاشِ الْمَبْثُوْثِۙ    (4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The Day when people will be like scattered moths.

{یَوْمَ یَكُوْنُ النَّاسُ كَالْفَرَاشِ الْمَبْثُوْثِ: The Day when people will be like scattered moths.} Just as moths become scattered when falling upon a flame, with no one fixed direction for them, and each goes in a direction opposite to the others, such will be the state of creation's scattering on the Day of Judgement. When they are raised from their graves, they will be scattered like moths, and each will be going in a direction opposite to the others. (Khazin, Al-Qariah, under verse 4, 4/403)

It should be remembered that this verse compares the scattering of creation while they are raised from their graves to scattered moths, whereas in these verses:

“یَوْمَ یَدْعُ الدَّاعِ اِلٰى شَیْءٍ نُّكُرٍۙ(۶)خُشَّعًا اَبْصَارُهُمْ یَخْرُجُوْنَ مِنَ الْاَجْدَاثِ كَاَنَّهُمْ جَرَادٌ مُّنْتَشِرٌ” (Al-Qamar: 6–7)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “The Day when the caller will call towards a harsh, unfamiliar thing. Their eyes will be lowered, and they will come out of their graves as though they are scattered locusts.”

creation is compared to scattered locusts because of the great multitude of creation.

## Ayat 101:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54477

**Metadata:** surah=101; ayat=5; ayatId=6262; tafseerId=54477; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ تَكُوْنُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ الْمَنْفُوْشِﭤ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And mountains will be like scattered particles of wool.

{وَ تَكُوْنُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ الْمَنْفُوْشِ: And mountains will be like scattered particles of wool.} The terror and dread of the Day of Judgement, which causes hearts to tremble, will reduce the lofty and strongest mountains to fragments that will fly through the air just as particles of different-coloured wool fly through the air when wool is carded. If this is the state of the mountains, then what will become of a weak human being! (Khazin, Al-Qariah, under verse 5, 4/403; Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Qariah, under verse 5, 10/500, excerpted)

## Ayat 101:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54478

**Metadata:** surah=101; ayat=6; ayatId=6263; tafseerId=54478; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاَمَّا مَنْ ثَقُلَتْ مَوَازِیْنُهٗۙ    (6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So, as for the one whose scales (of good deeds) are heavy.

{فَاَمَّا مَنْ ثَقُلَتْ مَوَازِیْنُهٗ: So, as for the one whose scales (of good deeds) are heavy.} After mentioning the state of the Day of Judgement, two groups of creation on the Day of Judgement are described here. The summary of this verse and the five verses after it is that the scales of the one whose good deeds are heavy because he followed the truth, and whose weighty good deeds are greater in number, will be in a desirable life in Paradise. The scales of the one whose good deeds become light because he used to follow falsehood will be light; his abode will be Hawiyah. And what have you understood what it is? It is a blazing fire of extreme heat and intensity.

Two matters concerning the weighing of deeds should be kept in mind:

1. Regarding the weighing of deeds, one opinion is that on the Day of Judgement the good deeds of a believer will be brought in a beautiful form and placed in the Scale. If they prevail, Paradise is for him. The evil deeds of a disbeliever will be brought in the ugliest form and placed in the Scale, and his weighing will be light because the deeds of disbelievers are false and have no weight; therefore, they will be admitted into Hell. Another opinion is that only the deeds of believers will be weighed on the Day of Judgement. If a believer's good deeds prevail over his evil deeds, he will enter Paradise. If his sins prevail over his good deeds, he will enter Hell, then he will be taken out of Hell and admitted into Paradise after completing the punishment for his sins, or Allah Almighty will forgive him through His grace, generosity and mercy and admit him into Paradise. The deeds of disbelievers will not be weighed, as Allah Almighty has stated:

“فَلَا نُقِیْمُ لَهُمْ یَوْمَ الْقِیٰمَةِ وَزْنًا” (Al-Kahf: 105)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “So We shall not establish any weight for them on the Day of Judgement.” (Khazin, Al-Qariah, under verses 6–10, 4/403; Madarik, Al-Qariah, under verses 6–10, p. 1370, excerpted)

However, the researched position concerning this is that Allah Almighty will admit into Hell without weighing their deeds those disbelievers whom He wishes to place in Hell quickly, while the deeds of the remaining disbelievers will be weighed. Similarly, Allah Almighty will admit some Muslims into Paradise without weighing their deeds and without reckoning.

2. Establishing the Scale on the Day of Judgement and weighing deeds is true. Allah Almighty states:

“وَالْوَزْنُ یَوْمَىٕذِ ﹰالْحَقُّۚ- فَمَنْ ثَقُلَتْ مَوَازِیْنُهٗ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الْمُفْلِحُوْنَ(۸)وَ مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَازِیْنُهٗ فَاُولٰٓىٕكَ الَّذِیْنَ خَسِرُوْۤا اَنْفُسَهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا بِاٰیٰتِنَا یَظْلِمُوْنَ” (Al-A'raf: 8–9)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And weighing on that Day is certainly true. So those whose scales are heavy, they are the successful. And those whose scales are light, they are the ones who have put themselves into loss because they used to wrong Our signs.”
