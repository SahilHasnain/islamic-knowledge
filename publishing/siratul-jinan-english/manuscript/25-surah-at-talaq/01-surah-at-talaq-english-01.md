# Surah At-Talaq: English Translation

## Ayat 65:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54063

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=1; ayatId=5282; tafseerId=54063; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّبِیُّ اِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ فَطَلِّقُوْهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ وَ اَحْصُوا الْعِدَّةَۚ-وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ رَبَّكُمْۚ-لَا تُخْرِجُوْهُنَّ مِنْۢ بُیُوْتِهِنَّ وَ لَا یَخْرُجْنَ اِلَّاۤ اَنْ یَّاْتِیْنَ بِفَاحِشَةٍ مُّبَیِّنَةٍؕ-وَ تِلْكَ حُدُوْدُ اللّٰهِؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَعَدَّ حُدُوْدَ اللّٰهِ فَقَدْ ظَلَمَ نَفْسَهٗؕ-لَا تَدْرِیْ لَعَلَّ اللّٰهَ یُحْدِثُ بَعْدَ ذٰلِكَ اَمْرًا(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O (Beloved) Prophet! (Tell the believers) when you people divorce women, so divorce them (when they are in the state of purity, with one divorce) at (the start of) their prescribed period, and keep (an accurate) calculation of their 'Iddah (i.e. waiting period). And (O Muslims) fear Allah, your Lord, Do not banish them from their (husband's) homes, nor should they leave themselves during the 'Iddah period unless this that they commit any open indecency (i.e. fornication, or theft, etc.). And these are the limits of Allah; and whoever transgresses the limits of Allah, indeed he has done wrong to himself. You do not know, maybe Allah will send a new command afterwards (so that you may take her back if that was the first or second divorce).

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّبِیُّ اِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ فَطَلِّقُوْهُنَّ لِعِدَّتِهِنَّ: O Prophet! When you divorce women, divorce them at their prescribed period.} The occasion of revelation of this verse is that Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا divorced his wife during her menstrual period. The Sovereign of both worlds صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ commanded him to take her back. Then, if he wished to divorce her, he was to divorce her during the days of purity. Sahih Bukhari relates from Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا that, during the blessed era of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, he divorced his wife while she was menstruating. Sayyiduna Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ asked the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ about it, and he said: “Command him to take her back so that she remains with him until she becomes pure, then menstruates and becomes pure again. Thereafter, if he wishes, he may keep her, and if he wishes, he may divorce her before touching her. This is the waiting period regarding which Allah Almighty has commanded that women be divorced in this manner.” (Bukhari, Book of Divorce, chapter on the saying of Allah Almighty: یایّہا النبی اذا طلّقتم النسائ۔۔۔ الخ, 3/478, hadith 5251)

{اِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ: When you divorce women.} This verse explains the method of divorcing a wife and the religious rulings concerning the waiting period of a divorced woman. Its opening portion means: O Beloved ﷺ, tell your Ummah that when you intend to divorce women, divorce them at the beginning of their waiting period, meaning during the days of purity, so that their waiting period does not become lengthy. (Khazin, At-Talaq, under verse 1, 4/277)

### Five religious rulings concerning divorce

The following five religious rulings concerning divorcing women are noted here.

1. The women mentioned in this verse are those with whom their husbands have consummated the marriage and whose waiting period is calculated through menstruation. If such a woman is to be divorced, one divorce should be given during a period of purity in which intercourse has not taken place, and no revocation should be made until the waiting period ends. This is called Talaq-e-Ahsan.

2. If a woman is divorced during menstruation, or during a period of purity in which intercourse took place, or if two or three divorces are given together or separately during one period of purity, even if intercourse did not take place in that period, all these forms are Talaq-e-Bid'at. Talaq-e-Bid'at is disliked, but it takes effect, and the person who gives such a divorce is sinful.

3. Women who do not menstruate, such as a young girl, a pregnant woman, or an aged woman whose menstruation has stopped, are not included under the ruling of this verse.

4. A woman with whom her husband has not consummated the marriage, and with whom he has not been alone in a way that intercourse could have taken place, has no waiting period. As for other women who do not menstruate, their waiting period is not calculated through menstruation.

5. It is permissible to divorce a woman with whom the marriage has not been consummated during her menstrual period. (For more information concerning divorce, read Talaq ke Aasan Masail, published by Maktabat-ul-Madinah.)

{وَ اَحْصُوا الْعِدَّةَ: And keep count of the waiting period.} Here men are commanded to count the woman's waiting period after divorce until she has menstruated three times. Remember that both the man and woman will count the waiting period, but men are specifically addressed here because women often become heedless in this matter.

{وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ رَبَّكُمْ: And fear Allah, your Lord.} Fear Allah Almighty, your true Lord, concerning lengthening women's waiting periods and violating the commands of Allah Almighty.

{لَا تُخْرِجُوْهُنَّ مِنْۢ بُیُوْتِهِنَّ: Do not remove them from their homes.} During the waiting period, do not remove women from their homes, nor should they leave their residence themselves. However, if they commit an open indecency and an open act of disobedience for which a legal punishment applies, such as fornication or theft, then they may be removed from the home. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 1, p. 1251; Ruh al-Bayan, At-Talaq, under verse 1, 10/28; Khazain al-Irfan, At-Talaq, under verse 1, p. 1032)

### Five rulings concerning completing the waiting period at home

1. A woman must complete her waiting period in her husband's home. It is not permissible for the husband to remove a divorced woman during the waiting period, nor is it permissible for her to leave, because this residence is not merely the husband's right that can be waived with his consent; it is also a right established by the Shariah.

2. If a woman uses obscene speech and causes harm to the members of the household, it is permissible to remove her because she has the ruling of a disobedient woman.

3. A woman observing the waiting period of a revocable or irrevocable divorce may not leave the home at all. A woman observing the waiting period after her husband's passing may leave during the day when there is a need, but she must spend the night in her husband's home.

4. During the waiting period of an irrevocable divorce, the woman and her former husband must observe separation. It is better that another woman remain between them.

5. If the husband is sinful or the dwelling is very cramped, the husband should leave that dwelling.

{وَ تِلْكَ حُدُوْدُ اللّٰهِ: And these are the limits of Allah.} The commands given to you are the limits of Allah Almighty. It is necessary for servants to remain within them. Whoever crosses the limits of Allah Almighty commits sin and wrongs himself.

{لَا تَدْرِیْ: You do not know.} The person giving divorce is encouraged here to give a revocable divorce, meaning one divorce followed by separation. You do not know: perhaps after the divorce Allah Almighty will create affection for the woman in the husband's heart, he will regret his action and become inclined to revoke it. If he gave a revocable divorce, revocation will then be easy; or, if he gave fewer than three irrevocable divorces, he may return to her through a new marriage.

## Ayat 65:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54064

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=2; ayatId=5283; tafseerId=54064; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاِذَا بَلَغْنَ اَجَلَهُنَّ فَاَمْسِكُوْهُنَّ بِمَعْرُوْفٍ اَوْ فَارِقُوْهُنَّ بِمَعْرُوْفٍ وَّ اَشْهِدُوْا ذَوَیْ عَدْلٍ مِّنْكُمْ وَ اَقِیْمُوا الشَّهَادَةَ لِلّٰهِؕ-ذٰلِكُمْ یُوْعَظُ بِهٖ مَنْ كَانَ یُؤْمِنُ بِاللّٰهِ وَ الْیَوْمِ الْاٰخِرِ۬ؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَّقِ اللّٰهَ یَجْعَلْ لَّهٗ مَخْرَجًاۙ    (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Hence, when they are about to reach their appointed term (nearing completion of 'Iddah), so hold them back with kindness or separate from them with kindness, and make two credible men amongst you as witnesses, and establish testimony for the sake of Allah; whosoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, he is advised with this; and whosoever fears Allah, Allah will create for him a way of salvation.

{فَاِذَا بَلَغْنَ اَجَلَهُنَّ: When they are about to reach their appointed term.} This verse explains the rulings concerning taking back a divorced woman. When the woman divorced according to the method described above approaches the end of her waiting period, you have a choice: if you wish to live with her in good companionship, take her back and do not intend to divorce her again; and if you do not hope to live with her properly, separate from her after paying what is due to her, such as her dower. Do not harm her by taking her back at the end of the waiting period and then divorcing her, thereby lengthening her waiting period and causing her distress. In both cases, whether taking her back or separating, appoint two just Muslims from among your people as witnesses, to remove accusations and prevent disputes. The witnesses should be legally acceptable, and their appointment should be for the sake of Allah Almighty, with no corrupt purpose besides establishing the truth and obeying Allah's command. This is the counsel given to one who believes in Allah Almighty and the Last Day: if he divorces according to the Sunnah, does not harm the woman observing her waiting period, does not remove her from her residence, and appoints Muslims as witnesses according to Allah's command, Allah Almighty will create a path of deliverance for him through which he will be freed from the troubles of this world and the Hereafter and protected from every difficulty and distress. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 2, p. 1251, abridged)

### Four rulings concerning taking back a divorced woman

1. Keeping a woman who has received a revocable divorce in the original marriage during the waiting period is called ruju, or taking her back.

2. The Sunnah method of ruju is to use words indicating that he has taken her back, appoint two just men as witnesses, and inform the woman so that she does not marry someone else after the waiting period. If she unknowingly marries another man after her husband has taken her back, she must be separated from the second man, even if he has consummated the marriage, because that marriage was invalid. If the husband says the words of ruju without appointing witnesses, or appoints witnesses without informing the woman, the ruju is valid but disliked and contrary to the Sunnah. If he takes her back through an action, such as intercourse or kissing her with desire, the ruju takes effect but is disliked; therefore, he should repeat the words of ruju before witnesses.

3. The woman's consent is not required for ruju. Even if she refuses, ruju takes effect. If the husband said after divorcing her, “I have cancelled my ruju” or “I have no right to ruju,” he may still take her back.

Note: For further rulings concerning ruju, read the chapter on Ruju in Bahar-e-Shariat, Part 8. (Bahar-e-Shariat, The Chapter on Ruju, 2/170–172, abridged)

{وَ مَنْ یَّتَّقِ اللّٰهَ یَجْعَلْ لَّهٗ مَخْرَجًا: And whoever fears Allah, Allah will create for him a way of salvation.} One meaning of this passage was given above. According to most exegetes, the occasion of revelation is that the polytheists imprisoned the son of Sayyiduna Auf ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ. He came to the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ and complained that his son had been imprisoned and that he was suffering poverty and need. The Sovereign of both worlds صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ said: “Fear Allah, be patient, and frequently recite لَاحَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّۃَ اِلَّا بِاللّٰہِ الْعَلِیِّ الْعَظِیْم.” Sayyiduna Auf رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ returned home and told his wife, and both began reciting this litany. While they were reciting it, the son knocked at the door. The enemy had become heedless, and he escaped from captivity. According to one narration he brought one hundred enemy camels with him, and according to another he brought four thousand goats. Sayyiduna Auf رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ came to the Noble Prophet's court and asked whether the camels or goats were lawful for them. The Noble Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ permitted it, and this verse was revealed. (Khazin, At-Talaq, under verse 2, 4/279; Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 3, p. 1252, excerpted)

### An ayat sufficient for people

Sayyiduna Abu Dharr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ said: “I know an ayat which, if all people acted upon it, would suffice for them.” The Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ asked, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, which ayat is it?” He said:

“وَ مَنْ یَّتَّقِ اللّٰهَ یَجْعَلْ لَّهٗ مَخْرَجًا”

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And whoever fears Allah, Allah will create for him a way of salvation.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Zuhd, chapter on piety and abstinence, Part 2, p. 1411, hadith 4220, Matba'at Dar Ihya al-Kutub al-Arabiyyah, Cairo)

## Ayat 65:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54065

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=3; ayatId=5284; tafseerId=54065; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَّ یَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَیْثُ لَا یَحْتَسِبُؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللّٰهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهٗؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ بَالِغُ اَمْرِهٖؕ-قَدْ جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ لِكُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدْرًا(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And (Allah) will provide him sustenance from where he could not (even) imagine; and whoever relies upon Allah, so He is Sufficient for him; indeed, Allah is to complete His task; indeed, Allah has set a proper measure for all things.

{وَ یَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَیْثُ لَا یَحْتَسِبُ: And He will provide him sustenance from where he could not imagine.} The final part of the preceding verse gave a glad tiding to the one who fears Allah Almighty. Here he is given a further glad tiding: Allah Almighty will provide him sustenance from a place he could not imagine. Whoever relies upon Allah Almighty and entrusts all his affairs to Him, Allah Almighty is sufficient for him in both worlds. Indeed, Allah Almighty completes His command and has appointed a measure for everything. Therefore, whether you rely upon Him or not, you will receive only what has been decreed; so why abandon reliance and deprive yourself of its reward? (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 3, pp. 1251–1252)

### Encouragement to rely upon Allah

This verse teaches that every Muslim should rely upon Allah Almighty and place trust in Him in all affairs. Sayyiduna Umar Farooq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ said: “If you relied upon Allah as reliance upon Him truly requires, you would be given provision as birds are given provision: they leave hungry in the morning and return satiated in the evening.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Zuhd, chapter on reliance upon Allah, 4/154, hadith 2351)

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Masud رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ said: “Whoever is afflicted by hunger and describes his hunger before people, Allah Almighty does not remove his hunger. But whoever is afflicted by hunger and presents his complaint before Allah Almighty, Allah Almighty will soon make him independent, either by granting him an early passing from this world or by granting him provision after a delay.” (Abu Dawud, Book of Zakat, chapter on abstinence from asking, 2/170, hadith 1645)

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to rely upon Him as reliance upon Him truly requires. Amin.

## Ayat 65:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54066

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=4; ayatId=5285; tafseerId=54066; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اﻼ یَىٕسْنَ مِنَ الْمَحِیْضِ مِنْ  نِّسَآىٕكُمْ اِنِ ارْتَبْتُمْ فَعِدَّتُهُنَّ ثَلٰثَةُ اَشْهُرٍۙ- وَّ اﻼ لَمْ یَحِضْنَؕ -وَ اُولَاتُ الْاَحْمَالِ اَجَلُهُنَّ اَنْ یَّضَعْنَ حَمْلَهُنَّؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَّقِ اللّٰهَ یَجْعَلْ لَّهٗ مِنْ اَمْرِهٖ یُسْرًا(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And for those of your women who have no hope of menstruation, if you have any doubt (about the ruling of their waiting period), so their 'Iddah is three (lunar) months, and (three lunar months is) also for those who have not yet had menstruation; and the appointed period for the pregnant women is this that they deliver their burden (i.e. give birth). And whoever fears Allah, Allah will create ease for him in his matters.

{وَ اﻼ یَىٕسْنَ مِنَ الْمَحِیْضِ مِنْ نِّسَآىٕكُمْ: Those of your women who have no hope of menstruation.} The Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ asked the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ: “We know the waiting period of women who menstruate; what is the waiting period of those who do not menstruate?” This verse was revealed in response. It means: women whose menstruation has stopped because of old age, if there is any doubt concerning their ruling, know that their waiting period is three months. The same applies to those who have not yet menstruated because of their young age. The waiting period of pregnant women ends when they give birth. Whoever fears Allah Almighty, Allah Almighty creates ease for him in his affair. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 4, p. 1252)

### Four rulings concerning women who do not menstruate

1. When menstruation stops because of old age, this is the age of menopause, and the waiting period of a woman who reaches this age is three months.

2. Whether a girl is a minor or has reached the age of maturity but menstruation has not yet begun, the waiting period of both is three months.

3. The waiting period of pregnant women is the delivery of the child, whether the waiting period follows divorce or the husband's passing away.

4. No particular duration is fixed for the waiting period ending with childbirth. Whenever the child is born after death or divorce, the waiting period ends, even if this occurs one minute later. Likewise, if a miscarriage occurs after the child's limbs have formed, the waiting period ends; if it occurs before the child's limbs have formed, the waiting period does not end.

## Ayat 65:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54067

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=5; ayatId=5286; tafseerId=54067; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** ذٰلِكَ اَمْرُ اللّٰهِ اَنْزَلَهٗۤ اِلَیْكُمْؕ-وَ مَنْ یَّتَّقِ اللّٰهَ یُكَفِّرْ عَنْهُ سَیِّاٰتِهٖ وَ یُعْظِمْ لَهٗۤ اَجْرًا(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** This is Allah's command which He has sent down towards you; and whosoever fears Allah, Allah will erase his sins and bestow upon him great reward.

{ذٰلِكَ اَمْرُ اللّٰهِ اَنْزَلَهٗۤ اِلَیْكُمْ: This is Allah's command which He has sent down towards you.} The commands mentioned here are the command of Allah Almighty, which He has sent down to you. Whoever fears Allah Almighty, acts upon the commands He has revealed, and carefully fulfils the rights made obligatory upon him, Allah Almighty will erase his sins and increase his reward. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 5, p. 1252)

### Piety is a cause of religious and worldly blessings

This teaches that piety is a cause of religious and worldly blessings. It removes calamities, brings mercies in the world, and earns the favour of Allah Almighty in the Hereafter.

## Ayat 65:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54068

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=6; ayatId=5287; tafseerId=54068; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَسْكِنُوْهُنَّ مِنْ حَیْثُ سَكَنْتُمْ مِّنْ وُّجْدِكُمْ وَ لَا تُضَآرُّوْهُنَّ لِتُضَیِّقُوْا عَلَیْهِنَّؕ-وَ اِنْ كُنَّ اُولَاتِ حَمْلٍ فَاَنْفِقُوْا عَلَیْهِنَّ حَتّٰى یَضَعْنَ حَمْلَهُنَّۚ-فَاِنْ اَرْضَعْنَ لَكُمْ فَاٰتُوْهُنَّ اُجُوْرَهُنَّۚ-وَ اْتَمِرُوْا بَیْنَكُمْ بِمَعْرُوْفٍۚ-وَ اِنْ تَعَاسَرْتُمْ فَسَتُرْضِعُ لَهٗۤ اُخْرٰىﭤ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** House the women (in 'Iddah) where you reside, according to your means, and do not harass them to make it difficult for them; and if they are pregnant, so give them provision till their child is born; then if they suckle the child for you, so pay them its due compensation; and consult with each other in a reasonable manner; and if you mutually find it difficult, so it is likely that another (woman) may be found to suckle him.

{اَسْكِنُوْهُنَّ مِنْ حَیْثُ سَكَنْتُمْ مِّنْ وُّجْدِكُمْ: House the women where you reside, according to your means.} This verse explains the rulings concerning a woman's residence during her waiting period, her expenses, and breastfeeding the child if she gives birth. Its summary is that divorced women should be housed where you live, according to your ability. Do not harm them by surrounding their dwelling and making it narrow, or by lodging an unsuitable person with them. Do not trouble them in a way that forces them to leave the home. If the divorced women are pregnant, continue spending on them according to the Shariah until they give birth, because their waiting period ends only at childbirth. If they breastfeed the child for you, pay them their due compensation. Consult one another properly about the payment, ensuring that neither the man falls short of the woman's right nor the woman becomes harsh in this matter. If you cannot settle the matter, for example because the mother demands more than another woman would receive and the father does not wish to pay more, then another woman may be arranged to breastfeed the child. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verse 6, p. 1253; Khazin, At-Talaq, under verse 6, 4/280, excerpted)

### Two rulings concerning residence and maintenance during the waiting period

1. It is obligatory for the husband to provide a divorced woman with a dwelling suitable to his means until her waiting period ends. It is also obligatory to provide her expenses during the waiting period.

2. Just as maintenance is obligatory for a pregnant woman, it is obligatory for a non-pregnant woman as well, whether she received a revocable or an irrevocable divorce.

### Rulings concerning breastfeeding a child

1. Breastfeeding a child is not ordinarily obligatory upon the mother; it is the father's responsibility to arrange breastfeeding by paying compensation. However, if the child will not drink the milk of any woman other than the mother, or if the father is poor, breastfeeding becomes obligatory upon the mother. While the child's mother remains in the father's marriage or observes the waiting period of a revocable divorce, she may not take payment for breastfeeding; after the waiting period, she may take payment.

2. It is permissible to appoint a woman to breastfeed a child for a specified payment.

3. For breastfeeding for payment, the mother has greater right than another woman.

4. If the mother demands a higher payment, there is fundamentally no objection to appointing another woman.

{لِیُنْفِقْ ذُوْ سَعَةٍ مِّنْ سَعَتِهٖ: The one who has means should spend according to his means.} A person with financial means should spend according to his ability, while a person in difficulty should spend according to his condition on divorced women and women breastfeeding children. Allah Almighty places upon each person only the burden corresponding to the provision He has given him. A poor person should not fear spending; soon Allah Almighty will grant him ease after financial difficulty.

The severe account refers to the reckoning of the Hereafter. Since it will certainly occur, it is expressed in the past tense. The terrible punishment may refer to the punishment of Hell, or to worldly calamities such as famine and killing. The outcome of their deeds was loss: they were deprived of benefit and afflicted by punishment. (Ruh al-Bayan, At-Talaq, under verse 9, 10/40)

## Ayat 65:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54070

**Metadata:** surah=65; ayat=10; ayatId=5291; tafseerId=54070; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَعَدَّ اللّٰهُ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا شَدِیْدًاۙ-فَاتَّقُوا اللّٰهَ یٰۤاُولِی الْاَلْبَابِ ﲬ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْاۚۛ-قَدْ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ اِلَیْكُمْ ذِكْرًاۙ    (10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Allah has kept ready a severe punishment for them, therefore fear Allah, O men of intellect! Those who believed; indeed, Allah has sent down honour for you.

{اَعَدَّ اللّٰهُ لَهُمْ عَذَابًا شَدِیْدًا: Allah has prepared a severe punishment for them.} Along with worldly punishment, Allah Almighty has prepared a severe punishment for them in the Hereafter. Therefore, fear Allah, O people of understanding who have believed. Take a lesson from the condition of the former nations who denied the truth and from the punishment that descended upon them, and avoid violating the commands of Allah Almighty. (Ruh al-Bayan, At-Talaq, under verse 10, 10/40–41)

{قَدْ اَنْزَلَ اللّٰهُ اِلَیْكُمْ ذِكْرًا: Indeed, Allah has sent down a reminder to you.} The summary of this part and the following verse is that Allah Almighty has sent down a reminder to you, and the reminder refers to the Holy Quran. Another interpretation is that “reminder” refers to the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ. The word at the beginning of the next verse explains this reminder: Allah Almighty sent His Messenger, who recites to you Allah's clear verses containing explanations of the lawful and unlawful, so that he may bring those who believe and perform good deeds from the darknesses of disbelief and ignorance into the light of faith and knowledge. Whoever believes in Allah Almighty and performs good deeds, Allah Almighty will admit him into Gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will remain forever. Indeed, Allah Almighty has prepared excellent sustenance for him, whose blessings will remain forever and never cease. (Madarik, At-Talaq, under verses 10–11, p. 1254; Khazin, At-Talaq, under verses 10–11, 4/282, excerpted)

### Seven matters learned from Ayat 11 of Surah At-Talaq

1. Disbelief is darkness and Islam is light.

2. In this verse, Allah Almighty mentions the plural form “darknesses” for disbelief and the singular form “light” for Islam. This indicates that disbelief has thousands of forms, while Islam is one.

3. The Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ brings people from disbelief to faith, from ignorance to knowledge, and from sinfulness to piety.

4. Faith comes before action.

5. Salvation requires good deeds along with faith.

6. Allah Almighty may grant one believer several Gardens.

7. There is everlasting life in Paradise. Neither passing away occurs there, nor will anyone leave it.
