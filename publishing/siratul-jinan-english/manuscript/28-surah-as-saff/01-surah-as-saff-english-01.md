# Surah As-Saff: English Translation

## Ayat 61:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54017

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=1; ayatId=5224; tafseerId=54017; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** سَبَّحَ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِۚ-وَ هُوَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth glorifies Allah, and only He is the Most Honourable, the Wise.

{سَبَّحَ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِ: Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth glorifies Allah.} Allah Almighty states that all things present in the heavens and the earth declare Allah Almighty pure from everything that is not worthy of His exalted and magnificent court. He alone is the Most Honourable and Wise in all His actions. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, Surah As-Saff, under verse 1, 9/493)

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: O believers!} Occasion of revelation: Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Salam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: “A group of several Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُمْ was sitting together. We discussed which deed was most beloved to Allah Almighty. If only we knew it, we would act upon it.” Upon this, Allah Almighty revealed these verses: “سَبَّحَ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِۚ-وَ هُوَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ(۱)یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لِمَ تَقُوْلُوْنَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ”

**Kanz-ul-Irfan translation:** “Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth glorifies Allah, and only He is the Most Honourable, the Wise. O believers! Why do you say that which you do not practice?”

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Salam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ also states: “The Noble Prophet ﷺ recited these verses before us.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Tafsir, chapter concerning Surah As-Saff, 5/202, hadith 3320)

It should be remembered that there are several other statements concerning the occasion of revelation of this verse. One of them is that it was revealed concerning the hypocrites who falsely promised the Muslims that they would help them. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 2, 4/262) In this respect, it condemns the hypocrites, and addressing them as believers is because of their apparent faith. If the verse was revealed concerning the Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُمْ, then it is not a condemnation of them; rather, it is their instruction that making such claims is not correct, because no one knows what the future will bring. It is possible that, for some reason, they may then be unable to fulfil that claim.

### There should be no contradiction between word and deed

This verse teaches that there should be no contradiction between word and deed. One should act according to one's words. There are many forms of this contradiction, such as telling people good things while not acting upon them oneself, or promising someone to do something while already intending not to do it and making only a verbal promise. In other words, one says something but does not fulfil it. The ahadith particularly mention severe condemnation and warning concerning these matters.

Sayyiduna Usamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “On the Day of Judgement, a man will be brought and thrown into Hell. His intestines will spill out in Hell, and he will revolve around them just as a donkey revolves around a millstone. The people of Hell will gather around him and say: ‘O so-and-so! What happened to you? You used to command us to do good and forbid us from evil.’ He will say: ‘I used to command you to do good, but I did not do good myself, and I used to forbid you from evil, but I did evil myself.’” (Bukhari, Book of the Beginning of Creation, chapter on the description of Hell and that it has been created, 2/396, hadith 3267)

Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crown of Messengership ﷺ said: “On the night of the Ascension, I passed by people whose lips were being cut with scissors of fire. I asked, ‘O Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, who are these people?’ He replied, ‘These are the preachers of your Ummah who used to say things upon which they themselves did not act.’” (Mishkat-ul-Masabih, Book of Manners, chapter on speech and poetry, second section, 2/188, hadith 4801)

Concerning those who break promises, it is narrated from Sayyiduna Ali al-Murtada کَرَّمَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی وَجْہَہُ الْکَرِیْم that whoever breaks a covenant with a Muslim is cursed by Allah Almighty, the angels and all people, and neither his obligatory nor his voluntary deeds will be accepted. (Bukhari, Book of Holding Fast to the Quran and Sunnah, chapter on what is disliked of excessive disputation in knowledge, 4/505, hadith 7300)

Likewise, the verse can mean that one should not claim to do what one does not do. For example, a person does not help the poor but claims that he helps them greatly; this is merely a false claim. Or a person may claim that he will do something, while intending in his heart not to fulfil it. For example, he says that he will give such-and-such help to the poor of a particular place, while intending not to do so. Thus, he does not do what he says.

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to avoid contradiction between word and deed. Ameen.

## Ayat 61:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54018

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=4; ayatId=5227; tafseerId=54018; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ الَّذِیْنَ یُقَاتِلُوْنَ فِیْ سَبِیْلِهٖ صَفًّا كَاَنَّهُمْ بُنْیَانٌ مَّرْصُوْصٌ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Verily, Allah befriends those who fight in His cause (i.e. against the enemies of Islam) in rows as if they are a solidified structure.

{اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ الَّذِیْنَ: Verily, Allah befriends those people.} Allah Almighty states that He befriends those who fight in His path in rows as though they are a wall of molten lead: each one joined to the other, each firmly fixed in his place, and all of them like one body against the enemy. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 4, 4/262, abridged)

The purpose is that Allah Almighty likes brave fighters who stand firm against the disbelievers and do not turn their backs. Since rows were formed in jihad in that era, rows are mentioned here. In our time, jihad may be carried out by forming rows and by other methods. Every method that contains useful organisation and discipline, strengthens one another, and becomes a means of victory over others is included in this.

## Ayat 61:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54019

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=5; ayatId=5228; tafseerId=54019; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى لِقَوْمِهٖ یٰقَوْمِ لِمَ تُؤْذُوْنَنِیْ وَ قَدْ تَّعْلَمُوْنَ اَنِّیْ رَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ اِلَیْكُمْؕ-فَلَمَّا زَاغُوْۤا اَزَاغَ اللّٰهُ قُلُوْبَهُمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الْفٰسِقِیْنَ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And remember when Moosa said to his nation, 'O my people! Why do you trouble me, whereas you know (with certainty) that I am Allah's Messenger towards you?' So, when they deviated, Allah caused their hearts to deviate; and Allah does not guide the transgressing people.

{وَ اِذْ قَالَ مُوْسٰى لِقَوْمِهٖ: And remember when Moosa said to his nation.} O Beloved ﷺ, mention before your nation the event when Sayyiduna Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام said to his people: “O my people! Why do you trouble me by denying the verses and making false accusations against me, whereas you know with certainty that I am Allah Almighty's Messenger towards you?” The honouring of Messengers عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام is obligatory, their reverence and respect are necessary, and causing them harm is strictly unlawful and the greatest misfortune.

When they troubled Sayyiduna Moosa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and deviated and became crooked from the path of truth, Allah Almighty deprived them of the ability to follow the truth and caused their hearts to become crooked. Allah Almighty does not guide those whom He knows to be disobedient.

This verse warns that troubling the Messengers عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام is an extremely serious crime. Its consequence causes hearts to become crooked and a person to be deprived of guidance. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 5, 4/262; Madarik, Surah As-Saff, under verse 5, p. 1237, excerpted)

## Ayat 61:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54020

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=6; ayatId=5229; tafseerId=54020; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ قَالَ عِیْسَى ابْنُ مَرْیَمَ یٰبَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ اِنِّیْ رَسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ اِلَیْكُمْ مُّصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَیْنَ یَدَیَّ مِنَ التَّوْرٰىةِ وَ مُبَشِّرًۢا بِرَسُوْلٍ یَّاْتِیْ مِنْۢ بَعْدِی اسْمُهٗۤ اَحْمَدُؕ-فَلَمَّا جَآءَهُمْ بِالْبَیِّنٰتِ قَالُوْا هٰذَا سِحْرٌ مُّبِیْنٌ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And remember when 'Eisa; the son of Maryam, said, 'O Children of Israel! I am Allah's Messenger towards you, confirming the Book; the Tawrah which was before me, and giving glad tidings of a (great) Messenger who will come after me; his name is Ahmad.' Then, when Ahmad came to them with clear proofs, they (i.e. the disbelievers) said, 'This is clear magic.'

{وَ اِذْ قَالَ عِیْسَى ابْنُ مَرْیَمَ: And remember when 'Eisa, the son of Maryam, said.} Allah Almighty states: Remember when Sayyiduna 'Eisa ibn Maryam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام said: “O Children of Israel! I am Allah Almighty's Messenger sent towards you. I confirm the Tawrah, the books of Allah Almighty, and all the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام who came before me, and I give glad tidings of that great Messenger who will come after me; his name is Ahmad.” Allah Almighty states that when Ahmad came to the disbelievers with clear signs and miracles, they said: “This is clear magic.” (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 6, 4/262; Jalalayn, Surah As-Saff, under verse 6, p. 459, excerpted)

### The glad tidings given by Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام

Many ahadith and reports also mention the glad tidings given by Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. Three of them are recorded below.

1. Sayyiduna Abu Musa al-Ashari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that the Noble Messenger ﷺ ordered us to go to the kingdom of the Negus. When we went to him, the Negus said: “I testify that Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ is the Messenger of Allah Almighty, and he is the very Messenger concerning whom Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام gave glad tidings. If I were not bound by the affairs of the kingdom, I would come into his presence and carry his shoes.” (Abu Dawud, Book of Funerals, chapter on praying over a Muslim who passes away in the land of polytheism, 3/285, hadith 3205)

2. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Salam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: “The description of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ is mentioned in the Tawrah, and it is also mentioned that Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام will be buried near him.” Abu Mawud said that one place for a grave remains in the Sacred Chamber. (Tirmidhi, Book of Virtues, chapter concerning the virtues of the Prophet ﷺ, 5/355, hadith 3637)

3. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Ka'b al-Ahbar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the disciples asked Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام: “O Spirit of Allah! Will there be another nation after us?” He said: “Yes, the Ummah of Ahmad Mujtaba ﷺ. They will be people of wisdom, knowledge, righteousness and piety, deputies of the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام in jurisprudence, content with little provision from Allah Almighty, and Allah Almighty will be pleased with little deed from them.” (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 6, 4/262)

### Five matters related to this verse

1. Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was attributed to his mother, which shows that he was born without a father.

2. Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was a Prophet only for the Children of Israel, whereas our Noble Prophet ﷺ is the Messenger of the entire world.

3. The Beloved Prophet ﷺ is the Final Prophet, because Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام gave glad tidings only of him.

4. After Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, no Prophet came except the Radiant Prophet ﷺ.

5. The name of the Radiant Prophet ﷺ had already become famous before his arrival, because the Children of Israel had been explicitly informed of it.

## Ayat 61:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54021

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=7; ayatId=5230; tafseerId=54021; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ مَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَى اللّٰهِ الْكَذِبَ وَ هُوَ یُدْعٰۤى اِلَى الْاِسْلَامِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And who is more unjust than the one who fabricates lie against Allah whilst he is being called towards Islam? And Allah does not guide the unjust.

{وَ مَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَى اللّٰهِ الْكَذِبَ: And who is more unjust than the one who fabricates lie against Allah?} The meaning of this verse is: Who is more unjust than the one whose Lord عزوجل calls him, through the sacred tongue of His Beloved ﷺ, towards Islam, in which lies the prosperity of both worlds, but instead of accepting the invitation he calls the verses of Allah Almighty magic and fabricates lies against Him? Allah Almighty does not grant such unjust people the ability to be guided, because He knows through His eternal knowledge that they will remain disbelievers. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 7, 4/263)

## Ayat 61:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54022

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=8; ayatId=5231; tafseerId=54022; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یُرِیْدُوْنَ لِیُطْفِــٴُـوْا نُوْرَ اللّٰهِ بِاَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَ اللّٰهُ مُتِمُّ نُوْرِهٖ وَ لَوْ كَرِهَ الْكٰفِرُوْنَ(8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** They wish to extinguish the light of Allah (by blowing) with their mouths, and Allah is to complete His light, even if the disbelievers get annoyed.

{یُرِیْدُوْنَ لِیُطْفِــٴُـوْا نُوْرَ اللّٰهِ بِاَفْوَاهِهِمْ: They wish to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths.} Their intention is to call the Holy Quran magic and thereby invalidate Islam. However, they will never succeed in this intention, because Allah Almighty will make the religion of Islam prevail in every way, even if the disbelievers dislike it. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 8, 4/263)

This shows that the religion of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and his name will continue to shine, no matter how much enmity his enemies show. Even today this can be witnessed.

## Ayat 61:9

  (9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** It is He Who has sent His Messenger with guidance and the true religion that He may make it prevail over all other religions, even if the polytheists get annoyed.

{هُوَ الَّذِیْۤ اَرْسَلَ رَسُوْلَهٗ بِالْهُدٰى وَ دِیْنِ الْحَقِّ: It is He Who has sent His Messenger with guidance and the true religion.} It means that He is the very Allah Almighty Who sent His Messenger Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ with the means of guidance towards the straight path, namely the Quran and miracles, and with the true religion which Allah Almighty chose for His Messenger and his Ummah, so that He may make it prevail over all religions, even if the polytheists dislike this dominance.

By the grace of Allah Almighty, Islam became dominant and all other religions were overcome by Islam. It is reported from Imam Mujahid رَحْمَۃُاللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ that when Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام descends, there will be no religion on the earth except Islam. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, Surah As-Saff, under verse 9, 9/504; Madarik, Surah As-Saff, under verse 9, p. 1237, excerpted)

## Ayat 61:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54025

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=10; ayatId=5233; tafseerId=54025; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا هَلْ اَدُلُّكُمْ عَلٰى تِجَارَةٍ تُنْجِیْكُمْ مِّنْ عَذَابٍ اَلِیْمٍ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Shall I tell you of that trade which can save you from the painful punishment?

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: O believers!} The summary of this verse and the two verses after it is: O believers! Shall I tell you of that trade which can save you from the painful punishment? Listen: that trade is that you remain steadfast in believing in Allah Almighty and His Messenger ﷺ and strive in Allah Almighty's path with your wealth and your lives. If you knew your real benefit, remaining steadfast upon faith and striving would be better for you than your life, wealth and everything else. If you do this, Allah Almighty will forgive the sins you committed in the world and, on the Day of Judgement, admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow and into pure dwellings in the everlasting gardens. Receiving this reward is the great success. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, Surah As-Saff, under verses 10–11, 9/505–506; Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verses 10–12, 4/263, excerpted)

Note: It should be remembered that prayer comes after faith in rank. However, because jihad was urgently needed at that time, jihad is mentioned here after faith.

Here, believing in Allah Almighty and striving with one's life and wealth in His path is called a trade because, just as profit is hoped for through trade, the greatest profit, namely Allah Almighty's pleasure, Paradise and salvation, is attained through these deeds.

### Matters learned from verse 12 of Surah As-Saff

Two matters are learned from this verse:

1. All the sins of a fighter are forgiven, including the rights of people, because the Lord Almighty will satisfy the person whose right was violated by admitting him into Paradise and will have the right forgiven.

2. Becoming a wealthy or influential person in the world is not the great success. Rather, the great success is that a person performs good deeds in the world and becomes deserving of Paradise and its blessings.

## Ayat 61:12

## Ayat 61:13

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54024

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=13; ayatId=5236; tafseerId=54024; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اُخْرٰى تُحِبُّوْنَهَاؕ-نَصْرٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ وَ فَتْحٌ قَرِیْبٌؕ-وَ بَشِّرِ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ(13)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And He will give you another favour which is dear to you; the help from Allah and a forthcoming victory (of Makkah, Persia and Rome, etc.); and O Beloved, give glad tidings to the Muslims.

{وَ اُخْرٰى تُحِبُّوْنَهَا: And He will give you another favour which is dear to you.} O believers! In addition to the blessings of the Hereafter, forgiveness and reward, Allah Almighty will grant you another blessing in this world which you love. That blessing is Allah Almighty's help and a forthcoming victory. And, O Beloved ﷺ, give the Muslims glad tidings of victory in this world and Paradise in the Hereafter. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 13, 4/263; Madarik, Surah As-Saff, under verse 13, p. 1237, excerpted)

Note: The victory mentioned in this verse may mean the victory of Makkah, or the conquest of the cities of Persia and Rome. According to another statement, this verse points towards the caliphates of Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq and Sayyiduna Umar Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْهُمَا, because the cities of Persia and Rome were conquered during their eras. This shows that their caliphates were rightful and that their victories were greatly beloved to Allah Almighty, as they are given glad tidings here.

## Ayat 61:14

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54026

**Metadata:** surah=61; ayat=14; ayatId=5237; tafseerId=54026; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا كُوْنُوْۤا اَنْصَارَ اللّٰهِ كَمَا قَالَ عِیْسَى ابْنُ مَرْیَمَ لِلْحَوَارِیّٖنَ مَنْ اَنْصَارِیْۤ اِلَى اللّٰهِؕ-قَالَ الْحَوَارِیُّوْنَ نَحْنُ اَنْصَارُ اللّٰهِ فَاٰمَنَتْ طَّآىٕفَةٌ مِّنْۢ بَنِیْۤ اِسْرَآءِیْلَ وَ كَفَرَتْ طَّآىٕفَةٌۚ-فَاَیَّدْنَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا عَلٰى عَدُوِّهِمْ فَاَصْبَحُوْا ظٰهِرِیْنَ۠    (14)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Become the helpers of God's religion, just like 'Eisa; the son of Maryam, had said to the disciples, 'Who will help me whilst inclining towards Allah?' The disciples said, 'We are the helpers of God's religion.' So, a group among the Children of Israel believed and another group disbelieved. We henceforth helped the believers against their enemies, so they (i.e. the believers) were dominant (over the disbelievers).

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا كُوْنُوْۤا اَنْصَارَ اللّٰهِ: O believers! Become the helpers of Allah's religion.} This verse encourages Muslims to help the religion and strive against its opponents. Its summary is: O believers! Become the helpers of Allah Almighty's religion, just as the disciples of Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام helped Allah Almighty's religion when he said to them: “Who will help me whilst inclining towards Allah Almighty?” The disciples replied: “We are the helpers of Allah Almighty's religion.” Then one group among the Children of Israel believed in Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام and one group disbelieved. When the two groups fought, We helped the believers against their enemies, so the believers became dominant.

It has also been stated in the explanation of the final part of the verse that when Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was raised to the heavens, his nation divided into three sects. One sect said concerning Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام: “He was Allah and has gone to the heavens.” Another said: “He was the son of Allah Almighty, and He called him to Himself.” The third said: “He was the bondsman and Messenger of Allah Almighty; He raised him.” The people of this third sect were believers. They fought the other two sects, but the disbelieving groups remained dominant over them until the Leader of the Prophets, Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ, appeared. At that time, the believing group became dominant over the disbelievers.

According to this explanation, the final part of the verse means that by causing those who believed in Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام to affirm Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ, We helped them; through this blessing, they became dominant over the disbelievers. (Khazin, Surah As-Saff, under verse 14, 4/263–264; Jalalayn, Surah As-Saff, under verse 14, p. 459; Madarik, Surah As-Saff, under verse 14, p. 1238, excerpted)

### Matters learned from “O believers! Become the helpers of Allah's religion”

Three matters are learned from this verse:

1. Seeking help from the bondsmen of Allah Almighty in times of difficulty is the Sunnah of the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. It is not polytheism and does not contradict “اِیَّاكَ نَسْتَعِیْنُ”.

2. Christians are also called Nasara because their forefathers said to Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام: “نَحْنُ اَنْصَارُ اللّٰهِ”.

3. Helping the beloved servants of Allah Almighty is in reality helping Allah Almighty's religion, because the disciples helped Sayyiduna 'Eisa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, but they said: “We are the helpers of Allah Almighty.”
