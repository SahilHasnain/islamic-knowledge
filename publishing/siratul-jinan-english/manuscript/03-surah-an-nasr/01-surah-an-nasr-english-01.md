# Surah An-Nasr: English Translation

## Ayat 110:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54494
**Metadata:** surah=110; ayat=1; ayatId=6323; tafseerId=54494; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِذَا جَآءَ نَصْرُ اللّٰهِ وَ الْفَتْحُۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** When help and victory of Allah comes.

{اِذَا جَآءَ نَصْرُ اللّٰهِ وَ الْفَتْحُ: When help and victory of Allah comes.} The summary of this Surah is that when the help and victory of Allah Almighty comes to you, O Beloved صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, against your enemies, and you see that previously people were entering Islam one by one or two by two, while now they are entering the religion of Allah Almighty in hosts, then at that time glorify your Lord عَزَّوَجَلَّ while praising Him, and seek forgiveness from Him for your Ummah. Indeed, He is the One Who accepts repentance abundantly. It should be remembered that the victory mentioned in this verse may refer to the general victories of Islam, or it may refer specifically to the Conquest of Makkah. (Khazin, An-Nasr, under verses 1–3, 4/423–424; Madarik, An-Nasr, under verses 1–3, p. 1380, excerpted.)

### Information derived from verse 2 of Surah An-Nasr

Six matters are learned from this verse:

1. The number of the noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ was not five or seven; rather, it was in the thousands.

2. The faith of those who embraced Islam at the time of the Conquest of Makkah and after the Conquest of Makkah was accepted. Sayyiduna Abu Sufyan, Sayyiduna Amir Muawiyah, and Sayyiduna Wahshi رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ are all included among them.

3. These people remained steadfast upon the religion afterwards as well, because their entering the religion is established by this verse, whereas their leaving the religion is not established by any proof. Moreover, if these people were destined to apostatise, Allah Almighty would not have described their faith in this splendid manner.

4. This verse contains news of the unseen. This news was fulfilled at the time of the Conquest of Makkah, when people came from different places, group after group, longing for the servitude of the Crown of Messengership صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, and continued to be honoured with the distinction of Islam.

5. The Radiant Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ knew about the duration of his blessed life, that it would not end before he witnessed the Conquest of Makkah and these events.

6. In the era of the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, the greatest good fortune was to be present in the service of the Sacred Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ and embrace faith.

{فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَ اسْتَغْفِرْهُ: Thus, glorify your Lord whilst praising Him, and seek forgiveness from Him.} After the revelation of this Surah, the Crown of Messengership صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ frequently recited: “سُبْحَانَ اللّٰہِ وَبِحَمْدِہٖ اَسْتَغفِرُ اللّٰہَ وَاَتُوْبُ اِلَیْہِ”.

It is narrated from Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا that this Surah was revealed at Mina during the Farewell Pilgrimage. After this, the verse “اَلْیَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِیْنَكُمْ” was revealed. After its revelation, the Radiant Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ remained in this world for 80 days. Then the verse “الْكَلٰلَةِؕ” was revealed, after which the Sacred Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ remained in this world for 50 days. Then the verse “وَ اتَّقُوْا یَوْمًا تُرْجَعُوْنَ فِیْهِ اِلَى اللّٰهِ” was revealed, after which the Noble Presence صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ remained in this world for 21 days or 7 days. (Jalalayn ma'a Jaml, An-Nasr, under verse 3, 8/426.)

After the revelation of this Surah, the noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ understood that the religion had become complete and perfect, and that the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ would therefore not remain in this world for much longer. Thus, Sayyiduna Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ wept upon hearing this Surah because of this thought. It is narrated that after the revelation of this Surah, the Sovereign of Both Worlds صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ said in a sermon: “Allah Almighty gave a bondsman the choice of whether he wished to remain in this world or accept the meeting with Him, and that bondsman chose the meeting with Allah Almighty.” Upon hearing this, Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ submitted: “O Messenger of Allah صَلَّی اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہ وَ سَلَّمَ, may our lives, our wealth, our fathers, and our children all be sacrificed for you.” (Tafsir Kabir, An-Nasr, under verse 3, 11/346; Ruh al-Bayan, An-Nasr, under verse 3, 10/531; Madarik, An-Nasr, under verse 3, p. 1380, excerpted.)

## Ayat 110:2

## Ayat 110:3
