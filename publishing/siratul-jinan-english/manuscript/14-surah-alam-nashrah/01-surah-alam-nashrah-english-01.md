# Surah Alam Nashrah: English Translation

## Ayat 94:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54435

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=1; ayatId=6184; tafseerId=54435; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَۙ    (1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Did We not expand your chest for you (O Beloved)?

{اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ: Did We not expand your chest for you (O Beloved)?} The occasion of revelation of this Surah is that one day the Master of the universe ﷺ presented his plea in the Divine court: “O Allah Almighty! You granted Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام the honour of being Your intimate friend, honoured Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام by speaking with him, granted Sayyiduna Idris عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام access to a lofty place up to Paradise, made the mountains and iron obedient to Sayyiduna Dawud عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, and made jinn, humans, and all animals subject to Sayyiduna Sulayman عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. With which honour and miracle have You distinguished me?” Thereupon this Surah was revealed. It was as though it declared: “O Beloved ﷺ! If We made Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام Our intimate friend, then for your sake We opened your chest with the light of knowledge, wisdom, and gnosis, so that the delight of supplication, concern for the Ummah, eagerness to attend Our court, and longing for the abode of the Hereafter might enter your heart; bearing the heavenly revelation might become easy for your heart; and you might endure the hardships that come in conveying the invitation towards Allah Almighty. Through these qualities and miracles, you attained such a rank that the honour of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام being Our intimate friend has no comparison with it. If We granted Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام the honour of speaking with Us and granted Sayyiduna Idris عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام access to a lofty place, then We freed you from the grief that had weighed heavily upon your back and called you to the realm beyond place, honouring you with Our vision, until the distance between Us and you became equal to two bows, or even less, in a manner befitting Us, and your nearness and rank became renowned throughout the entire dominion of the heavens. If We gave Sayyiduna Dawud and Sayyiduna Sulayman عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام authority over some things of the world, then We gave you power over the upper world, meaning the heavens: their angels attend your court like servants and fight your enemies like your soldiers, and there is nothing in the heavens that is unaware of your prophethood and messengership or that disobeys your command.” (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Inshirah, under verse 1, 10/465; Al-Kalam al-Awdah fi Tafsir Al-Inshirah, p. 14, excerpted.)

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ describes the rank of Allah Almighty’s Beloved ﷺ in these lines:

The origin of all, the leader of all,
The master of all, the commander of all;

The rule of all, the sainthood of all,
For you, before God:

Angels as servants, the Messenger’s retinue,
All nations as servants, in gracious submission;

The existent and nonexistent, the originated and eternal,
In the world, manifest for you.

What is this mountain, and what are the heavens?
Even the Highest Throne remained far behind;

You received union beyond every direction:
Such is the elevation of your rank.

Mufti Na'im al-Din Muradabadi رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ writes in the commentary of this verse: “That is, O Beloved ﷺ! We expanded and enlarged your sacred chest for guidance, gnosis, admonition, prophethood, and knowledge and wisdom, to such an extent that the unseen world and the witnessed world were contained within its breadth, bodily relationships could not obstruct spiritual lights, and divinely bestowed sciences, Divine commands, Lordly gnoses, and Merciful realities became manifest in your pure chest. Some exegetes say that the outward opening of the blessed chest is intended by this verse. It is mentioned in hadith that the outward opening of the Noble Prophet’s ﷺ blessed chest occurred several times: at the beginning of his blessed life, at the beginning of the descent of revelation, and on the Night of Ascension. Its manner was that Sayyiduna Jibril Amin عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام split open the pure chest, took out the blessed heart, washed it with Zamzam water in a golden basin, filled it with light and wisdom, and placed it back in its position.” (Khazain al-Irfan, Al-Inshirah, under verse 1, p. 1110; Khazin, Al-Inshirah, under verse 1, 4/388; Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Inshirah, under verse 1, 10/461–462, excerpted.)

### Matters Learned from “اَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ”

Two matters are learned from this verse:

1. The opening of the chest through knowledge of the worthlessness of the world and the perfection of the Hereafter is a tremendous favour of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty says elsewhere:

“فَمَنْ یُّرِدِ اللّٰهُ اَنْ یَّهْدِیَهٗ یَشْرَحْ صَدْرَهٗ لِلْاِسْلَامِۚ-وَ مَنْ یُّرِدْ اَنْ یُّضِلَّهٗ یَجْعَلْ صَدْرَهٗ ضَیِّقًا حَرَجًا كَاَنَّمَا یَصَّعَّدُ فِی السَّمَآءِ” (Al-An'am: 125)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And whoever Allah wants to guide, He opens his chest for Islam; and whoever He wants to send astray, He makes his chest tight, extremely tight, as though he is climbing into the sky by force.”

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Masud رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that when the Crown of Messengership ﷺ recited this verse, the noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ asked what was meant by this opening. He ﷺ said: “It means the light placed into the believer’s heart, through which his heart opens.” He ﷺ was asked whether there was a sign by which it could be recognised. He ﷺ replied: “Yes. There are three signs: (1) eagerness for the Hereafter, (2) aversion to the world, and (3) preparing for the Hereafter before death.” (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah, Book of Asceticism, what is mentioned from our Prophet ﷺ concerning asceticism, 8/126, hadith 14.)

2. The Esteemed Presence ﷺ is such a Beloved of Allah Almighty that Allah Almighty opened his sacred chest for guidance and gnosis without his asking and granted him this favour.

## Ayat 94:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54436

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=2; ayatId=6185; tafseerId=54436; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ وَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَۙ    (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And (We) removed that burden from you (O Beloved).

{وَ وَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَ: And (We) removed that burden from you (O Beloved).} Regarding what is meant by this burden, one statement of the exegetes is that it refers to the grief which the Illuminated Presence ﷺ felt because the disbelievers did not accept faith. A second statement is that it refers to the grief over the sins of the Ummah, with which his blessed heart remained occupied. The meaning is: “O Beloved ﷺ! We made you one whose intercession is accepted and thereby removed the burden of grief that had broken your back.” (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Inshirah, under verses 2–3, 11/207–208; Khazin, Al-Inshirah, under verses 2–3, 4/388–389; Khazain al-Irfan, Al-Inshirah, under verse 3, p. 1110, excerpted.)

### The Esteemed Presence’s ﷺ Concern for the Ummah

The verse below gives an indication of how much grief the Noble Messenger ﷺ felt because the disbelievers did not accept faith. Allah Almighty said to His Beloved ﷺ:

“فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ عَلٰۤى اٰثَارِهِمْ اِنْ لَّمْ یُؤْمِنُوْا بِهٰذَا الْحَدِیثِ اَسَفًا” (Al-Kahf: 6)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “If they do not believe in this word, then perhaps you will exhaust yourself in grief, following after them.”

His condition regarding the Ummah can be understood from this verse:

“لَقَدْ جَآءَكُمْ رَسُوْلٌ مِّنْ اَنْفُسِكُمْ عَزِیْزٌ عَلَیْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ حَرِیْصٌ عَلَیْكُمْ بِالْمُؤْمِنِیْنَ رَءُوْفٌ رَّحِیْمٌ” (At-Tawbah: 128)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Indeed, there has come to you a Great Messenger from among you, upon whom your falling into hardship is very heavy; he is extremely desirous of your well-being, and most compassionate and merciful towards Muslims.”

This can also be understood from a hadith. Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا states that the Noble Messenger ﷺ recited from the Holy Quran the saying of Sayyiduna Ibrahim عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام:

“رَبِّ اِنَّهُنَّ اَضْلَلْنَ كَثِیْرًا مِّنَ النَّاسِۚ فَمَنْ تَبِعَنِیْ فَاِنَّهٗ مِنِّیۚ-وَ مَنْ عَصَانِیْ فَاِنَّكَ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ” (Ibrahim: 36)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O my Lord! Indeed, the idols have led many people astray; so whoever follows me is surely of me, and whoever disobeys me, then surely You are Most Forgiving, Most Merciful.”

He ﷺ also recited the verse containing the saying of Sayyiduna Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام:

“اِنْ تُعَذِّبْهُمْ فَاِنَّهُمْ عِبَادُكَۚ-وَ اِنْ تَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ فَاِنَّكَ اَنْتَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ” (Al-Ma'idah: 118)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “If You punish them, then they are Your servants; and if You forgive them, then surely You alone are the Dominant, the Wise.”

The Noble Prophet ﷺ began to weep. Raising his blessed hands, he supplicated: “O Allah Almighty! My Ummah, my Ummah.” Allah Almighty said to Sayyiduna Jibril: “O Jibril! Go to My Beloved ﷺ. Your Lord knows best, but ask him what is making him weep.” Sayyiduna Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام came to the court of the Noble Prophet ﷺ and asked him. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ informed him of his plea. Allah Almighty then said to Sayyiduna Jibril: “Go to My Beloved ﷺ and tell him: ‘We shall certainly please you concerning your Ummah and shall not grieve you.’” (Muslim, Book of Faith, chapter on the Prophet’s ﷺ supplication for his Ummah, etc., p. 130, hadith 346 (202).)

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ states: “O dear brother! Have you ever heard that one who has sincere love for you becomes displeased on hearing your good condition, remains concerned with erasing it, and then what a Beloved he is: the life of faith and mine of excellence, whose world-adorning beauty has no equal anywhere, and upon whose image the pen of destiny drew its final stroke, never to write such a one again? What a Beloved he is, whom his Master sent as mercy for all the worlds; what a Beloved he is, who bore upon his person the burden of an entire world; what a Beloved he is, who abandoned the food of the day and sleep of the night because of your grief, while you remain immersed day and night in disobedience and occupied in amusement and play, and he remains weeping and sorrowful night and day for your forgiveness.”

“The night, which Allah جل جلاله made for comfort, has spread its wings of tranquillity. Morning is near, cool breezes are fanning the air, everyone’s work inclines towards rest, kings sleep in blissful comfort in warm beds and soft pillows, and even the needy and destitute have stretched their feet beneath a two-yard blanket. At such a pleasant time and in such cool weather, that innocent, sinless, pure and protected one leaves his comfort, turns away from sleep and rest, and places his humble forehead at the threshold of honour, saying: ‘O Lord! My Ummah is sinful; pardon them and save all their bodies from the Fire of Hell.’”

“When that source of comfort and mine of mercy came into existence, he prostrated in the Divine court and said, ‘My Lord, grant me my Ummah.’ When he was placed in the noble grave, his life-giving lips were moving; some Companions listened and heard him softly saying, ‘My Ummah, my Ummah.’ On the Day of Judgement, a day of astonishing severity, with a copper earth, bare feet, tongues parched with thirst, the sun above heads and no sign of shade, the anxiety of reckoning and the encounter with the Sovereign, the entire world will be occupied with its own concern, and helpless criminals will be caught in the snare of calamity. Wherever they go, they will hear no answer except ‘Myself, myself; go to someone else.’ At that time this Beloved, the comforter of the sorrowful, will be of help. The lock of intercession will open through the strength of his blessed arm. They will remove the turban from his sacred head, and he will fall in prostration and say, ‘O my Lord, my Ummah.’” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/716–717.)

Thus, how great an injustice it is to try to erase the true excellences of such a Beloved, grief-bearing and grief-comforting Master, day and night deny his attributes, turn away from obeying him, and remain intent upon disobeying him.

## Ayat 94:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54437

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=4; ayatId=6187; tafseerId=54437; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَﭤ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And We have elevated your remembrance for your sake.

{وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ: And We have elevated your remembrance for your sake.} The exegetes have given various explanations of the elevation of the remembrance of the Leader of the Messengers ﷺ.

1. The elevation of the remembrance of the Illuminated Presence ﷺ means that Allah Almighty made belief in His Beloved ﷺ and obedience to him obligatory upon creation. A person’s belief in Allah Almighty, acknowledgement of His Oneness, and worship of Him are not accepted until he believes in the Crown of Messengership ﷺ and begins to obey him. Allah Almighty says:

“مَنْ یُّطِعِ الرَّسُوْلَ فَقَدْ اَطَاعَ اللّٰهَ” (An-Nisa: 80)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Whoever obeys the Messenger has indeed obeyed Allah.”

Allah Almighty also says: “فَلَا وَ رَبِّكَ لَا یُؤْمِنُوْنَ حَتّٰى یُحَكِّمُوْكَ فِیْمَا شَجَرَ بَیْنَهُمْ ثُمَّ لَا یَجِدُوْا فِیْۤ اَنْفُسِهِمْ حَرَجًا مِّمَّا قَضَیْتَ وَ یُسَلِّمُوْا تَسْلِیْمًا” (An-Nisa: 65)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “So, by your Lord, they will not be Muslims until they make you judge in their disputes, then find no discomfort in their hearts concerning whatever you decide and accept it wholeheartedly.”

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Abbas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا states that if someone worships Allah Almighty, affirms Him in every matter, but does not testify to the messengership of the Master of the universe ﷺ, all of this is futile and he remains a disbeliever.

2. His remembrance is elevated because his mention is made together with the remembrance of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty has mentioned him with His own remembrance in the adhan, iqamah, Salah, tashahhud, sermon, and many other places.

Sayyiduna Abu Sa'id al-Khudri رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of both worlds ﷺ asked Sayyiduna Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام about this verse. He replied: “Allah Almighty says that your remembrance is elevated in that whenever I am mentioned, you are mentioned with Me.” Sayyiduna Qatadah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that Allah Almighty elevated his remembrance in this world and the Hereafter. Every preacher and everyone reciting tashahhud says “اَشْھَدُ اَنْ لَّا ٓاِلٰـہَ اِلَّا اللّٰہ” together with “اَشْھَدُ اَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَّسُوْلُ اللّٰہ”.

3. His remembrance is elevated because Allah Almighty related his name to His own name and mentioned him with the attributes of prophethood and messengership, whereas the other noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام were mentioned by their names.

4. The remembrance of the Messenger of both worlds ﷺ is elevated because Allah Almighty took a covenant from the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام that they would believe in him. (Ta'wilat Ahl al-Sunnah, Al-Inshirah, under verse 4, 5/482; Tafsir Baghawi, Al-Inshirah, under verse 4, 4/469, excerpted.)

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan رَحْمَۃُ اللّٰہِ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says concerning this verse: “It is declared: O our Beloved! We elevated your remembrance for you, so wherever We are remembered, your fame will also be proclaimed, and faith will never be complete without your remembrance. The layers of the heavens and the veils of the earth will resound with your honoured name. Muezzins will remember you with our remembrance in their adhans, preachers in their sermons, those who remember Allah in their gatherings, and exhorters upon their pulpits.

“Trees and stones, deer and monitor lizards, other living creatures, suckling children, and the idols worshipped by disbelievers will recite your proclamation of messengership with eloquent speech and sound expression, just as they will declare Our Oneness. Throughout the four corners of the world there will be a clamour of ‘لَآ اِلٰـہَ اِلَّا اللّٰہُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَّسُوْلُ اللّٰہ’. Every particle, except the wretched ones of eternity, will recite the testimony of faith. I will occupy the inhabitants of the Highest Assembly with glorifying and sanctifying Me, and I will command your praiseworthy and blessed salutations. Wherever I write ‘Allah’, upon the Throne, the Chair, the seven leaves of Sidrah, and the palaces of Paradise, I will also write ‘مُحَمَّدٌ رَّسُوْلُ اللّٰہ’. I will command My Prophets and the Messengers of firm resolve to fill their breaths with you at all times, to bring light to their eyes, coolness to their hearts, tranquillity to their hearts, and adornment to their gatherings through your remembrance. In every Book I send down, I will describe your praise, your beauty, and the perfection of your character with such explanation that the hearts of those who hear will incline towards you involuntarily, and the unseen candle of your love will blaze in their ears and breasts. If an entire world becomes your enemy and occupies itself with diminishing your rank and erasing your virtues, I am the All-Powerful; what power can anyone have against Me? The effect of this very promise was that the Jews had for hundreds of years removed your mention from their books and thrown dust upon the moon, while the people of faith recited your praise in such a loud voice that a fair-minded listener would call out spontaneously. Millions of irreligious people girded themselves to erase your virtues, but those who sought to erase them were themselves erased, while your excellence continued to advance day by day.” (Fatawa Razawiyyah, 30/718–719.)

The elevation of your remembrance is your portion;
In both worlds, your fame is proclaimed.

After praising God, the birds of Paradise
Praise and glorify you alone.

He also says:

“وَ رَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ” casts its shade upon you;
Your remembrance has the highest renown, it is lofty.

Your enemies have vanished, are vanishing,
And shall vanish;

Neither has your fame ever diminished,
Nor will it ever diminish.

No one’s reducing you has reduced you,
Nor will you ever be reduced;

When Allah Almighty elevates you,
Who can lower you?

## Ayat 94:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54438

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=5; ayatId=6188; tafseerId=54438; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًاۙ    (5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So indeed, with hardship there is ease.

{فَاِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًا: So indeed, with hardship there is ease.} That is, O Beloved ﷺ! Along with the severity and hardship you are enduring in confronting the disbelievers, there is ease: We shall grant you victory over them.

Some exegetes state that the polytheists used to taunt the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and the noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ because of poverty. This continued until the Noble Prophet ﷺ thought that the Muslims’ poverty was preventing these disbelievers from accepting Islam. Allah Almighty comforted His Beloved ﷺ, saying: “O Beloved ﷺ! Do not grieve over the words of these disbelievers. Soon this hardship of poverty will end.” (Madarik, Al-Inshirah, under verse 6, p. 1358; Khazin, Al-Inshirah, under verse 6, 4/389, excerpted.)

### One Should Not Become Frightened by Difficulties

This verse teaches that one should not become frightened when a difficulty, calamity, or hardship arrives. Rather, one should supplicate to Allah Almighty while hoping that the difficulty and calamity will be removed and the hardship will become ease. If Allah Almighty wills, ease will come very soon. Allah Almighty says:

“لَا یُكَلِّفُ اللّٰهُ نَفْسًا اِلَّا مَاۤ اٰتٰىهَاؕ-سَیَجْعَلُ اللّٰهُ بَعْدَ عُسْرٍ یُّسْرًا” (At-Talaq: 7)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Allah does not burden any soul beyond what He has given it. Soon Allah will bring ease after hardship.”

## Ayat 94:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54439

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=6; ayatId=6189; tafseerId=54439; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًاﭤ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, with hardship there is ease.

{اِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ یُسْرًا: Indeed, with hardship there is ease.} Repeating this verse shows that one hardship is accompanied by two facilities and two eases. Sayyiduna Hasan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that one day the Noble Prophet ﷺ came out of his blessed residence smiling with happiness and joy and said: “One hardship will never overcome two eases. So indeed, with hardship there is ease; indeed, with hardship there is ease.” (Al-Mustadrak, Book of Tafsir, commentary of Surah Al-Inshirah, 3/380, hadith 4004.)

## Ayat 94:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54440

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=7; ayatId=6190; tafseerId=54440; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْۙ    (7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** So, when you complete Salah, strive in supplication.

{فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْ: So, when you complete Salah, strive in supplication.} One commentary of this verse is: “O Beloved ﷺ! When you complete Salah, strive in supplication for the Hereafter, because supplication after Salah is accepted.” There is disagreement concerning the supplication mentioned here. Some exegetes say it refers to the supplication made within Salah at its end, while others say it refers to the supplication made after saying salam. A second commentary is: “O Beloved ﷺ! When you complete inviting creation towards religion, occupy yourself with worshipping your Lord Almighty.” (Madarik, Al-Inshirah, under verse 7, p. 1359, abridged.)

### Matters Learned from “فَاِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَانْصَبْ”

Three matters are learned from this verse:

1. One should especially supplicate to Allah Almighty after Salah, because Allah Almighty accepts supplications made after Salah.

2. A servant should not remain idle, nor should he occupy himself with an action that has no religious or worldly benefit. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Among the excellences of a person’s Islam is that he leaves what does not benefit him.” (Tirmidhi, Book of Asceticism, chapter 11, 4/142, hadith 2324.)

3. Preachers, exhorters, and propagators who address the public in gatherings and meetings until late at night should also strive to worship Allah Almighty after completing this work. Sadly, in the present age there are many such people who remain extremely energetic in delivering their speeches and lectures in gatherings until midnight or even later; their speeches display enthusiasm and fervour, and they recite with tears the verses and hadith concerning the punishments for leaving the congregation, missing Salah, or not offering it at all. Yet after completing this, their own laziness regarding Salah is such that they offer Fajr without congregation or make it up later, and they are extremely lazy in offering the other Salah as well. Such people should read these verses and hadith and reflect upon their practical condition and its consequence. Allah Almighty says:

“یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لِمَ تَقُوْلُوْنَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ(۲)كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ اَنْ تَقُوْلُوْا مَا لَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ” (As-Saff: 2–3)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “O believers! Why do you say what you do not do? It is extremely displeasing to Allah that you say what you do not do.”

Allah Almighty also says:

“اَتَاْمُرُوْنَ النَّاسَ بِالْبِرِّ وَ تَنْسَوْنَ اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ اَنْتُمْ تَتْلُوْنَ الْكِتٰبَؕ-اَفَلَا تَعْقِلُوْنَ” (Al-Baqarah: 44)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “Do you command people to goodness and forget yourselves, although you read the Book? So do you not possess understanding?”

Sayyiduna Usamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “On the Day of Judgement, a man will be brought and thrown into Hell. His intestines will spill out into Hell, and he will revolve around them as a donkey revolves around a mill. The people of Hell will gather around him and say, ‘O so-and-so! What is the matter? You used to invite us to goodness and prevent us from evil, yet you are suffering punishment here!’ He will say, ‘I used to invite you to goodness but did not do good myself, and I used to prevent you from evil but committed evil myself.’” (Bukhari, Book of the Beginning of Creation, chapter on the description of Hell and that it has been created, 2/396, hadith 3267.)

Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Esteemed Presence ﷺ said: “On the Night of Ascension, I passed by people whose lips were being cut with scissors of fire. Whenever they were cut, they joined again, and then they were cut again. I asked, ‘O Jibril عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام! Who are these people?’ He replied: ‘These are the preachers of your Ummah who used to invite people to goodness but forgot their own selves, although they recited the Quran. Did they not possess understanding?’” (Musnad Abu Ya'la, Musnad Anas ibn Malik, what was narrated by Ali ibn Zayd from Anas, 3/370, hadith 3979.)

## Ayat 94:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54441

**Metadata:** surah=94; ayat=8; ayatId=6191; tafseerId=54441; para=30; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ۠    (8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And incline towards only your Lord.

{وَ اِلٰى رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ: And incline towards only your Lord.} That is, O Beloved ﷺ! Direct your eagerness especially towards your Lord Almighty, remain a seeker of His favour, and rely upon Him. (Madarik, Al-Inshirah, under verse 8, p. 1359.)

### Encouragement to Rely Upon Allah Almighty and Seek His Favour

This verse teaches that every Muslim should rely upon Allah Almighty and ask Him for His favour. Allah Almighty says elsewhere:

“وَ عَلَى اللّٰهِ فَلْیَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ” (Ibrahim: 11)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And Muslims should rely only upon Allah.”

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Masud رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “Ask Allah Almighty for His favour, for Allah Almighty likes being asked.” (Tirmidhi, Miscellaneous Hadith, chapter on awaiting relief and other matters, 5/333, hadith 3582.)
