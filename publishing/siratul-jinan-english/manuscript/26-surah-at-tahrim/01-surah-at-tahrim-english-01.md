# Surah At-Tahrim: English Translation

## Ayat 66:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54072

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=1; ayatId=5295; tafseerId=54072; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّبِیُّ لِمَ تُحَرِّمُ مَاۤ اَحَلَّ اللّٰهُ لَكَۚ-تَبْتَغِیْ مَرْضَاتَ اَزْوَاجِكَؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ غَفُوْرٌ رَّحِیْمٌ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O Conveyor of the unseen news (i.e. Prophet), why are you making unlawful for yourself that thing which Allah has made lawful for you; you wish to please (some of your) wives. And Allah is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا النَّبِیُّ: O Prophet!} Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا states that the Noble Prophet ﷺ used to drink honey at the home of the Mother of the Believers, Sayyidatuna Zaynab bint Jahsh رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, and would remain there somewhat longer. Sayyidatuna Hafsah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا and I agreed that whichever of us he ﷺ visited should say to him: “Have you eaten maghafir? I smell the odour of maghafir from your blessed mouth.” When this was said and the Noble Prophet ﷺ understood their intention, he ﷺ said: “No, but I drank honey at Zaynab bint Jahsh’s home. I shall never drink it again,” and he ﷺ swore this oath. He ﷺ also said, “Do not tell anyone about this.” (Bukhari, Book of Tafsir, Surah At-Tahrim, chapter “O Prophet, why do you make unlawful what Allah has made lawful for you?”, 3/359, hadith 4912.) This noble verse was revealed concerning this incident.

At the end of the verse, Allah Almighty states that He is Forgiving and Merciful. He forgave the shortcoming of these two blessed wives and prescribed expiation for the oath, thereby making the matter easy for the entire Ummah.

Two points are learned from this verse:

1. When a person swears an oath, the thing concerning which he swore becomes unlawful for him in the sense that, if he uses it, expiation becomes necessary.
2. The Noble Prophet ﷺ declared honey unlawful for himself only to please his purified wives, not because of ignorance. The odour of something in one’s own mouth is not unseen; it is perceived. Therefore, misguided people cannot use this verse as evidence of ignorance on the part of the Radiant Prophet ﷺ.

## Ayat 66:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54073

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=2; ayatId=5296; tafseerId=54073; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** قَدْ فَرَضَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ تَحِلَّةَ اَیْمَانِكُمْۚ-وَ اللّٰهُ مَوْلٰىكُمْۚ-وَ هُوَ الْعَلِیْمُ الْحَكِیْمُ(2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Indeed, Allah has ordained expiation for you for your oaths; and Allah is your Master; and Allah is the All-Knowing, and the Wise.

{قَدْ فَرَضَ اللّٰهُ لَكُمْ تَحِلَّةَ اَیْمَانِكُمْ: Indeed, Allah has ordained expiation for you for your oaths.} “Undoing the oath” means that Allah Almighty has prescribed expiation for it. Thus, the Noble Prophet ﷺ could resume serving Sayyidatuna Mariyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا or drink honey. According to some exegetes, undoing the oath means saying “if Allah wills” after making an oath, so that doing the contrary does not constitute breaking it.

As to whether the Noble Prophet ﷺ offered expiation, it is narrated from Muqatil that the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ freed a slave as expiation for declaring Sayyidatuna Mariyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا unlawful for himself. It is narrated from Sayyiduna Hasan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ that the Radiant Prophet ﷺ did not offer expiation because he ﷺ is forgiven, while the command of expiation was given to teach the Ummah. (Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 2, pp. 1256–1257.)

Two matters are learned from this verse:

1. Declaring a lawful thing unlawful for oneself is also a type of oath. The reverse, declaring an unlawful thing lawful for oneself, is not an oath. For example, saying, “If I do this, my wife is unlawful for me,” is an oath, whereas saying, “If I do such-and-such, I will eat pork,” is not an oath.
2. Expiation for an oath exists only in this religion. It did not exist in earlier laws. Therefore, Allah Almighty did not command Sayyiduna Ayyub عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام to offer expiation; rather, He showed him a way to fulfil his oath by striking his wife with a broom.

{وَ اللّٰهُ مَوْلٰىكُمْ: And Allah is your Master.} This means: O my Beloved and his household, Allah Almighty is your Helper. Therefore, He Himself manages your household and teaches you its manners. He knows your interests and is Wise in His acts and commands. He commands and forbids you only according to your strength. (Nur-ul-Irfan, At-Tahrim, under verse 2, p. 894; Ruh-ul-Bayan, At-Tahrim, under verse 2, 10/50, excerpted.)

## Ayat 66:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54074

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=3; ayatId=5297; tafseerId=54074; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** وَ اِذْ اَسَرَّ النَّبِیُّ اِلٰى بَعْضِ اَزْوَاجِهٖ حَدِیْثًاۚ-فَلَمَّا نَبَّاَتْ بِهٖ وَ اَظْهَرَهُ اللّٰهُ عَلَیْهِ عَرَّفَ بَعْضَهٗ وَ اَعْرَضَ عَنْۢ بَعْضٍۚ-فَلَمَّا نَبَّاَهَا بِهٖ قَالَتْ مَنْ اَنْۢبَاَكَ هٰذَاؕ-قَالَ نَبَّاَنِیَ الْعَلِیْمُ الْخَبِیْرُ(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And when the Prophet (Muhammad) disclosed a matter in confidence to one of his wives, then when she happened to mention it and Allah made it known to the Prophet, so the Prophet told her part of it and held back a part; then when the Prophet informed her about it (i.e. disclosing the secret), she said, 'Who informed your respected self?' He said, 'The All-Knowing, the All-Aware has informed me.'

{وَ اِذْ اَسَرَّ النَّبِیُّ اِلٰى بَعْضِ اَزْوَاجِهٖ حَدِیْثًا: And when the Prophet disclosed a matter in confidence to one of his wives.} The summary of the incident mentioned here is that the Noble Prophet ﷺ told his wife, Sayyidatuna Hafsah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, a secret consisting of two matters and instructed her not to disclose it. She then informed Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا of it. Allah Almighty made her action known to His Beloved ﷺ through the words of Sayyiduna Jibra'il عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام. The Noble Prophet ﷺ mentioned one of the two matters to Sayyidatuna Hafsah, telling her that she had disclosed it, but did not mention the other. This was an expression of his ﷺ noble character: he ﷺ overlooked one matter instead of taking her to task. When he ﷺ informed her of it, she asked, “Who told you?” He ﷺ replied, “The All-Knowing, All-Aware Allah Almighty told me, from whom nothing is hidden.” (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 3, 4/284–285; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 3, p. 1257, excerpted.)

## Ayat 66:4

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54075

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=4; ayatId=5298; tafseerId=54075; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** اِنْ تَتُوْبَاۤ اِلَى اللّٰهِ فَقَدْ صَغَتْ قُلُوْبُكُمَاۚ-وَ اِنْ تَظٰهَرَا عَلَیْهِ فَاِنَّ اللّٰهَ هُوَ مَوْلٰىهُ وَ جِبْرِیْلُ وَ صَالِحُ الْمُؤْمِنِیْنَۚ-وَ الْمَلٰٓىٕكَةُ بَعْدَ ذٰلِكَ ظَهِیْرٌ(4)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** (O) you two wives of the Prophet (Muhammad), if you turn towards Allah with repentance, so your hearts have certainly moved away a little; and if you (two) strengthen each other against him, so Allah is indeed his Helper, and Jibra'eel and the virtuous believers; and after that the angels are also (his) supporters.

{اِنْ تَتُوْبَاۤ اِلَى اللّٰهِ فَقَدْ صَغَتْ قُلُوْبُكُمَا: If you turn towards Allah with repentance, so your hearts have certainly moved away a little.} Sayyidatuna Aishah and Sayyidatuna Hafsah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا are addressed: O the two wives of my Beloved ﷺ, it is necessary for you to turn towards Allah Almighty because your hearts have moved somewhat away from the truth. You liked that Sayyidatuna Mariyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا was declared unlawful for the Noble Prophet ﷺ, although this was difficult for him ﷺ. If you join forces against the Noble Prophet ﷺ and adopt together an approach displeasing to him ﷺ, then know that Allah Almighty is his Helper, as are Sayyiduna Jibra'il عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, the righteous believers and, after them, the angels. (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 4, 4/285; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 4, p. 1257, excerpted.)

Three matters concerning this verse are also worth noting:

1. Although Sayyiduna Jibra'il عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام is included among the angels, he is mentioned separately because he is the leader of all the angels.
2. The Noble Prophet ﷺ is the helper of the Muslims like a king is the helper of his subjects, while believers are helpers of the Noble Prophet ﷺ like servants and soldiers are helpers of a king. Therefore, this verse cannot be used to say that the Radiant Prophet ﷺ is dependent upon Muslims.
3. Sayyiduna Jibra'il عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام and righteous Muslims are called masters, meaning helpers, while the angels are called supporters. This proves that the servants of Allah Almighty are helpers. Wherever help from other than Allah is negated, real and independent help is intended; thus, there is no contradiction between the verses.

## Ayat 66:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54076

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=5; ayatId=5299; tafseerId=54076; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** عَسٰى رَبُّهٗۤ اِنْ طَلَّقَكُنَّ اَنْ یُّبْدِلَهٗۤ اَزْوَاجًا خَیْرًا مِّنْكُنَّ مُسْلِمٰتٍ مُّؤْمِنٰتٍ قٰنِتٰتٍ تٰٓىٕبٰتٍ عٰبِدٰتٍ سٰٓىٕحٰتٍ ثَیِّبٰتٍ وَّ اَبْكَارًا(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** If he divorces you, it is close that his Lord will give him wives better than you in your place; submissive, believing, obedient, repenting, worshiping, fasting, widows and virgins.

{اِنْ طَلَّقَكُنَّ: If he divorces you.} O the wives of my Beloved ﷺ, if he divorces you, his Lord is powerful enough to grant him wives better than you: women obedient to Allah Almighty, sincere in believing in His Oneness, obedient to Allah Almighty and His Messenger ﷺ and desirous of their pleasure, frequently repentant, devoted to worship, fasting, widowed and virgin. (Tafsir Kabir, At-Tahrim, under verse 5, 10/571; Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 5, 4/286; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 5, pp. 1257–1258, excerpted.)

In saying this, the purified wives were in fact warned that if they hurt the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ and he ﷺ divorced them, Allah Almighty would grant him ﷺ better wives through His grace and kindness. They were affected by this warning, considered the honour of serving the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ greater than every blessing, and placed his ﷺ comfort and pleasure first. Therefore, he ﷺ did not divorce them. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, At-Tahrim, under verse 5, p. 1037, abridged.)

### Qualities of a Good Wife

This verse teaches that a good wife obeys Allah Almighty and His Beloved ﷺ, obeys her husband, worships Allah and avoids sins, even if she is poor. Thus, when choosing a wife, one should not look only at beauty and wealth; her religious commitment should be considered and given preference.

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “A woman is married for four reasons: wealth, family status, beauty and religion. Give preference to the religious woman.” (Bukhari, Book of Marriage, chapter on compatibility in religion, 3/429, hadith 5090.)

Sayyiduna Anas ibn Malik رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crown of Messengership ﷺ said: “Whoever marries a woman because of her honour, Allah Almighty will increase his disgrace; whoever marries her because of her wealth, Allah Almighty will only increase his poverty; whoever marries her because of her lineage, He will increase his lowliness. But whoever marries so that he does not look elsewhere, so that he may remain chaste, or in order to maintain family ties, Allah Almighty will place blessing in that woman for the man and in that man for the woman.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat, chapter alif, Ibrahim, 2/18, hadith 2342.)

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to marry a righteous and religious woman and to prefer a religious woman over other women. Ameen.

## Ayat 66:6

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54077

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=6; ayatId=5300; tafseerId=54077; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا قُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ اَهْلِیْكُمْ نَارًا وَّ قُوْدُهَا النَّاسُ وَ الْحِجَارَةُ عَلَیْهَا مَلٰٓىٕكَةٌ غِلَاظٌ شِدَادٌ لَّا یَعْصُوْنَ اللّٰهَ مَاۤ اَمَرَهُمْ وَ یَفْعَلُوْنَ مَا یُؤْمَرُوْنَ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Save yourselves and your families from the Fire, the fuel of which are humans and stones; appointed over it are extremely powerful angels who do not refuse the command of Allah and they only do what they are commanded.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا قُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ اَهْلِیْكُمْ نَارًا: O believers! Save yourselves and your families from the Fire.} O believers, protect yourselves and your families from this Fire by obeying Allah Almighty and His Messenger ﷺ, performing acts of worship, refraining from sins, instructing your family to do good and forbidding evil, and teaching them knowledge and manners.

“People” here refers to disbelievers and “stones” to idols and the like. The meaning is that the Fire of Hell is extremely hot. Unlike the fire of this world, which burns wood and similar fuel, the Fire of Hell burns the things mentioned here.

It is further stated that angels who are harsh towards the people of Hell and extremely powerful are appointed over Hell. They have no softness in their nature, do not disobey Allah Almighty, and do only what they are commanded. (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 6, 4/287; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 6, p. 1258, excerpted.)

### Islamic Education of One’s Family Is Obligatory

This verse teaches that just as a Muslim must reform himself, he must also provide Islamic education and upbringing to his family. Every Muslim should teach and remind his wife, children and all those under his care of Islamic commands and raise them under Islamic teachings so that they too remain safe from the Fire of Hell.

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا narrates that the Crown of Messengership ﷺ said: “Every one of you is a guardian, and every one of you will be questioned about those under your care. The ruler is a guardian and will be questioned about his subjects. A man is a guardian over his household and will be questioned about them. A woman is a guardian in her husband’s home and will be questioned about it. A servant is a guardian over his master’s property and will be questioned about it. A man is a guardian over his father’s property and will be questioned about it. In short, every one of you is a guardian and will be questioned about those under your care.” (Bukhari, Book of Friday, chapter on Friday in villages and cities, 1/309, hadith 893.)

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Amr رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا narrates that the Leader of the Messengers ﷺ said: “Command your children to pray when they are seven years old. When they reach ten, strike them if necessary to make them pray, and separate their beds.” (Abu Dawud, Book of Prayer, chapter on when a child is commanded to pray, 1/208, hadith 495.)

Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “May Allah have mercy on the man who rises at night, prays and wakes his wife for prayer. If she does not rise, he sprinkles water on her face. May Allah have mercy on the woman who rises at night, prays and wakes her husband. If he does not rise, she sprinkles water on his face.” (Abu Dawud, Book of Voluntary Prayers, chapter on night prayer, 2/48, hadith 1308.) Sprinkling water is permitted when it is done playfully to wake the other person, or when the other person has requested it. May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to give our families sound Islamic education and upbringing. Ameen.

### A Soul Fled Out of Fear of Hell

Sayyiduna Mansur ibn Ammar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ relates: I performed Hajj and stayed at an inn in Kufah during the journey. One dark night, I went outside and, at midnight, heard a voice full of pain saying: “O Allah Almighty, by Your honour and majesty, I did not knowingly disobey and oppose You. Whenever I disobeyed You, I was not unaware of it, but my misfortune helped me to err, and hope in Your concealment of faults encouraged me to sin. I disobeyed and opposed You because of my ignorance. Who will save me from Your punishment now? If You cut off the rope of Your mercy and grace from me, whose rope will I hold?”

When he finished supplicating, I recited this verse of the Holy Quran:

“قُوْۤا اَنْفُسَكُمْ وَ اَهْلِیْكُمْ نَارًا وَّ قُوْدُهَا النَّاسُ وَ الْحِجَارَةُ عَلَیْهَا مَلٰٓىٕكَةٌ غِلَاظٌ شِدَادٌ لَّا یَعْصُوْنَ اللّٰهَ مَاۤ اَمَرَهُمْ وَ یَفْعَلُوْنَ مَا یُؤْمَرُوْنَ”

I then heard a violent movement, after which there was no further sound. The next day, when I returned to my lodging, I saw a bier. An old woman who did not know me told me that a man had passed by during the night while her son was praying. The man recited a Quranic verse, and upon hearing it her son had passed away. (Mustadrak, Book of Tafsir, interpretation of Surah At-Tahrim, another account concerning fear of Allah Almighty, 3/318, hadith 3882.)

## Ayat 66:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54079

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=8; ayatId=5302; tafseerId=54079; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا تُوْبُوْۤا اِلَى اللّٰهِ تَوْبَةً نَّصُوْحًاؕ-عَسٰى رَبُّكُمْ اَنْ یُّكَفِّرَ عَنْكُمْ سَیِّاٰتِكُمْ وَ یُدْخِلَكُمْ جَنّٰتٍ تَجْرِیْ مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْاَنْهٰرُۙ-یَوْمَ لَا یُخْزِی اللّٰهُ النَّبِیَّ وَ الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا مَعَهٗۚ-نُوْرُهُمْ یَسْعٰى بَیْنَ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ بِاَیْمَانِهِمْ یَقُوْلُوْنَ رَبَّنَاۤ اَتْمِمْ لَنَا نُوْرَنَا وَ اغْفِرْ لَنَاۚ-اِنَّكَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ(8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Repent to Allah with such repentance which becomes advice for the future; it is close that your Lord may remove your sins and admit you into Gardens beneath which rivers flow on the Day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and the believers along with him; their light will be running ahead of them and (running) on their right; they will submit, 'O our Lord, complete our light for us, and forgive us; indeed, You have power over all things.'

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا تُوْبُوْۤا اِلَى اللّٰهِ تَوْبَةً نَّصُوْحًا: O believers! Repent to Allah with sincere repentance.} O believers, repent sincerely to Allah Almighty, in a way whose effect appears in your deeds, so that your life becomes filled with obedience and worship and you continue to avoid sins. Sayyiduna Umar Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ and other Companions said: “Sincere repentance means that a person does not return to sin after repenting, just as milk that has left the udder does not return to it.” (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 8, 4/287; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 8, p. 1258, excerpted.)

{عَسٰى رَبُّكُمْ: It is close that your Lord.} It is close that, after accepting your repentance, your Lord will erase your sins and take you on the Day of Judgement into Gardens beneath which rivers flow. On that day Allah Almighty will not disgrace the Noble Prophet ﷺ and the believers with him. Their light will run before them and on their right across the Bridge. When the believers see the disbelievers falling into Hell, they will say: “O our Lord, keep this light with us until we enter Paradise, and forgive us.” (Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 8, p. 1259; Ruh-ul-Bayan, At-Tahrim, under verse 8, 10/65–66, excerpted.)

### Encouragement to Repent Sincerely

In the present age, committing sins has become extremely easy, while avoiding them and performing good deeds has become difficult. Every Muslim should therefore strive fully to avoid sins and perform good deeds, and should sincerely repent of sins already committed. Sincere repentance erases sins from a person’s record. Allah Almighty states:

“وَ هُوَ الَّذِیْ یَقْبَلُ التَّوْبَةَ عَنْ عِبَادِهٖ وَ یَعْفُوْا عَنِ السَّیِّاٰتِ وَ یَعْلَمُ مَا تَفْعَلُوْنَ” (Ash-Shura: 25)

Kanz-ul-Irfan translation: “And He is the One Who accepts repentance from His servants, pardons sins and knows what you do.”

Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Masud رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “The one who repents from sin is like one who has no sin.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Asceticism, chapter on mentioning repentance, 4/491, hadith 4250.)

Sayyiduna Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “When a servant repents from his sins, Allah Almighty makes the recording angels forget his sins, makes his limbs forget them, and erases their traces from the earth. Thus, when he meets Allah Almighty on the Day of Judgement, no witness will remain against him concerning his sins.” (At-Targhib wat-Tarhib, Book of Repentance and Asceticism, encouragement to repent and hasten to it, 4/48, hadith 17.)

May Allah Almighty grant us the ability to sincerely repent of previous sins and continue avoiding future sins. Ameen.

Five matters are learned from this verse:

1. Repentance is a means of forgiveness and of becoming deserving of Paradise.
2. A pious believer will be with the Noble Prophet ﷺ on the Day of Judgement.
3. The Day of Judgement will be a day of honour for the Noble Prophet ﷺ and those with him, and a day of disgrace for the disbelievers.
4. Although a believer may be sinful, he will, if Allah wills, remain safe from disgrace in the Hereafter. Even if he is punished, he will not be disgraced.
5. At first, the hypocrites will have light on the Bridge, but when they reach its middle, their light will be extinguished.

## Ayat 66:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54081

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=10; ayatId=5304; tafseerId=54081; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** ضَرَبَ اللّٰهُ مَثَلًا لِّلَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوا امْرَاَتَ نُوْحٍ وَّ امْرَاَتَ لُوْطٍؕ-كَانَتَا تَحْتَ عَبْدَیْنِ مِنْ عِبَادِنَا صَالِحَیْنِ فَخَانَتٰهُمَا فَلَمْ یُغْنِیَا عَنْهُمَا مِنَ اللّٰهِ شَیْــٴًـا وَّ قِیْلَ ادْخُلَا النَّارَ مَعَ الدّٰخِلِیْنَ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Allah explains the example of the disbelievers; the wife of Nooh and the wife of Loot; they were bound in marriage to two of Our bondsmen deserving Our closeness, then they (i.e. the wives of these Prophets) betrayed them, so they (both) did not benefit from them (i.e. these two Prophets) against (the punishment of) Allah in the least. And it was stated that 'Both of you women enter Hell along with others who enter (Hell).'

{ضَرَبَ اللّٰهُ مَثَلًا لِّلَّذِیْنَ كَفَرُوا امْرَاَتَ نُوْحٍ وَّ امْرَاَتَ لُوْطٍ: Allah explains the example of the disbelievers: the wife of Nooh and the wife of Loot.} Allah Almighty presents the wives of Sayyiduna Nooh and Sayyiduna Loot عَلَیْہِمَا الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام as examples for the disbelievers. They were married to two righteous, close servants of Allah, but they committed treachery in the matter of religion by choosing disbelief. Those two honoured servants could not benefit them at all before Allah Almighty. At their death, or on the Day of Judgement, they will be told: “Enter Hell with the disbelievers of your peoples.” Their marital relationship with the Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام did not remain beneficial because of their disbelief. Likewise, O disbelievers of Makkah, your relationship with my Beloved ﷺ will not benefit you while you remain in disbelief. (Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 10, p. 1259; Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 10, 4/288, excerpted.)

The name of Sayyiduna Nooh’s wife was Wahilah. She used to tell her people that Sayyiduna Nooh عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was insane. The name of Sayyiduna Loot’s wife was Wa'ilah, and she concealed her hypocrisy. (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 10, 4/288.)

This verse teaches that the companionship of the pious will not benefit a person in the Hereafter without faith. It also teaches that a prophet’s relationship or lineage does not benefit a disbeliever, and that on the Day of Judgement every person will be with the one whom he loved in the world.

## Ayat 66:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54082

**Metadata:** surah=66; ayat=11; ayatId=5305; tafseerId=54082; para=28; volume=10; page=561

**Arabic:** وَ ضَرَبَ اللّٰهُ مَثَلًا لِّلَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا امْرَاَتَ فِرْعَوْنَۘ-اِذْ قَالَتْ رَبِّ ابْنِ لِیْ عِنْدَكَ بَیْتًا فِی الْجَنَّةِ وَ نَجِّنِیْ مِنْ فِرْعَوْنَ وَ عَمَلِهٖ وَ نَجِّنِیْ مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الظّٰلِمِیْنَۙ    (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And Allah explains an example of the Muslims; the wife of Fir'awn; when she prayed, 'O my Lord! Build a house for me in Your closeness in Paradise, and rescue me from Fir'awn and his actions, and rescue me from the unjust people.'

{وَ ضَرَبَ اللّٰهُ مَثَلًا لِّلَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوا امْرَاَتَ فِرْعَوْنَ: And Allah explains an example of the Muslims: the wife of Fir'awn.} The previous verse gave an example for disbelievers; this verse gives an example for Muslims, showing that another person’s sin does not harm them.

When Sayyiduna Musa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام defeated the magicians, Fir'awn’s wife, Sayyidatuna Asiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, accepted faith in him. When Fir'awn learned of this, he severely punished her, had her hands and feet tied to four stakes, placed a heavy millstone on her chest and left her in the intense heat. When his cruelty increased, Sayyidatuna Asiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا supplicated: “O my Lord, build a house for me with You in Paradise.” Allah Almighty showed her the house prepared for her in Paradise, and the joy of seeing it made Fir'awn’s cruelty easy for her to bear. She then supplicated: “Save me from Fir'awn, his disbelief, polytheism and oppression, and save me from the oppressive people who follow Fir'awn’s religion.” Her supplication was accepted, and Allah Almighty took her soul. Thus, just as Fir'awn’s disbelief did not harm Sayyidatuna Asiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا or cause her punishment, the disbelief of their relatives will not harm Muslims or cause them to be punished. (Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 11, 4/288; Jalalayn, At-Tahrim, under verse 11, p. 466, excerpted.)

Three matters are learned from the words “When she said, ‘My Lord, build for me a house with You in Paradise’”:

1. The house in Paradise is of a higher rank when the servant has greater closeness to Allah Almighty.
2. Out of love for Allah Almighty and longing to meet Him, it is permissible to desire and supplicate for death.
3. Seeking refuge with Allah Almighty, supplicating before Him and asking Him for relief from difficulties and calamities is the way of the righteous.

## Ayat 66:12

Allah Almighty presented Sayyidatuna Maryam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا, daughter of Sayyiduna Imraan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, as an example. She confirmed the words of her Lord and His Books and was among the obedient. “The words of her Lord” means the religious commands Allah Almighty prescribed for His servants, while “His Books” means the Books revealed to the noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. (Ruh-ul-Bayan, At-Tahrim, under verse 12, 10/70; Khazin, At-Tahrim, under verse 12, 4/288; Madarik, At-Tahrim, under verse 12, p. 1259, excerpted.)

### The Excellence of Sayyidatuna Maryam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا

Sayyidatuna Maryam رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا has the distinction that, apart from her, no woman’s name is mentioned in the Holy Quran. She will also have the distinction of being among the wives of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ in Paradise. The Radiant Prophet ﷺ counted her among the perfect women. Sayyiduna Abu Musa Ashari رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “Many men attained perfection, while among women, Asiyah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا and Maryam bint Imraan رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا attained perfection. The excellence of Aishah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا over women is like the excellence of tharid over all foods.” (Bukhari, Book of the Stories of the Prophets, chapter on the statement of Allah Almighty “And Allah presents an example for those who believe: the wife of Fir'awn,” 2/445, hadith 3411.)

Three matters are learned from the words “We breathed into her a spirit from Ourselves”:

1. Sayyiduna Jibra'il عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام blew, yet Allah Almighty said, “We breathed.” This proves that the action of the accepted servants of Allah Almighty is, in reality, Allah Almighty’s action.
2. Blowing for the purpose of conveying benefit is the practice of angels. This noble verse is the basis of the blowing and prayers of the spiritual guides, and blowing is also established by many sound hadith.
3. Sayyiduna Isa عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام is called Ruhullah because his birth occurred through the blowing of Ruh-ul-Amin.
