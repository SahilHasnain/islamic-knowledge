# Surah Al-Hujurat: English Translation

## Ayat 49:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 53684

**Metadata:** surah=49; ayat=1; ayatId=4661; tafseerId=53684; para=26; volume=9

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا لَا تُقَدِّمُوْا بَیْنَ یَدَیِ اللّٰهِ وَ رَسُوْلِهٖ وَ اتَّقُوا اللّٰهَؕ-اِنَّ اللّٰهَ سَمِیْعٌ عَلِیْمٌ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! Do not advance yourselves before (the command of) Allah and His (Beloved) Messenger, and fear Allah; indeed, Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْا: O believers!} This verse teaches the believers to observe the manners and respect of the Beloved of Allah Almighty, the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ. The summary of the verse is: O believers! It is necessary for you not to precede Allah Almighty and His Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ in any word or deed at all, without their permission, because going ahead of the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ is contrary to his reverence and respect, while humility and observance of manners are necessary in the august court of Prophethood. Fear Allah Almighty in all your words and deeds, because if you fear Allah Almighty, this fear will prevent you from going ahead. In any case, Allah Almighty hears all your words and knows all your deeds. The right of the One whose attribute this is, is that He be feared. (Khazin, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, 4/163–164; Jalalayn, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, p. 426; Madarik, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, pp. 1149–1150, excerpted.)

There are various reports concerning the occasion of revelation of this verse. Two of them are given below.

(1) A few people performed their sacrifice before the Sovereign of Both Worlds صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ on the day of Eid al-Adha, so they were commanded to sacrifice again.

(2) It is narrated from Sayyidatuna Aishah رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہَا that some people would begin fasting one day before Ramadan. This verse was revealed concerning them, and they were commanded not to go ahead of their Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ in beginning the fast. (Khazin, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, 4/163–164; Jalalayn, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, p. 426; Madarik, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, pp. 1149–1150, excerpted.)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Naeemi رَحْمَۃُاللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: “Whatever the occasion of revelation may have been, this command is general for everyone. It is forbidden to be ahead of the Noble Prophet عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام in any matter or deed. If one is travelling along with the Noble Prophet عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام, it is forbidden to walk ahead of him, except in the capacity of a servant or after obtaining permission for a need; in that case, walking ahead is not forbidden. If one is eating with him, it is unlawful to begin eating first. Similarly, giving one’s own intellect and opinion precedence over the opinion of the Noble Prophet عَلَیْہِ السَّلَام is forbidden.” (Shan-e-Habib al-Rahman, p. 224.)

The verse mentions Allah Almighty and His Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ together, although being ahead of Allah Almighty is impossible, because He is not confined to time or place, and being ahead occurs either in time or in place. It is therefore known that the purpose of the verse is that you must not go ahead of the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ; disrespect towards him is, in reality, disrespect towards Allah Almighty. Going ahead of him after obtaining permission, in the capacity of a servant, or because of a need is not included in this prohibition. Therefore, the reports in which some Companions رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ are mentioned as walking ahead of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ do not fall under this verse, because they walked in the capacity of servants. Similarly, Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ leading the prayer is not included in this prohibition, because he did so with the permission of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ.

Allamah Ismail Haqqi رَحْمَۃُاللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: The honourable scholars are heirs of the Noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, so going ahead of them is also included in this prohibition. Its proof is the narration reported from Sayyiduna Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, in which he says: The Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ saw me walking ahead of Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, so he said: “O Abu Darda! Do you walk ahead of the one who is better than you, indeed better than the entire world?” (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, 9/62.)

It should be remembered that this respect is for those honourable scholars who are people of truth and practise their knowledge, because these scholars are, in reality, the heirs of the Noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. Scholars of misguidance and scholars who do not practise their knowledge are not deserving of this respect.

Some respectful people do not turn their backs towards elders or the Holy Quran; the source of this practice is also this verse.

Five matters related to the verse **لَا تُقَدِّمُوْا بَیْنَ یَدَیِ اللّٰهِ وَ رَسُوْلِهٖ** are as follows:

Here are five matters related to this verse:

(1) The rank of the Chief of the Messengers صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ is so exalted in the court of Allah Almighty that Allah Almighty Himself has stated the manners of his court.

(2) In this verse, it has been commanded not to go ahead of both Allah Almighty and the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ, although being ahead of Allah Almighty is impossible. This is because Allah Almighty is not in time or place, while being ahead occurs either in time or in place. It is known from this that the purpose of the verse is that you must not go ahead of the Noble Messenger صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ; disrespect towards him is, in reality, disrespect towards Allah Almighty. (Shan-e-Habib al-Rahman, pp. 224–225, abridged.)

(3) Going ahead of the luminous Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ in the capacity of a servant or because of a need, after obtaining his permission, is not included in this prohibition. Therefore, the Companions رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ who are mentioned in the ahadith as walking ahead of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ do not fall under this verse, because they walked in the capacity of servants. Similarly, Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ leading the prayer is not included in this prohibition, because this action of his was by the permission of the Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ.

(4) Allamah Ismail Haqqi رَحْمَۃُاللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ says: Since the honourable scholars are heirs of the Noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام, going ahead of them is also included in this prohibition. Its proof is the narration reported from Sayyiduna Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, in which he says: The Noble Prophet صَلَّی اللہ تَعَالٰی عَلَیْہِ وَاٰلِہٖ وَسَلَّمَ saw me walking ahead of Sayyiduna Abu Bakr Siddiq رَضِیَ اللہ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ and said: “O Abu Darda! Do you walk ahead of the one who is better than you, indeed better than the whole world?” (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Hujurat, under verse 1, 9/62.)

It should be remembered that this manner is for those honourable scholars who are people of truth and practise their knowledge, because only these scholars are, in reality, the heirs of the Noble Prophets عَلَیْہِمُ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام. Scholars of misguidance and scholars who do not practise their knowledge are not deserving of this respect.

(5) Some respectful people do not turn their backs towards elders or the Holy Quran; the source of this practice is also this verse.
