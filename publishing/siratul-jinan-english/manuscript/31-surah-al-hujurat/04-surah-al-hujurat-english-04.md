# Surah Al-Hujurat: English Translation

## Ayat 49:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 53690

**Metadata:** surah=49; ayat=7; ayatId=4667; tafseerId=53690; para=26; volume=9

**Arabic:** وَ اعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّ فِیْكُمْ رَسُوْلَ اللّٰهِؕ-لَوْ یُطِیْعُكُمْ فِیْ كَثِیْرٍ مِّنَ الْاَمْرِ لَعَنِتُّمْ وَ لٰكِنَّ اللّٰهَ حَبَّبَ اِلَیْكُمُ الْاِیْمَانَ وَ زَیَّنَهٗ فِیْ قُلُوْبِكُمْ وَ كَرَّهَ اِلَیْكُمُ الْكُفْرَ وَ الْفُسُوْقَ وَ الْعِصْیَانَؕ-اُولٰٓىٕكَ هُمُ الرّٰشِدُوْنَۙ    (7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And know that Allah's (Beloved) Messenger is amongst you; if he acts according to your liking in many of your matters, so you would certainly be in trouble. But Allah has made faith dear to you and has beautified it in your hearts, and has made disbelief, and disobedience, and sinning detested to you. The very same people are on the path (of guidance).

**وَ اعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّ فِیْكُمْ رَسُوْلَ اللّٰهِ:** Know that Allah Almighty's Messenger is present among you. If any of you tells him a lie, Allah Almighty will inform him, and he will expose your condition by His command and disgrace you. Therefore, do not tell him anything false. Remember that if he were to follow your advice in many of the matters you present to him and issue rulings according to your opinion, you would certainly fall into hardship. However, Allah Almighty, out of mercy for you, protected him from this, placed the love of faith in your hearts and adorned it within your hearts. Through its blessing, you do not disobey the Noble Prophet ﷺ. He made disbelief, disobedience and sin detestable to you, causing you to turn away from disobedience. Such people are rightly guided and remain firm upon the straight path through the grace and favour of Allah Almighty. The attribute of Allah Almighty is that He knows their conditions and is Wise in bestowing favours upon them. (Madarik, Al-Hujurat, under verses 7–8, p. 1152–1153; Khazin, Al-Hujurat, under verses 7–8, 4/167; Jalalayn with Sawi, Al-Hujurat, under verses 7–8, 5/1991–1992, excerpted.)

Six matters obtained from the verse **وَ اعْلَمُوْۤا اَنَّ فِیْكُمْ رَسُوْلَ اللّٰهِ**:

(1) Lying before the Noble Prophet ﷺ is a grave sin.

(2) Those who write and recite praises of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, and those who present their requests before him, should present their true pain and difficulties and should not exaggerate there.

(3) Finding faith beloved is a great mercy of Allah Almighty.

(4) The perfection of faith is attained not through one’s own effort, but through the grace of Allah Almighty.

(5) Not committing sin is itself a perfection, but for hatred of sin to arise in the heart is a greater perfection, because this hatred permanently protects a person from sins.

(6) All the Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰهُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ were inwardly averse to disbelief, sinfulness and sin. Faith, piety, and right guidance had become rooted in their hearts like colour and fragrance are rooted in a rose.
