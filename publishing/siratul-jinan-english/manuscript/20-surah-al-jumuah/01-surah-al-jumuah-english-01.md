# Surah Al-Jumu'ah: English Translation

## Ayat 62:1

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54027

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=1; ayatId=5239; tafseerId=54027; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یُسَبِّحُ لِلّٰهِ مَا فِی السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ مَا فِی الْاَرْضِ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوْسِ الْعَزِیْزِ الْحَكِیْمِ(1)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth glorifies Allah; the King, the Most Pure, the Honourable, the Wise.

{یُسَبِّحُ لِلّٰهِ: Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth glorifies Allah.} All things present in the heavens and the earth declare Allah Almighty to be pure from every defect and shortcoming. His glory is that He is the true King, the Extremely Pure, the Honourable and the Wise. (Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 1, p. 1239, excerpted.)

### Three Types of Glorification

Glorification is of three kinds:

1. **The glorification of creation.** Every thing's very being and its creation point to the power and wisdom of the Creator, the Omnipotent Lord, to His Oneness, and to His being pure from every defect and shortcoming.
2. **The glorification of knowledge.** Allah Almighty, through His kindness and grace, places knowledge of Himself in creation, and creation declares Him pure.
3. **Essential glorification.** Allah Almighty causes every substance to glorify Him, and every substance glorifies Him even without knowledge of Him. (Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 1, p. 1239, excerpted.)

## Ayat 62:2

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54028

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=2; ayatId=5240; tafseerId=54028; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** هُوَ الَّذِیْ بَعَثَ فِی الْاُمِّیّٖنَ رَسُوْلًا مِّنْهُمْ یَتْلُوْا عَلَیْهِمْ اٰیٰتِهٖ وَ یُزَكِّیْهِمْ وَ یُعَلِّمُهُمُ الْكِتٰبَ وَ الْحِكْمَةَۗ-وَ اِنْ كَانُوْا مِنْ قَبْلُ لَفِیْ ضَلٰلٍ مُّبِیْنٍۙ   (2)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** It is He Who has sent in the unlettered people a (great) Messenger from amongst themselves who recites His verses to them and purifies them, and gives them the knowledge of the Book and wisdom. And indeed, before this, they were definitely in open error.

{هُوَ الَّذِیْ بَعَثَ فِی الْاُمِّیّٖنَ رَسُوْلًا مِّنْهُمْ: It is He Who sent a Messenger from among the unschooled people.} Allah Almighty sent among the unschooled people a Messenger from among them, whose noble lineage and distinction they knew well. His blessed name is Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ. He recites before them the verses of the Holy Quran, which explain prophethood, the lawful and unlawful, and truth and falsehood. He purifies them from false beliefs, blameworthy morals, the impurities of the Age of Ignorance and evil deeds, and gives them knowledge of the Book and wisdom, that is, the Quran, Sunnah and jurisprudence; the commands of the Sacred Law and the secrets of the spiritual path. Indeed, before the advent of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, the people were in manifest error: they were caught in polytheism, false beliefs and vile deeds, and were in great need of a perfect spiritual guide. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 2, 4/264; Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 2, p. 1239, excerpted.)

### Three Reasons for the Description “Unschooled Prophet”

The Leader of the Messengers ﷺ is described as the “Unschooled Prophet” for many reasons. Three are mentioned here:

1. He was sent to the unschooled community. The Book of Isaiah states that Allah Almighty says: “I shall send an unschooled one among the unschooled, and I shall seal prophethood through him.”
2. His advent took place in Umm al-Qura, meaning Makkah al-Mukarramah.
3. The Luminous Master ﷺ neither wrote nor read from a book, and this was his excellence: because of his perfect retention of knowledge, he had no need of it. Writing is a mental craft produced through a bodily instrument. If a being has the Supreme Pen under his command, what need has he of this writing? The fact that the Noble Prophet ﷺ did not write, yet was an expert in writing, is a magnificent miracle. He taught scribes the knowledge and methods of writing, taught craftsmen their trades, and Allah Almighty made him more knowledgeable than all creation in every perfection of this world and the Hereafter. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 2, p. 1023, excerpted.)

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan, may Allah have mercy upon him, beautifully says:

From earth to the Throne, all secrets' mirrors are present,
Only swear by the wisdom of the Unschooled, resplendent.

### Five Lessons from Verse 62:2

Five matters are learned from this verse:

1. Purity of the heart is attained through the gracious gaze of the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ; faith and righteous deeds are means of purification.
2. The Quran and Hadith are not so simple that everyone can understand them merely through personal reason; otherwise the Luminous Master ﷺ would not have been sent to teach them.
3. Hadith is also necessary for guidance.
4. The Holy Quran must not be understood merely through personal reason; it must be understood through the teaching of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, otherwise one will go astray.
5. The Crowned Messenger ﷺ was not anyone's student in this world, because at the time of his advent ordinary people were ignorant.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan, may Allah have mercy upon him, beautifully says:

Why should such an Unschooled One be indebted to a teacher's aid?
Would “Read, your Lord is the Most Generous” not suffice for his need?

## Ayat 62:3

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54029

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=3; ayatId=5241; tafseerId=54029; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَّ اٰخَرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ لَمَّا یَلْحَقُوْا بِهِمْؕ-وَ هُوَ الْعَزِیْزُ الْحَكِیْمُ(3)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And he (i.e. the Beloved Prophet Muhammad) also purifies and bestows knowledge to others amongst them who have not yet joined the former ones; and only He is the Most Honourable, the Wise.

{وَ اٰخَرِیْنَ مِنْهُمْ: And others from among them.} This verse is connected with the preceding verse and mentions further people whom the Noble Messenger ﷺ purifies and teaches. “Others” among the unschooled may mean non-Arabs, or it may mean all those who enter Islam after the Noble Prophet ﷺ until the Day of Judgement. Their not joining the former people means that they did not live in their time but came after them. (Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 3, p. 1239; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 3, 4/264.)

A blessed hadith indicates that non-Arabs are meant by “others.” Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states: When Surah Al-Jumu'ah was revealed, we were present in the blessed court of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. When he recited, “And others among them who have not yet joined them,” a man asked, “O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who are those who have not yet joined the former ones?” The Prophet ﷺ did not answer until the man asked two or three times. Sayyiduna Salman al-Farsi رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ was among us. The Noble Prophet ﷺ placed his hand upon Sayyiduna Salman and said: “By the One in Whose power is my life, even if religion were near the Pleiades, the sons of Persia would go there and attain it.” (Muslim, Book of the Virtues of the Companions, chapter on the virtue of Persia, p. 1378, hadith 230–231 (2546).)

This verse shows that the spiritual grace of the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ is not limited to the noble Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ; rather, it will remain until the Day of Judgement. People are purified and cleansed through his gracious gaze, and will continue to be so; they learn knowledge and will continue to learn it.

{ذٰلِكَ فَضْلُ اللّٰهِ: This is Allah's grace.} The excellence of the Noble Messenger ﷺ and his Ummah mentioned here is Allah Almighty's grace. He grants this grace to whomever He wills, and Allah Almighty is immensely gracious to His creation: for their guidance, He sent His Beloved Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ. (Sawi, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 4, 6/2163; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 4, 4/265, excerpted.)

## Ayat 62:5

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54030

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=5; ayatId=5243; tafseerId=54030; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** مَثَلُ الَّذِیْنَ حُمِّلُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ ثُمَّ لَمْ یَحْمِلُوْهَا كَمَثَلِ الْحِمَارِ یَحْمِلُ اَسْفَارًاؕ-بِئْسَ مَثَلُ الْقَوْمِ الَّذِیْنَ كَذَّبُوْا بِاٰیٰتِ اللّٰهِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ لَا یَهْدِی الْقَوْمَ الظّٰلِمِیْنَ(5)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** The example of those who were entrusted with the Tawrah, they then did not carry out its commands, is like the example of a donkey carrying books on its back; what an evil example it is of the people who belied the verses of Allah; and Allah does not guide the unjust.

{مَثَلُ الَّذِیْنَ حُمِّلُوا التَّوْرٰىةَ: The example of those upon whom the Torah was placed.} The preceding verses stated that the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ was sent to the unschooled. The Jews objected that the Leader of the Messengers ﷺ had been sent only to the Arabs and not to them. Allah Almighty presents their example here. Those who were commanded to follow the rulings of the Torah did not act upon it and thus did not bear its responsibility; despite finding in it the praise and description of the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ, they did not believe in him. Their example is like a donkey carrying books on its back, gaining nothing beyond the burden and knowing nothing of the knowledge within them. Such is the state of these Jews: they carry the Torah, memorise its words, gain no benefit from it and do not act according to it. What an evil example are those who deny Allah Almighty's verses; and Allah Almighty does not guide those whom He knows to be unjust. (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 5, 10/539; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 5, 4/265; Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 5, p. 1240, excerpted.)

### Reasons for Comparing the Jews to a Donkey

The Jews are compared to a donkey rather than another animal for several reasons. First, a donkey carries more load than a horse or mule. Second, ignorance and foolishness are more associated with a donkey than with other animals. Third, customarily a donkey is regarded as more contemptible than other animals. (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 5, 10/540, excerpted.)

### The Example of Those Who Neither Understand nor Act upon the Quran

Allamah Ali ibn Muhammad Khazin, may Allah have mercy upon him, says that the example given in this verse of the Jews who did not act upon the Torah also applies to those who do not understand the meanings of the Holy Quran, do not act upon it and turn away from it. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 5, 4/265.) Therefore every Muslim should try to understand the Holy Quran and act upon its commands, so that this example does not apply to him.

### Five Warnings against Failing to Act upon Knowledge

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “On the Day of Judgement, the scholar who received no benefit from his knowledge will suffer the most severe punishment among people.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Saghir, chapter Al-Ta, under Tahir, p. 182, part 1.)
2. Sayyiduna al-Walid ibn Uqbah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “Some people of Paradise will see some people of Hell and ask them, ‘Why did you enter Hell when we entered Paradise through the very knowledge we learned from you?’ They will reply, ‘We did not do what we used to say.’” (Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat, chapter Alif, under Ahmad, 1/41, hadith 99.)
3. Sayyiduna Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ says that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said to him: “O Uwaymir! What will be your condition when, on the Day of Judgement, you are asked: Did you acquire knowledge or remain ignorant? If you say, ‘I acquired knowledge,’ you will be asked: How much did you act upon your knowledge? If you say, ‘I remained ignorant,’ you will be asked: What was your excuse for remaining ignorant? Why did you not acquire knowledge?” (Ibn Asakir, letter Meem, 8793—Abu Muhammad al-Kalbi, 67/181.)
4. Sayyiduna Hudhayfah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crowned Messenger ﷺ said: “Destruction is for the one who does not acquire knowledge, and destruction is also for the one who acquires knowledge and then does not act upon it.” (Kanz al-Ummal, letter Ain, Book of Knowledge, section of sayings, chapter 2, 5/86, part 10, hadith 29036.)
5. Sayyiduna Umar al-Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: “I fear for you every knowledgeable hypocrite who speaks words of wisdom but acts upon sins.” (Kanz al-Ummal, letter Ain, Book of Knowledge, section of sayings, chapter 2, 5/86, part 10, hadith 29040.)

## Ayat 62:6

**Arabic:** قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْۤا اِنْ زَعَمْتُمْ اَنَّكُمْ اَوْلِیَآءُ لِلّٰهِ مِنْ دُوْنِ النَّاسِ فَتَمَنَّوُا الْمَوْتَ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِیْنَ(6)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Say you (O Beloved) 'O Jews! If you assume this that you are friends of Allah and other people are not, so wish for death if you are truthful.'

{قُلْ یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ هَادُوْا: Say (O Beloved), “O Jews!”} The Jews used to say, “We are Allah Almighty's sons and beloved ones. The home of the Hereafter belongs exclusively to us, and only Jews will enter Paradise.” Allah Almighty commanded His Beloved ﷺ to expose their lie by saying: “O Jews! You claim that you alone, excluding other people, are Allah Almighty's friends. If you are truthful in this claim, wish for death, so that death may take you to that destination, because the Hereafter is better than the world for Allah Almighty's friends.” (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 6, 9/518; Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 6, 4/265, excerpted.)

## Ayat 62:7

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54032

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=7; ayatId=5245; tafseerId=54032; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ لَا یَتَمَنَّوْنَهٗۤ اَبَدًۢا بِمَا قَدَّمَتْ اَیْدِیْهِمْؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمٌۢ بِالظّٰلِمِیْنَ(7)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And they will never wish for it (i.e. for death) due to the misdeeds which their hands have sent ahead; and Allah knows the unjust people.

{وَ لَا یَتَمَنَّوْنَهٗۤ اَبَدًا: And they will never wish for death.} Because the Jews disbelieved and denied the Noble Messenger ﷺ, they would never wish for death. This was news of the unseen from Allah Almighty and proved true: the Jews concerning whom it was stated that they would never wish for death never wished for it.

### The Sacred Ruling on Wishing for Death

The hadith prohibit wishing for death. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “None of you should wish for death. A righteous person should not wish for it, because he may perform more good deeds; and a sinner should not wish for it, because he may repent.” (Bukhari, Book of Patients, chapter on a patient's wishing for death, 4/13, hadith 5673.)

He also narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “None of you should wish for death, nor should he pray for it before it comes, because when he passes away his deeds end, while a believer's life only increases him in goodness.” (Muslim, Book of Remembrance and Supplication, chapter on the dislike of wishing for death because of harm, p. 1441, hadith 13 (2682).)

However, if someone is compelled to wish for death, the blessed hadith also gives its method. Sayyiduna Anas رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crowned Messenger ﷺ said: “None of you should wish for death because of an affliction that has befallen him. If he must do so, let him say: ‘O Allah, عزوجل, keep me alive as long as life is better for me, and give me death when death is better for me.’” (Bukhari, Book of Patients, chapter on a patient's wishing for death, 4/13, hadith 5671.)

Mufti Ahmad Yar Khan Naeemi, may Allah have mercy upon him, says that this hadith explains the preceding two: one should not ask for death out of distress from illness and suffering. The manner in which supplication is permitted is extremely beautiful, because it includes the good and evil of religion and the world. Thus one has expressed the wish for death, but according to the proper rule; the aim is achieved and the prohibition is also obeyed. (Miraat al-Manajih, Book of Funerals, chapter on wishing for death and its mention, first section, 2/421, under hadith 1513.)

He further says: Wishing for death can be good or bad. If one wishes for death in order to behold the Noble Prophet ﷺ or to escape worldly trials, it is good; if one wishes for death out of distress from worldly hardships, it is bad. (Miraat al-Manajih, Book of Funerals, chapter on wishing for death and its mention, first section, 2/420.)

{وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِیْمٌۢ بِالظّٰلِمِیْنَ: And Allah knows the unjust.} “The unjust” here means disbelievers: Allah Almighty knows the disbelievers well and will give them severe punishment.

## Ayat 62:8

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54033

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=8; ayatId=5246; tafseerId=54033; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** قُلْ اِنَّ الْمَوْتَ الَّذِیْ تَفِرُّوْنَ مِنْهُ فَاِنَّهٗ مُلٰقِیْكُمْ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّوْنَ اِلٰى عٰلِمِ الْغَیْبِ وَ الشَّهَادَةِ فَیُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُوْنَ۠    (8)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Say you (O Beloved), 'The death from which you (are trying to) flee will definitely meet you, then you will be returned to Him Who knows all the unseen and the seen, He will then inform you of what you did.'

{قُلْ اِنَّ الْمَوْتَ الَّذِیْ تَفِرُّوْنَ مِنْهُ: Say (O Beloved), “The death from which you flee.”} O Beloved ﷺ, tell these Jews: You cannot escape the death from which you flee because of the consequences of your disbelief. It will certainly come to you, and this flight will not benefit you. After passing away, you will be returned to Allah Almighty, Who knows every unseen and visible matter; no state of yours is hidden from Him. He will then inform you of your deeds, meaning what you did in the world, and punish you for them. (Tafsir Kabir, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 8, 10/541; Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 9, 9/519–520, excerpted.)

### Three Ways Deeds Will Be Shown on the Day of Judgement

People will be informed of their deeds on the Day of Judgement in various ways. Three are mentioned here:

1. Their deeds will be shown by presenting their records. Allah Almighty says: “وَ وُضِعَ الْكِتٰبُ فَتَرَى الْمُجْرِمِیْنَ مُشْفِقِیْنَ مِمَّا فِیْهِ وَ یَقُوْلُوْنَ یٰوَیْلَتَنَا مَالِ هٰذَا الْكِتٰبِ لَا یُغَادِرُ صَغِیْرَةً وَّ لَا كَبِیْرَةً اِلَّاۤ اَحْصٰىهَاۚ-وَ وَجَدُوْا مَا عَمِلُوْا حَاضِرًاؕ-وَ لَا یَظْلِمُ رَبُّكَ اَحَدًا” (Al-Kahf: 49). Translation: “And the Book of Deeds will be placed, so you will see the sinners fearful of what is in it and saying, ‘Alas for us! What is the matter with this Book that it has left nothing small or great unrecorded?’ And they will find all their deeds present before them; and your Lord does not wrong anyone.”
2. A person's limbs will testify to his deeds. Allah Almighty says: “یَوْمَ تَشْهَدُ عَلَیْهِمْ اَلْسِنَتُهُمْ وَ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ اَرْجُلُهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَعْمَلُوْنَ” (An-Nur: 24). Translation: “The Day when their tongues, hands and feet will testify against them concerning what they used to do.” He also says: “اَلْیَوْمَ نَخْتِمُ عَلٰۤى اَفْوَاهِهِمْ وَ تُكَلِّمُنَاۤ اَیْدِیْهِمْ وَ تَشْهَدُ اَرْجُلُهُمْ بِمَا كَانُوْا یَكْسِبُوْنَ” (Ya-Sin: 65). Translation: “Today We shall seal their mouths, their hands will speak to Us and their feet will testify concerning what they used to earn.”
3. The earth will relate people's deeds. Allah Almighty says: “یَوْمَىٕذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَاۙ(۴) بِاَنَّ رَبَّكَ اَوْحٰى لَهَا” (Az-Zilzal: 4–5). Translation: “On that Day it will narrate its news, because your Lord has commanded it.”

## Ayat 62:9

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54034

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=9; ayatId=5247; tafseerId=54034; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا نُوْدِیَ لِلصَّلٰوةِ مِنْ یَّوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا اِلٰى ذِكْرِ اللّٰهِ وَ ذَرُوا الْبَیْعَؕ-ذٰلِكُمْ خَیْرٌ لَّكُمْ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُوْنَ(9)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** O believers! When the call for Salah is given on the day of Jumu'ah (i.e. Friday), so hurry towards the remembrance of Allah, and stop buying and selling; this is better for you if you know.

{یٰۤاَیُّهَا الَّذِیْنَ اٰمَنُوْۤا اِذَا نُوْدِیَ لِلصَّلٰوةِ مِنْ یَّوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ: O believers! When the call to prayer is given on Friday.} This verse explains the rulings of the Friday prayer. Three matters related to it are noted here:

1. “The call” in this verse means the first call, not the second call connected with the sermon. Although the first call was added during the era of Sayyiduna Uthman Ghani رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ, the obligation to prepare for the prayer and abandon buying and selling is connected with it.
2. “Hurry” does not mean run. It means to begin preparing for the prayer. According to the majority of scholars, “the remembrance of Allah” means the sermon.
3. This verse establishes the obligation of Friday prayer, the prohibition of worldly occupations such as buying and selling, the obligation of making effort for the prayer, and the sermon. (Khazain-ul-Irfan, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 9, p. 1025, excerpted.)

### Why Friday Is Called Jumu'ah

In Arabic, this day was called Arubah; later it was named Jumu'ah. The first person to call it Jumu'ah was Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy. There are various explanations for the name; one is that it is called Jumu'ah because congregations gather on this day for prayer. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 9, 4/265.)

### The First Friday Prayer of the Crowned Messenger ﷺ

Historians of the Prophetic biography state that when the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ migrated and reached Madinah Tayyibah, he stopped at Quba on Monday, 12 Rabi al-Awwal, at forenoon. He stayed there from Monday through Thursday and laid the foundation of the mosque. On Friday he set out for Madinah Tayyibah. When Friday time came in the valley of Banu Salim ibn Awf, the people made that place a mosque, and the Sovereign of both worlds ﷺ performed the Friday prayer and delivered the sermon there. This was the first Friday prayer that the Noble Prophet ﷺ performed with his Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 9, 4/266.)

### Four Virtues of Friday

Many hadith mention the virtues of Friday. Four are presented here:

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crowned Messenger ﷺ said: “The best day upon which the sun has risen is Friday. On it, Sayyiduna Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام was created, on it he entered Paradise, on it he was commanded to descend from Paradise, and the Day of Judgement will be established on Friday.” (Muslim, Book of Friday, chapter on the virtue of Friday, p. 425, hadith 18 (854).)
2. Sayyiduna Abu Darda رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Leader of the Messengers ﷺ said: “Send abundant blessings upon me on Friday, for it is a witnessed day; angels attend it, and whoever sends blessings upon me, they will be presented to me.” Sayyiduna Abu Darda asked, “And after passing away?” He replied: “Indeed, Allah Almighty has forbidden the earth from consuming the bodies of the Prophets. Allah Almighty's Prophet is alive and provided sustenance.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Funerals, chapter on mentioning his passing away and burial ﷺ, 2/291, hadith 1637.)
3. Sayyiduna Abu Lubabah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Friday is the master of all days and the greatest in Allah Almighty's sight. It is greater in His sight than Eid al-Adha and Eid al-Fitr. It contains five qualities: (1) Allah Almighty created Sayyiduna Adam عَلَیْہِ الصَّلٰوۃُ وَالسَّلَام on it; (2) He sent him down to earth on it; (3) He called him back on it; (4) there is an hour on it in which a servant asks Allah Almighty for anything and He gives it, provided he does not ask for something unlawful; and (5) the Day of Judgement will be established on it. There is no close angel, heaven, earth, wind, mountain or sea that does not fear Friday.” (Ibn Majah, Book of Establishing Prayer and its Sunnah, chapter on the virtue of Friday, 2/8, hadith 1084.)
4. Sayyiduna Jabir رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever passes away on Friday or its night will be protected from the punishment of the grave and will come on the Day of Judgement bearing the seal of the martyrs.” (Hilyat al-Awliya, mention of a group of the successors of Madinah, Muhammad ibn al-Munkadir, 3/181, hadith 3629.)

### The Hour in Which Supplication Is Accepted on Friday

There is an hour on Friday in which Allah Almighty especially accepts supplication, as mentioned in the third hadith above. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Crowned Messenger ﷺ said: “There is an hour in it when a Muslim servant finds it while standing and praying; whatever he asks Allah Almighty for will be granted.” He indicated with his hand that the time is very short. (Bukhari, Book of Friday, chapter on the hour on Friday, 1/321, hadith 935.)

There are many narrations concerning which time this is. Two are strong: (1) from when the Imam sits for the sermon until the prayer ends; (2) the last hour of Friday. (Bahar-e-Shariat, part 4, chapter on Friday, 1/754, excerpted.)

### Two Virtues of the Friday Prayer

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever performs ablution properly, comes on Friday, listens and remains silent, will be forgiven the sins committed between that Friday and the next, along with three additional days. Whoever touches pebbles has engaged in vain activity.” (Muslim, Book of Friday, chapter on the virtue of listening and remaining silent during the sermon, p. 427, hadith 27 (857).) That is, while listening to the sermon, even moving a pebble that has fallen is included in vain activity.
2. Sayyiduna Abu Sa'id رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Allah Almighty will record as a person of Paradise whoever does five things in one day: (1) visits a sick person; (2) attends a funeral; (3) fasts; (4) goes to Friday prayer; and (5) frees a slave.” (Al-Ihsan in the arrangement of Sahih Ibn Hibban, Book of Prayer, chapter on Friday prayer, 3/191, part 4, hadith 2760.)

### Warnings for Abandoning Friday Prayer

1. Sayyiduna Abu Hurayrah and Sayyiduna Abdullah ibn Umar رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا narrate that the Most Revered Prophet ﷺ said: “People must stop abandoning Friday, or Allah Almighty will seal their hearts, and then they will become among the heedless.” (Muslim, Book of Friday, chapter on the stern warning for abandoning Friday, p. 430, hadith 40 (865).)
2. Sayyiduna Usamah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ narrates that the Noble Messenger ﷺ said: “Whoever abandons three Fridays without an excuse will be recorded among the hypocrites.” (Al-Mu'jam al-Kabir, Musnad al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, chapter on what is narrated concerning the evil woman, 1/170, hadith 422.)

For the conditions of the obligation of Friday prayer and other rulings, consult Bahar-e-Shariat.

### Three Islamic Rulings Concerning the Obligation of Friday Prayer

1. Friday prayer is an individual obligation, and its obligation is more emphatic than that of Zuhr. Whoever denies it is a disbeliever. (Bahar-e-Shariat, part 4, chapter on Friday, fiqh rulings, 1/762.)
2. There are six conditions for performing Friday prayer. If even one is absent, Friday prayer is not valid: (1) the place where it is performed must be a city or its surrounding area; (2) the one conducting it must be the Islamic Sultan or his deputy whom he has authorised to establish Friday prayer; (3) it must be Zuhr time, meaning the prayer must be completed within Zuhr time. If Asr time begins during the prayer, even after the sitting for testimony, Friday becomes invalid and Zuhr must be performed as a make-up prayer; (4) a sermon must be delivered; (5) there must be a congregation of at least three men besides the Imam; and (6) general permission must be given, meaning the mosque door is open so that any Muslim who wishes may come and no one is stopped.
3. There are eleven conditions for Friday prayer to be obligatory. If even one is absent, it is not obligatory, although if the person performs it, it is valid: (1) residing in a city; (2) health, meaning it is not obligatory for a sick person who cannot reach the congregational mosque, or who could reach it only at the cost of worsening the illness or delaying recovery; (3) being free; (4) being male; (5) being sane; (6) being an adult; (7) having sight, meaning not being blind; (8) being able to walk; (9) not being imprisoned; (10) having no fear of a ruler, thief or other oppressor; and (11) there must not be such rain, wind, hail or cold that there is a genuine fear of harm.

**Note:** For further Islamic rulings concerning Friday, study “The Chapter on Friday” in Bahar-e-Shariat, volume 1, part 4.

{ذٰلِكُمْ خَیْرٌ لَّكُمْ: This is better for you.} “Better” here means better in the lexical sense: Friday prayer and its sermon are better than worldly business. This does not imply that attending is not obligatory and is merely recommended.

## Ayat 62:10

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54035

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=10; ayatId=5248; tafseerId=54035; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** فَاِذَا قُضِیَتِ الصَّلٰوةُ فَانْتَشِرُوْا فِی الْاَرْضِ وَ ابْتَغُوْا مِنْ فَضْلِ اللّٰهِ وَ اذْكُرُوا اللّٰهَ كَثِیْرًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ(10)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** Then when Salah is completed, so disperse in the land and seek Allah's benevolence (i.e. lawful sustenance), and abundantly remember Allah in the hope of attaining success.

{فَاِذَا قُضِیَتِ الصَّلٰوةُ: Then when the prayer is completed.} When the prayer has been completed, it is permissible for you to engage in earning a livelihood, acquire good deeds by seeking knowledge, visiting the sick, attending funerals, visiting scholars and engaging in similar activities. Remember Allah Almighty in every state besides prayer as well, so that you may attain success. (Khazin, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 10, 4/268; Madarik, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 10, p. 1241, excerpted.)

## Ayat 62:11

**Source:** Sirat-ul-Jinan tafseer 54036

**Metadata:** surah=62; ayat=11; ayatId=5249; tafseerId=54036; para=28; volume=10

**Arabic:** وَ اِذَا رَاَوْا تِجَارَةً اَوْ لَهْوَاﰳ انْفَضُّوْۤا اِلَیْهَا وَ تَرَكُوْكَ قَآىٕمًاؕ-قُلْ مَا عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ خَیْرٌ مِّنَ اللَّهْوِ وَ مِنَ التِّجَارَةِؕ-وَ اللّٰهُ خَیْرُ الرّٰزِقِیْنَ۠    (11)

**Kanz-ul-Iman English translation:** And when they saw some trade or sport, they walked towards it and left you standing delivering the sermon. Say you (O Beloved), 'That (i.e. the reward) which is with Allah is (far) better than sport and trade, and the sustenance of Allah is the best.'

{وَ اِذَا رَاَوْا تِجَارَةً اَوْ لَهْوًا: And when they saw some trade or sport.} **Occasion of revelation:** Sayyiduna Jabir ibn Abdullah رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُ states that once the Noble Prophet ﷺ was standing on Friday and delivering the Friday sermon when a trading caravan arrived in Madinah Tayyibah. A drum was beaten to announce it according to custom, so the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ went towards it until no one remained in the mosque except twelve men. I, Sayyiduna Abu Bakr al-Siddiq and Sayyiduna Umar al-Faruq رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمَا were among those twelve. This verse was then revealed. (Muslim, Book of Friday, chapter concerning His statement, “And when they see trade or sport, they disperse towards it,” p. 429, hadith 38 (863).)

The meaning of this verse is: O Beloved ﷺ, when they learned of some trade or heard of some amusement, they went towards it and left you standing on the pulpit while delivering the sermon. Say to them: The reward of prayer and the blessing and good fortune of remaining in the presence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, which is in reality with Allah Almighty, is better than sport and trade. Since Allah Almighty is the Best Provider, go towards Him and seek provision from Him. (Ruh al-Bayan, Al-Jumu'ah, under verse 11, 9/528.)

It should be remembered that at the time this incident occurred there was great hardship and inflation. The Companions رَضِیَ اللّٰہُ تَعَالٰی عَنْہُمْ left out of fear that goods might run out and they would fail to obtain them; in such circumstances this is natural. Moreover, before this verse was revealed they had not been prohibited from such an act. Since the command had not yet been revealed, doing this was not a sin. Therefore Allah Almighty did not condemn them in this verse; rather, He instructed them that such conduct was not worthy of their station. No objection can be made against their action.

**Note:** This proves that the preacher should deliver the sermon while standing.
