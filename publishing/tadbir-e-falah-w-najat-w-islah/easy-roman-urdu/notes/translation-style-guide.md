# Easy Roman Urdu Style Guide

- Translate the complete source into smooth, familiar Roman Urdu without summarizing or omitting.
- Preserve every ruling, argument, example, quotation, citation, endorsement, and section boundary.
- Preserve Quranic verses, complete hadith quotations, duas, Arabic honorifics such as
  `رضی الله تعالی عنہ`, and `ﷺ`.
- Romanize ordinary Arabic and Persian vocabulary, names, titles, and book names when pronunciation
  is clear. Keep Arabic only where its exact form is necessary.
- Follow the repository adab rules. In Roman Urdu prose use `Unschooled Nabi ﷺ`, never
  “Unlettered Prophet ﷺ”.
- Prefer short, natural sentences and familiar words without weakening the source's certainty.
