# Standard Faithful English Style Guide

- Translate the complete source faithfully without summarizing, omitting, or adding commentary.
- Preserve every ruling, argument, example, quotation, citation, endorsement, and section boundary.
- Preserve Quranic verses, complete hadith quotations, duas, `ﷺ`, and Arabic honorifics such as
  `رضی الله تعالی عنہ`.
- Use reverent English for the Prophet ﷺ and never use “died,” “death of the Prophet,” “no more
  than,” or “unlettered” for him. Use “passed away” and “Unschooled Prophet ﷺ” where required.
- Keep the author's scholarly, legal, and devotional tone.
