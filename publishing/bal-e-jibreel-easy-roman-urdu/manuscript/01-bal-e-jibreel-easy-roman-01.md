# Bal-e-Jibreel: Easy Roman Urdu Tashreeh

Yeh manuscript `content/books/bal-e-jibreel.json` ke pehle paanch kalam ki source-traceable tashreeh hai. Har kalam ka Roman Urdu matn source ke mutabiq diya gaya hai; sirf Latin honorific ko adab ke liye `ﷺ` mein badla gaya hai.

## Kalam 001: meri-nawa-e-shauk

**Title:** Meri Nawa’ay Shauq Se

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/meri-nawa-e-shauk/

### Preserved Roman Urdu Kalam

```text
Meri Nawa’ay Shauq Se Shor Hareem-e-Zaat Mein
Ghalghala Ha’ay Al-Aman But Kada’ay Sifaat Mein

Hoor-o-Farishta Hain Aseer Mere Takhaiyulat Mein
Meri Nigah Se Khalal Teri Tajalliyat Mein

Garche Hai Meri Justujoo Dair-o-Haram Ki Naqsh Band
Meri Faghan Se Rustkhaiz Kaaba-o-Somanat Mein

Gah Meri Nigah-e-Taiz Cheer Gyi Dil-e-Wujood
Gah Uljah Ke Reh Gyi Meri Touwahamaat Mein

Tu Ne Ye Kya Ghazab Kiya, Mujh Ko Bhi Fash Kar Diya
Main Hi Tou Aik Raaz Tha Sina’ay Kainat Mein !
```

### Band 1: Hareem-e-Zaat aur But-Kada-e-Sifaat

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Hareem-e-zaat`: Allah Ta'ala ki zaat ka muqaddas aur posheeda maqam.
- `Ghalghala-e-al-aman`: panah ki buland pukar aur zor-dar halchal.
- `But-kada-e-sifaat`: zahiri sifat aur nishaniyon mein ulajhne ka isti'ara.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair ke shauq bhare naghme se Allah Ta'ala ki zaat ke muqaddas parde mein bhi shor paida hota hai. `Ghalghala-e-al-aman` se aisi pukar aur halchal murad hai jaise har taraf panah ki aawaz uth rahi ho. `But-kada-e-sifaat` un zahiri tasavvur aur sifat ke ghar ka isti'ara hai jahan insaan asal zaat ke bajaye alag alag nishaniyon mein ulajh sakta hai. Shaair ka shauq roohani sukoon ko be-hissi nahin rehne deta; woh gehri haqeeqat ko jagata hai.

### Band 2: Takhaiyul ki quwwat

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Aseer`: qaid ya bandha hua.
- `Takhaiyulat`: buland khayalat aur tasavvur.
- `Tajalliyat`: ilahi jalwe aur zahoorat.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Hoor aur farishte bhi shaair ke buland tasavvur ke qaid nazar aate hain. Is ka matlab yeh nahin ke makhlooq haqeeqatan qaid hai, balki shaair ka khayal husn, paaki aur roohani manazir ko apne andar samet leta hai. Saath hi woh kehta hai ke meri nigah se teri tajalliyat mein khalal padta hai. Insani nigah jab ilahi jalwe ko samajhne ki koshish karti hai to us mein apni had, hairat aur beqarari ka rang aa jata hai.

### Band 3: Dair-o-Haram se aage

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Dair-o-haram`: mukhtalif mazhabi markazon ki alamat.
- `Naqsh-band`: naqsha banane ya nishan qaim karne wala.
- `Rustkhaiz`: bohat zor-dar inqilab aur hungaama.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair ki justuju dair aur haram dono ke naqsh banati hai, yani woh mukhtalif mazhabi alamat aur markaz ko samajhne ki koshish karta hai. Lekin us ki faryad Kaaba aur Somanat dono mein rustkhaiz paida kar deti hai. Yahan Kaaba aur Somanat mukhtalif aqeede aur bandishon ki alamat ban kar aate hain; sachchi talab sirf zahiri naqsh par nahin rukti aur dilon ko gehri bedari ki taraf jhinjhor deti hai.

### Band 4: Nigah aur wahm

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Nigah-e-taiz`: gehri aur tez basirat wali nazar.
- `Dil-e-wujood`: wujood ki andarooni haqeeqat ka markaz.
- `Tawahhamaat`: wahm, andeshe aur zehni uljhanen.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Kabhi shaair ki tez basirat wujood ke dil ko cheer deti hai, yani woh cheezon ki satah se guzr kar un ke andar chhupi haqeeqat ko dekh leti hai. Magar kabhi wahi nigah apne hi wahm aur andeshe mein phans jati hai. Is band mein insani idraak ki dono suratain hain: gehri kashf jaisi samajh aur apne tasavvur ki uljhan.

### Band 5: Kainat ka raaz zahir hona

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Fash`: posheeda baat ka zahir ho jana.
- `Sina-e-kainat`: kainat ka seena, yani us ka andarooni raaz.
- `Tajalli`: ilahi jalwa jo batin ko roshan kare.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair Allah Ta'ala se mukhatab hokar kehta hai ke Tu ne mujhe bhi zahir kar diya. Woh apne aap ko kainat ke seene mein chhupa hua ek raaz samajhta tha. Ab ilahi tajalli aur shauq ki aag ne us ke batin ko khol diya. Yahan `fash` hone ka ma'ni ruswai nahin, balki posheeda roohani sach ka zahir ho jana hai.

### Markazi Paigham

Sachchi roohani talab insaan ko zahiri nishaniyon se utha kar kainat aur apne batin ke gehre raaz tak le jati hai. Is raah mein nigah kabhi haqeeqat ko cheer kar dekhti hai aur kabhi apne wahm se ladti hai.

## Kalam 002: agar-kaj-ro-hain-anjum-asman-tera-hai-ya-mera

**Title:** Agar Kaj Ro Hain Anjum…(If the stars have strayed…)

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/agar-kaj-ro-hain-anjum-asman-tera-hai-ya-mera/

### Preserved Roman Urdu Kalam

```text
Agar Kaj Ro Hain Anjum, Asman Tera Hai Ya Mera
Mujhe Fikar-e-Jahan Kyun Ho, Jahan Tera Hai Ya Mera?

Agar Hangama Ha’ay Shauq Se Hai La-Makan Khali
Khata Kis Ki Hai Ya Rab ! La-Makan Tera Hai Ya Mera?

Usse Subah-e-Azal Inkaar Ki Juraat Hui Kyun Kar
Mujhe Maloom Kya, Woh Raazdan Tera Hai Ya Mera?

Muhammad ﷺ Bhi Tera, Jibreel Bhi, Quran Bhi Tera
Magar Ye Harf-e-Shireen Tarjuman Tera Hai Ya Mera?

Issi Koukab Ki Tabani Se Hai Tera Jahan Roshan
Zawal-e-Adam-e-Khaki Zayan Tera Hai Ya Mera?
```

### Band 1: Sitare aur aasman

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Kaj-ro`: tedhi ya seedhi raah se hati hui.
- `Anjum`: sitare.
- `Fikr-e-jahan`: duniya ki zimmedari aur us ki fikr.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Agar sitare apni seedhi raah se hat gaye hain to shaair Allah Ta'ala se sawal karta hai: aasman Tera hai ya mera? Phir woh kehta hai ke mujhe duniya ki fikr kyun ho, jab poori duniya bhi Teri ya meri zimmedari ke sawal se judi hai. Lehja shikayat ka hai, lekin asal mein yeh banda aur Rab ke darmiyan gehri qurbat aur jawab-talab rishta dikhata hai.

### Band 2: La-makan ki khamoshi

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `La-makan`: jagah ki hud se pare aalam.
- `Hangama-e-shauq`: roohani talab ka zor-dar shor.
- `Khata`: qasoor ya kami.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Agar la-makan, yani jagah ki hud se pare aalam, shauq ke hungame se khaali hai to is mein qasoor kis ka hai? Shaair apni roohani beqarari ko itna wasee samajhta hai ke us ka shor la-makan tak pahunchna chahiye. Sawal apne aap ko bhi samet-ta hai: kami bande mein hai ya us ke liye banaye gaye aalam ki samajh mein?

### Band 3: Azal ka inkaar

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Subah-e-azal`: ibtida se pehle ka azal ka waqt.
- `Juraat-e-inkaar`: inkaar karne ki himmat.
- `Raazdan`: asal raaz jaanne wala.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Iblis ke azal ke inkaar ki taraf ishara karte hue shaair poochta hai ke usay inkaar ki jurat kaise hui. Phir woh kehta hai ke mujhe kya maloom, woh raazdan Tera hai ya mera. `Raazdan` yahan us hasti ka ishara hai jo is azeem waqia ka asal raaz jaanti hai. Shaair jawab ka da'wa nahin karta; woh hairat aur sawal ko zinda rakhta hai.

### Band 4: Muhammad, Jibreel aur Quran

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Harf-e-shireen`: meetha aur dil-nasheen lafz.
- `Tarjuman`: paigham ko bayan karne wala.
- `Tera ya mera`: ilahi ata aur shaair ki zaban ke talluq ka sawal.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Muhammad ﷺ, Jibreel aur Quran sab Allah Ta'ala ke hain. Magar jo meetha aur dil-nasheen lafz is paigham ka tarjuman banta hai, shaair poochta hai ke woh Tera hai ya mera. Is mein shaairi ki zimmedari ka sawal hai: kya shaair apni aawaz se ilahi ma'ni ko bayan kar raha hai, ya us ki zaban bhi ilahi ata ka hissa hai?

### Band 5: Koukab ki roshni aur insaan ka zawal

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Koukab`: sitara, yahan roshni ki alamat.
- `Tabani`: chamak aur roshni.
- `Adam-e-khaki`: mitti se bana hua insaan.
- `Zawal`: girawat ya khatme ki taraf jana.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Usi ek sitare ki roshni se Allah Ta'ala ki duniya roshan hai. `Koukab` roshni aur hidayat ki alamat ban jata hai. Shaair phir poochta hai ke mitti se bane insaan ka zawal kis ka ziyan hai. Insaan girta hai to nuqsan sirf us ka nahin; us roshni se roshan poori duniya bhi apni ek azeem salahiyat kho deti hai.

### Markazi Paigham

Yeh kalam bande aur Rab ke darmiyan sawal, qurbat aur zimmedari ka gehra mukalma hai. Kainat ki har roshni aur insaan ki har girawat ko ilahi nizam aur insani kirdar ke saath jor kar dekha gaya hai.

## Kalam 003: tere-shishe-mein-mai-baqi-nahin-hai

**Title:** Tere Shishe Mein May Baqi Nahin Hai

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/tere-shishe-mein-mai-baqi-nahin-hai/

### Preserved Roman Urdu Kalam

```text
Tere Shishe Mein May Baqi Nahin Hai
Bata, Kya Tu Mera Saqi Nahi Hai

Samundar Se Mille Piyase Ko Shabnam
Bakheeli Hai Ye Razzaqi Nahin Hai
```

### Band 1: Khaali shisha aur Saqi

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Shisha`: dil ya rooh ka bartan.
- `May`: roohani faiz, josh ya zindagi dene wali kaifiyat.
- `Saqi`: faiz aur ata dene wala mukhatab.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair kehta hai ke tere shishe mein may baqi nahin rahi, phir poochta hai: kya tu mera Saqi nahin? Shisha yahan dil ya rooh ka bartan hai aur may roohani faiz, josh ya zindagi dene wali kaifiyat ka isti'ara. Khaali pan ke bawajood shaair Saqi se umeed nahin chhorta; woh yaad dilata hai ke pilane wala agar waqai Saqi hai to shisha phir bhar sakta hai.

### Band 2: Samundar ke samne shabnam

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Shabnam`: subah ke waqt jamne wale pani ke chhote qatre.
- `Razzaqi`: rizq aur zaroorat poori karne ka amal.
- `Bakheeli`: zaroorat ke muqable mein bohat kam dena.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Piyase ko samundar ke bajaye sirf shabnam mile to yeh ata nahin, kanjoosi hai. `Razzaqi` rizq aur zaroorat poori karne ka amal hai; shaair is lafz ko sawal bana kar kehta hai ke zarooratmand ko us ki haisiyat ke mutabiq dena chahiye. Samundar be-panah imkan ka isti'ara hai aur shabnam bahut chhoti miqdaar ka. Yahan roohani aur insani dono satah par kami par shikayat hai.

### Markazi Paigham

Jis ke paas wasee faiz ho us ka zarooratmand ko bohat thoda dena karam nahin. Talib apne Saqi se mukammal aur zindagi-bakhsh ata ka mutalaba karta hai.

## Kalam 004: gaisu-e-tabdar-ko-aur-bhi-tabdar-kar

**Title:** Gaisuay Tabdar Ko Aur Bhi Tabdar Kar (Bright are Your tresses: brighten them even more)

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/gaisu-e-tabdar-ko-aur-bhi-tabdar-kar/

### Preserved Roman Urdu Kalam

```text
Gaisuay Tabdar Ko Aur Bhi Tabdar Kar
Hosh-o-Khirad Shakar Kar, Qalb-o-Nazar Shakar Kar

Ishq Bhi Ho Hijab Mein, Husn Bhi Ho Hijab Mein
Ya Tu Khud Ashkar Ho Ya Mujhe Aashkar Kar

Tu Hai Muheet-e-Bekaran, Mein Hun Zara Si Aabjoo
Ya Mujhe Hamkinar Kar Ya Mujhe Bekinar Kar

Main Hun Sadaf Tou Tere Hath Mere Guhar Ki Abroo
Mein Hun Khazaf Tou Tu Mujhe Gohar-e-Shahwar Kar

Naghma’ay Nau Bahar Agar Mere Naseeb Mein Na Ho
Iss Dam-e-Neem Souz Ko Taeerak-e-Bahar Kar

Bagh-e-Bahisht Se Mujhe Hukam-e-Safar Diya Tha Kyun
Kaar-e-Jahan Daraz Hai, Ab Mera Intizar Kar

Roz-e-Hisab Jab Mera Paish Ho Daftar-e-Amal
Tu Bhi Sharamsaar Ho, Mujh Ko Bhi Sharamsaar Kar
```

### Band 1: Roshni aur bedari

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Gaisu-e-tabdar`: chamakdar aur roshan zulfein.
- `Hosh-o-khirad`: hosh aur aqal.
- `Qalb-o-nazar`: dil aur andar dekhne wali basirat.
- `Shikar karna`: yahan apni taraf kheench lena.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair mukhatab se kehta hai ke roshan zulfon ko aur roshan kar, aur meri hosh-o-khirad, dil aur nigah ko shikar kar. Yahan zulfein jalwe aur husn ki alamat hain. `Hosh-o-khirad ko shikar karna` ka matlab yeh hai ke ilahi jalwa itna ghalib ho ke sirf thandi aqal nahin, poora dil aur basirat bhi us ki taraf kheench jaye.

### Band 2: Hijab aur zahoor

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Hijab`: parda ya posheedgi.
- `Ashkar`: zahir aur wazeh.
- `Aashkar karna`: pehchan aur samajh ke liye roshan kar dena.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Ishq bhi parde mein hai aur husn bhi parde mein. Shaair dua karta hai: ya to Tu khud zahir ho ja, ya mujhe apni pehchan ke qabil bana de. `Ashkar` hona zahir aur wazeh hona hai. Talib apni taraf se parda uthane ka da'wa nahin karta; woh ilahi zahoor ya apni basirat ke khulne ki dua karta hai.

### Band 3: Qatra aur samundar

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Muheet-e-bekaran`: be-had aur har taraf phaila hua.
- `Aabjoo`: chhoti si pani ki dhaar.
- `Hamkinar`: kisi kinare tak pahuncha hua.
- `Bekinar`: jis ki koi hadd ya kinara na ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Mukhatab be-kinar samundar hai aur shaair apne aap ko chhoti si pani ki dhaar kehta hai. Woh chahta hai ke ya to usay samundar ke kinare tak pahuncha diya jaye, ya phir us ki apni hud hi mita di jaye. Is mein bande ki mahdood zaat ka ilahi be-had wus'at mein milne ka shauq hai.

### Band 4: Sadaf, moti aur khazaf

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Sadaf`: seep jis mein moti banta hai.
- `Guhar`: moti.
- `Khazaf`: mamooli mitti ka tukra ya thikri.
- `Gohar-e-shahwar`: qeemti aur buland martabe ka moti.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Agar main sadaf hoon to mere andar banne wale moti ki izzat Tere haath mein hai. Aur agar main sirf khazaf, yani mamooli thikri, hoon to Tu mujhe qeemti moti bana de. Shaair apni salahiyat par fakhr nahin karta; woh apni qeemat ko ilahi tarbiyat aur karam ka natija samajhta hai.

### Band 5: Kamzor saans aur bahar ka parinda

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Naghma-e-nau-bahar`: nayi bahar ka geet.
- `Dam-e-neem-souz`: aadha jalta hua, dard se bhara kamzor saans.
- `Taeerak-e-bahar`: bahar ka chhota parinda.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Agar nayi bahar ka naghma mere naseeb mein nahin to mere aadhe-jale saans ko hi bahar ke parinde ki aawaz bana de. `Dam-e-neem-souz` thaki hui, dard se jalti saans hai. Shaair chahta hai ke us ka apna dard bhi be-maqsad aah na rahe, balki bahar, umeed aur zindagi ka paigham suna de.

### Band 6: Jannat se duniya ka safar

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Bagh-e-bahisht`: jannat ka baagh.
- `Hukm-e-safar`: rawangi ka diya hua hukm.
- `Kaar-e-jahan`: duniya ka lamba aur mushkil kaam.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair poochta hai ke mujhe Bagh-e-Bahisht se safar ka hukm kyun diya gaya. Duniya ka kaam lamba aur mushkil hai, is liye woh mukhatab se kehta hai ke ab mera intezar kar. Is band mein duniya ko imtihan, mehnat aur zimmedari ki jagah samjha gaya hai; wapas lautne ki umeed bhi is mein maujood hai.

### Band 7: Roz-e-hisab

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Roz-e-hisab`: aamaal ka hisaab hone ka din.
- `Daftar-e-amal`: insaan ke aamaal ka record.
- `Sharamsaar`: apni kami par sharminda.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Qayamat ke din jab us ka daftar-e-amal pesh ho to shaair dua karta hai ke Tu bhi sharminda ho aur mujhe bhi sharminda kar. Yeh lafz adab ke daire mein ek shairana shikayat hai: agar banda apni di hui roohani salahiyat ka haq ada na kar saka to us ki kami numayan ho. Sharmindagi yahan bedari aur hisaab ki sakht sachchai hai.

### Markazi Paigham

Insaan ilahi husn, ishq aur roshni ka talabgar hai. Woh apni kamzori ko karam se quwwat, apne dard ko bahar ka naghma aur apni mitti ko qeemti moti banwane ki dua karta hai, saath hi apne aamaal ke hisaab se be-khabar nahin rehta.

## Kalam 005: asar-kare-na-kare-sun-to-le-meri-faryad

**Title:** Asar Kare Na Kare, Sun To Le Meri Faryad (Whether or not it moves you, At least listen to my complaint)

**Source URL:** https://allamaiqbal.org/bal-e-jibril/asar-kare-na-kare-sun-to-le-meri-faryad/

### Preserved Roman Urdu Kalam

```text
Asar Kare Na Kare, Sun To Le Meri Faryad
Nahin Hai Dad Ka Talib Ye Banda’ay Azad

Ye Musht-e-Khaak, Ye Sar Sar, Ye Wusaat-e-Aflak
Karam Hai Ya Ke Sitam Teri Lazzat-e-Ijad

Thehar Saka Na Huwa’ay Chaman Mein Khaima’ay Gul
Ye Hai Fasal-e-Bahari, Ye Hai Baad-e-Murad

Kasoorwar, Ghareeb-ud-Diyar Hun, Lekin
Tera Kharaba Farishte Na Kar Sake Abad

Meri Jafa Talbi Ko Duaen Deta Hai
Woh Dasht-e-Sada, Woh Tera Jahan-e-Bebunyad

Khatar Pasand Tabiyat Ko Saazgaar Nahin
Woh Gulistan Ke Jahan Ghaat Mein Na Ho Sayyad

Maqam-e-Shauq Tere Qudsiyon Ke Bus Ka Nahin
Unhi Ka Kaam Hai Ye Jin Ke Hosle Hain Zayyad
```

### Band 1: Azaad bande ki faryad

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Faryad`: dard bhari pukar.
- `Dad`: inaam, daad ya tareef.
- `Banda-e-azad`: ghair-Allah ki chaploosi se azaad rooh.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair kehta hai ke meri faryad asar kare ya na kare, kam az kam use sun to liya jaye. Yeh banda azaad hai aur use kisi inaam ya dad ki talab nahin. `Banda-e-azad` ghair-Allah ki chaploosi aur zaleel talab se azaad rooh ka naam hai. Us ki faryad ka maqsad sach kehna hai, badla lena nahin.

### Band 2: Mitti, hawa aur aflak

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Musht-e-khaak`: mitti ki ek muthi.
- `Sar-sar`: tez aur sarsarahati hawa.
- `Wus'at-e-aflak`: aasman ki be-panah wus'at.
- `Lazzat-e-ijad`: paidaish ke amal ki dilchasp shaan.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Ek taraf mitti ki muthi aur tez hawa hai, doosri taraf aasman ki be-panah wus'at. Shaair is poore paidaishi manzar ko dekh kar poochta hai ke yeh karam hai ya sitam, aur is mein paida karne ki kya luzzat hai. `Lazzat-e-ijad` paidaish ke amal ki dilchasp aur hairat-angez shaan hai; sawal mein shukr, hairat aur shikayat teenon rang milte hain.

### Band 3: Phool ka naazuk khaima

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Khaima-e-gul`: phool ka naazuk aur aarzi thikana.
- `Fasal-e-bahari`: bahar ka mausam.
- `Baad-e-murad`: tamanna ke mutabiq chalne wali hawa.
- `Na-payedari`: zyada der tak qaim na rehna.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Chaman ki hawa mein phool ka khaima tik nahin saka. Phir shaair poochta hai: kya isi ko bahar ka mausam aur tamanna ke mutabiq chalne wali hawa kehte hain? Phool ki na-payedari se duniya ki khubsurti ki kamzori samne aati hai. Sirf bahar ka naam kaafi nahin; us mein thaharne wali zindagi aur hifazat bhi honi chahiye.

### Band 4: Gharib-ud-diyar aur ujri duniya

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Ghareeb-ud-diyar`: apne watan se door shakhs.
- `Kharaba`: ujri hui jagah ya bigri hui duniya.
- `Abad karna`: ujri jagah ko phir basa dena.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair apne aap ko apne watan se door aur qasoorwar manta hai, lekin kehta hai ke Teri ujri hui duniya ko farishte bhi abad na kar sake. `Kharaba` yahan duniya ki bigri hui halat ka isti'ara hai. Apni ghalati ka iqrar karne ke saath woh is baat par bhi zor deta hai ke asal islaah sirf zahiri quwwat se nahin hoti.

### Band 5: Jafa-talabi aur saada dasht

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Jafa-talabi`: sakhti aur imtihan ki talab.
- `Dasht-e-sada`: seedha, saada aur be-aasra sehra.
- `Jahan-e-bebunyad`: aisa jahan jo mazboot bunyaad se khaali ho.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair ki mushkil talab ko woh saada dasht dua deta hai. `Jafa-talabi` se murad aasaan aaraam ke bajaye imtihan, sakhti aur tajurbe ki talab hai. Saada sehra us jahan ki alamat hai jahan bunyadi sach aur khula maidan hai; woh shaair ki is sakht pasandi ko samajhta hai, kyunki aisi talab insaan ko mazboot banati hai.

### Band 6: Sayyad ke baghair gulistan

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Khatar-pasand tabiyat`: khatre aur imtihan ko pasand karne wali fitrat.
- `Gulistan`: phoolon ka baagh, yahan aaraam ki zindagi.
- `Ghaat`: chhup kar hamle ka mauqa.
- `Sayyad`: shikari.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Khatar pasand tabiyat ko aisa gulistan pasand nahin jahan shikari ki ghaat na ho. Yani jis zindagi mein muqabla, khatra aur imtihan na ho woh buland himmat rooh ko be-maza lagti hai. `Sayyad` shikari hai; us ki ghaat zindagi ke un khatron ki alamat hai jo quwwat, hosh aur bahaduri ko jagate hain.

### Band 7: Maqam-e-shauq

**Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni:**
- `Maqam-e-shauq`: roohani talab aur jazbe ki buland manzil.
- `Qudsiyan`: muqaddas farishte.
- `Hosle zayyad`: bohat buland aur mazboot hausle.

**Easy Roman Urdu Explanation:** Shaair kehta hai ke shauq ka yeh buland maqam sirf muqaddas farishton ke bas ka nahin. Yeh un logon ka kaam hai jin ke hosle bohat zyada hain. Is ka matlab yeh hai ke beqarar talab, mushkil safar aur khatre se guzarna kamzor irade walon ka kaam nahin; roohani bulandi ke liye insani himmat aur lagataar koshish darkar hai.

### Markazi Paigham

Zinda aur azaad rooh aaraam, inaam aur be-khatri ki talabgar nahin hoti. Woh sach ki faryad karti hai, paidaish ke raaz par sawal uthati hai, mushkil ko qabool karti hai aur buland shauq ke liye bade hosle ka mutalaba karti hai.
