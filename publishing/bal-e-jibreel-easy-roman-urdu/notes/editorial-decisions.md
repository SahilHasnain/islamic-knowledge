# Editorial Decisions

- Is pehle batch mein sirf `content/books/bal-e-jibreel.json` ko source banaya gaya hai.
- JSON ke pehle paanch kalam ko usi tartib mein shamil kiya gaya hai.
- `title_roman`, `url` aur `transliteration` ko source-traceability ke liye preserve kiya gaya hai; sirf `S.A.W.` ko `ﷺ` mein normalize kiya gaya hai.
- Roman Urdu transliteration mein source ki spelling, punctuation aur khaali line wali stanza breaks ko jaan-boojh kar correct ya modernize nahin kiya gaya.
- Explanation mein `band` se ek khaali line se alag hone wala stanza murad hai.
- Jahan isti'ara ka ma'ni mumkin taur par roohani ya falsafiyana hai, wahan tashreeh ne lafzon ki simt wazeh ki hai, magar source se bahar koi qissa ya aqeeda nahin jora.
- Source mein `S.A.W.` ya `Muhammad (S.A.W.)` ho to isay adab ke liye `ﷺ` mein normalize kiya jayega.
- Allah Ta'ala ke liye tashreeh mein `Tu` aur `Tera` likha jayega; `Aap` ya `Aap ka` istemal nahin hoga.
- Mushkil lafz pehli baar aane par bracket ya seedhe jumle mein aasaan Roman Urdu ma'ni ke saath samjhaya jayega; baad mein glossary mein darj faisla follow kiya jayega.
- Har band ki tashreeh se pehle alag `Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni` section diya jayega. Is mein
  sirf us band ke zaroori mushkil alfaaz aur context-based isti'are shamil honge; ek kalam ke
  tamam alfaaz ki mushtarka list nahin banegi.
