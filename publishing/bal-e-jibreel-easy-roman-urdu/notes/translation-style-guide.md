# Bal-e-Jibreel Easy Roman Urdu

## Maqsad

Yeh project Bal-e-Jibreel ke kalam ki stanza-ba-stanza tashreeh pesh karta hai. Asal kalam ko badla, mukhtasar, ya prose mein tabdeel nahin kiya jayega. Har kalam ke baad seedhi, fitri aur aasaan Roman Urdu mein us ke har band ka matlab samjhaya jayega.

## Source aur traceability

- Wahid source `content/books/bal-e-jibreel.json` hai.
- Har entry mein JSON ka kalam number, slug, title, source URL aur mukammal `transliteration` shamil hogi.
- Roman Urdu kalam mein title, line order, punctuation aur khaali line ke zariye band ki taqseem jaisi source mein hai waisi hi rakhi jayegi. Sirf adab ke liye `S.A.W.` ko `ﷺ` mein badla jayega.
- Tashreeh sirf source ke alfaaz aur un ke zahir mafhoom se nikali jayegi; bahar ka hawala ya ghair-mustanad ma'ni shamil nahin hoga.

## Zaban

- Chhote jumle, aam lafz aur fitri Roman Urdu istemal ki jayegi.
- Mushkil Farsi, Arabi aur Urdu alfaaz ka seedha ma'ni pehli baar wazeh kiya jayega, aam tor par
  bracket ya usi jumle mein. Agar lafz shaairi, Islami istilah, ya asal mafhoom ke liye zaroori
  ho to asal lafz bhi rakha jayega aur us ka aasaan ma'ni diya jayega.
- Har band ke baad nahin, balki har band ki tashreeh se bilkul pehle `Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni`
  ka alag section hoga. Is mein sirf us band ke mushkil alfaaz, compounds aur zaroori isti'are
  honge; un ke ma'ni usi band ke context ke mutabiq aasaan Roman Urdu mein diye jayenge.
- English-style plural `-s` nahin lagaya jayega. `ash'aar`, `aayaat`, `ahadees`, `band` aur doosre munasib plural istemal honge.
- Allah Ta'ala ke liye `Tu` aur `Tera` istemal hoga; `Aap` ya `Aap ka` nahin.
- Shaair ke isti'are aur falsafiyana ishare ko sada alfaaz mein samjhaya jayega, lekin un ki shiddat ya simt kam nahin ki jayegi.

## Adab aur hifazat

- Arabic matn agar source mein ho to bilkul waisa hi rakha jayega.
- Duas, Quranic matn, hadees, honorific aur `ﷺ` ko nahin badla jayega.
- Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ke liye adab qayam rakha jayega. Source mein maujood `S.A.W.` ya `Muhammad (S.A.W.)` ko `ﷺ` mein normalize kiya jayega.
- Tafseer ko shaair ke kalam ka badal nahin banaya jayega; pehle poora preserved kalam, phir tashreeh hogi.
- Har band ka tarteeb yeh hogi: heading, `Mushkil Alfaaz aur Ma'ni`, phir `Easy Roman Urdu
  Explanation`. Ek hi kalam ke liye ek lambi combined vocabulary list nahin banayi jayegi.
